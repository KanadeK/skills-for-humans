## 中文

当你已[核对蒸锅适用](../check-an-open-pot-steamer-setup/SKILL.md)，但本批食物还没放入蒸篮时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**蒸汽能按配方接触本批食物、篮具和盖仍稳定**，不是把最多食物压进同一只篮。这里优先处理普通蔬菜或有可靠蒸制说明的同类食物；混合生动物性食物与即食食品、婴幼儿食品和保存加工不在本流程内。

### 准备与输入

确认配方的食物种类、每件大小/厚度、建议批量、单层或多层要求和结束检查。洗切或其他预处理照可靠说明，不在蒸篮里用危险刀工补救大块。篮底开孔、锅盖空间和承载量已确认。若食物中有需不同时间、不同安全终点的类型，先分别计划批次，不把“都在蒸篮里”当成同一完成条件。

### 执行步骤

1. **按真实一次量摆放。** 将食物放进适用篮具，按配方尽量铺成松散单层；留出可见或可确认的孔隙与上方空间，让蒸汽能环绕。若说明允许多层，仍按它的层数与厚度限制做，不自行塞满。
2. **让同批有相近的加热需求。** 太大的一块和细碎的一把若结束条件不同，分批或按可靠配方明确先后；不要等细小部分过熟才发现厚块还未达到要求。配方要求特定切块尺寸时照做，不靠随意加时弥补。
3. **核对衬垫与容器。** 只有器具说明和配方允许时才使用蒸垫或耐热盘，并保留它要求的蒸汽孔和通道。普通塑料、密封袋或一整张遮满孔的纸不是默认蒸具；材料适用性不明就不用。
4. **冷态试盖。** 盖子应按说明稳放，不压住最高的食物，也不因为篮子过满而翘起。若盖不上、篮子摇晃、食物垂入水里，先减小批量或改器具，别用手持续按住盖子开蒸。
5. **为检查留下访问路径。** 确认能在配方规定时用合适工具取一件代表性的食物、按其终点核对；需食物安全核心温度时，使用适用的[熟度流程](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)，不因外层冒热气就宣布整批完成。

### 完成与停止

本批食物排列符合配方与器具限制，蒸汽通路、盖子和取样路径都可用，就完成摆放。若篮具过满、材料不明、食品类型有不同安全要求却无法分批，或盖子/支撑不稳，停止这批并改量或查可靠配方；不靠“蒸久一点”覆盖结构性不均匀。

### 常见报错与补救

- **篮子里堆成一座小山：** 分成小批，按每批实际开始事件重新计时；第一批时间不能自动送给第二批。
- **衬垫把孔全盖住：** 改按说明留孔或用适用篮具直接盛放，先确定食物不会掉入水里。
- **大块和细块混放：** 按配方切成相应尺寸或分开烹调，不用同一个计时器判两个终点。
- **盖子压住食物：** 减量或换适合的锅/篮，别让盖子靠食物支撑。
- **水已经沸腾才发现摆放不稳：** 先安全停热、让蒸汽减弱，再按冷态方式重排；不伸手进热篮里整理。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里按普通非压力蒸锅和具体配方摆放，不断言不同食物以同样时间熟。蒸篮形状、孔、食品尺寸和层数随设备变化；器具说明及适用食品安全指导优先。行动或视力不便时可在冷态用托盘分批整理、请现场可信的人描述空隙，或用有合适提手的篮具；开火后不以裸手调整。现实副作用是多一批等待，但每批都能被同一配方真正看见。

## English

Load this Skill after [checking the steamer setup](../check-an-open-pot-steamer-setup/SKILL.md) but before loading this batch. Arrange food so **steam can reach it as the recipe intends and the basket and lid remain stable**. The aim is not to compress the maximum food into one basket. This covers ordinary vegetables or similar foods with reliable steaming directions. Mixed raw animal food above ready-to-eat food, infant food, and preservation processing are outside this flow.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the food, piece size and thickness, recommended batch, one-layer or permitted-layer instruction, and completion check. Wash, cut, or otherwise prepare it according to reliable directions; do not fix oversized pieces with unsafe knife work in a basket. Basket perforations, lid space, and supported load have been checked. If foods need different times or safe endpoints, plan separate batches instead of treating “in one basket” as one completion condition.

### Execution steps

1. **Lay out a real batch.** Place food in the approved basket, preferably in a loose single layer when the recipe calls for it. Leave observable or otherwise confirmed gaps and upper space for steam to move around it. If the directions allow layers, keep their actual layer and thickness limits rather than improvising a pile.
2. **Group similar heating needs.** A thick piece and a handful of small pieces may have different endpoints. Divide them or follow a reliable stated loading order. Do not overcook the small pieces while hoping the thick one catches up. Use the cut sizes the recipe specifies instead of adding arbitrary time later.
3. **Check liners and containers.** Use a liner or heat-safe dish only when the equipment and recipe permit it, preserving the steam openings they require. Ordinary plastic, sealed bags, and a sheet covering every hole are not default steamer accessories. Leave out a material whose suitability is unknown.
4. **Test the lid while cold.** It should sit as intended without crushing the highest food or tilting because the basket is full. Reduce the batch or change equipment if the lid will not close, the basket rocks, or food hangs into water. Do not plan to press the lid down by hand during steaming.
5. **Leave a path for an endpoint check.** Be able to lift a representative piece with a suitable tool when the recipe says to check it. If food needs a safe internal temperature, use the applicable [doneness process](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md); steam around the outside is not proof that the centre is done.

### Success and stop

This batch matches recipe and equipment limits, with an open steam path, stable lid, and workable sample access. Stop and resize or check reliable directions if it is overcrowded, a material is unverified, foods with different safety requirements cannot be separated, or lid or support is unstable. “Steam longer” does not repair a blocked layout.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The basket is a mound:** Divide it into smaller batches and restart timing at the real event for each. The first batch cannot lend its time to the second.
- **A liner covers every opening:** Adjust it under its instructions or use the approved basket directly, while keeping food from falling into the water.
- **Large and small pieces are mixed:** Use the recipe's cut size or separate cooking paths; one timer does not certify two endpoints.
- **The lid rests on food:** Reduce the amount or change pot or basket. Food is not a lid support.
- **You notice an unstable layout after water boils:** Stop heat safely and let steam subside before rearranging as you would while cold. Do not reach into a hot basket with bare hands.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary non-pressure steaming to a specific recipe, not a claim that different foods finish together. Basket shape, perforations, food dimensions, and allowable layers vary by equipment; its manual and food-specific safety guidance take priority. For movement or visual access, arrange cold batches on a tray, ask someone present to describe spacing, or use a basket with a manageable handle. Do not rearrange hot food with bare hands. An extra batch may wait, but each batch gets the steam path its recipe actually requires.
