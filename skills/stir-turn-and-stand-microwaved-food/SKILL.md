## 中文

当食品包装或可靠配方的微波加热已经进行到**指定搅拌、翻面、转向或静置事件**时，加载这份 Skill。你要完成那些**让热量重新分布的实际动作和食品要求的等待**，再交给完成检查；不是在外圈很烫时直接宣布整份可吃。此流程不为未说明的生动物性食品发明安全做法，也不替设备维修。

### 准备与输入

确认当前食品、份量、已加热时长、食品说明要求的是搅拌/翻面/转盘中的哪一种，以及盖/膜怎样安全打开和重新放回。备好适用工具、隔热用品、耐热落点和计时器；容器可能比外观看上去烫。食物或机器有火花、烟和异常声响时先停止并退出日常加热，不在热容器前继续按计划搅拌。

### 执行步骤

1. **真正暂停再开门。** 按机型操作停止微波运行后再取容器；不要在炉内运行时伸手调位置。使用合适隔热工具，把盖/膜从远离脸和手的一侧按说明掀开，让热蒸汽离开身体。
2. **按食物状态重分布。** 可搅拌的食物按配方把边缘与中心、上层与下层充分交换；整块或片状食物按说明翻面/转向。转盘自动旋转不保证内部没有冷点；若容器太大、份量太密或无法执行说明要求的动作，暂停并改小份或换可靠加热方式。
3. **恢复合规覆盖与后段加热。** 重新盖好微波适用且可排气的盖/膜，拿走不应随食物进炉的勺或金属工具，再按原食品说明完成剩余段。忘记开盖后是否还需加热时回看文字，不自己补一个固定分钟数。
4. **执行真正的静置。** 加热结束后，按该食品包装/配方要求保持指定静置状态与时长；静置期间余热继续分布，不是被忽略的“空白等待”。按说明决定盖是否保留、容器在哪里安全放置，防止旁人误触。没有指定静置时间时查食物与机型说明，不用通用数字代替。
5. **静置后才转完成核对。** 对需要安全温度或均匀热透的食物，按食物特定方法在多个位置检查，使用合适[熟度流程](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)；感觉碗边烫手或外面冒汽都不是中心证据。

### 完成与停止

要求的中途重分布、剩余加热和静置均有真实执行记录，食物已准备进入安全完成检查，就完成本任务。容器盖不能安全揭开、无法按要求搅动、时间线遗失、食品条件不明或设备异常时停止，不让一个热边缘替冷中心发言。

### 常见报错与补救

- **转盘一直在转，所以省略搅拌：** 若食品仍要求搅拌/翻面，照做；转盘只改变容器方向。
- **揭盖蒸汽冲向手：** 先放稳并从安全侧按说明揭开，拿不稳就等稳定/请现场协助。
- **金属勺搅完留在碗里重启：** 停止，移除不适用工具，按机型说明复核容器后再开机。
- **铃响后跳过静置：** 依食品说明补足指定静置，再检查完成；不要把静置秒数改成追加加热。
- **搅不动厚重的大份：** 分成小份或换有可靠说明的加热路线，不把不能重分布的问题藏在延长时间里。

### 假设、替代与副作用

不同食品、微波功率、容器与盖子改变重分布/静置要求，本文不给所有食物统一次数或分钟数。视力、握力或站立受限时用较小份量、易握工具、可访问计时与现场可信协助；热盖无法安全打开就先停热等待或换方式。现实副作用是一段静置等待换来更可靠的检查基础，外圈不再独自充当发言人。

## English

Load this Skill at the **stir, flip, rotate, or standing event specified** by the food package or reliable microwave recipe. Carry out the **actual redistribution and required wait** before the finish check. A hot edge does not declare the whole dish ready to eat. This does not invent safety directions for raw animal food without them or repair a microwave.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the food, amount, elapsed heat, which movement the instructions call for, and how the lid or film must be opened and replaced safely. Have a suitable tool, heat protection, landing place, and timer; a dish may be hotter than it looks. Sparks, smoke, or abnormal sounds from the food or oven mean stopping and leaving the ordinary flow rather than stirring beside a faulty appliance.

### Execution steps

1. **Pause before opening.** Stop the microwave under its controls before handling the dish. Do not reach into a running cavity to change position. Use heat protection and peel lid or film from a side that sends steam away from face and hands.
2. **Redistribute for this food.** Stir edge and centre, upper and lower portions together where the recipe calls for it. Flip or turn a solid item under its directions. An automatically rotating plate does not prove the food has no interior cold spot. Pause and split the portion or change to a reliable method if size or density prevents the required action.
3. **Restore the approved cover and later interval.** Replace the microwave-safe vented cover or film and remove any spoon or metal tool that cannot run with the food. Finish the remaining interval under the food directions. If you have lost which stage follows, reread rather than adding a fixed guessed minute count.
4. **Perform real standing time.** After heating, keep food under the package or recipe's stated conditions and duration. Residual heat keeps distributing; this is not unused time. Follow directions on whether the cover stays and place the hot dish so nobody touches it accidentally. If no standing period is supplied, check food and oven directions rather than applying one universal number.
5. **Check completion after standing.** Food needing a safe temperature or even heating requires food-specific checks in several locations and appropriate [doneness guidance](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md). A hot bowl edge or outside steam is not a centre measurement.

### Success and stop

The required midpoint movement, remaining heating, and standing have been executed and recorded, and food is ready for a safe-completion check. Stop if its cover cannot be opened safely, required movement is impossible, the timeline is lost, food conditions are unclear, or equipment behaves abnormally. One hot edge may not speak for a cold centre.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The turntable rotated so you skip stirring:** Follow the food's stir or flip instruction. A turntable changes the dish's direction, not all internal positions.
- **Steam from the cover reaches your hand:** Settle it and peel from a safe side under directions. Wait or arrange in-person help if you cannot hold it securely.
- **A metal spoon stays in the bowl for restart:** Stop, remove an unsuitable tool, and recheck the container under oven directions before running.
- **The bell ends and standing is skipped:** Complete the food's standing period, then check; standing seconds are not a request for more microwaving.
- **A dense portion cannot be stirred:** Divide it or use another instructed heat route; longer guessed heating does not fix missing redistribution.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Food, oven power, vessel, and cover change the movement and standing requirements, so there is no universal count or duration here. Use smaller portions, manageable tools, an accessible timer, or trusted in-person help when sight, grip, or standing is limited. Stop heat and wait or change method if a hot cover cannot be opened safely. A standing period provides a better basis for checking, and a hot edge loses its role as spokesperson for the dish.
