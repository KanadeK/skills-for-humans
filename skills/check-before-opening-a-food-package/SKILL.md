## 中文

当一件普通食品仍封着、你只想用其中一部分时，先加载这份 Skill。目标是在**拆开食品密封层以前**决定：现在开封并能按说明处理余量，还是保持未开封/延期。它不计算餐食营养，也不把打开之后的实际时间和容器安排预先写成已完成。

### 准备与输入

拿着具体包装确认本次要用多少、剩余会有多少、下次真的何时用。找出标签的“未开封保存”“开封后保存”“开封后多久使用”等原文；外盒里如有独立密封小份，辨认这次会拆到哪一层。核对可用且可靠的冷藏/冷冻空间、包装是否能依说明关闭，以及是否有适合食品的干净有盖容器。看不清时放大清晰照片或请人读出关键字，不用记忆代替这件产品的说明。

### 执行

1. **先找真正要破坏的封口。** 拆外盒未必等于打开每一份食品；剪开共用的内袋则可能让全部余量同时进入开封状态。按实际包装和标签判断，别让纸盒替里面的密封层报时。
2. **抄下开封后的要求。** 是否要立刻冷藏、保持密封、转入合适容器、在特定时间内用完？以这件产品的说明和当地食品安全指导为准。未开封的日期不能单独证明开封余量仍可放到那天，也不从另一种食品推断统一天数。
3. **把余量和真实使用机会对齐。** 如果只需一小部分，余量是否能在开封后要求内被正常使用？计划里没有具体用途，就不要把“总会吃掉”当储存能力；也不用为避免浪费而勉强一次吃完整包。
4. **核对实际保存位置。** 要求低温时，确认设备可靠、空间足够且到手可及时放入；需要覆盖或防漏时，确认原包装可按说明继续使用，或已有干净食品适用的容器。详细容器材质和冷藏分区另行核对；这里只验证本次余量确有合格去处。
5. **决定是否开封。** 条件都成立，才拆开本次会使用的食品密封层；拆开的当时记录真实日期，之后按标签及时处理余量。必要条件未知、冷藏不可用、余量无法保存时，保持封口、改用已有合适小份或延后，不把开封后的问题留给明天的你。已经拆开时，本 Skill 不能退回未开封状态，立即改做[实际余量处理](../store-an-opened-food-package/SKILL.md)。

### 完成条件

你能说出本次会拆哪一层封口、开封余量按**这件产品**要去哪里、何时使用，且这些条件已经具备；或者决定保持食品未开封。日历上的原保质日期、闻起来正常或“冰箱应该有位置”都不能替代这三个答案。

### 常见报错与补救

- **外盒拆了，内份仍各自密封：** 先确认标签是否把这些小份视为独立包装；不要自动给所有内份写同一个开封日。
- **常温货架买的，开封后却要求冷藏：** 在拆封前确认可靠冷藏空间；没有就保留原封口，不能用购买时的货架位置覆盖开封说明。
- **说明缺失或看不清：** 拍照放大、查制造商对应产品或当地权威信息；安全关键条件仍不明时暂不打开供以后保存。
- **开封计划临时改变：** 若尚未拆封就保持原状；若已拆开，按真实开封时间和实际储存史重新决定，不能把日期回写成原计划。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这份 Skill 只用于普通家庭食品，排除婴幼儿食品、罐藏、发酵、过敏处置和医疗饮食。日期字样及安全含义随地区而异；具体标签与当地权威优先。若阅读、握持或高处取物不方便，用放大、语音读取、可信协助或低处可达的位置；不能确认关键条件时保持未开封。副作用是你可能晚一点吃到它，但不会提前制造一份没有住所的余量。

## English

Load this Skill while an ordinary food package is still sealed and you intend to use only part of it. **Before breaking the food seal**, decide whether you can open it now and handle the remainder as directed, or leave it sealed and wait. This does not calculate nutrition or pretend that a future opening date and container are already real.

### Preparation and inputs

With the actual package in hand, confirm how much you need now, how much will remain, and when you would really use it next. Read its directions for unopened storage, storage after opening, and any use-after-opening period. If an outer box contains separately sealed portions, identify which seal you will break. Check reliable cold space if required, whether the package can close as directed, and whether a clean food-suitable covered container is available. Enlarge a clear photo or ask someone to read the critical wording; memory is not this item's label.

### Execution

1. **Find the seal that matters.** Opening an outer carton may leave individual portions sealed, while cutting one shared inner pouch may expose all the remaining food. Follow the actual package and its directions. A cardboard box does not start a timer for a seal it did not contain.
2. **Record the after-opening rules.** Must the food be refrigerated promptly, kept sealed, moved to a suitable container, or used within a stated period? Follow this product and local food-safety guidance. The unopened date alone cannot prove the remainder lasts that long after opening, and another food's number of days is not a universal rule.
3. **Match the remainder to a real use.** If you need only a small portion, can you use the rest under those after-opening conditions? “We'll eat it somehow” is not a storage plan. You do not need to consume an entire pack at once just to avoid waste.
4. **Check the actual destination.** If cold storage is required, confirm reliable equipment, room, and prompt access. If covering or leak containment is required, confirm the original package can be used as directed or that a clean food-suitable container is ready. Choose the detailed container or refrigerator zone separately; here you verify that the remainder has a workable destination.
5. **Decide whether to open.** Break the needed food seal only when the conditions are in place. Record the real opening date when you do, then handle the remainder promptly as labelled. If a critical condition is unknown, cooling is unavailable, or the rest cannot be stored, keep it sealed, use an already available suitable smaller portion, or defer. If you have already opened it, this Skill cannot restore a sealed state; [handle the actual remainder](../store-an-opened-food-package/SKILL.md) instead.

### Success

You can name the seal you will break, where this product's remainder will go, when it can be used under its directions, and that those conditions exist—or you leave the food sealed. The printed unopened date, a normal smell, or “the fridge probably has room” cannot replace those answers.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The outer box is open but inner portions remain sealed:** Check whether the label treats them as separate packages. Do not automatically give every inner portion one opening date.
- **The product sat on an ambient shelf but requires chilling after opening:** Confirm reliable cold space before breaking its seal. The original shelf location does not override the after-opening instructions.
- **Directions are missing or inaccessible:** Enlarge a photo, find the manufacturer's information for this exact product, or consult local authority guidance. Do not open it for later storage while a safety-critical condition remains unknown.
- **The plan changes mid-task:** If it remains sealed, leave it that way. If you have opened it, decide again from the real opening time and actual storage history rather than backdating the old plan.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary home food, excluding infant food, canning, fermentation, allergy handling, and medical diets. Date wording and safety meanings vary by region; the specific label and local authority govern. If reading, gripping, or reaching is difficult, use enlargement, audio reading, trusted help, or a reachable storage spot. Leave a safety-critical unknown sealed. You may eat this food later, but you will not create a remainder with no place to live.
