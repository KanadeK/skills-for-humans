## 中文

你写的原创乐句需要让演奏者知道从这里开始更轻或更强，而不是只改自己电脑的音量时，加一处力度标记。成果是标记在正确起点、符号可读，试听相对变化可观察。力度表示音乐意图，不能把合成器音量当作现场声压或保证所有乐器都有相同幅度变化。

### 准备与输入

保存谱面，先写出相对前一乐句要轻、同样还是更强，选一个自己知道含义的常见力度符号。找到需要开始生效的确切音，核之前是否已有力度，以免无意让标记范围覆盖后续全曲。

### 执行

1. 选该乐句起点音符，从 Dynamics 调色板加入一个常见力度记号。
2. 核记号附着位置与谱面避让，不把它误放到上一句或歌词上。
3. 从前一句播到本句，听相对变化；若合成音源没有明显反映，也先核谱面语义而非无限提高数值。
4. 保存重开后再看生效起点，若只想本句有效，计划好后续恢复力度的位置。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

力度符号在目标乐句起点可读，前后相对关系清楚，播放核查结果有记录。

- **标记跟错音：** 撤销并单选目标起点重加。
- **符号被遮：** 先核锚点再轻微调整排版。
- **试听差异太大：** 核本机音源和已有力度，不用声卡音量掩盖。

### 假设与边界

只加一处普通力度标记，不提供现场配器、混音响度标准或复杂渐强线；不同音源的响应不完全相同。

## English

Use this when a phrase in your own score needs a written softer or stronger level rather than a change to your computer volume. Finish with a readable dynamic anchored at its start and an observable relative playback change. Dynamics express musical intent; synthetic loudness is not live sound pressure and varies by instrument.

### Preparation and inputs

Save and decide whether this phrase is softer, similar or stronger than the prior one. Choose a standard symbol whose meaning you understand. Find its first effective note and inspect any earlier dynamic so the new level's reach is intentional.

### Execution

1. Select the phrase's first note and add one conventional symbol from the Dynamics palette.
2. Check its anchor and visual clearance so it is not attached to the previous phrase or colliding with lyrics.
3. Play from the preceding phrase into this one for a relative change; if the sound library expresses it weakly, inspect notation before forcing extreme levels.
4. Save and reopen to check effective start; if only this phrase should change, plan a later return marking.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The dynamic is readable at the phrase start, relative intent is clear and playback findings are noted.

- **Wrong anchor:** Undo and select the exact start note.
- **Collision:** Check anchor, then adjust layout locally.
- **Exaggerated sound:** Review sound source and prior dynamics rather than changing system volume.

### Assumptions and limits

This adds one ordinary dynamic, not live orchestration, audio loudness standards or a complex hairpin; sound libraries may respond differently.
