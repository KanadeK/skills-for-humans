## 中文

当清单上的普通食品或日用品买不到，而你还没有取得替代品时，加载这份 Skill。目标是在**改买、延后、改计划**中选一条可执行的路，并处理购物篮里受影响的其他东西。它不负责烹饪中食材的功能替代、商品规格的详细判读、商家交涉或买后退换。

### 准备与输入

写下原商品要完成的事、最晚什么时候需要、这次最多愿意支付多少，以及不能改变的条件，例如尺寸、设备兼容性、明确的饮食限制或可搬运重量。区分“必须用这个型号”和“只需要完成这个用途”；货架没有资格替你修改需求。

如果与别人共用清单或预算，先确认你能决定哪些变更；没有授权的额外支出先不提交。你不需要为了一个缺货项默认多跑一家店。

### 执行

1. **确认缺的是这一项。** 看清商品名与规格、货架或订单状态。值得花这一步时，查当前商家提供的库存信息或请店员确认；线上显示“有货”也要看实际可取/送达时间。无法确认就把状态记为“未核实”，不要编补货日期。
2. **设定搜索止点。** 依据截止时间、额外路程或配送费、体力和可用交通，决定这次最多再查几个实际可达的选择。查不到不代表你搜索能力不足。
3. **筛掉不合格的改买项。** 只考虑当前确实可取得的候选。逐项核对用途、必需规格、标签上的明确限制、所需数量、取得时间和本次实际应付总额。不同包装只在可比单位下比较价格；不能核实关键条件的候选先不买。这里决定是否改买，不推导食材配方或治疗性替代。
4. **给三条路各写一句结果。**
   - **改买：** 至少一个候选满足所有硬条件，并能在期限和支出上限内取得；写下具体商品与数量。
   - **延后：** 原用途可以等，而且你有可执行的再次查看、预订或另一次采购时间；只记录商家实际给出的信息，不把预计到货当承诺。
   - **改计划：** 不买这一项仍能完成一个缩小或不同的目标；同时删掉只为原计划购买的配套商品，并通知受影响的人。
5. **按期限和代价选一条。** 先排除不合格方案；在剩下的方案里选你能承担的最简单路线。如果三个结果都不成立，停止这项采购，记录缺口和需要谁来决定下一步，不用相似包装强行结案。
6. **线上订单再查替换设置。** 若商家会在拣货时替换，查看该订单是否能指定替代品、要求先确认或选择不替换；设置方式和价差随商家而变。你无法核实限制、额外费用或确认机会时，若可选就设为不替换；若无法关闭且不能接受未知替换，就不提交这项订单。
7. **收尾购物篮。** 记录“买了什么 / 何时再查 / 改成什么计划”之一，核对连带商品和本次总额，然后继续购物或离开。

### 成功条件

你有一个明确决定、依据、下一动作和时间；购物篮不再包含只为失效计划服务的商品。完成条件是作出并落实这次采购决定，不是商家必须补货。

### 常见报错、停止与补救

- **只看名称就认定可替换：** 回到用途和硬条件。尺寸、配料或兼容性不清楚时不结算，改选延后或改计划。
- **替代品超出上限：** 把额外数量、配送或路程成本算回这次决定；缩小计划或延后，不因已经排队就悄悄提高上限。
- **线上默认替换不合适：** 在付款前修改允许替换的项目或关闭自动替换；若订单已在处理，按商家实际提供的确认/拒绝流程操作。交涉和退换另行处理。
- **关键限制无法核实、时间已经不够或没有可达商品：** 停止寻找“差不多”的东西，标记未买及下一步。尤其不要为明确的饮食限制猜标签。
- **改了主商品却忘了配套品：** 回看购物篮，删除或重新指定用途；已经买下的物品不靠这份 Skill 处理售后。

### 假设、可及替代与现实副作用

你能看到现场商品或取得当前商家信息；当地库存、价格、订单替换设置及配送规则都需当场核实。看不清小字时可放大商品照片或请人读出完整标签；够不到货架、无法多跑店或不便搬重物时，把店员协助、可达的配送或延后放进可行方案，不把额外行程当作义务。替别人采购时，无法确认其硬条件就留下缺口并联系决定者。

现实副作用是购物单变短，某项计划可能要重排。空着一格比带回一个不能用的“差不多”更诚实。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary grocery or household item on your list is unavailable and you have not obtained a replacement. Choose an actionable path: **buy another item, wait, or change the plan**. Then fix anything else in the basket that depended on the missing item. This does not teach cooking substitutions, detailed product-specification checks, negotiations with a seller, or returns after purchase.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the job the original item had to do, the latest time it would still be useful, your spending limit for this purchase, and conditions that cannot change: perhaps size, equipment compatibility, a stated dietary restriction, or a weight you can carry. Separate “this exact model is required” from “this job needs doing.” The shelf is not your requirements manager.

If you share the list or budget, confirm which changes you can authorize. Hold any extra spending that requires someone else's decision. A stockout does not obligate you to visit another store.

### Execution

1. **Confirm what is missing.** Check the name, specification, shelf, or order status. When it is worth the effort, check the seller's current stock information or ask staff. For online listings, check when the item can actually be collected or delivered. If that remains unknown, record “unconfirmed”; do not invent a restock date.
2. **Set a search limit.** Use your deadline, extra travel or delivery cost, energy, and available transport to decide how many reachable options you will check this time. An unsuccessful search is still a valid result.
3. **Reject unsuitable replacements.** Consider only options you can actually obtain now. Check each against the intended use, essential specifications, stated label restrictions, amount needed, arrival time, and the total you would pay now. Compare different pack sizes only with compatible units. If an essential condition cannot be checked, leave that option unbought. This is a purchase decision, not a new recipe or therapeutic substitution.
4. **Write one possible result for each route.**
   - **Buy another item:** A specific available item and quantity meet every hard condition within your deadline and spending limit.
   - **Wait:** The original job can wait, and you have a feasible time to check again, reserve, or shop later. Record only what the seller actually says; an estimated restock is not a promise.
   - **Change the plan:** You can still finish a smaller or different goal without this item. Remove companion purchases needed only for the old plan and tell anyone affected.
5. **Choose by deadline and burden.** Discard routes that fail their conditions. Pick the simplest remaining route you can carry out. If none works, stop this purchase, record the gap and who needs to decide next, and do not force a similar-looking package into the answer.
6. **For online orders, check substitution settings.** If the seller may replace an item while picking the order, check whether this order permits a chosen replacement, approval first, or no substitution. Controls and price differences vary by seller. If you cannot verify restrictions, extra cost, or a chance to approve, choose no substitution when available. If you cannot turn it off and cannot accept an unknown replacement, do not place this item in the order.
7. **Clean up the basket.** Record one outcome: what you bought, when you will check again, or the revised plan. Recheck dependent items and the order total, then continue shopping or leave.

### Success

You have a clear choice, reason, next action, and time. The basket no longer contains items that only served a cancelled plan. Success means making and applying this purchase decision; it does not require the seller to restock anything.

### Common errors, stop points, and recovery

- **A similar name looks interchangeable:** Return to the job and hard conditions. If size, ingredients, or compatibility are unclear, do not check out; wait or change the plan.
- **The replacement breaks your limit:** Include extra quantity, delivery, or travel cost in this decision. Reduce the plan or wait instead of silently raising the limit because you are already in line.
- **An online default substitution is unsuitable:** Before payment, change the eligible items or turn off automatic substitution. If the order is already being picked, use the seller's actual approval or rejection process. Handle disputes and returns separately.
- **A critical restriction is unverifiable, time has run out, or nothing is reachable:** Stop looking for “close enough.” Mark the item unbought and record the next step. Do not guess a label where a stated dietary restriction applies.
- **You changed the main item but kept its companions:** Review the basket and remove them or give them a real new purpose. This Skill does not handle a purchase you already made.

### Assumptions, accessible alternatives, and side effects

You can inspect goods or obtain current seller information. Local stock, prices, substitution controls, and delivery terms must be checked when you shop. If small print is hard to read, enlarge a clear product photo or ask someone to read the full label. If shelves are out of reach, another shop is inaccessible, or carrying is difficult, include staff help, reachable delivery, or waiting among your feasible routes; an extra trip is not mandatory. When shopping for someone else, leave the gap open and contact the decision maker if you cannot confirm their hard conditions.

The list may get shorter and a dependent plan may need rescheduling. An honest blank is more useful than bringing home something that cannot do the job.
