## 中文

当你想从一件**自有或明确允许接触的普通纹理物**上，用纸和蜡笔实际留下凹凸图案时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张可以指认来源、看见纹理转移的纸面拓印；这与 H271 观察后自己画边线、H273 已有折纸/拼贴不同。文物、墓碑、租借艺术品、受保护图案、脆弱表面或不能接触的公共设施都不在这里试拓。

### 准备与输入

找一件稳定、干燥、无锐边、你有权轻触的日常物品，例如自己的一段瓦楞纸或纹理板。看表面是否掉色、松碎或可能被蜡笔染上；有疑问就换物品。拿较薄且足够覆盖纹理的自有纸，蜡笔侧面能平贴的画材，及平整台面；手部抓握或视觉不便可用较粗画材、放大和现场可信协助。不要从公园采走未经许可的叶或在历史物件上贴胶带。

### 执行

1. **先做轻触许可和材料试验。** 手指或合适工具轻摸纹理，确认它固定、不会划破纸或自身受损。把纸覆盖其一小角，用蜡笔侧面轻扫；若蜡、颜色或摩擦影响原物，立即停并换另一个自有来源。
2. **固定纸但不伤源物。** 将纹理物平稳放桌上，纸盖在它上面，用手在纸边压住；需要固定时只把胶带贴在自己的底板/桌面允许处，不贴文物或脆弱表面。让纸与纹理贴合，却不按到撕裂。
3. **用侧面逐步显出纹理。** 蜡笔或画材侧面以轻、均匀的动作扫过纸面，从一小块扩到目标区域。随时掀起纸边看是否出现所需凹凸，确认源物仍干净完整；不是越重压越清晰。
4. **收尾并记来源。** 慢慢移开纸，检查纹理是否能辨认、纸未破、原物无新痕；在纸角写“来自自有瓦楞纸”之类的真实来源和日期，清掉自己留下的碎屑。想公开分享时，不包含未获许可的特殊图案或私人对象。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

纸上留下与实际物件相符的可见凹凸纹理，纸与源物都完好，来源可回查。只在纸上凭想象画几道线，没有纹理转移，不算本次拓印。

- **纸总在滑：** 暂停，改压纸边或固定到自己的底板，避免在源物表面粘贴。
- **线条糊成一片：** 减轻压力、用画材侧面重新从小区试，不靠猛涂抹淹没纹理。
- **纸被锐角弄破：** 停止使用该物件，换圆润、稳固的自有纹理。
- **原物掉色或掉碎：** 立刻停并清理自己的台面，不继续“为了完成”磨它。
- **手部或感官限制：** 用更大纸、更粗蜡笔、桌面高度和可信协助固定；是否真的转移纹理由可访问放大/触觉或描述核实。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一项低风险的私人纸艺练习，不是文物拓片、鉴定或复刻别人的艺术作品。薄纸、画材和纹理物的组合会影响图像清晰度，没有通用压力量。若唯一可用表面可能损坏或无权触摸，选别的材料或停止；作品不值得让真实物件承担磨损。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to transfer the relief of an **owned or clearly permitted ordinary textured object** onto paper with a crayon. Finish with a rubbing whose source you can name and whose texture is visible. This differs from H271's observed outline sketch and H273's prior fold or collage. Do not rub artifacts, gravestones, borrowed artworks, protected designs, fragile surfaces, or public fixtures you may not touch.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a stable, dry, non-sharp everyday source you may touch, such as your own corrugated card or textured board. Check whether it sheds colour or pieces or could be marked by wax; switch if uncertain. Have owned thin paper large enough to cover the texture, a crayon or mark-maker with a usable side, and a flat table. For grip or vision needs use a thicker tool, magnification, or trusted in-person help. Do not take leaves from places where collecting is not permitted or tape historic objects.

### Execution

1. **Test permission and material gently.** Touch the texture lightly to check that it is fixed and will neither cut paper nor be harmed. Cover a corner with paper and sweep the crayon's side lightly. If wax, colour, or friction affects the source, stop and choose another owned object.
2. **Hold paper without harming the source.** Place the object securely on the table and cover it with paper, holding an edge down by hand. If fixation is needed, tape only an owned backing or a permitted table area, never a fragile source. Keep contact with the relief without pressing hard enough to tear.
3. **Reveal texture with the side.** Sweep the crayon side in light even strokes, from a small patch toward the chosen area. Lift a paper edge to see whether relief appears and the source remains clean and intact. More pressure is not automatically more detail.
4. **Finish and name the source.** Lift the paper slowly. Check recognisable texture, unbroken paper, and no new marks on the original. Write a true note such as “my corrugated card” and date on the corner, and clear your own scraps. Do not publish another person's protected pattern or private object without permission.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Paper shows a visible relief pattern matching the actual object, both paper and source are intact, and the source is traceable. Imagined lines drawn onto paper without transferred relief are not this rubbing.

- **Paper slips repeatedly:** Pause, hold an edge or secure it to your own backing rather than taping the source.
- **Marks turn into an opaque patch:** Ease pressure and test a small area with the tool's side instead of burying texture under wax.
- **A sharp edge tears the sheet:** Stop using that object and choose a rounded stable owned texture.
- **The source sheds colour or fragments:** Stop and clean your table rather than abrading it to finish.
- **Grip or sensory access is limited:** Use larger paper, a thicker crayon, table height, and trusted help holding it; verify transfer through accessible magnification, touch, or description.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a low-risk private paper craft, not heritage rubbing, appraisal, or reproduction of someone else's artwork. Paper, crayon, and texture affect clarity, and there is no universal pressure. If the only source might be harmed or cannot be touched lawfully, change materials or stop. A picture is not worth wear on the real object.
