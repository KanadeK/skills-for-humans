## 中文

当你打算把一批普通杯盘碗和非尖锐餐具放入**确切型号已知的家用洗碗机**时，用这份 Skill 先核对每件器皿的材质、可机洗标示与本机限制。这里的结果是“可入机/按说明手洗/暂缓确认”的分组，不是选程序或保证机器洗后全干。H071 的普通手洗流程接收不适合机洗的器皿；难洗锅具、尖锐刀具和砧板各有范围。

### 准备与输入

读器皿包装、底部标记或制造商护理说明和准确洗碗机手册，特别看耐热、易碎、涂层、木、装饰、可拆密封件、尺寸/架位限制。标记读不清时可拍照放大、查厂家或先手洗，不用“以前放进去没坏”推断所有同类都适用。严重裂纹、尖锐破口、化学品残留或不明食物污染退出普通机洗，按对应安全处置。

### 执行

1. **逐件找许可。** 明确标“可机洗”且附条件的按条件记录；禁止或只建议手洗的另放。没有标签的贵重、古董、手绘、结构复杂件先停并查制造商，不靠材质外观看起来结实就入机。
2. **核对特殊材质与部件。** 木、养护铸铁、某些不粘/铝、手绘器皿、易碎玻璃、带封圈/胶接或真空结构的物品可能需要不同方法；以**该件**说明为准，而非一张“塑料都能上层”的万能表。可拆部件只有在厂家明确允许拆洗时才拆；H077 负责餐盒密封件细洗。
3. **检查尺寸和污染方式。** 能机洗的件还需能稳放在本机规定架位，不能卡住喷臂、门、洗剂仓或排水路径。先把大块食物残渣移到适用垃圾/厨余去向；焦锅/大片硬结先走锅具清理，不能靠一次正常周期代替产品说明的预处理。
4. **明确尖锐物的去向。** 普通非尖锐餐具可按本机说明入篮；长刀、锋利刀具、砧板等不混进本篇通用组，按刀具/砧板制造商与专属安全流程处理，不把刀刃藏在拥挤餐篮里。
5. **形成可执行的两组。** 可机洗组每件有制造商与本机共同允许的条件；不可或未确认组已有手洗、专门护理或等待来源的去向。没有足够合格件也可以选择先不启动机器，不用为凑满负载塞一件不合格的。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你能为任意一件说明“为何可入机、放在什么限制内”或“为何不入机”；脆弱/尖锐/无说明器具没有被普通盘子掩盖。分组后还须实际正确装载和运行。

- **玻璃上没有可读标记：** 先查厂家或采用适用手洗，不以“是玻璃”推出上层可洗。
- **锅表面有焦结却写可机洗：** 先按锅的材质护理处理焦结，再决定剩余正常清洁路径。
- **塑料容器可以机洗但盖子未说明：** 分别核实容器和盖，不能让容器的许可自动覆盖附件。
- **刀刃朝上挤在餐具篮里：** 停止装载这件，回到准确机型与刀具护理的安全安排。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

仅限普通家用器皿和正常工作的洗碗机，不替代食品安全消毒认证、特殊器具专业护理或设备维修。不同品牌“可机洗”条件可能限定架位/程序，准确说明优先。看不清底部小字时放大、用可读说明或可信现场协助；取放困难时用较轻批量，不能安全拿稳玻璃就暂停。被排出的件可能增加一次手洗任务。

## English

Use this Skill before placing ordinary cups, plates, bowls, and non-sharp cutlery in a **known household dishwasher model**. Check each item's material, dishwasher permission, and this machine's limits. The output is a machine/hand-wash/hold group, not a cycle choice or a guarantee that everything will come out dry. The ordinary hand-wash task receives items not suitable for this machine; difficult pans, sharp knives, and boards have their own routes.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the item's packaging, bottom mark, or maker care instructions and the exact dishwasher manual, especially heat, fragility, coating, wood, decorations, removable seals, size, and rack limits. Enlarge a mark or ask the maker when unclear; “a similar item survived before” is not proof. Serious cracks, sharp broken edges, chemical residue, or unknown food contamination leave ordinary machine washing for the relevant safety route.

### Execution

1. **Find permission item by item.** Record conditions for a marked dishwasher-safe item. Hold items marked hand wash or no machine. Stop and seek maker guidance for unmarked valuable, antique, hand-painted, or complex pieces rather than judging strength by appearance.
2. **Check special material and parts.** Wood, maintained cast iron, some nonstick or aluminium, painted ware, delicate glass, glued joints, and sealed or vacuum structures may need different care. Follow **that item**, not a universal “all plastic goes on top” chart. Remove a component only if its maker explicitly allows removal and washing; lunchbox seals have a separate cleaning task.
3. **Check size and soil.** A permitted item still must fit a documented rack without blocking the spray arm, door, dispenser, or drainage. Move large food scraps to the relevant waste route. A scorched pan or hard deposits need the pan's own preparation; a normal cycle is not proof it can handle them.
4. **Give sharp items their own route.** Ordinary non-sharp cutlery follows this machine's basket directions. Do not hide long or sharp knives or boards inside this general group; use the item's maker and the knife/board safety workflow.
5. **Make real groups.** Every machine item has permission from both item and machine directions; each excluded or uncertain one has hand wash, special care, or a hold for evidence. You may wait to run a small load rather than insert an unqualified item just to fill the machine.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can explain why each item may enter under a particular limit or why it stays out. Fragile, sharp, and unmarked pieces have not been hidden among ordinary dishes. Correct loading and a cycle still follow this screen.

- **A glass has no readable mark:** Ask its maker or use suitable hand washing; glass alone does not imply top-rack permission.
- **A pan says dishwasher safe but has hard char:** Treat the char under the pan's material care first, then decide the remaining normal-cleaning route.
- **The plastic body is permitted but the lid is unmarked:** Verify body and lid separately. Permission does not transfer to an attachment.
- **A sharp edge points up from a crowded basket:** Stop loading it and use exact-model and knife-care guidance instead.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers everyday home dishes and a working dishwasher, not sanitation certification, professional care, or appliance repair. A dishwasher-safe mark can still limit rack or cycle. Use exact directions. Enlarge small labels or ask trusted help. Load lighter batches if lifting is difficult, and pause if glass cannot be held safely. Excluded items may add a hand-wash task.
