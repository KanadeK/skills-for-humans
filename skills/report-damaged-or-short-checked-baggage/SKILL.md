## 中文

你已拿到**自己的托运行李**，却发现外壳/拉链/轮子明显损坏，或安全清点后发现普通内件缺少时，用这份 Skill区分可观察的箱体损伤和内件差异，保留行李及标签，向负责航司报障。目标是获得正式受理编号和下一步，不承诺修理、补物或赔付。整件箱子没有出现在提取区，则先走“未见行李”报告；漏液、尖片、鼓胀电池等危险情况停止自行翻找并联系现场人员。

### 准备与输入

确认行李标签属于你并与托运收据相符，记下实际到达航班、机场、领取时间和箱体原有状态（若已知）。在离开机场前先检查显著损伤；若离场后才发现，立刻查该航司当期报告入口和期限，不用别家天数。拍照时保留必要的箱体、标签、破口/凹痕和内件事实，遮住完整住址、支付信息或旁人；不可安全触碰的内容不为照片打开。

### 执行

1. **先判断能否安全检查。** 有漏液、刺激气味、锋利物、损坏电池或其他异常时停止开箱/试用，隔离并向机场/航司工作人员求助。普通可安全接触的箱子才继续；不要因追求证据扩大损坏。
2. **把外部与内部事实分开。** 拍/记行李各面、损坏部位、标签、锁/拉链和可见封装。可安全打开时列出确实少的普通内件及原有证据，不推断“机场一定拿走了”。如箱子有安检检查通知，保留该通知并问正式航司/安检渠道谁处理；不要自行认定责任方。
3. **保留实物和交运证据。** 暂留受损箱、行李牌、托运收据、内衬和实际收到物品，直到负责渠道说明可怎么处理。不要先丢箱、私自寄走或修好再试图重造旧损伤照片。无法安全储存大型破损箱时向航司说明限制并问正式方案。
4. **尽快向负责方报具体事实。** 在机场按航司行李服务柜台/官方扫码或网站规则报告，离场后按该航司正式入口及时提交。给出航班、标签号、外损/内缺分别如何发现、必要照片及希望的下一步（检查、维修/替换信息、缺物核查等）。读回受理编号、还需上传什么、是否要现场检查和后续时间；报告受理与索赔获批是不同状态。

### 成功条件

普通可安全处理的损伤/缺物有外部与内部事实记录，行李、标签和必要实物按要求留存，负责服务方收到报告并给出编号和下一动作；若发现危险情况则安全停止并联系现场人员。本文不证明是谁造成损伤，也不保证赔偿。

### 常见报错与补救

- **刚出机场就把破箱扔了：** 留存剩余收据、照片与实际物品，向航司如实说明证据缺口，不伪造旧照。
- **箱体划痕就称内件全丢：** 只写已观察事实，安全可打开时再清点，未知就标未知。
- **有安检检查卡就推定航司或安检必赔：** 保存卡和照片，按负责渠道核实，不在本 Skill 判责。
- **航司只给了行李标签号：** 请求本次破损/缺物报告的独立案件编号及正式后续入口。
- **已经离场：** 尽快查本航司适用地点/航段的当期报告方式与期限，不借用网络帖子中的统一时限。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只做普通托运行李箱体/内件异常的事实记录和服务报告，不解释法律、海关、保险、损失金额或赔偿资格。Delta、American Airlines、British Airways 的流程与期限互不相同，实际航司/机场规则是权威。不能拍照可用精确文字/现场人员记录；行动、听觉、语言障碍可请求航空公司正式协助。破损行李会暂时占地方，实际处理可能还需进一步材料。

## English

Use this Skill after **your own checked bag has arrived** but its shell, zip, wheel, or ordinary contents are visibly damaged or short after a safe inventory. Separate outside damage from inside discrepancy, retain the bag and tag, and report facts to the responsible airline. The result is an accepted case and next action, not promised repair, replacement, or payment. If the whole bag never appeared, use the missing-bag report first. Stop opening for leaks, sharp fragments, swollen batteries, or another hazard and ask on-site staff.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the bag tag is yours and matches the check-in receipt. Note actual arrival flight, airport, claim time, and prior bag condition when known. Inspect obvious damage before leaving if possible. If found later, promptly check this airline's current report route and deadline, not another airline's number of days. Photograph or write only needed bag sides, tag, tear/dent, and content facts, hiding full address, payment details, and bystanders. Do not open unsafe contents for a picture.

### Execution

1. **Check whether inspection is safe.** For a leak, strong smell, sharp item, damaged battery, or other abnormality, stop opening or using the bag, isolate it, and seek airport/airline staff. Continue only for safely approachable ordinary damage; evidence is not worth increasing the hazard.
2. **Separate outside from inside facts.** Record the bag sides, damaged area, tag, lock/zip, and visible packing. If safe to open, list only contents actually missing and the evidence for what was placed there. Do not infer “airport staff took it.” Keep any screening-inspection notice and ask the formal airline/screening channels about routing rather than assigning liability yourself.
3. **Keep physical evidence.** Retain damaged bag, tag, check-in receipt, packing and actual contents until the responsible channel says how to proceed. Do not discard, privately mail, or repair it then reconstruct old pictures. Explain storage limits to the airline when a large damaged bag cannot be kept safely.
4. **Report promptly with specific facts.** At the airport follow its airline baggage desk, official QR, or site route; after leaving, use that airline's published process. Give flight, tag, separately observed outer/inner state, minimal photos, and requested next action such as inspection, repair/replacement information, or missing-content review. Read back case ID, further evidence, inspection need, and next contact time. Report acceptance and claim approval are different states.

### Success

Ordinary safe damage/shortage has separate outside and inside records, bag and tag are retained as directed, and the responsible service accepted a report with reference and next action. For hazardous conditions, stopping safely and involving on-site staff is the correct result. This does not prove who caused damage or guarantee compensation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The bag was discarded just after leaving:** Keep remaining receipt, pictures and goods; explain the gap honestly rather than fabricating earlier photos.
- **A scuff is reported as all contents lost:** State observations only. Inventory safely when possible and mark unknowns as unknown.
- **A screening notice is treated as proof of who must pay:** Retain it and ask the relevant official channels; this file does not assign liability.
- **Airline gave only the bag tag number:** Ask for this damage/contents report's separate case ID and formal next route.
- **You left the airport:** Quickly check this airline's current method and applicable deadline for the location/flight; internet stories do not set a universal window.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is factual ordinary checked-bag damage or contents-shortage reporting, not legal, customs, insurance, loss valuation, or compensation eligibility advice. Delta, American Airlines, and British Airways have different methods and deadlines; actual carrier/airport rules control. If photos are inaccessible, use precise written or staff records. Ask airline support for mobility, hearing, or language needs. The bag may occupy space pending further evidence requests.
