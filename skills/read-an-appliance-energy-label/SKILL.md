## 中文

当你已经确认两个家电型号都能完成同一普通用途，却想用它们的**正式能耗标签**比较使用量时加载。这份 Skill 只解释购买前的标示估算，不负责家电尺寸、安装、电气或燃气作业，也不预测你的实际账单。

### 准备与输入

记下两个准确型号、品类、可用容量或服务量，以及你所在地适用的官方能耗标签体系。取得这两个型号的完整标签及其测试基准；商家网页若只显示徽章，可查制造商的准确型号资料。视觉或语言受限时放大标签、使用可访问 PDF/朗读或请人读出**数值、单位和基准**，不只读等级字母。

### 执行

1. **先确认用途可比。** 两个型号须都通过空间、容量和功能需求；大容量与小容量、不同品类或不同测试条件的数字不能直接排名。尺寸与兼容性另用相应 Skill 核对。
2. **找到实际用量。** 在标签上读每年或每次的能源消耗数值和单位，确认它属于这一个准确型号。美国 EnergyGuide 的年度费用是典型使用和全国平均能源价格下的估算，不是你将收到的账单；ENERGY STAR 标志与一张可比较的数值标签也不是同一字段。
3. **对齐口径。** 只比较同一能源形式、相同时间或使用次数基准以及相近服务能力的数字。一个写 kWh/年的数字与另一个写 kWh/次的数字，除非你有可靠的次数和同一测试前提，否则先标“不可直接比较”。
4. **注明差异和前提。** 若 A 与 B 分别是 400 与 320 kWh/年，且容量和测试口径相当，可记“B 的标示年用量低 80 kWh”；不据此承诺电费节省多少。用量、气候、当地能源价和产品状态会改变实际结果。
5. **输出有限结论。** 写下型号、标签来源和日期、可比前提、两个数值与差异。缺标签、型号不符或条件不等时暂缓能耗排名；可以继续比较其它已确认的购买条件。

### 成功与停止

你能说明**在该标签测试口径下**哪台的标示能源用量较低，或为什么目前不可比。不要把最高效率标志当成商品整体最适合，也不要因为标签上的估算费用较低就跳过购买价格、安装条件与维护需求。涉及接线、燃气或故障排查立即停止本购买流程，交给专业人员。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看等级或徽章：** 找数值、单位、测试基准和准确型号；没有就不做定量排名。
- **把标签的年费用当成本地账单：** 回到标示能源使用量与估算前提，不猜当地费率或自己的使用次数。
- **大小不同仍排名：** 先确认两者都满足同一用途，再比较相近服务量的型号。
- **网页和实物型号尾码不同：** 暂停，索取准确型号的标签，尤其不要省略地区或修订后缀。

### 假设、替代与副作用

此处以美国 EnergyGuide 为有来源的实例；其它地区标签、分级和测试方法不能直接翻译。若你无法取得相同口径的正式标签，就把能耗比较记为未知，不让销售话术代填。标签数值是采购证据的一部分，购物车仍需承受现实的空间和价格。

## English

Load this Skill when two exact appliance models already serve the same ordinary purpose and you want to compare their **official energy labels**. It interprets stated estimates before purchase. It does not settle appliance fit, installation, electrical or gas work, or your future utility bill.

### Preparation and inputs

Record each exact model, product category, usable capacity or service, and the official energy-label system for your market. Get the full label and test basis for each model. If a seller page shows only a badge, look up the exact model from the maker. If vision or language is a barrier, enlarge the label, use an accessible PDF or read-aloud tool, or have someone read the **number, unit, and basis**, not merely a grade letter.

### Execution

1. **Confirm comparable uses first.** Both models must pass your space, capacity, and function needs. Numbers for different sizes, categories, or test conditions cannot simply be ranked. Check fit and compatibility in their own workflow.
2. **Find measured or estimated use.** Read the stated energy consumption per year or use, including its unit, and match it to the exact model. The US EnergyGuide annual cost is an estimate using typical use and a national average energy price, not your future bill. An ENERGY STAR mark is not the same field as a numeric comparison label.
3. **Align the basis.** Compare only the same energy form, time or use-count basis, and reasonably similar service. A kWh/year number and a kWh/cycle number are not directly comparable without a trustworthy cycle count and matching test premises.
4. **Record the difference with its conditions.** If comparable labels show 400 and 320 kWh/year, you can say B's labelled annual use is 80 kWh lower. You cannot promise a particular bill saving from that alone. Your usage, climate, local energy price, and product condition affect reality.
5. **Make a limited result.** Note both models, label source and date, comparison premises, numbers, and difference. If a label is missing, the model differs, or the conditions do not align, pause the energy ranking; other confirmed purchase checks may continue.

### Success and stop

You can say which appliance has lower stated energy use **under that label's test basis**, or why comparison is unavailable. A high-efficiency badge does not establish overall suitability, and a low estimated yearly cost does not replace purchase-price, installation, or maintenance checks. Stop this shopping workflow at wiring, gas work, or fault diagnosis and use qualified help.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a grade or badge is visible:** Find the exact model's number, unit, and test basis; otherwise do not make a numeric ranking.
- **Treating estimated yearly cost as a local bill:** Return to stated energy use and its assumptions; do not invent a local tariff or personal use count.
- **Ranking unlike sizes:** Confirm that both can serve the same purpose before comparing similarly capable models.
- **Online and physical suffixes differ:** Wait for the label of the exact model, including regional or revision suffixes.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

US EnergyGuide is the sourced example here. Other regions' labels, grades, and tests are not directly interchangeable. If comparable official labels are unavailable, keep the energy decision unknown instead of filling it with sales copy. The label supplies one piece of purchase evidence; the cart still has to carry real space and price constraints.
