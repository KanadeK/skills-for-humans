## 中文

你有一个原创主题，却还没决定主体位置与大轮廓时，先在独立绘画层做粗草图。成果是看得出主体、留白和主要比例的可撤回构图，不是要一次画完线稿。草图层与背景分开，后面可调透明度、移动或隐藏而不伤底色。

### 准备与输入

保存现有画布，在图层面板确认背景层和当前层；新建绘画层并起名“草图”或等价描述。选择看得见但不易与最终线稿混淆的笔色，先想画面中心和边缘要留的呼吸空间。

### 执行

1. 在草图层先画几个大形和轴线，只分配主体、配角与留白位置。
2. 以简单比例线核主体是否太靠边、太大或太小，必要时在本层擦改，不动背景。
3. 缩小到目标观看尺寸，确认第一眼能辨认主次；再放大看关键连接处是否足以指导线稿。
4. 切换草图层可见性验证它独立存在，恢复显示并保存 .kra，留给后续描线。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

草图能清楚表达大构图与主次，独立隐藏后背景不变，原稿已保存。

- **线条过细看不清：** 用较大笔径重新概括大形。
- **误画在背景：** 尽早撤销并切换草图层，核背景没有残笔。
- **主体贴边：** 在草图层局部重摆或变换，保留必要留白。

### 假设与边界

这里完成粗构图，不要求比例写实、人体解剖正确或形成最终轮廓；可把不满意的尝试作为一层撤回。

## English

Use this before committing to ink or color for an original subject. The finish is a loose composition showing the main subject, empty space and broad proportions, not a polished contour. Keep it on a separate paint layer so it can be dimmed, moved or hidden without damaging the background.

### Preparation and inputs

Save the canvas and inspect the background and active layer. Add a paint layer named Sketch or equivalent. Choose a visible stroke color distinct from later ink and decide where the subject and breathing room should sit.

### Execution

1. On the sketch layer, mark big masses and axes for subject, supporting elements and negative space.
2. Check rough proportions and whether the subject crowds an edge; erase and revise on this layer rather than touching the background.
3. Zoom out to intended viewing size to judge hierarchy, then zoom in to ensure key connections can guide ink.
4. Toggle only the sketch layer to prove independence, restore it and save the .kra for later inking.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The rough sketch communicates composition and hierarchy; hiding it leaves the background intact and the master is saved.

- **Too faint:** Use a broader stroke to restate the main masses.
- **Wrong layer:** Undo promptly, select sketch layer and inspect background.
- **Crowded edge:** Reposition the rough subject on its layer with room around it.

### Assumptions and limits

This delivers a rough composition, not anatomical accuracy, photorealistic proportions or final contour. An unsatisfactory sketch remains easy to revise or discard as a layer.
