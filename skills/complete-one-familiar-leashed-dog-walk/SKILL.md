## 中文

当一只你熟悉、能普通牵引的狗已有合格装备和今天可行的短路线，准备**真正出门并返回**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是人和狗平安回到交接点；计划路线和通过装备检查都不能冒充已经走完。

### 准备与输入

出门前再看天气/路面、犬只准入和自己能否持绳，带上废物袋及必要饮水。遵从这只狗的兽医运动限制和当地实际牵绳规则。狗出现明显不适、你不能控制它，或路线突然不适用时不要开始；反应性/攻击性犬的训练需合格人员指导，不由本 Skill 处理。

### 执行

1. **安全离开住所。** 先扣好已检查装备、拿稳牵绳，再开门；确认狗没有在门缝或电梯口脱出。步行保持在获准路线，交通穿越与复杂路口按当地规则和你的能力另作判断，不为完成这条 Skill 冒险。
2. **用狗与人的实际状态定速度。** 留足正常嗅闻和短暂停顿，不拖拽、不强迫跑或走满公里数；注意狗的步态、呼吸、足部、牵引者体力和地面变化。
3. **为相遇留距离。** 看见陌生狗、人、牲畜或野生动物时维持牵绳、给彼此空间，不默认允许接触。若狗拉扯到无法控制或明显恐惧，转到安全距离或按折返点结束，不现场尝试强制训练。
4. **即时处理可处理的排泄物。** 用随身袋按当地规则收集，若现场条件使你无法安全完成，先保证人狗安全并按适用场地规则后续处理；不把排泄物留给路人。
5. **按事实折返与归家。** 天气变差、地面不适、狗或人疲劳就提前折返；回到家后关好门、解开装备、供正常饮水，查看普通泥盐或异常足伤。普通清洁与伤病判断分开。

### 成功条件

人狗实际按获准路线短走并一同安全回到家，牵引和互相避让有记录，废物有去向，后续饮水和足部检查已交接。因状态不适而提前返回也是合格结束；走满预定路程不是必要条件。

### 常见报错与补救

- **狗突然猛烈拉扯：** 保持自己站稳，撤到可控安全位置并结束普通散步；向合格训练人员求助，不用猛拽作“纠正”。
- **陌生犬靠得太近：** 主动拉开距离，不把两犬接触当作社会化必修课。
- **路面突然烫、冰或破损：** 折返或换已确认可行路径，不让足垫和人跌倒来试错。
- **狗出现异常喘息、跛行或崩溃迹象：** 立即停止活动并联系兽医或当地紧急兽医服务；本文不诊断或提供急救。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只是熟悉可控犬与具能力者的一次普通短距离牵绳散步，不教危险犬约束、道路指挥、跑步训练或兽医处理。需要无障碍路线就按实际路面与帮助选择；无法安全牵引时请合适的人代行。现实副作用是废物、脏足和装备都可能需要收尾。

## English

Load this Skill when a familiar, ordinarily manageable dog has checked equipment and a feasible short route for today and you are about to **actually leave and return**. The finish is the dog and handler safely back at handoff. A chosen route or passed gear check is not a completed walk.

### Preparation and inputs

Recheck weather, ground, dog access, and your ability to hold the lead. Carry waste bags and water as needed. Follow this dog's veterinary activity limits and actual local lead rules. Do not start for an unwell dog, inadequate control, or a newly unsuitable route. Reactive or aggressive-dog training needs qualified help.

### Execution

1. **Leave home safely.** Fasten checked gear and hold the lead before opening the door; confirm the dog does not slip through a doorway or lift. Stay on the permitted route. Crossing traffic and complex junctions depend on actual local rules and handler ability; do not take a dangerous crossing to finish this Skill.
2. **Let real condition set pace.** Allow normal sniffing and short pauses. Do not drag, force a run, or chase a kilometre quota. Watch gait, breathing, paws, handler stamina, and ground changes.
3. **Leave space at encounters.** Keep the lead and distance around unfamiliar dogs, people, livestock, and wildlife. Contact is not assumed. If pulling or fear exceeds control, move to a safe distance or return at the planned point instead of imposing training on site.
4. **Handle waste when it appears.** Use a bag and the actual local route. If pickup cannot be done safely at that moment, first secure dog and handler, then follow applicable site rules; do not leave it as a default for others.
5. **Turn back on evidence and return.** Shorten for changing weather, unsuitable ground, or tired dog/handler. At home, close the door, remove gear, make normal drinking water available, and check for ordinary mud/salt or abnormal paw injury. Basic cleaning is distinct from diagnosing harm.

### Success

Dog and handler actually made a permitted short outing and returned safely. Lead control, space at encounters, waste route, water and paw follow-up have real states. Returning early for discomfort is a valid finish; the planned distance is not a requirement.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The dog pulls hard unexpectedly:** Steady yourself, retreat to a controllable safe place, and end the ordinary walk. Seek qualified training help rather than jerking as correction.
- **An unfamiliar dog comes too close:** Create distance; a dog-to-dog meeting is not compulsory socialization.
- **Ground becomes hot, icy, or broken:** Turn back or use an already verified route; do not test with paws or a human fall.
- **The dog has abnormal panting, limping, or collapse-like signs:** Stop activity immediately and contact a veterinarian or local urgent veterinary service. This Skill does not diagnose or provide first aid.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is one ordinary short leashed walk by a capable handler with a familiar manageable dog. It does not teach dangerous-dog restraint, traffic direction, running training, or veterinary care. Choose an actually accessible route and a capable alternate handler when needed. Waste, paws, and gear may need real cleanup afterward.
