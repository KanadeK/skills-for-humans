## 中文

当你要买普通微波炉或烤箱，并且有一只**确实想继续使用的餐盘、饭盒或烤盘**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是购前确认这件器皿在准确机型内能进入、关门，并满足转盘旋转或烤架留空条件；不靠标称升数猜它一定放得下。本文不判断器皿材质能否加热、不操作电器、不处理安装接线。

### 准备与输入

量你的器皿最宽、最深、最高位置，包含把手、盖子和实际盛放后的高度。找候选准确型号的**内腔可用宽深高**、门口净尺寸；微波炉另找转盘直径、是否必须旋转及制造商允许的餐盘条件，烤箱另找可用烤架位置和该型号要求的烤盘周围空隙。外部机身尺寸用于摆放判断，不能替代炉腔尺寸。

### 执行

1. **先定一件真实器皿。** 用你确实会用的最大常用盘或烤盘作为测试物，按实际方向量外缘。圆盘看外径；方盘别忘把手和转动时可能扫过的对角线。
2. **核入口和关门。** 用器皿完整外形与准确机型的门口、腔内宽深高比较。盘子已放进却顶住门，仍是不适合；不要假设商品页的“20 升”会改变门的位置。
3. **按加热方式分支。** 转盘微波炉应按说明让器皿在允许位置**自由旋转且不碰壁/门**；平板式或可关闭转盘的机型只能按该型号说明使用。烤箱则核盘子在目标烤架位置后是否还有该机型要求的热空气流通空间，不把整个标称内腔都当可贴满的仓库。
4. **核一顿实际使用量。** 若你需要同时放两盘，按两盘与烤架位置重新检查，而不是把单盘通过乘以二。器皿材质、盖子和食品安全要求另按准确产品说明核对；尺寸通过不能盖章其他条件。
5. **写出有限裁决。** 记录“器皿实际外形 → 机型门口/内腔 → 转动或烤架/留空条件 → 适合、不适合或未确认”。缺准确内腔、门口或转盘数据时，可在商家允许下用纸板模板比照展示机，或索取规格；没有证据就暂停候选。

### 成功与停止

你能指出某一件器皿在准确机型、指定使用方式下能进入、关门并保留必要运动或空气空间，才完成购前尺寸判断。若机型要求专业嵌装、电气连接，或器皿的耐热/微波适用性未知，停止本尺寸结论外的推断，分别查询准确说明或专业人员。纸面通过不是一次实际烹调验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看升数：** 找内腔和门口尺寸，比较器皿外缘；同升数机型可能有不同可用形状。
- **方盘边长小于转盘直径就判通过：** 把把手和旋转扫过的对角线纳入核对；不确定能否自由旋转就不选该转盘方案。
- **烤盘刚好贴住烤箱壁：** 查目标烤架和制造商留空要求，换更小的盘或不同容量配置。
- **无法搬盘或看不清内腔图：** 用实尺寸纸板样板、放大官方图或请店员读数；不需要为了购前核对搬动沉重烤盘。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 只做普通家用烹调设备**内腔与器皿的几何核对**，不教加热温度、烹饪时间或材料安全。品牌、转盘设计、烤架位置和地区单位不同，以准确机型说明为准。你可能发现一台外观看着更大的机器，依旧不能放进你的常用盘。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary microwave or oven when you have a **real dish, container, or baking tray you want to keep using**. Your result is whether that object can enter the exact model, let the door close, and meet turntable movement or rack-clearance conditions. A nominal litre rating cannot answer that alone. This does not decide material heat-safety, operate the appliance, or install wiring.

### Preparation and inputs

Measure the dish at its widest, deepest, and tallest points, including handles, lid, and the height when filled as intended. Find the candidate model's **usable cavity width, depth, and height** and clear door opening. For a microwave, get the turntable size, whether rotation is required, and the maker's dish conditions. For an oven, get rack positions and the exact model's surrounding pan-clearance guidance. Exterior body dimensions answer placement, not cavity fit.

### Execution

1. **Choose a real test vessel.** Use the largest everyday dish or tray you genuinely need, measuring the outer envelope in its intended orientation. Include handles on round dishes and the swept diagonal of a square dish that must rotate.
2. **Check entry and closure.** Compare the whole vessel with the exact door opening and cavity width, depth, and height. A dish that enters but prevents the door closing fails. A “20 litre” label cannot move the door.
3. **Branch by heating design.** In a turntable microwave, the vessel must **rotate freely without touching the wall or door** under the model's instructions. A flatbed or turntable-off design is usable only under its exact guidance. For an oven, check the intended rack position and leave the maker-required space for hot air rather than treating all nominal volume as packable storage.
4. **Check the real batch.** If two trays must be used together, check both at their planned rack positions. One tray passing cannot simply be doubled. Check the vessel's material, lid, and food-safety requirements separately under exact product instructions; a size pass proves none of them.
5. **Write a limited decision.** Record “vessel envelope → exact door/cavity → rotation or rack/clearance condition → fits, fails, or unconfirmed.” If door, cavity, or turntable data are missing, compare a same-size card template with a shop sample when allowed or ask for the dimensions. Pause a candidate without evidence.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when a named vessel can enter, close, and retain the required motion or airflow in the exact model and use mode. Stop extending this size conclusion when professional built-in installation, electrical connection, or unknown heat/microwave suitability is involved; consult the relevant exact instructions or professional. A paper pass is not a real cooking acceptance test.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only litres were read:** Find door and cavity dimensions and compare the vessel's outer shape. Models with similar volume can have different usable geometry.
- **A square tray's side is shorter than the turntable diameter, so it must fit:** Include handles and the diagonal swept during rotation. Reject an unproven turntable path.
- **The baking tray touches the oven wall:** Check the intended rack and maker's airflow allowance, then choose a smaller tray or different capacity configuration.
- **The dish is heavy or the drawing unreadable:** Use a dimensioned card template, enlarge the official diagram, or ask staff to read the measurements. No heavy tray needs lifting for this screen.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This Skill checks **cavity geometry against an actual vessel** for ordinary home cooking equipment. It does not teach temperatures, cooking times, or material safety. Brands, turntables, rack positions, and regional units differ; the exact model instructions govern. A physically larger-looking appliance may still fail your familiar dish.
