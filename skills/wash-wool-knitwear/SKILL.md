## 中文

当一件羊毛衫需要清洗，而“普通模式应该也差不多”开始听起来有说服力时，加载这份 Skill。先决定能否在家洗，再让水接触衣物；湿了以后才读标签属于延迟加载。

这份 Skill 不适合皮革、皮草、结构复杂西装或标签要求专业护理的衣物，也不承诺恢复已经毡化或严重缩水的羊毛。

### 准备与输入

找到清晰洗护标签，确认材质、针织结构、装饰、破损、污渍与衣物价值。准备标签允许的羊毛适用温和洗涤剂、干净水、毛巾和平整通风的晾干位置。机洗还需要准确机型说明书和明确的羊毛/精细程序。

如果读标签、搬湿衣物或在地面操作不方便，可把平铺位置设在桌面高度，使用小水量和带支撑的盆，或请现场可信的人协助搬运。羊毛吸水后会增加重量，这是物理属性，不是你握力的缺陷。

### 执行前选择路线

1. 标签禁止水洗或要求专业护理：停止，选择合格专业护理。
2. 标签允许机洗，且机器说明明确有兼容羊毛/精细程序：可以走机洗路线。
3. 标签只允许手洗：走手洗路线。
4. 标签不清、衣物昂贵、结构复杂、有脆弱装饰或已经破损：先暂停，查制造商建议或专业意见。

### 机洗执行

1. 按标签处理翻面、扣件和洗衣袋；不要与粗糙、重型或可能勾挂的衣物混洗。
2. 使用羊毛适用洗涤剂和标签剂量，不加漂白剂或临时发明的清洁组合。
3. 选择说明书确认的羊毛/轻柔程序、标签允许的冷/低温和轻柔脱水。
4. 程序结束后及时取出，用双手或毛巾托住整件衣物，避免湿重集中拉扯。

### 手洗执行

1. 用干净、标签允许的凉或温水加入适量羊毛洗涤剂。
2. 轻轻浸没和按压，让水通过纤维；不搓、不刷、不扭、不拧。
3. 浸泡保持短而有边界，以产品和标签说明为准。
4. 用相近、允许的水温轻柔漂洗，减少温度骤变和摩擦。
5. 托住整件衣物取出，轻压排水，不从一个肩膀把全部湿重提起来。

### 平铺晾干

1. 把衣物平放在干净毛巾上，卷起毛巾轻压吸水，不扭拧。
2. 移到干燥平面，按原轮廓和尺寸轻轻整理衣身、袖子、领口和下摆。
3. 在阴凉、通风处平铺，避免直接热源和强烈日晒；除非标签明确允许，不悬挂湿毛衣、不滚筒烘干。
4. 一面明显较湿时可在有支撑的情况下翻面。完全干燥前不要收进密闭衣柜。

### 成功条件

洗涤剂已漂净，衣物由整个平面支撑，轮廓接近清洗前，晾干方式符合标签，且没有超过温度、摩擦、脱水或热源限制。成功可能需要一整天，但不需要每十分钟刷新一次状态。

### 常见报错与补救

- **ERR_WET_HANGER：** 发现湿毛衣挂着时，托住整件取下，改为平铺并轻柔整理，不从领口猛拉。
- **泡沫未净：** 用标签允许的相近水温轻柔再漂，不加强搓揉来“加速”。
- **轻微走形：** 停止热和拉扯，趁仍湿润时按原尺寸轻柔整理；贵重或严重变形交给纺织护理专业人员。
- **疑似缩水或毡化：** 不继续热水、搓洗或烘干。记录发生了什么并寻求专业意见；网络秘方不是可靠回滚。
- **颜色转移或严重掉色：** 立即分开，停止继续洗其他衣物，按标签/制造商建议处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

“羊毛”可能包含混纺，不同标签、洗涤剂和机器程序不可互换。这里假设衣物处于普通家庭护理范围；蛀损、结构修复、染色和严重污染另找专业帮助。

现实副作用是一个平面会暂时失去其他用途。请先选择不会被宠物、儿童、阳光或晚餐征用的位置。

## English

Load this Skill when wool knitwear needs cleaning and “the normal cycle is probably close enough” begins to sound persuasive. Decide whether home washing is allowed before water touches the garment. Reading the label after wetting is late loading.

This Skill does not cover leather, fur, tailored suits, or garments that require professional care. It cannot promise to reverse felting or serious shrinkage that already happened.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the care label and confirm fibre content, knit construction, embellishment, damage, stains, and value. Prepare a label-compatible mild wool detergent, clean water, a clean towel, and a ventilated flat-drying surface. Machine washing also requires the exact manual and a documented compatible wool/delicate program.

If labels, lifting wet fabric, or floor-level work are inaccessible, prepare a table-height drying area, use a smaller supported basin, or arrange in-person lifting help. Wool gains weight when wet; that is a material property, not a grip-strength failure.

### Choose the route before execution

1. If the label forbids washing or requires professional care, stop and use qualified professional care.
2. If machine washing is allowed and the exact manual documents a compatible wool/delicate program, use the machine route.
3. If only hand washing is allowed, use the hand route.
4. If the label is unclear or the garment is valuable, structured, embellished, or damaged, pause for manufacturer or professional advice.

### Machine-wash execution

1. Turn, fasten, or bag the garment only as its label allows. Keep it away from rough, heavy, or snagging items.
2. Use a wool-suitable detergent at the labelled dose. Do not add bleach or improvised cleaner combinations.
3. Select the documented wool/gentle program, a permitted cool/low temperature, and gentle spin.
4. Remove it promptly. Support the whole wet garment with both hands or a towel instead of lifting all weight from one point.

### Hand-wash execution

1. Add the measured wool detergent to clean, label-compatible cool or lukewarm water.
2. Submerge and press gently so water moves through the fibres. Do not scrub, brush, twist, or wring.
3. Keep soaking short and bounded by product and garment instructions.
4. Rinse gently at a similar permitted temperature to limit sudden temperature change and friction.
5. Support the whole garment when lifting and press out water without twisting.

### Flat drying

1. Lay the garment on a clean towel, roll, and press gently to absorb water.
2. Move it to a dry flat surface and ease the body, sleeves, neck, and hem toward the original outline and measurements.
3. Dry flat with shade and airflow, away from direct heat and strong sun. Do not hang wet knitwear or tumble dry unless the label explicitly permits it.
4. Turn it with full support if one side stays much wetter. Do not close it into a wardrobe before it is fully dry.

### Success

Detergent is rinsed, the entire garment is supported, its outline remains close to the starting shape, and drying follows the label without exceeding heat, movement, spin, or friction limits. Success may take a day; refreshing the status every ten minutes is optional.

### Common errors and recovery

- **ERR_WET_HANGER:** Support the garment as you remove it, then lay it flat and reshape gently. Do not pull it down from the neck.
- **Detergent remains:** Rinse gently again at a similar permitted temperature. More rubbing is not a rinse optimization.
- **Minor distortion:** Stop heat and pulling; while damp, ease it toward the original measurements. Use a textile professional for valuable or serious damage.
- **Possible shrinkage or felting:** Stop hot water, rubbing, and drying heat. Record what happened and seek professional advice; an internet trick is not a reliable rollback.
- **Colour transfers or runs badly:** Separate it immediately, stop washing other items with it, and follow label/manufacturer guidance.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Wool may be blended, and labels, detergents, and washer programs are not interchangeable. This assumes ordinary household care. Moth damage, structural repair, dyeing, and serious contamination require other expertise.

The known side effect is temporary loss of a horizontal surface. Choose one that will not be reassigned to pets, children, sunlight, or dinner.
