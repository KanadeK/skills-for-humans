## 中文

当一项普通低风险手工具任务已完成，或必须安全暂停，其他人又要使用这个台面/区域时加载。你要**把工具和未用零件逐项收回、标明半成品状态、清理普通碎屑并恢复通路**。这里不判断组装是否可承重，不处理危险粉尘、化学品、电气/机械维修或损坏件修复。

### 准备与输入

找开工时的工具与零件记录、准确说明和原袋/存放处。先判断眼前半成品能否稳定放下；会倒、仍带张力或需人支撑时先让合适人员按说明处理，不能为了腾出台面随便立在门边。若工作产生未知粉尘、锐利大碎片或危险污染，停在隔离/通知路径，不当普通清理。

### 执行

1. **区分完成与暂停。** 对照准确用品说明，能实际完成的步骤才标完成；剩余件、松留连接或未确认位置写“未完成/勿使用”和下一步。收工具不自动使半成品可以投入使用。
2. **按开工清单点回工具。** 螺丝刀、刀头、量具和其他实际使用的普通手工具逐件看是否在场、是否损坏。遗漏的工具先在获准区域找；断裂、磨损的隔离并按厂家方法处理，不悄悄放回可用工具盒。
3. **把零件送回能辨认的位置。** 未用紧固件、垫片和配件按原袋、准确编号或步骤单独放好，写仍缺或多出的数量。地上未知小件不直接猜属哪包；留下其发现位置和待核状态。
4. **清除普通碎屑并恢复表面。** 按实际材质和场所要求收纸、包装、少量普通干碎屑，确认桌边与地面没有会被踩到的小件。未知/危险碎屑交有权人或专业渠道，不在这里规定化学清洁方法。
5. **把区域还给下一位。** 移走保护层和自己的物品，复查门与走道可通、下位使用者不会误碰未完物件。把已完成/待做、剩余零件位置和下一位负责人简短交接；无人在场就留在其确实能收到的方式里。

### 完成与停止

工具与开工记录对得上，损坏件被隔离，未用零件可找回，普通工作面与通路恢复；半成品真实状态清楚。工具失踪、危险碎屑、物件不稳或零件身份不明时不要宣布“全部完成”，保留具体缺口并交实际负责人。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一支刀头没回盒：** 查工作面、原袋和获准的落点，仍找不到就标缺，避免后来把它误算进套件。
- **“多了一颗”没人知道来源：** 单独标发现位置和候选步骤，不随意装入成品或当作废物丢掉。
- **半成品虽收好但可被拿去用：** 标“未完成/勿使用”并放在不会被误取的允许位置，说明下一步。
- **工具手柄裂或刀头磨损：** 隔离，不放进随手可拿的盒子；实际更换按厂家渠道。
- **清完桌面却地上有螺丝：** 再看周边地面和通路，收回小件；不要求你进入危险或私有区域找寻。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只用于普通小型手工具活的现实收尾，确切产品完成条件仍由它自己的说明决定。家庭、工具数量、可及性和收纳方式不同；用现有袋、小盒或纸标签即可，不必购买磁垫。走动或视力不便时可由获准的可信者检查地面和读回清点结果。副作用是“做完了”现在要等工具和通路也归位。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary low-risk hand-tool job is finished or must be paused safely and somebody else will use the area. You will **account for tools and unused parts, mark the partial item's true state, remove ordinary debris and restore access**. This does not certify load safety, handle hazardous dust or chemicals, repair electrical or mechanical equipment, or fix damaged parts.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the start-of-work tool and part note, exact item guide and original bags or storage. First check whether the partial item can rest stably. If it may topple, holds tension or needs a person supporting it, ask suitable help under its instructions rather than standing it by a doorway to clear a table. Unknown dust, large sharp fragments or hazardous material exits this ordinary cleanup into isolation and the responsible contact.

### Execution

1. **Separate finished from paused.** Against the exact item guide, mark only steps physically completed as done. Note remaining pieces, deliberately loose joints or unknown locations as “unfinished / do not use,” with the next step. Putting tools away does not make a partial item usable.
2. **Account for tools against the start list.** Check each screwdriver, bit, measure and other ordinary hand tool actually used, including visible damage. Search for a missing tool only in permitted places. Isolate broken or worn ones and follow the maker's route; do not quietly return them to a ready-to-use box.
3. **Return parts to identifiable places.** Put unused fasteners, washers and fittings in original bags or separate groups by exact ID or step. Record missing or extra counts. Do not guess which bag owns an unknown piece found on the floor; note its discovery place and pending identity.
4. **Remove ordinary debris and restore the surface.** Under the material and site guidance, collect paper, packaging and small ordinary dry debris. Check the table edge and floor for small pieces someone could step on. Hazardous or unknown fragments go to the responsible person or specialist route, not a made-up chemical cleanup method.
5. **Return the area to its next user.** Remove your protector and goods, check doors and paths again, and keep an unfinished item away from accidental use. Hand over done/pending state, remaining-part location and next owner in a way the recipient will actually receive.

### Success and stop

Tools reconcile with the start note, damaged pieces are isolated, unused parts remain findable, and the ordinary surface and route are restored; the item's true unfinished state remains visible. Do not say “all done” when a tool is missing, debris is hazardous, the item is unstable or a part's identity is unknown. Keep the specific gap open with its real owner.

### Common errors and recovery

- **One driver bit has not returned:** Check the area, original bags and permitted landing spots. Mark it missing if still absent so it is not later mistaken for a kit part.
- **Nobody knows where an extra screw came from:** Label its discovery place and possible steps. Do not install it arbitrarily or discard it as waste.
- **A partial item could be mistaken for ready:** Mark “unfinished / do not use” and place it where it will not be taken accidentally, with a next step.
- **A handle is cracked or a tip worn:** Isolate it from the ready box and use the maker's replacement route.
- **The table is clear but a screw lies on the floor:** Recheck nearby floor and paths and recover small parts, without entering dangerous or private areas.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is practical closeout of one small ordinary hand job. The item's own guide defines its completion. Homes, tool counts, access needs and storage differ; existing bags, boxes or paper labels suffice without a magnetic mat purchase. An authorised trusted person can inspect the floor and read back the count when movement or sight is difficult. “Done” now waits for tools and routes to go home too.
