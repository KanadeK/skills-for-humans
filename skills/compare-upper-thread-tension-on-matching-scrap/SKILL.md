## 中文

当你的家用机能正常直线试缝，但**换了布或线，需核本机允许的上线张力设置**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是同材废布上至少两段可比针迹与记录的设置，或发现穿线/梭芯问题而停下重穿/求修。它不是第一条直线，也不让初学者拧梭芯张力螺钉。不同机型默认数值不同，不把某 Brother 的“4”写成通用设定。

### 准备与输入

先确认上线走完整张力/挑线杆路径、梭芯型号和安装方式正确、针适合布和线；否则调张力会把根因藏起来。用两块相同或折法相同的自有废布，记录机型、原默认设置和本机允许的调节范围。手册不开放张力调节、机针损坏、漏电或异常声响时停并联系厂商。

### 执行

1. **在原设置下做基线线迹。** 按本机方式低速缝一小段，停机后看正反：上线/下线交接是否大致在布层内，有无背面松圈、正面下线、皱褶或跳针。写“布/线/针/设置/观察”，不只凭正面美观判断。
2. **先排除穿线问题。** 背面大量上线圈或上线不受压脚张力控制时，先关机按本机图抬压脚重穿上线；正面下线异常先核梭芯方向和沟槽。问题仍在时，不立刻猛拧张力或梭芯螺钉。
3. **仅在手册允许且线路正确时改一个小步。** 按本机说明在上线张力控件上朝对应方向调一小格，保留原数值，用同材废布再缝同样短段。一次只改上线张力，不同时换针、线和压脚来制造无法解释的差异。
4. **对照并决定保留或退回。** 两段正反对照，找线结位置、皱与线圈是否改善；改善才记录可用于此布/线的临时设置。变坏就回原值；仍不能得到可接受线迹则停并求本机厂商/维修，正式布不继续试。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

记录含原设置、一次允许的小调整、同材正反两段结果与明确保留/退回/求修判断；若穿线错路，已先修根因。只转旋钮却没同材试缝，不算完成。

- **背面一团松线：** 先重穿上线、检查压脚与挑线杆，再试；不把旋钮拧到极端。
- **正面看到下线：** 先查梭芯和线材，再按手册允许范围轻调。
- **布皱或针脚跳跃：** 核布/针/线相容，停用正式料并求指导。
- **调后更差：** 回原设定并保留两段样，不连续乱改。
- **手眼操作不便：** 对比色试线、放大照明和可信协助看两面，由你留真实设置记录。

### 假设与边界

厂商故障指南提示很多“张力问题”其实是错误穿线，真实机型手册拥有张力方向与允许范围。本篇不碰梭芯预设螺钉、内部定时、针杆或专业调校。

## English

Load this Skill when a home machine can make an ordinary straight trial seam but **a fabric or thread change calls for checking its manual-allowed upper tension**. Finish with at least two comparable matching-scrap lines and a recorded keep/revert decision, or discover a threading/bobbin fault and stop to correct or service it. This is not the first straight test and never asks a beginner to turn a bobbin tension screw. A Brother example “4” is not universal.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm upper thread fully enters tension and take-up path, bobbin class and insertion are right, and needle suits cloth/thread. Otherwise knob changes hide the root cause. Prepare equal owned scrap or the same fold, recording model, original default, and allowed setting range. Stop for a manual without user adjustment, damaged needle, electrical issue, or unusual sound and seek the maker.

### Execution

1. **Sew a baseline at original setting.** Make a short slow line by the model method and inspect front and reverse after stopping. Are upper and lower threads meeting broadly within the fabric layers? Look for underside loops, lower thread on top, puckers, or skipped stitches. Write cloth/thread/needle/setting and observations, not just “front looks nice.”
2. **Rule out misthreading first.** For large underside upper loops or no upper tension response, power down and rethread with foot raised by this manual. For unusual lower thread on top, verify bobbin orientation and slot first. Do not immediately crank tension or a bobbin screw.
3. **When the manual permits and paths are right, change one small step.** Move only upper tension control in the model-indicated direction by one small increment and preserve original number. Sew the same short line on matching scrap. Do not also change needle, thread, and foot or the comparison becomes uninterpretable.
4. **Compare and keep or revert.** Compare both sides for thread interlock, loops, and puckers. Record a temporary setting for this cloth/thread only if better; otherwise return to original. Stop and seek model-aware service if no acceptable seam appears, without testing on finished fabric.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The record shows original setting, one allowed change, two same-material front/reverse results, and keep/revert/service decision; a threading cause was corrected first when found. Turning a dial without a matching scrap test is unfinished.

- **Underside becomes a loose nest:** Rethread upper path, presser foot and take-up lever before another test; avoid extreme knob positions.
- **Bobbin thread appears on top:** Verify bobbin and materials before manual-limited fine adjustment.
- **Fabric puckers or stitches skip:** Recheck compatible fabric, needle, and thread; stop using final material.
- **Adjustment worsens it:** Return to original and keep both samples instead of random repeated changes.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Contrasting test threads, magnification, and trusted two-sided inspection can help you retain true setting notes.

### Assumptions and limits

Maker guidance shows many apparent tension defects are misthreading. The exact model manual owns direction and range. No factory bobbin screw, internal timing, needle-bar, or professional calibration is included.
