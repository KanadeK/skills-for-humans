## 中文

当你已到正确机场的安检区域，准备把普通随身物交给**眼前这条通道的实际流程**检查时加载。目标是在不阻挡别人、不遗失物品的前提下，按官方和现场人员的要求整理、出示与取回自己的东西。这里不判断争议物品能否上机、不解释证件资格、不提供规避安检的方法，也不把某机场的“电脑不用拿出来”当全球规则。

### 准备与输入

用出发机场和其安检机构的当期页面核对一般准备要求；到了现场再读这条通道的标牌、屏幕及工作人员指示。确认自己拿的是本次行程所需的登机/身份材料，并在可随手取出的安全位置放好，不把证件、二维码或账号信息展示给无关人。随身物能否携带属于前一项行李规则核对；对具体物件仍不确定时，先问正式人员，不塞到包底碰运气。

### 执行

1. **站在不挡通道的准备位置。** 在排队前或现场允许的整理区停一下，看这条通道是否要提前出示证件/登机牌、是否要求特殊队列或协助。队伍和设备不同，先观察正式指示，不照搬上次旅行的动作。
2. **让小物有固定去处。** 把钥匙、硬币、手机等口袋物品按现场规则收进本人可认的随身包或指定托盘；不要把多件小物散放在他人托盘或公共台面。外套、鞋、腰带、液体袋、电脑和其他电子物是否取出，按**这里的标牌和工作人员**决定；在尚未被要求时无需先全部拆开。
3. **到检查点时按指示摆放。** 在要求的时机取出或展示所需物品，把包与托盘放到指定位置，听不清或看不懂就向工作人员请其重复/说明。若通道设备、额外检查或物件判断与你预期不同，以工作人员的正式程序处理；不争抢、隐匿、换队绕过或替别人拿不明物件。
4. **本人按安排接受检查。** 听从该安检机构对人员与随身物的指引。有行动、感官、语言或其他实际协助需求，可在现场明确提出需要怎样的说明或帮助；具体检查方式由正式人员决定，不因本 Skill 获得免检资格。
5. **在允许处收回并点清物品。** 通过后在不会挡住出料口的位置核对本人托盘、包、证件、手机及随身物件；有物件被继续检查，按工作人员给出的流程等待。发现缺失先在安全位置告知正式人员，不逆向穿越安检区。随后再看官方登机口信息。

### 完成与停止

你已按**本次、此地、此通道**的正式要求摆放与出示物品，完成要求的检查，并取回或记录仍由工作人员处理的物件，本轮完成。能否通过由现场安检决定，不能靠这份文案保证。若标牌、旧网页和工作人员要求冲突，先停下问清眼前规则；对受限制或特殊物品、医学设备等按机场/安检机构专门规定与人员指引处理，不自行编造例外。

### 常见报错与补救

- **上次飞行要求取电脑，这次标牌说留包里：** 以当前安检机构和这条通道的指示为准；不要为了“保险”反复拆包挡住队伍。
- **证件或登机牌一时找不到：** 退到不挡人的位置核对本人材料，必要时联系实际航司/机场；不借他人二维码或闯过查验处。
- **小物散在托盘或拿错包：** 等人员允许取物时逐项点清，拿不准就问工作人员，不带走别人的东西。
- **电子设备、液体或其他物件被要求复检：** 按现场程序提供或等待，不尝试改变包装来躲避检查。
- **不能听清/看清或无法自行整理托盘：** 明确告知人员你需要重复说明、文字/语言支持或实际协助，不猜下一步。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通机场离境安检前后的一次整理与物品交接，不覆盖携带资格、安检技术、危险物品、医疗建议、移民/海关或执法争议。机场、通道、设备与当天规则会变；例如有些机场允许电脑与液体留在包内，其他地方可能要求取出。手机不便用可准备纸质材料；视觉、听觉、行动或语言不便可请求机场实际提供的协助。副作用是口袋变空，但证件与手机还要自己点清。

## English

Load this Skill at the correct airport's security area when you are ready to present ordinary carry-on belongings under **this actual lane's current process**. The result is staging, presenting and retrieving your own items without blocking others or losing them. This does not decide whether a disputed item may fly, interpret identity eligibility, offer a screening bypass or turn one airport's “leave your laptop in the bag” into a world rule.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the departure airport and screening authority's current general guidance, then read the signs, displays and staff directions for the lane in front of you. Keep your own boarding and identity materials for this trip accessible but secure; do not display documents, codes or account details to unrelated people. Whether an object may be carried belongs to the earlier baggage-rule check. If a particular item remains uncertain, ask official staff rather than hiding it at the bottom of a bag.

### Execution

1. **Use a staging spot clear of the lane.** Before joining or where the airport allows preparation, see whether this lane wants documents ready, directs you to a particular queue or offers assistance. Equipment and queues differ; read official directions instead of performing last trip's routine from memory.
2. **Give small items a known home.** Put keys, coins, phone and other pocket items in a bag you can identify or a tray as this lane instructs. Do not scatter them on a public counter or another person's tray. Whether to remove a coat, shoes, belt, liquids bag, laptop or other electronics depends on **these signs and staff**. You need not unpack everything before being asked.
3. **Present items when instructed.** At the requested point, show or remove what is required and put the bag and trays where directed. If you cannot hear or read the instruction, ask staff to repeat or explain it. If equipment, further inspection or an item's treatment differs from what you expected, follow the official process. Do not conceal it, switch lines to bypass a check or carry an unknown item for someone else.
4. **Take your own directed screening.** Follow this authority's instructions for people and belongings. If mobility, senses, language or another practical need calls for help, state what explanation or assistance you need. Official staff decide the screening method; this Skill does not exempt anyone.
5. **Retrieve and count in the permitted area.** Once through, step clear of the belt or output and check your tray, bag, documents, phone and other belongings. Wait under staff directions for anything still being inspected. If something is missing, tell official staff from a safe position; do not go backward through security. Then check current official gate information.

### Success and stop

This round is complete when you have presented items under the **current place and lane's** official directions, completed required checks, and retrieved or recorded anything staff still hold. Screening clearance is the authority's decision, not a promise from this text. If signs, an old webpage and staff instructions conflict, pause and ask for the current rule. Restricted or special items and medical devices need the airport or authority's specific process, not an invented exception here.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Last airport removed laptops but this lane says leave them packed:** Follow today's authority and lane; repeated unpacking “just in case” can block the queue.
- **Boarding pass or ID cannot be found:** Move out of the flow and check your own materials; ask the actual airline or airport if needed. Do not borrow another person's code or push past a check.
- **Small items scatter or a bag looks unfamiliar:** Count items when staff permit collection and ask if uncertain. Do not take another person's property.
- **Electronics, liquids or another item needs further inspection:** Provide or wait for it under the posted process; do not repack it to evade a check.
- **You cannot hear, see or set up a tray independently:** Tell staff the explanation, written or language support, or practical assistance you need rather than guessing the next step.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is one ordinary pre-screening and belongings-handoff routine at an airport. It does not cover item eligibility, screening technology, dangerous goods, medical advice, immigration, customs or an enforcement dispute. Airports, lanes, scanners and rules change; some let laptops and liquids remain packed while others may require removal. Use paper travel materials if a phone is difficult, and request assistance the actual airport offers for sight, hearing, mobility or language needs. Your pockets may be empty; you still need to count your documents and phone.
