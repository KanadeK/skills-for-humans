## 中文

你要寄一件普通、不易腐且已知内容的日常物品，还没封箱时，用这份 Skill 先确认**选定承运商、具体服务和寄送路线**是否接收，再包装、核对资料并准备交寄。完成是包裹符合你刚核对的规则、内容在箱内不明显晃动、旧标识被处理、当前标签可读，并知道去哪里交；实际承运商收件、价格和到达日期仍待现场或下单确认。商家退货有自己的授权与标签流程，先按商家规则办理；本文不决定退款。

### 准备与输入

写下物品是什么、有多少、是否含电池/液体/气雾剂/香水/易腐品/磁性件等可能受限成分，寄出地与目的地、拟用的承运商及服务。准备寄件与收件资料、结实且可完整封合的箱或该服务允许的包装、相容填充物与胶带。若为重复使用的箱子，先检查是否压塌、破洞或受潮，找出旧地址、条码及危险品标记。准备能查承运商当期官方规则的方式；无网络时可先向其柜台或客服确认再购买标签或交件。

对任何内容不明、危险品/受限品可能性不清、跨境、需特殊温控、活体、现金或高价值物，停止这份普通寄件流程，向该承运商查专门规则或换合适渠道。不要把电池或香水“当作普通小物”封进去，也不要通过隐瞒内容试探承运商会不会发现。

### 执行

1. **先确认承运条件。** 在拟用承运商的当前官方页面或柜台核对物品类别、路线、包装、尺寸/重量、标签和交件点限制。规则因服务和地区而异；查到一个承运商允许，不代表另一个也允许。遇到限制或说明不清，先暂停普通寄件并求该方确认。
2. **选稳固包装并固定物品。** 用完整、能承载内容重量的包装。若物品会碰撞，分别包护并填住空隙，尤其保护边角；合箱前检查内容及必需附件，不把无关个人文件塞进去。轻轻移动未封箱的包装看是否明显移位；会晃动就补合适填充，不靠外贴“易碎”替代保护。易碎、形状异常或价值高的物品需按承运方专用包装条件另行确认。
3. **处理旧外观再封合。** 重复使用的包装仅在材质仍完好且该服务允许时用。彻底去掉或遮住旧地址、条码和不适用的危险品/特殊处理标记。按选定承运商要求封牢开口和接缝；不能仅用松绳或一小片普通文具胶带把箱子合上。
4. **核对并贴当前标签。** 按已确定的服务准备寄件/收件资料及它要求的标签。贴之前再次与自己可信的收件资料核对姓名、地址、邮编/区域、必要联系方法和退回地址；不要把陌生聊天中临时出现的地址当成已确认目的地。贴在承运方要求的可读平面，别盖住条码，也别让多个旧条码同时出现。若服务提供柜台打印或无障碍寄件方式，先确认该点实际支持，再带必要资料前往。
5. **做交件前最后一遍。** 包裹外没有矛盾标签，内容不会明显晃动，箱体与封口完好，尺寸/重量与所选服务一致；记录物品和封箱状态所需的最少证据，并保管寄件资料。确认交件地点、营业/截件时间以及如何取得收件凭证。到柜台仍被要求补充信息或改包装时，按该方实际要求返工，不把“已贴标签”说成“已寄达”。

### 成功条件

一件已知普通物品已按**某个明确的承运商和服务**核过准入条件，包装和标签准备到可提交，交件点与凭证获取方式已知；或者你识别规则不清/物品受限并在付费、封箱或交件前停下。真正寄出以承运商接收并给出凭证为准，本文的完成不保证准时送达或赔偿。

### 常见报错与补救

- **物品里发现电池、液体、香水或气雾剂：** 停止普通流程，核对承运方对实际物品及路线的专门规则；不拆掉警示标记继续寄。
- **旧箱有其他地址或危险品标识：** 若箱子仍合格，完全去除或遮住旧标记再贴新标签；不能消除歧义就换包装。
- **合箱后物品会移动：** 重新打开，增加适合的缓冲和固定，再按规则封箱；一张“易碎”贴纸不承担物理保护。
- **没有打印机或不好到柜台：** 查该服务是否支持柜台打印、预约取件或其他可及方式，以及费用和所需信息；支持与否由该服务当期规则决定。
- **尺寸、重量或地址仍不确定：** 先测量/称重或向收件人核对，不支付错误服务的标签，不将不确定资料填成猜测。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里假设是普通国内包裹，内容和路线可确定；实际可寄性由承运商及当期规则决定。跨境海关、危险品分类、法律责任、保险赔付、专业包装与商家退货授权不在本 Skill。无打印机可选承运方明确支持的现场方案；行动或阅读不便时可使用官方可及服务或请可信的人协助核对，但不要把个人地址公开贴到求助帖。包装会消耗材料；费用、时效、收件凭证都在选择服务和实际交件时才确定。

## English

Use this Skill before sealing an ordinary, known, durable domestic item for shipment. Check whether your **chosen carrier, service, and route** accept it, then protect it, clear old markings, verify details, and prepare it for handover. The result is a parcel ready to present under the rules you actually checked, with contents that do not obviously shift and a readable current label. Carrier acceptance, price, and arrival date still depend on the transaction. A seller return follows its own authorization and label process first; this file does not decide a refund.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the item and quantity, any battery, liquid, aerosol, fragrance, perishable, magnetic, or other possibly restricted component, origin, destination, and intended carrier/service. Have sender and recipient details, a sound closable box or other packaging allowed by that service, suitable cushioning, and tape. For a reused box, check for crushing, holes, or damp damage and locate old addresses, barcodes, and hazard markings. Have a way to read the carrier's current official rules; without online access, ask its counter or support before buying a label or tendering the parcel.

Stop this ordinary workflow for unknown contents, unclear regulated status, cross-border shipping, temperature-controlled goods, live animals, cash, or unusually valuable items. Ask the chosen carrier for the specific rules or find a suitable channel. Do not seal a battery or perfume as an “ordinary small thing” or hide the contents to see if it passes.

### Execution

1. **Check acceptance first.** Read the intended carrier's current official guidance or ask its counter about the actual item class, route, packaging, size/weight, label, and tender-point restrictions. Rules vary by service and region; one carrier's permission does not transfer to another. If restricted or unclear, pause this ordinary route and seek a decision from that carrier.
2. **Choose sound packaging and immobilize the item.** Use intact packaging strong enough for the contents. Wrap separate parts and fill voids if they might strike one another, especially at corners. Before closing, check the item and necessary accessories; leave unrelated personal papers out. Move the unsealed package gently to check for obvious shifting. Add appropriate cushioning if it moves; a “fragile” sticker does not replace physical protection. Fragile, unusual-shape, or high-value items need the carrier's specific packing rules.
3. **Clear old markings, then seal.** Reuse packaging only when sound and permitted by the service. Fully remove or cover previous addresses, barcodes, and inapplicable hazard or handling marks. Secure openings and seams under the chosen carrier's instructions. Loose string or a small strip of office tape is not a reliable closure.
4. **Verify and apply the current label.** Prepare sender and recipient details and the label required by the selected service. Before attaching it, compare name, address, postal/area code, required contact method, and return address with your trusted source. A new address seen only in an unfamiliar chat is not verified. Place the label on the readable flat surface the carrier specifies, without obscuring its barcode or leaving competing old codes. If you need a counter-printed or accessible alternative, confirm that the particular location offers it before you travel.
5. **Make one pre-handover check.** There is no conflicting outside label, the item does not obviously shift, box and seals are sound, and the measured size/weight fits the chosen service. Keep only the minimum useful record of contents and sealed condition with your shipping details. Confirm the tender location, opening/cutoff time, and how to obtain an acceptance receipt. If the counter asks for more information or different packaging, meet its actual requirement; a printed label is not proof of shipment.

### Success

One known ordinary item has passed the current acceptance check for **a named carrier and service**, is protected and labeled for presentation, and has a known handover and receipt route. Recognizing unclear or restricted contents and stopping before purchase, sealing, or tender is also a valid result. Only carrier acceptance with a receipt proves dispatch; preparation does not promise arrival time or compensation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A battery, liquid, perfume, or aerosol appears among the contents:** Stop and check the carrier's special rules for the actual item and route. Do not hide or remove required warnings to continue.
- **A reused box still shows another address or hazard marking:** If the box remains sound, fully remove or obscure old marks before the new label. Replace the packaging if ambiguity remains.
- **The item moves after closure:** Reopen, add suitable cushioning and restraint, and reseal under the rules. A “fragile” label cannot do the cushioning work.
- **No printer or counter access:** Check whether this service actually offers counter printing, pickup, or another accessible route, with its costs and required information.
- **Size, weight, or address remains uncertain:** Measure and weigh or verify with the recipient first. Do not buy the wrong service or fill uncertain fields by guessing.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary domestic parcel with known contents and route. Actual eligibility follows the current carrier rules. Cross-border customs, dangerous-goods classification, legal liability, insurance compensation, professional packing, and seller return authorization are outside this Skill. Use a confirmed counter option if you lack a printer. For mobility or reading barriers, use official accessible service or trusted help with details; do not publish private addresses in a help post. Packaging consumes material. Price, timing, and proof of acceptance become definite only when you choose and tender the actual service.
