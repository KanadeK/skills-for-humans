## 中文

当你的一轮普通家用洗碗机显示结束，准备把器皿放回可用位置时，用这份 Skill **实际核查清洁与干燥**。它从机器结束开始，不重新教授装载或选程序；铃声、完成百分比和“消毒”按钮名称都不能代替检查具体器皿。异常漏水、电气警报、焦味或破玻璃时先停止普通取出，按机型和现场安全指导处理。

### 准备与输入

确认机器已按说明停稳并可安全开门，留意热水、蒸汽与烫的器皿；打开时让脸和手避开蒸汽。找干净、干燥的放置处和可隔离需重洗器皿的区域，读本机取出/过滤网与洗剂仓的使用者检查说明。准备按器皿标签允许的自然晾干方式；严重破损器皿不直接混入待用柜。

### 执行

1. **安全开门并看整体状态。** 在停稳和适当降温后按说明开门，先看是否有未溶洗剂、明显食物残渣、积水、碎玻璃或异常警示。需要先处理故障或碎片时不要盲伸手进架缝；关机并按厂家/管理者指导求助，不拆内部电器或排水结构。
2. **按本机适用顺序逐层取出。** 若上层杯底积水可能滴到下层，先取下层；注意餐具与边缘，玻璃或陶瓷有裂纹先隔离。不同机器抽屉与架位不同，仍按准确手册调整顺序。每件拿出时看实际食品接触面，不让未净器皿混进干净柜。
3. **分清干净、未净与未干。** 无可见食物/明显洗剂残留且完好的器皿进入干净组；仍有污物或产品残留的单独放，查原装载遮挡、洗剂仓和该器皿说明，再按允许的手洗或另一受支持周期处理。干净但仍潮的器皿按材质允许方法晾干，不把积水扣进密闭柜或餐盒。
4. **按说明核查可见的机器交接。** 洗剂仓若有残留、滤网/喷臂可见被大块食物阻挡，按本机**使用者可做的**步骤处理；反复不净、排水异常或警报持续，停止下轮使用并报管理者/专业服务。不要往洗剂仓追加手洗液或其他清洁品“补一下”。
5. **收尾去向。** 确认干净且已干的器皿归到清洁位置；未干/未净件有下一步，不遗忘在机内。按本机说明使门与机内保持适当状态，清理自己的洒水与残渣，不让开放的机门挡住通道。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

这批器皿被分成“可归位”“需继续干燥”“需重洗/停用待查”并各有明确去向；取出过程没有烫伤、玻璃割伤或被忽略的机器故障。完成程序只是检查起点。

- **杯底一圈水滴到下层：** 暂停，改按本机适用的下层先取方式，潮的干净器皿另放晾干。
- **一只碗里面还有食物：** 不归柜，查是否被套叠/遮住喷臂后按其材质允许方式重洗。
- **洗剂仓还有整块产品：** 检查是否被器皿挡住及产品/机型说明；反复发生则停用求助，不直接加量重跑。
- **看到裂玻璃或锐边：** 不徒手伸入，隔离相关区域，按本机和当地安全指导处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

仅针对正常家用洗碗机的一轮普通器皿，不声明商业食品安全消毒合格或处理设备内部维修。机器款式、器皿材料、洗剂和水质不同，完成后的水珠或残留需按实际观察判断。视力、握力或弯腰受限时用明亮台面、少量逐件取出和可信现场协助；无法安全够到热/破损器皿就停止。可能多出晾架或重洗工作。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary household dishwasher signals completion and you are about to put dishes away. **Check actual cleaning and dryness.** This starts after the cycle, not at loading or program choice. Chimes, progress percentages, and a cycle named Sanitize do not inspect a particular dish for you. Stop ordinary unloading for leakage, electrical warning, burning odour, or broken glass and use the model and site's safety guidance.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the machine has stopped and the manual allows opening; expect hot water, steam, and ware. Keep face and hands clear of escaping steam. Have a clean dry landing area and a separate place for items needing rewashing. Read this model's unloading, filter, and dispenser user-check directions. Prepare label-compatible air drying for damp items. Broken ware does not go straight to a ready-use cupboard.

### Execution

1. **Open safely and inspect the overall state.** After a full stop and suitable cooling, open by the manual. Look for undissolved detergent, visible food, standing water, broken glass, or warnings. Do not reach blind into racks for a fault or shards; stop use and follow maker or site help without dismantling electrical or drain parts.
2. **Unload in this model's suitable order.** If cups above could spill pooled water below, take the lower rack first. Watch cutlery and edges; separate chipped or cracked glass and ceramic. Drawer and rack layouts vary, so follow the exact manual where it differs. Inspect each food-contact face as it leaves; unclean dishes do not enter the clean cupboard.
3. **Separate clean, unclean, and damp.** Intact ware without visible food or obvious detergent residue enters a clean group. Set aside soil or product residue and check loading shadow, dispenser, and item directions, then use permitted hand washing or another supported cycle. Clean but damp ware air-dries under its material directions rather than trapping pooled water in a closed cupboard or lunchbox.
4. **Check user-accessible machine handoff under the manual.** If detergent remains in the cup or a visible filter or spray arm is blocked by large food, use only this model's **user-permitted** steps. Stop the next cycle and contact a manager or qualified service for repeated poor cleaning, drainage trouble, or persistent alerts. Do not add hand dish soap or another cleaner to “top up” the dispenser.
5. **Give everything a destination.** Return clean dry ware to a clean place. Damp and unclean items have actual next steps and are not forgotten inside. Leave door and interior as this model requires, clear your spills and scraps, and do not block a path with an open door.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

The load has three real outcomes—put away, dry more, rewash or hold for fault assessment—and each item has a destination. Unloading did not cause a burn or glass cut or overlook a machine fault. Cycle completion began the inspection.

- **Water from a cup spills onto the lower rack:** Pause and use the model-compatible lower-first sequence. Air-dry the newly damp clean item.
- **Food remains inside one bowl:** Do not store it. Check nesting or blocked spray and wash again by that item's material permissions.
- **A whole detergent piece remains in the dispenser:** Check for blocked opening and product/manual directions; stop and seek help if it repeats instead of increasing dose.
- **Broken glass or a sharp edge appears:** Do not reach in bare-handed. Isolate the area and follow machine and local safety guidance.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is one normal household cycle of ordinary dishes, not a commercial food-safety sanitising claim or internal equipment repair. Model, ware, product, and water vary, so evaluate drops and residue as observed. Use good light, a small item-by-item lift, and trusted help for limited vision, grip, or reach. Stop when hot or broken ware cannot be handled safely. More rack drying or another wash may be needed.
