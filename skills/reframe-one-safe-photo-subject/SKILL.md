## 中文

你已看见一个想拍的对象，但预览画面中背景杂乱、主体不清楚时，用这份 Skill 在**原地或近旁的安全位置**拍两个不同机位，现场比较哪一个更表达你的视觉重点。结果是同一对象的两张可对照照片和一句取景理由；这是拍摄时的构图判断，不是事后对整组照片做挑选。

### 准备与输入

先确认对象所在地点允许普通拍摄，现场标志或人员要求不明就询问或放弃。优先选静物、建筑细节或景观；避开可识别的人、住户窗内、车牌或敏感信息。设备能显示预览、存下两张即可。决定你希望读者先看哪里，例如一处轮廓、颜色对比或一段线条；不需要把三分法当硬规定。检查脚下和通行空间，尤其在道路、台阶、水边和人群附近；任何角度都不值得越过栏杆、堵路或后退到看不见的边缘。

### 执行

1. **做第一张基准图。** 在安全稳定的位置拍一张，并在预览中指出主体。看四边有没有抢眼的杂物或私人内容；若有私人内容，先改方向或换对象，不留作“待修图”。
2. **只改机位或画框的一件事。** 可在安全范围内向一侧移动半步，或原地改变相机高度/横竖方向；手部或行动不便时让设备移动而非勉强移动身体。让背景、空白或主体与边缘的关系明显不同，再拍第二张。改变的是你能看见的关系，不是器材规格。
3. **现场并排比较。** 站稳后看两张：主体在两张中都可辨吗？哪张的背景更帮助你原定的视觉重点？背景中的无关人脸、地址或受限制内容若出现，停止使用该张并按设备方式删除或重拍；不要边走边看屏幕。
4. **说出一次选择。** 写或口述“我想突出____；第二张因为____更清楚”，也允许第一张更好。保留两张作对照或按你的文件需要处理；这里不上传、不公开，也不把照片好坏归咎于昂贵设备。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有同一允许对象的两张机位关系确实不同、隐私边界合适的照片，并能指出**背景或画框怎样改变主体的可读性**。仅连拍两次而画面几乎相同，不算完成比较。

- **换位后主体反而消失：** 回到基准位置，只调整设备高度或横竖方向再试，不为“新角度”保留无主体画面。
- **没有安全的第二站位：** 原地改变取景方向或高度；仍无可见差异就停止并换对象，不冒险跨路或越界。
- **人进入第二张或出现私密细节：** 不使用该张，等人离开、改方向或换对象；不靠后期模糊来替代现场的隐私判断。
- **屏幕反光看不清比较：** 移到安全阴影处再看，或用相机放大功能检查主体；无法核对时留到能核对的地点，不虚称完成。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

你无需专业镜头、自动修图或远距离步行；相机、手机和固定座位上的小幅取景都能做。视觉不便时可以用放大、可访问的口述或可信协助比较，但不要编造看不到的背景。现场许可和他人的意愿因地点而异，本篇不提供法律结论。照片可能带地点信息，之后分享前另行检查；完成本篇不需要分享。

## English

Use this Skill when you have found a subject but the preview has a cluttered background or weak visual focus. From **your current safe position or another nearby safe one**, take two views and compare which shows your intended point more clearly. The result is two comparable frames of the same subject and one framing reason. This is an on-site composition decision, not later selection of a whole photo set.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm that ordinary photography is allowed at the subject's location. Ask or leave if signs or staff instructions are unclear. Prefer a still object, architectural detail, or landscape; avoid identifiable people, private windows, plates, and sensitive information. Your camera or phone only needs a preview and room for two frames. Decide what you want a viewer to notice first, perhaps an outline, colour contrast, or line. The rule of thirds is not mandatory. Check your footing and shared space, especially near roads, steps, water, or crowds. No angle justifies crossing a barrier, blocking a path, or backing toward an unseen edge.

### Execution

1. **Take a baseline frame.** Shoot from a stable safe place and point out the subject in the preview. Check every edge for distracting clutter or private material. If private material appears, change direction or subject now rather than keep it for later editing.
2. **Change one framing condition.** Shift slightly sideways within a safe area, or change camera height or orientation in place. If moving is difficult, move the device rather than force your body. Make the relation among subject, background, and empty space visibly different, then take a second frame. The useful change is visual, not a camera specification.
3. **Compare on site.** Once stationary, view both frames. Is the subject legible in each? Which background better serves the point you chose? If an unrelated face, address, or restricted detail appears, stop using that frame and delete or retake it using the device. Do not study the screen while walking.
4. **State one choice.** Write or say “I want to show ____; the second frame is clearer because ____.” The first may be better instead. Keep both for comparison or handle them as your files require. No upload or publication is needed, and expensive equipment is not the success condition.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have two privacy-appropriate photos of the same permitted subject with a real framing difference and can explain **how background or frame edges changed the subject's readability**. A near-identical burst of two frames does not make a comparison.

- **The subject disappears after moving:** Return to the baseline point and try only camera height or orientation; do not keep a subjectless shot merely to claim a new angle.
- **No second standing place is safe:** Reframe in place. If there is still no visible difference, stop and choose another subject instead of crossing a road or boundary.
- **A person or private detail enters the second frame:** Do not use it. Wait, turn away, or choose another subject; a promise to blur later does not replace the on-site privacy decision.
- **Glare makes comparison impossible:** Move to safe shade or enlarge the image on the camera. If you still cannot check, wait until you can rather than claim success.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

You need no professional lens, automatic editing, or long walk. A camera, phone, or small changes from a fixed seat can work. If seeing the preview is difficult, use magnification, accessible description, or trusted help, without inventing details you cannot inspect. Permissions and people's wishes vary by location; this Skill offers no legal conclusion. Files may contain location data, so check before any later sharing. Sharing is not needed to finish.
