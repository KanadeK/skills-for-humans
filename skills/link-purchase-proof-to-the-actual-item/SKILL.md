## 中文

当你买到一件普通用品，收据/订单和书面保修或使用条款可能以后要找，而实物与纸张还没建立联系时加载。你要让**这件实际用品对应哪一张凭证、哪一行商品、哪份书面条款，以及资料放哪里**都可被本人找回。这里不判断保修期限、范围、退款资格或法定权利，也不做消费核算。

### 准备与输入

核对眼前物品的厂家、准确型号/变体及购买方式；准备纸质收据、订单确认或店家给的其它真实凭据，和随商品附的书面说明/保修文件。多件同买时需要找到这件在单据中的实际行，不能用订单总额代表每件。原件、电子原始邮件和复印件身份不同，按实际商家需求保留来源，不将敏感资料传到公开网页。

### 执行

1. **从实物读回身份。** 记商品的中性名称、厂家与可安全读出的型号/变体；不拆壳找序列号。若凭据只写系列或与实物不符，标“对应待确认”，不要假装两者是同一款。
2. **从凭据定位到这一件。** 记商家/开具方、纸上写的购买日期、收据/订单识别信息及对应商品行。订单含多件时只将这件与其行关联；礼物或他人购买时先确认谁有权保管与分享凭据。
3. **把书面条款并在同一条线索里。** 保留商品随附的准确保修/使用条件、文档标题或适用型号，记其来自包装、商家还是厂商。条款没有、版次不匹配或只见口头许诺时写未知，向实际提供方问清，不自己推断当然享有某服务。
4. **给纸本或已有电子件一个可控位置。** 在纸质索引写中性“物品 → 凭证所在文件夹/订单邮件位置 → 条款位置”。私密价格、地址、支付资料和完整序列号不写在文件夹外侧或共享表；已有电子件可保留在本人可控位置，不要求为本 Skill 扫描上传。
5. **做一次反向找回。** 从物品名称按索引找到对应凭据的那一行和书面条款，再按凭据反查确实是眼前这一件。找不到、信息冲突或只有难读照片时保留待问状态，不能写“保修已确认”。

### 完成与停止

你能从实际用品找到准确凭证与书面条款的位置，或明确哪一环还缺；可见关系是事实记录，不是权利证明。凭据属他人、型号对不上、条款缺失或需要法律/财务判断时停在待确认，联系实际商家或合适专业渠道，不丢弃原始资料。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一张收据买了四样：** 在私密记录里标出对应商品行，不用整张总额代替物品身份。
- **纸上只有系列名，实物后缀不同：** 记冲突与实物完整型号，向商家确认对应关系。
- **店员说了某保修但没有书面条款：** 记录谁在何时说的，并向提供方索取其适用的书面说明；不凭口头计算期限。
- **收据照片模糊：** 保留原件/原邮件并核对可读行，不自行补猜日期或商品号。
- **凭据涉及别人的付款资料：** 只让有权者管理，索引用中性代号，不把详情放公共夹。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只为普通用品保留“物品—购买证据—书面资料”关联；各商家及地区对凭证接受方式不同，本 Skill 不宣称哪种副本具有同等效力或保留多久。视力/阅读不便时可请获授权的人在私密环境读回，或使用本人已有的可访问邮件。副作用是那张长收据终于不用独自解释整间屋子的每件东西。

## English

Load this Skill after buying an ordinary item when its receipt or order and written warranty or use terms may be needed later but are not linked to the real object. You will make it findable **which proof, which product line, which written terms and which safe location belong to this actual item**. Do not decide warranty duration, coverage, refund eligibility or legal rights or use this for expense accounting.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the item maker, exact model or variant and purchase route. Gather its actual paper receipt, order confirmation or other seller record plus the written guide or warranty supplied with it. On a multi-item order, find the real line for this item; the order total does not identify each piece. Original paper, original email and a copy are distinct. Keep their source under the actual seller process without publishing private information.

### Execution

1. **Read the object identity back.** Note a neutral name, maker and safely readable model or variant; do not open a housing to find a serial. If the proof names only a family or conflicts with the item, mark “link to confirm” rather than claiming they are the same model.
2. **Locate this item in the proof.** Record issuer, printed purchase date, receipt or order reference and the matching item line. Link only that line when several goods share one receipt. For a gift or another buyer, first confirm who may hold and share proof.
3. **Connect the written terms.** Keep the exact warranty or use terms, title or covered model and whether they came from package, seller or maker. If absent, mismatched or only spoken about, record unknown and ask the actual provider; do not infer a service is owed.
4. **Give paper or existing digital files one controlled place.** In a private index write neutral “item → proof folder or order-email place → terms place.” Do not put price, address, payment details or full serial on a folder exterior or shared sheet. An existing digital record can stay in your controlled location; no new scan or upload is required.
5. **Test the reverse lookup.** From item name, find the exact line and written terms; from the proof, verify it matches the item in front of you. Leave a missing, conflicting or unreadable image as ask-first rather than “warranty confirmed.”

### Success and stop

You can get from the actual object to the right proof and written-terms location, or state exactly which link is absent. This is a factual association, not a rights decision. Pause for another person's receipt, model mismatch, missing terms or legal or financial interpretation; ask the real seller or suitable qualified channel without discarding source records.

### Common errors and recovery

- **One receipt covers four products:** Mark the matching product line privately. The order total is not the item's identity.
- **Proof has a family name but the item suffix differs:** Record conflict and full item model and ask the seller to confirm correspondence.
- **A salesperson spoke about warranty but no terms are present:** Note who said it and when and request the applicable written terms. Do not calculate a period from a spoken phrase.
- **Receipt photo is blurry:** Keep the original paper or email and check a readable line, not a guessed date or code.
- **Proof contains another person's payment detail:** Let the authorised holder manage it and use a neutral code in any shared index.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This links item, purchase evidence and written information for ordinary goods. Seller and region rules differ; it does not declare copies equivalent or set a retention time. An authorised person can read materials back in private or use your already accessible email when sight or reading is difficult. One long receipt no longer has to explain everything in the home by itself.
