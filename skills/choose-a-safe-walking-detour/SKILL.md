## 中文

当普通城市步行路线遇到施工、活动或临时封闭，人行道/公共通道不能按原计划走时加载。目标是**使用这次现场开放、公开且适合你实际通行条件的替代路线**，或在没有可信路线时安全暂停。它不提供某城市逐街导航、过街法律意见、施工合规裁判或翻越围挡的办法。

### 准备与输入

在封闭区外允许停留的安全位置，记你要去的目的地与入口、当前路口或地标、被封的是哪一段。读现场管理方/道路主管部门的正式牌、绕行箭头、临时人行通道和公告；导航 App 的旧路径可作背景，不是眼前仍开放的证明。考虑你的行动速度、台阶/坡道需求、视力、照明、天气和同行人条件，不把“别人走过去了”当你的路也可用。

### 执行

1. **在围挡前停止旧路线。** 看封闭起点、是否有“人行绕行/临时通道”及官方指示要从哪里离开；不跨施工线、穿车道、走车辆出入口或沿未保护的路肩赌一段。标识和实际现场矛盾时先不进入。
2. **找公开、连续的替代路径。** 依据正式路标或当期主管部门信息，确认它从你所在的公共位置到下一处可核验的人行点，途中经过的过街位置/临时通道确实开放。绕到对侧人行道或受保护临时步道在一些工程中可能存在，不能假设所有封路都这样安排。
3. **按你的实际条件验一遍。** 在能看到的范围核对路面、宽度、坡道/台阶、障碍、照明和需要的过街方式对你及同行人是否可行。看不见后半段、标牌没有说明或通道不适用时，问现场正式工作人员或当地管理方的官方联系渠道，必要时选另一公开路线或暂停，不要求自己证明路肯定安全。
4. **同时确认目的地还可到达。** 绕行可能把你带到建筑另一侧；看场所当日入口公告，确认你要找的访客门没有随工程关闭。记“当前__；经__公开通道到__；下一核对点__；入口__”。不需要实时上传自己的完整轨迹给不相关的人。
5. **边走边复查。** 沿实际开放的行人路线到下一核对点，遇第二处封闭或临时指挥变化就停在安全公共位置重选。要因此晚到时另按普通迟到沟通，不把赶时间当作越栏或冲灯理由。

### 完成与停止

你已辨认并开始走一条有现场/官方依据、对你可用、通向仍可进入的目的地或下一公共核对点的路线；或者明确无法验证而在安全处停下问路/改计划，本轮完成。**选出绕行不等于已到目的地。** 没有连续公开人行路线、过街方式不明或通道对你不可用时不进入封闭区，也不替现场施工人员宣称已经合规。

### 常见报错与补救

- **地图仍显示原人行道可走：** 现场封闭事实优先，查当期正式绕行并从当前位置重选。
- **绕行箭头指向对侧，但找不到可用过街点：** 停在安全位置，问正式人员或查主管部门公告，不直接穿车流。
- **临时步道够别人走，却有台阶/过窄/不适合推车：** 明确自己的限制并找官方可用替代，不被“路线已开放”四字压过去。
- **路走了一半又封：** 回到最近可停留的公共核对点，再读现场牌/问工作人员；不硬闯下一段。
- **绕到建筑后发现访客门关了：** 查场所当天入口信息，重新接到可用门；路的终点和门的开放是两个检查。
- **手机无电或没有网：** 使用现场实体路标、已有地址和正式人员，不借未知二维码进入陌生登录/付款页。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只适用于可退出的普通城市步行与临时封路；不覆盖野外、严重天气、紧急避险、交通执法或道路工程法律建议。场地、时间、施工阶段和当地交通规则不同，必须以现场正式指示和个人可行性为准。视觉、听觉、行动或语言不便时可寻求可读/可听告示、工作人员或可信陪同，不被迫走未验证的坡道或穿机动车道。副作用是可能多走一段；若没有安全选择，暂停也是有效结果。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary city walking route meets construction, an event or another temporary closure and its pavement or public passage cannot be used as planned. Your result is a **currently open, public, usable detour for your real access needs**, or a safe pause when none can be verified. This does not supply turn-by-turn directions for one city, interpret crossing law, judge construction compliance or teach entry through barriers.

### Preparation and inputs

From a safe public place outside the closure, note your destination and entrance, current junction or landmark and the blocked section. Read the responsible authority's or site manager's actual signs, pedestrian arrows, temporary walkway and current notices. An old app route is background, not proof that a path remains open. Consider your pace, ramps or steps, sight, lighting, weather and companions. Someone else walking through does not prove it works for you.

### Execution

1. **Stop the old route at the barrier.** Identify where closure begins and any signed pedestrian diversion or temporary path. Do not cross a work line, enter a vehicle lane or driveway, or try an unprotected shoulder. If sign and physical scene conflict, do not enter yet.
2. **Find a continuous public alternative.** Use current formal signs or local authority information to confirm a route from your actual public position to the next identifiable pedestrian checkpoint. Verify that its crossing or protected temporary passage is open. Some works divert to the opposite footway or a protected path; that cannot be assumed for every closure.
3. **Test it against your actual needs.** For the visible part, check surface, usable width, ramps or steps, obstructions, light and crossing method for you and your companions. If the farther section is hidden, signs are unclear or the path is unusable, ask official on-site staff or the authority's contact route, choose another public route or pause. You do not have to pretend the path is safe to proceed.
4. **Confirm the destination remains reachable.** A detour may lead to another side of the building. Check today's venue entrance notice to make sure the visitor door is not also closed. Note “now at __; public passage via __ to __; next checkpoint __; entrance __.” Do not share a complete live route with unrelated people.
5. **Recheck while walking.** Use the actually open pedestrian path to the next checkpoint. For another closure or changed site direction, stop in a safe public place and choose again. If this makes you late, use the separate ordinary late-arrival communication task rather than treating urgency as permission to climb a barrier or rush through traffic.

### Success and stop

You have identified and started a signed or officially supported route usable for you that reaches a still-usable destination entrance or next public checkpoint; or you have explicitly paused somewhere safe to ask or change plans because none can be verified. **Choosing a detour is not arriving.** Do not enter a closed work area when the public path is not continuous, crossing is unclear or the route cannot be used by you. This Skill does not certify a contractor's compliance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The app still shows the old pavement:** Use the real closure as the current fact and search for today's formal diversion from your position.
- **An arrow points across the road but no usable crossing is clear:** Pause somewhere safe and ask site staff or read the responsible authority's notice. Do not step into moving traffic.
- **A temporary path is open but too narrow or stepped for you or a stroller:** State the condition and find an official usable alternative; the word “open” is not proof of access.
- **The detour closes halfway through:** Return to the nearest safe public checkpoint and read updated signs or ask staff. Do not force the next segment.
- **You reach the far side but the visitor entrance is closed:** Check the venue's current door information and reconnect to a usable entrance; route and door are separate checks.
- **Phone is flat or offline:** Use physical signs, saved address and official people, not an unknown QR leading to a login or payment page.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for ordinary, exit-able city walking around a temporary closure, not wilderness, severe weather, emergency escape, traffic enforcement or street-works legal advice. Place, time, construction phase and local crossing rules vary; follow current official site direction and your actual route needs. For sight, hearing, mobility or language needs, use accessible signs, staff or a trusted companion. No one must use an unverified ramp or vehicle lane. You may walk farther; pausing is a valid result if there is no safe route.
