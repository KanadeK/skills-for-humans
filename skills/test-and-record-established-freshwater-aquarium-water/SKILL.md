## 中文

当已稳定淡水缸的**例行水质检测到期**，你有合用试剂及本缸鱼种目标范围时，加载这份 Skill。结果是能回查日期、采样点、试剂、读数与是否偏离基线的记录；异常转专业人员，不自行调酸碱或加药。水看着清不代表氨、亚硝酸盐等安全；新缸建立、鱼病诊断不在本篇。

### 准备与输入

核试剂有效期、项目、使用说明、规定等待/读色时间和废液处理；按原饲养方案选氨、亚硝酸盐、硝酸盐、pH、温度等真正需要的项目。准备清洁专用取样容器、色卡、记录表与可访问灯光。试剂别放餐桌或儿童可碰处，手不要带肥皂残留入缸。色觉不便可请可信的人在同一光下读色或使用合规数字仪，仍记录“谁读的”。

### 执行

1. **先记录测试条件。** 写日期时间、缸名、最近换水/投喂与鱼种计划目标，查上次读数。若试剂过期或目标范围不明，记“不可比较”，先求可靠资料，不用旧试纸确认水安全。
2. **按试剂说明取样和操作。** 从缸主要水体取规定水量，避开滤材/排水口局部；按这套试剂的顺序、滴数或浸泡时间做，每项用其对应器具，不混盖子。未读之前不把试剂液倒回缸。
3. **在规定时点读数。** 到试剂说明的时间在合适光线与本色卡比对，逐项记数值/范围、单位和不确定处；不要过一小时再回看变色液当正式读数。温度读缸用温度计而非手感。
4. **对照本缸目标并交接。** 与本鱼种和已稳定基线对比，明显异常、读数冲突或鱼行为变化时保留结果并联系水族专业人员，按其建议确认复测/下一步；不凭一条值乱加化学品。废液按试剂标签处置、洗手。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一份带试剂版本、单位、采样与读数时间的真实水质记录，偏离有具体求助/复测入口；不能比较时诚实标“不确定”。看了一眼清水或用过期试纸乱判“合格”不算检测。

- **试剂过期或色卡丢失：** 暂停判定，换合用材料或由可靠水族专业者检测。
- **颜色介于两档/无法辨色：** 记录区间与不确定性，请可信读色/合用数字方法，不编整数。
- **氨/亚硝酸盐等结果异常：** 保留读数、联系专业人员并按既有应对计划行动，不自行投药。
- **把试剂液误当可回倒水：** 不倒回缸，按产品废液要求处理。
- **手眼/语言限制：** 大字号记录、翻译试剂说明和可信协助计时，仍保留原说明与读数证据。

### 假设与边界

OATA 的一般水质框架指出氨/亚硝酸盐应被严格关注，但 pH、硝酸盐、温度与频率须按鱼种/缸况；不同测试盒时点和单位不通用。本文做读数与交接，不提供化学调整方案或疾病判断。

## English

Load this Skill when an established freshwater aquarium's **routine water test is due** and you have a suitable kit plus species-specific target ranges. Finish with traceable date, sampling point, kit, readings, and comparison with this tank's baseline. Route abnormal values to an aquatics professional rather than guessing pH correction or medicine. Clear-looking water does not prove ammonia or nitrite safety. New-tank cycling and illness diagnosis are outside scope.

### Preparation and inputs

Check kit expiry, parameters, operating directions, timing, colour chart, and disposal. Use the existing care plan to choose actual needed tests such as ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, and temperature. Have a clean dedicated sample vessel, chart, record, and accessible light. Keep chemicals off food surfaces and from children; no soap residue enters the tank. For colour-access needs, trusted reading in consistent light or an approved digital method can help, recording who read it.

### Execution

1. **Record test context.** Note date, time, tank, recent water change or feed, species target, and last results. With expired kit or unknown target, record “not comparable” and obtain reliable guidance rather than using an old strip to certify water.
2. **Sample and run this kit exactly.** Take its stated volume from the main body of water, not a filter outlet pocket, and follow this kit's sequence, drops, or dip time. Keep each test's tools separate and never pour reacted test liquid back into the aquarium.
3. **Read at the specified time.** Under suitable light and the kit's own chart, record value or range, units, and uncertainty. A sample changing an hour later is not the timed result. Use an aquarium thermometer rather than hand feel for temperature.
4. **Compare with this tank's target and hand off.** Check actual fish-specific plan and established baseline. For clearly abnormal, conflicting, or behaviour-linked readings, retain them and contact an experienced aquatic veterinarian or retailer for retest/next action. Do not add chemicals from one guessed number. Dispose of test liquid by label and wash hands.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A real dated water record includes kit, units, sample and read timing, with a concrete help/retest route for deviations. Mark “uncertain” when comparison is impossible. A glance at clear water or an expired strip called “safe” is not testing.

- **Kit expired or chart missing:** Hold the conclusion and use an in-date suitable kit or a reliable aquatics professional.
- **Colour lies between two steps or is inaccessible:** Record a range and uncertainty, ask trusted reading or an approved digital method, without inventing an integer.
- **Ammonia, nitrite, or another target is abnormal:** Save results and seek professional guidance under the existing response plan, not self-medication.
- **Reacted liquid is mistaken for tank water:** Never pour it back; follow test disposal directions.
- **Grip, language, or sight is limited:** Larger records, translated kit instructions, and trusted timing help can preserve the original method and evidence.

### Assumptions and limits

OATA's general framework treats ammonia and nitrite as critical, but pH, nitrate, temperature, and frequency depend on species and tank. Kits differ in timing and units. This records and routes readings, not chemical adjustments or diagnosis.
