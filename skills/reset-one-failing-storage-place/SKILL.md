## 中文

当某件普通非食品物品或一小组物品**已经有收纳位置，却反复找不到或放不回去**时加载。你的任务是根据真实取放失败，只改一个有问题的路径，再看是否更容易找回和归位。这里不重做全屋、强迫丢弃物品、替别人决定私人物品位置，也不处理重要文书、数字文件或危险物品。

### 准备与输入

写下最近两三次具体失败：想找什么、从哪里先找、最后在哪里发现、为何没能放回原处。请实际使用者说明他们通常在哪使用、能否够到旧位置、标识是否看得懂。旧位置和标签先拍照或画图留作回退，不先把整个柜子倒出来寻找“灵感”。

### 执行

1. **判断问题是不是位置。** 如果物品只是当前遗失，先做有限范围搜索；如果是私人借还或共用权限不清，先解决归属/约定。只有“原收纳路径反复失败”才改位置。
2. **给失败选一个主因。** 从“太远离常用地点、够不到、位置过满、标识与实际不符、多人不知道同一个地址”中选证据最足的一项。不要同时修五个原因，改完也不知道哪一项起作用。
3. **做一项可回退的改动。** 例如把这一小组移到更可达的已获允许位置、给现有位置换可读的提示，或为它腾出取放空间。不要未经同意占别人的区域，不先购买整套盒子，也不以扔掉纪念物作为通关条件。
4. **撤掉旧的错误提示。** 把新地址告诉相关使用者，更新旧位置的标签或说明，避免两处都自称正常归位点。需要保密的物品只向有权使用的人提供位置。
5. **用真实动作试。** 请至少一名经常使用者在没有你临场指路时找到该物品、使用后放回；另一次或另一位使用者再试。记录找错的位置和放回障碍。若变得更难，先退回旧安排，再针对这条证据改另一小步。

### 成功与停止

一个受影响的小组有一个被相关使用者认可的新地址和提示，两次实际取放不再重复原来的失败；或者试验失败，已回退并记下原因。没有可靠失败记录、无权移动物品、位置不安全或有关使用者不同意时，停止改动而不是启动全屋整理。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只换了漂亮标签，仍够不到：** 主因是可达性，换位置或求助合适的人，不再堆叠字样。
- **新旧位置都留着同一名称：** 撤销旧提示并给使用者读回唯一归位点。
- **“找不到”其实是别人正在借用：** 暂停收纳重置，转借还或共用状态记录；移动盒子不能解决占用。
- **使用者暂时不在，无法试用：** 留临时标记和回退信息；待其实际取放后再决定是否把新地址定为长期规则。
- **中途被打断：** 把已移动的小组和旧地址记清，别留半个柜子进入无名状态；恢复时从尚未验证的一步继续。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这适用于可安全手持或由合适的人搬动的普通物品，不涉及重家具、住房改造或隐私文书。低视力、行动或握力差异会改变“方便”的含义，因此由实际使用者测试可达性；大字、触感、音频或固定位置都只是可选工具。不同家庭的美观偏好不决定取放成功。副作用是一个旧地址正式退休，最好顺手撤掉它的宣传牌。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary non-food item or small group **already has a storage place, yet people repeatedly cannot find or return it**. Use actual retrieval failures to change one affected path and check whether it now works. You do not reorganise a whole home, force disposal, relocate someone else's private item, or manage important papers, digital files or hazardous goods.

### Preparation and inputs

Record two or three concrete episodes: what you wanted, where you looked first, where it turned up, and why it was not returned to the old place. Ask actual users where they use it, whether they can reach that place and whether its cue makes sense. Photograph or sketch the old place for rollback. Do not empty every cupboard in search of inspiration.

### Execution

1. **Check whether storage is the problem.** If the item is missing only this time, use a bounded search. If it is privately borrowed or shared-use permission is unclear, resolve ownership or agreement first. Move storage only for a repeatedly failing return or retrieval path.
2. **Choose one supported cause.** Select the best-evidenced issue: too far from use, inaccessible reach, overflow, stale cue, or users who know different “usual” locations. Changing five things at once will hide which one helped.
3. **Make one reversible change.** Move this small group to an allowed reachable place, change an existing cue to one users can read, or clear enough space to take it out and put it back. Do not occupy another person's area without permission, buy a matching-box system first, or make discarding a keepsake the admission fee.
4. **Retire the wrong old cue.** Tell relevant users the new address and correct the old place's label or note so both do not claim to be the normal home. Give a private item's location only to people authorised to know it.
5. **Test with real actions.** Have at least one regular user find the item without your live directions and return it after use. Try again later or with a second regular user. Note wrong turns and return obstacles. If the change makes things harder, roll back first and make another small change based on the evidence.

### Success and stop

One affected group has a new place and cue accepted by relevant users, and two real retrieval/return checks no longer repeat the original failure; or the trial failed and you restored the old arrangement with a recorded reason. Without concrete failures, permission to move the item, a safe place or user agreement, stop the change rather than launching a whole-home reset.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A prettier label still leaves the place out of reach:** The likely issue is access; move it or get appropriate help instead of adding more lettering.
- **Old and new places both show the same name:** Remove the stale cue and have users repeat the single return place.
- **“Missing” means someone currently has it on loan:** Pause storage changes and use the loan or shared-status route. Moving a box cannot fix custody.
- **The regular user is absent and cannot test:** Keep a temporary marker and rollback note. Wait for their real retrieval before making the change permanent.
- **You are interrupted mid-change:** Record the group already moved and its old address. Do not leave half a cupboard unnamed; resume at the unverified step.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary items safe to hold or move with appropriate help, not heavy furniture, building alterations or private records. Low vision, mobility and grip differences change what “convenient” means; the actual users must test reach. Large print, touch, audio and a fixed place are optional tools. Decoration preference is not a retrieval result. The side effect is one old address retired, preferably along with its misleading sign.
