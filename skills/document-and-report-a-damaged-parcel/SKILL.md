## 中文

你的普通包裹在交接现场或回家后发现箱体开裂、受潮、压坏，或内件明显损伤时，用这份 Skill 先判断能否安全接触，再留下足够事实，问负责的商家/承运商下一步。目标是外部、内部与追踪事实得到记录并进入正式处理渠道；不承诺理赔或退款。只是收到错型号/少数量而无损伤时，用错件/少件 Skill；不是你的包裹，先用误投 Skill。

### 准备与输入

记录追踪号、订单/寄件人、收件日期和当前状态。先从外部观察：有无漏液、刺激气味、碎玻璃、尖锐突出物、鼓胀电池或其他危险。出现这些情况就不要继续拆开或触碰内容，隔离并联系承运方/寄件方及当地适合的帮助；本 Skill 不指导危险品清理。普通可安全处理的破损才继续，准备一个不会公开住址的拍摄位置。

### 执行

1. **在改变现场前记录。** 若在柜台或送货人仍在场，指出外箱可见事实并询问该服务的损伤备注或接收/拒收选项；不代替服务方决定签名的法律含义。回家后发现也尽快记录。拍包裹四周、标签/追踪的必要部分、破口或压痕，避免无关人、完整地址和支付资料入镜。
2. **安全可开时核对内件。** 在不会碰尖片、漏液或其他危险时，按正常方式打开，拍原有内衬和受损部位，记下商品/部件、数量与损伤表现。不要为证明损伤而反复试运行受损电器或丢掉“看起来没用”的泡棉。若内件并无可见损伤，也如实写“外箱受损，内件尚未见损伤”，不虚构。
3. **保留可能要检查的实物。** 将箱体、填充、标签、损伤物和实际收到的内容放在安全处，别立刻丢弃、退回或自行修复。不同承运方可能要求检查或授权退回；先按相关商家/承运方的真实说明行动。大型或无法安全存放的物品，向服务方说明限制并问可行方案。
4. **向正式渠道提交一份事实请求。** 按订单与承运服务关系找到有权处理的一方，在其官方入口报告时间、追踪、外箱/内件各自状态、照片与希望的下一步（检查、换货、维修、退货说明等）。问清谁需要保留什么、是否需要现场检查、受理号与下一次联系时间。不要把自己的承运方索赔规则当成商家必然赔偿承诺。

### 成功条件

普通安全的损伤已按外观/内件分别记录，原包装与商品按要求留存，相关服务方已受理或明确告知合适渠道和下一步。已提交报告不等于获批赔偿，也不证明物品继续使用安全。

### 常见报错与补救

- **先丢箱才想报损：** 保留剩余实物并如实说明缺少什么证据；无法重建的照片不要伪造。
- **外箱压凹就写“商品全坏”：** 仅陈述实际观察；安全可开时再核对内件，不能确认就说未知。
- **发现漏液或鼓胀电池仍拆箱拍照：** 停止接触、隔离并找合适服务方/当地帮助，不为了证据扩大风险。
- **商家和承运商互相转接：** 保留两个渠道的受理编号及转接理由，问谁有权安排检查或退回，别把未确认承诺当结果。
- **私下寄走损坏件：** 等正式退回地址和标签/授权；未经确认的寄走可能丢失检查机会。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

此 Skill 只处理可安全接近的普通包裹损伤记录与服务交接，不解释运输责任、赔偿、保险或法律期限。UPS、FedEx、USPS 的要求是地区/服务实例；你的渠道可能不同。不能拍照时可写清外箱位置、内件状态与时间，并问服务方可接受何种证据；行动受限时可请可信人协助而不冒险搬重箱。破损物与包装会暂时占用空间，待服务方给出处理方法。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary parcel you may receive has a torn, wet, or crushed outer package or obviously damaged contents at handover or later. First decide whether it is safe to touch, then keep enough facts and ask the responsible seller or carrier for the official next step. The result is a record of outside, inside, and tracking facts in a service channel, not a promised payment. A wrong model or short quantity without damage uses the contents-mismatch Skill; a parcel not yours uses the misdelivery Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Note tracking, order or sender, receipt date, and current status. Look from outside for leaking liquid, sharp glass, protruding edges, swollen batteries, strong smell, or another hazard. Do not open or handle such contents further. Isolate and contact the carrier/sender and appropriate local help; this is not a hazardous cleanup guide. Continue only for ordinary damage you can approach safely, with a photo or written-record method that does not publicize your address.

### Execution

1. **Record before changing the scene.** At a counter or while the courier is present, point out visible outer damage and ask that service about its damage notation and acceptance/refusal options; do not guess the legal meaning of a signature. Record promptly if noticed at home. Photograph outer sides, necessary label/tracking portion, and tears or dents without bystanders, full address, or payment data.
2. **Inspect contents only when safe.** Open normally if no sharp, leak, or other hazard is present. Record original internal cushioning and any damaged part, item/part, quantity, and observed defect. Do not repeatedly operate a damaged electrical item for proof or discard packing foam. If no inner damage is visible, say “outer package damaged; no inner damage observed yet,” rather than inventing it.
3. **Keep possible inspection evidence.** Store box, fill, label, damaged goods, and received contents safely. Do not immediately discard, reship, or repair them. A carrier may request inspection or authorized return; follow the actual seller/carrier instructions first. State storage or lifting constraints if the item is large or hard to keep safely.
4. **File one factual request through an official channel.** Use the order and carrier relationship to find who can handle this case. Provide time, tracking, separate outer/inner observations, minimal photos, and a desired next step such as inspection, replacement, repair, or return instructions. Ask what to retain, who may inspect, the case reference, and next contact time. A carrier's claim rule is not a promise that a seller must pay.

### Success

Ordinary safe damage has separate outside and inside facts recorded, packaging and goods are kept as needed, and the relevant provider accepted the report or directed you to the responsible route. Submitting a report does not approve compensation or prove the goods safe to keep using.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The box was discarded first:** Keep remaining evidence and state honestly what is missing. Do not fabricate an earlier photo.
- **A crushed box is described as a destroyed item:** Report only observations; inspect contents if safe, or mark their condition unknown.
- **A leak or swollen battery is opened for pictures:** Stop touching, isolate, and seek suitable provider/local help. Evidence is not worth expanding the risk.
- **Seller and carrier refer you to each other:** Keep both case numbers and referral reasons; ask who can arrange inspection or return instead of assuming a promise was made.
- **Damaged goods are mailed away privately:** Wait for official return address, label, and authorization so an inspection route is not lost.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers safely approachable ordinary parcel damage records and service handoff, not liability, compensation, insurance, or legal deadlines. UPS, FedEx, and USPS are regional service examples; your provider may differ. Without photos, write location, condition, and time and ask what evidence is accepted. For lifting barriers, ask trusted help rather than carrying a heavy damaged box unsafely. Packing and damaged goods may need temporary storage until instructions arrive.
