## 中文

当你已有普通家用或办公室打印机，要买相纸、厚卡纸、信封等**非默认纸张**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是购前确认候选纸的尺寸、类型、克重以及你需要的单/双面方式在**某一实际进纸路径**受准确机型支持。这里不设置驱动、不试印，也不清除卡纸或维修设备。

### 准备与输入

记下打印机完整型号、打算印什么、是否需要自动双面或无边距，以及要使用的进纸盒/后托盘。找该型号官方“支持的纸张/介质”表，注意不同纸路可能分别列出尺寸、类型、克重或厚度范围。准备候选纸包装上的长宽、单位面积重量（如 g/m²）、表面类型和有无喷墨/激光专用说明。

### 执行

1. **先确定打印任务。** 列出成品需要的实际尺寸、单面/双面、普通文档还是相片/卡片；若必须无边距，单独核无边距支持尺寸。不要先被“万能相纸”吸引再找任务。
2. **选一条可用纸路。** 在准确型号手册中找你能实际使用的纸盒或手动/后进纸路径。核对候选纸的尺寸与方向、类型、克重/厚度是否在该路径允许范围。某机型“支持 A4”并不表示每个托盘都支持所有 A4 厚纸。
3. **再核所需功能。** 双面、无边距或特殊纸张可能各有单独限制；对你的具体纸型与纸路逐项查。纸能进入机器，只说明入口尺寸可能够，不证明会正常输送或达到预期输出。
4. **比对当前候选。** 把纸张包装的具体尺寸、克重、表面、用途与手册同一口径比；磅重和 g/m² 不是同一个数字，缺可靠换算或厂家对应表时不猜。
5. **记结论。** 写“打印任务 → 准确型号与纸路 → 允许尺寸/介质/克重/单双面条件 → 候选规格 → 满足、不满足或未确认”。关键栏缺失时向制造商或卖家索取准确资料，或买有明确支持的纸。

### 成功与停止

完成时，你能指出候选纸在指定纸路和打印方式下有准确机型资料支持。若机型手册不列该纸型，纸张被要求用于食品接触、医疗、证件防伪或其他特殊用途，停止普通打印纸选择并使用专门规范。购买前不靠“塞进去试试”证明适配。

### 常见报错与补救

- **尺寸相同就认为厚纸可用：** 另查克重/厚度和进纸路径；有的厚纸只支持手动托盘。
- **纸张可单面，却预设自动双面：** 查该介质与克重的双面限制；必要时改任务或选合适纸，而不是购买后强行双面。
- **把相纸、标签纸、普通纸混成一个类型：** 按手册的介质类别逐项匹配；名称相近不等于涂层与送纸条件相同。
- **表格太小或机型难辨：** 放大官方说明、请可信的人读回型号与纸路；不能可靠识别时标未确认。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程假设你能取得打印机的准确支持表。各地区纸张命名和英制/公制重量标法不同，按手册和产品包装统一口径；不能把某品牌单一型号的克重上限当成通用数值。无网络时用随机器附的纸质手册。你可能改买薄一点或换进纸路线，但本 Skill 到购买决定为止。

## English

Load this Skill before buying **non-default paper** such as photo stock, heavy card, or envelopes for a printer you already own. Your outcome is whether the exact model supports the candidate's size, type, weight, and required simplex/duplex use through **one actual feed path**. This does not configure a driver, run a test print, clear a jam, or repair the printer.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the printer's full model, the intended print job, any automatic two-sided or borderless requirement, and the cassette or rear/manual feed you can use. Find the maker's supported-media table for that model; different paths may list different sizes, types, and weight or thickness ranges. Read the candidate package for dimensions, area weight (such as g/m²), finish/type, and any inkjet/laser restriction.

### Execution

1. **Define the job first.** State the required finished size, one or two sides, document versus photo/card, and whether borderless output is essential. Borderless support needs its own size check. Do not buy “universal photo paper” before naming the job.
2. **Choose a documented feed path.** In the exact-model manual, find a cassette or manual/rear route you can use. Check the candidate's dimensions and orientation, media type, and weight/thickness against that path. “Supports A4” does not mean every tray handles every heavy A4 sheet.
3. **Check the requested feature.** Duplex, borderless, and specialty media can have separate restrictions. Look them up for this paper and route. A sheet physically entering the printer says little about reliable feeding or the intended output.
4. **Compare the candidate.** Align the package's size, weight, surface, and intended printer type with the manual's terms. Pound-based paper weight and g/m² are not the same number; use a reliable conversion or maker table rather than guessing.
5. **Record the decision.** Write “print job → exact model and feed path → allowed size/type/weight/simplex-duplex condition → candidate → supported, unsupported, or unconfirmed.” Ask the maker or seller for missing exact facts, or choose documented media.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when the candidate has exact-model support for the specified feed path and print method. Stop this ordinary paper choice when the manual does not list the media or the output has food-contact, medical, anti-counterfeit, or other specialist requirements. Do not turn “try forcing it through” into pre-purchase evidence.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The dimensions match, so thick paper must work:** Check weight/thickness and path; some heavy stock is manual-feed only.
- **The paper works one-sided, so automatic duplex was assumed:** Read that media and weight's two-sided limit; change the job or stock instead of forcing it after purchase.
- **Photo, label, and plain paper were treated as one type:** Match the manual's media categories separately. Similar names do not mean identical coatings or feed behaviour.
- **The table is tiny or the model unclear:** Enlarge the official guide or ask someone trusted to read back model and path. Keep an unreliable identification unconfirmed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes you can get the exact printer's media table. Paper names and imperial/metric weight labels vary by market; align them using the manual and package. One Canon model's weight limit is not a universal printer limit. A paper manual works offline. You may buy thinner stock or select another feed route, but this Skill ends at the buying decision.
