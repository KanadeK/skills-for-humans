## 中文

当你有一份普通纸质文件需要一个可读副本，接收方允许这种副本，而原件还要由你保留时加载。你要**按实际设备或服务说明复制需要的页面/正反面，检查副本完整可读，并把原件拿回可控位置**。这里不制作公证/认证副本，不判断法定提交要求，也不将私密纸张上传不明网站。

### 准备与输入

核对实际接收方对副本的当前要求：需要哪几页、正反面、大小/颜色、份数及是否承认普通复印件；未知就先问，不自称复印件等于原件。确认你有复制权限。原件若脆、破、粘连、装订后不能平摊或需特殊保密处理，就不强压在普通玻璃上，向拥有者/专业服务询问合适途径。

### 执行

1. **先数原件和要复制的面。** 记当前有几页、每页是否双面、哪些边缘/浅色标记也必须读出。用一个安全位置放未处理原件，另一个位置放完成副本，避免机器吐出的纸和原件混堆。
2. **按这台机器/服务的准确说明放纸。** 原件朝向、玻璃角线、自动进纸或双面设置随型号不同；不照搬另一台机的按键。薄、折、脆或特殊纸不适合自动进纸时按设备说明改用安全支持方式，不能安全复制就停止。
3. **先出一份看结果。** 对照原件检查四边、页码、印章/标记、浅字、图表以及背面有没有漏掉，比例和方向是否使内容仍可读。裁边、阴影或反面空白若影响用途，按实际设备说明调整后重新做，不把不完整副本凑数。
4. **分清副本与原件。** 把通过检查的副本放在标“副本”的夹/信封，原件回到自己的保护夹；不在原件正文上写“副本”。多余或失败副本若含个人资料，按有权者的安全处理方法收走，不留在公共复印机旁。
5. **离开前做原件回收检查。** 数回原件页数和正反面，看看玻璃、进纸盘和输出口有无遗留。将副本数量与接收方要求对照；实际交给谁由后续交接流程负责。

### 完成与停止

经核对的普通副本覆盖要求的页/面且内容可读，原件完整回到有权者手里，失败副本没有暴露私密资料；这不保证接收方认定它为有效证明。原件易损、格式不明、机器安全/隐私无法保证或需要认证副本时暂停，问实际机构或合适专业服务。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只复了正面，背面也有内容：** 依实际设备说明补背面并核对两面对应。
- **最右边的号码被裁掉：** 调整原件位置/输出尺寸并重做，裁掉信息的副本不算可用。
- **副本浅到读不出：** 在机器允许范围内调整或换官方/可信服务；不凭记忆补写原件内容。
- **原件留在玻璃上：** 先回收核页，再离开；公共机器上的私密纸需立即按场所流程处理。
- **脆弱原件翻页会裂：** 停止强行复印，联系保管者或专业复制方式。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只做本人有权复制的普通低风险文件，厂商菜单和进纸限制各异，不给通用复印机按键脚本。视力、握持或行动不便时可让获授权者在私密环境操作和读回，重要的是你能确认原件回收与副本内容。副作用是原件不会被复印机玻璃收养。

## English

Load this Skill when you need a readable copy of an ordinary paper document, the real recipient accepts this kind of copy, and you must keep the original. **Copy the required pages and sides under the actual machine or service instructions, inspect the result and recover the original to a controlled place.** This does not make a certified copy, decide legal submission rules or upload private paper to an unfamiliar website.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the recipient's current format request: pages, sides, size or colour, count and whether an ordinary photocopy is acceptable. Ask when unknown; do not declare a copy equivalent to an original. Confirm you may copy it. If paper is brittle, torn, stuck, bound too tightly to lay safely or subject to special confidentiality, do not force it against normal glass; ask the holder or a suitable specialist service.

### Execution

1. **Count originals and sides first.** Note page count, whether each has a back, and any edge or pale detail that must remain visible. Give unprocessed originals a safe spot and completed copies another, so printed sheets do not mix with the source.
2. **Place pages under this machine's real guide.** Face direction, glass corner, feeder and two-sided setting vary by model; another copier's buttons do not govern this one. Use an allowed safe placement for thin, folded, brittle or unusual paper rather than assuming it belongs in a feeder. Stop if no safe copying route exists.
3. **Inspect one result before making more.** Against the original, check four edges, page numbers, marks or stamps, pale text, diagrams and any back side. Ensure scale and orientation leave it usable. If crop, shadow or a blank back matters, adjust under this machine's guide and remake it. An incomplete copy does not count.
4. **Keep copies and originals distinct.** Put passing copies in a folder or envelope labelled “copy,” and return originals to their protective place; do not write “copy” on the source content. Collect extra or failed copies with personal detail for the holder's secure disposal rather than leaving them by a public copier.
5. **Recover the originals before leaving.** Count source pages again and check glass, feeder and output area. Compare the passing copy count with the recipient's request. A later handoff task covers who receives the packet.

### Success and stop

A checked ordinary copy includes required pages and sides legibly, the original is intact and back with the authorised holder, and failed private copies have not been exposed. This does not guarantee the recipient treats it as valid proof. Pause for fragile paper, unknown format, unsafe equipment or privacy conditions, or a certified-copy requirement, and ask the real institution or qualified service.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the front was copied though the back has content:** Copy and check the back using this machine's instructions.
- **The rightmost number is cropped:** Reposition or change output size and remake it. A missing field is not a usable copy.
- **Copy is too faint to read:** Adjust only within the device guidance or use a trusted service. Do not rewrite unreadable source content from memory.
- **Original is left on the glass:** Retrieve and count it before leaving. Handle a private page left on a shared machine under site process immediately.
- **Turning a brittle page may tear it:** Stop the ordinary copy and ask the holder or specialist route.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary low-risk documents you may copy. Maker menus and feeder limits differ, so there is no universal copier button script. An authorised helper can operate and read back in private when sight, grip or movement is difficult; you still need to confirm original recovery and copy content. The original will not be adopted by the copier glass.
