## 中文

当你准备给一盆普通室内盆栽浇水，却只知道“上次是星期几”时，加载这份 Skill。目标是先判断**现在需不需要水**，需要时让盆土得到适合这盆植物的水并安全排掉多余的水。不是给所有品种同一个毫升数。

### 准备与输入

确认植物或可信养护说明、盆器是否有排水孔、适用的盆土干湿判断，以及底部托盘。看不清盆土或读不懂说明时可请人描述、用可触摸的探测工具或请可信的人协助；不要只凭叶子下垂下结论。若没有排水孔、盆土已持续湿透、闻到腐败味或植株状态异常，先停下并查该品种/盆器的指导。

### 执行

1. **查真实状态。** 按品种资料检查所需深度的盆土干湿，可结合端盆时的重量变化；表面干不必然意味着根部也干。不要仅按固定星期表浇。
2. **先裁决。** 若仍湿且资料说应等干，今天不浇，记下下次查看时间。若需要水，先把盆放到允许排水且不会伤家具的地方；检查排水孔没有被装饰套盆封死。
3. **按盆器与品种浇。** 对普通有孔盆，缓慢均匀地浇，使水进入介质并从底孔流出；品种如有特殊给水方式，以其可靠说明为准。不要把水直接倒到电器、插座或不能承水的表面。
4. **结束排水。** 等多余的水流出后倒掉托盘或套盆积水，再放回稳定位置。湿漉漉的托盘不是额外的浇水积分。
5. **留下一条可用记录。** 记今天的干湿判断、是否浇水、异常现象和下次检查入口；季节、室温与盆器变化时重新判断，不复制旧间隔。

### 完成与停止

完成可以是“今天不浇，已核实盆土仍湿”，也可以是“按这盆植物的要求浇透，排水已清理”。如果盆器无法排水、介质持续潮湿或品种要求不明，先处理信息/盆器问题，不追加水。

### 常见报错与补救

- **水立即沿盆壁流走，内部仍干：** 不继续无限加水；检查介质是否严重干缩或根系过密，查该品种与换盆指导。
- **盆土湿、叶却垂：** 不把下垂自动诊断为缺水；先查根、光、温度与虫害等可能因素，必要时请园艺人员判断。
- **套盆中留了一汪水：** 排空并确认内盆能自由排水；下次先安排接水位置。
- **手不方便端盆：** 在稳定、可及的水槽或防水面操作，使用小水壶或请人安全搬运，判断步骤不省略。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里是普通有孔室内盆栽的一次浇水判断，不包括水培、自动灌溉系统、特殊附生植物或食用作物的专门栽培。RHS 的通用建议要服从具体品种资料；它不是所有植物都“表面干就浇”的许可证。真实副作用是需要清理排水，并可能发现你的旧日历提醒只适合提醒**检查**。

## English

Load this Skill when you are about to water an ordinary houseplant but know only the day of its last watering. First decide **whether it needs water now**. If it does, wet the growing medium in a way suited to this plant and clear the runoff. There is no universal millilitre dose.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the plant's identity or reliable care guidance, its pot's drainage holes, the relevant growing-medium moisture test, and a place to catch runoff. If you cannot read the guidance or inspect the medium, ask for a description, use an accessible moisture-checking tool, or arrange trusted help. A drooping leaf alone does not settle the decision. If the pot has no drainage, the medium stays saturated, there is a rotting smell, or the plant seems seriously abnormal, pause and consult plant- and container-specific guidance.

### Execution

1. **Check the actual medium.** Follow the plant's guidance for how deep to check; compare pot weight when useful. A dry surface does not prove the whole root zone is dry. Do not water solely because a calendar says so.
2. **Make the yes/no decision.** If the medium is still wet and the care guidance says to wait, do not water today; choose a next check. If water is due, move the pot to a runoff-safe place and make sure a decorative outer pot is not blocking drainage.
3. **Water for this pot and plant.** For an ordinary pot with a drainage hole, water slowly and evenly so it enters the medium and excess can emerge below. Follow reliable species instructions if they specify another method. Keep water away from sockets, equipment, and surfaces that cannot get wet.
4. **Clear runoff.** Let excess drain, empty the saucer or outer pot, then return the pot to a stable spot. A full saucer is not a bonus dose.
5. **Leave a usable record.** Note the moisture finding, whether you watered, any abnormal sign, and when to check again. Reassess when season, room temperature, or pot changes instead of copying an old interval.

### Success and stop

Either “not watered because the checked medium is still wet” or “watered according to this plant's guidance and runoff cleared” is a valid finish. If drainage is blocked, the medium remains wet, or care requirements are unknown, resolve that input instead of adding water.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Water runs around the edge but the centre stays dry:** Do not keep pouring indefinitely. Check for severely shrunken medium or congested roots and seek guidance for this plant and a possible repot.
- **The medium is wet but leaves droop:** Do not diagnose thirst from posture alone. Consider roots, light, temperature, or pests and seek horticultural advice when needed.
- **Water remains in an outer pot:** Empty it and confirm the inner pot can drain. Arrange a runoff place before the next watering.
- **You cannot lift the pot safely:** Work at a stable accessible sink or waterproof surface, use a small watering vessel, or ask for lifting help. Keep the moisture decision.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This handles one ordinary indoor plant in a draining pot. Hydroponics, automatic irrigation, unusual epiphytes, and edible crop production need their own guidance. RHS general advice yields to species-specific instructions; it does not mean every plant is watered as soon as its surface feels dry. The practical side effect is runoff to clear. A calendar can remind you to **check**, not command you to pour.
