## 中文

当一段公交或铁路**实际延误、取消或改道**，导致后续多段出行、目的地到达或已选衔接不再可靠时加载。目标是从你此刻真正所在的位置和运营方的实时信息出发，找到一条有证据的下一安排，或安全暂停。单纯按计划换下一辆公交由普通换乘流程处理；还没发生变化时不用把预测延误当作事实。这里不判定法定赔偿、改签资格或退款金额。

### 准备与输入

记下当前车站/站点或所乘车辆、实际时刻、原定下一段和最终目的地；保存你已有的票、通行证或预约信息。看真实运营方的服务公告、实时到发、站内屏/站牌或工作人员答复。把“已确认取消”“预计晚点”“还没更新”和“我推测赶不上”分开写。确认行动、无障碍、行李、天气及是否接近末班等个人约束；不假定你能跑着换乘。

### 执行

1. **冻结失效的旧计划。** 对照当前实际位置/时间与运营方最新状态，圈出原行程中第一处不再可行的接续。还在车上时先按安全乘车规则到允许下车处；不要在行车中要求特殊开门或跑去下一站追车。
2. **从现实起点重搜后续。** 用运营方当期规划器、实时出发和服务公告，从**现在可达**的站点与时间查到目的地，不只把旧截图里的下一班改成更晚一班。逐项核对每段的实际线路/车次、方向、停靠、换乘站、开放的站台/站点和预计到达；规划器与改道公告不一致就向官方渠道问清。
3. **比较至少一个可用方案和一个失败出口。** 看路线是否留有你实际需要的换乘时间、楼梯/电梯或其他可及路径、末班/停运限制及安全候车位置。可选项可能是后续同运营服务、另一条已确认路线、推迟出发或在安全地点等候/接应。没有被证实可达的终点，不写成“已安排好”。
4. **在换服务前单独核对票务。** 对你真实持有的票或通行证，查看运营方当期公告或问工作人员：改乘的线路/班次是否允许、是否需要重新验证/预约、票证或座位条件是否改变。不同运营方、分开购买的车票和跨模式衔接可能不互认；官网显示“替代线路”也不自动证明你的票有效。答复不清就记“票务待确认”，不要凭猜测登车或承诺免费。
5. **写一张新行程卡并复核第一步。** 写“现在__；已确认延误/取消__；下一段__、从__、约__；票务__；到__时再核对__；失败则__”。到实际站台或站点再查车辆/站位和实时公告。若原约定因此受影响，可另用迟到通知流程告诉相关人；不拿通知代替行程重排。

### 完成与停止

你有一条从**实际当前位置**出发、已核对首段服务与后续可行性、票务状态明确或标待确认、并含失效回退的方案，或确认暂时没有安全可行公共交通而暂停，本轮完成。**完成的是后续安排，不是实际抵达、票务获批或赔偿成立。** 若末班已过、候车点不安全、可及路径中断或没有可信实时信息，先到安全/有工作人员的地方求证或联系可信接应，不穿越封闭区抢时间。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看旧计划显示“还能接上”：** 用当下实际到站时刻和换乘所需时间重算，确认第二班是否仍在服务。
- **规划器给出路线，但公告说改道：** 把两者冲突记下，核对运营方实时公告或问工作人员，别按旧站位等车。
- **下一段是最后一班或已取消：** 查正式替代和可安全等待/接应方式；不保证末班等候或擅自推荐付费交通。
- **票上写一个车次，替代路线写另一个：** 查看这张票的当期适用规则及预约条件；分开购买的票尤其不能假设自动互认。
- **手机无电、信息仍不全：** 用站内屏、纸质站牌、售票/服务窗口或工作人员。能证实的只写能证实的，无法确认就暂停而非编出新班次。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通、可退出的公共交通延误恢复，不是危机处置、消费索赔或财务建议。运营商、地区、票种、末班时刻和无障碍设施变动较大，以当前实际服务为准。视觉、听觉、行动或语言不便时可用运营方文字/播报、站员或可信陪同核对信息与路径，不交出账户控制权。副作用是到达时间和后续安排可能全部重算；一张漂亮但失效的原行程不会自动恢复服务。

## English

Load this Skill when a bus or rail leg is **actually delayed, cancelled or diverted** and later legs, arrival or a planned connection are no longer reliable. Start from where you really are and the operator's live information to choose a grounded onward arrangement or pause safely. An ordinary on-time bus-to-bus transfer belongs to the transfer task. Do not turn an unconfirmed forecast into an event. This Skill does not decide statutory compensation, rebooking entitlement or a refund amount.

### Preparation and inputs

Note your actual stop, station or vehicle, the current time, planned next leg and final destination. Keep your real ticket, pass or booking details. Check the actual operator's service alerts, live departures, station display or staff reply. Separate “confirmed cancellation,” “estimated delay,” “no update” and “I think I will miss it.” Consider your mobility or access needs, luggage, weather and whether this is near the last service. Running is not a default interchange method.

### Execution

1. **Freeze the invalid part of the old plan.** Compare your actual place and time with the newest operator status and mark the first connection that no longer works. If you are still aboard, use normal safe travel to a permitted exit; do not demand a mid-route door opening or chase a bus down the road.
2. **Search onward from the real starting point.** With current operator planning, live departures and alerts, search from a stop you can **reach now** at a usable time to your destination. Do not merely substitute a later number into an old screenshot. Check each leg's service, direction, calls, interchange point, open platform or stop and expected arrival. If a planner and diversion alert conflict, clarify through an official channel.
3. **Compare a usable route with a way out if it fails.** Check actual change time, lifts or other accessible route, last-service or cancellation limits and a safe place to wait. Options may include a later service, a verified other route, postponing the trip or waiting safely for staff or a trusted pickup. A destination that cannot be verified as reachable is not “arranged.”
4. **Check fare use separately before changing services.** For the ticket or pass you really hold, read this operator's current notice or ask staff whether the new route or train is permitted, whether to validate or reserve again, and whether fare or seat conditions changed. Operators, separate tickets and modes may not accept each other's tickets. A planner showing an alternative does not prove your ticket is valid on it. Mark “fare use unconfirmed” if the answer is unavailable; do not board on a guess or promise a free transfer.
5. **Write the new trip card and verify its first step.** Note “now at __; confirmed disruption __; next service __ from __ around __; fare status __; recheck at __; if it fails __.” At the actual stop or platform, check the vehicle, position and current alert again. If an appointment is affected, use the separate late-arrival notification flow; a message does not replace an onward route.

### Success and stop

This round succeeds when your plan starts from your **actual location**, has a verified first service and feasible onward steps, states fare status or the need to confirm it, and has a fallback. It may end with a finding that no safe public-transport route is currently available and a decision to pause. **An onward plan is not arrival, ticket approval or compensation.** If the last service has gone, the waiting area is unsafe, access fails or live information is unavailable, seek a safe or staffed place or trusted pickup instead of rushing through a closed area.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The old planner still says the connection works:** Recalculate from actual arrival and your real change time; verify that the next service is running.
- **Planner suggests a route but alerts say diversion:** Record the conflict and use a current operator notice or staff answer before waiting at the old stop.
- **Next leg was the last service or is cancelled:** Check official alternatives and safe waiting or pickup; do not assume a last train will wait or recommend an unverified paid ride.
- **Your ticket names one service but the route names another:** Check this ticket's current validity and reservation conditions. Separate purchases especially do not guarantee mutual acceptance.
- **Phone is flat or information incomplete:** Use station boards, stop signs, ticket office or staff. State what is confirmed; pause if a new service cannot be verified.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers ordinary, safely exit-able transit disruption, not crisis response, consumer claims or financial advice. Operators, regions, fare types, last services and accessibility change. Use the actual current service. If sight, hearing, mobility or language affects your access, use the operator's text or announcements, staff or a trusted companion to confirm the route without handing over account control. Your arrival time and later steps may all change; the attractive old itinerary does not restore the service.
