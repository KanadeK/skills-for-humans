## 中文

当你刚把普通可水洗衣物从洗衣机或手洗水里拿出，发现**原来的痕迹似乎还在**，用这份 Skill 在烘干或熨烫前判断它是残余污渍、染色/面料改变，还是尚看不清，并决定下一步。它从洗后检查开始，不重新选择整桶洗衣程序；洗后串色、褪色和变形交对应的外观变化流程。未知或危险污染不靠再次洗涤解决。

### 准备与输入

保留衣物当前状态，找原污渍照片或记忆中的位置、已用产品与程序、衣物标签以及产品说明。找明亮台面并支撑湿衣，避免拉伸。先查衣物允许的干燥方式；若需等布面不那么湿才能看清，只能按标签允许的自然晾干方法观察，不先用热烘或熨斗“测试”。

### 执行

1. **暂停热干燥。** 将有痕迹的衣物从可烘干的一桶中取出，不让整桶为它升级处理；其余衣物仍按各自标签去向处理。若整桶有新颜色或结构变化，先转洗后外观变化流程。
2. **比对原位置和变化。** 看痕迹是否仍在同一处、颜色/形状是否与洗前相近，摸起来是否有洗剂残留只在污染风险已排除时进行。大片新染色、原布色变浅、印花脱落或纤维受损不是“继续去同一污渍”的证据；停止本流程并记录变化。
3. **只在来源与方法都清楚时再处理。** 原污渍仍在、来源已知、衣物和去渍产品标签都允许重复时，先在不显眼处复查相容性，再按**该污渍类型**和产品说明作局部处理，正常洗涤并再次检查。不要自动加更多洗剂、升温、混用去渍剂或宣布一定可洗掉。
4. **无法判断时保留可回滚状态。** 若湿布看不清，按标签允许的方法自然晾至可观察，再决定；若污渍来源不明、产品使布色变化或一次合理复试仍无改善，停止家庭试验，咨询制造商/专业护理或接受永久痕迹。
5. **给衣物实际去向。** 污渍消退且无新的颜色/结构问题，按标签完成晾干或烘干；仍有痕迹的衣物标记并单独放好，避免误入下一轮热烘。湿衣物不能无限期堆在密闭桶里等决定。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你已在热干燥前辨认“可按已知污渍重试 / 属于布色或结构变化 / 证据不足停止”之一，并且衣物有标签允许的去向。结果不要求每件旧污渍都消失。

- **湿布上看不清：** 按标签自然晾至可观察；不要先高温烘干再反悔。
- **重试后新出现淡斑：** 停止局部去渍，记录产品与位置；这可能是染料变化，转外观变化流程。
- **洗剂残留像白印：** 先核对产品、洗衣机和标签允许的漂洗路径；不要把残留自动当色素污渍加去渍剂。
- **第二次仍没改善：** 停止试错，查制造商或专业护理；反复加热和化学处理可能扩大损伤。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

仅限普通衣物上已完成一次正常洗涤的小面积痕迹，不处理危险污染或专业护理织物。湿布颜色、灯光与视力会影响判断；可用固定光源、洗前照片、放大查看或可信现场协助。若无法及时作下一步决定，按标签尽快安全展开晾干，避免把湿衣密闭滞留。也许要再洗一次，但这不是成功保证。

## English

Use this Skill just after you remove an ordinary washable garment from a washer or hand wash and **the original mark seems to remain**. Decide before tumble drying or ironing whether it is residual soil, a dye or fabric change, or still unclear. This starts at the post-wash check; it does not choose a new program for the whole load. New dye transfer, fading, or distortion belongs to the appearance-change workflow. Another wash does not solve unknown or hazardous contamination.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the garment as it is. Find a photo or remembered location of the original mark, products and cycle already used, the garment label, and product directions. Use a well-lit work surface that supports the wet fabric. Check the allowed drying method first. If the fabric must become less wet to judge it, observe it after label-permitted air drying, not after tumble heat or an iron used as a “test.”

### Execution

1. **Pause heat drying.** Take the marked garment out of a dryer-bound load; do not change the whole load's process for it. Other items follow their own labels. If the entire load shows new dye or structural change, go to the post-wash appearance-change workflow first.
2. **Compare the original location and change.** See whether the mark remains in the same place and resembles the prewash colour and shape. Check for detergent feel only after contamination risk has been ruled out. Broad new colour, fading of the original cloth, lost print, or fibre damage is not evidence to keep treating the same stain. Stop this route and record the change.
3. **Retry only with a known source and supported method.** If the original stain remains, its source is known, and both garment and product labels permit another treatment, test compatibility again in a hidden area. Spot-treat by **that stain type** under product directions, launder normally, and inspect again. Do not automatically add more detergent, raise temperature, mix removers, or promise complete removal.
4. **Keep an uncertain case reversible.** If wet fabric obscures the mark, air-dry by its label until you can see it. If the source is unknown, the product changes the dye, or one reasonable repeat has no effect, stop home trials and ask the maker or professional cleaner or accept a permanent mark.
5. **Assign a real destination.** If the stain is gone with no new dye or fabric problem, finish drying by the label. Keep a still-marked item identified and separate so it does not enter another hot dryer by mistake. Wet clothes cannot remain piled in a closed tub indefinitely while you decide.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Before heat drying, you chose one of “supported retry for a known stain,” “dye or structure change,” or “stop for insufficient evidence,” and the garment has a label-compatible destination. Success does not require every old mark to vanish.

- **The wet fabric hides the mark:** Air-dry by the label until you can inspect it. Do not use high dryer heat and then try to undo it.
- **A pale patch appears after the retry:** Stop spot treatment and record product and location. It may be a dye change; use the appearance-change route.
- **Detergent residue looks like a white mark:** Check product, washer, and label-permitted rinse directions before applying a stain remover to what may be residue.
- **A second attempt does not help:** Stop guessing; ask the maker or professional cleaner. Repeated heat and chemicals can increase damage.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a small mark after one normal wash on ordinary clothing, not hazardous contamination or professional-care fabric. Wet colours, lighting, and vision can change what you see. Use steady lighting, a prewash photo, magnification, or trusted in-person help. If a decision cannot be made soon, spread and air-dry promptly as the label permits rather than leaving damp clothing closed up. Another wash may be needed but is not a success guarantee.
