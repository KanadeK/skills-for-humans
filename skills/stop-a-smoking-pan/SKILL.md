## 中文

当普通少油煎炒的锅或油**刚开始冒烟、出现明显焦味或油有异常剧烈运动**，但还没有明火时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**停止继续加热、让锅原位安全冷却，并决定是否还能按可靠配方重新开始**。出现明火、燃气异味、电气危险或你不能安全触及热源时，立即退出本流程，按当地紧急指导离开并求助；这里不教灭火或维修。

### 准备与输入

不用靠近烟去判闻起来像什么。先看锅是否稳、热源开关能否从安全方向够到、烟是否已向人或易燃物扩散。食物、油和锅具的实际状态可能无法立即确认；不要为弄清来源而抓锅柄、掀锅底、加入水或抛进新食材。让旁人离开热锅路径。

### 执行步骤

1. **安全可达时立即关热。** 留锅在稳定炉头上，退开身体；不要端着冒烟的锅去水槽或室外。油烟若已转成火或控制无法安全触及，离开危险并按当地消防/紧急指导求助，不在本日常 Skill 内继续尝试。
2. **别用食材或水压住异常。** 不往热油里加水、不用新食材覆盖烟，也不在锅边试更多油。美国消防局明确提醒油火不能用水处理；即使尚无火，当前任务也没有理由用水试探异常热油。
3. **让锅在原位冷却。** 关热后余热仍在；避免碰锅面和把手，阻止旁人误触。只在没有危险且符合环境与设备说明时处理通风；若烟持续、加重或出现设备异常，离开并求助，不等到自己确定化学成分。
4. **安全后分别检查锅与食物。** 锅具涂层、把手或炉头有损伤/异常时停用并找制造商或合格支持；不在此修理。食物严重烧焦或安全史不清时不靠刮去黑处、重口味或再加热保证可食；按对应食品安全与成品补救边界决定弃置或另行处理。
5. **仅在原因清楚且条件恢复时再开一批。** 清洁/更换合格的锅、按配方使用适用少量油、降低到允许的热力并让食材事先就位。原因不明、锅具受损或你无法持续看守，就取消这次煎炒，换一条可安全执行的做法。

### 完成与停止

热源已关闭，锅与周边无继续升级的烟/热异常，锅已在原位等到可安全处理，并明确“可按修正条件重做 / 停止并处理”之一，就是完成。只要出现火、燃气味、电气异常、持续浓烟或烫伤风险，任务立即转为紧急处置并离开；这份 Skill 不承诺保住食物。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“冒烟才算锅热”：** 关热并冷却；下一次按配方与锅具限制预热，不用烟当温度计。
- **端锅去水槽：** 不搬动冒烟热锅；人在安全侧关热后让它留在稳固位置。
- **倒水想让油降温：** 停止，远离飞溅路径；热油/油火不使用水作调温或灭火工具。
- **锅冷了就继续用焦油：** 先弄清是否仍适用且无损；原因不明或器具异常就换锅/停止。
- **烟后食物表面仍好看：** 外观不证明中心熟度或污染/过热史安全，按食物具体规则决定。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只覆盖**尚未出现明火的早期异常**；失控油火、高温专业烹调和设备维修在范围外。烟可能来自油、食物或锅具，单凭气味不作诊断。若看不清烟但听到异常或闻到焦味，使用同样的停热原则；身体不能安全触及开关时先退开求助，不跨过锅去完成“亲自关火”任务。现实副作用是取消一批煎炒，但厨房不需要烟雾绩效指标。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary shallow pan with a little oil **begins smoking, gives a clear scorched odour, or shows abnormal violent oil movement**, but no flame is present. **Stop further heating, leave the pan to cool in place, and decide whether a fresh attempt can follow reliable directions.** Flame, gas odour, electrical danger, or a heat control you cannot reach safely exits this flow immediately for local emergency help. This is not firefighting or equipment repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Do not approach smoke to identify its smell. See whether the pan stands securely, you can reach the heat switch from a safe side, and smoke threatens people or nearby combustible materials. Oil, food, and pan condition may not be knowable immediately. Do not grab a handle, inspect the underside, add water, or drop new food in to investigate. Keep others away from the hot pan.

### Execution steps

1. **Switch off heat immediately when safely reachable.** Leave the pan on its stable hob and step back. Do not carry a smoking pan to a sink or outside. If smoke becomes flame or control is inaccessible, leave danger and follow local fire or emergency guidance rather than attempting more under this everyday Skill.
2. **Do not smother an abnormality with food or water.** Add neither water to hot oil nor more food to hide smoke. Do not test it with extra oil. The US Fire Administration warns not to use water on a grease fire. Even before flame, this task has no reason to test abnormal hot oil with water.
3. **Let the pan cool in place.** Residual heat remains after the switch is off. Avoid touching the surface or handle and prevent others from contacting it. Address ventilation only when safe and consistent with the equipment and setting. If smoke persists or grows or equipment behaves abnormally, leave and get help instead of waiting to identify a chemical cause.
4. **Check pan and food separately once safe.** Stop using cookware or a hob with damaged coating, handle, or another fault and use maker or qualified support; this Skill does not repair it. Do not claim severely scorched food or food with unclear safety history is edible because you scrape away the dark part, add strong seasoning, or heat again. Use the applicable food-safety and finished-food recovery boundary to decide.
5. **Restart only with a known cause and restored conditions.** Clean or replace suitable cookware, use the recipe's small amount of suitable oil, use permitted lower heat, and have food prepared first. Cancel this sauté and choose a safer method if the cause remains unknown, cookware is damaged, or you cannot attend the heat.

### Success and stop

Heat is off, smoke and heat are not escalating, the pan has cooled in place to a manageable state, and you have a clear “retry under corrected conditions” or “stop and handle separately” result. Fire, gas odour, electrical faults, persistent dense smoke, or scald risk moves this to emergency help immediately. Preserving the meal is not a success requirement.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Smoke means ready”:** Turn heat off and cool. Next time preheat under the recipe and cookware limits rather than using smoke as a thermometer.
- **Carrying it to the sink:** Leave the smoking hot pan where stable. Stop heat from a safe side and let it settle.
- **Adding water to cool oil:** Stop and avoid the splatter path. Water is not a heat or fire control for hot oil or a grease fire.
- **Reusing scorched oil after the pan cools:** First decide whether the pan and contents remain suitable. Unknown cause or faulty equipment means change the pan or stop.
- **Food still looks attractive after the smoke:** Appearance does not establish its centre, contamination, or overheat history; use food-specific rules.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers an **early abnormality before flame**. An uncontrolled oil fire, extreme-heat professional cooking, and repairs are outside scope. Smoke may arise from oil, food, or cookware; smell alone is not a diagnosis. If smoke is hard to see but the sound or odour is abnormal, use the same stop principle. If your body cannot safely reach the switch, retreat and get help rather than crossing above the pan to turn it off yourself. The batch may be cancelled; the kitchen has no smoke-performance target.
