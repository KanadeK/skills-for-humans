## 中文

当你带着普通事项的纸本材料来到约定地点，需要把其中一些文件交给正确对象时加载。你要**核对服务/接收者，逐项确认哪些文件被实际接收、只看后归还、拒收或仍在你手里**。这里不解释文件的法律效力、不代签敏感声明，也不保证事项被受理或获批。

### 准备与输入

拿出预约确认、当期清单和你出发/抵达时的原件副本点数。确认接收方官方名称、窗口或经授权的人；保留文件在夹内，只在需要核对时向实际人员出示。若对方要求提交比原清单更多的私人资料，先核对官方规定和你的权限，不因有人穿制服就默认可以交出全部原件。

### 执行

1. **先问清是不是这个服务。** 在指定地点核对预约/事项名称、柜台或工作人员职责和将如何处理原件与副本。地点同楼、职员看起来熟悉都不是正确接收者的充分证据；有疑问让官方引导确认。
2. **按清单逐项出示/交付。** 每项说明中性名称、份数与原件/副本身份，依接收者当前要求决定只核看、留下哪份或退回。不要在公开队列展开无关的个人信息；未经授权的他人文件不交。
3. **把实际结果逐行记录。** 标“对方已收、看后退回、拒收/要求补项、尚未递交”，必要时写官方给的收件编号/回执与时间。对方口头说“都好了”但你仍拿着未交的纸时，不把状态自动改为收到。
4. **核回手中原件与副本。** 交接结束后看被返还的原件是否与出发清单一致，哪份被机构留下，哪份还在你的夹内。若机构按实际规则留原件，你只记事实并索取其提供的确认方式；本 Skill 不判断是否应当留。
5. **处理未完成交接。** 接收者不是正确人、材料不被收、回执未给或要求与你准备时不同，保留纸张与对方具体说明，问实际机构下一步或更正窗口；不要把“到了现场”或“递过窗口”写成完整交付。

### 完成与停止

每一份准备的文件都有可观察的已收、已返还、被拒或仍由你持有状态，接收对象与服务可追溯；这不是机构已批准或某种法定提交效果的判定。身份/权限不清、敏感资料额外索取、原件去向不明或需签专业声明时停在问官方/有权者，保护尚未交出的文件。

### 常见报错与补救

- **排错窗口却把资料交过去：** 立即向机构说明并记录接手人/时间，核查材料去向，不假设会自动转交。
- **原件被看过但没归还：** 当场按清单询问是正式留存还是未返还，记对方答复/编号。
- **只收一部分页：** 对未收项标原因与下次要求，别写整包已交。
- **前台说“放这里”，无人能确认接收：** 问官方可记录的收件办法；无法确认则保留未交状态。
- **别人要求额外私密原件：** 核实其服务、权限和机构要求后再决定，不当场被催促就交。

### 假设、替代与副作用

用于普通低风险纸本交接，不替代法律、税务、财务、医疗或身份认证程序。机构对原件/副本和回执的规则不同，美国 FTC 的消费者投诉信只是“留原件、交副本”的一种例子。听力、视力或语言沟通不便时可以用可访问的清单、文字确认或经授权的可信协助者，交接事实仍要能读回。副作用是“给了前台”会得到可核对的人、页和状态。

## English

Load this Skill when you have arrived with ordinary paper materials and need some of them to reach the right receiving person. **Confirm service and recipient, then record which documents were actually kept, viewed and returned, refused or still held by you.** This does not interpret legal effect, sign a sensitive declaration for someone or guarantee the matter is accepted or approved.

### Preparation and inputs

Use booking confirmation, current list and the original/copy count from departure and arrival. Confirm the official service name, counter or authorised person. Keep papers in the folder until the real worker needs to check them. If someone asks for more private material than the list, confirm the official rule and your authority first. A uniform alone does not authorise surrendering every original.

### Execution

1. **Check this is the service.** At the stated place, confirm booking or task name, recipient's role and what will happen to originals and copies. Same building or a familiar-looking worker is not enough; ask official directions when uncertain.
2. **Show or hand over one line at a time.** State a neutral document name, count and original or copy status. Under the real recipient's current instruction, show, leave or take back each one. Do not unfold unrelated private data in a public queue or give away another person's papers without authority.
3. **Record observed results per line.** Mark “received and retained / viewed then returned / refused or more needed / not yet presented,” adding any official reference, receipt and time provided. An informal “all done” does not turn papers still in your hand into received items.
4. **Reconcile originals and copies still with you.** After the exchange, check returned originals against the departure list, which paper the institution kept and which copies remain. If the real process retains an original, record that fact and obtain its available acknowledgment method; this Skill does not decide whether it should.
5. **Keep unresolved handoffs open.** Wrong recipient, rejected page, missing acknowledgment or a new requirement gets a factual note and a question to the actual institution. Do not call arriving onsite or passing a paper across a counter a complete transfer without the recipient's actual state.

### Success and stop

Each prepared document has an observed received, returned, refused or still-held state, with service and actual recipient traceable. This does not determine approval or formal legal submission. Pause for unclear authority, unexpected requests for sensitive papers, an unaccounted original or a specialist declaration. Protect documents not yet handed over and ask the official or authorised holder.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Papers went to the wrong counter:** Tell the institution immediately, record person and time and trace the papers rather than assuming internal forwarding.
- **An original was viewed but not returned:** Ask on the spot whether it was formally retained or missed, and record reply and reference.
- **Only some pages were accepted:** Mark remaining lines with reasons and next request, not “whole packet delivered.”
- **Reception says “leave it here” with no receipt:** Ask the official trackable intake method; keep it unconfirmed if nobody receives it.
- **Someone requests extra private originals:** Check service, authority and institution rule before deciding, even under time pressure.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary low-risk paper handoff, not a legal, tax, financial, medical or identity-authentication procedure. Originals, copies and receipts follow the actual institution's rules. The US FTC's consumer-letter example is only one keep-originals/send-copies situation. An accessible list, written confirmation or trusted authorised helper can support different hearing, sight or language needs. “Left at reception” now has a checkable person, pages and state.
