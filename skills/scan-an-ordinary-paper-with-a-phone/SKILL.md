## 中文

当一份普通、低敏感的纸张需要数字副本，而你已有带扫描功能的手机应用时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**完整、清楚地拍下每一页或每一面，保存到你知道的位置，并重新打开核对**。扫描不会自动证明副本可被任何机构接受，也不意味着可以扔掉纸质原件。

只处理你有权复制的普通材料。身份证件、医疗或财务记录、保密文件及有专门提交规则的材料不在这里；先按实际隐私与接收方要求处理。不要为了这一步安装陌生扫描应用或默认把纸张上传到不想使用的云端。

### 准备与输入

数清要保留的页数、顺序，以及是否有背面内容。选一个你已在用、且知道保存位置的扫描功能；例如 iPhone/iPad 的“备忘录”或“文件”，或手机上的 Google Drive。它们的实际菜单会变。先决定保存在哪里：本地文件夹、已有笔记，或你明确愿意使用的云端文件夹。若应用的保存方式不符合你的隐私需要，暂停并改用合适的已有工具。

### 执行

1. **把纸摊平并照亮。** 选择稳定、对比明显的背景，尽量避开手指、阴影、反光和折痕压住文字。手机尽量与纸面平行；如难以稳住，可把手机靠在稳定支撑上，或请你信任的人协助对齐。
2. **进入已有应用的“扫描文稿/文件”功能。** 在画面中框住整张纸，使用自动或手动拍摄都可以。拍完看裁切边缘，确保页眉、页脚、页码、签注和角落没有被切掉；偏斜或模糊就重拍这一页。
3. **按原顺序加上每一页。** 有背面就扫描背面；多页时一页一页检查缩略图顺序。别只数拍摄次数：重复拍了一页仍会漏掉另一页。调整色彩或对比度时以原文清晰为准，别让滤镜抹掉浅色笔迹。
4. **逐页读一次关键内容。** 放大查看最小的文字、表格边缘和手写部分，并与原纸核对页数与顺序。若应用提供文字识别，可用作查找辅助，但不要把识别结果当作完整性的证明；看不清的内容重拍，不靠猜。
5. **命名并保存。** 用纸张实际用途和有意义的日期起名，在应用里明确选定本地位置、笔记或云端文件夹后保存。不要在只看到预览时就以为已保存；等待应用完成并查看实际文件或笔记条目。
6. **离开扫描界面后找回。** 从你选的存放位置重新打开，检查页数、顺序、边缘、清晰度和名称。只有通过这个检查，才把它记作可用数字副本。原纸要不要保留，按实际用途和保留要求另行决定。

### 完成条件

你能说出副本保存在哪，并从那里重新打开；每个相关页面或背面都在，顺序正确，关键文字和边缘能与原纸核对。若某页模糊、缺失、应用尚未保存或位置不明，继续补拍/查找，不把相机里曾经出现过画面当成完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **自动裁切吞掉边角：** 调整裁切框或改手动拍摄，重拍受影响页；不要用放大后的视觉效果掩盖缺失。
- **浅色字或反光看不清：** 换更均匀的光，转动纸或手机，选择能保留笔迹的模式后重拍；不靠文字识别猜内容。
- **页数对不上：** 把纸页和扫描页按顺序对照，找出漏页/重复页再修正，不能只看文件总页数。
- **保存后找不到：** 回到应用查看它是存进笔记、本地文件夹还是云端；搜索实际名称，并核对是否仍显示保存中。别再次上传一份不知位置的副本。
- **存储或网络不足：** 停止追加，确认已保存部分能打开；条件恢复后补齐，未完成前保留原纸。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通低敏感纸张的阅读副本流程。应用对 PDF/JPG、文字识别、自动裁切和存储位置的支持不同；以实际界面为准。若视觉检查困难，可以放大、使用屏幕阅读器或让可信协助者帮忙核对页边，但仍需确认实际图像完整。扫描多一份文件，也多一份需要管理的位置；纸不会因为像素化就自动退休。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary, low-sensitivity paper needs a digital copy and your phone already has a scanning feature. **Capture every relevant page or side clearly, save it somewhere you know, and reopen it to check.** A scan does not prove that an institution will accept the copy or that the paper original may be discarded.

Use this only for ordinary material you may copy. Identity documents, medical or financial records, confidential files, and materials with special submission rules are outside this flow; follow their actual privacy and recipient requirements. Do not install an unfamiliar scanner app or default to a cloud destination you do not want just for this task.

### Preparation and inputs

Count the pages, their order, and any writing on the backs. Choose a scanning feature you already use and whose save location you understand, such as Notes or Files on iPhone/iPad, or Google Drive on a phone. Exact menus may change. Decide now whether the destination is a local folder, an existing note, or a cloud folder you knowingly chose. If the app's storage route does not meet your privacy needs, pause and use another suitable existing tool.

### Execution

1. **Flatten and light the paper.** Use a stable contrasting background and avoid fingers, shadows, glare, and folds covering text. Hold the phone roughly parallel to the page. If it is hard to steady, support the phone or ask a trusted person to help align it.
2. **Use the available “scan document” feature.** Frame the whole page. Automatic or manual capture can work. After each capture, inspect the crop so no header, footer, page number, annotation, or corner is cut off. Retake a skewed or blurry page.
3. **Add every page in the original order.** Scan the back if it contains content. For multiple pages, check the thumbnail sequence as you go. Counting captures is insufficient: two shots of page one do not include page two. Use color or contrast adjustments only if the original writing stays visible; a filter can erase light marks.
4. **Read the critical content page by page.** Zoom into small type, table edges, and handwriting, and compare the page count and order with the paper. If the app offers text recognition, use it only as a finding aid, not proof that every mark was captured. Retake unclear content instead of guessing.
5. **Name and save.** Use the paper's real purpose and a meaningful date. Explicitly choose the local folder, note, or cloud folder in the app and save. A preview on screen is not proof of a saved file; wait for completion and locate the actual file or note entry.
6. **Find it after leaving the scanner.** Reopen from the chosen location and check page count, order, edges, legibility, and name. Only then call it a usable digital copy. Decide separately whether the paper original must remain under the real purpose and retention rules.

### Success

You can say where the copy is saved and reopen it there. Every relevant page or back is present, in order, with key text and edges matching the original. If a page is blurred, missing, still unsaved, or stored somewhere unknown, recapture or locate it. A camera preview that existed once is not completion.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Automatic crop swallows a corner:** Adjust the crop or use manual capture and retake that page. Zooming into the result cannot restore missing content.
- **Light writing or glare hides text:** Change to more even light, rotate the paper or phone, choose a mode that preserves the writing, and retake. Do not ask OCR to guess it.
- **Page count does not match:** Compare paper and scan page by page; identify the missing or duplicated page and correct it rather than trusting the total alone.
- **You cannot find it after saving:** Check whether the app saved into a note, local folder, or cloud folder. Search the actual name and see whether saving is still in progress. Do not upload another unlocated copy.
- **Storage or network runs out:** Stop adding pages and check that the saved portion opens. Complete the set when conditions permit, and keep the paper until then.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This creates a reading copy of ordinary low-sensitivity paper. PDF/JPG, text recognition, automatic crops, and save destinations vary by app; follow the actual interface. If visual review is hard, zoom, use a screen reader, or ask a trusted helper to check page edges, while still confirming the image itself is complete. One scan means one more file location to manage; the paper does not retire merely because pixels exist.
