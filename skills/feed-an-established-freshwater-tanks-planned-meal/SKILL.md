## 中文

当你负责一个**鱼种、饲料种类、每餐份量和时间都已由原饲养方案确定的稳定淡水缸**，需要实际喂这一餐时，加载这份 Skill。结果是本餐按方案投下、鱼是否摄食与剩料得到记录；不由本篇重新设计鱼的饮食或统一规定几分钟/几次。鱼若病弱、突然不吃、缸水异常，先测水并找懂该鱼种的专业人员，不拿加餐试探。

### 准备与输入

核上次喂食记录，避免两人重复喂；读该饲料标签和鱼种方案，确认浮/沉型、保存期和已约定份量。用干净干燥的小工具先取计划量，不把整罐放缸边倒。不同鱼种和缸内位置摄食方式不同，RSPCA 的示例频率不能代替本缸方案；方案缺失先向原负责人/水族专业者核实。

### 执行

1. **先看鱼和设备是否在正常基线。** 鱼明显异常、过滤停摆、水质已报警时暂停例行投喂并按专业建议行动，不把饲料加入可能已污染的水。
2. **按已定份量分散投放。** 在该鱼种平时可取得食物的水面/合适位置少量投入，避免一次把计划量堆成一团；对底层/夜行鱼只遵照其原方案，不用热带鱼饲料随意替其他鱼种。手、勺不带肥皂残留入缸。
3. **按原方案观察摄食与剩料。** 注意是否所有鱼能吃到、是否有食物长期漂/沉不被吃；记录实际情况。剩料按本缸既定安全方法移除或联系原负责人，不能通过再加食物解决不吃。
4. **收好食物并写交接。** 密封饲料放回标签要求位置，记日期、种类、份量及异常。若下一餐由别人喂，让记录可见，避免重复投喂。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一餐按既有鱼种方案投下，鱼摄食/剩料已观察并有记录，饲料妥善收好；问题交专业人员。只把饲料倒进去就离开，不看剩料或上次记录，不算完整喂食。

- **上次是否喂过不清：** 先向负责人核实，不猜一次双份“保险”。
- **鱼突然不吃或异常游动：** 记录、测水、求水族专业建议，不用换饲料/加量治疗。
- **食物大量剩下：** 按既定安全办法处理剩料并查计划，不让它在水里腐坏。
- **饲料过期或种类不符：** 停用，核原方案与可用饲料，不拿人食替代。
- **视觉/行动不便：** 预先分装已批准份量、可信协助观察摄食，由你保留实际记录。

### 假设与边界

RSPCA 等机构给一般喂食提示，但各鱼种、生命阶段与缸中数量差别大；此 Skill 只执行已确定的一餐。过量投喂可使水质恶化，然而本篇不提供治疗、药物或具体热量建议。

## English

Load this Skill when an **established freshwater tank has known species, food type, amount, and timing in its existing care plan** and you must serve this one meal. Finish with the planned feed given and intake/leftovers recorded. This does not redesign diet or set a universal minutes-per-meal or feed frequency. If fish suddenly refuse food, look ill, or water is abnormal, test and contact a species-aware aquatics professional rather than testing with extra food.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the last feeding record to avoid two carers feeding twice. Read this food's label and species plan for floating/sinking presentation, expiry, and agreed portion. Pre-measure the planned amount with a clean dry tool instead of pouring the tub at tank edge. Species and feeding positions differ; an RSPCA example schedule does not replace this tank's plan. Ask the original carer or an aquatics professional if that plan is missing.

### Execution

1. **Check normal baseline and equipment first.** Pause routine feeding for clearly abnormal fish, stopped filtration, or an existing water alert and act under qualified advice rather than adding food to possibly polluted water.
2. **Offer the decided portion gradually.** Put a little at the usual accessible surface or place for these fish, rather than one heap. Follow the original plan for bottom feeders or nocturnal species; do not exchange tropical and other species' food by guess. Keep soap residue off hands and tools entering the water.
3. **Observe intake and leftovers for the plan's interval.** Note whether fish reach food and whether much remains floating or settling. Record what happened. Remove leftovers only by this tank's safe established method or ask the responsible carer; more food will not fix refusal.
4. **Store food and hand off a record.** Close and store food under its label. Note date, type, amount, and anomalies. Make it visible to the next feeder so the meal is not duplicated.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One meal follows the known species plan, intake and leftovers are observed and recorded, food is stored, and a concern is routed to a professional. Dumping feed and leaving without checking prior feeds or leftovers is unfinished.

- **Previous feed is uncertain:** Ask the responsible carer instead of adding a speculative double meal.
- **Fish suddenly refuse or swim abnormally:** Record, test water, and seek aquatics advice, not an improvised food change or extra dose.
- **Much food remains:** Follow the established safe leftover route and review the plan before it rots in water.
- **Food is expired or wrong species:** Stop, check the plan and suitable supply, not human-food substitution.
- **Sight or movement is limited:** Approved pre-portioned meals and trusted observation help can support an honest record.

### Assumptions and limits

General RSPCA examples cannot determine this species, stage, and stocking plan. This Skill executes one already-decided meal. Overfeeding can impair water quality, but no medicine, treatment, or calorie prescription is provided.
