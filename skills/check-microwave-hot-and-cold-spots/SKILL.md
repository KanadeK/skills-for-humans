## 中文

当普通食物已经按微波说明完成加热和[中途重分布/静置](../stir-turn-and-stand-microwaved-food/SKILL.md)，你需要决定**整份是否可按本食品用途食用或还须继续加热**时，加载这份 Skill。微波会留下不均匀的冷热位置，不能只看碗边冒汽、表面沸腾或摸着很烫。动物性食品、剩菜等的安全核心温度与静置规则必须取自适用权威和[既有熟度流程](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)，这里不发明通用温度。

### 准备与输入

确认食品类型、份量、包装/配方的完成条件、中途是否按要求搅拌/转向、静置是否真正完成，以及安全的开盖方式。需要测温时用清洁、适合该食物的温度计；除非温度计明确标为可留在微波炉内，否则只在停止微波后使用。大块或不均匀食物要有可触及的多个检查位置；无法检查中心就不把外缘当中心。

### 执行步骤

1. **先结束说明要求的静置。** 食品包装或可靠配方规定的等待属于烹饪过程，不能按响铃立刻跳过。用隔热工具从远离身体方向打开盖/膜，避开蒸汽和热凝水；不要以手触食物试烫。
2. **按食品要求选择检查法。** 对需要安全核心温度的食物，用干净探针在最厚/中心及其他可能偏冷的位置测量，避开容器和骨头；依当地对应食品规则判断。对无需这类温度的普通食物，使用可靠包装/配方的质地、均匀热透等条件，不用一次冒汽概括全份。
3. **至少比较多个有意义的位置。** 边缘、中心、上层与厚处可能不同，按食物形状选择实际最可能冷的位置。一个位置达到目标，不能让另一个冷点自动合格；记录最低或未达条件的位置，并且工具触过未熟食物后按说明清洁。
4. **未达要求就走一次受控续热。** 先按食品说明搅拌/转向或调整份量，再用其允许的下一段加热，之后按要求静置并重新分点检查。没有可靠续热方法、温度计失效或食物时间/温度史不清时暂停食用并按适用食品安全指导处理，不自行循环“再来一分钟”。
5. **全部位置符合才结束。** 达到该食物的时间、温度、静置及配方结果后，才标记可按用途继续或上桌；食物存放、冷却与剩菜去向另走现有流程。

### 完成与停止

你能指出食品要求、静置记录、检查的各位置与结果，并给出“完成 / 继续受控加热并复查 / 条件不明停止”之一，就完成这次判断。没有适用食品安全规则、无法到达中心、温度计不可信或出现异常气味/容器损坏时不宣称已热透。

### 常见报错与补救

- **碗边烫手就说整份热了：** 先按说明静置，再查中心和厚处；容器热不是食品中心读数。
- **一个点达标就结束：** 检查其他可能冷点，按最低/未达条件的位置决定下一步。
- **把普通金属温度计留炉内运行：** 按温度计/炉具说明，只在停止微波后测量，除非它明确获准随炉运行。
- **跳过静置直接测试：** 先完成食品规定静置，再比较多个位置；静置不能只用延长加热替代。
- **冷点依旧却没有续热说明：** 停止猜分钟数，查食品/制造商或当地食品安全指导，必要时改可靠方法。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本流程只检查普通食物的微波后完成，不提供医疗、婴幼儿或不明生食的微波安全配方。食品、份量、设备功率与容器会改变冷热分布；按具体说明和当地安全标准。视觉、手部或阅读受限时可用大字/语音温度计、可信的现场读数协助和较小可触及份量；若无法取得必要核心证据，就停止。现实副作用是多测两个点，但最热一口不再替整碗发表声明。

## English

Load this Skill after ordinary food has completed its instructed microwave heating and [redistribution and standing](../stir-turn-and-stand-microwaved-food/SKILL.md), when you must decide **whether the whole portion meets its food-specific use or needs further heat**. Microwaves can leave hot and cold spots. A steaming edge, bubbling surface, or hot-feeling bowl is not a whole-food check. Safe core temperatures and standing for animal foods and leftovers come from the applicable authority and existing [doneness process](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md), not a universal number here.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the food, amount, package or recipe endpoint, whether the required stir or turn was done, whether standing really finished, and how to open the cover safely. Use a clean suitable thermometer when temperature is required. Unless the probe explicitly permits microwave use, put it into food only after the microwave stops. A large or uneven item needs several reachable test points. No access to the centre means its edge cannot stand in for it.

### Execution steps

1. **Finish the food's standing period.** The package or reliable recipe's wait is part of cooking and cannot be skipped at the bell. Use heat protection and lift the cover or film away from your body, avoiding steam and hot droplets. Do not touch food by hand as a heat test.
2. **Choose the food's appropriate check.** For required safe core temperatures, measure the thickest or central part and other likely cold locations with a clean probe, avoiding vessel and bone. Apply the food's local safety rule. For ordinary food without that temperature requirement, use the reliable package or recipe's texture and even-heating checks rather than one steaming patch.
3. **Compare several meaningful locations.** Edge, centre, top, and thick areas can differ; choose the places most likely to lag for this shape. One passing point does not automatically pass another. Record the lowest or unmet result, and clean a tool after contact with undercooked food under its directions.
4. **Continue under an instructed path if needed.** Stir or turn and adjust amount under the food's directions, then heat for a supported further interval, stand as required, and check several points again. If no reliable continuation exists, the thermometer is faulty, or food time and temperature history are unknown, hold off eating and follow applicable food-safety guidance. Do not loop on “one more minute” by guess.
5. **Finish only when the relevant points pass.** Mark food ready for its intended next step or service only after its temperature, time, standing, and recipe result are met. Cooling, storage, and leftovers use their separate flow.

### Success and stop

You can state the food's requirement, standing record, locations tested, their results, and one of “complete,” “continue under directions and recheck,” or “stop with unknown conditions.” Do not claim even heating without applicable safety rules, centre access, trustworthy measurement, or with an abnormal odour or damaged vessel.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A hot bowl edge is called hot food:** Stand as directed and test centre and thick areas. Dish heat is not a core reading.
- **One point passes and the meal is served:** Test other likely cold spots and decide from the lowest or unmet location.
- **An ordinary metal probe stays in the running oven:** Measure only after stopping, unless probe and oven directions expressly permit in-oven use.
- **Standing is skipped before measurement:** Complete the food's required standing first. Extra microwaving alone does not perform a standing step.
- **A cold spot remains but no later interval is supplied:** Stop guessing minutes and consult food, maker, or local safety guidance, or switch to a reliable method.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This checks ordinary food after microwaving, not a medical, infant-food, or unknown raw-food heating protocol. Food, amount, power, and vessel change hot spots; use exact directions and local safety standards. An accessible talking or large-display thermometer, trusted in-person reading, and smaller testable portions can help with sight, hand, or reading limits. Stop when required core evidence cannot be obtained. You may check two extra places; the hottest mouthful no longer represents the bowl.
