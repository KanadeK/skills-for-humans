## 中文

当你要短时离开，**已有健康稳定的淡水鱼缸**需要另一个已同意的人按既定计划照看时，加载这份 Skill。结果是具体日期、投喂、观察、水质/设备检查、求助联系和进门方式被对方复述确认；不是丢一罐鱼食就叫交接。E005 的猫交接不覆盖鱼缸水质、滤器和电水相邻风险。本篇不让未经培训者做新缸循环、药物、复杂换水或电气维修。

### 准备与输入

先确认照料者愿意、能到场且愿意按鱼种方案做，核离开和返回日期。拿鱼种/数量、正常水温与行为、原喂食分量/时点、最近测试值及频率、设备型号与平时流量、专用用品位置和熟悉本缸的水族专业联系人。可能要进门时仅在双方同意的渠道交接必要权限，不把门锁密码写进公开纸张或 PR。找不到能胜任的人则调整行程/求合适服务，不能无人照看却假设自动喂食器万无一失。

### 执行

1. **用一页实际日程写任务。** 明确哪天谁看鱼和设备、哪餐多少已批准饲料、哪些水质项目何时测、是否需要例行换水（只有已学会且同意才分配），以及记录回传入口。饲料可按本方案预分装，避免照料者从大罐随手多倒。
2. **带对方走一次现场。** 指出缸、温度显示、过滤水流、饲料/试剂/鱼缸专用桶、正常记录与设备说明；示范不敲缸、不嘴吸虹吸、试剂液不回倒及水电危险要退出。对方不能安全操作的项目转专业人员而非口头硬派。
3. **约定异常和联系顺序。** 写鱼突然异常、读数偏离、设备停/漏、缺食时应先停止哪一步、如何联系你与熟悉鱼种的水族兽医/零售者；不授予对方自行加药、改 pH 或拆电设备权限。写明有时差/电话不通时的可联系替代人。
4. **请对方复述并确认。** 照料者用自己的话说出下一次到场、饭量、测试、异常联系人和进入方式；双方确认日期与责任范围。若关键点复述不对，修正后再走一次；无人能明确承接就不能把交接当完成。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

愿意且能到场的照料者已拿到鱼种本缸既有方案、用品位置、行动边界与备援联系人，并正确复述首日任务。仅有自动喂食器或一条“帮我看看鱼”的消息，不是照料交接。

- **帮忙人不懂试剂或换水：** 只交可安全执行的观察/已分装喂食，其余约水族服务，不强派。
- **饲料未分量、会重复喂：** 按原方案预分装并留每日记录表。
- **设备停了或读数异常：** 照料者按清单记录/联络专业者，不自行修电或调药。
- **你失联时无人可问：** 在离开前补足获准备援联系人，否则重排离开计划。
- **文字/语言可及性不足：** 大字双语/图示加现场复述，由照料者确认实际理解。

### 假设与边界

RSPCA 建议离开时有负责的人每天检查鱼与设备，自动喂食器仍需检查；真正频率和特殊事项由鱼种方案决定。本篇是责任交接，不代表收件人具备兽医/设备维修资质，也不保证动物最终状态。

## English

Load this Skill when you will be away briefly and an **existing healthy established freshwater aquarium** needs another consenting person to follow its current plan. Finish when dates, feeding, observation, testing/equipment checks, help contacts, and access are acknowledged in the carer's own words. Leaving a food tub alone is not a handoff. E005's cat handoff does not cover water chemistry, filtration, or electricity near water. This does not assign untrained people new-tank cycling, medication, complex water change, or electrical repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the carer agrees and can attend for the absence dates. Gather species/count, usual temperature and behaviour, approved food portions and times, recent test results and schedule, device model and baseline flow, dedicated supplies, and species-aware aquatics contacts. Give necessary access only through an agreed private route, not public notes or a PR. If no capable carer exists, adjust the trip or arrange suitable service rather than assuming an auto-feeder is infallible.

### Execution

1. **Write an actual daily one-page schedule.** Name who checks fish and equipment on each day, when and how much approved food, which water tests and when, any planned partial change only for a trained consenting person, and where to report. Pre-portion approved meals if useful so the carer does not pour from a large tub.
2. **Walk the person through the real tank.** Show temperature display, filter flow, food, test kit, dedicated bucket, normal records, and manuals. Demonstrate no glass tapping, no mouth siphon, no reacted kit liquid back into water, and an exit for water/electricity risk. Route a task they cannot safely perform to an aquatics professional rather than assigning it verbally.
3. **Agree abnormal and contact order.** Write which action to stop and whom to call for unusual fish, out-of-range tests, stopped/leaking equipment, or missing food. Include you, a species-aware aquatic vet or retailer, and an approved backup for time-zone or phone failure. Do not authorize improvising medicine, pH changes, or device disassembly.
4. **Ask for teach-back and confirmation.** Have the carer restate first visit, portion, testing, abnormal contact, and access. Confirm dates and responsibility together. Correct a missed fact and repeat. A handoff is incomplete when no one can clearly take responsibility.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A willing available carer has this tank's plan, supply locations, action limits and backup contact, and correctly restates the first day's task. An auto-feeder or a vague “please check the fish” message alone is not care transfer.

- **Helper cannot use a test kit or change water:** Assign only safe observation or pre-portioned feed and arrange aquatics service for the rest.
- **Food is unmeasured or double-feeding likely:** Pre-portion under the existing plan and keep a daily record.
- **Equipment stops or a result is abnormal:** Carer records and contacts the professional under the sheet, without live electrical repair or drugs.
- **No one is reachable when you are not:** Add an approved backup before leaving or change the absence plan.
- **Written language is inaccessible:** Use large-print translated or pictured notes plus in-person teach-back and the carer's actual confirmation.

### Assumptions and limits

RSPCA recommends responsible daily fish/equipment checks during absence, including checking an automatic feeder; actual frequency and exceptions depend on the species plan. This transfers responsibility, not veterinary or equipment qualifications, and cannot guarantee animal outcomes.
