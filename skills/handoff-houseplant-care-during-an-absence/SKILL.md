## 中文

当你将离家一段时间，平时自己检查的室内盆栽可能无人照料时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一份**有人明确接手、每盆都有检查方法和停止规则**的短期交接；若无人接手，也要知道哪些植物仍有风险，不虚构保证。

### 准备与输入

列出实际在家的盆栽、离家和返回日期、品种说明、通常查看的盆土条件、接水方式和可安全到达的位置。确认房屋与钥匙的实际授权边界；不要在公共清单里写门禁码或密码。看不清标签或搬不动盆时，先安排可读说明与适当协助。

### 执行

1. **按实际需要筛选。** 依据品种、盆器、季节与离家时长，区分可能需要中途检查的植物和可等到返家的植物；不要把所有盆都设成“每天一杯”。RHS 的假期建议是情境例子，不是跨品种的安全时限。
2. **征得照料者同意。** 说明何时可能需要到场、可以照料哪些盆、能否安全搬动或接近，以及遇到异常怎样联系你。没有答复不是默认接单。
3. **给每盆可执行的信息。** 标识植物与位置，写明按何种干湿条件才浇、哪里取水、如何让盆底排水并清空托盘，以及“仍湿就不浇”。若品种有不同要求，单独标出，不让照料者猜。
4. **核对现场可行性。** 一起确认盆器、排水、取水和检查方法；必要时用一盆演示。可视、触觉或语音方式都行，只要照料者能独立辨认。重盆不安排单人搬举。
5. **确定意外和返回交接。** 约定照片或简短记录的时间、异常时暂停加水并联系的渠道、无法到场时谁能接替。回来后先听实际记录并检查盆土，不因为行程结束就立即给所有植物补水。

### 没有照料者时

先按品种资料确认能否在离家前做一次正常浇水与位置调整；任何毛细垫、滴灌等方法都应在有人时对**这盆植物和这个容器**试过，确认不会溢水或断水。若离家时长与植物需要无法匹配，明确告知风险并另找照料办法，不把未经测试的装置称作可靠替身。

### 完成与停止

完成时，愿意帮忙的人已确认时间、权限、每盆判断与异常联系；或已验证可行替代办法，并记录仍有的风险。若无人同意、进屋条件不合法或关键盆栽无法安全照料，交接尚未成立。

### 常见报错与补救

- **照料者只收到“每周浇一次”：** 补上按盆土与品种判断、排水和“不浇”的条件，再请对方复述。
- **出发前才装上新滴灌：** 不把首次无人值守当测试；改用已验证方案或请求可信协助。
- **到场者无法辨认哪盆：** 给每盆贴可读/可触标记并核对位置，不靠模糊昵称。
- **盆栽出现异常：** 请照料者记录观察并暂停猜测性补水或用药，联系你或可靠园艺服务。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通室内盆栽的自愿短期照料安排，不创建保姆雇佣、房屋进入或宠物照料权利。钥匙、门禁和隐私按真实关系及当地规则单独处理。RHS 的建议会随季节和植物改变；人类照料也不能保证每盆健康。副作用是一张需要人回应的清单和一次返家复核。

## English

Load this Skill when you will be away and the houseplants you normally check may have no caretaker. Finish with a **consenting person and plant-specific check and stop rules**. If no one takes over, identify the remaining risk instead of inventing a guarantee.

### Preparation and inputs

List the actual plants, departure and return dates, species guidance, normal moisture checks, drainage arrangements, and safe locations. Confirm the real authority for entry and keys; do not put door codes or passwords on a shared plant list. Arrange readable instructions or lifting help if labels or pots are inaccessible.

### Execution

1. **Screen actual need.** Use each plant, container, season, and length of absence to distinguish plants that may need an interim check from those that can wait until your return. Do not give every pot a “daily cup” rule. RHS holiday examples are conditional, not a species-wide safe interval.
2. **Ask for the helper's consent.** Explain possible visit times, which pots are included, whether the person can reach or move them safely, and how to contact you about a problem. Silence is not acceptance.
3. **Give instructions per pot.** Identify plant and location, the moisture condition for watering, where water comes from, how the pot drains, how to empty its saucer, and “do not water if still wet.” Mark any distinct species requirement rather than leaving it to guesswork.
4. **Check the real setup.** Confirm pots, water source, drainage, and inspection method together; demonstrate on one pot if useful. A visual, tactile, or spoken note is fine if the helper can use it independently. Do not assign solo lifting of a heavy pot.
5. **Set exceptions and return handoff.** Agree on a short observation or photo note, a route to contact you and pause speculative watering if something looks wrong, and a backup if a visit is missed. On return, read what actually happened and check the medium before adding water.

### When no helper is available

Check species guidance for a normal pre-departure watering or suitable temporary position. Test a capillary mat, drip device, or other alternative on **this plant and container while someone is present**, confirming it neither floods nor runs dry. If the absence exceeds what you can reasonably support, record that risk and seek another care route. An untested device is not an assured stand-in.

### Success and stop

A willing helper has confirmed timing, access, each pot's decision rule, and an exception contact; or a tested alternative is in place with remaining risk documented. Without consent, legitimate entry, or a safe care method for an essential pot, the handoff has not been completed.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The helper receives only “water weekly”:** Add plant- and medium-based checks, drainage, and a no-water condition, then ask the helper to read it back.
- **A new drip device appears just before leaving:** Do not make an unattended trip its first test; use a tested route or trusted help.
- **The helper cannot distinguish pots:** Add readable or tactile labels and confirm actual positions instead of ambiguous nicknames.
- **A plant looks abnormal:** Ask for observations and pause guessed watering or chemicals. Contact you or a reliable horticultural source.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a voluntary short-term arrangement for ordinary houseplants, not a right to enter property or a pet-care or employment agreement. Handle keys, access codes, and privacy under the actual relationship and local rules. RHS advice varies with plant and season; even a human helper cannot promise survival. The side effect is a list that needs a real reply and a return check.
