## 中文

当你已经在现场拍到一张**主体在明亮背景前过暗**的照片，想用本机允许的曝光补偿/锁定方式做一次有对照的重拍时，加载这份 Skill。结果是两张能指出“主体清楚度与背景高光取舍”的照片，不承诺两处同时完美。H277 的换机位减背景干扰是另一问题；这篇处理相机把哪一部分曝光为准。不可拍的人/地点先不拍，镜头不朝太阳直射，也不在道路中央调设置。

### 准备与输入

先看第一张确实是主体暗而背景亮，而非相机没对焦、镜头脏或屏幕亮度造成错觉；记录原曝光模式和补偿值。找本机说明书的曝光补偿或主体测光/锁定控制，手机则按其可用的点按曝光；如果自动模式不给控制，可调整机位/光线而不假装改了设置。主体若是人要获得同意；静物须自有或可拍。

### 执行

1. **定你要保留的主体细节。** 指出主体脸/表面哪一块必须可读，背景亮区哪些可接受变白；先在安全位置站稳，避免把太阳本体收入镜头或看向强光伤眼。
2. **只改变一处曝光决策。** 按本机说明把主体选为曝光参考或向使主体变亮的方向微调一次曝光补偿，不照搬 Canon/Nikon 特定按钮或 EV 数字。对焦仍落在主体上，保持相近机位与光线，拍第二张。
3. **现场并排看两张。** 在可用回放里看主体细节是否可读、背景是否过曝到丢信息、颜色是否变怪；记录原值、新值及变化。第二张主体仍暗可再小幅按本机规则试；过亮则退回或接受背景/主体不可兼得的取舍。
4. **复位临时设置并收尾。** 若曝光补偿是临时试验，按本机说明复位，防下一张普通照片被意外提亮；保存原照与试照为私人比较，不未经同意传播人像。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

两张相近场景照片有明确曝光操作记录，主体和背景取舍可看；或本机/现场不能做安全改动而停止。只把第一张后期拉亮却声称“现场曝光试拍”，不算本任务结果。

- **主体亮了但背景全白：** 记录取舍，试较小调整/换合法安全机位，不宣称无损恢复高光。
- **只有屏幕亮度变而文件没变：** 核本机真正的曝光控制，再拍看文件。
- **人不同意被拍：** 选获准静物，不偷拍。
- **相机模式无法补偿：** 使用其允许方法或停止本对照，不乱改多项菜单。
- **视力/操作不便：** 可访问回放、稳定位置和可信协助描述明暗，仍由你决定取舍。

### 假设与边界

逆光补偿会改变整张或测光区域的亮度，具体影响取决于相机模式和场景动态范围。Canon 示例的正方向是解释，不是所有机器的操作路径或万能数值。

## English

Load this Skill when a first on-site frame shows **a subject too dark against a bright background** and you want one controlled retake using this camera's exposure compensation or subject metering/lock. Finish with two frames that make the subject-detail versus highlight tradeoff visible; both may not be perfect. H277's new viewpoint for a distracting background is another issue. This changes what the camera exposes for. Do not photograph unconsenting people or places, aim into the sun, or adjust in a roadway.

### Preparation and inputs

Check that the first image really has a dark subject and bright background rather than failed focus, dirty lens, or only screen brightness. Note original exposure mode and compensation. Locate this model's exposure compensation or subject-meter/lock in its manual, or phone's available tap exposure. If automatic mode gives no control, a safer permitted position or light change is honest instead of claiming a menu change. Obtain consent for a person; use an owned or permitted object otherwise.

### Execution

1. **Name the subject detail to preserve.** Identify the face or object surface that must remain readable and what background highlights can be lost. Stand safely and keep the sun itself out of optics and direct eye view.
2. **Change one exposure decision.** Under the exact model, meter on the subject or make one small adjustment in the direction that brightens it, without copying a Canon/Nikon button or EV number. Keep focus on subject and similar position/light, then take frame two.
3. **Compare on site.** In playback, check subject readability, blown background highlights, and unexpected colour. Record original and trial control values and effect. If still dark, make another small model-guided trial; if too bright, revert or admit a tradeoff that the scene cannot fully avoid.
4. **Reset a temporary control.** Restore compensation under this model when done so the next ordinary picture is not unintentionally bright. Keep original and trial as private evidence; do not share a person's image without permission.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Two comparable scene frames have an explicit exposure-operation record and a visible subject/background tradeoff, or the model/scene prevents a safe change and you stop. Brightening only a first file later and calling it an on-site exposure trial is not this result.

- **Subject brightens but background clips white:** Record tradeoff, try a smaller change or permitted safer viewpoint, not a claim of recovered lost highlights.
- **Only display brightness changes:** Find real exposure control and inspect a new file.
- **Person does not consent:** Choose a permitted still object, not a candid substitute.
- **Mode has no compensation:** Use its approved path or stop instead of altering many unknown menus.
- **Sight or control is limited:** Accessible playback, stable position, and trusted brightness description can support your decision.

### Assumptions and limits

Backlight compensation changes the whole image or metered region depending on mode and dynamic range. A Canon positive-direction example explains a principle, not every camera's control path or a universal setting.
