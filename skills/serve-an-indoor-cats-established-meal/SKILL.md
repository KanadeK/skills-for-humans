## 中文

当你照料的普通室内猫到了**现行喂食方案**的下一餐时，加载这份 Skill。你执行已定的食物、份量、时间和饮水安排；不在这里选择新食谱、估计药量或判断猫应不应该减肥。E005 第一批的看护交接只把任务交给真人，这一篇完成眼前一次喂食。

### 准备与输入

确认这只猫是谁、主人/兽医既定的食物与本餐份量、实际包装的保存/开封说明，以及洁净食碗、水碗。多猫家庭要能辨认每只猫的碗和特殊方案；处方食物或药物必须按兽医原文，不自行替代。标签不清、食物变质、份量冲突或猫突然不吃不喝时，先停并联系负责人与兽医。

### 执行

1. **核本餐而非整袋。** 用可读的现行方案和包装说明确认产品、量具、份量、时间，查上次是否已经喂过。没人能确认时不要补一餐“保险量”。
2. **准备安全食水。** 用已经清洁并按材质适用的碗，依实际计划量取食物，放在猫能安全接触的位置；饮水保持新鲜可得，水碗与食碗、砂盆分开。不给人类剩菜、陌生零食或未经兽医同意的补充剂替代。
3. **让猫自主进食。** 放稳碗，避免其他动物抢食；不追着猫喂或强行掰嘴。观察是否开始吃喝，但不把“碗空了”当作足量或健康证明。
4. **按包装处理剩余。** 罐头/湿粮的留置与冷藏按实际产品标签和现行计划；剩食异常、猫持续拒食或饮水行为突然变化时及时联系兽医，不靠添加新食物实验诊断。
5. **记录本餐事实。** 记时间、产品和已给量，若由多人照料要告诉下一个接班者；碗的后续清洗交独立清洁流程。

### 成功条件

这一餐按已确认的方案实际端到正确的猫面前，饮水可得，食物余量有去向，下一照料者能核对是否已喂。若无可信份量/产品、疑似重复喂食或猫异常，先暂停并询问负责人或兽医，不能把猜量当完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两个人都以为自己该喂：** 先查上次记录和实际碗况，确认再喂。
- **湿粮开封时间不明：** 服从包装的保存边界；无法确认安全状态时不把它喂给猫。
- **猫没有按平时进食：** 记录时间与变化并及时联系兽医，不凭一餐自行换药或换食谱。
- **多猫抢碗：** 依既有分餐安排给每只猫可到达的独立位置，必要时向兽医或合格行为人员求助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只执行一只已照料室内猫**现行、非紧急**的一餐，不制定营养配方、药物或体重计划。视力受限可请人读包装与份量单位；手部操作困难可用清晰刻度量具或请可信的人按既定方案协助。现实副作用是碗稍后需要清洗，且记录要给接班人。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary indoor cat in your care is due its next meal under an **existing feeding plan**. Carry out the established food, quantity, time, and water arrangement. Do not invent a new diet, medicine dose, or weight target. E005 part 001's sitter handoff assigns a real person; this finishes one present serving.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the cat, current owner/veterinary food and meal quantity, the actual package's storage/opening rules, and clean food and water bowls. In a multi-cat home, distinguish bowls and any special plan. Prescription food or medication follows the veterinarian's exact written directions, never your substitution. Hold and contact the owner or vet for an unreadable label, spoiled food, conflicting dose, or sudden refusal of food or water.

### Execution

1. **Check this meal rather than the bag size.** Use the readable current plan and package to confirm product, measuring unit, amount, time, and whether the last shift already fed the cat. If nobody can confirm, do not give an “insurance” second meal.
2. **Set out safe food and water.** Use clean material-suitable bowls, measure the actual planned amount, and place it where the cat can reach safely. Keep fresh water available and separate water, food, and litter stations. Do not substitute human leftovers, unfamiliar treats, or an unapproved supplement.
3. **Let the cat eat voluntarily.** Set bowls stably and prevent another animal taking the meal. Do not chase or force the cat's mouth open. Notice whether eating and drinking begin, but an empty bowl alone is not proof of an adequate diet or health.
4. **Handle leftovers by the package.** Apply actual wet-food/open-can holding and refrigeration directions and the current plan. For unusual leftovers, persistent refusal, or sudden drinking changes, contact a veterinarian promptly rather than experimenting with new foods as diagnosis.
5. **Record what happened.** Note time, product, and amount served and tell the next caretaker when there is one. Later bowl washing belongs to the separate cleaning process.

### Success

The confirmed meal was physically served to the right cat, water is available, leftovers have a route, and the next caretaker can verify whether feeding happened. Hold and ask the owner/vet if product, quantity, previous serving, or cat condition is unclear; a guessed quantity is not completion.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Two people both think they are feeding:** Check the last actual record and bowl state before another serving.
- **Wet food's opening time is unknown:** Follow package storage limits; do not feed an item whose suitability cannot be confirmed.
- **The cat does not eat as usual:** Record the change and contact a veterinarian promptly; do not change medication or diet on one meal guess.
- **Cats take each other's bowls:** Use the established separated feeding arrangement and seek veterinary or qualified behavior advice if needed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This performs one **established, non-urgent** meal for an indoor cat already in your care. It does not formulate nutrition, medication, or a weight plan. Have someone read units if vision is limited, or use a clear measuring vessel/help if grip is difficult. Bowls will need cleaning and the record belongs with the next caretaker.
