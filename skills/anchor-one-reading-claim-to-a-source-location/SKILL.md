## 中文

当你读到一条以后可能要讨论或核对的说法，想在几天后仍能**找到作者到底写了什么及依据在哪**，用这份 Skill 做一条短注。结果是“我理解的说法—作者提供的依据/未提供依据—可回查位置”，不是把整段涂黄，也不是证明作者一定正确。初次选择读哪段归 H252，整合多条笔记归 H254。

### 准备与输入

先确认你有权阅读与个人标记该纸本、借阅本、网页或电子书；不能在借来的书上画就用自己的纸/本地便签。记录题名、作者或提供方、版次/发布日期（可见时）、章节/页码/网页小标题/音频时间码。文本若涉及医疗、法律、财务等高风险决定，本篇只帮你定位说法，不帮你判断专业真伪或行动依据。不抄录大段受版权保护的文字；用自己的话写摘要。

### 执行

1. **只选一条与你目的有关的说法。** 在本段找到作者真正主张什么，写“作者说____”的短句；若只是你的猜测，另写“我推测____”，不要合在一起。不是每个好句子都必须标记。
2. **查它在本段用什么支持。** 看作者是否给了例子、图表、数据、来源或理由，指出它在同页/邻段哪里。若没有可见支持，就写“本段未给依据”，不从网上随手找一个替它补上。
3. **留短而精确的回查位置。** 用本版页码加小标题、网页标题加段首短语、或音频章节加时间码；只存你有权使用的链接/书目信息。测试自己能否按线索回到原位置。电子页码会随字体变，单靠“第 14 页”可能失效。
4. **写成一行可核注释。** “说法____；文中依据/缺口____；位置____；我的疑问____（可空）。”必要时只圈关键词，不整页荧光。标记的是回查入口，不能代替读懂整段。
5. **回看一次是否歪曲。** 把你的短句与原文再比：作者是否有“可能/在此条件下”等限制？若漏了条件就补上。仍不清楚时标“解释未确认”，交给提问任务，不强写确定结论。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有一条可用同版原文复查的**作者说法、支持或明显缺口、位置**，并分清作者与自己的推断。只有彩色高亮、没写原因和位置，不满足本篇。

- **涂了整页看不出重点：** 只挑与当前目的最有关的一条，写你为何选它，其余先不标。
- **作者没有给本段支持：** 记录缺口和可能需查的前后段，不自作一段“证据”。
- **电子书换设备后页码变了：** 增加章节标题与段首短语或内容标识，不依赖动态页码。
- **只记了一句强断言，原文有前提：** 补“在____条件下/作者称可能”，不让自己的短注比原文更绝对。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

有的文本是叙事或诗歌，没有学术意义的“论点+证据”；若本段只是情节，可改记“我对这一场景的理解及让我这样想的一处文字”，仍分开原文和推断。纸本、网页、听书的定位方式不同，可用语音便签或可信协助，但来源位置要能由你回找。标记原文不自动证明它可信，更不等于你已能独立应用该知识。

## English

Use this Skill when you meet a statement you may later discuss or check and want to **find exactly what the author said and where the support appears** days later. Make one short note: your paraphrase, the author's support or lack of it, and a retrievable location. A page of highlighting does not prove understanding or that the author is correct. Choosing the first section belongs to H252; combining many notes belongs to H254.

### Preparation and inputs

Make sure you may read and personally mark this print, loaned, web, or digital copy. If you cannot mark a borrowed book, use your own paper or local note. Record title, author or provider, edition or date when visible, and chapter, page, web heading, or audio timestamp. For medical, legal, or financial decisions this Skill only locates claims; it does not judge professional truth or tell you to act. Paraphrase in your own words rather than copy long protected text.

### Execution

1. **Choose one statement tied to your purpose.** Find what the author actually asserts in this passage and write “the author says ____.” If it is your inference, label “I infer ____” separately. Not every striking sentence needs a marker.
2. **Find its support here.** See whether the author provides an example, figure, data, citation, or reason and locate it on this page or nearby. If no support is visible, write “none supplied in this section.” Do not fetch a random outside fact to supply the author's missing support.
3. **Leave a short exact locator.** Use this edition's page plus heading, a web heading plus opening phrase, or audio chapter plus timestamp. Store only the link or bibliographic detail you may use. Test that you can return to the spot. Dynamic e-book pages can change with font size; “page 14” alone may fail.
4. **Make a checkable one-line note.** “Claim ____; text support or gap ____; location ____; my question ____ (optional).” Circle a keyword if useful rather than colour the whole page. The mark is a return path, not a replacement for reading the section.
5. **Check for distortion.** Compare the note with the passage again: did the author say “may” or “under these conditions”? Add any missing limit. If meaning remains unclear, label “interpretation unconfirmed” for the question task rather than force certainty.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have a retrievable note for **one author claim, its textual support or visible gap, and location**, with your own inference clearly separate. Colour alone, without reason and source location, does not meet this task.

- **You highlighted a whole page and lost the point:** Choose the statement most relevant now and say why; leave the rest unmarked.
- **No support is in the section:** Note the gap and which neighbouring part may need checking rather than invent evidence.
- **Page numbers changed across devices:** Add heading, opening phrase, or content marker instead of relying on dynamic page alone.
- **Your note is absolute but the source is conditional:** Restore “if ____” or “the author suggests” so your note is not stronger than the text.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Stories and poems may lack an academic “claim plus evidence.” For a narrative scene, note your interpretation and one passage that led to it, still separating text from inference. Print, web, and audio use different locators; voice notes or trusted help are fine if you can revisit the source. Marking does not prove source reliability or independent application of the idea.
