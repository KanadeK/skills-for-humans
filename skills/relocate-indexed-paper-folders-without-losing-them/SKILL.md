## 中文

当你已知道文件夹在哪里，但准备换抽屉、收纳盒或住处，需要防止纸张搬完后索引仍指向旧位置时加载。你要让**每个实际移动的文件夹保持身份和隐私，只有它真的到新位置后才更新唯一索引，并验证可找回**。意外丢失而不知道位置时转文件搜索任务；这里不决定保存年限或丢弃原件。

### 准备与输入

拿出当前唯一索引，确认要移动的文件夹和保管权限，以及新位置有容量、合适访问控制和纸张能平放或得到支撑。只按你有权接触的文件夹操作；别为了搬动翻阅不属于自己的内页。大型、沉重或脆弱资料需合适协助，不要求你单独抬箱。

### 执行

1. **先记录可回溯的旧→新计划。** 列“文件夹名/码 → 旧位置 → 预定新位置 → 实际是否已移动”。给同名文件夹补可见区分码；搬前不要先把全部索引改成新地址。
2. **以可控的小批移动。** 文件夹外侧可辨，但私密内容不朝外暴露，原件不从夹中散落。一次拿得安全的数量，按同一批次到达新位置后才勾实见；搬不动、放不下或无权进入新位置就暂停那批。
3. **完成一批就改唯一索引。** 每个已实见到新位置的文件夹，将原索引位置改为真实新路径；临时放置则写“过渡处 + 接手人”，不能同时保留两个看似有效的地址。没移动的仍保持旧位置。
4. **检查有无遗漏。** 逐项对照搬动表、旧位置和新位置，确认每个文件夹有一个真实位置。碰到实际未见的文件夹，不把它记作“已搬”，交给专门搜索流程；另一人带走时记中性借出状态与接手人。
5. **从更新后的索引找回一件。** 选本批一个文件夹，由你或授权的人按索引走到新位置，取出再归位。找不到就核对这一批真实搬动记录并纠正；全部试验完成后清除过时的临时标记，而不是留下两套权威目录。

### 完成与停止

每个已知文件夹在新旧或过渡位置恰有一个实见状态，唯一索引指向真实位置，授权者可按它找回一件；纸张隐私和原件完整性保持。任何文件夹实际不见、所有权不明、目标位置不安全或无法访问时停止相关批次，记录未知并转合适负责人，不把计划移动写成已完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **索引先改了，箱子还没搬：** 改回旧实见位置，待实际搬到后再更新。
- **“暂放桌上”没写人和位置：** 补具体过渡位置与接手人，尽快核对归位。
- **两本目录都在更新：** 指定一份唯一权威索引，另一份标历史快照或收起，避免下次按旧地址找。
- **换了太小的新文件夹：** 保持原夹或换能保护纸张的容器，不折/裁原件来迁就新尺寸。
- **搬完少一夹：** 标最后实见处和哪批在搬，转错放纸张搜索，不在索引里虚构新位置。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理已知纸质文件夹的计划搬动，不重新判断内容的法律、税务或医疗保存要求。纸本索引、现有盒和简短迁移表足够，不需扫描到云端。视觉、行动或握持不便时可请授权可信者移动并读回标签，保管权限不随协助自动扩大。副作用是抽屉换家时索引也跟着搬，不会守着旧地址等人。

## English

Load this Skill when you know where paper folders are but plan to change drawers, boxes or homes without leaving an index pointed at the old place. You will **keep each moved folder identifiable and private, change the one index only after it physically reaches the new place, and verify retrieval**. An unexpectedly lost folder whose location is unknown belongs to the paper-search task. This does not set retention periods or discard originals.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the current single index, confirm the folders you may move and their actual custodian, and check that the new place has room, suitable access control and support for the papers. Handle only folders you are allowed to see; moving one does not authorise reading somebody else's pages. Seek suitable help for heavy, large or fragile material rather than lifting a box alone.

### Execution

1. **Write a traceable old-to-new plan first.** List “folder name/code → old place → intended new place → physically moved yet?” Give lookalike folder names distinct codes. Do not change the whole index to planned addresses before moving anything.
2. **Move controlled small batches.** Keep outer identities visible without exposing private contents, and keep originals inside their folders. Carry only a safely manageable batch. Mark it observed at the new spot only after arrival. Pause a batch if you cannot carry it, the new place is full or you lack permission to enter.
3. **Update the sole index per observed batch.** For each folder you actually see in its new place, replace its indexed location with that real route. A temporary stop gets “interim spot + holder,” not two plausible permanent addresses. Unmoved folders keep the old address.
4. **Check for gaps.** Compare moving list, old and new places so every folder has exactly one observed state. Do not mark an unseen folder “moved”; pass it to the dedicated search flow. Note a neutral loan-out and recipient when another person takes one.
5. **Retrieve from the updated index.** Have yourself or an authorised holder follow the new route to one folder in this batch, then return it. If that fails, correct it against the actual batch record. Remove obsolete interim markers after checks rather than keeping two authoritative catalogues.

### Success and stop

Each known folder has one observed old, new or interim location, and the sole index points to reality; an authorised person can retrieve and return one. Privacy and original condition stay intact. Pause an affected batch for a physically missing folder, uncertain ownership, unsafe destination or inaccessible place, recording the unknown with its real owner. Planned movement is not completed movement.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Index was changed before boxes moved:** Restore the old observed place and update only after actual arrival.
- **“On the table for now” has no location or owner:** Add the specific interim spot and holder, then verify the later destination.
- **Two catalogues are both being edited:** Name one as authoritative and mark the other a historical snapshot or put it away to prevent stale searches.
- **The new folder is too small:** Keep the original or choose a supportive container, rather than folding or cutting original pages.
- **One folder is absent after the move:** Mark its last observed place and batch and use the misplaced-paper search, not a fictional new index position.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a planned move of known paper folders, not a new legal, tax or medical retention decision. A paper index, existing boxes and a short transfer note suffice without cloud scanning. A trusted authorised person may carry and read back labels when mobility, sight or grip is limited; permission does not broaden merely because they help. The drawer can move without leaving the index waiting at its old address.
