## 中文

你已经有一处能认出形状的原创草图，需要把它转成后续可上色的清楚线稿时，用本流程处理这一处主体。结果是轮廓开合有意、交叠关系看得懂，草图仍在下层可修改。不要机械追每条草线；线稿应重新判断哪些边真正帮助读者理解形状。

### 准备与输入

保存工程，把草图层调低不透明度，在其上新建并命名线稿层。选已测试的画笔和适合正常观看尺寸的线宽；先指出主体最需要读清的外轮廓与前后遮挡处，别在细节里丢失整体。

### 执行

1. 先画外轮廓的大段，每段尽量有明确起止，不把颤抖的草线全部重描。
2. 再加能说明遮挡关系的内部线，避免同一交叉位置出现多条互相矛盾的边。
3. 隐藏草图，在实际观看尺寸判断主体是否仍可辨认；若线条断得不合适，只修关键缺口。
4. 恢复草图供后续编辑，切换线稿层可见性核它独立，保存 .kra。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

关闭草图后，主体仍由独立线稿清楚呈现，遮挡与主要轮廓可读。

- **线条太多：** 隐藏草图后删减不能帮助读形的杂线。
- **误画在草图：** 撤销重画到线稿层，确认两层可独立开关。
- **缩小看不清：** 调主要轮廓粗细，别只在放大状态判断。

### 假设与边界

只完成一个原创主体的线稿，不要求商业插画交付、人体解剖教学或临摹授权。线的省略和强调由画者决定。

## English

Use this when one original rough subject is ready for clear contours that later color can follow. Finish with intentional open and closed edges and understandable overlaps, while preserving the sketch below. Inking is a second judgment about shape, not a mechanical trace of every rough mark.

### Preparation and inputs

Save, lower sketch opacity and create a named ink layer above it. Select a tested brush and width readable at normal scale. Identify the outer silhouette and a few crucial overlap edges before getting lost in small details.

### Execution

1. Ink the main outer contour in deliberate segments with clear starts and ends instead of retracing every rough sketch stroke.
2. Add only internal edges that clarify overlap, avoiding contradictory crossings in the same area.
3. Hide the sketch and judge recognizability at intended size; repair only gaps that interrupt the form.
4. Restore sketch for later use, toggle ink visibility to confirm independence and save the .kra.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

With sketch hidden, the ink layer alone conveys the subject, its silhouette and main overlaps clearly.

- **Too many marks:** Remove lines that do not help form read when sketch is hidden.
- **Wrong layer:** Undo and redraw on ink layer, checking independent visibility.
- **Unreadable small:** Adjust primary contour width and inspect at normal scale.

### Assumptions and limits

This inks one original subject. It does not provide commercial illustration acceptance, anatomy instruction or copying rights. You decide which contours to omit or emphasize.
