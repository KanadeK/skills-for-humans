## 中文

当一件普通联网家用品展示了你想要的功能，但包装没有说清需要什么网络、手机、App、账号、网关或额外服务时，在付款前加载。你的结果是**这项功能的实际运行依赖**，不是当场注册账号、安装软件或证明它一定能接入你家设备。安防警报、儿童监护、医疗用途与家庭网络维修不在本 Skill 范围。

### 准备与输入

记下品牌、完整型号、硬件版本和销售地区。只挑一两项你真的要用的功能，例如“到家后用普通开关开灯”与“人在外面用手机控制灯”；同一商品的这两项可能走不同路径。列出你目前允许使用的条件，如是否愿意安装 App、建立账号或维持联网，不需要交出你的设备、密码或私人网络资料。

### 执行

1. **先固定准确功能。** 从制造商的当前产品页或说明书找到这款商品的功能名称，分别写下基本动作和宣传的联网动作。演示机已经由店员配置好，不代表开箱即能同样操作。
2. **逐项问前置条件。** 查或问清每项是否需要网络类型、手机系统、特定 App、账号、网关/配套设备，以及是否依赖仍在运营的远端服务。只写制造商对准确型号说明的条件，不用同系列另一个商品补空白。
3. **分清功能与服务层。** 如果某功能需要订阅或购买后另行开通，请记录究竟是哪一项、是否有不依赖该服务的基本用法。费用与取消条款交给该服务的正式页面核对；这里不预测将来的价格，也不替你接受条款。
4. **查离线和中断状态。** 查正式资料说明断网、没有手机或不用账号时，哪些普通功能仍可操作、哪些会停止。没有资料就写“离线行为未知”，不要把店里的在线演示当离线测试。
5. **形成一张依赖表。** 每行写“我需要的功能 → 必需条件 → 可选条件 → 对应来源 → 未知”。关键条件不明或你无法接受时，暂停购买、问制造商或找说明更清楚的商品。

### 成功与停止

你能说出想用的每项功能靠什么运行，以及没有其中一项条件时已知会怎样；或明确保留未知后停止。完成不要求店员借你账号，也不等于完成网络兼容性测试。

### 常见报错与补救

- **演示机正常，却看不到账号或网关：** 请店员指出它实际连接的设备与服务；不能确认就不推断家里也能运行。
- **只有“支持手机控制”：** 查准确 App、手机系统和账号要求；只知道品牌名称还不够。
- **服务页把试用与长期功能混写：** 分别记录试用时与试用后可用的功能；价格、续费和取消以当前正式条款为准。
- **中途断网或资料过期：** 保存型号和未核实项，稍后查制造商页面；购买前提仍未知就暂停。

### 假设、替代与副作用

联网条件和配套服务随地区、版本与时间变化；某店可演示的功能不代表每地都提供。若读不清、听不清或无法使用智能手机，可索取纸质/可读的正式说明或请可信同伴协助核对，但不为了提问而交出账号凭据。现实副作用是一张让“开箱即用”必须解释“用”的依赖清单。

## English

Load this Skill before paying for an ordinary connected household item when a feature you want is advertised but its network, phone, app, account, hub, or extra-service requirements are unclear. Your result is **the operating dependencies of that feature**. You do not register an account, install software, or prove compatibility with your home setup at the counter. Security alarms, child monitoring, medical uses, and home-network repair are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the brand, full model, hardware revision, and sales region. Pick one or two functions you actually want, such as “turn the light on with a wall switch at home” and “control it from my phone while away.” The same item may use different paths for each. State conditions you are willing to use, such as installing an app, creating an account, or keeping it online. You need not hand over your phone, password, or private network details.

### Execution

1. **Name the exact function.** Find this model's feature names on the maker's current product page or guide. Separate a basic action from the advertised connected action. A showroom unit is already configured; that is not proof of out-of-box operation.
2. **Ask for prerequisites one by one.** For each feature, find the required network type, phone system, app, account, hub or companion device, and any continuing remote service. Use exact-model information rather than filling gaps from another item in the series.
3. **Separate feature from service tier.** If a feature needs a subscription or later activation, record which feature it is and whether any basic use remains without that service. Check costs and cancellation terms on the current official service page; this Skill neither predicts future prices nor accepts terms for you.
4. **Check offline and interrupted use.** Read the formal description of what still works without internet, a phone, or an account. If none is given, write “offline behaviour unknown.” A connected store demo is not an offline test.
5. **Make a dependency table.** For each row write “wanted feature → required condition → optional condition → source → unknowns.” If an essential requirement remains unclear or unacceptable to you, defer, ask the maker, or choose a better documented item.

### Success and stop

You can name what each wanted function depends on and what is known to happen when a condition is absent; or you stop with the uncertainty explicit. Completion does not require borrowing a staff account or passing a home-network compatibility test.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The store demo works but its account or hub is invisible:** Ask staff what equipment and service it actually uses. Do not infer it will work the same way at home without that answer.
- **The card only says “phone control”:** Find the exact app, phone system, and account requirements. A brand name is not enough.
- **A service page mixes trial and continuing features:** Record the function during and after trial separately. Use current official terms for price, renewal, and cancellation.
- **The network drops or information is outdated:** Keep the model and unknowns, then use the maker's current page later. Pause a purchase that depends on the missing answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Connected requirements and services vary by region, revision, and time. A store's working demo does not promise the feature everywhere. Ask for a paper or accessible official guide, or have a trusted companion check it if print, speech, or phone use is difficult. Never give account credentials just to ask. The side effect is a dependency list that makes “works out of the box” explain what “works” means.
