## 中文

你在普通工作、学习或志愿协作中被邀请承担一项任务，但还不清楚**交什么、做到哪里、何时交、谁看结果**时，用这份 Skill。在说“我接了”之前，形成双方能核对的小型任务约定，或明确尚缺什么、此时尚未承诺。它不替你做专业行业判断、订立劳动法律关系，也不负责把已明确的任务拆成执行步骤；后者归 H243。

### 准备与输入

保留原消息或口头说明的真实措辞，写下已知目的、产物、时间和请求人，不把猜测填成要求。估计自己的可用时间与所需材料、权限、工具和协作人员。涉及私人资料、危险作业、医疗/金融/法律专业内容或你没有的权限时，先转给对应负责人确认。只问会影响你能否接、如何完成的关键问题，别让小事卡在过长表格上。

### 执行

1. **问产物与用途。** “做一份整理”可能是一页笔记、供他人编辑的表格或正式文件；请对方说清谁用、用来做什么、交付后应能看到什么。没有格式要求就不要自行发明复杂模板。
2. **划清本次边界和输入。** 问“这次只需〔范围〕，还是也包括〔另一项〕？” 整理会议决定不自动包括替每个人执行行动项。写清要等谁提供材料、参考版本和使用许可；未拿到的关键输入不能算作已具备。
3. **核对时间与验收者。** 写明截止的日历日期和当地时间，是草稿检查还是最终交付，等待输入可能影响进度时联系谁。问谁能判断“这样可用/还缺什么”，以及最关键的完成条件。请求方、实际使用者和验收者可能不同；未确认的“尽快”不是一个具体时点。
4. **用短话读回。** 例如：“我理解这次交一页会议决定摘要，周四 17:00 前发给林审阅；只记录决定与负责人，不代办后续任务。需要你先给会议记录。这样对吗？” 对方纠正时改记录；“差不多”若仍影响范围、时间或验收，就针对那一项继续问。
5. **给真实接受状态。** 条件清楚、与你的能力时间和授权相符，再明确接下；还缺关键输入或验收者时说“待〔条件〕确认后我才能决定/开始”，不把待澄清写成已承诺。无法按此版本完成就及时说明可做的真实范围或请对方另作安排。后续加新产物要重新核对，不让最初一句“可以”无限扩张。

### 成功条件

你能说出可观察的产物、范围外内容、关键输入、实际截止和验收者；请求方对同一版本有明确回复，你的状态是“接受此范围 / 有条件待确认 / 不接受此版本”。读回后无人答复仍为待确认。约定成立不等于产物完成，更不等于组织已验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“做个材料”没有用途或接收人：** 问谁用、为哪个决定、需要看到什么，别先做三个猜测版本。
- **“尽快”被你理解成今天：** 读回一个双方可核对的时点，包括所需输入何时到。
- **请求人说完成，真实使用者仍要另一种文件：** 找实际验收者核对本次可用标准，记录差异，不暗中扩范围。
- **关键文件或权限未给，记录却写“已开工”：** 标明缺口和负责人，先做独立准备，不宣称核心任务可交付。
- **一页摘要后来被加成完整演示稿：** 指出这是新增产物，再核对时限与是否接受。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于低风险普通协作，不裁定公司职责、报酬、知识产权、专业合规或志愿岗位资格。正式审批和验收权限按实际组织制度。不能长会时，用双方可读的短消息列关键条件；听力、视觉或语言不便可用可访问记录或可信协助回读。多花几分钟说清“交什么”，通常比做完才发现验收者想要另一份文件更省工。

## English

Use this Skill when you are offered an ordinary work, study, or volunteer task but do not know **what to deliver, where the scope ends, when it is due, or who reviews it**. Before saying yes, create a short version both sides can check, or name missing conditions and keep it unaccepted for now. This does not decide professional work or employment legal terms, or break an already clear task into execution steps; H243 does the latter.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the requester's actual words and note known purpose, output, time, and owner. Do not turn guesses into requirements. Check your real time and needed material, authority, tools, and people. For private records, hazardous work, medical/financial/legal professional content, or permissions you lack, involve the responsible party before accepting. Ask the few questions that affect whether and how you can do it, not a long questionnaire for a small task.

### Execution

1. **Ask for output and use.** “Make a summary” could mean a one-page note, an editable table, or a formal file. Ask who uses it, for what, and what observable state makes it usable. Do not invent a complex template without a format requirement.
2. **Mark this scope and its inputs.** Ask “Is this only [scope], or does it also include [other action]?” Summarizing decisions does not make you responsible for everyone's follow-up. Identify who supplies material, which reference version applies, and who may authorize its use. A missing critical input is not available yet.
3. **Check time and reviewer.** State calendar date and local time for the deadline, whether it is a draft check or final delivery, and whom to contact if awaited input affects timing. Ask who can say “usable” or “missing,” and what criterion matters most. Requester, user, and reviewer may differ. Unconfirmed “ASAP” is not a concrete time.
4. **Read back briefly.** For example: “I understand I will send a one-page meeting-decision summary for Lin to review by Thursday 17:00. It records decisions and owners but does not perform follow-up. I need the meeting notes from you first. Is that right?” Update from corrections. If “roughly” still affects scope, time, or review, ask about that item.
5. **State real acceptance.** Once clear and within your capacity, time, and authority, explicitly accept this scope. With a key input or reviewer missing, say “I can decide or start when [condition] is confirmed,” not “accepted.” If infeasible, promptly name only a narrower scope you can actually do or ask for another arrangement. New outputs require a new check; an early yes does not expand forever.

### Success

You can identify the observable output, exclusions, critical inputs, real deadline, and reviewer. The requester answered the same version, and your state is “accepted this scope / conditional and pending / declined this version.” No reply to a readback remains pending. Agreement is not finished output or organizational acceptance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Make a document” has no use or recipient:** Ask who uses it, for which decision, and what they need to see instead of producing three guessed versions.
- **“ASAP” becomes “today” in your head:** Read back a calendar time both sides can check, including when required input will arrive.
- **The requester likes it but the real user needs another format:** Check the actual reviewer's usable standard and record the difference without silently widening scope.
- **A key file or permission is missing but the task is marked started:** Name the gap and owner. Do independent preparation without claiming the deliverable is ready.
- **A one-page summary grows into a slide deck:** Name the new output, check timing, and decide whether to accept it separately.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for low-risk ordinary collaboration, not deciding job duties, pay, intellectual property, professional compliance, or volunteer role eligibility. Use real organizational approval rules where they apply. If a long meeting is inaccessible, use a short written readback. For hearing, sight, or language barriers, use an accessible record or trusted help. Minutes spent on “what to deliver” can cost less than finding after delivery that the reviewer wanted another file.
