## 中文

当你需要判断“熟了吗”“现在能吃吗”或“剩下的怎么放”时，加载这份 Skill。熟度和剩菜是连续任务：食物离开锅以后，时间仍在运行。

它不诊断食源性疾病，也不会凭颜色、气味或一句“应该没事”把未知温度史改成安全。

### 准备与输入

确认：

- 食物种类、最厚部位、是否带骨、烹饪方法；
- 你所在地区，以及当地权威对该食物的内部温度和静置时间要求；
- 食品温度计是否可用、读数在哪里取得；
- 已经烹饪多久、是否曾离开温控，以及大致时间线；
- 现在要立即吃、保温、冷却、储存还是复热；
- 冰箱、容器和复热设备是否可用。

若视觉、握持或读取温度计困难，可使用大字/语音/远程探针等可访问温度计，或让现场可信的人在正确位置读数。不要用手触摸或切开高温食物来补偿不可访问的显示。

### 执行：判断熟度

1. **找到对应的当地规则。** 不同食物与地区可能使用不同的内部温度和静置时间组合。香港食物安全中心给家庭彻底烹煮/翻热的一般核心温度基线是至少 75°C；在其他地区或特定食物上，使用当地食品安全机构的具体表。
2. **在正确位置测量。** 将干净探针插入最厚、最中心位置，避开骨头、锅壁和容器底。大块或形状不规则的食物测多个位置。
3. **读取后清洁探针。** 按温度计说明清洁，避免把生食污染带到熟食。
4. **没有温度计时降低结论强度。** 只使用当地权威明确支持的食物特定线索，例如鱼肉不透明并易分片、贝壳张开、蛋凝固或汤羹沸腾；这些线索有局限。肉的单一颜色和汁液不能替代适用的温度规则。
5. **不确定就继续安全加热并复查。** 无法建立可靠熟度证据时，不试吃生肉、禽、蛋或海鲜来调试。

### 执行：吃、冷却、储存或复热

1. **立即吃或按当地规则保温。** 不让易腐熟食在室温下无限等待“大家到齐”。
2. **决定保留多少。** 只保留你计划在安全期限内使用、且时间温度史清楚的部分。
3. **分成较小、较浅的干净容器。** 按当地指导尽快冷却和冷藏；不要把一大锅高温食物长时间留在台面等它自行完成后台任务。
4. **盖好并标记。** 写名称和日期；冰箱里的“昨天还是前天”不是有效时间戳。
5. **生熟分开。** 熟食和即食食品避免接触生食、脏器具和可能滴漏的包装。
6. **复热是再次彻底加热。** 使用当地权威要求的温度/时间，搅拌或翻动以减少冷点；不要只把表面加热。尽量避免同一份食物反复冷却和复热。
7. **超过适用时间、包装异常或温度史不可靠时丢弃。** 正常气味不会覆盖失败的时间线。

### 成功条件

你拥有与食物和地区相符的熟度证据，以及明确的立即食用、保温、冷却、冷藏/冷冻、复热或丢弃决定。保留的容器有日期，下一次不用询问冰箱的口供。

### 常见报错与补救

- **中心温度未达要求：** 继续受控加热，在最厚处重新测量；防止外部烧焦时可调整火力、覆盖或分小，但不降低安全目标。
- **外面过熟、里面不足：** 降低直接高热、分小或改用更均匀的方式继续加热，并复测中心。
- **温度计读数跳动：** 检查插入位置、电量和说明书；换位置测。无法信任设备时，延后食用或使用可靠替代。
- **忘记剩菜放了多久：** 不用气味补写日志。风险和历史无法确定时，按保守的当地指导丢弃。
- **出现烟、失控热油、明火、燃气异味或设备异常：** 停止烹饪，按当地消防/紧急指导离开危险并求助；只在安全时关热源，不维修燃气或电器。
- **食用后不适或过敏反应：** 停止食用并寻求适当医疗/紧急帮助。这份 Skill 不继续诊断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 假设普通家庭烹饪和可查到当地规则；海拔、设备、食物配方和法规会改变时间或温度。温度计比猜测更可靠，但也要正确放置和维护。没有安全冷藏空间时，不把“稍后再处理”当成储存方案。

现实副作用是多几个要清洗的浅容器和清晰标签。未来的你可能因此失去一次对剩菜年龄的哲学讨论。

## English

Load this Skill when you need to decide “is it done,” “can we eat now,” or “what happens to the rest.” Doneness and leftovers are one continuous job: time keeps running after food leaves the heat.

This Skill does not diagnose foodborne illness or turn colour, smell, or “probably fine” into a replacement for missing temperature history.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm:

- food type, thickest part, bone, and cooking method;
- your location and its official internal-temperature/rest-time rule for this food;
- whether a food thermometer is available and where the reading was taken;
- cooking time and any time outside temperature control;
- whether you will serve, hold, cool, store, or reheat now;
- refrigerator, container, and reheating equipment available.

If seeing, gripping, or reading a thermometer is difficult, use a large-display, talking, or remote-probe accessible model, or ask a trusted person present to read the correct location. Do not touch or cut dangerously hot food to compensate for an inaccessible display.

### Execution: check doneness

1. **Find the applicable local rule.** Foods and jurisdictions may use different internal temperature and rest-time combinations. Hong Kong CFS gives a household general baseline of at least 75°C core temperature for thorough cooking/reheating; elsewhere and for specific foods, use the local food-safety table.
2. **Measure in the right place.** Insert a clean probe into the thickest central part, avoiding bone, pan, and container surfaces. Check more than one location in large or irregular food.
3. **Clean the probe afterward.** Follow its instructions so raw-food contamination does not move onto cooked food.
4. **Reduce certainty without a thermometer.** Use only food-specific visible clues explicitly supported by your authority, such as opaque/flaking fish, opened shellfish, set egg, or boiling soup. These are limited. One colour or juice observation for meat does not replace an applicable temperature rule.
5. **When uncertain, keep cooking safely and recheck.** Do not taste raw meat, poultry, egg, or seafood as a debugging method.

### Execution: serve, cool, store, or reheat

1. **Serve now or hold under local rules.** Do not let perishable cooked food wait indefinitely at room temperature for everyone to arrive.
2. **Choose what to keep.** Retain only food with a known time/temperature history that you plan to use within the applicable safe limit.
3. **Use smaller, shallow, clean containers.** Cool and refrigerate promptly under local guidance instead of leaving a large hot pot on the counter to complete a background job.
4. **Cover and label.** Add identity and date. “Yesterday or the day before” is not a valid timestamp.
5. **Keep raw and cooked food separate.** Protect cooked and ready-to-eat food from raw food, dirty tools, and leaking packages.
6. **Reheat thoroughly, not cosmetically.** Follow local time/temperature requirements and stir or turn food to reduce cold spots. Avoid repeated cooling and reheating of the same portion.
7. **Discard when the applicable time passed, packaging failed, or temperature history is unreliable.** Normal smell cannot overwrite a failed timeline.

### Success

You have food- and location-appropriate doneness evidence plus one explicit serve, hold, cool, refrigerate/freeze, reheat, or discard decision. Kept containers have dates, so the next session does not require interviewing the refrigerator.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The centre is below the required result:** Continue controlled cooking and measure the thickest part again. Adjust heat, cover, or divide food to protect the outside without lowering the safety target.
- **Outside is overdone while the centre is not:** Reduce direct high heat, divide when safe, or use a more even method; then recheck the centre.
- **Thermometer readings jump:** Check placement, battery, and instructions, then measure elsewhere. Delay serving or use a reliable replacement if the tool cannot be trusted.
- **You do not know how long leftovers have been stored:** Smell cannot write the missing log. Discard under conservative local guidance when risk and history cannot be resolved.
- **Smoke, uncontrolled oil, flame, gas odour, or equipment failure:** Stop cooking and follow local fire/emergency guidance to leave danger and get help. Turn off heat only when safe; do not repair gas or electrical equipment.
- **Illness or allergic symptoms follow eating:** Stop eating and seek appropriate medical/emergency help. This Skill does not continue into diagnosis.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This assumes ordinary home cooking and access to local rules. Altitude, equipment, recipe, and regulation can change time or temperature. A thermometer is stronger evidence than guessing only when placed and maintained correctly. Without safe cold storage, “deal with it later” is not a storage plan.

Known side effects include shallow containers to wash and labels that remove a future philosophical debate about leftover age.
