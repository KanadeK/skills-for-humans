## 中文

当一台**说明书可查、已正确上线下线的家用缝纫机**准备好，你想在自有非穿戴废布上试一条直线时，加载这份 Skill。结果是机器确实送布并形成一小段可从正反面查看的直线，或因异常停机查因；这不是修衣服或确认针线张力对所有布都合适。手指不靠近针，脚不误踩踏板；声音异常、针碰压脚或电线破损时停用。

### 准备与输入

按本机手册确认机针、直线针迹、针位、压脚与普通布料/线相容，选两层或一层稳定废布（按你要试的真实材料）。用可移导线标短直线，线尾按本机要求放压脚后，针板盖装妥。开机前用本机允许的手轮/针位检查针不会碰压脚，脚踏可控；不把手伸到压脚下调整已启动设备。

### 执行

1. **将废布放对位置。** 抬压脚并放布，让起点对齐针位，线尾在后；按本机说明放下压脚、检查布能被送布牙带动。压脚没降就不踩踏板。
2. **以低速试缝一小段。** 按本机控制缓慢走针，双手只在针前方远处轻导布的方向，不推拉布强迫针弯；跟着导线走，出现跳针、线团或异常阻力立刻停机。
3. **按本机步骤停针取布。** 让针处安全位置，抬压脚，取出废布并按机型切线，不向外猛拽让针弯；关机或回到安全待机状态。不要为了“直”连续空踩超出废布边缘。
4. **看正反两面和机器。** 检查线迹是否连续、废布有没有皱或针孔过大、背面是否有线团、针脚是否松；记使用的布、线、针和机器设置。问题先走穿线/张力/缠线排查，不拿此试样担保正式成品。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

废布有一段可辨连续直线、正反都可检查，机器停在安全状态；或出现明确异常并停机。只装布未动针、或出现线团仍往前踩，不算成功试缝。

- **布不向前走：** 停并查压脚是否按本机说明放下、送布设置正确，不硬拉。
- **底部突然缠线：** 立刻停，按单独缠线恢复流程处理，不继续踩。
- **针碰压脚或断针：** 停机远离针区并求本机厂商/维修，不徒手找碎针继续。
- **线迹歪/皱：** 用同类废布再试导布与兼容针线，别用手强拽针脚。
- **视觉/行动不便：** 大字号本机图、缓速控制、可信现场协助看正反，但只有能安全控制启动/停止才自己操作。

### 假设与边界

各机型启动、停车、穿线和默认针迹参数不同，真实手册优先。废布直线是第一次运行证据，不等于正式缝口强度或衣物维修质量。

## English

Load this Skill when a **sound home sewing machine with its manual and correctly routed upper/lower threads** is ready for one straight line on owned non-wearable scrap. Finish with a short real stitch line whose front and reverse can be inspected, or stop for a named fault. This is neither clothing repair nor proof that thread tension suits every fabric. Keep fingers away from needle and foot off pedal by default. Stop for abnormal noise, needle hitting foot, or damaged power cord.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the exact manual to check sound needle, straight stitch, needle position, presser foot, and compatible ordinary cloth/thread. Choose stable scrap similar to your intended material and lightly mark a short line. Arrange tails behind foot by this model's rule and close plate cover. Before power motion, use the model-approved handwheel/needle-position check to ensure needle clears foot; control the pedal. Never reach under a running foot.

### Execution

1. **Place scrap correctly.** Raise foot, place fabric with its start point at the needle, keep tails back, lower foot by the manual, and verify feed dogs can move fabric. Do not press pedal with the foot raised.
2. **Sew a short line slowly.** Start by this machine's control at low speed. Hands gently guide from a distance, without pulling fabric to force a needle bend. Follow the mark and stop immediately for skipped stitches, thread nest, or unusual resistance.
3. **Stop and remove by model steps.** Place needle safely, raise foot, remove scrap, and cut thread by the machine's method without yanking against the needle. Switch off or return to safe idle. Do not run off the edge in pursuit of a straighter line.
4. **Inspect both sides and machine.** Check continuity, puckering, oversized holes, reverse nesting, and looseness. Record fabric, yarn/thread, needle, and settings. Route problems to threading/tension/nesting checks instead of certifying a final product from this one trial.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Scrap has a recognisable continuous straight seam viewable on both sides and the machine is safe-idle, or a clear fault stopped it. Placing cloth without moving the needle, or continuing despite a thread nest, is not a successful test.

- **Fabric does not feed:** Stop and check lowered foot and model feed setting, without pulling by force.
- **Underside nests suddenly:** Stop and use the separate nesting recovery instead of continuing to press pedal.
- **Needle hits foot or breaks:** Stop and use maker or repair help, not a bare-hand shard search and resumed motion.
- **Line wanders or puckers:** Trial guidance and compatible needle/thread on similar scrap again, without tugging stitched fabric.
- **Sight or movement is limited:** Large model diagrams, slow control, and trusted front/reverse inspection can help, but operate only if you can safely start and stop it.

### Assumptions and limits

Machine start/stop, threading, and defaults differ. Its actual manual is authoritative. A straight scrap line is first runtime evidence, not a final seam-strength or garment-repair acceptance test.
