## 中文

当你想用**一张自己的普通纸**完成一个不靠剪刀、刀片或胶水、能在桌上自行站住的小纸结构时，用这份 Skill 折出三面左右的手风琴形。结果是一个可放文字/画面的轻量展示物，不是书挡、灯罩、儿童安全玩具或承重部件。纸材若有锋利硬边、金属装饰或可疑涂层，换普通干净纸。

### 准备与输入

选一张你有权使用的干净纸，厚度足以保持轻微折痕、但手能安全折；桌面要干燥、平稳，有足够空间展开。决定它只作桌面展示或练习，不放在火源、湿处或会有人碰倒的通道。看纸纹/印刷方向，若上面已有字画，先确定哪一面要朝外；不复制他人付费模板或受保护角色图样。手部或视觉不便时用大一些的纸、浅色标记或可信协助。

### 执行

1. **试出大致相等的三个面。** 将纸横放桌上，沿长边轻标两个分段位置，先不压死折痕；不需精确数学三等分，只要三面宽度接近且底边都能落桌。若纸太软站不稳，换稍厚但仍能折的纸，不用危险工具补强。
2. **交替折出两条主线。** 沿第一条线把一面向前折，沿第二条线把下一面向后折，让纸从侧面看成展开的 Z/手风琴。用手掌或平滑安全工具在桌上轻压折线，不靠锋利尺边、刀口或使纸起裂的反复强折。
3. **打开并调底座。** 把三面稍微展开，让每个接触桌面的底边都着地，前后转角不过度收紧。离手观察它是否能自行站住；先调整折角和底边间距，再考虑重折，不把倾倒归罪于“你没有艺术天赋”。
4. **给结构做一次小测试。** 在桌面轻推旁边的空气或从不同方向看，确认它不会在普通静止状态下立刻倒下；若一侧倾斜，重新对齐底边或换纸。不要把手机、书或液体放上去试承重；这份结构没有这种验收项目。
5. **完成或留一个可改点。** 自己的简短文字/图案可放在外侧面，保持站立；若纸已破、折痕失控或平面始终不稳，保存一次观察结果，换一张纸再试或停下，不用胶带堆成一团假装折纸成功。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

一张纸形成可见交替折线，底边在稳定桌面可自行站住，且没有刀胶风险或未经授权的作品复制；站不住时有明确的角度、底边或纸材调整结果。

- **三面全部朝同一方向折：** 展开第二条主线，改成与第一条相反的折向，再测试 Z 形。
- **轻风一吹就倒：** 调宽底部接触间距或换稍厚纸，不加重物压住。
- **纸边裂开：** 停止在同处猛压，换纸并减少折叠次数。
- **已有图案折在里面看不见：** 先展平确认朝外面，再顺原折线重合，不撕纸强翻面。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理低风险纸张小制作，不教复杂机械纸艺、锋利裁切、加热或化学固化。纸尺寸、纹理、湿度和手部动作不同，展示效果以实物能自立为准。可把纸固定在桌面边缘以便单手轻折，但不要让胶接成为必须；视力不便可用触觉浅折线和可信协助。作品易被水与重物损坏，收工时放在不会被压的位置。

## English

Use this Skill when you want **one sheet of your own ordinary paper** to become a small tabletop form that stands by itself without scissors, a blade, or glue. Fold roughly three accordion panels. It is a light display or practice object, not a bookend, lampshade, child-safety toy, or load-bearing part. Use plain clean paper instead of a sharp-edged, metal-decorated, or suspiciously coated sheet.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a clean sheet you may use. It should hold a light crease but still fold safely by hand. Work on a dry steady table with room to open the sheet. This is display or practice only, away from fire, water, or a path where people knock things over. If text or drawing already exists, choose which side faces out. Do not copy a paid template or protected character art. Use a larger sheet, high-contrast marks, or trusted help for reach or sight needs.

### Execution

1. **Find three roughly similar panels.** Place the sheet sideways and lightly mark two fold positions along its long dimension without pressing a final crease. Exact mathematical thirds are unnecessary, but panel widths should be close and bottom edges should reach the table. If paper is too limp, choose slightly firmer foldable paper instead of adding a hazardous reinforcement tool.
2. **Alternate two main folds.** Fold the first panel forward at one line and the next panel back at the second so the opened sheet looks like a Z or accordion from the side. Press creases gently against the table with your hand or a smooth safe tool, not a knife or sharp ruler edge or repeated force that tears the sheet.
3. **Open and tune the base.** Spread the panels enough for all table-contact edges to meet the surface. Avoid closing both angles tightly. Let go and see whether it stands. Adjust fold angles and base spacing before recreasing; a fall is structural feedback, not a verdict on your talent.
4. **Give it a small physical check.** Look from another side or make a gentle movement nearby and see whether it stands in ordinary still conditions. If one side leans, realign base edges or try another sheet. Do not test by placing a phone, book, or drink on it; load bearing is outside the contract.
5. **Finish or note one next adjustment.** Put your own short text or art on outer panels if wanted. For torn paper, uncontrolled creases, or a form that will not stand, keep one observation, try a fresh sheet, or stop. A mound of tape is not evidence that folding succeeded.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

One sheet has visible alternating folds and stands on its bottom edges on a stable table, without blades, glue, or unlicensed imagery. If it fails, you have an actual angle, base, or paper adjustment rather than a mystery.

- **Both folds face the same way:** Open the second main fold and reverse its direction, then check the Z shape.
- **A light air movement topples it:** Widen base spacing or use slightly firmer paper, not a weight on top.
- **An edge tears:** Stop forcing the same crease. Use new paper and fewer hard folds.
- **Existing art faces inward:** Open gently, choose the outward face, and reuse the crease direction rather than rip the sheet around.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a low-risk small paper make, not advanced paper mechanics, sharp cutting, heat, or chemical curing. Size, grain, humidity, and hands differ; judge the real object standing. A single hand can use the table edge for light folding with safe assistance, without making adhesive required. Use tactile light creases or trusted help for limited vision. Water and weight damage the result; leave it where it will not be crushed.
