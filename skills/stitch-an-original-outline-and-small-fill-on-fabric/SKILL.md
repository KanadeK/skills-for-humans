## 中文

当你已有一块**自有绷布及原创的小闭合图形**，想实际用回针勾边、在小区域用缎面针填色时，加载这份 Skill。结果是正面可认出的轮廓与一小块被线覆盖的填色区，背面还有待最终整理的线尾；两种针法共同完成一个图，不分标题凑数。这里不是在衣服上补缝或复制受保护图案。针尖和剪刀远离儿童、宠物及眼睛。

### 准备与输入

核布仍均匀绷紧、导线没有被水/摩擦模糊，选与布重量相容的绣线和完整针。线段不宜长到总缠结；按绣线厂商建议分股，不把所有六股当任何布都能穿。可从背面穿过已有起始针脚固定线尾，避免正面突起大结。坐姿稳定，光线足；扎到手指或手疼就停。

### 执行

1. **先稳住起线。** 穿针并按材料说明在背面固定短尾，把针从轮廓起点带到正面；持针数清楚，未用针收盒。线拉到贴布即可，不能把绷布拉皱。
2. **用回针逐段勾边。** 先向前落下一小针并从前方下一段导线处出针，再向后落回上一针终点；沿导线重复短而相近的段，使正面成连续线。转角用更短段，不把针跨到图案另一侧拉出长浮线。
3. **在小形内做缎面填色。** 改用合用线色时先把旧尾在背面固定，再用新线在填区一侧出针、另一侧落针，平行相邻、覆盖小范围。针目不挤到重叠隆起，也不跨太大区域造成易勾住的长浮线；布皱或针目松时先停调整。
4. **检查两面与停点。** 正面能指出勾边与填色，背面无过长松线或缠成团；当前线尾先按说明安全固定，记下哪里还需最终整理。临时放下时针进针盒而非留在椅面或未看护的布上。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

原创小图的轮廓连续、填色覆盖一个可辨小区，布未被针距拉皱，针与待藏尾可清点。只在布上画导线、没实际下针，不算完成。

- **回针轮廓有空隙：** 查上一针终点与新针回落点，退一小段补齐，不在正面用线打结盖住。
- **填色露大片布或线跨太长：** 改小填区/增加相邻针，不把松浮线硬拉紧。
- **布开始起皱：** 停针放松线张力、均匀调整绷圈，不继续加密遮皱。
- **背线打结或针刺手：** 暂停、移针到针盒并处理线结；受伤时先退出练习。
- **视力/握持不便：** 大图案、对比线、合用针眼和可信协助穿线，由你核正背两面。

### 假设与边界

回针适合小轮廓，缎面针适合较小填区，绣线股数与布的相容性须按实际材料确定。此样不承担衣物磨损或结构拉力，也不声称每个针法单独成为一项生活任务。

## English

Load this Skill when **owned fabric is hooped with your own small closed design** and you want to stitch a back-stitch outline plus one little satin-filled area. Finish with a recognisable stitched edge and covered small fill on the front, while final reverse-side finishing remains. Both stitch methods serve one image rather than two inflated titles. This is not clothing mending or copied protected art. Keep needle and scissors away from children, pets, and eyes.

### Preparation and inputs

Check fabric remains evenly taut and guide visible. Choose floss and a sound needle compatible with its fabric weight. Keep lengths short enough to avoid tangles and separate strands as the floss maker advises; all six strands are not suitable for every cloth. Secure a starting tail on the reverse under early stitches rather than a large bump on the front. Sit steadily with enough light, stopping for finger puncture or hand pain.

### Execution

1. **Secure starting thread.** Thread the needle and hold a short tail on the back under material directions. Bring the needle up at the outline start. Account for needles and case unused ones. Pull only until thread rests on fabric, not enough to pucker it.
2. **Back-stitch the outline in sections.** Take a small stitch forward, come up one short segment ahead, then take the needle back down at the previous stitch endpoint. Repeat along the guide to make a continuous front line. Shorten steps at corners and avoid long floats across the motif's reverse.
3. **Satin-fill one small shape.** Secure old colour on the back before introducing compatible new floss. Bring needle up at one edge of the fill and down at the opposite edge in adjacent parallel stitches over a small span. Avoid raised overcrowding and long snagging floats. Pause if cloth puckers or stitches slacken.
4. **Check both sides and pause safely.** Point to a visible front outline and fill and inspect reverse for long loose strands or tangles. Secure the current tail under directions and note final cleanup still due. Put needle in its case whenever leaving, never on a chair or unattended cloth.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The original motif has a continuous stitched outline, one visibly filled small area, no obvious puckering, and accounted-for needle and tails. Guide lines without actual stitches are unfinished.

- **Back-stitch line has gaps:** Find the prior endpoint and next return point, undo a short segment, and close it without a front knot.
- **Fill exposes a wide gap or long float:** Use a smaller fill region or closer adjacent stitches, not a hard pull on loose floats.
- **Fabric puckers:** Stop, ease thread tension, and adjust hoop evenly instead of stitching over wrinkles.
- **Reverse tangles or needle punctures hand:** Pause, case the needle, and address the tangle; leave the activity if injured.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Larger art, contrast floss, compatible needle eye, and trusted threading help can support your front/reverse inspection.

### Assumptions and limits

Back stitch suits small outlines and satin stitch small fills. Thread strands and fabric compatibility depend on actual materials. This sample is not rated for garment wear or structural load, and each stitch need not become its own life task.
