## 中文

当你的纸档索引说某张普通纸在某个文件夹，但你实际打开后没看见时加载。你要在**自己有权查看的档案和已知借出/搬动链内做一轮有限搜索，找到实物或留下可复查的“未定位”状态**。这里不翻他人私人物品、不补办法律/身份文件、不推断法定期限，也不当作全屋小物寻宝。

### 准备与输入

先把要找的纸用中性线索界定：来源/用途、大致日期、文件夹码或一两项不泄露隐私的可见特征。确认索引的记录位置、最后一次实见/借出信息以及哪些夹、盒和人你有权限询问。若纸可能含个人资料，搜索记录只写中性名称和位置，不抄内容到公开表。

### 执行

1. **先查索引路径本身。** 按索引走到对应盒与夹，看文件夹名称、码和实际放置是否仍一致；若整夹都不在，转查借出或计划搬动记录，不把“夹不在”写成“纸没了”。
2. **在目标夹内有序查。** 依已有日期、标签或页序轻翻你有权查看的纸；检查夹内前后页、夹层和相连的一组，防止一张纸被贴在别页后或多页套在一起。脆纸不强拆、不把整个夹倒在地上。
3. **按有依据的近邻扩一圈。** 目标夹没有时，看同盒相邻夹、同用途的近似标签，以及已记录的上次借出/复印/携带位置。每个已查位置打勾，问实际借用者是否仍持有；没有许可的夹/房间不进入。
4. **命中时先验证再修索引。** 对照目标的来源、日期或可辨特征，确认是这张而不是同名纸；得到有权者同意后归到真实文件夹。只有实见落位后才把唯一索引改到实际位置；若暂借他人，记接手者而不虚写在位。
5. **没命中就结束这一轮。** 写“最后实见处 → 已查夹/盒 → 问过谁与答复 → 下次可查处”，状态仍为未定位。确有近期普通事项要用它时，向实际接收方问可否提供其它材料或改安排；这不等于补办或法律替代资格已成立。

### 完成与停止

目标纸被实际辨认并回到可索引位置，或一轮有边界的档案搜索留有未定位记录和下一步；索引不因希望而指向虚构的新位置。涉及他人隐私/所有权、易损档案、身份原件或超出你权限的地点时停住相关部分，交有权者或专业渠道。

### 常见报错与补救

- **同名纸很多，随便拿一张：** 用来源、日期、页数等非公开线索再核对，未确认就不认领。
- **目标夹不在，直接认定原件丢了：** 先看借出/搬动状态，区分“文件夹不在”与“页不在”。
- **一直重复翻同一盒：** 用已查位置清单划掉，下一轮只查有新线索的地方。
- **找到纸但没有改索引：** 在实见归位后改唯一位置，再从索引重试一次。
- **有人说“我好像拿过”：** 记录可能持有人与待确认，不把记忆当实际接收或归还。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只做普通纸档范围内的有限查找。档案规模、隐私等级、视觉和行动条件不同；可以让有权限且可信的人读回夹名与实见位置，但不扩大到其私人物品。机构档案的专业半径或搜索制度不套用家庭。副作用是“肯定在那儿”会让位给最后实见和已查事实。

## English

Load this Skill when your paper index says an ordinary sheet belongs in a folder but you do not actually see it there. Make **one bounded search in the archive you may inspect and the known loan or move chain, ending with the real paper or a revisit-able “unlocated” state**. This is not a search of other people's private places, legal or identity-document replacement, a statutory deadline judgment or a whole-home lost-object sweep.

### Preparation and inputs

Define the target by neutral clues: source or purpose, rough date, folder code and a few visible features that do not expose its contents. Check the indexed location, last physically seen or loaned event and which folders, boxes and people you may ask. For personal material, put neutral names and places in the log, not full content on a public sheet.

### Execution

1. **Check the index route first.** Follow it to the box and folder and compare real label and position. If the entire folder is absent, look at loan or planned-move notes next. “Folder not here” is not yet “page lost.”
2. **Search the target folder systematically.** Using existing date, label or order, turn authorised pages carefully and check adjacent pages, inner pockets and grouped sheets that could stick together. Do not pull brittle paper apart or dump the folder on the floor.
3. **Expand one evidenced circle.** If absent, check neighbouring folders in the same box, similar-purpose labels and the recorded last loan, copy or carrying place. Tick off each area searched and ask actual borrowers whether they still hold it. Do not enter folders or rooms you lack permission to inspect.
4. **Verify before repairing the index.** Compare source, date or features so a same-named sheet is not misidentified. With the right holder's approval, return it to a real folder. Update the sole index only after seeing that physical placement; if another person still holds it, record the holder rather than claiming it is in storage.
5. **End an unsuccessful pass.** Record “last observed place → folders or boxes checked → people asked and answers → next place with a real lead.” Keep status unlocated. If an ordinary near-term task needs it, ask the receiving body whether another material or arrangement is possible; that does not establish a legal replacement right.

### Success and stop

The target was physically identified and restored to an indexed place, or one bounded archive search ended with an unlocated log and next action. Do not move the index to an imagined place. Pause the relevant part for another person's privacy or ownership, fragile archives, an identity original or an area beyond your authority, and ask the right holder or specialist.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Several papers share a name and one is picked at random:** Compare source, date, page count or other private clues. Do not claim an unconfirmed match.
- **The whole folder is absent, so the original is declared lost:** Check loan or move state to separate missing folder from missing page.
- **The same box is searched repeatedly:** Tick it off and use a new lead before returning.
- **The paper is found but the index is stale:** Update after physical return, then test lookup from the index once.
- **Someone says “I might have taken it”:** Record possible holder and await confirmation. Memory is not physical receipt or return.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a bounded search within an ordinary paper archive. Scale, privacy, sight and mobility differ; a permitted trusted person can read back folder labels and observed locations without searching their own private goods for you. Institutional archive radii and formal search protocols are not imposed on homes. “It must be there” yields to last-observed and checked facts.
