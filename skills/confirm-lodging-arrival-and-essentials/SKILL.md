## 中文

已经选定或预订普通住宿，但出发前还不清楚**怎么进入、从入口到房间是否适合你、必需物品是否真的提供**时，用这份 Skill。产物是一张本次住宿的已确认/未确认/不可用清单和未确认事项的处理点。它不替你办理入住、决定带什么行李，也不处理到店后发现缺物的交涉。

### 准备与输入

打开**这一次**预订的住宿名称、地址、日期、房型和消息记录；核对预订状态，别把“已发送请求”当“已确认住宿”。写下预计到达的当地时间，以及无法妥协的进入或使用需求：例如晚到时如何进入、从入口到房间是否有台阶、能否使用电梯、是否有你需要的浴室布局或文字/听觉提示。再列出只会影响便利的项目，如吹风机、毛巾、洗衣设施、早餐或停车。把“必须满足”和“有更好”分开，避免为了吹风机的问题淹没真正不能进入房间的问题。

### 执行

1. **查这次预订，不查品牌印象。** 在实际住宿方或预订平台的本次预订页核对地址、房型、入住日期、入住时段、前台/自助进入方式和你要用的设施。品牌可能提供某项服务，但地点、房型和时间会改变可用性；“网页写有泳池”也不表示本次一定开放。
2. **沿真实进入路径核对。** 从抵达点、入口、接待或取钥匙处，到电梯/楼梯、房门及必用的卫生间或公共设施，逐段问自己能否使用。若某一段关系到你能否入住，用具体问题询问住宿方，例如“从街道到这间房是否全程无台阶？这间房是无门槛淋浴还是浴缸？”不要只问“无障碍吗”；一个通用标签不能回答你的具体需求。需要沟通辅助时，也问清住宿方提供何种实际方式。
3. **核实到达与进入条件。** 对照预计到达时间查当次的入住时段、迟到时的联系办法、取钥匙/接待位置以及详细指引何时会发出。自助入住、手机钥匙或提前选房若依赖账号、设备或资格，先确认这次预订是否可用，并问有没有你能使用的替代交接方式。不要把门禁码、完整证件或付款资料复制到共享行程表；只记录从官方渠道找回指引的位置。
4. **逐项确认真正要用的提供物。** 对每个必需项目写“本次房型及日期已确认 / 仍待住宿方答复 / 明确不提供”，并写清使用地点、时段及是否需提前申请。对可选便利也可记录，但不要把它们冒充已承诺的房内物品。若平台描述与住宿方回复不同，保存两处原文和日期，请住宿方澄清具体房型与时间。
5. **给未知项设出发前的处理点。** 用本次预订的官方消息或住宿方公布的联系方式发一条短消息，列出不可妥协的具体问题、到达时间和需要答复的最晚时点。没有答复时，标“未确认”，按你能否承担失去该条件来决定继续等、另找可行安排或调整计划；不要把未回复当默认同意。临近出发再查一次最新指引；只有实际确认的变化才改状态。

### 成功条件

你能说出本次预订从到达点到房间的实际进入办法、关键路径和必需设施的状态；每个未确认的必需条件都有询问渠道、答复时点和可行的下一步。若一个不可妥协的条件仍未确认或明确不可用，停止把这处住宿标作“可用”，先解决住宿安排。记录完成不等于房间已实地验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **同品牌另一家店有该设施：** 回到这家住宿、这类房型和本次日期核对；品牌介绍不是当次承诺。
- **写着“无障碍”，但不清楚入口或浴室：** 按实际路径和所需设备提具体问题；未得到可用信息就保留未确认。
- **到达时间晚于接待时段：** 在出发前联系实际住宿方确认交接方法；别把通用自助入住说明当你的预订已启用。
- **详细进入指引尚未发出：** 查平台公布的发送时间及官方消息；到该时点仍没有就从平台内联系住宿方，并准备可用替代安排，不向陌生链接提交资料。
- **回复只说“应该有”：** 追问本次房型、日期、位置和可用时段；若这项决定能否入住，维持未确认状态。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于普通酒店、旅舍和短租的行前核实；每个服务的规则要看你的实际预订。没有电话或听力不便时，优先用住宿方可用的文字渠道；看不清页面时可用可访问格式或可信协助，但无需分享完整门禁和付款信息。不同地区的身份核验、费用及入住规则不能从示例平台外推。你可能需要为了一个尚未兑现的必要条件改变行程；这是比抵达门口才发现无法进入更早、更可控的发现。

## English

Use this Skill when you have chosen or reserved ordinary lodging but, before leaving, still need to know **how to get in, whether the route and room work for you, and whether essential items are actually provided**. Finish with a confirmed / unanswered / unavailable list for this stay and a decision point for unanswered essentials. This does not perform check-in, decide what to pack, or handle an item found missing after arrival.

### Preparation and inputs

Open **this reservation's** property name, address, dates, room type, and messages. Check that the reservation is confirmed; a sent request is not an accepted stay. Note your expected local arrival time and any non-negotiable access or use needs: entry after a late arrival, a step-free route from entrance to room, a usable lift, a specific bathroom layout, or a communication format you can use. Then list conveniences such as a hair dryer, towels, laundry, breakfast, or parking. Separate essentials from preferences so a hair dryer does not bury the question of whether you can enter the room.

### Execution

1. **Check this booking, not the brand's reputation.** In the actual property's page or your booking platform's reservation, check the address, room type, dates, arrival window, staffed or self-service entry, and facilities you plan to use. A brand may offer a service while the property, room, and hours determine availability. A pool on a website is not proof it is open for your stay.
2. **Trace the real access path.** Follow your route from arrival point to entrance, reception or key collection, lift or stairs, room door, and any bathroom or shared area you must use. For a critical segment, ask a concrete question such as “Is the route from the street to this room step-free throughout?” or “Does this room have a roll-in shower or a bath?” A generic “accessible” label cannot answer a specific need. Ask which communication support is actually available if that matters too.
3. **Confirm arrival and entry conditions.** Compare your arrival time with this stay's check-in window, late-arrival contact method, key or reception location, and when detailed entry instructions will be sent. If self check-in, a mobile key, or advance room selection depends on an account, device, or eligibility, confirm it applies to this reservation and ask about a handover method you can use. Do not put door codes, full ID details, or payment data in a shared itinerary; record where to retrieve instructions through the official channel.
4. **Mark each item you will actually use.** For every essential, record “confirmed for this room and date / awaiting an answer / explicitly unavailable,” along with its location, hours, and whether it must be requested. Record optional comforts too, without presenting them as promised in-room items. If the listing and property reply differ, keep both statements and dates and ask for the answer for your room and stay.
5. **Set a decision point before departure.** Through the booking platform's messages or the property's published contact, send one short message with your specific non-negotiable questions, arrival time, and when you need an answer. If no answer comes, keep “unanswered” and decide whether you can tolerate that missing condition, need another workable arrangement, or must adjust the plan. Silence is not confirmation. Recheck the latest instructions near departure; change status only when something is actually confirmed.

### Success

You can state the entry method from arrival point to room, the critical access path, and the status of every essential facility for this reservation. Every unanswered essential has a contact channel, reply deadline, and workable next step. If a non-negotiable condition remains unanswered or is unavailable, stop marking this lodging “usable” until the arrangement is resolved. A completed note is not an on-site room inspection.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Another property in the chain has the facility:** Check this property, room type, and dates; a brand overview is not this stay's promise.
- **The listing says “accessible” without explaining entrance or bathroom:** Ask about the route and feature you need. Keep the status unanswered without usable detail.
- **Arrival is after reception hours:** Confirm the actual handover with the property before departure; generic self check-in advice does not activate it for your booking.
- **Detailed entry instructions have not arrived:** Check the platform's stated release time and official messages. If they remain absent when due, contact the host through the platform and prepare a usable alternative, rather than entering details into an unfamiliar link.
- **A reply says only “probably”:** Ask for this room, date, location, and operating hours. Keep a stay-critical item unanswered until you get a usable answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a pre-arrival check for ordinary hotels, hostels, and short stays; use the actual booking's rules. If phone calls or hearing are barriers, use an available written channel. If the page is hard to read, use an accessible format or trusted help without sharing complete door or payment details. Identity checks, fees, and check-in rules vary by place and service; none of the example platform rules are universal. An unmet essential may force a plan change, which is easier to handle before you reach the door.
