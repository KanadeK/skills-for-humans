## 中文

当一份普通工作、学习或志愿成果已经收到具体反馈，你需要决定怎么修、哪些要问清、哪些不采纳时加载。目标是**把每条有用反馈接到实际位置和要求，修订可改部分，并给出可查的新版本与逐条状态**。收到建议不表示全得照做；自己改完也不表示审阅者已经验收。专业行业、人事、医疗、法律、财务判断仍归相应有权限的人。

### 准备与输入

找回审阅者看到的那一版、原任务/验收要求及其实际评论，保留评论原意与时间。若反馈只说“再好一点”却没对象，先问具体段落、结果或例子，不凭自己的焦虑无限改写。弄清你可以独立修改什么，冲突要求由谁决定；不为了让反馈列表变绿而篡改必须保留的事实或证据。

### 执行

1. **把评论落到具体地方。** 在一张短清单记“谁说的、针对哪一处、希望什么变化、根据哪项要求”。把可核事实错误、表述偏好、缺失信息和新范围分开；同一问题的重复评论合并，保留来源而不虚增工作项。
2. **作三类真实裁决。** 必改：明确违背已确认要求或有可核实错误，且你有权修；待确认：评论含糊、彼此冲突、要求新范围/专业决定；暂不采纳：与已确认目标无关、会带来明显损失或只是偏好冲突。后两类写简短理由并找实际负责人，不把“我不喜欢”伪装成任务要求。
3. **在唯一当前稿上修。** 先修关键错误和依赖它的地方，再处理低风险表达。保留原版可追溯记录，给新稿一个明确版本/日期；不要同时维护两个都叫“最终”的权威稿。改一处表格、图、链接或数值后看附近引用是否也需同步。
4. **做与改动相称的检查。** 读受影响段落与接收人看到的导出/展示，核对原问题已消除、没有引出新的不一致。普通小文案不必造自动化测试；若改了真正的运作逻辑或专业内容，交适当负责人做对应验证，不因自读就宣布被批准。
5. **逐项回报而不藏未决。** 发“新版本__；已按意见 A 修改__，意见 B 待__确认，意见 C 未采纳因为__；请复核__”。对方的决定、仍未回复与实际接受分开记录。若期限无法覆盖关键修改，提前给真实范围和需要的决定，不提交一份看似全改完的稿。

### 完成与停止

你有一份可定位的新稿，收到的相关反馈逐条标**已修、待确认或不采纳并有理由**，已检查受影响输出，并知道下一位审阅/决定者是谁，本轮完成。**修订完成不是最终验收。** 涉及你无权改变的范围、相互矛盾的指示或专业高风险结论时停在待决定状态，交给真正负责人，不悄悄选一边。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“再清楚一点”没有具体对象：** 请反馈者指一段/一个预期效果，或给出你理解的候选问题让其确认。
- **两位审阅者要求相反：** 找决策负责人核实优先级，不同时做两个相反版本。
- **照单全收后原任务目标被改掉：** 回到已确认需求，标出新范围并请求授权。
- **只写“已修复”却没有位置或版本：** 给出新文件/段落和受影响的实际变化。
- **不采纳但不告诉对方：** 写出与目标或证据相关的简短理由，允许负责人复核。
- **改了一个值，图表还旧：** 检查依赖引用，避免新版本内部互相矛盾。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通可修订的小型协作成果，不处理人事绩效、专业审核权、法律或医疗裁断。反馈渠道、文件格式、谁有最终决定权按实际任务而定。听觉、视觉、语言或操作条件不同，可将口头评论读回成可确认的简短文字、使用可访问批注或请可信授权者协助整理，不把评论者的情绪当事实。副作用是未采纳意见也会被诚实记录，版本数量可能增加但权威当前稿只有一个。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary work, study or volunteer draft has received actual specific feedback and you need to decide what to change, clarify or not adopt. Your result is to **tie each relevant point to the reviewed location and request, revise the parts you own, and provide a findable new version and disposition list**. A suggestion need not always be adopted; your edits are not automatically reviewer acceptance. Qualified people retain specialist, personnel, medical, legal and financial decisions.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the exact version the reviewer saw, the real task and acceptance requirements, and what was actually said, keeping comment meaning and date. For “make it better” with no target, ask for a passage, result or example before rewriting endlessly from anxiety. Know what you may change and who resolves conflicting requirements. A green feedback list does not justify altering required facts or evidence.

### Execution

1. **Place each comment.** In one short list, note who said it, which part, desired change and linked requirement. Separate verifiable factual error, expression preference, missing information and new scope. Combine duplicates of one issue without losing their sources or inflating work count.
2. **Make three honest dispositions.** Change: a confirmed requirement is unmet or a checkable fact is wrong and you own the fix. Clarify: wording is vague, comments conflict, scope expands or specialist judgment is needed. Do not adopt: it does not serve the agreed outcome, would cause an evident loss or conflicts with another preference. Give brief reasons and refer decisions to the real owner; “I dislike it” alone is not a requirement.
3. **Edit one current draft.** Fix important errors and their dependents before lower-risk wording. Preserve a traceable prior version and name the new date or version. Do not maintain two authoritative files both called final. For a changed table, figure, link or number, inspect nearby references that may now be stale.
4. **Check what the change affects.** Read changed passages and the export or view the recipient will get. Confirm the original issue is resolved without introducing inconsistency. A small copy edit does not need a fabricated automated test. For real operational logic or specialist content, ask the appropriate owner for relevant verification; your own reading is not approval.
5. **Report each point without hiding open decisions.** Send “new version __; A changed at __; B waits on __; C not adopted because __; please review __.” Keep their decision, no reply and actual acceptance distinct. If the deadline cannot cover a key edit, state the real scope and decision needed early rather than submitting a draft that pretends everything changed.

### Success and stop

You have a findable revision, each relevant feedback item is marked **changed, waiting for clarification or not adopted with a reason**, affected output has been checked, and the next reviewer or decision owner is clear. **Revision is not final acceptance.** For work outside your authority, conflicting directions or high-risk specialist conclusions, stop at a pending decision and give it to the real owner rather than silently choosing a side.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Clearer” names no passage or outcome:** Ask for a specific section or effect, or offer your understanding for confirmation.
- **Two reviewers ask for opposite changes:** Ask the decision owner to set priority instead of creating contrary current versions.
- **Following all suggestions changes the original goal:** Return to confirmed requirements and ask about new scope.
- **“Fixed” has no location or version:** Point to the new file or passage and actual change.
- **A point is declined silently:** Give a short reason grounded in outcome or evidence and let the owner review it.
- **One value changed but the diagram stayed old:** Check references that depend on it so the new version agrees with itself.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for ordinary revisable collaboration, not performance management, specialist approval authority, law or medical rulings. Channel, format and final decision owner depend on the real assignment. For hearing, sight, language or device needs, read verbal comments back as short confirmable text, use accessible annotations or ask trusted authorised help to organise them. A reviewer's tone is not itself a fact. Non-adopted comments remain visible, and versions may grow while one current draft remains authoritative.
