## 中文

当你想实际比较**同一种颜料的均匀铺色和深浅渐变**，加载这份 Skill。结果是同纸相邻两块干后的水彩练习，能指出哪块大致均匀、哪块从深到浅；两种 wash 不拆成两篇计数。H272 的构图决定位置，本篇操练颜料、水和纸的流动。

### 准备与输入

准备自有水彩纸、标签允许的普通水彩、一支能覆盖小区块的笔、调色处、清水和吸水纸。画两个不相碰的小长方形区，先试纸是否能承受湿笔；纸起毛或墨水印会渗时换纸。颜料水远离食物，台面可擦，杯子不在纸边。

### 执行

1. **调够同一色的稀释液。** 在调色处先调一小池能连续覆盖一个长方形的水彩液，不在半途才找新色。笔含色但不滴成水坑。
2. **做均匀区。** 在第一格上沿向下逐行轻刷，相邻一行稍搭前一行仍湿的边，保持相近色量；完成后停止反复回刷半干处。底边积水可用近干画笔轻吸。
3. **做渐变区。** 第二格从较浓颜色开始，逐行向下时逐渐用清水稀释笔上或调色处的同色，让后段更浅，接边仍轻叠。目标是深浅方向可辨，不要求机器般无痕。
4. **晾干再核对。** 平放自然干燥，写“均匀”和“深→浅”及颜料名。干后看第一格是否大体同深、第二格是否真的变化；记录水痕和下一次要少水或加快衔接的具体点。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

同纸干后有两块可比较区域，分别呈大致均匀与可识别深浅梯度，并标明顺序。湿纸上的暂时光泽不算最终色差。

- **颜色在半途断掉：** 记录断带，下次先调足液；在当前半干面来回补涂易留边痕。
- **平涂出现深色水痕：** 少留底边积水，先在下一块小试；不对湿纸猛擦。
- **渐变始终同深：** 下一次更明显加清水或少取颜料，干后诚实记下本次失败。
- **纸起毛或翘得无法续画：** 停止反复刷，平放干后换适合水彩的纸再试。
- **手眼控制困难：** 缩小区块、用更宽笔或可信协助固定纸；用可见深浅而非笔迹完美度判断。

### 假设与边界

水彩颜料与纸的吸水差异会改变流动和色差；没有万能用水量或笔号。纸面练习不复制示范图，也不把“平”和“渐变”伪装成两个独立生活任务。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to compare **an even field and a dark-to-light gradient using the same watercolour**. Finish with two neighbouring dry practice areas on one sheet, one roughly even and one visibly graded. Flat and graded washes are a single comparison exercise here. H272 chooses composition; this exercise controls pigment, water, and paper flow.

### Preparation and inputs

Use owned watercolour paper, ordinary watercolour permitted by its label, a brush broad enough for small fields, a palette, clean water, and absorbent paper. Mark two separate small rectangles and test whether the sheet tolerates a damp brush. Change paper if it pills or print bleeds. Keep pigment water away from food and the cup away from the sheet's edge.

### Execution

1. **Mix enough one-colour wash.** Prepare a small pool able to cover one rectangle continuously. Do not discover halfway through that the mix is gone. Load the brush without dripping a puddle.
2. **Make the even field.** Work from the top down in adjacent horizontal strokes, slightly joining each still-wet edge while keeping a similar pigment load. Stop repeatedly brushing partly dry areas. Lift a bottom puddle gently with an almost dry brush if needed.
3. **Make the graded field.** Start the second rectangle with stronger colour and gradually dilute the same pigment with clean water as you work down, lightly joining edges. A clear dark-to-light direction matters more than machine-perfect smoothness.
4. **Dry and compare.** Let the sheet dry naturally flat. Label “even” and “dark → light” with the pigment name. Check whether the first area is broadly even and the second actually changes, recording a tide mark or a specific next adjustment.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One dry sheet contains two labelled comparable regions, roughly even and visibly graded. Temporary wet shine is not a dry colour check.

- **Colour runs out midway:** Record the break and mix enough before a later attempt; repeatedly painting over partly dry colour often leaves an edge.
- **Even field gets a dark tide mark:** Leave less pooled water at the bottom and test on another small area; do not scrub wet paper.
- **Gradient stays the same shade:** Use more water or less pigment on a later pass and record this attempt honestly.
- **Paper pills or buckles beyond workable:** Stop brushing, dry it flat, and use suitable watercolour paper next time.
- **Fine control is hard:** Use smaller fields, a broader brush, or trusted help holding the paper; judge visible value change rather than perfect strokes.

### Assumptions and limits

Pigment and paper absorb water differently, so no universal water volume or brush number works. The original practice sheet does not copy an example image or count two close wash methods as two separate tasks.
