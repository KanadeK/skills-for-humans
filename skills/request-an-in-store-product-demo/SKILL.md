## 中文

当普通商品的文字说明没有展示你在意的**一个实际动作**，而店里有展示品时，加载这份 Skill。例如台灯有“三档亮度”，但你想看切换时每一档是什么样。结果是一次经店方许可、条件可复述的观察，或明确知道现场无法演示；不是性能认证、长期耐久测试，也不要求店员必须演示。

本 Skill 不做食品试吃、化妆品试用、儿童或医疗器械试验，也不让你接线、拆机、操作危险机械或测试安全关键功能。尺寸能否适合用途要另核对；样品是否对应要买的型号也不能从“看着一样”推断。

### 准备与输入

先把需求压成一句可观察的问题：“能否演示这件准确型号的〔动作〕，让我看到〔可见/可听结果〕？”记下展示品的完整型号、版本或展示牌，以及待买商品型号。准备纸笔或普通备忘录，只记录设备与动作，不记录店员或其他顾客的私人资料。

### 执行

1. **先问许可和边界。** 礼貌问店员是否有可用展示品，能否由合适人员演示这一个普通动作。未获许可不拆封商品、不拔插线路、不改店内设备设置；被拒绝就接受，不把演示变成现场争执。
2. **核对演示的究竟是哪款。** 请店员指出展示品完整型号、配置和与待买商品的关系。样品是另一型号时，观察只属于那件样品；如果这项差异影响决定，先停下再找同款或正式说明。
3. **说明只看一个结果。** 请店员按通常使用方式演示，观察开始状态、采取的动作和结果。记录是否借助特别电源、配件、App、账号或店内预设；演示机已经设置好不代表买回去就能免设置运行。
4. **复述范围。** 说：“我看到在〔条件〕下，这件〔型号〕做到了〔结果〕；这能说明待买的〔型号〕吗？”只记录实际观察和店方可出示的说明。短暂演示不能证明寿命、全部功能或未来服务一直可用。
5. **给出结束状态。** 写“已观察到且条件清楚 / 只观察到另一款或特殊条件 / 未获准或无法演示”。后两种若涉及购买前提，查准确型号说明、约可用样品，或暂缓购买。

### 成功与停止

你能把看到或听到的动作、展示型号、设置条件与仍未知的地方说清；或者得到一次明确的“现场无法演示”并停止。完成不要求购买，也不要求对方保证实际售卖品在所有环境下表现相同。

### 常见报错与补救

- **店员说“不能开箱”：** 请求现有展示品或正式演示资料；都没有就保留未知，不自行拆封。
- **演示过程中换成另一个型号：** 把结果标为只适用于演示型号，重新核对待买商品。
- **效果只在店内账号或特殊配件下出现：** 记录依赖；不能确认你将得到相同条件时，不把效果写成商品裸机能力。
- **动作中断、环境太吵或看不清：** 在店方方便时请求重复、近看、文字说明或可信同伴协助；仍无法观察就停止，不能用猜测补结果。

### 假设、替代与副作用

不同商店对触碰、拍摄、开箱与演示有不同规则，本 Skill 只要求征求许可，不声称任何地区都必须提供试玩。站立、听觉、视觉或语言沟通不方便时，可请店员把展示品移到可达位置、提供说明卡或允许可信同伴观察；危险搬动由店方处理。现实副作用是“能用”从一句话变成“在这些条件下看到它做了这件事”。

## English

Load this Skill when the description of an ordinary item leaves **one action you care about** unshown and the shop has a display. A lamp may claim three brightness settings, for example, while you still need to see what switching between them looks like. Your result is a permitted observation under conditions you can repeat, or an explicit “no demo available.” It is not a performance certificate or longevity test, and staff do not have to grant a demo.

This does not cover food tasting, cosmetic testing, children's or medical-device trials, wiring, disassembly, dangerous machinery, or safety-critical demonstrations. Check physical suitability separately. A sample's relationship to the item for sale cannot be inferred from looking alike.

### Preparation and inputs

Make one observable request: “Could you demonstrate [action] on this exact model so I can see or hear [result]?” Record the display's full model, revision or card, and the product you might buy. Have paper or ordinary notes ready. You need the device and action, not personal details about staff or other shoppers.

### Execution

1. **Ask permission and boundaries first.** Ask politely whether an available display can be used and whether appropriate staff can demonstrate this one ordinary action. Do not unseal stock, reconnect wires, or change store equipment settings without permission. Accept a refusal instead of turning a demo request into a dispute.
2. **Identify the demonstrated model.** Ask for its full model and configuration and how it relates to the item offered. If it is another model, the observation belongs to that sample. Pause for a matching sample or exact documentation if the difference matters.
3. **Watch one stated result.** Have staff use the item in its ordinary way. Note its starting state, action and result, including any special power source, accessory, app, account or store preset. An already configured showroom unit does not prove out-of-box operation needs no setup.
4. **Repeat the limits.** Say, “I saw [model] do [result] under [conditions]. Does the item for sale have the same conditions?” Record only what you observed and what the shop can show in product information. A short demo cannot establish lifetime, every feature or continued future service.
5. **End in one of three states.** Write “observed with clear conditions / observed only on another model or special setup / not permitted or unavailable.” For either of the last two, find exact-model documentation, arrange an available display later, or defer if the answer matters to buying.

### Success and stop

You can name the action you saw or heard, the display model, setup conditions and remaining unknowns; or you have a clear “not available here” and stop. Completion does not require buying or a promise that the sale unit behaves identically in every setting.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Staff say “we cannot open a box”:** Ask whether an existing display or formal demo information is available. If neither is, keep the result unknown; do not open stock yourself.
- **The demo switches to a different model:** Mark the observation as applying to that model only and recheck the item for sale.
- **It works only with a store account or special accessory:** Record the dependency. Do not present the effect as a stand-alone product ability unless the same conditions are confirmed for you.
- **The action is interrupted, noisy or hard to see:** If convenient for the shop, ask for a repeat, a closer position, written description or a trusted companion's observation. If still inaccessible, stop rather than inventing the result.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Stores differ in rules for touching, photos, opening boxes and demonstrations. This Skill asks permission; it does not claim that every region guarantees a tryout. If standing, hearing, sight or language access is difficult, ask staff for a reachable display, a written card or permission for a trusted companion to observe. Staff handle any unsafe lifting. A side effect is replacing “it works” with “I saw it do this under these conditions.”
