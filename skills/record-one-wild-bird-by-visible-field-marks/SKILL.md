## 中文

当你从**获准的安全位置看到一只野鸟**，想在它飞走前留下能回查的可见线索时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一条时间、地点范围、体形、颜色图案、动作和生境都明确的观察笔记，即使物种未知也成立。它不同于下一步用图鉴判种、听声记录或整段清单统计。不要为细看靠近巢、离开开放路径、诱叫或驱鸟起飞。

### 准备与输入

在安全站位拿笔记或可选已有望远镜，手机拍照仅在不妨碍他人隐私/鸟的情况下用。先看周围道路、栏杆和光线，不能边走边举镜；日光附近不用光学器材。对鸟的距离以它仍能自然活动为界，鸟惊飞/警戒就退开。确切巢位或稀有物种细位置不公开。

### 执行

1. **先抓大形而非名字。** 记与熟悉物/常见鸟相比的大致体量、身形、喙、尾、翅和站姿；看不清就记“未知”，不从一抹颜色跳到物种。
2. **再记整体色块和动作。** 记录主要浅深/花纹、明显翼斑或眼周线，及它在地面、树冠、水面如何走、停、取食或飞。强背光时记“逆光”，不要凭想象补颜色。
3. **补时间与生境。** 写日期、观察时段、允许公开的粗地点、周围是树丛/草地/水边等，保留天气或距离造成的限制。飞走后不用追进禁区或靠近巢。
4. **划开观察与推断。** 写“确实看见”与“可能是”两栏；若只看到一秒/局部，完整记录也可只到“未识别小鸟”。图鉴判种在另一 Skill 中做，不强制本篇下结论。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一条可回查的一只鸟目视记录含大小/形状、色纹、动作、生境、日期与不确定性；哪项看不清就标未知。只有“看见某某鸟”却没有线索，不算完成观察证据。

- **鸟飞太快：** 只记实际看见的形状/方向，留未知，不追逐。
- **光线让颜色失真：** 标逆光/阴影，依轮廓和行为记录。
- **鸟明显警戒或从巢起飞：** 停观察并退远，不用更近的照片补证。
- **镜头扫到私人窗/人：** 移开方向，遵守现场隐私与摄影规则。
- **感官/行动不便：** 固定安全位置、可信协助描述与大字记录均可，保留所见/代述来源。

### 假设与边界

Cornell 的大小形状、色纹、行为、生境是判种的前置线索，不保证这条记录足以判到种。日期和粗地点能帮助复核；本篇不处理保护物种定位公开或野生动物接触。

## English

Load this Skill when you see **one wild bird from a permitted safe viewpoint** and want traceable visible clues before it leaves. Finish with a note of time, coarse place, size/shape, pattern, behaviour, and habitat; unknown species is a valid result. This differs from later guide comparison, audio evidence, or a whole-session count. Do not approach nests, leave open paths, lure with calls, or flush birds for a better view.

### Preparation and inputs

Use a safe stationary spot with notes and optional existing binoculars. Photograph only without intruding on people or disturbing birds. Check roads, barriers, and light; never walk with binoculars raised or aim optics near the sun. Keep distance where the bird behaves naturally and retreat if it alarms or flushes. Do not publish exact nest or sensitive-species sites.

### Execution

1. **Capture broad form before a name.** Compare approximate size with a known bird/object, noting body, bill, tail, wings, and posture. Mark unknowns rather than naming a species from one colour patch.
2. **Note overall pattern and behaviour.** Write main light/dark areas, clear wingbar or eye marking if visible, and whether it walks, perches, feeds, or flies on ground, canopy, or water. Write “backlit” when lighting conceals colour rather than inventing it.
3. **Add time and habitat.** Record date, period, publicly safe coarse locality, tree/shrub/grass/water context, and weather or distance limits. Do not chase beyond permitted access after it departs.
4. **Separate sight from inference.** Use “actually seen” and “might be” fields. A one-second or partial look may end as “unidentified small bird.” A field-guide decision is another Skill, not a compulsory claim here.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One traceable visual note includes size/shape, pattern, behaviour, habitat, date, and uncertainty. Mark unseen details unknown. Writing only a confident species name without field evidence is not an observation record.

- **Bird leaves quickly:** Note only real form and movement, keep unknown, and do not chase.
- **Lighting distorts colour:** Mark backlight/shade and use form and behaviour as observed.
- **Bird alarms or leaves a nest:** Stop and retreat without a closer photo.
- **Lens points at a window or person:** Redirect under site privacy and photo rules.
- **Senses or mobility limit viewing:** A safe fixed spot, trusted description, and large notes work if you name what was seen versus described.

### Assumptions and limits

Cornell's size/shape, colour pattern, behaviour, and habitat are evidence before identification, not a guarantee of species-level certainty. Date and coarse place aid review. Protected-location publication and handling wild birds are outside scope.
