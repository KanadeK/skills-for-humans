## 中文

当你的普通手织小样**刚掉了一针**，下方出现少数几条横线，想在继续织前先止住并补回时，加载这份 Skill。结果是丢失的活圈被安全抓住、按顺序勾回且针数恢复，或明确退几行重做。复杂花样、多色提花、断线或很长的跑针不套此简法；它不是 H062 衣服小破口缝补。

### 准备与输入

马上停织，把线团放稳。对照原针数定位消失的圈和垂直梯状横线，备匹配纱线粗细的钝钩针或临时固定件、亮光和稳定桌面。若圈已经跑到看不见、横线交叉复杂或线已断，先固定圈并查专门教程/可信熟手，不硬勾错结构。

### 执行

1. **先挂住最低活圈。** 在掉针列的最低可见完整圈中穿入钩针或固定件，避免继续往下跑；轻拉平两侧织物，看上方横线顺序。不要直接扯最上方长线把梯子拉更长。
2. **逐条横线向上恢复。** 对普通正针列，让钩针从正面穿过活圈，钩住其正上方最近一条横线并拉过圈，形成上一行的新圈；一行一条往上，保持圈未扭。若这是反针列或图样结构不同，按对应说明处理，不把正针法硬套进去。
3. **把最高圈放回原针。** 回到仍在针上的行，把修回的圈按相邻针脚朝向放回对应位置，数全行针数并看掉针列是否已无松梯。圈扭了可停下调整；不能确认时退回出错前一两行或重做小样。
4. **继续前记真实状态。** 记录掉针位置、补回行数和是否有轻微松紧差；只有针数和结构可辨才继续织。工具离手收好，不让松圈再次滑脱。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

最近的掉针已被抓住并逐条补回、行针数恢复，或在无法安全补时固定并退行重做。只把梯子藏在背面、针数仍少一针，不算修复。

- **一勾又掉出新圈：** 立即固定最低可见活圈，先别往上织。
- **横线顺序交叉不清：** 停在固定状态，请懂该针法的人看或小样拆回重做。
- **补回后少/多一针：** 重数该列和全行，不用松线团把数字凑齐。
- **圈扭或形成大洞：** 在这一行调整朝向，不能辨认就退行。
- **视力/握持不便：** 用浅色粗线、放大灯与可信协助抓圈，由你核对针数与停点。

### 假设与边界

Craft Yarn Council 的钩回法适用于最近、结构简单且横线可见的掉针。长距离跑针、复杂反针/花样或损坏纱线需更具体方法，不能承诺无痕。

## English

Load this Skill when a **recent loop drops from your ordinary hand-knit sample** and a short ladder of horizontal bars appears. Before knitting farther, catch it and try to rebuild. Finish with the live loop secured, bars lifted in order, and stitch count restored, or unknit and restart clearly. Complex patterns, stranded colours, broken yarn, or a long run need specific guidance. This is not H062 seam repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Stop knitting and steady the yarn ball. Compare the original count to locate the missing loop and ladder. Have a blunt crochet hook or stitch holder suited to the yarn, bright light, and stable surface. If the loop is no longer visible, bars cross confusingly, or yarn is broken, secure what remains and consult a technique-specific guide or trusted knitter rather than building the wrong structure by force.

### Execution

1. **Catch the lowest live loop first.** Put the hook or holder through the lowest intact loop in the dropped column so it cannot run farther. Spread neighbouring fabric gently and identify the order of bars above. Do not yank a top strand and lengthen the ladder.
2. **Lift one bar at a time.** In a simple knit column, put the hook through the live loop from the front, catch the nearest bar directly above, and pull it through to create the next loop. Continue upward one bar per row, avoiding a twist. A purl column or different pattern needs its own instructions; do not impose the knit route.
3. **Return the top loop to its needle.** At the row still held on the needles, place the rebuilt loop in its original position and orientation. Count all stitches and inspect the column for remaining loose ladders. If twisted, adjust before proceeding. If uncertain, unknit a row or two to the mistake or remake the small sample.
4. **Record the state before continuing.** Note location, number of restored rows, and any tension mark. Knit on only with a count and structure you can identify. Put the hook away without dislodging the loop again.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The recent dropped stitch is caught and rebuilt bar by bar with original row count, or the loop is secured while you deliberately unknit/restart. Hiding a ladder behind the fabric with one stitch still missing is not a repair.

- **Another loop drops during the attempt:** Secure the lowest live loop immediately and stop knitting upward.
- **Bars cross or their order is unclear:** Pause with the loop secured and seek the right stitch guide or remake this small sample.
- **Count is still one short or extra:** Recount column and full row instead of tugging yarn to make numbers appear.
- **Loop twists or leaves a large hole:** Adjust orientation at this row; unknit if you cannot identify it.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Light bulky yarn, magnification, and trusted loop-catching help can support your count and stop decision.

### Assumptions and limits

Craft Yarn Council's hook method suits recent, simple, visible ladders. Long runs, complex purl/pattern rows, or damaged yarn need specific repair and may leave a mark; no invisible-fix promise is made.
