## 中文

当你要把借来的实物还给图书馆时，用这份 Skill **选择允许的归还通道、实际交还，并核对系统核销**。把书投入箱子只是交付动作，馆方从你账户移除才是可确认的完成状态。续借、遗失赔偿或费用争议另走馆方流程。

### 准备与输入

确认物品仍列在你自己的借阅清单，并核对封面/条码与到期日。看馆方是否允许在目标馆点、服务台或书箱归还这类材料，以及该书箱当时是否开放；馆际借阅、设备、特殊资料可能必须交到指定柜台。不同馆可能允许跨馆归还，也可能不允许，不借用别馆规则。若物品损坏、缺附件或不在你手里，直接告诉馆员实际情况，不把它悄悄放箱里冒充完好。

### 执行

1. **选对归还点。** 按该馆当前说明找开放的柜台、归还机或明确接受此类型的书箱；看入口标识和时段。无法确认时问馆员或等能确认的通道，不把书放在门口地上或通道里。
2. **实际交还具体物品。** 柜台/归还机逐件交接并核对屏幕或工作人员确认；书箱则按标识投进正确入口，确认物品完全进入，不留在外沿。不要拍入旁人借阅卡或账号作为“证据”。
3. **区分交付和核销。** 可当场拿到归还收据就保留，核对物品和日期；书箱没有即时收据时私下记地点、时间、物品，并标“已投递，待馆方核销”。以后在馆方账户或按馆方通知时间核对它是否从已借清单移除。不要删除唯一线索，也不立即在日历里写“已核销”。
4. **处理状态未变化。** 过了馆方正常处理时间仍列为借阅、收据内容不符或物品有异常时，带记录向馆员核实。物品已经投递也要如实说明；不重复投递别的书来试机器，也不自行认定费用免除。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

馆方收据或账户明确显示**该具体物品已核销/不再在你的借阅清单**。若只完成投递，状态是“已交付、核销待确认”，这是实际进展但不是本篇最终完成。

- **书箱标识不清或满了：** 不放在旁边；找允许的柜台或其他正式归还点。
- **归还机报错：** 留在现场找工作人员，不带着不明确状态离开，也不把物品硬塞进机器。
- **几件物品只少了一件：** 逐件核收据或账户，未核销的单独跟进。
- **书箱投递后账户仍显示借阅：** 先按馆方说明的处理时间核查，再提供时间/地点/物品线索联系馆员；不要把状态延迟直接认定为丢失。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

归还地点、书箱时段、跨馆规则和系统更新速度随馆而异；本文不提供通用处理小时数。手部、行动、视听条件需要协助时可选工作人员服务或馆方可及入口，并核对交付结果。任何丢失、损坏或费用事项应由馆方给出具体处理，不靠本篇推断责任。一次书箱投递与馆方核销之间可能有等待，保留真实状态。

## English

Use this Skill when returning a physical item borrowed from a library. **Choose an allowed return route, hand over the item, and verify that the library checked it in.** A book entering a drop box is a hand-in action; the loan leaving your account is the confirmed completion state. Renewal, loss claims, and fee disputes use the library's other procedures.

### Preparation and inputs

Check that the item is still on your own loan list, and match its cover or barcode and due date. Read whether this type may be returned to the planned branch, desk, machine, or book drop, and whether that drop is open now. Interlibrary loans, devices, or special materials may require a desk. Some systems accept returns at another branch and some do not; do not borrow a different library's rule. If an attachment is missing, the item is damaged, or you do not have it, tell staff the real condition rather than quietly put it in a box as if complete.

### Execution

1. **Choose the correct return point.** Follow this library's current instructions for an open desk, return machine, or clearly eligible book drop. Check signs and operating times. If uncertain, ask staff or wait for a route you can confirm; do not leave an item on the ground by a door or aisle.
2. **Hand over the exact item.** At a desk or machine, hand in each piece and check its screen or staff acknowledgement. For a book drop, use the marked opening and make sure the whole item goes inside rather than remaining at the lip. Do not photograph another patron's card or account as “evidence.”
3. **Separate hand-in from check-in.** Keep an immediate return receipt if available and check item and date. When a drop gives no receipt, privately note place, time, and item as “handed in; awaiting check-in.” Later check your official account or the library's stated notification route to see whether it left your loans. Do not discard your only lead or immediately write “checked in” on your calendar.
4. **Follow up if status does not change.** If the item is still listed after the library's ordinary processing period, the receipt is wrong, or there is an exception, bring the details to staff. State honestly that you already handed it in. Do not put other books through the machine to test it or decide a charge is waived.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

An official receipt or account clearly shows **the exact item checked in or removed from your active loans**. A drop-off alone is “handed in, check-in pending,” real progress but not this Skill's confirmed finish.

- **The book drop is unclear or full:** Do not leave the item beside it. Find an allowed desk or other formal return point.
- **The machine reports an error:** Ask staff on site; do not leave with an ambiguous status or force the item into the machine.
- **Only one of several items disappears from your loans:** Verify each against receipt or account and follow up on the unresolved one.
- **Your account still shows a dropped item:** Allow the library's stated processing time, then use place, time, and item details with staff. A lag alone is not proof of loss.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Return points, drop hours, cross-branch rules, and account update times vary by library. This Skill gives no universal processing hour count. For hand, mobility, sight, or hearing needs, use staff service or an accessible entrance and verify the handoff. Staff handle the actual terms for lost, damaged, or charged items; do not infer liability here. Keep the honest pending state between a drop and check-in.
