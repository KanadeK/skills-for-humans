## 中文

当同住者都使用共用区域，但反复清洁、垃圾处理或使用后恢复总靠默契时加载。你要建立**具体任务、触发点、负责人、可替换安排和完成信号**。这里不教危险清洁化学品操作，不决定共享物品归属，不处理一次来客后的临时占用恢复，也不解释住房责任。

### 准备与输入

列出实际共同使用、可由住户普通维护的区域和任务；看现有房屋或管理规定、物品标签和谁有使用权限。请实际居住者说出可做的时间、体力、感官及用品限制，不要求公开私人原因。费用分摊和专业清洁不在此决定；缺少合适用品时先记为缺口。

### 执行

1. **把“干净”拆成看得见的事。** 分开用后即时恢复与周期任务，例如清走自己留下的普通物品、擦自己使用后的台面、按实际约定轮值处理共用垃圾。指定区域和完成信号，不用“打扫一下”覆盖整个家。
2. **给每件事一个现实触发点。** 可以是“每次使用后”“垃圾到约定状态时”或双方能执行的周期；不要把某机构的每日/每周选项当成所有家庭规定。任务需要的用品、可进入时间和房间使用安排一并核对。
3. **按能力和同意分工。** 为每个周期任务写负责人、轮替或协作方式；本人无法做某项时可交换为同等清楚的任务或找经同意的帮助，不能靠默默缺席。一个人主动做过一次不自动永久承包。
4. **约好交接和缺班。** 写完成后如何告知、赶不上时提前通知谁、谁同意代做、何时补回。任务不完成时先核对事实和障碍，再谈改表，不贴人格标签。
5. **试行并复看。** 让各人读回自己下一次具体要做什么、到什么状态算完成。试行一轮后看是否有人无法执行、区域仍不可用或安排影响他人使用，再由参与者修改。

### 完成与停止

重复任务有具体区域、动作、触发、同意的负责人、替代办法和可观察完成状态；各人能读懂轮次。无人同意负责、用品不适用、任务需要危险操作或涉及专业维修时标未定并请有权人员处理，不用加重别人的默契负担。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“每个人保持整洁”没人知道做什么：** 写出一次使用后恢复和周期轮值各自的可见动作。
- **某人长期补别人的班：** 核对实际记录和能力，重新协商轮次，不把一次善意变成默认义务。
- **排了班却没有用品：** 把任务标为待用品，按实际产品说明取得合适用品；不混用未知化学品。
- **有人因时间或能力做不了：** 同意换成另一件有相近结果的任务、调整时段或取得帮助，写在表上。
- **“已经打扫”仍挡着别人使用：** 按之前约定的完成信号看缺项，具体补做或修改不清楚的标准。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通低风险区域维护；霉菌、危险污染、管线/电气维修或需要专业设备的工作交合适渠道。不同文化、地区、家务能力和共住规则改变任务清单。纸表、大字、语音或可信的人读回都可用；轮次表不是监督人格的记分牌。副作用是“大家”这个主语获得具体动词和日期。

## English

Load this Skill when housemates all use common areas but recurring cleaning, waste removal or after-use reset depends on silent assumptions. You will establish **specific tasks, triggers, willing owners, substitutes and visible finish points**. This does not teach hazardous chemical use, decide ownership of shared goods, restore a one-off guest event or interpret housing duties.

### Preparation and inputs

List the actual common areas and ordinary tasks residents may handle. Check building or management rules, product labels and who may access each area. Ask residents what time, strength, sensory access and supplies they can use without demanding private explanations. Cost division and professional cleaning are outside this decision; mark missing suitable supplies as a gap.

### Execution

1. **Turn “clean” into visible actions.** Separate immediate after-use reset from periodic tasks, such as removing your own things, wiping a surface you used, or taking a turn with shared waste under the actual agreement. Name the area and done signal instead of making “clean the house” one unbounded task.
2. **Give each task a real trigger.** It may be “after each use,” “when waste reaches the agreed point,” or a period residents can manage. A university's daily or weekly form options are not a universal household rule. Check supplies, access time and facility-use arrangements together.
3. **Allocate by ability and consent.** Name a person, turn or joint method for each recurring task. Someone unable to perform one job can agree to a comparably clear swap or willing help; silence is not a substitute. A person who volunteered once has not accepted a permanent assignment.
4. **Set handoff and absence rules.** Record how completion is announced, who hears about a missed turn early, who agrees to cover it and when it is made up. Check facts and obstacles before revising a missed task; do not turn it into a character verdict.
5. **Try one cycle and review.** Each person reads back their next concrete action and done signal. After one cycle, check whether the job was feasible, the area stayed usable and timing worked, then revise with participants.

### Success and stop

Each recurring job has an area, action, trigger, consenting owner, substitute and observable finish; residents can read the rota. Mark an unowned job, unsuitable supply, hazardous operation or specialist repair unresolved and ask the responsible person rather than loading it onto somebody's unspoken goodwill.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Everybody keeps it tidy” gives no action:** Write visible after-use and periodic actions separately.
- **One person keeps covering another's turn:** Review actual records and capacity, then renegotiate. One helpful act is not a lifelong assignment.
- **A turn exists but supplies do not:** Mark it pending supplies and get an appropriate product under its actual instructions. Do not combine unknown chemicals.
- **Someone cannot do a job at that time or with that ability:** Agree on a different task with a similar outcome, a new time or help, and write it down.
- **“I cleaned it” still leaves the area unusable:** Compare with the agreed done signal, fill the specific gap or clarify the standard.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary low-risk area care. Mould, hazardous contamination, pipe or electrical repair and specialist equipment belong with appropriate professionals. Culture, region, household abilities and building rules change the tasks. Paper, large print, audio or a trusted readback can all carry the rota; it is not a scorecard of someone's character. The side effect is that “everyone” now has verbs and dates.
