## 中文

当你已经确认一门普通社区课程适合自己、到了第一节却还不知道报到、材料和参与方式时，用这份 Skill **完成真实首次到场**：找到正确场次，按主办方规则加入一项允许的活动，并留下下一次路径。报名确认只是进入条件，不等于你已上过课；课程教学内容和专业技法另行学习。

### 准备与输入

带上当期报名确认或官方“可直接到场”说明，核日期、地点/房间、开始时间、要带的普通材料和到达方式。看主办方当日更新、拍摄/分享规则及可及支持，不以另一家机构经验代替。费用或材料如果临时变化，先问清并决定是否接受。这里仅限低风险普通活动；危险工具、热源、个人健康训练或需专业监管的设备，必须遵守主办方培训并超出本篇操作指导。

### 执行

1. **到对人和房间。** 找课程名称、主办方、日期与导师/工作人员，按要求签到；同楼的别班不是你报的班。找不到房间时在服务台问，不擅自进锁着的工作区。
2. **核材料和参与边界。** 简短问“今天哪些材料是提供的，哪些活动需要先示范/培训？这门课的出入与拍摄规则是什么？”你需要的语言、听觉、行动或其他可及支持可直接提出，不需要公开无关的私人经历。若你无法接受新出现的费用或拍摄条件，可先停并问退出方式。
3. **参与一项允许的起步活动。** 听完导师说明后做一项适合自己水平的普通练习或讨论动作，不抢用未获许可的工具，不为了“证明来过”做超出能力或安全边界的操作。听不懂指令就先澄清；课堂同意别人参与不等于允许拍下或分享他们。
4. **记录实际进展。** 结束前写“今天我完成/尝试了____；不明白____；下次需要____”。如果课程只有观摩而没有实作，就如实写观摩与具体一处所见，不把没有发生的练习记为已做。
5. **确认下次路径并离开。** 问清后续日期、作业/材料、是否还需报名或退出通知，按场地说明归还共用材料并离开。若这不是你能持续参加的班，按主办方取消/退出渠道处理，不无声占用有限名额。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你在正确场次完成馆方或主办方要求的首次签到/进入，参与至少一项实际允许的学习动作或明确的观摩内容，并知道下一次怎么继续或退出。只到了门口、拿了宣传单而未进入，不算参加第一节。

- **名字不在报名表：** 出示自己的正式确认请工作人员核对；没被接纳就记真实状态，不跟别人混进去。
- **材料不足或课程突然变难：** 问是否有适合的低门槛替代；没有就选择观摩或退出，不借危险器材硬撑。
- **听不懂导师说明：** 在合适时机请重复、文字/字幕或示范；得不到可用说明就不操作不明工具。
- **首节与公告时间/照片规则不同：** 核实际安排并明确你的接受范围；不满意可按主办方路线离开。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

不同课程可能是一节体验、多周系列、讨论或动手活动；本篇只约束首次参与和继续路径，不规定具体课程技法。在线课程可把“房间”换成官方会话链接与主持人签到，但不把线上线下复制成两个 Skill。可信协助、无障碍路线和主办方提供的辅助方式可帮助你参与。实际学会多少需要后续练习，不能由一次签到推断。

## English

Use this Skill when you have already checked that an ordinary community class fits you but still need to navigate check-in, materials, and participation at its first session. **Actually join the class**: reach the correct session, do one permitted learning action, and leave with a path for next time. Registration is an entry condition, not proof you attended. Subject teaching and specialist techniques are separate.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring the current registration confirmation or official drop-in instruction. Check date, room or location, start time, ordinary materials, and travel. Review day-of updates, photography or sharing rules, and access support. Do not import another organisation's habits. If cost or supplies change at the door, ask and decide whether to accept. Only low-risk ordinary activities are covered; hazardous tools, heat, health training, or supervised specialist equipment require organiser training beyond this procedure.

### Execution

1. **Reach the right person and room.** Match class name, organiser, date, and instructor or staff, and check in as required. Another class in the building is not yours. Ask at a service desk when the room is unclear; do not enter locked work areas.
2. **Confirm materials and participation boundaries.** Ask briefly what is supplied, what needs demonstration or training, and the exit and photography rules. State a language, hearing, mobility, or other access need without disclosing unrelated private history. If a new charge or photo condition is unacceptable, pause and ask about leaving.
3. **Try one permitted starting activity.** After the instructor's guidance, do one ordinary exercise or discussion move suited to your level. Do not take unapproved tools or perform beyond your safe ability just to prove you attended. Clarify instructions you did not understand. Others' attendance is not consent to photograph or share them.
4. **Record actual progress.** Before leaving write “today I completed or tried ____; I still need ____; next time bring/do ____.” If the class was observation-only, state one actual thing you observed rather than invent practice that did not happen.
5. **Confirm the next route and leave.** Check the next date, assignment or materials, whether another booking is needed, and how to withdraw. Return shared items as instructed and leave by the venue route. If the class is not one you can continue, use its cancellation channel instead of silently occupying a limited place.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You entered or checked in to the correct session as required, joined at least one real permitted learning action or clearly identified observation, and know how to continue or leave the series. Reaching the doorway or collecting a flyer without entering is not first-session attendance.

- **Your name is absent from the list:** Show your own official confirmation and ask staff to check. If not admitted, record that instead of slipping in.
- **Materials are short or the class is unexpectedly advanced:** Ask for a suitable lower-step alternative. If none exists, observe or leave rather than force risky equipment use.
- **You cannot follow the instructor:** Ask for repetition, text or captions, or a demonstration at an appropriate moment. Do not operate an unclear tool.
- **Time or photo rules differ from the listing:** Verify the real terms and state what you accept. Leave through the organiser's route if necessary.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Classes may be one-off, multi-week, discussion, or hands-on. This Skill covers first participation and next route, not a subject technique. For an online class, use the official session link and moderator check-in instead of a physical room; venue format alone does not require a duplicate Skill. Trusted help, accessible routes, and organiser support may enable participation. Actual learning requires later practice; attendance alone proves no mastery.
