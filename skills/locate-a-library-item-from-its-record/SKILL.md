## 中文

当你已经确定**某条具体馆藏记录**，到馆后却要把屏幕上的馆点、索书号和状态变成眼前的实物时，用这份 Skill 找到它，或与馆员核实为什么现在拿不到。这里的输入不是模糊主题搜索，输出也不是完成借书或预约；找到架上资料只是下一步使用的前提。

### 准备与输入

带上馆方记录的题名、作者、格式、馆点、藏区和完整索书号（若有），并再次看当前状态。确认你走进的是记录所列馆点，而不是同一系统的别处分馆。若记录标闭架、馆内请求、电子资料、已借出或预约架，遵守馆方相应服务指示，不自行闯入工作人员区。无需为普通开放书架查找输入账户凭据；如果某服务需要账号，那是另一流程。行动、视觉或语言支持可在服务台提出，不必独自摸遍所有书架。

### 执行

1. **先确认真正的藏区。** 按馆内指示牌或馆员指引找到该记录对应的开放书架区域；把书籍、期刊、影音或特殊收藏分别核对。藏区与实际楼层不明就问，不把目录的一个数字直接当楼层号。
2. **按本馆索书号逐段定位。** 先找大类或架端范围，再沿架标比对完整号码/字母，注意小数、作者代码或分册号；本馆使用的排列法优先，不把另一国家/系统的排列规则硬套过来。把记录放大或请馆员读出标签也可以。
3. **核对拿到的实物。** 比较书脊/封面上的题名、作者、版次或卷号与记录；相邻册、另一语言版或同名新旧版不自动算同一本。若你只需同主题替代，先明确更改目标，再看它是否满足需求。
4. **找不到时做有限排查。** 看相邻架、展示台/回收车是否按馆方允许可查看，重新核对馆点和状态。不要翻工作人员物品或进入封闭区。带题名和索书号问馆员是否有改架、借出、处理中或预约等当前原因；空架本身不能证明馆藏不存在。
5. **记录真实结论。** 找到时记你拿到哪本、在哪个允许区域；未找到时记馆员确认的当前状态和下一条正式服务路径。若馆员也无法确定，写“当前未找到，原因待核”，不升级为“图书馆没有”。归还未借出的浏览物按馆方指示放置；借出/预约另做。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你拿到了与目标记录吻合、允许现场取用的实物；或者经有限排查和馆员确认，知道当前为什么不能取得或仍未确认。只站在空架前猜“丢了”不算核实，不得擅自带走尚未借出的资料。

- **目录显示可用，架上没有：** 再查一次状态、相邻位置，再向馆员提供完整记录；“可用”也可能有状态延迟。
- **索书号读不懂：** 请馆员指示本馆排列法和正确架区，不用网上另一馆的代码表瞎猜。
- **拿到相似却非目标版次：** 退回馆方指定浏览归位方式，重新查完整记录；需要替代时明确改目标。
- **书架或路线不适合你的行动/视觉能力：** 请馆员或现场正式服务协助，不钻进不能进入的通道。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

馆点布局、索书号体系、闭架和预约规则因地区与馆而变，本篇只给可迁移的核对顺序，不承诺特定馆的架位。馆员可以帮助定位，实际排队时间也不同。找到实物不等于可带出馆；续借、预约、借书与归还属于服务任务。此流程留下实物或经过核实的不能取得状态，不需要复制整本书。

## English

Use this Skill when you already have **one specific catalog record** and need to turn its branch, call number, and status into an item you can see at the library—or verify with staff why you cannot reach it now. The input is not a vague topic search, and the output is not a completed loan or hold. Finding an item is only the first step toward using it.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring the official record's title, author, format, branch, collection location, and full call number if shown, then check its current status again. Make sure you entered the branch named in the record, not another branch in the same system. For closed stacks, request-only, digital, checked-out, or holds-shelf items, follow the actual library's service instructions; do not enter staff-only space. Looking on an ordinary open shelf need not require account credentials. If a service does, that is another workflow. Ask at the desk for mobility, sight, or language support instead of searching every aisle alone.

### Execution

1. **Find the actual collection area.** Use in-building signs or staff directions for the open-shelf section named in the record. Distinguish books, magazines, media, and special collections. Ask when the section or floor is unclear; a number in the catalog is not necessarily a floor number.
2. **Follow this library's call number in parts.** Find the broad range on shelf-end labels and compare the full letters and numbers along the shelf. Include decimals, author codes, or volume marks where present. Follow this library's shelving order rather than importing another system's rules. Enlarge the record or ask staff to read a label if needed.
3. **Check the physical item.** Match title, author, edition, or volume on cover or spine to the record. A nearby volume, language, or edition does not automatically satisfy the exact request. If a same-topic substitute would work, explicitly change your goal and verify the alternative.
4. **Troubleshoot within bounds.** Recheck the branch and status and look at adjacent shelves or a display or return cart only where visitors are allowed. Do not search staff property or closed rooms. Ask staff, with title and call number, whether it moved, was borrowed, is being processed, or is held. An empty shelf alone does not prove the library lacks it.
5. **Record the real outcome.** If found, note which item and allowed area. Otherwise note the staff-confirmed current reason and next official service route. If staff cannot establish the reason, write “not found now; reason unconfirmed,” not “the library has none.” Put browsed items back or on a return cart as the library directs. Borrowing and holds happen separately.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have the matching physical item you may handle on site, or a bounded search plus staff confirmation of why it cannot be obtained now or remains unconfirmed. A guess at an empty shelf is not verification. Do not carry an unchecked-out item out of the library.

- **Catalog says available but shelf is empty:** Recheck current status and nearby places, then show the full record to staff. Status can lag the shelf.
- **You cannot parse the call number:** Ask staff about this library's order and area instead of applying another library's chart.
- **A similar copy is the wrong edition:** Follow the library's instructions for returning it to the shelf or cart, then search the exact record again. Explicitly change the goal if an alternative will do.
- **The route or shelf is inaccessible:** Ask staff or the library's formal assistance service; do not enter restricted passages.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Branches, call-number systems, closed stacks, and holds differ by region and library. This is a transferable check order, not a promise about a particular shelf. Staff can help locate material, with variable waiting time. Finding a copy does not make it borrowable; borrowing, renewing, placing holds, and returning are service tasks. The outcome is an item or a verified current access state, not a copied book.
