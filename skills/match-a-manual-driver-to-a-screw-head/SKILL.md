## 中文

当某件普通、非承重用品的说明允许你用手动螺丝刀操作，而你面对几种相似刀头时加载。你要在用力前**选出与真实螺丝头型及大小贴合、状态完好的刀头，或停下确认**。这里不清点包装零件、不拆修电器机械燃气设备，也不处理承重、防护或其他安全关键紧固件。

### 准备与输入

先看准确用品/步骤说明是否给出刀头类型和尺寸；有给出就核对实物，不能只凭图上“像十字”。选择你能安全看见并接近、不会带电的普通螺丝头，按手工具说明戴合适护目镜。螺丝刀若手柄裂开、刀头磨圆或变形，不继续试配。必要时放大照片或请可信者读回规格。

### 执行

1. **辨认槽型。** 对照说明与实物，先区分一字、不同十字、星形等真实形状；形状不清、特殊防拆头或头部已损坏时标未知，别把一字刀尖横插进十字槽代替。
2. **在不转动的状态比较大小。** 用对应槽型的候选刀头轻放进槽内，比较相邻尺寸。合适刀头能充分坐入并贴合边缘，不在槽内明显晃动，也不因为太大只浅浅搭在顶部；一字刀头还不能伸出螺丝头刮到周围材料。
3. **检查整件工具与操作空间。** 刀头、手柄牢固完好，轴线能对准螺丝头，手和旁人不在刀尖滑出方向。空间太窄无法对正时换合适短柄或经厂家允许的方法，不斜着硬拧。
4. **记选择或停点。** 写“实物槽型／说明指定规格 → 贴合刀头 → 仍未知的条件”。没有真正贴合的刀头就先找正确工具或向制造商确认；不要用更大力气测试“差不多”。

### 完成与停止

同一类普通螺丝有准确资料与实物都支持的手动刀头，轻放时充分咬合，工具无明显损坏；这只证明工具匹配，不证明组装、承重或结构安全。头型不清、刀头打滑/损坏、附近是电气或其他危险组件时停下，交给适当说明或专业人员。

### 常见报错与补救

- **十字刀能放进去却松晃：** 对照确切十字种类与相邻尺寸，换贴合者；不靠向下猛压弥补不匹配。
- **刀头只碰到槽口：** 可能过大或头型不同，重新核对，别开始转。
- **一字刀宽于螺丝头：** 换不会刮周围材料的正确宽厚规格。
- **刀头已磨圆、手柄有裂：** 换完好工具；继续用会增加滑出与伤物风险。
- **看不清槽型或说明缺失：** 放大、补光或请可信者核对；仍未知则停，不猜。

### 假设、替代与副作用

仅限厂家允许的低风险、非结构性手动操作。螺丝刀不是撬棍、錾子或带电作业工具；绝缘外观也不授权你维修电气设备。不能稳定握持、看清或对正时，可以由有能力且获准的人完成工具匹配，自己保留停止决定。副作用是“十字差不多都一样”的经验需要申请复审。

## English

Load this Skill when the instructions for an ordinary non-load-bearing item permit a hand screwdriver and several tips look similar. Before applying turning force, **choose an undamaged tip that truly matches the real head profile and size, or pause for confirmation**. This does not inventory supplied parts, repair electrical, mechanical or gas equipment, or handle load-bearing, protective or other safety-critical fasteners.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether the exact item's instructions name a driver type and size, then compare with the physical screw. A drawing that “looks like a cross” is not enough. Use only an ordinary, visible and safely reachable screw away from electrical work, and wear suitable safety eyewear under the hand-tool instructions. Do not trial-fit a tool with a cracked handle or rounded or bent tip. Enlarge a photo or ask a trusted person to read the specification if needed.

### Execution

1. **Identify the recess.** Compare instructions and head to distinguish slotted, distinct cross profiles, star shapes and other actual forms. Mark an unclear, tamper-resistant or already damaged head unknown. Do not substitute a flat blade across a cross recess.
2. **Compare size without turning.** Rest candidate tips of the matching profile gently in the recess, including adjacent sizes. A fitting tip seats well and contacts the edges without obvious wobble; an oversized one only touches the top. A slotted tip must not extend past the head and scrape surrounding material.
3. **Check tool and working access.** Tip and handle are secure and intact, the shaft can line up with the screw, and hands or bystanders are not in a possible slip path. If the space prevents alignment, use an appropriate short driver or a method the manufacturer permits, not angled force.
4. **Record a choice or stop.** Note “actual profile / stated size → fitted manual tip → remaining unknowns.” If nothing truly fits, obtain the right tool or ask the manufacturer. More force is not a fit test.

### Success and stop

This type of ordinary screw has a manual tip supported by both the exact information and physical fit; it seats fully and the tool is sound. This proves tool fit, not completed assembly, load capacity or structural safety. Stop for an unclear profile, slipping or damaged tip, or nearby electrical or other hazardous components, and use appropriate instructions or qualified help.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A cross tip enters but wobbles:** Recheck the precise cross type and adjacent sizes. Do not replace fit with force.
- **The tip rests only on top:** It may be too large or the wrong profile. Reassess before turning.
- **A slotted tip extends beyond the head:** Use a correctly sized blade that will not scrape the material.
- **A rounded tip or cracked handle:** Replace the tool; continuing increases slip and damage risk.
- **The head or instruction is unreadable:** Enlarge, improve light or ask a trusted readback. Stop if it remains unknown.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is only a low-risk manufacturer-permitted non-structural hand operation. A screwdriver is not a pry bar, chisel or tool for live electrical work; an insulated appearance does not authorise equipment repair. If you cannot grip, see or align it reliably, a capable authorised person can match the tool while you retain the decision to stop. “All cross heads are close enough” needs review.
