## 中文

当你有一块**已经完全干燥的自己画的水彩**，想在不盖住底色的情况下改变它的色相或深度时，加载这份 Skill。结果是可对照的透明叠色小区。它不同于湿纸混色、调色盘里预混或 H271 铅笔明暗图；关键门槛是底层已干、第二层足够薄。

### 准备与输入

用手背靠近但不蹭画面确认纸面不凉湿、无光亮湿斑；若不确定，继续平放待干。选标签显示透明或适合薄涂的普通水彩，在边角测试；不透明颜料可能遮住底层。准备干净画笔、清水、吸水纸，把颜料水远离餐食和电子设备。原作品珍贵时先在同种纸的练习色块试。

### 执行

1. **留一部分底色作对照。** 在干色块上轻标一个小叠色区，旁边保留未叠区域；这样干后能确认变化，而非依记忆猜。
2. **调薄且可控的第二色。** 在调色处把少量透明颜料与水调开，笔载液不过量。先在边缘小试，见底色仍能透出来再继续。
3. **轻铺一次，停止回刷。** 用少数平稳笔触覆盖小区，避免反复摩擦已上色纸纤维；如果底色开始移动或混浑，立即停，不加更多水试图“修正”。
4. **自然干燥后比较。** 平放晾干，再对照未叠区看色相或深度是否改变、底层是否仍可见。记下颜料名和“底层干/疑似未干”的实际情况，不把糊掉的湿混称为成功叠色。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

干后小区颜色可见改变，底色仍透出，旁有未叠区域可比。只有第二层湿亮、或底层被搓掉，不算合格的透明叠色。

- **底色被带起：** 停笔待干，下次更轻更少回刷，并确认底层干；纸/颜料仍会有不同耐受。
- **新色把底层盖住：** 检查颜料透明度、减薄并在练习纸重试，不强刷原作品。
- **水形成边缘深痕：** 下次减小笔载水并一次完成小区；半干时不要来回擦。
- **干后看不出变化：** 真实记录“变化不明显”，下次选择可区分的色或小幅提高浓度。
- **视觉或手部控制不便：** 用较大色块、稳定垫板和可信颜色描述，由你决定是否达到可比变化。

### 假设与边界

不同颜料透明度和染色性不同，不能承诺底层永不移动或与屏幕颜色相同。本篇只在自己的普通练习纸上做一层，不讲专业作品修复、特殊媒介或强制配方。

## English

Load this Skill when you have a **completely dry watercolour area you painted** and want to change its hue or depth while leaving the first colour visible. Finish with a small comparable transparent second layer. This differs from wet-paper blending, mixing on a palette, or H271's pencil value map. The first layer must be dry and the second thin.

### Preparation and inputs

Check for remaining cool dampness or wet shine without rubbing the painted area; when unsure, leave it flat longer. Choose ordinary watercolour labelled transparent or suitable for thin use, and test at an edge; opaque paint may cover the first layer. Set out clean brush, water, and absorbent paper, keeping paint water away from food and electronics. Practice on the same kind of paper before a valued original.

### Execution

1. **Leave a comparison area.** Lightly define a small glazing region on the dry colour, retaining adjacent unglazed colour so a dry result can be checked without memory.
2. **Mix a thin controlled second colour.** Dilute a little transparent pigment on the palette. Avoid a dripping brush. Test a corner and proceed only if the first colour still shows through.
3. **Lay one gentle coat and stop.** Cover the small area in a few steady strokes without scrubbing already painted fibres. If the first colour begins to move or muddy, stop rather than adding more water as a supposed fix.
4. **Dry naturally and compare.** After drying flat, compare glazed and unglazed areas for visible hue or depth change and a still-visible first colour. Note both pigment names and whether the base really was dry. A muddy wet mix is not successful glazing.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The dry glazed area visibly changes while the underlying colour remains visible, with an unglazed part beside it. A shiny wet coat or a scrubbed-away base is not a finished transparent glaze.

- **Base colour lifts:** Stop and dry. Next time use a lighter touch, fewer passes, and a fully dry base; papers and pigments still differ.
- **Second colour hides the first:** Check transparency, dilute, and retry on practice paper instead of brushing a valued original harder.
- **Water makes a dark edge:** Carry less water and finish a small region in one pass next time; do not rub partly dry paper.
- **No change appears after drying:** Record the weak result and choose a more distinct colour or slightly stronger mix next time.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Use a larger test patch, stable board, and trusted colour description while you decide whether the change is comparable.

### Assumptions and limits

Pigments differ in transparency and staining; no promise says the base never moves or matches a screen colour. This is one layer on owned ordinary practice paper, not professional conservation, special mediums, or a fixed formula.
