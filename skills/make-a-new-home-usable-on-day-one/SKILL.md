## 中文

当你刚到新住处、箱子可能还没分完，但今天就需要睡眠、洗漱、饮水或其他基本日常活动时加载。你要让**至少一条从入口到实际可用区域的生活路线成立**，并说清哪些活动仍不能做、用什么可行替代。这里不做长期归位、维修水电燃气、结构调整或承重家具组装。

### 准备与输入

与当天会使用空间的人核对今晚到明早的实际活动和可及需求；不默认每人都用相同床、浴室或厨房安排。找已随行或已抵达的用品、已允许使用的区域，以及能安全协助的人。先看门、地面和明显状态：若有可见漏水、裸露电线、异常气味、无法正常进入等问题，停止使用受影响区域，联系物业、服务方或合适专业人员；不要尝试修理。

### 执行

1. **按今天的活动定最小范围。** 先选实际需要的睡眠位置、可用的洗漱点、饮水或简单进食位置，以及它们之间的路径。没有哪项活动发生，就不要为一个通用清单先布满整个家。
2. **清出可通行的到达路线。** 从入口走向这些点，移走你有权且能安全挪动的轻量空箱和包装；较重、阻路或共用空间的东西交给合适负责人。不要堵住要使用的门或出口，也不要把暂存箱放进使用者必经的位置。
3. **把可达用品带到对应区域。** 将已到达的寝具、普通洗漱用品、水杯、照明或充电用品等按实际活动放在能取到的位置。床或其他设备只有在已经可安全使用、或由合适人员按实际说明完成安装后才算可用；本 Skill 不教安装或接线。个人物品由本人或有权者决定摆放。
4. **让真正使用的人试走一次日常路径。** 在不做危险操作的前提下，确认能进出、找到用品、使用已正常工作的设施，并返回。看不清标识、够不到用品、打不开门或路线被箱子再次占用，都说明这个区域尚未对该人可用。
5. **记下可用与未可用。** 用简单的“活动 → 位置 → 已能使用／缺什么 → 谁跟进或用何替代”表交给同住者。缺关键物品时，选经本人同意的实际替代或其他可用地点；真正的到货延误另交后续追踪。非必需箱、装饰与长期收纳可以后做。

### 完成与停止

今天确实要发生的基本活动至少有一条可被相关使用者走通的路径和可取用物品；不能做的活动明确标出缺口和实际替代/负责人。只把箱子放进房间、或在图上写“卧室”，不等于今晚能使用。设施异常、路径无法安全通行或替代尚不存在时停在“未可用”，联系有权处理者而不是自己排修。

### 常见报错与补救

- **寝具到了，睡眠位置还不能用：** 先确认现有安全可用位置；需要组装或专业处理就安排合适的人，或选择使用者接受的其他现实住处，不把箱子堆成临时承重家具。
- **浴室有用品但无法正常用：** 停止使用受影响处，联系有权处理者；在可行条件下选另一个经许可的洗漱点，不自行拆管线或电器。
- **清出的路又被后到箱件占满：** 告诉接收者保留的通道，轻量物品按权限移走，再让实际使用者复核。
- **某人看不清或够不到：** 用本人能辨识的位置、对比标记、触觉/语音说明或愿意的协助者调整，再由本人验证，而不是替对方宣布“可用”。
- **第一晚物品迟到：** 标出缺项、责任人及经确认的临时方案，转后续到货追踪；不把预计送达写成已到。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里处理普通生活区域的首日可用性，不判断房屋法律义务、设施安全认证或医疗需求。地域、家庭结构、费用、感官及行动能力会改变最小范围；有些人需要更多辅助用品或不同的现实住宿选择。你可以在固定位置协调，委托愿意且有能力的人处理轻量物品，不为完成表格强迫任何人搬重箱。副作用是漂亮的全屋拆箱进度要为真实可用区让路。

## English

Load this Skill when you have just reached a new home, boxes may still be in transit or unsorted, and you need to sleep, wash, drink or carry out another basic routine today. You will make **at least one real route from entry to the areas the household can actually use**, and identify any routine still unavailable with a feasible alternative. This does not establish permanent storage, repair water, electrical or gas systems, change the building or assemble load-bearing furniture.

### Preparation and inputs

Ask the people using the space what they will really do between now and tomorrow morning, including their access needs. Do not assume one bed, bathroom or kitchen arrangement suits everybody. Locate carried or arrived supplies, areas you may use, and people able to help safely. Check doors, floor and obvious conditions first. If you see a leak, exposed wiring, unusual smell or inability to enter normally, stop using the affected area and contact building management, the provider or an appropriate professional. Do not attempt repair.

### Execution

1. **Choose the smallest scope from today's routines.** Identify the sleep spot, usable washing point, water or simple food area, and paths between them that are actually needed. Do not fill a whole home from a generic list for activities nobody will do today.
2. **Clear reachable routes.** From the entrance to those points, move light empty cartons and packing materials that you are allowed and able to move. Ask an appropriate person about heavy objects or shared space. Do not block doors or exits that must work, or stage boxes in someone's necessary path.
3. **Put reachable supplies with their activity.** Bring arrived bedding, ordinary washing supplies, a drinking cup, lighting or charging items where their users can get them. Count a bed or other equipment as usable only when already safe to use or set up by suitable people following its real instructions; this Skill does not teach assembly or wiring. The person or authorised holder decides where private items go.
4. **Have real users try their routine path.** Without doing hazardous work, confirm they can enter, find supplies, use facilities that already work normally, and return. An unreadable mark, unreachable item, door that will not open or a path refilled with cartons means the area is not yet usable for that person.
5. **Record usable and unavailable routines.** Share a short `activity → place → works now / missing item → follow-up owner or alternative` list with the household. If a key item is absent, choose a real substitute or other permitted place with the user's agreement. Track genuine delivery delays separately. Ordinary boxes, decoration and permanent storage can wait.

### Success and stop

Each basic activity truly needed today has a path the relevant person has tried and supplies they can reach, or an explicit missing part and real alternative or owner. A box in the room or “bedroom” on a sketch does not prove tonight's use. If a facility behaves abnormally, the path is unsafe or the alternative does not exist, mark it unavailable and contact the responsible party rather than attempting repair.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Bedding arrived but the sleep place is unusable:** Find an existing safe option. Arrange appropriate assembly help if required or another real place the user accepts; do not stack cartons into load-bearing furniture.
- **Washing supplies are present but the bathroom cannot work normally:** Stop using the affected area and contact the responsible party. Choose another permitted washing point if one is actually available; do not dismantle pipes or appliances.
- **Later boxes refill the clear route:** Tell receivers which path must stay open, move light items within your authority and ask the person using the route to check it again.
- **Someone cannot see or reach it:** Adjust location, contrast, tactile or audio cues or willing help to that person's needs, then have them verify. Do not pronounce a zone usable on their behalf.
- **First-night goods are delayed:** Record what is absent, who follows up and a confirmed temporary plan. Expected arrival is not observed arrival.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is first-day usability for ordinary living areas, not legal housing duties, facility certification or medical guidance. Region, household, money, sensory access and mobility change the minimum set. Some people need more assistive items or a different real place to stay. You can coordinate from one position and ask willing capable people to handle light objects; nobody must lift heavy cartons to complete a list. The side effect is that a photo-ready whole-home unpacking plan yields to a few areas people can actually use.
