## 中文

当你要买一台用于普通家庭食谱的厨房秤，却只看到“最大 5 千克、精确到 0.1 克”之类宣传时，加载这份 Skill。目标是确认**容器加食材的最大总重量**能放入量程、最小常用食材能获得有意义读数，且你能看见或听见读数。这里不用于药品、婴儿喂养、商业计价或食品安全关键称量。

### 准备与输入

从真实会做的几道食谱找最小和最大称量，例如少量香料与一大碗面团；记录常用碗/盘本身重量或可靠估计。准备候选准确型号的最大容量、显示分度、制造商声明的最小可称量和准确度/误差资料、去皮功能、单位、秤面尺寸及显示位置。没有误差资料时不要把显示位数当准确度。

### 执行

1. **先量任务两端。** 写出最小一次要加的食材和最大整碗重量。碗在秤上的重量仍占秤的载荷，即使按“去皮”后显示归零；只看净食材重量可能超量程。
2. **核最大载荷。** 把最大容器与食材总量同准确型号额定容量比较，留下实际操作余量。若需用大锅搅拌，先确认秤面能稳放锅且读数不会被遮挡；本 Skill 不要求在秤上混合食材。
3. **核小量读数。** 比较最小常用量与显示分度、制造商最小载荷及准确度说明。显示每 0.1 克跳一次只描述显示间隔，不自动证明 0.1 克可可靠称量。小量达不到说明范围时考虑另一个适合小量的工具，不靠猜测加料。
4. **核现实可用性。** 看是否提供你需要的 g/oz 等单位、稳定的去皮操作、可见或可用其他方式取得的屏幕读数，以及电池/供电条件。碗压住屏幕或按钮时，容量再大也难用。
5. **留下裁决。** 记录“最小食材与最大容器总重 → 候选量程/分度/误差资料 → 平台和读数条件 → 覆盖、只覆盖部分或未确认”。没有最小量或误差信息时，不能宣称细微配料得到精确结果。

### 成功与停止

你能说明候选对常用大负载和小负载分别能否给出有根据的读数，才完成购前核对。涉及药物、医学营养、婴儿配方、按重量计价交易或需要已校准精密测量时停止普通厨房秤流程，转用相应专业要求。本 Skill 也不负责实际校准或称重操作。

### 常见报错与补救

- **去皮后忘记碗仍占量程：** 用碗加食材的总重量重查最大载荷。
- **显示 0.1 克就当准确度 0.1 克：** 查制造商的最小称量和误差说明；缺资料时保留不确定。
- **能称大碗，却称不出少量香料：** 明确“只覆盖大负载”，改用适合小量的秤或其他可靠方法。
- **大碗挡住屏幕、细字看不清：** 查看显示位置、可读说明或语音/外接显示等可用方案；不能安全读数就换候选。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程只处理普通家用食谱，不把商业秤的认证规则直接套在家庭秤上。单位和配方会随地区变化，先统一质量单位。纸面规格只能筛选型号，不能证明新机到手后的实际准确度；你可能发现一台大秤不能顺便承担所有微量任务。

### 来源

- [美国 NIST：公制厨房工具](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/metric-kitchen/metric-kitchen-cookware-and-tools) — 购厨房秤时应考虑食材加碗的总重，小量食材可能需要较小量程，去皮不消除物理载荷。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a kitchen scale for ordinary home recipes when the listing mainly says “5 kg maximum” or “reads to 0.1 g.” Your task is to check whether the **heaviest bowl plus ingredients** lies within its load range, the smallest common ingredient gets a meaningful reading, and the display is accessible. This is not for medicine, infant feeding, commercial pricing, or food-safety-critical dosing.

### Preparation and inputs

Take a few recipes you really make and identify the smallest and largest amounts, such as a pinch-sized ingredient and a large bowl of dough. Record the usual bowl or plate's weight or a reliable estimate. Find the exact model's maximum load, display increment, stated minimum useful load and accuracy/error information, tare function, units, platform size, and display position. Missing error data cannot be filled in by counting decimal places.

### Execution

1. **Record both ends of the job.** Name the smallest addition and the heaviest complete bowl. The bowl still loads the scale after tare makes the display show zero; checking only net ingredients may exceed the physical capacity.
2. **Check maximum load.** Compare bowl-plus-ingredients with the rated capacity and leave practical headroom. For a large vessel, confirm it sits stably and does not cover the readout. This Skill does not ask you to mix ingredients on the scale.
3. **Check small readings.** Compare the smallest ordinary amount with the display division, maker-stated minimum and accuracy information. A screen changing every 0.1 g describes an increment, not proof of reliable 0.1 g weighing. If the task falls outside the supported range, choose another small-load tool rather than guessing an amount.
4. **Check real usability.** Look for the units you need, workable tare, a display you can read or access another way, and acceptable power requirements. A bowl hiding the screen or controls makes an otherwise large scale hard to use.
5. **Record a limited decision.** Write “smallest ingredient and heaviest gross load → range/division/error information → platform/readout → covers both, covers some, or unconfirmed.” Without minimum-load or error information, do not claim precision for tiny ingredients.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can explain separately whether the candidate gives supported readings for your common heavy and light loads. Stop this ordinary kitchen-scale route for drugs, medical nutrition, infant formula, weight-priced transactions, or calibrated precision work; those have separate professional requirements. This Skill does not calibrate or operate the scale.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The bowl was forgotten after tare:** Recheck capacity using total bowl-plus-food weight.
- **“Displays 0.1 g” became “accurate to 0.1 g”:** Find the maker's minimum and error information; otherwise keep precision unknown.
- **It handles a bowl but not a small spice amount:** State “covers heavy loads only” and use an appropriate small-load scale or another reliable method.
- **The bowl blocks the display or fine print is unreadable:** Check display placement and readable or audio/remote-output options. Choose another candidate if readings cannot be obtained safely.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary home recipes. Commercial-scale certification rules are not automatically household rules. Align mass units across recipes and listings. Specifications screen candidates but do not prove the delivered unit's actual accuracy. You may discover that one large scale cannot also solve every tiny-quantity job.

### Sources

- [US NIST: Metric kitchen tools](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/metric-si/metric-kitchen/metric-kitchen-cookware-and-tools) — include bowl plus ingredients when selecting capacity; small ingredients may need a smaller-range scale, and tare does not remove physical load.
- [US NIST: Scale divisions and selection](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/06/considerations-class-ii-scale-applications) — display division is related to but not identical with accuracy, and selection considers minimum and maximum loads; commercial Class II rules are not imposed on home scales here.
