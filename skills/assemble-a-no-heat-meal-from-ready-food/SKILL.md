## 中文

当你想**现在就吃一顿普通饭**、不使用炉具，而且手边已有几项按[即食状态核对](../confirm-ready-to-eat-food-for-a-no-heat-meal/SKILL.md)可直接用的食物时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一份有明确份量与用途、干净组装并可在当前条件下直接食用的餐食；不靠营养治疗公式或把生食藏在“免烹调”三个字里。规划未来几天菜单属于 H021/H022，食物入户保存另有流程。

### 准备与输入

确认现在几个人吃、这顿大致需要多少、有哪些**已核实可即食**的材料、谁有不能忽略的标签限制、是否有可靠冷藏及清洁的手/盘/餐具。已有但状态不清楚的食材先不算；需清洗的果蔬要有可用的安全流动水。若需严重过敏或医疗饮食方案，按已有专业方案，不在这份普通 Skill 中临时设计。

### 执行步骤

1. **从实际吃饭需求定一份量。** 按你们真实饥饿、时间和已有东西选一个能作为这顿主要部分的即食食品，再加你愿意吃且安全适用的配料或伴食；不要求每餐按外部固定比例凑齐类别。若数量不够，明确是小餐/加一份可用即食物，而不是拿“需要煮”的东西填空。
2. **让组装区与餐具干净。** 洗手，使用清洁盘、刀、勺与台面。原来碰过生肉/禽/海鲜或未清洁物品的工具先按食品安全要求处理，不直接切即食食物；普通果蔬按其标签和当地可靠指导清洗，明确预洗即食的按包装使用。
3. **把冷藏材料留到最后一段。** 若有需冷藏食品，只有冷藏史可信且能到食用时维持其条件时才取出；先摆好其余材料和餐具，再开封/取用所需份量。没有冷藏或时间条件时改用符合条件的常温即食方案，不让“反正不做饭”覆盖标签。
4. **按实际用途组合。** 在干净盘中把主要食物、配料与口感/调味按个人习惯放在一起；酱料、油醋或脆食可在快吃时加入以保留预期质地。不要把未核实的原料或过敏限制食品当作装饰。需要刀工或复杂调制时可改用已切/可直接取用的合格产品。
5. **立即收尾为一份可食用输出。** 每人拿到与其限制和份量相符的一份，确认没有任何“还需加热/洗净/解冻”的步骤。已开封剩余品按实际包装、冷藏与[保存流程](../put-away-groceries/SKILL.md)处理；若无法满足保存条件，不把它默默留在台面等下一顿。

### 完成与停止

你有一份现在可以直接吃、来源与保存条件清楚、份量足够本次用途的餐食，以及剩余食物的明确去向，就完成了。关键材料并非即食、卫生/清洗/冷藏条件缺失或食物安全史不清时停止那项，缩小餐食或改计划；成餐不要求消灭所有库存。

### 常见报错与补救

- **搭了一半才发现某项须加热：** 从当前盘中撤掉，改用确认即食的东西；不能靠切小或多加酱把“须煮熟”变成可冷食。
- **果蔬需要洗但没有安全水：** 换包装明确预洗即食且保存合格的材料或暂缓，不用清洁剂擦洗食物。
- **冷藏食品开封后一直放桌上：** 按标签和当地指导尽快食用/保存；时间温度史不清时不用气味解释。
- **配料越加越多却不成一餐：** 回到本次实际份量和一个明确主要部分，删掉无用途的零散材料。
- **别人有明确限制但你猜口味/配料：** 分开已知安全部分并先确认，不以“大家都吃”代替对方条件。

### 假设、替代与副作用

“一餐”份量与组合受个人、文化、预算和食欲影响，没有通用营养比例；本 Skill 不给医疗饮食或食品过敏处置。普通即食不等于无保存要求，地区和商品标签优先。视力、握力、站立或厨房设备有限时可坐着用易开、预切、明确即食的商品与易握餐具，或请现场可信的人协助；无法安全开封就换可用食品。现实副作用是你可能少洗一口锅，但多看到一次包装的“开封后”小字。

## English

Load this Skill when you want **one ordinary meal now without a cooker** and already have several foods confirmed usable as-is under the [ready-to-eat check](../confirm-ready-to-eat-food-for-a-no-heat-meal/SKILL.md). Finish with a portion for the actual eating occasion, assembled with clean tools and ready to eat under current conditions. No therapeutic nutrition ratio or hidden raw-food step is required. Multi-day menu planning and food storage are separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm who eats now, roughly how much this meal must provide, which foods are **verified ready to eat**, any restrictions you must honour, reliable chilling, and clean hands, dishes, and tools. Exclude food whose condition is unknown. Produce requiring washing needs safe running water. Follow an existing professional plan for severe allergies or medical diets rather than inventing one in this ordinary Skill.

### Execution steps

1. **Start with the actual meal amount.** Choose one suitable ready-to-eat component that can serve as the main part for your own appetite and occasion. Add safe, wanted sides or toppings. There is no fixed category ratio for every meal. If it is too little, call it a small meal or add another verified ready food instead of filling the plate with something that requires cooking.
2. **Use a clean assembly area and tools.** Wash hands and use clean plates, knives, spoons, and work surfaces. Tools that touched raw meat, poultry, seafood, or dirty objects need proper cleaning before ready food. Wash ordinary produce under its label and reliable local guidance; use clearly pre-washed ready-to-eat produce as its package directs.
3. **Leave chilled items until the last stage.** Use them only with credible prior refrigeration and a way to meet their conditions until serving. Set out other components and dishes first, then take or open the amount needed. If chilling or time conditions cannot be met, choose suitable shelf-stable ready food instead of assuming “no cooking” overrides the label.
4. **Combine for a real use.** Put the main food, safe additions, and a preferred texture or seasoning together on clean dishes. Dressings or crisp parts can be added near service for the intended texture. An unverified ingredient or restricted item is not decoration. Use approved pre-cut or ready portions if cutting or mixing would exceed your safe capacity.
5. **Close with an edible output now.** Each person has an appropriate portion, with no unresolved “heat, wash, or thaw before eating” step. Handle opened remaining food under its actual package, chilling, and [storage process](../put-away-groceries/SKILL.md). Do not leave it silently on a counter for another meal when storage requirements cannot be met.

### Success and stop

You have a meal that can be eaten now with clear source and storage conditions, enough for this occasion, and an explicit path for remaining food. Remove an item and shrink or change the meal when it is not ready to eat or clean washing, chilling, or reliable history is missing. A meal does not have to eliminate the pantry.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Halfway through assembly, an item says heat before eating:** Remove it and use confirmed ready food. Cutting smaller or adding sauce cannot make “cook thoroughly” safe to eat cold.
- **Produce needs washing but no safe water is available:** Use clearly pre-washed ready-to-eat food with valid storage, or wait; do not wash food with cleaning chemicals.
- **Chilled food sits out after opening:** Follow its label and local guidance for eating or storage. Smell cannot fill an unknown time-temperature record.
- **Many toppings still do not form a meal:** Return to the actual amount and one clear main component, then remove pieces without a job.
- **Another person's restrictions are guessed:** Keep known suitable parts separate and confirm first. “Everyone eats it” does not establish their conditions.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Meal size and composition vary with person, culture, budget, and appetite; there is no universal nutrition ratio here. This does not manage medical diets or allergy reactions. Ordinary ready food can still require chilling; regional and package rules take priority. For limited sight, grip, standing, or kitchen equipment, assemble while seated with easy-open, pre-cut, clearly ready products and manageable utensils, or use in-person help. Change the item if it cannot be opened safely. You may wash one pan less and read one more “after opening” label.
