## 中文

当你已经确认眼前用品的厂家和完整型号/货号，需要在使用、普通设置或非结构性组装前找到真正适用的说明时加载。你要拿到**来源可靠、型号/变体和文件用途均对应的使用或组装说明，或明确还不能操作**。这里不执行说明里的维修、接线、燃气、钻孔、承重/防护安装，也不凭相似型号猜按钮。

### 准备与输入

写清要做的一个普通任务和完整标识，必要时保留地区或修订后缀。先检查随件纸质说明，再到厂商官方产品或支持页面找文档；从搜索结果进入时核对域名与产品页，不下载/运行未知程序或安装包。无法访问在线资料时可问厂家客服要准确文件或请可信者读纸本，不依赖 AI 才能完成。

### 执行

1. **按准确标识检索官方入口。** 在厂家站内使用完整型号/货号；如果只有系列页，继续找到包含准确变体的产品记录。不同网站可能把说明放在 `Product details`、`Assembly & documents` 或 `Owner support`，别把某品牌菜单名当通用规则。
2. **区分文档用途。** 你需要的是实际任务对应的用户说明、使用/护理或组装页。安装专业文件、维修技师手册、零件图和宣传页不能因为同型号就自动替代用户步骤；看见专业工具、内部部件或安全关键操作时退出本 Everyday 范围。
3. **核对封面与适用范围。** 比对文件封面/页脚的完整型号、变体、地区、修订日期或版本，以及“覆盖型号列表”里是否真的包含实物。一个 PDF 涵盖多个型号时，只读与你这一款相关的条件和图示；缺后缀或文档相互冲突就联系厂家确认。
4. **确认你的任务在允许范围。** 在说明中找到对应章节、所需条件、停止提示与最终状态。若当前设备或零件状态不符合前提，不照做；按钮外观相似、网帖写“通用做法”也不能替代准确说明。
5. **留一个可再次找到的记录。** 记官方产品页、文档标题/版本、对应型号和你要读的章节；在权限允许下保存纸本页码或官方 PDF 的可访问副本。让实际操作者读回“这本是给哪款、哪一步、哪些事不能做”。

### 完成与停止

你有明确的官方来源、正确文档类型、实物型号/变体对应和可找到的具体用户级章节；这表示找到说明，不表示实际操作已完成。型号不匹配、只有服务/安装文档、版次冲突、无可读的必要步骤或说明要求危险/专业工作时暂停，向厂家/有资质渠道确认。

### 常见报错与补救

- **搜索排名第一是相似款的 PDF：** 回到厂家产品页，比较完整后缀与覆盖型号，错款不操作。
- **把维修图当用户说明：** 换成使用、护理或组装文件；找不到就问厂家。
- **只有同系列通用章节：** 找到明确覆盖实物型号的条件；缺失时标未确认。
- **纸本与官网版本不同：** 记录两者编号、日期和对应的实物变体，向厂家问适用版本，别自行选“更新的一定对”。
- **文字太小或语言不便：** 放大官方 PDF、使用官方可访问版本或请可信者逐条读回；安全符号与停止条件必须保留。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这适用于普通用户级说明的检索核对，产品和地区网站结构会变，示例菜单不保证各厂一样。联网、阅读、视力或语言受限时，官方纸本/客服、可访问格式和可信协助者都可用；翻译不能悄悄删掉警告。费用、保修及法律解释不在此范围。副作用是第一个搜索结果不再自动获准指挥你的手。

## English

Load this Skill after confirming the maker and full model or article ID of the item in front of you, before an ordinary use, setup or non-structural assembly step. Your result is **a reliable owner or assembly guide whose model, variant and document purpose fit the task, or a clear pause**. Do not carry out repair, wiring, gas, drilling, load-bearing or protective work from whatever document appears, or guess buttons from a similar model.

### Preparation and inputs

Name the specific ordinary job and write the complete ID with any relevant region or revision suffix. Check the paper guide supplied with the item, then the maker's official product or support pages. If entering from search results, verify the actual maker domain and product record; do not download or run unknown programs or installers. If online material is inaccessible, ask official support for the accurate file or have a trusted person read the paper guide. No AI is required for the core task.

### Execution

1. **Search the official source with the exact identifier.** Use the full model or article ID on the maker's site. A family page is only a starting point until the exact variant's record is found. Sites may call sections `Product details`, `Assembly & documents` or `Owner support`; a menu name from one brand is not universal.
2. **Separate document purposes.** Choose owner, use-and-care or assembly instructions for the real task. An installer document, technician service manual, parts diagram or advert does not automatically supply user steps just because it names the model. Leave the Everyday scope when the document calls for specialist tools, internal parts or safety-critical work.
3. **Check cover and applicability.** Compare the document's full model, variant, region, revision date or version and any covered-model list with the actual item. In a PDF covering several models, keep only conditions and pictures that apply to yours. Ask the maker when a suffix is missing or documents conflict.
4. **Find the permitted section.** Locate the real task's prerequisites, stop signs and finish state. Do not act when the item or parts fail those prerequisites. A similar control panel or “universal” forum method does not replace the exact guide.
5. **Leave a retrievable record.** Note official product page, title or version, matching model and relevant section. Keep paper page numbers or an accessible copy of the official PDF where allowed. Before acting, read back which item, which step and which actions remain out of scope with anyone helping you.

### Success and stop

You have an official source, correct document type, matching real model or variant and a findable user-level section. Finding a guide is not completing the physical job. Pause and ask maker or qualified support when models differ, only service or installer material exists, revisions conflict, necessary steps are unreadable or the guide requires hazardous or professional work.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The highest search result is a similar model's PDF:** Return to the maker's product page and compare full suffix and covered models; do not operate from a wrong guide.
- **A repair diagram is treated as user guidance:** Find the owner, care or assembly file or ask the maker.
- **Only a family-wide chapter exists:** Find an explicit condition covering this exact item; mark unconfirmed if absent.
- **Paper and website editions differ:** Record both numbers, dates and item variants and ask which applies; “newer must be right” is a guess.
- **Print is too small or language is inaccessible:** Enlarge the official PDF, use an official accessible format or ask a trusted line-by-line readback, retaining safety symbols and stops.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This finds and checks ordinary user instructions. Product and regional website structure changes, so example menus do not apply everywhere. Official paper, support, accessible formats and trusted help can substitute when internet, reading, sight or language is limited. Translation must not silently drop warnings. Fees, warranty and legal interpretation stay outside. The first search result no longer automatically gets to direct your hands.
