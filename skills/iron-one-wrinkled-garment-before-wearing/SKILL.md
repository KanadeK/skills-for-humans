## 中文

当你要穿的一件**普通、干净衣物**有明显褶皱，而且衣物标签允许熨烫、手边熨斗完好且准确说明可查时，用这份 Skill 在稳定平面上整理一件。目标是褶皱减轻、衣物没有新亮斑或烫伤、熨斗安全断电冷却后再穿。标签禁熨或工具条件不齐时，选择另一件衣物比把身体当温度计更快也更可靠。

### 准备与输入

读衣物标签的熨烫图示/文字：是否禁止、点数或其他上限、是否禁止蒸汽、是否要求反面或垫布。标签模糊就放大、查对应符号指南或问制造商，不凭“摸着像棉”猜设置。查手中**准确熨斗机型**的开关、温控、蒸汽/干熨、放置和断电说明；本文默认用设备允许的干熨路径，不额外发明喷雾和蒸汽方法。确认衣服没有待处理污渍、潮湿不明部位、脆弱印花/装饰；设备电线、插头、底板完好。准备稳固熨衣板或厂家允许的耐热熨衣面，清开电线和落脚处，儿童/宠物远离。

标签禁止熨烫、机型说明找不到、设备损坏、没有稳定耐热平面时停止。这份 Skill 不维修电器，不对穿在身上的衣服熨烫或喷蒸汽。

### 执行

1. **先把两个上限交叉核对。** 衣物标签决定可承受的最高处理强度，熨斗说明决定对应的设置和使用方式；任一边不允许就不加热。混纺、印花或局部装饰可能比主体更敏感，按最严格的已知限制处理。点数是衣物标签上的限制，不用一个全球通用温度数字代替实际图示和机型。
2. **把衣服从身体转到稳定熨衣面。** 展平一个小区域，确认袖管、口袋或里布没有折在即将熨的层下面；不直接熨拉链、金属扣、胶印或不明贴饰。照标签要求选择反面或相容垫布。插电前安排熨斗可直立或放在机型指定支架上的位置，手和线不在底板行进路线。
3. **按准确机型设置并等待就绪。** 选择同时符合衣物标签和熨斗说明的设置，以设备就绪提示而非手摸底板判断是否可用。本文只走干熨；若机型需把蒸汽调到关闭，照其说明操作。不要向热熨斗随意加水，也不要把完好的电线压在熨衣板热处。
4. **先试一小块，再分段熨平。** 在不显眼、相容的衣料处按机型方法短暂试一下，查看有无发亮、收缩、变色、熔化或粘底；任何变化都停。小样正常后，轻缓移动熨斗处理可见褶皱，不把底板长时间停在一点，不用大力压出新折痕。每换一处先展平布料，让手离开热底板和蒸汽出口。
5. **停机、冷却、再穿。** 达到这次可接受的平整度就停止，不为最后一条小皱追逐越来越高的设置。按机型说明关闭、拔电并放在安全位置冷却，未冷前不触摸底板、不收进柜里。衣物也先在合适位置散热、检查是否有新痕，再穿上；急着出门时换另一件，不熨着身上补最后一道线。

### 成功条件

这件衣服的明显褶皱减少，原标签和设备限制均得到遵守，布面无新亮斑、印花损伤或烫痕，熨斗已断电且安全冷却，衣物适合穿用。标签或设备不允许时，停止热处理并换衣/找专业护理同样完成判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **标签看不清就按面料名字设温：** 停止加热，查可读标签/制造商和准确机型；纤维名不是这件衣物的完整许可。
- **热底板摸起来好像不烫：** 不用手或皮肤试温；看设备就绪指示与设置，仍不确定就断电停止。
- **布面发亮、印花变软或熨斗粘住：** 立即抬离并安全停机，不在同处继续加热；按衣物和设备制造商说明处理损伤或粘底。
- **为了更平反复升温：** 回到衣物上限与小样；已到上限还不能达到理想外观，就接受有限改善或换衣。
- **有污渍仍先熨：** 停下，先按衣物与污渍来源选择处理；热可能让污渍更难去。
- **电线破损、底板异常、离开现场：** 不继续使用或无人照看热熨斗；照设备说明断电并交合格维修/更换。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只整理普通衣物的穿前褶皱，不熨防护材料、未知涂层、结构复杂或贵重衣物，也不代替 H065 以外的清洗任务。工具品牌和机型不同，按钮、底板与蒸汽限制不能凭通用词推断。若手部稳定性、视力或站立条件不足，可用明亮灯、桌面高度的稳定熨衣面和可信协助；不能安全控制热工具就停。平整度改善有限时，换衣仍是合理结果，热设备冷却需要实际时间。

## English

Use this Skill for **one clean ordinary garment** with visible wrinkles when its care label permits ironing and you have an intact iron with the exact model instructions. Smooth it on a stable surface. The result is fewer wrinkles without new shiny patches or scorch marks, followed by safe power-off and cooling before wear. If the label forbids ironing or equipment conditions are missing, another garment is more reliable than using your skin as a thermometer.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the garment's iron symbol or text: prohibition, dots or other ceiling, any no-steam condition, and inside-out or press-cloth requirement. Enlarge an unclear label or ask the maker; do not infer a setting from a cotton-like feel. Read the **exact iron model** for controls, dry/steam route, safe resting, and power-off. This Skill uses only its permitted dry-ironing path, not an improvised spray or steam treatment. Check for a stain awaiting treatment, uncertain dampness, fragile print or trim, and a sound cord, plug, and soleplate. Set up a stable board or maker-approved heat-safe surface. Clear the cord and floor path and keep children and pets away.

Stop for a no-iron label, missing model instructions, damaged device, or no stable heat-safe surface. This Skill does not repair electrical equipment or iron or steam a garment someone is wearing.

### Execution

1. **Cross-check both limits.** The garment label sets the greatest allowed treatment; the iron manual tells you how its settings work. If either rules out this method, use no heat. Blends, prints, or trim may have stricter limits than the main cloth; follow the strictest known one. Dots are a care limit, not a licence to substitute one worldwide temperature number for the actual label and model.
2. **Move the garment from body to board.** Flatten a small area and check that a sleeve, pocket, or lining is not folded underneath. Avoid ironing directly on a zipper, metal button, adhesive print, or unknown decoration. Use the wrong side or a compatible press cloth when the label requires it. Before power-on, arrange where the iron can rest upright or on its maker-specified stand. Keep hands and cord out of the soleplate's path.
3. **Set the exact model and wait for readiness.** Choose a setting allowed by both garment and iron. Use the model's ready indicator rather than touching the soleplate. This path is dry ironing; switch steam off as its manual directs. Do not add water casually to a hot iron or press a sound cord against a hot board.
4. **Trial a hidden area, then work in sections.** Make a brief model-directed test on compatible fabric out of sight. Stop for shine, shrinkage, colour change, melting, or sticking. If the test works, move the iron gently through visible creases without leaving the hot plate on one point or pressing a new fold into place. Flatten cloth before each new section, keeping your hand away from the plate and steam outlets.
5. **Power off, cool, then dress.** Stop when the garment is acceptably flatter; do not raise the setting beyond the label to chase the last line. Switch off and unplug as the manual directs, then leave the iron in a safe cooling position. Do not touch or store the plate while hot. Let the garment cool in a suitable place and inspect for new marks before wearing. If you must leave, choose another item rather than touching up the one already on your body.

### Success

Obvious wrinkles are reduced under both garment and device limits, with no new shine, print damage, or scorch. The iron is unplugged and safely cooling or cool, and the garment is ready to wear. Stopping heat and choosing another item or professional care when a label or device forbids it is also a completed decision.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An unreadable label becomes a setting based on fibre name:** Stop heating and find a readable label, maker, and exact model. A fibre name is not this garment's full permission.
- **The soleplate seems cool enough to touch:** Do not test with skin. Use the device's setting and ready indicator; if still uncertain, unplug and stop.
- **Cloth shines, print softens, or the iron sticks:** Lift it away and stop safely. Do not heat the spot again; follow garment and device maker advice for damage or residue.
- **Temperature rises repeatedly for a flatter result:** Return to the care ceiling and test result. If the allowed setting cannot deliver the desired look, accept limited improvement or choose another garment.
- **A stain is ironed first:** Stop and choose treatment from the garment and stain evidence. Heat may make removal harder.
- **Cord damage, plate anomaly, or departure:** Do not continue with or leave a hot iron unattended. Power down per its instructions and arrange qualified repair or replacement.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers wrinkles before wearing ordinary clothing, not protective material, unknown coatings, complex construction, or valuable garments. Iron models differ; buttons, soleplates, and steam limits cannot be inferred from generic names. For limited hand steadiness, sight, or standing, use bright light, a stable table-height board, and trusted help. Stop if heat cannot be controlled safely. Accepting a few wrinkles or changing garments may be the reasonable result; a hot device takes real time to cool.
