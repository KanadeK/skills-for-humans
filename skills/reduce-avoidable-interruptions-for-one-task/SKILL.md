## 中文

你已选好一项普通、非紧急任务，准备做下一小段工作，却总被**自己能调整的通知、页面、物件或接触方式**打断时，用这份 Skill。目标是为这一次任务建立可用的环境，同时保留必要联系和辅助功能。它不诊断注意力或睡眠，不要求你凭意志消除所有声音，也不承诺无干扰就能完成任务。选下一项任务归 H262，重新进入已中断任务归 H266。

### 准备与输入

写下这段时间要做的一项具体动作，以及你能现实安排的起止点；不必使用某个固定分钟数。找出本次最常见的可避免干扰：屏幕通知、无关标签页、桌面杂物、旁人不知道你暂时不可打断等。另列**必须保持的通道**，如约定的接送、照护、工作值守、必要提醒或你依赖的辅助功能。若无法保证必要联系可达，不要一键静音所有设备。

### 执行

1. **只改本次最碍事的一两处。** 关掉与任务无关的标签页或把物件移到可找回的位置，关闭一个无关应用的弹窗，或告诉相关人“我处理这项任务到〔实际时间〕，急事请用〔仍可达渠道〕”。不动别人共用的提醒或设备，不删除待办来制造清爽感。
2. **核对通知例外后再启用临时模式。** 若用设备的勿扰/专注功能，先看该设备上哪些联系人、应用、闹钟和提醒仍会响，以及模式会持续多久；Android、Windows 和 iPhone 的选项与默认行为不同。必要联系人或辅助提示必须能到达，无法确认时改为只关闭一个无关应用、把手机移出视线等更可控办法。
3. **开始一段有结束点的工作。** 把当前材料放在手边，只做写下的下一动作。冒出不属于本任务的普通念头或消息时，简短记一个稍后处理的入口，不立刻开另一个任务；真正需要即时处理的现实责任则正常处理，不把本 Skill 当拒绝必要沟通的理由。
4. **有中断就保留状态。** 临时有人找、环境噪声无法改变或任务本身缺材料时，记录做到哪里和下一步，按实际情况响应或换场所；不能靠更强的静音解决缺输入或工作权限。下次回到任务可用恢复点，而不是把这一段标“失败”。
5. **到时收尾和恢复。** 关闭临时模式或核对是否自动结束，查看可能延后显示的重要消息、闹钟和提醒；把为了这段工作移动的共用物放回去。若这种设置会影响晚些的必要联系，立即调回。只根据实际做出的部分记录进度，别用“进入专注模式”代替完成工作。

### 成功条件

本次下一动作可以在一个更可控的环境中开始，必要联系人、提醒和辅助方式仍可用；结束时临时调整已恢复或被明确保留，并复查延后的消息。出现不可控干扰不算道德失败；本 Skill 的完成是调整和收尾可观察，不是证明你拥有完美注意力或已完成整项任务。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一键静音后错过必需联系：** 停用该模式，按实际设备核对例外；下次用单一应用静音或其他可逆办法。
- **收拾桌面变成全屋大扫除：** 只移动眼前挡住这项任务的物件，并保留可找回位置。
- **关闭通知却仍需要某个辅助提示：** 保留该提示或换非设备层面的干扰调整，不牺牲可及性。
- **突然需要他人提供材料：** 标出缺口并联系负责人；环境优化不能替代输入。
- **任务段结束后忘了恢复：** 立即检查模式、延后通知和共用物状态，别假设系统会自动还原。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于普通非紧急任务的当次可控环境，不提供心理、注意力、睡眠或医疗建议。不同设备的勿扰功能和例外各异，必须看本机当前说明；官方 Android 与 Windows 文档都说明可管理允许的通知，但不能证明所有消息都会按你的期望到达。没有个人设备控制权时，可用纸面恢复点、少开窗口或与相关人约定短暂工作时段。必要照护、值守和辅助通道优先于安静。少一点干扰可能让工作容易继续，但不把人的能力问题归咎于开关设置。

## English

Use this Skill after choosing one ordinary, non-urgent task when **notifications, pages, objects, or contact patterns you can adjust** keep interrupting the next work interval. Create a usable setting for this task while preserving necessary contact and accessibility. This does not diagnose attention or sleep, demand that you will away all noise, or guarantee completion. H262 selects the next task; H266 covers re-entering after an interruption.

### Preparation and inputs

Write one action for this interval and choose a start and end you can actually manage. There is no required number of minutes. Identify the most common avoidable interruption now: screen notifications, unrelated tabs, nearby clutter, or people who do not know when you are available. Separately list **channels that must remain** for arranged pickup, care, on-call duties, needed reminders, or accessibility aids. If you cannot keep essential contact available, do not silence every device at once.

### Execution

1. **Change only one or two high-impact things.** Close an unrelated tab, move an object to a retrievable place, silence one nonessential app, or tell relevant people “I'm doing this task until [real time]; for urgent matters use [reachable channel].” Do not change shared reminders or devices without permission, or delete tasks to make the surface look clean.
2. **Check notification exceptions before a temporary mode.** If using a device's Do Not Disturb or Focus, inspect which people, apps, alarms, and reminders can still reach you and how long the mode lasts. Android, Windows, and iPhone options and defaults differ. Keep needed contacts and accessibility cues. If you cannot verify that, silence one irrelevant app or move the phone from view instead.
3. **Work through a bounded interval.** Keep needed material nearby and do the stated next action. For an unrelated ordinary thought or message, jot down a later entry point instead of starting a second task. Respond normally to a real immediate responsibility; this Skill is not a reason to refuse necessary contact.
4. **Preserve state when interrupted.** If someone needs you, noise cannot be changed, or the task lacks material, record where you reached and the next action, respond, or change location as appropriate. Stronger silencing cannot supply a missing input or permission. Return later through that recovery point rather than marking this interval a failure.
5. **Close and restore.** Turn off the temporary mode or verify that it ended. Review important messages, alarms, or reminders that may have been delayed and return shared items moved for this interval. Restore anything that would block later needed contact. Record only work actually done; entering Focus mode is not completing the task.

### Success

The next action could begin in a more controlled setting while essential contacts, reminders, and accessibility aids remained usable. At the end, temporary changes are restored or intentionally retained, with delayed messages reviewed. An uncontrollable interruption is not a moral failure. Completion here is observable setup and closeout, not perfect attention or proof the whole task was finished.

### Common errors and recovery

- **One-tap silence blocks a needed contact:** End the mode and inspect actual device exceptions. Next time silence one app or use another reversible change.
- **Clearing a desk becomes a whole-house cleanup:** Move only the objects obstructing this task and leave a retrievable location.
- **A needed assistive cue disappears with other notifications:** Keep that cue or use a non-device adjustment instead of sacrificing access.
- **The task needs material from another person:** Name the missing input and contact its owner. Environment changes cannot replace it.
- **The interval ends but settings stay changed:** Check mode, delayed notices, and shared objects now; do not assume automatic restoration.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for the controllable setting of one ordinary non-urgent task, not psychological, attention, sleep, or medical advice. Device modes and exceptions differ; check the current device. Official Android and Windows help explain allowed notifications but cannot guarantee every message behaves as you expect. Without control of a personal device, use a paper recovery note, fewer open windows, or a short availability agreement with others. Care, on-call, and accessibility channels take priority over quiet. Fewer interruptions can make work easier to continue without treating human ability as a switch setting.
