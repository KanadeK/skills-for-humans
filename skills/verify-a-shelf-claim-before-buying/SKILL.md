## 中文

当货架牌、包装或店员已经对普通商品说出一个非环保类卖点，而你无法看出它究竟覆盖**哪件商品、哪一部分、什么条件**时，加载这份 Skill。例如“耐刮”可能只指桌面涂层，并且只在说明的测试条件下成立。你的结果是把原话与可查看的依据对上，或把它标为未核实；不在现场审理商家是否违法。

本 Skill 不验证环保或回收宣称、医疗和营养治疗、金融收益或安全认证，也不处理广告投诉和售后争议。尺寸与兼容性决策归商品用途核对；价格优惠归价格比较。涉及人身安全的关键说法若没有适用资料，暂停购买并找合格来源。

### 准备与输入

记下宣传语原文或在允许的情况下拍货架牌，同时确认品牌、型号、款式和它摆放的位置。写出你会据此改变购买决定的那一点，例如“耐刮指桌面涂层，还是整件商品？在哪种测试下？”不要先把模糊词翻译成未标出的等级或年限。

### 执行

1. **固定说法。** 把“耐刮”“耐用”等词连同限定小字一起看；口头承诺则复述一次：“你的意思是这件型号的哪一部分？”
2. **问适用范围。** 明确它指商品主体、表面涂层、某个部件，还是某种使用条件，并核对是否包含你面前的这个款式。一个品牌页不能自动覆盖品牌所有货品。
3. **请求可查看的依据。** 询问对应包装、制造商产品页、说明书或其他能解释说法依据的材料。再看它是否提供材料构成、测试条件等具体根据，而不只是重复同一句标语。核对来源日期、型号和限定；打不开的链接和没有解释范围的图标先不计依据。
4. **只做三态判断。** “这件商品的范围和具体依据均可查看”；“有依据但范围比原话窄”；“仍不足以核实”。第二种要记下缩小后的条件，第三种不要改写成“已证伪”。你也无需把找到商家资料等同于独立证明商品的一切效果。
5. **回到你的决定。** 如果卖点是必要条件，只在足够明确时继续考虑。否则暂缓、选择其他商品，或按正式渠道进一步查询。不要为了得到肯定答案逼店员重复标语。

### 完成与停止

你留下一条可复述的记录：原话、确切商品、适用范围、看到的来源和三态结论。无法提供依据也是可结束的状态；它不自动证明商家有意欺骗，也不要求你现场争论。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“全系列都这样”：** 查你手中型号是否真的列在资料里；对不上则未核实。
- **图标或二维码只有品牌介绍：** 找与该说法直接相关的说明；没有就不要给标语补上自己想象的证据。
- **小字改变了原话范围：** 记录小字，并以更窄的实际条件做决定。
- **不能拍照或读不清：** 手写关键措辞，请店员提供可读版本、口述限定文字或正式资料链接；遵守现场规则，不拍工作人员。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是一种购买前核对方法，不是任何地区的消费者权利声明。证据可得性、宣传用语和标签规则因地区而异；澳大利亚监管资料只支持为什么要看范围与依据。听说或阅读不方便时使用文字问题、放大、朗读或可信同伴。可能的副作用是一个漂亮卖点变得不那么漂亮，但判断更清楚。

## English

Load this Skill when a shelf card, package, or salesperson has already made an ordinary, non-environmental claim, but you cannot tell **which item, which part, or which conditions** it covers. “Scratch resistant,” for example, may describe only a tabletop coating under stated test conditions. Your result is a match between the wording and readable support, or an explicit “unverified.” You are not holding a hearing about whether the seller broke a law.

This does not verify environmental or recycling claims, medical or therapeutic claims, financial returns, or safety certifications, and it does not handle advertising complaints or after-sale disputes. Size and compatibility belong to a product-use check; discounts belong to price comparison. If an unsupported claim is critical to personal safety, pause and find a qualified source before buying.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the exact claim, or photograph the shelf card if the shop permits it. Identify the brand, model, variant, and display position. State the point that would change your decision, such as “Does scratch resistant mean the tabletop coating or the whole item, and under what test?” Do not translate vague wording into an invented rating or lifespan.

### Execution

1. **Preserve the wording.** Read qualifying small print alongside words such as “scratch resistant” or “durable.” For a spoken promise, repeat it back: “Which part of this exact model do you mean?”
2. **Ask for scope.** Find out whether the claim covers the main item, a coating, one component, or a particular use condition, and whether this variant is included. A brand page does not automatically cover every product from that brand.
3. **Ask for inspectable support.** Request the matching package text, manufacturer page, manual, or another document that explains the basis of the claim. Look for concrete detail such as material composition or test conditions, not just the slogan repeated. Check its date, model, and qualifications. An unopened link or unexplained logo is not support you have inspected.
4. **Use three outcomes only.** “Scope and concrete basis are available for this item”; “the available basis covers a narrower claim”; or “still not enough information to verify.” Record the narrower conditions for the second outcome. Do not turn the third into “disproved,” or treat seller material as independent proof of every effect.
5. **Return to your decision.** If the claim is a requirement for you, keep considering the purchase only when its scope is clear enough. Otherwise defer, choose another item, or inquire through an official channel later. Pressing staff to repeat a slogan does not add evidence.

### Success and stop

You can repeat the original claim, exact item, scope, source you inspected, and one of the three outcomes. An unavailable source is a valid endpoint. Even matching seller material does not independently prove every claimed effect; an information gap does not prove deliberate deception or require an argument at the counter.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“The whole range is like that”:** Check whether your model is actually listed. If not, leave the claim unverified.
- **A logo or QR page offers only brand promotion:** Look for information tied to the particular claim; do not supply imaginary support for the slogan.
- **Small print narrows the promise:** Record it and decide using the narrower real condition.
- **Photos are forbidden or print is inaccessible:** Write down the key wording. Request readable text, an oral reading of the qualification, or an official document link. Follow store rules and do not photograph staff.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a pre-purchase checking method, not a statement of consumer rights in every region. Evidence availability, advertising language, and labelling rules vary. The Australian regulator's guidance supports checking scope and support, not giving you worldwide legal advice. Use a written question, magnification, a read-aloud option, or a trusted companion if speech, hearing, or reading is difficult. A side effect is that a beautiful slogan may become less beautiful and more precise.
