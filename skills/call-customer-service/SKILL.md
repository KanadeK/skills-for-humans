## 中文

当你必须给客服打电话，却担心接通后会忘记自己为什么打时，加载这份 Skill。目标不是赢一场辩论，而是让问题、证据、请求和下一步都离开你的短期记忆，进入纸面。

这份 Skill 处理普通售后、账单询问、配送、预约和账户服务。欺诈、身份盗用、法律争议、金融交易授权或危机求助不在这里处理。

### 准备与输入

只从产品官网、App、账单或合同上的官方入口取得号码，不使用陌生短信或搜索广告里要求你“立即处理”的号码。准备：

- 订单号、工单号、产品/服务名称和相关日期；
- 一句话问题：“我在……遇到……，已经……，现在希望……”；
- 最希望的结果，以及可以接受的第二结果；
- 三到五条关键事实，按时间排序；
- 你愿意等待多久，何时必须结束；
- 纸笔或能在通话中快速记录的工具。

只准备客服合理需要的身份信息。不要告诉任何人密码、完整支付凭据或一次性验证码；对方索要异常信息时挂断，从官方入口重新核实。

记录只写解决问题需要的信息，遮住不必要的账户数字，并保存在他人不能随手看到的纸张或设备位置；问题结束后按你的实际保留需要处理。

若听觉、语言、说话、注意力或电话操作不方便，先找官方文字客服、邮件、字幕电话、转接服务或授权陪同。替代渠道是正式接口，不是低配模式。

### 执行

1. **写下唯一主问题。** 其他相关问题放在第二栏。一次电话先让一个问题获得负责人，避免开场就递交整个宇宙。
2. **把证据排成时间线。** 只写可核对事实：何时购买、何时发生、你做过什么、系统显示什么。把猜测和情绪留作背景，不当证据。
3. **拨打官方号码并记录开始时间。** 机器人菜单里选择最接近主问题的类别；菜单不匹配时找“其他”或人工服务，不随机输入敏感信息。
4. **接通后使用四句开场。**
   - 你好，我想处理的是……
   - 相关订单/账户末尾信息是……
   - 现在的具体问题是……
   - 我希望今天能确认……
5. **回答必要问题，但守住边界。** 可以核对姓名、订单和按官方流程需要的信息；密码、一次性验证码和不必要的完整支付数据不提供。
6. **一次只推进一个动作。** 对方给出解释时，复述：“我确认一下，您的意思是……，下一步是……，预计……？” 复述不是不信任，是防止语音数据包丢失。
7. **结果不合适时回到目标。** 说“这个方案不能解决……，我能接受的是……。如果您没有权限，能否转给负责该事项的人？” 不威胁、不辱骂，也不让礼貌删除你的请求。
8. **记录姓名或编号。** 记下客服称呼/工号（若提供）、工单号、承诺、截止时间和你要做的动作。不要秘密录音；录音必须符合所在地法律和明确同意。
9. **挂断前做最终确认。** 逐项念回金额、日期、渠道、下一步和参考编号，并问是否会有短信/邮件确认。
10. **挂断后保存记录。** 把时间线、结果和下次跟进日期放在同一处。此时循环音乐进程可以正式退出。

### 成功条件

你得到以下之一：问题已经解决；明确下一步、负责人和时间；或一次有编号、有证据的正式拒绝，足以让你决定是否换渠道或结束。成功不要求客服同意你最初的全部请求。

### 常见报错与补救

- **等待超过上限：** 记下时间并挂断，改用回拨、文字/邮件或另一个官方时段。沉没的等待不要求追加等待。
- **接通后脑中空白：** 直接读四句开场和时间线，不需要即兴表现自然。
- **对方打断或话太快：** 说“请慢一点，我需要记录”，然后一次复述一个结论。
- **电话中断：** 有工单号就用它重拨；尚未取得编号时，提供上一通的开始时间、进入的部门、客服称呼（若知道）和已确认内容。
- **你开始生气或疲惫：** 暂停几秒、喝水、回到唯一主问题；必要时结束并另约时间。礼貌不是无限运行时间。
- **没有权限解决：** 请求转接或书面确认；若仍无路径，查看合同/官方投诉渠道。法律权利问题另找当地合格消费者机构或专业意见。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

身份核验、录音、服务时间、语言和升级流程因地区与公司而异。按官方流程操作，不把本 Skill 当法律或金融授权。电话不可访问时，文字、邮件、现场服务或可信授权代理都可以完成同一工作。

现实副作用是你可能拥有一个工单号、一个后续日期和比打电话前更多但更有结构的信息。问题没有立即解决，不代表这通电话没有产出。

## English

Load this Skill when you must call customer service and worry that the reason for calling will disappear the moment someone answers. The goal is not to win a debate. Move the issue, evidence, request, and next action out of working memory and onto a page.

This covers ordinary support, billing questions, delivery, appointments, and account service. Fraud, identity theft, legal disputes, financial transaction authorization, and crisis calls are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Get the number only from the official product site, app, bill, or contract. Do not use an unfamiliar text message or search advertisement that demands immediate action. Prepare:

- order, case, product/service, and relevant dates;
- one issue sentence: “On [date], [problem] happened. I have already [action]. I need [result].”
- your preferred result and an acceptable second result;
- three to five verifiable facts in time order;
- your maximum hold time and hard stop;
- paper or another fast note-taking tool.

Prepare only identity information reasonably required by the official process. Never provide a password, complete payment credentials, or a one-time security code. Hang up and verify through the official entry point if a request is abnormal.

Keep only notes needed to resolve the issue, mask unnecessary account digits, and store paper or device notes away from casual access. Dispose of them according to your real retention need after the issue closes.

If hearing, speech, language, attention, or phone controls are inaccessible, find official chat, email, captioned calling, relay service, or an authorized support person first. An alternative channel is a real interface, not a reduced mode.

### Execution

1. **Write one primary issue.** Put related questions in a second list. Give one problem an owner before submitting the universe.
2. **Arrange evidence as a timeline.** Record checkable facts: purchase, event, attempts, and displayed message. Keep guesses and feelings as context rather than evidence.
3. **Call the official number and note the start time.** Choose the menu category nearest the primary issue. Use Other or live help when nothing fits; do not enter random sensitive information.
4. **Use a four-line opening.**
   - Hi, I am calling about…
   - The relevant order/account ending is…
   - The specific problem is…
   - Today I need to confirm…
5. **Answer necessary questions while keeping boundaries.** Verify name, order, and official-process information; do not provide passwords, one-time codes, or unnecessary complete payment data.
6. **Advance one action at a time.** Restate the answer: “To confirm, you mean…, my next step is…, and that should happen by…?” Repetition is packet-loss prevention, not distrust.
7. **Return to the outcome when needed.** Say, “That does not resolve [issue]. I can accept [result]. If this is outside your permission, could you transfer me to the team responsible?” Do not threaten or insult, and do not let politeness erase the request.
8. **Record identity or reference.** Note the representative name/ID when offered, case number, promise, deadline, and your action. Do not secretly record; recording must follow local law and explicit consent.
9. **Confirm before ending.** Read back amount, date, channel, next step, and reference number. Ask whether written confirmation will arrive.
10. **Save the record after the call.** Keep the timeline, result, and follow-up date together. The hold-music process may now exit.

### Success

You receive one of three results: resolution; a named next step, owner, and time; or a documented refusal with a reference number that lets you change channel or stop. Success does not require every original request to be accepted.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Hold exceeds your limit:** Record the time, hang up, and use callback, chat/email, or another official service window. Time already held does not authorize more.
- **Your mind goes blank:** Read the four-line opening and timeline. Natural improvisation is not required.
- **Speech is too fast or interrupted:** Say, “Please slow down; I need to write this,” then confirm one conclusion at a time.
- **The call drops:** Use the case number if one exists. If the call ended before a number was issued, give the prior start time, department, representative name when known, and confirmed facts.
- **You become angry or exhausted:** Pause, drink water, return to the one issue, or end and reschedule. Politeness does not require unlimited runtime.
- **The representative lacks authority:** Request a transfer or written answer. If no route exists, use the contract or official complaint channel; take legal-rights questions to a qualified local consumer body or professional.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Identity checks, recording law, service hours, languages, and escalation vary by company and region. Follow the official process; this Skill is not legal or financial authorization. Chat, email, in-person service, relay, or a trusted authorized representative can complete the same job when calls are inaccessible.

Known side effects include a case number, a follow-up date, and more information than before, now with structure. A problem that remains open does not make the call output empty.
