## 中文

当你照看**已有正常进食、排便和活动基线的室内兔**，想做一次不强抓的普通观察时，加载这份 Skill。结果是吃草、饮水、是否按平常间隔继续排新粪、行动/躲藏及毛/尾部可见状态被如实记下，异常有正确兽医联系。停止进食或较其平常停止排新粪是应**立即联系兽医**的出口，不是“明天再看”的选项；本篇不诊断肠胃病、牙病或虫病。

### 准备与输入

看原主人/兽医方案、上一次正常进食和新粪记录、同伴兔平时互动与食水厕位。站在兔平常能活动处远观察，不为检查把兔强抱离地或追赶；可用可信协助指认不同兔与粪便来源。若没有基线，标“不足判断”并向负责者核实，但**没有吃/没有新粪仍要立即求兽医**，不能因资料少延迟。

### 执行

1. **先查即时退出信号。** 看兔是否实际在吃其原干草/食物，对照平常排便间隔与上次记录看是否继续有新粪；若已停止进食或较其平常停止排新粪，马上联系熟悉兔的兽医，提供最后一次正常时间。明显虚弱/受伤也按兽医入口，不继续完成普通表格才打电话。
2. **在正常时记录几个事实。** 观察水位/取水、一般移动、躲藏、与原伴兔的日常关系、眼鼻/毛的明显变化和尾部是否脏湿；写“看见/未看见”，不要把“未看见喝水”写成“已喝”。不把纸上的打勾当作实物接触诊断。
3. **与本兔基线比并保留未知。** 将观察时间、吃草/粪便证据、实际行为与上次正常记录并列；小变化或看不到的位置可再在原时段观察/问原负责人，显著异常及时问兽医。没有医学把握时不用药、不随意禁食。
4. **交给下次看护一个入口。** 无异常时写明下一次按原方案何时查；已经求助时记联系方式与建议来源，让下一位照料者知道风险而非重复默认正常。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有日期与实际吃草/新粪/活动观察及与基线的比较，异常已按紧迫程度联系兽医。只说“兔看起来可爱”、没有确认进食与粪，不算日常健康观察。

- **兔不吃或无新粪：** 立即联系兽医，记最后正常时间，不等夜里/明天。
- **尾部脏湿、突然躲、动作明显异常：** 及时咨询熟悉兔的兽医，不在家猜治疗。
- **分不清哪只兔吃/排：** 记身份不确定，安排安全区分观察或问原负责人；仍不忽视整群无新粪。
- **食物没少但看不见兔进食：** 不用食物重量独证健康，继续观察并在疑似不食时马上问兽医。
- **观察有可及障碍：** 明亮可访问位置、可信协助与照片比较，仍写明看见和未知。

### 假设与边界

PDSA 建议每天看兔况并强调厌食/无新粪紧急性，个体正常行为需原方案决定。此篇只记录和转介，不是疾病诊断或兽医体检，不让普通流程吞掉紧急信号。

## English

Load this Skill when you care for an **indoor rabbit with a known normal eating, droppings, and movement baseline** and need one non-forceful routine observation. Finish with truthful notes on hay intake, water access, droppings against its usual interval, movement/hiding, visible coat/rear state, and a vet route for change. **Stopped eating or stopped producing new droppings compared with normal means call a vet immediately**, never “check tomorrow.” This does not diagnose gut, dental, or parasite disease.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the owner's/vet plan and last known normal meal and fresh dropping record, usual companions, and food/water/toilet places. Watch from a normal activity place instead of chasing or lifting the rabbit for inspection. Trusted help can identify individual rabbits and droppings. With no baseline, record “cannot judge normal” and ask the carer; **absence of eating or new droppings still needs immediate vet advice** without waiting for the missing data.

### Execution

1. **Check urgent exits first.** See whether it actually eats its established hay/food and whether fresh droppings continue within its normal observed interval. If it has stopped eating or stopped producing new droppings compared with normal, contact a rabbit-aware vet immediately with the last normal time. Obvious weakness or injury also belongs to veterinary help before finishing a routine form.
2. **Record a few facts when ordinary.** Observe water access, general movement, hiding, normal companion interaction, visible eye/nose/coat changes, and a soiled or wet rear. Write “seen/not seen”; not seeing a drink is not proof it drank. A tick on paper is not a physical diagnosis.
3. **Compare with this rabbit and keep unknowns.** Put observation time, eating/dropping evidence, and behaviour beside the last normal record. A minor unseen area can be observed at its usual time or clarified with the owner; a significant change gets prompt vet advice. Do not invent medicine or fasting for an uncertain condition.
4. **Leave the next carer an entry.** If ordinary, say when the next planned check occurs. If help was sought, record whom you contacted and source of instructions so the next carer does not reset risk to “normal.”

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A dated real observation of eating, fresh droppings, and movement compares with baseline and abnormalities reach a vet at appropriate urgency. “Rabbit looks cute” without eating/dropping evidence is not a completed daily check.

- **No food eaten or no new droppings:** Call a vet immediately with last normal time, not tonight/tomorrow.
- **Soiled rear, sudden hiding, or markedly abnormal movement:** Seek rabbit-aware vet guidance promptly, not home treatment by guess.
- **Cannot tell which rabbit ate or passed stool:** Record identity uncertainty and arrange safe individual observation or ask owner, without ignoring a group with no fresh droppings.
- **Food amount changed but no eating was witnessed:** Do not use food weight alone as health proof; observe and call for suspected refusal.
- **Observation is inaccessible:** Good light, trusted viewing help, and photos can support an honest seen/unknown record.

### Assumptions and limits

PDSA calls for daily rabbit observation and treats no eating/droppings as urgent; each rabbit's normal behaviour comes from its own plan. This records and routes, not medical diagnosis or exam. Routine steps must not swallow emergency signals.
