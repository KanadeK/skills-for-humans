## 中文

当一件普通物品**确实借入或借出**，容易在家里混成“好像一直是我的”，或到期时找不到时，加载这份 Skill。目标是让物品归属、当前由谁保管、放在哪、何时怎样归还，以及是否已经真正交还都可查。它从实际交接开始；购买前考虑借不借、物品永久赠送、争议追索和危险设备使用不在这里。

### 准备与输入

在交接时确认物品的可辨认名称或特征、所有者、借用者、实际交接日、约定的归还日期或事件、取还方式和随附的小配件。正式借用服务另看它**当前**的预约、续借、地点、费用与损坏报告规则；熟人之间则以双方真正说定的条件为准。没有谈好归还条件就先补问，不把“以后给你”当完整安排。

用私密纸条、日历或自己可访问的记录。只记找回和归还所需的信息，不把别人的电话、住址、账号或借用细节发到公开清单。看不清标记或难以够到存放处时，用一致位置、较大字、已有的朗读方式或可信协助；物品本身的安全和使用说明仍照原要求。

### 执行

1. **先写方向与真实状态。** 选“借入：别人的物品在我这里”或“借出：我的物品在对方那里”。尚未交手的预约只记“待交接”，不能写成已借到或已还。
2. **在交手时记一行。** 写`物品与配件｜所有者 / 当前保管者｜交接时间｜应归还时机与方式｜当前存放或联系渠道｜状态`。必要时共同看一眼原有磨损或缺件，免得归还时靠记忆重建；不需要拍下对方的私人资料。
3. **若借入，把它和自有物区分。** 选一个你能找回、符合对方要求的暂放位置；普通外部提示写“借入·何时归还”，不公开敏感详情。物品移动到别处时更新记录。正式服务要按实际条款使用、续借与归还；不把另一个城市图书馆的规则套进来。
4. **若借出，留下一个“物品外出”的线索。** 在你原本会找它的位置留私密提示，指向借出记录；不把借用者姓名贴给路人看。确认对方知道归还条件和随附物；到约定时间仍未收到，先按约定渠道礼貌核对，不直接把未答复写成已归还。
5. **变化发生时改状态。** 提前归还、双方同意延期、物品换地点或发现损坏时，写实际时间和下一步。延期需对方或服务规则确认；日历上把日期改掉，不等于借期已获批准。损坏按提供方要求如实报告，别擅自维修或藏起来。
6. **归还时关闭记录。** 借入时把物品与应有配件交给所有者或指定渠道，确认接收/取得回执后标“已归还”；借出时确认实际收回、核对配件，把它放回原位后标“已收回”。若收件人不在、投放未确认或物品未到手，保留“归还待确认”，不要为了清空提醒提前关单。

### 完成与停止

借期内你能回答“谁拥有、谁保管、物品在哪里、何时怎么归还”；真正交还并获确认后，你能指出最后状态。若所有权、借用许可、损坏或归还规则有争议，停下普通记录流程，按双方约定或提供方的正式渠道处理；本篇不替你裁决责任或给法律意见。

### 常见报错与补救

- **借来后混进自己的同类物：** 用可辨认特征和私密借入提示区分，重新核对配件与位置，不凭“看着熟悉”宣布归属。
- **到期前想多用几天：** 先征得所有者同意或按服务规则续借，得到确认后再改期限；无回复就按原约定安排归还。
- **借出的东西原位置空了，大家都在找：** 在原位置留不泄露私人信息的外出提示，指向可查记录；实际归还后撤掉。
- **对方说已还，但你没收到：** 先核对交接时间、地点或回执，保持待确认；不要在记录里制造已收回的事实。
- **中途换了存放处或被打断：** 更新实际位置和下一步；回来从未确认状态继续，不从头猜谁拿走了它。

### 假设、替代与已知副作用

借用关系、礼貌提醒、押金与归还规则因人和服务不同。没有手机也能用私密纸条；记录必须让需要的人能找到，但不必公开个人资料。高风险设备、证件和凭据的借用另有专门要求，不由这个普通物品表授权。副作用是多一条到期事项；它比物品失踪后重新谈判归属便宜。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary item has **actually been borrowed or lent** and might blend into the household as “perhaps it was always mine,” or go missing when due back. Keep its owner, current holder, location, return plan, and actual handoff status findable. This starts at the real handoff; deciding whether to borrow before buying, giving an item away permanently, pursuing a dispute, and using hazardous equipment are different tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

At handoff, confirm the item's distinguishing name or features, owner, borrower, actual handoff date, agreed return date or event, return route, and small accessories. For a formal service, check **its current** booking, renewal, place, fee, and damage-reporting terms. With a person, use what you both actually agreed. If the return terms are unclear, ask rather than treating “sometime later” as a complete arrangement.

Use a private note, calendar, or record you can access. Keep only information needed to find and return the item; do not publish another person's phone number, address, account, or loan details. If labels are hard to read or the place hard to reach, use a consistent location, larger print, an existing reading aid, or trusted help. The item's original safety and use instructions still apply.

### Execution

1. **Name the direction and actual state.** Choose “borrowed in: someone else's item with me” or “lent out: my item with someone else.” A booking before any handoff is only “awaiting handoff,” not already borrowed or returned.
2. **Record one line when it changes hands.** Write `item and accessories | owner / current holder | handoff time | return occasion and route | present place or contact route | state`. If useful, both parties can note visible existing wear or missing pieces rather than rebuilding memory later. You need not photograph private details.
3. **For a borrowed-in item, separate it from yours.** Choose a findable temporary place that meets the owner's terms. An ordinary external cue may say “borrowed · return by [occasion]” without exposing sensitive details. Update the record if it moves. Follow the actual formal service's use, renewal, and return terms; another town's library rules do not apply automatically.
4. **For a lent-out item, leave an away cue.** At the place you would normally look for it, leave a private pointer to the loan record, not the borrower's name on public display. Confirm that the borrower knows the agreed return and what went with it. If it is overdue, check politely through the agreed route; a lack of reply is not a return.
5. **Change the state when reality changes.** Record an early return, an agreed extension, a new location, or observed damage with the actual time and next action. An extension needs the owner or service to confirm it; editing your calendar alone does not approve it. Report damage as the provider requires instead of hiding or repairing it yourself.
6. **Close the record on return.** When you borrowed in, return the item and accessories to the owner or specified route and mark “returned” only after receipt or confirmation. When you lent out, confirm it is physically back, check accessories, restore its normal place, then mark “received back.” If the recipient is absent, a drop-off is unconfirmed, or you have not received the item, keep “return to confirm” rather than clearing a reminder early.

### Success and stop conditions

During the loan, you can answer who owns it, who holds it, where it is, and when and how it returns. Once it is actually returned and acknowledged, you can point to the final state. If ownership, permission, damage, or return rules are disputed, stop this ordinary record flow and use the parties' agreement or provider's formal process. This Skill does not decide liability or give legal advice.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A borrowed item blends with your own version:** Use a distinguishing feature and private borrowed-in cue to check accessories and place. Familiar appearance is not evidence of ownership.
- **You want a few more days:** Ask the owner or renew under the service's rules first. Move the due date only after confirmation; without a reply, plan for the original return.
- **A lent item is absent from its usual home and everyone searches:** Leave a discreet away cue pointing to the record; remove it after actual return.
- **Someone says it was returned, but you have not received it:** Check the handoff time, place, or receipt and keep it pending. Do not manufacture a received state.
- **Its place changes or you are interrupted:** Update the actual location and next step. Resume from the unconfirmed state instead of guessing where it went.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Relationships, polite reminders, deposits, and return rules vary by people and service. A private paper note works without a phone. People who need the record should be able to find it without exposing personal details publicly. High-risk equipment, identity documents, and credentials require specialist rules; this ordinary-item record does not authorize their loan. One extra due-date note is a side effect, but it beats reconstructing ownership after the item vanishes.
