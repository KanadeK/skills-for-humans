## 中文

当你要手洗**一件普通非羊毛衣物**，且衣物标签或制造商明确允许在家水洗、手洗条件能核对时，用这份 Skill 完成清洗、漂净和不扭拧去水。目标是让衣物进入标签允许的晾干步骤，形状和颜色没有因洗法受到新损伤。羊毛针织物已有专用 Skill；丝绸、皮革、结构复杂或需专业护理的衣物不靠这份通用流程猜洗法。

### 准备与输入

先读衣物的洗涤与干燥标签：是否允许水洗、是否指定手洗、最高水温或冷水要求、禁止动作和晾干方式。看衣物有没有破损、松脱装饰、明显掉色史或需单独处理的污渍；这些可能需要先暂停。准备干净的盆/水槽、标签与面料适用的衣物洗涤剂、产品说明可执行的小量用法、干净水、吸水毛巾和之后能支撑衣物的晾干位置。不要在刚用过不明清洁剂的水槽里把衣服当最后一道冲洗工序。

标签禁止水洗、关键信息看不清、衣物严重污染，或没有办法按其要求晾干时，先停止。放大标签或查制造商说明；不从“看着像棉”猜温度。贵重或复杂衣物可改请专业护理。

### 执行

1. **只做这件且确定边界。** 按衣物标签选允许的水温和动作，按洗涤剂标签决定适用产品与小量用法。手洗符号若规定温度上限，就不能把较热的水当成“洗得更干净”。若衣物原本只写特定机洗条件、无法确认手洗是否适用，先问制造商，不临时改流程。
2. **先把洗剂分散在水里。** 在干净盆中放入足够让这件衣物能轻柔移动的水，把产品规定的小量洗剂先溶解或分散；避免浓缩液直接停在一小块面料上。新深色衣物或已知易掉色者单独洗，先遵守标签的测试/警告，别让另一件衣物参与试验。
3. **轻柔洗，不用力证明勤奋。** 把衣物浸入，让洗液流过脏的部位，轻轻按压、提放或移动；不要用刷子硬刷、长时间搓揉、拉扯或扭拧。浸泡和接触时长以衣物与洗剂说明为准，没有通用“泡一小时”命令。颜色明显继续流失、接缝开裂或装饰松动时停下，转为按标签/制造商判断。
4. **把洗剂漂出去。** 托住衣物换到干净水中，按压让水通过纤维，按需要换水直到泡沫和明显滑腻感不再残留。不要靠更强搓揉代替漂洗；每次抬起湿衣时支撑它的重量，不只拎领口或一根肩带。
5. **压去多余水，别扭。** 让水先自然滴回盆里，双手托住或平放后轻压；把衣物摊在干净吸水毛巾上，按面料允许的方式卷起并轻压，让毛巾带走部分水。若毛巾已湿透可换干毛巾。不要扭成绳，也不要用热源抢去水。
6. **交给标签规定的晾干方式。** 趁仍湿润轻轻整理被移动的衣身，立即按标签指定的平铺、悬挂或其他自然晾干方式放置；具体位置与支撑见 H056 范围。晾干之前不要堆在密闭盆里。把本次用过的盆和毛巾清理好。

### 成功条件

衣物按已核实标签手洗，洗涤剂已充分漂去，湿重得到支撑，多余水以轻压或毛巾吸走而未扭拧，且已进入允许的晾干方式；形状和颜色无新损伤。若标签、产品或面料条件无法确认，停止手洗并找到可靠说明同样是完成判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **标签写禁止水洗或看不清：** 先停，不以“只用一点水”绕开限制；查制造商或专业护理。
- **倒多了洗剂，漂洗总起泡：** 用干净水轻柔再漂；下次按产品的小件剂量，别在这一轮加入另一种清洁品“抵消”。
- **用力拧后衣物走形：** 停止拉扯，湿时按原轮廓轻柔整理；贵重或明显损伤向专业护理咨询，不能保证完全恢复。
- **洗水持续带色或装饰松脱：** 停止继续摩擦和与其他衣物混洗，按标签或制造商处理，记录变化交洗后外观问题范围。
- **湿衣物太重、无法安全托起：** 分小水量在靠近晾干位置的盆里操作，或请可信的人协助；不要单手提起整件湿衣。
- **没想好晾在哪里：** 暂不开始新的清洗，先安排标签允许的支撑与通风位置，避免洗完被迫长时间留盆。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程只覆盖普通、可在家水洗、无需专门材料护理的非羊毛衣物；不同颜色、涂层、装饰和洗剂不能用统一水温或浸泡时间。视力不便时可放大标签并核对来源，手部或站立受限时用桌面高度的小盆、轻量水和可信协助。毛巾吸水减少对衣物的扭力，却也带来一条湿毛巾；应一起安排晾干。

## English

Use this Skill for **one ordinary non-wool garment** when its label or maker allows home washing and the hand-wash conditions can be confirmed. Complete washing, rinsing, and water removal without wringing. The result is a garment ready for its label-directed drying step, without new damage to shape or colour from the wash. Wool knitwear has its own Skill. Do not guess this general process for silk, leather, complex construction, or professional-care items.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the garment's wash and dry label first: whether water washing is allowed, whether hand washing is specified, water-temperature ceiling or cool-water requirement, prohibited actions, and drying method. Look for damage, loose trim, known dye bleeding, or a stain that needs a separate decision. Have a clean basin or sink, a suitable clothing detergent with usable instructions for a small amount, clean water, an absorbent clean towel, and a place that can support the garment for its required drying route. A sink with unknown cleaner residue is not a laundry-rinse plan.

Stop if the label forbids washing, critical details are unreadable, contamination is severe, or no label-compatible drying place exists. Enlarge the label or ask the maker; do not infer water temperature from a cotton-like appearance. Valuable or complex items may need professional care.

### Execution

1. **Set limits for this one item.** Use the label's water temperature and handling limits, and the detergent label's suitable product and small-load amount. A warmer wash does not override a hand-wash temperature ceiling. If the garment only specifies a particular machine process and hand-wash suitability is unclear, check with the maker before inventing another process.
2. **Disperse detergent before the garment enters.** Put enough allowed-temperature water in a clean basin for gentle movement of this item. Dissolve or disperse the product's small directed amount of detergent first so concentrated liquid does not sit on one patch. Wash a new dark or known bleeding item alone and heed its label's test or warning; do not recruit another garment as a colour test.
3. **Wash gently, without proving effort by force.** Immerse the garment and move wash water through soiled areas with gentle pressing, lifting, or swishing. Do not brush hard, rub for a long time, pull, or wring. Soak and contact time follow the garment and product, not a universal hour. If colour keeps running, seams open, or trim loosens, stop and use label or maker guidance.
4. **Rinse the detergent out.** Support the garment while moving it into clean water. Press water through the fibres and replace rinse water as needed until visible suds and a clearly slippery feel no longer remain. Do not substitute harder rubbing for rinsing. Support the wet weight rather than lifting everything by a neck or strap.
5. **Press away excess water; do not twist.** Let water drip back into the basin, then support and press gently. Lay the garment on a clean absorbent towel, roll it as the fabric permits, and press so the towel takes some water. Use a dry towel if the first becomes saturated. Do not twist it into a rope or use a heat source to hurry water removal.
6. **Hand off to label-directed drying.** Ease the damp garment toward its original outline and promptly place it flat, hanging, or otherwise as its actual label directs. H056 owns the detailed location and support choice. Do not leave it in a closed wet basin. Clean the basin and handle the damp towel.

### Success

The garment has been hand-washed under confirmed care limits, detergent has been thoroughly rinsed, wet weight has been supported, and excess water pressed or towel-absorbed without wringing. It is now in an allowed drying position, with no new shape or colour damage. If label, product, or material conditions cannot be confirmed, stopping for reliable instructions is also a complete decision.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The label forbids washing or cannot be read:** Stop. A small amount of water does not evade the restriction; check the maker or professional care.
- **Too much detergent leaves repeated suds:** Rinse gently again with clean water. Use the product's small-item amount next time; do not add a second cleaner to “cancel” this one.
- **Wringing distorts the garment:** Stop pulling and ease it toward its original shape while damp. Seek professional advice for valuable or substantial damage; full recovery is not guaranteed.
- **Water keeps taking colour or trim loosens:** Stop friction and washing with other items. Follow the label or maker and record the change for the post-wash appearance task.
- **The wet item is too heavy to lift safely:** Work with smaller water amounts in a basin near the drying area or ask a trusted person to help. Do not lift the whole wet item from one point.
- **There is nowhere to dry it:** Do not start another wash. Arrange a label-compatible supported, ventilated place before an item must sit wet in a basin.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary non-wool garments that permit home water washing and need no specialist material care. Colours, coatings, trim, and detergents do not share one universal temperature or soak time. Enlarge and verify a label if sight is limited; use a table-height small basin, lighter water loads, or trusted help if hand use or standing is difficult. Towel pressing reduces twist on the garment but creates a wet towel that also needs drying.
