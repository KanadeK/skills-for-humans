## 中文

当你把本季暂时不用的普通衣物移入较长期存放，且衣物**已经洗净、干透并确认不需先修理**时，用这份 Skill 为它们选一个干净、干燥、可实际容纳的地方，按形状和标签放好。它不重新决定洗衣程序，也不使用熏蒸剂或杀虫化学品给衣柜“保险”。完成后还需给容器和位置做可找回的标识。

### 准备与输入

逐件确认没有明显污渍、口袋中遗留物、潮湿厚缝、异味变化、虫蛀或破口；有问题先单独保留给清洁、晾干、修补或专业判断，不把它密封进同一箱。读护理标签与制造商的折叠/悬挂、涂层和特殊材料要求。检查候选衣柜、箱子、架面是否清洁、干燥、没有可见虫害或冷凝，并确定尺寸/承重/取出通道。贵重、结构复杂、皮革、专业护理或防护装备按其专用说明处理。

### 执行

1. **先定这一组要保留什么。** 只放确实计划下季再用且已达到可收存状态的衣物；不确定是否保留的独立放，别让存储容器替你做永久去留决定。按实际材料、厚薄与折叠/悬挂要求分组，而非仅按季节颜色。
2. **选适用环境和容器。** 存处应干燥、清洁、避免持续直晒或热源且能日后查看。衣物或房间仍潮时，先停止封箱并解决干燥条件。若洁净干衣需要防虫且容器/衣物说明支持，合适的完整密闭箱可隔绝虫进入；但密闭不消除已经夹进去的水分或虫。环境与材料更适合通风支撑时，不强用塑料袋密封；没有一个容器适合全部衣物。
3. **按形状支持衣物。** 对衣物与制造商允许折叠的，平整放入且不超满，重的放下、轻的放上；需要悬挂保持结构的，选能承重且不拉变形的合适衣架和罩法。不要用细肩带承担整件湿重，也不把仍潮的衣物压进下面。碰到未知涂层或附件，不通过用力压缩测试耐久度。
4. **保持存放后可接触。** 箱子、挂杆不要堵住门、电箱、通道或他人的空间；放在你能安全取放的位置。较重箱子先减量或请可信的人协助，不踩不稳家具举过头顶。装好后检查箱盖/衣罩和周围是否干爽，确认没有把坏衣或脏衣误混。
5. **把装好的单元交给标识步骤。** 每个箱、袋或一段挂杆作为一个可指认的单元；先别把未标识的相同箱子堆深处。若发现存处开始凝水、可见虫害或衣物仍未干，停止这次收存并退出到对应处理流程。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

本季不用的每件普通衣物被放入适合其护理限制和形状的干净干燥位置，湿/脏/损坏件已隔离；容器未超满，仍可安全接触和日后标识。没有合适存处而暂停，是比密封潮衣更完整的判断。

- **箱盖合上后内壁起雾：** 打开、取出衣物，查湿衣或潮湿存处；完全干燥前不要再次封闭。
- **发现小洞或虫迹：** 隔离受影响衣物和箱，查可靠虫害/衣物护理指导，不把香袋或杀虫剂当无条件修复。
- **箱子太重或挂杆弯曲：** 减少单元负载，换可承重位置，不再往上叠。
- **一件衣物其实需要先洗或修：** 移入待处理组，完毕且干透后再收，不以“反正下季才穿”跳过。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

面向普通衣物的家庭换季收存，衣物标签和实际环境优先。气候、虫害、租住空间与行动能力各异；干燥环境中的密闭防虫与潮湿环境中避免封闭是不同条件。看不清标签或箱内时可用放大照片、良好照明和可信协助；不便搬重箱则分小单元放在可及高度。以后仍要按现场情况复查存处，并给单元标记以免找不到。

## English

Use this Skill when moving ordinary off-season clothes into longer storage after they are **clean, fully dry, and not waiting for repair**. Choose a clean dry location and support that fits their actual shape and labels. It does not select a new wash cycle or use fumigants or insect chemicals as closet “insurance.” Label the resulting containers and locations in a separate step so they can be found again.

### Preparation and inputs

Check each item for marks, pocket contents, damp thick seams, changed odour, pest damage, or small tears. Hold affected pieces apart for cleaning, drying, repair, or professional assessment rather than sealing them with ready clothes. Read care labels and maker guidance for folding, hanging, coatings, and special materials. Confirm a candidate closet, box, or rail is clean and dry, with no visible pests or condensation and enough capacity, strength, and access. Valuable, structured, leather, professional-care, and protective-function items need their own directions.

### Execution

1. **Choose what this group will keep.** Include only items you actually plan to use next season and that are ready for storage. Hold undecided items separately; a box does not make a permanent keep/discard decision for you. Group by material, thickness, and folding or hanging needs, not only season colour.
2. **Choose environment and container under real conditions.** Use a clean dry place without sustained direct sun or heat that you can check later. If clothes or the room are damp, stop closing boxes and solve drying first. For clean dry clothes where pests matter and the item and container allow it, an intact sealed box can block insects entering. It does not remove water or insects already sealed inside. Use ventilated support when the environment and fabric call for it; a plastic bag is not one universal solution.
3. **Support the garment shape.** Fold only items whose label and maker allow it, laying them without overpacking, with heavy pieces below lighter ones. For items that need hanging to preserve structure, use a strong suitable hanger and cover without pulling out of shape. Do not ask a thin strap to carry the whole item, and do not bury damp clothing below dry. Unknown coatings or trim are not tested by forceful compression.
4. **Keep the stored unit accessible.** Boxes and rails must not block doors, electrical panels, access paths, or another person's space. Place them where you can retrieve them safely. Reduce heavy boxes or ask trusted help instead of standing on unstable furniture to lift above your head. Check that covers, supports, and nearby surfaces stay dry and no damaged or dirty item slipped in.
5. **Pass each packed unit to labelling.** Make each box, bag, or rail segment a distinct identifiable unit. Do not bury several unlabelled identical boxes deep in a closet first. Stop storage and route to appropriate care if the location begins condensing, pests become visible, or an item is still damp.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Each ordinary off-season item is in a clean dry location compatible with its care and shape; damp, dirty, or damaged pieces were held separately. Containers are not overfilled and remain safe to access and label. Pausing because no suitable location exists is more complete than sealing damp clothing.

- **The inside of a closed box fogs:** Open it and remove clothes. Check damp items and place; do not reseal before fully dry.
- **Small holes or pest traces appear:** Separate affected clothes and box, use reliable pest or fabric guidance, and do not assume sachets or insecticide fix everything.
- **The box is heavy or rail bends:** Reduce the unit load and choose adequate support rather than stacking more.
- **An item actually needs washing or repair:** Move it to a pending-care group; store after that work and complete drying, not because it is out of season.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers home seasonal storage of ordinary clothes, with care labels and the actual room controlling choices. Climate, pests, rented space, and mobility vary. A sealed pest barrier for clean dry items and avoidance of sealing damp ones are different conditions. Enlarge a label photo or use good light and trusted help if needed. Use smaller accessible units if heavy boxes are hard to carry. Check storage conditions later and label units so they can be found.
