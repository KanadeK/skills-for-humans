## 中文

当你已经选好与实际地面和接触点相容的普通自粘保护垫，且可在不危险搬动的情况下接近家具脚时加载。你要让**垫在厂家要求的准备后贴牢，物件正常落地时各接触点仍有保护，并知道何时更换**。这里不修腿、不处理防倾倒或承重结构，也不抬、翻、拖重家具。

### 准备与输入

核对垫和家具/地面的说明，确认该位置确实允许自粘材质。用品需稳定，脚底能安全看见或由有能力的人在合适姿态处理；需要多人或专业搬动的先停。按垫产品说明准备适用的清洁材料，避免在不明涂层上用溶剂、刀具或强磨；旧垫脱落或破损时按说明处理残留，不撬坏家具。

### 执行

1. **查看脚底与旧垫。** 确认没有裂腿、松动固定件或需要结构维修的缺陷，旧垫是否磨薄/脏污。结构异常就不以新垫覆盖，先停用或交合适人员。
2. **按产品要求清洁并干燥接触面。** 只用厂家允许的普通方法，去掉影响粘附的灰尘和细砂，等待完全达到说明要求的状态。没有明确可用的清洁方法时先问准确产品说明，不自配化学溶液。
3. **按脚底位置贴合。** 选先前核对的尺寸，揭去背纸后将垫放在正常着地点，尽量覆盖硬边而不过分外凸；按垫说明施压和等待。多个脚逐一核对，不只贴最容易够到的一边。
4. **安全落地后实看。** 由能够安全控制物件的人使它回到正常放置状态；检查每个脚确实接触垫、没有翘边、物件没有新增晃动，地面不被露出的硬边摩擦。需要拖动重物才能测试时不要测试，按实际安全方式让合适人员处理。
5. **安排磨损复查。** 日常清洁或物件轻微移动后看垫是否薄、脱胶或夹沙；按产品说明及时更换。垫失效时先停止会继续刮擦的用法，不用旧垫“撑到下次大扫除”。

### 完成与停止

可安全处理的普通脚底已按准确产品说明清洁、贴合、落地检查，各接触点没有明显漏贴或翘边；这只支持表面保护，不证明家具稳定或安全承重。脚腿裂、物件晃、垫不粘、表面适用性未知或需要危险搬动时停下，换合适方案而非加胶硬贴。

### 常见报错与补救

- **垫很快掉下：** 查接触面是否灰尘、潮湿或材质不合，按产品说明重新处理并换垫；不随意加未知强力胶。
- **只贴了两脚，另外两脚刮地：** 将未处理接触点标出，在能安全接近时逐一补齐。
- **贴好后家具反而晃：** 停止当作正常使用，检查是否垫厚度或脚腿原本异常；不要叠垫找平。
- **垫边粘了砂粒：** 按产品说明清洁/更换，避免让颗粒继续磨表面。
- **需要抬起大型物品：** 停在未安装，找合适搬运帮助；脚垫任务不授权重物操作。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通家具脚的可消耗性防刮垫。不同胶面、地板涂层和家具重量有各自限制，没有一个固定等待分钟数或万能粘法。视力、握持或行走受限时可请可信者在安全姿态下处理并让你读回检查结果；你的身体不必成为抬重物的工具。副作用是“贴上了”仍要接受一次真正的落地复查。

## English

Load this Skill after choosing an ordinary self-stick protector compatible with the real floor and contact point, when the furniture foot can be reached without dangerous moving. You will **attach it after the product's required preparation, check protection at every normal floor contact and know when to replace it**. This does not repair a leg, serve anti-tip or structural support, or lift, flip or drag heavy furniture.

### Preparation and inputs

Check pad, furniture and floor guidance to confirm a self-stick material is allowed there. The item must be stable, and the underside safely visible or handled in a suitable position by capable people. Stop if moving it requires extra people or specialist work. Use only cleaning materials the pad maker permits. Do not apply solvent, knives or hard abrasion to an unknown finish. Handle old adhesive residue under its instructions rather than prying off furniture material.

### Execution

1. **Inspect foot and old pad.** Look for a cracked leg, loose structural joint or other defect that needs real repair, and note a thin or gritty old pad. Do not conceal a structural problem with a new pad; stop use or ask suitable help.
2. **Clean and dry the contact under the product instructions.** Use an approved ordinary method to remove grit and dust that can affect adhesion, waiting until the stated condition is reached. If no suitable cleaning method is known, check the exact product rather than mixing a chemical solution.
3. **Attach at the normal contact.** Use the size already checked. Peel backing and place the pad under the real landing point, covering hard edges without protruding excessively. Press and wait as the pad product directs. Check every foot rather than only the easiest side.
4. **Check after safe placement.** Have people who can safely control the item return it to normal position. Verify each foot rests on a pad, edges have not lifted, the item has no new wobble and no exposed hard edge rubs the floor. Do not drag a heavy object to test; ask suitable handling help instead.
5. **Plan a wear check.** After ordinary cleaning or light movement, look for thinning, loose adhesive or trapped grit and replace under product guidance. Stop a use that keeps scratching when a pad has failed; do not wait for a distant deep clean.

### Success and stop

The safely accessible ordinary feet were prepared, padded and checked against the exact product guidance without an obvious missed contact or peeling edge. This supports surface protection only, not furniture stability or load safety. Stop for a cracked leg, wobble, failed adhesion, unknown surface suitability or dangerous moving; choose a real alternative rather than extra glue.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A pad falls off quickly:** Check dust, moisture and material compatibility, then prepare again and replace under instructions. Do not add unknown strong glue.
- **Two feet are padded but two scrape:** Mark unprotected contacts and address them when they can be reached safely.
- **The furniture wobbles after padding:** Stop treating it as ready. Check whether pad thickness or the original leg is wrong; do not stack pads to level it.
- **Grit catches at a pad edge:** Clean or replace under product instructions before particles keep abrading the floor.
- **A large item must be lifted:** Leave it uninstalled and arrange suitable moving help. A pad task does not authorise heavy handling.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This concerns replaceable ordinary scratch pads. Adhesives, finishes and furniture loads differ; there is no universal wait time or glue method. A trusted person can work in a safe position and read back checks if sight, grip or movement is limited. Your body need not be the lifting equipment. “It's stuck on” still needs a real floor-level check.
