## 中文

当你看过某款普通商品的旧评测、网页或说明，现场却有新旧包装、不同批次或地区版本混在一起，而且**你在意的一项功能可能随版本改变**时加载。你要确认手里这件商品适用哪一版资料，或明确“版本无法对应”。这里不鉴定真伪、不判断安全认证，也不把食品、药品或化妆品配方差异简化为普通功能差异。

### 准备与输入

写下你准备依据的那一条具体说法，例如“这款台灯有可更换的开关模块”，并保存该说明的准确型号、发布日期、地区和已注明的修订版。再看待买商品未拆封包装或可见铭牌上的完整型号、版本、批次或生产日期线索。只记录核对所需字段，不公开完整序列号或其他不必要编号。

### 执行

1. **先固定实物身份。** 对照包装、本体可见标签与货架牌，逐字记录型号后缀、修订标记和销售地区。未经许可不拆封寻找隐藏铭牌；外包装换色也不自动表示内部换代。
2. **固定资料的适用版。** 找制造商正式说明、更新记录或准确型号手册，读它写明适用的版本、时间或地区。旧评测和店员记忆可以提示要查哪一项，不能替代制造商对这件实物的映射。
3. **只判断你依赖的变化。** 问清“这个包装/版本是否包含〔功能〕？若有差异，哪一版开始改变？”需要时请店员联系制造商或展示版本对照资料。没有可核对的版本关系，就不要把当前网页直接套到旧库存。
4. **正确使用商品码。** 条码或 GTIN 可帮助查找商品，但不能单凭相同数字证明每个批次的所有内部细节相同，也不能单凭不同数字证明你在意的功能变了。以制造商针对实物版本的说明为最终核对对象。
5. **记录三态结果。** 写“实物版本与资料相符 / 资料只适用于另一版 / 版本仍未知”，附来源和查询日期。若功能是购买前提，而结果为后两项，就暂缓或换有清楚版本资料的商品。

### 成功与停止

你能指出手里这件的版本线索、所用资料的适用版，以及那项功能能否据此成立；或诚实停在未知。成功不要求店员保证以后所有批次永远相同，也不等于完成整机性能测试。

### 常见报错与补救

- **包装设计不同但编号相同：** 先问制造商是否仅换包装；别自行宣布功能改变或完全不变。
- **型号名字相同，网页没有修订日期：** 找正式版本对照或要求书面答复；没有就把旧评测的对应关系标为未确认。
- **店员只说“都是这一系列”：** 请其核对完整后缀及你关心的功能；系列名不足以结束。
- **看不清细小代码：** 拍包装标签放大、请可信同伴读回，或请店员提供可读的店内记录；代码仍无法确认就暂停。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设你只要核对普通功能，而制造商有公开或可索取的版本资料。编码规则会因品牌、商品和地区不同，不要用一套猜码方法套所有包装。商品码是供应链线索，不是消费者对每个细节的保证；GS1 标准也允许品牌在适用范围内自行决定更频繁地分配新编码。没有网络时可请店员展示制造商纸本或稍后查询，不把购买现场的压力当成版本证据。副作用是“同一款”终于写出它是哪一版。

## English

Load this Skill when you rely on an old review, webpage, or guide for an ordinary item, but old and new packaging, batches, or regional editions are mixed on the shelf and **one feature you care about may have changed**. Your endpoint is to match the physical sale unit to the applicable information, or to mark its revision unresolved. This does not authenticate goods, certify safety, or turn food, medicine, or cosmetic formulation differences into an ordinary feature check.

### Preparation and inputs

Write the exact statement you rely on, such as “this lamp has a replaceable switch module.” Keep the document's full model, date, region, and stated revision. Then look for the full model, revision, batch, or production-date clue visible on the unopened sale package or label. Record only fields needed for matching. Do not publish a full serial number or unrelated identifiers.

### Execution

1. **Identify the physical unit first.** Compare visible package or device labels with the shelf card. Record model suffix, revision mark, and sales region character by character. Do not open stock for a hidden label without permission. New package art does not automatically mean a new internal version.
2. **Identify the document's coverage.** Find a formal maker guide, change notice, or exact-model manual and read its applicable revision, time, and region. An old review or staff memory can suggest a question but cannot map this physical unit to the maker's edition.
3. **Check only the change that matters to you.** Ask, “Does this package/revision have [feature], and which edition changed it?” Staff may contact the maker or show a revision crosswalk. Without a checkable mapping, do not apply today's product page to old shelf stock automatically.
4. **Use product codes correctly.** A barcode or GTIN can help locate information, but the same number alone does not prove every internal detail is identical across batches. A different number alone does not prove your chosen feature changed. Check maker information that applies to the physical revision.
5. **Record three outcomes.** Write “unit revision matches the information / information covers another revision / revision still unknown,” with source and lookup date. If the feature is a buying condition, defer or choose an item with clearer revision information for either of the latter outcomes.

### Success and stop

You can identify this unit's revision clue, the document edition, and whether the particular feature follows from that match; or you stop at unknown. Completion does not require staff to guarantee all future batches stay identical or certify the whole product's performance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Package art differs but the product code matches:** Ask the maker whether only packaging changed. Do not announce either a feature change or perfect identity yourself.
- **The model name matches but the page has no revision date:** Seek a formal revision comparison or written answer; otherwise leave the old review's applicability unconfirmed.
- **Staff only say “same series”:** Ask for the complete suffix and the feature you care about. A family name is not an endpoint.
- **Tiny codes are inaccessible:** Enlarge a label photo, have a trusted person read it back, or ask staff for a readable stock record. Defer if the code remains unresolved.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary feature question and maker revision information that is public or obtainable. Coding varies by brand, product, and region; there is no universal package-code guessing trick. A product code is a supply-chain clue rather than proof of every detail. The GS1 standard also permits brands to assign new codes more often within its scope. Without internet, ask for maker paper information or check later; pressure at the counter is not version evidence. The side effect is a suffix attached to “the same model.”
