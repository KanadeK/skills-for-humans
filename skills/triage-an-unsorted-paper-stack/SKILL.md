## 中文

当桌上或信封夹里积了一叠你有权处理的普通纸张，而你不知道哪张下一步要做事、哪张要留下时加载。你要把每张纸先送往**需行动、需保存、确认可处理、待问**四条路；原工单的三类结果里，“待问”是不能安全归类时的必要停点。这里不解释法律、税务、财务或医疗内容，不规定保存年限，不接管他人身份资料。

### 准备与输入

在不让他人私密内容暴露的地方设四个临时区域或文件夹。先看纸张是否属于你或你已获准处理；写给他人的纸只按外部可见信息转给有权者，不擅自拆读。准备一份只写中性标题/来源、下一步和负责人/核对时间的本地短表，不把账号、证件号码或详细私密内容抄进共享清单。

### 执行

1. **一张一张看最低必要信息。** 先看标题、发送方和是否提出具体下一步；需要时只读足以分流的部分。看不懂、真伪不明、涉及特殊权利/义务或不属于你的，直接放“待问”，不靠猜测把它丢掉或执行。
2. **把需行动放在第一路。** 纸上若要求回覆、带材料、确认信息或处理普通事项，记“纸张简名 → 写明的请求与原文日期（如有） → 谁去核实/做下一步”。日期来自纸面但仍需向实际来源核对；本 Skill 不判断是否有法定期限，也不替你付款或填写敏感表格。
3. **把明确还需参考的放保存路。** 已知会用于普通后续核对、说明或凭证的原件先安全留下；文件索引与存放期限留给相应流程。若同一张先要行动、之后仍需保存，先标“行动后转保存”，不是复印成两份互相打架的真源。
4. **只在充分确定时走可处理路。** 明确无后续用途、你有处理权、也不属于应留原件或未知类别的普通重复页才列为可处理。含个人信息者先放待安全销毁，不直接扔进公开废纸；按实际场所和本人权限选择碎纸或其他不会露出信息的方法。不能确定是否可弃就回“待问”。
5. **清掉原始一叠的悬空状态。** 每张纸要么在有标签的四路之一，要么由有权者接手。对“需行动”设一次核对点，待问项写要问谁；保存项仍保持私密，待处理项未实际安全处置前不记为已销毁。

### 完成与停止

原来那叠纸不再混放：需行动有下一步负责人，需保存暂留，确认可处理的按权限与隐私要求处理，未知归“待问”。这不是对所有纸张保存合法性、事实真假或事项已经完成的证明。遇到法律、税务、财务、医疗或身份认证判断时停止内容解释，找实际机构或合适专业人士。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一封信同时要回复又要留底：** 先放需行动并写“完成后保存原件”，不要在两堆中各自找不到它。
- **看见印了日期就当成真实截止：** 抄下“纸上写的日期”，向实际来源核对，不据此给法律意见。
- **重复页上仍有个人资料：** 即使可弃也先按私密纸处理，别整张丢公开回收箱。
- **文件不是你的：** 只用已获准的外部信息交给有权者，不打开、转发或自行销毁。
- **不确定原件能否扔：** 暂存待问，记需要向谁确认；一时腾出桌面不是丢弃授权。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是一轮低风险物理分流，不产生统一保留年限、丢弃权或机构办理结论。地区、机构和文件性质不同；美国 FTC 的具体年限不被移植。识字、视力、行动或隐私条件不同，可由经授权的可信者在私密环境读回最小必要信息，决定仍由有权人确认。副作用是“待问”会留下一小撮诚实的纸，而不是让整叠纸继续假装一样重要。

## English

Load this Skill when a desk or folder holds a stack of ordinary papers you may handle and you cannot tell which page needs a next step or should be retained. Route each sheet first to **action, preservation, confirmed disposal or ask first**. “Ask first” is the necessary stop when the work order's three outcomes cannot be chosen safely. Do not interpret legal, tax, financial or medical content, set retention periods or take over another person's identity papers.

### Preparation and inputs

Make four temporary spots or folders where private information is not exposed. Check whether the paper is yours or you have permission to handle it. Route another person's paper by permitted outside information only; do not open or read it on your own. Keep a local short list of neutral title or source, next step, owner and check time. Do not copy account or ID numbers or private detail into a shared list.

### Execution

1. **Read the least information needed, one sheet at a time.** Start with heading, sender and any concrete requested step, reading only enough to route it. Put unclear, authenticity-uncertain, specialised-rights or somebody else's papers in “ask first.” Do not act on or discard them by guesswork.
2. **Send action requests to the first lane.** If the paper asks for a reply, materials, fact confirmation or another ordinary step, record “neutral page name → stated request and printed date if any → person to verify/do next.” A printed date still needs checking with the real source. This Skill does not judge a legal deadline, pay a bill or fill a sensitive form for you.
3. **Preserve a known future reference.** Keep an original safely when you know it will be needed for ordinary follow-up, instructions or proof. Filing index and retention length have their own process. If the same page needs action first and retention afterward, mark “action, then preserve”; do not create two competing sources of truth.
4. **Use disposal only with enough certainty.** An ordinary duplicate enters this lane only when you have disposal authority and know it has no remaining use, original requirement or unresolved category. Personal-information papers wait for secure destruction; do not put an intact sheet in open recycling. Follow the real site and your permissions for shredding or another method that does not expose the information. Uncertain disposal goes back to “ask first.”
5. **Remove the original pile's undefined state.** Each page reaches one labelled lane or an authorised holder. Give action items a review point and ask-first items a named contact. Keep preserved pages private. A paper awaiting secure destruction is not yet destroyed.

### Success and stop

The old pile is separated: action has a next-step owner, preservation is held, confirmed disposal follows permission and privacy handling, and unknowns are in “ask first.” This does not prove legal retention, truth of the page or completion of the underlying matter. Exit content interpretation for legal, tax, financial, medical or identity-authentication decisions and ask the real institution or suitable professional.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A letter needs both a reply and a retained original:** Put it in action first with “preserve original afterward,” instead of losing it between two stacks.
- **A printed date is assumed to be an official deadline:** Record “date printed on page” and verify with the actual source; do not issue legal guidance from it.
- **A duplicate still carries personal details:** Even if it is disposable, handle it privately rather than placing it intact in open recycling.
- **The document belongs to someone else:** Use permitted outside information to hand it to its owner; do not open, forward or destroy it.
- **You do not know whether an original may go:** Hold it in ask-first with a person to contact. A clear desk is not disposal authority.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a low-risk physical triage, not a universal retention period, disposal right or institutional decision. Rules differ by place, issuer and paper type; the US FTC's specific time periods are not imported. If reading, sight, mobility or privacy needs differ, an authorised trusted person can read back only the minimum in a private setting; the right holder still confirms decisions. A small honest ask-first stack may remain instead of one mysterious pile pretending every sheet has the same importance.
