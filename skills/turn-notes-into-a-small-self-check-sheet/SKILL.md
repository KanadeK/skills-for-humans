## 中文

当你已有一堆笔记，准备以后检查自己是否真的记得，却发现它们只适合重读时，用这份 Skill 把**已核来源的内容**转成少量**题目—答案—出处**分开的自测卡。结果是一份可用来遮住答案再作答的材料，不是你已经会了；隔一段时间真正自测归 H257，从例题自己做新题归 H255。

### 准备与输入

选一个真实要复查的小范围，拿出对应已有笔记和能回原文/课堂材料的位置。若笔记中的一条说法无来源、前后冲突或你还没弄懂，先标“待核”，不要把错误做成权威答案。用纸正反面、折页、可访问的本地文档或语音问答形式都可以；保证作答时能把答案真正遮住。不要把私人课堂材料、付费原题或别人的笔记全文上传到公共卡片平台。

### 执行

1. **只挑将来需要自己做的事。** 从笔记中选 3–5 个有用点，问是要回忆定义、解释关系、区分两种情况，还是按步骤判断；按真实学习目标选择题型，不因手头有荧光笔就把每句话变一题。
2. **把要点变成有边界的问题。** 例如“这两个概念在什么条件下不同？”或“给这个例子，先检查哪一步？”题目要足够具体，不能只写“第 2 章是什么”，也不能把答案直接放在题干里。
3. **答案用自己的话并附出处。** 在背面或另栏写可核对的短答案、必要条件和原文位置；来源本身不清楚时标“此卡未就绪”并回去核，不凭熟悉感填答案。多种可接受回答时写出判断点，而不是只留唯一关键词。
4. **做一次材料可用性测试。** 先遮住答案读题，问“我能看懂问什么吗？翻面后能按来源检验吗？”若题目歧义、答案太长或两卡重复，就改写或删卡；此时检查的是卡的质量，不算正式自测成绩。
5. **放到下次可取处。** 写主题和制作日期，留在下次回顾会打开的位置；把待核卡与可用卡分开。实际回顾时再用真实作答结果决定哪里还没掌握，不因做卡数量多就声称学习完成。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有 3–5 道**答案可遮住、问题明确、答案能回来源核对**的提示卡，且待核条目未混入“正确答案”组。只有重新抄整页笔记、答案始终在眼前，还没有可用的自测材料。

- **题目只问“这个是什么”：** 加上下文或一项具体判断，避免背一个词却不知何时使用。
- **答案里有未核事实：** 把该卡移到待核组，回原文或可靠人员确认后再用。
- **题目和答案写在同一行：** 折页、分栏或用可访问方式隐藏答案，保证下次能先作答。
- **做了很多重复卡：** 只留能检验不同概念/关系的几张，不按页数机械制造题目。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

并非所有学习任务适合问答卡；需要实际操作、写作或创造的能力还要做真正练习。本篇只把已有笔记变成一种复用材料，不承诺某种间隔、成绩或长期记忆。大字、读屏、语音和可信协助可以改变卡的形式，但答案须可独立隐藏并留来源。下次发现卡有误，改卡并保留错误线索，不把卡片当唯一真源。

## English

Use this Skill when you already have many notes and want to check later whether you remember them, but the notes only invite rereading. Convert **source-checked content** into a few separate **prompt—answer—source** cards. The outcome is material you can use with answers hidden, not proof you know it. Actually testing yourself after time passes is H257; solving a new problem independently is H255.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose one small real review scope and gather its existing notes and routes back to text or class material. If a note has no source, conflicts with another, or remains unclear to you, mark it “to verify” instead of making an authoritative answer. Paper fronts and backs, folded pages, an accessible local document, or audio prompts can work if answers can be genuinely hidden during response. Do not upload private class material, paid original questions, or someone else's notes in full to a public card site.

### Execution

1. **Pick what you will need to do later.** From the notes choose 3–5 useful points: recall a definition, explain a relation, distinguish cases, or decide a step. Match prompt type to the actual learning goal rather than make a card for every highlighted sentence.
2. **Write a bounded prompt.** “Under what condition do these concepts differ?” or “For this example, what do you check first?” is more answerable than “what is chapter 2?” Do not put the answer in the question itself.
3. **Write your own answer with a source.** On the back or separate field, give a short checkable answer, necessary conditions, and original location. If the source itself is unclear, mark “card not ready” and verify rather than answer by familiarity. For several acceptable responses, give the judging points instead of a single magic word.
4. **Test usability, not your score.** Hide the answer and read the prompt. Is it clear what is asked? After revealing it, can you verify against the source? Rewrite or remove ambiguity, overly long answers, or duplicate cards. This is a material-quality check, not an official self-test result.
5. **Leave them retrievable.** Label topic and creation date and store where you will open them for later review. Keep unverified cards apart from ready ones. In an actual later session, use your real response to find weak areas; card count alone proves no learning.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have 3–5 prompts with **hideable answers, clear questions, and source-checkable answers**, while unverified notes remain out of the “correct answer” set. A neater copied page with every answer visible is not usable self-check material.

- **A prompt only says “what is this?”:** Add context or one decision so you test when a concept applies, not just its name.
- **An answer contains an unchecked fact:** Move it to the to-verify group until the source or a reliable person resolves it.
- **Prompt and answer share the same line:** Fold paper, separate fields, or use an accessible hide method so you can answer first.
- **Many cards repeat one point:** Keep a few that test different ideas or relations instead of creating cards by page count.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Not every learning task suits question cards; hands-on, writing, or creative ability still needs real practice. This Skill turns notes into one reusable form, without promising a spacing schedule, grade, or memory gain. Large print, screen reading, audio, and trusted help may change card form while answers remain independently hideable and sourced. Correct a wrong card later and retain the error clue; cards are not the only source of truth.
