## 中文

当你打算买一件需要按说明进行普通使用或保养的商品，却只有难读的小纸片、打不开的二维码或一个“网上有说明”的口头回答时加载。你要找到**这个准确型号、你实际能读懂并在需要时再次取得的完整说明**。这不是把商品标签上的某一项限制判成可买，也不教安装、维修或危险操作。

### 准备与输入

记下品牌、完整型号和可能的版本/地区后缀。写出你将要做的普通动作，例如装好无需工具的部件、清洁表面或更换日常可换耗材，并明确你能用的语言和格式：普通文字、放大字、可复制的屏幕文字、音频、盲文或纸本。格式由你的实际可用性决定，不靠别人猜。

### 执行

1. **找准确版本。** 查看箱内说明样张、制造商正式产品页或店方能展示的准确型号说明。页面只有系列名称时，继续找型号及版本；未经允许不拆封新商品。
2. **实际打开，不只保存链接。** 试读与你的普通操作相关的一节、图示说明和停止条件。能看到文件名不等于能读取正文；只有图片而读屏器读不出关键步骤，也不算可用。
3. **请求可用的替代。** 请店员或制造商提供可读电子文本、放大字、纸本、音频等实际存在的选项；确认是否收费、何时可取得。不能保证所有商家提供每种格式，不把“有人可以口述一次”当作可反复使用的手册。
4. **确认带回家的路径。** 记录正式链接、文件版本或随箱文件位置；需要离线使用时先确认能保存或取得纸本。试着再次打开同一节，避免回家后只剩过期二维码。
5. **按缺失程度决定。** 若你必须依赖的操作或警告仍不可读，暂停购买，选择说明更完整的商品，或等制造商答复。无需说明的简单物品不因本 Skill 增加一份假手册。

### 成功与停止

你能指出准确型号的说明来源，亲自使用选定格式读取必要章节，并知道购买后如何重新取得；或明确记录哪一节不可用而停止。完成不要求店员保证未来网页永远在线。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有另一个型号的说明：** 不套用按钮、部件和限制；请求准确型号资料。
- **PDF 能下载却无法读图：** 要可复制文字、带说明的图、音频或纸本；关键步骤仍不可达就保留未确认。
- **店员只作一次口头演示：** 用它定位问题，但需可带走的正式说明才能反复核对依赖步骤。
- **中途断网或二维码失效：** 改查制造商正式资料或店内纸本；不能恢复必要信息时暂缓。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设商品需要普通家庭说明且制造商或店方可提供某种正式版本。不同地区、语言、版本的说明可能不一致；只按你准备买的型号核对。没有手机、网络、视力、听力或稳定握持能力时，优先选你实际可用的格式及现场协助，但关键依赖信息仍须可复查。副作用是购物前多读一页，通常比买回家才寻找“第 1 步”容易。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary item whose use or care depends on instructions, when all you have is tiny print, a QR page you cannot open, or “the manual is online.” Your endpoint is a **complete guide for the exact model that you can actually use and retrieve later**. This does not decide a single printed label restriction or teach installation, repair, or hazardous operation.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the brand, full model, and any revision or regional suffix. Name the ordinary action you expect to perform, such as fitting a tool-free part, cleaning a surface, or replacing a routine consumable. Choose a language and format you can use: ordinary print, large print, selectable screen text, audio, braille, or paper. Your access need is an input, not a guess for staff to make.

### Execution

1. **Find the exact edition.** Check a sample booklet, the maker's official product page, or an exact-model guide the shop can show. A series-only page needs another model check. Do not open sealed stock without permission.
2. **Open the content, not just the link.** Try reading a section for your ordinary task, its diagrams, and its stop conditions. A filename is not readable instructions; an image-only page that hides essential steps from your screen reader is not usable either.
3. **Request an available alternative.** Ask the shop or manufacturer whether they can provide accessible text, large print, paper, audio, or another existing format. Confirm any cost and when it can be obtained. No store is assumed to offer every format, and a one-time oral summary is not a guide you can check again.
4. **Keep a route for later.** Record the official link, file edition, or location of the included guide. If offline use matters, confirm that you can save it or obtain paper. Reopen the same section once so you do not take home only an expired QR code.
5. **Pause when the missing section matters.** If an operation or warning you must rely on is still inaccessible, defer the purchase, choose a better documented item, or wait for the maker. A simple item that needs no instructions does not require an invented manual.

### Success and stop

You can name the source for the exact model, use your chosen format to read the necessary section yourself, and retrieve it after purchase; or you record the unavailable section and stop. Staff do not need to guarantee that a website will remain online forever.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a different model's manual appears:** Do not borrow its buttons, parts, or limits; request the exact model.
- **A PDF downloads but key diagrams cannot be read:** Ask for selectable text, described diagrams, audio, or paper. Keep essential steps unconfirmed if access is still missing.
- **Staff give only a one-time demonstration:** Use it to locate questions, then seek instructions you can retain and revisit for dependent steps.
- **The network drops or QR code fails:** Try official maker information or a shop paper copy. Defer if the necessary section remains unavailable.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary household item needs instructions and that the maker or store has a formal edition. Versions can differ by region, language, and revision; match what you plan to buy. Without a phone, internet, sight, hearing, or steady grip, choose a format and in-store help you can actually use, while keeping essential information available for later checking. The side effect is reading a page before purchase instead of searching for “step 1” after bringing the item home.
