## 中文

当你为一件普通商品的退换咨询、服务询问或其他日常后续事项需要找回**纸质收据中真正对应这件商品的一行**时加载。你要找到并核对实际单据，或清楚记录尚未找到及下一步；这里不核算账目、不判断退款、保修或法定期限，也不造一张“差不多”的凭证。

### 准备与输入

写下商品的中性名称、可安全读出的型号/变体、可能的商家与大致购买时间。看你已有的物品—凭证索引、纸档位置和实际商家当前关于所需凭证的说明；它可能接受别的资料，但本文不替商家作决定。只检查你有权查看的收据和私人物品，不借查纸之名翻别人的付款资料。

### 执行

1. **先走已有线索。** 从物品记录或纸档索引找到收据文件夹；没有索引时按现实路径依次查你自己的钱包/购物袋、商品原包装附袋、指定收据夹等少数位置，查过就划掉，不无边界地翻遍整间屋子。
2. **核到正确一行。** 看开具方、纸上日期、商品描述/型号或可识别的商品行是否与实物和已有订单事实相符。一张纸可能含多件商品，商店名相同不等于这件商品就在其中；名称缩写、型号后缀或日期冲突时标待确认。
3. **看纸张是否仍可读。** 保留原件和可辨的凭据号、商品行、必要日期与商家信息，不靠记忆补热敏纸上已淡掉的字符。按实际服务需要判断需原件、复印件还是其他形式；复制由后续任务处理，不把照片自动当成被接受的原件。
4. **保护无关私人信息。** 一张长收据上的其他购买、支付尾号或地址不必向无关者公开；在家先按授权存放，正式提供时遵守实际机构要求。不要把整张未经处理的收据贴到公开群求网友认。
5. **找不到就留下可执行下一步。** 记查过的范围、最可能商家和仍不明的商品/日期，再经商家官方渠道询问是否存在它认可的查单或替代凭据方式。对方未答时保持“未找到/待答”，不说已经具备材料；找到后归回真实索引位置。

### 完成与停止

你手上有一张能对应眼前商品实际行的可读纸质收据及其存放位置，或一条诚实的未找到状态与下一联系人；这不保证商家接受或事项有利解决。单据属于他人、商品行冲突、文字不可读或问题转入法律/财务判断时暂停推断，向有权者或实际商家确认。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看见熟悉商店名：** 逐行找这件商品，找不到就不认领这张收据。
- **一张收据有四件商品：** 标真正对应的商品行，不用合计金额代替物品证明。
- **纸张褪色：** 保留原件与可读部分，查本人已有订单/商家正式渠道，不伪造看不见的号码。
- **型号后缀不同：** 将实物和纸上的完整标识并列，问实际商家是否同一笔，不靠外观判断。
- **查找陷入无限翻找：** 停在已查位置清单，改用商家官方查询渠道或等待有权者提供线索。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这只处理普通商品纸质收据的物理查找与交易行核对，不计算税、费用、信用卡账单或法律权利。地区、商家和服务目的改变可接受的凭证类型；美国 FTC 与 Apple 的资料只是具体情境示例。视力、阅读或行动不便时请可信授权者读回关键行，不必把其它私密交易也朗读给旁人。副作用是“看着像那家店的票”不再被当成商品对应证据。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary return question, service enquiry or other everyday follow-up needs **the actual line on a paper receipt that corresponds to one item**. Find and check the real receipt, or record honestly that it has not been found and what happens next. Do not reconcile finances, decide refunds, warranty or legal deadlines, or manufacture a close-enough receipt.

### Preparation and inputs

Write a neutral item name, a safely readable model or variant, possible seller and rough purchase period. Check any existing item-to-proof index, physical paper location and the real seller's current statement about acceptable proof. It may accept something else, but this Skill does not decide for the business. Search only receipts and personal places you may inspect; a receipt search does not permit reading another person's payment records.

### Execution

1. **Follow existing clues first.** Use an item note or folder index. Without one, check a few actual likely places in order, such as your own wallet or shopping bag, the item's original package pocket and your designated receipt folder. Mark each searched place. Do not turn one receipt into an unbounded house search.
2. **Match the correct line.** Compare issuer, printed date, item description or model and identifiable product line with the real item and known order facts. One receipt can list many goods; the right store name does not prove your item is on it. Treat abbreviated names, suffixes or conflicting dates as uncertain.
3. **Check that the paper can still be read.** Keep the original and legible reference, item line, necessary date and seller information. Do not reconstruct faded thermal print from memory. Check the actual service's original, copy or other format need; copying is a later task, and a photograph is not automatically an accepted original.
4. **Protect unrelated private detail.** Other purchases, payment digits or addresses on a long receipt need not be shown to unrelated people. Store it under your authority, then follow the actual institution's requirements when providing it. Do not post an unredacted receipt in a public group to ask strangers what it is.
5. **Leave an actionable next step if absent.** Record places checked, likely seller and unknown item or date details, then ask through the seller's official channel whether it has an accepted lookup or alternate proof route. No reply means “not found / awaiting answer,” not “materials ready.” Return a found receipt to its real indexed place.

### Success and stop

You have a readable paper receipt whose real line matches the item in front of you, with a findable storage place, or an honest missing state and next contact. This does not guarantee seller acceptance or a favourable outcome. Stop inferring when the receipt belongs to someone else, item line conflicts, print is unreadable, or the matter needs legal or financial judgment; ask the authorised holder or actual seller.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a familiar store name is visible:** Search the lines for this item. Do not claim the receipt if it is absent.
- **One receipt lists four products:** Mark the actual product line, not the total as item proof.
- **Print has faded:** Keep paper and what remains legible and check your own original order or the merchant's official route. Do not invent invisible numbers.
- **The model suffix differs:** Compare complete item and paper IDs and ask the seller about the transaction rather than deciding by appearance.
- **Searching becomes endless:** Stop at your list of checked locations and use the seller's official lookup or wait for a clue from the person authorised to hold it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is physical retrieval and transaction-line matching for an ordinary item, not tax, fee, credit-card or legal analysis. Provider, region and purpose change acceptable proof; the FTC and Apple are contextual examples only. A trusted authorised person can read the relevant line when sight, reading or mobility is limited without broadcasting other private purchases. “Looks like that store” no longer proves an item match.
