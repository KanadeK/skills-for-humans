## 中文

当你管理的**非食用观赏植物**已经形成成熟种荚或种头，并且你想留一小份供今后播种时，加载这份 Skill。任务终点是品种、来源、日期和后续处理明确的种子，而不是“随手摇下一把”。E003 摘残花会阻止这朵花结种；本 Skill 从已保留并成熟的种头开始。E002 的室内播种则从可用种袋开始。

### 准备与输入

确认品种及所有者许可，查这种植物的种子成熟迹象、是否宜立即播、能否干燥储存，以及当地采集/传播限制。野生、受保护、入侵性不明或不能辨认的植物不采。母株有明显病虫、种头潮湿发霉或种子仍未成熟时先停下。准备干净纸袋或浅盘、可读标签；有刺或刺激性汁液风险则交合适人员。

### 执行

1. **核成熟而非日期。** 按品种资料看种荚颜色、触感和开裂状态，选择健康母株与合适天气。RHS 建议多种种子在干燥日采收，但有些品种宜刚熟即播；不套用一种晾干法。
2. **少量收集并即时标源。** 在纸袋或盘上先写品种、地点/母株和采收日期，再轻取已成熟种头，避免抖散到别人地块。留给景观或野生动物的种头按事先约定保留。
3. **依品种分离与处理。** 适合干燥储存者，在干净、通风且不混袋的位置让种头达到该品种要求，再去掉容易霉变的碎屑；需鲜播或保湿的品种按可靠资料转到相应路径，不强行烘干封袋。
4. **核对并存放。** 把可存种子装入清楚标记的合适容器，记录“未测发芽率”和下一次复查/播种入口。发现虫、霉或身份混淆时隔离该批并求教，不与健康种子混装。

### 成功条件

你手上的一小批种子来源、品种和日期可追溯，已按品种完成必要干燥或明确进入鲜播路径，后续存放条件有依据。没有成熟种子、授权或可确认身份时停止，不把采空一株当作成功。

### 常见报错与补救

- **种荚还是绿的：** 查品种成熟特征并等候；提早摘下通常不能靠储存补熟。
- **纸袋上只写“花”：** 立即补品种、来源与日期；无法确认的批次不冒充确定身份。
- **种子潮湿就密封：** 暂停，按该品种资料处理水分；别给霉菌安排封闭办公室。
- **杂交植株收种：** 下一代性状可能不同，标记为未知，不保证复制母株颜色。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理有权管理、可安全接触的普通非食用观赏植物，不处理野生保护物种、种子贸易、检疫或食用种子安全。种子可能不发芽，湿度和储藏条件依品种不同。手部操作困难可用托盘和协助，视力受限可请人读标签和描述成熟迹象。副作用是一小包要妥善标记与复查的材料。

## English

Load this Skill when a **non-edible ornamental you manage** has ripe seedheads or pods and you want a small future-sowing lot. Finish with traceable species, parent, date, and next handling route, not an unlabelled handful. E003 deadheading prevents that flower setting seed; this starts after selected heads were retained and ripened. E002 indoor sowing starts with usable seed.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm species and owner permission. Check its ripeness signs, whether seed must be sown fresh or can be dried and stored, and any local collection or spread restriction. Do not collect unknown, wild, protected, or possibly invasive plants. Hold if the parent shows obvious pest/disease, heads are mouldy, or seed is immature. Prepare a clean paper bag or shallow tray and readable label; arrange help for spines or irritating sap.

### Execution

1. **Check ripeness, not only the date.** Use species guidance for pod colour, texture, and opening stage and choose a healthy parent and suitable weather. RHS advises dry-day collection for many seeds, but some are best sown as soon as ripe. One drying rule will not fit all.
2. **Collect a small amount with source on the bag first.** Write species, parent/location, and date before gently taking ripe heads into the bag or tray. Do not shake seed across another person's bed. Leave seedheads intended for wildlife or display as previously agreed.
3. **Separate and handle by species.** For dry-storable seed, let heads reach the required state in a clean airy labelled place, then remove chaff that could encourage mould. For fresh-sown or moisture-dependent seed, follow reliable specific directions instead of baking it dry and sealing it.
4. **Check and store.** Put suitable seed in a clearly labelled container, noting “germination untested” and the next review or sowing route. Isolate a lot with insects, mould, or confused identity and seek advice rather than mixing it with sound seed.

### Success

One small lot has traceable species, parent, and date; it was dried appropriately or explicitly routed for fresh sowing, with a supported next storage state. Stop if ripe seed, authority, or identity cannot be confirmed. Emptying the whole plant is not a success requirement.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The pod is still green:** Check that species' ripeness signs and wait. Storage rarely matures an early picked seed.
- **The bag says only “flower”:** Add species, source, and date now; a confused lot is not identified seed.
- **Damp seed is sealed:** Pause and follow species moisture guidance. Do not give mould a closed office.
- **The parent is a hybrid:** Offspring may differ. Mark that uncertainty rather than promising identical flowers.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is for ordinary non-edible ornamentals you may handle and collect, not protected wild plants, seed trade, quarantine, or edible-seed safety. Germination is not guaranteed, and humidity/storage needs vary. Use a tray and helper if gripping is difficult; ask for label reading and ripeness description if vision is limited. A small marked lot now needs storage and review.
