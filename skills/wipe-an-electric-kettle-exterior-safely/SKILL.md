## 中文

当你的一台**运行正常的普通电热水壶**外壳有手印、水滴或轻微污迹，想在下次烧水前清理外表面时，用这份 Skill 先断电冷却，再按准确机型允许范围轻擦、擦干。结果仅是外部可接近部分清洁、连接器保持干燥；壶内除垢、滤网、插头和线路维修均不由“擦外面”完成。不可把电壶整只浸进水盆里。

### 准备与输入

找到准确型号和说明书，确认开关、插头、底座、壶身与连接点的位置，说明书对可擦范围、清洁品、壶是否可离开底座有何要求。查看电线、插头、外壳是否完整，运行是否正常；有破损或进液疑虑时停止用机并找制造商/合格维修。准备稳定干燥台面、厂家允许的柔软微湿布与干布；不把水槽、喷瓶和电气底座放在同一条水流线上。

刚烧完水时按机型关闭、从电源安全断开并等到壶身可安全接触；不摸加热面试温。家用电壶的底座、线、插头及连接器都不靠水浸或冲洗清洁。

### 执行

1. **先停机、断电并冷却。** 按说明关闭并从电源断开，等壶外部到说明允许的清洁状态。若型号支持壶身从底座取下，按其方法稳妥分开；不拖着电线移动底座，也不在热壶上喷水制造蒸汽。
2. **确认可擦外面与避水区。** 只对说明书允许的壶体外壳、外把手等可见表面操作；认清底部接点、底座接口、开关缝、电线、插头和可能进液的开口。顽固水垢在**壶内**不由此步处理，不能把清洁液灌入壶体来“顺便除垢”。
3. **微湿软布小处试擦。** 把允许的清洁品按标签少量放在布上，而不是向电壶或底座喷洒；用稍湿不滴水的软布在不显眼外表面试擦，确认涂层/标识不掉色。机型若只允许清水湿布则不用额外洗剂。避免钢丝球、强研磨和让水流过开关或连接器。
4. **轻擦、去残留并彻底干燥。** 小范围处理手印和水迹，用按说明准备的另一块洁净微湿布去掉允许的洗剂，再用干布擦干壶体；底座如说明允许擦拭，也仅用规定方法处理可见外面，不让湿布碰连接器、线或插头。全部可擦外部和接点周围按机型要求干燥后才考虑归位。
5. **回到正常使用或明确停用。** 外壳无普通污迹和洗剂膜、电气接点未沾水、机型说明的干燥条件满足，才把壶和座按原结构放回。若发现线/插头损坏、连接点进液、壶体漏水或异常运行，保持断电并找制造商或合格维修；外壳洁净不构成电气自检。

### 成功条件

已断电冷却的普通电热水壶按准确机型说明擦净可接近外壳、去残留并干燥，底座连接器、电线与插头没有被浸水或喷湿，归位条件清楚。不能确认型号、已经损坏或疑似进液时停用并转合格服务，同样完成判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **忘了拔电，就在水槽旁擦：** 停止并按准确机型安全断电、移到干燥稳定台面；湿擦不与带电底座同时进行。
- **壶刚烧完，靠手掌试外壳：** 等制造商允许的冷却状态，不用皮肤替代温度指示。
- **直接把壶或底座浸进水：** 停止使用；按产品说明处理可能进液，必要时请合格服务，不通电“烘干”内部。
- **喷瓶朝开关或连接器喷：** 停止喷洒，维持断电并按制造商进液处理路线，别拆壳自行擦电路。
- **外壳擦亮仍有壶内白垢：** 另按准确机型的除垢说明处理；外擦不宣称改变饮水接触面。
- **电线破损或壶体漏水：** 不继续烧水测试，交制造商/合格维修或更换。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只清理**运行正常且型号可确认的家用电热水壶外部**，不触及内部除垢、滤网、加热元件、插头或线路维修。不同型号对壶身能否离座、可擦底座部位与洗剂许可不同，原机手册优先。握力、站立或视力受限时在冷却后用稳定台面、明亮光线和可信协助；不能保证接点干燥就停止。外壳洁净不会替内壁水垢或饮口完成任何处理。

## English

Use this Skill when the outside of a **normally working ordinary electric kettle** has fingerprints, drips, or light soil and you want to clean it before the next boil. Unplug and cool it first, then wipe and dry only the exact model's permitted exterior. The result is clean accessible outside material with dry electrical connections. Descaling the inside, filter care, and cord or plug repair are not achieved by an outside wipe. Never immerse the whole kettle in a basin.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the exact model and manual. Identify switch, plug, base, body, and connector, and which exterior areas, products, and body/base separation the manual permits. Check the cord, plug, and shell are sound and operation has been normal. Stop use and ask the maker or qualified service for damage or suspected liquid ingress. Set up a stable dry counter with permitted soft slightly damp and dry cloths. Do not line up the sink or spray bottle to flow across an electrical base.

After boiling, switch off and disconnect from power as the exact model directs, then let the outside cool to a safe cleaning state. Do not test a heating surface with skin. Base, cord, plug, and connector are not immersed or rinsed under a tap.

### Execution

1. **Stop, unplug, and cool.** Switch off and disconnect under the manual, then wait for the permitted cleaning state. If this model allows lifting the kettle body from its base, separate it steadily as directed. Do not drag the base by its cord or spray water at a hot vessel.
2. **Map wipeable and keep-dry zones.** Work only on visible exterior body or handle areas the manual allows. Identify bottom contact, base connector, switch seam, cord, plug, and openings that can take liquid inside. Scale **inside** is not this task. Do not pour a cleaner through the kettle for an incidental descaling step.
3. **Trial a slightly damp soft cloth.** Put any allowed product on the cloth in the labelled small amount rather than spraying the appliance. Test an inconspicuous outside spot with a cloth that is damp but not dripping; stop if finish or markings change. If the model permits only water-damp cloth, use no extra soap. Avoid steel wool, harsh abrasion, and water running over controls or connectors.
4. **Wipe, remove residue, and dry completely.** Work in small outside areas. Use another clean slightly damp cloth under the product instructions for any cleaner film and a dry cloth for the body. If the manual permits wiping an accessible base exterior, keep moisture off connector, cord, and plug. Meet the model's dry condition on the body and around contacts before considering reassembly.
5. **Return to normal use or hold out of service.** Only if ordinary marks and soap film are gone, contacts stayed dry, and maker drying conditions are met should body and base be returned as designed. For cord or plug damage, liquid at a connector, a leaking body, or abnormal operation, keep unplugged and seek maker or qualified service. Exterior cleanliness is not an electrical self-test.

### Success

The unplugged cooled kettle has its allowed outside gently cleaned, residue removed, and surfaces dry. Connector, cord, and plug were not immersed or sprayed, and return-to-use conditions are known. Stopping for an unknown model, damage, or suspected ingress and seeking qualified help is also a complete decision.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You wipe beside a sink without unplugging:** Stop and disconnect safely under the exact manual. Wet cleaning and a powered base do not overlap.
- **A just-boiled kettle is tested with a palm:** Wait for the maker's cooling condition; skin is not a temperature indicator.
- **The kettle or base goes into water:** Stop use. Follow the product's ingress guidance and qualified service when needed; do not power it on to “dry the inside.”
- **Spray points at the switch or connector:** Stop spraying. Leave unplugged and follow maker ingress handling; do not open the shell to wipe circuits.
- **The shell shines but white scale remains inside:** Handle that later under the exact model's descaling instructions. Outside wiping is no claim about drink-contact surfaces.
- **Cord damage or leaking body appears:** Do not boil water as a test. Seek maker or qualified repair or replacement.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This cleans **the exterior of a normally working identified household electric kettle**, not inside descaling, filters, heating elements, plugs, or wiring. Models vary in body/base separation, wipeable base parts, and permitted products; the real manual governs. For limited grip, standing, or sight, use a stable bright counter and trusted help after cooling. Stop if dry connectors cannot be assured. A clean outside does nothing to interior scale or drinking surfaces.
