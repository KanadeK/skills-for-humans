## 中文

当你准备把一批**已经洗净的普通湿衣物**放入家用滚筒烘干机时，用这份 Skill 先决定哪些能一起入机、哪些要自然晾干或暂停。洗衣机里同桶洗过不代表烘干条件也兼容；本篇不决定烘干程序，也不修理设备。来源不明的易燃物、工业油、溶剂或其他危险污染衣物不入普通烘干流程，即使看起来洗过。

### 准备与输入

逐件读干燥标签的禁止滚筒、温度/轻柔等级、自然晾干等要求，查与你标签体系相符的图例。读确切烘干机型号说明的负载、过滤网及警报要求；确认机器无明显损坏或未处理的排风警示。准备可放被排除衣物的安全晾晒位置。标签或污染史不明时先隔离该件，不用多数衣物的许可替它投票。

### 执行

1. **排出禁止滚筒或需专门护理的衣物。** 标签叉掉滚筒、明确平铺/阴干、结构易因热和翻滚受损，或制造商要求特殊方法的衣物另放；没有可读说明的衣物暂停并查来源。衣物的洗涤许可不是烘干许可。
2. **筛安全与污染史。** 若有燃油、溶剂、危险化学品、工业油或不明易燃接触史，停止这件衣物入机并按产品、设备或专业安全指导处理；不要以“已经机洗一次”清除未知风险。检查口袋无打火机、电池等物品，衣物无易脱落会损伤机器的附件。
3. **按共同限制分小组。** 温度上限、轻柔要求、体积和预计干燥差异太大的衣物不强行同桶；可烘组要能在该机型允许负载内自由翻动，不能因关得上门就算通过。厚重与轻薄衣物若无法用同一条件兼顾，分批处理。
4. **核对机器可用。** 按本机说明检查并清理可由使用者操作的绒毛过滤网，安装到位。过滤网缺失/损坏、排风警报、焦味、烟、火花或异常噪声时停止使用并联系合适的维修/紧急帮助；不要拆机、绕过警报或用更长时间替代排风问题。
5. **形成入机清单。** 把可用的一组装到不超载的机器里；不能入机的衣物有标签允许的晾晒或等待可靠说明的去向。接下来才按这组的标签和准确机型说明选程序。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

烘干机里只有标签相容且风险史可确认的一组，装载符合本机限制，过滤网在位且设备没有未处理警报；其余湿衣物有明确去向。没有安全可烘组时停止开机就是完整结果。

- **标签写不可滚筒却同桶洗过：** 取出按其自然晾干说明安放，不能用低温滚筒猜豁免。
- **厚毛巾与薄衣挤满滚筒：** 减少负载或分批，按各组条件烘；不复制某款设备的容量数字。
- **过滤网缺失或警示仍亮：** 停用并查该机说明/联系管理者或专业人员，不用一次试跑验证故障。
- **湿衣未入机部分没有晾处：** 先安排合适场地和湿衣去向，避免在密闭桶内无计划滞留。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

面向普通家用、完好设备；共享洗衣房还需服从现场规则。不同地区标签符号和机器负载定义不同，按实际衣物与机型读取。看不清小标签或过滤网时可放大、使用可及说明和可信现场协助；端湿大件困难时分小组操作。筛选可能增加轮次和晾衣空间，但不替代设备维修。

## English

Use this Skill before putting a load of **already washed ordinary wet clothes** into a household tumble dryer. Decide what may dry together and what needs air drying or a pause. Sharing a washer load did not prove compatible dryer conditions. This does not choose a cycle or repair equipment. Clothes exposed to unknown flammables, industrial oil, solvents, or other hazardous contaminants leave ordinary tumble drying even if they appear washed.

### Preparation and inputs

Read each item's tumble prohibition, heat or mild-process limit, and natural-dry instruction with the matching symbol guide. Read the exact dryer model's load, lint-filter, and alert requirements. Confirm it has no visible damage or unaddressed vent warning. Have a suitable drying destination for excluded items. Hold an item whose label or contamination history is unclear; the majority's permission does not vote for it.

### Execution

1. **Remove items forbidden from tumbling or needing special care.** Hold clothes marked no tumble, flat/shade drying, heat or movement-sensitive construction, or another maker-specific method. Seek directions for unreadable labels. Wash permission is not tumble permission.
2. **Screen safety and contact history.** Stop an item exposed to fuel, solvents, hazardous chemicals, industrial oil, or unknown flammable material and use the relevant product, equipment, or professional safety guidance. One washer cycle does not establish that the risk has gone. Empty lighters, batteries, and other pocket items and remove accessories that may break loose in the drum.
3. **Group by a shared limit.** Do not force together incompatible heat limits, gentle-action needs, bulk, or markedly different drying behaviour. Keep the allowed group inside this model's load limit with room to tumble; a door that closes does not certify capacity. Divide heavy and light items if one process cannot serve both.
4. **Confirm the machine is usable.** Inspect and clean the user-accessible lint filter by this model's manual and reinstall it properly. Stop using the dryer for a missing or damaged filter, vent warning, burning smell, smoke, sparks, or abnormal noise. Contact the appropriate manager, qualified service, or emergency help. Do not dismantle it, bypass an alert, or substitute more time for a vent problem.
5. **Make the dryer load.** Put one safe compatible group in without overloading. Every excluded wet item has an air-dry route allowed by its label or a hold for reliable directions. Only then choose the cycle using this group and the exact manual.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

The drum contains a label-compatible group with known safe contact history and model-compliant load, its lint filter is in place, and no alert is being ignored. Other wet items have destinations. Stopping without running an unsafe group is a complete result.

- **No-tumble item was washed in the same load:** Remove it for label-compliant air drying. A low-heat tumble is not an invented exception.
- **Heavy towels and thin items pack the drum:** Reduce or divide the load; do not copy capacity numbers from another model.
- **Filter missing or alert persists:** Stop and use this manual, the site manager, or qualified service. A trial run does not validate a fault.
- **Excluded wet items have nowhere to dry:** Arrange a suitable place and wet-laundry plan first rather than leaving them closed up indefinitely.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary home use of an undamaged dryer; shared laundries also have site rules. Care-symbol systems and load definitions vary, so read this garment and this model. Enlarge labels, use accessible instructions, or ask trusted help for a small filter or label. Handle bulky wet items in smaller groups if lifting is difficult. Screening may use another cycle or more rack space; it is not appliance repair.
