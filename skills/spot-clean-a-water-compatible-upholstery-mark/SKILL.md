## 中文

当你已确认一块普通纺织家具的**准确护理标签允许水基局部处理**（例如该厂的 W 或 W-S/WS），已知日常小污点仍在表层，而且适用清洁产品的隐藏小处试验没有伤色或变硬时，用这份 Skill 轻点清。若泼洒还在扩散，先用普通小泼洒控制流程；若渗入内垫、通电部件、霉斑、体液、危险化学品或不明污染，则退出普通点清。

### 准备与输入

重新核对家具厂家、代码与产品标签：是否可用水基泡沫/洗剂、用量、需否去产品残留、通风及干燥方式。准备干净白布/纸、少量适用产品与干燥处，移开易被清洁剂碰到的物品。确认房间能通风、你能在稳定位置处理污点，坐垫下没有带电机构可能进水。标签或隐藏测试结果不明时停止，别因污点显眼就跳过。

### 执行

1. **先吸走可松动的污物。** 对安全、已知的表面液体用白布轻按，从外围往内更换干净面；半固体用不伤纤维的适用工具轻挑表面，不把物质揉进填充。不要大面积浇水“洗匀色差”。
2. **只按这件家具和一个产品说明轻试显眼处。** 把厂家认可的水基泡沫/清洁剂按标签用于小范围布面，轻轻按/擦，控制湿度，保持垫芯不被浸透。不能用溶剂、强力蒸汽或不同清洁品叠加来加速。
3. **按产品要求处理残留。** 如要求清水去泡沫，用仅微湿的干净布按压/擦去，不要冲洗固定坐垫；持续有泡沫或白印就停下核对产品说明，不能随意再加别的清洁剂。
4. **让表面和可检查部分真正干。** 按材料允许的方式通风、保持座位暂不使用，必要时用干净布吸去表层余水；不用未获厂家允许的热风直吹或封套把潮气关进坐垫。表面干但内垫仍潮、出现水圈或持续异味时停，找厂家/专业处理；不宣称深层已除害。
5. **干后按结果结束。** 自然干后在相同光线下检查颜色、纹理和污点：可接受且无新损伤时恢复使用；仍明显、掉色、起毛、泛圈或变硬时停止重复试验，记录产品与照片求厂家/专业意见。局部改善不等于整件家具被全面清洁。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

一个确知可水基处理的表层小污点按两份标签与隐藏测试条件得到有限清洁，坐垫可检查处已干、没有新水圈/掉色；若不改善或湿入填充，明确停用待查也是完整结果。

- **白布沾上家具原色：** 立即停止局部擦，保留测试和产品记录问厂家。
- **污点中心变淡但外围一圈湿印：** 不扩大浸湿试图“抹平”，先干燥并停下评估。
- **清洁后有人立刻坐下：** 先让区域按说明干透，避免把潮处与残留压进垫芯。
- **产品标签要求溶剂但家具是 W：** 不使用；找与家具同意的水基产品或停止。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理经确认的普通表层小污点，不做 S/X 面料溶剂、深层抽洗、除螨/杀菌或电动家具拆修。面料染色、填充和产品不同，清洗后仍可能留印。视觉不便时用相同光源照片、可读标签及可信协助；手部力量不便可用稳定小布轻按，不以大力刷代替判断。座位会暂时不可用，干燥需要真实时间。

## English

Use this Skill only when the **actual upholstery tag permits water-based spot care** (for example this maker's W or W-S/WS), an ordinary known small mark remains superficial, and a compatible product passed a hidden test without dye or texture change. If a spill is still spreading, contain it first. Leave ordinary spot care for wet padding, powered components, mold, bodily fluid, hazardous chemical, or unknown contamination.

### Preparation and inputs

Recheck the furniture maker, code, and product label for water-based foam or cleaner, amount, residue removal, ventilation, and drying. Have a clean white cloth or paper, a small amount of compatible product, and a place to dry. Move objects that cleaner could touch. Confirm ventilation, stable reach, and no powered mechanism under a cushion that could get wet. Stop when the tag or hidden-test result is unknown rather than skip it for a visible stain.

### Execution

1. **Lift loose matter first.** Blot a known safe surface liquid with a clean white cloth, working inward and switching to clean sections. Gently lift semisolid surface matter with a fibre-safe tool rather than rubbing it into padding. Do not pour across a whole cushion to “even out” the mark.
2. **Use one approved product on a small visible patch.** Apply maker-compatible water-based foam or cleaner by its label. Press or wipe gently and keep moisture limited so filling is not saturated. Do not stack solvent, forceful steam, or several cleaners to speed it up.
3. **Handle product residue as directed.** If a water wipe is required, use a barely damp clean cloth to press it away; do not rinse a fixed cushion like a dish. If foam or a white mark persists, stop and check the product rather than add a second cleaner.
4. **Let surface and checkable layers dry.** Ventilate by material directions and keep people off the seat. Blot remaining surface water with clean cloth where allowed. Do not use unapproved direct hot air or close a cover over a damp cushion. Stop for a dry-looking face with damp inner padding, a ring, or persistent changed odour; ask maker or professional help rather than claim deep hazard removal.
5. **Finish after real drying.** In the same light, inspect colour, texture, and mark. Restore use only for an acceptable result with no new damage. Stop repeats and keep product and photos for maker or professional advice if the mark remains, dye fades, fibres fuzz, a ring develops, or fabric stiffens. Spot improvement is not whole-furniture cleaning.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

One confirmed water-compatible superficial spot received bounded treatment under both labels and hidden-test evidence. Checkable areas are dry with no new dye loss or ring. No improvement or padding moisture leads to a clear hold, which is complete.

- **The white cloth takes up original furniture dye:** Stop rubbing immediately; keep product and test records for the maker.
- **The centre fades but a wet halo appears:** Do not soak a larger area to blend it. Dry and reassess.
- **Someone sits before it dries:** Hold the seat until care directions are met so moisture and residue are not pressed in.
- **Product says solvent while the furniture says W:** Do not use it; get a compatible water product or stop.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers one known ordinary superficial spot after confirmed permission. It does not perform S/X solvent cleaning, deep extraction, pest or germ eradication, or powered-furniture repair. Dye, filling, and products vary, so a visible mark may remain. Use comparable-light photos, readable labels, or trusted help if vision is limited. Press with a small stable cloth rather than hard scrubbing for limited grip. The seat is unavailable while truly drying.
