## 中文

你已经答应一次普通、自愿的约见或活动，**尚未开始**，现在确定自己不能参加或原安排必须停止时，用这份 Skill。结果是相关人及时知道原约定不再成立，实际预订和物品交接有明确负责人及状态。它不把“取消”伪装成“我先不回消息”，也不自动创建新日期；若你确实想提出改期，走 H185 的提议与对方选择。

### 准备与输入

对照双方确认过的版本，写下这次活动的日期、当地时间、地点/线上入口、参与者，以及谁订了位、买了票、准备了材料或要来接你。分开“已经发生的实际成本/交接”和“我以为有人会做”。先确认你是只取消**自己的参加**，还是有权代表组织者取消**整场活动**；不能代别人撤销他们仍想进行的计划。若还只是未答复的邀请，按拒绝邀约处理。

### 执行

1. **先通知会因此行动的人。** 一旦确定取消，尽早通过他们能收到的渠道直说是哪一次计划、你的变化及其范围：“我不能参加周五六点的晚餐，请不要按我会到场安排。” 若你是组织者且整场取消，明确写“这次活动取消”，不要让成员猜是你个人缺席还是全体散会。
2. **承认真实影响，不编造解释。** 可给一句你愿意说的理由或简单道歉；不必公开私人细节，也不以假理由规避后续安排。若对方正在路上或需要提前准备，优先用通常能及时到达的方式联系；只在一个没人看的群里留句“算了”不算可靠通知。
3. **逐项核对已发生的安排。** 问清餐位、门票、交通接送、借来物品或已经交付的材料由谁实际持有、谁能操作取消或取回，以及具体期限。按该服务本次规则办，不猜退款、转让或费用责任；需要对方同意的操作先征求同意。把每项标“未处理 / 已请求 / 对方确认 / 已实际完成”。
4. **读回并关闭旧计划。** 对关键联系人确认“你已收到我不参加/活动取消的消息吗？” 尤其当他们可能已经出发时。未收到时换双方常用的及时渠道，不把平台的发送状态当本人已知。更新你自己的提醒或日历，若由你创建共享邀请，按实际工具的规则通知参与者变更；光删自己手机里的提醒不会通知别人。
5. **把未来提议留为新决定。** 如果希望改天再见，只说你愿意另提时间，并让对方选择；没有双方同意，就让当前记录保持“原计划取消，尚无新约”。若没有新意愿，不加空泛的“下次一定”。

### 成功条件

你能指出原约定的状态是“我退出但其他人仍可进行 / 整场由有权者取消”，受影响的人已收到准确范围的通知或留下尚未联系到谁的明确待办；每个真实预订、费用疑点或物品交接有负责人、当前状态和下一行动。**消息发送、退款申请、收到确认与实际退款**不是同一件事；本文只记录已发生的事实，不许诺服务结果。

### 常见报错与补救

- **你只取消自己，群里以为活动全取消：** 立即澄清“我不参加，其他人是否继续由组织者决定”，不要替全员作决定。
- **只删了个人日历项：** 向实际参与者发清楚消息；若你管理共享邀请，再核对工具是否发了更新通知。
- **对方已在路上，文字迟迟未读：** 尝试双方常用的更及时渠道；仍无法联系时标未送达并继续合理尝试，不写“已通知”。
- **某人买了不能退的票：** 先核对具体规则、实际持有人和可选处理，记录需要共同决定的部分；不擅自保证退款或把未知费用推给对方。
- **“改天吧”被听成新约：** 澄清原计划已取消；新时间需双方另行确认。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份 Skill 只处理普通非强制活动在开始前的取消，不处理工作命令、紧急危机、已发生的多人活动事故或法律/财务争议。礼貌表达与回复习惯因关系、文化和渠道而变，但影响他人行动的状态应明确。不能通话时可用对方实际能及时读到的文字；听力/表达受限可用双方可访问的方式并要求简短读回。可能有人失望或要重排时间，这是取消的实际副作用，不等于你必须承诺另约。

## English

Use this Skill when you already agreed to an ordinary voluntary meeting or activity that **has not started**, and now know you cannot attend or the original arrangement must end. Finish with affected people told accurately and any real booking or item handover assigned a next action. Silence is not cancellation, and cancellation does not create a new date. If you want to propose a different time, use the rescheduling task and let others choose.

### Preparation and inputs

Read back the version everyone agreed: date, local time, place or online entry, participants, and who actually booked seats, bought tickets, prepared materials, or planned to collect you. Separate real costs and handovers from guesses about what someone may have done. Decide whether you are canceling **your own attendance** or are authorized as organizer to cancel **the whole activity**. Do not end other people's plan just because you leave it. If this was only an invitation you never accepted, decline it instead.

### Execution

1. **Notify people who would act on the old plan first.** Once the decision is real, use a channel they receive and identify the event, your change, and its scope: “I cannot come to Friday's six o'clock dinner; please don't plan for my attendance.” If you are the organizer ending the whole event, say “This event is canceled.” Do not make others infer whether only you are absent or everyone should stay home.
2. **Acknowledge impact without inventing a story.** Give a brief reason or apology if you wish; you need not disclose private detail or use a false reason to avoid logistics. If someone may already be traveling or preparing, use a channel likely to reach them promptly. “Never mind” in a quiet group chat is weak delivery.
3. **Reconcile arrangements already made.** Identify who holds a restaurant booking, ticket, transport pickup, borrowed item, or delivered material; who can actually cancel or retrieve it; and the real deadline. Follow this service's current rules rather than guessing refunds, transferability, or who owes money. Get agreement before doing something that requires another person's consent. Mark each item “untouched / requested / acknowledged / actually completed.”
4. **Read back and close the old plan.** For a critical contact, ask whether they received that you are absent or the event is canceled, especially if they may have left already. If not, try another timely channel both normally use. A sent status is not knowledge in their head. Update your own reminders or calendar; if you created a shared invitation, follow that tool's real method to notify participants. Deleting only your personal reminder does not tell them.
5. **Keep a future proposal separate.** If you want another meeting, say you can propose a new time and let others decide. Without mutual agreement, record “old plan canceled; no new plan.” If you do not want a new date, avoid an empty “definitely next time.”

### Success

You can tell whether the state is “I am out, others may continue” or “the authorized organizer canceled the whole event.” Affected people received that exact scope, or the unreachable person remains a named open action. Each real booking, cost question, or item handover has an owner, current state, and next action. **A sent message, a refund request, an acknowledgment, and an actual refund** are different facts. Record only what happened; do not promise a service outcome.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You cancel only yourself but the group thinks everyone is off:** Clarify that you are absent and the organizer decides whether the rest continue.
- **You delete only a personal calendar entry:** Tell actual participants. If you manage a shared invitation, check whether the tool sent a change notice.
- **Someone is already traveling and has not read the text:** Try another mutually used timely channel. Until reached, mark that contact pending rather than “notified.”
- **Someone bought a non-refundable ticket:** Check the real rules, holder, and available options. Record what needs a shared decision; do not guarantee money back or assign an unknown cost to someone else.
- **“Some other time” sounds like a new booking:** Clarify that the old event is canceled; any new time needs fresh agreement.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers cancellation of an ordinary optional activity before it begins, not an employment order, emergency, disruption of a group event already underway, or legal or financial dispute. Relationship, culture, and channel affect polite wording, but a state that changes others' actions should be explicit. If calls are inaccessible, use text they can actually read in time. For hearing or expression barriers, use a mutually accessible channel and a short readback. People may be disappointed or need to rearrange time; that does not oblige you to promise another outing.
