## 中文

当你的一包普通观赏种子已核适合此时室内播、干净排水小格与介质也已准备好时，加载这份 Skill。结果是这包种子按自身说明**实际入格、轻柔补水并可回查品种/日期**；发芽或后期照料属于下一阶段。包上有化学包衣、特殊冷藏/浸泡或身份不明的种子，不在本篇通用播法内。

### 准备与输入

再读一次本包的播种深度、每格粒数、需光/需暗、温湿要求和特殊前处理；不能用另一包的“种子宽度倍数”覆盖包装。准备已排水、已预湿的小格、清楚标签、适合手部/视力的取种工具和轻柔给水方式。不同包不混在无标识格里；台面稳定，避免儿童/宠物接触不明品种。

### 执行

1. **按本包决定摆放方式。** 有的需盖一层介质，有的需表面见光；照本包/同品种权威说明设深浅与粒数。说明缺失或互相矛盾时先查生产商，不靠“我觉得小种子就浅”猜。
2. **小批逐格放种。** 按计划取少量种子，逐格落到预备介质上，按获准方法覆土或不覆；轻压仅到说明要求，不把介质压实。种子太细难辨时用浅色纸或可信协助计格，不用随手倒整包造成拥挤。
3. **给水而不冲走布局。** 用种子说明允许的轻柔喷/淋或托盘吸水方式，让介质整体保持适用湿度；托盘积水按要求倒掉。只把表面雾湿却内部干燥，不能证明种子有可用水分。
4. **立刻标记身份与日期。** 每个品种的格位或盘有可读名称、播种日期和来源包；保存包的发芽条件。若罩盖或遮光是包装明确要求，按其可检查方式设置并留下一次查看点；不把密封罩当可永远不用开。
5. **作真实状态检查。** 盘稳定、孔可排水、种子位置与标签对应、介质未被冲散时写“已播，待发芽”。若深度或身份无法核对，记待确认并暂停下一批，而不是把空白格随意补满。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

这包种子按本包条件进入可排水格位，标签和后续查看点可找回；发芽**尚未发生**。如果没看到苗，不能倒推“播种没有完成”，但可能需要以后核实际条件。

- **说明说需光却厚覆土：** 先停，按本包说明及时轻调，不把其它种子的覆土规则套来。
- **水流把种子冲到角落：** 停水，用允许的轻柔方式复位并检查介质水分。
- **两包混格但没标记：** 暂停，不继续混种；能据位置可靠辨认才补标签，不能就记身份未知。
- **播下后介质泡在托盘水里：** 倒掉余水，核排水孔和介质；以后不靠更多水加速发芽。
- **手部/视觉难以点种：** 减格数，用可握小勺、对比色纸、放大或可信协助；身份和深度仍要核。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理一包普通非食用观赏种子的小批室内播种，包上特殊处理与化学包衣转制造商专门说明。种子活力、室内光与品种不同，任何播法都不保证出苗。后续小苗照料、间苗和出室外适应另有独立结果；本篇完成后盘上暂时只有土和标签是正常的。

## English

Load this Skill when one ordinary ornamental packet has been cleared for indoor sowing now and clean draining cells with medium are ready. Finish with seeds **actually placed under that packet's conditions, gently watered, and traceable by variety and date**. Germination and later care are different phases. Chemically coated, specially chilled or soaked, or unidentified seeds are outside this general route.

### Preparation and inputs

Reread this packet's sowing depth, seeds per cell, light or darkness need, moisture and temperature, and any special preparation. Another packet's “multiple of seed width” cannot override it. Have the prepared moist draining cells, clear labels, a seed tool suited to your hands and vision, and a gentle water method. Do not mix varieties in unmarked cells. Use a stable surface away from children or pets when the species is not known safe.

### Execution

1. **Choose placement by this packet.** Some seeds need a covering; others need light at the surface. Use packet or same-variety authoritative directions for depth and count. With missing or conflicting directions, check the maker rather than guessing that all small seeds go shallow.
2. **Place a small batch cell by cell.** Take a few seeds at a time into the prepared medium, cover or leave exposed as instructed, and press only as stated instead of compacting soil. For tiny hard-to-see seeds, use contrasting paper or trusted help to count cells, not a full-packet pour that creates crowding.
3. **Water without washing placement away.** Use a packet-permitted gentle spray, pour, or tray-wicking method that moistens the medium appropriately. Empty excess tray water as required. A misted surface over dry lower medium does not prove usable moisture.
4. **Label identity and date immediately.** Mark each variety's cells or tray with readable name, sowing date, and packet source, retaining germination directions. Use a cover or darkness only when the packet requests it and leave a next check. A sealed cover is not permission to ignore the tray forever.
5. **Check the actual state.** Record “sown, awaiting emergence” when the tray is stable, holes drain, seed locations match labels, and medium was not displaced. If depth or identity cannot be checked, mark it and pause further sowing rather than filling unknown gaps.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Seeds from this packet are in draining identified cells under its conditions, with a findable next check. **Germination has not yet happened.** No visible sprout today does not retroactively make sowing unperformed, though later conditions may need review.

- **A light-needing seed was buried heavily:** Pause and adjust gently under this packet, not another species' covering rule.
- **Water washed seeds into a corner:** Stop water, reposition through an allowed gentle method, and check medium moisture.
- **Two packets were mixed without labels:** Stop adding seeds. Label only what can still be identified reliably by position; otherwise mark identity unknown.
- **The tray holds standing water after sowing:** Empty it and check holes and medium. Extra water cannot speed germination by decree.
- **Fine motor or vision needs make individual placement hard:** Use fewer cells, an easy-grip small spoon, contrasting paper, magnification, or trusted help while preserving depth and identity checks.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a small indoor sowing of one ordinary non-food ornamental packet. Special treatments and coated seeds use maker-specific guidance. Seed viability, light, and species vary; no sowing route guarantees emergence. New-seedling care, thinning, and acclimation have later distinct outcomes. A tray containing only medium and labels just after sowing is normal.
