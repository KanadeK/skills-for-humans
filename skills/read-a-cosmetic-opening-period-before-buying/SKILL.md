## 中文

当你准备买普通洗护或化妆品，想知道标签上的日期或“开罐 12M”能否覆盖你的使用计划时加载。它只解释**买前的时间条件**，不判断皮肤问题、治疗效果、防晒药品、已变质产品能否继续用，或任何医疗用途。

### 准备与输入

找到准确商品的完整包装和容器标识、销售地区、预计何时**第一次打开**，以及你预计使用的频率。查固定日期、开罐图形旁的月数或年数、储存前提和特别警示。标签看不清时拍清晰照片放大、使用朗读或请人逐字读出图形旁的数字与单位；线上照片若是另一款或旧包装，不能代替当前实物。

### 执行

1. **先分清两种钟。** 固定“最好在某日之前使用”的日期与开罐图形旁的 `12M` 不是同一个起点；后者在适用标签体系中表示打开容器后的月份数，不是从购买日、出厂日或你看到商品那天开始。
2. **找当地解释。** 英国消费者指南列出 best-before 与 period-after-opening 两类标记；美国 FDA 说明普通化妆品不普遍要求打印到期日。不要把英国的标示期待套在美国包装上，也不要把无日期自动解释为无限期可用。
3. **核对购买计划。** 若有固定日期，确认你预计开封和使用的时间没有跨过它；若有开封后期限，估计在真正开封后能否在该时段内用完。你若打算长时间当备用品，开封后期限无法回答它在未开封货架上已经放了多久。
4. **处理缺失和异常。** 只见一串批号、不认识图形、包装曾被打开、标记被遮住、或线上与实物冲突时，记为“无法确认”。问制造商准确批次的说明或另选信息清楚、包装完好的商品；不靠试闻、试涂判定安全。
5. **留下有限结论。** 写下“固定日期 / 开封后期限 / 两者都有 / 未确认”，加上计划开封日及依据。时间条件符合只能让它继续候选，不能保证未来实际使用一定安全。

### 成功与停止

你能说出标签计时从哪一天开始、计划中哪一段受到限制、何处仍未知。标签显示期限已过、包装似乎被使用过，或你无法核实必须的时间条件时，不买这件。真正开封后可另记开封日期并遵守产品完整说明；本 Skill 在付款前结束。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把 `12M` 当成“买回家后 12 个月”：** 它指适用体系下的首次开封后期间；回到真实开封时机重算。
- **把批号当日期：** 查产品说明或制造商，不自行翻译生产代码。
- **打算囤多个备用：** 分别确认可能开封的时间和未开封日期信息；无法确认就减少或延后购买。
- **只有模糊商品图：** 索取准确版本的清晰标签；不能取得就保留未知。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里限于普通个人护理与化妆品，不包括在当地归药品的防晒、祛痘等产品。不同地区的标签要求、符号与储存条件会变；用销售地官方说明解释现售包装。视觉、语言或获取批次信息受限时可请人核对、联系制造商或改买更清楚的商品。你可能因此少囤一件，浴室柜会获得一点空白。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary toiletry or cosmetic when you want to know whether a printed date or an open-jar `12M` mark fits your expected use. It interprets a **pre-purchase time condition**. It does not assess a skin condition, treatment effect, sunscreen regulated as a drug, a degraded product's safety, or any medical use.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the exact item's complete box and container markings, the selling market, when you expect to **open it for the first time**, and roughly how often you expect to use it. Look for a fixed date, months or years beside an open-jar symbol, storage conditions, and special warnings. Enlarge a clear photo, use read-aloud help, or have someone read the number and unit next to the symbol. A different variant or old online pack image cannot replace this item.

### Execution

1. **Separate the two clocks.** A fixed “best used before” date and `12M` by an open-jar mark do not start together. In markets using that mark, the latter counts months after opening the container, not from purchase, production, or the day you saw it on the shelf.
2. **Check local meaning.** UK consumer guidance shows both best-before and period-after-opening marks. The US FDA says ordinary cosmetics generally need not print expiration dates. Do not impose a UK label expectation on a US package, and do not read a missing date as unlimited life.
3. **Compare with your plan.** For a fixed date, check whether planned opening and use would cross it. For a period after opening, ask whether you expect to finish within that period after the real first opening. If you intend to keep it as a backup for a long time, a post-opening period alone does not reveal how long it has already sat unopened.
4. **Keep gaps and anomalies unresolved.** A lot code with no explanation, unfamiliar symbol, apparently opened pack, obscured mark, or conflict between website and item means unconfirmed. Ask the maker for exact-batch information or choose a sealed, better-documented item. Smelling or trying it is not a safety test.
5. **Record a limited answer.** Mark “fixed date / opening period / both / unconfirmed,” then add your planned opening date and source. A fitting time window lets the item remain under consideration; it cannot guarantee safe future use.

### Success and stop

You can state when the clock begins, which part of your plan it constrains, and what remains unknown. Do not buy an item whose stated limit has passed, whose package seems used, or whose necessary time condition cannot be checked. After a real opening, you may separately record that date and follow the full product directions; this Skill ends before payment.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Reading `12M` as 12 months after purchase:** In the applicable scheme it starts at first opening. Recalculate from the expected opening time.
- **Reading a lot code as an expiry date:** Use product information or ask the maker; do not decode a production code by guesswork.
- **Buying several backups:** Check when each might be opened and what is known about unopened dating. Buy fewer or later when that remains unclear.
- **Only a blurred listing image exists:** Request the exact version's legible label; keep it unknown if unavailable.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary personal-care cosmetics, not sunscreen or acne products treated as drugs in the relevant market. Labelling rules, symbols, and storage premises differ by region; interpret the current pack through its selling market's official guidance. If vision, language, or batch information is a barrier, get read-aloud help, ask the maker, or choose a clearer item. You may buy one fewer backup, leaving some space in the bathroom cabinet.
