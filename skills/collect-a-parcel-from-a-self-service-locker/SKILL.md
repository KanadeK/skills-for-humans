## 中文

某包裹已被服务方标为可在**指定自助柜**领取，且你是有权领取的人时，用这份 Skill 完成现场开柜、核对和关门。目标是取到与你通知匹配的包裹并关好柜门；若柜内为空、包裹不符或门故障，保留现场事实并走服务方正式求助渠道。有人值守的柜台领取归 H133；错件/损坏后的交涉归 H136。本文不教找码、猜码或接管他人账号。

### 准备与输入

在服务方官方通知、应用或追踪页核对包裹状态、指定柜址、取件期限及该设备支持的扫码、取件码或官方 App 开门方式。不同服务有不同规则，不能把一个柜子的九位码、手机号输入方式或保留期移植到别处。确认自己可到达、能看见或识别柜门，包裹体积重量可安全拿取；无法够到柜格时先问服务方可及方案，不踩物品攀爬。保持验证码/二维码只在有权设备或按服务规则许可的代领人之间使用；不要贴到公开求助帖。

### 执行

1. **到对的柜子再操作。** 对照通知里的具体地点、柜体标识和包裹仍可领的状态。不要在旁边同品牌的另一排柜子反复试码。来源可疑的短信链接先从官方 App 或网站独立核对。
2. **按该服务给你的授权方式开柜。** 在指定设备上扫码、输入该件取件资料，或通过自己有权使用的官方 App 选择该包裹并开门。只操作系统指向的柜格；不拉扯锁住的邻格，不让陌生人替你输入或拍走凭证。界面/扫码故障时，按服务方正式备用方式或客服求助，不尝试绕锁。
3. **先看再取。** 留意实际打开的是哪扇门，确认柜内包裹标签上的收件标识/件数与你的通知一致。柜内为空、出现别人的包裹、外观明显损坏或无法安全取出时，不把错件拿走；记录柜址、时间、包裹编号和可见状态，联系服务方。别把自己的身份资料贴到柜体上求助。
4. **完成一次交接。** 包裹相符且能安全取出时拿走，确认没有遗漏你的其他件；按设备指示关好刚才打开的柜门并查看是否有完成提示。留存必要通知或取件记录。取件记录说明一次柜门交接，不证明内件无损；发现后续问题转 H136。

### 成功条件

你在指定柜址以获准方式取得匹配包裹、柜门已关，能找到该次取件记录；或你因空柜/错件/故障停止，并有服务方可追查的柜址、时间与编号。反复试码不能把错误柜址变成正确柜址。

### 常见报错与补救

- **二维码扫不出来：** 先核对柜址、取件状态及该设备是否支持此方式；按服务方官方备用输入/App 流程或客服处理，不用第三方“解锁”工具。
- **屏幕不响应或柜门不开：** 保留柜址与编号，按运营方客服/故障入口报修；不能自己撬门。
- **开门后空柜或别人的包裹：** 不拿错件，不把空柜当成功；记录现场事实并立即按官方渠道申报。
- **包裹或柜格够不到：** 停止强行伸手、踩凳或攀爬，查该服务可及柜格、现场协助或合法代领方案。
- **码已过期：** 用官方追踪查真实去向；不沿用旧通知去碰运气，超过期限可由服务方转移或退回。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本文只处理一个合法授权、已显示可领的普通包裹和可正常接近的自助柜。各地服务的码、App、代领和期限不同；英国 InPost 的说明只是可查的实例。没智能手机时仅使用该服务明确支持的短信/邮件码、柜机或客服方案；无可行方案时停止并求助。取件信息属于权限凭证，分享须符合服务方授权方式。

## English

Use this Skill when a parcel is officially ready at a **specified self-service locker** and you are authorized to collect it. Open the indicated compartment through the provider's method, compare the parcel with your notice, and close the door. For an empty compartment, wrong parcel, or malfunction, keep facts and use official support. A staffed counter belongs to H133; later wrong/damaged-item follow-up belongs to H136. This does not teach code guessing or account takeover.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the provider's official notice, app, or tracking for ready status, the exact locker site, deadline, and supported QR, collection-code, or official-app method. Services differ; one provider's code length, phone-number form, and hold period do not apply everywhere. Make sure you can reach and identify the compartment and safely carry the parcel. Ask for an accessible option if you cannot reach a door; do not climb. Show a QR/code only to the authorized device or a collector whom that service permits, not a public help post.

### Execution

1. **Arrive at the exact locker.** Compare address, locker marking, and still-ready status with your notice. Do not try the code repeatedly at a nearby bank of the same brand. Verify suspicious text links independently through the official app or site.
2. **Open through the authorized method.** Scan or enter this parcel's collection details at the designated device, or select it in an official app you may use. Touch only the indicated compartment. Do not pull locked neighbouring doors or ask a stranger to enter or photograph your credential. For an unresponsive screen or scan, use the provider's official fallback or support rather than bypassing the lock.
3. **Look before removing anything.** Note which door opened. Compare visible recipient marking and parcel count with your notice. For an empty compartment, another person's parcel, obvious exterior damage, or an item you cannot safely lift, leave the wrong item in place. Record locker site, time, parcel reference, and visible state, then contact the provider. Do not post your identity details on the locker.
4. **Finish this handover.** If the parcel matches and is safe to lift, take it and check whether another item of yours remains. Close the opened door under on-device instructions and check for a completion message. Keep a minimal collection record. A locker handover does not prove the contents are undamaged; use H136 for later issues.

### Success

You used an authorized method at the specified site, collected a matching parcel, closed the door, and retained the collection record. An empty/wrong compartment or failed door with site, time, and reference recorded for support is a valid stop. Repeated code attempts do not repair a wrong location.

### Common errors and recovery

- **QR will not scan:** Check location, ready status, and method support; follow the provider's own code/app fallback or contact support, not a third-party unlock tool.
- **Screen is frozen or door will not open:** Record the site and reference, use the operator's fault channel, and do not pry the door.
- **The compartment is empty or holds a stranger's parcel:** Leave the wrong item, record the facts, and report through the official channel. Do not count an empty opening as collection.
- **The parcel or compartment is unreachable:** Do not climb or strain into the cabinet. Ask about accessible compartments, staffed help, or a permitted collector.
- **Code has expired:** Check current tracking. An old notice does not prove the parcel remains at the same locker.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This concerns one ordinary authorized parcel at an accessible locker with a ready status. Codes, apps, collection rights, and deadlines vary by operator and region. UK InPost rules are examples. Without a smartphone, use only the provider's supported SMS/email code, screen, or help route. If none works, stop and seek assistance. Collection credentials confer access, so sharing follows the provider's permission rules.
