## 中文

当你准备为书、器皿或普通杂物买置物架，尺寸看起来够，却不知道它能否**按计划的每层重量**安全使用时，加载这份 Skill。目标是购前确认准确型号、完整配置和安装前提下的额定承重能覆盖用途；本 Skill 不教你打孔、选墙体锚栓或安装架子。

### 准备与输入

列出要放的物品及预计落在哪一层。用现有包装重量、家用秤或其他可靠记录估算；不方便称重的物品先标“重量未知”。找候选的完整型号、层板、支架/立柱数量、每层和整组额定承重、重量应如何分布，以及必须固定到墙面或地面的条件。不同长度、支架数或墙体类型可能对应不同额定值。

### 执行

1. **按层排物品。** 把预计物品放到具体层位，分别合计重量；不要只看全架总重量。预留将来新增物品的空间时，也要为重量留真实余地，而不是把空白格自动当成承重许可。
2. **读完整额定条件。** 查准确型号与所选支架、层板、连接件的说明，分清“每层”“整组”“均匀分布”及“正确安装后”的限定。商品页若只列一个数，不能猜它是任何配置下的保证值。
3. **把计划和上限逐层比较。** 每层计划重量、整组计划重量以及重物放置方式都要落在制造商允许的条件内。局部集中放一件重物与均匀排放可能不同；没有分布说明时不要自行提高额定值。
4. **核对固定前提。** 若商品要求上墙或防倾倒固定，确认你有权限并能安排符合说明书的安装；租住、墙体不明或需要专业评估时先标“安装前提未确认”。额定承重不能替代防倾倒措施。
5. **作出购前裁决。** 记录“准确配置 → 每层/整组额定值及条件 → 计划负载 → 安装前提 → 可考虑/不适合/未确认”。任一关键条件不明时暂停下单，换有明确资料的产品或向制造商询问。

### 成功与停止

完成信号是每层和整组都能按有据可查的额定条件覆盖计划负载，且必要固定有可行、合规的安排。这只是选购筛查，不保证实际安装质量。若产品将支撑人、攀爬、吊挂重物或涉及墙体/结构改造，停止本普通置物架流程，寻找适用产品和专业判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看全架承重：** 回到每层上限和实际排布；一层超限不会被其他空层抵消。
- **忽略支架数量或墙体条件：** 找准确组合的说明。条件不匹配时不能沿用展示图的较高数值。
- **物品重量不明：** 能安全称重就称重；否则查可信包装资料或减小计划负载，并保持未确认而非猜一个轻重量。
- **不能看清标签或不便搬动物品：** 放大制造商说明、请人读数或代称；不需要为了预估而搬起超出自己能力的重物。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只讨论普通家用置物架的购买决定，不替代安装说明、墙体承载判断或儿童防倾倒措施。各地重量单位先统一，品牌和销售地不自动代表相同配置。不能按要求固定的高架不因“东西很轻”就被视为安全；选择更低且符合说明的方案，或先取得合适的安装帮助。

## English

Load this Skill before buying shelving for books, dishes, or ordinary household items when the dimensions seem sufficient but you do not know whether **each planned shelf load** is supported. You finish with a pre-purchase check of the exact product, full support configuration, and its rating conditions. This Skill does not teach drilling, wall anchors, or installation.

### Preparation and inputs

List the intended objects and which shelf will hold each one. Use package weights, a household scale, or another reliable record; mark anything you cannot weigh as unknown. Find the exact product, board and bracket or upright configuration, per-shelf and whole-unit ratings, weight-distribution rules, and any required wall or floor fixing. Length, number of brackets, and wall type can change a rating.

### Execution

1. **Plan by shelf.** Allocate objects to specific levels and total their expected weights separately. Do not use only a whole-unit total. If you expect to add items later, leave real weight allowance rather than treating empty space as permission to exceed a rating.
2. **Read all rating conditions.** Find the instructions for the exact board, supports, and connectors you will buy. Distinguish “per shelf,” “whole unit,” “evenly distributed,” and “when correctly installed.” One number on a listing is not a guarantee for every configuration.
3. **Compare every level.** Check each shelf and the whole unit against the relevant documented limits, including how heavy objects are distributed. A concentrated load may differ from evenly spread items; an absent distribution rule is not a licence to raise the limit.
4. **Confirm fixing is feasible.** If the product requires wall anchoring or anti-tip fixing, confirm that you have permission and a way to arrange installation under its instructions. In a rental, with an unknown wall, or when professional assessment is needed, mark the installation condition unconfirmed. A load rating does not replace tip-over protection.
5. **Write the purchase decision.** Record “exact configuration → per-shelf/whole-unit ratings and conditions → planned loads → fixing condition → consider, unsuitable, or unconfirmed.” Pause when any crucial condition is missing, and choose a better-documented product or ask the maker.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when every shelf and the whole unit cover the intended loads under documented rating conditions and required fixing can be arranged. This is a shopping screen, not a guarantee of installation quality. Stop this ordinary shelving workflow if the product will support a person, climbing, suspended heavy loads, or structural changes; use a suitable product and professional assessment.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the unit total was checked:** Return to each shelf's limit and planned allocation. An overloaded shelf is not offset by empty ones.
- **Bracket count or wall condition was ignored:** Find the exact combination's instructions. Do not borrow a higher rating from a display with different supports.
- **Object weight is unknown:** Weigh it safely if possible, use a credible package record, or reduce the plan and keep the remaining question unconfirmed. Do not invent a light weight.
- **The label is inaccessible or the item is too heavy to lift:** Enlarge the maker's instructions or ask for a read-back or help weighing. You need not lift beyond your ability to estimate a purchase.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary household shelving at the buying stage. It does not replace installation instructions, wall-capacity assessment, or protection against children climbing and furniture tipping. Convert regional weight units before comparing; brand and sales region do not imply an identical configuration. A tall unit that cannot be fixed as required does not become safe because its contents are light; choose a lower documented option or arrange proper installation help.
