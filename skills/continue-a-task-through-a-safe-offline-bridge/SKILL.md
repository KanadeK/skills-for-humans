## 中文

当原本要用的设备、应用或网络暂时不可用，但一件**普通、低敏感**任务的核心动作仍可用纸面或已能用的工具完成时，加载这份 Skill。目标是留下可核的临时成果和转回路径，不在断联时假装云端已经保存。若任务必须在原设备上交付、无法安全替代，改用本域的交付降级/延期 Skill；不在这里维修设备。

### 准备与输入

写下原任务要交什么、真正必需的输入、截止或对方预期。确认替代材料是否**已经可用**：纸笔、本机已打开且可读的文件、另一个获授权设备，或你有权使用的普通工具。云盘图标显示文件名，不等于离线能打开；受管/私密资料不搬到个人账号、公共电脑或陌生 App。需要读屏、语音或其他可及方式时，选择你实际能用的替代载体。

### 执行

1. **判定能否降级。** 把任务分成“现在可做的核心动作”和“必须等原设备/网络才可做的提交、同步或专用操作”。若缺少可靠原始资料或正式格式是结果的一部分，只做可明确标临时的准备，不把草稿充成已交付。
2. **固定一个临时真源。** 在纸页或已获授权工具上写任务名、日期、输入来源、这一轮要产出的具体内容，以及“未同步/待转回”。不要同时在三个设备各改一份后靠记忆合并。若替代工具可能自动共享，先核保存和可见范围。
3. **做能安全做的部分。** 按原任务要求完成提纲、计算草稿、普通记录或整理；每个重要事实标来源及待核点。不能访问原文件时只写自己确认的内容，不从模糊记忆填数字、引用或他人决定。
4. **给转回设检查点。** 结束时记下临时成果在哪里、下一步由谁执行、需重核哪些地方。原工具恢复后，先打开真正的现有最新版，与临时稿比对再人工转入；不要直接覆盖同步回来的新稿。转入后打开核内容和实际保存/发送状态，才说完成交付。
5. **没有安全替代就停。** 若唯一可用载体会泄露受管材料、缺必需输入，或正式操作只能在原系统里完成，停止这条桥接路径并通知相关人设备限制和可能的新时间，不靠陌生软件绕过访问规则。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你有一份注明来源、未同步状态和转回步骤的临时成果；恢复后若已转入，还核对了最新版与保存/发送状态。纸上写完但正式渠道尚未收到，只能称“临时完成”，不能称“已提交”。

- **云文件列表可见却打不开：** 把它视为在线依赖，不根据文件名猜正文；等网络或用真实已有纸面资料。
- **另一个设备登录了私人账号：** 不把受管材料发过去；只做不涉及该资料的普通准备或暂停。
- **原设备恢复后出现新改动：** 逐项对照临时稿和现有稿，保留双方有效内容；不要“粘贴全部”覆盖。
- **重新联机时出现冲突提示：** 先保留双方副本，按应用提供的比较入口核差异；不要直接选“替换另一份”。
- **纸笔或语音形式不适合你的可及需求：** 换已获许可且实际可用的输入方式，或停下请求必要支持。
- **临时稿来源不明：** 标待核，不能以“设备坏了”作为省略核对的理由。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

离线可用性随应用、设备和预先配置而异；有的文件仅在线显示占位符。替代工具也可能不能完成最终格式或发送。纸面/本地稿增加一次人工转回成本，却保留任务进展的可核边界；它不是对原系统已同步的证明。

## English

Load this Skill when the intended device, app, or network is temporarily unavailable but the core of an **ordinary, low-sensitivity** task can still be done on paper or an already usable tool. Produce a checkable interim result with a transfer path. Do not pretend a cloud service saved work while disconnected. If the required deliverable cannot safely be made or delivered this way, use the separate delivery fallback Skill. This does not repair a device.

### Preparation and inputs

Name the required result, essential inputs, deadline, and any other person's expectation. Check that the alternative is **already usable**: paper, a readable file already open locally, another authorised device, or an ordinary tool you may use. A cloud filename icon does not prove the contents open offline. Do not move managed or private material into a personal account, public computer, or unfamiliar app. Choose a form you can actually access, including screen reading or speech if needed.

### Execution

1. **Decide what can be downgraded.** Separate the core action you can do now from submission, sync, or special steps that require the original system. If reliable source material or an official format is essential, do only preparation clearly labelled interim; do not pass a draft off as delivered.
2. **Fix one temporary source of truth.** On paper or an authorised tool, note task, date, source inputs, specific output, and “not synced/to transfer.” Do not edit three devices separately and hope memory merges them. If the alternative tool may share automatically, check its save and audience settings first.
3. **Do the safe part.** Complete an outline, calculation draft, ordinary record, or organisation step as the task needs. Mark sources and unknowns for important facts. Without the original file, use only what you can confirm rather than filling figures, quotations, or others' decisions from vague memory.
4. **Set a transfer checkpoint.** At the end, record where the interim result is, who transfers it, and what must be rechecked. When the original tool returns, open the real current version first and compare before transferring by hand; do not overwrite newly synced work. Reopen and verify the transferred content and actual saved or sent state before claiming delivery.
5. **Stop when no safe alternative exists.** If the only tool would expose managed material, essential inputs are missing, or the formal action can occur only in the original system, stop this bridge and tell relevant people about the device limit and possible new timing. Do not bypass access rules with unfamiliar software.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You have an interim result labelled with source, unsynced state, and transfer steps. After transfer, you also verified the current version and actual save or send state. Paper work before formal receipt is “interim complete,” not “submitted.”

- **A cloud file is listed but will not open:** Treat it as an online dependency; do not infer content from the filename. Wait or use genuine existing paper material.
- **Another device uses a personal account:** Do not send managed content there. Continue only independent ordinary preparation or pause.
- **New edits appear when the old device returns:** Compare them with the interim work and retain both valid parts rather than pasting over all new work.
- **A sync conflict appears on reconnection:** Preserve both copies and use the app's comparison path; do not choose “replace the other copy” blindly.
- **Paper or speech is inaccessible for you:** Use an authorised usable input method or pause for needed support.
- **Interim content has no source:** Mark it to verify. Device failure does not remove the need to check it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Offline availability depends on app, device, and prior setup; some files are online-only placeholders. An alternate tool may not support final format or sending. Paper or a local draft adds manual transfer work but keeps a checkable boundary. It is not proof that the original service synced.
