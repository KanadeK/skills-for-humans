## 中文

当你想用**有明确产品说明的普通风干黏土**捏一个自立的小空心装饰形时，加载这份 Skill。结果是开口、底部和周边形状稳定、可以进入晾干阶段的作品；它不是食品容器、水杯、烛台或耐热陶器。不要照抄博物馆文物纹样或产品示范动物造型。

### 准备与输入

读本品标签的年龄、厚度、用水、干燥和清理要求；不同品牌不能套一个固定数字。洗净双手，在不怕染色的可移垫板上取一小团黏土，余料先按包装盖好。远离食物、宠物和幼儿可误食处；手上有不宜接触的伤口或材料警示不适合自己时停，改由可信协助者操作。

### 执行

1. **先把一团黏土压实成球。** 双手缓慢滚圆，排掉明显开裂的大缝；若太硬，只按产品说明用极少量水润手或换新料，不能把整团泡软。
2. **用拇指开中间凹处。** 一手托住底，一手由中心轻压出浅洞，逐步转动作品并捏薄周边；保持底部与壁厚大致均匀，且符合本品最低厚度说明。不要一次把底戳穿。
3. **检查能否自立。** 在垫板上放下，从四周看底是否稳、壁是否塌、开口是否仍可见。局部太薄就停止扩张或用同一团泥轻回补，不做大型承重器皿。
4. **留给晾干步骤。** 把作品连垫板移到安全不沾家具的位置，标记“未干、只作装饰”；不急着上色或倒水测试。清理手上黏土，余料按产品储存指示处理。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

湿作品有完整底、可见凹腔、周壁不明显裂穿，放下可自立；之后还需专门晾干检查。一个被戳穿、拿起就坍塌的湿团，不算完成。

- **底部穿孔：** 在仍可塑时轻合并修复并重新查厚；无法稳固就重新捏小团。
- **边缘越捏越薄裂：** 停止拉开，回到较小更厚的形；不靠大量水糊住裂口。
- **黏土过湿变软：** 按产品说明暂放让多余水分退去，别继续捏高壁。
- **作品粘坏台面：** 用合适垫板承托，不从脆弱家具表面硬剥。
- **握持或视觉不便：** 用更大而浅的形、稳定垫板或可信现场协助；仍由你确认自立和壁厚。

### 假设与边界

风干黏土可能收缩、裂或染色；形状稳定不等于已干或防水。厂家安全指南排除食物、盛水、火源与烤箱使用；本篇把“容形”当纯装饰品。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to shape a small self-supporting **hollow decoration from ordinary labelled air-dry clay**. Finish with a stable opening, base, and wall ready for drying. It is not tableware, a water cup, a candle holder, or heat-safe pottery. Do not copy a museum object's motif or a product's sample animal design.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this product's age, thickness, water, drying, and cleanup directions; brands do not share one fixed number. Wash hands, protect a movable work board against stains, and take a small lump while storing the rest by its package. Work away from food, pets, and young children who may mouth material. Stop or seek trusted help if skin condition or label warnings make handling unsuitable.

### Execution

1. **Compact a small ball.** Roll gently until large splits close. If too stiff, use only a tiny amount of water as the product permits or choose fresh clay; do not soak the whole lump.
2. **Press a central hollow.** Support the base with one hand and push a shallow dent with a thumb. Rotate and pinch the wall outward gradually, keeping base and wall reasonably even and within the product's minimum thickness. Do not punch through the bottom in one motion.
3. **Check that it stands.** Set it on the board and look around for a steady base, wall that does not sag, and visible opening. Stop stretching a thin area or gently bring it back with the same clay. Do not make a large load-bearing vessel.
4. **Hand off to drying.** Move the piece on its board to a safe non-staining spot and mark it “wet, decoration only”. Do not paint or test it with water yet. Clean hands and handle unused clay under the product's storage rules.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The damp object has a sound base, visible hollow, no obvious wall-through cracks, and stands when set down. Full drying is a later task. A pierced or collapsing lump is not complete.

- **Bottom is pierced:** While plastic, gently close and recheck thickness; restart smaller if it will not hold.
- **Rim thins and cracks:** Stop pulling, return to a smaller thicker form, and avoid hiding the split with a lot of water.
- **Clay becomes too wet and soft:** Let extra moisture leave under product directions instead of building higher walls.
- **Piece sticks to a valuable surface:** Carry it on a suitable board; do not pry it from fragile furniture.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Make a larger, shallower form on a stable board or use trusted in-person help while you verify standing and thickness.

### Assumptions and limits

Air-dry clay may shrink, crack, and stain; a stable wet form is neither dry nor waterproof. Manufacturer safety guidance excludes food, water, flame, and oven use. “Hollow form” means decoration only here.
