## 中文

当你负责一个**已稳定、鱼种与正常状态已知的淡水缸**，要做普通当天的不侵入观察时，加载这份 Skill。结果是鱼、设备、水位/水温按现有方案有真实记录，或异常被及时转给合适的水族专业人员；它不是诊断、治疗或仅凭清澈水判定水质安全。新缸、陌生鱼或已明显危急的缸不按“普通巡视”拖延处理。

### 准备与输入

拿该缸的鱼种照料卡、平常活动/投喂/水温与设备流量记录，查看上次水质测试日期。站在稳定位置，不敲玻璃、不把手伸进缸；孩子或宠物不能拉倒电线。设备旁有漏水、火花、异常热或损坏电线时远离、按设备安全指引停用并找合格人员，不能边观察边拆修。

### 执行

1. **看鱼与环境而不惊扰。** 在原本日常活动时段看鱼数、游动位置、呼吸/鳃动、是否躲藏、外观和彼此互动，与本缸已知基线比较。不为“看得更清楚”反复开灯、追鱼或敲缸。
2. **看水与设备的可见状态。** 查看水位是否突然变化、过滤水流或气泡是否与平时一致，温度显示是否在已知鱼种方案范围；漏水、堵塞、断电迹象标明。透明水也可能有看不见的氨/亚硝酸盐，水质要由测试篇另证。
3. **写下事实而不下诊断。** 记日期、时间、鱼的具体变化、设备状态与最近一次读数；无法看到某条鱼就记“未观察到”，不称其必然健康/失踪。异常行为或外观先测水并联系熟悉该鱼种的水族兽医/专业零售者，不自行加药或调 pH。
4. **留下下一次入口。** 无异常时按原方案继续喂食和定期测试；发现问题时把记录交合适人员，按其指导行动。家人/照料者能从记录分辨“正常巡视”和“已求助”。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一条基于该缸正常基线的鱼、设备、水温/水位观察记录，异常有明确转介；若基线缺失，写“不足以判断正常”并先补来源。看水很清就宣称全缸健康，不算完成。

- **鱼活动比平时异常或外观改变：** 记录并测水，联系水族专业人员，不猜病因用药。
- **过滤水流变弱/停了：** 记实际状态并按设备和水质计划求助，不拆带电设备。
- **温度显示缺失或读数异常：** 不凭手感代替，核设备/鱼种方案并求助。
- **水漏到电源附近：** 停止接近，按安全指引交合格人员处理。
- **视觉或行动受限：** 用可访问显示、可信协助观察或照片比对，仍记录看到/没看到的界线。

### 假设与边界

日常观察不替代例行试剂检测，也不能确诊疾病。OATA 指出水中污染可肉眼不可见；鱼种与个人缸正常行为差异大，现有照料方案优先。危急状况由水族专业者处理，不以本文拖延。

## English

Load this Skill when you care for an **established freshwater tank with known species and normal baseline** and need an ordinary noninvasive check today. Finish with true observations of fish, equipment, level, and temperature under the existing plan, or route an abnormality promptly to an appropriate aquatics professional. This is not diagnosis or a claim that clear water is safe. A new, unknown, or clearly distressed tank needs another route, not a routine check delay.

### Preparation and inputs

Have species care notes, usual activity/feeding/temperature and equipment-flow baseline, and last water-test date. Stand steadily without tapping glass or putting hands in water. Keep children and pets from pulling cables. If water leaks near power, wires spark, equipment overheats, or a cord is damaged, stay back and follow device safety instructions with qualified help rather than repairing while observing.

### Execution

1. **Watch fish and surroundings without startling them.** At a normal activity time, note fish count, swimming position, gill/breathing movement, hiding, appearance, and interactions compared with this tank's baseline. Do not repeatedly switch lights, chase fish, or knock glass for a better look.
2. **Inspect visible water and equipment state.** Look for unexpected level changes, filter flow or bubbles unlike usual, a temperature display compared with the species plan, and leaks, blockage, or power loss. Transparent water may still hold invisible ammonia or nitrite; the testing task supplies that evidence.
3. **Write facts, not a diagnosis.** Date the observation and describe a change, device state, and most recent test reading. Write “not seen” for a fish you could not view, not “healthy” or “missing” as certainty. For abnormal behaviour or appearance, test water and contact an aquatics veterinarian or experienced species-aware retailer, without self-medicating or changing pH by guess.
4. **Leave a next entry.** With no anomaly, follow the existing feeding and scheduled test plan. With a concern, pass the record to the appropriate person and act under their guidance. A carer can distinguish routine observation from help already sought.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A dated fish, equipment, level/temperature observation is compared with this tank's baseline and abnormal findings have a real route. Without a baseline, record “cannot judge normal” and gather it. Calling clear water proof of healthy fish is unfinished.

- **Fish acts or looks unlike usual:** Record, test water, and contact an aquatics professional instead of guessing medicine.
- **Filter flow weakens or stops:** Record the state and use equipment/water-plan help, never dismantle live electrical gear.
- **Temperature display is missing or abnormal:** Do not replace it with touch alone; check device and species plan with qualified help.
- **Leak reaches power:** Stop approaching and use qualified safety assistance under device instructions.
- **Sight or movement is limited:** Accessible displays, trusted observations, or photo comparison can help while you mark what was and was not seen.

### Assumptions and limits

Daily observation does not replace kit testing or diagnose disease. OATA notes water pollution can be invisible. Species and tank baselines differ, and the established care plan comes first. A serious problem belongs to aquatics professionals, not a delayed routine.
