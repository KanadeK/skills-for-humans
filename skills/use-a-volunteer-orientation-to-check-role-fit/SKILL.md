## 中文

当你对博物馆、图书馆或社区机构的**普通低风险志愿岗位**感兴趣，收到一场说明会信息时，用这份 Skill参加并核对角色、培训、支持、时间和退出方式，再决定是否继续申请。结果不是说明会一结束就替主办方服务，更不是接受你并不懂的专业作业；“志愿”两个字不会自动把责任变轻。

### 准备与输入

从主办方正式页面核说明会日期、地点或线上入口、是否需要报名、岗位名称与已公开的职责。写下你能提供的时间、行动/语言/感官条件和不愿承担的任务。把问题缩成五项：具体做什么、谁负责指导、要先学什么、每次/总共多久、无法继续时找谁。涉及医疗护理、儿童独立监管、公共安全、危险工具、车辆或受保护数据的岗位不属于本篇 Everyday 操作范围；只能向主办方核资格与正式培训，不现场照做。

### 执行

1. **确认是正确的说明会。** 用官方邀请入场，核岗位与组织名称；线上时只进正式会议入口，不向陌生链接提交证件、密码或个人资料。取消或进不了场就如实记“未参加”，联系主办方下一安排。
2. **听清岗位而非口号。** 记真实服务地点、对象、普通职责和明确禁止/需转给工作人员的事情；“帮助大家”不足以说明你具体要做什么。和最初岗位描述不一致时标出差别。
3. **核支持与前置条件。** 问负责你的联系人、培训/影随要求、需要谁在场、必要材料由谁提供、可及支持和遇到不懂或不适时的报告渠道。若主办方要求背景核验、证明或协议，只记官方要求与安全提交渠道，不在公共笔记中抄证件号码，也不跳过正式审核。
4. **核时间与退出。** 问班次、最短承诺、通知缺席/改期/退出的办法，以及何时才能正式上岗。若说明会临时请你做未培训的具体服务，先说明“我还没完成岗位培训”，请负责人给出合适安排，不靠热心顶替专业流程。
5. **做一张决定卡。** 写“我理解的职责____；指导人/培训____；时间____；退出联系____；我决定接受后续申请/暂缓问清/拒绝”。未答的关键问题留“待主办方确认”，不把主持人的笑容当正式录用。若愿继续，按正式申请流程走；若不愿，按主办方方式礼貌通知即可。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你实际参加了说明会，能说出岗位职责、指导/培训、时间承诺与退出通道，并给出**接受后续申请、暂缓或拒绝**的理由。若四项核心条件中有影响决定的未知，结束状态是“待澄清”，不是已同意上岗。

- **职位描述和现场讲法冲突：** 指出具体两处差别，请正式联系人确认，以更新后的书面或明确答复决定。
- **对方只说“有人会教你”：** 问谁、何时、上岗前还是上岗后、哪个任务不能先做；无明确支持则暂缓。
- **时间或可及条件不合：** 问是否有其他合适岗位/班次，若无则拒绝，不靠承诺未来能硬挤出时间。
- **现场催签大量私人资料：** 核表单用途和官方提交渠道；不明白先带回询问，不把本篇当法律审阅或身份核验指南。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

组织对志愿者的资格、培训、保险、保密和退出规定因地区和岗位而变；以正式岗位说明与适用规则为准。可用纸笔、大字、字幕、翻译或可信协助参会，但不要在开放群聊发个人证件。志愿者可愿意提供时间，也有权知道自己具体会做什么；说明会上的决定不代表你已获准开展服务。本篇止于了解与选择，不教授任何专业志愿作业。

## English

Use this Skill when an **ordinary low-risk volunteer role** at a museum, library, or community group interests you and you are invited to an orientation. Attend, check duties, training, support, time, and how to leave before deciding whether to apply further. The outcome is not doing the organisation's work as soon as the meeting ends. “Volunteer” does not make an unclear responsibility automatically light.

### Preparation and inputs

Check date, location or official online link, registration, role name, and published duties on the organiser's current page. Write your available time, mobility, language, or sensory conditions and tasks you will not accept. Bring five questions: what work, who supervises, what training first, how much time, and whom to contact if you cannot continue. Roles in medical care, unsupervised child care, public safety, hazardous equipment, driving, or protected data are outside this Everyday action guide. Ask the organiser about formal eligibility and training, but do not perform such work from this Skill.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the real orientation.** Enter through the official invitation and match role and organisation. Online, use the formal meeting link; do not submit identity documents or passwords to an unfamiliar link. If cancelled or inaccessible, record “did not attend” and contact the organiser about another route.
2. **Listen for work, not slogans.** Note actual service place, people served, ordinary tasks, and what must be referred to staff. “Help everyone” is not a job description. Mark differences from the original listing.
3. **Check support and prerequisites.** Ask who is responsible for you, what training or shadowing is required, who must be present, who supplies materials, what access support exists, and how to report an unclear or uncomfortable task. If official screening, proof, or agreements are required, note the requirement and secure submission channel rather than copying identification numbers into public notes or bypassing checks.
4. **Check time and exit.** Ask about shifts, minimum commitment, absence or schedule changes, withdrawal, and when work can actually begin. If asked to do an untrained service on the spot, say you have not completed role training and ask the supervisor for an appropriate alternative. Good intentions do not replace a professional process.
5. **Make a decision card.** Write “duties I understood ____; supervisor/training ____; time ____; withdrawal contact ____; I choose to apply further / hold for answers / decline.” Leave a decision-changing unknown as “awaiting organiser confirmation.” A host's smile is not formal acceptance. Follow the real application if you continue; notify politely through their route if declining.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You actually attended and can name duties, supervision and training, time commitment, and exit route, with a reason for **applying further, waiting, or declining**. If a key condition is still unknown, the status is “needs clarification,” not accepted work.

- **Listing and meeting disagree:** Identify the exact differences and ask the official contact to confirm before deciding.
- **Someone says only “we will teach you”:** Ask who, when, before or after beginning, and which tasks are not allowed first. Hold if support remains vague.
- **Time or access does not fit:** Ask about another suitable role or shift; decline if none rather than promise hours you cannot supply.
- **You are pressed to sign forms with private data immediately:** Check purpose and official channel. Take time to ask rather than treat this Skill as legal review or identity verification.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Volunteer eligibility, training, insurance, confidentiality, and exit rules vary by region and role. Use the formal role description and applicable organiser rules. Paper, large text, captions, interpretation, or trusted assistance can support attendance without posting identity documents to open chats. You may choose to give time and still need to know the work. An orientation decision is not permission to begin service. This Skill stops at understanding and choice, never teaches a specialised volunteer task.
