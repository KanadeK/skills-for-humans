## 中文

当你有一台**确实支持多层的普通蒸具**，想让两种已适用的普通食物在不同蒸制时长后接近同一时间完成时，加载这份 Skill。结果是**按该机型层位差异和每种食物的完成条件安排下料、取出**，而不是把“下层快”“上层慢”当作所有蒸锅的通用定律。压力蒸锅、婴幼儿食品，以及会让生动物性食物液滴接触即食食物的不明组合不在本流程内。

### 准备与输入

找到确切型号说明书，确认可用层数、层的编号/方向、允许的食物重量、蒸汽通道和是否能在运行中安全加取层。分别读取两种食物的可靠配方、切块大小、预估时间、需要的安全完成检查。若说明书不解释层位加热差异，就不要照搬别家蒸锅的“下层更快”；改为分批或使用单层。准备能分别计时的纸笔/计时器和两个热容器。

### 执行步骤

1. **先把相容性过关。** 两种食物的气味、汁液、过敏限制和卫生要求必须允许同机同时处理。原料生熟交叉污染路径不清、温度要求无法分别核验时，分开蒸；一台蒸锅不能把两个不同安全终点自动合并。
2. **按本机型安排层位。** 设备若明确说明哪层更热、哪层应放较久的食物，照它安排；若没有，先用单层/分批。各层仍按[蒸汽通路摆放](../arrange-food-for-even-steaming/SKILL.md)，不堵孔、不压盖。
3. **最长任务先开始。** 依据各自配方和该机型允许的途中加层动作，先开始需更长时间的那份；短时食物在预定节点加入。每次开盖/加层都要先降低热源或按说明操作，让蒸汽避开脸和手，确认重新盖稳后为新加入的一层单独计时。
4. **不要让一只总计时器替两种食物结案。** 记录每层实际入蒸汽的时刻、盖子是否重开、各自需要的终点。到其中一层的时间时按它的配方和适用[熟度检查](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)判断；未达条件就继续该层，不让另一层因等待而默默过熟。
5. **逐层收尾。** 在器具说明允许且能安全操作时取出已完成的一层，放在近处耐热位置；另一层继续前复核水量、盖与蒸汽。若中途拆层会不稳或你无法安全拿取，就改为分批而非硬追求同时上桌。

### 完成与停止

你有一张明确的“层位—食物—何时放入—如何单独检查—何时安全取出”记录，两种食物均由各自条件完成，才算完成。机型说明缺失、层位不可确定、相容性不明、不能安全中途操作或水位不足时，不开多层任务；改成单层/分批不会让晚餐降级。

### 常见报错与补救

- **照别的型号把长时食物放下层：** 回到手上机型的层位说明；查不到就不要假设层位速度。
- **短时食物从第一分钟就一起入锅：** 先按各自可靠时间重排，已过热的食物不能靠调换层位逆转。
- **新增一层后不重记时间：** 为新层写实际开始事件；总蒸锅计时器不自动知道你中途加了什么。
- **某层完成、另一层仍生：** 分别按可靠终点处理；需要安全温度的食物不能用邻层热气作证。
- **揭盖或提层不稳：** 关热、等蒸汽减弱并按说明收尾，下一次改分批，不举着烫手层具协调时间。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 只在设备明确支持多层和安全中途操作时使用；不同品牌的水箱、计时、保温及层位热差不一样。配方时间是相应食物/尺寸的起点，实际完成仍要检查。触觉、视力或握力不便时，可用大字/语音计时、冷态贴层位标记、按序摆盘，或让现场可信的人协助提层；无法安全提层就分批。副作用是你需要两只计时器或几行纸，却不必让一个总倒计时替所有食物签字。

## English

Load this Skill when your ordinary steamer **explicitly supports multiple tiers** and you want two suitable foods with different steaming times to finish near each other. Build an **add, check, and remove schedule** using this exact model's tier behaviour and each food's endpoint. “Lower is faster” is not a law for every appliance. Pressure steamers, infant food, and any unclear raw-animal-food drip path onto ready-to-eat food are outside this procedure.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the exact model's manual. Check supported tier count, tier numbering and orientation, load, steam openings, and whether adding or removing a tier during operation is allowed and safe. Read reliable directions for both foods, including cut size, expected time, and any safe-completion check. If the manual does not establish tier differences, do not copy another maker's lower-tier rule; use one tier or separate batches. Prepare two separate clocks or notes and two heat-safe dishes.

### Execution steps

1. **Screen compatibility first.** The foods' odours, drips, exclusions, and hygiene needs must permit shared steaming. If raw-to-ready cross-contamination or separate safe temperatures cannot be controlled, steam separately. One appliance does not merge two food-safety endpoints.
2. **Place tiers under this model's rules.** If its manual states which tier receives stronger steam or longer-cooking food, follow it. If it does not, use a single tier or batches instead of guessing. Keep every tier [open to steam](../arrange-food-for-even-steaming/SKILL.md), with holes and lid unblocked.
3. **Begin the longer job first.** Use food-specific directions and only the mid-cook tier actions your model permits. Add the shorter food at its planned point. Before opening or adding a tier, lower heat or act as directed, keep steam away from face and hands, reseat the lid, and start an independent clock for the new tier.
4. **Do not let one master timer finish both foods.** Record the real steam-entry event for each tier, any lid opening, and each endpoint. At one tier's stated time, check that food against its recipe and applicable [doneness evidence](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md). Continue that tier if needed without silently overcooking the other while it waits.
5. **Unload separately.** Remove a completed tier only when the equipment allows it and you can place it on a nearby heat-safe surface. Before the other tier continues, recheck water, lid, and steam. If mid-cook tier handling is unstable or beyond your capacity, use batches rather than forcing simultaneous service.

### Success and stop

You have a clear “tier, food, addition time, separate completion check, safe removal time” record, and each food reaches its own endpoint. Do not start a multi-tier run without the model's instructions, known tier placement, compatible foods, a safe way to handle tiers, and enough water. One tier at a time is a valid result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You put longer food below because another model said so:** Return to your actual manual. Unknown tier behaviour is not a speed rule.
- **The short food was added at the beginning:** Rebuild the schedule from each reliable time. Switching tiers cannot reverse food already overcooked.
- **You add a tier without recording its own start:** Write the real event for that tier. The appliance timer does not automatically know what you added.
- **One tier is done while another is undercooked:** Use separate endpoints. Steam near a food is not its safe core-temperature evidence.
- **Lifting a lid or tier feels unstable:** Turn off heat, wait for steam to subside, and finish as the manual allows. Use separate batches next time rather than coordinating while holding a hot tier.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Use this only when the device supports tiers and safe mid-cook handling. Brands differ in water tanks, timing, warming modes, and tier heat. Recipe time belongs to the actual food and size; check completion rather than assuming the clock guarantees it. For visual or grip limits, use large or speaking timers, label tiers while cold, lay out dishes in order, or arrange safe in-person help. If a tier cannot be lifted safely, divide the work. You may need two clocks or a few lines of notes instead of asking one countdown to sign off on every food.
