## 中文

当普通搬迁货物或关键箱件已经确认晚于原计划到达，而你在它们来前仍要生活时加载。你要为**实际缺口期间的基本活动找到能执行的临时方式和下一次复看点**。这里不索赔、解释承运合同、诊疗或用危险办法替代不可用设施。

### 准备与输入

把“预计到达”和承运方/接收者最新确认的时间分开记录；不知道就是未知。核对受影响者在下一次可靠消息前实际要睡、洗漱、饮水、进食、沟通或使用可及用品的需求，以及现有随行物和已能使用的设施。不要把商家建议的“首夜箱”当成它一定在手边。

### 执行

1. **先定真实时间窗。** 向实际承运或接收方问清目前能确认的状态、下一次更新由谁在什么时候给；不给确定时刻就写“待更新”，不要替它承诺今天晚些时候一定到。
2. **按活动排缺口，不按箱名排焦虑。** 为这段时间每位参与者列“需要做的活动 → 已有可用物 → 缺什么 → 最晚什么时候需要”。先处理今天确实会影响基本使用的项目；装饰和长期收纳可等。
3. **选已核实的临时路径。** 用真正到手的随行用品、可安全使用的现成设施、经同意的借用或确实可入住的其他地点。住宿、用品或交通有费用、权限和可及性条件时先核对；不凭一张网页假设能获得它。
4. **明确谁去做以及何时复看。** 每项临时方案写负责人、地点/物品、能覆盖到的时间和下一次核对点。若有人因身体、感官或家庭条件无法用所选替代，重选而不是把“有地方住”当成人人可用。
5. **向相关人同步状态。** 告知同住者/收货人哪些活动已能进行、哪些仍缺、承运方何时再答复。货物真的到达时重新核对，撤掉已无用的临时安排；延误延长就重算时间窗。

### 完成与停止

在下一次可信更新前，每项近期基本活动都有已确认可用的做法，或明确缺口、负责人和升级对象；未到货仍写未到货。没有安全可行的睡眠、洗漱或其他必要路径时，不靠危险替代硬撑，联系合适服务、房屋负责人或当地支持渠道；这份 Skill 不给医疗或紧急处理方案。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“说不定今晚到”导致没人准备：** 把承诺与猜测分开，为没有明确到货时间的时间窗找实际替代。
- **随行包也在晚到货车上：** 以眼前真正有的东西重新列缺口，别把清单里的物品算作可用。
- **临时住宿或借用还没得到同意：** 保持待确认，联系实际提供者；不要把发出请求当成预订成功。
- **方案对某位成员用不了：** 问其需要的可及条件，换可行地点、协助或用品，不做统一假设。
- **货物又推迟：** 按新消息重估活动、时间和负责人，及时告诉受影响者。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通搬迁的短期生活降级，不负责财务赔偿、租赁解释或医疗护理。地区、费用、同行者、辅助用品和可用住处不同；有预算压力时优先核对已有物品和经同意的免费/低成本选项，不许诺它们一定存在。阅读或沟通不方便时请可信的人读回真实时间与安排。副作用是今天终于有一份能执行的计划，而非一遍遍刷新未到的货车。

## English

Load this Skill when ordinary moving goods or a key group are confirmed later than planned and you still need to live before they arrive. You will find **workable temporary ways to cover real basic activities during the gap, with a next review point**. This does not make a claim, interpret a carrier contract, give medical care or improvise unsafe substitutes for a failed facility.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the difference between an expected arrival and the latest time actually confirmed by the carrier or receiver. Unknown is unknown. Ask what each affected person needs to do before the next reliable update: sleep, wash, drink, eat, communicate or use access equipment. Check what is genuinely carried or already usable. A provider's example “first-night box” does not prove that yours is at hand.

### Execution

1. **Set the real time window.** Ask the actual carrier or receiver what status is confirmed and who will give the next update when. If there is no firm time, write “awaiting update” rather than promising arrival later today on its behalf.
2. **List activity gaps, not anxious carton names.** For each person's near-term routine, record “activity → available item → missing part → latest time needed.” Handle what affects basic use today first; decoration and permanent storage can wait.
3. **Choose verified temporary paths.** Use supplies truly in hand, facilities that are safe and already usable, a loan with consent or another place you can actually stay. Check price, permission and accessibility for lodging, supplies or travel before counting them. A web page does not make an option available to you.
4. **Name an owner and review point.** Record who arranges each option, where it is, what time span it covers and when you check again. If a substitute does not work for someone's mobility, sensory or household needs, choose another rather than treating one available place as usable for everybody.
5. **Share the current state.** Tell housemates or receivers what routines now work, what remains missing and when the carrier will update you. Recheck when goods actually arrive and retire temporary plans that are no longer needed. Recalculate if delay extends.

### Success and stop

Until the next credible update, each near-term basic activity has a confirmed usable route or an explicit gap, owner and appropriate escalation contact; undelivered goods remain marked undelivered. If no safe way to sleep, wash or meet another necessary need exists, do not force an unsafe workaround. Contact an appropriate service, housing contact or local support; this Skill gives no medical or emergency procedure.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Maybe tonight” prevents any plan:** Separate a confirmation from a guess and cover the window without a firm delivery time.
- **The carry kit is also on the late truck:** Rebuild the gap list from what is actually in hand, not what the packing list says exists.
- **A temporary stay or loan has not been accepted:** Keep it pending and contact its real provider. A request is not a successful booking.
- **The plan is unusable for one person:** Ask about their access conditions and find a feasible place, help or supplies instead of using one assumption for all.
- **Delivery moves later again:** Recalculate routines, timing and owners from the new information and tell affected people.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a short-term downgrade for an ordinary move, not financial compensation, tenancy interpretation or medical care. Region, cost, companions, assistive items and available places vary. If money is tight, first verify what you already have or can borrow by agreement, without promising such options exist. A trusted person can read back times and plans when reading or communication is difficult. Today's plan becomes executable instead of repeatedly refreshing a truck that is not here.
