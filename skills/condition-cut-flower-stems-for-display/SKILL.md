## 中文

当一把已买或已采的**已知普通观赏切花**还没正式插瓶，你需要把它整理成能吸水、不会让叶片泡水的状态时，加载这份 Skill。结果是清洁、补水中的花枝，不是已构图的作品。身份不明、会刺激皮肤的汁液、特殊热水封口或专业药剂处理不在这里猜做。

### 准备与输入

先看花种或供货者附的特殊处理说明；有明确条件时按它，没把握就避开该枝。准备稳定的清洁容器、清水、干净且能利落切茎的工具、可保护手的用品，以及凉爽不挡路的位置。若有切花保鲜剂，只按其包装对实际水量配制；没有也可先用清水，不自配消毒剂/糖酸混合液。剪具不能安全握持时请可信的人协助。

### 执行

1. **清理容器与花枝。** 洗净并冲净容器，去掉破损或明显腐烂的花叶。预估水线，把会浸在水中的叶片轻轻去掉；保留水线以上健康叶子，不把整枝剥光。
2. **复核花种后修剪末端。** 对可普通剪切的茎，用干净合用工具剪去一小段不新鲜的基部，切口平整、不压碎茎。需特别处理的木质、空心或流汁茎先按该种可靠资料判断；不照搬别人的热水、敲砸或深纵切法。
3. **立即入水并让它吸水。** 将新切端放进足够的清水，保持水线覆盖切口、花头在水上；把容器放在凉爽处，远离热源和强日晒。给花一段按花种建议的补水时间；等待时别把裸茎再次晾干。
4. **入瓶前检查。** 看水是否清澈、切口浸水、叶片未泡水、花枝可轻拿。若水变脏，换干净水并重查花枝；已严重萎坏、发霉或汁液风险不明的枝不塞进同束。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

容器清洁、水覆盖新鲜切端、没有叶子淹在水里，花枝可进入下一步瓶插；“补水中”不保证任何固定寿命。

- **水很快浑浊或有异味：** 倒掉、洗容器、换水，去掉变坏叶枝；不往水里随意加清洁剂。
- **茎被剪具压扁：** 停用钝具，换合用工具或请人协助；不能继续挤压同一末端。
- **花头倒垂：** 先查切端是否入水、花种是否需要特殊处理；无法确认时暂时分开，不用热水试偏方。
- **容器小或水线太高：** 改用稳固的较大容器或调整水量，再去除确实浸水的下部叶片。
- **手部或视觉受限：** 可用宽口稳定桶、放大/高对比水位标记和现场帮助；仍需由你确认浸水与叶片状态。

### 假设与边界

只处理普通切花，不把物种差异抹成一个万能切角、浸水小时数或保鲜剂用量。来源资料有些列出特殊茎处理，本篇把它们转回该花种的可靠说明，不让初学者照通用步骤执行危险热水法。

## English

Load this Skill when a bunch of **known ordinary ornamental cut flowers** has been bought or harvested but is not yet displayed. Prepare stems to take up water without submerging leaves. Finish with clean stems hydrating, not a composed arrangement. Do not guess treatments for unidentified flowers, irritating sap, hot-water sealing, or professional chemicals.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the species or supplier's special handling directions first; set aside a stem whose requirements you cannot establish. Set out a stable clean vessel, water, a clean tool capable of a neat stem cut, hand protection if needed, and a cool unobstructed place. If using flower food, mix only the packet amount for the actual water volume. Plain water is an option; do not improvise disinfectant, sugar, or acid mixtures. Ask trusted help if you cannot control the cutter safely.

### Execution

1. **Clean vessel and stems.** Wash and rinse the vessel. Remove damaged or clearly decaying leaves or blooms. Estimate the waterline and gently remove leaves that would sit under it, leaving healthy foliage above the waterline.
2. **Check species, then renew cut ends.** For ordinary stems suited to cutting, use a clean suitable tool to remove a small stale base portion with a neat, uncrushed cut. Check reliable species guidance before treating woody, hollow, or sap-bearing stems. Do not copy a hot-water, hammering, or deep-slit method from another flower.
3. **Hydrate promptly.** Put new cut ends into enough clean water to cover them while keeping flower heads above it. Place the vessel somewhere cool, away from heat and harsh sun. Allow a hydration period appropriate to the flowers; do not let bare stems dry again while waiting.
4. **Check before arranging.** Look for clear water, submerged cut ends, no immersed leaves, and stems you can handle gently. If water becomes dirty, change it and recheck stems. Keep badly wilted, moldy, or uncertain sap-risk stems out of the bunch.

### Success, problems, and recovery

The vessel is clean, water covers renewed cut ends, no foliage is submerged, and stems are ready for a display. “Hydrating” promises no fixed vase life.

- **Water quickly clouds or smells:** Empty, wash, refill, and remove deteriorating plant parts; do not add random cleaning chemicals to the water.
- **Tool crushes a stem:** Stop using the blunt tool; change tools or ask help rather than squeezing the same end repeatedly.
- **Flower heads droop:** Check water contact and species-specific care first. Isolate an uncertain stem instead of trying hot-water folklore.
- **Vessel is narrow or waterline too high:** Change to a stable wider vessel or adjust water, then remove only leaves truly below the line.
- **Grip or vision is limited:** A broad stable bucket, magnification or high-contrast waterline marker, and in-person help can make the check accessible; you still confirm immersion and foliage.

### Assumptions and limits

Ordinary cut flowers vary by species. No single cut angle, hydration duration, or food dose applies to all. The sources include special stem treatments, which this beginner Skill routes to reliable species guidance instead of making a hazardous hot-water recipe universal.
