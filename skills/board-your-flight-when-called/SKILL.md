## 中文

你已在经官方信息核对的登机口，航司开始组织这趟普通航班登机，需要按**这次实际叫号和工作人员指示**进入正确飞机时加载。结果是本人登机牌被正式接受、沿指定路线进入该飞机；进入客舱后放行李和找座位由下一份 Skill 接手。这里不判断证件/签证资格、不处理改签，也不教越过未叫到的队伍或关闭的舱门。

### 准备与输入

在登机口现场屏、广播或航司工作人员处核对航班号、日期、目的地与这扇门是否对应。准备本人的登机牌和按航司要求出示的身份材料，确认手机亮度/电量或纸质票可读；别向无关人展示完整行程码。看登机牌上的组别、区域或航司给出的其他登机顺序。同行人可能组别不同；是否能一起进以当次航司/登机口安排为准。

### 执行

1. **听这趟航班的当前叫号。** 留意现场口头广播、屏幕与排队标识是否叫到你的组别；需更多时间或辅助登机时按实际航司流程向登机口工作人员询问可用安排，不凭个人需要自行穿过未开放的队伍。
2. **排到被指定的队伍。** 按工作人员引导进本航班的正式通道，给其他旅客与协助设备留路。优先、家庭、普通或迟到组别的规则因航司而异；别把另一家航司的组号套在这次登机牌上。
3. **出示本人通行材料。** 按现场方法让工作人员扫描/检查登机牌并提供实际要求的材料。若票码不被接受、名字/航班不符或工作人员要求进一步核验，退到不挡人的位置，交由航司正式人员处理；不重复抢闸或借别人的票试。
4. **沿被确认的路线到飞机。** 只走工作人员指示的廊桥、摆渡车或其他开放路线，注意停留、等车和步行指示。到了舱门再核对航司人员确认的航班，按机组指示进入。路线暂停、换机或指示不清时停下问工作人员，不跟着人群走进未开放的门。

### 完成与停止

本人本段航班的登机牌已被航司接受，且你经指定路线进入**这趟正确的飞机**，登机动作完成；此时还需找座位和放好随身物。若航班号/门位冲突、票码被拒、叫号未轮到你或舱门已关，停止自助推进，问登机口航司人员当前可行办法。已值机不保证一定可以越过关闭的登机口。

### 常见报错与补救

- **看到长队就跟上：** 先核对航班号和组别；其他航班或未叫到的队伍不是登机证据。
- **App 仍显示旧门位：** 对照现场官方显示或问本航班工作人员，再决定站哪一队。
- **票码不亮、手机没电或扫描失败：** 请正式人员核实可用的纸质/系统记录，不让陌生旅客代替扫码。
- **同行人的组别不同：** 向登机口询问本次同行规则，等允许的叫号，不自行假定可提前。
- **错过叫号或门已关：** 立即到航司柜台/登机口按其真实说明处理；不夹门、不要求机组违反程序。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通旅客的登机口到飞机入口。叫号、预登机、移动路线、材料核查和关闭时间随航司、机场、机型与当日运行改变。听觉、视觉、行动或语言不便时，可用航司实际提供的屏幕、文字、广播、工作人员或已安排的协助；不因此获得绕过正式核验的权利。副作用是你可能发现队伍比预想慢，但站对航班仍比排得早重要。

## English

Load this Skill at an officially verified gate when the operating airline starts boarding your ordinary flight and you need to enter the **correct aircraft under this actual call and staff direction**. Your result is an accepted pass and entry to that aircraft by the assigned route. Finding your seat and placing belongings is the next Skill. This does not decide document or visa eligibility, rebooking or a way around an uncalled group or closed aircraft door.

### Preparation and inputs

Match flight number, date, destination and gate using the on-site display, announcement or airline staff. Have your own boarding pass and whatever identity materials the airline actually asks to see, with a readable screen or paper copy. Do not display the full code to unrelated people. Note the group, zone or other boarding order on your pass. Companions may have different groups; ask this airline or gate if you want to board together.

### Execution

1. **Listen for this flight's current call.** Watch the gate announcement, display and queue signs for your group. If you need more time or boarding assistance, ask gate staff for this airline's actual option; a need for help does not by itself open a closed lane.
2. **Use the directed queue.** Join the official lane for this flight when staff allow you to, leaving room for others and assistance equipment. Priority, family, regular and late-group rules vary by carrier; another airline's group number is not your call.
3. **Present your own boarding materials.** Let staff scan or check the pass by the gate's method and show other materials actually required. If the code is refused, name or flight differs, or further verification is requested, step clear and ask airline staff. Do not force the scanner or test another traveller's pass.
4. **Follow the confirmed path to the aircraft.** Use only the jet bridge, transfer bus or other open route staff direct, including its waiting or walking instructions. At the aircraft door, follow the crew's confirmation and enter. If the path pauses, aircraft changes or directions are unclear, ask staff rather than following a crowd into an unopened door.

### Success and stop

Your pass for this leg has been accepted and you have entered **the correct aircraft** by the assigned route. You still need to find your seat and stow belongings. If flight or gate information conflicts, the pass fails, your group is not called or the door is closed, stop trying to advance and ask airline gate staff about the real next option. Being checked in does not guarantee passage through a closed gate.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You join the longest queue:** Verify flight and group first; a queue for another flight or an uncalled group proves nothing.
- **The app still shows an older gate:** Compare current on-site official information or ask this flight's staff before lining up.
- **The code is unreadable, phone flat or scan fails:** Ask official staff about a paper or system-record option. Do not hand scanning to a stranger.
- **Companions have different groups:** Ask this gate's rule and wait for an allowed call; do not assume earlier boarding.
- **Your call was missed or the door is closed:** Ask the airline counter or gate for its current option. Do not hold a door or demand a procedural exception.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers an ordinary passenger's path from gate to aircraft entry. Calls, preboarding, vehicle route, document checks and closing times vary by airline, airport, aircraft and day. Sight, hearing, mobility or language needs may call for the carrier's actual display, text, announcement, staff or arranged assistance; they do not bypass official verification. The queue may move slowly, but the right flight matters more than an early line.
