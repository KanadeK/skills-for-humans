## 中文

当你已有多张照片，想让少量作品表达一个观察或小故事，却不知道该留哪几张时，用这份 Skill **选出并排列一组**。完成物是能说清目的的 2–4 张照片及顺序，不是清空相册、修完所有照片或自动公开它们。

### 准备与输入

只用你自己拍摄或获得编排许可的照片，在本机或纸质小样中查看。先写一句想表达的点，例如“同一条路上由亮到暗的变化”，不要把“好看”当唯一筛选条件。决定用途是自己看还是以后向特定人展示；若图中有可识别的人、私人地点、地址、车牌或敏感内容，先排除不适合该用途的图，不因裁切方便就忽略同意和规则。原始文件先保留；本篇不是备份或删除流程。放大、打印小样、屏幕阅读说明或可信协助可以替代小缩略图。

### 执行

1. **快速看一轮。** 标出与那句话有关的候选，不在此时修色或逐张打分。排除根本看不清、主体不符合目标或不适合预定用途的照片；不删原片。
2. **比较近似图。** 同一场景若有多张，问每张增加了什么新信息或视角。只留能改变表达的一张或两张；几乎重复的十张不会让小组更完整。
3. **排成短序列。** 从候选中先取 2–4 张，试一个开头、中间（若有）和结尾。顺序可按时间、空间、细节到全景或对照关系，但要能让你说出后一张为何接前一张；不用为了凑满四张留下无关图。
4. **做一次无说明检查。** 按顺序看一遍，问“即使不听长篇解释，主题是否还能看见？”若不能，换掉最弱的一张或改顺序；再写一行“我选这些图，是要让人看到____”。这是你自己的初步检查，不假称观众已经理解。
5. **记下结果。** 用本地相册收藏、纸上编号或简单清单保存顺序；确认标记能指向正确原片。不要为了建选集擅自上传到云端、删除原片或公开图像。若后来要展示，再做权限、同意和文件元数据检查。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你能按顺序找到 2–4 张有权编排、符合预定用途的照片，并用一句话说明共同表达；各张至少提供一个不同信息或视角。若素材中只有一张合适，保留单张并标记“序列未完成”，无需用无关图凑数。

- **全是同一主体的近似连拍：** 只留最清楚的一张，再找能提供不同视角的图；找不到就暂停序列。
- **顺序换了也看不出区别：** 先选一张能建立地点/主题的开头和一张产生对照的结尾，其余可不放。
- **某张很美但偏离目标：** 单独收藏供以后用，不硬塞进这一组。
- **发现隐私或使用许可不明：** 从预定组中撤下，改用自己的安全图；本篇不作法律结论。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

照片的主题与美感是你的创作判断，2–4 张是让首次选片可结束的范围，不是摄影标准。设备、视觉或手部条件不同可用大图、纸样、口述与可信协助；你仍决定顺序。原片、备份和相册清理不在本篇；选集和一行说明会留在你的设备或纸上，是否分享另作决定。

## English

Use this Skill when you have several photos and want a small set to express one observation or story but cannot decide which to keep in the set. **Choose and order a sequence** of 2–4 images with a clear purpose. This does not empty your camera roll, finish every edit, or publish the images.

### Preparation and inputs

View photos you took or have permission to arrange, on your own device or as paper proofs. Write one point you want to express, such as “the same street changing from light to shadow”; “looks nice” need not be your only criterion. Decide whether this is for yourself or possible later display to specific people. If a frame contains identifiable people, private places, addresses, plates, or sensitive details, exclude it when unsuitable for that use; easy cropping is no substitute for consent or site rules. Keep the originals for now. This is not a backup or deletion workflow. Enlarged views, paper proofs, accessible description, or trusted help can replace tiny thumbnails.

### Execution

1. **Scan once.** Mark candidates related to your sentence. Do not begin colour editing or scoring every file. Set aside photos whose subject is illegible, unrelated to the point, or unsuitable for the intended use. Do not delete originals.
2. **Compare similar frames.** If one scene has several shots, ask what new information or view each adds. Keep one or two that change the expression. Ten near-identical frames do not make a short sequence fuller.
3. **Arrange a short order.** Start with 2–4 candidates: an opening, perhaps a middle, and an ending. Order by time, place, detail to wider view, or contrast, but be able to say why one follows the previous one. Do not add an unrelated fourth image just to reach four.
4. **Check without a long explanation.** Look through in order. Can the theme still be seen without a speech? If not, replace the weakest frame or change the order. Write “I chose these to show ____.” This is your first check, not proof that an audience understood.
5. **Record the result.** Keep the order in a local favourite list, numbered paper, or simple note and confirm each marker points to the right original. Do not silently upload, delete originals, or publish to make a set. If you later present it, check permission, consent, and file metadata then.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can find 2–4 photos in order that you may arrange and use as intended, and explain the shared point in one sentence. Each contributes a different fact or view. If only one suitable image exists, keep it as a single photo and mark the sequence incomplete rather than fill with irrelevant material.

- **All candidates are near-duplicate bursts:** Keep the clearest and seek a different view; pause the sequence if none exists.
- **Order makes no difference:** Try an opening that establishes place or topic and an ending that adds contrast; omit the rest if unnecessary.
- **One beautiful frame misses the purpose:** Save it separately for later, without forcing it into this set.
- **Privacy or permission is unclear:** Remove the frame from the intended sequence and choose a safe photo you made. This Skill offers no legal verdict.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Theme and aesthetic value are your choices. Two to four is a bounded first-pass size, not a photography standard. Use enlarged images, paper proofs, spoken description, or trusted help for visual, device, or hand-access needs; you still choose the order. Originals, backup, and photo-library cleanup remain separate. The selection and sentence stay on your device or paper until you choose whether to share.
