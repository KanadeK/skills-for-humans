## 中文

当一个普通工作、学习或家庭事务日将结束，你要从当前活动退出，而清单上仍有未完成项时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**有限收尾**：记真实状态、处理确实影响别人的事、归还普通共用物，并留一个明天能找回的入口。它不要求今天清空清单、不做睡眠治疗；单个任务中断前的详细恢复点归 H265，第二天重入归 H266。

### 准备与输入

给收尾设一个短停点，拿出今天实际用过的任务记录、约定和借用物品。只看今天动过或明天确实会影响到的项目，不重排全年计划。先检查是否有对别人承诺当日交付、需要交接或明确归还的普通事项；若涉及紧急安全或专业规则，按其渠道处理，不塞进日常收尾清单。

### 执行

1. **按事实标今天的状态。** 对少量活跃项写“已完成并已交/做了但未交/待他人回复/未开始”，必要时附文件或材料位置。完成本机草稿不等于对方已经收到；没做完的不要用“差不多”掩盖。
2. **先处理会影响别人的缺口。** 若今天承诺的普通交付尚未送出，按已约定渠道准确说明当前状态和下一次更新时间；消息没发出去就记待通知。若无他人受影响，不为礼貌制造一堆收尾消息。专业截止/合同事项另按正式规则。
3. **归还或交代共用物。** 把当天借用的普通工具、材料或场地按原约定放回/交还；不能归还时告诉有权负责的人物品在哪和何时处理。对未约定的私人物品不清理、不替别人作去留决定，也不碰共享设备的未知设置。
4. **留下明天的一行入口。** 对确实要继续的一项，写“从哪份材料/位置，先做什么”，并核明天真实已有的时间约定或截止。余项保留原状态，等下一次择项；不把所有未完事项都自动改成“明天首要”。
5. **按停点结束。** 关上本次工作窗口、收好自己的普通材料，确认共用空间不因你而阻塞下一位。收尾时间到了就停；要继续做是新的决定，不让“最后整理一下”无限延长一天。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你能在记录中找到今天实际完成/等待/未完的状态，影响别人的缺口已有真实告知或明确待通知，共用物去向可查，明天有一条可执行入口。未完成项存在不使这次收尾失败。

- **每件都想再做五分钟：** 只处理确实影响他人或明天入口的最小动作，按原停点结束。
- **草稿写完却尚未提交：** 标“做了未交”，不要写“已完成并已交”。
- **借来的东西一时找不到：** 停在已知最后位置，告知负责人并约定查找；物品本身搜索归对应物品流程。
- **有人等你的答复但渠道不可用：** 记录准确待通知对象和下一可用渠道/时间，不宣称消息已送达。
- **明天有安排变更：** 核最新有效通知，再更新个人入口；不凭今天的旧截图。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

纸、可访问待办、语音或工作场所批准的记录都可以，但受管信息须留在获授权位置。共用物和场地归还方式由真实约定决定；本 Skill 不替代行业交班、钥匙/凭据管理或正式合规程序。留一点未完工作是正常状态，清楚标记比把它们全部假装完成更有用。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary work, study, or household day is ending, you need to leave the current activity, and the list still has unfinished items. Make a **bounded close**: record true states, handle effects on other people, return ordinary shared items, and leave one retrievable entry for tomorrow. It does not require clearing today's list or provide sleep treatment. A detailed checkpoint before interrupting one task belongs to H265; re-entry tomorrow to H266.

### Preparation and inputs

Set a short stopping point and gather the actual tasks, appointments, and borrowed items touched today. Look only at active items and things genuinely affecting tomorrow, not the whole year's plan. Notice any promised delivery, handover, or agreed return of an ordinary item that affects someone else. Route urgent safety or specialised requirements through their proper channel rather than stuffing them into daily closeout.

### Execution

1. **Mark today's states truthfully.** For a few active items write “finished and delivered / worked on but not delivered / waiting for someone / not started,” with file or material location if useful. A finished local draft does not prove the other person received it. Do not hide incomplete work behind “basically done.”
2. **Handle only gaps affecting others.** If an ordinary promised delivery did not go out today, state the true position and next update through the agreed route. If the message did not send, mark notice pending. With no external effect, do not manufacture many polite closeout messages. Specialist deadlines or contracts use formal rules.
3. **Return or account for shared items.** Put ordinary borrowed tools, materials, or space back as agreed. If you cannot, tell the responsible person where they are and when you will address it. Do not clear other people's private items or decide their disposal, and do not change unknown shared-device settings.
4. **Leave one entry for tomorrow.** For one item genuinely continuing, write which material or location to open and the first action. Check tomorrow's real existing appointment or deadline. Leave other items with their actual states until the next selection round; do not automatically promote every unfinished task to “tomorrow's top priority.”
5. **Stop at the boundary.** Close this work window, put away your ordinary materials, and make sure your use does not block the next person in a shared space. End when closeout time is up. More work is a new choice, not an endlessly expanding “one last tidy.”

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Your record shows actual completed, waiting, and unfinished states; an externally affecting gap has a real notice or an explicit pending notice; shared-item locations are known; and tomorrow has one doable entry. Remaining unfinished work does not make closeout fail.

- **Everything invites “five more minutes”:** Do only the smallest action affecting another person or tomorrow's entry, then stop at the original boundary.
- **A draft is complete but not submitted:** Mark “worked on, not delivered,” not “finished and delivered.”
- **A borrowed item is temporarily missing:** Record the last known place, tell the responsible person, and agree on search. The item search itself uses its relevant process.
- **Someone needs your reply but the channel fails:** Record the actual person and next usable route or time; do not claim notice was delivered.
- **Tomorrow's plan changed:** Check the latest valid notice before updating your personal entry, not today's old screenshot.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Paper, an accessible list, speech, or an approved workplace record can hold this closeout, while managed information stays in authorised places. Shared-item and space return depends on actual agreements. This does not replace specialist shift handover, key or credential management, or formal compliance. Some work remaining is normal; a clear state is more useful than pretending every task is complete.
