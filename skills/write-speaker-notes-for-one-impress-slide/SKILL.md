## 中文

你有一张适合投影的简洁内容页，但讲述时还需记住一个来源、转场或提醒时使用本篇。成果是备注仅附在该页 Notes 区，放映主屏仍只见页面内容；备注不是提词全文，也不是能替你核事实的隐藏证明。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 副本，选准目标页并写下最多几条本人能扫一眼就懂的短提示。确认提示不含不应保留在文件中的私人信息，因为 ODP 仍保存 Notes。记下目标页标题与前后页以免写错页。

### 执行

1. 在目标页切到 View > Notes，确认预览图确实是目标页。
2. 在 Notes 区写简短来源、转场和一个需要口头补充的事实，不把长段文字复制到投影页。
3. 回 Normal 与放映预览，确认主屏不显示这些备注、目标页内容没有变。
4. 重开目标页 Notes 检短提示仍在，保存后核相邻页没有同样的误粘备注。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

备注简短、对应正确页，投影画面未被堆满，保存重开能找回。

- **写到邻页：** 核 Notes 预览并移回正确页。
- **主屏显示太多字：** 把只供讲述的细节移回备注。
- **备注含敏感信息：** 删去并核文件副本披露范围。

### 假设与边界

备注仍存于 ODP，打印或导出备注页时可能显示；本篇不保证保密。你负责事实与讲述时长，未进行现场演讲测试。

## English

Use this Skill when one concise projected slide needs a source cue, transition or reminder for your spoken explanation. Finish with notes attached to that slide in Notes view while the main show screen keeps only slide content. Notes are not a full script or hidden proof that facts are correct.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP copy and select target slide. Draft a few short cues you can scan quickly. Avoid private details that must not remain in the file, because ODP stores Notes. Note slide title and neighbours to avoid attaching them to the wrong page.

### Execution

1. On target slide switch to View > Notes and verify its slide preview.
2. Write short source, transition and one spoken fact in Notes, without copying a long paragraph onto projected slide.
3. Return to Normal and show preview; verify notes are absent from main screen and slide content unchanged.
4. Reopen target Notes, check cues persist and adjacent slides have no accidental pasted notes.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Notes are concise, attached to correct slide, projected view stays uncluttered, and they persist after reopening.

- **Notes on neighbour:** Use Notes preview and move to correct slide.
- **Main slide overloaded:** Move speaker-only details to Notes.
- **Notes contain private data:** Remove it and review file sharing.

### Assumptions and limits

Notes remain inside ODP and may appear in notes print/export; this is no secrecy guarantee. You own facts and timing; no live talk is tested.
