## 中文

当你已选中一件馆藏，但它借出中、在别的馆点或本馆暂时拿不到，而馆方提供预约取件服务时，用这份 Skill 提交**一次有确认记录的预约**。结束物是馆方接受的请求和你知道怎样等通知，不是书已送达或借阅已完成。

### 准备与输入

再次核对题名、作者、版本和格式，确认馆方允许这件物品预约；参考资料、特殊馆藏或某些格式可能不适用。看本馆资格、可能费用、排队/取消和取件期限的现行说明，选择你能实际到达的取件馆点。使用你本人有权使用的卡与馆方正式网站、电话或柜台；本篇不要求把卡号/PIN 写在记录里。若时间紧急、取件条件不合或费用不接受，可以不提交请求，另找现成资料。

### 执行

1. **在馆方记录中选对版本。** 确认要预约的是你实际需要的纸本、音频或其他可预约格式，不因同名结果默认相同。记录当前馆点/状态，避免重复提交已有同一请求。
2. **按正式流程请求。** 在馆方网站/App 登录自己的账户、电话请馆员操作，或到柜台办理；只在官方界面输入凭据。查看取件馆点、通知方式和馆方显示的条件，在最终确认前再核一遍。
3. **核对请求确实被接受。** 找到馆方给出的预约记录、确认页面或工作人员回执，确认是该物品、该取件馆点、你的账户。只点了“预约”但页面报错或没有记录，就先排查，不重复盲点造成多条请求。
4. **设置等待边界。** 记下馆方说明的通知方式和你愿意查看的频率；等待时间、队列和取件期限以馆方实际更新为准。收到“正在处理”并不等于“可取”；未收到准备好通知前，不把出门取件列为完成动作。
5. **在状态变化时转流程。** 收到“可取”后核对馆点、截止、取件标识和借出要求，再去领取并完成借阅登记。若请求被拒、超时或你改变主意，按馆方说明取消/询问；不把未取到的物品记作已借。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

馆方系统或工作人员确认了**具体物品、你的取件馆点和待处理状态**；你知道等待什么通知。物品实际到手是后来的一步，不属于本篇成功条件。

- **条目不允许预约：** 看馆方原因，换可预约版本或请馆员提供合法替代；不要试图绕过限制。
- **选错馆点或格式：** 在允许时立即修改/取消重提，确认新请求；不假定系统自动更正。
- **提交后只见转圈、没有确认：** 查账户预约清单或请馆员核实，避免重复请求。
- **已排队很久：** 看馆方当前状态和取消条件，必要时找替代资料；不承诺固定到货日期。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

预约资格、费用、通知和取件期限随馆与馆藏而变，本篇不复述某馆的数字为通则。可用柜台/电话或馆方可及通道替代自己操作屏幕；账号始终由你或馆方正式渠道管理。预约可能占用你的账户额度，改变主意时按馆方流程取消。尚未取件，也尚未借出。

## English

Use this Skill when you have identified an item but it is checked out, at another branch, or otherwise unavailable now and the library offers holds for pickup. Place **one confirmed hold request**. The outcome is an accepted request and a clear waiting route, not an arrived or borrowed item.

### Preparation and inputs

Recheck title, author, edition, and format, and whether this item is eligible for a hold. Reference and special items or some formats may be excluded. Read this library's current eligibility, possible charges, queue or cancellation rules, and pickup window. Choose a branch you can actually reach. Use your own authorised card and the library's official site, phone, or desk; do not put card number or PIN in your notes. If the need is urgent, pickup conditions do not work, or you do not accept a charge, you may decline the request and find material available now.

### Execution

1. **Choose the right edition in the official record.** Make sure you request the print, audio, or other eligible format you need, not a namesake. Check current branch or status and whether you already have the same request pending.
2. **Use the approved request route.** Sign into your own account on the library site or app, ask staff by phone, or use the desk. Enter credentials only in the official interface. Review pickup branch, notification route, and shown conditions before final submission.
3. **Verify acceptance.** Find a library hold record, confirmation page, or staff receipt with the item, branch, and your account. If you only pressed a button and got an error or no record, investigate before repeatedly clicking and creating duplicate requests.
4. **Set a waiting boundary.** Note the library's notification method and how often you will check. Wait times, queues, and pickup deadlines come from actual updates. “Processing” is not “ready for pickup”; do not count a pickup trip as due until the ready notice arrives.
5. **Switch workflow when status changes.** On a ready notice, check branch, deadline, pickup identifier, and checkout requirements. Then collect it and complete checkout. If rejected, timed out, or no longer wanted, cancel or ask according to library rules. Do not mark an uncollected item borrowed.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

The official system or staff confirms **the specific item, your pickup branch, and pending status**, and you know what notice to await. Having the item in hand is a later step.

- **The item cannot be held:** Read the reason, choose an eligible version, or ask staff for an authorised alternative. Do not bypass a restriction.
- **Wrong branch or format was chosen:** Modify or cancel and request again if allowed, checking the new record rather than assuming automatic correction.
- **Submission spun but no confirmation appeared:** Check your hold list or ask staff before making duplicate requests.
- **The queue is long:** Check the real status and cancellation options and seek another suitable item if needed. Do not promise a fixed arrival date.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Hold eligibility, charges, notices, and windows vary by library and item. No one library's numbers apply everywhere. Desk, phone, or an accessible official route can replace solo screen use; your account stays within your control and library channels. A hold may consume an account allowance. Cancel through official procedures if plans change. The item is neither collected nor checked out yet.
