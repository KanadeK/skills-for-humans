## 中文

当你打算今天带已熟悉的狗牵绳出门，但不确定哪条普通步行路合适时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一条**今天准入、地面与天气可承受、有折返点**的短路，或明确改时/不出。H291 选人自己的公园散步；这里还要核狗能否进入、足垫接触面与排泄物去向。

### 准备与输入

确认这只狗的既有兽医运动限制、牵引者可控制它的能力、当地官方天气预报、路线当下开放与犬只准入/牵绳规定。狗有明显身体不适或异常呼吸、牵引者无法控制、气温或路面极端时，不把规划一条“更短”路当医疗替代。

### 执行

1. **选一个可折返的候选。** 标起点、允许狗进入的连续路段、安静的折返/退出点和回家方式，不用陌生死胡同或依赖一个已关的门。
2. **核当下天气和地面。** 热、潮湿、强冷、结冰或烫的路面可能使普通外出不合适。用当地当前预报和可观察路况判断，不用一个全球通用温度阈值；PDSA 提醒热地面会伤足垫，RSPCA 提醒避开极热时段。
3. **核路权与互相避让。** 查实际地方、时段的犬只准入、牵绳和野生动物/牲畜边界；为相遇的人与狗留空间，不把“我们狗很友善”当别人同意接触。
4. **核人和狗的资源。** 装上废物袋、必要的饮水与回程联系手段，路线长度符合已定兽医方案与牵引者的体力/可及性；不要预设每天必须完成固定公里数。
5. **作 go/shorten/defer 决定。** 把路线、折返点和因天气/路况停止的信号说清；当前条件不够就改变时间、地点或按实际兽医/照料计划做合适的室内替代。

### 成功条件

你能指出今天真实可走的犬只获准路径、天气/路面依据、折返点和清理用品；或明确因为哪一项不合而延期。路线计划不等于已经遛过狗，也不证明它能承受这段距离。

### 常见报错与补救

- **公园允许人进但禁犬：** 换真实允许狗进入的路线，不靠路口没人看守。
- **气温看似不高但路面烫：** 改时或改地面，别让足垫代你验证。
- **突然施工封路：** 回到最近安全点，改短或返回，不临时横穿危险车道。
- **牵引者体力不够回程：** 缩短并保留易到达出口，请合适的人帮助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只为一只已熟悉、可普通牵引的狗选短途路线，不制定兽医运动量、处理反应性犬、教过街交通动作或解释当地法条。行动不便者应选真实可及路面或请能控制狗的人出行。天气与场地会变化，出门时必须重看关键条件。

## English

Load this Skill when you plan an ordinary leashed outing with a familiar dog today but have not chosen a suitable route. Finish with a **dog-permitted short route matching today's weather and surface, with a return point**, or an explicit later/no-walk decision. H291 chooses a person's park stroll; this also checks dog access, paw surface, and waste handling.

### Preparation and inputs

Check existing veterinary activity limits, whether this handler can control this dog, the current official local forecast, present path status, and actual dog-access/lead rules. An unwell dog, abnormal breathing, inadequate control, or extreme surface/weather conditions do not become medically safe just because the route is shorter.

### Execution

1. **Choose a route with a return.** Name a start, continuous dog-permitted path, quiet turn-back or exit, and way home. Avoid an unfamiliar dead end or reliance on a gate already closed.
2. **Check today's weather and ground.** Heat, humidity, strong cold, ice, and hot pavement may rule out ordinary walking. Use current local information and observed surface, not a global temperature number. PDSA warns that hot surfaces can hurt paws; RSPCA advises avoiding the hottest hours.
3. **Check access and space for others.** Read actual place/time dog access, lead rules, and wildlife or livestock boundaries. Leave room around people and dogs. “My dog is friendly” does not grant another person's consent to meet.
4. **Check resources for both walkers.** Take waste bags, water when needed, and a way to arrange the return. Route length follows the existing veterinary plan and handler's reach and stamina; there is no compulsory kilometre target.
5. **Decide go, shorten, or defer.** State the path, turnaround, and weather/ground signals to stop. If conditions fail, change time/place or use an appropriate indoor alternative under the real care plan.

### Success

You can identify today's actually permitted path, forecast and surface basis, return point, and waste supplies, or name the reason for postponement. A route plan is not a completed walk or proof the dog can tolerate the distance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A park admits people but excludes dogs:** Choose a genuinely dog-permitted route rather than relying on an unstaffed entrance.
- **Air feels tolerable but pavement is hot:** Change time or surface; do not use paws as a test instrument.
- **Construction closes the path:** Return to the last safe point and shorten or go home, not across a dangerous road.
- **The handler cannot complete the return:** Choose a shorter accessible route or a capable alternative handler.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This plans a short trip for one familiar ordinarily leashed dog. It does not prescribe veterinary exercise, manage a reactive dog, teach road-crossing technique, or interpret local law. Use an actually accessible surface or a capable helper when mobility limits the handler. Weather and access can change; check again at departure.
