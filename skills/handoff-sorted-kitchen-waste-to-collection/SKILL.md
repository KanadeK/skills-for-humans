## 中文

当你已把普通餐后垃圾、厨余和回收物按本地准入规则分好，接下来需要**从厨房或家中交给楼宇/路边/投放点**时，用这份 Skill 核对时间和容器、控制搬运、完成实际投放并复位厨房。它不重新判每件包装的可回收性，也不处理危险废物、医疗垃圾、尖锐破损玻璃或垃圾设施维修。

### 准备与输入

查实际物业、市政或收运方公布的投放点、可投时间、桶/袋要求、是否分类散放、是否需要关盖，以及回收和厨余是否不同日期。核对每袋有没有漏液、超重、尖锐外露或错误混类；不确定的材料先留待查而非交给错误桶。规划从厨房到投放点的安全路线和回程洗手，若行动/搬运受限，分成更轻的批次或找可信协助。

### 执行

1. **先确认本次可以交。** 投放点开放且你有使用资格，桶类别与本次收运安排匹配。过了收运时间或容器不合规时，按现场规则改期/暂存，不把袋子留在路边或别人门口占位。
2. **关好并保持类别。** 按本地规定收紧普通垃圾袋、厨余容器和回收物，不将回收物临时塞进垃圾袋“省一次路”。漏液先用允许的容器重新装好并处理污染外表；破损、锋利或危险物不混进普通袋。
3. **安全搬运到指定位置。** 每次只提你能控制的重量，走清楚、不会碰食物或他人的路径；水滴、碎屑或漏液一旦发生就停下按适用清洁方式处理，不留给下一人踩。不可把袋子挂在不稳定门把/扶手上，或堵住通道和紧急出口。
4. **核对并实际投放。** 到点再看桶标和现场更新：普通垃圾、厨余、回收物各进允许的容器/区域，按规则关盖、不要超满或把盖撑开。不确定的不要凭桶颜色猜，先问管理者或查本地清单。保留送回去的可重复用周转桶，不把公共桶当自己的储物箱。
5. **回到厨房复位。** 检查走过的路和原桶位有无洒漏；按本地要求清理外表、换合适内衬或放回可用容器。接触垃圾后洗手，再准备食物或拿干净餐具。记住下一次正确投放时间，避免同样垃圾在台面反复排队。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

已分类的普通餐后物实际到达**有资格、在正确时间、正确类别**的收集点；家庭/公共通道不漏不堵，厨房桶位与双手已复位。无法安全搬运或收集点不开放时，按规则暂存并约定下次移出是完整降级。

- **厨余袋半路漏液：** 停止搬运、控制泄漏并清理经过的地面，再换不漏容器；不要把漏袋直接塞回回收桶。
- **回收桶满到关不上：** 不把物品堆在外面，按现场备用桶或下一次收运规则处理。
- **楼下桶颜色和家里不一样：** 读实际文字/图示，不用颜色匹配猜类别。
- **错过规定时间：** 带回合规暂存，查下一次时段；不在公共走道留下无人接收的袋子。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理一般家庭餐后物，收运规则与无障碍条件按实际地点。电池、药品、油漆和其他危险物需另查专门回收，不跟厨余打包。推车、轻量分袋或可信协助可替代独自提重物；如果路线对你不安全就停下并求助管理者。投放完成并不保证市政最终回收加工，成功只指按规则交接。

## English

Use this Skill after ordinary meal trash, organics, and recyclables are already sorted under local rules and must **leave the kitchen or home for a building, curbside, or drop-off point**. Check time and containers, move a controllable load, deposit it, and restore the kitchen. It does not reclassify every package or handle hazardous waste, medical waste, broken sharp glass, or collection equipment repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the actual property, municipality, or hauler's drop point, hours, bin and bag rules, loose-item requirements, lid rules, and separate collection days. Check each container for leaks, unsafe weight, exposed sharp edges, or a wrong item. Hold unclear materials for checking rather than send them to the wrong stream. Plan a safe path and handwashing on return. Divide loads or arrange trusted help when carrying is limited.

### Execution

1. **Confirm handoff is available now.** The point is open and you are entitled to use it. Match each stream to this collection's schedule and container. If outside hours or improperly packed, use local compliant holding and a new time; do not leave bags at a kerb or another person's door without collection.
2. **Close and keep streams separate.** Secure trash, organics, and recycling under local instructions. Do not stuff recycling into a trash liner merely to save a trip. Recontain liquid leaks and clean the outside as appropriate. Broken, sharp, or hazardous materials do not slip into ordinary bags.
3. **Carry to the designated place safely.** Lift only a weight you can control through a clear route away from food and other people. Stop for drips, scraps, or a leak and clean the affected floor by an applicable method; do not leave it for the next person to step in. Do not hang bags from unstable handles or block passages and exits.
4. **Check and actually deposit.** Read the bin sign and any site update at the point. Use the accepted container or zone for trash, organics, and recyclables, close lids as required, and do not overfill or prop them open. Do not guess from bin colour when uncertain; ask the manager or check the local list. Bring back any reusable carrying container rather than treating a public bin as personal storage.
5. **Restore the kitchen.** Check path and original bin spot for spills. Clean outer surfaces, replace any locally allowed liner, and return a usable bin. Wash hands after garbage before food or clean dishes. Record the next correct collection time so waste does not repeatedly wait on the worktop.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Sorted ordinary post-meal material reached an **authorised point, at an accepted time, in the right stream**. Home and shared routes are clear, nonleaking, and kitchen bin and hands are ready again. If moving safely or collection is impossible now, compliant temporary holding with a definite later route is a complete downgrade.

- **An organics bag leaks mid-route:** Stop, contain and clean the path, and use a nonleaking container. Do not move a leaking bag into recycling.
- **The recycling bin cannot close:** Do not pile material outside. Follow a posted spare-bin or next-collection route.
- **Outdoor bin colours differ from home labels:** Read words or pictures, not colour alone.
- **You miss the permitted time:** Take it back for compliant holding and check the next window; do not leave an uncollected bag in a shared path.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary household meal waste. Actual collection rules and access vary. Batteries, medicine, paint, and other hazardous items need their own collection route. Use a cart, smaller bags, or trusted help instead of lifting beyond your ability. Stop and ask management if the route is unsafe. A correct handoff does not certify that the municipality later recycled the item; success here is following the collection rules.
