## 中文

当一件**自己的普通风干黏土小装饰物已按产品说明全干**，想在上面画原创颜色或记号时，加载这份 Skill。结果是漆层也干、可室内安全摆放的装饰物；这与捏形或等待泥芯干燥不同。上色、透明封层都不会自动把它变成餐具、防水花盆、户外雕塑或烛台。

### 准备与输入

先核作品底部、厚处和接缝已全干且未松裂；只有表干就退回晾干。核黏土和颜料标签相容、年龄/接触警示可满足，使用普通水基水彩、蛋彩或产品允许的涂料；喷漆、溶剂漆与不明封层不在这里。准备画笔、少量颜料、护台垫、清水和通风稳定位置，不在食品台面操作。

### 执行

1. **先做小试色。** 在底部或同泥碎件上薄涂，等它干，确认颜色能附着且泥表面不软化、掉粉。若颜料说明与黏土说明冲突，停。
2. **决定自己的简图和边界。** 选简单色块、线或符号，不照抄付费图案、文物或他人造型。避免把过多湿颜料堆在薄边、修补缝上；需要两色时先让第一层干。
3. **薄涂并放回垫板。** 用合用笔少量上色，转动垫板而非握着易碎件到处擦。留出不沾台面的晾放点；按颜料说明干燥，不用烤箱或热风。
4. **干后核对。** 看颜色是否基本附着、手轻碰不粘、细节和底部未被颜料泡软，记录仍为“室内非食物装饰”。若有剥落或裂纹，暂停摆放，查兼容性或重新做作品。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

泥件原先全干，上色层也按产品要求干燥，颜色可见且件体稳定；只能用作室内装饰。刚涂好、表面仍黏手，不算完成。

- **底部或厚处仍软：** 停止上色，退回全干检查，不能用漆封住水分。
- **试色起粉或不附着：** 换标签兼容的材料或保留素泥，不多刷几层掩盖。
- **湿漆流入修补裂缝：** 停笔、平放待干，再判断裂纹是否仍稳定。
- **想盛水、食物或点烛：** 不用这件作品执行；选真正为该用途制造并标明适用的器具。
- **手眼不便：** 用大色块、稳定垫板、粗笔或可信协助；由你确认颜色干与结构稳。

### 假设与边界

具体颜料是否附着与干燥时间由产品说明决定。Crayola 明确全干的该种黏土可用若干画材装饰，但透明封涂仍不能保水；其它品牌按自身标签，不移植该厂的时数或涂料清单。

## English

Load this Skill when **your own small ordinary air-dry clay decoration is fully dry under its product's directions** and you want original paint or marks on it. Finish with a dry paint layer on a stable piece suitable for indoor display. This differs from forming the shape or waiting for a damp centre to dry. Paint and clear coating do not turn it into tableware, a waterproof planter, outdoor sculpture, or candle holder.

### Preparation and inputs

Check base, thick parts, and seams for full dryness and no loose cracks; return a touch-dry-only piece to drying. Confirm clay and paint labels are compatible and that age or contact warnings are met. Use ordinary water-based paint, such as watercolour or tempera, when this product permits it. Spray, solvent paint, and unknown sealers are outside scope. Set out brush, a little paint, protected board, water, and an airy stable spot away from food work.

### Execution

1. **Test a small colour patch.** Paint thinly on the underside or matching clay scrap, let it dry, and check adhesion without softening or chalking clay. Stop if paint and clay instructions conflict.
2. **Choose your original simple marks.** Pick blocks, lines, or symbols you design, without copying paid patterns, artifacts, or another person's form. Avoid pooling wet paint on thin edges and repaired seams. Let one colour dry before a later layer.
3. **Paint thinly and rest on the board.** Apply small amounts with a suitable brush, turning the board instead of rubbing a fragile object in hand. Leave a no-contact drying position and follow paint drying guidance without oven or hot air.
4. **Check after drying.** Look for paint attached, no tack when lightly touched, and clay not softened at detail or base. Keep the label “indoor non-food decoration”. If paint peels or a crack develops, pause display and check compatibility or remake.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The clay was fully dry first, its paint layer is dry under directions, visible colour remains, and the object is stable for indoor decoration only. Fresh paint still tacky to touch is unfinished.

- **Base or thick part is soft:** Stop painting and return to full-dry checks; do not seal moisture under paint.
- **Test paint chalks or fails to adhere:** Choose label-compatible material or keep bare clay, not extra coats to hide failure.
- **Wet paint enters a repaired crack:** Stop and dry flat, then assess seam stability again.
- **You want it to hold water, food, or a candle:** Do not use this piece; choose an object manufactured and labelled for that purpose.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Use large colour areas, stable board, broad brush, or trusted help while you verify dry paint and intact structure.

### Assumptions and limits

Paint compatibility and time follow each product. Crayola says its fully dry clay accepts several paints but even a clear seal does not make it waterproof. Other brands require their own labels; do not transplant this maker's times or paint list.
