## 中文

当你要给孩子买普通玩具，或家里还有可能拿到它的更小孩子时，付款前加载。这里核对包装给出的年龄和小零件警示与实际接触情境；不替制造商做安全检测，不用“看起来结实”宣布安全，也不处理已发生的窒息或其他紧急情况。

### 准备与输入

确认预定使用儿童的年龄、基本操作能力，以及玩具会放在哪里、是否有更小的孩子可拿到。拿到这件准确款式的完整包装或制造商说明，读年龄建议、醒目的警示、零件/气球等特别提示和所需成人协助。网购页面没有这些信息时先取得包装图；小字看不清可放大或请人逐字读出警示，不只听“适合儿童”。

### 执行

1. **把建议和警示分开读。** 年龄段会提示预定使用者；“小零件”“窒息危险”“不适合某年龄以下”等是明确限制，不可用孩子“很聪明”来跳过。只看包装正面的“益智”不是核对。
2. **比对实际会接触的孩子。** 先看预定使用者是否落在标示年龄范围内，再看家中或送礼地点更小的孩子能否接触。若任何可能接触者落在明确警示的排除年龄内，只有在能可靠隔离时才继续考虑；无法保证就换一件。
3. **核对玩法条件。** 标签要求成人监督、特殊场地或保护装备时，确认现实中能做到；做不到就不按“先买再想办法”放行。户外骑乘等任务还须遵守相应安全指导，本 Skill 不提供这些操作步骤。
4. **让缺失保持缺失。** 警示被贴纸遮住、线上商品图不显示背面、型号或附件不同，均记为“未确认”。索取准确款式的信息或改买说明清楚的玩具；不要自行用小零件试验判定等级。
5. **记录购买结论。** 输出“标签条件和接触安排相容 / 明确冲突 / 未确认”，并记下限制来自哪行字。相容只是通过这些标签条件，不保证玩具没有其它风险。

### 成功与停止

你能说出预定孩子和其他可接触孩子的年龄、包装上的关键警示，以及为什么这件可继续考虑或要放弃。发现与儿童年龄冲突或无法落实隔离、监督条件时停止购买；若已有孩子把物品放入口中并发生呼吸异常，立即停止此购物流程，按当地紧急指引求助。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只按收礼孩子年龄判断：** 再考虑同住或常来往的更小孩子会不会拿到它。
- **把年龄建议当成安全证书：** 继续读全部警示与使用条件；通过年龄项不等于整体安全。
- **警示被价签遮住：** 请店员展示完整包装或制造商对应款式资料，仍看不到就保持未确认。
- **礼物计划改变：** 重看新玩具的警示；同系列包装不共享结论。

### 假设、替代与副作用

各地标签语言和具体规则不同；美国 CPSC 资料只支持其美国示例。这里假设你知道会接触玩具的孩子及存放情境。视觉、语言或照护安排受限时可请求可访问说明、可信照护者共同核对，或选条件更明确的替代礼物。换一件礼物不等于少一份心意。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary toy for a child, especially when younger children may also reach it. You compare the package's age and small-parts warnings with real access. You are not testing the toy for the maker, certifying safety by appearance, or handling a choking emergency here.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the intended child's age and basic ability to use the toy, where it will be kept, and whether younger children can reach it. Get the full package or maker information for the exact variant. Read the age guidance, prominent warnings, small-parts or balloon notices, and any adult-help condition. If an online listing omits these, request a package image first. Enlarge small print or have someone read each warning verbatim rather than accepting “for kids” as a summary.

### Execution

1. **Separate guidance from warnings.** An age range indicates intended users; “small parts,” “choking hazard,” and “not for children under” set explicit limits. A child's cleverness does not override them. “Educational” on the front is not a warning check.
2. **Check every child who may reach it.** Compare the intended child's age with the stated range, then ask whether a younger child at home or at the gift location may handle it. If someone who may reach it falls below an explicit warning age, continue only if you can reliably keep the toy inaccessible to them; otherwise choose another toy.
3. **Check use conditions.** If the label calls for adult supervision, a particular setting, or protective gear, decide whether those conditions will actually be met. Do not approve the purchase on a plan to solve them later. Riding or outdoor products need their own safety guidance; this Skill does not teach their use.
4. **Keep missing facts missing.** A price sticker covering warnings, an online page without the back panel, or a different model or accessory means unconfirmed. Request information for the exact item or choose a better-documented toy. Do not invent a small-parts test to assign an age grade yourself.
5. **Record a narrow result.** Mark “label conditions fit the child and access plan / clear conflict / unconfirmed,” with the relevant wording. A fit to these label conditions does not prove the toy has no other risks.

### Success and stop

You can name the intended child and other children who may reach the toy, its key warnings, and why you will continue considering or reject it. Stop buying if the age condition conflicts or you cannot meet separation or supervision conditions. If a child has put an object in their mouth and has breathing trouble, stop this shopping workflow and seek help under local emergency guidance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Checking only the gift recipient's age:** Include younger children who live with or regularly visit them.
- **Treating the age range as a safety certificate:** Read every warning and condition. Passing the age check is only one check.
- **A price sticker hides a warning:** Request the full package or maker's exact-variant text. If unavailable, keep unconfirmed.
- **The gift choice changes:** Recheck the new item. Similar boxes do not share results.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Label languages and rules vary by market; US CPSC material supports only its US example. This assumes you know the children who may reach the toy and where it will be kept. If vision, language, or caregiving arrangements are a barrier, request accessible text, review it with a trusted caregiver, or select a gift with clearer conditions. Changing the gift does not reduce the thought behind it.
