## 中文

当两人或多人约在普通公共场所见面，却发现“正门”“楼下”或一枚地图点可能指向不同位置时加载。你的目标是让大家**事前同意一个可到达、可辨认的具体位置，并知道变更规则**。这不是实时跟踪朋友、催人赶路或保证每个人准时到场；各自找楼的过程另有流程。

### 准备与输入

确认场所全称、门牌/楼栋、当日可用的公开入口与时间，查场所正式地图、通知或实体标识。考虑同行者是否需要无台阶、室内/遮雨、可坐下、语言/感官可辨的标志，避免只凭“我觉得很明显”选点。只与相关参加者共享必要位置和联系方式，不索取任何人的持续实时定位。

### 执行

1. **挑一个站得住、找得到的点。** 选公共、允许停留、不会堵门/车流的具体位置；描述楼名、街道一侧、入口名称及固定标志（如正式信息台旁），而不是仅写“楼前”。同一机构有多扇门时，用场所官方入口说明确认是哪一扇；可及入口不同就选择所有参加者真实可到的点，或公开可达的共同替代点。
2. **把时间和等待界限说清。** 写明日期、当地时间及约定的等候/复核时刻；不要让“等一会儿”由每人分别解释。若有人需要更长到达时间，先商量，不把行动条件当迟到缺点。
3. **设一个可执行的变更规则。** 例如入口临时关闭、天气不适合或人群太密时，先在约定渠道报告眼前事实，双方确认后才改到事前选好的第二点；联系不上时按已约定的等候时点留在原安全位置或去已约定的备选处。没有事先共识就不把“我换到附近了”当成功通知。
4. **发一条完整消息并收回确认。** 模板：“__日__时，在__楼__街侧的__入口外/内（公开可站处）__标志旁见；入口关闭则__；到__时联系/复核；请回一句你理解的地点。” 让每名参加者回复“我去__”，发现门名/街侧理解不同就立刻修正。地图链接可辅助，但文字应在手机无网时仍能用。
5. **到场按约定核对。** 看现场门名和标志是否与消息一致；若关闭或不可达，按已确认规则发新信息并取得对方确认。不要为找人进入员工区、尾随门禁或穿越车流。没有回复时如实记“未确认”，不推断对方一定在另一个门。

### 完成与停止

每个需要参与的人已确认**同一入口、同一标志、同一日期时间和同一变更办法**，或有一人明确未确认而你暂不把约定当定案，本轮完成。真正会合要等人实际到场；消息送达不等于同意。场所关闭、位置不安全或可及条件无法满足时，先停在安全公共处重新协商，不让某人独自猜替代门。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只发一枚地图 pin：** 补上门牌、入口街侧和标志；定位点可能落在楼体中间。
- **两人说的都是“正门”，却在不同街：** 对照官方入口名称与各自看到的门牌，发一条统一修正消息并收确认。
- **入口临时关闭或无法通行：** 用事先约定的第二点或重新协商，未经回应不单方面移动。
- **手机没电/没网：** 依事先发出的文字说明和等候/备选规则行动；别靠不断换门赌能碰见。
- **同行者需要另一通行方式：** 把入口与路线条件摆出来协商实际可达点，不只给对方一个“你到后门来”的命令。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通、可退出的城市公共会面，不处理失踪求助、危机联络、限制区域或交通执法。场所开放、入口和临时变更以管理方当日信息为准。视觉、听觉、行动或语言条件不同，可选有文字、声音、触觉或工作人员可识别的共同标志，允许纸质说明或可信陪同；无需所有人安装同一 App。副作用是“门口见”要多写一行，但少走一次楼的周长。

## English

Load this Skill when two or more people plan to meet at an ordinary public venue but “front door,” “downstairs” or one map pin may mean different places. The result is **agreement in advance on one reachable, recognisable spot and a rule for changes**. It does not track anyone continuously, push them to hurry or guarantee arrival. Each person's route to the building is a separate task.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the venue's full name, street or building number, public entrance and hours for this day using its formal map, notice or signs. Ask what participants actually need: no steps, shelter, a seat, or a landmark they can identify by sight, sound, touch or language. Do not assume the obvious landmark is obvious to everyone. Share only necessary place and contact details with participants; continuous live location is not required.

### Execution

1. **Choose a usable, identifiable spot.** Pick a public place where waiting is permitted and doors or traffic stay clear. Give building name, street side, entrance name and a fixed landmark such as an official information desk, not just “in front.” For several doors, check the venue's official entrance information. If an accessible entrance differs, choose a point everyone can actually reach or a shared public alternative.
2. **State time and waiting boundary.** Include date, local time and an agreed wait or recheck point. “A while” should not be independently interpreted by every person. Discuss extra travel time someone needs instead of turning an access condition into blame.
3. **Set a workable change rule.** For a closed entrance, unsuitable weather or crowding, report what you see on the agreed channel and move to a preselected second point only after all relevant people confirm. If contact fails, follow the agreed safe waiting time or agreed fallback. “I moved nearby” is not a confirmed change.
4. **Send one complete message and get acknowledgments.** Template: “On __ at __, meet by __ landmark outside or inside the public __ entrance on the __ street side of __ building; if closed, __; check or call at __. Please reply with the spot you understood.” Have each person say where they will go and correct any different street or door. A map link may help, but the words should still work offline.
5. **Check on arrival.** Match the actual door name and landmark to the message. For a closure or unusable route, follow the agreed rule, send the update and receive confirmation. Do not search staff areas, tailgate access control or cross live traffic for someone. With no reply, record “not confirmed,” not “they must be at the other door.”

### Success and stop

Everyone who needs to attend has confirmed the **same entrance, landmark, date and time, and change rule**, or a person has explicitly not confirmed and you do not treat the plan as final. Physical meeting still requires people to arrive; delivery of a message is not consent. For a closed, unsafe or inaccessible spot, pause in a safe public place and agree on another point rather than leaving someone to guess a side door.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a map pin was sent:** Add street number, entrance side and landmark; a pin may fall in the centre of the building.
- **Both say “main entrance” from different streets:** Compare the venue's official door names and actual street numbers, then send one corrected message and get replies.
- **Entrance closes or becomes unusable:** Use the agreed second point or renegotiate. Do not move unilaterally before people receive it.
- **Phone has no power or data:** Use the earlier written description and waiting or fallback rule rather than circling every door.
- **A companion needs a different route:** Discuss entrance and route conditions to find a genuinely shared spot rather than ordering them to a back door.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for ordinary public meetings you can leave, not a missing-person response, crisis contact plan, restricted site or traffic-law instruction. Check opening and entrance changes against the venue's information for this day. For sight, hearing, mobility or language needs, choose a shared landmark identifiable through usable text, sound, touch or staff and allow paper directions or a trusted companion. No one needs to install the same app. “Meet at the door” takes one more line but less walking around the block.
