## 中文

当一次普通**水基水彩**练习结束、画笔还带颜料和水，你想在收起前把画笔洗净并晾干时，加载这份 Skill。结果是刷毛不再带可洗出的颜料、笔尖整好、笔身有安全晾干位置。这比 H280 的一般创作收工更具体，处理刷毛根部积色与潮湿收存；不适用于油画、溶剂漆或已硬化的陌生涂料。

### 准备与输入

辨认这是普通水彩笔且颜料按产品标签可用清水清洗；特殊笔毛或画笔生产商另有护理要求时优先遵守。准备清水、软布/纸、必要时温和皂和有空间晾笔的干净位置。水杯远离饮品，颜料水按当地普通处置要求处理；不用强溶剂或热水。

### 执行

1. **先卸下多余颜色。** 在软布或纸上轻轻擦去笔身多余颜料，不逆毛猛拉。看刷毛根部是否还有积色，别只洗尖端。
2. **清水温和冲洗。** 在清水中轻摆或用合适水流冲，从刷毛根部向笔尖让颜料离开；需要时按画笔说明用少量温和皂，再彻底冲净。不要把笔头长期泡在杯底压弯。
3. **轻整笔尖和去水。** 用软布轻压吸去水，不用力拧；按笔型用手指轻整刷毛。笔箍与柄若湿，也擦干；颜料色染留在毛上不一定等于还有可洗出的颜料。
4. **充分晾干再收。** 按画笔适用说明放在稳定通风位置，让刷毛、笔箍和柄干透后再竖放或装盒；避免湿笔长期密闭，别让笔尖顶住盒底。最后清理自己的水杯和台面。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

冲洗水基本不再带可洗出颜料、刷毛形状已整理、笔有不压尖的晾干位置；真正收进盒时还需干透。洗完仍在水杯里顶着杯底，不算完成。

- **刷毛根部仍出色：** 温和再洗根部并冲净；不为颜色染痕无限用皂。
- **笔尖散开或被杯底压弯：** 轻整毛束并改晾放方向；严重损坏别硬拧。
- **笔身一直潮湿：** 延长通风晾干，暂不密闭装盒。
- **标签说明不能水洗或不确定颜料：** 停止按本篇操作，查对应画笔/颜料说明。
- **握持或视觉不便：** 稳定宽口水杯、对比色布和可信协助可帮助核对洗出色与笔尖形状。

### 假设与边界

这里只处理普通水彩，具体天然/合成刷毛维护仍以产品说明为准。生产商会推荐少量温和皂，但过量或强洗反而伤毛；本篇不保证修好已断毛或已胶化的笔。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary **water-based watercolour** session ends and pigment and water remain in the brushes. Finish with removable pigment rinsed out, tips reshaped, and a safe drying place before storage. This is more specific than H280's general creative-session closure: it deals with pigment at the ferrule and storing wet bristles. It does not apply to oils, solvent paint, or unknown hardened coatings.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm that these are ordinary watercolour brushes and the paint label permits water cleaning. Follow any special brush-maker directions for that hair type. Have clean water, soft cloth or paper, optional mild soap, and a clean airy place to dry. Keep the rinse cup distinct from drinks and dispose of paint water under ordinary local rules. Do not use strong solvent or hot water.

### Execution

1. **Remove excess colour first.** Wipe gently on soft cloth or paper without pulling against the hair. Notice pigment near the bristle base instead of cleaning only the tip.
2. **Rinse gently with clean water.** Swirl lightly or use a suitable stream so pigment leaves the base toward the tip. If brush directions allow, use a little mild soap and rinse it fully. Do not leave the head pressed at the bottom of a cup for a long soak.
3. **Blot and reshape.** Press lightly with soft cloth to remove water, without twisting hard. Reform the tip gently for its brush type. Wipe a wet ferrule and handle too. Staining of hairs alone does not always mean removable pigment remains.
4. **Dry fully before storage.** Rest brushes in a stable aired position under their maker's directions until hair, ferrule, and handle are dry, then store upright or in a case without crushing tips. Avoid sealing wet brushes. Clean your own water cup and work surface.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Rinse water no longer carries significant removable pigment, bristles are shaped, and brushes can dry without tip pressure; they must be fully dry before boxing. A brush left bent against a cup bottom is not finished.

- **Base still releases colour:** Wash the base gently again and rinse; do not endlessly soap a harmless stain.
- **Tip splays or bends on the cup bottom:** Reshape gently and change drying position; do not twist broken hairs back by force.
- **Handle stays damp:** Allow more aired drying and delay closed storage.
- **Label says water cleaning is unsuitable or paint is uncertain:** Stop using this Skill and find the correct paint and brush directions.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** A stable broad cup, contrasting cloth, and trusted help can assist in checking released colour and tip shape.

### Assumptions and limits

This covers ordinary watercolour only. Natural and synthetic bristles may have different maker care. A little mild soap may help, while harsh washing harms hair. No claim is made to repair broken or glue-hardened brushes.
