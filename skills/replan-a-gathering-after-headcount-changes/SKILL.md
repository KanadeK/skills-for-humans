## 中文

当一次普通聚餐、游戏夜、出游或其他小型集体活动**已经约好**，却有人加入或退出时，加载这份 Skill。你的目标是让实际人数、容量、安排和参与者意愿重新一致；不是替任何人决定必须参加，也不是把新增者悄悄塞进原计划。

若变化来自天气或场地不可用，先处理场地问题；若大家对原约定理解不同，先核对共同事实。大型活动、工作排班、收费合同及紧急疏散不在这里处理。

### 准备与输入

写下一行：原计划的日期、地点和已确认人数；现在确认参加、确认退出、仍未回复的人各有多少。不要把“可能来”和“已经答应”相加。找出谁负责场地、预订、食物、交通或其他会随人数改变的项目，以及最晚需要给出最终人数的时间。

看实际限制：可用座位与无障碍位置、住宿或车辆的合法容量、餐食份数、活动材料、票额、预订人数和费用。场地、运营方或票务条款以你这次实际收到的规则为准；不知道时先问，别假设多一个人总能加椅子。

### 执行

1. **先算两个数。** 分开记“已确认参加”和“仍待答复”，并标出变化来自谁。若新增者还没有被邀请方或场地主人接受，他们暂不算已确认；若有人退出，不公开猜测其原因。
2. **找出第一个超限或浪费点。** 对照人数检查位置、出入方式、食物、材料、交通、票和预订。任何安全、无障碍或运营方限制被突破，就不要以挤一挤作为方案。有人退出时检查哪些订单还可改，哪些已发生的共同费用需要按原约定单独沟通。
3. **核对受影响者是否愿意。** 新人能否加入，先问负责邀请、场地或隐私边界的人；原参与者的活动性质会因新人而改变时，也要给他们选择。新增或退出的人若有实际时间、饮食、通行需求，只问会改变这次安排的条件，不要求私人原因或诊断。
4. **提出一个可执行版本。** 仍在容量内就更新人数和物资；超出时询问能否合法扩容，或改成分组、换地点、改期。没有可行且被相关人接受的版本，就取消这次安排。不要先下不可退的单，再等待大家被动接受。
5. **把选择和截止时间发给相关人。** 例如：“现在确认 7 人，原订 6 人位。我正在问店家能否改为 7 人；请在今晚 8 点前确认是否仍参加。若不能扩容，我会发替代地点或取消通知。”只共享必要安排，不转发退出者的私人解释。
6. **得到答复后落实一次。** 向实际场地或服务方确认新人数、条件和可能费用；调整份数、座位或交通；给相关人发最终日期、地点、人数与仍待解决的事项。若某项尚未确认，就写“待确认”，不要把查询当成预订成功。

### 完成条件

你有一个按**已确认人数**可实行、遵守实际容量并被必要参与者接受的计划；相关人收到相同的最终安排。若还缺回复、场地方确认或可用替代方案，你明确暂停扩容和下单，给出下一次核对时间或取消决定。所有人都开心不是你的完成条件。

### 常见报错与补救

- **临近开始才多了人：** 先确认主人和容量，再决定是否加入；在对方同意前不承诺座位、食物或进入私人空间。不能加入时给出诚实拒绝或下次安排。
- **有人退出而预订不能改：** 查这次真实条款，再按原本约定与承担费用的人沟通；不要公开施压让退出者回来，也不要编造退款权利。
- **人数够，但需求冲突：** 停止假定同一场地适合所有人，问受影响的人可接受的替代；做不到时分组、换场或取消。具体饮食和通行条件由实际参与者确认。
- **有人不回复：** 截止前只按已确认人数安排；截止后说明无法保留未确认位置。紧急要下不可退的单时，先把未确认者排除于这笔承诺之外。
- **群里出现两个版本：** 发一条标注时间的最终更新，列出已定、待定和谁会再确认。不要让旧截图继续担任日程表。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一场自愿、普通的小型活动，且你有权协调自己的安排；你没有代替场地、司机、主办者或每位参与者作决定的权力。朋友关系、隐私边界和“带人来”的习惯各不相同，直接问这次相关的人。改动可能增加费用或让某人失望；先说清，再决定是否接受。椅子不会因为群聊里多一个“+1”自动复制。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary meal, game night, trip, or other small group event was **already agreed**, and someone joins or drops out. Bring the real headcount, capacity, arrangements, and participants' willingness back into agreement. You cannot decide that someone must attend or quietly add a person to a plan others accepted.

If weather or an unavailable venue caused the change, resolve that first. If people understood the original arrangement differently, confirm the shared facts first. Large events, work rosters, paid contracts, and emergency evacuation are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Write one line with the original date, place, and confirmed headcount. Separately count people now confirmed to attend, confirmed to leave, and still unanswered. “Might come” is not “confirmed.” Identify who controls the place, booking, food, transport, or other items affected by the count, and the deadline for a final number.

Check real constraints: seats and accessible places, legal vehicle or accommodation capacity, meal portions, supplies, tickets, booking size, and cost. Use the actual venue, operator, or ticket terms for this event. If you do not know them, ask; an extra chair is not a universal policy.

### Execution

1. **Count two groups.** Record confirmed attendees separately from unanswered people and name the change. A new person is not confirmed until the host or person responsible for the invitation accepts them. Do not broadcast a reason for someone's withdrawal.
2. **Find the first limit or wasted commitment.** Compare the new count with access, seats, food, materials, transport, tickets, and reservations. Do not solve a safety, accessibility, or operator limit by squeezing people in. When someone leaves, check which orders can change and discuss any already incurred shared cost under the original agreement.
3. **Ask affected people whether the change works.** Check with whoever controls the invitation, space, or privacy boundary before adding someone. If an added person changes the kind of gathering others accepted, give those people a choice too. Ask joining or leaving people only about practical time, food, and access conditions that change this plan; do not demand private reasons or a diagnosis.
4. **Offer one workable version.** If the count still fits, update supplies and numbers. If it exceeds capacity, ask whether lawful expansion is available or offer a split group, another place, or a later date. If no feasible version is accepted by the necessary people, cancel this event. Do not place a nonrefundable order first and hope everyone complies afterward.
5. **Send the choice and a reply deadline.** For example: “Seven people are confirmed; our booking is for six. I am checking whether the restaurant can change it to seven. Please confirm by 8 tonight if you are still coming. If it cannot expand, I will send another place or cancel.” Share only the arrangement, not someone's private explanation.
6. **Apply one confirmed revision.** Confirm the new headcount, conditions, and possible charges with the actual provider; adjust portions, seats, or transport; then send the relevant people the final date, place, headcount, and unresolved items. Label an unanswered request “pending”; asking is not a successful booking.

### Success

You have a workable plan for the **confirmed attendees** within real capacity and accepted by the necessary participants. Everyone affected has the same final arrangement. If replies, operator confirmation, or a feasible alternative are missing, pause expansion and new orders, and name the next check or cancellation decision. Making everyone happy is not a completion requirement.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An extra person appears shortly before the event:** Confirm the host's agreement and capacity before promising a seat, food, or entry into private space. If they cannot join, decline honestly or offer a later occasion.
- **Someone drops out but the booking cannot change:** Read the real terms, then discuss the original cost agreement with whoever bears it. Do not pressure the person publicly to return or invent a refund right.
- **The number fits but needs conflict:** Stop assuming the same place works for everyone. Ask affected people what alternative they can accept; split, move, or cancel if needed. Confirm specific food and access needs with the actual participants.
- **Someone does not reply:** Plan only for confirmed people before the deadline. After it, say that an unconfirmed place cannot be held. Exclude unanswered people from an urgent nonrefundable commitment.
- **Two versions circulate in the group:** Send one time-stamped final update listing what is settled, what is pending, and who will confirm next. An old screenshot is a poor calendar.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a voluntary ordinary small gathering that you may help coordinate. You cannot decide for the venue, driver, host, organizer, or each attendee. Friendship, privacy, and customs around bringing someone vary; ask the people affected this time. A change may cost more or disappoint someone; state that before deciding whether to accept it. Chairs do not duplicate when “+1” appears in a group chat.
