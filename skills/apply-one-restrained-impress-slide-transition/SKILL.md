## 中文

你有两张内容衔接生硬的 Impress 页，想在一处加入短而不抢注意力的过渡时使用本篇。成果是过渡只作用于目标页，默认由讲者手动翻页，预览不会遮挡文字或带意外声音。这里不是给整份稿堆满不同特效，也不把动画当作内容质量。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 副本，记录目标页及前后页，说明过渡帮助区分哪一层信息。查本次是否有对动效敏感的观看条件；不确定时可保持无过渡。先确认选中的是目标页，避免 Apply to All Slides。

### 执行

1. 打开侧栏 Slide Transition，只给目标页选择一种短、简单、无声的效果。
2. 将 Advance Slide 设为手动，核没有误填 After 自动计时。
3. 用 Play 或放映预览目标前后页，观察文字不闪断、没有声音、讲者能自主前进。
4. 核旁边不应有过渡的页仍原样，保存重开；不合适就选 None。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

只有指定换页有克制过渡、手动前进，无意外声音与文字遮挡。

- **所有页都有特效：** 撤销 Apply to All，逐页恢复 None。
- **自动跳页：** 关闭 After 计时，保留手动。
- **观看不适：** 撤销效果，用 None。

### 假设与边界

只调整一处普通过渡，不保证不同设备渲染或适合所有观众。你负责动效可接受性；无过渡始终是有效选择。

## English

Use this Skill when one pair of adjacent Impress slides needs a short, unobtrusive transition. Finish with an effect only at intended point, manual presenter advance and no obscured words or surprise audio in preview. This is not a reason to scatter different effects across the deck or treat motion as content quality.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP copy, note target and neighbours and why transition helps. Consider viewers sensitive to motion; when uncertain, no effect is valid. Confirm only target selected and avoid Apply to All Slides.

### Execution

1. Open Sidebar Slide Transition and choose one short simple silent effect for target only.
2. Set Advance Slide to manual and check no unintended After timer.
3. Use Play or show preview across the pair; check text remains readable, no sound, and presenter controls advance.
4. Check unrelated neighbouring slides remain as before, save/reopen; choose None if unsuitable.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Only intended boundary has restrained transition, advances manually, and has no unexpected sound or obscured text.

- **All slides animate:** Undo Apply to All and restore others to None.
- **Auto-advances:** Disable After and keep manual.
- **Motion uncomfortable:** Remove effect and use None.

### Assumptions and limits

This adjusts one ordinary transition, not cross-device rendering or universal viewer comfort. You decide tolerable motion; no transition is always valid.
