## 中文

当你洗后发现一件衣服**原本的布色或印花变浅、出现淡斑**，用这份 Skill 判断是原色流失、表面洗剂残留还是另一件衣物的外来染色。它的结果是可信的下一步决定，不教用家庭化学品“补色”，也不把任何淡色都叫污渍。若衣物仍有危险污染，先停止普通洗衣判断。

### 准备与输入

找洗前照片、内侧或不显眼的原色区域，记录这次洗涤和烘干程序、温度/设置、使用的洗剂与去渍产品及其施用位置。读衣物标签的洗、漂白、干燥限制；光线一致地比较。产品倒在衣物局部、面料有印花/涂层或曾掉色的记录会影响判断。不要为了识别问题立刻重洗整件。

### 执行

1. **先区分“少了原色”与“多了东西”。** 淡斑与原布色不一致、没有表面粉末或触感残留时，原色流失更可能；白色粉末、滑腻残留或另一件的颜色落上来，分别回到漂洗/串色流程。只靠一张偏色灯下的照片不足以定论，不确定就记录并暂停新产品。
2. **固定可能原因的证据。** 记下变浅位置是否正对应预洗产品接触点、折痕、太阳直晒区或整件均匀变化；核对实际程序是否超过标签上限、产品是否标有不适用面料。不要把关联猜测写成已证实根因，也不自行推断化学反应。
3. **对确定的原色流失停止去污试验。** 洗剂与去渍产品是用来去污，不能自动把已失去的原染料填回去。别用更多漂白剂、酸或长时间浸泡“均匀色差”；停下、保留照片与标签，考虑接受外观变化、询问制造商/专业护理是否有适用处理，或根据用途不再穿用。
4. **只对确有残留的情况作对应处理。** 若证据显示是洗剂残留且衣物/机器允许，按准确机器和衣物说明选择合适漂洗；若是外来串色，走串色流程。结果复查前不烘热或熨烫受影响区域，不混用清洁品试错。
5. **记录后续限制。** 如果衣物继续掉色或标签与实际结果冲突，单独放置并查制造商说明或专业建议；未来分组时使用这次证据，不把一次失败变成全桶重复实验。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你能区分或明确尚无法区分原色流失、残留和外来串色，并有保留证据、适用漂洗、咨询或停止的实际去向。不会用更多去污剂假装“恢复原色”已发生。

- **淡斑正好是预处理位置：** 停止在此处继续用同一产品，保存用量与说明，向制造商/专业护理咨询。
- **白印擦一下会移动：** 先查洗剂残留/漂洗条件，不立即判断染料永久流失。
- **照片与眼前颜色不一致：** 用同一光源对比内侧未受影响处，仍不确定则停止化学操作。
- **整件变浅却还想继续高温洗：** 暂停，将实际温度、标签上限和产品说明逐项核对。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里是普通衣物洗后外观判断，不负责染色工艺、商品退换法律或专业织物护理。染料稳定性、光线、显示器和色觉不同；可用实物未受影响处、可读记录和可信现场协助。严重或贵重衣物不做盲目的第二次化学处理。可能需接受色差、另找专业护理或替换衣物。

## English

Use this Skill when a garment's **own fabric colour or print looks lighter or patchy after laundering**. Decide whether its original dye was lost, detergent remains on the surface, or another garment's dye arrived. The result is a credible next-step choice. This does not teach home chemical recolouring or call every pale patch a stain. Stop ordinary laundry triage for hazardous contamination.

### Preparation and inputs

Find a prewash photo or an inner or hidden area retaining the original colour. Record the wash and dry cycles, temperatures and settings, detergent and pretreatment products, and where each was applied. Read the garment's wash, bleach, and dry limits; compare under consistent light. A local application, print or coating, and past bleeding history change the interpretation. Do not immediately rewash the whole item merely to identify the problem.

### Execution

1. **Separate “original colour missing” from “something added.”** A pale patch inconsistent with the original cloth and without loose powder or residue may be lost dye. White powder or slippery residue or a second garment's hue points to the rinse or dye-transfer route instead. One colour-shifted photograph is insufficient; record and hold products if uncertain.
2. **Preserve evidence for possible causes.** Note whether the pale area tracks a pretreatment application, folds, direct sun exposure, or the whole garment. Check whether the actual cycle exceeded a label limit or the product excluded this fabric. A correlation is a clue, not proven chemical causation.
3. **Stop stain-removal trials for confirmed colour loss.** Detergent and stain remover remove soil; they do not automatically replace dye that has left the fabric. Do not add more bleach, acid, or long soaks to “even out” the colour. Keep photos and labels, accept the appearance change, ask the maker or professional cleaner about a suitable option, or retire the item from this use.
4. **Treat only actual residue by its own route.** If evidence supports detergent residue and garment and washer permit it, choose a suitable rinse from the exact machine and garment instructions. For incoming dye, use the dye-transfer route. Do not heat dry or iron the affected area before reassessing, and do not mix cleaners to experiment.
5. **Record later limits.** If the garment continues bleeding or its label conflicts with observed results, keep it separate and ask the maker or a professional. Use this evidence during future load sorting rather than repeating the failure with a whole batch.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can distinguish, or explicitly say you cannot yet distinguish, lost original dye, residue, and incoming dye. The garment has a real destination: preserved evidence, supported rinse, consultation, or stop. More stain remover has not been mistaken for colour restoration.

- **The pale patch matches pretreatment placement:** Stop using the same product there and keep the amount and directions for the maker or professional cleaner.
- **A white mark moves when wiped:** Check detergent residue and rinse limits before declaring permanent dye loss.
- **Photo and fabric colours differ:** Compare an unaffected inside area under the same light. Stop chemistry if still uncertain.
- **The whole item faded but you plan another hot wash:** Pause and compare actual cycle, care ceiling, and product directions.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an appearance decision for ordinary clothes, not textile dyeing, consumer-rights law, or professional fabric care. Dye stability, lighting, screens, and colour vision vary. Use a real unaffected area, readable records, or trusted in-person help. Do not blindly run a second chemical process on a valuable or badly changed item. You may accept fading, seek professional care, or replace the garment.
