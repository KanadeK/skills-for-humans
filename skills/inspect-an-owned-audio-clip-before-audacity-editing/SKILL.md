## 中文

你已有一段本人录制或获准的普通声音，准备在 Audacity 改它，却还不清楚开的是哪条、几秒、单/双声道时使用本篇。成果是只读输入记录：源文件、时长、声道、可闻的问题和本次一项编辑目标；音频本身未变。不要拿私密谈话或有版权的歌曲作练习。

### 准备与输入

确认录音许可、内容不敏感，记下原文件位置；导入不等于拥有对外发布权。用安静且音量适中的方式试听，先找头尾和一处典型问题。波形高度与耳朵听到的音量有关，但不能仅凭图形判是否真正破音。

### 执行

1. 使用 File > Import 将文件载入为音轨，核标题或文件名与原件对应。
2. 从时间线记下大致起止与总时长，观察轨道面板是一条单声道还是一对立体声。
3. 试听开头、中段、末尾，观察播放电平是否有削波指示，并描述一个可闻问题。
4. 记录本次只要修的一个结果，未保存任何改动；来源或权限不清时停止。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

输入信息与实际轨道一致，问题来自重听而非臆测，源文件仍未改变。

- **开错同名文件：** 核路径与声音并重导正确源。
- **图上尖峰却听不出：** 先记录不确定，不断言破音。
- **声道不确定：** 看轨道面板与播放表再核。

### 假设与边界

只读检查不能验证专业音质、听力安全或许可范围。你负责音源和目标；不作医疗声音分析。

## English

Use this Skill before editing a short owned or permitted ordinary recording in Audacity when its identity, length or mono/stereo status is unclear. Finish with a read-only intake note: source file, duration, channels, audible issue and one intended edit, while audio remains unchanged. Do not practise on private conversations or copyrighted songs.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission and non-sensitive content, noting original location; import grants no publication right. Listen privately at a comfortable level and identify start/end plus one issue. Waveform height can guide but cannot alone prove audible distortion.

### Execution

1. Use File > Import and check track/file identity against source.
2. Note approximate start/end and duration on timeline and mono versus stereo panel.
3. Listen to start, middle and end, watch playback clipping indicator and name one audible issue.
4. Record one bounded edit result without changing audio; stop if source or rights unclear.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Intake matches the actual track, issue comes from listening rather than guesswork, and source file is unchanged.

- **Wrong same-name file:** Check path/sound and reimport right source.
- **Spike but no audible fault:** Record uncertainty, not a clipping claim.
- **Channels unclear:** Inspect track panel and meters.

### Assumptions and limits

Read-only intake cannot certify professional quality, hearing safety or rights scope. You own source and goal; no medical audio analysis.
