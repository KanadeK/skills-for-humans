## 中文

当一双普通鞋的**外底沟槽卡着可见泥沙、小石或草屑**，你想清走这些外物并检查底面是否完好时，加载这份 Skill。结果是可见沟槽不再塞满、鞋面未被清洁剂沾污；它与洗鞋面不同。尖锐玻璃、针、疑似化学物或鞋底穿孔不徒手处理；磨平的外底也不能靠刷洗恢复抓地。

### 准备与输入

在稳定可清理的垫纸上把鞋底朝上，戴合适手套（如需），备软刷、钝木/塑料挑具和少量清水/标签允许的温和洗剂。确认外底材质与说明允许局部清洁；麂皮或特殊鞋面不能被水和洗剂溅到。发现可能伤人的尖物先停并用适当专业/当地处理方式，不用手指抠。

### 执行

1. **先看底面风险。** 转动鞋检查沟槽、胶合边和磨耗。能安全识别的普通砂土用软刷先去表面；若底已松脱、裂穿、严重磨平，记录停穿/交修，不试图靠清洁证明安全。
2. **逐槽清普通碎屑。** 用钝木/塑料工具轻拨可见小石与干泥，方向远离手和鞋面，随后软刷。别用金属刀刮穿橡胶或把嵌件往深处压。
3. **按底材少量湿擦。** 若外底说明允许，用微湿布/软刷和合用温和洗剂只擦外底，再用清洁微湿布去残留。水不能流到不宜湿的鞋面或鞋内；清不出的深嵌件停在这里。
4. **干后复核并收尾。** 外底自然干后看沟槽是否清楚、没有新割痕/松胶，清走垫纸碎屑并洗手。清洁不是防滑认证；若鞋底已损，先停用或求合适维修。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

安全可处理的普通碎屑已取走，外底干、鞋面没受洗剂影响，损伤得到如实判断。沟槽仍塞满或外底裂开却继续穿，不算完成。

- **发现玻璃或不明尖物：** 不徒手抠，停止常规流程并按适当安全方式处理。
- **工具刮出新痕：** 换更软钝工具或停，不能靠大力“清彻底”。
- **洗剂碰到麂皮/纺织鞋面：** 立刻停湿擦，按该鞋面的说明处理并晾干。
- **鞋底开胶/明显磨平：** 停穿或交专业维修，清洁不会修复结构或抓地。
- **视觉/手部不便：** 大灯光、稳定鞋架和可信协助可帮助定位，仍由你确认停用风险。

### 假设与边界

## English

Load this Skill when visible everyday **grit, small stones, or plant debris are lodged in an ordinary shoe outsole's grooves** and you want to clear them while checking the sole. Finish with grooves no longer clogged and upper untouched by cleaner. This differs from upper cleaning. Do not pick glass, needles, suspected chemical material, or a punctured sole bare-handed. Brushing cannot restore traction on a worn-flat outsole.

### Preparation and inputs

Turn shoes sole-up over a stable cleanable sheet. Use suitable gloves if needed, a soft brush, blunt wooden or plastic pick, and only a little water or label-compatible mild cleaner. Confirm local sole material allows spot cleaning. Keep water and cleaner off suede or other sensitive uppers. Stop for a potentially injurious sharp item and use an appropriate professional or local disposal route rather than your fingers.

### Execution

1. **Inspect before brushing.** Rotate each shoe to see grooves, glued edges, and wear. Brush safely recognised ordinary sand and soil. Record a loose, split, punctured, or badly worn outsole as a stop-wear or repair issue; cleaning cannot certify it safe.
2. **Clear ordinary debris groove by groove.** Use a blunt wood or plastic tool to ease visible pebbles and dry mud away from your hands and upper, then soft-brush. Do not scrape through rubber with a metal blade or force an embedded item deeper.
3. **Spot-wipe the sole if permitted.** Under outsole directions, use a barely damp cloth or soft brush with compatible mild cleaner only on the sole, then remove residue with a clean damp cloth. Keep liquid from uppers and inside. Stop at a deeply embedded item that will not move safely.
4. **Dry, recheck, and clear the area.** After natural drying, inspect visible grooves, new cuts, or loose bonding. Dispose of debris from the work sheet and wash hands. Cleaning is not slip certification; stop wearing or seek proper repair if the sole is damaged.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Safely removable ordinary debris is gone, the outsole is dry, the upper is unaffected, and structural damage is honestly identified. Grooves still clogged or a split sole worn anyway is not completion.

- **Glass or unknown sharp item appears:** Stop routine picking and use an appropriate safe handling route.
- **Tool cuts new marks:** Switch to softer blunt material or stop instead of increasing force.
- **Cleaner reaches suede or textile upper:** Stop wet work and follow that upper's care directions before drying.
- **Sole is separating or worn smooth:** Stop wear or use professional repair; cleaning cannot restore structure or grip.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Bright light, stable shoe holder, and trusted help can reveal grooves while you confirm stop-wear risk.

### Assumptions and limits

Clarks rubber-sole cleaning applies only to compatible ordinary outsoles. No slip, braking, or specialised sport-shoe performance is guaranteed. Upper care is a separate material-based task.
