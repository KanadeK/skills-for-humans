## 中文

当室友、家人或合住者要在同一时段用同一间厨房**各做各的餐**，且火口、台面或水槽可能相撞时，在开工前加载。你要得到双方同意的使用时段与“交给下一人时是什么状态”，不替对方选菜单，也不把先到厨房的人变成永久管理员。

### 准备与输入

每个人只报与共用资源有关的信息：希望开工和离开的时间、必须占用的灶口/台面/水槽、是否有需要持续在场照看的加热步骤，以及可否调整的部分。私人菜单、饮食原因和个人身体情况无需向所有人解释；如果确有接触风险，只说出要保持的明确边界。先看住处已有的使用与清洁约定。

### 执行

1. **画出冲突位置。** 把各人的使用窗口放在同一张纸或消息里，圈出真正同时抢用的火口、台面、洗涤位置和通道。没有冲突的动作不用排队等许可。
2. **选一条可执行的使用方式。** 可用不同时间段、不同工作区，或约定某一资源的先后；由相关使用者同意。不要替不在场的人决定让位，也不要把另一人的“快做完了”当时间保证。
3. **写清离开和交接状态。** 每个时段结束前，使用者确认自己的热源已有在场且同意接手的人负责；没人接手而自己必须离开时，先关闭灶具热源。再确认食材与器具移到约定位置、台面和水槽按共享规则清理，未完成事项已说清。生熟接触的清洁另遵守适用食品安全指导，不能靠“台面看起来空了”放行。
4. **处理没人认领的等待物。** 正在加热、易腐或来历不明的食物不能留给下一位猜；原使用者保留责任，先按适用规则处理或明确请愿意接手的人签收。没有安全等待方案就推迟该步骤或调整各自计划。
5. **约一个变更信号。** 若前一位超时、临时离开或设备故障，立即通知下一位重新约定，不越过对方的物品或擅自接管烹调。无法取得双方同意时，结果是“尚未排好，先不开工或改用可行时段”。

### 成功与停止

每位使用者都能说出自己可用的时间和区域、交接前要留下的状态、清理责任和变更通知方式；或者清楚知道冲突尚未解决。安排成功不要求所有人做同一顿饭，也不要求任何人接受未同意的工作。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两个时段仍在水槽重叠：** 单独给水槽安排先后和清空信号，不把整个厨房无谓地封给一人。
- **前一位留下脏板和不明食物：** 下一位先不接触或使用，要求原负责人按适用规则处理；不能确定食品状态时不替其决定可食用。
- **有人需要更久但没提前说：** 发出变更通知并重新同意时段；不靠沉默占用。
- **文字安排不可访问：** 用大字、语音、图示或当面复述，确保每个人都能读回自己同意的安排。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这份 Skill 假设共享厨房有基本清洁与安全使用规则，且相关使用者可以协商；宿舍、租房和家庭惯例可能不同。行动不便时将可达台面、站立时间和搬动责任纳入本人愿意公开的约束，不要求解释医疗原因。具体菜序和少设备重排仍各自另做。副作用是水槽不再依赖口头传说决定下一个使用者。

## English

Load this Skill **before starting** when housemates or family members will use one kitchen at overlapping times to make **separate meals**, and the hob, counter or sink may collide. Your result is an agreed access window and a clear handoff condition for the next person. You do not choose anyone else's menu or appoint the first person in the kitchen as permanent manager.

### Preparation and inputs

Each person shares only what affects common resources: desired start and finish times, needed hob/counter/sink, any heating step that needs continuous attention, and what can move. Nobody has to explain a private menu, diet reason or medical condition to everyone. If contact creates a risk, state the practical boundary needed. Check existing household access and cleaning rules first.

### Execution

1. **Locate the actual collision.** Put each person's time window on one paper or message and circle only overlapping use of heat, counter, washing point or passage. Work that does not collide needs no artificial queue.
2. **Choose a workable access plan.** Use different time blocks, separate work areas, or an agreed order for one shared resource. Get the relevant people's agreement. Do not assign an absent person to yield or turn “nearly finished” into a guaranteed time.
3. **Specify the exit and handoff state.** At the end of a window, the user confirms that an active heat source has a present, willing receiver; if nobody takes over and the user must leave, turn off the hob burner first. Then confirm ingredients and tools are in agreed places, counters and sink are cleaned under shared rules, and any unfinished state is told to the next person. Raw/cooked-contact cleaning still follows applicable food-safety guidance. An empty-looking counter alone is not clearance.
4. **Keep ownership of anything waiting.** Food still heating, perishable, or of unknown history is not left for the next person to guess about. Its original owner handles it under applicable rules or hands it to a willing named receiver. Without a safe waiting route, delay the step or change individual plans.
5. **Agree a change signal.** If the earlier user runs late, leaves suddenly or equipment fails, tell the next person and renegotiate. Do not move their belongings or take over their cooking silently. Without agreement, mark the schedule unresolved and defer or choose another feasible time.

### Success and stop

Each person can state their agreed time and area, the condition they will leave, their cleanup responsibility and how changes will be sent; or everyone knows the collision is still unresolved. Success does not require a shared meal or unconsented work.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Both windows still need the sink:** Give the sink its own order and empty-state signal instead of needlessly reserving the whole kitchen for one person.
- **The earlier user leaves a dirty board or unexplained food:** Do not use or touch it as if cleared. Ask the owner to handle it under applicable rules; do not declare the food safe on their behalf.
- **Someone needs longer than planned:** Send the change signal and agree a new slot. Silence is not an extension.
- **The written schedule is inaccessible:** Use large text, voice, a diagram or spoken readback so each person can repeat the arrangement they accepted.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes the kitchen has basic cleaning and safe-use rules and that the people involved can agree on access. Dorm, rental and household customs differ. For mobility needs, include reachable counter space, standing time and lifting responsibility among the constraints a person chooses to share; no medical explanation is required. Dish sequencing and limited-equipment meal redesign remain separate work. A side effect is that the sink's next user is named rather than passed down as folklore.
