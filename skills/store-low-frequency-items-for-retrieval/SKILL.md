## 中文

当一组只在特定季节或偶尔活动中使用的普通物品占着日常取用位置，而你又想下次顺利找回时，加载这份 Skill。你会让**一组已决定保留的东西**有可找回、可取出的储位和必要的完整性记录。这里不决定要不要丢弃、不处理衣物换季、食品保存、危险物、专属文书或搬迁运输；按物品说明需要特殊条件时先服从其说明。

### 准备与输入

写下这组物品下一次可能服务的真实用途，例如节庆布置或偶尔的野餐；没有确定日期也可以，只要能说出用途。确认它们属于谁、你是否能安排位置、哪些部件下次必须一起取用、目前是否完整且适合保存。准备现有的盒、袋或原包装与一个可记录位置的方法；不为收起一组东西先购买整面新柜子。

看不清标记、举不起盒子或够不到高处时，选择更容易接近的储位或请有权协助的人处理。放置前查看容器和物品自身关于清洁、干燥、材料或其他储存条件的说明；本篇不给不同材料编造同一个保存环境。

### 执行

1. **以未来取用动作定一组。** 把下次会一起拿的普通物品列在一起，而不只是按颜色、季节名称或同一购物来源分组。常用物继续留在日常位置；他人所有或是否保留未定的东西先转相应决定流程，不偷放进本组。
2. **核对是否“下次可用”。** 看看关键配件是否齐、现有损坏是否已知。缺件或状况不明要写出来，不把封盖当作修复。物品需清洁或按说明另行处理时，先完成或标待处理；不能安全存放就停下该项。
3. **用已有容器保持可辨认。** 原包装、稳固袋或现有盒子能防止小件分散即可；别把重物压在易损小物上。外部用一行“用途/大类 + 区分线索 + 待处理缺件”让你知道是否值得打开。若盒外信息需要更细设计，另作标签任务；不用抄全量清单到门外可见处。
4. **选可取出的储位。** 选你有权使用、下次能接近并搬出的稳定位置；不要以楼道、门口或通道代替储物空间。把较重的东西放在你能安全取放的高度，不为节约一格空间造一个摇晃的高叠层。实物位置写成你自己能理解的短记录，例如“储藏柜下层，右侧第二盒”，不公布私人储物图。
5. **做一次找回测试。** 合上容器，从日常出发点依位置记录找到它，确认外部线索能指向正确一组、关键配件或缺件状态可查。若必须把所有箱子搬下才够得到，改位置或拆成你能取的小组；若找不到，修正记录而不是靠下次记忆。
6. **取用后更新。** 下次拿出或添入物品时，更新缺件、外部线索和储位；借给别人则保留归属/归还记录。季节到了并不自动证明盒内仍完整，取用前按实际状态再看一次。

### 完成与停止

你能从位置记录找到这组普通物品，并能在不开遍所有盒子的情况下识别它、知道关键配件是否齐或哪项待处理；通道保持可用，取放不要求危险举高。没有适合的安全储位时，先缩小本轮组数或保留在可及的临时位置并记下一步，不硬塞、不谎称已长期收好。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只写“冬天用品”却有几个一模一样的盒：** 加用途或一项区分线索；外部仍无法决定开哪盒时先更新标签。
- **下次打开才发现少一个部件：** 当下记缺件和最后确认使用处，别把“存了一季”当作完整性证明。
- **最深处的盒子必须搬开重物才取到：** 改成可及储位或分成较小组；不要独自拆不稳的堆。
- **位置变了，记录没改：** 更新实际储位，旧记录先撤销，按新位置做一次找回测试。
- **中途被打断：** 留下已核对组、未处理物和尚未测试的储位；下次从未完成部分继续，不把待装盒状态说成已收存。

### 假设、替代与已知副作用

季节、住处、储物量、行动能力、视力及与他人的共用规则不同。用你自己的用途和能到达的储位安排；纸笔、大字、触觉或已有的可访问记录都可指路，不必购买 App。衣物、食物、设备或材料若有专门保存要求，转用其标签和适用说明。副作用是日常位置更清爽，但这组物品多了一个需要随内容改变而更新的“地址”。

## English

Load this Skill when a group of ordinary items used only for a season or occasional activity occupies everyday space and you want to find it easily next time. Give **one group you have chosen to keep** a retrievable, reachable place and a short completeness record. This does not decide what to discard, handle seasonal clothing, preserve food, store hazardous items or special papers, or move house. Follow item-specific storage instructions when they apply.

### Preparation and inputs

Name the group's next real kind of use, such as festive decorating or an occasional picnic. You need not know the exact date, but you should know the job. Confirm ownership and permission to place it, which parts must come out together next time, and whether the group is currently complete and fit for storage. Use an existing box, bag, or original pack and a short location record; do not buy a wall of shelving as a dependency for one group.

If a label is hard to read, a box too heavy to lift, or a shelf out of reach, choose a more accessible place or ask someone entitled to help. Check the item's and container's instructions for cleaning, dryness, material, or other storage needs before putting it away. This Skill does not invent one environment for all materials.

### Execution

1. **Group by the future retrieval action.** Put ordinary items you will take out together in one group, not merely those of the same colour, season name, or shop. Keep everyday items in their everyday places. Send other-owned or undecided items to the appropriate decision instead of quietly adding them here.
2. **Check whether it will be usable next time.** Identify missing key parts and known damage. Record a gap or unknown condition; closing a lid does not repair it. Clean or handle an item as its instructions require, or leave it marked to do. Stop on an item that cannot be stored safely yet.
3. **Keep the group identifiable with what you have.** An original pack, stable bag, or existing box is enough if it keeps small parts together. Do not crush fragile items under heavy ones. One outside line of “use/category + distinguishing clue + missing-part note” should tell you whether the box is worth opening. Detailed outside labelling is a separate task; do not publish a full private inventory on the door.
4. **Choose a place you can retrieve from.** Use a stable place you may access and from which you can later remove the group. A shared corridor, doorway, or passage is not a storage area. Put heavier things where you can safely lift them rather than building an unstable high stack to gain one shelf. Record the real location in words you understand, such as “lower storage shelf, second box on right,” without publishing a map of private storage.
5. **Test one retrieval.** Close the container, start from your everyday route, and follow the location note to this group. Check that the outside cue points to the correct group and that key parts or a missing-part note can be found. If you must move every box to reach it, change the place or divide the load. If you cannot find it, correct the record rather than trusting future memory.
6. **Update after use.** When you take something out or add to the group, update missing parts, outside cue, and location. If you lend it, keep a separate owner/return record. The arrival of a season does not prove the contents are still complete; inspect their actual condition before use.

### Success and stop conditions

You can follow the location record to the ordinary group, identify it without opening every box, and tell whether key parts are present or still need attention. The route remains usable and retrieval does not require hazardous lifting. If no safe place exists, shrink this batch or use an accessible temporary place with a next action. Do not force items in or claim long-term storage is complete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Several boxes all say “winter things”:** Add a use or distinguishing clue. If you still cannot tell which to open, improve the outside cue.
- **A part is missing when you open it next time:** Record the gap and the last confirmed use place. A season in storage is not a completeness certificate.
- **The deepest box requires moving heavy ones:** Choose a reachable place or smaller groups; do not dismantle an unstable pile alone.
- **The location changes but the record does not:** Update the real place, withdraw the old note, and test retrieval once from the new one.
- **You are interrupted:** Keep the checked group, unprocessed items, and untested place separate. Resume those unfinished parts rather than reporting a waiting box as stored.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Seasons, homes, storage volume, movement, sight, and sharing rules vary. Use your actual next use and reachable space. Paper, large print, touch, or an accessible record you already have can point to the place; no app purchase is required. Clothing, food, equipment, or materials with special storage requirements follow their own labels and applicable instructions. Everyday space may open up while this group gains an “address” that must be updated when it moves.
