## 中文

当你**仍在会议里**，但声音、画面或共享因连接不稳而断续，且要继续参与普通讨论时，加载这份 Skill。目标是让别人知道你能通过哪条通道接收和回应，并验证它真的可用；不诊断网络设备，不保证恢复高清画面。完全退出后重新加入并补遗漏信息归本域另一份 Skill。

### 准备与输入

先确认你还在会议中，以及主持人允许文字、仅语音、电话拨入或改期中的哪些方式。查看会议实际提示和同伴反馈，不只凭自己画面“还在动”判断连接正常。电话拨入只有邀请中有号码、当地可用且你接受可能的通话费用时才考虑；有的会议/组织没有这个选项。不要在公开聊天里贴私人号码或会议凭据。

### 执行

1. **告诉别人故障影响。** 若文字还能发，简短发“我这边连接断续；我先关画面试语音，关键决定请在文字里确认”。若语音还能用，则口头说清。发不出去时用事先约定的独立联系渠道通知主持人，不把未送达写成已通知。
2. **先减掉非必要画面。** 在会议里关自己的摄像头，并停止不必要的共享。等待片刻，请一位参与者确认是否还能双向听到或看到你的文字；只看自己网络图标不能证明对方收到。
3. **选择一条实际可用的通道。** 语音清楚就保留语音；语音断但文字送达就改用会议文字；若邀请和权限允许，才考虑电话音频或会议认可的另一设备。切换到电话时核对号码来自会议邀请，并把电脑的一路音频静音，避免双路回声。不要为切换而同时占用多个不稳定通道。
4. **确认你的参与能力。** 用一句简短回应测试“我能听/读到，也能回答”；让对方复述或确认收到。若只能单向接收，明确说“我现在只能看/听，不能可靠回应”，并请主持人决定是否暂停需要你参与的环节。
5. **设停止条件。** 两次有针对性的切换后仍不能可靠双向参与，就通知主持人你要退出/改期或请其发一份许可的简短纪要。不要在重要决定上用断续的半句话冒充同意。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你与会议至少一人实际完成一次双向确认，并明确当前可用通道；或你如实通知无法可靠参加并安排下一步。画面好看不是成功条件。

- **你听到了，但发言常被切断：** 转可送达的文字并标明要回应的议题；未得到确认前不算已表达意见。
- **文字发出后无人回应：** 检查发送状态并用已约定渠道联系主持人；“消息在输入框”不等于送达。
- **电话和电脑同时有声音：** 只保留一路会议音频，避免回声；电话费用和组织政策不明就不用电话。
- **共享或摄像头还在占带宽：** 关闭不必要画面后只重测一次；共享内容错误另见 H233，不在这里盲目切窗口。
- **听力、视觉或语言条件使剩余通道不可用：** 明确告诉主持人需要的文字、字幕、口述或延期方式；不要把形式上在线当有效参与。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

应用、组织、国家和设备决定可用的文字、电话和无障碍选项。按当前会议提供的界面与邀请操作，不假设电话一定免费或能拨入。讨论可能继续而你只收到一部分；对关键决定须在稳定渠道再次确认。

## English

Load this Skill while you are **still in the meeting** but audio, video, or sharing keeps breaking up and you need to participate in an ordinary discussion. The goal is to tell others which channel can carry your responses and verify it works. It does not diagnose network equipment or promise HD video. Rejoining after a complete drop and recovering missed context is a separate task.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm that you remain in the meeting and which options the host permits: chat, voice only, phone dial-in, or rescheduling. Use meeting notices and participant feedback rather than your moving local picture alone. Consider dial-in only if the invitation provides a number, it works in your region, and you accept possible call charges; some meetings and organisations offer no such option. Do not post a private phone number or meeting credentials in a public chat.

### Execution

1. **Tell others what is failing.** If chat still works, write, “My connection is breaking up. I'll turn video off and try audio; please confirm key decisions in text.” If voice works, say it. If neither reaches them, use a previously agreed independent route to contact the host. An unsent notice is not a notice.
2. **Remove nonessential video first.** Turn your camera off and stop sharing anything unnecessary. Wait briefly and ask one participant whether two-way voice or chat now works. Your own network icon cannot prove delivery to them.
3. **Choose one working channel.** Keep voice if clear; use meeting chat if voice fails but text arrives. Use phone audio or another meeting-approved device only when the invitation and permissions support it. Check a dial-in number against the invitation and mute one of the computer/phone audio paths to avoid echo. Do not keep several unstable channels active just to feel connected.
4. **Prove that you can participate.** Exchange one short “I can hear/read and answer” message and have someone confirm receipt. If you only receive but cannot respond reliably, say so and ask the host whether to pause any decision that needs you.
5. **Set a stopping point.** After two targeted channel changes without reliable two-way participation, tell the host you need to leave, reschedule, or receive a permitted short record. Do not let a broken half sentence stand for consent to an important decision.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You completed one actual two-way check with another participant and named the current channel, or clearly notified the meeting that you cannot participate reliably and arranged a next step. Video quality is not the success condition.

- **You can hear but speech keeps cutting out:** Move to deliverable chat and identify the issue you are answering. Your view is not conveyed until receipt is confirmed.
- **No one replies to your chat:** Check sending status and reach the host through an agreed channel. Text sitting in an input field has not arrived.
- **Phone and computer both make sound:** Keep only one meeting audio path to avoid echo. If charges or organisation rules are unclear, do not use the phone option.
- **Video or sharing still consumes the connection:** Stop nonessential video and retest once. Wrong shared content belongs to H233; do not blindly switch windows here.
- **The remaining channel is inaccessible for hearing, vision, or language needs:** Tell the host what text, captions, spoken description, or postponement you need. Being nominally online is not effective participation.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

App, organisation, country, and device determine chat, phone, and access features. Follow controls and invitations in the current meeting, without assuming dial-in is free or available. Discussion may continue while you receive only part of it; confirm key decisions again through a stable channel.
