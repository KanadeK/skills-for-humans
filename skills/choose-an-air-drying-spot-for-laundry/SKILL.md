## 中文

当你洗完一批普通衣物，知道它们**要自然晾干**，却还没有决定在哪里晾时，用这份 Skill 找到符合标签、能容纳湿衣且不会把水滴到危险地方的位置。本篇只确定位置和支撑资源；每件衣服是平铺还是悬挂，另按标签安排。若衣物其实只能专业护理或标签读不清，先停止推测。

### 准备与输入

读每件衣物的晾干符号/文字：平铺、悬挂、滴干、阴干及禁止的方式；符号体系不明就查对应来源。估算湿衣件数、重量、滴水可能性、晾衣架承重和需要占用的面积。检查室内是否已有冷凝水或潮湿、室外是否会淋雨/暴晒、家中通道和出入口是否必须保持通畅。准备可稳放的架/平面及接水方式。

### 执行

1. **先把标签要求变成场地需求。** 有“阴干”就找避开直射日光的位置；有“平铺”就找够长、能承重且可通风的水平支撑；滴干则预留滴水去向。一个只有横杆的地点不能接收必须平铺的衣物。
2. **确认支架和路线。** 空架先放稳，不挡门、走道、楼梯或他人必经位置；湿重后不会倾倒，衣物也不会碰到脏地面。接住或引走滴水，避开插座、电线、地毯和易滑通道。架子不稳、晾处必须占用出口时停止，换位置或分批。
3. **让空气有通路。** 衣物之间保留实际间隙，不把大件叠成湿墙；室内已有窗面冷凝或持续潮湿时，减少同处湿衣量，使用安全可用的通风/除湿方式或另找地点。户外若不符合衣物的阴干要求或天气会再次打湿，改用合适室内位置，不把阳光当所有衣物的加速按钮。
4. **把位置分给这批衣物。** 先放需要平铺的面积，再分配可悬挂的位置与滴水区域；容纳不了就分批晾并按洗后湿衣管理流程处理等待的部分，不能把新洗湿衣无期限留在密闭机桶。把架和湿衣放到你能安全够到的位置，不攀爬家具挂高处。
5. **确认可开始晾。** 再看标签、架的稳定性、气流、滴水和通道。满足后交给每件衣物的实际支撑方式；若缺合适场地，暂停这批晾晒并重新安排设备或可信协助。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

每件湿衣都有与标签相容的位置容量，架子稳定，空气可流动，水不会让地面或电器变成风险；没有位置的衣物有明确分批/等待方案。计划完成不等于衣物已干。

- **架上挂满后相互贴住：** 减少数量或增加合适支撑，不靠延长不明天数弥补没有空气。
- **窗边开始凝水：** 检查室内湿度与通风，减少湿衣负荷或换地点，并擦干已出现的水。
- **水滴到行走区：** 先移开衣物和架、清理地面，重新设置接水和安全通道。
- **只有衣架，没有平铺处：** 不把平铺标签改读为悬挂；分批、借合适架面或查制造商/专业护理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇针对普通衣物和安全可用的住家/共享晾衣空间；地区天气、房屋湿度、公共晾晒规则与衣物标签不同。视觉或行动不便时，在可及高度摆稳轻量架，请可信的人搬较重湿衣；读不清符号时放大并用对应符号指南。位置可能暂时占用家庭空间；如不允许占用公共区域，先按现场规则改位。

## English

Use this Skill after washing ordinary clothes that **need air drying** when you have not yet chosen where they will dry. Find a place compatible with the labels, large enough for wet items, and clear of unsafe runoff. This chooses location and support capacity. Arrange each garment flat or hanging under its own label afterward. Stop guessing if an item needs professional care or its label cannot be read.

### Preparation and inputs

Read every garment's natural-drying symbols or words: flat, line, drip, shade, and prohibitions. Use the matching symbol source if the system is unclear. Estimate the number and wet weight of items, likely dripping, rack load, and area needed. Check for indoor condensation or persistent damp, outdoor rain or direct sun, and corridors or exits that must stay clear. Have a stable rack or surface and a route for water.

### Execution

1. **Turn labels into space requirements.** “Dry in shade” needs a place out of direct sun. “Dry flat” needs enough level supported area with airflow. Drip drying needs a safe water destination. A site with only one rail cannot receive a flat-dry item.
2. **Check rack and route.** Place the empty rack securely, away from doors, stairs, and necessary paths. It must not tip under wet weight or drop clothing onto a dirty floor. Catch or direct drips away from sockets, cords, carpets, and slippery walking areas. Stop and choose another location or smaller batch if the rack is unstable or the site blocks an exit.
3. **Leave an air path.** Space items rather than making a wall of damp cloth. If a window is already condensing or the room stays damp, reduce the load, use safe available ventilation or moisture removal, or change location. If outdoors violates a shade label or weather will wet the load again, use a suitable indoor place. Sun is not a universal garment accelerator.
4. **Allocate the real space.** Reserve flat area first, then hanging and drip zones. If not everything fits, use batches and an appropriate wet-laundry holding plan rather than leaving fresh wet clothes in a closed washer indefinitely. Place the rack at a height you can reach safely; do not climb furniture to hang items.
5. **Confirm it is ready.** Recheck labels, stability, airflow, runoff, and clear paths. Then pass each item to its own support method. If no suitable site exists, pause this drying setup and arrange equipment or trusted help.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Every wet item has label-compatible location capacity, the rack is stable, air can move, and water does not create a floor or electrical hazard. Items without space have a specific batch or holding plan. Planning a place does not mean clothes are dry.

- **Items touch all along the rack:** Reduce the load or add suitable support. A vague longer wait does not create airflow.
- **Condensation appears near the window:** Recheck room moisture and ventilation, reduce the wet load or move it, and dry the collected water.
- **Drips reach a walking area:** Move the load and rack, clean the floor, and reset runoff and the safe route.
- **Only hangers exist for a flat-dry item:** Do not reinterpret the label. Batch it, obtain a suitable flat surface, or ask the maker or professional cleaner.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary clothes and safe available home or shared drying space. Weather, indoor humidity, common-area rules, and labels vary. Place a light rack at reachable height and ask a trusted person to lift heavier wet items if vision or movement is limited. Enlarge a label or use its matching symbol guide. The space is temporarily occupied; follow local shared-space rules before using a common area.
