## 中文

当你有几枝**获准、已知且适合普通风干的观赏花**，想保留立体花形时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一小束摸起来干透、没有发霉的花材；这与把花压成平面的书页标本不同。不要把身份不明或可能有毒、刺激性花材做成可接触装饰，也不把风干花当食品或药材。

### 准备与输入

查该花种是否适合悬挂风干；有些花会塌、掉瓣或变色，不能许诺保留原样。挑尚未枯坏、表面干燥的少量花枝，准备软扎线或会随茎缩小收紧的合用圈。找不被生活动线碰到、阴暗、干燥、通风的挂点；墙面和固定方式须获准且能承重一小束，不钻孔、不接电热设备。

### 执行

1. **筛选并去多余叶。** 去掉明显损坏、潮湿或已有霉点的花枝，保留可展示的花头，按材料适用情况去茎下部叶。若来源、花种或接触风险无法确认，停止这束。
2. **小束松散捆扎。** 把少数枝的茎聚在一起，用软带或橡皮圈固定，不能把花头挤成潮湿团。随着茎失水缩小，普通松绳可能滑落；结或束带要能稍后复查调整。
3. **倒挂并留气流。** 花头朝下挂在安全挂点，束与束之间留空，避开强日晒和潮湿浴室。标记开始日期；不要用吹风机、烤箱或不明干燥剂赶时间。
4. **分次检查到干。** 之后查看是否掉落、结松、霉味或白/灰霉点；发现霉变就停止保存、隔离丢弃并清理挂点。花材摸起来整体干燥、茎较硬、无潮湿或霉迹时轻取下，放在不会被挤压或误食处。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

花束保持立体形态且整体干透、无霉，安全取下；单是挂上去或表面摸着干不等于完成。等待时间受花种和空气湿度影响，不能把某个天数当通用合格证。

- **束内发潮或有霉点：** 停止该束，安全丢弃；下次选更小束与更通风干燥处。
- **扎线随茎缩小滑落：** 在可安全接触时重新扎稳，不爬高追逐掉花。
- **花头碎裂或大幅塌陷：** 承认这种花不适合此法，改用另一个可靠的保存方式或结束；不胶封湿花。
- **挂点挡路或有人会触碰：** 换获准的安全位置，不把束放到儿童/宠物可碰或食物上方。
- **手部/视觉不便：** 选低位安全挂点、粗扎带、可靠照明和可信协助检查；仍由你判断霉与干燥状态。

### 假设与边界

大学推广资料给出的小束、倒挂、阴暗通风是普通空气干燥路径，不是所有物种的最佳方法。干花脆、会积尘，也可能对宠物不合适；未知者不入口、不摆在可误食位置。

## English

Load this Skill when you have a few **permitted, known ornamental stems suited to ordinary air drying** and want to retain three-dimensional flower shape. Finish with a fully dry, mold-free small bunch. This differs from flattening a flower in a book. Do not turn unidentified, potentially toxic, or irritating material into touchable decor, and never treat these dried flowers as food or medicine.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether the species is suitable for hanging; some collapse, shed petals, or change colour. Do not promise the fresh look. Choose a few sound, surface-dry stems and a soft tie or suitable elastic that can hold shrinking stems. Find a permitted dark, dry, airy hanging point outside ordinary traffic. Its fixing must support a small bunch; do not drill holes or use electric heat for this task.

### Execution

1. **Select and remove excess foliage.** Reject damaged, damp, or already moldy stems. Retain usable blooms and remove lower leaves as the material allows. Stop if source, species, or contact risk cannot be confirmed.
2. **Tie a small open bunch.** Gather a few lower stems with a soft tie or elastic without packing flower heads into a damp ball. As stems shrink, loose string may slip; make the tie inspectable and adjustable.
3. **Hang upside down with airflow.** Use a safe hanger with blooms downward and space between bunches, out of strong sun and damp rooms. Note the start date. Do not rush with a hair dryer, oven, or unknown desiccant.
4. **Inspect until dry.** Over subsequent days check for slipping, loose ties, musty smell, or grey/white mold. If mold develops, stop preserving, discard the bunch safely, and clean the hanging area. Once material feels dry throughout, stems are firmer, and no damp or mold remains, remove gently and store away from crushing or accidental eating.

### Success, problems, and recovery

The bunch retains a three-dimensional form, is dry throughout, mold-free, and safely removed. Hanging it or feeling only a dry outer petal does not finish the task. Species and humidity change the waiting period, so a calendar day alone cannot certify dryness.

- **Inside of bunch is damp or mold appears:** Stop and discard safely; next time choose fewer stems and a drier, airier spot.
- **Tie slips as stems shrink:** Retie only from a safe position; do not climb after falling flowers.
- **Bloom crumbles or collapses:** Accept that this species may not suit the method; choose another evidenced preservation approach or end, without sealing wet material in glue.
- **Hanging point blocks people or invites touching:** Move to another permitted safe spot, away from children, pets, and food.
- **Grip or vision is limited:** Use a low safe point, broad tie, good light, and trusted help for inspection while you decide whether it is dry and mold-free.

### Assumptions and limits

University extension advice supports small bunches hanging in a dark, dry, airy place as an ordinary method, not the best method for every species. Dried flowers are brittle, gather dust, and may be unsuitable around pets. Unknown material is never eaten or placed where it may be eaten.
