## 中文

你已输入一份原创短谱，准备把它交给别人看之前，做一次有记录的听看对照。成果不是“试听好像不错”，而是把谱面每小节与本人草案核一遍，用播放发现可能的节奏、音区和反复错误，并将问题定位到小节与拍位。合成音源不能证明作品可由真人演奏，也不能替你检查版权来源。

### 准备与输入

保存本机 .mscz，准备原始音高、时值、歌词与标记草案。确认输出设备能听到声音、播放反复设置符合本次检查目的；如果多乐器，先决定从总谱还是目标分谱看，避免漏掉某声部。

### 执行

1. 从第一小节逐行读到末尾，按拍数核音高、休止、弱起、延音和反复边界，记录不一致的小节号。
2. 从这些小节前一点开始短播，听音区跳跃、停顿和重复顺序，核发现的问题是输入错误还是原计划改变。
3. 对每个确认的错处写“第几小节第几拍、现值、目标值”，逐项修后只重播相关片段。
4. 再从头至尾读一次，不把本机试听称作乐手验收；保存并记录未核实的演奏问题。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

每个小节都与本人计划核过，确认的错处有准确位置及修订记录，未知演奏问题另列。

- **听不到声音：** 检查本机输出与播放状态，视觉核谱继续进行。
- **总谱太密：** 分声部看并保持同一小节编号。
- **反复没播放：** 核 Play repeats 设置，再测路径。

### 假设与边界

这是一轮作者自核，不等于音乐老师、演奏者、版权方或正式制谱审校的验收。

## English

Use this after entering an original short score and before anyone else relies on it. Compare each bar visually with your own plan and use playback to expose likely rhythm, octave or repeat errors. Finish with defects located by bar and beat, not a vague 'sounds fine.' Synthetic sound cannot prove live playability or rights provenance.

### Preparation and inputs

Save the local .mscz and bring the original pitch, duration, lyric and marking plan. Confirm audible output and repeat playback settings match this audit. For multiple instruments, decide whether to inspect the full score or a target part so no line is missed.

### Execution

1. Read from first to last bar, checking beats, pitches, rests, pickup, ties and repeat boundaries and noting each mismatch by bar.
2. Play just before suspect bars, hearing octave jumps, pauses and repeat order, then decide whether the score or creative plan changed.
3. For each confirmed defect write bar, beat, current value and intended value; fix one at a time and replay only the affected passage.
4. Read the whole score once more, save and record any live-performance question that remains unverified.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Every bar has been compared with the author's plan; confirmed issues have exact locations and fixes, with live-performance uncertainties listed separately.

- **No audio:** Check output and playback status while continuing the visual audit.
- **Too dense:** Inspect one part at a time while retaining shared bar references.
- **Repeat skipped:** Inspect Play repeats before judging the score path.

### Assumptions and limits

This is the author's own check, not approval from a teacher, player, rights holder or professional engraver.
