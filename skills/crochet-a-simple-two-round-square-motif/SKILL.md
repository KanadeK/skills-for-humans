## 中文

当你已能控制锁针、引拔针与**美式长针（US double crochet）**，想从中心向外钩一片小四角花片时，加载这份 Skill。结果是两圈、四角可指认、能独立拿起的原创普通方片；它与 E013 平排短针长方形在起点、方向和检查点不同。美英术语名称会冲突，本文只用美式；不复制具体品牌图样的配色、尺寸或成品组合。

### 准备与输入

备合用纱线/钝钩针、好光线、一个可标记第一角的小记号。熟悉美式长针：绕线，钩入指定圈洞，拉出线至钩上三圈，绕线拉过两圈、再绕线拉过两圈。引拔针是穿入目标后绕线一次穿过目标和钩上原圈。用少量线试这些动作；连单一长针都无法成形时先练基础，不凭口诀赶圆周。无缝立体物、儿童穿戴物不在本篇。

### 执行

1. **起中心圈并做第一角。** 钩少量锁针（如四针）后在第一针引拔成小圈；钩三锁针当作本圈第一枚美式长针，在圈洞再钩两枚长针，形成第一组三针，再钩两锁针作为第一个角洞。
2. **完成第一圈四角。** 依次在中心圈里再做三组三枚长针，每组后钩两锁针留角；最后引拔到起始三锁针顶端。检查正好四组、四个可进入的角洞，没有一组无端多出来。
3. **从一个角开始第二圈。** 沿邻近针用引拔针移到一个角洞。角洞内钩三锁针算第一长针，再两长针、两锁针、三长针；每到下一个角洞重复“三长针、两锁针、三长针”，相邻角组之间用一锁针留空。做完四角并引拔闭合，方向保持一致。
4. **检查并暂定收口。** 四角能平放辨认、各边有明显三针组、中心与第二圈相连；过紧翘起或多出第五角时先停并退到错误处。若此片到此为止，按合用方法留足尾线拉过末圈，后续再藏线；不把长线尾藏在照片背后假装已完工。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一片可拿起的两圈四角小花片，圈首尾相接，没有松开的活圈；待藏尾状态如实标明。只有一个中心圆环或三个角，不算方片。

- **第一圈角数不是四：** 在钩第二圈前退回重数三针组与角洞。
- **长针钩法与英式术语混淆：** 停并按美式长针动作核对，不能混一行两种系统。
- **边卷曲或第五角出现：** 检查角洞是否重复钩、组数是否多；退回错处重做，不靠拉扯整形。
- **中心圈太松或脱开：** 先固定活线、重做小圈并核引拔点。
- **手眼协调不便：** 粗浅色线、四角标记、放大光与可信协助指认，由你确认圈数角数。

### 假设与边界

四角三针组是普通公共基础结构，不承诺任何品牌图样尺寸或可直接拼成成衣。针数和张力因纱、钩与手劲变化；本文只教小型个人样片。

## English

Load this Skill when you can already control chains, slip stitches, and **US double crochet** and want a small motif worked out from a centre ring. Finish with an original two-round four-corner square that can be lifted. This differs from E013's flat single-crochet rectangle in starting point, direction, and checks. US and UK stitch names conflict; this uses US terms only and copies no brand project colours, dimensions, or finished arrangement.

### Preparation and inputs

Have suitable labelled yarn, blunt hook, good light, and a small marker for the first corner. Know a US double crochet: yarn over, insert into target space, pull up yarn to three hook loops, yarn over through two, then yarn over through the last two. A slip stitch enters the target and pulls the new yarn through both target and original hook loop. Trial these on spare yarn. If a single double crochet is not working, practise that foundation first rather than racing through round instructions. Wearable or load-bearing projects are outside this sample.

### Execution

1. **Make a centre ring and first corner.** Chain a few (for example four) and slip stitch into the first to close a small ring. Chain three as this round's first double crochet, work two more double crochets into the centre to form the first group of three, then chain two for a corner space.
2. **Complete four first-round corners.** Make three more groups of three double crochets into the centre, each followed by two chains for a corner. Slip stitch into the top of the starting chain-three. Check exactly four groups and four accessible corner spaces, not a fifth added by accident.
3. **Start round two at a corner.** Slip stitch along nearby stitches into one corner space. There, chain three as the first double crochet, add two more, chain two, and add three more. At each next corner repeat three double crochets, two chains, three double crochets, with one chain between adjacent corner groups. Complete all four corners and slip stitch closed in one direction.
4. **Inspect and secure the end.** Four corners lie recognisably, each side has its groups, and both rounds connect. If overly tight or a fifth corner appears, pause and undo to the error rather than stretching it into shape. If stopping at two rounds, leave enough tail to pull through the last loop; record tails awaiting weaving instead of hiding them behind a photo.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A liftable two-round four-corner motif has connected round ends and no open live loop, with tail-weaving state marked honestly. Only a centre ring or three corners is not a finished square.

- **First round has other than four corners:** Undo and count three-stitch groups and corner spaces before round two.
- **US double crochet is confused with UK naming:** Pause and verify the US motion rather than mixing two terminology systems in one row.
- **Edge curls or a fifth corner appears:** Check repeated corner spaces or extra groups, and undo the error instead of forcing the shape flat.
- **Centre ring loosens or opens:** Secure the live yarn, remake the small ring, and check its slip join.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Light bulky yarn, four corner markers, magnification, and trusted pointing help can support your round and corner count.

### Assumptions and limits

Four corners with three-stitch groups are a common basic structure, not a promise of any branded pattern's dimensions or a ready-made garment. Yarn, hook, and hand tension change size. This is a small personal sample.
