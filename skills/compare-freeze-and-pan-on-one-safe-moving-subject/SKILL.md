## 中文

当你已有可按说明选择快门或运动模式的相机，想用**同一个获准且安全重复的小物件动作**比较“冻结运动”和“平移跟随造成背景流动”时，加载这份 Skill。结果是两张可并排核差别的原创试片；两种处理合在一篇，不能拆成两个凑数。可用自有滚动球或摆动纸片，不要拿马路车辆、陌生人、野生动物或危险边缘当练习道具。

### 准备与输入

在不挡路、无车流/跌落危险的固定位置安排小幅、可重复的物件运动，光线足够，镜头方向不侵犯他人隐私。查本机说明书的运动对焦与快门控制，手机没有手动快门时可用内置运动模式或只记录无法控制的限制，不借买新相机解决。先拍一张静止参照，确认镜头可对焦并能回放。

### 执行

1. **先试冻结。** 让物件按原路径移动，选择本机允许的较快快门/运动冻结设置，焦点对在运动路径上，保持相机稳定拍一张。回放放大看物件是否较清楚，记实际模式/快门而非假设所有相机能设同一数值。
2. **再试平移跟随。** 在相同安全拍位让物件重走相近路径，按本机允许方式选较慢快门，身体在站稳时顺运动方向平滑转动相机并拍摄，继续轻跟随一瞬；不边走边追或把镜头转向私人窗。没有足够空间/光线就不试此段。
3. **并排检查两个结果。** 看第一张物件和背景是否都较清楚，第二张是否至少有物件部分可辨而背景出现方向性流动；两张全糊不能称成功平移。记录拍位、光线、模式与失败张数。
4. **只在安全范围小幅重试。** 冻结失败可调整本机快门/对焦或改善光线，平移失败可先练平滑跟随；一次改一个条件，限制内试几次后停，不去不安全场地追更快对象。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

两张同一安全动作的试片能解释冻结和有意平移的不同，或本机/环境限制被明确记录；不是必须拍出专业运动大片。只拍到两张无法辨认的糊片，不算有对照结果。

- **冻结片仍拖影：** 核模式、光线与对焦，再按本机允许方向调整，不盲报某个万能快门。
- **平移片全糊：** 降低物件速度、练跟随、检查至少一处可辨，别把所有模糊称艺术。
- **相机/身体跟随会撞人或失稳：** 立即停，回固定安全拍位或取消平移。
- **光线不足导致快门无法满足：** 换安全亮处或记限制，不用未经许可闪光。
- **运动/感官不便：** 可由可信协助者驱动物件、用稳定座位小幅跟镜；无法安全跟随时只看静态说明。

### 假设与边界

Nikon 描述快门速度与运动呈现的方向关系，但不同机身/光线没有统一可用数值；手持平移成功率可能低。本文是受控安全练习，不拍车辆/行人或承诺成片清晰率。

## English

Load this Skill when a camera with model-supported shutter or motion controls is available and you want to compare **freezing and panning the same permitted safe repeatable movement**. Finish with two original trial frames that show a difference. Both treatments belong in one comparison Skill. An owned rolling ball or paper moving on a safe table works; do not use road traffic, strangers, wildlife, or a fall edge as a practice target.

### Preparation and inputs

Arrange a small repeatable moving object at a fixed position without traffic or fall risk, with enough light and no privacy intrusion. Check the camera manual for motion focus and shutter controls. A phone without manual speed can use its actual motion mode or record that it cannot control this trial, not buy new gear. Take a still reference to confirm focus and playback.

### Execution

1. **Try a freeze first.** Move the object along the chosen path, use a model-supported faster shutter/freezing mode, aim focus at its path, hold camera steady, and take a frame. Enlarge it to see whether the object is relatively sharp, recording actual mode or speed instead of a universal number.
2. **Try following motion.** From the same safe camera position, repeat a similar object path with a model-supported slower shutter. While standing stable, rotate smoothly with the moving object, release, and continue the follow briefly. Do not walk after it or sweep private windows. Omit this trial when space or light makes it unsafe.
3. **Compare both frames.** Ask whether the freeze has relatively clear subject/background and the pan has at least part of the subject recognisable with directional background motion. Two fully smeared images are not a successful intentional pan. Record position, light, mode, and failed attempts.
4. **Retry only within safe bounds.** For failed freeze, adjust one model control, focus, or light; for failed pan, practise smooth following. Change one condition at a time, stop after a few safe trials, and never chase a faster subject into risk.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Two frames of the same safe movement explain a freeze versus intentional pan or a real camera/setting limit is documented. Professional action quality is not required. Two unrecognisable smears are not a comparison result.

- **Freeze still trails:** Check mode, light, and focus, then adjust by model directions rather than a magic shutter number.
- **Pan is blurred everywhere:** Slow the object, smooth following, and require one recognisable element; not all blur is art.
- **Camera/body follow risks collision or instability:** Stop and return to a fixed safe position or cancel the pan.
- **Light cannot support the setting:** Move to a safe brighter place or record the limit, without unauthorized flash.
- **Movement or senses limit the trial:** Trusted help can move the object while you use a steady seated small pan; skip if following is unsafe.

### Assumptions and limits

Nikon explains direction of shutter effects, not one value for every body and light. Hand panning often fails. This is controlled safe practice, not traffic or wildlife shooting or a promised keeper rate.
