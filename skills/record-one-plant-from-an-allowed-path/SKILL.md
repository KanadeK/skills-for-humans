## 中文

当你在公园或社区公共路径上看到一株植物，想记住它**当时实际是什么样**，用这份 Skill 写一条短观察。结果是有日期、非敏感地点线索、可见特征和环境描述的原始记录，不需要猜对物种名，更不告诉你它能否食用、药用或触碰。以后拿旧记录回来比较变化是另一任务。

### 准备与输入

选管理方允许停留、不会挡路的公共位置；只看能从那里观察到的植物，不踩入花坛、湿地或私有地，不采摘、折枝、摸叶、尝味或闻不明物。纸笔、本机便签或经场地允许的照片都可；不拍入旁人脸、住址或需要保护的精确稀有生物位置。你可以只给它自定义代号，如“北门旁大叶植物”，不必检索物种数据库。视觉或行动不便时选择更近、更可及的位置或可信描述协助，标清哪些来自他人。

### 执行

1. **先写时间与可回找的线索。** 记日期、大致时段、公共场所/路径和一个不会泄露私人住址的固定参照物；若不适合公开位置，记录只留在自己的私下笔记里。不要为了精确坐标离开允许路径。
2. **只写看得见的特征。** 从安全距离描述株形、叶片大致形状与排列、可见花/果是否有、颜色或一个独特边缘；看不清就写“未看清”，不是“没有”。避免根据模糊照片断言品种或用途。
3. **给环境一行上下文。** 写它在阳面/阴处、旁边是草地还是铺装、地面看起来干/湿，以及当时的普通天气感受；这些是现场印象，不证明哪项环境因素造成了植物状态。
4. **核一遍观察与推断。** 在纸上分成“我看见____”和“我猜测/想问____”两行，去掉未经证实的“有毒/可吃/快枯死”。若想画，画可见轮廓即可，不去摸实物补细节。
5. **从原路离开。** 检查没留下纸屑或改变植物/周围土壤。若周围有蜂虫、路面不稳或人员不便通行，立即结束，不为写完一条笔记挤近观察。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有一条能辨认**哪一天、在什么允许位置、看见了哪些特征和环境**的记录，并把不知道之处标出来。贴一张无法定位的照片再写“应该是药草”不满足本篇。

- **看不清花/叶细节：** 换安全视角、用允许的放大方式或记“未见清”，不踏出路径。
- **几株长得很像：** 只描述眼前这一株和固定参照物；不能分辨个体时记“这片植物”，不伪称找到同一株。
- **标签和自己观察冲突：** 分别记“标牌写____”和“我看见____”，不擅自裁决物种。
- **有人建议摘一片回家认：** 不采集；若仍想了解，另找场地允许的教育资源，本篇不作食用/药用识别。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

公园、花园和私有地对摄影、停留与采集规定不同，以实际管理方为准；“只观察不拿走”是本篇更窄的选择。不同季节、光线与感官条件会让特征不可见，记录未知比补猜更有用。若有可信协助，标明描述来源。此记录可供个人回看，不自动成为科学鉴定或公共物种数据。

## English

Use this Skill when a plant catches your eye along a park or community path and you want to remember **what it actually looked like then**. Make one short field note with date, a nonsensitive place clue, visible features, and surroundings. You need not identify a species and this does not say whether the plant can be eaten, used medicinally, or touched. Returning to compare against an old note is a separate task.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a public place where stopping is allowed and does not block others. Observe only a plant visible from there. Do not step into a bed, wetland, or private land or pick, break, touch, taste, or smell an unknown plant. Paper, local note, or a venue-permitted photo can work. Avoid bystanders' faces, home addresses, and exact locations of sensitive rare wildlife. Give the plant your own label such as “large-leaf plant by north gate” without needing a species database. For sight or mobility access, choose an easier viewpoint or trusted description and distinguish what another person supplied.

### Execution

1. **Write time and a retrievable clue.** Note date, approximate time, public place or path, and one fixed landmark that does not disclose a private home. Keep sensitive place details in a private note. Do not leave the allowed path merely for exact coordinates.
2. **Describe only visible features.** From a safe distance record overall shape, rough leaf form or arrangement, whether flowers or fruit can be seen, colour, or one edge feature. Write “could not see clearly” when true, not “absent.” A blurry photo cannot establish a species or use.
3. **Give one environmental line.** Note sun or shade, lawn or paving nearby, whether ground appeared dry or wet, and ordinary weather as you perceived it. Those are context impressions, not proof of what caused the plant's condition.
4. **Separate observation from inference.** Use “I saw ____” and “I wonder ____” lines. Remove unsupported claims such as “poisonous,” “edible,” or “dying.” If sketching, draw visible contours without touching for extra detail.
5. **Leave by the same allowed route.** Take scraps away and do not change the plant or its soil. End immediately for insects, unstable footing, or blocked passage instead of edging closer to finish a note.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Your record says **when, where from an allowed place, what features you saw, and what surroundings were visible**, with unknowns marked honestly. An unlocatable photo labelled “probably medicinal” does not meet this task.

- **Flower or leaf detail is unclear:** Use another safe viewpoint or permitted magnification, or mark it unseen. Do not leave the path.
- **Several plants look alike:** Describe this one with a fixed landmark. If individuals cannot be distinguished, note “this patch” rather than claim the same plant.
- **A label conflicts with your view:** Keep “sign says ____” separate from “I saw ____”; do not assert a species verdict yourself.
- **Someone suggests collecting a leaf to identify it:** Do not collect. Use venue-approved education later if wanted; food and medicine identification are outside this Skill.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Parks, gardens, and private sites have different rules for photography, stopping, and collecting; use the actual manager's rules. “Observe, leave in place” is this Skill's narrower choice. Season, light, and sensory access can make a feature unavailable; an honest unknown is more useful than a guess. Mark the source of trusted assistance. The note helps personal recall; it is no automatic scientific identification or public species submission.
