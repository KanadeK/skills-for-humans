## 中文

当你准备照可靠配方做**普通家用烘烤**，但还未确认烤箱该用哪种加热模式、哪层架子和哪只容器时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**配方的温度与步骤能落在这台烤箱的实际模式、层位和适用烤盘上**，预热完成后才下料。这里不把对流、热风、空气炸或上下火视作自动等价，不处理燃气/电气维修、罐藏或极端高温操作。

### 准备与输入

记录配方的食物、份量、烤盘类型/尺寸、模式、温度单位、预热要求、层位、预计时长和成品判断。打开确切机型说明书，找到它的模式含义、允许的架位、烤具/衬垫限制和预热提示。没有手册时可查制造商对应型号资料；仅有同品牌另一机型的按钮照片不够。烤盘材料标示不明时先换明确适用的，不用一次未碎来证明耐烤。

### 执行步骤

1. **把配方词与机型模式逐项对上。** 配方若要求常规烘烤，就找手上烤箱与之匹配的真实功能；若只有对流、风扇、空气炸等不同模式，先查配方和手册是否给出该模式的调整，不擅自沿用原温度和时长。核对 °C/°F 单位，不把数字相同当温度相同。
2. **冷态安排层架和烤盘。** 按配方和机型说明确定层位；需要移动架子时在烤箱冷态做，确认水平稳固。烤盘应有实际放入/取出的空间，不压门、不触及说明禁止的炉底或加热元件。盖子、垫纸、铝箔是否适用也按烤具/烤箱说明判断，不堵通风口。
3. **让锅具大小与配方相符。** 过深、过浅或不同材料的容器会改变食物厚度和受热；能按配方使用指定规格就优先。没有时查可靠替代说明，不在烤箱已热时随手换一个尺寸来赌时长。
4. **按正确模式预热。** 先清空不应在炉内的包装和无关物品，设置配方与本机相符的模式/温度，在场等待设备真正完成预热；预热提示、温度显示和预热时间因机型而异。若配方明确不需预热，按该可靠做法和器具说明执行，不套此步。
5. **记录开烤事件与停点。** 准备好计时器、隔热工具和成品检查方法；食物放入到配方位置后，按配方定义的事件开始计时。烤箱无法达到/保持设置、出现烟火、燃气异味或设备异常时停止，按当地紧急/专业渠道处理，不调大温度补偿故障。

### 完成与停止

你能指明这台烤箱的正确模式、配方适用的层位/容器、预热状态、真正开始计时的事件与食品完成线索，就完成开烤配置。模式等价性、容器耐热或架位信息缺失时先查证或换可靠做法；不把“能把盘塞进去”当成适用。

### 常见报错与补救

- **配方写常规烘烤，你只找到热风键：** 查本机手册和配方是否允许调整；无依据时不自行复制原温度/分钟数。
- **盘太大，关门时顶到门或风口：** 先换适用烤盘或缩小批量，别按住门烤。
- **架子在预热后才发现位置不对：** 在安全可行时关热、等冷却并按手册调整，不徒手跨过热元件搬架。
- **预热灯亮/灭含义不清：** 查确切机型说明或可靠测温方式，不把一个陌生图标翻译成“已经好了”。
- **容器标示不明：** 换有明确烤箱适用说明的器具；不靠触摸或一次成功试验补标签。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通家用烤箱与可靠配方的匹配，不提供跨机型的统一层位、温差修正或安全熟度数字。视觉、握力、站立或听觉限制可通过大字/语音计时、冷态预摆盘、适用轻烤盘和现场可信协助处理；无法安全放入/取出时改用可执行技法。现实副作用是你可能在预热前多翻两页说明书，却少把烤盘当作烤箱尺寸测试卡。

## English

Load this Skill before an **ordinary home-oven bake** when a reliable recipe is ready but oven mode, rack, and vessel are not yet confirmed. Match **the recipe's heat and process to this exact oven's real mode, rack, and approved bakeware**, then load after any required preheat. Convection, fan, air-fry, and conventional bake are not automatic equivalents. Gas or electrical repair, canning, and extreme-heat work are outside scope.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the recipe's food, amount, pan type and size, mode, temperature unit, preheat instruction, rack, expected time, and endpoint. Read the exact oven model's manual for what each mode means, supported racks, bakeware or liner limits, and preheat signal. Find the maker's exact model page if the paper manual is unavailable; a button photo from a related oven is not enough. Use clearly oven-suitable bakeware rather than treating one unbroken attempt as a material test.

### Execution steps

1. **Match recipe words to a real oven mode.** Find the actual function that matches conventional baking when that is what the recipe specifies. If you only have a different convection, fan, or air-fry setting, consult recipe and manual adjustment instructions rather than copying temperature and time. Check °C against °F; identical numbers are not identical heat.
2. **Set racks and pan while cold.** Use the position supported by recipe and model. Move and level a rack before heating. Ensure there is room to load and remove the pan without pressing the door or touching a forbidden oven bottom or element. Lids, liners, parchment, and foil must suit both bakeware and oven rules and leave vents open.
3. **Match pan size to the recipe.** A deeper, shallower, or different-material vessel changes food thickness and heating. Prefer the specified size. Find a reliable substitution before heating rather than gambling on a different pan while the oven is hot.
4. **Preheat in the matching mode.** Remove packaging and unrelated objects that should not be inside. Set the compatible mode and temperature and attend the oven until its required preheat is genuinely complete. Signals, displays, and time vary by model. If this recipe explicitly starts cold, follow its reliable instructions rather than forcing this step.
5. **Record the load and stop events.** Have a timer, heat protection, and the recipe's finish check ready. Start its clock from the event it names after loading food. Stop for an oven that cannot reach or hold the setting, smoke, fire, gas odour, or equipment failure; use local emergency or qualified support rather than compensating with a hotter dial.

### Success and stop

You can name this oven's mode, compatible rack and vessel, preheat state, actual timer start, and food finish check. Pause when mode equivalence, heat-safe material, or rack information is missing. Fitting a pan through the door is not proof it belongs in this oven.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Recipe says conventional bake but only a fan mode is found:** Check this manual and recipe for a supported adjustment. Do not blindly reuse the original heat and minutes.
- **Pan pushes the door or blocks a vent:** Choose suitable bakeware or a smaller batch instead of forcing the door shut.
- **Rack position is wrong after preheating:** When safe, stop heat, let it cool, and adjust under the manual rather than moving a rack bare-handed across a hot element.
- **Preheat light or icon is unclear:** Read the exact model's meaning or use a reliable measurement method. An unfamiliar symbol is not a confirmed “ready.”
- **Bakeware material is unknown:** Choose a vessel clearly approved for this use, not one you test by touch or a previous lucky bake.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This matches a reliable recipe to an ordinary domestic oven. It does not supply universal rack numbers, convection conversion, or food-safety temperatures. For visual, grip, standing, or hearing limits, use accessible timers, lay out pans cold, choose manageable approved bakeware, and arrange trusted in-person help. Choose another workable technique if loading or removal cannot be safe. Reading two more manual pages before preheating may save using a baking tray as an oven sizing card.
