## 中文

当一块**自有原创建好的泡棉印版**要第一次把图案转到纸上时，加载这份 Skill。结果是标“试印”的第一张纸及可见的漏印、过湿或镜像问题记录；它不是正式小批多张成品。这里用可清洗水基画材、手压和钝工具，不用油墨、溶剂或印刷机。

### 准备与输入

确认版面没破，准备自己可试的纸、少量标签允许的可洗水基马克笔/版画墨或合用水基颜料、可清洗滚筒/刷子（按画材需要）、护台垫与吸水纸。Getty 的泡棉马克笔示例会轻微润湿以转印，但你的材料须小试；纸太湿会糊，太干可能不转。颜料远离餐食，水杯稳放。

### 执行

1. **薄而均匀地上色。** 只在版凸面轻上合用色，别把凹线填满；若用可洗马克笔，依该材料说明在版或纸上极轻补湿，先试一角。用太多水形成 puddle 时先吸走，不急着压纸。
2. **对位并只压一次。** 将试印纸按预定方向放在版上（或版面朝纸），一手稳住，用掌或圆滑勺背均匀轻压，不让纸在颜料上滑动。若要看转移程度，只掀一角并保持其余位置，再继续压未印处。
3. **慢慢揭纸。** 从一侧抬起纸，平放晾干；在旁记版的方向和使用的水基画材。检查图案是否镜像、凹线留白是否可辨、哪些区域过浅/糊成一片。
4. **给下步一个明确判断。** 将它标“第 1 张试印”，写下“继续用原版”“需清洁/改上色”“版上凹线需修”等具体下一动作。湿时先别叠放，不把试印当零缺陷宣传稿。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一张晾放中的实际纸面试印和至少一处真实观察，可决定修版或小批重复印。只把版涂色而没有转印到纸，不算试印。

- **图像局部很淡：** 下次查上色与该区压力；别让纸在版上横移来补。
- **凹线被墨填死：** 减少颜料量并清版重试，别用尖具剔刻。
- **整张糊或渗：** 减少水/颜料，换更合用的试印纸。
- **有字印成镜像：** 记录版的方向错误，返回版面设计；不在纸上假写正向当原印。
- **手眼控制不便：** 大纸大版、定位边线和可信协助稳纸，由你检查转印与停点。

### 假设与边界

画材与纸吸水性不同，Getty/Museum 例子的喷湿方法不能强制照搬。第一张试印用来发现可见问题，不需要完美；文字或标志未经许可不印作公共物。

## English

Load this Skill when a **prepared original foam plate you own** is about to transfer its image to paper for the first time. Finish with a first sheet marked “proof” and real notes on gaps, excess wetness, or reversal. This is not a final run of several identical prints. Use washable water-based material, hand pressure, and blunt tools, without oil ink, solvents, or a press.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the plate is intact. Set out your proof paper, a little labelled washable water-based marker, print ink, or compatible paint, a washable roller or brush if the product needs it, protected board, and absorbent paper. Getty's washable-marker example uses slight moisture to transfer, but test your material: too wet smears and too dry may fail. Keep pigment away from food and the water cup stable.

### Execution

1. **Apply a thin even colour.** Lightly coat the raised plate area without filling recessed lines. For washable markers, lightly moisten plate or paper only as that material permits after a corner test. Blot a puddle before pressing.
2. **Align and press once.** Place proof paper in the planned orientation over the plate (or plate face down on paper). Hold steady and press evenly with palm or smooth spoon back without sliding. If checking transfer, lift only a corner while keeping the rest registered, then press a missed area further.
3. **Lift slowly.** Peel from one side and leave the sheet flat to dry. Note plate orientation and water-based colour used. Check reversal, visible uninked recessed lines, faint sections, and muddy patches.
4. **Make the next decision explicit.** Label “proof 1” and write one actual next action such as “use plate again,” “clean/change colour load,” or “revise recessed line.” Do not stack wet sheets or market a proof as a flawless edition.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

An actual paper proof is drying, with at least one honest observation that guides plate revision or a later small run. Colour on the plate alone is not a proof impression.

- **Image is faint in patches:** Check pigment and pressure next time; sliding the sheet on the plate is not recovery.
- **Recessed lines fill with paint:** Use less colour and clean before trying again, without pointed carving tools.
- **Whole print smears or bleeds:** Reduce water or pigment and try more suitable proof paper.
- **Text prints backward:** Record an orientation/design error and return to the plate; handwriting over the paper is not an original print fix.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Use larger plate and paper, alignment marks, and trusted help holding the sheet while you inspect transfer.

### Assumptions and limits

Paint and paper absorb differently; a museum's spray method cannot be imposed on every product. A proof finds visible issues and need not be perfect. Do not print unlicensed marks or private text for public distribution.
