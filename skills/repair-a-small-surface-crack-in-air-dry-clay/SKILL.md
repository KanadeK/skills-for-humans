## 中文

当一件**自有风干黏土小装饰物**出现不贯穿、不承重的细小表面裂纹，且产品说明允许水或同泥修补时，加载这份 Skill。结果是裂纹被有限平整并重新晾干，或明确判断不适合修，停止使用该件。已断成两半、深层开裂、挂钩支承处或食品/盛水器具不按此法“修好”。

### 准备与输入

读本品裂纹 FAQ 和手边包装；确认材质与原件相同。作品放在可保护的平稳垫板上，查看裂纹长度、深度、是否穿透或让部件松动。备一点清水或同品种新泥、干净手/钝工具。裂纹若严重或作品结构失稳，先隔离；不靠胶水、封漆遮蔽它。

### 执行

1. **划定可试范围。** 只选一小段表面细裂，在边角轻试少量水是否让表面可抹合、是否把周边染坏。看见新裂扩大或材料表层脱落就停。
2. **少量润湿或填同泥。** 按产品说明在裂处滴少量水，手指轻抹；仍有浅缝可用极少同泥填入并抹平。不要把整个干件浸泡、把大量水和湿泥堆进裂缝。
3. **支撑并再晾干。** 把修补件稳放在允许空气流通的垫板上，标记“修后未干”，按本品全干条件重新等待；表面暂时平了不代表已经恢复强度。
4. **干后核对。** 检查裂缝是否重开、邻近是否出现新裂、部件是否稳。若仍开裂或松动，记录未修复、停用或重做；不要把一次抹平称为保证长期结实。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

小裂在干后仍闭合/变浅且作品作为**纯装饰**稳定，或你及时记录修复失败并停止后续上色/使用。湿时抹平而未重干、未复查，不算完成。

- **裂纹贯穿或部件活动：** 停止这条表面修补，重新制作或按厂商其他方法求助。
- **水让周边变糊：** 立刻停加水，让材料按说明自然干，再判断是否重做。
- **补泥干后掉落：** 不继续层层堆湿泥，记录不适用并重做或弃用。
- **修后又出现新裂：** 回看原件壁厚、过量水或刚性支撑问题；不能承诺此法根治。
- **精细动作不便：** 用更大的支撑垫板、少量可控水和可信协助，但由你决定是否停修。

### 假设与边界

厂商明确说修补成败取决于裂纹严重度。这里只处理允许的轻微表面缺陷，完全干燥后的装饰稳定也不使空气干燥黏土防水、耐热或安全接触食品。

## English

Load this Skill when an **owned small air-dry clay decoration** has a shallow surface crack that does not run through or support weight, and its product permits a water or matching-clay repair. Finish with a limited smoothing attempt redried and checked, or a clear decision that repair is unsuitable. A broken half, deep split, hanging support, or food/water vessel cannot be declared fixed by this path.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this product's crack FAQ and package, confirming the material matches. Set the piece on a protective stable board and inspect length, depth, penetration, and any loose part. Have only a little water or fresh matching clay with clean hands or blunt tool. Isolate a severe or unstable piece rather than hiding it under glue or coating.

### Execution

1. **Limit a test spot.** Select a short shallow section. Test a little water at an edge for gentle blending and unwanted staining. Stop if the crack widens or surface flakes.
2. **Dampen or fill lightly.** Under product directions, add a few drops at the crack and smooth with a finger. For a remaining shallow gap, add a tiny amount of matching wet clay and smooth. Do not soak the whole dry piece or pile water and mud into the gap.
3. **Support and dry again.** Rest the piece on an aired board marked “repaired, still wet.” Wait for the product's full-dry condition again. A smooth wet surface is not restored strength.
4. **Check after drying.** Inspect reopening, new neighbouring cracks, and part stability. If still split or loose, record failure and retire or remake it. A single smoothing pass promises no lasting strength.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

After redrying, the small surface crack stays closed or reduced and the piece is stable **as decoration only**, or you record failed repair and stop further painting or use. Wet smoothing without redrying and rechecking is unfinished.

- **Crack penetrates or a part moves:** Stop surface repair and remake or seek product-specific help.
- **Water turns surrounding clay mushy:** Stop adding water, let it dry naturally under directions, then decide whether to remake.
- **Filler falls out after drying:** Do not pile repeated wet layers. Record the limitation and remake or retire.
- **New cracks appear:** Review original thin walls, excess water, or rigid support; this method cannot promise a root-cause cure.
- **Fine control is hard:** Use a larger support board, controlled tiny water amount, and trusted help while you decide when to stop.

### Assumptions and limits

The manufacturer says repair success varies with severity. This handles only permitted small surface defects. Decorative stability after complete drying does not make air-dry clay waterproof, heat-safe, or food-safe.
