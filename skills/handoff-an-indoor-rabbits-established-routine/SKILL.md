## 中文

当你要短时离开，**原本健康稳定的室内兔或兔群**由另一个愿意、能到场的人按原方案照料时，加载这份 Skill。结果是每天谁来、草水厕所、活动/同伴边界、吃草新粪观察和兽医紧急联系方式被对方复述确认；它不授权对方诊断、给药或强行抓兔。E005 猫、E008 狗和 E015 鱼的交接各有不同福利条件，不能拿一张通用宠物清单互换。

### 准备与输入

核离开/返回日期、照料者明确同意且有时间到场，准备原兽医/主人方案、兔的正常吃草排便/脏尾基线、获准食物份额、草/水与厕区用品、伴兔与活动空间规则。必要门禁只经双方同意私下交接，不把密码写公开纸张。若无人能实际进门或不愿处理异常，先改行程/找合适服务，不能让兔独处多天指望多放一盆草。

### 执行

1. **写成实际日期与负责人。** 每日谁来、何时补草水、清厕所、确认兔实际进食和新粪、观察行为与尾部，按原方案列清；多兔标身份与各自资源。颗粒粮/菜只按既有定量，不给照料者凭感觉加零食。
2. **现场走一遍资源和界线。** 带对方看到兔的熟悉藏身处、饮水/草、厕所、获准活动区、电线/危险植物隔离、清洁用品和记录处；不强抓兔演示。不会安全执行的特殊操作转原兽医/合适服务，而非临行前口头分派。
3. **写即时兽医出口与备援。** 清楚写“兔不吃或没有新粪便 → 立即联系兽医”，并有本兔兽医电话、离线时备援联系人、你可接通的方式；其它显著异常也按兽医建议。照料者不自行投药、改饮食或让陌生猫狗接触兔。
4. **要求复述与实际承诺。** 请对方说出第一天到场、原饲料份额、要看什么、兽医紧急条件和备用联系人；双方确认日期和权限。答不出关键项就当场修正、再复述；没人承接则不能称交接完成。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

已同意且能实际到场的人得到本兔原有方案、物品/门禁、每日观察和紧急兽医路线，能正确复述首日任务；或者没有可靠人手而调整离开计划。只留一堆草和一条短信不是交接。

- **照料者不了解不吃/无新粪紧急性：** 当场说明并让其复述兽医联系顺序，不能留到出门后猜。
- **食物份额不明：** 找回原方案/按原定量预分装，不由帮忙人发明。
- **进门或备援不可靠：** 改行程或找合适服务，不能靠希望。
- **伴兔/其他宠物管理不清：** 维持原分区/同伴规则，问原负责人或兽医，不临时配对。
- **文字/语言可及性不足：** 大字图示、现场示范和对方复述，保留紧急联系人可读性。

### 假设与边界

兔需规律照料与伴兔福利，具体次数/食物及特殊情况由原兽医方案决定。交接是普通非医疗责任转移，不保证兔最终健康，也不能代替紧急兽医判断。

## English

Load this Skill when you will be away briefly and an **otherwise stable known indoor rabbit or bonded group** will be cared for by a willing person who can visit under the established plan. Finish with dates, hay/water/toilet, activity and companion boundaries, eating/fresh-dropping checks, and urgent vet contact correctly repeated by that person. It does not authorize diagnosis, medication, or forced handling. Cat, dog, and fish handoffs in E005/E008/E015 have different welfare conditions, not one interchangeable pet sheet.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm departure/return dates and that the carer explicitly agrees and can attend. Gather owner/vet plan, normal eating/dropping/soiled-rear baseline, approved portions, hay/water and litter supplies, companion and area rules. Give necessary access only privately by agreement, not a public note. With no real visitor or no one willing to handle abnormalities, change the absence plan or arrange suitable service; extra hay alone is not days of care.

### Execution

1. **Write actual dates and responsibility.** Name who visits each day and when to restore hay/water, clean toilet, confirm actual eating and new droppings, and observe behaviour and rear under the plan. Identify each rabbit and resources in a group. Pellets/greens remain at their established portions, not a helper's guessed treats.
2. **Walk through resources and boundaries in person.** Show familiar hide, food/water, toilet, allowed activity area, cable/unsafe-plant separation, cleaning supplies, and record. Do not force-catch a rabbit to demonstrate. Special care the helper cannot safely do routes to the original vet or suitable service rather than a hurried verbal assignment.
3. **Write urgent vet exit and backup.** State “not eating or no new droppings → call the vet immediately,” with this rabbit's vet number, approved backup if you are unreachable, and how to contact you. Other major changes follow vet advice. No self-medication, diet redesign, or new cat/dog contact is authorized.
4. **Require teach-back and commitment.** Have the carer say first visit, approved portion, observation, emergency condition, and backup contact in their own words. Confirm dates and authority together. Correct a missing key point and repeat. With no accountable person the handoff is not complete.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A willing available carer has this rabbit's plan, supplies and private access, daily observations and immediate vet route, and correctly restates the first day, or unreliable coverage changes the trip plan. A heap of hay and a vague text are not a handoff.

- **Carer misses urgency of no eating/droppings:** Explain now and request the vet-call order back, not after departure.
- **Food amount is unknown:** Recover the established plan or pre-portion it, rather than asking a helper to invent it.
- **Entry or backup is unreliable:** Change trip or arrange proper service instead of hope.
- **Companion/other-pet management is unclear:** Keep original grouping/barriers and ask owner or vet, not surprise bonding.
- **Written language is inaccessible:** Use large readable diagrams, in-person demonstration, and teach-back with clear emergency contacts.

### Assumptions and limits

Rabbits need regular care and companionship welfare; exact visits, portions, and exceptions follow the original veterinary plan. This transfers ordinary nonmedical responsibility, not a guarantee of health or a substitute for urgent vet judgment.
