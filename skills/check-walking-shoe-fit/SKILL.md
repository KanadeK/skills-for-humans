## 中文

当你准备买一双普通步行鞋，旧号码或网上尺码表看起来合适，却还需要知道**双脚在实际活动时**是否舒服时，加载这份 Skill。目标是现场得到“这双可考虑/不合适”，或在线购物时诚实记录“尺码预测，未试穿”。它不选择医疗矫形鞋、安全防护鞋，也不解释疼痛原因。

### 准备与输入

带上你通常会穿的袜子和自有鞋垫（如有），查看该具体鞋款的本地尺码表与宽度选项。旧鞋的尺码是线索，不是同品牌所有鞋型的保证。能到店时选择可安全站立和短距离移动的试穿区；不方便走路时以你实际使用的站立、移动或辅助动作作判断，并说明未验证的使用场景。

### 执行

1. **两脚都试。** 穿上计划用的袜子，左右脚都穿入候选；双脚可能有不同长度或宽度。以更需要空间的那只脚排除太小的候选，不用单脚通过替另一只脚签字。
2. **站立核长度、宽度和脚背。** 让脚趾能自然活动，前脚掌不被侧边挤压，脚背不被强勒；后跟和中足应有稳定感，但不是疼痛或麻木。需要宽度选项时找相应鞋型，不只靠增加长度解决宽度不足。
3. **做与用途相符的短动作。** 在允许的安全区域按你能做的方式向前走、转身或站起坐下，注意后跟是否反复滑脱、脚趾是否顶前、两侧是否摩擦。不能试这些动作就把动态合脚记为未确认，不把静态脚长当结论。
4. **排除靠“以后会撑开”的候选。** 穿着当下持续夹脚、麻、疼或明显不稳时，换宽度、鞋楦或尺码；一时可忍耐不等于适合经常步行。
5. **留下购前结果。** 写明具体鞋款/尺码/宽度、两脚静态情况、短动作结果和未测试的距离。网上没有试穿机会时只用商品尺码表和旧鞋尺寸挑候选，并先查实际可行的试穿/退换条件；不宣称已确认合脚。

### 成功与停止

两脚在你实际能做的普通动作下都没有压迫、反复滑脱或限制活动，才把这双列为可考虑；这仍不保证长距离体验。出现疼痛、麻木、显著步态问题或需定制支撑时停止普通选购并找合适的专业适配。无法试穿时完成的是“候选筛选”，不是合脚验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只照旧鞋号码下单：** 查看这双鞋自己的长度和宽度表，能试就试双脚；旧号码不跨鞋楦自动生效。
- **长度宽裕却侧边挤脚：** 找较宽鞋型或不同鞋楦，不盲目把鞋号加长。
- **站着不错，走两步后跟滑：** 调整可正常使用的系带再短试；仍滑就换候选，不靠长期磨合解决。
- **没法看清尺码或完成步行试验：** 使用可读尺码表、请店员读回标识，并以你实际会做的安全动作试穿；缺少动作证据时明确保留未知。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 针对普通步行，不把 REI 或 New Balance 的一个鞋楦当成所有品牌的尺寸。鞋款、袜子、时段和个人舒适偏好会影响试穿；在线购得的静态数据只能缩小候选。防护、治疗或特殊足部需要单独的专业方案。你可能花更多时间试两只鞋，也可能少买一双只能在鞋盒里舒服的鞋。

## English

Load this Skill before buying ordinary walking shoes when your old size or an online chart looks plausible but you still need to know how **both feet behave in the shoes during real movement**. The result is a shop trial that supports or rejects this pair, or an honest online “size candidate, not tried on.” It does not choose medical orthotics or protective footwear or explain pain.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring the socks and any existing insoles you normally use. Read this exact shoe model's local size and width chart. An old shoe size is a clue, not a guarantee across every style from the same brand. In a shop, choose a safe area for standing and a short movement trial. If walking is difficult, use the standing, movement, or assistive action relevant to your own use and state which conditions remain untested.

### Execution

1. **Try both feet.** Put on the intended socks and both shoes. Your left and right feet can differ in length or width. Let the foot needing more space rule out a too-small pair; one foot cannot sign for the other.
2. **Check length, width, and top-of-foot room while standing.** Toes should move naturally, the forefoot should not be squeezed, and the upper should not pinch. Heel and midfoot should feel held without pain or numbness. When width is the problem, seek an appropriate width or shape rather than only adding length.
3. **Make a short task-relevant movement.** In an allowed safe area, walk, turn, or stand and sit as you are able. Notice repeated heel slip, toe contact at the front, or rubbing at the sides. If that movement cannot be tried, record dynamic fit as unconfirmed rather than treating static length as acceptance.
4. **Reject “it will stretch” as the only fix.** Ongoing pinching, numbness, pain, or obvious instability now calls for another width, last, or size. Being able to endure a minute is not evidence for frequent walking.
5. **Record the buying result.** Name the exact model, size and width, both feet's standing fit, the short movement result, and distance not tested. Online, use the item's chart and a well-fitting old shoe only to choose a candidate; check a workable try-on/return route first and do not claim confirmed fit.

### Success and stop conditions

You may consider the pair when both feet have no pressure, repeated slipping, or movement restriction during an ordinary action you can actually perform. This is still not a guarantee for a long walk. Stop ordinary shopping for pain, numbness, a significant gait concern, or a need for custom support and seek an appropriate fitting professional. Without a try-on, the result is candidate selection, not fit acceptance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The old shoe-box number was reordered:** Check this model's length and width chart and try both shoes when possible. Size labels do not carry across lasts automatically.
- **Length is ample but the sides pinch:** Look for a wider option or another shape rather than blindly adding length.
- **Standing feels fine but the heel slips after a few steps:** Adjust normal lacing and try briefly again. If it still slips, change the candidate instead of relying on prolonged break-in.
- **The chart is inaccessible or walking trial impossible:** Use readable size information, ask staff to read labels back, and test the safe movement relevant to your use. State what remains unknown.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This Skill covers ordinary walking shoes. A REI or New Balance fit example does not set a universal last for every brand. Style, socks, time of day, and personal comfort affect the trial. Static online measurements only narrow candidates. Protective, treatment, or special foot needs require a separate professional route. You may spend longer trying both shoes and buy fewer pairs that are comfortable only inside their boxes.
