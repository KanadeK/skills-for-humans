## 中文

当你的普通家用机**说明书明确允许用户清理梭芯区**，可及处积了绒屑且机器仍可安全停机时，加载这份 Skill。结果是断电后只刷走允许区域的松屑、零件按原位复装、废布试缝正常；不是内件维修或“给机器加点油”。某些 Brother 机型明确禁止用户上油，不能移植其他机型的润滑说法。

### 准备与输入

先查本机型号的用户维护图：哪些盖和梭壳可由用户取下、何种刷子、是否允许吸尘、是否有需要厂商服务的边界。没有图或机型不明就停。备稳定明亮台面、小容器放可拆件、随机刷或手册合用软刷；相机可记录原排列。机器异响、漏电、针断在内部、零件损坏时先交厂商/维修，不从清绒开始深拆。

### 执行

1. **先完全停机并拔电。** 按本机安全顺序升针/压脚，关机，拔掉电源插头；等运动件完全停。脚离踏板、手和工具不接触可能带电部位，清洁全程不接电。
2. **只打开用户允许的盖。** 按本机图移开梭芯、针板盖或规定的用户可拆件，逐件放在桌上并记方向；需要专用螺丝或较大力才开得动的封闭部分不撬。不要把不同机型梭壳拆装顺序混用。
3. **轻刷可见绒屑。** 用合用软刷从本机允许的梭芯/旋梭可及处带走松屑与断线，避免把屑吹进内部。按说明可用吸尘器时才用，水、强溶剂和随手加油都不进入机身；不要碰传感器或校准件。
4. **原位复装并低速试缝。** 依图放回梭壳、梭芯、盖、压脚/针（若本机要求拆过），确认盖平整、零件无毛刺/缺口，再按手册恢复供电，在新废布上低速短试；检查没有碰针、漏线、异响。仍不正常就停机并联系厂商服务。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

梭芯区允许部位的松屑已移除，零件和盖按本机图复位，废布低速试缝无新异常；封闭机型正确不拆同样是安全结论。开盖后零件摆不回、仍有异常声，不算完成。

- **盖需要暴力或专用工具：** 停，核本机用户维护范围，不撬。
- **梭壳位置/标记对不上：** 不开机，查图或交厂商支持。
- **滤绒刷掉入深处或针断片找不到：** 停止复装通电，求维修人员。
- **看到机油/润滑建议互相矛盾：** 本机说明书优先，不在此通用流程加油。
- **手眼操作不便：** 断电照片、分件小盒和熟悉机型的可信协助可帮复装，通电试缝只能由能安全停机的人做。

### 假设与边界

Brother 公开机型说明书展示了断电、用户可及梭芯区除绒与精确复装；有些机型不允许用户打开或加油。本篇绝不把一个型号的旋梭结构视为所有机器的内部图纸，也不保证清绒能修复针时序故障。

## English

Load this Skill only when the **exact manual of a sound ordinary home machine permits user cleaning of its bobbin area** and loose accessible lint has built up. Finish with permitted lint brushed away after unplugging, parts restored exactly, and a sound scrap test. This is neither internal repair nor a generic “add oil” step. Some Brother models explicitly prohibit user oiling, so another model's lubricant advice does not transfer.

### Preparation and inputs

Find this model's user-maintenance diagram: allowed covers and case, brush type, vacuum permission, and service-only boundary. Stop without a manual or identity. Have a bright stable table, small parts dish, included or suitable soft brush, and optional photo of original layout. Abnormal noise, electrical leak, broken needle inside, or damaged part routes to maker/service before lint work.

### Execution

1. **Stop fully and unplug.** Under this model's order raise needle/foot, switch off, remove plug, and wait for moving parts to stop. Keep foot off pedal and hands/tools away from power. Cleaning occurs without power.
2. **Open only allowed user covers.** Remove bobbin, plate cover, or other explicitly approved user pieces by the model diagram, keeping order and orientation on the table. Do not pry a sealed section needing force or specialist screws. Another model's case sequence is not yours.
3. **Brush visible loose lint.** Use the suitable soft brush on permitted bobbin/race surfaces, bringing lint out rather than blowing deeper. Vacuum only when the manual allows. No water, strong solvent, or guessed oil enters the mechanism; leave sensors and calibration parts untouched.
4. **Restore and make a slow scrap test.** Reinstall case, bobbin, cover, and any foot/needle this model required removing, checking flush covers and no burr/damage. Restore power only under manual steps and sew a short slow fresh-scrap line. Check for no needle strike, thread fault, or odd sound. Stop for maker service if abnormal.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Permitted accessible lint is removed, covers and parts match this model diagram, and a slow scrap test has no new fault; correctly declining to open a sealed model is also safe. A cover left loose or persistent abnormal noise is unfinished.

- **Cover needs force or special tools:** Stop and confirm this model's user-access boundary rather than prying.
- **Bobbin case marks will not align:** Do not power up; consult diagram or maker support.
- **Brush falls deep or a broken needle fragment is missing:** Stop reassembly and power for service.
- **Oil advice conflicts across models:** Follow this exact manual; add no generic oil here.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Unplugged photos, parts tray, and trusted model-aware reassembly help may assist; only a person able to stop safely should run the powered test.

### Assumptions and limits

Brother manuals show unplugged user-area lint brushing and exact replacement on some models; others may prohibit opening or oiling. This is not a universal internal drawing and cannot promise to fix machine timing faults.
