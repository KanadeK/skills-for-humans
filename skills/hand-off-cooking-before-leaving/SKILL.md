## 中文

当普通家常饭已经开始准备或加热，你却必须离开当前岗位，另一位在场的人可能接手时，加载这份 Skill。结果是**接手者准确知道眼前每道菜、热源、食品接触情况与未知事项，并明确接受责任**；如果没人能接，结果是安全停止而非把计时器当人。这里不教具体烹调技法、补救失败的菜或判定食物已经安全可吃。

### 准备与输入

先看清每道正在处理的菜和食材，不从原计划推断现在的状态。记下在用的灶具或设备、最近一次可靠观察的时间和食品状态、已经完成的动作、尚未完成的检查，以及哪些器具或台面接触过生肉、禽、海鲜或蛋。问接手者是否有时间、能力和意愿继续；人在厨房里不自动等于已接单。

### 执行

1. **先处理无人看守的热源。** 正在用的灶火需要一个在场且明确接手的人；你必须离开而无人接手时，先关闭灶具热源。烟、失控热油、燃气异味或设备异常不是普通交接，停止此流程并按当地紧急指导处理。
2. **逐道报实际状态。** 对每道菜写“现在在哪个容器/位置、最后可靠观察到什么、何时观察、正在受什么加热或等待、下一步应核对什么”。计时器响或还剩几分钟只是线索，不是熟度证明。计划里写过的步骤如果没做，要明说没做。
3. **把不确定和接触史说出来。** 不知道某食物离开冷藏多久、测量结果是否可信，或哪块板接触过生食时，明确写“未知”；不要把空白解释成安全。按适用食品安全指导处理被生食接触的工具和表面，接手者不能直接把它们用于即食物。
4. **让接手者复述并接管。** 对方用自己的话说出当前热源、各菜下一次检查、已知风险和未知项。你们明确说“从现在起由你负责这一步”，接手者确认后，你才离开岗位。文字、语音或大字记录均可，交接完成要能被两人确认。
5. **无法交接就停止。** 没有愿意且能在场的人，先停止无人看守的加热。对不能安全等待的易腐食物按所在地适用指导做实际处理；若时间或温度史无法确认，不继续宣告可食用，也不让下一位凭味道猜。需要缩小菜单或改餐时，另作决定。

### 成功与停止

你有一张接手者能复述的“菜品/位置 → 已做与已知 → 热源 → 下一次检查 → 未知/生食接触 → 新负责人”记录，且没有无人看管的灶火；或者已停止热源并明确没有接手。交接成功不等于菜已煮熟，也不保证原计划时间仍成立。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只说“差不多好了”：** 补上实际观察、尚缺的熟度证据和下一次检查位置；不知道就写不知道。
- **接手者没听清或正在做别的事：** 暂停交接，请其复述并确认是否真能在场；未确认不离开运行中的灶具。
- **多道菜只交了一锅：** 逐一看台面、灶台、烤箱等当前设备和等待中的食物；遗漏项补进记录。
- **时间/温度记录缺失：** 不把“应该没多久”填进去。先停止依赖该假设的供餐，按当地权威指导判断后续处置。
- **记录方式不可访问：** 改用大字、语音或可信在场人复述；关键状态仍不能准确传达时，停止交接与无人看守的加热。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 假设普通家庭饭菜、现场有人可询问，且你能安全触及关火控制；火灾、危机、专业厨房和燃气/电气维修不适用。食物所需的具体温度、时限与冷却方法依所在地官方和准确食物说明，不由这张交接卡发明。不能站立、看清或听清时让合适的人协助观察和记录，但热源责任必须有明确在场者。现实副作用是“差不多好了”需要补齐几项可核对字段。

## English

Load this Skill when preparation or heating for an ordinary home meal has begun but you must leave your current station and someone present may take over. Your result is **a willing receiver who knows each dish's actual state, active heat, food-contact history and unknowns, and explicitly accepts ownership**. Without a receiver, the result is a safe stop rather than treating a timer as a person. This does not teach cooking technique, rescue a failed dish or declare food ready to eat.

### Preparation and inputs

Look at every dish and ingredient in progress rather than inferring status from the original plan. Note each active hob or appliance, the last reliable observation time and food state, actions completed, checks still due, and any board or surface that touched raw meat, poultry, seafood or eggs. Ask whether the receiver has the time, ability and willingness to continue. Being in the kitchen is not automatic acceptance.

### Execution

1. **Deal with unattended heat first.** An active hob needs a present person who explicitly takes over. If you must leave and nobody accepts it, turn off the hob burner first. Smoke, uncontrolled oil, gas odour or equipment failure is not an ordinary handoff: stop this flow and follow local emergency guidance.
2. **State the observed position of every dish.** Write “current container/place, last reliable observation and when it happened, current heat or waiting state, next check due.” A timer sounding or showing minutes left is a clue, not proof of doneness. Say plainly when a step in the plan was never done.
3. **Disclose unknowns and contact history.** If you do not know how long a food was out of refrigeration, whether a measurement was reliable, or which board touched raw food, write “unknown.” A blank field does not mean safe. Handle raw-contact tools and surfaces under applicable food-safety guidance; a receiver must not use them straight for ready-to-eat food.
4. **Get a readback and transfer ownership.** The receiver states in their own words the active heat, next check for each dish, known concerns and unknowns. Say explicitly that they own the step from now on; leave only after they confirm. Text, voice or large-print notes can work, but both of you must be able to confirm the transfer.
5. **Stop when transfer is impossible.** Without a willing person who can remain present, stop unattended heating. Handle perishable food that cannot safely wait under applicable local guidance. If time or temperature history is unknown, do not declare it edible or ask the next person to guess by smell. Reducing the menu or changing the meal is a separate decision.

### Success and stop

You have a receiver-readable “dish/place → done and known → heat → next check → unknowns/raw contact → new owner” record and no unattended hob; or the hob is stopped and the lack of receiver is explicit. A successful handoff is not proof of doneness or of the original meal timing.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You say only “almost done”:** Add actual observations, missing doneness evidence and the next check location. Write unknown when you do not know.
- **The receiver did not hear or is busy elsewhere:** Pause and ask for readback and a real availability decision. Do not leave an active hob without confirmation.
- **Only one pot was handed over:** Scan counters, hob, oven and waiting food, then add omitted items to the record.
- **Time or temperature notes are missing:** Do not fill them with “probably not long.” Stop serving decisions that depend on that guess and use the applicable local authority's guidance for next steps.
- **The note format is inaccessible:** Use larger text, voice or a trusted person present to read back. If essential status still cannot be transferred accurately, stop the handoff and unattended heating.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary home meal, someone present to ask, and safe access to the heat control. Fire, crisis, professional kitchens and gas/electrical repair are outside this Skill. Actual food temperatures, limits and cooling methods come from local official guidance and exact food instructions, not this status card. If standing, sight or hearing is difficult, have an appropriate person help observe and record, while a named present person owns the heat. The side effect is that “almost done” needs several checkable fields.
