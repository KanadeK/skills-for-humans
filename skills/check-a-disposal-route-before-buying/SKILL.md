## 中文

当“这件可回收/可堆肥”会改变你的购买决定时，在付款前加载。你要验证的是**你所在地能否实际接收这件东西**；宣称的真实性、认证章和商家宣传范围由 H005 的商品宣称核对处理。这里不教你拆解危险物、处理化学品或执行购买后的垃圾分类。

### 准备与输入

拿到准确商品或包装上的完整处理标示，确认文字指商品本体、外盒、内衬还是某一部件，并知道你实际可使用的居住地收集服务。记下材料、形态、是否写着“仅工业堆肥”等条件。若对象或声明本身不清楚，先按商品宣称核对取得依据；在这一步不能偷偷把“外盒可回收”改成“整件可回收”。看不清小字时放大、朗读或请人逐字说明。

### 执行

1. **固定你要送走的对象。** 例如纸质外盒与内层薄膜是两件待处理的东西；同一个标志未必覆盖两者。把标签明确写出的材料和限制抄下。
2. **找所在地的正式接收说明。** 查你实际使用的市政收集、物业服务或回收点是否接收这种**材料和形态**。产品理论上能回收，不等于你附近的桶或站点会收；“可堆肥”也要核对家庭堆肥还是特定设施。
3. **核对使用条件。** 若当地只接收分开的部件、限定清洁程度或要求专门投放点，确认你能按正式说明做到。去不了指定地点、容器无法分开或当地没有该设施时，就不能把这条宣称当作此次购买的环境收益。
4. **给出三态结果。** 记为“本地可执行 / 本地不可执行 / 未查明”，并记录准确部件、当地规则来源与查看日期。找不到本地规则或在线信息互相矛盾时，向当地收集机构确认或暂缓购买。
5. **回到购买决定。** 若这条处理路线是硬条件，后两种结果都不足以放行；若只是加分项，未查明不加分。商品其它用途、价格和安全限制仍分别核对。

### 成功与停止

你能指出**哪个部件**在**哪个本地服务**里按**什么条件**会被接收，或清楚说出为什么现在无法确认。一个三角箭头、树叶图案或“可降解”宣传不足以完成本地路线证明。正式回收/堆肥规则改变时，重新核对；本 Skill 不承诺商品整体环境净收益。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把包装标志套到商品本体：** 重新定位标示对象；对象不明就先核实宣称，不继续推算路线。
- **本地只有普通垃圾桶：** 查正式可达的专门收集点；没有就记录为不可执行，不发明一个虚构设施。
- **标签写“可堆肥”但没有场所：** 核对家庭或设施条件和本地接收；无法对应则保持未查明或不可执行。
- **网页规则与物业告示冲突：** 以实际收运方的最新确认解决，不在货架前猜。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设你知道商品最终将在哪个地区处理，并能查询实际收运方。地区、住处和服务时间不同；没有网络、视觉或行动能力受限时，可电话询问收运方、请人读出规则或选择无需此宣称也能满足你需求的商品。结果只用于买前决策，实际处理仍服从届时的当地指引。绿色标记并没有自带一辆回收车。

## English

Load this Skill before paying when “recyclable” or “compostable” would change your purchase decision. Your question is whether **a service you can actually use will accept this item where you live**. Verification of the marketing claim, certification seal, or seller's scope belongs to H005's claim check. This Skill does not teach hazardous dismantling, chemical disposal, or the physical sorting job after purchase.

### Preparation and inputs

Get the exact item's complete disposal marking. Identify whether it refers to the product, outer box, liner, or another component, and which collection service you can really use at home. Note the material, form, and conditions such as “industrial composting only.” If the claimed object or evidence is unclear, resolve that claim first. Do not silently turn “outer box recyclable” into “entire item recyclable.” Enlarge small print, use read-aloud help, or have someone read it verbatim.

### Execution

1. **Name the object being discarded.** A paper carton and inner film are two different items. One symbol may not cover both. Copy the material and limits the label actually states.
2. **Find your local service's current rules.** Check whether your real municipal collection, building service, or reachable drop-off site accepts that **material in that form**. Technical recyclability does not mean your nearby bin accepts it. For compostable claims, distinguish a home route from a facility-only route.
3. **Test whether you can meet the route's conditions.** If the service requires separated parts, a particular condition, or a special drop-off point, decide whether you can realistically comply. An unreachable site, inseparable components, or no local facility means this claim gives you no usable disposal benefit for this purchase.
4. **Record one of three outcomes.** Mark “locally workable / not workable / unconfirmed,” with the exact component, local-rule source, and date checked. Ask the collector or delay the purchase if current rules are missing or contradictory.
5. **Return to the buying decision.** If this route is essential, neither not workable nor unconfirmed passes. If it is only a bonus, award no bonus while unconfirmed. Check purpose, price, and safety requirements in their separate workflows.

### Success and stop

You can name **which component** goes to **which local service** under **which conditions**, or clearly state why you cannot confirm that yet. A recycling triangle, leaf image, or “biodegradable” slogan cannot prove a usable local route. Recheck when collection rules change. This Skill does not promise a net environmental benefit for the whole product.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Applying a package mark to the product:** Locate the marked object again. If unclear, verify the claim before assessing the route.
- **Only a general trash bin is nearby:** Check for an official, reachable special collection point. If none exists, record not workable; do not invent a facility.
- **“Compostable” appears without a destination:** Check home versus facility conditions and local acceptance. Leave it unconfirmed or not workable when no route matches.
- **A web page conflicts with the building notice:** Ask the service that actually collects your waste for its current rule rather than guessing at the shelf.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes you know where the item will eventually be discarded and can reach the relevant collector. Rules and service hours vary by location and building. Without internet, readable print, or easy travel, call the collector, request accessible text, or choose an item that meets your needs without relying on the claim. The result informs a purchase; actual disposal follows the local rules in force then. A green mark does not arrive with its own collection truck.
