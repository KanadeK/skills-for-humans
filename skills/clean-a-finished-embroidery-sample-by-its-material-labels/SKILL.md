## 中文

当你的一片**自有小绣样已完成并从工作绷取下**，出现可去的导线或少量手汗/灰尘，想决定能否轻清时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按布、绣线和标记工具三方说明进行兼容清理并全干，或明示不水洗。它与制作线迹或背面收尾不同；贵重/历史织物、衣物穿洗要求与不明染料不按本普通练习法处理。

### 准备与输入

找布、绣线和转印笔/纸的原说明，核是否都允许水、所需温度和去标记方式；最严格的一项控制全片。DMC 的某些线与标记可洗，不等于所有品牌或布可水洗。先检查背面尾线已固定，隐蔽处用清水/合用清洁方法做小试；掉色、渗色或布缩水迹象就停。作品本来洁净且导线已藏，可选择不洗。

### 执行

1. **先定“清什么”。** 指出导线、局部表土或整体汗迹，不为追求“洗过”而浸泡整片。禁水或不明材料就记“不湿处理，转厂家/专业护理”，保护作品。
2. **只按相容说明轻清。** 如果布、线、导线均允许，按最严格标签以合用清水/温和用品轻触目标处或手洗小片；不猛搓绣线、不拧绞图案，也不把绷圈一同泡。导线按其说明去除，不用强漂白剂或不明溶剂。
3. **轻吸水、平放晾干。** 用干净毛巾轻压去水，正面针迹不受重压；平放在通风干净处，自然晾到正反面与厚针处全干。标签另有晾法时照标签；未干不入框或密闭袋。
4. **干后核对。** 看导线/表土是否减少、颜色是否稳定、布有无缩水起皱、背线是否松动；记录仍留的痕迹，必要时停在此处求对应材料指导，不重复搓坏绣面。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有依据地完成兼容清理并全干且线迹稳定，或在不允许/资料不足时明确不湿洗；作品状态可回查。湿布直接装展示绷，不算完成。

- **隐蔽小试掉色：** 立刻停水，隔离晾干并求材料对应护理。
- **导线没按说明去掉：** 查笔/纸产品要求，不用更热的水盲试。
- **线尾松或背面起长线：** 停洗、干后按收线方法处理，不继续搓。
- **布皱或缩水：** 不强拉回原形，记录实际材质反应。
- **视力/手部不便：** 小面积处理、平桌与可信协助吸水，由你核查颜色和干燥。

### 假设与边界

很多展示小绣样无需洗；只有明确兼容、实际有污物/导线时才动水。DMC 的产品色牢性不能替其他品牌背书，也不保证洗后无痕或档案级保存。

## English

Load this Skill when a **small owned embroidery sample is stitched and out of its work hoop** and removable guide marks or a little hand soil/dust call for a care decision. Finish with compatible cleaning under fabric, floss, and marking-tool directions and full drying, or an explicit no-water route. This differs from making stitches or finishing reverse threads. Historic or valuable textiles, garment laundering, and unknown dyes are outside this practice method.

### Preparation and inputs

Find actual fabric, thread, and transfer-pen/paper directions. Check all three for water permission, temperature, and mark removal; the most restrictive governs. Some DMC thread and marks wash, but that does not approve every brand or fabric. Confirm reverse tails are secure and patch-test a hidden place with the permitted cleaning route. Stop for dye transfer, bleeding, or apparent shrinkage. A clean sample with no visible guide may simply remain unwashed.

### Execution

1. **Name what needs cleaning.** Identify guide marks, local soil, or broader hand oils instead of soaking for the sake of saying it was washed. For no-water or unknown material, record “do not wet; seek maker or specialist care” and protect the piece.
2. **Clean only under compatible directions.** If fabric, floss, and guide all permit, use the strictest label with suitable water/mild product for a gentle local touch or small hand wash. Do not scrub stitches hard, wring the motif, or immerse its hoop. Remove guide under its own directions, without bleach or unknown solvents.
3. **Blot and dry flat.** Press water gently between clean towels without heavy pressure over raised stitches. Air dry flat in a clean spot until reverse and thick areas are fully dry. Follow any different label drying method. Do not frame or seal damp cloth.
4. **Inspect after drying.** Check whether guide/soil reduced, colour stayed, fabric shrank or puckered, and back threads held. Record remaining marks and seek material-specific advice instead of repeated abrasion.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Cleaning had actual label authority, ended fully dry with stable stitches, or a material conflict caused an honest no-wet hold. The state is traceable. Mounting wet fabric into a display hoop is unfinished.

- **Hidden test releases colour:** Stop water, dry separately, and seek material-specific care.
- **Guide remains:** Check the marking product rather than blindly making water hotter.
- **Tail loosens or long reverse float appears:** Stop washing and secure after drying; do not scrub farther.
- **Cloth shrinks or creases:** Do not stretch it to its old dimensions by force; record the material response.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Treat a small area at a flat table with trusted blotting help while you check colour and dryness.

### Assumptions and limits

Many display samples need no wash. Use water only for real soil/marks with proven compatibility. DMC product colourfastness cannot endorse another maker, and washing promises neither no trace nor archive conservation.
