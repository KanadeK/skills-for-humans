## 中文

当你准备买普通家用清洁用品，却还不知道标签要求的通风、防护、避开儿童或存放方式能否在家落实时加载。这里仅作**付款前适用性判断**，不教你稀释、混合、使用、除污或处理化学品。强腐蚀、剧毒、易爆等高危险产品不在本日常 Skill 的购买建议范围。

### 准备与输入

找到准确商品的完整正背面标签及说明，确认你的清洁目标、使用地点、是否有儿童或宠物会接触、是否有原包装存放位置，以及你能否提供标签要求的通风或防护。包装只给外语、二维码或极小字时，要求可访问的制造商说明或放大照片，核对型号与版本；关键警示无法读懂就暂停购买。

### 执行

1. **先读用途与警示。** 对照标签的适用表面/地点、警示词、图形和“不得”条款。品牌名或“多用途”字样不能覆盖背面的限制；图标含义按销售地区官方说明核对，不凭颜色猜。
2. **列出必须具备的条件。** 把标签要求的通风、防护、避开儿童或宠物、原容器存放和其它前置条件写成短清单。这里只确认条件是否现实可得，不执行清洁操作。
3. **对照你家的现实。** 例如使用地点没有标签要求的通风方式，或无法把原容器放在儿童拿不到的位置，就判为“不适合这次购买”。“先买了以后再想”不算满足条件。
4. **处理未知或高危险信号。** 标签不完整、说明相互矛盾、危险图形不认识、要求你做超出普通家务的化学操作，均保持“未确认/停止”。向制造商、当地主管机关或合格人员核实，或改找无需这些条件的普通商品；不在货架前试开、试闻或混合。
5. **留下购买结论。** 记为“标签条件可落实 / 明确不能落实 / 未确认”，附准确商品版本与导致决定的那条警示。第一种只表示这些要求在购买前可安排，不是无风险证明。

### 成功与停止

你能指出这件商品要求什么，以及你是否确实有对应环境与存放办法；缺任一必要条件就不买。若你已经接触商品并出现刺激、不适、漏液或异常气味，立即停止这份买前流程，远离现场并按标签及当地专业或紧急指引求助；本 Skill 不提供现场处置步骤。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看正面“温和”“通用”：** 找完整警示与限制；读不到就保留未确认。
- **把危险图形当装饰：** 查销售地区官方图例，仍不懂就不买这件。
- **家里没有安全存放位置：** 先解决实际存放条件或选另一种商品，不把原容器换成无标签瓶来省空间。
- **网页说明和实物不同：** 以准确现售版本重核对，必要时联系制造商；旧照片不能批准新配方。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设你面对的是普通家务采购，且能看到完整、适用本地区的商品标签。不同地区的危险图形和披露规则不同，加拿大示例不能充当全球标准。视觉、语言、握持或通风条件受限时可要求可访问说明、请可信的人逐字核对或直接选无需无法落实条件的用品。副作用是一瓶看起来方便的商品可能继续待在货架上。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary household cleaner when you have not checked whether the label's ventilation, protective, child-access, or storage conditions can actually be met at home. It is a **pre-purchase feasibility check**. It does not teach dilution, mixing, use, stain removal, or chemical disposal. Highly corrosive, poisonous, explosive, or similarly hazardous products are outside this everyday purchase guide.

### Preparation and inputs

Get the complete front and back label and directions for the exact item. Identify the intended cleaning task, location, children or pets who may reach it, a place to keep the original container, and whether you can provide the ventilation or protection named on the label. If the pack uses an inaccessible language, QR page, or very small print, request readable manufacturer information or enlarge a photo and confirm the variant. Pause if you cannot understand a critical warning.

### Execution

1. **Read use and warning together.** Compare permitted surfaces and settings, signal words, symbols, and “do not” clauses. A brand name or “multi-purpose” on the front cannot erase a back-panel limit. Interpret symbols using official guidance for the selling market rather than guessing from their colour.
2. **List required conditions.** Make a short list of labelled ventilation, protection, separation from children or pets, storage in the original container, and other prerequisites. For now you are checking whether these conditions exist, not cleaning anything.
3. **Compare with your real home.** If the use location lacks the ventilation the label requires, or you cannot keep the original container away from children, mark it unsuitable for this purchase. “I will work it out later” does not satisfy a condition.
4. **Keep unknowns and serious hazards out.** An incomplete label, conflicting directions, an unfamiliar hazard symbol, or directions requiring chemical work beyond ordinary household care means unconfirmed/stop. Ask the maker, local authority, or a qualified person, or choose an ordinary item without conditions you cannot meet. Do not open, smell, or mix it in the shop to test it.
5. **Record a narrow result.** Mark “label conditions feasible / clearly infeasible / unconfirmed,” with the exact variant and deciding warning. Feasible means you can plan for these stated conditions before purchase; it is not a guarantee of zero risk.

### Success and stop

You can point to the item's requirements and a real way to meet each one; reject the purchase if a necessary condition is missing. If you have already been exposed and experience irritation, a leak, or an unusual odour, stop this shopping workflow, leave the area, and follow the label and local professional or emergency guidance. This Skill gives no incident-response procedure.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Trusting only “gentle” or “multi-purpose” on the front:** Read the full warnings and limitations; keep unconfirmed if unavailable.
- **Treating a hazard symbol as decoration:** Use an official local symbol guide; if it remains unclear, do not buy this item.
- **No safe storage place exists:** Solve the storage constraint first or choose another product. Do not decant into an unlabelled bottle to save space.
- **The web page differs from the item:** Recheck the exact current version and ask the maker if necessary. An old photograph does not approve a new formula.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary household purchase and a complete label applicable to your market. Hazard symbols and disclosure rules differ by region; the Canadian example is not a worldwide standard. If vision, language, grip, or ventilation is a barrier, request accessible directions, review exact wording with a trusted person, or choose a product without conditions you cannot meet. An apparently convenient bottle may remain on the shelf.
