## 中文

当某件普通搬迁负载已经确定、容器也已选好，但你还没有决定谁搬、是否需要合适工具或专业搬运时，在任何人提起来之前加载。结果是**一条有实际人员与路线依据的处理安排，或暂缓**。本 Skill 不示范提举姿势，不为任何人设通用安全重量，也不处理钢琴、大件承重家具、危险设备或需要结构改造的通道。

### 准备与输入

确认这一件负载的可查重量范围、形状、把手或握点、完整容器状态，以及已核对的出入口、楼梯、电梯和转角。请可能搬的人自己说明能否看清前路、握稳和完成这段路，是否愿意参加；不问医疗诊断。手推车等辅助工具也要有实际可用的额定条件与通道。

### 执行

1. **先把路线和负载写清。** 哪一段要提、哪一段能用车，是否有台阶、窄门或没有地方放下。路线尺寸未确认时先做通道预检，不靠搬到门口才发现过不去。
2. **给实际人员一次否决权。** 如果物品笨重、看不到脚下、握点不可靠、容器变形，或任何参与者认为自己无法稳妥处理，就不要试提来“确认一下”。先减轻/拆成允许的较小单元、选择适用辅助工具，或请合适的搬运人员。
3. **核对帮助真的存在。** 写下愿意且有能力的人、到场时段、负责哪一段；需要手推车或专业服务时，确认设备/服务可用且适合这条路线。一个“到时叫人”不是已安排的协助。
4. **把高风险留在范围外。** 需要吊装、拆门、拆设备、危险化学处理或无法核实载重的情况停止普通自助路线，交给对应专业方决定。疼痛、失稳或设备异常出现时停止继续搬运，必要时求助。
5. **写出决定。** 记录“负载/路线 → 谁负责与愿意程度 → 工具或帮手 → 未确认条件 → 可开始/暂缓”。只有条件均真实可用才进入实际搬运；这份表不保证动作本身安全。

### 成功与停止

你能指出实际能处理该负载的人和辅助条件，参与者知晓路线且可以拒绝；或者负载被拆小、改由专业人员评估或暂缓。没有核实路线、人员同意或设备适用性时，不因搬家日程紧就临时硬搬。

### 常见报错与补救

- **箱子不重但太大，挡住视线：** 改小容器或安排合适协助，不按重量一项宣布可搬。
- **有人答应帮忙却没确认时间：** 保持“未安排”，等到明确时段和职责后再排这件。
- **推车过不了门槛：** 暂停该工具路线，核对实际通道与工具条件，必要时换专业方案。
- **参与者说不舒服或走不稳：** 立即停止让其承担此负载；不追问身体原因或继续劝试一次。
- **清单或路线难看清：** 用大字、语音、现场同行说明，让实际搬运者确认能辨认路线；仍不能确认就暂缓。

### 假设、替代与副作用

每个人的能力、路线、箱子和工具都不同，职业搬运资料只支持“笨重或不好握时求助”的方向，不提供家庭通用重量保证。本 Skill 只作搬前判断；实际搬运应由合适人员按具体物品、设备和所在地要求执行。行动、视觉或听觉不方便时可以负责清单与交接，不应被默认安排提举。副作用是“我一个人应该行”必须补上真实人手和路线。

## English

Load this Skill **before anyone lifts** an ordinary moving load whose contents and container are known, but whose handler, aid or professional route has not been decided. Your result is **a handling plan backed by real people and route conditions, or a pause**. It does not demonstrate lifting technique, assign one universal safe weight, or cover pianos, load-bearing furniture, hazardous equipment or building alterations.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the best available load-weight information, shape, handle or grip points, soundness of the container, and the checked entrances, stairs, lift and turns. Each possible handler should state whether they can see the route, grip the load and complete it, and whether they agree; no medical diagnosis is needed. A trolley or other aid also needs an applicable rating and a route it can actually use.

### Execution

1. **Describe the load and route.** Mark where carrying is needed, where a trolley is possible, and any step, narrow door or lack of a safe set-down point. If route dimensions are unknown, get a route check rather than discovering the problem at the doorway with a load in hand.
2. **Give actual handlers a veto.** If the item is awkward, blocks the view, lacks a secure grip, deforms its container, or any participant judges they cannot manage it steadily, do not trial-lift to “make sure.” Lighten or split it into permitted smaller units, choose a suitable aid, or use capable professional help.
3. **Check that help really exists.** Name a willing capable person, their arrival window and the segment they will handle. For a trolley or professional service, confirm it is actually available and suitable for this route. “We'll find someone on the day” is not arranged help.
4. **Keep high-risk work outside this route.** Hoisting, removing doors, dismantling equipment, hazardous handling or an unverified load rating stops ordinary self-handling; ask the relevant professional to decide. Stop if pain, instability or equipment failure appears and seek appropriate help.
5. **Record the decision.** Write “load/route → willing handler → aid or helper → unknowns → ready to start/defer.” Only real available conditions can lead to handling. This planning card does not certify the later movement.

### Success and stop

You can name the actual people and aids for this load, and each participant knows the route and may refuse; or the load is split, sent for professional assessment or deferred. Unknown access, consent or aid suitability is a reason to stop despite a moving-day deadline.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The box is light but blocks sight:** Use a smaller container or suitable help. Weight alone does not clear the route.
- **Someone offers help without a confirmed time:** Keep the task unarranged until the time and role are explicit.
- **A trolley cannot cross the threshold:** Pause that aid route and check the actual path and device; use a professional alternative if needed.
- **A participant feels pain or unsteady:** Stop assigning this load to them. Do not demand a diagnosis or urge one more attempt.
- **The route or note is inaccessible:** Use large print, voice or an in-person description and have actual handlers confirm they can identify the route. Defer if it remains unclear.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

People, routes, boxes and aids differ. Occupational moving guidance supports asking for help with bulky or uncontrollable loads; it does not supply a safe household weight for everyone. This Skill ends before lifting. Suitable people must carry out any actual move under the item, equipment and local conditions. A person with mobility, sight or hearing limits may own the list and handoff without being assumed to lift. The side effect is that “I can probably do it alone” needs real people and a real route.
