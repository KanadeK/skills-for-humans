## 中文

当你的一件**已塑形、尚湿的普通风干黏土小装饰物**需要从湿态到可上色/可摆放的干态时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按本品条件充分晾干、无明显软芯或新裂的作品，或诚实隔离仍未干/已裂的作品。表面摸着不黏不等于内部干透；不用烤箱、微波、吹风机或明火催干。

### 准备与输入

读包装标示的厚度、干燥时间、温湿条件与翻动建议；Crayola 的“约 24 小时表干、约 72 小时全干”只适用于其产品且受尺寸环境影响。准备许可的稳定通风室内位置和不会染坏家具的可移垫板，远离食物、热源、宠物及通道。大的、厚薄差极大的或带刚性骨架的作品不按此小件流程保证。

### 执行

1. **稳放并记起点。** 让作品在垫板上自稳，不靠墙或压住细节；标注开始日期、产品名及“未干勿碰”。若平片的底面被完全闷住，按产品允许的方式用架或适时轻翻以让空气接触，不在尚软时猛翻。
2. **按本品说明等待。** 维持普通室内通风与安全位置，不把产品示例时数当闹钟一响就上色。看到外部表干，只记“表干”，继续等全干门槛与内侧/底部变化。
3. **分阶段检查。** 用干净手在不易损处轻触，观察底部、厚处和接缝是否仍凉湿、软、颜色与已干区不同；看是否翘曲或出现新裂。无法确认时多等并保持不碰，而不是用尖物戳芯检查。
4. **给下一步骤一个真实状态。** 全件按产品说明与实物检查已干且无明显结构损坏，才标记“可按标签装饰”；若局部仍软或裂，记下位置并转处理，不用颜料盖住湿裂。作品仍只可室内装饰，不承诺防水。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

作品在稳定位置晾至产品要求并通过厚处/底部检查，或仍湿/损坏被明确记录并停在这一步。只测上表面、不查底面而称“全干”，不算验收。

- **表干但厚处发软：** 延长通风等待，暂不上色或封装。
- **平片边缘翘起：** 按本品允许方式改善两面空气接触；已脆时别强压回平。
- **新裂或接缝分离：** 先隔离评估裂缝，不靠再快干解决；必要时按小裂补救或重做。
- **台面被染或作品粘住：** 下次用合适可移垫板；本次勿硬从家具剥离。
- **难以查看背面：** 在足够硬且可安全翻时请可信协助者轻托垫板，避免搬动脆弱湿件。

### 假设与边界

厂家时间是材料特定估计，实物厚度与环境改变干燥。干透也不会变成窑烧陶器；封涂亦不必然防水。本篇不处理大型雕塑、专业湿度控制或强制热源。

## English

Load this Skill when a **small shaped ordinary air-dry clay decoration** must move from wet to sufficiently dry for later paint or display. Finish with a piece dry under its own product directions, without an obvious soft interior or new crack, or an honest hold for one still damp or damaged. Dry to touch does not mean dry through. Do not speed it with an oven, microwave, hair dryer, or flame.

### Preparation and inputs

Read package thickness, drying time, humidity, and turning directions. Crayola's roughly 24 hours to touch and 72 hours to full dry are product-specific estimates affected by size and conditions. Choose a permitted stable airy indoor spot on a movable board that cannot stain furniture, away from food, heat, pets, and traffic. Large, highly uneven, or rigid-armature forms are not guaranteed by this small-piece path.

### Execution

1. **Set it down and mark the start.** Keep the object stable on its board without pressing detail against a wall. Mark date, product, and “wet, do not touch.” For flat pieces whose underside is trapped, use a rack or gentle later turn only if product directions and material firmness permit; do not flip soft clay abruptly.
2. **Wait under actual product directions.** Maintain ordinary indoor airflow and safe placement. Do not paint just because an example number of hours has passed. Call an outer dry feel only “dry to touch” and wait for full-dry guidance plus base and thick-part checks.
3. **Check in stages.** Touch lightly in an inconspicuous place with clean hands. Look at base, thick spots, and seams for cool dampness, softness, or colour difference from dry parts, plus new warping or cracks. If uncertain, wait longer without prodding the centre with a point.
4. **Give the next task an honest state.** Mark “ready to decorate under label” only when product guidance and the whole piece support dryness without obvious structural failure. Note and route a damp area or crack instead of painting over it. The piece remains indoor decoration, not waterproof ware.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The piece has waited under its product rules and passes checks at thick parts and base, or a wet/damaged condition is recorded and work pauses. Checking only the top and declaring full dryness is not acceptance.

- **Surface dry but thick part soft:** Continue aired waiting; delay paint or boxing.
- **Flat edge warps:** Improve two-sided airflow as the product permits. Do not force a brittle dry edge flat.
- **New crack or separated seam:** Isolate and assess; faster drying will not solve it. Use a small-crack route if suitable or remake.
- **Surface stains or piece sticks:** Use a movable protective board next time and do not pry clay from fragile furniture.
- **Underside is hard to inspect:** Once firm enough to move safely, ask trusted help to support the board, avoiding a fragile wet lift.

### Assumptions and limits

Manufacturer times are material-specific estimates; thickness and environment change drying. Fully dry air-dry clay is not kiln-fired ceramic and a coating does not automatically waterproof it. No large sculpture, professional humidity control, or forced heat is covered.
