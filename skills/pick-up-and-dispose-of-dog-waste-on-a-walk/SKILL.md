## 中文

当你牵的熟悉狗在允许通行处排便，而废物需要你现在收走时，加载这份 Skill。终点是**排泄物被装好、按真实本地规则投入可用去处、手部完成清洁**。完成散步不代表路边那袋会自行消失。

### 准备与输入

先让狗在你能稳妥牵住的安全位置，确认不会为了拾取而跑到车道或逼近别的动物。准备完好袋和备用袋、可用的拾取辅助工具和附近实际准许的垃圾点；没有公共箱时带回按本地规则处理。儿童不操作污物。看见尖锐物或未知危险物与废物混在一起时先停止徒手拾取并按场地规则求助。

### 执行

1. **先控狗和现场。** 缩小它可以冲出去的范围，但不要把绳缠在手腕或固定到不稳物体。确认交通与行人安全，再蹲下或使用可及拾取器。
2. **让袋成为隔层。** 检查无破洞，隔着袋或工具收集看得到的排泄物，翻合、扎紧且避免手碰内容物。若袋破损，用备用袋再套住；没有备用就暂停投放并找实际清理办法，不甩掉不管。
3. **走真实投放路线。** 看当地标识或管理方说明，投到允许收犬只废物的箱；不要猜厨余、家用堆肥或可回收桶能接收，也不要把封袋留在路边待“以后”。
4. **洗手并补库存。** 回到有水处用肥皂洗手，CDC 提醒即使用了袋也要洗。没有即时水源时按公共卫生指导用可用洗手替代并尽快用水；回家补足下次袋数。
5. **记录异常而不诊断。** 明显血迹、持续腹泻或狗其他异常时联系兽医；不能用捡拾步骤解释病因。

### 成功条件

现场无你这只狗留下的可见排泄物，袋有已核实的实际投放或安全带回状态，狗仍受控，双手已清洁。没有袋、狗失控或投放路线不明时这是未完成项，先保证现场安全并安排合法实际后续。

### 常见报错与补救

- **拿起袋才发现漏：** 再套完好袋，避免污染手和公共物体。
- **只有回收桶：** 不把污袋投入，带到准许的去处。
- **狗往人群冲：** 先控制狗并请合适的人协助，不能蹲下后放任牵引。
- **手碰到污物：** 避免碰面部/食物，尽快按公共卫生指导用肥皂水洗净。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一只已由你照料、可控的狗在普通步行处的一次排泄物收拾，不教传染病诊断、咬伤处置或危险废物清理。弯腰困难时带适配拾取器或请能控制狗并清理的人协助；手部敏感时可用适合个人情况的防护但仍需洗手。真实副作用是带着袋到准许去处，以及补袋。

## English

Load this Skill when a familiar dog you are walking has defecated in a permitted area and you need to collect it now. Finish with **waste contained, delivered to an actually permitted local route, and your hands cleaned**. Completing a walk will not make a bag beside a tree disappear.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the dog safely under control without stepping into traffic or pressing toward another animal. Have an intact bag and spare or an appropriate pickup aid, plus a real permitted disposal point. If no public bin is available, take the sealed bag home under local rules. Children do not handle the waste. Stop bare-hand pickup for sharp or unknown hazardous material mixed in and follow site guidance.

### Execution

1. **Control dog and surroundings first.** Limit its reach without wrapping a lead around your wrist or tying it to an unstable object. Check people and traffic before bending or using an accessible pickup tool.
2. **Make the bag a barrier.** Check for holes, collect visible waste through the bag or tool, invert/close and tie without hand contact. Double-bag a leak with a spare. Without a spare, secure the situation and find a real cleaning route; dropping it is not resolution.
3. **Use an actual disposal route.** Read local signs or operator guidance and place it in a bin that accepts dog waste. Do not assume food scraps, home compost, or recycling does. Never leave a sealed bag by the path for “later.”
4. **Wash hands and restock.** Use soap and water at the next facility. CDC notes handwashing is required even after bagged pickup. If water is temporarily unavailable, use a public-health-supported interim method and wash with water as soon as possible. Restock bags at home.
5. **Record unusual signs without diagnosis.** Blood, persistent diarrhea, or other changes call for veterinary contact; pickup cannot explain the cause.

### Success

The spot has no visible waste from your dog, the bag has a verified disposal or safe carry-home state, the dog remains controlled, and hands are cleaned. Missing bags, loss of control, or unknown disposal leave an unfinished task; secure the site and arrange a lawful actual follow-up.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The bag leaks after pickup:** Cover it with a sound spare bag and avoid contaminating hands and public surfaces.
- **Only a recycling bin exists:** Keep the bag for a permitted route.
- **The dog rushes a crowd:** Secure dog control and get capable help before bending down.
- **Waste touches a hand:** Avoid face and food and wash thoroughly with soap and water promptly under public-health guidance.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is one ordinary waste pickup during a walk with a familiar controllable dog, not infection diagnosis, bite response, or hazardous-waste cleanup. Use a suitable reach tool or capable helper if bending is difficult. Personal protection does not replace handwashing. The bag must go somewhere permitted and supplies need replenishment.
