## 中文

当你已经有普通低敏感纸稿，需要在公开复印机或服务点做一份/几份可读副本时加载。目标是**副本内容和页数正确、原件与所有输出都被你拿回，设备上没有你能控制的明显遗留**。从手机/电脑传文件去打印是另一项任务。证件、财务、病例或其他高敏感原件不适合在留存方式不明的公共设备上照此操作；法律与版权许可也不由这份 Skill 判定。

### 准备与输入

查场所现在是否提供复印，哪些原稿允许自助复制、是否需工作人员批准、收费与机器位置。场所馆藏、易损装订材料及别人的文件可能有限制；不是“机器能扫描就一定能复印”。整理你自己的纸张顺序、需要的页数、每页份数、单双面与颜色，先移除无关私人附页。机器若说明会保存扫描任务，留存不明就不要放入敏感原稿。

### 执行

1. **先核对原件和权限。** 点一下原稿页数、正反面及是否有订书钉、折页或易损边。非本人/馆藏材料按场所实际许可问工作人员；无需拆毁装订或强行塞入进纸器。
2. **按机器标识摆放一页。** 用机身图示选择玻璃板或文件进纸器的正反面与方向；薄、破、装订或不适合进纸的原件先问工作人员适用方式。把扫描范围对齐，合盖或按现场要求操作，不把原件留在旁边无人看管。
3. **设定再少量试印。** 看纸型、缩放、彩色/黑白、单双面和份数及可见费用，必要时先做一张测试；确认文字边缘、上下方向和背面是否符合用途，再完成剩余副本。不要因按错份数连续点击直到机器吐出一堆无人要的纸。
4. **本人取回原件和全部副本。** 先从玻璃板/进纸器收回原稿，按原顺序点页数；再取出出纸口与任何输出托盘的副本，检查第一页、最后一页、总张数和是否有他人文稿混入。卡纸或原件被机器夹住时停止拉扯，请正式工作人员处理。
5. **收尾可见痕迹。** 按该机提供的功能结束自己的复印任务、清除预览或退出公共账号，取走付款卡/零钱/USB（如有），检查台面与进纸盘没有你的一页纸。清掉屏幕预览不等于服务商后台一定不留扫描数据；需要保密保障时另用可核实的渠道。

### 完成与停止

你手中有可读、份数正确的副本和完整原件，机上你能看到/控制的任务已经结束，周围无自己的遗留纸张，本轮完成。**副本完成不表示服务商已删除任何内部扫描记录。** 若原件受损、机器吞纸、机构规定不得复印或隐私要求无法满足，停止自助操作并找工作人员或可控替代。

### 常见报错与补救

- **出纸是空白或上下颠倒：** 查玻璃板/进纸器图示，先用不敏感的一页测试摆放方向。
- **边缘被切掉或文字太小：** 检查纸型与缩放，只修正受影响页，别重复整叠。
- **原稿留在玻璃板/进纸器：** 离开机器前先数原件再数副本；发现遗漏立即回到机器并按现场规则找回。
- **馆藏/装订/脆弱原件：** 问场所是否允许、是否须由工作人员操作，不擅自拆页或压平书脊。
- **机器卡纸或多收费用：** 保留实际页面/付款状态并请工作人员按场所流程处理，不强拉原件或假定自动退款。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通低敏感纸稿和公开可用机器，不解释版权、证件、医疗、金融或法律用途。场所政策、价格和机器布局不同，以当前实际服务为准。视觉、行动或设备使用困难时，请场所提供可及设备/正式协助或可信陪同，且只交付此次确需复印的普通纸张。副作用是纸更多、费用可能发生；每张原件仍由你负责带走。

## English

Load this Skill when you already have an ordinary low-sensitivity paper original and need one or several readable copies at a public copier or service desk. Your result is **correct pages and copy count, with original and every output back in your hands and no visible trace you can control left on the machine**. Sending a phone or computer file to a printer is a different task. IDs, financial, medical and similarly sensitive originals need a more controlled service when retention is unknown. This Skill does not decide legal or copyright permission.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether the venue currently copies, what originals it allows for self-service, whether staff approval is needed, cost and machine position. Venue collections, fragile bindings and other people's material may be restricted; a machine that can scan is not permission to copy. Order your own sheets and choose pages, copies per page, sides and colour, removing unrelated private inserts. If the machine says it stores scanned jobs but retention is unclear, do not place sensitive originals in it.

### Execution

1. **Check original and permission first.** Count originals, fronts and backs, and notice staples, folds or fragile edges. For material that is not yours or belongs to a collection, ask staff about this venue's permission. Do not break a binding or force it into a feeder.
2. **Place one page by the machine's guide.** Use its diagram for face and orientation on the glass or feeder. Ask staff how to handle thin, damaged, bound or unsuitable paper. Align the intended area and close or operate it as directed, keeping control of the original.
3. **Set options and make a small test.** Check size, scaling, colour or black and white, sides, copies and visible charge. If unsure, copy one page and inspect text edges, orientation and reverse before doing the rest. A mistaken copies field is not fixed by pressing Start repeatedly.
4. **Retrieve original and every copy yourself.** First take the source sheets from glass or feeder and count them in original order. Then collect output from all trays, checking first and last pages, total sheets and any other person's paper. For a jam or trapped original, stop pulling and ask official staff.
5. **Close visible traces.** End your task, clear a preview or sign out of a public account using actual machine features. Take your payment card, change or USB if used, and inspect the desk and feeder for your pages. Clearing the visible preview does not prove a provider erased an internal scan. Use a service with verifiable privacy when that matters.

### Success and stop

You have readable copies in the right count and the complete original, your controllable visible job is closed, and no paper of yours is left around the machine. **Finished copies do not prove the provider deleted internal scans.** Stop self-service for a damaged or trapped original, venue restriction or privacy requirement this service cannot meet, and ask staff or choose a controlled alternative.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Blank or upside-down output:** Follow glass or feeder diagrams and test one harmless page for face and orientation.
- **An edge is cut or text too small:** Check paper size and scale, then redo only affected pages.
- **Original stays on the glass or in the feeder:** Count originals before copies before leaving. Return promptly under venue rules if a page is missing.
- **Collection item, binding or fragile original:** Ask if copying is allowed or staff-operated; do not pull pages or flatten the spine by force.
- **Jam or extra charge:** Keep the real pages and payment state for staff to handle under venue rules; do not yank the original or assume an automatic refund.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for ordinary low-sensitivity paper and a publicly available copier, not copyright, identity, medical, financial or legal advice. Venue policy, price and machine layout vary. For sight, mobility or device-use needs, request accessible equipment, official staff help or a trusted companion, and share only ordinary pages needed for this job. You get more paper and may incur a charge; each original still has to leave with you.
