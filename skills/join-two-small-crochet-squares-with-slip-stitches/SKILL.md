## 中文

当你已经有**两片独立完成、可对齐边缘的普通钩针小方片**，想用引拔针把它们接成一块非承重练习面板时，加载这份 Skill。结果是角对角、整条缝保持连接而不拉皱的双片结构；它与钩出单片或从一团旧线接新线不同。若两片尺寸差很大、针目难对应，不强拉成同长。

### 准备与输入

先确认两片断线藏尾完成、材质护理相容，选长度接近的一条边；用可移标记指出两个起角和末角。备合用线、钝钩针、平整台面与好光线。这里用美式 crochet slip stitch（引拔针）；成衣、婴儿用品、承重包带和必须很平的隐形缝，需要相应图样或专业要求。

### 执行

1. **先在桌上空摆。** 两片正面相对（若希望缝在背面），对齐起角、末角与中段相应针顶；相差一两个针位时先决定均匀对应方案，不把多针全挤到一个角。无法对应就停并先重做/整理片边。
2. **在起角固定接线。** 钩针穿过两片对应的第一针顶线，带入新线形成一活圈，钩一个起始锁针作固定；留够后续藏入的尾。钩针始终同时穿过两片，不只钩表面那片。
3. **逐对做引拔针。** 每组对应针位从前片穿到后片，绕工作线后一次拉过两片与钩上活圈；逐对向末角前进。线张力只需让缝贴合，不把两片边缩成皱褶。中途转面看是否漏接一片。
4. **锁尾并检缝。** 末角引拔后留尾、将尾拉过活圈固定，按各自织纹藏起头/末尾。轻打开面板看缝是否贯通、角仍相对、边没有硬拉卷起；必要时拆回太紧的一段重做。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

两片沿选定整边相连、角对应、缝不脱或明显拉皱，线尾有稳定收法。只在角上打结、两片中段分离，不算接成面板。

- **一片多出很多边针：** 停并重选匹配片，不把多针堆在角上。
- **缝越钩越紧：** 退回几针放松张力，不拉扯已完成片形。
- **钩针只穿过单片：** 回到漏接点重做，检查每枚都穿两层。
- **角错位或缝扭转：** 先拆到对齐点，重新摆正标角。
- **手眼不便：** 用对比色临时标记、桌面平放与可信协助对位，由你轻检缝的连续性。

### 假设与边界

引拔缝的可见度、厚度和弹性随线材与针目不同；这是一块非承重学习面板，不承诺服装接缝强度或图案的商业版权许可。

## English

Load this Skill when **two separate finished ordinary crochet squares have edges that can align** and you want a non-load-bearing practice panel joined with slip stitches. Finish with corners paired and one continuous seam that neither releases nor puckers badly. This differs from crocheting one square or joining a new yarn ball. Do not pull greatly mismatched squares to the same length by force.

### Preparation and inputs

Check both squares are fastened off with ends secure and their yarn care compatible. Pick two edges of similar length and mark starting, ending, and middle corresponding stitches removably. Have suitable yarn, blunt hook, flat table, and light. This uses a crochet slip stitch under US terminology. Garments, infant items, load-bearing straps, and invisible flat seams have their own pattern or professional requirements.

### Execution

1. **Lay out before stitching.** Put right sides together if the seam should sit behind, aligning start and end corners and middle stitch tops. If one or two positions differ, choose an even correspondence rather than stuffing extras into one corner. Stop and remake or prepare edges when they cannot align.
2. **Attach yarn at first corner.** Put hook through both matching first stitch tops, draw in new yarn for one live loop, and chain once to hold it, leaving a tail for later weaving. The hook must enter both layers, not just the front square.
3. **Slip stitch through each pair.** At each matching pair, enter front then back, yarn over and draw through both pieces and the live hook loop in one motion. Continue to the ending corner with enough tension to meet edges but not pucker them. Turn the work briefly to check neither layer has been missed.
4. **Secure tails and inspect.** After the last slip stitch, leave a tail and pull it through the live loop, then weave beginning and ending tails under their fabric texture. Open the panel gently to check a continuous seam, aligned corners, and no forced curling. Undo and redo an overtight segment if needed.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The two squares connect along the chosen full edge with corners matched, no open seam, and tails secured without strong puckering. A knot at only the corners with separated middle edges is not a joined panel.

- **One square has many extra edge stitches:** Stop for a closer pair rather than pile stitches in a corner.
- **Seam becomes too tight:** Undo a few stitches and ease tension rather than stretching finished fabric.
- **Hook passes through one layer only:** Return to the missed pair and remake, checking both layers every time.
- **Corners shift or seam twists:** Undo to the alignment point and restore the layout and markers.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Contrast removable markers, flat table, and trusted alignment help can support your gentle continuous-seam check.

### Assumptions and limits

Slip seam visibility, bulk, and give vary with yarn and stitch. This is a non-load-bearing learning panel, not guaranteed garment-seam strength or permission to reuse a commercial design.
