## 中文

当普通微波加热中出现**火花、异常烟、焦味或设备仍在开门时运行**，加载这份 Skill。目标是**立即停止加热、让人离开危险，并在安全后分别决定食物与设备的去向**。若有可见火苗、浓烟持续加重、无法安全关机或电气危险，按当地紧急指导疏散求助；本 Skill 不教灭火、拆机或自行修门封。

### 准备与输入

不为找火花来源把门立刻拉开。先判断你能否从安全位置按停止键或关闭电源、是否有人靠近机器、出口是否可通行。手机、勺、金属边包装、容器材料、食物溅出等可能是线索，但不要求在机器仍工作时去取证。门不能可靠关闭或开门仍运行本身就要停用，不以“关上门再试”恢复。

### 执行步骤

1. **在安全可达时停止运行。** 按停止键或按机型说明关机，远离门与排气路径。火或烟仍存在时保持门关着，不开门供氧或伸手取容器。能否安全断开电源以当地紧急指导和机型说明为准；触电风险下不碰插头。
2. **判断是否已超出日常流程。** 火苗、烟持续变浓、异常声响、电气气味、门或控制失效时离开、让旁人撤离并按当地消防/紧急渠道求助。不要往微波炉内泼水，不搬机器去水槽，也不在旁边等它“自己烤完”。
3. **只有完全安全后才考虑检查。** 停机、烟散、机器与容器冷却且确切说明允许时，再看是否放了不适用金属/容器、是否有包装融化、门或腔体是否损伤。不要徒手摸热物或打开设备外壳；看不清也可以直接停用交合格支持。
4. **把设备与食物分开判。** 门、腔体、转盘、电线或运行有异常时停用并联系制造商/合格人员，不能清掉错误码再当没发生。食品包装熔化、烟污染或时间温度史不明时不要凭外观试吃，按食品安全指导处理；食物损失不要求你冒险继续加热。
5. **记录本次停止，不在现场重启试错。** 记下食品、容器、机型、发生了什么和可安全看到的提示，供正式支持渠道排查；不在仍有疑点的机器里放一杯水“测它还会不会冒火花”。

### 完成与停止

机器已停，人员远离危险，食物和设备分别有明确“安全时另行处理 / 停用求助”的结果，就是完成。火、持续烟、电气危险或门安全失效立即退出日常流程，按当地紧急渠道处理；不以保住一份晚餐为终点。

### 常见报错与补救

- **看见火花就打开门找金属：** 先停机并保持安全距离；有火/烟时门保持关闭，求助优先。
- **把食物拿出后再运行空炉测试：** 不做；某些机器也不允许空炉运行，异常设备交合格支持。
- **以为小火花是正常灯光：** 不确定时按异常停止，不用下一次加热验证判断。
- **发现包装局部融化仍想吃：** 不凭未融部分推断安全，按食品安全指导保守处理。
- **门有损坏但关得上：** 停用并联系制造商/合格人员，不自行扳回门或拆封条。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只处理停止和隔离，设备维修、火灾应对及烫伤治疗不在范围内。不同机型开关/电源位置、内部材料和错误提示不同；遵守确切型号说明与当地紧急规则。视听不便时利用可及警报或现场可信人的描述，但不为了确认火花靠近仍运行的炉门。副作用是一份食物可能报废，设备也可能暂停使用；火花不是烹饪附赠特效。

## English

Load this Skill when **sparks, abnormal smoke, a scorched smell, or operation with the door open** appears during ordinary microwaving. **Stop heating immediately, keep people out of danger, and decide separately what happens to the food and oven once safe.** Visible flame, thickening smoke, an unreachable off control, or electrical danger requires local emergency help. This is not firefighting, disassembly, or a door-seal repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Do not open the door immediately to find the spark. Judge whether you can press stop or switch off safely, who is nearby, and whether the exit is clear. A phone, spoon, metal-trimmed pack, vessel, or spilled food may be clues, but none requires evidence collection while the oven runs. A door that will not close securely or an oven that runs open is a stop condition by itself; closing it and retrying is not a fix.

### Execution steps

1. **Stop operation when safely reachable.** Use the stop control or the exact model's off method and step away from door and vent. Keep the door closed while fire or smoke remains; do not open it for oxygen or reach for a dish. Disconnect power only if local emergency guidance and the model permit it safely. Do not touch a plug under electrical risk.
2. **Decide whether the everyday flow has ended.** Flame, growing smoke, abnormal noise, electrical odour, or door or control failure means leave, move others clear, and call local emergency help. Do not pour water into the oven, carry it to a sink, or wait beside it for the timer to finish.
3. **Inspect only after it is fully safe.** Once stopped, smoke cleared, equipment and vessel cooled, and this manual permits access, check for unsuitable metal or vessel, melted packaging, and door or cavity damage. Do not touch hot objects bare-handed or remove covers from the appliance. Stopping use for qualified support without investigating is valid.
4. **Judge equipment and food separately.** Faulty door, cavity, turntable, cord, or operation calls for maker or qualified support, not resetting an error and pretending nothing occurred. Melted packaging, smoke exposure, or unknown food time and temperature history cannot be declared edible by appearance. Use applicable food-safety guidance; losing food is preferable to risky reheating.
5. **Record the stop without a test restart.** Note food, vessel, model, event, and only alerts visible from safety for a formal support route. Do not place a cup of water in a suspect oven to see whether it sparks again.

### Success and stop

The oven is off, people are away from danger, and food and equipment each have an explicit safe-later or stop-for-help path. Flame, continuing smoke, electrical risk, and door safety failure move directly to local emergency guidance. Saving dinner is not the endpoint.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Opening at the first spark to look for metal:** Stop and keep distance. Keep the door closed if fire or smoke is present and seek help.
- **Running an empty oven after removing food to test it:** Do not; some models prohibit empty operation, and a faulty oven belongs to qualified support.
- **Calling a small spark normal light:** When unsure, stop for an abnormality rather than using the next heating as a test.
- **Eating around partly melted packaging:** Unmelted appearance does not prove food safety; follow food-safety guidance conservatively.
- **The door is damaged but still closes:** Stop use and contact maker or qualified service instead of bending or opening a seal yourself.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This only stops and isolates a problem. Equipment repair, fire response, and burn treatment are outside it. Off controls, power, internal materials, and alerts differ by model; follow the exact manual and local emergency rules. Use accessible alarms or a trusted person's description for sight or hearing limits, but do not approach a running door merely to confirm a spark. The food or oven may be unavailable; sparks are not a cooking bonus effect.
