## 中文

当你的**自有水彩练习纸**上有一小处颜色太重、流过边界或需要留亮，想试着局部提浅时，加载这份 Skill。结果是损伤受控的浅色区，或确认该颜料/纸提不动而停止。它不是叠色，也不是刮掉纸纤维；尤其不对借来的画、重要原作或纸质文件做试验。

### 准备与输入

先判断颜料还湿还是已干，找相近的边角/废纸试轻触。不同水彩有染色性，干后可能几乎不能取回白纸；特殊预处理媒介的示范不适用于普通未处理纸。准备干净软笔或无绒软布、少量清水、吸水纸和稳定台面。纸已起毛、破或旧层很脆时停止。

### 执行

1. **限定要变浅的小区。** 看清多余色究竟在哪里，旁边哪些颜色必须保留。先在不显眼处测试一笔水和轻触，确认纸没起毛、邻色没大范围流动。
2. **轻湿、轻吸。** 若颜料仍湿，用干净吸水纸一角轻碰多余液；若已干，用几乎不滴水的软笔轻润目标点，再用干净纸轻吸。一次只处理小范围，不用刷子来回磨。
3. **停下看实际结果。** 颜色略浅就先停止并平放干燥；若根本不浅、纸起毛、边界扩散或底层被带起，立即停止，接受残色或在干后重新规划作品。不要靠漂白剂、刮刀、酒精或强力冲水。
4. **干后核对。** 看目标处是否比原来浅、纸面还完整、旁边颜色没有被拖脏。即使留痕，也记录纸/颜料限制；只称“提浅”，不许诺恢复原白。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

你在小范围内得到可见变浅且纸未受损，或及时确认无法安全提色并停手。扩大损伤来追求完美白纸，不算修复。

- **颜料牢牢染进纸：** 承认染色限制，转为画面的一部分或另起练习；不磨穿纸。
- **纸起毛、发软或破：** 立即停、平放干，后续换更适合的纸。
- **提色水流到邻色：** 先吸走多余水，干后再判断；下一次缩小湿润范围。
- **反复吸也不变浅：** 停止；更多次数不等于会出现白色。
- **手眼精细操作不便：** 放大小区、用粗一点的软笔或可信协助定位，仍由你决定停点。

### 假设与边界

颜料染色性、纸表面和干燥阶段决定提色上限。生产商展示的特殊提色预处理涂层不是本 Skill 的默认条件；此处不保证完全去色，不处理馆藏或他人作品。

## English

Load this Skill when a **small area of your own watercolour practice paper** is too dark, has crossed an edge, or needs more light, and you want to try lifting it gently. Finish with a lighter area without paper damage, or a clear stop when this paint and paper will not release colour. This is neither glazing nor scraping away fibres. Do not experiment on borrowed, important, or archival work.

### Preparation and inputs

Notice whether the mark is wet or dry and test a light touch on a similar margin or scrap first. Some pigments stain and may not return to white after drying. A branded lifting-preparation coating in demonstrations is not a default property of ordinary paper. Set out clean soft brush or lint-free cloth, a little clean water, absorbent paper, and a stable surface. Stop before starting if the sheet is already pilling, torn, or fragile.

### Execution

1. **Define a small target.** Identify the excess colour and neighbouring marks that must stay. Test one gentle wet touch somewhere inconspicuous; paper should not pill and adjacent colour should not run far.
2. **Dampen and blot lightly.** If still wet, touch excess liquid with a clean absorbent corner. If dry, lightly dampen just the target with a nearly drip-free soft brush, then blot with clean paper. Work in one small area without scrubbing back and forth.
3. **Stop at the actual limit.** Once colour lightens, pause and dry flat. If no lifting happens, fibres pill, edges spread, or the underlayer moves, stop and accept the stain or replan after drying. Do not use bleach, blades, alcohol, or forceful rinsing.
4. **Check when dry.** Compare target lightness, intact surface, and clean neighbouring marks. Record a remaining stain as a material limit. Call it lightening, not guaranteed restoration of paper white.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You either lighten a small area visibly without harming the sheet or stop promptly when lifting cannot be done safely. Enlarging paper damage to chase perfect white is not recovery.

- **Pigment stains firmly:** Accept the limit, integrate the mark or start another practice sheet; do not abrade through paper.
- **Paper pills, softens, or tears:** Stop and dry flat, then choose more suitable paper next time.
- **Lifting water runs into nearby colour:** Blot excess and assess after drying; use a smaller damp area next time.
- **Repeated blotting changes nothing:** Stop; more passes do not guarantee white.
- **Fine control is hard:** Make the test area larger, use a broader soft brush or trusted positioning help while you choose the stopping point.

### Assumptions and limits

Staining, paper surface, and drying stage limit lifting. A manufacturer's special lifting-preparation medium is not assumed here. Complete colour removal is not promised, and museum or other people's art is outside scope.
