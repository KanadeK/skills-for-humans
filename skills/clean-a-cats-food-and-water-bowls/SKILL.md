## 中文

当你照料的普通室内猫的食碗或水碗已经使用、留有残食或水垢时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**把碗本身清洁、冲净并放回猫可用的位置**。上一份喂食 Skill 完成端餐，不假装碗自动变干净。

### 准备与输入

确认碗的材质/制造商清洗说明与猫当前是否仍需要另一只水碗。准备与猫砂盆分开的清洁工具和适合的水槽/工作面；不要用清理排泄物的刷子洗食碗。CDC 的一般示例是湿粮碗每次用后清洗，干粮与水碗每天清洗；实际生病或家庭高风险情形应遵从兽医和个人医疗指导，不在这里自配消毒剂。

### 执行

1. **先给水留可用路径。** 在清洗水碗时确认猫还有安全饮水或尽快放回，不让长时间清洗变成断水。
2. **移走残食并分流。** 按产品标签与当地规则处理剩食；别把食物从垃圾里捡回喂，也不把猫砂废物带到碗的工作面。
3. **按材质清洗。** 用合适的温水与温和肥皂/洗涤剂清洗内外及边缘，必要时用专用干净刷子；裂口、剥落涂层或不能清理的缝隙先停用并找可替代的完好碗。
4. **彻底冲净并干燥。** 按材质要求冲去清洁剂，放到洁净处晾干或用干净布擦干；不要用香味掩盖残留。清洁和消毒不是同一回事，只有实际指导要求时再查适用于碗的专门消毒办法。
5. **放回与洗手。** 将水碗补上干净水，与食碗、猫砂盆分开放稳；食碗按下一餐计划使用。清洁用具按材质处理，最后洗手。

### 成功条件

碗无可见残食和清洁剂、材质完好、实际回到猫可用位置，水可得，工具与双手收尾。碗破损、猫患病需特殊清洁或清洁说明冲突时，改用完好碗并向兽医/制造商求证，不把普通水洗称作消毒验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **清水碗洗完忘了补水：** 立即补上并检查猫能否到达。
- **用过猫砂铲的刷子来刷碗：** 停止，换专用洁净工具，重新清洗碗与工作面。
- **碗有裂缝积污：** 停用，找材质和尺寸合适的替代碗。
- **清洁剂气味很重：** 依碗和产品说明充分冲洗；不再加另一种化学品“中和”。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只清洗普通家用猫食水碗，不处理疾病传播调查、食物配方或消毒液配制。弯腰困难可在稳固可及的水槽操作；视力受限可请人确认裂口和清洁剂残留。现实副作用是湿刷/布需要清洗晾干，猫需要重新有水可喝。

## English

Load this Skill when the food or water bowls used by an ordinary indoor cat in your care have residues or need routine washing. Finish with **the bowls washed, rinsed, and returned to cat access**. Serving a meal does not automatically clean its equipment.

### Preparation and inputs

Check each bowl's material and maker cleaning directions, and whether another water source will remain available during washing. Use clean tools separate from litter-box equipment and a suitable sink/surface; never use a waste-cleaning brush on a food bowl. CDC's ordinary example is to wash wet-food bowls after every use and dry-food and water bowls daily. Illness or higher-risk households may need veterinarian and personal healthcare instructions; this Skill does not mix a disinfectant.

### Execution

1. **Keep a water route available.** Confirm the cat still has drinking water while you wash a bowl, or return it promptly. Long washing should not become a water outage.
2. **Remove leftovers appropriately.** Follow product and local rules. Do not retrieve discarded food for feeding or bring litter waste into the bowl work area.
3. **Wash for the material.** Use suitable warm water and mild soap/detergent on inside, outside, and edges with a dedicated clean brush when needed. Retire a bowl with cracks, flaking coating, or uncleanable crevices until an intact suitable replacement is available.
4. **Rinse fully and dry.** Follow material directions to remove cleaner; air-dry in a clean place or use a clean cloth. Scent is not evidence of residue removal. Cleaning differs from disinfection; seek bowl-specific guidance only when actually required.
5. **Return and wash hands.** Refill a water bowl with clean water, set it stably apart from food and litter, and leave the food bowl for its next meal. Clean and dry tools as appropriate and wash hands.

### Success

There is no visible food or cleaner residue, bowls are sound and in cat-reachable positions, water is available, and tools and hands are finished. For a cracked bowl, illness-related special cleaning, or conflicting instructions, use an intact replacement and check with the maker/vet rather than calling ordinary washing disinfected.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The clean water bowl is empty:** Refill it now and verify access.
- **A litter-box brush touches a bowl:** Stop, use a dedicated clean tool, and rewash bowl and work area.
- **A crack traps residue:** Retire the bowl and find a sound suitable replacement.
- **Cleaner smells strong:** Rinse as product and bowl directions require; do not add a second chemical to “neutralize” it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is ordinary home bowl hygiene, not disease investigation, nutrition planning, or disinfectant mixing. Work at an accessible stable sink when bending is hard; ask someone to check for cracks and residue when vision is limited. The wet brush or cloth needs drying and the cat needs water restored.
