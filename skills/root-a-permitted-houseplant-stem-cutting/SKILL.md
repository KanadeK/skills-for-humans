## 中文

当你想从一盆健康的普通室内植物繁殖一株新苗，且可信的品种资料明确说它可以用**茎插**时，加载这份 Skill。这里的结果不是“剪下一段就算成功”，而是得到一段有节点、被正确安置并持续观察的插穗，最终看见根或诚实记录失败。

### 准备与输入

确认品种、合法所有权和茎插方法；叶插、分株、带刺或有刺激性汁液的植物不套用本流程。选择无明显病虫、仍有足够枝叶的母株，查适合的季节与生根介质（水或盆栽介质）。准备清洁剪刀、可标记日期的小容器和明亮但不暴晒的位置。不能安全操作剪刀时请可信的人协助。

### 执行

1. **选健康的枝段和节点。** 依品种说明选择母株可承受的一段非病弱枝，确认叶片长出的节点在哪里；若母株太小或不健康，就不剪。
2. **按该品种方法剪取。** 清洁剪刀，按资料在节点附近作干净切口，留适量上部叶片并去掉会浸入水或埋入介质的下部叶片。不要按统一长度强切所有品种。
3. **选一条生根路径。** 资料允许水插时，让需要生根的节点接触水而叶片保持在水面上；资料要求介质时，用合适的小有孔盆和介质，按说明插入并保持适宜湿度。两种方法都是条件选项，不是同时必须完成。
4. **标记与安放。** 写品种、剪取日期与所用路径；放在该品种允许的明亮、避开强直晒和儿童宠物误碰的位置。水插按资料和水质状态换水；介质插不让它长期泡水或完全干透，服从品种要求。
5. **观察根而不揪。** 定期记录叶片、切口和可见根。水中看到实质新根、介质中按品种方法确认有稳固新生长，再查上盆或后续养护要求；时间因品种与环境不同，不把若干周当保证期。

### 成功与停止

成功是插穗已形成可观察根系，并且下一容器或养护路径有依据；若持续腐烂、母株出现问题或超出品种资料的观察期仍无进展，记录失败并停止，不反复从同一弱株剪取。

### 常见报错与补救

- **不知道哪里是节点：** 查可靠品种图解或请园艺人员指出，不凭任意枝尖下剪。
- **水杯里泡着叶子：** 调整水位与叶片，避免把整个插穗淹没。
- **看到白点就说生根：** 继续观察明确根生长；不要在尚未稳定时强行上盆。
- **母株出现虫害：** 停止取插穗并先隔离辨认，不能把可疑枝条当礼物传播。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是有可靠资料支持的普通室内植物茎插，不涉及野外采集、农作物育种、禁止繁殖的品种或危险植物。此过程有失败率，AI 读到步骤也不能替你看见根。视力受限可用放大照片或请人描述根；握力受限可请人完成切口，后续观察仍要有日期记录。副作用是占一处光线合适的小位置和可能需要清理失败插穗。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to propagate one healthy ordinary houseplant and reliable species guidance explicitly supports **stem cuttings**. A severed stem is not success. Prepare a cutting with a relevant node, put it into a supported rooting setup, and monitor until roots or an honest failure are observable.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm species, permission to take a cutting, and its stem-cutting method. Leaf cuttings, divisions, spines, or irritating sap need different guidance. Choose a parent without visible pest or disease signs and with enough growth to spare. Check season and whether this species roots in water or a medium. Prepare clean scissors, a dated small vessel, and a bright spot out of harsh direct sun. Arrange help if scissors cannot be used safely.

### Execution

1. **Choose a healthy segment and node.** Follow species guidance for a stem the parent can spare; identify where a leaf joins the stem. Do not cut a weak or tiny parent.
2. **Make the species-appropriate cut.** Clean scissors, cut neatly near the specified node, retain suitable upper leaves, and remove lower leaves that would sit underwater or below medium. Do not force one length on every plant.
3. **Choose one rooting route.** If water rooting is supported, put the rooting node in water with leaves above it. If the guidance calls for medium, use a suitable small draining pot and medium, inserting the cutting as directed and maintaining the appropriate moisture. These are conditional alternatives, not two required stages.
4. **Label and place it.** Record species, cutting date, and route. Place it in species-appropriate bright light away from harsh sun and accidental access by children or pets. Change water when its guidance and condition call for it, or keep medium neither persistently waterlogged nor too dry for this plant.
5. **Observe roots without tugging.** Record leaves, cut end, and visible roots. When substantial new water roots are visible, or medium-rooted growth is confirmed by a species-appropriate method, check the next potting and aftercare guidance. Timing varies; “a few weeks” is not a promise.

### Success and stop

Success is observable rooting with a supported next container or care route. If the cutting keeps rotting, the parent develops a problem, or the relevant guidance no longer supports continuing, record the failed attempt and stop. Do not repeatedly harvest from a weakening plant.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You cannot identify a node:** Use reliable species illustrations or horticultural advice before cutting.
- **Leaves are submerged:** Adjust the water level and leaves instead of drowning the whole cutting.
- **A white dot is called a root:** Continue observing actual root growth before potting on.
- **Pests appear on the parent:** Stop taking cuttings and isolate and identify the issue; do not spread suspect material as a gift.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a documented stem-cutting route for an ordinary houseplant, not wild collection, crop breeding, a plant with propagation restrictions, or a hazardous species. Cuttings can fail; AI reading the instructions cannot observe roots for you. Magnify photos or ask someone to describe the roots if vision is limited. Ask for help with the cut if grip is limited, then keep a dated observation record. A small bright area remains occupied, and a failed cutting may need clearing.
