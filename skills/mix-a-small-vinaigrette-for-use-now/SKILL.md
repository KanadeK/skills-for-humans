## 中文

当你要为**这一餐**做一小份有可信配方的油与酸调味汁，而不是制作可常温存放的调味油或食品罐藏时，用这份 Skill。结果是适合当前用途、已经混匀的调味汁；油与水性酸重新分层是这种混合物的正常可能性，不是安全期限的判断。

### 准备与输入

确认这份调味汁将拌什么、实际需要多少，读所选配方的油、食用醋或果汁、调味品及混合顺序。逐项检查原料可食用、日期/开封状态可靠，尤其是有乳制品或鲜配料时的保存说明。准备干净带盖小瓶或可稳定放置的碗与搅拌工具，以及可读量具。这里不使用生蛋、自采植物、未知香草浸油，也不把酸味当作已验证防腐能力。

### 执行

1. **先做这次够用的量。** 按可信配方把所需量换成小份，写明每项单位；看不清数字时放大或请可信的人读出，不凭瓶身大小猜酸油比例。容器留出能安全搅拌或摇动的空间。
2. **按配方加入食材。** 用洁净量具放入酸性液体和其他指定食品，再加入食用油或按配方顺序处理。若用带盖容器，先确认盖能密合；开口碗用适合的搅拌器具，勿把玻璃瓶当摇铃测试。
3. **混到当前用途能用。** 摇动或搅拌到油滴在液体中暂时分散、每次取用不只舀到一层。若配方指定其他稳定材料或方法，以配方为准；不要为了追求永不分层而加未经核实的原料。
4. **在安全可尝时检查。** 少量尝味或看调味汁能否轻薄覆盖目标食物；过酸、过油或太浓时，参照配方在小量范围内调整一种原料并重新混匀，记录变化。调整风味不是修复不安全原料。
5. **现用并关闭保存分支。** 临上桌若分层，按容器安全条件再摇匀或搅匀。仅把合适份量拌入食物。若有多余，按**实际成分**及当地食品安全指导决定能否立即冷藏与何时使用；条件未知就不宣称它是常温耐放酱汁。不要把用于接触食物的脏勺伸回原调味汁里。

### 成功条件

你得到一小份可按既定用途取用的混合调味汁，配方量、原料状态和是否需要立即使用或冷藏都清楚。材料不可确认、容器不洁或后续保存条件不明时，停止大量制作也是完整决定。

### 常见报错与补救

- **油和酸又分层：** 若原料与保存史仍可靠，使用前重新搅匀；分层本身不是加更多酸或承诺常温安全的理由。
- **盖子漏了：** 先换稳定密合的适用容器或改在碗里搅，不带着漏瓶继续摇。
- **一次调太多又没有冷藏：** 缩回当餐用量；不能按实际成分安全保存的多余部分不要留到下次。
- **味道不对就加入陌生强味材料：** 先回到原配方和已知可用原料，小量改变一个变量；不要让酱汁变成过敏或原料识别实验。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通家庭现用的食品调味汁，不提供医疗饮食、保健、罐藏、发酵或常温保质期。酸度、油品、鲜配料和地区饮食习惯会改变口味与保存条件。握力或视觉受限时可用大刻度量具、稳定碗与小搅拌器，或请可信的人协助，不要求用力摇大玻璃瓶。可能留下要洗的容器，也可能因为只调小份减少丢弃。

## English

Use this Skill to make a **small dressing for this meal** from food-grade oil and an acidic ingredient under a trusted recipe. You are not making shelf-stable infused oil or canning food. The result is a mixed dressing for its immediate purpose. Oil and water-based acid may separate again; that observation does not establish a safe storage period.

### Preparation and inputs

Decide what this dressing will coat and how much this meal needs. Read the chosen recipe's oil, food vinegar or juice, seasonings, and order of mixing. Check that every ingredient is food-suitable and has a known opening and storage state, especially dairy or fresh additions. Have a clean lidded small jar or stable bowl, mixer and readable measures. This workflow excludes raw egg, gathered wild plants, unidentified herb oils, and the idea that a sour taste proves preservation.

### Execution

1. **Make only what this use needs.** Reduce a trusted recipe to a small amount with explicit units. Enlarge or ask someone trusted to read unclear numbers; bottle size is not an oil-to-acid ratio. Leave room to stir or shake safely.
2. **Add food as the recipe directs.** Measure the acidic liquid and other specified ingredients with clean tools, then add edible oil or follow the recipe's sequence. Check a lid seals before shaking; use a suitable utensil in an open bowl. A glass jar is not a percussion test.
3. **Mix enough for this use.** Shake or stir until oil is temporarily dispersed so one spoonful does not come only from one layer. Follow any recipe-specified stabilizing ingredient or method; do not add an unverified substance to make separation impossible.
4. **Check when tasting is safe.** Taste a little or see whether it lightly coats the intended food. If too sharp, oily, or strong, change one known ingredient in a small amount under the recipe and mix again. Flavour adjustment cannot make an unsafe ingredient safe.
5. **Use now and close the storage branch.** If it separates before serving, stir or shake again only when the vessel allows it. Dress only the amount needed. For any remainder, follow its **actual ingredients** and local food-safety guidance to decide prompt cooling and later use; an unknown mixture is not a room-temperature pantry sauce. Keep a spoon that has touched food out of the clean remaining dressing.

### Success

You have a small dressing that can be used for the chosen food, with known ingredient condition, recipe amounts, and an honest immediate-use or cold-storage decision. If food, vessel hygiene, or later storage cannot be established, making no extra dressing is also complete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Oil and acid separate again:** If ingredients and history remain sound, mix just before serving. Separation alone does not call for more acid or promise room-temperature safety.
- **The lid leaks:** Switch to a stable suitable container or stir in a bowl instead of shaking a leaking jar.
- **You made too much without cold storage:** Return to this meal's amount. Do not keep excess that cannot be safely stored under its actual conditions.
- **You add an unfamiliar strong ingredient to repair taste:** Return to the recipe and known foods, changing one small variable. Dressing should not become an allergy or identity experiment.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary household dressing for current use, not medical nutrition, supplements, canning, fermentation, or a shelf-life rule. Acidity, oil, fresh ingredients, and local habits affect taste and storage. If gripping or reading is difficult, use large-marked measures, a stable bowl and small mixer, or trusted help instead of shaking a heavy glass jar. You may wash another container, and a small batch may mean less waste.
