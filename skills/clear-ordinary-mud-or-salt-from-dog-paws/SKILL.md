## 中文

当你熟悉的狗普通散步归来，足垫和趾间沾着可见泥、砂或融雪盐，但**没有明显伤口或疼痛**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是污物温和去除、趾间不留湿团、狗能自行站稳离开；这不是治疗足病。

### 准备与输入

确认狗愿意让你短暂触足、你能稳妥处理而不与它拉扯。准备防滑地面或垫、清水和干净软布；冬季融雪盐可刺激足部，Blue Cross 建议回家后擦除。发现跛行、出血、裂伤、明显灼痛、异常肿胀或狗强烈抗拒时，停止并联系兽医，不用清洁流程掩盖伤情。

### 执行

1. **先安置狗。** 在不会滑倒或挡门的地方让它平静站立，确认牵绳与门的安全状态；可由熟悉的成年人协助稳定，不能强行扭爪。
2. **一只一只查看。** 轻看足垫边缘和趾间是否只是泥、砂、盐或小草屑。未知黏性化学品、尖刺、嵌入物或受伤立即退出普通清洁。
3. **温和清除。** 用清水轻湿软布，按狗可接受程度擦掉普通污物，必要时用少量清水冲洗趾间；不使用人用清洁剂、酒精或没有兽医/产品说明的消毒液。动作轻，不用力抠嵌入物。
4. **充分擦干并复核。** 用干净毛巾把足垫和趾间擦干，确认没有明显残留、狗走路仍自然；把湿布和入口地面按材质清理。狗舔足、疼痛或跛行持续时向兽医求助，不凭“擦干净了”判断安全。

### 成功条件

普通泥盐已从可见足部移除、趾间干爽、狗无明显新不适，用品和地面有去向。若有伤、强烈抗拒或不明污染，停止在安全状态并请兽医指导，不把完整清洗当任务配额。

### 常见报错与补救

- **狗一碰脚就挣脱：** 停止并找熟悉的合适协助者或兽医指导，不压倒它强擦。
- **盐水擦开却没干：** 换干净毛巾吸走趾间水分，防止湿污长期留着。
- **趾间发现锐物：** 不强拔，限制进一步伤害并联系兽医。
- **擦完仍跛：** 立即停止普通外出计划，联系兽医，别靠再多擦几次诊断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只是已照料、可配合狗的普通外出污物清理，不教伤口处理、足垫病诊断、药剂配制或大型犬强制约束。弯腰困难可在防滑可及平台请合适的人协助；视力受限时请人确认残留和皮肤异常。毛巾还需要清洗晾干。

## English

Load this Skill after an ordinary walk when a familiar dog has visible mud, grit, or road salt on paws and **no obvious wound or pain**. Finish with ordinary debris gently removed, spaces between toes dry, and the dog able to walk away normally. This is not paw-disease treatment.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the dog accepts brief paw handling and you can work without struggling. Prepare a nonslip place, water, and clean soft cloth. Winter road salt can irritate paws; Blue Cross advises wiping paws on return. Stop and contact a veterinarian for limping, bleeding, a cut, obvious burning pain, swelling, or strong resistance rather than using cleaning to conceal injury.

### Execution

1. **Settle the dog safely.** Choose a spot where it will not slip or block an exit, keeping lead and door controlled. A familiar adult may help it stand; do not twist paws by force.
2. **Inspect one paw at a time.** Check pad edges and between toes for ordinary mud, grit, salt, or small grass debris. Unknown sticky chemicals, a sharp embedded item, or injury end this ordinary cleaning route.
3. **Remove ordinary material gently.** Lightly wet a soft cloth with water and wipe only as the dog permits. A small rinse between toes may be appropriate. Do not use human cleaners, alcohol, or an unchecked disinfectant. Avoid digging out anything embedded.
4. **Dry and recheck.** Towel pads and toe spaces dry. Confirm no obvious material remains and gait is still normal; handle the wet cloth and entry floor under their material instructions. Persistent licking, pain, or limp needs veterinary advice, not a “clean means safe” inference.

### Success

Visible ordinary mud/salt is removed, toe spaces are dry, the dog shows no obvious new distress, and tools and floor have a route. For injury, strong resistance, or unknown contamination, stop safely and seek veterinary guidance instead of completing a cleaning quota.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The dog pulls away when you touch a paw:** Stop and seek familiar capable help or veterinary guidance; do not pin it down.
- **Salt was rinsed but toes stay wet:** Use a clean towel to remove retained moisture.
- **A sharp object appears between toes:** Do not pull it forcefully; prevent further harm and contact a vet.
- **It still limps after wiping:** End ordinary walking plans and contact a vet promptly, not another round of wiping as diagnosis.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is ordinary post-walk dirt removal for a cared-for cooperative dog, not wound treatment, pad-disease diagnosis, chemical mixing, or forceful restraint of a large dog. Work on a safe accessible surface with capable help if bending is difficult. Ask another person to check for residue or skin changes if vision is limited. The towel needs laundering and drying.
