## 中文

当你写下“我要学会这个”“今天多读点”，却不知道何时算这一轮完成时，用这份 Skill 把愿望改成**一个有输入、可见产出和检查方法的小目标**。结果是一张你能照着开始的目标卡，不是已经读完、练会或考试会得高分。阅读路径、独立练习、纠错和延迟复习分别属于后续任务。

### 准备与输入

写你想学的具体对象、目前已经能做的一点、还有哪一步不稳，以及今天能投入的实际时间。挑你有权使用的一份材料/例子，记下能在哪里拿到；没有材料就先解决来源，不能靠空想保证掌握。课程、工作和个人兴趣都可，但医学诊断、法律/财务决定和专业资格不由这张卡承诺。用你可访问的纸笔、语音便签或大字标记即可；目标属于你，不按年级或科目复制一套格式。

### 执行

1. **把大愿望缩成一段。** 从“学会某学科”选一个本次能接触的概念、步骤或小部分；写出起点，如“我会认两个术语，但不会用它们解释一张图”。只取一处核心缺口，其他列入以后。
2. **换成能看见的动词。** 用“写出、解释、比较、画一张、按步骤做一次”等动作，替换“了解、熟悉、学会”。产出可以是一段自己的话、一张对照表或一题作答；只写“读 20 分钟”是时长，不证明你能做什么。
3. **定义一个有限检查。** 写“我拿什么判断产出够不够”：是否能不看原文说清两点、能把自己的例子与材料核对、能解释哪一步卡住。检查最好用现有材料或清楚的参考答案；没有答案/标准时只判“已产生一个草稿和待核问题”，不要假装正确。
4. **把时间和材料写进去。** 例如“这 25 分钟用现有第 2 节，写三句话解释一个概念，然后对照原文核两个关键点”。时间按你实际可用值，不设人人通用的番茄钟。若完成前材料缺失、身体/感官条件不适或问题变大，缩小产出或停下标缺口，不硬补虚构证据。
5. **做一次可读性检查。** 只看你写的目标卡，问“开始要打开什么？最后留什么？谁能按这条检查我是否做了？到何时停？”不清楚就改一处；清楚后把卡放在本次材料旁。实际做与检验留给接下来的学习动作。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有一条含**本次起点、一个动作产出、现有材料、检查和停点**的目标句。别人读卡能知道你准备做什么，完成时也能诚实说“通过/未通过/缺依据”。把“学会全部”写得更漂亮仍不是小目标。

- **目标写“理解第一章”：** 改成“用自己的话解释本章一个关系，并用原文两处核对”，章节只是材料范围。
- **目标要做十件事：** 选最能检验本次缺口的一件，余下记以后；不把清单长度当学习质量。
- **没有可靠检查材料：** 把本轮定义为“形成问题或初稿，待下一轮核对”，不宣称知识正确。
- **时间到还没做完：** 记录实际做到哪一步，缩小下一轮目标；这张目标卡不对分数或进步速度做保证。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

学习速度、前提、语言、感官和可用资源因人而异；一句可检验目标帮助组织当前尝试，并不代表人人必须采用同一方法。需要导师或同伴反馈时可写在检查条件中，但他人不一定即时可用。产出可能失败或暴露你还不懂的地方，这仍是真实信息。此 Skill结束于设定可执行目标，不把计划冒充学习实测。

## English

Use this Skill when you write “I want to learn this” or “read more today” but cannot tell when this session is done. Turn the wish into **one small goal with inputs, visible output, and a way to check it**. The result is a card you can start from, not proof you have read, mastered, or will score well. Reading, independent practice, error analysis, and later review have separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Write the specific thing you want to learn, one part you can already do, the step that remains uncertain, and the time truly available today. Choose one source or example you may use and know where it is. If material is missing, resolve access first; imagining the topic is no mastery plan. This can be coursework, work learning, or a hobby, but the card does not promise a medical, legal, financial, or professional qualification result. Use accessible paper, voice notes, or large print. The goal is yours, not a template copied for each grade or subject.

### Execution

1. **Shrink the large wish to one part.** From “learn the subject” choose one concept, procedure, or small section you can encounter now. State your baseline: “I recognise two terms but cannot use them to explain this diagram.” Take one core gap and leave others for later.
2. **Choose an observable verb.** Replace “know,” “get familiar with,” or “master” with “write, explain, compare, draw, or do one step.” The product may be your own short explanation, comparison, or one answer. “Read for 20 minutes” is duration, not evidence of what you can do.
3. **Define one limited check.** Write how you will judge the product: can you state two points without looking, compare your example with the source, or locate the stuck step? Prefer material you already have or a clear reference answer. Without an answer or standard, call the outcome “draft and question to verify,” not correct knowledge.
4. **Add time and material.** For example: “In my available 25 minutes, use section 2 to write three sentences explaining one concept, then check two key points against the text.” Choose your real time, not a universal timer. If material, access, or scope fails, reduce the output or stop and mark the gap rather than invent evidence.
5. **Read the card alone.** Ask: what do I open first, what remains at the end, how could someone check it, and when do I stop? Repair one ambiguity. Put the clear card beside the material. The actual doing and checking are your next learning action.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have a sentence with **this session's starting point, one visible product, usable material, check, and stop point**. Someone reading the card can tell what you intend and you can later say passed, not passed, or evidence missing. Beautifying “master everything” does not make a small goal.

- **The goal says “understand chapter one”:** Use the chapter as source and write “explain one relation in my own words and check two places in the chapter.”
- **It contains ten tasks:** Pick the one most revealing of today's gap and leave the rest for later. List length is not learning quality.
- **There is no reliable checking material:** Define this pass as producing a draft or question for later verification, not proof of correctness.
- **Time ends before the product is complete:** Record where you reached and shrink the next goal. This card promises no score or fixed learning speed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Learning pace, prerequisites, language, sensory access, and resources differ. A checkable goal organises one attempt but is no universal method. If a teacher or peer must judge it, name that in the check and allow for their availability. The product may fail or reveal uncertainty; that is real information. This Skill ends with an executable goal and never calls a plan human learning test.
