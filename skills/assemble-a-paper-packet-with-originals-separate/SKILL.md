## 中文

当一次普通事项需要交一组多页纸本材料，副本和原件都可能在你手边时加载。你要把**接收方当期要求的页、份数、顺序与允许的装订方式**核到实物，同时把原件留在清楚分开的保管位置。这里不解释申请资格、决定何种副本有法律效力，也不替你实际递交。

### 准备与输入

找准确服务的当期清单或机构回复，写下它明确要求的页、正反面、份数、先后次序、是否必须分组/装订以及原件是否要携带或提交。未知不自创“常见做法”。副本已各自检查可读；易损、稀有或别人的原件只在权限内处理，不为装订打孔或贴胶。

### 执行

1. **先分两处放。** 原件放写明“原件/本人保管”的夹，提交候选副本另放，必要时用可移除的外层标签区分；不要在原件正文盖上“复印件”或与副本交错堆叠。
2. **对接收方清单逐项点页。** 对每项标实际页数、正反面、几份、来源于哪份原件以及是否可读。某页缺失、倒序、重复或页码不连贯时补查，不能用“厚度差不多”宣布齐全。
3. **按已确认顺序摆好。** 把副本逐项排成机构要的包；没有规定顺序时用自己能读回的中性封面清单保持完整，不声明这种顺序必然获接受。已准备的表格内容是否正确由实际填写者/机构核对，不在这里推断。
4. **只按允许方式固定。** 机构明确要钉、夹或不装订就照要求处理可复制页；没写时采用可取下、不遮字且不损坏资料的临时夹法，并向机构确认关键疑点。不要给珍贵原件打孔、强压脆纸或用胶带修订破损。
5. **做一次拿出—放回检查。** 按封面清单取出一份完整副本，逐页读出页数和顺序后放回；原件另数并确认不在提交包里。包装表面只写中性用途和接收方，不公开个人细节；何时/由谁交给对方留给下一步交接流程。

### 完成与停止

副本包的页、面、份数、顺序和固定方式与已核实的机构要求对应，原件仍可单独取回；不代表材料已提交或获批。要求不清、关键页缺、原件/副本混淆、脆纸可能受损或材料带敏感身份信息而场所不安全时暂停包装并问实际接收方/有权者。

### 常见报错与补救

- **双面原件只复了正面：** 补齐背面并重新核整包页数。
- **副本包里混了一张原件：** 立即取出回原件夹，重新点两处份数。
- **订书针压住字或机构不要装订：** 换机构允许的方式，不能毁损原件。
- **一组材料有两页相同外观：** 按页码/标题或机构清单分辨，未知时标待问，不凭纸厚决定。
- **缺关键页还要赶时间：** 如实标缺并问机构是否可改安排，不用空白纸占位伪装齐全。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通纸本成套的物理核对，不处理身份证明的法律效力、敏感内容填写、财务或医疗建议。机构和地区会改变原件/副本与装订要求；FTC 的“留原件、交副本”只在其示例情景成立。视力、行动或握持不便时由可信授权者私下按清单读回、协助摆页。副作用是“都在这一叠”需要接受顺序和份数检查。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary process needs several paper pages and both copies and originals may be at hand. Check **the real recipient's current pages, counts, order and permitted fastening method** against physical material while keeping originals clearly separate. This does not interpret application eligibility, decide a copy's legal status or perform the handoff.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the exact service's current list or issuer reply for required pages, backs, copies, order, grouping or binding, and whether originals must travel or be submitted. Do not invent a common convention for an unknown requirement. Copies have already been checked for legibility. Handle fragile, rare or another person's originals only within permission; do not punch holes or apply adhesive to make a packet.

### Execution

1. **Create two physical zones.** Place originals in a holder marked “original / held by owner” and proposed submission copies elsewhere, with removable outer labels if needed. Do not stamp “copy” on original text or interleave sources among duplicates.
2. **Count against the recipient list.** For each required item, mark actual pages, sides, sets, source original and readability. Recheck a missing, reversed, repeated or broken sequence. A stack that looks thick enough is not a complete packet.
3. **Put copies in confirmed order.** Arrange for the receiving body's stated sequence. Without a required order, use a neutral cover list you can read back, without asserting the institution will accept that arrangement. The person responsible for the form and institution still judge answer accuracy.
4. **Fasten only as allowed.** Follow explicit stapling, clipping or no-binding instructions for copies. If not specified, use a reversible method that does not cover text or harm paper and ask the issuer about a material uncertainty. Never hole-punch a valuable original, flatten brittle paper by force or tape over damage.
5. **Run a take-out and return check.** Pull one complete copy set by the cover list, count pages and order aloud, then return it. Count originals separately and make sure none entered the proposed submission packet. Put only a neutral purpose and recipient outside, not private details. A later handoff task covers who takes it when.

### Success and stop

Copy pages, sides, sets, order and fastener match verified issuer instructions, with each original retrievable separately. This does not mean submitted or approved. Pause for an unclear rule, missing key page, original/copy mix-up, likely damage to brittle paper or an insecure place for sensitive identity information, and ask the actual recipient or rights holder.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A two-sided source was copied only on the front:** Make the back copy and recount the packet.
- **An original entered the copy packet:** Remove it to the original holder immediately and recount both groups.
- **A staple covers text or the issuer forbids binding:** Change to an accepted method without damaging an original.
- **Two pages look identical at a glance:** Use page numbers, headings or the issuer list; mark unresolved when still uncertain.
- **Time is short but a key page is absent:** Record missing and ask about a changed arrangement. Blank filler paper is not a complete packet.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is physical checking of an ordinary paper packet, not legal status of IDs, sensitive form filling, financial or medical guidance. Institution and region govern originals, copies and fastening. The FTC's keep-originals/send-copies instruction belongs to its example only. A trusted authorised helper can privately count and arrange when sight, mobility or grip is limited. “It's all in this stack” now needs an order and count.
