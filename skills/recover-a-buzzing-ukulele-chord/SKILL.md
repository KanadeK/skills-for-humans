## 中文

你已经按一个和弦却听到**实在的持续嗡响或闷音**，想在不拆琴的前提下找出一处可修正的手法时使用本篇。结果是一根具体问题弦、一次单项手法调整和前后可听对照；若空弦也响、弦损坏或无法改善，就停止并请懂琴的人看结构。它从失败现场恢复，不是“如何第一次读和弦图”的重写。

### 准备与输入

保留同一和弦图与原来的舒适坐姿，先低音量扫一次确认问题仍在。检查琴弦是否明显松脱、破损或撞击过；有此情况不继续试压。找一个安静位置，别用更强力的扫弦盖住杂音，也别为消音用疼痛压力。

### 执行

1. **定位一根弦。** 按住原和弦，逐根轻拨图上应响的弦，记录哪一根嗡响、闷或无音；再放开手指轻拨同根空弦作对照。
2. **判断技术线索。** 若空弦清楚而按弦音有问题，看指尖是否离目标品丝太远、压在品丝金属上或被邻指碰住。若空弦也异常，先停，不把结构问题当成“多用力”。
3. **只改一个按点。** 轻微移近目标品丝靠琴头一侧、避开邻弦或调整到舒适的最低有效压力；每改一次重拨该弦并与原声比较。不拧琴颈杆、琴桥或旋钮结构件。
4. **再扫和弦核查。** 问题弦单独清楚后再扫完整和弦，检查其他弦没有因此闷掉；若前后无改善、会痛或空弦持续异常，停止并记录给合适维修者。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

明确哪根弦、哪一处手法改变让声音改善，而且整和弦仍清楚；或保留异常/求助结论。单凭一次更大声的扫弦不能证明已修复。

- **空弦也嗡响：** 停止按点试错，求助调校。
- **改一指后邻弦闷：** 逐根再核，不以局部改善盖过新问题。
- **按得越重越痛：** 放松并停止，不继续加力。
- **不知道弦名：** 用和弦图和物理 G、C、E、A 位置核对。

### 假设与边界

仅处理一个正常初学按弦中的可观察杂音。你决定是否继续；琴颈、品丝、弦高和弦材问题需本琴服务，不能靠本篇自诊或调校。

## English

Use this Skill after a chord **actually buzzes or mutes** and you want to locate one fixable fretting detail without opening the instrument. Finish with a named problem string, one tested contact change and an audible before/after, or stop for setup help if an open string is also abnormal, a string is damaged, or no technique change helps. This is recovery from a real miss, not first-time chord-chart reading.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the same chart and comfortable seat. Strum quietly once to confirm the issue persists. Look for an obviously loose, damaged or impact-affected string; stop pressure trials if present. Work somewhere quiet. Do not mask the buzz with a harder strum or use painful force to silence it.

### Execution

1. **Locate one string.** Hold the chord and gently pluck each string the chart requires. Name which buzzes, mutes or disappears, then release the fretting hand and pluck that same open string for comparison.
2. **Read technique clues.** If open is clear but fretted is not, check whether your fingertip is far from the target fret, directly on its metal, or touches a neighbour. If even open is abnormal, stop rather than just pushing harder.
3. **Change one contact point.** Move slightly closer behind the target fret on the headstock side, clear the neighbour, or use the least comfortable effective pressure. Re-pluck after each change against the original sound. Do not adjust truss rods, bridges or structural tuner parts.
4. **Recheck the full chord.** Once the problem string is clear alone, strum the chord and confirm other strings did not mute. If there is no improvement, pain, or persistent open-string fault, stop and record it for suitable repair help.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Name the string and contact change that audibly improved it while the full chord remains clear, or keep an honest fault/help record. A louder single strum is not proof of repair.

- **Open string buzzes too:** Stop fretting trials and seek setup inspection.
- **Neighbour mutes after change:** Retest every string, not just the repaired one.
- **Harder pressure hurts:** Ease and stop instead of increasing force.
- **String name unknown:** Map chart marks to physical G, C, E, A positions.

### Assumptions and limits

This covers one audible beginner fretting problem. You decide whether to continue. Neck, fret, action and string-material faults require instrument-specific service, not self-claimed diagnosis or adjustment here.
