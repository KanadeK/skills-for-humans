## 中文

当两件**已适合同一普通用途**的卷装或片装纸品，每包卷数、每卷张数或每张尺寸不同，而“每卷”“每 100 张”的标价不足以表示实际纸面大小时，加载这份 Skill。你要得到**每平方米标示纸面的当前价格**。它不证明吸收力、柔软度、层数或强度等质量相同；商品是否满足这些要求先另行判断。

### 准备与输入

确认两件纸品确实用于同一件事，而且你接受各自的层数、材质、尺寸和性能。记下每包现价、卷数或小包数、每卷或每包张数、每张标示长宽；若是连续纸卷，也可用标示总长度与纸宽。必须确认这些数字属于面前这一款，不能把“加长”“双倍”换算成自己猜的张数。包装没写全时可查同型号制造商资料；仍缺尺寸或张数就不能算面积价。

### 执行步骤

1. **固定比较的是纸面，不是卷芯。** 若包装写“4 卷 × 每卷 200 张”，总张数是 800，不是 4。若两件只有每卷价但张数不同，每卷价不足以排名。先排除不符合你用途或明确限制的纸品。
2. **把尺寸换到米。** 纸张长和宽都从厘米除以 100，例如 10 厘米是 0.10 米。每张面积为“长 × 宽”；整包面积为“卷数 × 每卷张数 × 每张面积”。若标的是连续卷总长度，则用“各卷长度合计 × 纸宽”。不要把厘米直接当米，或把一卷的长度误作整包。
3. **算同面积价格。** 例：A 有 2 卷，每卷 100 张，每张 10 × 10 厘米，整包 2 平方米，现价 4，即每平方米 2。B 也有 2 卷、每卷 100 张，但每张 12 × 10 厘米，整包 2.4 平方米，现价 4.20，即每平方米 1.75。B 每张的金额略高，却在**标示面积**口径下更便宜。
4. **写出有限结论。** 记录每包应付、总张数、总面积、每平方米价格，以及任何层数或性能差异。面积较多不保证实际一次用得更少、吸得更多或适合你的设备；这些条件不一致时只保留数值，不宣布“整体更值”。

### 完成与停止

你能说清两件已合适纸品各自的**标示总面积、每平方米现价与整包价**，就完成了面积比较。若一方缺张数、纸张尺寸、纸宽或长度，或规格无法确认，就输出“面积价格暂不可比”；不要用包装鼓起来的体积、卷径或“大卷”字样代替纸面数据。

### 常见报错与补救

- **按卷数判断谁便宜：** 先核对每卷张数、每张面积；卷芯和卷绕松紧不算纸面。
- **每 100 张更便宜就宣称用起来更省：** 若每张尺寸不同，重算每平方米；层数、强度和吸水性仍需按用途另判。
- **只见“加长卷”，无张数或长度：** 查该型号正式规格；拿不到数字就停止这组面积比较。
- **混用厘米和米：** 长宽先各除以 100，再相乘；面积单位转换是两次，不是一次。
- **包装价使用不同优惠条件：** 只采用你当前可获得的各自应付价，条件不明就先核对。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本流程只用于普通纸品的购前标示面积算术，不指导卫生、安全或设备使用。卷纸、纸巾、厨房纸的质量与适用场景不同，不跨用途排名；同面积也不保证同等表现。地区和商家标示内容不一，不要求你拆封测量。看不清时可放大照片、用读屏、问店员或找同型号可读规格；纸笔、计算器可代替心算。现实副作用是印着“大”的包装可能输给标示面积表。

## English

Load this Skill when two rolls or packs of sheet paper **already suit the same ordinary purpose**, but rolls per pack, sheets per roll, or sheet dimensions differ, so a price “per roll” or “per 100 sheets” does not describe the paper surface you get. The result is the **current price per square metre of labelled sheet area**. It does not establish equal absorbency, softness, ply count, or strength. Set those product requirements first.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm that the goods serve the same job and that you accept each one's plies, material, dimensions, and performance. Record the current pack price, number of rolls or inner packs, sheets in each, and stated sheet length and width. For a continuous roll, labelled total length and paper width may be used instead. Match every figure to the exact product; “long” or “double” is not a sheet count you may invent. Check the exact maker's specifications if the pack is incomplete. If dimensions or count remain missing, no area price can be calculated.

### Execution steps

1. **Count paper surface, not cores.** “4 rolls × 200 sheets each” gives 800 sheets, not 4. If the number of sheets differs, price per roll alone is not a fair ranking. Remove paper goods that fail your use requirements first.
2. **Convert dimensions to metres.** Divide centimetres by 100 for both length and width: 10 cm is 0.10 m. Area per sheet is “length × width”; pack area is “rolls × sheets per roll × area per sheet.” For a continuous roll, use “length of all rolls combined × paper width.” Do not treat centimetres as metres or one roll's length as the whole pack's.
3. **Calculate one area price.** A has 2 rolls of 100 sheets, each 10 × 10 cm. Its pack contains 2 square metres and costs 4, so the price is 2 per square metre. B also has 2 rolls of 100 sheets, but each is 12 × 10 cm. It contains 2.4 square metres and costs 4.20, or 1.75 per square metre. B costs a little more per sheet yet less per **labelled area**.
4. **Keep the conclusion limited.** Record amount payable, total sheets, total area, area price, and any differences in plies or performance. More area does not prove you use fewer sheets, absorb more, or suit an appliance. If those conditions differ, keep the arithmetic but do not announce an overall value winner.

### Success and stop

You can state each suitable product's **labelled total area, current price per square metre, and pack price**. Stop with “area prices not comparable yet” if either product lacks a reliable sheet count, sheet dimensions, paper width, length, or confirmed specification. Package bulk, roll diameter, and “jumbo” wording are not surface measurements.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Choosing by roll count:** Check sheets per roll and area per sheet. A cardboard core or loose winding is not paper surface.
- **The lower price per 100 sheets is called cheaper to use:** Recalculate per square metre if sheet sizes differ. Judge ply, strength, and absorbency for the purpose separately.
- **Only “long roll” is supplied:** Find the exact model's official count or length; pause area comparison without it.
- **Mixing centimetres and metres:** Divide both dimensions by 100 before multiplying. Area conversion applies to two dimensions, not one.
- **Different promotion conditions are used for pack prices:** Use only prices available to you now; confirm unclear conditions first.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is pre-purchase arithmetic for ordinary sheet paper, not instructions on hygiene, safety, or appliance use. Toilet tissue, facial tissue, and kitchen paper have different jobs and are not ranked across purposes. Equal area does not guarantee equal performance. Labels vary by retailer and region; no pack needs to be opened or measured. Magnify a photo, use screen reading, ask staff, or find readable specifications for the exact model if needed. Paper or a calculator can replace mental arithmetic. A package printed with “big” may lose to the area figures.
