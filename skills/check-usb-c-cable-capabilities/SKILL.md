## 中文

当你要买一根 USB-C 线，让两台已有设备完成**充电、传数据或接显示器**中的具体任务时，加载这份 Skill。目标是核对两端接口与线缆在所需功能上都具备可追溯规格，或明确缺哪项资料。接口形状相同不代表供电、速度或视频能力相同；这里不拆机、不改电路、不保证未知电源安全。

### 准备与输入

写出两端设备的准确型号、要插的具体接口，以及本次需要的功能：普通充电、制造商要求的特定充电条件、某类数据传输、显示输出，或同时多项。找到两端设备说明书中的接口功能与供电要求，再找候选线的两端接头、功率标示、数据速率及视频支持说明。若设备附有指定线缆或明确限制，先读该限制。

### 执行

1. **先写任务而不是接头名。** 例如“电脑向移动硬盘传文件”“符合电脑说明书的充电”“电脑接显示器”。多项同时要用就逐项列出；“USB-C 对 USB-C”只写了外形。
2. **查两端接口。** 在准确型号说明书中确认所选端口是否支持所需数据、USB 供电或视频模式。一个端口只支持数据时，换更贵的线也不会让它输出显示画面。
3. **查线缆本身。** 从制造商资料或可信认证标志核对线的功率能力、数据速率、视频/替代模式支持及长度。USB-IF 的功率与数据标记是不同信息；看到其中一个，不能脑补另一个。没有明确规格的候选保留“未确认”。
4. **对每项用途走完整条链。** 把源设备端口、目标设备端口、线缆，以及充电时制造商指定的电源要求逐项对照。所需功能只有在每段都支持时才标为“有规格依据”；实际可达速度还可能受较慢一端限制，不能把线缆标称值当设备承诺。
5. **写购前裁决。** 记录“用途 → 两端准确端口能力 → 候选线规格 → 满足、不满足、未确认”。如果卖家只展示接口照片，索取准确型号和说明；关键能力无法证实时，用设备原配或制造商明确推荐的替代方案，或暂不买。

### 成功与停止

成功是每一项你确实需要的功能都有两端设备及线缆的资料对应；它仍是购前规格判断，不是现场运行验收。若线缆、插头或供电器已损坏，设备使用非标准/专有供电，或说明书要求专业处理，停止自行匹配并求助制造商支持；不要靠反复插拔来试探风险。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两个头能插就判定通过：** 回到数据、功率和视频的单独规格；机械连接只解决第一步。
- **看到功率标志就推断高速数据：** 找独立的数据速率或认证说明；没有就只确认已证实的功率条件。
- **显示器没画面，归咎于线：** 先查电脑和显示器所用端口是否支持视频输出/输入；缺视频模式时换线无效。
- **小字、图标或接口位置难辨认：** 放大说明书、请可信的人读回准确型号与接口标识，或向厂商询问可读规格；不要根据模糊图标猜。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程只处理普通 USB-C 成品线的购前功能核对，不提供充电器改造、设备维修或电气测试。地区销售名称、协议版本和专有附件可能不同，按你的准确型号资料判断。没有网络时优先查随设备附的纸质手册；若只需基本功能，不必为用不到的最高速率购买，但仍要确认所需功能确实可用。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a USB-C cable for a specific **charging, data-transfer, or display** job between devices you already own. Your result is a traceable match across both ports and the cable, or a list of missing specifications. A matching connector shape does not imply matching power, speed, or video support. This Skill does not open devices, alter wiring, or guarantee the safety of an unknown power source.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the exact models at both ends, the particular ports you will use, and the job: ordinary charging, a maker-required charging condition, a kind of data transfer, display output, or several of these together. Get both devices' port-function and power guidance, plus the candidate cable's connector types, power marking, data rate, and stated video support. Read any required supplied-cable or proprietary-accessory condition first.

### Execution

1. **Name the job before the plug.** Write “computer to storage drive,” “charge the laptop under its manual,” or “computer to monitor.” List every function needed at the same time. “USB-C to USB-C” describes only the ends.
2. **Check both ports.** Use each exact model's guide to confirm that the chosen ports support the needed data, USB power, or video mode. A data-only port cannot acquire display output through a more expensive cable.
3. **Check the cable.** Find its supported power, data rate, video or alternate-mode capability, and length in the maker's information or credible certification markings. USB-IF power and data marks convey different facts. One cannot fill in a missing value for the other; an unclear cable stays unconfirmed.
4. **Trace the complete path for each job.** Compare the source port, destination port, cable, and, for charging, the power-source requirements stated by the device maker. Mark a function supported by specifications only when every needed part supports it. Actual transfer speed may be limited by a slower endpoint, so the cable's maximum is not a device promise.
5. **Record the purchase decision.** Write “job → exact capabilities of both ports → cable specification → supported, unsupported, or unconfirmed.” If a listing only shows connector photos, ask for its exact model and guide. When a crucial capability cannot be verified, use the supplied cable, a maker-documented option, or wait.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when each function you truly need has supporting information for both device ports and the cable. This is a pre-purchase specification check, not a live performance test. Stop if a cable, plug, or power adapter is damaged, the device uses non-standard proprietary power, or its manual requires specialist handling. Ask the maker rather than probing by repeated connections.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Both plugs fit, so everything must work:** Recheck data, power, and video separately. Mechanical connection settles only the first question.
- **A power mark was read as high-speed data:** Find a separate data-rate or certification statement; confirm only the power condition that is actually documented.
- **No monitor picture was blamed on the cable:** Check whether the chosen computer and display ports provide video output and input. A cable cannot add a missing video mode.
- **Small labels or port icons are inaccessible:** Enlarge the guide, ask a trusted person to read back exact model and port marks, or request readable specifications from the maker. Do not guess from a blurred icon.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers the buying decision for ordinary finished USB-C cables. It does not teach charger modification, device repair, or electrical testing. Market labels, protocol versions, and proprietary accessories vary; use your exact model's documentation. A paper manual can replace online lookup. If you only need basic operation, you need not buy a headline speed you will never use, but you still need evidence for the function you do need.

### Sources

- [USB-IF: Cables and Connectors](https://www.usb.org/cable_connector) — same-shaped cables have different capabilities, power and data markings represent distinct features, and cable certification cannot add a missing device-port feature.
- [Dell: USB Type-C features](https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000141238/guide-to-usb-type-c) — a particular device may implement only some data, power, or video functions, so read its exact guide; Dell is an example.
- [Dell: Video cable capabilities](https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000143545/video-cables-and-their-capabilities) — not every USB-C port carries display output; connector shape alone is insufficient.
