## 中文

当你已经拿到一本书或另一件**可外借的馆藏**，需要离馆带走时，用这份 Skill 完成馆方登记并确认到期日。结果是你自己的账户上出现这件物品的有效借阅记录；“书在手里”或设备响一声都不能单独证明完成。找目录和书架归 H281，预约排队、续借、归还各有另外的流程。

### 准备与输入

核对物品上是否标“馆内使用”“参考资料”“不可外借”等限制，检查馆方当前外借规则及你自己的借阅资格。带你有权使用的实体卡、馆方正式 App 或工作人员认可的方式；不借用他人的凭据，不把卡号/PIN 写在公共纸上。预约架物品可能有指定借阅人、取件期限或卡片要求，按馆方实际提示核对，不把“放在预约架上”当可随手取走。若规则、费用或账号状态不明，先问馆员再做决定。

### 执行

1. **选馆方允许的登记通道。** 到柜台、自助机或馆方官方 App 的正式借阅入口；不要把仅供馆内阅读的资料拿去尝试。看设备屏幕或听工作人员说明适用物品种类。
2. **提交自己的借阅身份和实物。** 按馆方步骤让每件物品被识别、登记；多件时逐件核对题名或条码尾码，不因一件成功就假定全批成功。App 若需要本机登录，自己在官方界面操作，别向旁人展示 PIN。
3. **读完结果再离开。** 在屏幕、收据或自己的馆方账户核对该物品确实列为“已借出/checked out”、记录属于你、到期日期是什么。把日期记在私人日历或保留收据；贷款期限按本馆和该物品类型，本文不给通用周数。
4. **处理失败或限制。** 若设备报错、账户不符、物品不外借或到期日不合理，留在馆内请工作人员核对；未确认登记前不把物品带过出口。若你改变主意，把物品放回馆方指定位置，不自行塞回任何看似空的书架。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

馆方系统或正式收据显示**该具体物品在你的借阅记录里及其到期日**，你才带走。没有可核对结果就把它视为未完成，尤其不能靠出口警报是否响来判断。

- **自助机扫了却没列入账户：** 再核对屏幕和条码，向馆员求助，不重复盲扫后直接离开。
- **预约物品不显示在你的名下：** 检查取件标识和馆方通知，误取则交回工作人员；不借别人的预约物。
- **到期日早于预期：** 以这件物品的实际收据为准，不能接受就现场询问或放弃借阅。
- **读屏、排队或设备不可用：** 选择工作人员服务或馆方可及通道，未登记成功不声称已借。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

卡片规则、可借种类、期限和费用因馆而异，现场馆方说明优先。单手、视听或语言条件不同可请工作人员协助核对，但借阅责任仍由实际账户承担。借到手后你要按真实到期日保管和归还；本篇不涉及费用争议、遗失赔偿或法律判断。

## English

Use this Skill when you have a book or another **circulating physical item** and want to take it out of the library. Complete its approved checkout and confirm its due date. The result is an active loan for this exact item in your own account. Holding the item, or hearing a device beep, does not by itself prove checkout. Catalog and shelf search belong elsewhere; holds, renewals, and returns have their own workflows.

### Preparation and inputs

Check for “in-library use,” “reference,” “non-circulating,” or similar restrictions and the current library's borrowing rules and your eligibility. Bring your own valid card, official library app, or another staff-approved method. Do not use someone else's credentials or write a card number or PIN on public paper. A holds-shelf item may have a named requester, pickup deadline, or card requirement; follow the actual notice rather than assume anything on that shelf is yours. Ask staff first if rules, charges, or account status are unclear.

### Execution

1. **Choose an approved checkout route.** Use a desk, self-check machine, or the library's official app checkout. Do not try to take an in-library-only item out. Read the screen or staff explanation for eligible item types.
2. **Present your own borrowing method and item.** Follow the library's steps to identify and record each item. For several items, check each title or barcode ending rather than assume one success covered all. If an app needs sign-in, use the official interface yourself without exposing your PIN.
3. **Read the result before leaving.** Check a screen, receipt, or your own library account for this item marked checked out to you and its due date. Note the date privately or keep the receipt. Loan lengths depend on the library and item, so this Skill gives no universal number of weeks.
4. **Handle errors or restrictions.** If the machine rejects the item, account does not match, item is non-circulating, or due date seems wrong, stay and ask staff to check. Do not carry it past the exit before checkout is confirmed. If you decide against borrowing, put it in the library's designated place rather than an arbitrary empty shelf.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

An official receipt or library system shows **this exact item in your loan list with a due date** before you take it away. Without a checkable result, treat the checkout as incomplete; an exit alarm is no proof either way.

- **The self-check scanned but the item is absent from your account:** Check screen and barcode, ask staff, and do not leave after blind repeated scans.
- **A held item does not match your account:** Check the pickup label and notice. Give a mistaken item to staff; do not borrow another person's hold.
- **Due date is earlier than expected:** Use the actual receipt for this item; ask or decline the loan on site if that does not work for you.
- **Screen, queue, or device is inaccessible:** Use staff or a formal accessible route. Do not claim a loan that was never confirmed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Cards, item types, periods, and charges vary by library; follow its current instructions. Staff can help with hand, sight, hearing, or language access, while responsibility follows the actual account. After checkout, keep and return the item by its real date. Fee disputes, loss claims, and legal decisions are outside this Skill.
