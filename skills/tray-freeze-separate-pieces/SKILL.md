## 中文

当你有一批**已确认适合单件托盘预冻、且完成该食品所需处理**的小件食品，未来想逐件拿取，而直接装一袋会冻成整块时，用这份 Skill。它分成“托盘暂冻 → 转入正式密封包装”两段；只在冰格有实际空间和低温能力时开始。它不教生肉分切、通用果蔬预处理或安全史不明食物的补救。

### 准备与输入

确认食品的名称、来源与保存历史、适用的冷冻及预处理说明，以及今天的真实日期。检查托盘在冰格里放得平、不会接触裸露的生动物性食品或被其汁液滴到，冰格可继续正常关闭。准备清洁且适合食品和冷冻的托盘表面、正式冷冻袋或盒、能在低温后读出的标签。手部操作或视力受限时，用不易滑动的浅托盘、可辨认的大字标记，或请可信的人协助摆放；不要在不稳的高层托盘上测试平衡。

### 执行

1. **先核对食品是否能走这条两段流程。** 不同果蔬或已处理小件可能需要不同的清洗、沥干、焯烫等前置步骤；只按适用的食品特定说明完成它们。本 Skill 从这些前置处理**已完成**开始；若不知是否需要，先停止，不用冰箱替答案卷签名。
2. **一层分开摆。** 将各件放在干净、食品适用的托盘面上，彼此留空隙，不堆成一包。为托盘标明名称和放入日期，必要时用适合食品与冰格的覆盖方式防止接触或污染；托盘暂放不是最终长期包装。
3. **保持可靠低温，等到能分别拿起。** 把托盘平稳放进可正常运行且有空间的冰格。按你的设备与食品说明等待各件冻硬；不要用一个对所有食物通用的小时数。中途若冰格故障、明显解冻或接触到生食汁液，停止转装并按实际食品安全情况处理。
4. **尽快转成密封包。** 一次准备好正式包装，在食品仍处于合适冻态时把小件移入，尽量减少不必要的室温停留。按包装要求排出多余空气并封好；若已有明显融化或历史无法确认，不用“重新冻硬”掩盖问题。
5. **把信息跟着食物走。** 在正式包装上写可区分的名称、实际转装/冷冻日期及其含义、份数或数量、必要的原食品说明。托盘上的记号不能留在冰格里当唯一记录。把包放回能满足适用低温要求的位置，检查以后能打开并取出一件，而其余仍可按要求保存。

### 成功条件

正式包装封好并可辨认；你能从中取出一件而无需解冻整块，其余食品仍在合适冷冻条件下。若托盘、预处理、污染隔离或低温条件不成立，明确停止并选适用的其他保存方式，而不是宣称完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **冻成一整片：** 这批不要强行敲碎或室温放软再冻；下次摆放时让小件间留空隙。当前食品的使用按其实际安全史和食品说明决定。
- **托盘一直占着冰格：** 转装阶段是任务的一部分。仅在仍可确认冻态与卫生条件时装进适用密封包装并补全标签；时间或温度史不明则停止推断。
- **要冻的食品需额外预处理：** 回到食品特定可信说明，不把这份一般流程当成生果、生菜和熟食的统一配方。
- **托盘可能被生食汁液滴到：** 不将受污染的即食食品继续转装；隔离并按当地安全指导处理。清洁托盘后才处理另一批。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份 Skill 不提供通用冷冻时长、解冻方法、果蔬焯烫时间或医疗饮食建议。不同食品的品质和安全条件不由形状决定；遵守原包装、食品特定来源及当地指导。若托盘和冰格容不下单层，可以改用已核对容量的固定单份密封包，或减少这次处理量。托盘占位与转装劳动是真实成本，但换来日后能只拿所需的几件。

## English

Use this Skill when you have small food pieces **confirmed suitable for individual tray freezing and already given any food-specific pretreatment**. You want to take out one piece later instead of thawing a solid block. This is a two-stage job: temporary tray freeze, then sealed labelled packaging. Start only if the freezer has real space and reliable cooling. It does not teach cutting raw meat, universal produce pretreatment, or repairing an unknown food-safety history.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the food's name, source and storage history, relevant freezing and pretreatment directions, and today's real date. Make sure a tray fits flat in the freezer, away from uncovered raw animal food and possible drips, while the door can still close. Have a clean food-suitable tray surface, final freezer bag or container, and a label you can read later. If placing pieces or reading print is difficult, use a stable shallow tray and large marks, or trusted help; do not test balance with an unstable high shelf.

### Execution

1. **Check whether this food can take the two-stage route.** Produce and prepared pieces may need different washing, draining, blanching, or other steps. Complete only the pretreatment specified for this food. This Skill begins **after** that work; if you do not know whether it is needed, stop and find the applicable guidance. A freezer cannot sign the missing answer sheet.
2. **Place one separated layer.** Arrange pieces on the clean food-suitable tray with gaps; do not stack them into one mass. Mark the tray with the food and placement date. Where needed, cover it with something suitable for food and freezer use to prevent contact or contamination. The tray is temporary, not long-term packaging.
3. **Keep the cold reliable until pieces lift separately.** Put the tray flat in a working freezer with room. Follow the appliance and food instructions until each piece is firm; do not invent one number of hours for all foods. If cooling fails, pieces visibly thaw, or raw juices contact them, stop the transfer and handle the actual food-safety situation.
4. **Move promptly to a sealed pack.** Have the final packaging ready. Transfer pieces while they remain appropriately frozen, avoiding unnecessary time at room temperature. Remove excess air as its directions permit and seal it. If the food has noticeably thawed or its history cannot be confirmed, do not conceal that by freezing it hard again.
5. **Move the information too.** Write an identifying name, actual freezing/packing date and its meaning, count or amount, and essential original directions on the final pack. A note left on the tray does not travel with the food. Return the pack to a suitable cold location and check that you can remove one piece later while keeping the rest stored as required.

### Success

The final pack is sealed and identifiable. You can remove one piece without thawing a block, while the rest stays in suitable freezing conditions. If tray space, pretreatment, separation from contamination, or cooling fails, a clear stop and another suitable storage decision is the honest result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The pieces become one slab:** Do not hammer or soften this batch at room temperature just to refreeze it. Leave gaps next time; handle the current food according to its real safety history and instructions.
- **The tray stays in the freezer indefinitely:** Transfer is part of the job. Pack and label only while the frozen and hygienic condition can still be confirmed; stop making assumptions if its time or temperature history is missing.
- **This food needs more pretreatment:** Return to a trusted food-specific guide. This general flow is not one recipe for raw fruit, vegetables, and cooked food.
- **Raw juices may have reached the tray:** Do not pack affected ready-to-eat food for later. Isolate it and follow local safety guidance. Clean the tray before another batch.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This Skill gives no universal freezing duration, thawing method, vegetable blanching time, or medical diet advice. Food shape does not determine quality or safety. Follow the original label, food-specific source, and local guidance. If your tray and freezer cannot hold one layer, use checked one-use sealed packs or process less this time. Tray space and transfer work are real costs; they buy the ability to take only the pieces you need later.
