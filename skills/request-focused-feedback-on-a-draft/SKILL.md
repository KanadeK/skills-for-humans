## 中文

你已有一份普通工作、学习或志愿协作的草稿/成果，需要别人给**能用于下一轮修改的具体意见**，而“你觉得怎么样”太宽时，用这份 Skill。结果是合适的人拿到正确版本、清楚你最想知道什么以及何时需要回复；得到反馈后的分类与修订归 H249，正式交付或验收也不由本 Skill 自动完成。

### 准备与输入

先说明这份材料的用途、目前是初稿还是接近交付、谁会使用或验收。挑一两个真正影响下一步的问题：结构是否能让读者找到结论、某段是否缺证据、操作步骤是否可按现有条件执行等。区分“普通读者体验意见”和“有权人决定是否通过”；找了解目标读者或有相应权限的人，不能请无权查看的朋友代审内部文件。留出对方阅读与你修改的实际时间。

### 执行

1. **先核对对象与可用时间。** 简短问适合的人是否愿意看这份具体材料，估计页数或所需时间，并给一个真实反馈时点；对方没空可以拒绝或建议你走正式渠道。若需要正式审批，找真正有权验收的人，不把一般同伴的意见记作批准。
2. **提供正确且能打开的版本。** 写明文件名/版本、位置和哪些部分需要看，确认共享权限和可读格式。初稿可以不完美，但不能让对方在三个同名文件里猜最新版。私有或敏感信息只走允许的渠道，不用“求反馈”获得额外访问权。
3. **问一两个可回答的问题。** 用“第 2 节是否清楚解释了这次选择？哪里需要补事实？”替代“整体怎么样”；同时允许对方指出你没想到的主要问题。若只需要大结构意见，别让对方逐字改所有标点；若真要校对，明确范围与期望。
4. **说清回复形式和下一动作。** 对方可用文档批注、短消息、纸面或约定的短谈，选双方都能用的方式。说明你何时要据反馈继续修改，以及哪些判断仍由你或正式验收者负责；反馈请求不是让对方代你重写或承担结果。
5. **按实际状态跟进。** 消息已发只记“已请求”；对方确认愿看才记“已接受反馈请求”，收到具体意见才记“反馈已收到”。真实截止临近可礼貌问一次是否仍方便；没有回复就调整自己的下一步或找合适渠道，不写“已审阅”。

### 成功条件

合适反馈对象通过获准渠道拿到可打开的正确版本，知道一两个具体问题、材料范围、预计投入和回复时点，能够接受或拒绝。若尚未确认接收，状态明确待定。完成的是**可回答的反馈请求**，不保证对方赞同、按时回复、替你修改或正式批准。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只发“麻烦看看”：** 补材料用途、当前阶段和一个最需要回答的问题。
- **链接能打开的是旧版：** 指出当前唯一版本，说明旧版只作参考或撤回错误链接。
- **对方是友好读者却无审批权：** 将其意见标读者反馈；需要验收时另找有权者。
- **周五截止，周四深夜才要求逐页批注：** 缩小问题或重新商量时点，不把不可行的工作量伪装成小忙。
- **对方说“我看到了”，你记为“已通过”：** 更正为材料可见/待反馈；意见和批准需实际表达。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于普通低风险成果的反馈请求，不要求别人免费承担专业审计，也不处理人事评价、法律或医疗意见。UW–Madison 与 Oregon State 的写作中心建议先定反馈目标、合适读者和具体问题；本文把这些移到一般可读成果，不把写作课程规则当所有协作的规定。视觉、阅读或软件限制下可提供双方可用格式或短口头说明，仍指向同一版本。对方可能发现更大的问题；这正是请求反馈的价值，而不是作者的失败。

## English

Use this Skill when you have an ordinary work, study, or volunteer draft or product and need **specific feedback you can use for a next revision**. “What do you think?” is too broad. Finish with an appropriate person able to open the right version and understand your main question and reply point. Sorting and applying comments belongs to H249; this request does not itself submit or approve the work.

### Preparation and inputs

State the work's purpose, whether it is an early draft or near delivery, and who will use or review it. Choose one or two questions that change your next step: can a reader find the conclusion, does a section need evidence, can the procedure run under actual conditions? Separate ordinary reader experience from an authorized pass/fail decision. Choose someone who knows the target reader or has the right authority; a friend without access must not review an internal file. Leave real time for their reading and your revision.

### Execution

1. **Check the person and their time.** Ask whether a suitable person can review this specific material, with an honest page count or effort and a real reply point. They may decline or direct you to a formal channel. For approval, ask the actual approver; do not log a peer opinion as authorization.
2. **Give the right openable version.** State filename or version, location, and what portion to read. Check permission and readable format. A draft may be imperfect, but three identically named files should not make them guess which is current. Private material stays in an allowed channel; requesting feedback is not a reason to grant unrelated access.
3. **Ask one or two answerable questions.** “Does section 2 explain this choice clearly? Where do I need another fact?” works better than “Any thoughts?” Allow them to flag a major problem you missed. If you need structure feedback, do not demand a line edit of every comma; if you need proofreading, define that scope.
4. **State reply form and next action.** Comments in a document, a short message, paper, or a brief talk can work if both people can use it. Say when you plan to revise and what judgment remains yours or the formal reviewer's. A feedback request is not a request that they rewrite the work or own the outcome.
5. **Follow the actual state.** Sent means only “requested.” When they agree to look, mark “review request accepted”; when comments arrive, mark “feedback received.” Near a real cutoff, check once whether the request is still feasible. Without a reply, adjust your next step or use an appropriate channel; do not log “reviewed.”

### Success

The suitable reviewer has the permitted, openable current version and knows one or two concrete questions, scope, expected effort, and reply point, with room to decline. If receipt is unconfirmed, the request remains pending. This is **an answerable feedback request**, not a guarantee of agreement, response, rewriting, or formal approval.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “Please take a look” was sent:** Add purpose, draft stage, and the one question that matters most.
- **The link opens an old version:** Point to the one current version and label the old one reference-only or withdraw the wrong link.
- **A friendly reader has no approval authority:** Log reader feedback as such; ask the actual approver for acceptance.
- **A Friday deadline meets a Thursday-night request for page-by-page edits:** Narrow the question or agree another time rather than disguising large work as a tiny favor.
- **“I saw the file” is logged as “approved”:** Correct it to visible/pending. Comments and approval require actual statements.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary low-risk feedback requests, not unpaid professional audits, personnel evaluation, legal opinion, or medical review. UW–Madison and Oregon State writing centers recommend feedback goals, suitable readers, and focused questions; this adapts those choices to ordinary readable work without exporting course-specific procedures. For sight, reading, or software barriers, offer a mutually usable format or short spoken context pointing to the same version. A reviewer may find a larger issue than expected. That is useful feedback, not the author's failure.
