## 中文

当你要为一组已知的普通非食品物品买收纳箱，却只能看到“多少升”或外部长宽高时，加载这份 Skill。目标是确认物品能从开口放进去、在箱内按你需要的方式摆放、盖得上并取得出来。它不判断食品接触、承重建筑结构或儿童使用安全；相关限制要按商品说明另行核对。

### 准备与输入

把本次要收的物品列成一组，选出最长、最宽、最高以及不能折叠或挤压的代表物。准备卷尺或可信的商品测量图，记录候选箱的**内部**底部尺寸、最窄开口、有效高度、盖子闭合后占用的空间，以及外部尺寸。包装标注的容积可用于粗筛，不能替代这些方向上的尺寸。

### 执行

1. **先描述实际装法。** 按会取用的频率决定平放、竖放或分层；不把易碎、怕压、需要通风的物品当成可无限压缩的体积。若一周会取多次，留出你能实际伸手取出的路径。
2. **量代表物和数量。** 给每件硬物记长宽高；软物按你愿意折叠后的实际包络测量。把手、突起和保护套也算进去。对于多件重复物，画一行或一层的排布，不只把升数相加。
3. **核对入口与内部。** 先看最大件能否以允许的方向穿过最窄开口，再看在箱底、侧壁和盖下能否放好。外尺寸通常包含壁厚；一个标着“大容量”的箱子也可能给长物件一个太窄的入口。
4. **核对关闭和取回。** 使用盖子的候选应能不压坏物品地闭合；把箱子放到预定位置后仍应能拉出或打开。商品若标了重量、堆叠或用途限制，按该型号的说明判断，不能由升数推导承重。
5. **给出结果。** 写“适合这组物品和装法”“不适合”或“缺内部/开口尺寸，待确认”。店内允许时，可用干净的代表物或同尺寸纸板试放；线上资料不足就向商家索取测量值，不能凭照片比例下单。

### 成功与停止

完成信号是最大件过得了入口、整组物品有可解释的排布、盖子与取物动作可行，而且商品标注用途允许。缺关键尺寸、重量限制或用途信息时暂停该候选，不把“标称容量更大”记作成功。

### 常见报错与补救

- **升数够，长物件放不进：** 改找更长的底面或更宽的开口；总容积不能补偿某一方向的短缺。
- **外部尺寸被当成内部尺寸：** 找产品图中的内部读数、现场实量，或问清；未知就标未知。
- **装得进但盖不上或拿不出：** 减少件数、改排布或换开口更合适的箱子；不要把脆弱物品压到闭合。
- **看不清细小标注或无法举箱试放：** 放大产品图、请人读数，或请店员展示内部和开口；不必为了验证而搬动重箱。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 用于普通非食品收纳，不把某地区的升、加仑、英寸或厘米当默认；比较前统一单位。箱子的材料、气味、食品接触许可、儿童接触风险和堆叠限制要看准确商品说明。你可能得到一张小小的排布草图，也可能发现现有箱子已经够用。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a box for a known set of ordinary non-food objects when the listing mainly offers litres, gallons, or outside dimensions. Your goal is to confirm that the objects pass through the opening, sit inside in a useful arrangement, allow the lid to close, and can be retrieved. Food-contact suitability, structural loads, and child safety require the exact product information outside this task.

### Preparation and inputs

List what will go in this box. Identify the longest, widest, tallest, rigid, and fragile examples. Get a tape measure or a reliable product drawing. Record the box's **internal** base dimensions, narrowest opening, usable height below the lid, and outside dimensions. Nominal volume is a rough filter, not a substitute for dimensions in each direction.

### Execution

1. **Choose the real arrangement.** Decide whether you will lay items flat, stand them up, or stack layers, and how often you need access. Do not treat fragile, crushable, or ventilation-sensitive objects as infinitely compressible. Leave a reachable path for frequently used items.
2. **Measure representative objects and count.** Record length, width, and height of rigid items, including handles and cases. Measure soft items after folding them the way you actually intend. Sketch a row or layer for repeated objects instead of adding up volumes alone.
3. **Check the opening, then the inside.** Confirm that the largest object can pass the narrowest opening in an allowed orientation. Then compare the planned arrangement with the inner base, sides, and closed-lid height. Walls occupy space; an impressive volume label cannot widen a narrow opening.
4. **Check closure and retrieval.** A lid should close without crushing contents, and you should still be able to pull out or open the box at its intended location. Follow any model-specific weight, stacking, and use restrictions; volume does not establish load capacity.
5. **Record a decision.** Mark the candidate suitable for this set and arrangement, unsuitable, or waiting for internal/opening measurements. If the store permits it, try a clean representative object or same-size card. Online, ask for the missing measurements rather than estimating from a photo.

### Success and stop conditions

You are done when the largest object passes the opening, the whole set has a plausible layout, the lid and access work, and the listed use allows it. Pause this candidate if a crucial dimension, load restriction, or use condition is missing. A larger nominal capacity alone is not a pass.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The volume is ample but a long item fails:** Find a longer base or wider opening; total volume cannot repair a shortage on one axis.
- **Outside numbers were mistaken for inside numbers:** Find an internal drawing, measure a shop sample, or ask. Keep an unknown value unknown.
- **The box fills but cannot close or be emptied conveniently:** Reduce the count, change the arrangement, or choose a better opening. Do not crush fragile contents to make the lid click.
- **Small print or a heavy sample is inaccessible:** Enlarge the product image, ask someone to read it, or ask staff to show the opening. You do not need to lift a heavy box to prove fit.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary non-food storage. Convert between regional units before comparing. Check the exact listing for material, odour, food-contact approval, child-contact risks, and stacking limits. The result may be a small layout sketch, or the discovery that a box you already own does the job.
