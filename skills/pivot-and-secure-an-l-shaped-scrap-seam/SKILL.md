## 中文

当你已在废布上成功做过短直线，想再练**停针转一个直角、用本机支持的倒缝或加固针固定两端**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一条可见 L 形练习缝，角点无大针孔/拉扯，起末端不轻易松开。它不同于第一条直线试缝，但仍不是衣服/承重布件的交付。电子机可能用加固键代替连续倒缝，一律按本机说明。

### 准备与输入

查本机直线针、针位、压脚、倒缝/加固功能与“针停在布里”的操作说明。废布画两条相交短线，布质与机针线相容；短线离布边留足空间。手/衣袖不靠近针与踏板，针弯、电线损或声音异常不练。不会控制针停位置的机型先查说明，不能用手转动正运行的布。

### 执行

1. **按本机允许方法固定起点。** 放布、降压脚，从起点慢缝几针；若说明允许，按规定用短倒缝/加固针回到起点，再向角点慢速前进。具体倒缝按键和针数取本机指引，不把别机视频的数字搬来。
2. **到角点完全停机。** 针保持落在布中；若机型停在上方，按本机针位/手轮步骤把针落到角点。手离开启动控制，才抬压脚，轻转布使下一段导线正对送布方向；不要靠拉布让针弯。
3. **降压脚再走第二段。** 角度对准后放下压脚，双手远离针区，低速缝到终点。按本机支持的短倒缝或加固步骤固定末端，随后停机、让针处安全位置，取布切线。
4. **检查 L 形与端部。** 看两段线与角是否连续、布不被针卡破，轻牵端部确认不立即散，正反面无线团；记录本机使用的转角/加固控制。若针受力、角起褶或线断，停止在废布诊断，不开始正式作品。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

废布上有连续的 L 形线，起末固定方式可指出，布与针完好、机器安全停；仅在运动中拽转布导致线歪或针弯，不算成功转角。

- **角点停不住：** 不在机动时拉布，查本机针停/手轮方式重新在另一块废布练。
- **抬压脚时针也抬起，布跑位：** 停、重新对准角点，依本机说明先落针再转。
- **倒缝成团或变成连续反走：** 松开控制，查本机加固功能和线张力，改小段重试。
- **针碰压脚或断线：** 立即停用并查设备/厂商，不徒手继续带电试。
- **手眼/脚踏协调不便：** 大 L 形导线、慢速/启停键与熟悉设备的可信协助可帮助，但须能可靠停机。

### 假设与边界

兄弟机示例用针在布里、抬压脚转布、再降压脚继续；其他机型按钮与倒缝行为不同。本文只练一处角与端点，不保证成衣缝口强度。

## English

Load this Skill after you have already sewn a short straight scrap seam and want to practise **stopping with the needle in cloth, pivoting one right-angle corner, and securing both ends by this model's reverse or reinforcement function**. Finish with a visible L-shaped practice seam, sound corner and ends that do not immediately unravel. This differs from the first straight test but is still not a wearable or load-rated deliverable. Electronic machines may use a reinforcement key instead of continuous reverse; follow the manual.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this model's straight stitch, needle-position, presser-foot, reverse/reinforcement, and needle-down stop directions. Draw two short meeting lines on compatible owned scrap with room from its edge. Keep fingers and clothing away from needle and pedal. Do not practise with a bent needle, damaged cord, or abnormal sound. If needle-down control is unknown, read the manual before touching fabric on a running machine.

### Execution

1. **Secure the start under this model.** Place cloth, lower foot, and sew a few slow stitches. If directed, use a brief reverse/reinforcement step back toward the start, then progress slowly toward the corner. The exact button and count come from this machine, not another model's video.
2. **Stop fully at the corner.** Leave needle down in fabric. If this model stops up, use its needle-position or handwheel procedure to place the needle at the corner. Remove foot from control before raising presser foot and turn fabric gently so the second line faces feed direction. Do not pull cloth against a needle.
3. **Lower foot and sew the second leg.** Align, lower foot, clear hands, and sew slowly to the end. Use a short model-supported reverse/reinforcement there, stop safely, raise needle by the manual, remove cloth, and cut threads.
4. **Inspect L and ends.** Check line continuity and a corner without large damaged holes, tug ends gently for immediate hold, inspect front and reverse for nests, and note which control you used. Stop at needle force, corner puckers, or broken thread and diagnose on scrap before real work.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Scrap shows a continuous L, start/end method is known, fabric and needle are sound, and machine is safely stopped. Pulling a moving fabric sideways into a crooked line or bent needle is not a successful pivot.

- **Cannot stop at the corner:** Do not pull fabric while moving; review needle-stop/handwheel and retry on new scrap.
- **Foot rises while needle is up and cloth shifts:** Stop, realign, and lower needle by this model's procedure before turning.
- **Reverse bunches or keeps moving backward:** Release control, check this model's reinforcement and tension, and retry a short section.
- **Needle hits foot or thread breaks:** Stop and check equipment with maker help, not more powered bare-hand trials.
- **Grip, sight, or pedal coordination is limited:** Larger L marks, slow/start-stop control, and trusted model-aware help may assist only if you can stop reliably.

### Assumptions and limits

A Brother example uses needle in cloth, raised foot to turn, then foot down. Other models differ in controls and reverse behaviour. This practises one corner and endpoint, not garment seam strength.
