## 中文

当普通食品要从原包装转入容器，或要分成几份保存，而你面前有盒、瓶、袋却不确定哪一个适合**这件食品和这次保存条件**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是选出有说明支持、状态良好、能盖好且保留食品身份的容器；没有合适的就停止转移。本篇只选保存容器，不规定食品能放多久，不决定是否该冷冻、如何加热，也不做罐藏、发酵、婴幼儿食品或医疗饮食处理。

### 准备与输入

先读食品的名称、开封状态、包装上的保存说明，以及是否需要冷藏、冷冻或仅常温。再明确这次容器会接触什么食品、食品进入容器时是否仍热、会在什么温度下放置、是否可能漏液、预计接触多久。把“存放”与“直接加热”分开；一个写着可冷冻的盒子并不自动适合加热。

找出候选容器的生产商说明、盖子、表面状态及是否能按说明清洁。塑料三角形里的数字主要告诉你材料类别或回收识别，不能独自证明它适合这次食品、温度或微波用途。看不清说明时放大照片、用读屏或向生产商询问，不用颜色或手感猜。

### 执行

1. **先问是否必须换装。** 完整原包装若仍符合食品的当前保存要求，通常不必只为“看起来整齐”另倒一次。若已开封、包装无法盖好、需要分份或原包装不适合当前存放，再进入容器选择；开封后的期限另按该食品说明决定。
2. **核对食品接触与具体用途。** 只选生产商表明可接触食品、且适用于这次存放温度和方式的容器。检查对冷藏、冷冻、热食、油脂或酸性食品及接触时间的限制；没有明确支持时不用“应该没事”补齐说明。曾用于非食品的盒瓶，不因洗过就自动变成食品容器。
3. **检查容器现在是否可用。** 容器和盖子应能按说明清洁、已清洁干燥，且无明显裂缝、变形、损坏表面或无法盖合的地方。一次性或外卖包装只有在原说明允许这次用途、且状态合适时才考虑复用；原先装冷食的盒子不自动适合热食。
4. **匹配封口和份量。** 需要防漏的食品用能实际防漏的容器；需要覆盖的食品有能盖好的盖或适用封口。选能装下这次份量并让你安全拿取的位置，不靠硬压盖子防止溢出。若要冷冻，另确认容器说明支持冷冻及相关装填限制；分装量和冷冻标记交给冷冻任务。
5. **转移时保留来源。** 写明食品名称、原包装需要继续遵守的保存条件、开封或转移日期，并保留仍有用的原标签、批次及日期信息。不要把不同批次或状态倒入同一个无从区分的盒子，也不要因写了今天的转移日而发明一个新的安全期限。
6. **没有合适容器就停。** 若原包装依然合格，继续按它的说明保存；否则先取得有明确用途说明的容器。无法安全保存的食品按其已知时间/温度史和当地可靠指导另作处置，不把无盖盒或非食品瓶当作临时长期方案。

### 完成条件

所选容器有可核对的食品接触及温度/用途说明，当前状态完好，封口符合这件食品的需要，食品身份和原有保存要求仍可辨认。若任一关键条件缺失，你能指出缺什么并停止转移；成功不是“所有食物都找到一个空盒”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有塑料编号，没有用途说明：** 编号不能替代生产商的食品接触、温度和使用限制；查原说明或换一件说明清楚的容器。
- **外卖盒曾装过食物：** 过去能装那一餐，不保证它适合冷冻、长期保存或再加热。按本次用途重新核对，说明缺失就不使用。
- **盖子不合、表面开裂或变形：** 不靠保鲜膜补救一个不适合的容器；换完好且封口合适的选择。
- **油多、酸性强或食物很热：** 核对该容器对这类食品、温度和接触时间的明确说明；无依据时先换适用容器，不自行推测某种塑料代码的耐受性。
- **转移后忘了原包装写什么：** 保留原包装或清晰照片，补上能证实的产品信息；不知道过去的开封或保存历史时不要新写一个假日期。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

容器材料和同一材料的不同产品配方、盖子及用途都可能不同，不能按材质名给所有产品一张通用许可。这里假设你能取得食品及容器说明；读字、开盖或握持困难时，可用大字说明、易开合且有明确用途的型号或可信协助，不要求使用难以操作的容器。清洗器具和摆放位置另按对应流程完成。

可能的副作用是需要洗一个合适的容器，或暂时不用那只外形很像万能工具的旧盒子。空盒子不是兼容性证明。

## English

Load this Skill when ordinary food must leave its original pack or be split into portions for storage, and you have boxes, bottles, or bags but do not know which one fits **this food and these storage conditions**. Finish with a container whose stated use supports the job, that is in good condition, closes as needed, and keeps the food identifiable. Stop transferring if none qualifies. This selects storage containers; it does not set a storage lifetime, decide whether to freeze, heat food, can, ferment, or handle infant foods or medical diets.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the food name, opening state, and original storage directions, including whether it needs refrigeration, freezing, or ambient storage. Identify the food that will touch the container, whether it is still hot, the storage temperature, possible leaks, and expected contact time. Separate storage from heating in the same vessel; “freezer suitable” does not automatically mean “safe for heating.”

Find each candidate container's maker instructions, lid, condition, and cleaning directions. A number in a plastic recycling triangle mainly identifies a material category; by itself it does not prove suitability for this food, temperature, or microwave use. Enlarge a photo, use reading access, or ask the maker when instructions are hard to read. Do not decide from colour or feel.

### Execution

1. **Ask whether transfer is needed.** An intact original pack that still meets the food's current storage requirements usually need not be emptied just for visual neatness. Continue if the pack has been opened and cannot close, portions are needed, or it cannot serve the current storage use. Decide the after-opening lifetime from that food's own instructions separately.
2. **Check food contact and intended use.** Choose a container whose maker states that it is for food contact and for this storage method and temperature. Read limits for refrigeration, freezing, hot contents, fatty or acidic food, and contact time. Do not fill a missing instruction with “probably fine.” Washing a vessel formerly used for non-food substances does not automatically make it a food container.
3. **Check its current condition.** Container and lid must be cleanable under their instructions, already clean and dry, with no obvious cracks, warping, damaged surface, or failed closure. Reuse a single-use or takeaway pack only if its instructions support this use and its condition still fits. A tub originally used for cold food is not automatically suitable for hot contents.
4. **Match closure and amount.** Food needing leak protection requires a closure that actually prevents leaks; food needing covering needs a suitable lid or seal. Choose a size that holds this portion and is safe for you to lift, without forcing the lid over a spill. For freezing, separately confirm freezer approval and filling limits; portion amounts and freezer labels belong to the freezing task.
5. **Preserve origin during transfer.** Record food name, original storage requirements still in force, and opening or transfer date. Keep useful original label, batch, and date information. Do not combine different batches or states in an untraceable container, or invent a new safe lifetime from today's transfer date.
6. **Stop when no container qualifies.** If original packaging still works, keep using it according to its directions. Otherwise obtain a container with clear intended-use information. Decide what to do with food that cannot be stored safely from its actual time/temperature history and applicable local guidance, not from an uncovered bowl or non-food bottle treated as long-term storage.

### Success

The chosen vessel has checkable food-contact and temperature/use instructions, is sound now, closes as this food requires, and preserves the food's identity and original storage conditions. If a critical condition is missing, you can name it and stop transfer. The finish line is not “every food found an empty box.”

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a plastic code is visible:** It does not replace the maker's food-contact, temperature, and use restrictions. Find the instructions or choose a better-documented container.
- **A takeaway tub held food once:** That does not prove freezer, long-storage, or reheating suitability. Check this use again; without instructions, do not use it.
- **The lid fails or a surface is cracked or warped:** Choose an intact, correctly closing option rather than using wrap to excuse the unsuitable vessel.
- **Food is fatty, acidic, or still hot:** Check explicit suitability for that food, temperature, and contact time. Without evidence, choose another container instead of guessing from a resin code.
- **You lost the original pack information after transfer:** Keep the pack or a clear photo and restore only facts you can verify. Do not invent an earlier opening or storage date.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Materials, formulas, lids, and intended uses vary even among products with the same material name. No material gets a blanket approval here. This assumes you can access food and container instructions. If print, lids, or grip are difficult, use larger instructions, an easier-to-open documented container, or trusted assistance. Cleaning and shelf placement follow their own workflows.

You may have one suitable container to wash, and one old tub that no longer qualifies as universal equipment. An empty vessel is not compatibility evidence.
