## 中文

当你还需要手上的图书馆物品、当前到期日将到，而馆方提供续借时，用这份 Skill **申请并核实一笔续借**。结果只能是馆方明确的新到期日，或明确的不能续借及下一步；点击按钮、收到自动提醒或“应该会自动续”都不能代替系统确认。

### 准备与输入

在馆方正式账户、原借阅收据或柜台确认该物品的当前到期日与借阅状态。核对馆方对该类物品的续借资格、次数、预约排队与可能费用；不同馆、特殊馆藏和借期规则可能不同。使用自己的账户或工作人员正式渠道，不把 PIN 写在共享日历或向别人发送。若已经收到别人预约或不可续借提示，预备按原日期归还或问馆员，不把“想再看几天”当审批。

### 执行

1. **锁定具体那一笔。** 在自己的已借清单里对照题名、条码尾码或其他馆方标识与当前到期日，避免对错书续借。若账户里不显示该物品，先找馆员核实，不凭记忆操作。
2. **用馆方允许的方式提交。** 按正式网站/App、电话、自助设备或柜台的当前流程申请。若页面提示不可续借、需要归还或受他人预约约束，保留提示，不反复点直到界面消失。
3. **核新日期，而不只核按钮反应。** 在账户、确认页、新收据或馆员答复中读出这件物品的新到期日，并和原日期比较。若日期没变或交易状态待处理，写“尚未确认续借”，按原到期日行动，尽快联系馆方。
4. **处理成功或失败。** 成功则把私人日历改成确切新日期，并保留馆方确认。失败则按原到期日和馆方允许的归还方式安排；物品已不在手、遗失或损坏时告知馆方按其流程处理，不自行假造续借。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你能出示馆方记录里的**该物品新到期日**，或明确知道它不能续借并据原到期日安排后续。只有自己把日历往后改，不是续借成功。

- **系统提示自动续借：** 仍核实际借阅记录的新日期；“可能自动”不等于已发生。
- **续借按钮没有响应：** 再查账户是否真的更新；未更新时换馆方正式通道或联系工作人员，不重复盲点。
- **同账户多件到期日不同：** 逐件核对，不把一件成功套到全批。
- **馆方拒绝但你暂时不能到馆：** 查实际馆的可用归还方式与开放时间，必要时联系馆员；不承诺例外或免除费用。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

馆方对续借资格、次数、自动续借和截止时间的规则会变，本文不设统一天数。可用电话、柜台或可及设备替代网页操作；你仍要确认具体结果。续借成功只改变这一笔借阅日期，不增加所有资料的借期，也不解决遗失、罚费或法律争议。

## English

Use this Skill when you still need a library item you have borrowed, its due date is approaching, and the library offers renewal. **Request and verify one renewal.** The outcome is either a confirmed new due date or a clear denial and next action. Pressing a button, seeing an automatic reminder, or assuming an automatic renewal does not replace confirmation.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the item's current due date and loan status in the library's official account, original receipt, or with staff. Read current eligibility for this type, renewal limits, other patrons' holds, and possible charges. Libraries and special collections differ. Use your own account or a formal staff route; do not put a PIN in a shared calendar or message it to someone. If another hold or restriction appears, prepare to return by the original date or ask staff instead of treating your need for extra time as approval.

### Execution

1. **Identify the exact loan.** Compare title, barcode ending, or other library identifier and current due date in your checked-out list. Do not renew the wrong book. If it is absent from your account, ask staff to establish its status before proceeding.
2. **Submit through an allowed route.** Use the actual site or app, phone, self-service station, or desk. If the screen says no renewal, return required, or another patron has a hold, keep that result; do not keep clicking until the message disappears.
3. **Check the date, not the button reaction.** Read the new due date for this item in your account, confirmation, new receipt, or staff answer, and compare it with the old one. If unchanged or pending, mark “renewal unconfirmed,” act by the original due date, and contact the library promptly.
4. **Act on success or denial.** On success, update a private calendar to the exact new date and keep the official confirmation. On denial, arrange an allowed return by the original date. If the item is missing or damaged, tell staff and follow their process rather than invent a renewal.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can show an official **new due date for the exact item**, or know it cannot be renewed and have a route based on the original date. Moving your own calendar date alone is not a renewal.

- **The system mentions automatic renewal:** Still check the loan record for an actual new date. “May renew automatically” is not “did renew.”
- **The button seems unresponsive:** Check whether the account changed. If not, use another official route or ask staff instead of repeatedly clicking.
- **Several items on one account have different dates:** Verify each separately; one success does not extend the whole set.
- **Renewal is denied and you cannot visit immediately:** Check actual return routes and hours, and contact staff if needed. Do not promise an exception or waived charge.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Renewal eligibility, count, automation, and cutoffs change by library; this Skill gives no universal number of days. Phone, desk, or accessible equipment can replace a website, but you still confirm the exact result. A successful renewal changes this loan's date only. It does not settle loss, damage, fees, or legal disputes.
