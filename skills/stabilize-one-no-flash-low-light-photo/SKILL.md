## 中文

当你在**允许拍照但不适合/不允许闪光的低光地点**，要拍一个静止获准物件且能安全稳住现有相机时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张放大后主体相对清楚、没有明显手抖的照片，或诚实承认此光线/设备限制。它与 E019 的静物焦点选择不同：镜头可能正确对焦，快门打开期间手抖仍会使全图糊。不能为了拍照把三脚架堵在通道，也不在明示禁拍处举机。

### 准备与输入

核地点允许摄影且闪光禁令、人物隐私和物件许可都满足；闪光按本机说明真正关闭，自动弹闪需检查。选不挡路的稳定站位或自有安全平面，已有三脚架仅在场地允许且稳固时用，不要求购买。查本机低光/防抖模式和自定时可用性；室内暗处别把相机架在高边缘或靠近水/热源。长曝光设备防抖与三脚架组合随型号，按手册不硬套“必开/必关”。

### 执行

1. **先定静止目标和支撑。** 找主体上可辨的纹理，双脚站稳、肘靠身体/稳固支点，或相机置于获准平面；不把手/镜头挡住。目标若自己在移动，本篇稳机不能解决主体运动。
2. **按本机模式设置与曝光。** 在不闪光条件下用相机允许的低光/稳像或手动设置，依据本机提示让画面不过度暗；提高感光度可能多噪点、慢快门可能抖，所以不要只追一个数值。稳固支架上可用本机自定时/遥控（已有时）减少按快门的瞬间抖动。
3. **拍一张并马上放大。** 保持相机直到曝光结束，不刚按下就移动；回放放大看主体纹理及静止背景是否一同拖影。如果全图同方向糊，先查支撑和曝光时间；只有目标糊时查对焦，而不是继续同设置连拍。
4. **做一次可解释的复拍。** 可安全改善支撑/允许光线或按本机缩短曝光后拍第二张，记改动和噪点/亮度代价。若无法同时满足禁闪、稳定与足够光线，就停并记未取得清晰照片，不冒险找禁止机位。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

至少一张低光无闪静物照片在放大后仍有可见主体细节，设置和支撑方式可回查；或物理限制被明确写出。缩略图看着亮却放大一片抖影，不算完成。

- **自动闪光仍弹起：** 停拍，按本机真正关闪流程，未解决则不拍。
- **全图拖影：** 加稳固支撑、用计时或按本机缩短曝光，不把失焦当唯一原因。
- **只有主体糊：** 查对焦/主体是否动，不无限提高 ISO 以掩盖。
- **三脚架/支撑挡路或不稳：** 立即撤下，改手持/另找获准位置或放弃。
- **行动/视觉不便：** 低位桌面、可访问回放和可信协助描述放大细节，仍须守场地规则。

### 假设与边界

慢快门、ISO、光圈及设备防抖能力因型号不同；本篇没有万能曝光数或专业夜景承诺。只拍静止普通对象，不在危险/禁拍场所试技术。

## English

Load this Skill at a **permitted low-light site where flash is unsuitable or forbidden** when a still permitted object can be photographed with your existing camera supported safely. Finish with an enlarged image retaining subject detail without obvious camera shake, or an honest equipment/light limit. This differs from E019's focus selection: focus may be right while camera movement during exposure blurs everything. Never block paths with a tripod or photograph where photography itself is forbidden.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm site photo permission, flash rules, people privacy, and object access. Actually disable automatic flash under the camera manual. Find stable footing or an owned safe support, using an existing tripod only if allowed and secure; no purchase is required. Check low-light/stabilisation mode and timer options. Do not balance a camera at a high edge near water or heat. Camera stabilisation with a tripod varies, so follow the exact model rather than a universal on/off rule.

### Execution

1. **Choose a still subject and support.** Find a visible detail, stand with elbows supported or set the camera on a permitted stable plane without covering lens. A moving subject will not be fixed by steadying only the camera.
2. **Use this model's no-flash exposure route.** Select permitted low-light/stabilisation or manual controls and keep image usable under its indications. Higher sensitivity may add noise and slower shutter may add shake, so no one magic number wins. On stable support, an existing timer/remote can reduce press shake.
3. **Capture and enlarge immediately.** Hold until exposure ends instead of moving as soon as shutter is pressed. Zoom playback for subject texture and still-background motion. If all of it streaks the same way, check support and exposure time; if only subject is soft, check focus rather than repeating blindly.
4. **Retake with one explained change.** Safely improve support or permitted light or shorten exposure under the model, then record change and noise/brightness tradeoff. If no safe combination meets flash prohibition, stability, and light, stop with a true no-clear-photo result rather than seek a forbidden viewpoint.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

At least one no-flash low-light still image keeps visible subject detail on enlarged review with support/settings traceable, or a real physical limit is stated. Bright thumbnail over enlarged smear is not completion.

- **Automatic flash still fires:** Stop, follow the model's true flash-off path, and do not shoot if unresolved.
- **Whole frame streaks:** Stabilise, use timer, or shorten exposure as the model permits; do not blame focus alone.
- **Only subject blurs:** Check focus and whether it moved rather than only raising ISO.
- **Tripod or support blocks or wobbles:** Remove it at once; handhold safely, move to a permitted site, or stop.
- **Movement or vision is limited:** Low table, accessible playback, and trusted enlarged-detail description help while preserving site rules.

### Assumptions and limits

Slow exposure, ISO, aperture, and stabilisation differ by body. No universal exposure number or professional night result is promised. This is for still ordinary objects at a lawful safe site.
