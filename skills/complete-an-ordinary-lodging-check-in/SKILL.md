## 中文

你已到达预订的普通酒店、旅舍或短租住宿，准备按**本次住宿的实际规则**进入被分配的房间或床位时，用这份 Skill。完成指的是必要交接结束、进入方式可用、你确实到了分配给自己的空间；手机显示“已入住”但门打不开不算完成。出发前核对设施归 H174，进房后发现缺物归 H176，退房归 H177。

### 准备与输入

先在安全、不挡人的位置核对住宿名称、地址、日期、入住时段和最新消息；同一品牌或同名街道不能替代地址。找到本次预订提供的接待处、自助入口或接头人的官方位置。准备住宿方实际要求出示的预订信息或身份证明，但只向真实接待人员或预订渠道要求的正式流程出示；不要把证件、门禁码、付款信息发进公共聊天。若指引与现场标识不同，先联系住宿方确认。

### 执行

1. **确认该走哪条入住路径。** 有前台或指定接待人，就到正确接待点说明本次预订及同行人数；按该住宿的实际要求完成核对和交接。自助入住则从本次预订的官方消息中找到指定入口、可使用时间、锁或钥匙位置；不要试隔壁相似房门，也不要把“自助”理解为任何时刻都能进。
2. **把分配结果听清或看清。** 对照本次预订的日期、房型或床位以及同行人数，核对实际分配的位置、可进入的门、房卡/实体钥匙/手机钥匙的使用方式，还有本次需要知道的进出时段。新出现的费用、押金或身份材料要求若与你收到的预订信息不一致，先请官方接待点说明，不在陌生链接上急着完成交易。
3. **在允许的时间测试进入。** 沿指定路径到分配的门口，确认房号或床位标识，使用住宿方提供的方式进入。手机钥匙只在这次入住实际启用后才算可用；有些服务仍可能要求先到前台核验。门打不开时，先核对房号、入住时间、最新指引和钥匙状态；不要反复试不属于你的门或拆弄门锁。
4. **做最短的到达交接。** 若确实能进入分配空间，确认钥匙或再次进入的方法、遇到问题该联系谁、是否还有必须遵守的安静时段或公共区域规则。暂时不能拿到房间时，向接待方确认下一次可核对的时间、你是否能安全等候以及行李是否有正式寄存服务；只有对方确实收下并给出取回方式时，才把行李记为“已寄存”。
5. **无法进入就留下可追踪状态。** 从预订内的号码、平台消息或住宿方公布的服务台联系负责人，说明预订、当前地点、时间、看到的错误和你已试过的正确步骤。若无法联系或仍不能进入，再按预订平台公布的支持路径求助；记录回复与下一次行动，而不是宣称已经入住。发生现实安全问题时先到安全的公共位置求助，不在封闭或陌生入口继续试。

### 成功条件

住宿方已完成必要核对和交接，你能按获准方式实际进入**分配给你的**房间或床位，并知道如何再次进入及如何联系负责人。若只有确认了预订却尚无可用房间/钥匙，状态是“入住待完成”，要保留下一次核对点。进房后设施或物品与承诺不符，另按实际问题与住宿方沟通，不把本 Skill 扩成房间验收或索赔流程。

### 常见报错与补救

- **地图把你带到同名或侧门：** 对照预订地址与官方入口说明；有疑问先联系住宿方，不跟随陌生人进未知房间。
- **自助密码、钥匙盒或手机钥匙不可用：** 核对本次指引和有效时段，按官方联系方法报告具体失败；不尝试猜码或自行拆修。
- **前台说房间还没准备好：** 请对方给下一次查询时间、可等候地点及实际寄存条件；没有交接凭据的行李仍由你照看。
- **前台找不到预订或分配不符：** 出示本次确认记录让接待点复核日期、姓名和房型；在问题解决前不要使用别人的房间。
- **原先确认的进入路径现场不能使用：** 停下并请住宿方给可用路线或其他安排；不要搬行李冲过不适合自己的楼梯。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程只覆盖普通住宿的到达、交接和获得使用权；证件、押金、付款与当地登记要求由实际住宿及适用规则决定，这里不提供法律或财务判断。没有智能手机可询问正式实体钥匙或工作人员交接；听不清口头说明可请对方写下房号和再进入方式；不能使用某条路径就请住宿方给实际可用的替代。平台显示的状态、预计开放时间和他人口头保证，都不能代替你能进入被分配空间这一结果。

## English

Use this Skill when you have arrived at an ordinary hotel, hostel, or short stay and need to enter the room or bed **actually assigned to this reservation** under its real check-in method. Completion means the necessary handover is done, your entry method works, and you have reached your assigned space. An app saying “checked in” while the door stays shut is not completion. H174 covers pre-arrival facility checks; H176 covers missing items after entry; H177 covers departure.

### Preparation and inputs

From a safe place that does not block others, compare the property name, address, dates, arrival window, and latest messages. A matching chain logo or similar street name is not the address. Find the official location of this booking's desk, self check-in entrance, or host handover. Prepare whatever booking details or identity proof the actual property requires, but show them only through its official desk or formal booking process. Do not post ID, door codes, or payment details in a shared chat. If instructions and signs disagree, confirm with the property first.

### Execution

1. **Choose the actual check-in route.** At a staffed desk or agreed handover, state the reservation and party size and complete the property's real verification. For self check-in, use this reservation's official messages to find the specified entrance, valid time, lock, or key pickup. Do not test a similar neighboring door. “Self check-in” does not mean “enter at any hour.”
2. **Confirm what was assigned.** Compare the actual room or bed, dates, room type, and party size with your booking. Learn which door to use, how the keycard, physical key, or mobile key works, and any entry hours you need to know. If a new fee, deposit, or document request differs from the reservation, ask the official desk to explain before using an unfamiliar payment link.
3. **Test entry at the permitted time.** Follow the assigned route and check the room or bed label before using the provided entry method. A mobile key counts only when activated for this stay; some services may still require an identity check at the desk. If the door fails, recheck the number, time, latest instructions, and key status. Do not keep trying other rooms or tamper with a lock.
4. **Complete the shortest useful handover.** Once inside the assigned space, confirm how to re-enter, whom to contact for a problem, and any essential quiet-hour or shared-space rule. If the room is not ready, ask when to check again, where you can wait safely, and whether formal bag storage exists. Mark a bag “stored” only after staff have actually accepted it and given a retrieval method.
5. **Track any failed entry.** Use the number in the booking, platform messages, or the property's published desk to report the reservation, current location and time, exact failure, and correct steps already tried. If no one responds or access still fails, use the booking platform's published support path. Keep the reply and next action; do not claim check-in is complete. If your immediate surroundings are unsafe, go to a safe public place for help rather than continuing at an isolated entrance.

### Success

The property has completed its necessary verification and handover, you can actually enter **your assigned** room or bed by an authorized method, and you know how to re-enter and reach the responsible person. A confirmed reservation without a usable room or key is “check-in pending,” with a next check point. Once inside, a missing or mismatched facility follows a separate communication task; this Skill is not a room-inspection or compensation process.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The map leads to a similarly named place or side entrance:** Compare the booking address and official entrance; ask the property before following a stranger into an unknown space.
- **A self check-in code, lockbox, or mobile key fails:** Recheck this booking's instructions and valid time, then report the exact failure through the official contact. Do not guess codes or repair the lock.
- **The desk says the room is not ready:** Get the next check time, a usable waiting place, and the real storage terms. Keep charge of bags without a completed handover.
- **The desk cannot find the booking or assignment differs:** Present the confirmation so staff can recheck date, name, and room type. Do not enter someone else's room while the mismatch remains.
- **The pre-confirmed access path does not work on site:** Stop and ask the property for a usable route or arrangement; do not force yourself and luggage up an unsuitable staircase.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary arrival, handover, and usable entry only. Identity, deposits, payments, and local registration depend on the actual property and applicable rules; this is not legal or financial guidance. Without a smartphone, ask about an official physical key or staff handover. If spoken directions are hard to hear, request a written room number and re-entry method. If a route does not work for you, ask for an actually usable alternative. App status, expected opening time, and another person's reassurance cannot replace access to the assigned space.
