## 中文

当厂家说明允许你对普通非承重用品的一颗螺丝做手动装入、微调或退出，且正确刀头已经选好时加载。你要让**这颗螺丝按说明到达允许的位置，或在打滑、偏斜和异常阻力出现时停止并说明未完成**。拧紧不等于结构安全；这里不装承重或防倾倒部件，不开电气/机械/燃气设备，也不使用动力工具。

### 准备与输入

核对准确说明里的螺丝/孔位、操作方向和完成状态；有指定力矩或专业安装要求而你没有对应方法时停止。用品应按说明稳定放置，按手工具说明戴合适护目镜，并留出让螺丝刀与螺丝轴线对正的空间；把手或身体不要放在刀尖可能滑出的方向。刀头/手柄损坏、螺丝头已滑圆或零件不符时，先退出本步骤。

### 执行

1. **轻放刀头并对直。** 刀头完整坐进槽中，手动杆尽量与螺丝轴线一致。别用倾斜手势绕开挡路物，也别把螺丝刀当撬具调整物件位置。
2. **按准确说明小幅转动。** 装入时先确认螺丝与指定孔位自然接合；退出时确认它确实在松开。方向以该产品和螺纹说明为准，不假设每种螺丝都是同一方向。保持足以让刀头贴合的平稳手压，不靠猛压或增加杠杆。
3. **边转边看阻力与头部。** 正常转动应有可辨的渐进变化。刚开始就歪、越拧越卡、刀头跳出、槽边被刮圆或材料开始裂时立即停；卸下重对或更换错误零件只在说明允许且状态完好时继续，已损坏交实际供应/专业渠道。
4. **在说明的完成点停。** 不同用品可能要求齐平、留活动余量或给定位置；按准确型号说明判断，不以“还能再拧”当作目标。没有明确终点或涉及承重/防护时保持未确认，不代替专业验收。
5. **做一次低风险复核。** 看这颗螺丝与指定位置一致、头部未明显损坏、相关普通部件按说明能进行下一步。拆下的螺丝暂存于能对应原位置的地方；完整组装和收尾由各自流程完成。

### 完成与停止

这颗普通螺丝按准确说明装入或退出，过程没有可见滑牙、偏斜、材料损伤或未解释的异常阻力；这只完成一个操作，不证明整件物品安全承重。无法对正、头部滑圆、孔位不符、需要过大力量或说明要求专业处理时停下，别用更长把手、钳子或电钻补力。

### 常见报错与补救

- **刀头刚转就跳出：** 停，重查头型/尺寸和轴线；头部已损坏时不继续试新偏方。
- **装入第一圈就卡：** 停，确认螺丝、孔位和方向；不要把阻力当作“越紧越牢”。
- **用品晃动，无法稳定对正：** 按说明重新安放或请合适的人协助，不能一手抓危险边缘一手硬拧。
- **头已坐到位还想加力：** 回到准确说明的终点；没有力矩依据就不自行加码。
- **取出后几颗长短相似：** 分别标原孔位，未知归属先不装回，不靠试错选择。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只适用于已允许的普通非结构性手动动作。若个人握持、姿态或视力不足以稳定对正，可请愿意且有能力的人执行或停下求助；不为了完成一颗螺丝冒险。绝缘外观不表示可以带电作业，本文没有通用拧紧力矩。副作用是“再来半圈”不再是默认成功条件。

## English

Load this Skill when the exact instructions allow you to insert, adjust or remove one screw in an ordinary non-load-bearing item by hand, and a properly fitting tip is already chosen. You will **reach the position the instructions permit, or stop and mark the operation unfinished if slipping, skewing or abnormal resistance appears**. Tightening one screw does not establish structural safety. This does not fit load-bearing or anti-tip parts, open electrical, mechanical or gas equipment, or use a power driver.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the exact instruction for this screw and hole, turning direction and finished state. Stop if it specifies a torque or professional installation you cannot carry out as instructed. Place the item stably under its instructions, wear suitable safety eyewear under the hand-tool instructions and leave room to align driver and screw. Keep your hands and body out of a possible slip path. Do not start with a damaged tip or handle, rounded screw head or wrong part.

### Execution

1. **Seat and align the tip.** Fully engage the recess and hold the shaft in line with the screw. Do not angle the driver to work around an obstruction or use it as a pry tool to reposition the item.
2. **Turn in small controlled steps under the actual instructions.** When inserting, first check that this screw naturally engages its specified hole. When removing, check that it is actually loosening. Use the product's direction and thread instructions; not every fastener follows a guessed convention. Apply steady pressure sufficient to maintain contact, not a sudden push or longer lever.
3. **Watch resistance and the head as you turn.** Normal movement gives a discernible gradual change. Stop immediately if it skews early, binds more with each turn, the tip jumps out, the recess rounds or material begins to split. Re-seating or replacing a wrong part is allowed only where the instructions permit and parts remain sound; damaged parts go to the actual supplier or specialist route.
4. **Stop at the instructed endpoint.** An item may call for a flush head, moving clearance or a particular position. Use the exact model's criterion, not “it still turns.” Leave an unclear endpoint or load-bearing/protective function unconfirmed instead of claiming professional acceptance.
5. **Make a low-risk check.** Confirm this screw sits in its intended place without visible head damage and the ordinary item can proceed as instructed. Hold a removed screw with its original location noted. Full assembly and workspace cleanup have their own steps.

### Success and stop

The ordinary screw was inserted or removed under the exact instructions, without visible recess damage, skewing, material damage or unexplained resistance. This is one operation, not proof that the whole item can bear a load. Stop for poor alignment, a rounded head, wrong hole, excessive force or a professional-only step. Do not add a longer handle, pliers or drill to overpower it.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The tip rises out on the first turn:** Stop and recheck profile, size and alignment. Do not keep experimenting once the head is damaged.
- **Insertion binds on the first turn:** Stop and confirm screw, hole and direction. Resistance is not a measure of strength.
- **The item moves so you cannot align:** Stabilise it under its instructions or ask suitable help; do not grip a hazardous edge and force the driver.
- **The head seems seated but you want more force:** Return to the exact completion criterion. Do not invent a torque.
- **Several removed screws look alike:** Keep each original hole identified. Leave an unknown one uninstalled rather than guessing.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a permitted ordinary non-structural hand action only. If grip, posture or sight cannot support steady alignment, ask a willing capable person or stop. An insulated-looking handle is not permission for live work. There is no universal tightening torque here. “One more turn” ceases to be the default finish condition.
