## 中文

当你有一款**包装说明为沸水煮后沥水**的普通干面，需要把面煮到这款产品适合的状态，再把多余水与面分开时，用这份 Skill。这和预先量好全部被食物吸收的水不同。它不让你用一个分钟数覆盖全部面形、材质或品牌，也不把倾倒一锅滚水当作必须完成的体力测试。

### 准备与输入

读这包干面的建议水量/锅容量、加入时机、烹煮时间、是否需搅动及沥水/冲洗说明；若之后要加酱，确认酱与盛装容器会在面完成时就绪。准备可饮用水、能安全放在热源上的锅、可使用的滤具或经核实的另一沥水方法、计时和防烫工具。**先走一遍从热锅到滤具的路线**：滤具能稳放、排出的热水去向明确、手臂能控制锅重，旁边没有人或易碰倒物。做不到安全沥水，就先改成可执行的小批量或另一可信方法。

### 执行

1. **按这款面准备足够但可控的水。** 使用包装给出的锅与水条件；不同面条不必抄同一盐量或锅尺寸。确认水不会因加入面而溢出，锅把手不伸向易碰到的通道。
2. **水达到包装所需状态再下面。** 按说明放入面，避开溅起热水；启动这包的计时。需要时用合适的器具轻轻分开或搅动，避免粘底；不要把脸和手伸到蒸汽正上方。
3. **按产品与用途检查。** 到包装的建议时间范围，用安全器具取少量、降至不烫口后检查软硬是否适合这餐，或采用包装给出的完成信号。计时器只叫你来检查；同一品牌的不同形状也可能有不同时间。
4. **先确认去处再沥水。** 面已到可接受状态、滤具稳定、手和通道安全、接面容器或下一步的酱已经就位，才把水和面分开。按你能稳控的方式慢慢沥，热蒸汽向远离脸和手的方向散。锅太重、滤具晃动时先停热并让情况安全下来，不强行一把倒完。
5. **按这款面结束。** 沥后是否冲洗、是否与酱混合，服从产品和这道菜的可信做法，不把一种厂商对某款面的建议写成所有面的规则。及时盛出；计划留下的熟面按当地食品安全指导冷却储存，不在室温长期等待。

### 成功条件

这款干面达到适用的熟度或口感，多余煮面水经可控制的路线排走，面没有落进不洁水槽，热锅与蒸汽风险已处理；下一步盛装或拌酱有真实容器。若产品说明或安全沥水路径缺失，明确停止或换方法也是合格结果。

### 常见报错与补救

- **煮好才发现没有滤具或滤具放不稳：** 先在安全状态下停热，改用可核实的小批量或适用工具；不端着整锅滚水到处找地方。
- **包装时间到了，但面还不适合入口：** 按这一款产品的检查和补救信息继续观察，不从别的面形复制分钟数。
- **面在锅里粘成团：** 按包装允许的搅动方式尽早分开；下次核对水量、锅容量与加入时机，不靠继续猛煮掩盖问题。
- **沥水时水花和蒸汽太近：** 放慢或停止动作，把热锅放回安全位置，重新安排滤具和握持；不要用裸手捞面来赶进度。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理按包装要求沸水煮后沥水的普通干面，不是某一种面形或医疗饮食。高海拔、设备、水量与产品不同，包装优先。握力、移动或视觉受限时，选更小可安全处理的批量、稳定滤具、可读时间提醒或可信协助；无法安全倾倒热锅时不开始这一方法。沥出的水和用过的滤具需要处理；熟面也有真实保存要求。

## English

Use this Skill for ordinary dry pasta **whose package calls for boiling in water and draining**. Cook it to a finish suitable for this product, then separate the pasta from surplus water. This differs from an absorption method that starts with only the water the food will take up. It gives no one cooking time for all shapes or brands and does not make lifting a pot of boiling water a required strength test.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this pack's suggested water and pot size, when to add pasta, cooking range, stirring, and draining or rinsing directions. If sauce follows, have it and a receiving dish ready for the finishing point. Use drinking water, a stable pot and heat source, a strainer or another verified draining method, timer, and heat protection. **Rehearse the route from pot to strainer before heating**: the strainer sits securely, hot water has a clear destination, you can control the load, and no person or object is in the spill path. If safe draining is unavailable, choose a smaller manageable batch or another supported method first.

### Execution

1. **Prepare enough but controllable water for this pasta.** Follow its package's pot and water conditions. Different pasta does not inherit one salt amount or pan size. Leave room so added pasta cannot cause overflow; turn the handle away from a busy walkway.
2. **Add pasta at its required water state.** Lower it as directed without splashing hot water, and start the package-based timer. If needed, use a suitable tool to separate or stir pieces before they stick. Keep face and hands away from steam above the pot.
3. **Check product and intended use.** At the package's time range, remove a little with a safe tool and let it cool enough to taste, or use its specified finish cue. A timer prompts inspection; even shapes under one brand can have different times.
4. **Confirm the destination before draining.** Separate pasta from water only when its finish is acceptable, the strainer is stable, hands and path are safe, and a serving dish or sauce stage is ready. Drain slowly in a way you can control, directing steam away from face and hands. If the pot is too heavy or the strainer wobbles, stop heating and make the situation safe instead of forcing one large pour.
5. **Finish for this product.** Whether to rinse or mix with sauce follows the package and reliable recipe. One maker's advice for one pasta is not a rule for every noodle. Serve promptly; any cooked pasta deliberately kept for later needs cooling and storage under local food-safety guidance, not a long wait at room temperature.

### Success

This pasta reaches its applicable doneness or texture, excess cooking water leaves through a controlled route, the food has not fallen into an unclean sink, and steam or pot hazards are addressed. A real dish or sauce awaits it. A clear stop when directions or safe draining are absent is also complete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The pasta is ready but the strainer is missing or unstable:** Stop heating when safe and use a verified smaller-batch or suitable tool route. Do not carry a full pot of boiling water around looking for somewhere to put it.
- **The package timer ends but the pasta is not ready:** Use this product's checks and recovery directions rather than another shape's minutes.
- **Pieces cling together in the pot:** Separate them early as the package allows. Next time check water, pan capacity, and addition timing instead of covering the issue with more boiling.
- **Draining brings steam or splashes too close:** Slow or stop, put the pot back safely, and reset the strainer and grip. Do not reach into boiling water with bare hands to save time.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary dry pasta with a boil-and-drain package method, not a shape-specific or medical-diet recipe. Altitude, equipment, water, and product vary; the package governs. If grip, movement, or reading is difficult, use a smaller manageable batch, stable strainer, readable timer, or trusted help. Do not begin this method if hot-pot draining cannot be made safe. Used water and strainer need handling, and cooked pasta still has real storage limits.
