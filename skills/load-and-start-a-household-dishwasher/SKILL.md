## 中文

当你已筛出一组可用**这台准确型号的家用洗碗机**处理的普通器皿，下一步要实际装架、放对洗剂并开始一个周期时，用这份 Skill。它不替代器皿材质筛选，也不把洗碗机运行完成直接等同所有餐具干净/干燥。机器漏水、门锁故障、电气异常或持续警报时停止使用并找专业帮助，不拆机修理。

### 准备与输入

读本机装架图、喷臂/洗剂仓位置、负载上限、程序与附加选项定义、普通残渣处理要求和启动/暂停方式。读自动洗碗机洗剂包装的机型适配、份量和投放方法。**手洗洗洁精不是自动洗碗机洗剂**，也不临时加醋、漂白剂或其他家用清洁品代替。确认器皿筛选已完成，洗后有干净位置取出。

### 执行

1. **按本机要求处理剩食。** 将大块食物残渣移到适用垃圾/厨余路径；是否预冲、冲到什么程度由机器/洗剂说明与实际污垢决定，不从另一代机器复制“必须冲净”或“绝不能冲”的规则。硬焦结、尖锐刀具和厂家禁机洗物先排出。
2. **让水和洗剂能到达脏面。** 按本机架位图把盘碗开口朝向可喷到的位置，器皿不套叠，杯子/容器按说明倒置或倾斜以排水。放入大件后确认喷臂可以在安全状态下按手册核对通路，洗剂仓能打开，门能正常关；不拿脏面贴在另一只碗背面遮挡。
3. **控制负载和尖锐附件。** 不超出本机的架位、重量和高度限制；上下架不会互相撞，轻薄件不会翻飞。普通餐具按篮/抽屉说明摆，锋利刀具仍按其专门说明处理，不能为了多装一件让刀刃暴露在取出路径。
4. **只按两份说明投放专用产品。** 自动洗碗机洗剂用包装与本机规定的正确仓位/剂量；特殊助剂只有在产品、机器和器皿都允许时才用。手洗液会产生不适用泡沫，不能替代；混用清洁品也不是“加强”。投放后把产品收好，远离食品和儿童。
5. **选本机真实合适的周期并启动。** 对照这批污垢、器皿耐受条件和准确机型程序表选择；Eco、Quick、Sanitize 等名字在各机上不保证同义，除非该机及器皿/洗剂明确支持，不声称消毒合格。关门按说明启动，确认没有漏水、异常声音、喷臂阻塞或报警；异常立即安全停用并按厂家/管理者渠道求助。
6. **准备结束交接。** 记录本机提示的完成事件，待机器停稳、降至可安全开门再取出；后续需检查可见残渣、洗剂仓残留和未干器皿。机器完成只表示程序结束，不自动给每个杯盘签发清洁证明。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

只有已确认可机洗的器皿、通畅喷臂和洗剂仓、厂家指定自动洗碗机洗剂及可解释的本机周期进入运行；没有未处理故障。若条件不足，停止启动并改手洗/寻求说明也是完整结果。

- **装完后喷臂碰到锅柄：** 在停机状态重新摆放或取出大件，不带着阻塞启动。
- **只剩手洗洗洁精：** 不替代自动洗碗机产品，改手洗或取得适用洗剂。
- **洗剂仓门被盘子挡住：** 调整盘子及架位，确认仓门可正常打开。
- **启动后漏水或报警：** 按本机说明安全停用，联系管理者/专业服务；不拆门、排水或电气部件。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

面向正常家用机型和普通器皿，不涉及商业消毒法规、洗碗机内部维修或化学消毒配方。机型、架位、洗剂浓度和本地水质不同，具体剂量与程序看准确说明。视觉、握力或弯腰受限时使用可读手册、轻量分批和可信协助；不能安全触及架位时暂停操作。机器要运行一段时间，仍需要结束后逐件核查。

## English

Use this Skill once you have screened ordinary ware for **this exact household dishwasher model** and must load the racks, use the right product, and start a cycle. It does not replace the item-material screen or treat a completed cycle as proof every piece is clean or dry. Stop using a machine with leaks, a faulty door, electrical abnormality, or persistent alert and seek qualified help; do not dismantle it.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this model's rack diagram, spray arm and dispenser positions, load limit, cycle and option definitions, food-scrap guidance, and start/pause method. Read the automatic dishwasher detergent label for machine fit, dose, and dispenser route. **Hand dish soap is not automatic dishwasher detergent**; do not improvise with vinegar, bleach, or other household products. Confirm the dish screen is done and a clean unloading place is available.

### Execution

1. **Handle leftovers for this model.** Move large food pieces to the proper waste route. Whether and how much to pre-rinse depends on the machine, detergent, and actual soil; do not copy “rinse everything” or “never rinse” from another generation. Keep hard char, sharp knives, and maker-excluded ware out.
2. **Leave a water and detergent path.** Use the rack diagram so dirty surfaces face the spray, items do not nest, and cups or containers drain by the documented orientation. After placing large items, check spray-arm clearance in the safe way this manual permits, ensure the dispenser can open, and the door closes normally. Do not hide a bowl's dirty face against another bowl.
3. **Respect load and sharp-part limits.** Stay within documented rack, weight, and height limits; upper and lower items must not strike each other and light pieces must not flip loose. Put ordinary cutlery in the basket or drawer as directed. Sharp knives still need their own safe care; adding one more item does not justify an exposed blade in the unloading path.
4. **Dispense only the intended product under both directions.** Put automatic dishwasher detergent in the correct compartment and dose from both pack and manual. Add an aid only when product, machine, and items permit it. Hand dish soap creates inappropriate suds; cleaners mixed together are not an intensive mode. Store the product after dosing, away from food and children.
5. **Choose and start a real cycle for this model.** Match this soil and item limits to the exact cycle guide. Eco, Quick, and Sanitize names do not mean identical things on every machine; do not claim sanitising unless model, ware, and product support the stated result. Close and start as directed. Check for leaks, odd noise, spray obstruction, or alarms; stop safely and seek maker or site help for an abnormal run.
6. **Plan the completion handoff.** Note how this model signals completion and wait for a full stop and safe opening before removing items. Later, check visible food, dispenser residue, and damp pieces. Completion means the program ended, not that every cup and plate received a cleaning certificate.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Only qualified items, clear spray and dispenser paths, automatic detergent, and an explainable exact-model cycle entered the run, with no fault ignored. When conditions are missing, stopping and hand washing or seeking directions is complete.

- **The spray arm touches a pan handle after loading:** Reposition or remove the item with the machine stopped; do not start it obstructed.
- **Only hand dish soap is available:** Do not substitute it; hand wash or get the proper detergent.
- **A plate blocks the dispenser door:** Move the plate or rack and confirm the dispenser can open.
- **Water leaks or an alert appears:** Stop safely under the manual and contact a manager or qualified service. Do not dismantle door, drain, or electrical parts.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for normal household machines and everyday ware, not commercial sanitation rules, appliance internals, or chemical disinfectant recipes. Model, rack, detergent concentration, and local water differ; use exact directions for dose and cycle. Use a readable manual, lighter batches, or trusted help if vision, grip, or bending is limited. Pause if racks cannot be reached safely. A cycle takes time and still needs item-by-item checks afterward.
