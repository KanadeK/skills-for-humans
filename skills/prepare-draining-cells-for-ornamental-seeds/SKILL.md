## 中文

当你已核定观赏种子可以室内起苗，但容器与介质尚未就绪时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一盘**能排水、可辨位置、介质已均匀湿润**的小格；这不是播种，也不保证后续发芽。E001 处理已成形室内盆栽，H296 处理外出观察植物，本篇只准备新种子的容器环境。

### 准备与输入

核种子包或可靠同品种说明对介质、容器和后续光照的要求。挑选体量可搬动、底部**本来就有排水孔**的干净小格/小盆及能接水的托盘；没孔的装饰杯不适合直接做本篇育苗格。准备标明育苗适用的干净介质和清水，不随手挖花园土，也不自己钻孔或混漂白剂消毒。重复使用的容器只有在按其材质和可靠说明安全清洁、完全冲净后才用；不确定就选现成干净容器。

### 执行

1. **先核每格都能真正排水。** 看孔不是仅有凹痕、底部不被托盘完全堵住，托盘稳放在不怕水的位置；倒一点水试排，之后倒掉托盘积水。不让“外盆很漂亮”代替通气排水。
2. **按种子用途准备介质。** 使用适合室内起苗、可让小根生长的干净育苗介质；包装要求特定配方时按其说明。把介质装进小格并轻平表面，留给放种和浇水的空间，不用力压成不透水的砖。
3. **均匀预湿而非只喷表面。** 少量多次加水并让其渗透，按介质说明达到整体微湿、可排多余水的状态；若表面湿而底部仍干，继续均匀调节。托盘有积水就倒掉，介质一直糊泥或水排不出时暂停处理容器，不在里面播种。
4. **标位置与下一步。** 在托盘或格位上留可读的品种/日期空白标签，放到已选的安全室内地点，核儿童、宠物、碰撞与水渍风险。种子还没入盆时，记录“格位已备，待播”，不把准备成果写成已播种。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

每个要用的小格干净、稳定、有可通的排水孔，介质整体适度湿润且托盘没有长期积水，位置可标识；若缺合适容器或介质就停止在未播状态。漂亮盆盖住孔、泥土泡在托盘里，不算准备成功。

- **托盘完全贴住排水孔：** 换合适托盘/承托方式，重新核排水，不让根区长期积水。
- **介质一浇就结硬块或泥水不下：** 核是否真是适合育苗的介质及容器孔，别靠再加水救结构。
- **想直接用无孔食品盒：** 选已有孔的育苗容器；本篇不教打孔或改造工具。
- **重复容器有旧病斑或不明残留：** 换干净容器或查可靠清洁方法，不自己调化学消毒液。
- **手部/站立或阅读有障碍：** 用轻小盘、桌面高度、清晰/触觉标签和可信搬运协助；不牺牲排水与身份标记。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是少量普通观赏种子的室内小格，不是食用育苗、专业温室、消毒配方或电热设备安装。不同种子的介质和盆深要求不同，包上信息优先。预湿的介质会占一块稳定、可清理的台面；没有可控排水时推迟播种更可靠。

## English

Load this Skill when an ornamental seed packet is ready for indoor starting but its containers and medium are not. Produce **small clean draining cells with evenly moist starter medium and identifiable positions**. This is preparation, not sowing or a germination guarantee. E001 covers established indoor houseplants; H296 observes outdoor plants. These cells are for new seeds.

### Preparation and inputs

Check packet or reliable same-variety guidance for medium, container, and later light needs. Use clean small cells or pots you can carry with drainage holes **already present**, plus a stable catch tray. A decorative cup without holes is not a direct seed cell for this route. Have clean labelled seed-starting medium and water. Do not dig garden soil, drill holes, or mix bleach disinfectant yourself. Reuse a container only after safe cleaning and thorough rinsing under suitable guidance for its material. Choose a ready clean container if unsure.

### Execution

1. **Verify each cell really drains.** Check the hole is open and not sealed flat against the tray. Place the tray on a water-safe stable surface. Test with a little water, then empty collected water. A handsome outer cover does not replace airflow or drainage.
2. **Prepare medium for this seed use.** Use clean starter medium suited to small roots, following any packet-specific mix direction. Fill cells and level gently with room for seed placement and watering. Do not compress the medium into a hard non-draining brick.
3. **Moisten throughout, not only the surface.** Add water gradually so it penetrates under the medium's directions, leaving it gently moist with excess able to drain. A wet surface over a dry lower layer needs more even adjustment. Empty standing tray water. If medium stays muddy or water cannot leave, correct the container before sowing.
4. **Mark positions and the next action.** Prepare legible variety and date labels for the tray or cells. Set it at the chosen safe indoor place, checking children, pets, bumps, and water marks. If seeds are not in it, record “cells ready, sowing pending,” not “planted.”

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Each intended cell is clean and stable with an open drain, evenly modestly moist medium, no long-standing tray water, and an identifiable position. Without suitable container or medium, stop before sowing. A decorative pot blocking its hole or medium submerged in a tray is not ready.

- **The tray seals the hole:** Choose a suitable catch or support and test drainage again rather than leaving roots in water.
- **Medium becomes a hard lump or muddy puddle:** Verify starter suitability and drainage rather than adding more water to solve structure.
- **A no-hole food tub looks convenient:** Choose ready draining seed cells. This Skill does not drill or modify tools.
- **A reused container has old spots or unknown residue:** Use a clean alternative or reliable cleaning guidance, not a homemade chemical disinfectant.
- **Grip, standing, or reading needs limit setup:** Use a light small tray, table-height work, clear or tactile labels, and trusted lifting help without giving up drainage or identity.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a small indoor tray for ordinary ornamental seeds, not edible seed production, a professional greenhouse, a disinfectant recipe, or electric heat installation. Medium and depth vary by packet. Moist starter medium occupies a stable wipeable surface. Without controllable drainage, deferring sowing is a sound result.
