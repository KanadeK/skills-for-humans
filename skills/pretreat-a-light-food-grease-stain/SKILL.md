## 中文

当你知道普通可水洗衣物上的小印迹来自**少量食用油、黄油或类似食物油脂**，且不是化学油、机油或重度浸透时，用这份 Skill 在正常洗涤前做单产品局部预处理。它与刚洒上的非油性饮料不同：不要只用大量清水冲整块油印，就认为洗剂已经完成任务。来源不明或衣物不可水洗时先停止。

### 准备与输入

读衣物的水洗、漂白、干燥限制，确认印花、涂层与镶边。读液体洗衣剂或预洗去渍产品是否允许局部使用、用量、等待和冲洗方式；两边条件都成立才继续。准备干净吸附纸/白布及稳定的操作面，在不显眼处按产品说明测试。掉色、变形或涂层变化立即停止；贵重/特殊材质交制造商或专业护理。

### 执行

1. **移走表面多余油脂。** 先用干净纸或白布轻按；若有固体食物，用不尖锐工具轻轻移走表层，不往纤维里推。换新的干净面，别把已吸到的油再压回去。
2. **选一个相容的预处理产品。** 小处测试无变化后，只按产品说明把适用的液体洗衣剂或预洗产品施在污渍上，控制份量与接触时间。轻轻按压，不刷到起毛；不同时加漂白剂、氨、溶剂或其他家用清洁剂。处理重油污需另查可信方法，不把本篇小油印步骤加倍当重度方案。
3. **按衣物标签洗涤。** 用产品、衣物和准确机器共同允许的洗法、水温及剂量；标签上限不能因油污而提高。若只能手洗，服从已核实的手洗流程，不把局部去渍直接当完整洗净。
4. **干燥前在同一位置复查。** 油印可能在湿布上不易看清；在充足光线下比较，必要时等衣物按标签允许的方式自然干到能判断，但不要先烘干或熨烫。明显仍在时按残留污渍流程决定是否再试；出现颜色/面料变化时停止去渍并记录。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

表面余油已移走，所用单一产品与衣物相容，衣物按标签正常洗过且干燥前复查。印迹尚在或条件不清时停止热烘并重新判断，而不是靠第二种化学品碰运气。

- **纸巾越按越油：** 换干净面并缩小处理范围；大面积浸透不在本篇轻污渍范围。
- **测试处褪色或起毛：** 停止产品处理，留存标签、产品名与照片，咨询专业护理。
- **衣服上还有饮料色素或其他混合污渍：** 不认为油脂处理同时解决色素，先复查每种痕迹及标签，再决定下一步。
- **洗后仍是半透明油印：** 不烘干；按已知来源与产品说明考虑一次相容的再处理，或停止并求助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只限少量、已知的食物油脂和普通可水洗衣物，不含工业润滑油、油漆、危险液体或严重污染。不同洗衣剂浓度与衣物固色不同，无统一剂量或热水指令。手部不便时可在桌面稳放衣物、用小块白布和可信协助；看不清油印时用侧光和照片比较，不用摸不明污染。局部洗剂可能需额外漂净，仍要安排衣物干燥。

## English

Use this Skill for a small mark you know came from **cooking oil, butter, or similar food grease** on ordinary washable clothing. This does not cover chemical oil, motor oil, or heavy saturation. It differs from a fresh non-oily drink spill: flushing a patch of oil with much water alone does not complete the detergent step. Stop when the source is unknown or water washing is not allowed.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the garment's water-wash, bleach, and drying limits and check prints, coatings, and trim. Read whether a liquid laundry detergent or prewash product permits spot use, including amount, waiting, and rinsing. Continue only where both sets of directions agree. Have clean absorbent paper or white cloth and a stable work surface. Test the product in a hidden area as directed. Stop if dye, shape, or coating changes; valuable or special fabrics need maker or professional advice.

### Execution

1. **Lift loose surface grease.** Blot lightly with clean paper or white cloth. If solid food remains, lift only its surface with a blunt tool, without pushing it into fibres. Switch to clean paper rather than pressing collected oil back onto the garment.
2. **Choose one compatible pretreatment product.** After a hidden test shows no change, use a suitable liquid laundry detergent or prewash product on the spot in its directed amount and contact time. Press gently; do not scrub until fibres roughen. Do not add bleach, ammonia, solvent, or another household cleaner on top. Heavy oil soil needs its own reliable method, not a doubled version of this small-mark process.
3. **Launder by the garment label.** Use a method, water temperature, and dose compatible with the product, garment, and exact washer. A grease mark does not raise a care-label ceiling. For hand washing, use a confirmed hand-wash process; local pretreatment alone is not a whole wash.
4. **Inspect the same spot before heat drying.** Oil can be hard to see in wet fabric. Compare in good light; if needed, let the garment air-dry only in a label-allowed way until you can judge it, without tumble drying or ironing first. If visible residue remains, use the remaining-stain workflow to decide on another attempt. Stop stain treatment and document dye or fabric change.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Loose grease was lifted, one compatible product was used, the garment was laundered under its label, and you checked before heat drying. A remaining mark or uncertain conditions mean holding heat drying and reassessing, not testing a second chemical by chance.

- **The blotting paper keeps taking up oil:** Change to a clean section and keep the work small. A heavily saturated garment is outside this Skill.
- **The hidden patch fades or grows fuzzy:** Stop using the product; keep labels, product name, and photos for professional care.
- **A drink dye or other stain came with the food:** Grease treatment does not automatically remove colour. Check each remaining mark and the labels before another step.
- **A translucent oil mark remains after washing:** Do not tumble-dry. Consider one compatible repeat under the known stain and product directions, or stop and get help.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for a light, known food-grease mark on ordinary washable clothing, not industrial lubricant, paint, hazardous liquid, or serious contamination. Detergent concentrations and fabric dyes vary; there is no universal dose or hot-water rule. For limited grip, work over a stable table with small clean cloths and trusted help. Use side light or photos when the mark is hard to see; do not touch an unknown contaminant to identify it. Spot detergent may need thorough rinsing, and the garment still needs drying.
