## 中文

当你的一片**普通小织样已经织到想要的长度，线圈仍活在针上**，需要使它离针而不散，并处理起头/收尾线时，加载这份 Skill。结果是松紧适当的收针边和藏在织物里的线尾；不是重新织正反针，也不承诺商业服装耐久。带复杂花样、弹力要求或特殊花边的作品要按其图样收针法。

### 准备与输入

先点数确认没有未处理的掉针，检查手头还有足够工作线做完整一行并留可藏的尾。备钝头毛线针、剪刀和稳定光线；纱线太细不便穿针可用合适粗眼针或可信帮助。活圈只在确认步骤后逐个放离织针，不能先把整排针抽掉。

### 执行

1. **松而稳地收前两针。** 按小样当前普通针法先织两针到右针，左针挑起右针上先织的那圈，把它越过后织圈并放离右针；右针应留一圈。不要勒紧这条边，收得比织物窄时放松或用合适稍大针重试。
2. **逐针重复直到剩一圈。** 每次再织下一针，右针成为两圈，再将旧圈越过新圈放下；保持每次只移除一个旧圈。若某圈滑脱而不是按顺序越过，先止住并恢复，别加快赶完。
3. **留尾固定末圈。** 最后一圈放大一点，按纱线与后续藏线需要留足尾再剪断工作线，将尾穿过末圈轻收紧。剪刀只剪工作线，不靠近织物主体盲剪。
4. **沿织纹藏线并核边。** 用钝头毛线针把每个线尾在织物背侧或较不显眼的织纹里走几针，再转向藏稳，避免把边拉皱；轻拉检查不会立即散，再按需要修短露出的尾端。样片从针上完全取下，边不脱圈、正面无明显长尾，即可交后续按纱线标签整理。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

样片脱离两根针，收针边可轻拿不脱，线尾藏稳且没有被剪到主体线圈。只把末圈打一结、前一排仍在针上，不算完整收针。

- **收针边勒得过紧：** 不硬拉松，退回重收并减张力或按材料允许换较大针。
- **中途旧圈滑跑：** 先抓住活圈和相邻线，不继续越针，按掉针/退行方法恢复。
- **尾线太短穿不进织物：** 暂停修剪、查该纱线适用补救；不要只打一个表面松结就称藏线完成。
- **藏线把样片拉皱：** 退一点线，顺织纹重新分布，不把尾拉到最紧。
- **手眼不便：** 粗眼钝针、浅色大线圈与可信协助穿针，由你核查边和线尾。

### 假设与边界

普通正针式收针适合这片简单样布，弹力边或特殊纹样可能需要不同做法。尾长与织纹藏法由纱线和后续用途决定；不复制某商家的成衣图样。

## English

Load this Skill when an **ordinary small knit sample has reached its intended length but live stitches remain on a needle**. Remove them without unravelling and secure beginning and ending tails. Finish with a reasonably loose bind-off edge and woven tails, not another knit/purl row or a claim of commercial garment durability. Complex patterns, elastic edges, or special lace need their own pattern bind-off.

### Preparation and inputs

Count and fix any dropped stitches first. Check enough working yarn remains for a full bind-off row and a tail long enough to weave. Have a blunt tapestry needle, suitable scissors, and stable light. Use a large-eye needle or trusted threading help for fine yarn. Release live loops one at a time only as the procedure directs; never pull the entire needle out first.

### Execution

1. **Bind the first two loosely.** Knit the first two stitches of this simple sample onto the right needle. With the left tip, lift the first of those over the second and off; one loop remains on the right. Avoid tightening the edge narrower than the fabric; relax or retry with a suitable larger needle if needed.
2. **Repeat one stitch at a time.** Knit the next left stitch, then lift the older right loop over the new one and off. Each repeat removes just the older loop. If one slips by accident instead of passing over in sequence, catch and restore it before going faster.
3. **Secure the final loop with a tail.** Enlarge the last loop slightly, leave enough tail for the yarn and intended finish, and cut only the working yarn. Pass that tail through the last loop and snug it gently. Keep scissors away from the main fabric loops.
4. **Weave tails along the fabric and inspect.** With a blunt tapestry needle, pass each tail through several stitches on the back or less visible texture, change direction for hold without puckering, and tug lightly to check it does not immediately slip. Trim exposed surplus only once secure. With the piece fully off needles and edges sound, it can later be cared for under its yarn label.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The piece is off both needles, its bound edge can be lifted gently without dropping loops, tails are woven in, and main fabric was not accidentally cut. A knot on the last loop while an earlier row remains live is unfinished.

- **Bound edge is too tight:** Do not stretch it forcibly; unbind and redo with less tension or a suitable larger needle.
- **An old loop runs during work:** Catch live loops and bars before more binding, then use dropped-stitch or unknitting guidance.
- **Tail is too short to weave:** Stop trimming and check a yarn-specific remedy; one loose surface knot is not a secured finish.
- **Weaving puckers the sample:** Ease the tail back and distribute it along stitch texture instead of drawing it tight.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Use a blunt large-eye needle, light bulky yarn, or trusted threading help while you inspect edge and tails.

### Assumptions and limits

This basic knitwise bind-off suits the simple sample. Elastic or patterned edges may require another method. Tail length and weaving path vary with yarn and purpose, and no paid garment pattern is copied.
