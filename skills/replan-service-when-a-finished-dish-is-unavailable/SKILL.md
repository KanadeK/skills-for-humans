## 中文

当你的一顿家庭餐已做完或快上桌，**原定的一道成品菜因失败、损坏或安全问题不能供应**时，用这份 Skill 决定取消该菜、换可行替代、延后或取消整顿餐。它处理的是一个菜品的缺席；若每道菜都能供应但份量不够，用“做好了却不够分”的流程。不要为维护菜单外观，把不安全的菜改名上桌。

### 准备与输入

先从待上桌区域移开不能供应的菜。列出其余已经安全完成的菜、每道菜在这餐的作用（主食、主要菜、配菜等）、实际就餐者与约定时间，以及手头可安全即食或按可信做法及时完成的替代品。若失败可能接触了其他菜、共用餐具或台面，先核对污染范围；不清楚就不要让相邻食品继续上桌。严重过敏、医疗饮食和婴幼儿食品的替代必须使用其已确认的方案。

### 执行

1. **固定不能供应的事实。** 安全史不明、未达到适用熟度或被污染的菜先停止供应，交给对应的食品安全流程；调味和摆盘不能解决这些问题。若只是焦糊、外观或口感问题且已确认安全，先走对应的成品补救任务；确实不能按本餐供应，再继续本篇。
2. **查这道菜缺席造成什么缺口。** 剩下的菜是否仍能组成这群人愿意吃且份量够的一餐？若只是可省略的小配菜，可以直接取消并说明；若缺的是主要组成或某个人唯一可吃的选项，不能让装饰或另一盘相同菜假扮替代。纯份量不足时转到“做好了却不够分”。
3. **选择最小可行变化。** 优先用已经核实可安全即食或安全保存的食物，按其包装和条件提供；若要现做，核对实际材料、设备、注意力、可信做法、所需时间与食品安全完成条件。赶不上就明确延后或删菜，不通过提高火力、忽略中心熟度或让热锅无人看守追赶原定时间。
4. **在大家开吃前说明。** 明确说哪道菜没有、剩余能供应什么、替代方案及真实等候时间；让当事人选择继续、等候、另找餐食或取消。已承诺的特定食物不能悄悄换成对方未确认的材料。
5. **按新菜单上桌或停止。** 移走失败菜和可能污染的餐具，使用干净器具端出确认安全的菜。需要保留的剩余成品走当地适用的保温、冷却和储存路径；没有可行的新菜单，就停止这次供应而不是拖着整桌熟食等待灵感。

### 成功条件与常见报错

这次实际上桌的菜与通知一致，每个人知道自己是否有可接受的餐食；不能供应的菜及受影响器具不再流入餐桌。若无法满足，已明确延后或取消，并妥善处理安全的剩余食物。

- **“只坏一道，其他应该没事”：** 先检查共用勺、盘、台面和接触史；污染范围不明时停止提供受影响部分。
- **替代品只有生食或包装要求加热：** 不称其为即食，按适用说明做完且来得及才使用。
- **替代菜做不完：** 告知真实延时或取消它；不能为赶时间跳过适用的烹调检查或让热源无人看守。
- **食客已开始吃原菜：** 立即说明并停止继续供应受影响食物；若涉及食品安全或健康问题，转相应专业/紧急指导，本篇不诊断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这针对普通家庭餐，不判断医疗营养是否等价，也不给万能替代菜单。文化、预算、口味、可及设备与行动能力不同；用可读文字或口头说明变化，并让当事人确认是否接受。若无法安全操作热源或清理污染区，就停止新做菜并使用已核实的安全替代或取消。一次失败可能留下要清洁的锅和未用食材；保存与弃置服从当地指导。

## English

Use this Skill when a household meal is ready or about to be served but **one planned finished dish cannot be offered** after a failure, damage, or safety issue. Decide whether to remove the dish, use a feasible replacement, delay, or cancel this meal. This is about a missing component. If every dish is usable but the portions are short, use the portion-shortage workflow. Do not rename unsafe food to preserve the appearance of the menu.

### Preparation and inputs

Move the unavailable dish away from the serving area first. List the remaining safely finished dishes, the role of the missing one in this meal (staple, main item, side), actual diners, agreed time, and food that can be eaten safely now or completed in time under reliable directions. If the failure may have affected other food, shared utensils, or surfaces, establish the contamination boundary. Do not serve neighbouring food when that boundary is unknown. A substitute for severe allergies, medical diets, or infant food must follow an already confirmed plan.

### Execution

1. **Establish why the dish is unavailable.** Stop serving food with an unknown safety history, unmet applicable doneness, or contamination and use the relevant food-safety workflow. Seasoning and plating cannot solve those problems. If scorching, appearance, or taste is the only issue and safety is confirmed, try the appropriate finished-food repair task first. Continue here when the dish truly cannot be served for this meal.
2. **Identify what its absence removes.** Can the remaining dishes still make a meal this group accepts, with enough food? A dispensable small side may simply be removed with an explanation. A missing main component or someone's only edible option cannot be replaced by garnish or another plate's title. Use the portion-shortage workflow when quantity is the only gap.
3. **Choose the smallest feasible change.** Prefer food already verified safe to eat now or safely stored, following its actual pack and handling conditions. For new cooking, confirm ingredients, equipment, attention, reliable directions, time, and the correct safety endpoint. If it will not fit, state a delay or drop it. Do not try to recover the original deadline with excessive heat, unchecked centres, or unattended cooking.
4. **Tell diners before they begin.** Name the missing dish, what remains, the replacement, and the real delay. Let people choose to continue, wait, arrange another meal, or cancel. Do not silently replace a promised specific food with an ingredient they have not accepted.
5. **Serve the revised meal or stop.** Remove the failed dish and potentially contaminated serving ware. Use clean tools to serve only confirmed safe food. Hold, promptly cool, or store food kept later under applicable local guidance. If no workable meal remains, cancel this service instead of leaving a table of cooked food waiting without a plan.

### Success and common errors

What is served matches what diners were told, and each knows whether they have an acceptable meal. The unavailable dish and affected tools do not reach the table. If the meal cannot be met, the delay or cancellation is explicit and safe remaining food is handled.

- **“Only one dish failed, so the rest must be fine”:** Check shared spoons, plates, surfaces, and contact history. Stop serving affected food if the boundary is unclear.
- **The proposed replacement is raw or labelled for heating:** Do not call it ready to eat. Use it only if its actual directions can be completed in time.
- **The replacement cannot be finished on time:** State the real delay or cancel it. Do not skip applicable cooking checks or leave active heat unattended to meet the deadline.
- **Someone has already eaten from the failed dish:** Tell them and stop further service. A food-safety or health concern goes to the relevant professional or emergency guidance; this Skill does not diagnose it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary home meals, without deciding medical nutritional equivalence or one universal substitute menu. Culture, budget, taste, accessible equipment, and mobility vary. Explain the change in readable writing or speech and let people confirm acceptance. If you cannot safely use heat or clear a contaminated area, stop extra cooking and use a verified safe alternative or cancel. A failed dish may leave a pan to clean and unused ingredients; handle storage and disposal under local guidance.
