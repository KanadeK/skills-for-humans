## 中文

当冰箱已经挤到门难关、通风口被挡、或生食与即食食物只能“哪里有缝放哪里”时，加载这份 Skill。目标是修复**现有冷藏布局**，让冷空气能流通，生食汁液不会滴到即食食物，并且你能安全取到需要的东西。一次买菜回家后给新物品找位置，已有[买菜回家后收纳](../put-away-groceries/SKILL.md)；本篇不决定食品的未知保存史、开封期限或共享所有权。

### 准备与输入

先确认冰箱能可靠达到你所在地及食品标签要求的冷藏条件；美国 FDA 对普通家用冰箱给出 40°F（约 4.4°C）或以下，香港食物安全中心给出 4°C 或以下。两者是各自地区的例子，不是用“差不多冷”代替读数。没有可靠读数时先[核对冷藏区域](../verify-a-refrigerator-storage-zone/SKILL.md)，不能仅靠重新摆放宣告安全。

看清各件食品是什么、需不需要冷藏、包装是否完整、是否有生食滴漏、谁有权移动共用冰箱里的物品，以及是否有另一处**已核实**的冷藏空间。准备干净台面和食品适用的带盖/防漏容器。不要一次把所有易腐食品搬到室温台面上慢慢研究；先在纸上或照片里画出大致分区，再分小组快速处理。

### 执行

1. **找出真正的空间阻塞。** 关门检查门封是否被包装顶住；打开后看哪些物品挡住出风口、堆得太紧或压在其他食品上。先记下这些位置，不用凭冰箱总容积猜可用空间。
2. **先隔离异常。** 漏液的生肉、禽肉或海鲜不要继续挤在即食食物旁；用合适的防漏容器隔离，并按当地可靠指导处理已经接触汁液的食品与表面。食品的日期或温度史不清时，标记待判，不能为了空出一格就先吃掉它。清洁受污染表面的具体操作交给相应清洁流程。
3. **只移动有明确去向的物品。** 包装说明允许常温保存且未触发开封后冷藏要求的食品，可以移到适当常温位置；其他食品不能为了腾空间自行改写标签。若要换容器，先确认容器符合该食品的保存用途；打不开或看不清的包装先保留原信息。
4. **重建三类位置。** 把已覆盖的熟食、即食食品和已清洗可即食物品放在不会被生食滴漏的位置；把生肉、禽肉、海鲜放在防漏容器中，并置于即食食物下方；为通风口和食品之间留出冷空气可流通的空间。具体抽屉与架层功能以设备说明和实际温度为准，别把“门格空着”直接等同于适合所有易腐食品。
5. **让人能拿到，门能关上。** 常用物品放在可及位置，重物不放在需要危险弯腰或举高才能取出的地方。共享冰箱里私人物品没有同意就不擅自移动或丢弃；需要调整时先联系物主。关门后确认没有包装被夹、没有容器倾斜漏液。
6. **复核容量和温度。** 按温度计/冰箱说明等待稳定，再核对实际摆放食品的位置是否符合当地和标签条件。若仍过满、无法分开生食与即食食物、或温度不合格，停止往里加食品；把仍安全且允许转移的食品移到另一处已核实冷藏空间。没有足够安全空间时，按食品历史及当地指导对无法保存的部分作保守处置，不把台面当第二层冰箱。

### 完成条件

门能完整关闭，通风口和物品之间有可用的冷空气通道；生食被防漏隔离且在即食食物下方，食品标签可识别，常用物品能安全取到，实际存放处的温度满足适用条件。做不到任一安全条件，就明确记为“空间不足或温度未证实”，停止新增冷藏物品并执行替代安排；整齐的照片不算完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **移走两件东西仍塞不下：** 不压住风口或硬关门。仅在标签允许时换保存位置；其余转移到已验证的冷藏处，或按真实时间/温度史及官方指引决定不能保留的部分。
- **低层放不下所有生食：** 先用合适防漏容器隔开；仍无法防止污染即食食物时，不把这些食品一起留在该冰箱。
- **整理中发现汁液或污渍：** 先隔离可能被污染的食品，再按食品接触区域适用的清洁流程处理；别在未清洁表面继续摆放即食食物。
- **共有物品的物主联系不上：** 保留其物品及信息，标出暂不能调整的区域；不要私自处置来制造“空位”。可先完成不受影响区域。
- **关门后读数偏高：** 停止把布局当作成功；减少负载、按设备说明检查并复测。持续异常交给合格维修，食品历史另作判断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

不同冰箱的风口、抽屉、门格及温差不同，具体位置按设备说明、食品标签和可靠温度读数确定。视觉、弯腰或握持不方便时，可用大字标签、可及架层、分次移动或请可信的人协助；不要求你搬动危险重物。共享空间的标识与权限另有任务，此处仅在有权移动的范围内重建布局。

你可能得到更容易使用的冰箱，也可能发现容量确实不足、必须减少要冷藏的物品。多买一个收纳盒不会扩大制冷能力。

## English

Load this Skill when a refrigerator is so full that its door struggles to close, vents are blocked, or raw and ready-to-eat foods are being stored wherever a gap appears. You are repairing the **existing cold-storage layout** so cold air can move, raw juices cannot drip onto ready-to-eat food, and you can reach what you need safely. Use [Put Away Groceries](../put-away-groceries/SKILL.md) for placing one new shopping trip. This Skill does not decide an unknown food history, after-opening period, or another person's ownership.

### Preparation and inputs

First confirm that the refrigerator can meet the cold-storage conditions applicable where you live and on the food labels. The US FDA gives 40°F (about 4.4°C) or below for an ordinary home refrigerator; the Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety gives 4°C or below. These are examples from their jurisdictions, not substitutes for actual readings. If you have no reliable reading, [verify the intended zone](../verify-a-refrigerator-storage-zone/SKILL.md) before claiming the rearrangement is safe.

Identify the foods, their storage labels, package integrity, any raw-food leak, who can authorize moving items in a shared fridge, and any other **verified** cold space. Prepare a clean work surface and food-suitable covered or leak-resistant containers. Do not empty all perishables onto a room-temperature counter while you study the problem. Sketch zones on paper or from a photo, then handle small groups promptly.

### Execution

1. **Find the actual blockage.** With the door closed, check whether packaging prevents a full seal. Look for blocked vents, tight stacks, and food crushed under other items. Record those places instead of guessing from the fridge's advertised total volume.
2. **Isolate exceptions first.** Do not leave leaking raw meat, poultry, or seafood next to ready-to-eat food. Contain it in a suitable leak-resistant vessel and follow applicable local guidance for food and surfaces touched by raw juices. Mark food with uncertain dates or temperature history for separate judgment; do not eat it merely to create space. Cleaning a contaminated surface follows its own food-area cleaning process.
3. **Move only items with a valid destination.** Food whose label allows room-temperature storage and that has not triggered an after-opening chill instruction can move to an appropriate ambient location. Do not rewrite the label on other food to gain space. If changing containers, confirm that the container suits the food; preserve the original information when a package cannot be read or opened safely.
4. **Rebuild three kinds of space.** Put covered cooked, ready-to-eat, and washed ready-to-eat foods where raw juices cannot drip on them. Put raw meat, poultry, and seafood in leak-resistant containers below ready-to-eat food. Leave room for cold air around food and vents. Use the appliance manual and actual temperature for drawer and shelf functions; an empty door bin is not automatically right for every perishable.
5. **Make it reachable and closeable.** Place frequent-use food within your reach and avoid heavy items that require unsafe lifting or bending. Do not move or discard another person's private food without consent; contact its owner when an adjustment is needed. Close the door and check for trapped packaging or a tipped, leaking container.
6. **Recheck capacity and temperature.** After the stabilization period required by the thermometer or refrigerator, check the actual food locations against applicable local and label conditions. If the fridge is still too full, raw and ready-to-eat foods cannot be separated, or temperature fails, stop adding food. Move food that can still safely be moved to another verified cold space. Without enough safe space, use the food's known history and local guidance for conservative disposition; a countertop is not a second refrigerator shelf.

### Success

The door closes fully, vents and foods have a path for cold air, raw food is leak-resistant and below ready-to-eat food, labels remain identifiable, frequent items are safely reachable, and actual food locations meet applicable temperatures. If any safety condition cannot be met, mark “insufficient space” or “temperature unverified,” stop adding chilled food, and take the alternative path. A tidy photo is not completion.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Removing two items still does not make room:** Do not block vents or force the door. Change a storage location only if the label allows it; otherwise use verified cold space or make a conservative decision from actual time/temperature history and official guidance.
- **There is not enough lower space for raw food:** Start with suitable leak-resistant containment. If ready-to-eat food still cannot be protected, do not store them together in that fridge.
- **You discover raw juice or a spill:** Isolate potentially affected food, then use an appropriate food-contact cleaning process. Do not replace ready-to-eat food on an uncleaned surface.
- **A shared item's owner is unreachable:** Keep their food and its information, mark the zone you cannot change, and work on unaffected areas. Do not dispose of it to create a convenient “empty” space.
- **The reading is high after closing:** Do not declare success based on layout. Reduce load, check and remeasure under the manual. Persistent appliance problems need qualified repair; evaluate food history separately.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Vents, drawers, door bins, and zone temperatures differ by refrigerator. Follow the appliance manual, food labels, and reliable actual readings. If vision, bending, or grip makes the task difficult, use large labels, accessible shelves, smaller moves, or a trusted helper; do not lift unsafe weights. Shared-space identification and permission are separate tasks; rearrange only what you may move.

You may gain a fridge that is easier to use, or learn that its capacity is genuinely insufficient. Another organizer box cannot enlarge its cooling capacity.
