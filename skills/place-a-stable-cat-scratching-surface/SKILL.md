## 中文

当你照料的普通室内猫缺少可用磨爪位置，或旧磨爪柱摇晃、太短时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一处**尺寸、方向、稳定性和位置符合这只猫实际习惯**的磨爪表面；不是命令猫立刻放弃所有家具。

### 准备与输入

观察猫是抓竖直家具还是水平地毯、常在何处醒来或经过，以及旧物是否被使用。检查新柱/垫的底座、表面和可能脱落的小件，按制造商说明组装；结构有问题时不用胶带掩盖。不要在公共通道或高处不稳位置放尖硬支架，保持猫砂、食水和藏身处可达。

### 执行

1. **按实际抓法选方向。** 竖抓需要猫伸展开仍够得着、底座不摇的柱；横抓更适合稳固的垫。材料和尺寸以猫的身体与喜好为输入，不把某个品牌当必需。
2. **先在高概率位置放置。** Cats Protection 建议可放在已被抓家具旁、床边或常经过处；不挡门、不压食水碗。多猫互相挡路时需要分别可达的资源，而不是挤在一起。
3. **测试稳定性与边界。** 用手轻推确认不会翻倒、刮脚或勾断，检查猫可接近且可离开。新物若晃动或材料掉屑，先修正或换物，不让猫测试危险结构。
4. **提供不强制的使用机会。** 猫靠近、闻或抓时让它自主完成，可用正常玩具在附近引导但不抓它的爪强按上去，也不惩罚它抓别处。旧习惯改变可能需要时间。
5. **观察并调整。** 记它是否实际用、使用时支架是否稳定、有无掉件；若不用，按姿势和位置调整。持续紧张或突然过度抓挠时先排查环境变化并向兽医或合格行为人员求助。

### 成功条件

一处磨爪表面稳固可及、适合猫的竖/横抓法，位置和复查方式清楚；猫真实使用才可记录为“接受”。若没有安全稳固的物品，暂停使用并找合适替代，不用强迫训练弥补摇晃。

### 常见报错与补救

- **柱一碰就倒：** 换重底座或可安全稳定的产品，不靠猫避开它解决。
- **竖柱不够高：** 选能让这只猫自然伸展的尺寸。
- **猫继续抓旧家具：** 试靠近原抓点放置并观察，不惩罚；若疑似焦虑须找专业帮助。
- **小件从柱上脱落：** 立即取走损坏物，防止吞咽，再选完好用品。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通室内猫的日常环境布置，不教断爪、行为惩罚、焦虑诊断或建筑固定。行动不便时选可移动但稳定的成品并请人搬放；视力受限时请人检查掉件和摇晃。磨爪柱会占一块空间，需要以后检查磨损。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary indoor cat in your care lacks a usable scratch location or its current post wobbles or is too short. Finish with a **direction, size, stability, and location that fit this cat's observed behavior**. It does not command the cat to stop using every piece of furniture immediately.

### Preparation and inputs

Observe whether the cat scratches vertical furniture or horizontal carpet, where it wakes or travels, and whether it used the old item. Check the post or mat's base, surface, and loose pieces; assemble as the maker directs. Do not mask a structural flaw with tape. Keep sharp or hard supports out of walkways and unstable high places, and preserve access to litter, food, water, and hiding.

### Execution

1. **Match the observed direction.** Vertical scratching needs a post tall enough for this cat to stretch against with a non-wobbling base. Horizontal scratching may call for a stable mat. Material and dimensions follow the actual cat, not a required brand.
2. **Start near likely use.** Cats Protection suggests beside already scratched furniture, near a bed, or along a normal route. Keep doors and bowls clear. If several cats block one another, provide separate reachable resources instead of clustering them.
3. **Test stability and access.** Push lightly to confirm it will not tip, scratch a person, or shed parts. The cat must be able to approach and leave. Fix or replace a wobbly or shedding product before inviting use.
4. **Offer without forcing.** Let the cat sniff, approach, and scratch by choice. A familiar toy nearby can invite interest, but do not press its paws onto the post or punish scratching elsewhere. Changing a habit may take time.
5. **Observe and adjust.** Record whether the cat really uses it, whether it remains stable, and whether pieces loosen. Adjust orientation or location when unused. Persistent distress or sudden excessive scratching merits environmental review and veterinary or qualified behavior advice.

### Success

One scratch surface is stable, reachable, and matched to vertical or horizontal behavior, with a clear review point. Call it accepted only after real use. If no safe stable item is available, pause rather than using force to compensate for a wobbly object.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The post tips easily:** Choose a heavier stable base or suitable product rather than expecting the cat to avoid danger.
- **A vertical post is too short:** Use a size that permits this cat's natural stretch.
- **The cat still scratches furniture:** Move the option nearer the known spot and observe without punishment; seek qualified help if anxiety is suspected.
- **Small parts fall off:** Remove the damaged item promptly and replace it with a sound one.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This arranges an ordinary indoor cat's environment; it does not teach declawing, punishment, anxiety diagnosis, or building anchorage. Use a movable but stable finished item and lifting help if needed; ask someone to check looseness and tipping when vision is limited. The surface takes room and needs wear checks.
