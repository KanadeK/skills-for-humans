## 中文

当你要为一间普通房间买**窗式或其他非便携式房间空调**，想判断候选的额定制冷量是否适合时，加载这份 Skill。它只形成购前容量判断；不设计整屋暖通、不安装设备、不改电路或制冷剂管路。便携式空调的测试口径可能不同，不直接套用这里的房间空调表。

### 准备与输入

量实际要降温的区域长宽与层高，记录是否与其他空间长期敞开、强日晒/遮阴、常在人数以及是否是厨房。找候选准确型号的额定制冷量及其单位、对应的本地选型表和安装说明。把“能否在该位置按说明安装”单独列为待确认条件；制冷量合适不等于设备能安全装上。

### 执行

1. **算被服务面积。** 矩形用长×宽，不规则房间分成小矩形后相加。门常开且要同时降温的连通区域也应纳入；不要只量空调下方一小块地面。
2. **确认选型口径。** 看准确型号写的是哪种房间空调及额定制冷量，不将便携式、整屋或其他测试条件的数值直接互比。单位不同先使用可信转换，仍不能确定就让商家提供同单位资料。
3. **按房间条件修正。** 用适用于该产品和地区的官方/制造商选型资料，把层高、日晒、常在人数、厨房热源等条件逐项考虑。美国 ENERGY STAR 面积表是有假设的参考，不是任何地区和房屋的通用处方。
4. **警惕过大。** 把候选与修正后的需求范围比较；不要因“以后可能用得上”一律升级。过大的房间空调可能很快降低温度，却没有足够时间处理湿气，结果仍不舒适。
5. **分开容量与安装。** 写下“制冷量候选：合适/偏小/偏大/资料不足”，再写“窗口、固定、电源及房东许可：已由适当人员确认/待确认”。后者未解决时暂停下单或预约合适的安装评估，不能由本 Skill 代办。

### 成功与停止

你能指出房间实测条件、选型资料的适用范围、准确型号及其制冷量，并说明为什么它在合理范围内，才完成容量核对。高层高、异常热负荷、建筑改造、线路条件不明或医疗相关温控需求应停止普通估算，咨询制造商或合适专业人员；本文不保证实际室温、湿度或费用。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只按宣传“适用面积”下单：** 找制冷量、选型表和表的房间假设；条件未知就不把宣传当实测。
- **将净化器 CADR 当空调制冷量：** 返回准确型号的制冷单位。CADR 描述颗粒物过滤速度，不能替代制冷能力。
- **面积相同但房间很晒或门长期敞开：** 重新按真实服务区域与条件核对，必要时请本地有资质人员评估。
- **不能测量高处或读小字：** 请有权限的人提供读数，放大可读的规格表；关键层高或型号不明时保留“待确认”。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程限普通购前选型，默认你可以取得目标位置的信息。地区气候、建筑隔热和制冷量标法不同；不要把美国示例数值直接当本地安装标准。你可能选到比最大号更合适的候选；空调的物理放置和专业安装仍是独立任务。

### 来源

## English

Load this Skill before buying a **window or other non-portable room air conditioner** for an ordinary room when you need to check its rated cooling capacity. It produces a pre-purchase capacity decision. It does not design whole-home HVAC, install equipment, change wiring, or handle refrigerant. Portable ACs can use different rating methods; do not apply a room-unit chart to one without matching guidance.

### Preparation and inputs

Measure the floor area and ceiling height you actually want to cool. Note connected spaces that stay open, strong sun or shade, usual occupants, and whether the room is a kitchen. Get the candidate's exact model, rated cooling capacity and unit, relevant local sizing guidance, and installation requirements. Keep “can this location be installed according to its instructions?” as a separate question; a suitable capacity does not prove a safe installation.

### Execution

1. **Measure the served area.** Multiply length by width for a rectangle and add simple sections for an irregular room. Include connected areas whose doors stay open if you expect them to be cooled. Do not measure only the patch of floor below the unit.
2. **Check the rating basis.** Identify the exact type of room unit and its stated cooling capacity. Do not compare portable, whole-home, or other test-basis numbers as if they were identical. Use a reliable unit conversion where needed, or ask for a same-unit specification.
3. **Adjust for the room.** Use guidance that applies to the product and region, considering ceiling height, sun, regular occupants, and kitchen heat. The US ENERGY STAR area chart has stated assumptions; it is not a universal prescription for every climate or building.
4. **Check oversizing as well as undersizing.** Compare the candidate with the adjusted need instead of always choosing the next larger unit. An oversized room AC may cool quickly without running long enough to remove much humidity, leaving the room uncomfortable.
5. **Keep installation separate.** Record “cooling-capacity candidate: suitable, too small, too large, or unknown,” then “window, mounting, power, and property permission: confirmed by an appropriate person or still unknown.” Pause the purchase or arrange an installation assessment when the latter is unresolved.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can identify the measured room, the sizing guide's scope, the exact model and its cooling rating, and why the candidate lies in a reasonable range. Stop a simple estimate for unusual height or heat load, building changes, uncertain wiring, or a medical temperature need; consult the maker or an appropriate professional. This does not guarantee actual temperature, humidity, or running cost.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only an advertised “room size” was read:** Find the cooling rating, sizing guide, and assumptions. Keep unknown conditions unknown.
- **Air-cleaner CADR was treated as cooling capacity:** Return to the exact model's cooling unit. CADR describes particle filtration rate, not cooling output.
- **The same area has strong sun or permanently open doors:** Recheck the real served area and load, and seek a qualified local assessment if needed.
- **Height or fine print cannot be checked:** Ask someone with access for measurements and use an enlarged, readable specification. Missing height or exact model stays unconfirmed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary pre-purchase screen and assumes you can obtain information about the intended location. Climate, insulation, and capacity labels differ by region; US example numbers are not local installation standards. You may end up with a candidate smaller than the biggest model. Physical fit and professional installation remain separate work.

### Sources

- [US ENERGY STAR: Room air conditioner buying guidance](https://www.energystar.gov/products/room_air_conditioners) — match rated cooling to room area, sun, and occupants; oversizing can impair humidity control. Its chart belongs to its stated assumptions and product class.
- [US Department of Energy: Purchasing room air conditioners](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/purchasing-energy-efficient-room-air-conditioners) — oversized units can short-cycle and remove humidity poorly; US purchasing guidance, not a worldwide installation rule.
