## 中文

当已摆放的**普通切花瓶**水位下降、水变浑或个别花开始枯败，你想判断继续展示还是撤掉时，加载这份 Skill。结果是能安全继续摆放的更新花瓶，或诚实结束展示；不是保证把死花救活。它与第一次整理花枝或插新花瓶的起点不同。

### 准备与输入

先看花枝的身份和是否有特殊处理说明；只碰可安全处理的普通切花。准备清水、可洗花瓶、稳定空处、合用干净剪具和防护手套（如需）。若水有明显霉、腐败气味或不明汁液接触风险，避免徒手处理，按当地普通垃圾/清洗条件安全结束，不继续展示。

### 执行

1. **作留或撤的判断。** 把花瓶移到稳固清理位置，查看水位、浑浊、茎下部软烂、花头和叶片状态。局部枯败可取走坏枝；多数枝已腐败或发霉就撤展，不为保持外观把坏花藏进后排。
2. **让可留花暂有水。** 取出仍健康的枝，暂置清洁水中。倒掉旧水，洗净、冲净花瓶；不把清洁剂残留混进新水。
3. **补水并小幅修整。** 对适合普通复剪的可留花枝，用干净合用工具修去一点旧切端；再次去掉会浸水的叶。加入新水，切端重新入水；切花保鲜剂若使用，仅按本包装配比。
4. **重排并记下一次检查。** 把留用花枝放回稳定瓶内，远离热源和通道，确认水线、花瓶稳定和花头状态。若花枝少到无法在原瓶站稳，换更合适的小瓶或结束展示；不要用腐败枝当支撑。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

要么清水与可留花枝组成稳定小展示、坏枝已移除；要么花瓶已撤下并清洗。只往浑水上加水、遮住枯花，不算维护。

- **换水后仍很快浑浊：** 排查遗漏的浸水叶、软烂茎和未洗净花瓶；持续异常就撤下。
- **花枝明显软烂或发霉：** 不复剪再用，安全丢弃并清洗容器。
- **清理时花瓶快倒：** 先放回稳固台面，分枝处理或请人协助，别抱着满水瓶操作。
- **无法判断特定花种是否能复剪：** 查该种可靠资料；无法确认时仅换水和移除坏枝，必要时结束展示。
- **手眼或体力受限：** 小水量、宽口轻瓶、分次移动和可信帮助可降低倾倒风险；由你核对留/撤决定。

### 假设与边界

时间表只作检查提醒，不是所有花通用寿命。RHS 建议定期查水、换水和去坏花，但当地温度、花种与初始状态会改变频率。没有“复活”承诺，也不处理杀虫剂或消毒剂配方。

## English

Load this Skill when an existing **ordinary cut-flower vase** has falling or cloudy water, or some blooms are fading, and you need to decide whether to continue the display or retire it. Finish with a safely refreshed display or an honestly cleared one, not a promise to revive dead flowers. The starting point differs from conditioning new stems or first arranging a vase.

### Preparation and inputs

Know what flowers you are handling and check any special care directions. Have clean water, a washable vase, a stable work area, clean suitable shears, and gloves if needed. With obvious mold, rotten smell, or uncertain sap exposure, avoid bare-hand handling and end the display safely under ordinary local waste and cleaning practice.

### Execution

1. **Decide what can stay.** Move the vase to a stable work area. Check water level and cloudiness, soft stem bases, blooms, and leaves. Remove a few spent stems if the remainder is sound. If most stems are rotten or moldy, retire the display instead of hiding failed flowers behind others.
2. **Keep sound stems watered.** Put retained stems briefly in a clean water vessel. Empty the old vase, wash it, and rinse away cleaner; do not mix cleaning residue into new flower water.
3. **Refill and trim modestly.** For stems suited to ordinary recutting, use clean suitable shears to take a small amount off stale cut ends. Remove leaves that would sit under water. Add fresh water and submerge cut ends again. Use flower food only at its own packet ratio if chosen.
4. **Rearrange and set the next check.** Put retained stems into a stable vase, away from heat and passage. Verify water contact, stable placement, and bloom condition. If too few sound stems remain for the original vase, use a suitable smaller vessel or end the display; rotten stems are not supports.

### Success, problems, and recovery

Either a stable smaller display has clean water and sound stems with failed parts removed, or the display is cleared and the vase washed. Adding water atop cloudy water and hiding dead blooms is not maintenance.

- **Water clouds rapidly again:** Look for immersed leaves, soft stems, and a poorly washed vase. Retire the display if the problem persists.
- **A stem is rotten or moldy:** Do not recut it for reuse; discard safely and wash the vessel.
- **Vase starts to tip during work:** Set it down on a stable surface and handle stems in groups or ask help; do not work while cradling a full heavy vase.
- **Species recutting rule is unclear:** Check reliable species guidance; otherwise change water and remove bad stems only, or retire the display.
- **Grip, sight, or stamina is limited:** A light broad vessel, small water quantities, staged moves, and trusted help can reduce spill risk while you confirm the keep-or-retire decision.

### Assumptions and limits

A check interval is a prompt, not a universal flower lifespan. RHS advises checking water, replacing it, renewing ends, and removing failed blooms, while temperature, species, and starting condition change actual timing. No revival promise or pesticide/disinfectant recipe is included.
