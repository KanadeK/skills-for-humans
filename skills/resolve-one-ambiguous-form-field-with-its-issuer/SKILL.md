## 中文

当一份普通表格只有某个字段的含义、适用条件或填写格式仍不清楚，继续写会变成猜测时加载。你要向**这份表格的实际发放机构提出一个能回答的问题，并保存其解释或明确仍未解决**。这里不请求机构代你作法律、税务、医疗或财务判断，也不把敏感答案交给陌生渠道。

### 准备与输入

先核对表格完整标题、版本、你要办的普通事项和字段原文/页码。查看紧邻提示、说明页、机构官方帮助页；没看到不等于没有，先把可读资料找全。确定哪个词、单位、条件或材料来源让你无法判断，且不要把身份证号、密码、账号或他人的个人资料抄进公开问询。

### 执行

1. **把模糊处缩成一句话。** 写“表格 X（版本）第 Y 项原文是……；我不明白 A 与 B 哪个含义/允许什么格式”。只给回答所需的最少背景；真需要私人详情时先核实官方渠道和隐私处理方式。
2. **问发放者，不问随机相似案例。** 用表格指向的官方客服、帮助页面或预约联系人询问。论坛答案、旧版截图或别家机构的习惯只能提供可能性，不能替发放者解释这一格。
3. **记清谁答、答什么。** 保存机构名称、渠道、日期、回复人/参考号（如有）和准确解释。若得到“这格可空/可写不适用/需某份材料”等答复，注明只适用于你的表格和具体条件，不把它扩展到整份表格。
4. **把不一致之处再次核对。** 口头回答与纸面说明冲突、客服未听清型号/版本或给出模糊“应该可以”时，读回字段和两种说法，请其确认适用的当前文件；未确认就保持待问，不先填一个看似合理的值。
5. **回到表格准备状态。** 有明确机构解释且你能从有权资料核对答案时，将这格标“可按来源回答”；资料仍缺就标缺，不因问题已解释而编造答案。后续实际填写、审核和提交另按机构流程。

### 完成与停止

这一格的含义/格式有可追溯的实际发放者解释，或有明确未答状态与下次询问点；敏感值没有被猜填或泄露。问题本身要求法律、税务、医疗、财务资格或他人授权决定时停在专业/机构边界，不用本 Skill 给出实体答案。

### 常见报错与补救

- **问了“怎么填这个表？”得到泛泛回复：** 给出表格版本、字段原文和具体二选一疑点重问。
- **客服说可以写 N/A，纸面却说必填：** 读回两处冲突，请其确认当前版本；确认前别填。
- **只有旧版网上示例：** 去当前发放者渠道找对应版本，不照抄。
- **对方要求在非官方聊天里发完整证件：** 停止发送，先核实渠道和实际必要范围。
- **问题已解释但事实资料仍没有：** 将字段标缺材料，而不是把解释当成答案。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只解决普通表格一个语义或格式疑点，不代填个人事实、签名或资格声明。机构与地区不同，允许未知/不适用的条件必须来自手中表格或实际机构。听力、语言、阅读或沟通受限时可用官方文字回复、翻译/辅助方式或可信授权者逐句读回，并保留问答记录。副作用是“随便填个 N/A”失去默认优先权。

## English

Load this Skill when one field's meaning, condition or format on an ordinary form is still unclear and continuing would mean guessing. Ask **the actual issuer an answerable question about that exact field and keep either its explanation or an explicit unresolved state**. This does not ask the institution to make a legal, tax, medical or financial judgment for you or hand sensitive answers to an unfamiliar channel.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the form's complete title and version, your ordinary task, and the field's exact words and page or section. Look at nearby hints, instructions and the issuer's official help first. Define the word, unit, condition or factual source that is unclear. Keep ID numbers, passwords, account information and another person's personal details out of a public question.

### Execution

1. **Make one precise question.** Write “Form X (version), field Y says ...; does A or B mean ... / which format is permitted?” Give only the background necessary to answer. If private detail really is needed, first verify the official channel and how it handles privacy.
2. **Ask the issuer, not a random similar example.** Use the contact or help page on the form or its official appointment channel. A forum, old screenshot or another agency's convention may suggest possibilities but cannot define this field for its issuer.
3. **Record who answered what.** Keep organisation, channel, date, responder or reference if any, and the exact explanation. If the response permits blank, not applicable or a certain source, note the condition under which it applies to this version and field, not the whole form.
4. **Read back conflicting guidance.** If a spoken answer clashes with printed instructions, support did not hear the version or the answer is only “should be fine,” repeat the field and both interpretations and ask which current document applies. Leave it ask-first rather than choosing a plausible value.
5. **Return to preparation state.** With a clear issuer explanation and authorised factual source, mark the field ready to answer. If the facts are still absent, mark missing; understanding the question does not create an answer. Actual filling, checking and submitting follow the institution's later process.

### Success and stop

This field's meaning or format has a traceable issuer explanation, or a clear awaiting-reply state and next contact. No sensitive value has been guessed or exposed. If the question itself requires legal, tax, medical, financial eligibility or another person's authority, stop at the institution or qualified boundary rather than supplying that substantive answer here.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“How do I fill this form?” yields a generic answer:** Ask again with version, exact field and one concrete ambiguity.
- **Support says N/A but paper says required:** Read both back and ask which applies to the current version. Do not fill before confirmation.
- **Only an old online example exists:** Use the current issuer's channel, not a copied answer.
- **Someone requests full ID through a non-official chat:** Stop sharing and verify the real channel and minimal necessary detail.
- **Meaning is clear but facts are absent:** Mark missing material, not a made-up answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This resolves one semantic or format question on an ordinary form. It does not fill personal facts, signatures or eligibility declarations. Institutions and regions differ; permission for unknown or not-applicable answers must come from this form or its issuer. Use an official written reply, translation or trusted authorised readback when hearing, language, reading or communication access differs, retaining the question and answer. “Just write N/A” loses its automatic first turn.
