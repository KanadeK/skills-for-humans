## 中文

当你听到“家里该补点东西”“买点早餐和日用品”，却还说不清缺什么时，加载这份 Skill。你会得到一张有存量依据的**缺口清单**，而不是立即决定商店、品牌或包装。若已经知道每顿饭、预算和路线，要安排一次采购，转用 [去菜市场前做计划](../plan-a-market-trip/SKILL.md)。本篇不处理入户保存、药品、保健品或危险化学用品。

### 准备与输入

用纸、可朗读的备忘录或你能继续编辑的方式，写下这次需求来自谁、要支持什么用途、最迟何时要用，以及哪些地方可查看。把“想买”和“已经确定要用”分开。若替别人采购，先确认哪些是共用物品；不要把别人私有或已预留的东西算作你的存量。

你不必翻遍全屋。只检查最可能的几个位置，并记下检查时间和位置。看不清标签时可放大、用已有的朗读方式，或请可信的人读出**品名和关键限制**；打不开或够不到的地方标“未查”，不要冒险搬动重物。照片只辅助记忆，不能证明照片之后没有被取用。

### 执行

1. **把愿望改成用途。** 对每句“补点什么”追问：这次要完成什么、给谁用、何时之前用？例如“周末前有两次要装垃圾”，比“买垃圾袋”更能核对缺口。若用途和期限都不明，先列“待确认”，不要猜一个商品。
2. **逐项查可用存量。** 记录物品、所在位置、实际可供这次用途的量。已开封但仍可用的、已预留的、损坏的，以及只在订单里尚未到家的，分别标明。**家里有**不等于**现在可用**；未到货只记为待确认，不按现货扣减。
3. **用同一口径对账。** 把需求与可用量换成相同的使用单位，例如“还要用两次，现有只够一次，缺一次”。无法可靠换算时写“数量待确认”，不要把“一点点”变成一包。这里算的是使用缺口；包装数量留到下一步选购时决定。
4. **给每项一个去向。** 可用量足够就标“已覆盖”；已核实可用量不足、且本轮必须满足的标“需补”；用途、共享权限或到货时间不明，或还能在使用前查清存量的，标“待确认”；没有本轮使用机会的标“非本轮”。若用途确定而存量赶不上核实，按**已确认可用量**列本次最低使用缺口，同时注明未查位置；不要靠猜测把待确认项悄悄放进购物车。
5. **只问能改变结论的问题。** 对同住者或提出需求的人，问“这件是共用的吗？”“周末前要用几次？”或“那份订单确定何时到？”得到答案后只改受影响的行。没人能回答时保留待确认，并先完成其他行。
6. **交出清单并停止盘点。** 把“需补”交给实际采购计划；没有缺口也可以交出空的采购清单。型号、标签限制、包装量、价格和携带方式在各自的选择步骤核对。清单不是库存数据库的建站申请。

你可以照着写一行：`用途与期限｜已查位置及时间｜可用存量｜使用缺口｜需补/已覆盖/待确认/非本轮`。例如“周末前两次装垃圾｜柜下，周五｜够一次｜缺一次｜需补”。它没有推断该买几卷或哪种尺寸。

### 完成与停止

你能指出每条“需补”对应的用途和期限、看过的存量及尚缺的使用量；每条未知都有一个待确认问题。到这里就完成。若食品日期、保存经历或标签限制不明，别把它当作安全可用的食物，也别靠外观或气味替它投票；按包装和当地可靠食安指引另行核实。无法核实就让该项保持未解决，而不是宣布全屋已经盘点完。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“家里明明有”但那份已被预留：** 从可用量中移走，记录谁能确认是否可共享；未获同意前不借用。
- **把在途订单当现货：** 记预计到货日和是否已确认；需要在它之前使用、且现货不足的部分仍是缺口，否则先待确认。
- **同一东西写了两次：** 按用途合并需求，再核一次可用量；不要把一份存量抵扣两遍。
- **只找到半瓶、半盒或看不清的包装：** 能确认足够完成一次用途才计入；否则记数量或身份待确认。食品安全不确定时停止把它算成可食用存量。
- **中途被打断或够不到存放处：** 留下已查位置、时间与未查位置，下次从未查处继续；用可及的记录方式或请人协助，不为了清单爬高或搬重物。

### 假设、替代与已知副作用

这里处理普通食品和低风险日用品的**需求核对**。家庭共用规则、购买频率、单位、标签语言及身体可及性都因人和地区而异；照你实际的使用机会和权限填写。若只是暂时不能检查某处，你仍可交出“已证实缺口 + 待确认”两部分，不必伪装全知。现实副作用是少买一件可能的重复品，也可能多出一条需要问人的问题。

## English

Load this Skill when “we should stock up” or “get some breakfast things and household supplies” is still too vague to shop from. You will make a **gap list backed by checked stock** before choosing a store, brand, or pack. If you already know the meals, budget, and route for one trip, use [Plan a Market Trip](../plan-a-market-trip/SKILL.md). This Skill does not cover putting purchases away, medicines, supplements, or hazardous household chemicals.

### Preparation and inputs

Use paper, an accessible notes app, or any record you can resume. Note who requested this, what each item must do, when it is needed, and which storage places you can check. Separate “it might be nice” from a confirmed use. If you shop for someone else, confirm what is shared; another person's private or reserved supply is not yours to count.

You do not need to search the entire home. Check the most likely places and record where and when you looked. If you cannot read a label, enlarge it, use an existing reading aid, or ask someone you trust to read the **identity and relevant restrictions**. Mark an unreachable or unopened place “not checked”; do not move heavy things just to complete the list. A photo helps memory but cannot show what was used after it was taken.

### Execution

1. **Turn a wish into a use.** For each vague request, ask what must be done, for whom, and by when. “Two more times taking out rubbish before the weekend” is easier to check than “buy bags.” If both use and timing are unclear, mark it “confirm” instead of guessing a product.
2. **Check usable stock for each use.** Record the item, location, and how much can actually serve this use. Distinguish opened but usable stock, reserved stock, damaged stock, and an order that has not arrived. **Present at home** and **available now** are different. Record an incoming order as pending; do not subtract it as stock on hand.
3. **Reconcile in one unit.** Express need and usable stock in the same use unit: “two uses needed, enough for one here, one use missing.” If you cannot convert reliably, write “amount to confirm” instead of turning “a little” into a pack. This is a use gap; pack size belongs to the later purchasing decision.
4. **Give each line a state.** Mark it “covered” if usable stock suffices; “buy” if confirmed usable stock falls short for a use this round; “confirm” if use, sharing permission, or arrival is unclear, or if stock can still be checked before use; or “not this round” if there is no use this round. If a confirmed use cannot wait for an inaccessible stock check, list only the minimum use gap against **confirmed usable stock** and name the unchecked place. Do not quietly move an unresolved request into the basket by guessing.
5. **Ask only questions that change the result.** Ask a housemate or requester “Is this shared?”, “How many uses before the weekend?”, or “When is that order actually due?” Update only affected lines. If no one can answer, leave that line pending and finish the others.
6. **Hand off the list and stop checking.** Send “buy” lines into the actual shopping plan. An empty buy list is a valid result. Check model, label restrictions, pack amount, price, and carrying needs in their own selection steps. This list does not require you to build a household inventory database.

One line can read: `Use and deadline | checked place and time | usable stock | use gap | buy/covered/confirm/not this round`. For example: “Take out rubbish twice before the weekend | under sink, Friday | enough for once | one use missing | buy.” It does not claim how many rolls or which size to purchase.

### Success and stop conditions

For every “buy” line, you can point to its use and deadline, checked stock, and missing use amount. Every unknown has a question to confirm. Stop there. If a food item's date, storage history, or label restrictions are unclear, do not count it as safe food or let looks and smell vote for it. Check the pack and applicable local food-safety guidance separately. If you cannot verify it, leave that line unresolved rather than claiming that the entire home has been inventoried.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“We have some” but it is reserved:** Remove it from usable stock and note who can confirm sharing. Do not borrow it without agreement.
- **An incoming order was counted as stock:** Note when it is expected and whether that is confirmed; a use before its arrival still has a gap if stock on hand falls short. Otherwise, confirm first.
- **The same need appears twice:** Combine the uses, then check available stock once. Do not spend the same supply twice on paper.
- **Only a part-full or unreadable pack is found:** Count it only if you can confirm it covers a use; otherwise mark amount or identity to confirm. Stop counting food of uncertain safety as edible stock.
- **You are interrupted or cannot reach a storage place:** Keep the checked places, times, and unchecked places; resume with the latter. Use an accessible record or ask for help instead of climbing or lifting for the list.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a needs check for ordinary food and low-risk household supplies. Sharing rules, shopping frequency, units, label language, and physical access vary by household and region; use your actual permissions and opportunities to use each item. If one area cannot be checked yet, you can still hand over “verified gaps + questions” without pretending to know everything. A possible side effect is one fewer duplicate purchase and one more question for a housemate.
