## 中文

当你买错尺寸、型号、颜色，收到不合适的商品，或在允许期限内改变主意时，加载这份 Skill。目标是根据实际政策提出一个清楚请求，拿到结果或可继续处理的书面证据；不需要先写一篇关于自己为什么会买错的悔过书。

这份 Skill 不判断法定权利、欺诈、拒付、贷款或高额争议。商家政策与所在地消费者权利不同；复杂争议请使用合格的当地渠道。

### 准备与输入

先确认：

- 商家、购买日期、渠道和订单/收据；
- 商品本体、包装、标签、附件、赠品和说明书现状；
- 退换期限、允许原因、原付款方式、运费/补货费和退款时间；
- 你要退款、换尺寸/型号、维修还是店铺额度；
- 现场退、快递寄回、上门取件或无障碍替代方式；
- 这件商品的退货申请是否已获准、每件对应的退货码/标签、指定承运方及是否必须由你装箱/打印；
- 一句真实原因，例如“尺寸不合适，未使用，希望按政策退款”。

先从商家官网、订单页或实体店官方信息读取政策，不依赖论坛里另一个年份、地区和商品类别的经历。

若商品保存个人数据，先按制造商官方方法备份并移除数据；无法确认清除时不要交出设备，先问官方支持。不要把密码或验证码写在退货说明里。

### 执行

1. **在截止前启动。** 截止日不是建议开始研究的日期。线上退货先在订单中创建申请，并读回是否获准、可用方式、交件期限及退货编号；保存申请时间。“申请已提交”还不是商品已被接收。
2. **把商品恢复到诚实可交付状态。** 集齐本体、原有附件、赠品和标签；不要伪装使用痕迹、故障或缺失。能清洁的普通表面按产品说明清洁，不做会进一步损伤的“翻新”。
3. **拍最少必要证据。** 记录商品、序列/型号（需要时）、包装、瑕疵和配件；不要把身份证、住址、完整支付信息或家中无关内容拍进去。
4. **选择准确请求。** 退款、换货、维修和店铺额度不是同一输出。先请求最希望的一个，并知道可接受的第二结果。
5. **现场退货时用四句。**
   - 你好，我想按这项政策退/换这个商品。
   - 订单/购买日期是……
   - 原因是……
   - 我希望的处理是……
6. **按这件商品实际开放的方式交接。** 现场退带商家要求的收据、订单条码或退货码；寄回时只用商家提供/确认的承运方、地址、标签或二维码，核对是否需要你装箱、打印及哪些商品可共用一张标签。需要包装才按要求包装并处理旧标签，交件前留下必要外观记录；没有打印机时查这件是否有官方扫码交件或现场退的选项，不自创标签。不要把包裹寄到聊天里临时出现的私人地址。
7. **核对金额和路径。** 确认退款到哪里、是否扣费、预计多久、换货何时发出，以及谁承担运费。退款“已提交”与资金“已到账”是两个状态。
8. **取得每一阶段的凭证。** 申请编号、柜台/承运方实际接收小票、运输追踪、商家接收与退款处理是不同状态。交出商品时索取该渠道提供的收件凭证，并保存到商家完成处理；仅有未使用的二维码或已打印标签，不是交件证据。纸质小票先拍清楚，热敏纸很擅长自动删除历史。
9. **设置一次跟进日期。** 到期仍无结果时，带着编号和证据联系官方客服；不要每天从头描述一次。

### 成功条件

你拿到已确认的退款、换货、维修/额度安排，或一份明确拒绝及依据。商品、金额、方式、预计时间和参考编号都可核对。成功也可以是确认不符合政策后停止继续投入时间。

### 常见报错与补救

- **找不到收据：** 查订单邮件、会员账户、银行交易线索或商家允许的其他购买证明；不要伪造凭证。
- **包装已拆或使用过：** 如实说明状态，查看该类别的实际政策。可接受部分退款、维修或转售是不同路径，不保证都存在。
- **退货期限刚过：** 礼貌询问一次例外或保修/维修选项；被拒后记录结果，不把不断升级语气当成新的政策。
- **寄回后追踪停滞：** 联系承运方和商家，提供追踪、寄出证据与受理编号；保留原件。
- **二维码、标签或退货箱不匹配：** 先核对该商品的官方退货方式和每张标签覆盖的商品；没有打印机或箱子时只选商家明确提供的免打印/免装箱方案，不把几件不同退货混进同一箱。
- **退款金额或方式不对：** 指出订单、政策和差额，要求书面核对。法定权利或支付争议转向当地合格消费者/法律渠道。
- **现场沟通压力过大：** 带一张书面四句说明、使用文字客服、请可信陪同或选择较安静时段。你不必即兴辩护。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

退换期限、卫生商品、定制品、数字商品、食品、折扣品和跨境订单规则差异很大。本 Skill 不承诺退款，也不把某国政策说成全球权利。移动、排队、打印、包装或听说沟通不可访问时，寻找官方邮寄、上门、文字或代理渠道。

现实副作用是一段排队或寄送流程，以及等待款项真正回来的时间。至少那件不合适的东西不必继续在家里扮演沉没成本纪念碑。

## English

Load this Skill when you bought the wrong size, model, or colour; received an unsuitable item; or changed your mind within an allowed window. Use the actual policy to make one clear request and obtain a result or evidence for the next step. You do not need to write an apology essay about the original purchase.

This Skill does not decide statutory rights, fraud, chargebacks, loans, or high-value disputes. Seller policy and local consumer rights differ; take complex disputes to a qualified local channel.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm:

- seller, purchase date, channel, and order/receipt;
- item, packaging, labels, accessories, gifts, and manuals present;
- deadline, allowed reasons, original payment route, shipping/restocking fees, and refund timing;
- refund, size/model exchange, repair, or store credit desired;
- in-store, mail, pickup, or accessible alternative route;
- whether this item has an authorized return, its item-specific code or label, named carrier, and whether you must box or print it;
- one truthful reason, such as “The size does not fit; it is unused; I am requesting a policy return.”

Read the current policy from the seller's official site, order page, or store. A forum story from another year, region, and product category is not your contract.

If the item stores personal data, back it up and remove data using the manufacturer's official method. Do not hand over a device when erasure cannot be confirmed; ask official support first. Never put passwords or one-time codes in return notes.

### Execution

1. **Start before the deadline.** The final day is not the recommended research date. For online orders, start in the official order flow, then read back whether it was authorized, the offered route, handover deadline, and return reference. Save the request time. “Request submitted” is not “item received.”
2. **Prepare an honest handoff.** Gather the item, original accessories, gifts, and labels. Do not disguise use, faults, or missing parts. Clean ordinary surfaces only as product instructions permit.
3. **Capture minimum necessary evidence.** Photograph the item, model/serial when relevant, package, defect, and accessories. Keep IDs, addresses, complete payment information, and unrelated home details out of frame.
4. **Choose the exact request.** Refund, exchange, repair, and credit are different outputs. Request your first choice and know the acceptable second choice.
5. **Use four lines in person.**
   - Hi, I would like to return/exchange this under this policy.
   - The order/purchase date is…
   - The reason is…
   - I am requesting…
6. **Use the route offered for this item.** For a store return, bring the receipt, order barcode, or return code actually required. For mail, use only the seller-provided or confirmed carrier, address, label, or QR code. Check whether you must box or print it and which items, if any, share one label. Pack and remove old labels when that route requires it, keeping minimal exterior evidence before handover. Without a printer, look for an official scan-at-drop-off or in-store option for this item; do not invent a label. Never send it to a private address found only in chat.
7. **Confirm amount and route.** Ask where the refund goes, deductions, expected time, exchange dispatch, and who pays shipping. “Refund submitted” and “funds received” are separate states.
8. **Keep proof for each stage.** A request number, actual counter/carrier acceptance receipt, transit tracking, seller receipt, and refund processing are different states. Ask for the channel's handover proof when you release the item and keep it until the seller finishes processing. An unused QR code or printed label is not proof of handover. Photograph thermal-paper receipts before they execute automatic history deletion.
9. **Set one follow-up date.** If the result is absent then, contact official support with the reference and evidence rather than restarting the story daily.

### Success

You have a confirmed refund, exchange, repair/credit arrangement, or explicit refusal with its basis. Item, amount, method, expected time, and reference are checkable. Confirming that policy does not apply and stopping can also be a complete result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Receipt is missing:** Check order email, membership account, transaction clues, or other proof the seller accepts. Do not fabricate evidence.
- **Package is open or item used:** Describe the state honestly and read the actual category policy. Partial refund, repair, or resale are different routes and may not exist.
- **The deadline just passed:** Ask once for an exception or warranty/repair route. Record a refusal; increasing tone does not create a new policy.
- **Tracking stalls:** Contact carrier and seller with tracking, dispatch proof, and case number. Keep originals.
- **QR, label, or return box does not match:** Check the item's official route and what each label covers. Without a printer or box, choose only a seller-supported print-free or box-free option; do not combine separate returns under one label.
- **Refund amount or route is wrong:** Point to the order, policy, and difference; request written review. Move legal-rights or payment disputes to a qualified local consumer/legal channel.
- **In-person discussion is overwhelming:** Bring the four-line request in writing, use official chat, take a trusted companion, or visit at a quieter time. Improvised self-defence is not required.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Deadlines and rules vary for hygiene goods, custom goods, digital products, food, sale items, and international orders. This Skill cannot guarantee a refund or promote one country's policy into global rights. If travel, queueing, printing, packaging, hearing, or speech is inaccessible, look for official mail, pickup, text, or authorized-representative options.

Known side effects include a queue or parcel, plus waiting for money to actually return. At least the unsuitable item does not have to remain a monument to sunk cost.
