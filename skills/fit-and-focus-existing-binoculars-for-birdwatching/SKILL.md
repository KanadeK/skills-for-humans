## 中文

当你已有一副完好的双筒望远镜，想在**原地观鸟前调到适合自己眼睛的一幅清楚画面**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是眼杯、两筒间距、中央对焦与视差轮完成个人校准，或确认今天改用裸眼观察；不需要购买新器材。**绝不通过望远镜看太阳或其附近，使用时不边走路、不窥看私人空间。**

### 准备与输入

选择获准、安全、不挡路的固定站位，找远处静止的非太阳招牌/树干纹理作对焦物；确保光路不指向太阳。检查镜身、背带和镜片无松脱/裂损，借用时获准调整可调部件。眼镜佩戴者先按本镜说明收下眼杯，未佩戴者适当升起；不把他人的视差设置改后不说明。眩晕、眼痛或无法稳握时停止，裸眼观察即可。

### 执行

1. **让视野合成一个圆。** 在静止参照物上拿稳望远镜，调两筒铰链宽度直到两侧成一幅无明显黑边的图；背带按自身安全佩戴，镜身不垂到能碰硬物的位置。
2. **先用中央轮对一只眼。** 按本镜常见方法轻闭有视差调节的那只眼，另一眼用中央对焦轮把参照物调清；不在太阳边缘找目标，不随意旋到机械尽头硬拧。
3. **再用视差轮对另一只眼。** 保持中央轮位置，换眼用视差调节让同一参照物清楚；两眼打开看是否融合为一幅图。具体视差轮在哪边由本镜说明决定，不假设所有机型右眼调节。
4. **记个人位置并试一次安全举镜。** 记视差位置以便下次找回；先裸眼找到远处鸟或树，再举镜中央轮微调，不靠朝行人窗户搜索。无法舒服融合或头晕时停用，回到裸眼/可信协助。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

双眼看到单幅清晰、无明显黑边的远处静止参照图，望远镜可稳握，或明确选裸眼；还没有识别任何鸟。两个像仍叠不起来却继续追鸟，不算完成调镜。

- **出现两个圆或黑边：** 先调镜筒宽度，再看眼杯是否合适。
- **一眼清楚另一眼糊：** 重走中央轮单眼、视差轮另一眼的顺序。
- **镜身太重/背带不稳：** 站稳、缩短使用或不用镜，不为看鸟走动举镜。
- **镜片破裂、眼痛或眩晕：** 停止使用，查器材或专业意见。
- **视力/手部不便：** 固定座位、可调背带或可信协助持镜；裸眼同样能参与观鸟。

### 假设与边界

各双筒视差轮位置和眼杯结构不同，厂商说明优先。Cornell 的调整法是普通双筒起点，不保证校正个人视力问题；望远镜仅辅助远距观察，不许可侵犯隐私或靠近鸟巢。

## English

Load this Skill when sound binoculars you own or may borrow need to show **one clear image for your eyes before stationary birdwatching**. Finish with eyecups, barrel width, central focus, and diopter personally set, or choose unaided eyes without buying gear. **Never view the sun or nearby sky through binoculars, walk while looking through them, or peer into private spaces.**

### Preparation and inputs

Stand safely at a permitted fixed place and choose a distant still sign or tree pattern well away from the sun. Check body, strap, and lenses for loose or cracked parts and permission to adjust a borrowed pair. Lower eyecups for glasses or raise appropriately otherwise under this model's directions. Tell an owner if you changed their diopter. Stop for dizziness, eye pain, or an unmanageable weight; unaided observation remains valid.

### Execution

1. **Merge the field into one circle.** Hold on the still target and change hinge width until both fields form one image without major black areas. Wear the strap safely so optics cannot swing into hard objects.
2. **Central-focus for one eye first.** By this model's ordinary route, close the eye controlled by its diopter and use the central wheel for the other eye on the reference. Stay away from the sun and do not force a wheel against its stop.
3. **Diopter-focus for the other eye.** Keep central focus fixed, change eyes and use the diopter on the same target. Open both for a fused image. The actual model's diopter location controls; it need not be on every right eyepiece.
4. **Record your setting and try a safe lift.** Note diopter position for later. Find a distant bird or tree with unaided eyes first, then lift binoculars and refine central focus without scanning windows or people. Stop for inability to merge comfortably or dizziness and use unaided eyes or trusted help.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Both eyes see one sharp still distant image without strong blackouts and the optics are manageable, or no-tool observation is chosen. No bird ID has yet been made. Chasing birds through double blurred circles is not completed setup.

- **Two circles or black edges remain:** Adjust hinge width first, then eyecup setting.
- **One eye sharp, the other blurred:** Repeat one-eye central focus followed by other-eye diopter in order.
- **Body is heavy or strap unsafe:** Stand stable, shorten use, or skip optics rather than walk with them raised.
- **Lens cracked, eye pain, or dizziness:** Stop and seek equipment or appropriate professional guidance.
- **Vision or grip is limited:** Seated use, adjustable strap, or trusted holding help can assist; unaided eyes still support birdwatching.

### Assumptions and limits

Diopter side and eyecups vary; maker directions win. Cornell's setup is an ordinary starting procedure, not an eye-care intervention. Optics assist distant observation, not privacy intrusion or nest approach.
