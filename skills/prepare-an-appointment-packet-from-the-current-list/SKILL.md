## 中文

当你已有一次普通、低风险的机构预约，却不确定当天要带什么纸张、照片、测量数据或物品时加载。你要按**这项服务、这一次预约的最新官方清单**做出实物已备、待机构确认和确实缺少的核对表。这里不解释医疗、法律、财务资格，不填写敏感表格、不代替复制装订或到场交接。

### 准备与输入

找预约确认里的机构、服务名称、日期/地点或线上形式，以及它指向的材料说明。只从机构官方页面、确认通知或官方客服核对；陌生转发链接若要求账号/身份资料，先从你已知的官方入口验证，不在此交出凭据。预约类型或地区不同，所需材料会变化，不能照搬朋友上次的清单。

### 执行

1. **确认对应的是哪一项服务。** 对照预约确认、官方页面标题和地点/渠道，记它们是否一致。名字相近但步骤不同、网页日期过旧或一封邮件与官网冲突时标待问，先联系实际机构，不自己推定哪份更有法律效力。
2. **逐行抄下实际要求。** 每项仅记中性名称、所需格式（原件/副本/照片/数字文件，若明示）、是在预约前提交还是到场带去、以及“仅当某条件成立”的原文条件。没有写的格式、份数、截止或费用不要加到清单里。
3. **用眼前材料逐项对照。** 把每项标“已在手且格式核实／手头没有／条件或格式待机构确认”。不用敏感号码或完整私人内容写共享清单。原件与副本不要因外观相似而混算；具体复制流程留给对应任务。
4. **把未知变成能回答的问题。** 对缺少或冲突项问机构：“我的预约是 X；当期清单写 Y；手头是 Z；是否适用、需什么形式、在何时提供？”记下机构回答或下一次核对点。对方没答时保持未知，不把发出问题当成“已备齐”。
5. **出发/连线前再做一轮实物核对。** 按最新已确认清单查看每件是否仍在可取用位置，预约信息是否改变，需提前提交的材料是否真的被确认收到。当天由谁携带和怎样交到正确对象属于后续交接；这里结束于一份真实准备状态。

### 完成与停止

当期官方要求的每项都标注真实备齐、确实缺少或待确认，预约类型与来源可追溯，敏感信息未外传；这不保证机构最终受理。若必需材料缺失、说明冲突或服务属于法律/税务/医疗/财务专业判断范围，不用猜填或伪造材料，问实际机构是否改期、换形式或转适当专业渠道。

### 常见报错与补救

- **拿的是类似服务的旧清单：** 回到本次确认和机构现行页面，重新对照服务名与地区。
- **截图只截到一半要求：** 找完整官方页面或请机构读回，缺失部分保持待确认。
- **网页说带原件，手里只有照片：** 标为未满足该形式，问机构有无认可替代；不自行宣布等价。
- **材料已发送，但不知道是否收到：** 保留发送事实，向机构核对接收状态；“发送”与“收到”是两回事。
- **有人要你在非官方链接输入密码：** 停止提供，回到已知官方渠道核实预约和所需材料。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通事项的材料准备，不决定资格、缴费义务或敏感资料应如何填写。机构、市场、预约方式和个人可及条件不同；纸本、电话或官方可访问页面都可作为来源。看不清要求、无法整理纸张时可请经你授权的可信者在私密环境读回，仍只记录必要状态。副作用是“应该带这个吧”需要在清单上找到出处。

## English

Load this Skill when you have an ordinary low-risk appointment but are unsure which papers, photographs, measurements or items to bring. You will use **the current official list for this exact service and booking** to mark what is ready in hand, what needs the institution's answer and what is missing. This does not interpret medical, legal or financial eligibility, fill sensitive forms, make copies or perform the eventual handoff.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the institution, service name, date and location or online mode in your confirmation, plus its material instructions. Use the official site, confirmation or support channel. If a forwarded unfamiliar link requests account or identity details, verify it from an official entry you already know before sharing credentials. Region and appointment type change the list; a friend's old checklist does not establish yours.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the exact service.** Compare booking confirmation, official page title and location or channel. If similar names refer to different services, a page seems old or an email conflicts with the site, mark it ask-first and contact the actual institution. Do not decide which document has legal priority yourself.
2. **Copy only the real requirements.** For each line note a neutral name, stated format (original, copy, image or digital file if specified), whether it goes before or at the appointment, and any condition written as “only if.” Do not add unstated counts, deadlines, fees or formats.
3. **Compare with items actually in hand.** Mark each “present and format confirmed / not in hand / condition or format to ask.” Keep private numbers and full sensitive detail out of a shared list. An original and an image are not the same thing merely because they show the same page; copying is a separate task.
4. **Turn unknowns into answerable questions.** For a missing or conflicting line, ask the institution: “My booking is X; the current list says Y; I have Z. Does it apply, in what format and when?” Record its answer or next check. No reply stays unknown; sending the question is not being ready.
5. **Recheck before leaving or connecting.** Against the latest confirmed list, verify each item is still reachable, booking details have not changed and any advance submission was actually acknowledged. Who carries and hands the items to the right recipient belongs to the later handoff task. This ends with a true preparation state.

### Success and stop

Every current official requirement has an honest ready, missing or ask-first state; service and source can be traced without exposing sensitive detail. This does not guarantee acceptance. If a required item is missing, instructions conflict or the matter needs legal, tax, medical or financial judgment, do not guess or fabricate a form. Ask the real institution about rescheduling, an accepted alternative or the right qualified channel.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An older list for a similar service was used:** Return to this confirmation and the institution's current page, checking service and region again.
- **A screenshot cuts off half the list:** Open the full official page or ask the institution to read it back. The missing portion remains unconfirmed.
- **An original is required but you only have a photograph:** Mark that format unmet and ask whether an alternative is accepted. Do not declare it equivalent yourself.
- **An item was sent but receipt is unknown:** Keep the sending fact and ask for receipt confirmation. Sent and received are separate states.
- **A non-official link asks for a password:** Stop providing it and verify the appointment and materials through a known official channel.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This prepares materials for an ordinary appointment. It does not decide eligibility, payment duties or how sensitive fields should be completed. Institutions, markets, channels and access needs differ; paper, phone and official accessible pages may all provide the real list. A trusted person you authorise can read details back in private when sight or paper handling is difficult, while your list still records only necessary states. “I guess they need this” now needs a source.
