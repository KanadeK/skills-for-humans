## 中文

当你想把**自己的简单图案**压到一块预切软泡棉上，做成可重复试印的印版时，加载这份 Skill。结果是轮廓清楚、表面未刺穿、知道印出会镜像的干净版；此时还没有纸面作品。它与 H273 的纸面拼贴/拓印不同，也不教刀具刻木、刻橡皮或复制他人作品。

### 准备与输入

用自有预切软泡棉片、钝头铅笔、废纸和稳定垫板。先在废纸画一个原创、线条不过密的小图；如含文字或方向箭头，记住转印会左右反向，先决定是否要在版上反写。泡棉若很脆、边缘锋利或需刀裁，换安全预切片。手部不便可选粗线、大版和可信协助固定垫板。

### 执行

1. **先定边界和方向。** 在版边留一点空白，确认图案由你设计且没有未经许可的角色、商标或私人信息。纸上原图放旁边作为参考；含字先画镜像练习，没把握就去掉字。
2. **轻试钝笔凹线。** 在边角用钝笔轻压一段，观察能留下凹痕却不穿透。再沿自己的轮廓缓慢压线，适当转动版而不是用尖笔猛戳。
3. **检查线与版的完整性。** 斜着看压线是否连续、背景凸面仍完整；浅处可轻补一次，出现撕裂或破洞则停止修补该区域。不要用剪刀/刀片把线“刻深”。
4. **标版背并留试印入口。** 在背面写日期和“印出左右反”，不在凸面涂色前就称完成成品。把版放平、避免压出意外凹痕，等下一步试印。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

干净泡棉版上有可见自绘凹线、印面不穿破，方向已知，可进入上色试印。只在废纸画图、泡棉仍空白，不算印版完成。

- **凹线太浅：** 先在边角校准压力，沿原线轻补，不换尖工具。
- **版被刺穿或撕开：** 停止扩大破口；用新安全预切版重做或接受不完整试验版。
- **含字印反了：** 此时改版不易，优先重做镜像文字或去掉文字；不谎称纸面会正向。
- **细线挤在一起：** 简化图案和线距，不以更大压力保住每条细线。
- **视觉/握持不便：** 用高对比原图、粗钝笔、大线距和可信固定协助，由你核查版面。

### 假设与边界

泡棉厚薄影响压痕，钝笔应按手边材料轻试；不保证所有包装泡沫都能安全做印版。版面可反复上色，但这一步只产出版，不计后续每张印数。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to press **your own simple design** into a pre-cut soft foam sheet for repeatable printing. Finish with a clean plate whose recessed lines are readable, surface is unpierced, and left-right reversal is understood; there is no paper print yet. This differs from H273 collage or rubbing and does not teach sharp carving into wood or rubber or copying others' artwork.

### Preparation and inputs

Use an owned pre-cut soft foam plate, dull pencil, scrap paper, and stable backing board. Sketch a sparse original design on scrap. If it contains text or a directional arrow, plan for a left-right reversed print and decide whether to reverse the plate design. Replace foam that is brittle, sharp-edged, or requires blade cutting. A broad line, larger plate, and trusted help holding the board can support limited grip.

### Execution

1. **Set margin and orientation.** Leave a little blank foam edge. Confirm the design is yours without unlicensed characters, marks, or private information. Keep the scrap drawing nearby. Trial mirror-writing if text matters, or leave text out when unsure.
2. **Test a recessed line with a dull tip.** Press a short line at an edge and check that it dents but does not puncture. Follow your own contour slowly, rotating the plate rather than stabbing with a pointed tool.
3. **Inspect line and plate integrity.** Tilt toward light for connected dents and sound raised background. Lightly reinforce a shallow segment once; stop at a tear or hole rather than carving it deeper with scissors or a blade.
4. **Label the reverse and leave a proof entry.** Date the back and note “print reverses left-right.” Store flat without making accidental dents. Do not call the plate a finished paper impression before proofing.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The clean foam has visible original recessed lines, no puncture, and known orientation, ready for inking and proof. A scrap drawing with blank foam is not a prepared plate.

- **Recessed line is faint:** Calibrate on an edge, then gently repeat along the line without a sharper tool.
- **Plate punctures or tears:** Stop enlarging it; use another safe pre-cut plate or accept an incomplete trial.
- **Printed words would reverse:** Redesign mirror text on a new plate or remove it, not pretend transfer will be forward.
- **Thin lines crowd together:** Simplify the drawing and spacing rather than pressing harder.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Use high-contrast sketch, broad dull tip, generous line spacing, and trusted holding help while you inspect the plate.

### Assumptions and limits

Foam thickness changes the mark, so test a gentle dull-pencil pressure on your material. Not every packaging foam safely acts as a plate. This step makes one reusable plate, not an edition of impressions.
