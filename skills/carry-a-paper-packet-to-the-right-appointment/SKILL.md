## 中文

当普通预约需要你把已准备的纸本材料带到指定地点，且文件可能含私人信息时加载。你要保证**出发时的文件与原件/副本状态在抵达时仍能核对**，并让材料在途中由有权者控制。这里不决定机构是否受理、不实际交给柜台，也不提供法律、医疗、税务、财务或身份核验操作建议。

### 准备与输入

核对预约确认、实际地点与当期材料表，带最少真正需要的纸；额外私密原件不因“以防万一”无条件随行。准备能保护纸边、不易散页的夹或封套，外侧用中性名称，不写完整证件号或私人事项。谁有权带、谁在目的地接手要事先明确；别把他人文件默认归自己携带。

### 执行

1. **出发前实点一次。** 按已确认清单核页、正反面/份数、原件与副本各几份，未备齐项诚实标缺。看清预约的地址、时间和接收服务，不用同楼另一部门的地址代替。
2. **把原件和副本分开保护。** 各自放在可识别夹层，纸边不露出易折损；若某文件只需出示原件但不应交付，单列提醒，具体按机构要求确认。原件不能与提交候选副本混成一束。
3. **途中保持实际控制。** 由本人或经同意的可信携带者拿着夹，避免在不受控的车内、公共桌面或其他人可翻看的地方暂放。临时换人时当面点包和接手人，未实交不把携带人改为“对方”。
4. **抵达时再点一次。** 在私密且允许的位置按同一清单复核包内每项与预约地点；少页、夹层打开、原件不见时先停在未齐状态，沿真实路线查找并联系有权者，不把预约材料写成“到了所以齐”。
5. **交给下一步时保留状态。** 记录实际在谁手里、到达时间及仍缺的项目；正式柜台接收/退还由另一流程确认。没到正确地点或接收人不明，文件仍由你保管，先问官方引导。

### 完成与停止

抵达准确预约处时，文件数量和原件/副本身份与出发记录一致，携带者/接手人明确，隐私未无谓暴露；或有具体缺项与最后实见处。这个结果只是“安全到达”，不是“机构已收”。路线/地点不明、重要原件遗失、未经同意换人或需要交给不明对象时暂停并用官方渠道确认。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“包里应该有”但抵达少一页：** 按出发清单查最后实见点，不把记忆当实物。
- **原件混在要提交的副本里：** 先分开并标记，不在拥挤柜台临时猜是哪张。
- **到错楼或同名窗口：** 对照预约确认的地点/服务，先问官方指引，不把纸递给看起来像工作人员的人。
- **临时让别人帮拿：** 只在其同意并有权时当面点数交接，记录谁带着。
- **夹子没关、纸露在外：** 找私密处整好并复点，不边走边让资料掉出来。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这只处理普通、已准备文件的物理携带，不建议法律/医疗/财务事项带什么材料。地区、交通、行动能力和机构隐私规则不同；有困难时可让有权可信者携带，用大字或可访问的最小清单回读。ICO 的原则提醒少带且不把私人文件无人看管留在车里，不等于所有地方统一交通规定。副作用是“我带上了”必须在抵达时再举证。

### 来源

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary appointment requires you to bring an already prepared paper packet to a named place, especially if it contains private information. Keep **departure contents and original/copy status checkable again at arrival** under an authorised person's control. This does not decide institutional acceptance, perform the counter handoff or give legal, medical, tax, financial or identity-authentication advice.

### Preparation and inputs

Check booking confirmation, real location and the current materials list and carry only papers actually needed. Extra private originals do not automatically travel “just in case.” Use a folder or sleeve that supports edges and keeps pages together, with a neutral exterior label rather than full ID or private topic. Name who may carry and who receives at destination. Another person's documents do not become yours to carry by default.

### Execution

1. **Count physically before departure.** Against the confirmed list, check pages, backs, sets and counts of originals and copies, marking a missing item honestly. Read the booking address, time and service; another desk in the same building is not automatically the destination.
2. **Protect originals apart from copies.** Give each an identifiable compartment with no exposed edge. If an original is only to be shown rather than retained by the institution, keep that note separate and confirm against its real rule. Do not interleave originals with proposed submission copies.
3. **Maintain custody en route.** You or a consenting trusted carrier holds the packet rather than leaving it in an uncontrolled car, public table or place others can browse. When people change, count the packet and name the real new holder face to face. Planned delivery is not a physical transfer.
4. **Count again on arrival.** In a private permitted spot, compare the same list with the packet and verify you reached the booked location. If pages, a compartment or an original are missing, keep the state incomplete, trace the real route and notify the authorised holder. “Arrived” does not mean “complete.”
5. **Preserve state for the next handoff.** Note who actually holds the papers, arrival time and any missing line. A later step confirms what a counter receives or returns. At a wrong location or with an unidentified recipient, keep the packet and ask official staff for directions.

### Success and stop

At the correct appointment location, actual counts and original/copy identity match the departure record, custody is known and private detail has not been needlessly shown; otherwise missing lines and last observed places are explicit. This is safe arrival, not receipt by the institution. Pause for unclear location, lost original, unauthorised change of carrier or pressure to give the packet to an unknown person.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“It should be in the bag” but one page is absent:** Use the departure count and last observed point, not memory as proof.
- **An original is mixed into submission copies:** Separate and label in private rather than guessing at a busy counter.
- **You reach the wrong building or same-named desk:** Read booking location and service again and ask an official direction before handing over papers.
- **Someone else offers to carry:** Transfer with consent and permission, count face to face and record the holder.
- **A folder is open with pages exposed:** Stop in a private place, close and recount it before walking on.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This physically carries prepared ordinary documents; it does not choose legal, medical or financial materials. Region, travel, mobility and local privacy rules vary. An authorised trusted person can carry while reading back a large-print or accessible minimal list. The ICO's advice to limit papers and avoid unattended personal documents in a vehicle is not a universal transport law. “I packed it” now needs an arrival check.
