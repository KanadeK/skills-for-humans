## 中文

当你**已经拍出一张技术上失败的照片**，还在允许且安全的现场，需要判断原因并只改一个因素重拍时，加载这份 Skill。结果是原片与重拍片的可见对比，以及一个有证据的原因判断；现场条件不允许改善时，留下诚实的停止记录。它从未知失误出发，不能替代预先学习对焦、逆光、低光或运动技巧。不要为补拍追随人车、进入禁止区域或干扰动物。

### 准备与输入

保留原片，不先删；确认你仍能从获准位置拍同一主体，且光线和主体没有剧烈改变。先说出原先想清楚呈现什么：主体细节、亮度还是动作。使用本机回放放大和可见的拍摄信息；没有参数显示也可以靠图像对比，但不要把猜测记成设备故障。

### 执行

1. **指出实际失败。** 在原片放大目标位置，记录“主体糊但背景清楚”“整张有同向拖影”“移动主体有拖影”或“主体过暗/高光丢失”等可见迹象。先区分技术失败与单纯不喜欢构图。
2. **选一个最可能的原因。** 背景清楚而主体糊，检查焦点是否落错；全画面相似拖影，检查手持稳定；只移动物体拖影，考虑主体运动；整体/主体亮度不合适，查看曝光预览或直方图。拍摄信息仅辅助，不从某个数字独断原因。
3. **只改一件事重拍。** 保持安全机位和目标，按本机说明书重新选焦点、稳住相机、使用可用的运动控制，或调整曝光中的一项。变化不能使你越界、遮挡通道或拍摄未获同意的人。若场景已消失、不能合法重拍或无法辨因，停在记录而非编造改善。
4. **并排核查两张。** 以相同放大程度比较目标区域，说明修改后哪一处改善、哪一处仍不理想。若无改善，撤回原因判断，记下下一次待试的单个因素，不把第二张自动称作成功。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

完成物是原片、重拍片、可见差异和一条与证据一致的原因/不确定性记录；无法安全重拍时，原片与停止原因也是完整结果。

- **放大后只看见背景清楚：** 重选目标焦点，别仅改曝光。
- **整张都模糊：** 先稳机；若主体也在运动，承认可能有两个原因，别用一次重拍宣称全解决。
- **重拍时光线突然改变：** 标记对比条件改变，别把亮度改善全归功于操作。
- **原片被删或无法回看：** 无可比证据；另起一次试拍，不虚构原片结论。
- **现场不安全或权限不清：** 停止补拍，保留问题供日后在安全场景练习。

### 假设与边界

本篇只处理一张已经发生的普通技术失误；曝光、焦点、运动和支撑的具体控制因型号而异，以本机手册为准。画质改善不等于获得公开传播、肖像或场地许可。

## English

Load this Skill when **one photo has already failed technically** and you are still at a safe, permitted scene where a retake may be possible. The result is an original/retake comparison with a supported cause, or an honest stop when the scene cannot be improved. This begins with an unknown failure; it does not replace planned focus, backlight, low-light, or motion practice. Do not chase people or vehicles, enter restricted ground, or disturb wildlife for a retake.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the original frame. Check that the same subject remains photographable from a permitted position under roughly comparable light. Name what you wanted to show clearly: subject detail, brightness, or action. Use enlarged playback and available shooting information. If metadata is unavailable, compare visible evidence without inventing a device fault.

### Execution

1. **Name the visible failure.** Enlarge the intended area. Record whether the subject is soft while the background is sharp, the whole frame has a similar streak, only the moving subject streaks, or the subject is dark/highlights lost. Separate a technical miss from mere dislike of composition.
2. **Choose one plausible cause.** Background sharp and subject soft suggests misplaced focus; similar streaks across the frame suggest camera movement; isolated moving-subject streaks suggest subject motion; brightness issues invite exposure preview or histogram review. Shooting information helps but one number alone does not prove a cause.
3. **Change one factor and retake.** Keep the safe position and target. Follow your model's controls to reset focus, support the camera, use an available motion control, or alter one exposure setting. Do not cross a boundary, block a path, or photograph an unconsenting person. If the scene vanished or permission is unclear, record a stop instead.
4. **Compare at the same enlargement.** State what visibly improved and what remains poor. If it did not improve, withdraw the cause claim and record one factor for a later trial. A second frame is not automatically a success.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Keep the original, retake, visible difference, and a cause or uncertainty record. If a safe retake is impossible, the original plus a clear stopping reason is the complete result.

- **Only the background is sharp:** Reacquire target focus rather than changing exposure alone.
- **Everything is blurred:** Stabilise first; if the subject moved too, note that both causes may exist.
- **Light changed during retake:** Mark the changed comparison condition rather than crediting only your setting.
- **Original deleted or unavailable:** Start a new trial; do not invent a before/after claim.
- **Unsafe or unclear permission:** Stop and practise in a safe setting later.

### Assumptions and limits

This covers one ordinary technical miss after it occurs. Focus, exposure, motion, and support controls vary by model; its manual governs. Image improvement grants no public-sharing, portrait, or location permission.
