## 中文

当收件地址已经核对，但你还没提交一次普通寄送，担心到时没人、前台不能收或所选服务到不了约定位置时加载。你要确认**有人愿意并能按这个服务的真实交付方式接收，或在提交前换经同意的方案**。这里不操作他人账号、不规避签收/认证、不分享门禁秘密，也不处理寄出后的改投；寄出后无法按时收件归 H135。

### 准备与输入

问收件人实际可接的地点与时段、是否有获准的前台/代收人、所选承运方式承诺到门口、指定地点还是需自提，以及是否有签收等实际条件。不要把购物页预计时间当成承运商已确认的到达时刻。物业、单位或快递柜有自身规则时核对可否用，不能借收件人姓名自行取得他人账号或取件码。

### 执行

1. **把地址与最后交付点分开。** 写“地址 → 承运服务实际交到哪里 → 由谁接”。同一建筑可能有大厅、前台、门口或自提点；选项由实际服务提供并由收件人接受，不凭猜测写“放门口即可”。
2. **核对接收能力和权限。** 收件人或获准代收者是否会在可行时间出现，能否接收该普通包裹，是否知道怎么领取/签收？只要一种条件没确认就标待问，不把“他们应该在家”当同意。需要身份证明或签收的服务按承运要求，不教规避。
3. **查入口和指示的正式渠道。** 楼宇门禁、访客入口或收件说明确需提供时，只在承运方支持并经收件人同意的受控渠道处理；密码、钥匙、私人取件码不上公开面单/群聊。某服务让收件人自己管理改投或留放时，由其按官方方式授权，寄件人不代点账号。
4. **比较可选路线。** 若标准交付无法实现，问实际承运方可否提供其官方自提、约定代收或其它适用选项，核对费用/时段并获收件人同意。USPS 的 Delivery Instructions 仅限合资格件且由收件人授权；别把它写成每票都可改。
5. **提交前回读结果。** 收件人能说出“这票到哪儿、谁接、需要什么正常步骤、若不在如何用官方渠道处理”。任何关键点仍未知就暂不提交，先联系相关方；本 Skill 不保证最终投递或对方实际签收。

### 完成与停止

在已选服务下有明确、获同意且实际可行的最终交付点与接收者，必要的服务条件和受控指示渠道已确认；或因未知条件停在草稿。地址正确却无人能收、代收无权、服务不支持入口、必须公开门禁秘密或涉及身份绕过时停止原方案。

### 常见报错与补救

- **收件人说“放前台”，前台没同意：** 向前台/物业确认其接收规则，未确认改为待问。
- **必须本人签收但本人那天不在：** 查服务官方是否允许适用的另一路线，获得收件人同意，不替其签名。
- **寄件人想在标签写门禁码：** 停止公开，问收件人和承运方是否有受控说明渠道。
- **网页显示“可选自提”，这票却不适用：** 以实际服务/运单资格为准，不能把营销列表当订单结果。
- **对方只给了预期到达时间：** 区分预计与可承诺的接收窗口，安排未确认项，不虚写有人值守。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通国内寄送提交前的接收可行性，不包含危险品、跨境、法律赔偿或寄出后改投。服务、地区、建筑和可及条件不同；快递柜、自提、邻居或前台接收均需实际资格及同意。听力/语言/行动不便时可使用收件人认可的书面回读或获授权的可信协助者。副作用是正确门牌号不再被误认为自动会开门。

## English

Load this Skill after the address is checked but before submitting an ordinary shipment when the intended recipient may be absent, reception may not accept it or the chosen service may stop somewhere different. Confirm **a willing authorised person and real handoff point under this service, or change the plan with agreement before submission**. This does not use another person's account, bypass signature or identity checks, share access secrets or reroute a parcel after shipping. H135 owns later collection changes.

### Preparation and inputs

Ask the recipient which place and period can actually work, whether reception or another person may accept it, whether the selected carrier delivers to a door or requires collection, and what actual signature or similar condition applies. A shopping page's estimated time is not a confirmed carrier arrival. Check building, workplace or locker rules instead of using another person's account or pickup code simply because you know the recipient's name.

### Execution

1. **Separate address from the last handoff point.** Record “address → where this service actually leaves it → who receives.” One building may mean lobby, reception, doorstep or pickup location. Choose only options the real service offers and recipient accepts, not a guessed “leave at door.”
2. **Check ability and permission to receive.** Will the recipient or authorised stand-in be present during a feasible window, able to take this ordinary parcel and know any collection or signature step? Mark any unconfirmed part ask-first. “They should be home” is not consent. Follow carrier proof or signature requirements without bypass instructions.
3. **Use the real access-instruction channel.** If a building entry or delivery instruction is genuinely necessary, use only a carrier-supported controlled channel with recipient permission. Do not print passwords, keys or private pickup codes on a public label or chat. When the service gives the recipient account control over redirection or placement, they authorise it themselves through the official route.
4. **Compare available routes.** If standard delivery cannot work, ask the actual carrier about eligible pickup, agreed authorised receipt or another offered route. Check any price or time and obtain recipient agreement. USPS Delivery Instructions, for example, appears only for eligible items and is authorised by the addressee; it is not guaranteed for every parcel.
5. **Read back before submission.** The recipient can say where this shipment goes, who takes it, what normal steps are needed and which official channel handles an absence. Hold the draft if a key part remains unknown. This does not guarantee physical delivery or actual signature.

### Success and stop

The selected service has a named, consenting and feasible final point and receiver, with required conditions and controlled instructions confirmed, or the shipment remains a draft. Stop the original plan when the address is correct but nobody can receive, a substitute lacks authority, service cannot reach the entrance, a secret would need public display or identity checks would be evaded.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Recipient says “leave it at reception,” but reception has not agreed:** Ask the desk or building about its receipt rule; mark pending until confirmed.
- **Recipient-only signature is required but they are absent:** Check official alternatives and obtain recipient agreement; do not sign for them.
- **Sender wants to print a gate code on the label:** Stop exposure and ask about a controlled official channel managed with recipient consent.
- **A site advertises pickup but this shipment is ineligible:** Check the real service or item, not a marketing list.
- **Only an estimated arrival is known:** Keep estimate distinct from a feasible receiving window; do not invent someone on duty.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is pre-submission feasibility for ordinary domestic shipping, not hazardous goods, cross-border rules, legal compensation or post-shipment changes. Carrier, market, building and access needs differ; lockers, pickup, neighbours and reception require actual eligibility and consent. Use the recipient's written readback or trusted authorised help when hearing, language or mobility differs. A correct street number does not automatically open a door.
