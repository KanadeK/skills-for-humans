## 中文

你已能单独弹两个和弦、缓慢换位并维持一个简单四拍，想把它们组成**自己设计的四小节伴奏**时使用本篇。结果是 A、A、B、A 各占一小节，能从起拍到终止拍走完；若中断，知道中断在哪一小节。这是把已会部件组装成一次完整小作品，不以某首歌的伴奏谱或版权旋律作为输入。

### 准备与输入

调好琴，选择两个各自清楚、往返换位可行的和弦，写下四小节顺序 A｜A｜B｜A。给每小节相同的简单规则，例如第 1、3 拍各下一次扫，2、4 拍留空并数出来。速度选能完成的慢速；先在纸上写开始提示与最后一次收音，避免不知何时结束。

### 执行

1. **口数结构。** 不弹琴先读“第一节 A、第二节 A、第三节 B、第四节 A”，每节数 1–4；确认 B 到 A 有换位时间。
2. **逐节轻弹。** 从第一节的第一拍开始，按既定有声拍扫弦，空拍继续口数；在第二节后提前准备换到 B，第三节后准备回 A，不临时改规则。
3. **处理一次中断。** 若漏拍、按错或疼痛，停在安全位置，标记节号；放慢并从该节或首节重来。不要为隐藏错误加速，也不要疼痛时强弹。
4. **弹完并收音。** 第四节结束时用轻放手指或停止扫弦收住声音，检查 A｜A｜B｜A 顺序、每节四拍及明确尾声是否完成。只把真正走完的那次记作完整伴奏。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

四小节有可辨认的开始、A｜A｜B｜A 顺序、共同拍数与结束；否则留下准确停在哪节的记录。

- **第三节换 B 赶不上：** 让第二节空拍承担移动，或整体减速。
- **第一、二节节拍不一：** 先只口数/拍手，保持同一规则。
- **第四节结束拖成第五节：** 预先定下最后一拍后收音的动作。
- **和弦开始闷：** 先返回单和弦检查，不用完整作品掩盖。

### 假设与边界

本篇只组合你已会的两和弦和一简单节奏。A、B 代表任意获准和弦，四节排列为原创练习；没有歌词、版权曲谱或公开表演许可。你决定节速与是否完成。

## English

Use this Skill after you can sound two chords, change between them slowly and keep one simple four-count rhythm. Build an **original four-bar accompaniment**: A, A, B, A, one chord per bar, with a clear first and last beat. If interrupted, name the bar where it stopped. This assembles known parts into one complete short piece without using a copyrighted melody or song chart.

### Preparation and inputs

Tune the instrument and choose two clear, changeable chords. Write A | A | B | A on paper. Give every bar the same simple rule, for example downstrum on counts 1 and 3, count silently through 2 and 4. Choose a slow completable pace. Mark the starting cue and final sound stop so the ending is not vague.

### Execution

1. **Count the shape aloud.** Without playing, say first bar A, second A, third B, fourth A, counting 1–4 for each. Confirm there is time to move from B back to A.
2. **Play bar by bar.** Start on the first count and strum only the planned sounding beats. Keep counting silent beats. Prepare B after bar two and A after bar three without changing the rhythm rule midway.
3. **Handle an interruption.** For a missed count, wrong shape or pain, stop safely and note the bar. Slow down and restart that bar or the beginning. Do not rush to hide a miss or play through pain.
4. **Finish and stop sound.** After bar four, lightly release or stop strumming to end the sound. Check the A | A | B | A order, four counts per bar and clear ending. Count only an actual complete run as a completed accompaniment.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Four bars have a recognizable start, A | A | B | A order, shared count and deliberate finish; otherwise keep the exact stopping bar.

- **B arrives late:** Use silent beats of bar two for the move or slow all bars.
- **First two bars drift:** Count or clap without playing first.
- **Ending runs into a fifth bar:** Preselect an after-last-count stop gesture.
- **Chord turns dull:** Return to a one-chord string check before continuing.

### Assumptions and limits

This combines two chords and a pulse you already know. A and B stand for any permitted shapes; the four-bar sequence is an original exercise. It supplies no lyrics, copyrighted score or public performance permission. You choose the pace and judge completion.
