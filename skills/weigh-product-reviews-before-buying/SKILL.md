## 中文

当你已选出一种普通日用品，却想靠网上评价决定是否购买时，加载这份 Skill。输出是“这些评价能支持什么、不能支持什么”，不是鉴定每条评价的真假，也不替代商品规格、标签或召回核对。医疗、保健或其他高风险选择不在这里。

### 准备与输入

记下准确商品名、型号或版本，以及你真正要验证的一件事，例如“盖子反复开合是否顺手”。给自己设一个 8–15 分钟的阅读止点。只有星数、没有文字，或者必须注册才能查看的页面，都不是必需依赖。

### 执行

1. **先锁定商品。** 看评价是否对应同一型号、尺寸和销售版本；混在一起的变体不能自动合并成你要买的那一件。商品页面与评价中的照片、名称或功能不一致时，先把它记为“可能混评”。
2. **按问题读，不按热闹读。** 跳过平均星数的第一印象，从近期的具体评价找你关心的实际使用条件、时间和结果。再看几条中低分评价，区分反复出现的同一问题与一次性的个人偏好。
3. **看评价的来源。** 记录页面是否标明赠品、报酬或其他激励；比较不同平台或可信的独立评测。评价账号只有一条记录、短时间集中发布、语言高度相似，可以降低你对这组评价的信任，但不能单凭这些特征宣称某条必假。
4. **核对可证实的事实。** 评价说“适配某型号”“可水洗”“容量是某数值”时，回到当前商品的制造商说明、完整标签或可核对规格。用户体验可以补充使用感，不能修改规格。
5. **给出三态结论。** “可参考”：多条与准确版本有关的具体经验，且没有关键矛盾；“有限参考”：版本、使用条件或来源不够清楚；“不采用”：混评、空泛星数或关键说法与可核对资料冲突。写下一两条真正影响你决定的观察，再继续其他购买检查或暂停。

### 成功条件

你能说出评价对应哪件商品、解决了哪个疑问、仍有哪些未知，以及你将采用、降低权重还是放弃这组评价。无需把所有评论读完；研究不是新的收藏爱好。

### 常见报错、停止与补救

- **几乎全是五星：** 不把高分自动判假，也不直接结算；换排序看近期与中低分内容，再找独立来源。
- **评论说的是另一版本：** 标为不采用，重新定位准确型号。不要把旧版的耐用性许给新版。
- **只有抱怨物流的评论：** 这能提示交易体验，不足以证明商品本身好坏；若你要判断卖家，另做卖家核验。
- **找不到可读的具体评价：** 停止继续滚动；用清晰的规格、独立测试或允许的现场查看完成决策，仍无依据就延后。
- **评价与标签或官方说明冲突：** 先核对是否同一版本；仍冲突则不要用评价覆盖明确限制。

### 假设、可及替代与现实副作用

不同地区、平台和语言的评价排序、购买验证标志会变化；“已购买”标签也不保证真实或适用于你。无法辨认星色、细字或视频声音时，用文本评价、放大或屏幕阅读、文字版独立测试；不强求任何单一平台。若商品需满足不能试错的明确安全限制，仅凭评价不足以放行。

副作用是少一些“大家都说好”的确定感，多一条可以复述的理由。

## English

Load this Skill when you have an ordinary household product in mind and want online reviews to inform the purchase. Your result is what the reviews can and cannot support. You are not proving which individual review is fake, and reviews do not replace product specifications, labels, or recall checks. Medical, wellness, and other high-risk choices are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the exact product name, model or version, and one question you need answered, such as whether a lid is comfortable to open repeatedly. Give yourself an 8–15 minute reading limit. A page that offers only stars, or requires an account you do not want, is not a dependency you must accept.

### Execution

1. **Fix the product identity.** Check that reviews concern the same model, size, and version you may buy. Mixed variants do not automatically describe yours. If photos, names, or features disagree with the listing, mark the set as possibly mixed.
2. **Read for your question.** Move past the average star rating. Look for recent, specific reports that state the use conditions, time in use, and outcome you care about. Read some middle and lower ratings too, separating a recurring fault from a single preference.
3. **Check where the evidence came from.** Note disclosed gifts, payment, or other incentives. Compare another source or a credible independent test. A one-review account, a sudden burst, or repeated wording can reduce confidence in a group of reviews; none lets you declare a particular review fake with certainty.
4. **Verify checkable claims.** If a review says a product fits a model, can be washed, or has a stated capacity, compare that with the current manufacturer's instructions, full label, or verifiable specification. Experience may describe use; it cannot rewrite the specification.
5. **Give the reviews one of three statuses.** **Useful:** several concrete reports concern the exact version without a critical conflict. **Limited:** version, conditions, or source remain unclear. **Do not use:** the set is mixed, offers only vague stars, or conflicts with checkable product information. Write one or two observations that affect your choice, then continue the other purchase checks or pause.

### Success

You can say which product the reviews describe, which question they helped answer, what remains unknown, and whether you will use, discount, or set aside the reviews. You do not need to read every comment; research is not a new collecting hobby.

### Common errors, stop points, and recovery

- **Almost every rating is five stars:** Do not automatically call them fake or immediately check out. Sort by recent and middle/lower ratings, then look for an independent source.
- **The comments describe another version:** Set them aside and find the exact model. The old model's durability does not transfer by name alone.
- **Most complaints concern delivery:** They may describe a transaction but not the product's quality. Check the seller separately if that is your question.
- **No readable, specific experience is available:** Stop scrolling. Use clear specifications, independent testing, or an accessible in-person inspection; if evidence is still missing, wait.
- **A review contradicts a label or maker instruction:** Check that both discuss the same version. If the conflict remains, do not use the review to override a stated restriction.

### Assumptions, accessible alternatives, and side effects

Review sorting and purchase badges vary by region, platform, and language. A “verified purchase” badge does not guarantee truth or relevance to you. If stars, small text, or video sound are inaccessible, use text reviews, magnification or a screen reader, or a written independent test; no single platform is required. Reviews alone cannot clear a non-negotiable safety restriction.

The side effect is less certainty from “everyone likes it” and one reason you can explain.
