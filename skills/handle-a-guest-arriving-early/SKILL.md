## 中文

当普通来客已在约定时间前到门口、楼下或附近，而你的准备还没完成或你还没到家时，加载这份 Skill。你要给出**此刻实际可行**的进入、短等、改时间地点或结束安排。对方提前到不自动取得进入私人空间的许可；你也不需要把没准备好的家瞬间变成样板间。本篇不处理紧急危险、陌生人核身或复杂门禁权限。

### 准备与输入

先确认确实是约定的来客、对方在什么位置、原定开始时间、你现在在哪里，以及门口到活动区是否已经可用。估出诚实的准备或到达时间；不知道时说“不确定”，别用“马上”作为计时单位。检查对方是否方便在当前位置等，是否已有你知道的行动、天气或感官限制；不要假设台阶、站立、室外等候或附近店铺都适合他。

### 执行

1. **尽快回应并说清状态。** 简短确认“我知道你到了；原约 15:00，我还需要约 10 分钟把入口和座位准备好”或“我还在路上，预计 15:05 到”。不要因为尴尬而消失，也不要把真实情况解释成客人的错。
2. **只提出你真的能给的选择。** 入口和可用区域已经安全且你愿意开放时，可以邀请对方先到那一处，并说明其余区域还不可用。若不能进入，可请对方选择是否在适合他的地方短等、稍晚再来、换一个双方可行的位置，或结束这次来访。不要未经确认就要求对方在门外、雨里或陌生地点等候。
3. **问清选择并复述新约定。** 例如“你愿意在楼下有座位的大厅等到 15:05，还是今天改到附近你方便的地方？”对方不愿等或有别的安排时，接受并确认改期/取消；关系不需要用强迫等候来修复。
4. **用等待时间只修最低入口。** 若对方同意短等，优先让门能开、路线无明显障碍、活动位置可坐，并保护私人空间。食物或设备若还不能安全提供，就撤回那项承诺，不抢时间用不确定的食品或故障设备。全屋清洁仍不是紧急后台任务。
5. **按真实进度再确认。** 到约定的新时间仍未准备好时及时再说一次，给出新的可选安排；对方没有回复时不要推断同意继续等。能接待时明确告知从哪里进入、哪些区域可用。若你仍不在家，不承诺他人会替你开门。

### 完成条件

来客知道你现在能提供什么、实际等多久或改成什么安排，并可选择接受或离开；你的私人及安全边界保持清楚。若联系暂时未接通，你已通过双方可用渠道发出真实状态和可选下一步，未擅自替对方决定等待。完成不要求客人必须理解、原谅或留下。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“再等一分钟”说了三次：** 停止滚动延期，给一个你能实现的时间或直接提出改期。
- **因内疚让来客进入未开放区域：** 温和说明可用位置与需要关闭的空间；你可以提供座位，也可以诚实说明暂不能进屋。
- **对方不方便等：** 立即确认可行的新时间/地点，或结束这次安排，不要求说明私人原因。
- **你还在路上且无法保证到达时间：** 说出不确定，建议对方自行选择是否回程或改约；不要传达没有证据的精确 ETA。
- **提前到达还带来额外人员：** 先核对你能否接待并取得同住者必要同意；人数变化另按相应协调流程处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 假设双方原本同意一次普通来访，且有能联系的渠道。楼宇门禁、居住空间、文化礼貌和等候地点没有统一默认值；用你实际能控制且对方接受的方式安排。听觉、视觉、站立或网络条件不方便时，可以电话、文字、门口当面或双方可用的其他方式确认。需要立即安全帮助的情况应按当地实际应急安排处理，不靠这份普通待客流程解决。

副作用是原计划可能晚十分钟开始，或者改成另一日。真实变更比同时假装准时与准备完成更省力。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary invited guest reaches your door, building, or nearby area before the agreed time and you are not ready or not home yet. Give an **actually workable** option for entry, a short wait, a changed time/place, or ending the visit. Early arrival does not automatically grant access to private space, and you need not turn an unfinished home into a showroom instantly. This does not handle immediate danger, identifying an unknown visitor, or complex access-control rights.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm that this is the expected guest, where they are, the original start time, where you are, and whether the entrance and activity area can be used now. Estimate an honest time to be ready or arrive; say “uncertain” if you cannot. “Almost” is not a time unit. Check whether the guest can and wants to wait where they are, including any mobility, weather, or sensory condition you already know. Do not assume steps, standing, outdoor waiting, or a nearby shop work for them.

### Execution

1. **Respond promptly with the real state.** Say briefly, “I know you're here; we agreed on 3 pm and I need about ten minutes to make the entrance and seat usable,” or “I'm still travelling and expect to arrive at 3:05.” Do not disappear from embarrassment or make your situation the guest's fault.
2. **Offer only options you can provide.** If the entrance and one area are safe and you choose to open them, invite the guest there and say which other areas are not available yet. Otherwise ask whether they prefer to wait briefly in a place that works for them, return later, meet at another mutually workable location, or end the visit. Do not assign them to wait outside, in rain, or somewhere unfamiliar without agreement.
3. **Ask for a choice and repeat the revised plan.** For example, “Would you prefer to wait in the seated lobby until 3:05, or meet somewhere nearby that works for you?” If they cannot or do not want to wait, accept that and confirm a new date or cancellation. A relationship does not need compulsory waiting as a repair tool.
4. **Use wait time for the minimum entrance only.** If the guest agrees to wait, make the door openable, remove clear route obstacles, provide a usable activity spot, and protect private rooms. Withdraw a promised food or equipment item that cannot yet be provided safely; do not use uncertain food or faulty equipment to make up time. A full-house clean is still not an emergency background task.
5. **Update when reality changes.** If the revised time arrives and you are still not ready, say so promptly and offer real options again. No reply does not mean agreement to keep waiting. When ready, explain how to enter and which area is usable. If you are still away, do not promise that someone else will open the door.

### Success

The guest knows what you can offer now, how long the actual wait is or what changed, and can accept or leave. Your private and safety boundaries remain clear. If you cannot reach them yet, you sent a true status and possible next action through a channel both of you can use without deciding for them that they will wait. Completion does not require praise, forgiveness, or staying.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“One more minute” has been repeated three times:** Stop rolling the deadline. Give a time you can meet or offer to reschedule.
- **Guilt makes you open a private area:** State which place is usable and which stays closed. You may offer a seat or honestly say entry is not yet possible.
- **The guest cannot wait:** Confirm a workable new time/place or close this visit. Do not demand a private reason.
- **You are travelling and cannot predict arrival:** State the uncertainty and let the guest decide whether to leave or reschedule. Do not issue an invented precise ETA.
- **Early arrival includes extra people:** Check capacity and any needed co-resident consent first. Handle changed headcount in its own coordination flow.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This assumes both people agreed to an ordinary visit and can use some contact channel. Building entry, household space, etiquette, and waiting places have no universal default; use only arrangements you control and the guest accepts. If hearing, vision, standing, or network access is difficult, confirm by call, text, at the door, or another shared channel. Immediate safety needs follow actual local emergency arrangements rather than this ordinary hosting flow.

The visit may start ten minutes later or on another day. A real change is easier than pretending both punctuality and readiness happened.
