## 中文

当一批**已停止续投的普通庭院植物性堆肥**经历较长分解，你需要判断能否静置、筛出看似成熟的材料时，加载这份 Skill。结果是完成真实外观/气味/未分解物检查、足够静置并将可用样与未熟物分开，或继续等待。只准备供获准非食用观赏植物土壤改良的材料；绝不以时间、颜色或温度猜测“已消毒”或适合直接接触食物。

### 准备与输入

确认这批没有宠物粪、肉奶油脂、病株、农药不明料或塑料；若投料史无法确认，不把筛出物用于植物。备合用手套、筛具与接料容器，选通风稳固站位；翻筛粉尘可能刺激，按自身情况采用防护或可信协助，别强行搬重桶。查 EPA/RHS 的成熟与静置建议，不把某月份数当所有大小气候的统一答案。

### 执行

1. **把这批从新投料中分离。** 停止向待验批次加新厨余，或将较老部分移到合适独立静置区，标起点。否则新旧混在一起，无法判一批何时完成。
2. **看是否具备静置前条件。** 翻动后若仍显著自热、能认出食物边角或闻到腐臭，继续分解和常规维护；若这些现象基本消退，按 EPA 的静置建议留充分时间。只凭上表层颜色深，不够。
3. **静置后小量筛查。** 安全取少量看是否暗色、疏松易碎、有土/林地气味，且大部分原料不可辨；若仍潮黏、臭或能看见果皮，退回继续分解。筛出较粗木块/果核等可合格回堆，塑料贴纸等杂质按当地规则另处置。
4. **分装并标用途限制。** 将通过外观和静置检查的材料放在获准干净容器，标来源批次与“供非食用观赏植物土壤改良，非经卫生验证”；未成熟部分回活跃堆。具体施用前按植物/场地需要另决定，不把此筛查当食品安全审批。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一批旧堆有可回查的停投/静置记录、筛过的看似成熟部分与未熟/杂质分开，或者明确继续等待。把正在升温、带果皮的整桶泥称熟肥，不算完成。

- **仍看见食物或持续腐臭：** 不筛作成品，继续分解并查水分/通气。
- **新旧料总混在一起：** 先分批或停止追加，重新建立可静置批次。
- **筛时粉尘多或太重：** 停止扬尘/搬运，改少量操作或可信协助。
- **出现塑料、贴纸或不合格投料史：** 杂质移除，但无法追溯高风险原料时不称可用肥。
- **不确定是否已熟：** 延长静置并复查，不用通用“几个月”掩盖未知。

### 假设与边界

EPA 给出停止升温、无可见食物、静置与暗色疏松土味等多项家庭判断，RHS 也强调等待时间跨度大。这些仅支持普通园艺成熟度判断，不能保证病原体、杂草种子或污染物已被消除。本篇把用途收窄到非食用观赏植物，仍需场地许可。

## English

Load this Skill when an **older ordinary plant-only backyard compost batch has stopped receiving new feed** and you need to decide whether to cure and screen apparently finished material. Finish with real appearance/odor/unbroken-material checks, enough curing, and ready-looking material separated from unfinished parts, or keep waiting. Prepare only a potential soil amendment for permitted nonfood ornamentals. Never infer “sanitized” or food-safe from time, colour, or heat alone.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the feed history contains no pet waste, meat, dairy, fats, diseased plants, uncertain pesticides, or plastic. If history is unknown, do not use the screened material on plants. Have gloves, a suitable screen and receiving container, and an airy stable work spot. Screening dust can irritate; use protection or trusted help as appropriate rather than hauling a heavy bin alone. Check EPA and RHS curing guidance without treating one number of months as universal across sizes and climates.

### Execution

1. **Separate this batch from new feed.** Stop adding scraps to the candidate batch, or move an older portion to a suitable separate curing spot and mark its start. Mixing fresh and old constantly prevents a batch maturity decision.
2. **Look for a reason to begin curing.** If the pile still heats noticeably after mixing, food pieces remain visible, or it smells rotten, keep decomposing and maintaining it. If those signs have mostly ceased, allow a sufficient curing period under EPA guidance. A dark top surface alone is not enough.
3. **Screen a small amount after curing.** Check for dark, loose, crumbly material with an earthy woodland smell and most ingredients no longer recognisable. Return sticky, foul, or visibly peely material to processing. Screen coarse eligible twigs or pits back into an active pile, and route plastic or stickers under local rules.
4. **Containerize with limits.** Put the screened apparently mature portion in a permitted clean container, labelled with batch and “nonfood ornamental soil amendment candidate, not hygiene-certified.” Return immature parts to the active pile. Decide actual application separately under plant and site needs, not as a food-safety approval here.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

An old batch has traceable no-feed and curing history, screened apparently mature material separated from unfinished and foreign material, or a clear decision to wait longer. A heating, peely whole bin is not finished compost.

- **Food scraps remain or odor persists:** Do not screen as finished; continue decomposition and check moisture and air.
- **Fresh and old materials stay mixed:** Separate batches or stop additions, then restart a real curing clock.
- **Dust is high or lifting too heavy:** Stop dust-raising work, use small portions or trusted help.
- **Plastic, stickers, or ineligible feed history appears:** Remove debris, but do not call material usable when high-risk inputs cannot be traced.
- **Maturity is uncertain:** Cure longer and recheck instead of hiding uncertainty behind “several months.”

### Assumptions and limits

EPA supplies several household maturity signs—no renewed heat, no visible food, curing, dark crumbly earthy material—and RHS notes broad timing. These support ordinary horticultural maturity assessment only, not proof that pathogens, weed seeds, or pollutants are gone. Use is narrowed to nonfood ornamentals and still requires site permission.
