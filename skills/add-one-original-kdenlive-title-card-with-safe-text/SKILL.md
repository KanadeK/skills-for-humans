## 中文

你有一段自有短片，需要在开头说明主题而不靠口头解释时使用本篇。成果是一张独立的原创标题卡，文字在安全区内可读、停留足够但不过长，卡片结束后第一镜头完整出现。它不是借用他人的品牌模板或用装饰动画抢掉主体。

### 准备与输入

保存工程，写短标题与可选日期/场合，确认不含私人资料或虚假成果声称。检查项目画幅与标题编辑器安全区。为标题卡选一段独立空位或简单底色，不让透明文字直接叠在第一镜头而误作下三分之一字幕。

### 执行

1. 在 Project Bin 新建 Title Clip，输入原创标题并保持层级简洁。
2. 把字放入安全区，配简单可读底色/背景，核屏幕边缘不截字。
3. 将卡片排在第一镜头前，设置有意停留时长，预览从卡到镜头的切换。
4. 核声音没有意外静音/抢入，保存重开并在项目监视器再看一次。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

原创标题可读、卡片时长合理、第一镜头和声音完整。

- **标题被边缘截：** 移回安全区。
- **卡片拖太长：** 缩短到能读完的时长。
- **透明叠在镜头上：** 补背景或重新放独立轨位。

### 假设与边界

只做一张普通原创标题卡，不保证商业字体/模板授权或公开发布。你负责文字和画面真实。

## English

Use this Skill when an owned short video needs one opening card naming its topic without spoken setup. Finish with a separate original title card whose words sit in a safe readable area for a bounded duration and first shot remains intact afterward. Do not borrow another brand template or let decorative motion overshadow content.

### Preparation and inputs

Save project, draft short title and optional date/context without private data or unsupported claims. Check frame and title-editor safe zones. Give card its own space or plain background; a transparent title atop first shot would be a lower-third, not a separate card.

### Execution

1. Create a Title Clip in Project Bin with original concise title.
2. Place text in safe area against a simple readable background and check no edge clipping.
3. Place card before first shot at intended duration and preview transition into footage.
4. Check audio is not unintentionally muted or early, save/reopen and preview again.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Original title reads, card duration fits purpose, and first shot/audio remain complete.

- **Text clipped:** Move into safe zone.
- **Card too long:** Shorten to readable duration.
- **Transparent overlay:** Add background or reposition as card.

### Assumptions and limits

This creates one ordinary original title card, not commercial font/template clearance or publishing. You own wording and truth.
