## 中文

当你发现一批普通衣物**洗完后仍湿着留在关着的洗衣机、烘干机或密闭篮里**，却不确定能否直接晾/烘时，用这份 Skill 根据可核实的时间、存放方式、衣物与机器状态作决定。单凭“闻起来还好”不能证明存放条件没问题；有异味则是需要进一步处理的信号。它不提供人人适用的“隔夜仍安全”小时数，也不声称重洗能处理危险污染或明显霉损。

### 准备与输入

记录上次程序何时结束、湿衣何时被发现、容器是否一直关闭、有没有停电/进水异常或污水接触、衣物标签的洗涤/干燥限制，以及能否立即安排真实晾干位置。看衣物与机器是否有新气味、可见斑点、滑腻残留或积水，但正常外观和气味只是一部分线索。体液、洪水污水、危险化学品、可见大片霉变或不明污染退出普通家洗，按当地/专业指导处理。

### 执行

1. **先安全取出并展开。** 按本机说明确保滚筒停稳，取出湿衣，分开摊在干净可通风的位置；不要立刻塞进烘干机或衣柜，也不让一团湿衣继续压在密闭篮里。若机器漏水、电气异常或不能安全开门，停止并联系管理者/专业维修。
2. **重建滞留史。** 若确定程序刚结束、取出过程符合本机/衣物正常交接且无新异常，按衣物标签立即进入正常晾晒/烘干并检查干透。若何时洗完不清楚、已长时间密闭潮湿、出现异味或新斑，**不要用正常气味反推安全**；先选择标签允许的重新洗涤或停止寻求专业判断，而不直接热烘掩盖问题。
3. **需要重洗时按原限制重做。** 核对衣物标签、洗涤剂产品和准确机器程序，再做一轮适用的完整洗涤/漂洗；不能因滞留就自动加倍洗剂、升高温度或混合漂白剂与其他清洁品。特殊材料、标签不可读或无安全可用设备时先停。
4. **重洗后及时验收与晾干。** 查看新斑、残留和气味是否改善；仍异常则停止反复家洗，咨询制造商/专业护理。结果可接受的衣物按标签及时摊开自然晾或使用许可的烘干程序，逐件确认真正干透再收纳。
5. **分开核查机器。** 衣物取出后若洗衣机本身仍有异味或残留，只按该型号说明做使用者可执行的空机清洁/通风；清洁程序若要求空桶，不能夹带衣物。警报、漏水、异常声音或问题持续时停用并联系管理者/专业服务，不拆机、不把另一种清洁剂倒进去“中和”。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

这批湿衣有基于实际滞留史的“及时干燥 / 标签允许的重洗后干燥 / 停止求助”结果；衣物没继续密闭堆湿，机器异常单独记录。完成并不等于证明衣物无菌。

- **只记得“可能是昨天洗的”：** 时间史不足，选择相容重洗或专业判断，不凭闻起来正常直接入柜。
- **重洗后仍有斑或明显异味：** 停止反复加洗剂和热烘，保留记录并求专业建议。
- **没有晾晒空间：** 先安排符合标签的晾处或适用烘干机；不启动重洗后又让它再次滞留。
- **机器也有异味：** 衣物和机器分开处理，空机清洁只按本机说明；问题持续就停止使用。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

仅处理普通衣物的潮湿滞留，不做霉菌诊断、健康认证或洗衣机维修。机器品牌、通风功能、衣物类型与环境湿度不同；厂商文章的“几小时”“隔夜”不是跨机型保证。看不到显示或记不清时间时保留不确定，使用可读程序日志、照片或可信同住者信息；仍无法核实时选更保守的重洗/专业路径。额外一轮耗水耗电，还必须有实际干燥去向。

## English

Use this Skill when ordinary washed clothes **remain damp in a closed washer, dryer, or basket after the cycle** and you cannot simply assume they are ready to dry or wear. Decide from a verifiable timeline, holding conditions, garment state, and machine state. “It smells fine” cannot prove that holding was safe; a new odour is a reason to investigate. There is no universal “overnight is fine” number here, and rewashing does not solve hazardous contamination or obvious mold damage.

### Preparation and inputs

Record when the last cycle ended, when you found the clothes, whether the container stayed closed, any power or water fault or contact with dirty water, the garment washing and drying limits, and whether a real drying place is available now. Look for changed odour, visible spots, slippery residue, or standing water in clothes and machine. Normal smell and appearance are only partial clues. Bodily fluids, floodwater, hazardous chemicals, extensive visible mold, or unknown contamination leave ordinary home laundry for local or professional advice.

### Execution

1. **Remove and spread safely.** Follow this machine's instructions and wait until the drum stops. Put damp items separately on a clean ventilated surface. Do not immediately pack them into a dryer or cupboard, or leave a damp bundle in a closed basket. Stop and contact a manager or qualified service for leaking, electrical warning, or a door you cannot open safely.
2. **Reconstruct the delay.** If the cycle has verifiably just ended, handoff was normal for the machine and garments, and no new issue appears, move promptly to label-compatible air or tumble drying and check full dryness. If finish time is unknown, the load stayed closed and damp for a prolonged time, or new odour or spots appear, **do not infer safety from a normal smell**. Choose a label-compatible rewash or stop for professional judgement rather than heat drying to hide the problem.
3. **Rewash under the original limits when needed.** Check garments, detergent product, and exact washer cycle, then run a compatible complete wash and rinse. A delay does not allow automatic double detergent, higher temperature, or mixing bleach with other cleaners. Stop for special materials, unreadable limits, or no safe working machine.
4. **Inspect and dry promptly after rewashing.** Check whether new spots, residue, and odour improved. If still abnormal, stop repeated home washing and ask the maker or professional cleaner. Give acceptable items their label-compatible air-dry or tumble route promptly, then check each is truly dry before storage.
5. **Check the machine separately.** If the empty washer itself retains odour or residue, use only the model's user-accessible empty-machine cleaning and ventilation instructions. A clean-washer cycle marked empty must contain no garments. Stop use and ask the manager or qualified service for alerts, leaks, abnormal noise, or a persistent issue. Do not dismantle it or add another cleaner to “neutralize” it.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

This damp load has an outcome based on actual holding evidence: prompt drying, compatible rewash followed by drying, or a stop for help. It is no longer closed up wet, and a machine problem is recorded separately. Completion does not certify sterile clothing.

- **You only remember “perhaps I washed it yesterday”:** The timeline is insufficient. Choose a compatible rewash or professional advice; a normal smell does not send it to the cupboard.
- **Spots or strong odour remain after rewashing:** Stop cycling detergent and heat, keep the record, and seek professional advice.
- **No drying space exists:** Arrange a label-compatible rack or dryer before rewashing so it will not be delayed again.
- **The washer also smells:** Treat clothing and machine separately. Run an empty-machine care route only under its manual; stop use if the problem persists.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary damp laundry, not mold diagnosis, health certification, or washer repair. Models, fresh-hold features, garment types, and humidity differ; a manufacturer's “few hours” or “overnight” is not a cross-model guarantee. When a display is inaccessible or timing forgotten, use a readable cycle log, photos, or reliable housemate information. Keep uncertainty if it remains, and choose a cautious rewash or professional route. Another cycle uses water and energy and still needs a real drying destination.
