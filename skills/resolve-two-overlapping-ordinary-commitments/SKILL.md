## 中文

当你发现自己对**两个尚未开始的普通安排**都作过或可能作过承诺，时间却重叠到无法同时参加时，用这份 Skill 核事实、选择能守住的部分并及时告知受影响的人。这里不处理已经错过的预约（H268），也不决定合同、劳动、人事或医疗法律后果；取消或改期规则按各安排真实政策核对。

### 准备与输入

找两边的原邀请、自己的答复及最新更新。写日期、当地时区、开始/结束时间、地点/线上入口及必要转场时间；不同日历视图可能只显示部分日历或把“暂定”当成忙/空，不能凭一张截图断言已冲突或没冲突。确认谁在等你、是否已经替你准备资源，以及自己能否参与其中一段；不要未经对方同意把“只来一半”当承诺已兑现。

### 执行

1. **先核冲突是否真实。** 并排看原通知与最新答复，尤其日期、时区、实际持续时间与转场。只要其中一个是未接受的邀请、已取消或时间已变，先更正自己的记录；不为不存在的冲突打扰别人。
2. **分清哪些已承诺。** 标“已确认/仅邀请/暂定/待对方答复”，再看哪一项有明确时间后果、别人依赖、可安全调整空间。费用、取消窗口或正式规则不靠常识猜；有实际条款就查。不要把“我比较喜欢”伪装成客观优先级。
3. **选一个可执行方案。** 可能是守住一项并请求另一项改期、在允许时把一项转给有权接手的人、或诚实说明两边都需重新协商。先看自己真能做到哪项；若要变更别人安排，只把它提出为请求，不替对方确认。
4. **尽早通知被影响的一方。** 沿原约定渠道直说：“我发现与已确认的另一安排冲突，原定 X 我无法按原样到场。我能做 Y，或请问能否改到 Z？目前仍待你确认。” 必要时也告知另一方可能影响；不把一封未送达消息当通知，更不等对方来问才承认双订。
5. **按真实回应更新两边状态。** 记录谁确认了新方案、谁仍待答，必要时更新自己的日历并核时间地点。若时点迫近却没人回复，遵守可执行且已确认的承诺，同时继续通过许可渠道说明另一项无法按原约参加；不要在两个日历项上都标“会参加”。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你有一份经原通知核实的可执行安排，已通过原渠道向受影响的人发出准确请求或无法履约通知，答复和待确认状态分别记录。对方同意改期不是你单方面完成的条件；在未回复前，原约状态不能被悄悄改写。

- **原来只是“可能参加”：** 先核 RSVP 状态，必要时清楚拒绝或确认；不把暂定当已确定的冲突。
- **时区或转场算错：** 回原消息核当地时间和实际移动时间，修正后再发变更。
- **对方不接受你的替代：** 如实记录，继续协商或承担不能到场的普通后果，不编政策或保证免责。
- **消息已写但未发/未送达：** 用原正式渠道核发送状态，必要时采用已约定备选方式。
- **含付费、受管或重要专业后果：** 以实际服务/组织政策为准，必要时找负责人员；本篇不替其作法律或财务判断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

两项约定均为普通非紧急事项。纸、可访问日历或语音记录都能核时间和状态；读屏者可逐项朗读原邀请，不依赖颜色标记。日历冲突提示只是线索，真正的承诺以参与者答复和原安排为准。你可能要承认一个不便事实，但比同时默认两边都能去更便于他人调整。

## English

Load this Skill when you discover **two ordinary plans that have not started** both have, or may have, your commitment but overlap so you cannot attend both. Check facts, decide what you can actually keep, and tell affected people promptly. A missed appointment after its time belongs to H268. This does not decide contract, employment, legal, financial, or medical consequences; use each plan's real change or cancellation rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Find both original invitations, your replies, and later updates. Note date, local time zone, start and end, place or online entry, and transfer time. Calendar views may show only some calendars or treat “Maybe” differently from confirmed busy time; one screenshot cannot prove a clash or its absence. Check who is waiting or preparing resources for you and whether partial attendance is possible. Do not assume “I will come for half” fulfils a promise without agreement.

### Execution

1. **Verify a real overlap.** Compare original notices with latest replies, especially date, time zone, duration, and travel. If an item was never accepted, was cancelled, or moved, correct your own record first rather than troubling people with a false conflict.
2. **Separate committed from tentative.** Mark confirmed, invitation only, tentative, or awaiting reply. Consider actual time consequences, others' dependencies, and safe room to adjust. Check real fee or cancellation terms instead of guessing. Do not disguise personal preference as an objective priority rule.
3. **Choose one feasible proposal.** You might keep one plan and ask to move another, pass one task to someone authorised when allowed, or honestly say both need discussion. Identify what you can actually do. A change to someone else's plan is a request, not your unilateral confirmation.
4. **Tell affected people early.** Through the original agreed route, say, “I found a conflict with another confirmed plan, so I cannot attend X as arranged. I can do Y, or could we move to Z? This still needs your confirmation.” Inform the other side too if affected. Text left unsent is not notice; do not wait for others to discover your double booking.
5. **Update both states from real replies.** Record who confirmed a new plan and who has not, then correct your calendar's actual time and place. If time approaches without a reply, honour a feasible confirmed commitment while continuing to state through allowed channels that the other cannot be met as planned. Do not mark attendance “Yes” on both.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You have a feasible plan checked against original notices, sent affected people an accurate request or inability notice through the agreed route, and recorded replies or pending states separately. Their agreement to a new time is not yours to assert. Silence does not secretly rewrite the original plan.

- **One event was only “Maybe”:** Check RSVP first, then decline or confirm clearly if needed rather than calling it a fixed clash.
- **A time zone or transfer was misread:** Recheck original local times and real travel before sending an update.
- **The other person rejects your proposal:** Record the answer and continue an honest discussion or accept ordinary consequences. Do not invent a policy or immunity.
- **Your note is drafted but unsent:** Verify sending in the original channel and use an agreed fallback if needed.
- **Fees, managed rules, or serious specialist effects apply:** Follow the actual service or organisation rules and ask responsible staff. This Skill cannot decide legal or financial effects.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Both commitments are ordinary and nonurgent. Paper, an accessible calendar, or audio notes can hold times and states; screen reading can inspect original invitations without colour coding. A calendar conflict alert is evidence to check, while actual commitments come from replies and agreements. Admitting an inconvenient clash gives others a chance to adjust.
