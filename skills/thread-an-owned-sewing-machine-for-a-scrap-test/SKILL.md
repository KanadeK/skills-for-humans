## 中文

当你的**普通家用缝纫机说明书可查、针线和梭芯兼容**，需要为一块废布做准备时，加载这份 Skill。结果是上线按本机张力/挑线杆路径、下线按本机梭芯方向装妥，压脚后方有可控制的线尾；不是第一道缝。顶装与前装梭芯方向可能相反，不能照另一台机器的视频硬套。

### 准备与输入

确认针完好且安装正确、线轴/梭芯型号匹配，本机没有破线、异响或针板损伤。把脚离开踏板，依手册先升针与压脚以便上线进入张力路径，再按本机要求关电/断电后手动穿针；带自动穿线功能的型号以其说明为准。手指不放针下，钩子/小盖不能强撬，模型手册缺失时停并找厂商。

### 执行

1. **按编号走上线。** 线轴按本机方向放稳，依说明逐个经过导线、张力槽、挑线杆、针杆导线和针眼；压脚通常在穿线时抬起，确保线进张力盘。用手轻牵确认没有绕到线轴盖或机壳缝里，不凭“看着差不多”跳一步。
2. **按本机类型装下线。** 确认绕线器已退位，机器处安全静止状态；打开用户可开的梭芯盖，把本机兼容梭芯按该型号图的转向放入顶装槽或前装梭壳，线走规定沟槽/张力簧。盖好可用盖，梭芯不翘起；不要把另一机型“顺时针/逆时针”当统一规定。
3. **按本机方式引出下线并摆线尾。** 若手册要求，握住上线轻转手轮按规定方向使针下上来，轻牵出下线环；把两线尾置于压脚下方朝后，足够在开始废布试缝时控制，不拉到线断。自动机型的引线法仍由手册决定。
4. **静态检查待试状态。** 针处安全位置，压脚/盖装妥，梭芯可按规定轻转，线没有钩在运动件之外；记上线/下线种类和本机默认试缝设置。真正动针前仍要放废布、放下压脚并遵本机安全说明。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

上线和下线均按**本机说明**在位、线尾可控、用户盖闭合，机器静止待废布试缝。只穿了针眼却跳过挑线杆或梭芯沟槽，不算完成。

- **上线牵拉没有正常阻力/太松：** 检查压脚升降与张力槽，关电后依图重穿，不先乱调梭芯螺丝。
- **梭芯方向/尺寸拿不准：** 取出查本机型号和梭芯类别，不能盲试。
- **线尾钩住、针弯或机盖合不上：** 停止动针、按手册纠正或交维修者。
- **自动穿线器或面板操作不熟：** 使用本机手册/厂商支持，不用通用机械步骤硬触发。
- **手眼不便：** 高亮针区、断电后的可信协助穿线和大图说明有用；由你核最终线与盖状态。

### 假设与边界

常见机型需要压脚抬起以进入上线张力系统，但某些电子机有独特顺序，因此手册是唯一具体按钮/路径来源。这个 Skill 不维修针杆或调厂设梭芯张力。

## English

Load this Skill when your **ordinary home machine has its exact manual and compatible needle, thread, and bobbin** and you want it ready for scrap-fabric sewing. Finish with upper thread through this machine's tension/take-up path, lower thread installed in this model's direction, and controllable tails behind the presser foot, without yet making a seam. Top-drop and front-load bobbin directions may differ; another model's video cannot control yours.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm a sound correctly fitted needle, compatible spool/bobbin, and no broken cord, strange noise, or plate damage. Keep foot off pedal. Under the manual raise needle and presser foot for upper threading; switch off or unplug for hand needle threading as this model directs. Automatic threaders follow their own instructions. Keep fingers out from under needle, do not pry covers, and stop for missing manual or model identity.

### Execution

1. **Route upper thread in numbered order.** Seat the spool as this model directs and follow each guide, tension channel, take-up lever, needle-bar guide, and needle eye. The presser foot is commonly raised while routing thread into the tension discs. Pull lightly to check no snag behind spool cap or casing, not skipping a step because the path looks close.
2. **Install lower bobbin by this model type.** Confirm winder is disengaged and machine safely still. Open only the user-accessible cover. Place the exact-compatible wound bobbin into this model's top slot or front case in its pictured rotation and through its prescribed slot or tension spring. Close the cover with bobbin seated; do not universalise another model's clockwise or anticlockwise rule.
3. **Raise lower thread and arrange tails as directed.** If the manual calls for it, hold upper thread and turn the handwheel in the approved direction to lower and raise the needle, drawing out the lower loop gently. Put both tails beneath the foot toward the back so they can be controlled on scrap. Do not yank until thread breaks. An automatic model still follows its own manual.
4. **Static ready check.** Needle is safely placed, foot and covers sound, bobbin moves as directed, and thread is free of stray moving parts. Record upper/lower thread and model default test setting. Before motion, place scrap fabric, lower the foot, and follow machine safety directions.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Upper and lower thread follow the **exact model manual**, tails are controllable, user cover is shut, and the machine is still ready for a scrap test. Thread through the needle eye while skipping take-up lever or bobbin slot is unfinished.

- **Upper thread has no normal resistance or is very loose:** Check foot position and tension path, then power down and rethread by diagram before touching bobbin screws.
- **Bobbin orientation or class is unclear:** Remove it and check model and bobbin type rather than trial by force.
- **Tails snag, needle bends, or cover will not close:** Stop sewing and correct under manual or use service.
- **Automatic threader or panel is unfamiliar:** Use the exact manual and maker support, not a generic mechanical sequence.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Bright needle-zone light, powered-off trusted threading help, and large diagrams can support your final path and cover check.

### Assumptions and limits

Many machines raise the presser foot to seat upper tension, but electronic models can have different order, so the manual owns every button and route. No needle-bar repair or factory bobbin-tension adjustment is taught.
