## 中文

你已经坐上普通公交，知道计划下车站，快到站时用这份 Skill **核对实际下一站、按该运营方方式请求停靠、在车停稳后下车**。结果是你和随身物品都在正确的安全停靠点；若车辆改道或目标站关闭，就在可停的站点停下并重新核对，不要求司机在非站点停车。路线与方向在上车前应已核实；本 Skill 不负责坐过站后的完整返回方案，也不提供交通执法或紧急事故指引。

### 准备与输入

记住目标站名/编号与前一站，查看本车屏幕、报站或运营方当前站序；如果没有可读/可听信息，向司机或熟悉该线路的工作人员礼貌确认，不凭窗外相似建筑猜。了解这家运营方如何请求停车：按钮、拉绳或其他正式方式。随身包放在可拿到且不挡通道的位置；行动、听觉或视觉不便时，按运营方可及服务申请帮助，不临到开门才冒险冲向出口。

### 执行

1. **用前一站做预警。** 当屏幕/报站/可靠站序显示目标站的前一站，核对下一站确实是目标。若显示支线终点、改道或跳站，与原计划冲突，先问司机或查官方实时信息；不自行要求在路中央开门。
2. **及时发出停车请求。** 按该车的有效按钮/拉绳等方式，在运营方要求的距离或前一站之后及时示意。确认车内有“停车”反馈或司机已听到；没有反馈时按该车指示再确认一次，不反复乱按设备。
3. **等车停稳再移动。** 按车内/司机指示选择出口，拿好个人物品并扶稳，给需要的人让出空间。不要为赶一个站在车仍行驶时冲到车门。开门后观察落脚处、行人和骑行者，再安全下车；若出口被阻、站台不安全或无法确认位置，告诉司机并按其合规方式处理。
4. **确认完成本段。** 在站牌、站名或官方信息中核对实际下车站，按该运营方规则完成可能需要的下车刷卡/验票动作。若目标站被跳过或你已坐过站，先在下一处可安全停靠的站下车，进入重新规划；不要催司机违法停车或直接横穿车流。

### 成功条件

你在计划或经运营方确认的替代停靠站下车，随身物品齐全，未在车辆行驶中或非安全站点强行下车；需要的该地票务结束动作按实际规则完成。若目标站因改道不可达，安全停靠并明确自己所在站是本次合格的暂停结果。

### 常见报错与补救

- **听不清/看不见报站：** 对照前一站和运营方站序，能安全询问时请司机或可信同行确认；无法确认就先在可辨站点安全下车重查。
- **按钮已按但车没停：** 不靠近行驶中的门；在下一合规站安全下车，记录实际站并重规划。
- **目标站临时关闭：** 听从车内和官方公告，选择开放停靠站，不让旧截图决定危险下车地点。
- **下车才发现包还在车上：** 不追赶车辆或冲进车道，记下线路、方向、车次/时间和失物，按运营方失物渠道处理。
- **当地要求下车刷卡但忘记：** 查该运营方实际补救/票务规则，不套用别城罚费或免费换乘说法。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理正常公交班次的普通下车。停车请求、报站、车门、刷卡、失物和可及协助因运营方不同；伦敦与温哥华的指南只是实例。无智能手机可用车内播报、屏幕、纸质站序或运营方人员；无法安全站立时提前请求帮助并等车停稳。离车后如何走到目的地或下一站另作判断。

## English

Use this Skill once you are on an ordinary bus, know the intended exit stop, and are approaching it. **Check the actual next stop, request a stop by this operator's method, and leave only after the vehicle stops.** The result is you and your belongings at a safe planned stop. If a diversion closes it, alight at a permitted stop and recheck rather than asking for a road-side exit. Your route and direction should already be chosen; this file does not fully solve riding past a stop or emergencies.

### Preparation and inputs

Know the exit stop name/ID and the preceding stop. Use the actual bus display, announcement, or current operator sequence. If information is inaccessible, ask the driver or appropriate staff when safe rather than guessing from similar buildings outside. Know whether this operator uses a button, pull cord, or another stop-request method. Keep your bag reachable without blocking the aisle. For mobility, hearing, or vision barriers, request available operator assistance early rather than rushing at the door.

### Execution

1. **Treat the preceding stop as the cue.** When the display, announcement, or reliable sequence reaches it, verify the next stop is yours. If a branch terminus, diversion, or skipped stop conflicts with the plan, ask the driver or check official live status. Do not demand a stop in moving traffic.
2. **Request the stop in time.** Use the valid button, cord, or operator method with the notice that system requires. Look or listen for a stop-request indication; if uncertain, confirm once according to the bus instructions rather than randomly pressing controls.
3. **Wait until stopped to leave.** Follow the driver/vehicle directions for an exit, gather belongings, steady yourself, and leave space for others. Do not rush to the door while the bus moves just to save one stop. When it opens, check the landing area and nearby pedestrians and cyclists before stepping out. For a blocked exit, unsafe curb, or unknown location, tell the driver and use the permitted procedure.
4. **Read back this leg's end.** Confirm the actual stop from its sign, name, or official information. Complete any local tap-off or ticket action the operator requires. If your stop was skipped or passed, exit at the next safe permitted stop and replan; do not press the driver to stop illegally or cross moving traffic.

### Success

You alight at the planned or operator-confirmed alternative safe stop, with belongings, without forcing an exit from a moving vehicle or unsafe place. Any local fare-ending action follows its actual rule. When a diversion makes the target unavailable, knowing the safe actual stop is a valid pause.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Announcement cannot be heard or read:** Cross-check the preceding stop and operator sequence. Ask the driver or trusted companion when safe; if still uncertain, alight at a recognizable permitted stop and recheck.
- **You requested but the bus passed the stop:** Stay clear of the moving door, leave at the next permitted stop, note where you are, and replan.
- **Target stop is closed:** Follow live operator and on-board notices. Do not let an old screenshot decide an unsafe exit.
- **A bag was left on the bus:** Do not chase into traffic. Record route, direction, vehicle/time details, and use the operator's lost-property route.
- **A local tap-off was missed:** Consult that operator's correction or fare rule, not another city's penalty or free-transfer claim.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary alighting from a normal bus run. Stop requests, announcements, doors, tap-off, lost property, and accessibility support vary. London and Vancouver rider guides are examples. A phone is optional: use announcements, signs, printed stop sequences, or staff. If standing or moving is difficult, request help early and wait until the bus is still. Walking onward or finding a transfer is another task.
