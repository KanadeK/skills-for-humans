## 中文

当你准备买一件依赖软件的普通联网商品，例如智能灯或音箱，但标签只写“持续更新”而没有清楚期限时加载。你要取得**准确型号的软件更新支持期及其含义**，或承认找不到。此处不设置账号、不配置网络，不审计商品安全，也不替代医疗、安防等高风险设备的专业评估。

### 准备与输入

记下完整型号、硬件版本和销售地区。说清你在意的功能，例如手机远程控制还是普通开关；再区分你要查的是安全修补、功能更新、配套 App，还是云端服务。它们可能有不同的运营方和期限，不能由“更新五年”自动合并。

### 执行

1. **从制造商当前资料找期限。** 查看准确型号的支持页、包装说明或正式更新政策。记录访问日期、地区和政策版本；别从同品牌新款推断旧款期限。
2. **读清起算方式。** 若写“上市后五年”，找到该型号适用的上市日期；若写明确截止日，直接记日期。仅写“定期更新”而没有期限就标为期限未知，不自行加几年。
3. **分开支持类型。** 确认写的是安全更新、功能更新还是仅配套 App 更新；问店员或制造商哪一项覆盖你在意的功能。不能用 App 仍在应用商店就推断设备固件仍获支持。
4. **问清支持结束后的功能。** 查正式说明能否离线完成你在意的普通用途，以及哪些能力依赖持续更新或外部服务；没有说明就记“结束后功能未知”，不现场登录测试私人账户。
5. **留下一条购前结论。** 写“型号/地区 → 更新类型 → 最短明确截止日或起算规则 → 来源与日期 → 功能未知点”。关键期限无法确认或太短时可暂缓、联系制造商或选资料更透明的产品。

### 成功与停止

你能说出制造商针对这款商品明确承诺的最短支持期、它具体指哪类更新，以及仍无法确认什么；或诚实停在“未公布/未知”。这不是未来必然安全或云服务永不关闭的保证。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看到品牌级口号：** 查准确型号与硬件版本；没有就标未知。
- **把出厂日当购买日：** 依政策原文找起算点，不自行重设时钟。
- **政策按上市日计算却找不到上市日：** 请制造商确认该型号适用日期；算不出截止日就标未知。
- **店员说“App 还能下载”：** 继续问设备更新类型与期限；下载状态不能回答支持期。
- **页面下线或各地区说法不同：** 保存差异并向制造商正式渠道询问；关键问题未解就暂缓购买。

### 假设、替代与副作用

更新政策和商品范围会随地区与时间变化，英国的公开期限规则不自动适用于你的所在地。没有网络、看不清页面或不方便通话时，可请店员展示准确型号的书面政策、使用制造商文字支持或可信同伴协助。软件期限只是购前信息之一，不能替你做安全认证。副作用是“智能”这个形容词需要附一条实际日期。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary connected product such as a smart light or speaker when the label says “ongoing updates” without a clear period. Your result is the **stated software-update support period for the exact model and what it covers**, or an honest unknown. You do not create an account, configure a network, certify security, or assess a medical or other high-stakes device here.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the full model, hardware revision, and sales region. Name the function you care about, such as phone control or an ordinary wall switch. Separate security patches, feature updates, the companion app, and any cloud service. Different parties may run them for different periods; “five years of updates” does not merge all four.

### Execution

1. **Find the current maker statement.** Read the exact model's support page, package information, or formal update policy. Note access date, region, and policy edition. Do not infer an older model's period from a newer product in the same brand.
2. **Read the starting point.** If the statement says “five years after launch,” find the applicable model launch date. If it gives an end date, record that date. “Regular updates” without a duration means duration unknown, not an invitation to invent years.
3. **Separate update types.** Determine whether the period covers security fixes, new features, or only the companion app. Ask the store or maker which one supports your important function. An app still available for download does not prove that device firmware remains supported.
4. **Ask what works after support ends.** Find formal information on whether your ordinary use still works offline and which abilities need continued updates or an external service. Mark post-support behaviour unknown when the maker does not say. Do not sign in to a personal account for a showroom test.
5. **Keep a pre-purchase conclusion.** Write “model/region → update type → minimum stated end date or starting rule → dated source → function unknowns.” If a decisive period is absent or too short for your plan, defer, contact the maker, or choose a better documented product.

### Success and stop

You can state the maker's minimum committed period for this model, which updates it covers, and what remains unclear; or stop at “not published/unknown.” The result cannot guarantee future security or that a cloud service will never close.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a brand-wide slogan appears:** Find the exact model and hardware revision; otherwise mark the period unknown.
- **You start counting from the purchase date:** Use the policy's stated start date rather than resetting the clock yourself.
- **The policy counts from launch but launch date is missing:** Ask the maker for the applicable date; mark the end date unknown if it cannot be calculated.
- **Staff say “the app is still downloadable”:** Ask for device update type and duration; download availability does not answer the support-period question.
- **The page disappears or regions disagree:** Record the conflicting statements and ask the maker through an official channel. Defer a purchase that depends on the answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Support policies and covered models change by region and over time. A UK disclosure rule does not automatically apply where you shop. Without internet, readable text, or phone access, request exact-model written information in store, use the maker's text support, or ask a trusted companion. A duration is one purchase fact, not a security certificate. The side effect is attaching a real date to the word “smart.”
