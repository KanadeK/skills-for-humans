## 中文

当普通米饭或面**已经完成原定烹调**，却比这顿饭需要的更湿、更软或更干时，用这份 Skill 决定是小范围调整、按新用途使用还是停止。还在锅里、米芯仍硬或干面仍未到包装熟度，先回到该产品的制作说明；这不是把未熟成品宣布为补救成功。

### 准备与输入

先确认食物是什么、原本应有的质地、何时煮好、煮后是否一直按当地食品安全指导处理。**时间或温度史不明，或曾被生食/脏工具污染时停止，不靠再次加热或调水补签安全证明。**熟饭尤其不能靠闻起来正常判断安全。准备干净的小碗和勺、原包装或可信食谱；如要加热，确认可安全使用的锅/容器和热源。任何医疗饮食、婴幼儿食品、发酵或罐藏成品不在此范围。

### 执行

1. **停止扩大失误。** 安全地停下正在进行的额外加水、收干或反复翻动。只取一小份，先辨别是表面有游离水、内部已经软烂，还是已熟透但入口偏干；别用一锅食物测试猜想。
2. **把未完成烹调退回原流程。** 米粒中心仍硬、锅里还有按吸水法本该被吸收的水，或面仍未达到这包产品要求时，按这款米/面与设备的可信说明继续检查或停止。这里不提供通用补水量和分钟数。
3. **过湿但形状还在：只试能移走的水。** 若有可见游离水、原做法允许沥水或放走蒸汽，先在小份上用稳定、不会烫伤你的方式处理，再看是否达到这餐需要的质地。吸水法米饭和需沥水的面并不共享一个倒锅动作；不能安全控制热水时停止该动作。
4. **已经软烂：承认用途变化。** 若小份放走表面水后仍失去原本粒形或咬感，不继续高温烘干整锅，也不声称能恢复原样。只在有相容、可信的实际做法和所需材料时，把安全的一部分转作容许软质地的菜，例如熟饭加入按食谱制作的汤；没有合适做法就少量上桌、换主食或弃置。
5. **已熟但偏干：先试一口份量。** 若中心已经达到这款产品的熟度，只取少量，按产品/食谱允许的方法加入少量相容液体并覆盖、回温或拌合；检查是否真的更适合入口，且没有变成糊状。有效才按可控份量处理剩余部分。若没有可信方法、设备或时间，不把整锅继续加热当成默认修复。
6. **结束并交接。** 选一种可观察结果：按新质地安全上桌、按具体食谱改用途、继续原产品未完成的烹调流程，或停止/弃置。暂时不吃的熟米面按当地规则保温或及时冷却保存；本 Skill 不延长它的安全期限。

### 成功条件与常见报错

你已根据**游离水、内部质地、是否真正熟透和安全史**作出决定；试验先在小份完成，整锅没有因补救越做越差。改用途或弃置是完整结果，不要求把软烂米饭编译回粒粒分明。

- **小份沥水后仍软烂：** 停止按“只差一点水”处理；仅找能接受软质地的可靠食谱，或换主食。
- **加液后仍有硬芯：** 这更像未完成烹调；回到该产品制作说明，不用另一种米的水米比。
- **复热后表面软、中心仍干：** 停止放大操作，重新核对份量和产品方法；不要以表面状态宣布整锅已改好。
- **过去的保存时间说不清：** 终止质量补救，按当地食品安全指导处理；水分观感不能替代时间记录。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这针对普通家用、已知安全的熟米或面，目标口感由这款产品和这顿饭决定。品种、包装、热源与锅具不同，故不提供万能比例。握力或视力受限时用稳定的小容器、易握勺和可信现场协助；无法安全拿取热食就停止。可能产生另一个需要材料和清洗工作的菜，也可能没有经济或安全上值得做的补救。

## English

Use this Skill after ordinary rice or noodles have **completed their intended cooking** but are wetter, softer, or drier than this meal calls for. Decide whether to adjust a small portion, use the food differently, or stop. If it is still cooking, rice has a hard centre, or pasta has not reached its own package finish, return to that product's cooking directions. Unfinished food does not become a successful rescue by being renamed.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the food, its intended texture, when cooking ended, and whether it has been handled under local food-safety guidance since then. **Stop if its time or temperature history is unknown or raw food or dirty tools contaminated it. Adding water or reheating cannot certify it.** Normal-smelling cooked rice is not proof of safety. Have a clean small dish and spoon, plus the pack or a reliable recipe. For any heating, confirm a safe pan or container and heat source. Medical diets, infant food, fermentation, and canning are outside this Skill.

### Execution

1. **Stop extending the mistake.** Safely pause further water additions, drying, or repeated stirring. Take only a small portion. Decide whether water is visibly free on the surface, the inside is already mushy, or the food is fully cooked but dry in the mouth. Do not test a guess on the whole batch.
2. **Return unfinished cooking to its own method.** If rice has a hard centre, water meant to be absorbed remains, or pasta has not reached this pack's finish, use the rice, noodle, and appliance directions to continue checking or stop. There is no universal extra water amount or number of minutes here.
3. **Wet but still shaped: test removable water only.** If free water is visible and the original method permits draining or releasing steam, try that on a small portion in a stable way that does not expose you to a hot-water spill. Check against this meal's texture. Absorption rice and boil-and-drain pasta do not share a single pot-pouring method. Stop that action if you cannot control the hot water safely.
4. **Already mushy: change the intended use.** If releasing surface water does not restore the small sample's grain shape or bite, do not keep heating the full batch at high heat or promise its original texture back. Only when you have a compatible reliable recipe and the needed ingredients, put some known-safe food into a dish that accepts softness, such as cooked rice in a recipe-based soup. Otherwise serve a modest amount as it is, choose another staple, or discard it.
5. **Cooked through but dry: trial one serving.** If the centre meets this product's finish, add a little compatible liquid to a small portion using a method the pack or recipe supports, then cover, warm, or mix as directed. Check that it is more pleasant to eat without becoming paste. Scale only if the sample works. Without a reliable method, equipment, or time, continued heating of the full batch is not the default fix.
6. **Finish and hand off.** Choose an observable outcome: serve safely at the new texture, repurpose using a specific recipe, resume the original unfinished cooking process, or stop and discard. Hold or promptly cool and store cooked rice or noodles you will not eat now under local guidance. This Skill does not extend their safe life.

### Success and common errors

Your decision uses **free water, internal texture, actual doneness, and safety history**. The trial happened on a small portion before changing the rest. Repurposing or discarding is a complete result; mushy rice need not compile back into separate grains.

- **The sample is still mushy after draining:** Stop treating it as a little extra surface water. Find a reliable use that accepts the soft texture, or choose another staple.
- **A sample remains hard after adding liquid:** This points to unfinished cooking. Return to this product's directions rather than another rice's water ratio.
- **The surface softens but the centre remains dry:** Do not scale the trial. Recheck portion size and product method; the surface does not certify the batch.
- **You cannot reconstruct the holding time:** Stop the quality repair and follow local food-safety guidance. Moisture appearance cannot fill in a missing record.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary household rice or noodles known to be safe. The exact product and meal determine acceptable texture. Variety, pack, heat source, and pan differ, so there is no universal ratio. If grip or vision is limited, use a stable small dish, easy-to-hold spoon, and trusted in-person help. Stop if handling the hot food cannot be made safe. A changed dish may require extra ingredients and washing, and sometimes no repair is worthwhile or safe.
