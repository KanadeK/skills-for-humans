## 中文

当普通柜台或公共服务用**正式号码票、预约签到或叫号屏**管理等候，而不是按站立队尾轮到你时加载。目标是取得或确认自己的有效轮候状态、在可接收叫号的位置等待，并在真正叫到时去对的窗口。这里不替场所判断你对具体服务的法律资格，不用别人的票，不让陌生二维码接管身份信息。

### 准备与输入

看现场或管理方正式页面：这项服务是否要先预约、到场后签到、再取号码；在哪台机器、哪个柜台或官方线上入口办理，票何时有效，叫号用屏幕、声音还是短信。预约确认邮件不一定就是号码票，号码票也不一定等于材料已审核。只在场所确实要求的官方流程中处理必要个人资料，保护票号与验证码不被公开拍摄。

### 执行

1. **先确定是哪一种轮候。** 对照事项、预约时段和现场指示，找官方取号机、接待台或管理方 App。若写“先签到再取票”，照真实顺序做；不要站进物理长队半小时才发现只需取号。入口模糊时向场所人员问清。
2. **取得并核对自己的状态。** 按官方操作拿到纸票/电子号码或柜台给的待叫确认，核对服务类别、号码、当日有效条件与等候区域。显示“预约成功”但未签到时仍要完成本场所要求的到场步骤；若出票失败，不自行借别人的号码代替。
3. **在能接收叫号的位置等待。** 按场所允许的位置坐或站，留意屏幕、播报或官方通知，并保留必要票据。对听觉、视力、行动或语言不便，可向工作人员问如何获得可用提示或辅助轮候；不必为看屏幕堵在柜台口。
4. **号码到来时再去对应窗口。** 对上票号、窗口或房间号和服务名称，按现场指示前往，出示本人的实际票据与办理材料。若数字相近、显示跳号或突然改柜台，先核对正式屏/工作人员，不替另一个号码答到。
5. **错过或失效时走官方补救。** 保留自己票据和实际叫号时点，问场所是否重叫、重取或改期；过期、关门或系统故障都不能凭本 Skill 宣称可插队或继续使用旧票。记下工作人员给的下一步。

### 完成与停止

你已取得并跟随本次服务的有效号码，被叫到正确位置，或明确知道未签到、错过、失效后的正式下一步，本轮完成。**被叫到不保证事项获批。** 无官方号码、票号与本人不符、叫号方式不可用或入口要求敏感信息却无法核实时，停下向场所人员求证，不用陌生人的票、码或账号。

### 常见报错与补救

- **有预约却没号码：** 查是否还需到场签到/取票，向场所人员确认，不把预约邮件直接当叫号结果。
- **纸票丢失或电子号码看不到：** 用官方柜台/系统询问可否找回或重新取号，不借他人的票。
- **叫号屏看不清或播报听不到：** 请求可读/可听的官方提示或工作人员协助，不站到窗口前挡住他人。
- **看到较大号码先被叫：** 可能是不同服务类别或窗口；核对本人的服务区域，不断言对方插队。
- **号码过期或错过：** 问工作人员重叫、重取或改期规则，记录实际答复，不自行占据原窗口。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通、可退出的公共服务轮候，不处理移民、执法、医疗、身份认证代办或具体业务资格。取号、预约、隐私与错号规则因机构而异，只从实际管理方确认。没有智能手机时用正式纸票/接待台；听觉、视觉、行动或语言不便时可要求场所提供可用的叫号信息或可信陪同，不交出账号控制权。副作用是你的排队位置可能看不见，但应能在正式号码/状态里查到。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary counter or public service uses an **official ticket number, appointment check-in or called-number display** rather than a continuously standing line. Your result is a valid waiting state, a place where you can receive the call, and the right counter when your number is called. It does not decide legal eligibility for a service, use someone else's ticket or hand identity information to an unknown QR page.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the venue's signs or official information for this service: is an appointment required, must you check in on arrival, and must you then obtain a number? Find its real machine, desk or official digital route, validity period and call method: screen, sound or message. An appointment email may not be a queue ticket, and a ticket does not mean documents were approved. Enter necessary personal data only in the venue's actual official process and keep tickets or one-time codes out of public photos.

### Execution

1. **Identify the waiting mechanism.** Match your service and appointment time to the posted machine, reception or official app. If it says “check in before ticket,” follow that order. Do not stand in a physical line for half an hour if the actual process calls for a number. Ask venue staff when the entrance is unclear.
2. **Obtain and verify your own state.** Get the paper or electronic number or staff-confirmed waiting state through the official process. Check service category, number, today's validity and waiting area. If a booking is confirmed but arrival check-in is pending, do the required on-site step. For a failed ticket issue, do not borrow someone else's number.
3. **Wait where you can receive the call.** Sit or stand where the venue permits and monitor the display, announcement or official alert, retaining your ticket. If sight, hearing, mobility or language changes what you can use, ask staff for a usable signal or supported wait. You do not need to block the counter to watch a screen.
4. **Go to the right counter when called.** Match your number with the counter or room and service name, then follow on-site direction with your own ticket and materials. For a similar number, skipped display or changed counter, confirm with the official board or staff rather than answering for another ticket.
5. **Use official recovery for a missed or invalid ticket.** Keep your ticket and actual call time and ask whether it can be recalled, reissued or rescheduled. Expiry, closure or system failure does not create a right to cut or reuse an old ticket. Record the staff's next step.

### Success and stop

You have received and followed a valid number for this service to the correct called place, or know the venue's actual next step after missing check-in, a call or validity. **Being called does not approve the underlying request.** If no official number exists, the ticket does not match you, you cannot use the call signal, or the entrance asks for sensitive information you cannot verify, stop and ask venue staff. Do not use a stranger's ticket, code or account.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Appointment confirmed but no ticket:** Check whether arrival check-in or ticket issue is another step. Do not treat the email as a call.
- **Paper ticket lost or digital number unavailable:** Ask official staff or system whether it can be found or reissued; do not borrow one.
- **Display cannot be read or call cannot be heard:** Ask for an accessible alert or staff help rather than blocking the counter.
- **A higher number is called first:** It may belong to another service category or desk. Verify your waiting area instead of assuming someone cut in.
- **Number expired or call missed:** Ask staff about recall, a new ticket or another appointment and record their real answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is an ordinary, exit-able public-service wait, not immigration, enforcement, medical work, identity-handling for others or a decision on service eligibility. Ticket, booking, privacy and missed-call rules differ by venue; verify with the operator. Without a smartphone, use a real paper ticket or reception desk. For sight, hearing, mobility or language needs, ask for usable call information or a trusted companion without handing over account control. Your position may be invisible as a physical line but should be visible in an official number or state.
