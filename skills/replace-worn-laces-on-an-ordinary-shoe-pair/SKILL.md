## 中文

当你的一双普通鞋有**断裂、磨薄或绳头散开的鞋带**，需要换成可稳定系紧的新带时，加载这份 Skill。结果是两鞋正确穿带、能正常系结、松端不拖地；它不是清洗尚可用的鞋带，也不改变鞋子的尺码或治疗脚部问题。专门的安全鞋、运动锁带或医疗辅具按制造商说明处理。

### 准备与输入

确认鞋眼/孔和原穿带方式无损，检查旧带长度、宽度和材料。Nike 建议优先量原带；其按孔数给的长度只是品牌参考，具体鞋型可能不同。选能顺利穿孔、两侧留足打结长度而不过长的新带，左右最好配对。眼孔掉落或鞋面裂开时先停，不靠新带掩盖鞋体损伤。

### 执行

1. **记录旧路径。** 在拆第一只鞋前拍照或记每排从内/外穿的方向，注意鞋舌位置和左右是否相同；再抽出旧带。若旧带已断，可用另一只完好鞋作参照，不凭想象跳孔。
2. **从底部逐排穿新带。** 将新带两端从适合本鞋的下方孔起穿，检查两端大致等长，再按原路径向上穿。每排带子平顺、不过度交扭，鞋舌留在中间；受阻时停查孔，不用尖工具戳穿鞋面。
3. **试系并调节。** 把鞋放在脚上按平日需要系结，脚可正常活动且鞋不会从脚上滑脱；鞋带也不勒出异常不适。走几步在安全平地看结会不会轻易散、松端是否触地或卷入鞋底。疼痛、麻木或支撑感异常就停穿并查合适鞋款/专业建议。
4. **完成另一只并收尾。** 对第二只同样穿带，比较孔位和松端。旧带若不能再用按当地普通废物规则处理，保留新带尺寸记录供下次更换。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

两只鞋带通过完整孔路、能可靠系结、两端不拖地，鞋体没有新损伤；实际穿着无明显异常不适。仅把新带塞进第一对孔，不算更换完成。

- **新带太短系不成结：** 停止穿着，按旧带实际长度/本鞋说明换合适长度。
- **新带太长拖地：** 换短带或在鞋款允许范围内规范收束，不让长环垂到脚下。
- **两端严重不等长：** 退回第一排重新居中，再逐排穿。
- **鞋眼松脱或孔边撕裂：** 停止拉紧，交合适维修，不靠更粗带强撑。
- **手眼协调不便：** 用对比色鞋带、照片参照或可信协助穿孔，由你确认长度和穿着检查。

### 假设与边界

替带长度取决于鞋的实际孔路和结法，不能靠品牌孔数表保证适配。装饰穿带样式只是变体；这里恢复普通鞋可系紧的功能，不证明运动、防护或医疗适用性。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary pair has **broken, thin, or fraying shoelaces** and needs compatible replacements that can be tied securely. Finish with both shoes fully threaded, knots usable, and loose ends off the ground. This is different from washing sound laces and does not change shoe size or treat a foot problem. Specialist safety footwear, sport locking systems, or medical supports follow their maker's directions.

### Preparation and inputs

Check that the eyelets and original route are intact. Note old lace length, width, and material. Nike recommends measuring an old lace first; eyelet-count lengths are brand examples and another shoe may differ. Choose new laces that pass through the holes, leave enough on each side to tie, and are not overly long. Stop at a detached eyelet or torn upper rather than hiding shoe damage with new laces.

### Execution

1. **Record the old path.** Photograph or note the in/out direction through each row before removing the first lace, including tongue position and whether both shoes match. If one lace is broken, use the sound shoe as a reference instead of skipping holes by guesswork.
2. **Thread from the bottom upward.** Start in the lower holes appropriate to the shoe, equalise the ends roughly, and follow the original path. Keep each crossing flat and the tongue centred. If a hole resists, inspect it instead of forcing a sharp tool through the upper.
3. **Tie and test.** Put on the shoes and tie to your normal comfortable hold. You should be able to move normally without the shoe slipping off or the laces causing unusual pressure. Walk a few steps on safe level ground and check that the knot does not immediately release and loops or ends do not reach the sole. Stop wear for pain, numbness, or unusual support and seek appropriate shoe or professional guidance.
4. **Finish the second shoe.** Thread it similarly and compare hole paths and ends. Dispose of unusable old laces under local ordinary waste rules and keep the replacement length for next time.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Both laces follow full hole paths, tie reliably without dragging ends, and have not damaged the shoes; short normal wear reveals no unusual discomfort. A new lace through only the first holes is unfinished.

- **New lace cannot make a knot:** Stop wearing and select a length based on the old lace or actual model guidance.
- **Loops drag on the ground:** Use a shorter lace or a maker-permitted tidy hold without leaving long loops below your feet.
- **Ends are badly uneven:** Return to the first row, centre them, and rethread.
- **Eyelet detaches or hole tears:** Stop pulling and use suitable repair, not a thicker lace forced through.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Contrast laces, a photo reference, or trusted threading help can support your length and wear check.

### Assumptions and limits

Length depends on the actual path and knot, so no eyelet chart guarantees fit. Decorative patterns are variants. This restores ordinary tying and does not certify sport, protective, or medical suitability.
