## 中文

当一条**不含敏感资料的普通消息**刚被你发给了错误的人或群，而你想停止误会、把必要内容送到正确地方时，加载这份 Skill。目标是有限纠正，不是保证别人没读过或把已经发出的信息从所有设备上抹去。若内容含凭据、私密影像、个人敏感资料，或涉及法律、医疗、金融及工作机密，停止本普通流程并使用相应的隐私/安全事件处理方式。

### 准备与输入

先别急着复制粘贴第二条。核对刚才实际发送到哪个对话、消息具体是什么、是否还有附件或可转发内容、谁原本需要收到。避免在惊慌中把原消息再次发到更大的群。你只能确认应用里显示的状态，不能凭一个“已撤回”字样知道对方此前是否看见、保存或转发。

### 执行

1. **先停止继续扩散。** 不在错误对话里追加原文解释，不把截图转给更多人求助。若这条消息只是普通约时间或闲聊，先把错误范围和需要纠正的事实写清。
2. **按当前应用的实际功能处理。** 若有撤回或“为所有人删除”，核对它适用于这条消息和所有相关收件人，再执行并读回状态；“只在我这里删除”不等于从对方那里消失。不同平台、版本和时间窗口效果不同，不能把按钮当成保证无人见过的证书。
3. **只发必要的澄清。** 错误收件人若可能因此误解或行动，可简短说“抱歉，刚才那条发错对话了，不是给你的；请忽略。”不要在道歉里重发整段内容，也不要求对方证明自己未读过。若平台已清楚撤回、内容又不会造成误会，追加说明可以省略。
4. **再核对正确收件人。** 只有原消息仍有实际用途时，打开对方正确对话，确认姓名/群名和最近上下文，再发恰当版本。不是所有失误都需要把同一句话补发；群聊的收件范围尤其要核对。
5. **把剩余不确定说准确。** 记录“撤回已显示 / 澄清已发 / 正确对象已收到或未确认”。对方不回复不等于没看到，也不要求你无限追问。若发现消息其实含敏感内容，立即转入相应事件流程，不在这里继续靠普通道歉解决。

### 完成条件

你已停止进一步传播，按实际可用功能尝试必要的撤回或作出简短澄清，并在需要时把正确内容送到核对过的对象。不能确认对方是否已读、保存或转发时，结果明确写为未知。对方必须说“没事”不是完成条件。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只点了“从我这端删除”：** 重新核对应用说明；本机看不见不代表收件人也看不见。
- **道歉又复制了原文：** 停止重播，用“发错对话，请忽略”描述动作即可。
- **紧张中又发给第二个错误对象：** 再次停下，回到收件人名称和上下文核对，不用速度修复速度造成的错误。
- **撤回成功就宣称没人看见：** 改成“应用显示已撤回”，对已发生的阅读或保存保持未知。
- **错误群里有人回应：** 只纠正必要事实，不把普通失误扩成争论；敏感传播立即转相应事件处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

应用、版本、设备和聊天类型会改变撤回、编辑及删除的效果。Apple 的 iMessage 支持文档只是一个平台条件不同的例子，不能推成所有应用的规则；使用你当前应用的正式说明。若看不清收件人或容易误触，可放大界面、请可信的人只读出对话名称，或先写草稿再确认，但不要因此向别人暴露内容。这里仅适用于低风险日常文字，不处理数据泄露或危机。

副作用是你可能留下一个简短的“发错了”提示，而不是一连串越解释越多的消息。

## English

Load this Skill when an **ordinary low-risk message with no sensitive information** went to the wrong person or chat, and you need to limit confusion and route any still-needed content correctly. The result is a bounded correction, not a guarantee that nobody read it or that it vanished from every device. If it contains credentials, intimate images, sensitive personal data, or legal, medical, financial, or work secrets, stop this ordinary flow and use the appropriate privacy or security incident process.

### Preparation and inputs

Do not rush to copy and paste a second message. Check the chat that actually received it, its exact content, any attachment or forwardable material, and who was supposed to receive it. Panic can send the same text to a larger group. You can see an app's reported state; an “unsent” mark cannot tell you whether someone previously saw, saved, or forwarded it.

### Execution

1. **Stop further spread first.** Do not append the full message to the wrong chat as an explanation or send screenshots to more people for help. For an ordinary time plan or small talk, identify the wrong audience and what fact actually needs correction.
2. **Use only the app's real current feature.** If it offers recall or delete-for-everyone, check that it applies to this message and relevant recipients, use it, and read back the state. “Delete for me” does not mean the recipient's copy disappeared. Platform, version, and timing can change the effect. A button is not a certificate that nobody ever saw the message.
3. **Clarify only what is necessary.** If the mistaken recipient might be confused or act on it, say briefly: “Sorry, I sent that to the wrong chat; it wasn't meant for you. Please ignore it.” Do not reproduce the whole content in the apology or demand proof that they never read it. If recall is visibly complete and the content cannot mislead, an extra note may be unnecessary.
4. **Check the intended recipient again.** If the original content still matters, open the correct conversation, verify name or group and recent context, then send a suitable version. Not every mistake requires repeating the sentence. Check the audience especially carefully for groups.
5. **Name remaining uncertainty honestly.** Record “app shows recalled / clarification sent / correct recipient reached or unconfirmed.” No reply does not prove the message was unseen and does not require endless follow-up. If you discover sensitive content, switch to the relevant incident process instead of relying on an ordinary apology.

### Success

You stopped additional spread, attempted applicable recall or sent a concise correction where needed, and routed still-needed content to a verified intended recipient. Whether the wrong recipient read, saved, or forwarded it remains unknown unless you actually have evidence. They need not say “it's fine” for your actions to be complete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You used “delete for me” only:** Check the app's instructions again. Invisible on your device does not mean invisible to the recipient.
- **The apology repeats the whole original:** Stop replaying it and name only the routing mistake.
- **Panic sends it to a second wrong chat:** Stop again and verify name and context instead of using speed to repair a speed error.
- **Recall appears successful, so you claim nobody saw it:** State only that the app reports recall; earlier reading or saving is still unknown.
- **Someone replies in the wrong group:** Correct only the necessary fact rather than extending the exchange. Sensitive spread requires the separate incident process.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

App, version, device, and chat type change the effect of recall, editing, and deletion. Apple's iMessage support is one example of conditional features, not a rule for every service. Use your current app's official instructions. If the recipient name is hard to read or taps are easy to miss, enlarge the screen, ask a trusted person to read only the chat name, or draft before sending without exposing message content unnecessarily. This covers low-risk everyday text, not a data breach or crisis.

The side effect may be one short “wrong chat” note instead of a chain of explanations that spreads the message farther.
