## 中文

当你需要把几枝已整理的**普通切花实际扎成可手持或交给他人的小束**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是握点稳定、花头可见、扎线不过紧的花束；是否送给某人、何时送归你决定。它与在花瓶里固定摆放的结果不同，不教专业花艺线材、特定文化花式或长途无水保鲜承诺。

### 准备与输入

确认花种已知且适合接触，花枝已去掉会泡水的下部叶片并补过水。准备可轻握的少量花、柔软细绳或不伤茎的束带，以及完成后能让切端再入水的清洁容器。先决定花束要能由谁握住、放进什么水容器；若枝上有刺或刺激性汁液，选别的枝或让有防护的可信协助者处理。

### 执行

1. **找一个自然握点。** 把一枝作中心，添加其余枝时让花头高低略错开，茎在手下方汇拢。每加一枝从预定展示方向看是否遮住主要花头；可以减枝，不必把所有材料用完。
2. **试握和承重。** 在茎交会处轻握，检查束的重量、外伸和是否有花头被折弯。若一手无法稳定握，做更小束或请人辅助；不让细脆茎负担重花头。
3. **轻扎而非勒紧。** 在握点用柔软细绳绕一两圈并打能固定的结，避免线滑落；让花束拿起时枝不散，但茎不被压扁。扎错可解开重排，不用层层缠紧掩盖结构问题。
4. **回水与检查。** 将切端放入合适清洁水容器，确定束带高于水线且叶片不泡水。轻拿起试一次，看花头仍可见、握点可靠、无刺外露；暂不交接时让它继续吸水。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

花束能轻拿起而不散，茎未勒伤，展示面可看，放下后切端有水。绑好但无水放着许久，不是可交接的完成状态。

- **束太大、握不住：** 分成更小束或减少枝数，不靠更紧的线解决重量。
- **线滑或茎散：** 松开调整握点、换合用软线，重扎后再轻提检查。
- **花头被遮住：** 重排高低和外侧枝；不要用力扳弯脆茎。
- **扎线压扁茎：** 立即解开，弃用受损枝或换更宽软的扎法。
- **需送出但暂时无法保水：** 缩短无水等待并寻得合适水容器；不能承诺花束在未知运输条件下保持鲜度。

### 假设与边界

这是日常小束，不是婚礼手捧花或商业包装规范。接受者对花种、香味或宠物的限制须在交接前另行确认；美感可主观，稳固与供水可核。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to turn a few conditioned **ordinary cut-flower stems into a small hand-held or handoff bouquet**. Finish with a stable grip point, visible blooms, and a tie that does not crush stems. You decide whether and when to give it away. This is separate from a fixed vase display and does not teach professional wiring, a cultural floral form, or a promise of waterless travel life.

### Preparation and inputs

Use known flowers suitable for handling, already hydrated with lower leaves removed. Set out a small number of stems you can hold, soft string or a suitable non-damaging tie, and a clean vessel that can take the cut ends afterwards. Decide who needs to hold it and what water vessel it will fit. Replace thorny or irritating-sap stems or seek protected trusted help.

### Execution

1. **Find a natural grip point.** Hold one stem as a centre and add others with bloom heights offset, gathering their lower stems under your hand. After each addition, look from the intended display side for hidden blooms. Leave unused material aside if it improves the bunch.
2. **Test grip and weight.** Hold lightly at the crossing point. Check weight, outward reach, and bent flower heads. If one hand cannot control it, make a smaller bundle or ask help rather than loading fragile stems.
3. **Tie gently, without choking stems.** Wrap soft string once or twice at the grip and secure a knot that does not slide. The bundle should lift without falling apart while stems keep their shape. Untie and rearrange if needed rather than wrapping layer upon layer over a poor structure.
4. **Return to water and check.** Put cut ends in a suitable clean water vessel, keeping the tie above water and leaves out of it. Lift once gently: blooms remain visible, grip holds, and no thorns project. Leave it hydrating until handoff.

### Success, problems, and recovery

You can lift the bundle gently without it scattering; stems are not crushed, the display face is visible, and cut ends have water when set down. A tied bunch left dry for a long unknown period is not a ready handoff.

- **Bunch is too large to grip:** Split it or use fewer stems instead of tying tighter to fight weight.
- **Tie slips or stems scatter:** Undo, adjust the grip point, use suitable soft string, and lift-test again.
- **Blooms hide one another:** Reorder heights and outer stems without bending fragile stems by force.
- **Tie crushes stems:** Release it at once; remove damaged stems or use a broader softer hold.
- **You must give it but cannot keep it watered yet:** Shorten the dry interval and find a water vessel; do not promise freshness under unknown transport conditions.

### Assumptions and limits

This is an everyday small bunch, not a wedding bouquet or commercial packaging standard. Check recipient restrictions concerning species, fragrance, or pets before handoff. Beauty is subjective; grip and water access can be checked.
