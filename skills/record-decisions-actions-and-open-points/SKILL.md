## 中文

你在普通工作、学习或志愿小组讨论中负责留下可用记录，容易把**有人提过的想法、真正作出的决定、接下来谁做什么**混在一起时，用这份 Skill。结果是一份可回读的事实性摘要，让未到场或后来接手的人知道当时实际状态。它不代替 H244 的会前议程，也不自动证明决定具有正式法律或组织效力；正式会议纪要按本组织规则处理。

### 准备与输入

确定你是做个人笔记，还是被授权给小组分享摘要；先看议题、参与者和正式决策者是谁。用本次允许的纸笔或文字工具，不擅自录音、截屏或把保密材料放进公开共享盘。预留四个标记：**讨论、决定、行动、未解决**。决定需要谁确认、行动要写谁做和何时再看；没有明确指定的人不能靠记笔记者猜出一个负责人。

### 执行

1. **按实际发言先记事实状态。** 对每个议题记一句讨论要点或选项，不必逐字抄所有人发言。把“有人建议 A”标为讨论/提议；只有按该小组实际程序由有权者确定时才写“决定 A”。若大家只是倾向 A，写“倾向，待确认”，不靠语气把倾向升级。
2. **把决定写成可辨认版本。** 写清决定的具体对象、版本或条件，以及谁、在何时确认；与已知依据或限制有关的一句说明可以保留，但别把个人揣测写成集体理由。若有人提出异议且影响是否已定，停在“仍有争议/待决策者确认”。
3. **每个行动都配真实负责人和下一时间点。** “整理资料”改成“〔人〕把〔范围内材料〕发给〔接收者〕，于〔实际约定日期〕再检查”。没人愿接、日期没谈成或要等输入时，分别标“负责人待定”“时间待定”“依赖某输入”，不默认你或未到场的人负责。
4. **在话题转场或收尾时读回关键项。** 问“我记录的是决定采用 A，林周四前给出一页摘要；B 仍未决定，对吗？” 让相应负责人和有权决策者纠正。会议赶时间可在结束后发给他们核对，但此时标“待确认草稿”，不要宣称全组已批准。
5. **整理与分享实际版本。** 用清楚标题、日期和四类状态整理，删掉不影响后续的闲聊，保留仍需追踪的疑点。按允许的渠道发给需要的人并说明反馈方式；有修订则标新版本和变更点。发送记录、对方读到、组内确认和行动实际完成是不同状态，分别记。

### 成功条件

读者能区分这次讨论过什么、真正决定了什么、每项已同意行动的负责人和下一时间点、哪些仍没定；记录者没有替缺席者领任务。若关键决定或负责人未获确认，文件明确标草稿/待确认并给出核对对象。写下行动不等于完成行动，也不等于组织正式批准这份纪要。

### 常见报错与补救

- **有人说“可以考虑 A”，笔记写成“决定 A”：** 改为提议或倾向，向有权者核对后才升级状态。
- **行动项只有动词，没有负责人或日期：** 标缺口并向相关人追问，不把空白藏起来。
- **记笔记者为求完整，把未到场的人写成执行人：** 撤回未经同意的分配，让负责人确认实际承接。
- **会后两份摘要版本不同：** 标明来源与最新确认版本，逐项读回差异，不把其中一份静默覆盖。
- **一句话触及不该扩散的私人资料：** 只保留与决定必要且获准共享的事实；共享范围按实际规则处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于普通低风险协作笔记，不处理董事会法定纪要、劳动处分、法律证据或专业判断。University of Utah 的会议模板将讨论、决定和行动项分栏，Nebraska 的会议指南强调清楚负责人和分享记录；这里只借鉴可读的分类，不照搬各机构审批制度。不能实时打字可在允许情况下用纸笔短标记，事后及时读回；视觉/听力或语言不便时使用可访问格式和确认方式。笔记有时会暴露“其实还没定”，这是比虚构共识更有用的状态。

## English

Use this Skill when you are recording an ordinary work, study, or volunteer discussion and need to keep **an idea someone mentioned, an actual decision, and who will do what next** separate. Finish with a factual readback for absent people or future handover. It does not replace H244's pre-meeting brief or establish formal legal or organizational validity. Formal minutes follow the organization's rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether you are taking personal notes or authorized to share a group summary. Know the topics, attendees, and who can actually decide. Use permitted paper or text tools; do not record audio, capture screens, or post confidential material to a public drive without permission. Set up four labels: **discussion, decision, action, open point**. Know who confirms a decision. An action needs a real owner and next date; the note-taker cannot guess one into existence.

### Execution

1. **Record the actual speech state for each topic.** Note one discussion point or option rather than transcribing everyone. “Someone suggested A” stays discussion or proposal. Write “decided A” only after the authorized person or the group's actual procedure establishes it. If people merely lean toward A, write “leaning, pending confirmation,” not a decision inferred from tone.
2. **Make a decision identifiable.** State the specific object, version, or condition decided, and who confirmed it and when. Keep one relevant reason or limit if known, but not your personal theory as the group's rationale. If an objection affects whether it is settled, leave “disputed / decision-maker to confirm.”
3. **Attach a real owner and next time to each action.** Replace “organize materials” with “[person] sends [bounded material] to [recipient], checked again on [agreed date].” If nobody accepts it, no date is agreed, or an input is missing, mark “owner open,” “time open,” or the actual dependency. Do not silently assign it to yourself or an absent person.
4. **Read back at topic changes or closing.** “I recorded that A was chosen, Lin will send a one-page summary by Thursday, and B remains undecided—is that right?” Let the owner and authorized decision-maker correct it. If time runs out, send it to them afterward as a “draft pending confirmation,” not a group-approved record.
5. **Tidy and share the actual version.** Use a clear title, date, and the four states. Remove unrelated chatter while keeping questions that need tracking. Share through an allowed channel with those who need it and a way to correct it. For revisions, identify the new version and changed items. Notes sent, notes read, group confirmation, and actual action completion are distinct states.

### Success

A reader can distinguish topics discussed, decisions actually made, each accepted action's owner and next time, and what remains unresolved. No absent person was assigned work by the note-taker. If a critical decision or owner is unconfirmed, the file says draft/pending and names whom to check. Writing down an action is not doing it or formally approving the minutes.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“We might consider A” becomes “decided A”:** Relabel it proposal or leaning until the authorized party confirms.
- **An action has a verb but no owner or date:** Mark the gap and ask the relevant people rather than hiding an empty field.
- **An absent person is named as executor for completeness:** Withdraw the unaccepted assignment and ask the real owner to confirm.
- **Two post-meeting summaries disagree:** Identify their sources and latest confirmed version; read back differences item by item rather than silently overwriting one.
- **A comment contains private detail that should not spread:** Keep only necessary, permitted facts and follow the real sharing boundary.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary low-risk collaboration notes, not statutory board minutes, employment sanctions, legal evidence, or professional judgment. University of Utah's template separates discussion, decisions, and actions; Nebraska's guide emphasizes owners and shared notes. These are format examples, not imported approval rules. If real-time typing is inaccessible, use short paper marks when permitted and read them back promptly. For sight, hearing, or language needs, use an accessible format and confirmation method. Notes may reveal that nothing was decided yet; that is more useful than invented consensus.
