## 中文

当普通柜台、服务窗口或公开活动使用**现场站立队伍**，你需要找到正确队伍、按当地规则等到工作人员叫你时加载。这里的顺序靠现场队列位置与工作人员引导；如果场所发号码或短信叫号，改用号码轮候流程。公交上车的专门步骤仍以运营方规则为准。目标是到达正确服务点，不假定所有场所都先到先办。

### 准备与输入

知道你要办理的事项、是否有预约、预约时间或实际允许的特殊通道。从场所标牌、工作人员或正式通知辨认哪条队伍服务此事项，是否有预约/无预约、领取/咨询或可及协助的不同入口。看实际开放时段；不要根据“人多的那队”判断服务种类。个人行动、站立时间、视觉/听觉或语言需求可以向场所人员说明，不需要公开诊断。

### 执行

1. **找到有依据的队尾。** 看围栏、地贴、标牌与最后一位等候者；若多条队伍交叉或队尾不明显，向工作人员或队尾的人确认“这是办理__的队尾吗？”。答复与官方标识不符时再问场所人员，不插到看起来较短的队中段。
2. **按当前场所规则加入。** 确认是否只允许预约者、有没有分时进入或优先/协助程序。资格由场所确认；不要用猜的优先身份代替工作人员安排。进入队伍后给出入口、通道与他人私人空间留路。
3. **随队前移并守住可用的等待方式。** 注意围栏/标线和工作人员改道，别替后面的人决定何时前进。需要坐下、离队取物或照顾同行者时，先问场所是否可以保留位置、怎样回来；没有确认就不让物品或别人替你“占位”。
4. **轮到时确认服务点。** 当工作人员或标识让队首前往指定窗口，带好办理材料，核对这是你的事项；如果被告知还需取号、换队或补预约，记下正式下一步后退出原队，不争辩“我已经排很久”。

### 完成与停止

你已在正确的物理队列中按现场规则到达被叫的服务位置，或明确得知应去另一机制/入口，本轮完成。**排到前面不保证事项办成。** 队伍关闭、服务规则不明、等候方式对你不可行或现场不安全时，离开人流到允许位置问工作人员，不越栏、堵门或强行占位。

### 常见报错与补救

- **站在队中段才发现队尾很远：** 先回到真正队尾；“我不知道”不会替已在等候的人重新排序。
- **排的是咨询队不是办理队：** 从正式标识/工作人员确认正确队列，记录是否可转队，不擅自插入另一队。
- **预约与现场排队规则不同：** 出示预约说明问本次入口，不自动排普通队或自动走优先通道。
- **无法久站或看不清移动方向：** 告知场所你需要可坐/可读/协助的轮候方式，按其真实安排操作，不要求自己硬撑在队尾。
- **必须暂时离队：** 问清是否可返回原位；无规则支持就预期重排，别让物品替你占座。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通、可退出的公共服务现场，不指导移民、执法、医疗或危机窗口的专门程序。各场所对预约、无障碍、儿童、优先和离队的规则不同，以真实标识/人员为准。听觉、视觉、行动或语言不便时可用文字标牌、工作人员、可信陪同或场所可提供的座位轮候；需要帮助不意味着必须向整队解释个人情况。副作用是你可能从一条错误的短队换到正确的长队。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary counter, service desk or public event uses a **standing line** and you need to join the right one and wait under its actual rules. Place and staff direction determine this line; a numbered ticket or text call has a separate waiting process. Bus boarding still follows its operator's specific instructions. Your result is reaching the right service point, not assuming every venue operates first come, first served.

### Preparation and inputs

Know the service you want and any actual booking, appointment time or assistance route. Use venue signs, staff or a formal notice to distinguish lines for booking, walk-ins, collection, questions or access help. Check current hours. The largest crowd does not tell you which service it is. You can tell staff a concrete mobility, standing, sight, hearing or language need without announcing a diagnosis to a queue.

### Execution

1. **Find an evidenced end of the line.** Read barriers, floor marks and signs and identify the last waiting person. If queues cross or the end is unclear, ask staff or the person at the end, “Is this the end of the line for __?” If that conflicts with official signs, ask venue staff before joining a shorter-looking middle.
2. **Join under this venue's rule.** Check whether entry requires a booking, timed arrival or a priority or assistance arrangement. The venue confirms eligibility; do not self-assign a lane from a guess. Keep entrances, walkways and others' personal space usable.
3. **Move with the line using an acceptable waiting method.** Follow barriers and staff changes without advancing other people yourself. If you need to sit, leave to collect something or help a companion, ask whether your place can be kept and how to return. Without a confirmed rule, do not use a bag or another person to “hold” your place.
4. **Check the service point when called.** When staff direct the front to a counter, bring your material and verify it handles your task. If you need a ticket, another line or a booking, record the formal next step and leave this line; waiting a long time does not itself change the venue's rule.

### Success and stop

You have joined the correct physical line under its rule and reached the called service point, or learned the real other route or entrance. **Getting to the front does not guarantee the service outcome.** If the line closes, its rules are unclear, waiting is unusable for you or the area is unsafe, step out to a permitted place to ask staff rather than crossing barriers, blocking a door or claiming a place by force.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You discover the tail was farther back:** Go to the actual end; not knowing does not reorder people already waiting.
- **You queued for inquiries rather than service:** Confirm the right line with signs or staff and ask whether transfers are allowed before entering another line.
- **Appointment and walk-in queues differ:** Show the appointment instruction to staff; do not assume either the regular or priority lane applies.
- **Standing is difficult or movement cues are hard to see:** Ask the venue for a real seated, readable or assisted waiting method rather than struggling at the tail.
- **You need to leave briefly:** Ask whether a place can be retained. Without that rule, expect to rejoin rather than leaving an object to occupy it.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for ordinary, exit-able public services, not specialised immigration, enforcement, medical or crisis counters. Booking, access, family, priority and temporary-leave rules differ by venue; follow actual signs and staff. For hearing, sight, mobility or language needs, use signs, staff, a trusted companion or venue-supported seated waiting. Help does not require explaining personal circumstances to the whole line. You may leave a wrong short queue for a correct longer one.
