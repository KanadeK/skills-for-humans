## 中文

在普通旅程中发现外套、伞、水杯、书或类似**低敏感普通物品**不见了，用这份 Skill 找到最可能接手它的地方，提交足够辨认的描述，并安排一次可追踪的后续核对。它不保证找回，也不处理护照/证件、支付卡、带账号的设备、危险品或疑似盗窃的紧急处置；那些物品和情形要走对应的安全、服务或官方程序。

### 准备与输入

先在安全、允许停留的地方复查自己仍可控制的口袋、包和同行人的公共物品区；不翻别人的私物。记下**最后一次确定拿到物品**的事件，而不是最后一次“应该还在”。列出从那时到发现遗失的少数几个位置：住宿房间、寄存柜、车厢或座位、站内、店铺或活动场所。写物品类型、颜色、大小、特殊标记和能证明归属但不必公开的细节；行程日期、班次/车厢/座位、住宿房号等只给相应官方渠道所需部分。

### 执行

1. **先缩小时间窗，再排保管方。** 按最后确实见到物品之后的顺序列可能地点，区分“落在车上”“落在车站”“留在住宿或商店”；同一线路的车、车站和第三方商铺可能由不同机构收失物。优先联系最近且最可能实际接手物品的一方；没有证据时不要把猜测写成确定地点。
2. **找该方的正式入口。** 用运营方、住宿方或场所公布的失物页、预订内消息或现场服务台查是否需在线表单、电话或当面登记，以及何时可查询。车上或站内不让旅客进入的区域交给工作人员查；不追车、不穿越轨道或后台，也不把完整身份资料发给社交平台上的陌生“帮找人”。
3. **提交能识别、不过度暴露的描述。** 说明日期、地点范围、具体物品和独特但不公开的识别点；按服务方要求补班次、车站或座位。保留一两个只在正式认领环节用于核实的细节。若有多个合理保管方，分别通过各自正式渠道报告，并在自己的记录中标记“已报给谁”，避免把一份报告当全城联网。Amtrak 会给其失物报告编号，但其他服务是否给编号要以实际页面为准。
4. **把查询和找到分成不同状态。** 记下每一方的受理时间、编号或回复渠道、下一次合理核对时点；没有编号就记录实际答复方式。对方说“我们会找”只算受理，不算已找到；通知找到后再核对认领地点、开放时段、实际领取/寄送方式及费用，以服务方当期说明为准。
5. **做一次有界后续。** 到约定时间用编号查状态；若物品未被找到，更新最后回复和下一次可能性，再决定是否继续。没有明确的新线索，不要反复轰炸同一服务台或擅闯已关闭地点。若找到的物品与描述不符，不认领为自己的。

### 成功条件

最可能的正式保管方已收到可辨认报告，或你已确认当前没有可用报告渠道；每条线索有“未报 / 已报待查 / 对方称找到待核实 / 已实际取回 / 结束追查”的真实状态和下一核对点。取回只有在你实际验明并拿到物品后才算成功；报失或收到编号是可追踪进展，不是找回证明。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只记得“下午还在”：** 借行程、照片或同行人回忆缩小最后确实持有的事件；仍不确定就给合理时间范围，不编精确时刻。
- **把车厢失物报给了车站商店：** 按可能保管链补报真正的运营方；保留原报告，避免同一服务被重复轰炸。
- **描述太普通：“一把黑伞”：** 补尺寸、伞柄、标签等可辨特征；敏感识别点只在正式核实时说。
- **有人自称找到了并要求先转钱：** 从运营方或住宿方公布的渠道独立核实报告编号和收费方式，不从陌生消息中的链接付款。
- **物品其实是证件、支付卡或带账号的设备：** 停止使用普通失物流程作为唯一动作，立即走相应发证、支付机构、账号或设备的安全程序；本文不代替那些流程。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于有常规失物招领渠道的旅行场所和低敏感物品；处理方、保管期限、运费和认领证据随地区与服务而变。不能打电话可找官方表单、文字消息或现场可及的服务台；阅读或听力不便可用可访问页面或可信协助，但不要公开完整行程和身份资料。找到物品可能还要实际去取；“已报”只是让负责方有线索，不是一个自动归还按钮。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary, low-sensitivity item—such as a coat, umbrella, bottle, or book—goes missing during a trip. Identify who may have taken custody, send a description they can use, and leave a traceable follow-up. There is no recovery guarantee. Passports or ID, payment cards, account-bearing devices, hazardous items, and suspected theft need their relevant safety, service, or official processes; this Skill does not replace them.

### Preparation and inputs

From a safe, permitted place, recheck pockets, your bags, and any shared area a companion agrees to check. Do not search another person's private things. Record the **last event when you definitely held the item**, rather than when it “should still have been there.” List the few places between then and noticing the loss: lodging, a locker, vehicle or seat, station, shop, or venue. Note type, color, size, marks, and a detail that could prove ownership without publishing it. Give trip date, service number, seat, or room only to the responsible official channel as needed.

### Execution

1. **Narrow the time window, then order possible custodians.** List places after the last certain sighting. Separate “left on the vehicle,” “left in the station,” and “left with lodging or a shop”; vehicle, station, and third-party shop may handle found items separately. Contact the nearest plausible custodian first. If the location is uncertain, label it as a possibility rather than a fact.
2. **Find each official reporting route.** Use the operator's, property's, or venue's published lost-property page, booking messages, or staffed desk to learn whether it takes a form, call, or in-person report and when you can follow up. Leave vehicles and staff-only areas to staff; do not chase a moving vehicle, cross tracks, or send full identity details to a stranger offering help on social media.
3. **Report enough to identify, not to overexpose.** Give the date, plausible place, specific item, and distinctive but nonpublic feature. Add service number, station, or seat if that service asks. Keep one or two details for formal ownership verification. Report separately to multiple plausible custodians through their official channels, recording who received each report; one report is not automatically shared across a city. Amtrak issues a Lost Item ID, but whether another service issues a number must be checked.
4. **Separate submitted from found.** Record each custodian's report time, number or response route, and next reasonable check time. If there is no number, record how they actually replied. “We will look” is acceptance of a report, not a find. After a claimed match, verify the pickup place, hours, actual collection or shipping method, and any fee from that service's current instructions.
5. **Make one bounded follow-up.** Use the number at the agreed time. If no item is found, update the last response and decide whether another check is useful. Without a new lead, do not repeatedly flood one desk or enter a closed place. Do not claim an item that fails your description.

### Success

The most plausible official custodians have usable reports, or you have confirmed that no reporting route is presently available. Each lead is honestly marked “not reported / reported and pending / claimed match to verify / physically recovered / search closed,” with a next check point where useful. Recovery means you actually verified and received the item. A form submission or reference number is traceable progress, not proof of recovery.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You only recall “I had it this afternoon”:** Use the trip sequence, photos, or a companion's memory to find the last certain event. If still unsure, give a time range rather than inventing a minute.
- **A vehicle loss was sent only to a station shop:** Report to the likely operator too. Keep the original report; do not submit duplicates to the same service.
- **“A black umbrella” could match many:** Add size, handle, label, or other distinguishing features. Reserve sensitive proof for formal verification.
- **A stranger claims to have it and asks for money first:** Independently verify report number and any fee through the operator's or property's published channel, not a payment link in the message.
- **The item is actually an ID, payment card, or account-bearing device:** Stop treating an ordinary lost-property report as the only action. Follow the relevant issuer, payment institution, account, or device safety process immediately; this Skill does not supply that procedure.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers low-sensitivity items and ordinary lost-property channels during travel. Custody, retention time, shipping charges, and proof of ownership vary by service and location. If phone calls are inaccessible, use an official form, written message, or accessible desk. For reading or hearing barriers, use an accessible page or trusted help without publicizing your complete trip or ID. Finding the item may still require collection; “reported” is a lead for a custodian, not an automatic return button.
