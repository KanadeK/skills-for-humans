## 中文

当主要搬运已经结束、你要离开旧住处前确认普通随身物和家用物有没有遗漏时加载。结果是**可进入区域有一次实际逐区检查，每件发现的物品有已确认去向或明确待问状态**。这不决定别人东西归谁、物品永久去留、房屋修理或合同责任。

### 准备与输入

拿到本次搬迁的物品/箱件记录，确认哪些房间、柜子和附属空间你有权查看，谁能决定共同或未知物品的去向。准备简短清单，先写需查的区域，包括日常容易漏看的抽屉、柜内、门后、清扫工具暂放处等；不擅自进入上锁或他人的私人区域。

### 执行

1. **按区域逐一实看。** 从一个入口开始，检查你有权进入且能安全查看的每个房间、柜、抽屉与经许可的附属空间。看过才记“已查”；人够不到、看不清或无权打开的地方记“未查”，不能因为搬家公司离开就算空了。
2. **区分发现物与固定物。** 只记录你实际看到的可移动普通物品和大致位置；别把房屋自带物件或别人标明要留下的东西擅自搬走。物主不明或存在约定冲突时保留原位并问有权决定的人。
3. **给每件发现物一个真实路线。** 根据已确认的搬迁决定，将其交给随行者、安排经同意的后续领取，或列入待问。不要因“只剩一点”就塞进已封箱件或扔掉别人的东西。需要危险处理或重物搬动时停下，交合适的人。
4. **回看暂放区和最后使用物。** 核对清扫用品、充电线、随行袋等在最后一次使用后是否已按约定拿走；仍在用的物品由实际使用者说清最后接手人，不提前写“已走”。
5. **交出一张状态表。** 每个区域写“已查／未查”；每个发现物写“位置 → 有权决定者 → 已带走／待领取／待确认”。让同行者按其负责区域读回，留给交接人的是事实和待处理项，不是“什么都没有”的无依据保证。

### 完成与停止

你有权检查的区域都真实检查并标记，发现的普通物品没有匿名地留在角落；不可进入或无法辨认的区域仍明示未查。遇到不明所有权、危险内容或无法安全移动的大件时，不擅自处置，交给有权或专业的人决定。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只扫了一眼空房中央：** 补查有权限的柜内、门后和暂放处，再标对应区域已查。
- **发现共用物，不知谁带：** 标位置与可能联系人，保持待确认；不把先看到的人当物主。
- **同伴说已经拿了，但物品还在：** 以实见状态更正清单，确定谁来接走。
- **高处、暗处或封闭空间看不到：** 请获准且有能力的人协助检查；仍无法查明就记未查，不冒险攀爬或拆开结构。
- **旧住处还有最后使用物：** 写清最后使用者和交接点，离开前再核对一次。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只做普通搬迁的事实清点，不解释租约、弃置权或收费责任。各场所关于遗留物的流程不同，向实际接收方核对。阅读、行动或视力受限时可由获准的人现场描述和拍摄经许可的局部照片，你在可访问清单上核对；不要把“未亲见”写成已查。副作用是少了几次为一根充电线折返的机会。

## English

Load this Skill after the main move when you need to check an old place for ordinary personal or household items before leaving. Your outcome is **a real area-by-area check wherever you may enter, with a confirmed route or explicit question for every item found**. This does not guess another person's ownership, decide permanent disposal, repair the place or interpret a contract.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring the move or box list, confirm which rooms, cupboards and auxiliary areas you are allowed to inspect, and identify who may decide about shared or unknown items. Make a short area list, including easy-to-miss drawers, cupboards, behind doors and temporary cleaning-supply spots. Do not enter a locked or another person's private area without permission.

### Execution

1. **Look in each area you may inspect.** Starting at an entrance, check each accessible room, cupboard, drawer and authorised storage area that you can inspect safely. Mark it “checked” only after seeing it. Mark an unreachable, unreadable or unauthorised place “not checked”; a departed moving truck does not make it empty.
2. **Separate found goods from fixtures.** Record ordinary movable items you actually see and where they are. Do not take built-in property or something another person has agreed to leave. Keep an item in place and ask the authorised person if ownership or the plan is uncertain.
3. **Give each find a real route.** Based on decisions already made, hand it to the person carrying it, arrange a later collection with consent, or hold it for a decision. Do not slip “just one more thing” into a sealed box or discard somebody else's property. Stop and ask suitable help for hazardous content or heavy moving.
4. **Recheck holding spots and last-use items.** Check whether cleaning supplies, chargers or carry bags went with their intended person after last use. The actual user should name the final recipient for something still in use; do not pre-mark it gone.
5. **Hand over a status list.** For each area record “checked / not checked.” For each found item record “place → authorised decision-maker → taken / later collection / confirm first.” Have companions read back areas they covered. Give the receiver facts and open items, not an unsupported guarantee that nothing remains.

### Success and stop

Every area you were allowed to inspect was actually checked and marked, and ordinary finds are not anonymously left behind. Inaccessible or unreadable areas remain visibly “not checked.” Do not dispose of or take an item of unclear ownership, hazardous content or an object you cannot move safely; ask the authorised or qualified person.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the middle of each empty room was seen:** Check permitted cupboards, behind doors and holding areas before marking the area done.
- **A shared item has no agreed recipient:** Record its location and possible contact, and leave it to confirm. Seeing it first does not make it yours.
- **Someone says it was taken, but it is still there:** Correct the list to observed state and name who will collect it.
- **A high, dark or closed area cannot be viewed:** Ask a permitted capable person to inspect. If still unknown, mark not checked rather than climbing unsafely or dismantling the building.
- **A last-use item remains:** Name its final user and handoff point and check again before departure.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is factual counting during an ordinary move, not advice about leases, disposal rights or charges. Ask the actual receiver about their process for items left behind. If reading, mobility or sight is limited, an authorised person can describe the area or make permitted close-up photographs while you verify an accessible list. Do not mark an unseen area checked. A side effect is fewer return trips for a lone charger.
