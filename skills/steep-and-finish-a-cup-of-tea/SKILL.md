## 中文

当你手里有一款**食品级、带冲泡说明**的茶叶、茶包或普通草本浸泡饮品，想做一杯而不让材料一直泡到最后时，用这份 Skill。它处理的是浸泡和结束浸泡，不宣称提神、治疗或其他健康功效；不拿来鉴定自采植物能不能入口。

### 准备与输入

读手中包装关于用量、水温或水的状态、浸泡时长与是否需要过滤的说明；不同茶、草本产品和杯量不能用一套数字。准备可饮用水、适合热水的稳定杯或壶、取出茶包或滤出散叶的方法，以及能看见、听见或摸到的计时提醒。握力、视力或端热水不便时，使用稳定台面、小壶、带防烫把手的适用容器或可信协助；不要在满杯热水旁试探能不能抓稳。

### 执行

1. **先确认水与容器。** 水应可饮用，容器按其说明能承受本次热水，摆在不会碰倒的地方。热水由你会安全使用的设备准备；设备型号、最低水量和倒水方式服从它自己的说明。
2. **按这一款茶量取。** 用包装推荐的茶包数或散叶量配合实际杯量；没有可信说明时先用小量、短时间的可调整尝试，而不是把陌生植物加进杯里。不要把“越多越有效”当理由。
3. **让材料与水接触并开始计时。** 按手中产品要求的水状态和浸泡时间注水，避免把手置于水流和蒸汽上方。材料必须接触到水，但不必为了“加速”搅拌到杯子要溢出。
4. **在规定范围内检查。** 到包装建议的观察点，先看颜色和香气；只有降到可安全入口温度时才尝味。想淡些，下一次减少用量或时间；想浓些，按该产品允许的范围调整，不把一杯茶无限续泡当标准流程。
5. **主动结束浸泡。** 按说明取出茶包、滤出散叶，或把茶液倒离浸泡材料；确实能看到杯中不再继续泡叶。处理热茶包或滤具时避免挤压烫水。需要加奶或其他配料时只使用适用、状态可确认的食品，并按个人偏好加入。
6. **放到适宜饮用的状态。** 确认杯子稳、温度可安全入口、材料已按需要移除；不准备马上饮用或打算留到以后时，按添加物和当地食品安全指导决定保存，别把热饮随手放在台面当永久存储。

### 成功条件

你得到一杯按**这一款产品**的说明浸泡并结束萃取的饮品，能说出用了多少材料、何时停止、是否已可安全饮用。说明读不懂、水或容器不安全、热水无法稳妥操作时，停止制作同样是合格决定。

### 常见报错与补救

- **茶包一直留在杯里，味道继续变：** 在安全温度下按说明移除；下次设提醒，别把忘记计时当成“重口味偏好”。
- **不知道该用多热或泡多久：** 找到该产品包装或生产者说明再做，不把另一款茶的参数抄来。
- **散叶进入杯中影响饮用：** 用适合热液体的滤具或先把茶液倒离叶片；不要伸手进热杯捞。
- **杯具或热水拿不稳：** 先停手，把杯子放到稳定可及位置、减少一次倒入量或请人协助；不拿烫伤风险换一杯茶。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一杯普通食品饮品，不涉及药草鉴定、保健品功效、医疗或婴幼儿饮食建议。地区饮水、产品、杯具和文化偏好不同；以当地饮水条件、包装与设备说明为准。视觉或听觉提醒不可用时可用大字、震动或可信协助。加糖、奶或其他配料会改变风味与保存条件，不能再把它当纯茶处理。

## English

Use this Skill with **packaged food-grade tea or an ordinary infusion with brewing directions** when you want one cup whose steeping actually ends. It covers infusion and removing the material, not claims about alertness, treatment, or other health effects. It does not identify wild plants as safe to drink.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this package's directions for amount, water condition or temperature, steeping time, and whether the leaves need filtering. Different products and cup sizes do not share one set of numbers. Have drinking water, a stable heat-suitable cup or pot, a way to remove the bag or loose leaves, and an accessible timer. If gripping, seeing, or pouring hot water is difficult, use a stable surface, a smaller kettle, an appropriate cool-handled vessel, or trusted help. Do not test your grip beside a full hot cup.

### Execution

1. **Check water and vessel first.** Water must be suitable to drink, and the container must tolerate the hot water as directed. Put it where it cannot easily tip. Prepare water with equipment you know how to use safely; its exact model instructions govern minimum fill and pouring.
2. **Measure this product.** Match its recommended bags or loose-leaf amount to your actual cup. If trustworthy directions are missing, use only a small adjustable trial with a known food-grade product, not an unidentified plant. “More must be better” is not a brewing instruction.
3. **Bring water and leaves together and start timing.** Use this product's water and time directions, keeping hands away from the stream and steam. Wet the brewing material without stirring so forcefully that the cup spills.
4. **Check within its suggested range.** At the package's check point, inspect colour and aroma. Taste only when the drink has cooled enough to sip safely. For a lighter cup next time, adjust amount or time; for a stronger one, stay within the product's supported range instead of treating endless steeping as standard.
5. **End the infusion deliberately.** Remove the bag, strain loose leaves, or decant the drink away from them as directed. Confirm the leaves are no longer steeping in your cup. Avoid squeezing hot liquid from a bag or filter. Add milk or other ingredients only when they are suitable food of known condition and you want them.
6. **Let it become drinkable.** The cup is stable, temperature comfortable for drinking, and the material removed where required. If you will not drink it soon, follow the ingredients' and local food-safety guidance for keeping it; a counter is not permanent storage for a prepared drink.

### Success

You have a cup brewed and finished according to **this product's** directions, and can say how much material you used, when steeping stopped, and whether it is safe to sip. Unreadable directions, unsafe water or vessel, or an unmanageable hot pour call for a clear stop instead.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The bag stays in the cup and flavour keeps changing:** Remove it safely as the product directs. Use a reminder next time rather than treating forgotten timing as a deliberate preference.
- **You do not know the water condition or steeping time:** Find directions for this product or its maker. Do not copy settings from a different tea.
- **Loose leaves make the cup hard to drink:** Use a strainer suitable for hot liquid or decant away from leaves. Do not reach into hot water with your fingers.
- **The cup or hot water is difficult to handle:** Stop, move to a stable reachable surface, pour less at once, or use trusted help. A cup of tea is not worth a burn.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary food drink, not herb identification, supplement efficacy, medical advice, or infant feeding guidance. Drinking water, products, vessels, and cultural preferences vary. Follow local water suitability and the exact package and appliance. Large print, vibration, or trusted help can replace an inaccessible visual or audio reminder. Milk, sugar, or other additions change flavour and possibly storage conditions; do not keep treating the result as plain tea.
