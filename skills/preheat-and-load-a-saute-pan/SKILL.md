## 中文

当锅具、食材和工具已经[冷态就位](../prepare-a-pan-for-sauteing/SKILL.md)，可靠配方要求先预热再下第一批普通食物时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**在配方和锅具允许的热度下让食物稳妥接触锅面，油不冒烟、热源仍可控制**。它不提供所有锅通用的“几分钟/几档火”，不做深油炸或高温专业锅法。

### 准备与输入

确认配方对预热、加油先后、油量和首批食物的要求；核对锅材/涂层的制造商热度限制。第一批食物已经按说明准备，需沥去表面多余水分的已做；用具和计时器都在手边。食物若需要特殊食品安全核心温度，先知道如何在后续[熟度核对](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)，不以煎上色替代。

### 执行步骤

1. **按说明预热，不让空锅无人看守。** 使用配方和锅具允许的火力，人在旁观察。锅具不允许空烧时照说明调整加油顺序；不要照抄别家锅的档位，也不要为了“更热”越过制造商限制。
2. **按配方放入少量适用油。** 只有配方需要油时才加，并让它在锅面形成所需薄层；不要倒成一锅油来证明煎炒。油开始冒烟、变色异常或有焦味时，在安全可达时立即关热，按[冒烟锅停点](../stop-a-smoking-pan/SKILL.md)处理，不先把食物扔进去压住烟。
3. **让第一批食物靠近锅面入锅。** 用合适工具将食物从靠近锅面处放下，避免从高处抛入油里；身体和旁人避开可能飞溅的方向。一次下锅量以配方和锅面可翻动空间为准，剩余食物等下一批。
4. **观察第一反应。** 配方若要求轻微持续的煎炒声或上色，就在食品入锅后观察；无反应不等于必须把火打满，先核对锅是否过冷、食物是否太多或油量/顺序是否正确。烟、剧烈飞溅和锅柄不稳是停点，不是“火候到了”。
5. **按食物入锅事件开始后续计时。** 预热用掉的时间不属于食物受热时间；按配方记录首批真正接触锅面的时刻，再进入翻动和完成判断。不能确定起点时不把“约摸差不多”升级成安全熟度证据。

### 完成与停止

第一批食物已在锅中受控加热，热源可调整、锅面有可翻动空间，油无烟，并且你知道该批的计时起点与下一观察点，就完成下锅。锅具发烟、油剧烈异常、食物溅向人、锅不稳或缺可用翻动工具时先关热停下；有明火或燃气异味按当地紧急指导处理，不继续本 Skill。

### 常见报错与补救

- **锅空烧太久：** 关热并按锅具说明等它安全稳定；不以更大火力追回等待时间。
- **油刚进锅就起烟：** 立即停止热输入；等安全后检查锅、油和配方，别用加食物或泼水处理。
- **食物一下锅油大幅飞溅：** 退到安全位置并关热；待稳定后核对食物表面水分和下料动作，不在热锅边继续试探。
- **下了整包食物，锅声瞬间消失：** 不用最大火硬拉回；按配方与安全条件分批，承认第一批起点已不同。
- **食物表面上色就想吃：** 依食物对应终点和必要温度核对，颜色只是一个线索。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只处理少油、可控的普通浅锅煎炒。锅材、炉头、油与食物改变热响应，没有通用烟点或精确档位可套所有组合；制造商说明及可靠配方优先。看不清油状态时可在安全距离观察气味/声音并请现场可信的人协助，但不靠把手伸到锅上方试温；握持不稳时用适合的夹具、较小批量或换技法。现实副作用是第一批可能晚一分钟下锅，却少一锅需要抢救的烟。

## English

Load this Skill after [cold setup](../prepare-a-pan-for-sauteing/SKILL.md) when you have a reliable recipe that calls for preheating before the first batch of ordinary food enters a shallow pan. Put food onto the pan **under heat the recipe and cookware permit, without smoking oil or losing control of the hob**. No universal preheat minutes or burner setting is supplied. This is not deep frying or an extreme-heat professional wok method.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the recipe's preheat, oil order and amount, and first-batch instruction; check the pan material or coating maker's heat limits. The first food is prepared as directed, including draining excess surface moisture if required. Tools and timer are within reach. If a food needs a safe core temperature, plan to use [doneness guidance](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md) later; a brown outside does not replace it.

### Execution steps

1. **Preheat under directions while attending the empty pan.** Use heat allowed by the recipe and cookware, and stay present. If the pan's directions prohibit empty heating, adjust the oil order accordingly. Do not copy another pan's dial setting or exceed a maker limit for “extra heat.”
2. **Add only the suitable small amount of oil directed.** Spread it over the needed cooking surface. A pool of oil is not evidence of sautéing. If it smokes, discolours abnormally, or smells scorched, switch off heat when safe and use [Stop a Smoking Pan](../stop-a-smoking-pan/SKILL.md). Do not throw food in to hide smoke.
3. **Lower the first batch close to the surface.** Use a suitable tool to place food near the pan rather than dropping it from above oil. Keep people and your body out of the likely splatter path. Add only the amount the recipe and pan can hold with room to turn; the rest can wait as another batch.
4. **Observe the first response.** If the recipe expects a gentle continuing sizzle or browning, assess it after food enters. A quiet pan is not an instruction to turn the hob to maximum. Check whether the pan is too cool, the batch too large, or the oil and order wrong. Smoke, violent splatter, or an unstable handle is a stop point, not “heat ready.”
5. **Start later timing from food contact.** Preheating time is not food cooking time. Record when this batch actually touched the pan and the recipe's next observation. An unknown start must not be promoted into safe-doneness evidence.

### Success and stop

The first batch is heating under control with turning room, adjustable heat, no smoking oil, and a known timing start and next check. Switch off and pause for smoke, abnormal oil, splatter toward people, pan instability, or no usable turning tool. Flame or gas odour leaves this Skill for local emergency guidance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The empty pan heats too long:** Stop heat and let it settle under the cookware directions. More heat cannot recover waiting time.
- **Oil smokes as soon as it enters:** End heat input. When safe, check pan, oil, and recipe instead of adding food or water.
- **Food causes major splatter:** Move to a safe position and stop heat. After control returns, check the food's surface moisture and how it was added; do not keep experimenting beside the hot pan.
- **The whole pack enters and the pan falls silent:** Do not use maximum heat to force it back. Divide batches as the recipe and safety permit; this first batch's conditions have changed.
- **A browned surface looks ready to eat:** Use the food's actual endpoint and required temperature. Colour is only one clue.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary shallow-pan cooking with a little oil. Pan, hob, oil, and food affect heat response, so no smoke point or dial setting is universal. Maker instructions and the reliable recipe come first. If oil is hard to see, observe sound and odour from a safe distance or ask someone present to help. Do not reach over the pan to test heat with a hand. Use a suitable tool, smaller batch, or another method if grip is unreliable. The first batch may enter a minute later and avoid a kitchen full of smoke.
