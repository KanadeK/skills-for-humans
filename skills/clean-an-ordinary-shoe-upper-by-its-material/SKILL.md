## 中文

当一双自有普通鞋的**鞋面有日常灰土或轻微污渍**，且你能辨认帆布/网布、光面皮或麂皮等材质时，加载这份 Skill。结果是以本鞋允许的方法去掉可去污物、表面不被浸坏；不同材质是同一清洁任务的条件，不能按鞋色或品牌复制 Skill。材质/涂层不明、专业防护鞋和已开胶鞋先停，查厂家或维修者。

### 准备与输入

读鞋标、产品页或随鞋护理说明，确认主要鞋面和装饰部分是否不同。备软干刷、干净布、厂家允许的少量清洁液及不怕染的垫板。先取下可拆鞋带以露出边缘；水、清洁液和刷法在隐蔽处小试。不要直接把整鞋放洗衣机，除非该型号明确允许；也不把 Clarks 某款配方当所有鞋通用。

### 执行

1. **先去松散干土。** 在通风稳固台面轻刷鞋面表土与接缝，刷毛不逆向猛刮；沙粒先去掉再湿擦，以免磨花材质。若有尖锐金属嵌入或表层剥落，停止普通清洁。
2. **按实际材质分支。** 可湿清的帆布/网布按该鞋说明用少量温和兼容清洁液与软布/刷点擦，别浸泡胶合处；光面皮用该皮面允许的微湿布或专用品轻擦，避免透湿；麂皮/反绒优先干燥状态下用合用刷沿纤维轻理，不照搬帆布水洗法。混合材质逐区按最脆弱部分处理。
3. **去残留并自然干。** 对允许水的区用微湿洁布去残液，麂皮按其专用说明而非直接冲水。让鞋在室内通风处自然干，远离散热器、吹风机和强日晒；湿时不穿、不封进鞋盒。
4. **核对结果与材料。** 干后看污物是否减少、表面有无掉色、起毛、变形或开胶；未去掉的深污记真实状态并转专业/厂家指导，不用强溶剂或不断加水。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

可去的日常污物已按材质处理、鞋面干、无新明显损伤；顽固斑仍在可诚实停止。把麂皮洗成水渍或把胶合处泡开，不算清洁成功。

- **材质或护理标签不明：** 不用通用清洁剂试整鞋，先查厂家。
- **小试掉色/发硬：** 停止该清洁液与刷法，让区域干后求合用方案。
- **麂皮一遇水有新痕：** 停止湿擦，自然干后按本材质专用说明处理。
- **鞋面仍湿或残留皂：** 先按标签轻去残液并晾干，不急着穿。
- **手眼操作不便：** 大面积软布、充足光和可信协助分辨材质，由你核对损伤和停点。

### 假设与边界

厂商产品与材质说明优先。鞋面污渍未必能完全去除，清洁也不能修复破损鞋底或证明防滑、防水性能。鞋底沟槽、鞋垫和鞋带分别有自己的结果。

## English

Load this Skill when an owned ordinary shoe's **upper has everyday dust or a light mark** and you can identify canvas/mesh, smooth leather, suede, or another actual material. Finish with removable dirt reduced under the pair's allowed method and no new soaking damage. Materials are branches of one cleaning task, not separate titles by brand or colour. Stop and consult the maker or repairer for unknown coatings, protective footwear, or a sole already separating.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the shoe label, product page, or supplied care directions, including whether trim differs from the main upper. Have a soft dry brush, clean cloth, a little maker-compatible cleaner, and a stain-tolerant board. Remove detachable laces for access. Patch-test water, cleaner, and brush action in an inconspicuous spot. Do not machine-wash a whole shoe unless that exact model permits it, and do not universalize a Clarks product recipe.

### Execution

1. **Remove loose dry soil first.** On a stable aired surface, gently brush surface grit and seams, without harsh against-grain abrasion. Remove sand before a damp wipe so it will not scratch. Stop ordinary cleaning for a sharp embedded object or peeling surface.
2. **Branch by real material.** For wet-cleanable canvas/mesh, use a little permitted mild cleaner with soft cloth or brush, without soaking glued joins. For smooth leather, wipe lightly with its approved barely damp cloth or care product without saturating. For suede or nubuck, favour a suitable dry brush along the nap under its directions rather than the canvas wash route. Work section by section on mixed materials, respecting the most delicate trim.
3. **Remove residue and air dry.** On water-tolerant areas, wipe permitted cleaner away with a barely damp clean cloth; follow suede-specific instructions instead of rinsing it under a tap. Dry indoors with airflow away from radiators, hair dryers, and strong sun. Do not wear or box wet shoes.
4. **Check the result and material.** Once dry, look for reduced dirt, colour loss, pilling, distortion, or loose adhesive. Record a stubborn mark honestly and seek maker or professional help rather than adding strong solvent or endless water.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Removable ordinary dirt has been treated for the material, the upper is dry, and no obvious new damage appeared. A resistant stain can remain with a truthful stop. Water-marking suede or soaking a glued seam is not successful cleaning.

- **Material or care label is unclear:** Do not test a generic cleaner over the whole shoe; find maker directions first.
- **Patch test removes colour or stiffens material:** Stop that product and brush action, dry the area, and find a suitable route.
- **Suede develops a water mark:** Stop wet wiping, air dry, then follow material-specific guidance.
- **Upper is still wet or soapy:** Remove residue gently as permitted and dry before wear.
- **Grip or vision is limited:** Use a broad soft cloth, good light, and trusted material-identification help while you inspect damage and choose to stop.

### Assumptions and limits

Maker and material directions control the process. Some stains cannot be fully removed. Cleaning does not repair torn soles or establish slip resistance or waterproofing. Sole grooves, inserts, and laces have separate outcomes.
