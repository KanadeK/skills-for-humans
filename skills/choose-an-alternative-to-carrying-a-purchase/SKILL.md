## 中文

当你已确定需要哪件普通食品或日用品，商品形式也不能再减轻到自己带得回家，但尚未付款时，用这份 Skill 选择**取得方式**：例如店家送货、可信的人代带，或仍能完成最后一段的取货点。它只筛选付款前可行的方式；实际下单、物流跟踪、签收与商家交涉留给相应流程。

### 准备与输入

写下准确商品、这次需要的数量与最晚使用时间。确认你真正做不到的是哪一段：店内拿取、从店到家、从大门到室内，还是冷藏食品在途中和到家后的储存。列出你所在地**实际可用**的两三种方式、可接受的总费用和接收时段。若不方便使用网站，可查商家公开说明、询问服务台或使用可信的人协助查询；查询不等于把账号密码交出去。

### 执行

1. **固定商品，不假装它已变轻。** 核对候选方式能提供你选定的品名、规格和数量。若允许商家换货，先写清哪些替代可接受；无法确认关键规格时，此方式仍未合格。商品本身还能换形式时，可先尝试商品替代的流程。
2. **画完最后一段路。** 店员协助拿上货架、路边取货或送到楼门口，仍可能把重物留给你。对每种方式确认商品会到达哪个可实际接收的位置、谁完成门槛与楼梯、是否需要你在现场等候。只选“交到你能接手的点”的方式；配送二字不会自动修复楼梯。
3. **核对可得性和全部费用。** 在承诺前查看当地当前是否提供此服务、可送/取时段、最低购买额、配送或代带费用、所需身份条件，以及费用是否仍在预算内。不要为了凑最低额加入本来不需要的商品。朋友或熟人代带也要先确认对方愿意、能带的数量和交付时间；不能把尚未答应当作已安排。
4. **遇到需冷藏品，单独过关。** 问清配送或代带过程是否能满足该商品的温度要求、何时有人接收，以及接收后是否有可靠冷藏。**没有家中冷藏时，送货不能把易腐品变成常温品。** 低温条件不能确认，就不把该方式用于这件商品；改选合格常温品或延期。
5. **选出一个可执行方案。** 记录商品、数量、方式、总费用、预计时间、交付点和待确认事项。关键条件已核实才付款或请人帮忙；如果时段、费用、交付点、冷藏或对方意愿不成立，输出“暂不取得”，再改需求或时间。停止比把困难转移到门口更诚实。

### 成功条件

你有一条在当前地点、预算、时间与身体条件下可行的取得路径，能说出**谁把哪件商品交到哪里**，以及何时仍需正式下单或确认；或者明确决定暂不买。此时还没有宣称货已到手。

### 常见报错与补救

- **路边取货仍要你搬回家：** 退回第 2 步，改查可送到可接收位置的服务、可信协助或暂缓。
- **网站写“可送货”，但时间、费用或地址不适用：** 按你当前订单和地点核实；不能核实就先不付款。
- **“放门口”不是可接收点：** 在承诺前确认可到达的位置和有人接收的时段；无法保证时改方式。事后交付争议归物流或售后流程。
- **代带的人临时无法来：** 把该方式从候选中删掉，重算时间与费用；不要把对方的善意记成库存。
- **冷藏品送来时无人接收或家中无冷藏：** 付款前改时段、改合格常温商品，或取消这项购买；已到货的温度与安全处置不属于本 Skill。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

店家服务、费用、可用时段和送达楼层依地区与具体订单而变；来源只说明需核对的条件，不保证你所在地有相同服务。网页、电话、店内询问或可信协助都可用于取得信息；需要账号或付款时走正规渠道，不把密码交给代带者。别人有权拒绝帮忙，拒绝后仍可得到完整的“暂不买”结果。某些方案更贵、等待更久或需要额外沟通，这些都是现实成本，不是你的个人失败。

## English

Use this Skill **before paying** when the ordinary grocery or household item is already chosen and changing its form will not make it possible for you to carry it home. Choose a **way to obtain it**: shop delivery, a trusted person willing to bring it, or a pickup point whose final leg you can manage. This Skill screens options before commitment. Placing an order, tracking it, receiving it, and resolving disputes are separate jobs.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the exact item, amount, and latest useful arrival time. Identify the impossible segment: reaching it in the shop, travelling home, getting it from the building entrance indoors, or keeping a chilled item cold in transit and afterward. List two or three options **actually available where you live**, your total cost ceiling, and when you can receive it. If a website is inaccessible, check published shop information, ask the service desk, or get trusted help with the inquiry. An inquiry does not require giving someone your account password.

### Execution

1. **Hold the item fixed.** Confirm each option can supply the chosen identity, specification, and amount. If the seller may substitute, state which alternatives you would accept. An option with an unconfirmed essential specification is not yet viable. If a different product form would solve the problem, consider that separate product-substitution route first.
2. **Trace the final leg.** Help retrieving an item, curbside pickup, or delivery to the building entrance may still leave you carrying it. For each option, find the place where the item will be handed over, who covers steps and thresholds, and whether you must wait there. Choose only a point you can actually receive from. The word “delivery” does not carry a bag upstairs.
3. **Check availability and full cost.** Before committing, confirm the service serves your location and this order, the delivery or pickup slot, minimum order, service or helper cost, any eligibility conditions, and your actual total. Do not add unwanted items just to reach a minimum. For a friend or other trusted helper, confirm they agree, can handle the amount, and know the time. A person who has not agreed is not a logistics plan.
4. **Give chilled food its own check.** Ask whether the service or helper can meet the product's cold requirements, whether someone can receive it promptly, and whether you have reliable cold storage afterward. **Delivery does not turn perishables into shelf-stable food when your fridge is unavailable.** If you cannot confirm the cold conditions, reject this route for that item; choose a suitable shelf-stable product or defer it.
5. **Choose one workable path.** Note the item, amount, method, total cost, expected time, handoff point, and anything still awaiting confirmation. Pay or request help only once essential conditions are confirmed. If timing, cost, handoff, cold storage, or someone's willingness fails, record “not obtaining it yet” and revisit the need or date. Stopping is better than moving the problem to your front door.

### Success

You have a feasible path for your location, budget, time, and physical needs. You can state **who will hand which item to you, and where**, plus what still needs a formal order or confirmation. Explicitly deciding not to buy is also complete. You have not claimed that the goods have arrived.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Curbside pickup still leaves the load for you:** Return to the final-leg check. Look for delivery to a reachable point, agreed trusted help, or defer.
- **A site says “delivery” but the slot, fee, or address does not qualify:** Verify against this order and location. If you cannot, do not pay yet.
- **“Leave at door” is not a usable handoff point:** Confirm a reachable point and an attended time before committing; otherwise change method. A dispute after delivery belongs to the logistics or after-sales workflow.
- **Your helper becomes unavailable:** Remove that option and recalculate time and cost. Goodwill is not stock on hand.
- **No one can receive chilled food, or there is no cold storage at home:** Change the time, choose a valid shelf-stable item, or cancel this purchase before paying. Safety assessment of food already delivered is outside this Skill.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Services, charges, slots, and delivery floor vary by region and order. The sources identify questions to check, not a service guaranteed in your area. Websites, a phone call, in-store questions, and trusted assistance are alternative information routes. Use ordinary service channels for account access and payment; do not hand passwords to a helper. Another person can decline to help, and “not buying yet” remains a complete outcome. An option may cost more, take longer, or require more coordination. Those are real costs, not personal failure.
