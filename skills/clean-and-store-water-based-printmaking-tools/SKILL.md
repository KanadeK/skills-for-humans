## 中文

当一次**普通水基可洗版画**结束，版面、钝工具仍沾色，纸印也尚湿时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按材料说明洗净并晾好的版/工具、互不粘连的印张，以及已保护的台面。这比 H280 一般创作收工多了版的凹线、光滑板残色、印张间隔三个具体检查；不适用于油墨、溶剂或锐利刻版工具。

### 准备与输入

分清泡棉版、光滑板和各自画材的清洗说明；有些泡棉不能泡水，先小试擦拭。准备软布、清水、合用温和清洁用品（仅产品允许时）、独立晾纸面和不会压坏版的存放处。水杯与餐饮分开，按当地规则处理颜料水；未干印张别堆叠。

### 执行

1. **先保护印张。** 把每张湿印平放在清洁干燥的独立位置，写上真实顺序/试印标签；手拿纸边，避免指印蹭图案。没有晾位就先给已印张找位置，再清版。
2. **清掉版上的可洗颜色。** 对泡棉凹线先用软布轻擦凸面与凹处，不把钝笔反向划入原凹线；对光滑板按材料说明擦/冲残色。需要水时少量逐步用，避免泡棉吸水变形或颜料漫流到台面。
3. **洗钝工具和工作区。** 按产品说明清滚筒、笔、塑料调色板，再擦护台垫，检查有无溅色；锐器、机器和油性材料若混入，停止本普通流程并按其专用指引处理。
4. **全干后存放。** 版与工具表面不再掉可洗色，干燥后平放版材、不压凹纹或弯折。印张彻底干、互不粘时再入夹；写版号与材料，方便下次找到同一版。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

普通水基颜料已从版/工具可洗部位去掉、版未伤、干燥入存，印张分开放至干；台面无本次残色。只把版擦一下却把湿印张叠成一团，不算完成。

- **泡棉擦拭时起毛或凹线受损：** 减压、停水，按版材说明改干/微湿擦；破版不再称可复用。
- **光滑板留色：** 查板与颜料的相容清洁法，不用未经确认的强溶剂。
- **印张开始粘一起：** 在未完全干且可安全分开时轻揭，平放重晾；已粘牢别猛撕。
- **台面/衣物染色：** 按材质和颜料标签立即处理，不靠烈性化学品试运气。
- **手眼/空间受限：** 少量印张分批晾、用大间隔与可信帮助清版，由你核对版完整和印张数量。

### 假设与边界

NGA 的水溶性或丙烯材料清洁与油墨不同，Getty 泡棉/马克笔材料也需按自身说明。清洁不能保证修复已经刺穿的版；保管原作不等于档案级保存或版权许可。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary **washable water-based print session** ends with pigment on plates and blunt tools and paper impressions still wet. Finish with plates and tools cleaned and dried under their material directions, prints drying apart, and a protected clean surface. This is more specific than H280's general creative close: foam recesses, smooth-plate residue, and print separation each need checks. Oil ink, solvents, and sharp carving tools are outside scope.

### Preparation and inputs

Separate foam and smooth plates and read cleaning directions for each paint. Some foam should not soak; test a gentle wipe. Set out soft cloth, water, mild cleaner only if allowed, separate paper drying spots, and plate storage without pressure on relief. Keep art water separate from drinks and dispose of it under local rules. Do not stack wet impressions.

### Execution

1. **Protect prints first.** Lay each wet sheet flat in its own clean dry place with true order or proof label. Hold edges to avoid fingerprints. Find drying space for existing prints before spending time cleaning the plate.
2. **Remove washable colour from plates.** Wipe foam raised areas and recesses gently with soft cloth without retracing dents with a hard point. Wipe or rinse a smooth plate under its maker's rules. Add water only as needed to avoid deforming foam or spreading pigment over furniture.
3. **Clean blunt tools and area.** Clean rollers, brushes, and plastic palettes according to product directions, then wipe the board and check splashes. If sharp equipment, a press, or oil ink entered this session, stop this ordinary route and use their specific instructions.
4. **Store only when dry.** When removable colour is gone and surfaces are dry, lay plates flat without crushing dents or bending the sheet. File impressions only once fully dry and non-sticking. Note plate identifier and material for next time.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Washable pigment has left plates and tools without damage, dry plate/tools are stored, sheets dry separately, and the surface has no session residue. Wiping a plate while wet prints stick together is unfinished.

- **Foam pills or a recess gets damaged while wiping:** Use less pressure and water, following plate directions. A torn plate is not reusable.
- **Smooth plate retains colour:** Check its material and paint cleaning method rather than using an unknown strong solvent.
- **Prints begin to stick:** If still safely separable, lift gently and dry apart. Do not yank sheets already bonded.
- **Table or clothes stain:** Respond promptly under surface and paint directions, not untested harsh chemicals.
- **Grip, sight, or space is limited:** Dry fewer sheets per batch with wide gaps and trusted plate-cleaning help while you verify plate integrity and sheet count.

### Assumptions and limits

NGA's water-soluble or acrylic cleanup differs from oil ink, and Getty's foam/marker needs its own product care. Cleaning cannot repair a punctured plate. Filing an original is neither archive-grade conservation nor copyright licence.
