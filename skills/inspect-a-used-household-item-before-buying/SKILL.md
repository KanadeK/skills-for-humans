## 中文

当你准备买一本旧书、普通收纳篮或其他**非电气、非安全关键**的二手日用品，想确认这件实物是否还能完成用途时，加载这份 Skill。输出是接受、补问或放弃这件物品；不鉴定真伪，不维修，不替代型号尺寸核对，也不承诺看得见的检查能发现隐藏缺陷。

婴幼儿用品、防护用品、承重安全关键物品、机械或电气设备、接触食品且材质状态不明的物品不适用。它们的风险不能靠这份简短外观检查放行。

### 准备与输入

先写下你要它做什么、缺了哪些部件就不能用、哪些外观磨损可接受、你是否有时间清洁，以及拿取和带回的条件。查看的是卖家**这件**物品的照片和描述，不是厂商样图。若规格是否适用还没确定，先做规格核对。

### 执行

1. **把描述变成核对表。** 记下卖家声称的状态、附带部件、明显瑕疵和任何改动。标出你必须亲眼看到或通过清晰照片确认的部位；“九成新”不是部件清单。
2. **看实物证据。** 线上请看这件物品的正反面、边角、接合处、瑕疵处和必要配件；库存示意图不能证明个体状态。线下在卖家允许、环境安全时查看实物。若只给一个角度或拒绝展示关键部位，标为未核实。
3. **分清外观与功能。** 可接受的划痕不会自动使物品失效；裂缝、松脱、缺件、受潮痕迹、无法清洁的污损或与你用途相关的损坏要单列。不要为了检查而拆开、通电、攀坐、负重或进行危险测试。
4. **只试正常的低风险动作。** 在卖家允许时，轻轻开合原有盖、拉链、扣件或提手，确认它们能做你需要的基本动作；没法安全试就记为未试，不凭想象补上结果。不能直接触摸或看清时，可请同行者描述、索取近照或选择能查看的另一件。
5. **核对取回后的代价。** 已知缺件、清洁负担和运回方式是否仍在你事先接受的范围内？若要靠未知修理、额外部件或“回家再想办法”才可用，就先补问信息或放弃，不把修复希望写成商品状态。
6. **给出决定。** **接受**：必要部件和可见状态符合用途，剩余不确定性可接受；**补问**：缺少关键照片或说明，在付款前取得答案；**放弃**：已见损坏、风险边界不合或无法核实关键条件。记录哪一条证据决定了结果。

### 成功条件

你能指出这件二手物品的实物证据、必要部件、可接受磨损、仍未知的地方和购买决定。没有成交也算完成；最佳角度的照片不能替物品上班。

### 常见报错、停止与补救

- **照片只有厂商样图：** 索取实物各面及瑕疵近照；没有就保持未核实，不先付款赌成色。
- **卖家说“能用”，但缺关键部件：** 要求确认准确部件和功能；你无法独立补齐时放弃。
- **现场比照片更旧或有新损坏：** 以当前实物为准，重新做接受/补问/放弃决定；不要靠已经到场的时间成本硬买。
- **看见疑似安全相关改装或明显危险磨损：** 停止检查并离开本 Skill，不自行测试、维修或推断安全。
- **无法访问实物或必要细节：** 使用清晰近照、文字描述或可信同行协助；仍无法确认就放弃这件，不把视觉或行动能力当门槛。

### 假设、可及替代与现实副作用

地区和平台对二手交易、展示与退回的规则不同，需查当前卖家的实际条件；本 Skill 不解释法律权利。外观检查只能记录可见事实，不能证明没有内部缺陷。若商品的安全依赖你看不见的内部结构、完整使用史或专业检验，就使用对应权威流程或选择其他商品。

副作用是可能少买一件，也可能把清洁时间诚实地算进决定。

## English

Load this Skill before paying for a used book, ordinary storage basket, or another **non-powered, non-safety-critical** household item when you need to know whether this particular object can still do its job. Your output is accept, ask for evidence, or leave. This does not authenticate goods, repair them, replace a size/model check, or promise that visible inspection reveals hidden faults.

Baby products, protective gear, items whose load-bearing safety matters, mechanical or electrical equipment, and food-contact items with unknown material condition are outside this check. A short visual inspection cannot clear their risks.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the job you need done, parts whose absence would defeat it, cosmetic wear you can accept, time available for cleaning, and how you can collect and carry the item. Inspect photos and descriptions of **this object**, not a maker's stock picture. Check size and compatibility separately if they are still unknown.

### Execution

1. **Turn the listing into a checklist.** Record the stated condition, included pieces, disclosed flaws, and any changes made to the item. Mark the parts you need to see directly or in clear photos. “Like new” is not a parts list.
2. **Find evidence of the actual item.** Online, look for front, back, edges, joins, flaws, and essential accessories. A stock image cannot establish this item's condition. In person, inspect it only with permission and in a safe setting. If a critical area is hidden or only one angle is shown, mark it unconfirmed.
3. **Separate appearance from function.** An acceptable scratch need not defeat use. Record cracks, loose joins, missing pieces, dampness, dirt you cannot reasonably clean, and damage relevant to your job separately. Do not dismantle, power on, climb, load-test, or perform a risky experiment for this check.
4. **Try only ordinary low-risk actions.** With the seller's permission, gently open and close an existing lid, zip, clasp, or handle to see whether it performs the basic action you need. If you cannot test safely, mark it untested. If seeing or handling it is inaccessible, ask for a detailed description or close-up images, use a trusted companion, or choose another inspectable item.
5. **Count what bringing it home requires.** Do known missing parts, cleaning effort, and transport still fit limits you chose beforehand? If the item needs unknown repair, extra parts, or “I'll work it out later” to function, ask for facts before paying or leave. Hope is not a condition grade.
6. **Decide.** **Accept:** essential parts and visible condition fit the intended use, with remaining uncertainty you can accept. **Ask:** a critical photo or explanation is missing; get it before paying. **Leave:** damage, a safety boundary, or an uncheckable hard condition rules this item out. Record the evidence that decided it.

### Success

You can state the actual-item evidence, essential parts, acceptable wear, remaining unknowns, and your decision. A purchase is not required. The listing's best angle cannot do the item's job.

### Common errors, stop points, and recovery

- **Only stock pictures are shown:** Ask for real photos from several sides and close-ups of flaws. Without them, keep condition unconfirmed rather than paying first.
- **The seller says it works but a key part is absent:** Confirm the exact missing part and its role. Leave if you cannot independently obtain what you need.
- **The item is worse in person than in the photos:** Use its current condition and decide again; the time spent reaching it is not a reason to buy.
- **A possible safety-related modification or dangerous wear appears:** Stop this inspection. Do not test, repair, or infer that the item is safe.
- **The item or needed detail is inaccessible:** Use clear close-ups, a written description, or a trusted companion. If the hard condition remains unknown, leave this item; sensory or mobility access is not a failing.

### Assumptions, accessible alternatives, and side effects

Second-hand display and return rules vary by seller, platform, and region; check the current terms. This Skill gives no legal interpretation. Visual inspection records observable facts, not the absence of internal faults. If safety depends on hidden structure, complete use history, or professional inspection, use the relevant authoritative process or choose another item.

The side effect may be one less purchase and a more honest allowance for cleaning time.
