## 中文

当一次来访、活动或临时工作已经结束，原本共用的空间要还给下一位使用者时加载。你要**移走这次带来的普通物品与可处理的痕迹、恢复获准改变的布局，并实看空间能否照常使用**。这里不做长期清洁轮值、处分他人物品、危险污染处理或重型家具搬运。

### 准备与输入

找开始前约定的可用范围、原状图或相关人的记忆，以及场所对归位、垃圾和清理用品的现行要求。确认哪些东西是你们带来的、哪些属于他人或场所，谁有权移动它们。没有原状记录时向有权使用者核对，不靠“反正差不多”改变共用区。

### 执行

1. **从入口看受影响范围。** 对照约定或原状，找本次遗留的普通垃圾、自己的物件、临时标记与获准挪动的轻量物品。仍有人在使用的部分先问，不把下一位使用者的东西当遗留物。
2. **先处理自己的可移动物。** 把个人物件交还主人或带走，按场所规定处理普通垃圾。借来的东西按有权者约定归还；不擅自丢弃无主但来源不明的物品，标位置并问负责人。
3. **恢复允许且安全的布置。** 将获准移动的轻量桌椅或设施按原状/约定位置放回。需要多人、重物、固定设施、拆装或可能伤人的改变交给实际负责者，不为了赶时间单独试搬。
4. **处理普通可清理的使用痕迹。** 按物品标签和场所规则，用适用的常规用品处理你们造成的小范围普通污迹；不混合清洁剂，不处理未知或危险污染。无法安全处理时隔离受影响位置并通知有权人员。
5. **让下一轮使用成立。** 走一遍入口、门、通道和原本要用的台面/座位，确认可进入、取用且没有你们的东西挡路。可请受影响使用者读回；仍有缺口就记“未恢复项 → 位置 → 负责人/下次处理”，明确告诉下一位，而非口头说“差不多好了”。

### 完成与停止

本次物品与普通痕迹已按许可处理，能安全恢复的布局已恢复，下一位可实际使用原有区域；未完成项有位置与负责人并已告知。存在危险污染、损坏、未知物主、需移动重物或设施异常时不要伪装已复原，停止相关操作并交有权人员处理。

### 常见报错与补救

- **垃圾清了但家具仍挡路：** 对照原状，安全且获准时归位；重物交合适的人。
- **不知道一件东西归谁：** 留在双方知道的安全位置，标记并联系负责人，不擅自扔。
- **场所还可走却无法按原用途使用：** 检查座位、台面、门扇和用品的实际可用性，补上遗漏的恢复动作。
- **清洁用品或污迹性质不明：** 不试混药剂或强擦，隔离位置并问场所负责人如何处理。
- **下一位已经到场：** 告知仍占用/未恢复的具体位置和预计处理人，协商实际可用的替代，不把问题藏在“活动已结束”里。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通共用区域一次性使用后的恢复，不判定合同费用、损害责任或专业卫生标准。校园、社区场地和家庭有不同规则，依实际场所与已获许可的布置。行动、握持或视觉不便时可请愿意且有权限的人处理轻量物品，使用可读/口述原状记录，不以身体能力证明谁更有责任。副作用是活动的最后一步变成真正可用的空间。

## English

Load this Skill after a visit, activity or temporary work session ends and an ordinarily shared area must be returned for its next use. You will **remove ordinary items and manageable traces from this use, restore permitted layout changes, and check that the space actually works again**. This does not set a recurring chore rota, dispose of someone else's goods, handle hazardous contamination or teach heavy furniture moving.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the agreed use boundary, original-layout note or relevant users' recollection, and the site's current rules for furniture, waste and cleaning supplies. Identify what your group brought, what belongs to others or the site, and who may move it. Without a baseline, ask an authorised user rather than changing the common area by “close enough.”

### Execution

1. **View the affected area from its entry.** Against the agreement or baseline, find ordinary waste, your belongings, temporary markers and light items your group was allowed to move. Ask about parts someone still uses; do not call the next user's goods leftovers.
2. **Remove your movable goods first.** Give personal items back to their owner or take them away, and handle ordinary waste under site rules. Return borrowed goods as agreed. Do not throw away an item with an unknown owner; mark its location and ask the responsible person.
3. **Restore changes you may handle safely.** Return approved light furnishings or equipment to the original or agreed positions. Ask the actual responsible people about a heavy, fixed, dismantled or potentially injurious change; do not trial-move it alone to meet a deadline.
4. **Address ordinary manageable traces.** Use an appropriate normal product under its label and site rules for small ordinary marks your group caused. Do not mix cleaners or handle unknown or hazardous contamination. If it cannot be dealt with safely, keep people away from the affected spot and notify the authorised person.
5. **Prove next use works.** Check the entry, doors, route and original counter or seats so the next person can enter and use them without your things in the way. Invite an affected user's readback. For a remaining gap, record “unrestored item → location → owner / next action” and tell the next user; “mostly done” is not an invisible handoff.

### Success and stop

This use's goods and ordinary traces are handled under permission, safely movable layout is restored, and the next person can use the original area. Remaining items have a location and owner communicated to the next user. Do not call it restored when there is hazardous material, damage, an unknown owner, heavy moving or abnormal equipment; stop that work and tell the authorised party.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Waste is gone but furniture still blocks the route:** Compare with the baseline and return it if safe and permitted; get suitable help for a heavy item.
- **An item's owner is unknown:** Leave it in a known safe spot, mark it and contact the responsible person rather than discarding it.
- **You can walk through but cannot use the area as before:** Check seats, counters, door swings and needed goods, then finish the missing reset step.
- **Cleaner or stain type is unknown:** Do not mix products or scrub blindly. Isolate the spot and ask site staff or the responsible person.
- **The next user has already arrived:** Name the occupied or unrestored spot and its follow-up owner, and agree on a real usable alternative; do not hide it behind “the event ended.”

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This returns an ordinary common area after one use. It does not decide fees, damage liability or professional sanitation standards. Campus, community and household rules differ; follow the actual site and permitted arrangement. If walking, grip or sight makes a task difficult, ask willing authorised people to handle light objects and use an accessible or spoken baseline. Physical ability is not a proxy for responsibility. The activity's last step is a genuinely usable space.
