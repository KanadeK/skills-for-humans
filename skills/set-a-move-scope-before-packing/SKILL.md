## 中文

当一次普通搬迁已经决定、但还没开始装箱，屋内物品的去向与决定权混在一起时加载。你要得出**这次由谁负责的哪些物品进入运输范围、哪些由本人保留、哪些不参加、哪些暂不能定**。这不是决定物品永远留还是丢，不解释租赁或承运合同，也不教装箱、搬重物或处理危险物品。

### 准备与输入

写下旧住处、新住处和实际计划使用的运输方式；若有搬运服务，找其当前可接收物品说明。按小区域观察物品，先辨别是谁的、谁能决定它的去向。共享或他人的物品不能因为放在你的房间就自动算进运输单。准备纸上四列：随运输走／本人保留／不参加本次搬迁／待确认。

### 执行

1. **逐区记录，不先装箱。** 对每个橱柜或小区域只列能识别的物品或自然成组物，标出现所在位置及决定人。整箱“杂物”不能掩盖里面的他人物品。
2. **分清去向而非价值。** 经有权决定的人确认，把每组放进四列之一。“不参加本次搬迁”可意味着原地保留、另行交接或暂不处理，不等于丢弃；处置意愿另作决定。
3. **给运输方式加边界。** 对有疑问的液体、易腐、受限或需要特殊处理的物品，先查你实际选用的搬运方和所在地要求。不能确认能否被接收就留在“待确认”，不混入普通运输列，也不在此处尝试自行处理危险内容。
4. **指明负责人。** 每列写谁能回答下一步问题：谁与搬运方核对、谁保管本人携带物、谁联系缺席的所有者。没有授权的人只能记录“待确认”，不能代签决定。
5. **作一次范围回读。** 让相关决定人逐列复述哪些物品会随这次运输走，并对争议或临时增加项留下处理期限。搬家当天改变范围时，先更新清单，再交给实际承运者核对。

### 成功与停止

每组相关物品有一个清楚的本次路线和有权负责的人；不能确定的组被隔离出来，未被默默装入箱。完成不要求清空房间或丢弃物品。所有者未答复、承运限制未确认或物品状态危险时，停在待确认并转合适渠道。

### 常见报错与补救

- **有人说“这个房间都搬”：** 抽查共享和他人物品，分别取得决定，不按房间替所有者发言。
- **把“不搬”误听成“扔掉”：** 在清单上写具体去向或“暂不决定”；永久去留是另一任务。
- **搬运方说这类物品不能收：** 从运输列移出，联系承运方或相关专业渠道决定合法可行的处理方式；不私自藏进普通箱。
- **装箱前又加了一件：** 补上所有者、路线和限制判断，再交给后续装箱者。
- **看不清角落或无法移动物品：** 请有权限的人描述或拍摄可见区域，保持未核实；不为完成清单冒险搬动重物。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这份 Skill 适用于普通家庭物品的购前式清点，不把美国州际搬运规则当成所有地区的法律。运输服务、住所权限与个人物品边界会不同；以实际提供方说明和有权人的决定为准。视觉、行动或认知负担大时每次只做一个区域、用大字或语音清单并安排休息。副作用是“全都搬”从口号变成四列实际可核对的范围。

## English

Load this Skill after an ordinary move is planned but **before packing** when the route and decision-maker for household goods are mixed together. You will record which authorised person's belongings join this shipment, which stay personally held, which are outside this move, and which remain unresolved. It does not decide permanent keep/discard choices, interpret a lease or carrier contract, pack boxes, move heavy items, or handle hazardous goods.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the old and new place and the transport route actually planned. If a moving service is involved, find its current accepted-item information. Look through small areas and identify whose property it is and who may decide its route. An item shared with or owned by someone else does not become yours to ship because it sits in your room. Make four columns on paper: shipment / personally retained / outside this move / confirm first.

### Execution

1. **Inventory small areas before boxing.** For each cupboard or small zone, list identifiable items or natural groups, current position and decision-maker. A carton named “miscellaneous” must not hide another person's belongings.
2. **Choose a move route, not an item's worth.** With the authorised person's agreement, place each group in one column. “Outside this move” may mean staying put, separate handoff, or no decision yet; it does not mean discard. Permanent disposition is another decision.
3. **Apply the actual transport boundary.** For questionable liquids, perishables, restricted goods or items needing special handling, check the chosen mover's current rules and local requirements. If acceptance is unknown, hold the item in “confirm first” instead of mixing it into normal shipment. Do not improvise hazardous handling here.
4. **Name an owner for each open question.** Record who will ask the mover, keep personally carried items, or contact an absent owner. Someone without authority may record “unknown” but cannot decide for that person.
5. **Read the scope back.** Have relevant decision-makers repeat which goods join this move and give disputes or late additions a check point. If scope changes on moving day, update the list before reconciling it with the actual carrier.

### Success and stop

Each relevant group has one route for this move and an authorised person responsible for it. Unclear groups are held apart, not silently boxed. Success requires neither an empty home nor disposal. Stop at “confirm first” when an owner has not replied, a carrier restriction is unclear, or an item presents a hazard; use the suitable channel.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Someone says “move this whole room”:** Check shared and other-owned items separately and obtain their decision rather than speaking for them.
- **“Not moving” is heard as “throw it away”:** Write the actual route or “not decided”; permanent disposition is a different task.
- **The mover says this category is not accepted:** Remove it from shipment and ask the mover or appropriate professional channel for a lawful feasible route. Do not hide it in an ordinary box.
- **One more item appears just before packing:** Add owner, route and restriction check before handing it to the packer.
- **A corner cannot be seen or an item cannot be moved safely:** Ask an authorised person for a description or visible photo and mark the rest unverified. Do not shift heavy goods to finish a list.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary household-goods scoping. A US interstate moving rule is not law for every place. Services, home access and personal ownership differ; use the actual provider's instructions and authorised person's choice. If seeing, moving or processing a whole house at once is difficult, work one zone at a time with large text or voice notes and breaks. A side effect is turning “everything moves” into four checkable columns.
