## 中文

当你准备买一件普通日常衣物，尤其无法先试穿，而“S/M/L”或过去买过的号数不足以判断合身时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一个有依据的候选尺码，或明确的“不确定，先试穿/补问/不买”。这不是压缩衣、医疗用品、防护装备或专业制服的适配流程。

### 准备与输入

找到**这一件商品在当前销售地区**的尺码表和商品说明。确认表里给的是身体尺寸、成衣尺寸，还是两者都有；单位是厘米还是英寸。准备一件版型相近、你穿着舒服的衣物，或在你愿意时量自己的相关身体尺寸。你不必把身体尺寸上传到陌生推荐工具，也不需要为了选衣服证明自己符合某个字母。

### 执行

1. **先说清合身的用途。** 你希望贴身、宽松、可叠穿，还是活动时不受限？选出最影响这件衣物的两三项，例如上衣的肩/胸和衣长，裤子的腰与内长。不同款式不必用同一处尺寸定胜负。
2. **判断尺码表的对象。** 身体尺寸表与自己的同位置身体读数比较；成衣尺寸表与平铺的同类衣物按同一种量法比较。不要把平铺衣物的半幅宽度直接和身体围度比。
3. **量一个可靠参照。** 把已合身的同类衣物平放、整理平整，按商品图示量相同起止点。没有合适参照时，可用品牌提供的身体尺寸测量说明；身体读数只在你愿意并能可靠测量时使用。
4. **核对具体款式。** 看商品标注的宽松/修身、面料弹性、衣长、可调节位置和你需要的活动空间。选择最接近预期的候选尺码；同一个字母在不同款式和地区也可能有不同成衣尺寸。
5. **处理剩余不确定。** 能试穿时，坐下、抬手或做这件衣服实际需要的普通动作。无法试穿时，把“尺码表预测”与“实际合身”分开，查看适用的试穿/退换政策；关键尺寸缺失或误差难接受就先问清或不买。

### 成功与停止

你能说出候选尺码、所用尺码表类型、对应的两三项尺寸和仍需试穿确认的地方，即完成购前核对。身体不适、活动受限或特殊防护需求不能靠“忍一忍就松了”解决；停止这一候选，试别的尺码或寻求该类产品的专业适配。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只按旧衣服的 M 号选：** 打开这件商品自己的尺码表，比较实际尺寸和版型；旧字母只是线索。
- **身体围度与平铺宽度直接相减：** 先确认两张表量的是什么，再按同一对象和单位比较；不清楚就不要计算。
- **胸/腰合适但袖长或内长不合适：** 明确哪个尺寸不能妥协，找短/长版本、可调整款或别的版型；不要把全部问题压到一个号数上。
- **无法看清尺码图或不便测量：** 放大图示、使用可读文本说明、请可信的人按你指定的位置协助，或去可试穿的店。身体尺寸由你决定是否分享。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程假设商品提供与当前版本对应的尺码信息；各地区标号、面料、版型和个人舒适偏好不同，不能自动换算。没有尺子可比较一件合身旧衣的平铺轮廓，但不能把目测当精确确认。尺码表只缩小试错范围；试穿和实际使用感觉仍是最终证据。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an everyday garment, especially when you cannot try it on and an S, M, L, or familiar number is not enough to predict fit. You finish with a reasoned candidate size or a clear “uncertain: try on, ask, or do not buy.” Compression wear, medical items, protective equipment, and specialist uniforms need their own fitting rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Open the chart for **this exact item in the region where you are buying it**, plus the item description. Find out whether the chart gives body measurements, finished-garment measurements, or both, and whether it uses centimetres or inches. Have a comparable garment that fits comfortably, or body measurements you choose to take. You do not need to upload body measurements to an unfamiliar recommendation service to shop for a shirt.

### Execution

1. **Name the fit you need.** Should it be close, loose, layered, or easy to move in? Choose the two or three measurements that matter most for this garment, such as shoulder, chest, and length for a top or waist and inseam for trousers. One dimension does not decide every style.
2. **Identify what the chart measures.** Compare a body-size chart with your body at the same measurement points. Compare a product-size chart with a similar garment laid flat and measured the same way. Do not compare a flat garment's half-width directly with a body circumference.
3. **Make one reliable comparison.** Smooth a well-fitting similar garment on a flat surface and use the item's diagram to measure matching start and end points. If you have no comparable garment, use the brand's body-measurement guidance if you are willing and able to measure reliably.
4. **Check this item's design.** Read its intended fit, stretch, length, adjustment points, and room needed for ordinary movement. Select the size that best matches your preferred fit. The same letter can describe different finished sizes across items and regions.
5. **Keep uncertainty visible.** If you can try it on, sit, raise your arms, or make an ordinary movement relevant to its use. If you cannot, treat the chart as a prediction, check the applicable try-on and return policy, and ask or pause when a crucial measurement is missing or a poor fit would be costly.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can name the candidate size, chart type, two or three compared measurements, and what still needs a try-on. Do not accept pain, restricted movement, or a special protective need on the promise that it will “stretch out.” Stop this candidate and find a different size or an appropriate specialist fitting route.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You ordered your old M again:** Open this item's own chart and compare measurements and cut; the old letter is only a clue.
- **Body circumference was subtracted from flat width:** Identify what each number measures and compare like with like in one unit. If unclear, do not calculate.
- **Chest or waist fits but sleeve or inseam fails:** Name the dimension that cannot be compromised and look for short/long options, adjustability, or a different cut.
- **The chart is unreadable or measuring is difficult:** Enlarge it, ask for readable text, ask a trusted person to help at points you specify, or shop where you can try on. Sharing body measurements is your choice.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes the seller offers a chart for the current item. Regional labels, fabric, cut, and comfort preferences differ, so a generic conversion table cannot establish fit. Without a ruler, a familiar garment can offer a rough outline comparison, but visual guessing is not a confirmed measurement. A size chart reduces uncertainty; a try-on and your own comfort remain the final evidence.
