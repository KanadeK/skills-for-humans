## 中文

当一盆普通室内植物有**明确枯死或受损的一小部分枝叶**，而你想做一次有限修剪时，加载这份 Skill。结果是许可范围内的干净小切口和妥当废料处理，不是凭外观治好整株。

### 准备与输入

确认植物品种、可修剪部位和时机，以及叶片损伤是局部还是正在扩散。准备清洁锋利的小剪刀和容器；不碰未知毒性、刺激性汁液、尖刺或搬不动的植株。手部操作、视力或触及位置不允许安全用剪刀时，请可靠的人协助，不在高处仰剪。

### 执行

1. **先判断是否该剪。** 明确完全枯死、折损或品种说明允许移除的部位；正常变色、季节性休眠或不明斑点不自动列入。若虫体、蛛网、软烂或大片迅速扩散，先隔离与辨认，不把修剪当诊断。
2. **限定本次范围。** 选择少量目标，保留健康生长点和足够叶面积。大幅造型、主茎切断或全株重剪需要品种专门资料，这份 Skill 不覆盖。
3. **清洁工具并稳住植株。** 依工具说明清洁小剪刀。把盆放在稳固可及的位置，一手轻托目标部位，另一手在品种允许的叶柄或枝条节点附近作清楚一剪；不要扯掉邻近健康组织。
4. **处理切下物。** 收入容器；疑似病虫材料不要带到其他盆或默认放入室内堆肥。工具接触异常部位后按可靠清洁说明处理，洗手。
5. **留基线并复看。** 记修剪日期、部位和原先的异常；过几天查是否继续扩散。持续恶化要检查水、光和病虫等因素并求助，不靠重复剪短掩盖原因。

### 成功与停止

成功是目标小部分被干净移除，健康部分未明显损伤，废料和工具收妥，后续复看有记录。若无法确认该部位是否可剪、损伤正在大范围扩散、工具不安全或植株有未知接触风险，停止并寻求适合的品种建议。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一片黄叶就想整株重剪：** 先查正常老化、光水状态和品种修剪要求，不扩大切口。
- **剪刀钝到把茎压烂：** 停手换合适的清洁工具，不反复挤压。
- **叶片上有虫或不明斑点：** 暂停在不同盆间使用工具，隔离并记录后再决定处理。
- **剪后伤口附近继续发黑：** 记录变化并问可靠园艺来源；不要连续追加猜测性修剪。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理可安全触及的普通室内盆栽少量枯损枝叶，不做大型、带刺或疑似有害植物的重剪，也不处理植物病原体或农药。视力受限可请人指出并确认目标，动作不凭摸索靠近刀刃。结果是一次有限整理，需要之后看植株是否仍有原问题。

## English

Load this Skill when a small part of an ordinary indoor plant has **clearly dead or damaged foliage** and you want a limited trim. Finish with a species-permitted clean cut and contained debris. The act does not cure the whole plant by appearance.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm species, permitted cut points and timing, and whether damage is local or spreading. Prepare clean sharp small scissors and a waste container. Do not handle an unidentified toxic or irritating plant, spines, or a pot you cannot move safely. If grip, sight, or reach makes scissors unsafe, arrange reliable help rather than cutting overhead.

### Execution

1. **Decide whether to cut.** Identify a fully dead, broken, or species-approved removable part. Ordinary colour change, dormancy, or an unexplained spot is not enough. If insects, webbing, soft rot, or rapidly spreading damage appear, isolate and identify the problem first; pruning is not a diagnosis.
2. **Bound this attempt.** Select a small number of targets and preserve healthy growing points and enough foliage. Major shaping, cutting a main stem, or a whole-plant reset needs separate species guidance.
3. **Clean tools and steady the plant.** Clean scissors according to their instructions. Work with the pot stable and reachable. Support the target gently and make one clear cut near a permitted leaf stalk or branch node. Do not tear neighboring healthy tissue.
4. **Contain cut material.** Put it in the waste container. Do not carry suspect pest or disease material to another pot or assume indoor compost will accept it. Clean a tool that touched abnormal material using reliable instructions, then wash hands.
5. **Record and review.** Note date, cut position, and the original sign. Recheck whether damage spreads. Persistent decline calls for water, light, and pest review or horticultural help, not repeated trimming to hide it.

### Success and stop

The limited target has been cleanly removed, nearby healthy growth remains intact, tools and debris are managed, and a review note exists. Stop if you cannot confirm a safe cut, damage is widespread, tools are unsafe, or the plant has unknown contact risk.

### Common errors and recovery

- **One yellow leaf prompts a whole-plant cut:** Check normal ageing, light and water, and species guidance before expanding the work.
- **Blunt scissors crush the stem:** Stop and use a suitable clean tool instead of repeated squeezing.
- **Insects or unknown spots appear:** Stop moving the tool between plants; isolate and record before deciding treatment.
- **The area darkens after cutting:** Record the change and seek reliable horticultural advice instead of making more speculative cuts.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a small trim on a safe-to-handle ordinary houseplant, not major pruning of a large or spiny plant, plant-pathogen treatment, or pesticide use. If vision is limited, have someone point out and confirm the target; do not feel around the blade. The result is a limited cleanup and a need to watch the original problem.
