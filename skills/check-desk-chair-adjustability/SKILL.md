## 中文

当你已有工作桌，要买一把供自己普通电脑/书写任务使用的椅子时，加载这份 Skill。目标是确认候选的座高、座深、扶手和靠背调节**覆盖你与现有桌子的共同使用位置**，或明确必须试坐的未知。这不是疼痛诊断、治疗方案或椅子组装说明。

### 准备与输入

量桌面高度和桌下真实可用高度、宽度、膝腿空间，记下键盘或书写位置。取得候选准确型号的座高范围、座深/坐面宽度、扶手高度及宽度调节、靠背位置和额定承重。你可以在允许的商店试坐；不能试坐时用相同型号的完整尺寸与调节范围作纸面筛选，保留实际舒适度未知。

### 执行

1. **先定义工作姿态。** 用你实际放键盘或纸张的位置判断手肘能否自然靠近身体，不需要为一张产品广告摆造型。桌面高度固定时，椅子需要适应桌子，必要时考虑有稳定脚踏的方案。
2. **核座高。** 看候选可调范围内是否有一个位置，让你背部受到支撑、双脚平放地面或稳定脚踏，同时双臂能舒适到达工作面。座高的最小/最大值本身不等于这个组合存在。
3. **核座深与宽度。** 坐面应支撑大部分大腿，前缘不要顶住膝后；两侧有足够活动空间。过深会迫使你离开靠背，过窄会限制动作。读准确型号的坐面而非只看椅子总宽。
4. **核扶手和桌下位置。** 扶手若妨碍椅子靠近桌面，或迫使你抬肩、向前伸手，找可调/可拆的型号；确认椅子在你的普通坐姿下能进入桌下，不只在空椅时能推进去。
5. **核额定与试坐。** 候选的制造商承重和稳定要求必须覆盖真实使用，不用外观代替额定值。能试坐时调到预定位置，坐下、起身、靠背和到达桌面；不能试坐时记“尺寸可能适合、体验未确认”，查看有无可行试用条件。

### 成功与停止

你能指出一组真实可调位置，同时满足脚部、背部、坐面、扶手/桌面和额定条件，才把候选列为适合进一步购买。椅子晃动、关键尺寸缺失、疼痛或特殊身体支持需求时停止普通筛选，向合适的商家或专业人员咨询；纸面通过不保证长期舒适。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看“人体工学”四字：** 找准确型号的座高/座深/扶手调节范围和额定资料，缺一项就记录未知。
- **座高让手舒服，但脚悬空：** 核是否有稳定脚踏和足够桌下空间；没有就换不同椅/桌组合。
- **椅子推进桌下却无法靠近键盘：** 把扶手、腿部空间和真实坐姿重新纳入测量。
- **不便试坐或看不清刻度：** 请店员展示和读回调节范围，使用可读规格表；别人可以帮你测量，但不能代替你的舒适感结论。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程适用于普通桌椅购买，不给医疗、残障辅助器具或职业病治疗建议。桌高、地面、脚踏及身体尺寸因人而异；不按性别或身高直接指定一个通用座高。若共享椅子，分别核每位实际使用者的可调范围。你可能得到一份比商品页短、但更有用的调节清单。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a chair for ordinary computer or writing work at a desk you already use. Your goal is to check whether its seat, arm, and back adjustment ranges contain **one workable position for both you and that desk**, or identify what still requires a trial. This is not a pain diagnosis, treatment plan, or assembly guide.

### Preparation and inputs

Measure the desk height and actual under-desk height, width, and leg room; note the keyboard or writing surface. Find the exact chair's seat-height range, seat depth and width, armrest height/width adjustments, backrest position, and rated load. If a store permits a trial, use it. Without a trial, use complete model dimensions for a paper screen and leave lived comfort unconfirmed.

### Execution

1. **Define the actual work posture.** Use where your keyboard or paper really sits to see whether your elbows can stay comfortably near your body. There is no need to pose for an advertisement. With a fixed-height desk, a stable footrest may be one way to make the combined setup work.
2. **Check seat height as a combination.** Within the candidate's range, look for a position with back support, feet flat on the floor or a stable footrest, and comfortable reach to the work surface. Printed minimum and maximum seat heights alone do not prove that such a combination exists.
3. **Check seat depth and width.** The seat should support most of your thighs without pressing behind the knees, with enough room to move at the sides. Too deep can separate you from the backrest; too narrow can restrict movement. Use seat dimensions rather than the chair's overall width.
4. **Check arms and desk entry.** If armrests prevent the chair approaching the desk or force raised shoulders and a forward reach, look for suitable adjustment or a different chair. Confirm usable knee and leg space with you seated, not only with an empty chair pushed in.
5. **Check rating and trial.** The maker's load and stability conditions must cover intended use; appearance is not a rating. If you can try the chair, adjust it, sit, stand, use the back, and reach your desk-height work surface. Without a trial, record “dimensions plausible; comfort unconfirmed” and check a workable trial route.

### Success and stop conditions

You can shortlist the candidate when you can name one achievable setting that covers feet, back, seat, arm/desk position, and rated use. Stop an ordinary screen for wobbling, missing essential dimensions, pain, or a special support need; ask an appropriate retailer or professional. Passing on paper does not guarantee long-term comfort.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “ergonomic” was read:** Find this model's seat, depth, arm range, and rating. Keep missing values unknown.
- **The seat lets arms reach, but feet dangle:** Check whether a stable footrest and adequate leg space can complete the setup; otherwise choose another chair/desk combination.
- **The empty chair fits under the desk, but you cannot approach the keyboard:** Include armrests, leg space, and your real seated position in the measurement.
- **Trial or small markings are inaccessible:** Ask staff to show and read the adjustment range or use a readable specification. Someone may help measure, but cannot certify your comfort for you.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary desk-chair purchase check, not medical, disability-device, or occupational-treatment advice. Desk height, floor, footrest, and body dimensions differ; no universal seat height is assigned by gender or stature. For a shared chair, check the range for each actual user. You may leave with a short, useful adjustment list instead of a slogan.
