## 中文

当一丛健康的小型**非食用观赏多年生草本**已经拥挤，且可信品种资料说明可在当前时机分株时，加载这份 Skill。你要得到各自带有健康根与芽的可栽小丛，并把它们安放到真实可用位置。E003 的幼苗移栽移动一株完整根团；E001 的茎插从剪下的枝段等生根，都不是眼前的分根团操作。

### 准备与输入

确认地块归属、品种、适合分株季节和一小丛的实际状态。预备至少两处符合光照、空间与排水要求的获准位置或容器；没有去向就不要先掘起。只处理可安全抬起的草本小丛，不处理树、灌木、大根团、尖刺或刺激性汁液不明的植物，也不在未知地下设施附近深挖。准备轻型工具、标签和水。

### 执行

1. **确认值得现在分。** 查品种说明是否允许分株与本季操作；病弱、虫害明显或刚受严重干旱的植株先查原因，不把分株当修复。多年生草本也并非每一株都要按同一间隔分。
2. **标记原位和新去向。** 清楚区分要保留的邻株及根区，确认新位置能容纳分出的植株。按实际土壤和品种说明预备水分，不用固定通用浇水量。
3. **轻起完整小根团。** 用小手铲在品种允许的根幅外小心松土，托住根团取出；不要拎叶片或茎。根盘太大、与邻株缠得太紧或工具需要明显用力时停止并请园艺协助。
4. **只沿自然分界分开。** 找每小段都有健康根和芽的位置，依品种资料用手轻分或合适工具分离；不为凑“两株”把无根叶片当成成品。可疑腐根或虫害另行隔离和判断，不丢给新位置。
5. **随即重新栽稳。** 将母株余部和合格小丛分别按品种原有深度放入已备位置，轻压周围介质、按说明浇水并标记来源/日期。清理工作面，记下下次看萎蔫、土壤与新生长的时间。

### 成功条件

原位与新位置都已清楚，分出的每段有根和芽、按合适深度栽稳并有复看记录。若品种不宜分、时机不对、缺新去向或根团无法安全抬起，保留原株并停止；没有“分一半放旁边以后再说”的完成状态。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有叶没有根：** 不当作可栽分株，查植物实际分株点。
- **挖起后发现新位置没准备：** 先保护根团免暴晒或干风并尽快按品种要求安回或求助；下次先安排目的地。
- **根盘需要大力撬：** 停止，交更合适的人或工具，不牵连邻株与地下设施。
- **分株后叶片垂下：** 核水分与品种护理、记录变化；持续恶化请教园艺来源，不把肥料当急救。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只做健康、可搬动、资料明确允许分株的小型草本，不做灌木移植、树根施工、化学处理或保障成活。握力与搬举困难时在可及高床操作或请人抬根团；视力受限时请人指出根和芽的实际分界。结果是两处及以上要观察的种植点，也可能因证据不足而合理暂缓。

## English

Load this Skill when a small healthy **non-edible clumping ornamental perennial** is crowded and reliable species guidance permits division now. Produce separately rooted, bud-bearing sections and plant them in real available places. E003's seedling transplant moves an intact young root ball; E001's stem cutting waits for roots on a severed shoot. Division separates an existing root mass.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission, species, division season, and the clump's actual health. Prepare at least two permitted destinations or containers with suitable light, room, and drainage before lifting anything. Work only on a small liftable herbaceous clump, not a tree, shrub, large root mass, spines, unknown irritating sap, or deep ground near possible utilities. Gather a light hand tool, labels, and water.

### Execution

1. **Confirm division is appropriate now.** Check species and season. Investigate a weak, pest-affected, or severely drought-stressed plant rather than treating division as repair. Perennials do not all have one mandatory division interval.
2. **Mark original and new places.** Identify neighboring plants and roots and confirm each new space fits. Prepare moisture according to species and actual soil; no universal water quantity.
3. **Lift one small root ball gently.** Loosen soil outside the supported root spread with a hand trowel and support the root ball, not leaves or stems. Stop and seek horticultural help if it is too heavy, tightly entangled with neighbors, or needs forceful digging.
4. **Separate along natural divisions.** Find sections with healthy roots and buds. Gently pull or use an appropriate tool as species guidance permits. Leaf-only pieces are not rooted plants for a two-plant quota. Isolate suspect rot or pests for a separate decision.
5. **Replant promptly.** Put the retained parent and each viable section at the species-supported prior depth in prepared sites. Firm lightly, water as directed, label origin/date, clear the work area, and set a check for wilt, medium, and new growth.

### Success

Original and new sites are clear; every divided section has roots and buds, is planted at a suitable depth, and has a review record. Keep the parent in place and stop if species, season, destination, or safe lifting is unresolved. “Divide now and leave half aside for later” is not completion.

### Common errors and recovery

- **There are leaves but no roots:** Do not count it as a viable division; locate the real division point.
- **The plant was lifted before a destination existed:** Protect roots from hot sun and drying wind and replant under species guidance promptly or seek help. Prepare the destination first next time.
- **The root mass needs force:** Stop and arrange proper help rather than damaging neighbors or buried services.
- **Leaves droop after division:** Check species water and placement and record change. Seek horticultural advice if decline continues instead of applying fertiliser as emergency treatment.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is for a healthy small liftable herbaceous clump with documented division guidance, not shrub relocation, tree-root work, chemical treatment, or guaranteed survival. Use an accessible raised bed or lifting help if grip or load is difficult. Ask someone to show the actual root/bud boundary if vision is limited. Two or more places now need observation, or the job may rightly be deferred.
