## 中文

你脑中已有一段自己写的短旋律，要把它记到五线谱而不是从别人的歌曲复制时，先建立一张参数正确的独奏空谱。成果是乐器、谱号、调号、拍号、标题和预计小节数能与构想相符；这一步不替你写音符。若音高或拍子尚未决定，可先记下不确定项，别为了让向导通过而凭空指定。

### 准备与输入

写一句作品用途和乐器，数一数旋律基本脉搏并判断是否有弱起。选自己确实会读或准备演奏的谱表，不把软件默认 4/4 当音乐事实。此时只在本机保存，关闭云端同步与发布入口；未获授权的旋律不应作为输入。

### 执行

1. 在 New score 里选合适独奏乐器或空白谱，确认谱号与实际音域。
2. 填写本人作品标题和必要署名，设定确定的调号、拍号以及大致小节数；不确定的参数先保留记录。
3. 建立后查看第一行谱表、谱号、拍号和调号的位置，核没有意外多出第二乐器或多余谱表。
4. 用纸上节奏计划对照空小节数量，调整明显错误的初始设置，然后保存本机原稿。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

独奏空谱的关键参数可以直接读出，标题属于本人原创构想，适合进入音符输入。

- **乐器不对：** 在正式输入前改乐器或重建，核谱号与音域。
- **拍号不合：** 回节奏计划数拍，修正设置而非硬塞音符。
- **多余谱表：** 核模板选择并移除未需要的乐器。

### 假设与边界

只建一张普通原创短谱，不能证明旋律可演奏、配器合理或符合特定出版规范。真正的音高、节奏与演奏方式仍由你决定。

## English

Use this when you have a short melody of your own and need an empty staff prepared for it. The outcome is a solo score whose instrument, clef, key, time signature, title and rough measure count reflect your plan. This step does not invent notes. If pitch or meter is undecided, record the uncertainty before committing to a wizard choice.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the piece's purpose and instrument, count its basic pulse and note any pickup. Choose notation you can read or plan to perform, rather than accepting default 4/4 as a musical fact. Keep work local and do not import an unlicensed melody or enter a publish flow.

### Execution

1. In New score choose a suitable solo instrument or blank score and verify its clef and practical range.
2. Enter your own title and attribution, then the known key, meter and approximate measure count, noting anything still uncertain.
3. Create the score and inspect first system for clef, key and meter, confirming no unintended extra staff.
4. Compare empty measures with the rough rhythm plan, correct obvious setup errors and save a local master.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The solo score visibly shows the intended setup and original title and is ready for note entry.

- **Wrong instrument:** Change it before note entry or recreate the score, checking clef and range.
- **Wrong meter:** Count the planned rhythm and correct setup instead of forcing notes into bars.
- **Extra staff:** Review the template and remove unintended instruments.

### Assumptions and limits

This creates one ordinary original short score. It does not prove playability, orchestration or publisher compliance; you decide the actual pitch, rhythm and performance.
