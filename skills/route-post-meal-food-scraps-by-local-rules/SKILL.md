## 中文

当你吃完普通家庭餐、台面和盘里有**剩余食物、不可吃的碎屑与包装**时，用这份 Skill 先区分可安全保留的食物和真正要丢的残渣，再按**你所在地的实际收集规则**放进厨余或普通垃圾。包装回收另有流程；本篇不教自制堆肥系统，也不把所有骨头、油脂、餐巾或“可降解”包装自动叫作厨余。

### 准备与输入

找本地市政、物业或收运方对厨余/有机物、一般垃圾、允许的袋子和密封方式的**当前**说明；没有厨余服务时不假设别人会收。准备不漏的临时容器，确认余食的烹煮/室温/冷藏史是否足以按已有熟度与剩菜流程保留。不能从“看着没坏”推断可吃或捐赠；食品安全未知先按当地指导处理。危险废物、药品、清洁剂与尖锐物退出餐后厨余流程。

### 执行

1. **先让可保留的食物离开清垃圾路线。** 对还有安全去向的熟食，按对应剩菜流程确定食用、保温、及时冷却/冷藏或丢弃；不因要减少浪费把不明温度史改写成安全。只是盘中不可再食用的残渣才进入本篇分类。
2. **去掉明显非食物材料。** 将塑料膜、金属、包装、餐具和可回收容器与食物碎屑分开；它们是否可回收另看本地项目。不要连包装一起倒进厨余桶，也别让可回收容器带满食物汁液。
3. **按本地准入判断每类碎屑。** 对果蔬皮、熟食残余、骨头、蛋壳、油脂、咖啡渣及纸制品，逐项核对当地厨余项目是否收。明确接受的放指定容器；未接受或无法确认的按当地普通垃圾或管理者指定路线处理，不通过“天然/可降解”字眼推断准入。
4. **把容器关好且保留出路。** 不装到溢出，不让液体漏在台面、地面或回收物上；按当地要求使用指定袋或不套袋。大块食物残渣不默认为应冲进水槽/碎食机，先查真实管道与当地规定。清理用过的取渣工具和可见洒漏。
5. **准备后续移出。** 容器有明确收运位置与时间，不在食物准备区长期开放等待；打算送到楼下/路边的，交后续垃圾移出任务。处理垃圾后洗手，再接触食物与干净器具。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

剩菜的可食决定与废弃分类不混淆，实际碎屑进入当地接受的厨余或垃圾路线，包装与危险物已分开，容器不漏并有移出安排。没有可靠本地分类信息时，停止往厨余桶乱投并先查规定也是完整结果。

- **一只“可堆肥”杯混进厨余：** 查该项目是否收该包装；不接受就移出，标志不等于收运许可。
- **骨头/肉/油脂不在准入表：** 走当地允许的普通垃圾或指定去向，别试图把它们藏在果皮里。
- **食物汁漏到回收箱：** 暂停投放，擦净/按本地要求处理受污染包装和箱体，再继续。
- **有人把还可安全吃的食物倒进垃圾：** 如尚未污染，先按食品安全与实际保存能力判断；已接触垃圾的不捡回餐桌。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理普通家庭餐后残渣，不处理危险化学、医疗、生物危害或垃圾设施维修。回收/厨余准入规则随地区和服务商改变，语言版本也不自动代表地区。看不清标识时用本地可读指南或物业说明，行动受限时选小份不漏容器或可信协助；垃圾搬运不能要求你独自提超重袋。分类成功还需后续实际收运交接。

## English

Use this Skill after an ordinary household meal leaves **food to keep, inedible scraps, and packaging** on the counter and plates. Separate food with a real safe next use from waste, then use the **current rules of your actual local collector** for organics or trash. Packaging recycling has its own route. This does not build a home compost system or assume every bone, fat, napkin, or “compostable” package belongs in an organics bin.

### Preparation and inputs

Find your current municipal, property, or hauler rules for organics, ordinary trash, allowed liners, and closure. Without an organics service, do not assume collection. Have a nonleaking container. Check whether remaining food's cooking, room-time, and refrigeration history supports keeping it under the existing doneness and leftovers workflow. Appearance alone does not certify food for eating or donation; use local safety guidance when the history is unknown. Hazardous waste, medicines, cleaners, and sharps leave ordinary meal scraps.

### Execution

1. **Take food with a safe next use out of the waste route first.** Use the leftovers workflow for food that can genuinely be eaten, safely held, promptly cooled or refrigerated, or discarded. Waste reduction does not turn an unknown temperature history safe. Only scraps no longer suitable to eat enter this classification.
2. **Remove obvious nonfood material.** Separate plastic film, metal, packaging, and cutlery from food pieces. Whether any packaging can be recycled is another local check. Do not pour a wrapped meal into organics or send a food-filled container to dry recycling.
3. **Check local acceptance for each scrap type.** For fruit and vegetable peel, cooked leftovers, bones, eggshells, fats, coffee grounds, and paper, check the actual organics program. Use its specified container for items explicitly accepted. Route rejected or unclear items to local ordinary trash or another stated destination rather than inferring acceptance from “natural” or “biodegradable.”
4. **Close the container and keep an exit route.** Do not overflow or leak food liquid onto the worktop, floor, or recyclables. Use a specified liner or no liner as the service requires. Do not assume large food scraps belong in a sink drain or disposal unit; check plumbing and local instructions. Clean the tools and visible spills used in collection.
5. **Arrange the removal handoff.** Give the container a real collection place and time rather than leaving it open in the food-preparation area. The later waste-removal task moves it downstairs or curbside. Wash hands after touching garbage and before handling food or clean tools again.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Keeping edible food was decided separately from waste sorting. Actual scraps use the locally accepted organics or trash route; packaging and hazardous items are apart, and the nonleaking container has a removal plan. Stopping organics guesses until local rules are found is a complete result.

- **A “compostable” cup slips into organics:** Check whether this program accepts the package. Remove it if not; a logo is not collection permission.
- **Bones, meat, or fat are absent from the acceptance list:** Use permitted trash or stated alternatives; do not hide them under fruit peel.
- **Food liquid leaks into recycling:** Pause, clean or handle the affected packaging and bin under local directions, then resume.
- **Someone throws away food that might still be safe:** If not yet contaminated, assess actual storage and safety capacity. Food that touched garbage does not return to the table.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary home meal scraps, not hazardous chemical, medical, biological waste, or waste-system repair. Recycling and organics rules change by locality and provider; language does not identify the locality. Use a readable local guide or property instructions if signs are unclear. Use small nonleaking containers or trusted carrying help for mobility limits; do not carry an unsafe heavy bag alone. Sorting still needs an actual collection handoff.
