## 中文

当普通约定已有足够清楚的活动、日期与参加人，你要**亲手创建并发出一份可理解的日历邀请**时加载。若还在提出初次邀约，先让对方有拒绝空间；若时间仍未获同意，在标题/说明明确“提议，待确认”，不把日历发送当双方约定。这里不替别人接受、不自动控制他们的日历，也不处理整个多人可用时间调查。

### 准备与输入

核对这次活动的名称、星期/日期、开始与预计结束、当地时区、可找到的地点/线上入口，以及实际应收到邀请的人。确认你选的日历账号能向外发邀请，且不会从不该公开的工作/共享日历发出；不同日历服务和账号对邀请功能支持不同。只写参加者真正需要的信息，别把证件、密码、私人地址或无关会话贴进说明。异地时区先按双方明确的本地时间复核。

### 执行

1. **在正确账号和日历建事件。** 选择活动归属的个人/工作日历，设清楚的标题，写日期、开始、结束及日历所显示的时区；不要让单击日历空白格自动产生的默认半小时替你决定真实持续时间。
2. **给地点和进入方式一个可执行值。** 线下写场所全称、实际入口/地址或双方确认的会合信息；线上放本次有效链接或说明谁稍后提供。不要把会议密码、私人门禁码或与受邀人无关的信息塞进可转发的描述里。尚未定案的项目明示待确认。
3. **只加真正应邀的人。** 逐个检查自动补全的姓名和完整邮箱，区分同名联系人；有“可选参加”功能才按本次意愿使用。查看这个日历是否会向宾客显示他人名单、允许转发或修改事件，按活动隐私需要调整可用设置，不擅自公开别人的地址。
4. **发送前做一次读者复核。** 从受邀人视角读标题、两端时间/时区、地点、参会方式、备注与收件人；特别核对跨日、周末和线上/线下有没有矛盾。确认后用该服务的“保存并发送邀请/更新”动作，留意服务是否还弹出“要通知宾客吗”；只保存自己的日历格子不一定发出邀请。
5. **读回状态，不替对方答应。** 在你创建的事件里核对它已保存且显示受邀人或发送状态；宾客可能收到邮件却尚未在自己日历显示，或回复“可能/拒绝”。若对方没收到，先核邮箱/垃圾邮件及服务设置，再用双方已有渠道提示查看，不反复创建重复事件。发现错误由组织者在原事件修正并正式发更新，而不是静默改自己那一份。

### 完成与停止

你在正确账号下有一份细节可读、对象正确的事件，已用该日历支持的方式发出邀请，宾客回复仍按实际标为待定/接受/拒绝，本轮完成。**发送成功不等于送达每个邮箱，更不等于对方同意或到场。** 账号不能发、对象地址不明、时区未核、隐私设置不合适时先不发送；用双方认可的其他方式确认信息。

### 常见报错与补救

- **自己日历有格子，别人说没收到：** 核是否真的添加宾客并选择发送，查邮箱/垃圾邮件与账号限制；不把本地保存当送达。
- **自动补全选了同名错邮箱：** 停止发送或尽快在原事件改正确对象并发正式更新；考虑错误收件人已看见的实际隐私影响。
- **时间看上去差一小时/跨一天：** 核日历时区和双方本地日期，先按跨时区表达流程确认，再发更新。
- **新事件与旧约定重复：** 保留唯一当前版本，需改期时更新双方已同意的事件，不让两个提醒都叫“正式”。
- **对方收到但未回复：** 保留 RSVP 待定，用正常沟通渠道适时确认；“已读”不是 Yes。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通可选活动的手动日历邀请，不是合同、群发营销、账号代管或跨组织权限配置。应用、设备、组织策略与日历服务器决定按钮/发送/回执差异；Google、Outlook 和 Apple 的功能不能强套。视觉、听觉、语言或设备不便时可先用简短文字读回信息，再在可用设备/可信协助下手动检查收件人，不交出账号密码。副作用是对方会收到一次需要决定的邀请，尽量不要用重复事件制造提醒噪音。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary plan has a clear enough activity, date and participants and you want to **manually create and send one understandable calendar invitation**. For a first proposal, leave the other person free to decline. If the time is not yet agreed, label the event “proposed / pending” rather than presenting a sent calendar item as a mutual plan. This does not accept for guests, control their calendars or run a group availability survey.

### Preparation and inputs

Check event name, weekday and date, start and expected end, local time zone, findable place or online entry, and who should actually receive it. Confirm the chosen calendar account can send invitations and that you are not sending from the wrong work or shared calendar. Services and account types differ. Include only information guests need, not identity documents, passwords, private access codes or unrelated conversation. For different time zones, first confirm the intended local times for both sides.

### Execution

1. **Create on the right account and calendar.** Select the personal or work calendar that owns this activity. Give a clear title, date, start, end and the zone shown by the app. A default half-hour block from clicking an empty slot does not define the event's real duration.
2. **Provide a usable place or way in.** For in-person plans, use the full venue and a confirmed address, entrance or meeting point. For online plans, use the real link or state who will provide it later. Do not put a meeting password, private door code or irrelevant personal data in text guests may forward. Mark unresolved details pending.
3. **Add only intended guests.** Check each autocomplete result and full email address, especially names shared by contacts. Use an optional-guest setting only if supported and appropriate. Review whether this calendar shows guest addresses to other guests or lets them forward or modify the event, adjusting supported settings for the activity's privacy. Do not publish others' addresses casually.
4. **Review from a recipient's view before sending.** Read title, both ends of time and zone, place, joining method, notes and guest list. Check overnight, weekend and online-versus-in-person conflicts. Then use the service's save-and-send or send-update action, including any “notify guests?” prompt. Saving a block only on your calendar may not send an invitation.
5. **Read back status without accepting for them.** In the organiser event, verify it was saved and shows guests or a send state. A guest may receive email without an immediate calendar entry or answer Maybe or No. For nonreceipt, check address, spam and service settings, then prompt them through your existing channel rather than creating duplicates. Correct an error in the organiser's original event and send a real update instead of silently editing only your copy.

### Success and stop

You have a readable event under the right account with the right recipients, sent by that service's actual invitation method; RSVP states remain pending, accepted or declined as observed. **Sending does not guarantee every inbox receives it, and neither delivery nor a calendar entry is consent or attendance.** For an account that cannot invite, unknown recipient, unverified time zone or unsuitable privacy setting, do not send yet; confirm through a channel both people can use.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Your calendar has the event but guests say nothing arrived:** Check guest list and actual send action, inbox or spam and account limits; a saved local item is not delivery.
- **Autocomplete chose the wrong person:** Stop before sending or correct the original event promptly with a real update, acknowledging the privacy consequence if the wrong person saw it.
- **Time shifts an hour or across a date:** Check the calendar zone and both local dates, confirm with the separate time-zone task and send an update.
- **A new event duplicates an older agreement:** Keep one current version. For an agreed change, update the shared event rather than calling two reminders official.
- **Invitation arrived but no reply:** Leave RSVP pending and ask once through ordinary communication when appropriate. Read is not Yes.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is a hand-created invite for an ordinary optional activity, not a contract, marketing broadcast, account takeover or cross-organisation permission setup. App, device, organisation policy and calendar server affect controls and receipts; Google, Outlook and Apple do not share one exact UI. For sight, hearing, language or device needs, read details back in short text and inspect recipients manually on an accessible device or with trusted help without handing over credentials. The guest may receive one item to decide on; duplicate events create avoidable reminder noise.
