## 中文

当你正在钩一片普通短针小样，**旧线快不够下一行**，需要接入同类新线并继续时，加载这份 Skill。结果是行边过渡明确、活圈由新线承担、旧新线尾留下待藏，不是一颗外露硬结。颜色切换只是此法变体；纤维/粗细不兼容会改变片形，不能在这里隐瞒。

### 准备与输入

读新旧纱线标签，确认材质、粗细和护理可相容；想换色也保留清楚的颜色顺序。备同一钩针、合适留尾长度和暂时放稳两团线的桌面。线团若极短，先停在当前行边，不钩到半针才发现无法收尾。为幼儿/负载制品接线的强度要求另查，不用练习方法担保。

### 执行

1. **找到合适行边。** 在本行最后一枚普通短针做到钩上还剩两圈时停住；若已完成最后针，也可在下一行开头按专门教程接线，但不改变本样片针数。旧线留够以后藏入织物的尾，不剪到针脚根部。
2. **用新线完成过渡。** 将新线留一段尾，用新线绕钩并一次拉过钩上两圈，最后一针由新线完成；此时钩上只剩新线的一圈。轻拉旧、新线尾使边缘平顺，不打一个永久硬结代替藏线。
3. **按原规则继续下一行。** 用新线做转向锁针、翻面，按短针行首/末规则继续几针。数新行针数，检查接点没有大洞或过紧。换色时把色序记下；尾先分开留着，不带入错误的针目数量。
4. **标记待收尾。** 记录接线行、两条待藏尾与所用新线标签，继续完成小样；最终断线藏尾时各自沿织纹处理。若接处松到一拉就开，退回重接，不将不稳连接埋到后续行里。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

新线实际承担后续针目、接点位于可找到的行边、针数不变且两条线尾可供后续藏入。仅把两团线绕在一起，旧线仍在钩，未完成交接。

- **接点留大洞：** 退回最后一针，调整尾线与张力再用新线穿过两圈。
- **新线粗细不同导致边缘突变：** 停并换兼容材料，或明确这是材料试验而非同口径小样。
- **旧尾剪太短：** 暂停后续剪线，查纱线适用补救，不靠松结保证耐用。
- **行针数多/少：** 查转向锁针与边缘针位，退回出错行重做。
- **手眼不便：** 用两色对比线、宽桌面和可信协助固定线团，由你核对活圈和尾线。

### 假设与边界

普通短针边缘接线只使小样继续，不等于线尾已固定或成品可受拉。不同纱线护理冲突时不能共用一个洗护方案。这里不写条纹图样或衣物接线强度标准。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary single-crochet sample is underway and the **old ball will not cover another row**. Bring in compatible yarn and continue. Finish with a clear edge transition, the live hook loop on new yarn, and two tails reserved for later weaving, not a permanent exposed hard knot. Changing colour uses the same mechanism; an incompatible fibre or weight changes the fabric and must be disclosed.

### Preparation and inputs

Read both yarn labels for compatible fibre, thickness, and care. Keep a colour order if intentionally changing shade. Have the same hook, enough tails for later hiding, and a stable place for both balls. If the old ball is very short, stop at the row edge rather than in the middle of a stitch without a tail. Infant or load-bearing projects need their own strength guidance, not this sample promise.

### Execution

1. **Find a row edge.** Work the last ordinary single crochet of a row until two loops remain on the hook and pause. If that stitch was already finished, a separate beginning-of-row join is possible under its directions; do not change your stitch count. Leave enough old tail to weave later instead of cutting against a stitch.
2. **Complete the transfer with new yarn.** Leave a new-yarn tail, yarn over with the new strand, and pull it through both hook loops to complete that last stitch. Only one new-yarn loop remains on the hook. Ease old and new tails so the edge sits flat without a permanent hard knot instead of weaving.
3. **Continue under the original rule.** With new yarn, make the turning chain, turn, and work a few single crochets into the correct first and later stitches. Count the new row and inspect for a large gap or tight lump. Record any colour sequence. Keep tails separate rather than counting them as extra stitches.
4. **Mark unfinished ends.** Record joining row, both tails, and new yarn label. Finish each tail along fabric texture at final fastening. If the join opens under a light pull, undo and rejoin instead of burying a loose connection under more rows.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

New yarn forms subsequent stitches, the edge join is findable, stitch count is unchanged, and two usable tails remain to secure later. Merely twisting balls while old yarn remains the hook loop is not a completed handoff.

- **A large hole opens at join:** Return to last stitch and adjust tails and tension while new yarn passes through two loops.
- **New yarn thickness changes the edge:** Stop for compatible material or label this a material experiment, not a same-gauge sample.
- **Old tail was cut too short:** Stop trimming and seek yarn-specific repair instead of trusting one loose knot.
- **Row count changes:** Check the turning chain and edge stitch, then undo the wrong row.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Contrasting colours, broad workspace, and trusted help holding balls can support your live-loop and tail check.

### Assumptions and limits

An ordinary edge join continues a sample but tails are not yet secured and the piece is not load-rated. Yarn care conflicts cannot be resolved with one wash route. No stripe pattern or garment-strength standard is claimed.
