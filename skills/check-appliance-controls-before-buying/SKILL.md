## 中文

当你准备买洗衣机、微波炉或其他普通家电，商品功能表都合适，却不确定**自己能否独立完成常用操作并知道机器已接受指令**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是准确型号的基本控制对你“可操作/有障碍/未验证”的购前判断。它不负责安装、不运行危险程序，也不要求你为了可访问性披露医疗信息。

### 准备与输入

选出你实际每周会做的两三件事，例如开关机、选择一个常用程序、调整时间或确认已结束。找到候选准确型号的控制面板图和可读说明；如果卖场有安全的展示机或演示，先问清允许操作什么。记录你自己需要的视觉对比、文字大小、触觉区分、按压力度、声音/振动反馈或无需手机的使用方式；这些是任务条件，不是“缺陷”。

### 执行

1. **把功能名称改成动作。** 不只看“有 12 个程序”，而写“我能找到常用程序、确认当前选项、开始与停止”。三项都应有你能识别的控制和状态反馈。
2. **查控制入口。** 看按钮/旋钮/触屏的实际位置、间隔与手感，文字和图标能否在预定环境读出。RNIB 指出视觉对比、可区分的触觉按钮和声音反馈是不同的可及路径；单一亮屏无法替代所有路径。
3. **查状态出口。** 你按下以后，能否知道选择已变化、机器正在运行或已停止？仅有颜色变化、短暂小字或不明确的蜂鸣，可能让你无法可靠确认结果。
4. **核辅助依赖。** 若某个必需操作只能通过手机 App、联网或他人代按，先问这是否是你愿意且能稳定使用的路径。不要把“理论上有语音控制”当成离线、免账户的保证；准确型号说明和展示应支持它。
5. **安全地试或标未知。** 在商家允许的展示模式下，走一遍两三项动作，不开启加热、带负载或其他危险运行；无法试时请店员展示并由你判断能否辨别动作与反馈。只看演示视频时，记录“纸面可用，实际未验证”。

### 成功与停止

你能完成并确认每个必用动作，或有你愿意使用且有证据的辅助路径，才把控制界面列为可用。只要某项动作或反馈无法辨认，就写具体障碍并换候选/补试；不要因为规格表漂亮就默认自己以后能适应。需要改装开关、拆机或处理电气故障时停止本 Skill。

### 常见报错与补救

- **说明书可读，就认定机器可操作：** 即使已经拿到可读说明，面板是否可按、状态能否辨认仍要分别验证。
- **仅凭 App 截图宣告可访问：** 查准确型号和当前地区的实际依赖、权限与替代控制；若你不接受这些依赖，别把它当通过。
- **能启动却不知如何停：** 停止条件是核心动作，找清楚的停止/取消入口与状态反馈；无法确认时不买这款。
- **展示机不能操作或细字看不清：** 请商家安排可用展示或提供清晰视频/说明，必要时选择可试用渠道；未亲自验证的路径保持未确认。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程关注购前操作可及性，不按年龄、残障或技术熟练程度替你设需求。视觉、听觉、触觉和动作能力可各有不同；你决定哪些反馈足够。能贴标记或加辅助工具的具体方案须符合产品安全和保修条件，本 Skill 不教改装。你可能因此选择按钮少一点、反馈更清楚的型号。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a washer, microwave, or another everyday appliance whose feature list looks suitable but you do not yet know whether **you can perform essential actions and tell that the machine accepted them**. The result is an exact-model pre-purchase judgment: operable for you, a specific barrier, or unverified. It does not install the appliance, run a hazardous programme, or require you to disclose medical information.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose two or three things you really do each week: power, a common programme, time adjustment, or knowing it has finished. Get the exact model's control diagram and readable instructions. If a retailer has a safe display or demonstration, ask what actions are permitted. Record your own needs for visual contrast, readable type, distinct touch, pressing force, audible or haptic feedback, and operation without a phone. These are task inputs, not flaws in you.

### Execution

1. **Turn feature names into actions.** Instead of only reading “12 programmes,” write “I can find my regular programme, confirm the selection, start, and stop.” Each action needs a control and feedback you can identify.
2. **Check the input controls.** Inspect button, dial, or touch positions, spacing, feel, and whether text/icons can be read in your intended setting. RNIB treats visual contrast, distinguishable touch controls, and audio feedback as different access paths; a bright screen does not replace all of them.
3. **Check the output state.** After an action, can you tell that the choice changed and whether the machine is running or stopped? A colour change alone, tiny transient text, or an ambiguous beep may be insufficient for you.
4. **Check auxiliary dependencies.** If an essential action requires an app, internet connection, or another person, decide whether that is a route you accept and can use reliably. A claimed voice feature is not proof of offline, account-free operation; check the exact model and demo.
5. **Try safely or mark unknown.** On a retailer-approved display mode, go through the essential actions without heating, loading, or starting a hazardous operation. If hands-on trial is unavailable, ask staff to demonstrate and judge whether you can distinguish the controls and feedback. A video alone leaves lived use unverified.

### Success and stop conditions

Shortlist the interface only when you can perform and confirm each essential action, or have a documented auxiliary path you willingly use. If a control or state cannot be identified, name the barrier and choose another candidate or seek a proper trial. A good feature list cannot promise later adaptation. Stop this Skill where using the product would require switch modification, opening the appliance, or electrical repair.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A readable manual was treated as an operable machine:** Even with readable instructions, the panel's actions and feedback still need a separate check.
- **An app screenshot was treated as accessibility proof:** Check real model/market dependencies and alternative controls. If you do not accept the dependencies, do not mark this route as passing.
- **You can start but cannot identify stop:** Stop/cancel is an essential action. Find its clear control and feedback; reject a candidate if it remains unconfirmed.
- **The display cannot be operated or text is too small:** Ask for a usable demo or clear video/instructions, or choose a route with a trial. Keep anything you have not personally verified as unconfirmed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This checks pre-purchase control access without assigning needs by age, disability, or technical experience. Visual, hearing, tactile, and motor abilities differ; you decide what feedback is enough. Any later marking or aid must respect product safety and warranty rules; this Skill does not modify appliances. You may prefer fewer controls with clearer confirmation.
