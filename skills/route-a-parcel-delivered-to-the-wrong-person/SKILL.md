## 中文

门口、信箱或交接处出现一个**外标签并非你或你确认的同住人/获准收件人**的普通包裹时，用这份 Skill 将误投事实交给原投递渠道处理，保护别人的姓名和地址。目标是服务方收到足够信息并给出正式交还或取回安排；你不需要猜邻居住址、代人拆箱或把包裹照片贴到社区群。若是你自己的订单里装错商品或少件，走另一份内容核对 Skill；H133 的柜台交件前发现错件应在现场止步。

### 准备与输入

先核对自己近期订单、门牌和外标签的可见收件信息，避免把同住人、旧姓、授权代收件误判。只读取判断所需的外部信息：承运商、追踪号（若可见）、投到的位置与时间；别打开包装寻找“真正主人”。保持包裹在不会妨碍通行、受雨淋或被他人误拿的安全位置。若有破损泄漏、刺激气味、露出尖锐物或其他风险，不搬动，隔离并联系投递方或当地合适帮助。

### 执行

1. **确认确实不是你的交接。** 对照自己的订单或可信家庭信息和外标签；地址或姓名不一致但仍可能是获准代收时先核对，不向陌生人公开完整标签求证。
2. **维持原状并记录最少事实。** 不拆、不使用、不转寄、不自行贴新地址。记下承运商、可见追踪尾号/编号、投递时间与位置；如服务方要求照片，只拍必要标签/外观并通过其官方渠道私下发送，遮住无关住户信息。
3. **找原投递方的正式入口。** 通过承运商官网、门口通知上可独立核对的客服电话或已知商家订单渠道报“收到不属于本地址/收件人的包裹”。按该方规则安排其取回、交到指定点或其他合规退回；美国 USPS 对错投与“旧住户”地址有不同处理，不能拿它的投箱办法套所有快递。
4. **读回处理安排。** 记录服务方给出的编号、会不会取回、何时/何处交还或其他明确处置指示，以及自己需不需要在场。完成交回时保留最少必要的受理或收取凭证。若服务方不回复，按官方渠道跟进一次；不要擅自把包裹送到你猜到的陌生人门口。

### 成功条件

误投包裹未被打开或扩散个人信息，原服务方已确认处理方式并实际取回/接收，或给出可追踪的受理与明确处置下一步。若服务方正式决定无需交还，也保留该答复；仅在群里问“谁的包裹”不算正式处理。

### 常见报错与补救

- **标签和你的订单不一致但可能是同住人：** 先私下核对授权收件人，不因名字不同就直接退走。
- **已经误打开：** 停止进一步翻看或使用，保持现状并向原服务方如实说明，按其指示处理；不公开内容。
- **陌生人自称是收件人来拿：** 不把外标签或验证码公开给对方证明身份；请其通过承运方正式安排交还。
- **承运商不明或客服入口可疑：** 从可识别的商家/平台官方订单和承运商官网独立找渠道，不使用短信中的未知链接。
- **包裹有泄漏或尖锐破损：** 不自行搬运或开封，隔离并寻求投递方及当地合适帮助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只涉及安全、普通、外部可辨的误投包裹，不涉及邮件法律解释、无人认领物权或危险品处置。不同承运商和地区的交还方式不同。行动不便可要求服务方提供取回或可及交件方式；保留包裹时注意不挡通道。最低限度的记录用于服务交接，不是给社交平台做寻人海报。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary parcel at your door, mailbox, or handover point is **not addressed to you or a confirmed household/authorized recipient**. Route the misdelivery through the original delivery service while protecting the other person's name and address. The result is an official collection or return arrangement, not your guess about a neighbour's door. A wrong or missing item inside your own order is a different task. If H133 catches a wrong parcel at a staffed counter, stop there.

### Preparation and inputs

Compare recent orders, your address, and only the visible outer recipient information needed to decide whether this may be a household member, former name, or authorized delivery. Note the carrier, visible tracking reference, and delivery place/time. Do not open the parcel to discover its owner. Keep it in a safe spot out of walkways, weather, and casual reach. For a leak, strong smell, exposed sharp edge, or other hazard, do not move it; isolate and contact the delivery provider or appropriate local help.

### Execution

1. **Confirm it is not your handover.** Compare your order or trusted household facts with the label. If a differing name could be an authorized recipient, verify privately before sending it away. Do not publish the full label for strangers to decide.
2. **Keep it intact and record minimum facts.** Do not open, use, forward, or relabel it. Record carrier, needed reference or its visible ending, place, and time. If the provider needs a photo, send only the necessary exterior view privately through its official channel, keeping unrelated residents' information out.
3. **Use the original delivery service.** Find its official site, independently verifiable contact number, or trusted merchant order route and report a parcel for another address or person. Follow its accepted collection, point drop-off, or return process. USPS distinguishes delivery to a wrong location from mail for a former resident at the correct address; do not apply its mailbox method to every courier.
4. **Read back the disposition.** Note the provider's case reference, whether it will collect, when/where to return it or any other explicit instruction, and whether you must attend. Retain minimal proof when the provider receives it. Follow up through that channel if unanswered; do not deliver it to a guessed stranger's doorstep.

### Success

The parcel remains unopened and its personal data private, and the original service has taken it back or confirmed a traceable next step. Keep an explicit provider reply if it says no return is needed. A public “whose parcel?” post is not a handling record.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Different name but possibly your household:** Verify the authorized recipient privately before returning it.
- **Opened by mistake:** Stop viewing or using contents, keep the state as found, tell the provider honestly, and follow its instructions without public disclosure.
- **A stranger claims it:** Do not show the full label or a release code as proof; ask them to arrange the handover through the carrier.
- **Carrier unclear or contact link suspicious:** Navigate independently from a trusted merchant order or carrier site, not an unfamiliar message link.
- **Leak or sharp damage:** Do not carry or open it. Isolate and seek the provider's or appropriate local help.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a safe ordinary misdelivery with enough exterior information to identify the original service. It gives no mail-law, unclaimed-property, or dangerous-goods ruling. Return methods vary by carrier and place. For limited mobility, request provider collection or accessible handover. Temporary storage should not block movement. The small factual record serves the handover, not a social-media search.
