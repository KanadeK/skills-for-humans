## 中文

当你在店里准备购买普通家用补充品，例如洗手液或餐具洗涤液，而补充桶旁没有足够资料、商品又不会带着原包装回家时，加载这份 Skill。你要在**灌装或付款前**确认它是什么，并让必要信息跟着你带走。这里不教配制、混合、稀释或使用化学品；食品、药品、婴幼儿用品、浓缩消毒剂和危险化学品不在范围内。

### 准备与输入

确定补充桶或售卖容器的名称、店内编号，以及你需要知道的身份、用途和限制。先看门店对容器、灌装和信息标签的规定；自带容器是否可用由店方及准确产品说明决定。已有不明残留的容器先不要交去灌装，也不要凭颜色或气味判断它原来装了什么。

### 执行

1. **灌装前停一步。** 指向确切补充桶，问：“这桶对应哪一款产品？能看原包装或制造商资料吗？”多桶相邻时，请店员把来源与这一桶逐一对上。
2. **读到能辨认的身份。** 至少确认准确品名、品牌或生产商、普通用途，以及原资料上有的使用限制。需要成分、批次或日期才能决定时，请店员查看原容器、店内记录或产品页；有些商品没有统一的固定保质期，不替它编一个。
3. **让信息能带回去。** 请求店方的标签或产品资料；也可以在允许时拍清原包装，或抄下准确产品名、关键限制和正式资料链接。确认这些内容确实属于将要购买的那一桶。只有一个店内昵称的空白瓶，回家后很难继续核对。
4. **按未知做停止判断。** 身份、关键限制或容器适用性无法确认，就先不灌装、不购买该补充品。选择信息完整的原包装商品，或等店方及制造商答复。不要把不同商品倒在一起，也不要以“都是洗涤液”推断可共用容器。
5. **资料齐了才走门店流程。** 这时仍按店方规定由合适人员处理灌装和收费；本 Skill 的结果是资料已经取得并留存，不是一次化学操作教学。

### 完成与停止

你能把带走的商品与准确品名、来源和适用说明对应起来，或在关键资料缺失时停止购买并记下缺口。店员是否愿意找资料、门店是否允许你的容器，都不由你保证。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“和架上那瓶一样”：** 请指出准确品名及相同版本；只是相似外观则仍未核实。
- **信息只在付款后的二维码：** 请求先查看可读资料或原包装；买前打不开就先暂停。
- **日期或批次查不到：** 若它影响你的决定，先不买。不要把“通常能放很久”记录成这桶的日期。
- **已开始灌装才发现不明：** 请店员暂停并按门店流程处理；不要自行倒回原桶或混入别的容器。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 假设店方售卖的是普通家用补充品，且能展示与补充桶对应的来源资料。各地标签要求、门店做法与产品保存条件不同。看不清或听不清时请店员提供纸卡、放大文字或逐项读出；不使用手机时可把准确名称和资料记在纸上。副作用是一张额外的身份记录，通常比一排无名瓶更省事。

## English

Load this Skill when you are about to buy an ordinary household refill, such as hand wash or dishwashing liquid, but the dispenser has too little information and the product will leave without its original package. Identify it **before filling or paying**, and retain the details you need at home. This Skill does not teach mixing, diluting, transferring, or using chemicals. Food, medicine, infant products, concentrated disinfectants, and hazardous chemicals are outside its scope.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the dispenser or sales container and any store number. Decide which identity, intended-use, and restriction details you need. Read the store's container, filling, and labelling rules; whether your own container is acceptable depends on the shop and the exact product. Do not hand over a container with unknown residue for filling. Colour and smell cannot identify what used to be inside it.

### Execution

1. **Pause before filling.** Point to the exact dispenser and ask, “Which product is in this one? May I see its original package or manufacturer information?” If dispensers stand together, have staff match the source to this particular one.
2. **Recover a usable identity.** Confirm the exact product name, brand or maker, ordinary purpose, and restrictions stated in the source. If ingredients, batch, or date matter to your decision, ask staff to check the source container, store record, or product page. Some products have no single fixed shelf life; do not invent one.
3. **Keep the information with your purchase.** Ask for a store label or product sheet. Where permitted, photograph the original package, or write down the exact name, key restrictions, and official information link. Match that information to the dispenser you will buy from. An unmarked bottle with only a store nickname is difficult to check later.
4. **Stop on a decisive unknown.** If identity, a critical restriction, or container suitability cannot be confirmed, do not fill or buy this refill yet. Choose a clearly labelled original package or wait for the shop or manufacturer to answer. Do not mix different products or assume all washing liquids can share a container.
5. **Use the shop process only after the information is complete.** The shop's rules and appropriate staff still govern filling and checkout. This Skill's result is information obtained and retained, not an instruction for chemical handling.

### Success and stop

You can connect what you take home to its exact name, source, and applicable instructions; or you stop the purchase and name the missing detail. You cannot guarantee that staff can retrieve records or that the shop will accept your container.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“It is the same as that bottle”:** Ask for the exact name and variant. Similar appearance alone does not confirm the match.
- **Details appear only in a QR page after checkout:** Ask to see readable information or the source package first. Pause if you cannot open it before buying.
- **The date or batch is unavailable:** If it matters to your decision, do not buy yet. “Usually lasts a long time” is not this batch's date.
- **Filling has begun before you discover the gap:** Ask staff to pause and follow the shop's process. Do not pour it back into the source or combine it with another container yourself.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary household refill and a shop able to show information linked to its dispenser. Local labelling rules, store practices, and product storage conditions differ. Ask for a paper card, enlarged text, or a read-aloud explanation if seeing or hearing the display is difficult. If you do not use a phone, keep the exact name and source on paper. The side effect is one extra identity record, generally easier than a row of anonymous bottles.
