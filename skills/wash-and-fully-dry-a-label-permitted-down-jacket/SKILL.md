## 中文

当一件**普通羽绒填充外套**确实需要清洗，而你能读到它的准确衣标、找到制造商对应产品的说明，并有符合说明的洗涤及干燥设备时，加载这份 Skill。关键结果是填充物**彻底干透并恢复可见蓬松**；这与羊毛湿重需平铺防拉伸的机制不同。睡袋、特殊防水膜复合羽绒、防护服、破损漏绒和专业护理标签不在本篇内。

### 准备与输入

先核衣标中的洗涤、漂白、烘干限制，再核产品型号/制造商说明是否要求羽绒专用洗剂、前开式机器、特殊程序或干燥方式；**衣标更严格时服从衣标**。不同品牌对无烘干机时是否可安全自然干燥的建议不同，不把一种写成普遍替代。看洗衣机/烘干机是否够容纳外套自由转动，能否选到说明允许的温度/程序，衣物是否有破损、装饰或严重污染。缺关键条件就停在水接触前，找制造商或合格护理服务。

### 执行

1. **确认居家路线确实获允许。** 标签禁止水洗或要求专业护理、机器结构不符、无法确认烘干温度或完成干燥时，先不要洗。羽绒失去蓬松后不是换个更热的程序就能自动修复。
2. **按制造商准备衣物和设备。** 清口袋，按本款说明处理主拉链、口袋拉链、魔术贴和抽绳；检查外层完整。按准确机型说明确认容量和程序。若制造商要求先清机器内残留或使用洗衣袋，按其要求做；不把某品牌步骤强加给所有衣物。
3. **用获批准的清洁剂洗并漂净。** 使用衣标/制造商允许的羽绒适用洗涤剂及其剂量，不随意加柔顺剂、漂白剂或混合清洁品。选本衣标与机器说明共同允许的程序、温度、脱水和必要额外漂洗；不引用另一品牌的固定 40°C 或分钟数代替你的衣标。
4. **准备漫长的干燥阶段。** 洗后托住湿衣避免集中拉扯。若标签和制造商允许滚筒低温干燥，先确认烘干机实际能稳定使用该档；按说明加入获允许的干净烘干球/球类并留足时间，中途停机翻动、轻柔拨散结团。高温快烘会伤外层或填充，不用“摸起来外面干了”结束。
5. **对照夹层与蓬松验收。** 逐个摸/看较厚隔仓、边角和缝线附近是否仍潮湿或有硬团；在允许方式下继续干燥并拨散，直到整件干透、填充物分布和蓬松接近正常。若没有合适烘干机而制造商要求机器干燥，转合格护理服务；若本款说明允许自然干，严格按其完整步骤而非自行风干后收柜。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

清洁剂已漂净，外层完好，所有隔仓**内部也干**且没有明显潮湿团块，衣标的洗烘限制未被突破。完全干透前不压缩、装袋或收进密闭柜。若干燥条件无法满足，停止家庭流程并明确交专业处理，也比半干收存更诚实。

- **衣标不清或机型不明：** 先查制造商/设备说明，不凭“都是羽绒服”猜温度和程序。
- **洗后填充物结团：** 若标签允许低温烘干，按本款说明继续翻动和轻柔拨散；不要加高温抢时间。
- **外面干而厚处仍潮：** 继续按允许方法干燥，逐隔仓再核；表面触感不能代表内部。
- **没有合适烘干机：** 查本款制造商是否允许完整自然干方案；要求烘干或信息不明时找合格护理服务，不照搬别家指南。
- **出现破缝、漏绒或异常气味：** 停止继续折腾并按制造商或专业意见处理；不承诺本篇能修复结构问题。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只适合标签允许的普通羽绒外套；合成填充棉、羊毛、睡袋和带特殊膜的复合外套不自动走同一程序。湿衣搬动或机器门口操作不便时，可请人协助搬运和核内部干燥；判断仍来自衣标和实际状态。制造商对无烘干机的建议确实不同，因此本篇不提供跨品牌的单一补救。羽绒完全干透可能明显长于洗涤本身。

## English

Load this Skill when an **ordinary down-filled jacket** genuinely needs cleaning, you can read its exact care label and matching maker guidance, and the available wash and dry equipment meets both. The key result is **fully dry fill with visible loft restored**. Wool knitwear instead needs support against wet stretching. Sleeping bags, down combined with a specialist waterproof membrane, protective garments, torn leaking shells, and professional-care-only labels are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the garment's wash, bleach, and drying limits, then check product-model guidance for a down-specific cleaner, washer design, program, or drying route. **Follow the stricter garment label.** Makers differ on whether air drying is an acceptable route without a dryer; one maker's answer is not a universal fallback. Check machine capacity for the jacket to move, the actual permitted temperature and program, damage, embellishment, and unusual contamination. Stop before wetting it if essential conditions cannot be verified, and ask the maker or a qualified care service.

### Execution

1. **Confirm home care is actually permitted.** If the label forbids washing or requires professional care, machine construction conflicts, or you cannot confirm and finish the required drying route, do not wash yet. Lost loft is not automatically repaired by choosing a hotter program.
2. **Prepare garment and machine by maker guidance.** Empty pockets; handle main and pocket zips, hook-and-loop fasteners, and drawcords for this model. Check the shell for damage and the exact machine manual for capacity and program. Follow any maker requirement to rinse the machine or use a mesh bag rather than applying another brand's steps universally.
3. **Use an approved cleaner and rinse thoroughly.** Use the label or maker's down-compatible product and dose. Do not improvise softener, bleach, or cleaner mixtures. Choose only the program, temperature, spin, and any extra rinse that garment and machine both allow. Another brand's fixed 40°C or minutes cannot override your label.
4. **Prepare for a long drying stage.** Support the wet jacket when moving it. If label and maker allow low-heat tumble drying, confirm the dryer can reliably hold that setting. Add clean dryer balls or similar aids only when permitted, allow enough time, and pause to turn and gently separate clumps. High heat to hurry can damage shell or fill. A dry-feeling exterior is not an endpoint.
5. **Check baffles and loft, not only the surface.** Feel or inspect thicker compartments, edges, and seam areas for dampness or hard clumps. Continue the allowed drying and gentle separation until the whole garment is dry and the fill looks reasonably distributed and lofty. Without a suitable dryer when the maker requires one, use a qualified service. When this exact product allows air drying, follow its complete maker procedure rather than simply hanging it and storing it later.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Cleaner is rinsed, shell is intact, **every baffle is dry inside**, no obvious damp clumps remain, and no wash or dry limit was exceeded. Do not compress, bag, or store the jacket in an enclosed place before complete drying. If a suitable drying route is unavailable, stop home care and route it to qualified help rather than storing it half dry.

- **Label or machine model is unclear:** Check maker and equipment instructions before guessing a temperature or cycle for “any down jacket.”
- **Fill clumps after washing:** If the label allows low-heat drying, continue the product's approved turns and gentle separation, without increasing heat to hurry.
- **The outside feels dry but thick areas are damp:** Keep drying by an allowed route and recheck every compartment. Surface feel is not proof of a dry interior.
- **No suitable dryer exists:** Check whether this maker allows a full air-dry route. If a dryer is required or guidance is unknown, use a qualified care service instead of another brand's advice.
- **A seam opens, down leaks, or an unusual smell appears:** Stop and seek maker or professional guidance. This Skill cannot promise structural repair.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This applies only to ordinary down jackets with home-care permission. Synthetic insulation, wool, sleeping bags, and composite membrane jackets do not automatically share a process. If moving wet garments or reaching machines is difficult, get help carrying and checking the interior; the label and real state still control the decision. Makers genuinely differ on no-dryer routes, so no single cross-brand fallback is offered. Complete drying may take far longer than washing.
