## 中文

你在普通城市里走路，手机快没电、地图断网或导航打不开，目的地仍需找到时，用这份 Skill先保住**我现在在哪、要去哪、下一处能核对什么**，再用已知可用的离线资料、街道标识或正式服务点做短段导航。目标是确认一个安全的下一段路线或得到有权人员给出的新路径；不是在手机熄屏后凭感觉一直走。野外迷路、危险天气、紧急救援与手机系统维修不在这里。

### 准备与输入

先在允许停留、不挡车流和人流的地方停下。若屏幕还亮，立即记下目的地的完整名称与门牌/入口、当前街名/路口或公交站编号，以及下一处已知地标；用纸笔、已有离线笔记或可读屏幕形式，优先保存这几项，而非一张缩小到看不清门牌的全程截图。核对自己使用的地图**确实**预先下载过该区域；Apple Maps 与 Google Maps 的离线步行/公交能力并不相同，也可能受地区限制。屏幕已无法用时，从实际街牌、楼名、站牌或可信纸图定位，不假装还有实时导航。

### 执行

1. **先确认当前位置。** 找一个可公开核对的街名+路口、门牌、建筑入口或站点编号，和目的地分开写。看不到清楚标识时，不把手机最后一个定位点等同此刻位置；先去附近可安全进入的官方信息点或有人值守处确认。
2. **只用实际可用的地图能力。** 已下载且覆盖此地的离线图可以作为静态线索，先看自己当前点和下一路口；不要假设离线图含实时封路、营业时间或所有换乘。若 App 离线状态下不提供步行/公交路线，就用可见街牌、纸图或官方现场导向，不反复耗掉最后电量搜同一按钮。
3. **定一段能核对的公开路线。** 选择到下一个清楚地标、公开入口或正式服务台的短段；到达后用地名/门牌读回，再决定下一段。路被围挡、需穿私人/受控区域或要危险过街时停止按旧图前进，改用公开、可行路线或求助。步行绕行的详细选择另作处理。
4. **必要时向有权渠道求助。** 找站方问讯处、公共建筑信息台、场所管理方或官方服务电话，说明“我在〔可见位置〕，去〔准确目的地〕，下一处公开标识是什么？” 无法确认对方身份时，不交出手机、账号、完整住址或验证码给陌生人。若需要通知约你的人迟到且仍有通信能力，给出已核对的位置和新的不确定到达状态，不编造 ETA。
5. **决定继续或停下。** 有清楚的下一可验证节点且路线安全，就逐段走；目的地、位置或开放路径仍不明时，在安全、可退出的公共点等待官方帮助或选择回到已知位置。手机若恢复网络/电力，再以现场实际位置核新路线；旧离线截图不自动变成新事实。

### 成功条件

你能说清当前可核对的位置、准确目的地和下一处公开验证点，并通过安全短段路线或正式服务点继续；或在信息不足时安全停在能求助的位置。仅保留一个蓝点截图、但不知道目的地入口和实际街口，不算恢复定位。

### 常见报错与补救

- **离线图有街道却不给步行路线：** 这是具体 App/地区能力差异；改用街牌、纸图或官方问讯，不假定 Google Maps 离线步行功能存在。
- **低电量时才想下载整座城市：** 优先抄下目的地、当前位置和下一地标；下载需联网、电量及存储，无法保证来得及。
- **最后定位点与眼前街名冲突：** 以现场可见街牌/建筑/站点核实，别用旧蓝点指挥过街。
- **路被围挡或入口关闭：** 停下，查管理方公告或到正式信息点；不走私人门、车道或无标识捷径。
- **电话也没电：** 用纸面信息和有人值守的官方服务点；请其帮助确认公开路线，不请求陌生人代你登录账户。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理白天或普通可退出的城市定位困难，不提供危机、犯罪、急救、野外生存或设备维修指导。Apple 和 Google 的离线地图功能随版本与地区改变，必须看你设备实际已下载和可用的功能；更不能把静态地图当临时封路公告。不会读地图、无法说话或行动不便时可写下当前位置/目的地给正式工作人员看，并请求可及路线。绕到信息点会增加路程，但比把手机最后百分之一电量用于反复猜路更可控。

## English

Use this Skill while walking in an ordinary city when your phone is nearly dead, maps are offline, or navigation will not open. Preserve **where you are, the exact destination, and the next thing you can verify**, then use genuinely available offline information, street signs, or an official staffed point for short route segments. Finish with one safe next segment or an accountable source for a new route. Walking forever after the screen dies is not a plan. Wilderness, hazardous weather, emergency rescue, and phone repair are outside this task.

### Preparation and inputs

Stop where you may stand safely without blocking traffic or people. If the screen still works, immediately capture the destination's full name, address/entrance, current street/intersection or stop ID, and one next known landmark. Use paper, existing offline notes, or a readable saved view; these few facts beat a full-route screenshot whose street names are too small. Check that your map app **really has** this area downloaded. Apple Maps and Google Maps differ in offline walking/transit features and may be limited by region. Once the screen is gone, use actual street signs, building names, stops, or a trusted paper map rather than pretending live navigation remains.

### Execution

1. **Establish actual position.** Find a publicly checkable street plus intersection, house number, building entrance, or stop ID, distinct from the destination. When signs are unclear, do not treat a last-known phone dot as your current position; use a nearby safely accessible official information point or staffed place to confirm it.
2. **Use only the app capability you have.** A downloaded map covering this place can be a static clue for the current point and next junction. It may lack live closures, hours, or connections. If that app provides no offline walking/transit route on your device, switch to signs, paper, or official on-site guidance rather than spending the last charge on repeated button taps.
3. **Choose one verifiable public segment.** Aim for the next clear landmark, open public entrance, or staffed desk. Read back its street/sign on arrival before planning the next segment. For a closure, private/controlled area, or unsafe crossing, stop following the old map and use a safe public route or ask for help. Detailed detour selection is a separate task.
4. **Use an accountable help channel if needed.** Ask a transit information desk, public-building desk, site management, or official service number: “I'm at [visible position], need [exact destination]; what's the next public sign?” Without verifying who someone is, do not hand over a phone, account, full home address, or one-time code. If you can still message a person waiting for you, give a verified location and uncertain new arrival status, not an invented ETA.
5. **Continue or pause deliberately.** Walk in segments when the next checkable node and path are safe. If destination, current position, or open route remains unclear, stay at a safe escapable public point for official help or return to a known position. If power or network returns, replan from where you actually are; an old offline screenshot is not new evidence.

### Success

You can state a verifiable current position, exact destination, and next public checkpoint, and continue by a safe short route or official staffed point. A safe pause at a place where help is available also counts. A blue-dot screenshot with no entrance or actual street confirmation has not restored your bearings.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Offline map shows streets but no walking route:** Capability varies by app and area. Use street signs, paper, or official information rather than assuming Google Maps offline walking navigation exists.
- **Trying to download a whole city at the last battery percent:** Copy destination, current point, and next landmark first. Downloading needs data, battery, and space and may not finish.
- **Last dot conflicts with the street sign:** Verify the on-site street/building/stop before using an old location to choose a crossing.
- **Route is fenced or entrance shut:** Stop and check manager notices or a formal information point. Do not use a private door, vehicle lane, or unsigned shortcut.
- **Phone is fully dead:** Use paper facts and an official staffed facility. Ask for a public route, not for a stranger to log into your account.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary escapable city wayfinding, not crisis, crime, first aid, wilderness, or device repair. Apple and Google offline map abilities change by version and region; verify what was actually downloaded and usable on your device. A static map is not a closure notice. If maps are hard to read, speech is difficult, or movement is limited, show the location/destination facts to official staff and ask for an accessible route. An information-point detour costs time but beats spending the last charge guessing.
