## 中文

当你发现普通衣物上的一块痕迹，却**不知道是什么或何时沾上**，用这份 Skill 判断能否做一个受控的小测试，还是先停下。这里的完成结果是选对处理路径，不是承诺把所有颜色差异洗掉。若是已知的普通饮料/食物水性污渍或油脂污渍，按相应流程；洗后才看见变化则先分辨是否串色、褪色或残留污渍。

### 准备与输入

读衣物洗涤、漂白、干燥限制，留意镶边、印花和不同材质；看标签是否真允许在家水洗。回想可能接触的东西与时间，检查痕迹位置、是否仍有可移除的表面残留。只用光线和记录判断，**不靠闻、舔、触摸不明液体来识别**。体液、危险化学品、燃油、虫害或刺激性不明污染不进入普通家洗；按适用专业/当地指导停止。

### 执行

1. **记录现状。** 拍清痕迹和标签，记下发现时间及可能来源；深色布可在侧光下看光泽，白布只用来轻触是否有尚未渗入的转移物。不要用力揉搓扩大范围。
2. **先排除衣物限制。** 标签禁止水洗、需要专业护理、面料结构复杂或边饰不耐处理时，不在明显位置试清洁剂；保留记录并咨询制造商或专业护理。无可读标签时先找可靠说明，不能靠“像棉”推断。
3. **只在有相容证据时试。** 若已能判断是普通可水洗污渍，选衣物与产品标签都允许的方法，在不显眼处按产品说明测试。先看颜色、涂层、手感是否变化；有变化立即停止，不在显眼污渍上放大处理。若仍不知来源，可先停，不把多种去渍剂逐一倒上去筛选。
4. **给这件衣服一个去向。** 确认是可按已知污渍流程局部处理、等待更多来源/厂家信息、送专业护理，或因污染风险隔离处置。待处理衣物单独放好并标记，不混入普通洗衣桶，也不先烘干或熨烫把线索改变。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你能说出衣物允许什么、污渍来源证据够不够、可用哪一个经过小测试的方法，或为何停止；衣物没有因猜测的化学品得到第二块新痕迹。

- **只凭颜色断定“像咖啡”：** 回到接触史和产品说明；同色痕迹未必同种污渍。
- **测试处掉色或起毛：** 停止局部处理，保留照片和产品名给制造商或专业护理。
- **不明污渍还湿着：** 仅在污染风险已排除时用干净白布轻按表面，避免摩擦；来源仍不明就停止加水和化学品。
- **有人建议把所有去渍剂混在一起：** 不混合产品；依衣物与产品标签重新选单一路径，或停止。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里仅涉及普通可水洗衣物上的小面积、非危险痕迹。地区符号体系、染色、涂层与去渍产品不同，清洁方法不能从面料外观猜出。看不清标签时可放大照片、用对应符号指南或请可信的人读出；不能取得来源信息时，停下比试错更可回滚。可能要暂缓一桶洗衣，且专业护理可能有费用。

## English

Use this Skill when you find a mark on ordinary clothing but **do not know what it is or when it appeared**. Decide whether a controlled small test is justified or whether to stop. The result is a sound treatment route, not a promise to remove every colour difference. Use a specific route for a known ordinary food or drink stain or grease. After a wash, first check whether a change is dye transfer, fading, or an actual remaining stain.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the garment's washing, bleaching, and drying limits, including trim, prints, and mixed materials. Confirm it really permits home water washing. Recall possible contacts and timing; note where the mark is and whether any surface material can still be lifted. Use light and records, **not sniffing, tasting, or touching an unknown liquid to identify it**. Bodily fluids, hazardous chemicals, fuel, pests, or irritating unknown contamination leave ordinary home laundry for appropriate professional or local guidance.

### Execution

1. **Record its present state.** Photograph the mark and label; write down when you found it and plausible sources. Side light may reveal a sheen on dark fabric. Touch a clean white cloth lightly only to check whether safe, loose surface matter transfers. Do not scrub the area wider.
2. **Exclude garment limits first.** If the label forbids washing, calls for professional care, or the construction or trim is too complex, do not test a cleaner in the visible area. Keep the record and ask the maker or a professional cleaner. If the label is unreadable, seek reliable directions rather than inferring them from a cotton-like look.
3. **Test only with compatible evidence.** If you can identify an ordinary washable stain, choose a route allowed by both garment and product labels and test it in an inconspicuous area as directed. Check for colour, coating, or feel changes; stop immediately if any appear. Do not scale to the visible mark. If the source remains unknown, hold it rather than applying several removers in succession to discover which reacts.
4. **Give this garment a destination.** Choose one: a known-stain spot-treatment route, wait for source or maker information, professional care, or isolation under a contamination protocol. Keep a held item labelled and out of the ordinary load. Do not tumble-dry or iron it while deciding and alter the evidence.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can explain what the garment permits, whether the stain evidence is sufficient, which single tested method applies, or why you stopped. Guesswork has not created a second mark on the fabric.

- **The colour alone looks like coffee:** Return to contact history and product directions. A similarly coloured mark need not be the same substance.
- **The test patch loses colour or grows fuzzy:** Stop spot treatment and keep the photo and product name for the maker or professional cleaner.
- **The unknown mark is still wet:** Once hazardous contamination has been ruled out, only blot loose surface material with a clean white cloth. If its source is still unknown, stop adding water and chemicals.
- **Someone suggests mixing every remover:** Do not combine products. Recheck both labels for one supported route, or stop.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a small nonhazardous mark on ordinary washable clothing. Care-symbol systems, dyes, coatings, and products vary by place and item; appearance alone cannot choose the chemistry. Enlarge a label photo, use the matching symbol guide, or ask someone trusted to read it if needed. When the source cannot be found, a pause is more reversible than blind trials. This may delay a load, and professional care may cost money.
