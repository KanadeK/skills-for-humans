## 中文

当你已[核对容器与盖可用](../check-a-microwave-heating-container/SKILL.md)，但食物还没摆进微波炉时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**这批食物的份量、厚度与覆盖方式允许按食品说明中途搅拌/转向，并能检查最难热透的位置**。微波加热可能边缘很热、中心仍冷；这份布置不替代后续实际熟度或食物安全检查。

### 准备与输入

读食品包装/可靠配方对份量、容器形状、加液/盖盖、搅动、转向、静置和完成条件的要求。确认食物没有已知储存或日期安全疑点，需生熟分开时按现有规范。对很大、厚薄差距明显或一部分尚冷冻的食物，不因为同盘放下就假定能同一时间完成；食物特定说明没有处理办法时暂停或换受控做法。

### 执行步骤

1. **按说明设可处理的份量。** 用该食物说明支持的容器和批量，尽量避免大块堆叠到无法触及中心。份量过大或容器太深时按可靠说明分成较小份；不要用更长的猜测时间掩盖热不均。
2. **让厚薄与位置可检查。** 同一批尽量按配方把相近大小的块或份放在能被搅动/翻面的状态。最厚、最密处在哪里先记住，完成检查时回到它，而不是只看最先冒汽的边缘。
3. **按包装或配方覆盖并排气。** 使用明确适用的盖或膜，保留说明要求的排气口；盖住可帮助保持水分并让加热更均匀，但密封不是“更高效”。盖不能压住食物到无法搅动，膜若要求不接触食物要保持距离。
4. **预排一次中途动作。** 说明要求搅拌、转盘或翻面的，准备合适工具和一个安全打开盖子的方向；如果食物是固体而不易搅动，按其说明计划翻转/转向。没有转盘时，只有机型与食物说明允许才人工转盘；不要在炉仍运行时伸手进去。
5. **给停止设线。** 你能指出容器适用、份量可重分布、覆盖可排气、最难热透的位置和后续检查方法，才开始。缺少食物特定加热/安全说明、无法按要求搅动或容器不能排气，就换方法或先补信息。

### 完成与停止

本批已经按食物说明放入适用容器，并为中途重分布、静置与冷点检查留了可执行路径，就是完成摆放。这里没有宣告食物已熟或可吃；加热后还要按时间/温度条件确认。未知材料、密闭盖、很大且无法重分布的份量，或生食安全条件不明时，先不启动。

### 常见报错与补救

- **堆满高而深的碗：** 按可靠说明分小份/换较浅的适用容器，不凭边缘热气估中心。
- **盖子密封或保鲜膜贴到食物：** 按产品和食品说明留出口与间距；无法确认就换明确适用的盖。
- **两种不同状态食物同盘：** 分开处理，分别按各自加热与安全终点；一个总计时器不能合并条件。
- **准备了计时却没准备搅拌工具：** 先放好适合的工具与耐热落点，别在热容器前临时寻找。
- **食物无法搅动且说明要求搅动：** 查是否允许翻面/转向；无可靠替代就改用其他受控技法。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里不设计婴幼儿食品、严重过敏或高风险生肉的全套加热参数；材料和食物具体说明优先。转盘、功率、容器形状会改变受热分布，不给统一时长。握力、视觉或听觉不便时用较小份、可读说明、易握的工具、可访问计时器或现场可信协助；若不能安全打开热盖，就换容器/做法。副作用是大份食物可能多排一轮，却不让冷点躲在中心。

## English

Load this Skill after [confirming the dish and cover](../check-a-microwave-heating-container/SKILL.md) but before food enters the microwave. Arrange **a portion, thickness, and cover that allow the food's instructed mid-cook stirring or turning and a check of its hardest-to-heat point**. Microwave edges can become hot while the centre stays cold. This layout does not establish actual doneness or food safety.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the food package or reliable recipe for portion, vessel shape, added liquid, cover, stirring, turning, standing, and finish condition. Confirm there is no known storage or date safety concern, and separate raw from cooked food as required. A large, uneven, or partly frozen item is not guaranteed to finish at once because it fits in one dish. Pause or choose a controlled method when no food-specific instruction handles that condition.

### Execution steps

1. **Choose a workable amount under directions.** Use a vessel and batch the food directions support. Avoid piling it so deeply that the centre cannot be reached. Split an oversized or deep portion under reliable instructions rather than extending a guessed time to cover uneven heat.
2. **Leave thickness and position checkable.** Arrange similar-size pieces where they can be stirred or turned as the recipe allows. Identify the thickest or densest point now and return to it for the finish check rather than looking only at the first steaming edge.
3. **Cover and vent under the package or recipe.** Use a clearly approved lid or film with the outlet required. Covering can help retain moisture and distribute heat; sealing is not “more efficient.” Do not press a lid down so food cannot be stirred, and keep film off food when directed.
4. **Plan a mid-cook action.** Have a suitable tool and safe cover-opening direction ready for required stirring, dish rotation, or turning. For food that cannot be stirred, follow its reliable turning directions. Rotate a dish by hand in a model without a turntable only when the oven and food directions allow it; never reach into a running cavity.
5. **Set a start boundary.** Begin only when the dish is approved, the portion can be redistributed, cover vents, the hardest-to-heat point is known, and a later check is possible. Find the missing food-specific heat or safety instructions, or change method, if stirring cannot be done or the vessel cannot vent.

### Success and stop

This batch sits in a suitable vessel under its food directions, with an executable route for redistribution, standing, and cold-spot checks. No claim of cooked or safe food has been made. Stop before starting with unknown material, a sealed lid, a large portion that cannot be redistributed, or unresolved raw-food safety conditions.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A deep bowl is packed full:** Divide under reliable directions or use a shallower approved dish. Edge steam is not a centre reading.
- **A lid seals or wrap touches food improperly:** Leave the required outlet and separation under both product and food directions. Use another approved cover if unsure.
- **Two different food states share a dish:** Separate and use each food's own heat and safety endpoint. One master timer cannot merge them.
- **A timer is ready but no stirring tool is:** Put a suitable tool and heat-safe landing place within reach before heating.
- **Food cannot be stirred even though its directions require it:** Check for an approved turn or rotation path; otherwise choose another controlled method.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This does not design a full heating plan for infant food, severe allergies, or high-risk raw meat. Follow material and food-specific directions. Turntable, power, and dish shape change distribution; there is no universal time. For grip, sight, or hearing limits, use smaller portions, readable directions, manageable tools, an accessible timer, or in-person help. Change vessel or method if you cannot open its hot cover safely. A large portion may need another round so the centre has fewer hiding places.
