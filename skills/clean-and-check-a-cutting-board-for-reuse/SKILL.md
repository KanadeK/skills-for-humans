## 中文

当你用完一块普通砧板，准备让它下次再接触食物时，用这份 Skill 按**材质、上次切过什么和表面磨损**决定清洗、可能需要的额外处理、干燥与去向。目标是可清洁的板按用途回到可用位置，难清洁或破损的板停止使用。木板和塑料板都不是“看着干净就一定能直接切即食食物”的许可证。

### 准备与输入

先确认上次是面包/即食蔬果，还是生肉、禽、海鲜或其他需严格分开的食物；看板是木、竹、塑料或其他材质，是否由多层胶合，以及制造商是否允许浸水或洗碗设备。准备适用手洗剂、洁净水、软洗具、能让两面干燥的干净位置。检查是否有深沟、裂纹、板层开胶或残留嵌在切痕里。生食板与即食板可用不同颜色/标记区分，但标记不能代替清洗。

若砧板接触洪水、危险化学品等特殊污染，或材料/护理说明不能确认，停止普通收尾并按当地权威/制造商指导处理。本文不教自配消毒液，也不把表面消毒剂与餐具可接触的食品安全产品混为一谈。

### 执行

1. **先判是否值得清洗复用。** 两面和边缘都看一遍；深而难刷净的划槽、裂纹、开胶、表面剥落或异物嵌入时，把板从可用队列移走。继续刷表面不能保证沟槽里的残留可清除。轻微正常刀痕与明显失去可清洁性不同，以实际表面为准。
2. **保持生熟用途分开。** 如果上一轮触过生肉禽海鲜，不让它在未完成相应清洁/必要额外处理前碰即食食物；旁边若有即食板，先保持两者不混放。不要把擦过生食板的布马上拿去擦已洗净板。
3. **按材质和制造商清洗。** 移走可见残渣，再用制造商允许的水、手洗餐具洗剂和适用软刷/海绵处理两面、边缘和把手/沟纹，随后用清洁水漂掉洗剂。木、竹、塑料或胶合板的浸水与洗碗机许可不同，不从“这块看着结实”推断可泡或可机洗。没有材料对应说明时只走可核实的温和手洗路径，仍不确定就停问制造商。
4. **需要额外食安处理时单独做。** 对生肉禽海鲜汁接触、当地指导或制造商要求额外处理的板，先洗净，再选择明确标注**适用于砧板/食品接触面**且与材质相容的产品，严格按其标签的用法、接触时间、是否漂洗执行。没有适用产品或说明时，暂不把这块板分配给即食用途，先取得可靠方法；不要把家用化学品随意混合来“加倍干净”。
5. **彻底干燥再归位。** 在干净位置把板两面都晾到干燥，避免一面一直贴湿台面；按原来的生食/即食用途分开放回可找的位置。若洗后仍有食物、油膜、异味或沟槽中无法清掉的残留，返洗或退役，不以“已经冲水”给它下一顿任务。

### 成功条件

板的材质与上次用途可追溯，表面仍可有效清洁，按其说明洗、漂、必要时完成适用食品接触面处理并充分干燥；已明确分配下一次允许的用途。深沟/破裂、特殊污染或缺少可靠额外处理方法时，隔离或退役同样是正确完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **切过生肉的板直接切水果：** 立即停止即食用途，按当地食安指导处理接触过的物品和板；以后保持用途分开。
- **只洗上面，底面、边缘或把手仍黏：** 翻板重新清洗所有接触区、漂净并让两面真正干燥。
- **深刀槽刷不出来，还继续使用：** 退役或问制造商是否有可验证的修复方式；不要凭表面光亮估计沟槽安全。
- **胶合木板被长泡或直接放入洗碗机：** 停止假设其许可，查准确制造商说明；已开胶/裂开时不要再作为食物板。
- **拿普通台面消毒喷剂直接喷食品板：** 先查是否明示食品接触面用途、材质许可和漂洗要求，不明就停。
- **未干就平叠收柜：** 移到清洁、有气流的位置晾干两面，再按用途归位。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理家庭普通砧板收尾，不提供商业厨房 HACCP 验收、灾后污染清理或化学品配方。木/竹/塑料及多层板的护理许可随产品而变，产品标签和所在地食安指导优先。看不清磨损沟槽时用亮光、放大镜或可信的人帮助观察；搬板不便时在可及台面分小动作，但仍需两面干燥。板若不再可清洁，替换有成本，却比给深沟槽颁发“看着没事”证明更诚实。

## English

Use this Skill after using an ordinary cutting board to decide, from its **material, last food use, and surface wear**, how to clean it, whether additional treatment is needed, how to dry it, and where it goes next. The result is a cleanable board assigned to a suitable next use, or a damaged board removed from service. Wood and plastic are not licences to cut ready-to-eat food simply because a surface looks clean.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify whether the last use was bread or ready-to-eat produce versus raw meat, poultry, seafood, or another food requiring separation. Identify wood, bamboo, plastic, or another material, whether it is laminated, and what the maker permits for immersion or machine washing. Have compatible hand dish soap, clean water, a soft washing tool, and a clean place where both faces can dry. Look for deep grooves, cracks, delamination, or residue trapped in scores. Colour or tactile labels can distinguish raw-food and ready-to-eat boards, but cannot replace cleaning.

Stop ordinary cleanup after floodwater, hazardous chemical contact, or when material and care guidance remain unknown. This Skill gives no homemade sanitizer recipe and does not treat a general countertop disinfectant as automatically suitable for food-contact tools.

### Execution

1. **Decide whether reuse is still possible.** Inspect both faces and edges. Remove a board with deep uncleanable scores, cracks, separating layers, peeling surface, or embedded matter from the usable set. More surface brushing cannot prove residue in a groove is gone. Minor normal knife marks differ from a surface that can no longer be cleaned effectively; judge the actual board.
2. **Preserve raw/ready-to-eat separation.** If raw meat, poultry, or seafood touched it, keep it away from ready-to-eat foods until the appropriate cleaning and any needed additional treatment are complete. Keep any separate ready-to-eat board distinct. Do not use a cloth from the raw board to wipe a cleaned one.
3. **Wash by material and maker rules.** Remove visible scraps. Use maker-permitted water, hand dish soap, and a compatible soft brush or sponge on both faces, edges, and grip or channels. Rinse away product with clean water. Wood, bamboo, plastic, and laminated boards have different permissions for soaking and dishwashers. Do not infer machine or soak safety from a sturdy appearance. Use only a verifiable gentle hand route when material guidance is incomplete; ask the maker if uncertainty remains.
4. **Apply any needed food-safety treatment separately.** For raw animal juices, local guidance, or maker instructions requiring more, wash first. Then choose a product explicitly labelled for **cutting boards or food-contact surfaces** and compatible with the material. Follow its directions for application, contact time, and rinsing. Without a suitable product or method, do not assign this board to ready-to-eat food yet. Obtain reliable guidance; do not mix household chemicals to make it “extra clean.”
5. **Dry thoroughly before assignment.** Use a clean position that allows both faces to become dry rather than keeping one wet against the counter. Store it separately or clearly marked for its original raw-food or ready-to-eat use. If food, grease, odour, or unremovable matter remains in a groove, wash again or retire it. A rinse alone does not make its next meal safe.

### Success

The material and last use are known. A cleanable board has been washed, rinsed, given any appropriate food-contact treatment, and fully dried under its maker guidance, with a clear next use. Isolation or retirement when grooves, damage, unusual contamination, or missing treatment guidance prevent reuse is also a correct result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A raw-meat board goes straight to cutting fruit:** Stop ready-to-eat use and follow local food-safety guidance for the board and contacted items; keep future uses separate.
- **Only the top is washed; underside or grip stays sticky:** Rewash all contact areas, rinse, and dry both faces.
- **Deep cuts do not clean out, but the board stays in service:** Retire it or ask the maker for a verifiable repair path; shine on the top does not assess grooves.
- **A laminated wood board is soaked or sent through a dishwasher by assumption:** Check the exact maker. Stop food use if layers separate or crack.
- **A general counter disinfectant is sprayed onto a food board:** Check explicit food-contact and material approval plus any rinse requirement before use; stop when unclear.
- **The board is stacked away while wet:** Give both sides clean air exposure, then return it to its assigned use.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary household board cleanup, not commercial HACCP acceptance, post-disaster recovery, or a chemical recipe. Wood, bamboo, plastic, and laminated products vary by maker; product labels and local food-safety guidance control. Use strong light, magnification, or trusted help to inspect scoring. If lifting is difficult, work in small actions at an accessible counter, still allowing both sides to dry. Replacement costs something, but a deep groove does not become cleanable by looking better from above.
