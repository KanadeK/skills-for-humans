## 中文

当你有能正常拍照和放大回看的现有相机，想对**一个获准静物的指定细节**正确对焦并立即核查时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张目标细节经放大确认的照片，或明确相机在该距离/光线不能合焦的停止结论。H277 处理主题与背景机位，本篇判断光学焦点是否真的落在目标，不靠缩略图猜。不要拍未经许可的私人对象或让相机靠近危险边缘。

### 准备与输入

选稳定、有可见纹理的自有物件和安全位置，决定照片中最重要的一个小细节，确认镜头干净、电池与存储足够。本机自动对焦点/手机点按位置、半按快门或锁焦方式以说明书为准；最近对焦距离有限，贴太近可能永远不清。手持要稳，脚步停下再举机；不在道路或楼梯中拍。

### 执行

1. **让焦点落在指定细节。** 在取景画面选择本机可用的对焦点或点按目标细节，等相机给出合焦反馈；若无法合焦，退到更远、增加允许的光线或换有纹理的焦点，不盲按一串。
2. **保持距离并轻拍。** 按本机半按/触控锁焦操作，必要时只小幅重新构图而不前后移动以免焦平面变了；用稳固姿势按快门。目标或相机一移动就重新合焦，不把旧锁焦沿用。
3. **在现场放大检查。** 回放到足以看目标纹理的尺寸，检查清楚的是你选的细节还是后方背景；如本机能显示对焦点也可辅助，但红框位置不替代真实清晰度。记录当前对焦方式与近距限制。
4. **必要时改一因重拍。** 若背景清楚、主体糊，重新选焦点；若整张都糊，先稳机/改善光线后再拍。第二张放大清楚才收口；设备无法达到时保留失败与原因，不声称合焦。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一张经现场放大确认目标细节清楚的照片及所用焦点位置，或本机/场景限制被诚实记下。只看小缩略图、没看目标细节，不算验焦。

- **背景清楚、目标糊：** 重新指定焦点，不只换画面裁切。
- **距离太近一直对不上：** 退到本机可合焦距离，不把物件贴镜头。
- **整张糊：** 查手抖/光线，稳机或暂停，不误判为自动对焦点错。
- **重构图后前后移动：** 重新锁焦再拍。
- **视力/手部不便：** 放大回放、稳定支撑和可信协助描述清晰区，仍由你决定收口。

### 假设与边界

相机品牌与手机系统的焦点选择/锁焦不同，说明书决定按钮。清晰度也受镜头、运动和光线影响；本篇不推断构图审美或成品传播许可。

## English

Load this Skill when an existing camera with playback must focus on **one chosen detail of a permitted stationary subject** and you can check it immediately. Finish with a photo whose intended detail is sharp on enlarged playback, or an honest stop when distance/light/device cannot focus. H277 chooses theme and background position; this judges where focus actually fell rather than trusting a thumbnail. Do not photograph a private object without permission or balance a camera near a hazard.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a stable owned textured object, safe location, and one most important detail. Check lens, battery, and storage. Focus point/tap, half-press, or lock behaviour follows this camera's manual. It has a minimum focus distance; too close may never sharpen. Stand still before raising it, never on stairs or in a road.

### Execution

1. **Place focus on the intended detail.** Select the model's focus point or tap the detail, waiting for focus feedback. If it fails, move back, add permitted light, or target a textured area instead of firing a blind burst.
2. **Hold distance and release gently.** Use the model's half-press or touch lock; if recomposing, avoid moving forward/back so the focus plane stays. Reacquire focus when subject or camera moves. Take one steady frame.
3. **Enlarge on site.** Playback large enough to see target texture. Is the chosen detail sharp or only the background? An optional red focus-point overlay helps but cannot substitute for actual image sharpness. Note mode and close-focus limit.
4. **Retake one cause at a time.** If background is sharp, reselect subject focus. If everything is blurred, stabilise or improve light first. Close only after the second enlarged image is actually clear; otherwise record limitation.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One frame has the chosen detail verified sharp by enlarged playback with focus place known, or an honest camera/scene limit recorded. A small thumbnail without target inspection is not focus verification.

- **Background sharp and target soft:** Choose target focus again, not just a crop.
- **Too close to focus:** Step back inside this model's close-focus ability.
- **Everything blurred:** Check shake or light before blaming focus selection.
- **You moved after locking:** Reacquire focus and shoot.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Magnified playback, stable support, and trusted description of sharp region can help you judge completion.

### Assumptions and limits

Brands and phones differ in focus controls; the actual manual owns them. Lens, motion, and light also affect sharpness. This makes no aesthetic or public-sharing claim.
