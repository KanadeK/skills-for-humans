## 中文

当普通鞋的**鞋带或可拆鞋垫有日常污物**，想在不把整鞋浸湿的前提下分别处理时，加载这份 Skill。结果是可清洗部分按说明清洁、全干后归回正确鞋只；它与清鞋面、换坏鞋带或晾一双雨湿鞋的起点不同。粘死的鞋垫不硬撬，带电子件/矫形定制件按专业说明。

### 准备与输入

先看鞋和配件标签：鞋带是否可手洗，鞋垫是否可拆、能否用水及其表层/胶结构。记左、右位置或拍自己可用的标记。准备小容器、兼容温和清洁液、软刷/布和通风晾位；不把鞋垫当普通衣物随手机洗。深度霉变、严重异味或材料剥离不靠香料掩盖。

### 执行

1. **只拆允许拆的件。** 解开并取出鞋带；能无力取出的鞋垫再轻拿出，粘住或连接特殊结构的留原位并查厂家。检查鞋带是否磨断、鞋垫是否碎裂；坏件转更换/服务而非强洗。
2. **鞋带按标签清洁。** 对允许水洗的普通鞋带，用少量温和合用清洁液轻搓或软刷，彻底去泡沫；不把带尖金属件的绳头猛揉断。若说明禁水，只按其专用方法。
3. **鞋垫单独轻处理。** 按可拆鞋垫的具体说明软刷表土并轻擦可湿部位，避免把泡沫芯或胶层泡透；去掉允许的清洁残留。颜色脱落、表层分离时停洗并记录，不能继续搓到材料散开。
4. **分开晾干、再归位。** 两类配件在室内通风处彻底晾干，远离直热；鞋垫底面也要查干。鞋内也干、左/右正确才放回，鞋带平顺穿回；湿鞋垫立即塞回会让这次清理失效。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

可拆配件按各自标签处理、无清洁残留且全干，回到正确鞋；不能拆/不能水洗部分被如实留在合适路线。只洗鞋带却把湿鞋垫放回，不算完成整组。

- **鞋垫粘住：** 停止撬，查鞋款说明或专业服务。
- **鞋带绳头损坏：** 不继续搓坏，换一条合适新带并重穿。
- **鞋垫吸水很久不干：** 延长通风，未全干不回装；不烘烤。
- **洗后表层起皮或深霉仍在：** 停用该鞋垫，问厂家/专业者，勿用香味遮掩。
- **手眼不便：** 左右标记、宽口小盆与可信协助拆装，有助于你核对干燥和归位。

### 假设与边界

不同鞋带和鞋垫材质不能共用机洗/浸泡规则。普通清理不治疗足部疾病，也不能恢复磨平或变形的支撑件；需特定矫形件护理时按其专业说明。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary shoe's **laces or removable insoles have everyday dirt** and you want to handle them separately without soaking the whole shoe. Finish with washable parts cleaned under their own directions and back in the correct shoes only when fully dry. This differs from upper cleaning, replacing a worn lace, or drying a rain-soaked pair. Do not pry a bonded insole; electronic or custom orthotic parts follow their specialist directions.

### Preparation and inputs

Read shoe and component labels: whether laces hand-wash, whether an insole removes, accepts water, and has layered foam or glue. Keep a left-right mark or your own reference. Have a small vessel, compatible mild cleaner, soft brush/cloth, and airy drying place. Do not put an insole into ordinary laundry without model permission. Do not perfume over major mold, strong persistent odor, or separating material.

### Execution

1. **Remove only permitted parts.** Unthread and take out laces. Lift insoles only if they come out gently; leave bonded or special structures and check the maker. Inspect for snapped lace fibres or crumbling insoles. Broken parts route to replacement or service rather than forceful washing.
2. **Clean laces under their label.** For ordinary washable laces, work a little compatible mild cleaner through them gently and rinse away suds. Avoid tearing hard lace tips. If the label disallows water, follow its specific care instead.
3. **Treat insoles separately.** Under the removable insert's instructions, soft-brush loose soil and wipe allowed surfaces without soaking foam or glue. Remove permitted cleaner residue. Stop when colour runs or layers separate rather than scrubbing the part apart.
4. **Dry separately, then return.** Let both components dry completely indoors with airflow away from concentrated heat, checking underside of insoles too. Reinsert only when shoe interior is also dry and left/right positions match, then thread laces neatly. A wet insole pushed straight back defeats the task.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Removable parts are treated under their own care, free of cleaner residue and fully dry in the correct shoe, while nonremovable or nonwashable parts are routed honestly. Cleaning laces and reinstalling a wet insole does not finish the group.

- **Insole is bonded:** Stop prying and check shoe guidance or a specialist.
- **Lace tip breaks:** Do not fray it further; replace with a suitable lace and thread it.
- **Insole takes a long time to dry:** Continue airflow and delay reinsertion without oven drying.
- **Surface peels or deep mold persists:** Stop using that insole and ask maker or professional help, not fragrance cover.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Left/right labels, broad small vessel, and trusted disassembly help can support your dry and placement check.

### Assumptions and limits

Laces and inserts vary and do not share one machine-wash or soaking rule. Ordinary cleaning does not treat foot disease or restore a worn support. A custom orthotic follows its specialist care.
