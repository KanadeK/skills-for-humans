## 中文

当你的家用缝纫机在**自有废布刚起缝时，布底突然堆成线团**，加载这份 Skill。结果是机器安全停下、废布不被强扯、可见缠线清掉、上线按本机图重穿并用新废布验证，或转维修。它与按计划比较张力不同；眼前根因常是上线没进张力盘/挑线杆，不能先随手调梭芯螺丝或继续踩踏。

### 准备与输入

立刻脚离踏板、按本机停止操作，手远离针。确认机针未断、无电线损坏或异常热/味；有任何安全风险直接关电并找专业，不拆内部。备本机说明书、好光线、合用小剪刀、新废布和可存证的第一团线照片。若线把针锁死，不能强转手轮或拉布。

### 执行

1. **先按本机安全步骤停针取布。** 让机器停止并关机，按手册抬针与压脚；缠住时轻剪可见线圈使布能离开，不在针板下盲伸手/剪刀，也不把布往外猛扯。不能安全取出就交维修者。
2. **看两条线路与针。** 检查上线是否按本机路径进入张力槽和挑线杆、压脚穿线时是否抬起；梭芯是否本机兼容、位置与线槽正确，针有无弯/钝。先记录找到的错误，不改动多处未知设置。
3. **断电状态按图重穿。** 按本机安全顺序完整抽出错误上线，抬压脚让张力盘可接线，重走各导线/挑线杆/针眼；梭芯错误则按本机图重装。清走**用户可及处**的松线，不拆深层机械或涂油；若针断/梭壳磨损，停交厂商。
4. **以新废布低速复试。** 确认盖、压脚、线尾和机针安全后做短直线，查看底面是否不再打结。若仍缠线，记录本次排查与结果并联系厂商/维修，不靠继续调张力掩盖。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

机已安全停、旧团不靠强拉取出、具体穿线/梭芯原因被检查，新废布试缝底面正常；或问题仍在明确停用交维修。把旧废布拉破取出后不检查又继续缝，不算恢复。

- **布/手轮卡死：** 不强拽或强转，停机求厂商维修。
- **上线未进挑线杆/张力盘：** 按本机图抬脚重穿后再试，先不动张力数值。
- **梭芯装反或线没进槽：** 用本机图重新安装，不照其他机的旋向。
- **针弯/断或针板有伤：** 停用并交合适维修，不能继续靠近针区运转。
- **视觉/行动受限：** 断电后熟悉机型的可信协助可定位线团，只有能安全停机才本人复试。

### 假设与边界

Singer 对布底松圈首先指向上线穿线路径，但并非所有机器故障都如此；此篇限定新近可见线团和用户可及线，不处理内部定时、马达、损坏零件或成衣重做。

## English

Load this Skill when a home machine **suddenly bunches thread under owned scrap at the start of stitching**. Finish with the machine stopped safely, fabric freed without force, visible loose loops cleared, upper path rethreaded by the exact model, and a new-scrap retest, or route to service. This is different from a planned tension comparison. A common cause is upper thread missing tension or take-up path, so do not first turn a bobbin screw or keep pedalling.

### Preparation and inputs

Remove foot from pedal and stop under the model control with hands clear of needle. Check for broken needle, cord damage, strange heat, or smell; power off and use qualified help for any danger without internal access. Have the exact manual, light, suitable small scissors, new scrap, and perhaps a photo of the first nest. If thread locks the needle, do not force handwheel or yank cloth.

### Execution

1. **Stop, raise needle, and remove fabric by safe model steps.** After motion stops and power is off, use the manual to raise needle and foot. If tied, gently snip visible loops enough to release cloth, without blind hands or scissors under the plate, and without yanking. If not safely removable, use service.
2. **Inspect both paths and needle.** Check upper thread against this model's tension and take-up routes and whether foot was raised for threading; check compatible bobbin, orientation, and slot plus bent/blunt needle. Record the found defect before changing multiple unknown settings.
3. **Rethread under powered-off safety.** Remove incorrect upper thread fully, raise foot as the model requires, and re-route all guides, take-up lever, and eye. Reinstall a wrong bobbin by its own diagram. Clear loose thread only from user-accessible area, without deep mechanical access or oil. Stop for broken needle or damaged case.
4. **Retest slowly on new scrap.** When cover, foot, tails, and needle are sound, sew a short line and inspect reverse for a new nest. If it persists, record checked items and contact maker/service rather than turning random tension controls.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Machine safely stopped, old nest released without force, a specific upper/bobbin path checked, and new scrap shows a normal reverse, or it remains stopped for service. Tearing the old scrap free and sewing onward without inspection is not recovery.

- **Fabric or handwheel is locked:** Do not force either; stop for maker service.
- **Upper path missed take-up or tension:** Rethread with foot raised by exact diagram before adjusting tension numbers.
- **Bobbin reversed or thread outside slot:** Reinstall by this model's picture, never another model's rotation.
- **Needle bends/breaks or plate is damaged:** Stop and use suitable repair; do not run near damaged needle zone.
- **Sight or movement is limited:** A trusted model-aware helper may locate loops after power off; operate retest yourself only if you can stop safely.

### Assumptions and limits

Singer points first to upper threading for underside loops, but it cannot explain every failure. This covers a recent visible nest and user-accessible thread, not timing, motor, damaged parts, or a finished garment remake.
