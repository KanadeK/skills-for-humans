## 中文

当普通庭院堆肥**已在运作**，你需要判断它太干、太湿或压实，并作一次不超出能力的小调整时，加载这份 Skill。结果是有记录的水分/通气状态和相应小修正，或因无法安全翻动而选择不动与求助。它不同于续投厨余，也不是为了追求某个高温读数。

### 准备与输入

确认场地获准、盖和通道安全，备合用手套、钝花园叉/搅拌工具、少量清水及干料。RHS 提醒翻动可接触粉尘，按个人情况选口罩或可信协助；若有呼吸、体力限制，停止扬尘大翻，不把“翻完整桶”当硬要求。动物活动、尖锐杂物或疑似不合格投料先走故障处理。

### 执行

1. **观察表层与中间一点。** 看明显干硬、湿黏成团、积水或压实；用手套和合用工具取少量材料测试，接近拧过海绵的微湿状态较合适，不能仅看颜色决定。闻到强烈腐臭或见动物先停止此常规维护。
2. **按实际状态只改一件事。** 太干就少量加水并松动表层；太湿或黏就加干叶/合格干纸并轻翻一小块；单纯压实则轻松动引入空气。一次别把水和湿料都猛加，以便下次判断变化。
3. **安全翻动或缩小动作。** 在稳固站位用钝工具把外侧一小部分带向内，手别伸进看不见的堆芯；不勉强搬重桶或让粉尘扑脸。RHS 说不易翻动时，平衡料与等待仍可分解，只是可能慢。
4. **重新关桶并记复查点。** 工具退出、盖合好、地上无溢出，记录“干/湿/压实/尚可”、本次加水/加干料/轻翻的动作。之后复查是否改善，不把一次调整写成已经成熟或无病原体。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

你能说明观察到的状态，做了与之匹配的安全小调整，桶又合好；或因风险而明确暂停并找到可信协助。盲目每天往堆里倒水、从不看湿度，不算检查。

- **干到活动停滞：** 少量补水并轻混，下一次再查；不一次灌满。
- **湿到滴水或底部积水：** 停加湿料，补干料并查桶排水。
- **翻动时粉尘扑面或体力吃不消：** 停翻、退到安全位置，改更小动作或求助。
- **出现腐臭/动物：** 转故障流程，不在这一步继续常规翻堆。
- **感官或行动不便：** 可信协助观察湿度/气味与小范围翻动，由你决定是否停止。

### 假设与边界

湿度、通气会影响分解，精确比例和翻堆频率不是所有家庭桶的固定要求。家用堆温没有被本 Skill 测量或认证，不能用本次维护推断卫生安全。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary backyard compost pile **already operates** and you need to judge dryness, wetness, or compaction and make a manageable adjustment. Finish with an observed air/moisture state and matching modest correction, or a clear defer and help route when turning is unsafe. This is separate from feeding the pile and is not a chase for a high temperature number.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission, lid, and safe footing. Have gloves, a blunt garden fork or suitable stirring tool, a little water, and dry browns. RHS notes dust exposure while turning; choose a suitable mask or trusted help under your situation. If breathing or stamina is limited, avoid a dust-raising full turn. Animal activity, sharp debris, or suspected ineligible feed routes to troubleshooting first.

### Execution

1. **Observe top and one inner sample.** Look for brittle dryness, wet clumps, standing water, or compaction. With gloves and a suitable tool, test a small sample for wrung-out-sponge dampness rather than judging by colour alone. Stop routine maintenance if a strong rotten odor or animals appear.
2. **Change only what the state calls for.** If dry, add a little water and loosen the surface. If wet and sticky, add dry leaves or accepted plain paper and mix a small area. If merely compacted, loosen for air. Do not dump both water and wet feed in at once; later results need to be interpretable.
3. **Turn safely or narrow the work.** From steady footing, move a small outer portion inward with a blunt tool, keeping hands out of unseen pile depths. Do not carry a heavy bin or lift dust into your face. RHS notes a well-balanced pile can still break down with less turning, though more slowly.
4. **Close and record a recheck.** Remove the tool, close the lid, clear spills, and note “dry/wet/compacted/acceptable” with water, browns, or small turning done. Check the change later. One adjustment is not mature compost or pathogen certification.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You can name the observed state, complete a matching safe modest adjustment, and close the bin, or pause for a real risk with trusted help identified. Pouring water daily without checking moisture is not assessment.

- **Dry enough to stall:** Add a little water and mix lightly; recheck later instead of flooding.
- **Wet enough to drip or pool:** Stop wet feed, add browns, and inspect drainage.
- **Dust reaches face or turning exceeds stamina:** Stop, step back, and use a smaller action or help.
- **Foul odor or animals appear:** Route to the failure procedure, not ordinary turning here.
- **Senses or movement are limited:** Trusted help may report dampness or smell and turn a small part while you decide whether to stop.

### Assumptions and limits

Moisture and air affect breakdown; exact ratios and turning frequency are not universal household-bin requirements. This Skill does not measure or certify a home pile's temperature or hygiene.
