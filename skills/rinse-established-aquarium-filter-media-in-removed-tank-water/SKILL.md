## 中文

当一个**已成熟运行的淡水缸过滤器**按厂家计划该轻洗滤材，或水流比本缸正常基线明显变弱，且你已有一次正常换下的缸水时，加载这份 Skill。结果是滤材可用水流恢复、仍按原结构装回，并安排水质复查；不是拆电器维修或把所有滤材换成全新。若鱼异常、缸漏电或过滤器损坏，先交水族/电气专业人员，不按此普通维护冒险。

### 准备与输入

读本型号的安全断电/开盖/复位说明及本缸滤材排列，拍自己的原顺序作参照。准备鱼缸专用桶里刚换下的缸水、手套与稳定台面，远离餐食；手上伤口覆盖，清洁后洗手。**不用自来水、洗洁精或热水**洗生物滤材，也不让滤材久置干燥。厂家规定不可自行维护的封闭机型直接停止。

### 执行

1. **先按设备说明确保安全。** 必要时安全停止过滤设备，手保持干燥处理插头，水位与线材不接触电源；湿插头、破线、漏水或异常热立即退开求合格人员。打开可维护部分时记滤材原位，鱼仍在缸内。
2. **只轻洗确有堵塞的合用滤材。** 在刚取出的缸水中轻摇或轻挤厂家允许的滤棉/滤材，让松散废物离开；不刷到全白、不用含氯自来水，不一次把所有成熟滤材彻底洗净或更换。特殊化学介质按厂家另行处理，不能混入通用生物滤材步骤。
3. **按原序复装并启动。** 滤材保持湿润且位置正确，封好盖和密封件，按说明恢复供电与水流。检查不漏水、声音/流量相对原基线合理；仍堵或漏时停并求厂家/水族专业帮助，不拆更深的电气件。
4. **记录与复测。** 记轻洗日期、哪块滤材、换下水来源及流量变化；按本缸计划复查氨/亚硝酸盐等水质，关注鱼行为。废水按当地规则处置，不把缸中生物倒进自然水体。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

限定滤材轻洗后按原位复装，设备安全无漏、流量与鱼状态检查并留水质复查；危险/损坏机型明确停止。把全部生物滤材用热自来水洗得洁白，不是清洁成功。

- **水流仍弱：** 核产品的其他堵塞点与水质记录，交厂家/水族专业者，不带电深拆。
- **滤材被自来水误洗：** 记录事件并及时向熟悉本缸的专业者求水质监测方案，不假称菌群不受影响。
- **复装后漏水：** 停止靠近电源，按设备安全程序交合格人员。
- **滤材在台面干掉：** 停止操作并求专业指导，不直接重装后宣称生物过滤完好。
- **手眼/体力不便：** 原序照片、可见标记和熟悉设备的可信协助可帮助复装，由你保留测试与异常记录。

### 假设与边界

滤材上有硝化菌，故“洗净”与“保持过滤生物功能”可能冲突；厂家和鱼种方案优先。本文只做可维护滤材的有限轻洗，不能保证过滤循环未受扰，也不指定通用更换频率。

## English

Load this Skill when an **established mature freshwater filter** is due for a maker-planned light media rinse or flow has clearly fallen below this tank's normal baseline, and removed aquarium water from a normal change is ready. Finish with permitted media reinstalled in original order, safe flow checked, and water retest planned. This is not electrical repair or replacing every medium with new. Abnormal fish, electrical leakage, or a broken filter routes to aquatics or qualified electrical help first.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this model's safe stop/open/restart directions and its filter-media order, recording your own arrangement by photo if useful. Have a dedicated bucket of just-removed aquarium water, gloves, and stable surface away from food. Cover hand cuts and wash hands after. **Do not use chlorinated tap water, dish soap, or hot water** on biological media or leave it dry for long. Stop if the maker says this sealed model is not user-serviceable.

### Execution

1. **Make equipment safe under its manual.** Stop the filter when directed, handle the plug with dry hands, and keep water and cords away from power. A wet plug, broken cord, leak, or abnormal heat means step back and use qualified help. Note the position of user-serviceable media while fish remain in the tank.
2. **Rinse only eligible clogged material lightly.** Gently swish or squeeze maker-approved sponge or media in removed tank water until loose debris comes away. Do not scrub it white, use tap water, or clean/replace all mature media at once. Special chemical media follow their maker's separate method, not a generic biological rinse.
3. **Reassemble in original order and restart.** Keep media damp, replace correctly, close cover and seals, and restore flow under device instructions. Check for no leak, sensible sound and flow against baseline. Stop and seek maker/aquatics help if blocked or leaking rather than opening deeper electrical components.
4. **Record and retest.** Note date, which media, source of old tank water, and flow change. Test ammonia/nitrite or other plan readings afterwards and watch fish behaviour. Dispose of waste water under local rules without releasing aquarium organisms to natural waterways.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Limited media have been rinsed and restored to original position, device is safely flowing without leaks, fish state checked, and water retest planned, or a dangerous unit was explicitly left for qualified help. Washing all bio-media white under hot tap water is not success.

- **Flow remains weak:** Check maker-listed other blockage points and water records with qualified aquatics help, without live deep disassembly.
- **Media was accidentally washed in tap water:** Record and promptly seek species-aware guidance on water monitoring, not a claim that bacteria are unharmed.
- **Leak appears after restart:** Stay away from power and use qualified safety procedure.
- **Media dries on the table:** Stop and seek professional advice rather than reinstalling and declaring biological filtration sound.
- **Sight or stamina is limited:** Original-order photos, visible labels, and trusted model-aware help can support reassembly while you keep test and anomaly records.

### Assumptions and limits

Nitrifying bacteria occupy filter media, so “sparkling clean” can conflict with biological function. Maker and species plan come first. This is a limited user-serviceable media rinse, not a guarantee that cycling is unchanged or a universal schedule.
