## 中文

当普通烘烤在**配方允许的检查点**发现一侧、一层或一只盘明显比另一边上色快，但没有烟火或设备故障时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**找到可验证的摆放/热区原因，在配方与机型允许时作一次安全位置调整，随后分别检查成品**。它不是以随意开门、调高温度或给食物盖满箔纸去救任何问题。

### 准备与输入

先记下配方食物、模式、原定层位、每盘在炉内的前后/上下位置、实际时间和已见的颜色差异。查这份食物是否能在此时移动：有些尚未定型的糕体过早震动会受影响；按配方而非猜测。还要核对本机型多盘间距、热风路径与推荐层位，准备干燥隔热工具和近处耐热落点。食物中心未熟/安全温度未达是另一条判断，不用上色差异盖过。

### 执行步骤

1. **先确认是真差异。** 用炉灯或在安全时短时查看，不只凭一处炉门反光。比较同批同规格盘的前后、上下或边缘；食物本身厚薄不同可能是原因，别把所有差异归咎于烤箱故障。
2. **查布置而不是先加热。** 对照[烤盘热风间距](../space-bakeware-for-oven-airflow/SKILL.md)与本机说明：盘是否靠壁、相互挡风、层位不符、门未关好或垫纸堵风口。能在不操作热盘的情况下确认问题时先记录，不能安全移动就暂缓。
3. **只在允许的节点调整。** 配方/烤箱支持时，用合适隔热工具稳稳将盘前后旋转或上下换位；开门避开热气，确认架子和盘重新稳放、门关好。若糕体尚未定型或机型禁止此动作，就不要为了“一样黄”硬挪；改在下次按可靠说明调整布置。
4. **调整后分别观察。** 记录移动发生的时刻和新位置，到下个配方检查点分别看每盘。颜色趋同不等于内部熟透；各盘继续按[成品结束检查](../check-an-oven-bakes-endpoint/SKILL.md)判断。
5. **必要时降级结论。** 一边快焦、一边内部仍不足且配方无可靠修正时停止“照常完成”的宣称，按食物与设备说明寻求下一步；持续烟味、火、燃气异味或明显温控异常退出日常流程。

### 完成与停止

你记录了差异、找到了可验证的摆放/热区线索、在允许时安全调整，并知道每盘下次何时分别检查，即完成这次恢复判断。没有移动许可、无法安全提盘、食物安全完成未知或烤箱明显故障时不强行旋转；结果可以是本轮不调整、留给下次改变摆放。

### 常见报错与补救

- **每见颜色差异就开门旋转：** 回到配方允许的检查点；过早动脆弱糕体可能让它塌下。
- **所有盘挤在炉壁旁：** 下次按机型间距重排；本轮只有可安全操作且配方允许时才调整热盘。
- **颜色浅就把温度调高：** 先查层位、门、盘材和配方，不用更热掩盖空气路径问题。
- **一盘已熟另一盘未熟：** 分别按食物完成证据取出/继续，不能用同一铃声为两盘签字。
- **热盘太重：** 不勉强换位，关热等待或请合适的人协助；下次分批或用较轻适用的烤盘。

### 假设、替代与副作用

烤箱热区、架位和风扇方式随机型变化；本 Skill 不校准温控器、维修热风或给通用旋转分钟数。视力、握力或站立受限时可用炉灯、可读记录、轻盘与现场可信协助；做不到安全换位时不要做。现实副作用是本轮可能留下一点上色差异，却不会用危险的热盘搬运把差异转移到手上。

## English

Load this Skill at a **recipe-approved checkpoint** when one side, rack, or pan in an ordinary bake is clearly browning faster, without smoke, fire, or oven failure. **Find a verifiable placement or hot-spot cause, make one safe position change only when recipe and model permit it, then check each food separately.** Repeated peeking, higher heat, or foil over everything is not a universal repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Record food, mode, intended rack, each pan's front/back and upper/lower position, actual time, and the colour difference. Check whether this food can move now; some unset cakes are affected by early movement. Use the recipe rather than guess. Recheck this oven's multi-pan spacing, airflow, and recommended racks. Have dry heat protection and a heat-safe landing place. An unfinished centre or unmet safety temperature is a separate result; browning cannot hide it.

### Execution steps

1. **Confirm the difference.** Use the oven light or one safe brief check rather than only a reflection in the door. Compare front, back, upper, lower, and edge positions among similar pans. Different food thickness may also matter; do not call every variation an oven fault.
2. **Check layout before adding heat.** Compare [oven airflow spacing](../space-bakeware-for-oven-airflow/SKILL.md) with this manual: walls, pans blocking one another, wrong racks, an unclosed door, or liners over vents. Record what you can establish without moving a hot pan. Pause when movement is unsafe.
3. **Change positions only at an allowed point.** If recipe and model support it, use proper heat protection to rotate a pan front to back or exchange upper and lower racks. Keep clear of the hot air, reseat pans and racks, and close the door. If an unset cake or manual forbids this, do not move it to chase matching colour. Change the layout for the next bake under reliable directions instead.
4. **Observe each after the change.** Note when movement happened and where each pan now sits. Check separately at the next recipe checkpoint. More even colour does not prove safe or complete centres; use the [food endpoint check](../check-an-oven-bakes-endpoint/SKILL.md) on each.
5. **Downgrade the conclusion if needed.** If one side nears scorching while another is still unfinished and no reliable adjustment exists, stop claiming a normal finish and seek food- and equipment-specific guidance. Persistent smoke, fire, gas odour, or clear temperature-control failure exits this everyday flow.

### Success and stop

You have recorded the difference, found a verifiable placement or hot-spot clue, adjusted safely when permitted, and know when to check each pan next. Do not force a rotation without permission, a secure grip, known food completion, or reliable oven function. “No adjustment this bake; change layout next time” is a valid result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Opening to rotate every time colours differ:** Return to the recipe checkpoint. Moving a fragile cake too early may collapse it.
- **Pans crowd a wall:** Arrange under this model's clearance next time. Move a hot pan now only if safe and recipe-supported.
- **Pale colour triggers maximum heat:** First check rack, door, pan material, and recipe rather than using hotter air to disguise a blocked path.
- **One pan is done and another is not:** Remove or continue each using its own food evidence; one bell cannot sign for both.
- **A pan is too heavy to rotate:** Do not force it. Stop heat and wait or arrange suitable help; use batches or lighter approved pans next time.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Oven hot spots, racks, and fans differ by model. This does not calibrate controls, repair fans, or give one rotation minute for every bake. With limited sight, grip, or standing, use an oven light, readable notes, lighter bakeware, or trusted in-person help. Skip a move that cannot be safe. Some colour difference may remain this time without transferring the problem to your hands.
