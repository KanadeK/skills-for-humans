## 中文

当你在可触及高度的普通浴缸、淋浴墙或隔板上看到**日常皂膜或少量硬水印**，要恢复表面而不损伤材质时，用这份 Skill。它不清霉斑、体液、堵塞的下水或结构漏水，也不教站到湿滑浴缸边缘够高处。皂膜、矿物印和被酸蚀的天然石面看起来可能相似；材料不明时先确认，不拿强酸试真假。

### 准备与输入

辨别表面是瓷砖、玻璃、亚克力、珐琅、涂层板还是天然石材，读安装方/制造商护理信息及拟用**单一**清洁剂的适用材料、用量、接触时间、冲洗/擦净、手套和通风要求。确认能稳定站在干燥区域，水不会溅到插座/带电设备。打开可安全使用的通风；无法通风、闻到刺激性混合气味、旧清洁剂不明或表面出现霉斑时停止。

### 执行

1. **先划出普通污垢范围。** 看白膜是表面可擦的皂残/水印，还是石材蚀痕、接缝损坏或霉样斑。只处理这次可见可及的普通污垢；未知深层变色、松动瓷砖或持续渗水，先交材料/专业指导。
2. **移开不耐清洁的物品。** 拿走洗浴用品，保护与产品不相容的五金和石材边，不让清洁液流到不准接触的区域；放稳清水与软布。地面若会变滑，先安排可见提示与擦干通路。
3. **选材质许可的最温和方法并小处试。** 按表面和产品说明清洁一个不显眼小块。碳酸盐类天然石材可能被醋、柠檬或酸性除垢剂蚀伤，应使用材质允许的中性/非酸产品；其他表面也不可由“浴室专用”四字推断适用。小处变色、失光或粗糙立刻停止。
4. **按单一产品说明处理与去残留。** 只在适用范围按标签要求的覆盖、接触和擦洗方式处理皂膜/水印；用厂家许可的软工具，不强刷破涂层/接缝。按标签冲洗或用清水擦去产品，避免残留在下一次洗浴会接触的表面。**不把漂白剂、酸、氨或其他清洁剂混用**，也不以“再加一种”补救第一种无效。
5. **干燥并验收通道。** 用材质允许的软布或刮水工具去水，擦干容易积水的边缘和地面，保持通风直至可用。表面普通可见膜减少、无明显产品残留与新损伤；仍有印但小处重复无效时停止加力，查制造商是否是矿物沉积、材料蚀痕或需专业处理。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

可触及范围的普通皂膜/轻水印已按材质与产品允许方式处理，清洁剂按标签去除，地面可安全行走且表面无新损伤；材料/污物类型不明而停下同样是合格结果。本篇不宣称做过霉害处理或消毒认证。

- **“浴室清洁剂”碰到石材后失光：** 立即停止，保存材质/产品信息问制造商或石材专业人员，不用更强酸继续擦。
- **地面仍滑：** 不撤提示，先按地材方式去水/去产品残留，再恢复通行。
- **第一种产品没起效：** 查类型与材料说明，不在未清掉残留前叠另一种化学品。
- **看到黑斑在胶缝内反复出现：** 退出普通皂膜任务，按霉害/结构问题找专业指导。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理小范围、已知普通皂膜或轻矿物印；不清除危险化学品、霉害、体液、高处或电气接触。天然石材与人造面材、镀层、封边对清洁剂反应不同，产品和材料说明共同决定路径。视力/手部能力有限时用好光线、可及范围、软工具和可信协助；不可安全够到就停止。用过的布要清洗，地面可能需要额外晾干。

## English

Use this Skill for an **ordinary soap film or light hard-water mark** on a reachable bath, shower wall, or screen when you want to restore it without damaging the material. It does not clean mold, bodily fluids, blocked drains, or structural leaks and does not put you on a slippery tub rim to reach high. Soap film, minerals, and acid-etched natural stone can look similar; confirm the material rather than test with a strong acid.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify tile, glass, acrylic, enamel, coating, or natural stone. Read installer or maker care and one proposed cleaner's material permission, amount, contact time, rinse, glove, and ventilation directions. Stand securely in a dry accessible position and keep water from electrical outlets or live appliances. Provide safe ventilation. Stop when ventilation is unavailable, mixed irritating fumes appear, an old product is unknown, or mold is visible.

### Execution

1. **Set an ordinary-soil boundary.** Check whether the white mark is a wipeable soap or water film rather than stone etching, joint damage, or a mold-like patch. Handle only the visible reachable ordinary area. Unknown deep colour, loose tile, or continuing seepage needs maker or professional assessment.
2. **Move vulnerable items.** Remove bathing products and protect metal and stone edges incompatible with the product. Keep cleaner off disallowed neighbouring surfaces. Put water and a soft cloth on a stable support. If the floor may become slippery, arrange a visible warning and a safe drying path first.
3. **Choose the mildest permitted method and test a patch.** Use the surface and product directions on a small hidden area. Carbonate natural stone can be etched by vinegar, lemon, or acid scale cleaners, so use material-permitted neutral or nonacid care. “Bathroom cleaner” alone proves no surface fit. Stop for lost shine, colour, or new roughness.
4. **Use one product, then remove it as directed.** Apply, contact, and wipe only within the label's conditions with maker-permitted soft tools. Do not scrub through coating or joint. Rinse or wipe away product where the label requires before the surface is next touched during bathing. **Do not combine bleach, acid, ammonia, or other cleaners**, and do not add another one because the first was ineffective.
5. **Dry and check the walking route.** Use permitted soft cloth or squeegee to remove water; dry edges and floor that collect water. Maintain ventilation until usable. The ordinary visible film is reduced, product residue is gone, and no new damage appears. If a small repeat still does not help, stop forcing it and ask whether the mark is mineral, material etching, or needs professional care.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Reachable ordinary soap film or light water mark was handled within surface and product limits, cleaner was removed as labelled, the floor is safe to walk, and no new damage appeared. Stopping when material or soil is unknown is complete. This is not mold remediation or sanitising certification.

- **A “bathroom” product dulls stone:** Stop immediately and keep material and product information for maker or stone specialist advice; do not escalate to stronger acid.
- **The floor is still slippery:** Keep the warning, remove water or cleaner under floor care, then reopen the route.
- **The first product did not work:** Recheck mark type and material. Do not layer a second chemical onto remaining product.
- **A black patch keeps returning inside caulk:** Leave ordinary soap-film care for mold or structural professional guidance.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers small known ordinary soap film and light mineral marks, not hazardous chemical, mold, bodily fluid, height, or electrical contact. Stone, synthetic surfaces, plated finishes, and seals react differently; both material and product directions control care. Use good light, a reachable area, soft tools, or trusted help for vision or grip limits. Stop when you cannot reach safely. Used cloth needs cleaning and the floor may need more drying.
