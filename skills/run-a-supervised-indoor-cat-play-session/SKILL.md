## 中文

当你和一只普通室内猫都在场，想做一次由它愿意参与的短时玩耍时，加载这份 Skill。结果是**猫有机会安全追逐、扑击或拍打，且结束后危险小部件/线绳被收好**；不是逼它“运动够时长”。

### 准备与输入

看猫当下是否清醒、愿意互动，是否有兽医限制、明显疼痛或呼吸异常；异常先咨询兽医，不用玩具测试。选符合这只猫体型和习惯的完整玩具，检查是否有松脱线、羽毛、小块、尖边或可吞咽部分；地面清掉绊倒物和容易摔落的东西。儿童参与时由成年人监督，不把手脚当猎物。

### 执行

1. **给选择，不抓猫。** 在猫看得见但可离开的地方轻轻移动玩具；猫不靠近或退走时结束，不追赶、堵住或拎起它强迫加入。
2. **模仿可追的目标。** 用合适玩具让猫能观察、接近、追逐和扑击；偶尔允许它抓到玩具。动作与长度按这只猫的兴趣和能力调整，避开楼梯、锐角与高处跌落风险。
3. **保持有人看着。** 线绳和逗猫杆仅在全程监督下使用，手指不直接卷进绳子，也不让猫啃食玩具；发现破损立即拿走。不要把食物奖励作为必需条件或擅自改变饮食计划。
4. **看猫的停止信号。** 它离开、躲避、过度激动或身体不适时停止，留清水和正常休息机会；持续异常联系兽医或合格行为人员。
5. **收尾。** 把线绳、可拆小件和破损玩具收在猫接触不到的地方，记录它愿意玩的方式供下次选择。玩完不等于猫永远不会无聊，日常环境还要按实际需要维护。

### 成功条件

猫有一次可自愿退出的安全互动，玩具未被吞咽或留下缠绕风险，场地已收好。猫今天不想玩而你尊重它的选择，也算正确停止；不能用延长时长强行追求“活跃”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **玩具掉小零件：** 立即停，找齐并移开；若怀疑吞入或出现不适，及时联系兽医。
- **猫把你的手当玩具：** 改用有距离的合适玩具，不惩罚也不继续逗咬。
- **猫躲起来：** 停止并给安静空间，下次从更短、更远的互动尝试。
- **玩完忘收绳：** 先收起再离开；无人监管的线绳可能缠绕或被吞。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只是你照料的普通室内猫一次非医疗玩耍，不教攻击行为纠正、幼猫发育计划或受伤处置。行动能力有限时可坐着移动合适逗猫杆，视觉受限时请人确认玩具完整和猫的位置；仍应全程监督。玩具清理是这次任务的实际收尾。

## English

Load this Skill when you and an ordinary indoor cat are present and you want one short play opportunity it may choose to join. Finish with **safe chances to stalk, chase, or bat a toy, followed by storage of strings and swallowable parts**. There is no compulsory exercise duration.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether the cat is awake and willing and whether a veterinary restriction, obvious pain, or unusual breathing makes play inappropriate. Contact a veterinarian for such concerns rather than testing them with a toy. Choose an intact toy suited to this cat's size and habits; inspect loose string, feathers, pieces, sharp edges, and swallowable parts. Clear trip and falling hazards. Supervise children; hands and feet are not prey toys.

### Execution

1. **Offer a choice, do not grab the cat.** Move the toy gently in view where the cat can leave. If it does not approach or withdraws, end without chasing, cornering, or carrying it into play.
2. **Move something it can follow.** Let it watch, approach, chase, pounce, and sometimes catch a suitable toy. Adjust motion and duration to its interest and ability, avoiding stairs, sharp corners, and high falls.
3. **Supervise continuously.** Use wand and string toys only while present. Keep fingers free of string and prevent chewing or swallowing parts; remove a damaged toy immediately. Food rewards are not required, and the established diet is not changed here.
4. **Read stop signals.** Stop if it leaves, hides, becomes overly excited, or seems unwell. Leave normal water and rest available; persistent problems need veterinary or qualified behavior advice.
5. **Close the session.** Store strings, detachable small parts, and damaged toys where the cat cannot reach them. Note the play style it chose for next time. One session is not proof that all future enrichment needs are met.

### Success

The cat had a safe optional interaction, no toy was swallowed or left as an entanglement risk, and the space is clear. Respecting a cat that declines to play is a correct stop. Do not force activity to meet a duration target.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A toy sheds a small part:** Stop, find and remove it. Contact a veterinarian promptly if swallowing is suspected or the cat seems unwell.
- **The cat treats your hand as a toy:** Switch to a suitable distanced toy rather than continuing play-biting or punishing it.
- **The cat hides:** Give quiet space; try a shorter, more distant offer another time.
- **String is left after play:** Put it away before leaving. Unsupervised string can tangle or be swallowed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is one nonmedical play session with an ordinary indoor cat in your care, not aggression treatment, kitten development planning, or injury care. Play from a seated position with a suitable wand if mobility is limited. Have a helper check toy integrity and cat position if vision is limited, while keeping full supervision. Putting the toy away is part of the actual finish.
