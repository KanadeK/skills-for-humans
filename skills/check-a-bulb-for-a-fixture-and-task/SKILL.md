## 中文

当你要为**已存在的普通灯具**买替换灯泡，同时关心够不够亮、装不装得下、能不能用于原有调光或封闭灯罩时，加载这份 Skill。目标是购前把灯具要求与候选的真实输入功率、流明和结构限制逐项核对；不拆接电线、不维修灯具，也不要求摸热灯泡读取铭牌。

### 准备与输入

从灯具说明书、可安全观察的标牌或可靠旧包装记下灯座/灯头类型、最大允许**实际输入瓦数**、可用灯泡外形与尺寸、是否全封闭、是否配调光器。旧灯泡不明或必须登高/拆机才能看时先标未知。准备候选标签上的灯头、外形尺寸、实际 W、亮度 lm、光色、封闭灯具和调光兼容说明。

### 执行

1. **先说清照明用途。** 你需要阅读、做普通家务、走廊指路，还是只是柔和背景光？记录现有光线哪里不足或过亮；用流明比较亮度，而不是把包装的“旧白炽灯多少瓦等效”当作新灯泡的真实耗电。
2. **核机械适配。** 灯头规格必须与准确灯具一致，灯泡的长度、直径和外形能放进灯罩/灯腔并符合说明。外观近似的螺口和更大的 LED 外壳都不能凭照片宣告适配。
3. **核灯具上限。** 候选标出的实际输入瓦数不得超过灯具说明的最大值；“某某瓦等效”是亮度类比，不能拿来替代输入功率。灯具标牌不可读时暂停，而不是猜一个常见上限。
4. **核环境和控制。** 全封闭灯罩需要候选明确允许；现有调光器或三路开关需要灯泡和控制设备的兼容资料。仅写“可调光”而没有与你的控制方式相符的信息时，保留未确认。
5. **作决定。** 记录“灯具灯头/空间/瓦数/封闭和调光条件 → 候选实际 W、流明和相容说明 → 满足、不满足或未确认”。每项限制都通过才进入购买；如亮度合适但结构不符，换候选，不改灯具来迎合灯泡。

### 成功与停止

你能指出满足日常用途的亮度候选、准确的机械匹配和灯具各项限制，才算完成购前核对。灯具损坏、发热异常、裸露接线、位置难以安全接近或标签无法辨认时停止；交由合适人员检查灯具。此处不保证最终照明体验，也不指导安装。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“60 瓦等效”被当成实际 60 W：** 分开找流明与真实输入 W；前者帮助比较亮度，后者才与灯具功率上限比较。
- **灯头合适但灯泡塞不进灯罩：** 查候选外形尺寸与灯具空间，换较合适外形，别硬挤。
- **有调光开关却只买写“LED”的灯泡：** 核灯泡的调光标示及与现有控制方式的兼容说明；不明则暂停。
- **小标牌在高处或看不清：** 查灯具纸质说明书、可靠购买记录或请有权限的人读数；不要为购物爬不稳的梯子或触碰热灯具。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程只处理普通可更换灯泡的选购；一体式灯具、特殊电压、应急照明或需改线的灯具不在本轮。不同地区灯头、电压和标签规定不同，使用准确灯具资料。光色偏好和亮度感受因人和房间而异，可先参照以前够用的流明记录，不把品牌广告当作人眼验收。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a bulb for an **existing ordinary fixture** when you need enough light and a match for the socket, enclosure, and any dimmer. Your pre-purchase result compares the fixture with the candidate's actual input power, lumens, and physical restrictions. It does not change wiring, repair the fixture, or require touching a hot bulb to read a label.

### Preparation and inputs

From the fixture manual, a safely visible label, or a reliable old package, record the base type, maximum **actual input watts**, permitted bulb shape and size, whether the space is fully enclosed, and any dimmer or three-way control. Keep an unknown old bulb or inaccessible high fixture unknown. Read the candidate's base, dimensions, actual W, brightness in lm, colour appearance, and enclosed-fixture/dimming statements.

### Execution

1. **Name the lighting job.** Is it for reading, ordinary work, a hallway, or gentle background light? Note whether the current light is too dim or bright. Compare brightness by lumens; an old incandescent “watt equivalent” on a new box is not the new bulb's power draw.
2. **Check physical fit.** The base must match the exact fixture, and the candidate's length, diameter, and shape must fit the shade or cavity under its instructions. Similar-looking screw bases and larger LED bodies cannot be approved from a photo alone.
3. **Check the fixture limit.** The candidate's actual input wattage must not exceed the fixture's stated maximum. A “watt equivalent” is a brightness analogy, not input power. Pause when the fixture label cannot be read rather than assuming a common limit.
4. **Check enclosure and controls.** A fully enclosed shade needs a bulb explicitly suitable for it. A dimmer or three-way switch needs supporting compatibility information for the bulb and existing control. “Dimmable” without a match to your control leaves that condition unconfirmed.
5. **Record the decision.** Write “fixture base/space/watts/enclosure/dimmer conditions → candidate actual W, lumens, and compatibility → supported, unsupported, or unconfirmed.” All relevant limits must pass before buying. If brightness fits but the hardware does not, change the bulb candidate rather than modifying the fixture.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can identify a brightness candidate for your use, a matching base and body, and support for every fixture restriction. Stop if the fixture is damaged, unusually hot, has exposed wiring, is unsafe to reach, or lacks a readable label; have an appropriate person assess it. This does not guarantee the final lighting experience or teach installation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“60-watt equivalent” was read as actual 60 W:** Find lumens and real input W separately. One compares brightness; the other is checked against the fixture's limit.
- **The base matches but the bulb cannot fit inside the shade:** Check body dimensions against the available space and choose another shape rather than forcing it.
- **There is a dimmer but the bulb only says “LED”:** Find its dimming statement and compatibility with the existing control; pause if unclear.
- **The small label is high or unreadable:** Use the fixture manual, a reliable purchase record, or help from someone with safe access. Do not climb an unstable ladder or touch a hot fixture for shopping data.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary replaceable bulbs. Integrated fixtures, special voltages, emergency lights, and rewiring are outside this task. Bases, voltage, and labeling vary by region; use the exact fixture guide. Colour and perceived brightness vary by person and room. A record of an older bulb's useful lumens can help, but an advertisement is not a live comfort test.
