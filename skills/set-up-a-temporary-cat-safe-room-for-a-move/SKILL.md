## 中文

当搬家当天有人频繁开门、搬箱和进出，而一只已由你照料的室内猫可能逃出或被打扰时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一处**资源齐全、开关门规则被搬家人员确认、有人负责检查的短期房间**。日常藏身窝允许猫自己退出；搬家安全房还必须处理开门的人和猫走失风险。

### 准备与输入

先决定猫是留在旧址某房间、在新址先入一间房，还是由已同意的可信照料者接走；具体兽医或寄养安排不在本文决定。若选择房间，确认你有使用权，门窗/猫门可关闭、空气和温度适合猫、无裸露电线/危险化学品，且房内不再需要大量搬运。找一名当天可实际负责猫的成年人；不能把“大家都会注意”当负责人。

### 执行

1. **在搬动前准备房间。** 放猫熟悉的垫物与可躲处、干净食水、可用砂盆、提笼和适当玩具；食水与砂盆分开。检查猫能从藏身处自己出来，家具没有夹困空间。
2. **明确门的状态。** 关闭窗、外门和猫门，确保猫在房内且不与搬运者混处。给门外清楚标识“猫在内—先找负责人再开门”，向实际搬运者逐人说明；标识是提醒，不能代替人确认。
3. **按约定安置并检查。** 让猫在熟悉资源附近安顿，减少噪声和频繁探视；指定负责人定时核对猫在房、食水和砂盆可用、室内温度/通风正常。若猫异常痛苦或需要医疗帮助，联系兽医，不靠长时间关门硬撑。
4. **交接开门权限。** 谁可以何时进房拿东西、谁先确认猫的位置、谁关门，讲明给每位当日工作人员。若房间必须频繁打开或门扣失效，暂停该方案并找可信照料者/合适替代。
5. **到新址重建与收口。** 先在新址核门窗、食水、砂盆和藏身点，再按独立安全运输安排让猫到房。等搬运和外门频繁开启结束、猫状态适合时再按可信指南逐步扩大活动范围；不因箱子搬完就立刻放猫出门。

### 成功条件

猫实际在安全房内，有食水、砂盆、藏处与人类负责人；搬运者知道谁能开门，检查和新址交接有时间点。若无可关门房间、猫被迫与搬运者混处或负责人不在，方案不成立，应改用已同意的照料/寄养途径。

### 常见报错与补救

- **工人不知道门后有猫：** 立即停止该门搬运，重新告知并找负责人核猫位置。
- **猫砂盆留在搬走的箱里：** 先找回可用设施或替代房间，不把猫单独关入空房。
- **窗或猫门未关：** 在猫进入前处理并核实，不能靠看门纸条堵逃逸口。
- **新址房间还在施工/搬重物：** 先不把猫送入，找已同意的安全暂住方案。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只规划一只普通室内猫在搬家开门作业中的临时房间，不教强制抓捕、运输固定、医疗镇静或未知猫的救援。行动受限时由具能力的负责人成立并复核房间，你仍需确认沟通与资源。副作用是一个房间暂时不能让搬运者自由进入，必须有明确的人类交接。

## English

Load this Skill when movers will open doors and move boxes repeatedly while an indoor cat in your care could escape or be disturbed. Finish with a **resource-complete temporary room whose door rule and human checker are known to the actual movers**. A normal hide lets the cat leave by choice; a move-day safe room must also control human door traffic and escape risk.

### Preparation and inputs

Decide whether the cat will stay in a room at the old home, first enter a room at the new home, or stay with a consenting trusted caretaker. Veterinary or boarding choices need separate judgment. For a room, verify permission, closable door/window/cat flap, suitable air and temperature, no exposed wiring or dangerous chemicals, and no ongoing heavy moving inside. Name one adult who can actually check the cat that day; “everyone will be careful” is not ownership.

### Execution

1. **Prepare before moving starts.** Add familiar bedding and a hide, clean food/water, usable litter tray, carrier, and appropriate toys; separate bowls from litter. Check the cat can leave the hide independently and cannot become trapped in furniture.
2. **Make the door state explicit.** Close windows, outside doors, and cat flaps, then verify the cat is inside and apart from movers. Put a clear “cat inside — ask the named person before opening” sign on the door and tell actual movers individually. A sign reminds people; it does not replace confirmation.
3. **Settle and check by plan.** Let the cat remain by familiar resources with limited noise and visits. The named person checks cat presence, food/water, litter, temperature, and ventilation at agreed times. Contact a veterinarian for marked distress or illness rather than extending confinement blindly.
4. **Handoff opening authority.** Tell every worker who may enter, when, who locates the cat first, and who closes the door. If the room must be opened repeatedly or its latch fails, stop this route and use an agreed caretaker or suitable alternative.
5. **Rebuild and close at the new home.** Verify new room doors/windows, bowls, litter, and hide before the cat arrives via a separately safe transport plan. Expand access gradually under reliable guidance only after heavy moving and repeated external-door opening end and the cat is ready. Finished boxes do not authorize outdoor release.

### Success

The cat is actually in a suitable room with food, water, litter, refuge, and a named person. Movers understand who controls the door; checks and new-home handoff have time points. Without a closable room, separation from movers, or an available checker, the plan is invalid; use a consenting care/boarding route instead.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A mover does not know the cat is behind the door:** Stop traffic at that door, brief them, and have the responsible person confirm the cat's position.
- **The litter tray is packed:** Retrieve a usable one or change the safe plan; do not shut the cat in an empty room.
- **A window or cat flap remains open:** Secure and verify before the cat enters; a door sign cannot close an escape route.
- **The new room still has heavy work underway:** Delay the cat's arrival there and use an agreed safe temporary arrangement.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a temporary room plan for an ordinary indoor cat during household moving. It does not teach forced capture, vehicle securing, sedation, or stray rescue. Arrange a capable checker if mobility limits room setup, but still confirm communication and resources. One room temporarily becomes unavailable to free mover access and requires a real human handoff.
