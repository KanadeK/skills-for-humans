## 中文

当一盆普通室内植物疑似根系挤满旧盆，浇水很快从边缘跑掉，或根从排水孔长出时，加载这份 Skill。你先确认它**确实需要换盆**，再完成一次有排水、不过度放大的移盆。换盆不是每逢春天的强制更新。

### 准备与输入

查这盆植物的品种说明、适合的介质和可换盆时机。准备比旧盆略大的有孔盆、接水盘和稳定工作面；不把石子铺底当作排水孔的替代。只处理可安全搬动、无尖刺或刺激性汁液风险的普通盆栽；不知身份或搬不动时请可靠园艺人员或协助者处理。铺好托盘，洗净可重复使用的盆器。

### 执行

1. **确认原因。** 看根是否密集盘绕、从孔中挤出，或原盆明显不稳；结合植物状态。单片黄叶或一次浇水快流出不足以证明必须换盆。证据不够就暂缓，继续观察或查品种说明。
2. **选容器与介质。** 选与品种相符的盆栽介质和略大的有孔盆。过大的湿介质量会增加滞水风险；若品种应留在原规格盆，改查专业的介质更新方法，别强行升级尺寸。
3. **安全脱盆。** 扶住根团，轻敲或挤压旧盆松开；不要拉扯茎或叶。若根、盆或重量使它不能安全脱出，停止并寻求协助，不用利器盲切。
4. **查看而不是强行修根。** 可轻轻松开外层明显缠绕的健康根；遇到大量软烂、异味、疑似病虫或无法辨认的损伤，隔离现场材料并请教可靠园艺来源。这里不教大幅修根或药剂处理。
5. **移入新盆。** 加适量新介质，使原有土面大致保持原深度；填入侧边空隙并轻压稳定，不把茎基埋深。留出浇水空间，检查盆稳、底孔通。
6. **交接后续护理。** 按品种与新介质说明决定首次浇水，允许多余水排出并清空托盘。记下换盆日期和理由；后续观察，不立刻以大量肥料补偿操作。

### 完成与停止

完成时，植物稳定在适配且能排水的新盆，原土面未被深埋，根团没有被强拽，旧介质已清理，首次水分决定有依据。若证据不足、根有异常、盆太重或没有适配介质，留下已核实的问题并停止。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有几根从孔中探出：** 结合整体根团和生长情况判断；不要把单一迹象当成紧急移盆令。
- **新盆特别大：** 换成仅略大的有孔盆，避免大量未被根利用的湿介质。
- **根团卡住：** 停手并请人协助或查盆器指导；拉断茎不算脱盆成功。
- **换盆后叶片短期下垂：** 记录并按品种条件复查水、光及根部；持续恶化时求助，不连续加水和肥料。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一盆普通可搬动室内植物的低风险换盆，不适用于大型树盆、带刺或有毒性不明的植株、破裂重盆和根病处理。手部握力或弯腰受限时在腰高桌面操作，并请人搬运；视力受限时让人描述根团与排水孔。盆土会有少量散落，完成后清理工作面和手。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary houseplant may have roots crowding its pot, water runs rapidly around its edge, or roots emerge from the drainage hole. Confirm that it **actually needs repotting**, then move it to a suitable draining container without a large jump in size. Spring is not a mandatory upgrade notification.

### Preparation and inputs

Check species guidance for medium and timing. Prepare a slightly larger pot with a drainage hole, a saucer, suitable potting mix, and a stable work surface. Gravel at the bottom does not replace a drainage hole. This is for a movable ordinary plant without sharp spines or an unknown irritating sap. If identity or safe lifting is uncertain, arrange horticultural or physical help. Lay out a tray and clean a reused pot.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the reason.** Look for a crowded root mass, roots pushing through holes, or a pot that has become unstable; compare the whole plant's state. One yellow leaf or one fast-draining watering does not prove repotting is required. Defer when evidence is weak.
2. **Choose pot and medium.** Use growing medium appropriate to this plant and a draining pot only slightly larger than the old one. A large volume of unused wet medium raises waterlogging risk. If the species should remain in its current size, seek the appropriate medium-refresh method instead of automatically upsizing.
3. **Remove it safely.** Support the root ball and gently tap or squeeze the old pot to release it. Do not pull by stems or leaves. If roots, pot, or weight prevent a safe release, stop and seek help rather than blindly cutting.
4. **Inspect without forcing root surgery.** Gently loosen obviously circling healthy outer roots when appropriate. Many soft roots, bad odour, suspected disease or pests, or unrecognised damage call for a pause and reliable horticultural advice. Major root pruning and chemicals are outside this Skill.
5. **Place it in the new pot.** Add medium so the previous soil surface stays about the same depth. Fill the sides and firm gently; do not bury the stem base. Leave headroom for water, and confirm the pot is stable and the hole open.
6. **Handoff to aftercare.** Decide the first watering from the species and new-medium guidance, let permitted excess drain, and empty the saucer. Record the date and reason. Do not use a large fertiliser dose to compensate for the move.

### Success and stop

The plant is stable in a matching draining pot, its old soil line is not deeply buried, roots were not pulled apart, the workspace is cleared, and the first moisture decision has a basis. Stop with the observed facts if the need is uncertain, roots look abnormal, the pot is too heavy, or no suitable medium is available.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a few roots show at a hole:** Consider the whole root ball and plant before declaring an urgent repot.
- **The new pot is much larger:** Choose a modest step up with a drainage hole to avoid excess wet medium.
- **The root ball will not release:** Stop and seek help or container guidance. Breaking a stem is not a successful release.
- **Leaves droop briefly after repotting:** Record and check species-appropriate water, light, and roots. Seek help if decline continues rather than repeatedly adding water and fertiliser.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a low-risk container change for one movable ordinary houseplant, not a large tree, spiny or unidentified irritating plant, broken heavy pot, or root-disease treatment. Work on a waist-height table and arrange lifting help if grip or bending is difficult. Ask someone to describe the root ball if vision makes inspection difficult. Some medium will scatter; clean the surface and your hands afterward.
