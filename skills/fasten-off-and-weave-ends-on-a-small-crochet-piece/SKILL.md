## 中文

当你的普通钩针小片**已经钩到要停的最后一针**，钩上仍有一活圈、起始或接线处还有线尾时，加载这份 Skill。结果是末圈锁住、所有可处理尾线沿织纹藏好，小片可以脱钩轻拿。它与 E012 整排活针收针不同；不是只剪断线就放开钩针。复杂弹力边、特殊纤维或承重连接按具体图样处理。

### 准备与输入

先看最后一行/圈是否针数、四角或边缘完成，错针要在断线前修。确认留足本纱线可穿钝头毛线针的线尾，准备合用剪刀、良好光线和平稳桌面。若线很滑、很粗或标签有特殊收尾说明，按其方法调整，不靠一个表面硬结代替藏线。

### 执行

1. **固定最后活圈。** 钩上留一圈时剪工作线、留足可藏的尾，把尾穿过这一圈并轻拉到圈锁住，不拉断纱线。没有确认尾长前别贴着织片剪。
2. **逐条辨尾并穿针。** 找起始、接线和收尾各条真实尾线，一次只处理一条。用钝毛线针沿布片背面或边缘的牢固针目来回穿过若干针，避免穿破纱线纤维或把边拉皱。颜色不同的尾按各自位置藏，不混成一个外露结。
3. **轻检稳定性。** 用手轻拉样片边而非用力扯尾，看末圈和接处是否会散；松就多走一段合用织纹、改变方向再查。材料光滑时按纱线/图样说明选适合的固定长度，不声称万能针数。
4. **只在稳固后修短。** 确认各尾藏稳再修短露头，不剪到主体针目。把钩针、毛线针和剪刀收好，记下该片纱线标签供后续护理。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

最后活圈已固定、各线尾藏入并在轻拿时不松，小片可以脱离钩针；若尾太短无法藏，明确保留待处理而不称成品。仅剪断工作线、末圈仍松开，不算。

- **末圈一拉就开：** 重新穿尾并轻锁住，先别修剪。
- **尾线太短：** 停止剪余线，查材质/图样适用补救，不靠单一松结保证牢固。
- **穿针拉皱边缘：** 退回一点，沿织纹更松地分布尾线。
- **毛线针戳穿主体纤维：** 停止硬穿，改走可见针隙或换合用钝针。
- **手眼不便：** 大孔钝针、浅色粗线、放大光与可信协助穿针，由你做轻拉检查。

### 假设与边界

普通练习片可用基础断线藏尾，纱线光滑度和后续用途决定固定要求。轻拉通过不等于商业耐用或承重认证；不复制品牌成衣图样。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary crochet practice piece **has reached its intended last stitch** but one live hook loop and starting or join tails remain. Finish with the last loop secured and all workable tails woven along stitches, so the piece lifts without its hook. This differs from E012's bind-off of an entire live needle row. Cutting yarn and simply dropping the hook is not enough. Complex elastic edges, special fibres, and load-bearing joins follow their own pattern.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the last row or round, stitch count, corners, and edge before cutting; fix a wrong stitch first. Leave a tail long enough for a blunt tapestry needle with this yarn. Have suitable scissors, light, and stable table. For slick or bulky yarn or a specific label finish, follow its method rather than trusting a hard visible knot.

### Execution

1. **Secure the final live loop.** With one loop on the hook, cut working yarn while leaving enough tail to weave, pull the tail through the loop, and snug gently without breaking yarn. Do not cut flush to fabric before checking tail length.
2. **Identify and weave each tail.** Find actual foundation, joining, and final ends. Work one at a time with a blunt yarn needle through several sound back-side or edge stitches, without piercing fibres or puckering the edge. Keep different-colour tails at their own places rather than making one exposed knot.
3. **Check hold gently.** Tug the fabric edge lightly rather than yanking a tail and see whether the final loop or joins loosen. If so, weave farther or change direction through suitable stitches. For slippery yarn follow its maker or pattern's secure length; no universal number of stitches is promised.
4. **Trim only after hold.** Once stable, cut exposed surplus without cutting main stitches. Put hook, needle, and scissors away and keep the yarn label for later care.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The last loop is secured, tails are woven and stay under a gentle lift, and the hook can leave the piece. A tail too short to secure remains honestly pending. Cutting working yarn while the final loop remains open is unfinished.

- **Final loop opens on a light pull:** Thread tail through again and snug before trimming.
- **Tail is too short:** Stop cutting and check a fibre or pattern-specific remedy instead of relying on one loose knot.
- **Weaving puckers an edge:** Back it out slightly and distribute yarn more gently along stitch paths.
- **Tapestry needle splits fibres:** Stop forcing it and use visible gaps or a suitable blunter needle.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Large-eye blunt needle, light bulky yarn, magnified light, and trusted threading help can support your gentle hold check.

### Assumptions and limits

Basic fastening suits an ordinary sample. Yarn slickness and later use change security needs. Passing a gentle pull is neither commercial durability nor load certification, and no branded garment pattern is copied.
