## 中文

你发现一项普通、非紧急服务预约的**开始时间已经过去**，尚不知道还能否继续、需要重新预约还是取消时，用这份 Skill。目标是按**本次服务**的实际规则问清状态并留下可执行下一步，不用羞愧或猜测替代联系。它不处理医疗、法律、证件、金融或交通票务专属程序；如果只是预感将迟到而尚未错过，走现有的迟到恢复 Skill。

### 准备与输入

打开真正的预约确认，核对服务方名称、地点/线上入口、当地日期时间、预约编号及最初条款；先排除看错时区、跑错分店或误把提醒时间当预约时间。记录现在几点、从何时发现错过、是否有人依赖你此次预约的结果。不要在陌生短信链接中输入完整身份或付款信息；用确认邮件、服务方官网或其官方联系方式找回预约。

### 执行

1. **先查看本次预约的真实状态。** 在服务方正式页面或消息中查是否标成待到场、已过期、已取消、允许迟到继续，或仍无明确状态。不要凭别家服务的缓冲分钟数决定直接到场；有些图书馆房间与电脑预约就有不同的迟到后果，具体规则必须看你的服务。
2. **向能决定的人说明事实并提一个问题。** 通过官方渠道说“我预约〔日期时间、编号〕，现在已错过开始。今天还能按原预约继续吗？如果不能，如何重新预约或取消？” 必要时说明你预计能到达的时间。不要把未被允许的晚到当作已获保留，也不用编理由求特殊待遇。
3. **分开三条实际去向。** 服务方明确允许继续时，核对仍可使用的时段与到达/签到方式；要求改期时，按正式渠道选择真实可用新时段并取得新确认；要求取消或已失效时，核对取消状态及是否还有需处理的材料或借用物。费用、名额和期限只能按这次服务当期规则和正式回复记录，不能保证退款或免罚。
4. **更新受影响的人与自己的记录。** 如果有同行者、接送人或协作方依赖此预约，及时告诉他们目前是“待服务方答复 / 今天还能继续 / 新预约已确认 / 已取消”，不要只说“应该没事”。日历条目可随**已确认**新时间修改；它只是提醒，不是重新预约证明。
5. **没有及时回复时保留未确认。** 服务台关闭、网页无状态或消息未回时，记下你已用的官方渠道、下一营业/核对时点和替代安排。若服务方后来说“可以”，再读回具体时段与要求。没有确认前，不把旧预约自动延续，也不要为求证明自己到过而擅闯非开放区域。

### 成功条件

你知道这次预约经服务方确认后是继续、重约、取消/失效，或明确仍待其答复；每种状态有实际时间、联系人或确认编号和下一行动。仅发出咨询不等于原预约仍有效；仅在日历里换日期不等于服务方已重订。真实可执行结果以该服务的确认和你能完成的后续为准。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把手机提醒误认成预约时间：** 回到原确认核日期、地点和时区，修正判断后再联系。
- **按另一家店的迟到宽限直接闯入：** 查本次规则或问服务方，未确认前不占用他人时段。
- **对方说“可以再约”，你写“已改期”：** 完成实际重订并拿到新确认后再改状态。
- **开始讨论费用就口头断言会退款：** 问清本次官方条款与实际处理结果，只记录已确认事实。
- **服务台没回复，你不断换渠道发送同一请求：** 保留一条可追踪记录，到其正常响应时点再跟进，不把沉默算许可。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只适用于普通非紧急预约；专业诊疗、法律手续、证件、金融和交通票务有各自规则。NYPL 的不同服务对迟到有不同后果，Seattle Parks 的不同活动也有不同取消条件；这些地点的数字均不外推到你的预约。无法用电话时可选官方文字/无障碍渠道，保留编号与回复；没有网络可在开放时间用官方服务台。错过时段可能造成真实损失，但本文不裁定费用权利，只帮你找对方确认后的下一步。

## English

Use this Skill after the start time of an **ordinary non-urgent service appointment has already passed** and you do not know whether you can still attend, must book again, or need to cancel. Ask under **this provider's** actual rules and leave an actionable next step instead of replacing contact with shame or guesses. Medical, legal, identity-document, financial, and transport-ticket procedures have their own paths. If you only expect to be late and the start has not passed, use the existing running-late Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Open the real confirmation and check provider, location or online entry, local calendar date and time, booking reference, and original terms. First rule out a wrong time zone, branch, or reminder time mistaken for appointment time. Note the current time, when you noticed the miss, and whether someone depends on this appointment. Do not enter full identity or payment details in an unfamiliar text link; find the booking through confirmation, official site, or published contact.

### Execution

1. **Check this booking's actual state.** On the provider's official page or message, look for still awaiting arrival, expired, canceled, late continuation allowed, or no clear status. Do not use another service's grace period to decide to enter. Even different library services can treat a missed start differently; your provider's rule controls.
2. **Tell a decision-maker the facts and ask one question.** Through the official channel: “My booking was for [date, time, reference], and I missed the start. Can I still use the original slot today? If not, how do I rebook or cancel?” Add your realistic arrival time if useful. Do not treat unapproved lateness as a held place or invent a story to seek special treatment.
3. **Separate three real routes.** If the provider permits continuation, verify remaining time and how to arrive or check in. If rebooking is required, choose a genuinely usable new slot through the formal channel and obtain new confirmation. If canceled or expired, check that state and any material or borrowed item still to handle. Fees, place availability, and deadlines come only from this service's current rules and reply; do not guarantee a refund or waiver.
4. **Update affected people and your own record.** If a companion, pickup person, or collaborator depends on the appointment, tell them the real state: “provider reply pending / can continue today / new booking confirmed / canceled.” Do not say only “should be fine.” Change a calendar event after the new time is **confirmed**; a calendar entry is a reminder, not proof of a booking.
5. **Keep no reply as unconfirmed.** If the desk is closed, page is unclear, or message is unanswered, record the official contact used, next open or check time, and workable alternative. Read back the precise time and requirements if the provider later says yes. Until confirmed, do not assume the old appointment extends or enter staff-only space to prove you arrived.

### Success

The provider-confirmed state is continuation, new booking, cancellation/expiry, or clearly pending response, with real time, contact or reference, and next action. Sending an inquiry does not keep the old appointment alive. Moving a date in your calendar does not rebook the service. The usable result comes from that provider's confirmation and the follow-up you can actually perform.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Phone reminder time is mistaken for booking time:** Check original confirmation for date, place, and time zone before contacting the provider.
- **Another business's grace period becomes permission to enter:** Read this service's rule or ask; do not occupy another person's slot without confirmation.
- **“You can book again” is logged as “rescheduled”:** Complete the real rebooking and obtain new confirmation first.
- **A fee discussion becomes a verbal guarantee of refund:** Ask for this booking's official terms and actual result; record confirmed facts only.
- **A closed desk does not reply and you duplicate messages everywhere:** Keep one traceable request and follow up at its normal response point. Silence is not permission.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary non-urgent appointments. Professional care, legal, ID, financial, and transport tickets follow their specific rules. NYPL services can have different late-arrival outcomes, and Seattle Parks activities have different cancellation terms; none of those numbers are exported to your booking. If calls are inaccessible, use an official written or accessible route and keep the reply or reference. Without internet, use an official desk during open hours. A missed slot may have real costs, but this Skill does not decide financial rights; it finds the next step after the provider's answer.
