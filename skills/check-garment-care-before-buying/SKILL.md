## 中文

当你还没买衣物，想确认它在你的生活里能否被**按标签持续护理**时加载。它的结果是买、不买或等信息；实际洗涤交给洗衣 Skill，尺寸和试穿另行判断。贵或好看不能自动变出干洗店、平铺空间和空闲时间。

### 准备与输入

找到实物吊牌、内侧洗护标签或准确款式的完整线上洗护说明。写下你真实拥有的机洗、手洗、平铺晾干、烘干或专业护理渠道，以及费用、路程和愿花的时间。若符号不认识，先对照商品销售地区适用的官方或制造商图例；不要根据面料触感推断可洗方式。看不清可拍照放大、请店员展示或朗读完整说明。

### 执行

1. **抄出整条护理路线。** 读洗涤、漂白、干燥、熨烫及专业护理标记，尤其保留“不可”“仅限”“平铺”等限制。正面材质名不是护理说明。
2. **找最难满足的一环。** 例如只能专业护理、只能手洗、不能烘干、必须平铺。问自己现有设备、空间、可达性和预算是否能反复满足，而非只在购买当天想象一次。
3. **核对组合与部件。** 多件套、可拆装饰或内衬可能有不同说明；不明时要求完整款式信息。不能把外套标签套到可拆内衬上。
4. **算现实负担。** 若需要专业服务，确认你所在地区实际可达、愿承担的单次费用和频率；不需预测未来总花费。若需要平铺，确认有不会妨碍生活的空间。
5. **给出决定。** 能持续执行标签允许的路线才把护理项记为“通过”。不能或不愿承担就不买。信息缺失或符号仍不明时记“待确认”，向品牌核对或选说明清晰的款式。

### 成功与停止

你能用一句话说出这件衣物的常规护理方式、最严格限制、你的实际完成途径和可能费用。不要把“轻柔程序大概可以”当成对“只能手洗/专业护理”的批准。护理项通过也不代表尺寸、耐穿或整体购买决定已通过。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看纤维成分：** 找准确款式的护理说明；同种纤维加上衬里或装饰可能改变路线。
- **误读“建议干洗”和“只能干洗”：** 逐字核对标签及制造商说明；含义不清就问清再决定。
- **标注可机洗却没有可用烘干方式：** 核对干燥步骤，确认你有晾晒空间或标签允许的替代。
- **线上页面没有护理图：** 索取准确商品的完整标签图片；不能获得就保持待确认。

### 假设、替代与副作用

不同地区的图标体系和标签要求会变；加拿大政府图例可帮读常见符号，但该页面注明相关国家标准已撤销，不能当所有市场的法律保证。若你无法读小字、操作湿重衣物或前往专业店，可请求可访问说明、现场协助或选择护理负担更低的衣物。衣架不会因拒绝购买而受伤。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a garment when you need to know whether you can **keep caring for it as labelled** in your actual life. It ends with buy, reject, or wait for information. Washing belongs to a laundry Skill; fit and sizing are separate. A high price or good silhouette cannot conjure a cleaner, drying space, or free time.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the physical tag, sewn-in label, or full care directions for the exact online style. List the machine wash, hand wash, flat-dry, tumble-dry, and professional-care routes you actually have, plus cost, travel, and time you will accept. If symbols are unfamiliar, use an official or maker's guide relevant to the selling market. Fabric feel does not prove a cleaning route. Enlarge a photo or ask staff to show or read the full directions if print is inaccessible.

### Execution

1. **Copy the whole care route.** Read washing, bleaching, drying, ironing, and professional-care marks. Preserve restrictions such as “do not,” “only,” and “dry flat.” A front-panel fibre name is not a care instruction.
2. **Find the hardest step.** It may require professional care, hand washing, no tumble drying, or flat drying. Check whether your equipment, space, access, and budget can meet it repeatedly, not just in an imaginary first week.
3. **Check sets and components.** A set, detachable trim, or lining may need different care. Request exact-style information when unclear; do not apply an outer garment's label to a removable lining without evidence.
4. **Estimate the real burden.** For professional service, confirm a reachable provider and a per-visit price and frequency you accept; no future-cost forecast is needed. For flat drying, identify usable space that will not block daily life.
5. **Decide.** Mark care as passes only if you can keep to a label-permitted route. Reject if you cannot or will not. If directions are missing or a symbol remains uncertain, mark wait, ask the maker, or choose a clearer garment.

### Success and stop

You can state the ordinary care route, its strictest limit, how you would actually do it, and any likely expense. “The delicate cycle is probably close” does not override “hand wash only” or professional care. Passing the care check does not approve fit, durability, or the whole purchase.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Reading only fibre content:** Find the exact style's care directions. Lining or trim can change what works for the whole garment.
- **Confusing “dryclean recommended” with “dryclean only”:** Read the exact wording and maker's explanation; ask before deciding if unclear.
- **Machine washing is allowed but drying is unresolved:** Check the drying step and whether you have space or a label-permitted alternative.
- **An online listing lacks the care label:** Request a full image of the exact item's directions; otherwise keep wait.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Symbol systems and labelling rules vary by market. Canada's public symbol guide is useful for reading common marks, but its page notes the related national standard was withdrawn; it is not a legal guarantee for every market. If fine print, handling wet weight, or reaching a professional cleaner is a barrier, request accessible directions, get in-person help, or choose a lower-maintenance garment. The hanger will recover from your decision.
