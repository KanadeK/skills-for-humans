## 中文

当你已有一个视觉主体，却一落笔就把它放得太满、太小或不知空白留在哪里时，用这份 Skill 在正式制作前画**两张缩略布局**，决定主体和留白的关系。结果是一张有理由的选定构图，不是完成绘画，也不是根据实物练轮廓或明暗。

### 准备与输入

写一句“观众先注意____，之后感到/看到____”。主体可以是你自己设计的图形、普通景物或无版权争议的静物；不复制受保护角色或付费构图模板。确定最终载体大致横向、纵向或方形，纸上画两个相同比例的小框，不需精确尺寸。手部、视觉条件不同，可用大框、粗笔、触觉边界或可信协助；你仍决定视觉意图。本篇不规划安全告示、医疗/法律/财务信息的关键阅读流程。

### 执行

1. **先定谁是主角。** 在第一个小框里只用一个简单块或几根线放下主体，标“主”。其余视觉元素最多画一两处模糊位置，不先填细节。问“缩到很小时，我还能看出先看哪里吗？”
2. **让空白有用途。** 圈出主体周围至少一块故意保留的空白，写它的作用：隔开杂乱、给运动方向留空间，或让观众停一下。空白不是画不完的借口，也不必按固定比例留。
3. **做第二种安排。** 在相同画幅小框里改变主体的大小、位置或空白所在侧，保持表达目的不变。不要只把第一张画得更精细；两张必须能比较一个布局决定。
4. **远看比较。** 把纸放远或眯眼看：哪张先让你注意目标主体？哪张空白更支持你的第二句意图？若主体被挤到边缘或淹没，就调整块的大小和间距，再看一次。不把“三分法”“一定居中”当机械验收。
5. **选一张并留下理由。** 在选定小框下写“我选这一张，因为主体____、留白____”。如果两张都不清楚，改一句更具体的意图再画第三个；在能说出理由前不急着扩大成成品。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有两个确实不同的纸上缩略布局、一个明确主体、一块有说明的空白，以及一句为什么选其中一张。别人是否喜欢不是本篇完成条件；只画一个精致版本却没作比较，还缺一次布局选择。

- **框里全是细节，找不到主体：** 用纸暂遮住次要部分，只留主体块和空白，再重新排。
- **两张几乎一样：** 在第二张明显改变主体大小或空白侧，不以笔触不同冒充新构图。
- **留白太多，主体看不清：** 加大主体或靠近对比边界；仍保持你想保留的呼吸空间。
- **不确定画幅方向：** 各试一个横/竖框并记用途，确认最终载体后再定稿；不强迫同一版适配所有尺寸。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一项小型原创画面规划，可用于插画、纸艺装饰或个人卡片，不决定专业设计规范或公共安全信息。文化和阅读习惯不同，视线不一定按同一路径移动；你的“先看”是设计意图，需实际观众验证才可当体验事实。纸框可放大、用高对比块或触觉边界；完成后只得到计划，正式制作还需另做。

## English

Use this Skill when you have one visual subject but tend to make it fill the whole page, disappear, or leave random empty areas once you start. Before making the final work, draw **two thumbnail layouts** to decide the relation between subject and empty space. The result is a chosen composition with a reason, not a finished drawing or an observational contour or value study.

### Preparation and inputs

Write “I want the viewer to notice ____ first, then see or feel ____.” Your subject may be an original shape, ordinary scene, or still object without a copyright issue; do not copy a protected character or paid layout template. Decide whether the final surface is roughly horizontal, vertical, or square. Draw two small frames with the same proportions; exact measurement is unnecessary. Use larger frames, thicker marks, tactile edges, or trusted help for hand or sight needs; you still choose the visual intention. This is not for planning the critical reading flow of safety, medical, legal, or financial information.

### Execution

1. **Choose the main subject.** In the first frame, put down one simple block or a few lines and mark it “main.” Indicate no more than one or two secondary areas, without details. Ask whether a tiny view still tells you where to look first.
2. **Give empty space a job.** Circle at least one region intentionally left open around the subject and name its use: separating clutter, leaving room in a movement direction, or giving a pause. Empty space is not an excuse for an unfinished picture and needs no fixed percentage.
3. **Try another arrangement.** In the second same-shaped frame, change subject size, position, or the side where space remains while keeping your expression goal. Do not merely refine the first drawing; the pair must compare a layout choice.
4. **Compare from farther away.** Step back or narrow your eyes. Which version leads you to the intended subject first? Which open region supports the second part of your intention? If the subject is pushed off an edge or swallowed, adjust block size and spacing and check again. Neither a rule of thirds nor centring is a mandatory pass condition.
5. **Choose and explain.** Under the chosen frame write “I choose this because the subject ____ and the space ____.” If neither is clear, sharpen the intention sentence and try a third thumbnail. Do not enlarge an unclear plan into a finished piece yet.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have two genuinely different paper thumbnails, one clear subject, an explained open region, and one reason for choosing a version. Audience praise is not the finish line. One polished layout without a comparison still lacks a layout decision.

- **Details fill every area and the subject is lost:** Temporarily cover secondary areas on paper, keep the main block and open space, and rearrange.
- **Both thumbnails are almost the same:** Change subject size or the side of empty space visibly; a different line quality alone is not a new composition.
- **There is so much empty space the subject vanishes:** Enlarge or move the subject near a contrasting edge while keeping the breathing room you intended.
- **Page orientation is uncertain:** Try one horizontal and one vertical frame and note the intended use; decide after the real surface is known rather than force one layout onto every size.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This plans a small original image, such as an illustration, paper decoration, or personal card. It does not establish professional design rules or public safety messaging. Culture and reading habits differ; your “look first” is an intention until a real viewer checks it. Enlarge paper frames or use high-contrast or tactile blocks if needed. The output is a plan; making the final piece is another action.
