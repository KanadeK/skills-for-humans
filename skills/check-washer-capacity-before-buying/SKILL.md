## 中文

当你准备买洗衣机，想知道标称桶容积是否足以处理**你实际的常规衣物和最大常洗件**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是得到一个基于准确型号及程序限制的“可覆盖/需要分批/未确认”判断；不负责选择洗涤程序、安装水电或搬运机器。

### 准备与输入

拿出最近一到两周的普通洗衣记录，或回想有依据的典型一周：每类衣物大概几次、哪一批最常积压、最大且确实需要机洗的物品是什么。读该大件的护理标签，排除本就不能机洗的物品。拿到候选准确型号的桶容积、外形尺寸、官方程序表及各程序允许的负载；不同品牌可用立方英尺、升或干衣重量表示，不自行编一个通用换算关系。

### 执行

1. **先写真实工作量。** 把一周分成普通衣物、床品、精细衣物等你确实分开的负载。记录会积到多大、多久能接受洗一次，而不是只根据人数推测。若你不方便称重，可先记“一篮普通衣物”“一件被套”等可重复的实物，后续与型号说明核对。
2. **找到瓶颈。** 分清是日常多次小负载占时间，还是某件大物品难以放入/展开。为最大件确认商品说明是否明确支持此类物品与所需程序；仅看标称容积或宣传中的“家庭装”不能证明。
3. **查程序上限。** 在准确型号说明书中找你会实际使用的程序、适用衣物类别和该程序的最大负载。一般最高容量可能只属于部分程序；精细、羊毛或快洗的上限可能更低。把实际负载与对应程序对比，不拿棉织物程序的数字给所有衣物盖章。
4. **分开外形与桶内容量。** 机器能放进家里是另一个判断。用候选外形、开门与安装要求核对位置；这里记录“位置另行确认”，不把机身宽度当成桶内容量。
5. **作出实际输出。** 对普通负载和最大件分别标“准确型号的适当程序能覆盖”“需分批或另用洗衣服务”“资料不足”。如果你不能方便地从机器取放重物，把可操作性也列为购前条件，不因为容量数字大就忽略它。

### 成功与停止

完成时，你能指出准确型号、常用程序的官方负载上限、实际常规负载和最大件的处理方式。找不到程序表、最大件护理限制矛盾，或需要水电改造才能安装时停止候选确认，向制造商或专业安装人员核实。此处只是购前容量判断，不是洗涤效果保证或机器操作指南。

### 常见报错与补救

- **以全机最高公斤数代表每个程序：** 找对应衣物程序的单独上限；没有就标未确认。
- **按人数直接买最大桶：** 回看真实负载频率和最大件，先区分日常重复任务与偶尔大件。宣传表里的毛巾数量不能替代你自己的物品。
- **大件“塞得进”就算能洗：** 核对护理标签、官方大件/床品说明及对应程序条件；不能确认就考虑可用的外部洗涤服务。
- **型号资料难读或不方便量/称：** 使用可放大的说明书、请店员提供可读程序表；用已知篮数做初步记录，但在购买前仍需准确型号依据。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程假设你购买的是普通家用洗衣机，且有权限根据家庭真实负载做决定。地区的容量标法、程序名和商品配置不同，语言不是地区假设。无法在家机洗的大件可以另行处理，不必让罕见单件独占整个机器选择。你可能发现更大桶不是你真正缺的东西；这是一条关于用途的结论，不是价格建议。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a washer when you need to know whether its advertised drum capacity can handle **your ordinary loads and the largest item you routinely machine-wash**. The outcome is an exact-model, programme-aware judgment: covered, split/use another service, or unconfirmed. It does not select a wash cycle for use, install water or electricity, or move the machine.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the last one or two weeks of laundry, or a typical week you can describe reliably: how often each kind of load occurs, which one backs up, and the largest item you actually need to machine-wash. Read that item's care label and exclude anything not allowed in a machine. Get the candidate's exact model, stated drum capacity, outside dimensions, and official cycle guide with load limits. Markets may state cubic feet, litres, or dry-clothing mass; do not invent a universal conversion between them.

### Execution

1. **Describe the real work.** Separate ordinary clothes, bedding, delicates, and other loads you actually keep apart. Note how large they become and how often you can wash, instead of predicting everything from household size. If weighing is difficult, record repeatable physical examples such as a usual basket or one duvet cover, then check them against model guidance.
2. **Find the bottleneck.** Is it many routine loads or one bulky item that cannot fit and move freely? For the largest item, check whether the candidate's instructions support that item and an appropriate programme. A headline capacity or “family size” badge alone is not proof.
3. **Read programme limits.** In the exact-model manual, find the programmes you would actually need, their suitable fabric types, and their maximum loads. The highest advertised capacity may apply only to some programmes; delicate, wool, or quick cycles may allow less. Compare each real load with its relevant programme, not every load with the cotton maximum.
4. **Keep footprint separate from drum capacity.** Physical placement is another decision. Check outside dimensions, door opening, and installation requirements against the site, and record that fit separately. The cabinet's width is not the drum's usable load.
5. **Record a usable outcome.** For normal loads and the largest item, mark supported by a documented suitable programme, needs splitting or another laundry service, or insufficient information. If lifting laundry in and out is difficult, include that access condition before purchase rather than letting a large number override it.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish with an exact model, official load limits for relevant programmes, your ordinary load pattern, and a plan for the largest item. Stop confirming a candidate if its programme guide is unavailable, the item's care rule conflicts, or installation needs water or electrical changes; ask the maker or an appropriate installer. This is a pre-purchase capacity check, not a wash-performance guarantee or operating guide.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The top kg number was applied to every programme:** Find the limit for the relevant fabric programme; otherwise mark it unconfirmed.
- **Household size alone selected the largest drum:** Review actual frequency and largest items. Towel counts in marketing tables are estimates, not measurements of your laundry.
- **A bulky item can be crammed inside, so it must wash:** Check its care label, the maker's bulky-item guidance, and the programme conditions. If uncertain, plan a suitable outside laundry option.
- **The manual is hard to read or loads hard to weigh:** Enlarge the official guide or ask for an accessible cycle table. A known basket count can start the record, but the purchase decision still needs exact-model evidence.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary household washers and assumes you can describe your household's own routine. Regions, capacity units, programmes, and product configurations differ; language does not imply a market. An occasional oversized item can be handled elsewhere rather than controlling every ordinary wash. You may learn that a bigger drum is not the missing capability; that is a use judgment, not price advice.
