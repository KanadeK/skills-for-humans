## 中文

当你带着自己的衣物来到共用洗衣房，却发现**可用机器都在运行，或某台已结束但别人的衣物仍在里面**时，用这份 Skill 找到不侵犯他人物品、符合现场规则的等待或接续方式。目标是获得一台真正可用且获准使用的机器，或明确改期；本篇不替房东/学校制定“过几分钟就可搬别人衣服”的通用规则。

### 准备与输入

看现场张贴的预约、排队、结束后宽限、遗留物、允许谁移动、提供的篮子和管理者联系方式；看机器是否实际运行/锁定，还是显示已结束。不要用肉眼猜屏幕剩余时间为真实结束；也不触摸别人的私人物品来找姓名。确认你自己的衣物可以安全等待，若已经是湿衣则按湿衣滞留流程处理，别为等机器让它无计划密闭。

### 执行

1. **先确认占用状态。** 正在运行、锁定或被预约的机器不使用、不强开、不暂停别人的周期。另一台若真的空且无预约/维护标记，按现场规则取其使用资格；不要只看到门开就宣布可用。
2. **已结束但衣物未取时读本地规则。** 有的场所不准用户移动他人物品，有的规定宽限后由工作人员处理，也有的允许在特定条件下放入指定干净篮；这些政策不互通。现场没有清楚授权、宽限未满足或衣物状态不明时，不动它，按公示渠道联系管理者或等待。
3. **只执行明确获准的接续。** 若现场规则明示你在可核实结束与宽限后能移出衣物，先确认提供的干净容器和指定放置处；不翻看、分类、拍摄或带走他人物品，不放地面，不替对方决定烘干或丢弃。做不到所有条件就请工作人员处理，或继续等。
4. **通知或改期时保持简短。** 若能联系到使用者/管理者，说机器编号、观察到已结束及现场规则，请对方处理；不要公开他人衣物细节或个人信息。无人回应时选择排队、另一台、预约新时段或离开，不因自己赶时间改变授权。
5. **拿到可用机器再开始自己的任务。** 确认设备空、没有故障/维护标记、自己能在周期末回来，才进入正常共用设备使用与交接流程。若本次等不到，记录下次可行时间并带走自己的衣物和用品。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你找到实际可用且获准占用的机器，或作出明确等待/改期决定；运行中的周期和他人物品没有被无授权操作。现场规则不清时停止移动别人衣物本身就是完整判断。

- **机器显示结束却门仍锁着：** 不强行打开，按本机和现场故障渠道求助。
- **没有张贴移动规则：** 不发明宽限分钟数，联系管理者或改用别台/别时段。
- **规则允许移动但没有干净指定位置：** 不把衣物放地上，等待主人/工作人员或改期。
- **其他人要求你立刻拿出正在运行的负载：** 礼貌拒绝并指向现场规则/管理者，不中断机器。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这针对普通共享设备与可查的现场规则，不解释财产权或租赁法律。哈佛、康奈尔、马萨诸塞大学等场所做法确有不同，所以不能从互联网摘一个宽限时间移植到你所在洗衣房。若阅读告示或站立排队困难，可拍照放大、请求管理者可及说明、用合法预约渠道或换时段；不要求向陌生人交出凭据。等待有现实时间成本，提前保留自己衣物的安全去向。

## English

Use this Skill when you arrive with your clothes and **every shared machine is running, or one has finished but someone else's load remains inside**. Find a way to wait or take over that respects property and the site's actual rules. The result is a truly available authorised machine or an explicit reschedule. This file does not set a universal number of minutes after which you may move another person's laundry.

### Preparation and inputs

Read posted booking, queue, grace-period, unattended-item, who-may-move, supplied-basket, and manager-contact rules. Check whether the machine is running or locked or really shows a completed cycle. Do not treat an estimated screen time as proof of completion or handle another person's things to find their name. Keep your own clothes safe while waiting. If yours are already wet, use the delayed-wet-laundry route instead of closing them in a basket without a plan.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the occupancy state.** Do not use, force open, or pause a running, locked, or reserved machine. Another machine may be available only when truly empty and without a booking or maintenance notice. An open door alone is not permission.
2. **For a finished uncollected load, read local rules.** Some sites forbid users from moving another person's things, some have staff handle items after a grace period, and others allow a limited move into a specified clean basket. These rules do not travel between sites. With no clear permission, an unmet grace period, or uncertain load state, leave it alone and use the posted manager route or wait.
3. **Act only on explicit permission.** If the rule clearly authorises you after a verified end and grace period, first confirm a clean supplied container and designated location. Do not search, sort, photograph, take away, floor-drop, tumble, or discard someone else's clothing. If any condition is missing, let staff handle it or keep waiting.
4. **Keep contact brief.** If you can reach the owner or manager, state machine number, observed completion, and the local rule and ask them to act. Do not publicise garment details or personal information. If no one replies, queue, use another machine, book a new time, or leave; your deadline does not change authority.
5. **Start your own task only on an available machine.** Confirm it is empty, without a fault or maintenance marker, and you can return at cycle end. Then use the shared-machine use and handoff workflow. If you cannot get one now, note a realistic next time and take your own clothes and supplies with you.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have an available machine you may use or a definite wait or reschedule decision. No running cycle or another person's property was handled without authority. When local rules are unclear, stopping before moving someone else's clothes is a complete decision.

- **The display says finished but the door is locked:** Do not force it. Use the machine and site fault route.
- **No posted movement rule exists:** Do not invent grace minutes. Ask the manager or choose another machine or time.
- **Movement is allowed but no clean designated place exists:** Do not put clothes on the floor. Wait for owner or staff or reschedule.
- **Someone asks you to remove a running load:** Decline politely and refer to site rules or staff; do not interrupt the cycle.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary shared machines with knowable site rules, not property or tenancy law. Harvard, Cornell, and UMass Lowell show materially different policies, so an online grace period is not permission at your site. Enlarge a notice, ask management for accessible directions, use an authorised booking route, or come at another time if reading or standing in a queue is hard. Do not hand credentials to a stranger. Waiting costs real time; keep your own load in a suitable state.
