## 中文

当你想把一朵**自有或明确获准、已知且表面干燥的普通观赏花**保存成可放在纸上的扁平材料时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一朵干透、能轻取出的压花；这是平面保存，不是倒挂保留立体花形。不要采未经允许的野花，不压身份不明、有毒或强刺激性植物，也不把压花当可食用装饰。

### 准备与输入

选一朵新鲜但表面无露水的花，优先较薄、能平摊的花瓣和叶；厚实多汁的花不适合这条简单路径。准备干净吸水纸、不会因可能染色而可惜的自有厚书或两块普通压板、平整干燥台面。书页与花之间多垫一层可替换纸；贵重书、借书和电子设备不当压板。手部不便可请可信的人搬书。

### 执行

1. **检查许可与材料。** 确认花的来源、种类和接触可行。花或纸已经潮湿、发霉或染料会污染他人物品时，先换材料；不靠加热把湿花快速烫干。
2. **铺开并夹入。** 花放在两张吸水纸之间，轻轻摊开花瓣和叶，尽量不重叠。纸包放到自有厚书内或压板之间，上下保持平整；让花远离书脊或尖锐夹物。
3. **加稳定重量并记日期。** 合上书或压板，放在干燥不被挪动处，外面标明开始日期和内容，避免别人误翻。重量只需让纸平贴，不让重物悬在桌边或压坏家具。
4. **隔时查干燥与取出。** 按花厚、纸湿和环境实际情况检查；如果纸潮就换干纸，并保持花瓣位置。发现霉味/霉点时停止保存并安全清理。花整体干、平、能轻轻离纸而不粘时慢慢取出，放入干燥纸夹保存。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

花已平整且干透，纸未持续潮湿、没有霉迹，作品可轻取并知道来源。只夹进书里一天、花仍软湿，不算完成；伊利诺伊 4-H 的等待天数只是示例，不能代替实际检查。

- **书页出现色迹或潮印：** 暂停，换厚些的可替换垫纸和不怕留痕的自有书/板；别把湿页合回贵重书。
- **花瓣重叠导致湿点：** 小心重铺，更换干纸；厚而多汁的花换其他有依据方法或结束。
- **纸或花发霉：** 停止该次保存，安全丢弃并清理接触处，不把霉花粘进作品。
- **取出时粘纸或易碎：** 不猛拉；先确认干燥，再用干净薄纸或可信协助轻揭，允许不完整并记录结果。
- **视觉或手部操作困难：** 用大张浅色垫纸、可及照明和可信协助搬压板；由你核对干燥和可取状态。

### 假设与边界

普通纸压会改变花色和形状，无法保证纪念花原貌，也不适合含水量高的所有种类。这里不使用熨斗、微波、树脂或干燥化学品。压花只做私人观赏材料，分享时仍尊重来源和他人的作品/植物权利。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to turn one **owned or explicitly permitted, known, surface-dry ordinary ornamental flower** into flat material you can keep on paper. Finish with a fully dry pressed flower that can be lifted gently. This is planar preservation, unlike hanging a bunch to keep depth. Do not pick wild flowers without permission, press unidentified, toxic, or strongly irritating plants, or use the pressed result as edible decoration.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a fresh flower with no dew. Thin petals and leaves that can lie flat are easier; a thick juicy bloom is not suited to this simple path. Have clean absorbent paper, an owned heavy book you can risk staining or plain press boards, and a dry flat surface. Put replaceable paper between flower and book pages. Valued or borrowed books and electronics are not press boards. Ask trusted help to lift a heavy book if grip is limited.

### Execution

1. **Check permission and material.** Confirm source, identity, and safe contact. Replace damp or moldy material, or material likely to mark another person's property. Do not heat a wet flower to rush drying.
2. **Spread and sandwich.** Place the bloom between absorbent sheets, arranging petals and leaves with little overlap. Put the paper packet inside your book or between boards, flat and away from the spine or sharp objects.
3. **Add steady weight and date it.** Close the book or boards in a dry undisturbed spot and label the start date and contents so no one opens it by surprise. Use enough weight for contact without balancing heavy objects at a table edge or crushing furniture.
4. **Check dryness later and lift.** Inspect based on flower thickness, damp paper, and actual conditions. Replace damp sheets carefully, keeping the flower spread. If mold or a musty smell appears, end the preservation and clean safely. When dry throughout, flat, and gently separable from paper, lift slowly and store between dry sheets.

### Success, problems, and recovery

The flower is flat and dry throughout, the paper is no longer damp, no mold appears, and you can lift it while knowing its source. A flower left in a book for one day while still soft and wet is not finished. The Illinois 4-H waiting period is an example, not a substitute for checking the material.

- **Book pages show colour or damp marks:** Pause and use thicker replaceable sheets and an owned book or boards that can bear marks. Do not close damp pages back into a valued book.
- **Overlapping petals trap moisture:** Gently spread them and replace paper. Change method or stop for thick juicy flowers.
- **Paper or flower molds:** Stop, discard safely, and clean the contact area; do not glue moldy material into a project.
- **The dried flower sticks or breaks on removal:** Do not pull hard. Confirm dryness and lift with a clean thin sheet or trusted help, accepting and recording an imperfect result.
- **Vision or grip is limited:** Use large pale sheets, accessible light, and trusted help moving the boards while you verify dryness and liftability.

### Assumptions and limits

Ordinary paper pressing changes colour and shape; it cannot preserve every keepsake as it looked fresh and does not suit all high-moisture species. This path does not use irons, microwaves, resin, or drying chemicals. The result is private ornamental material; sharing still respects its source and other people's work or plant rights.
