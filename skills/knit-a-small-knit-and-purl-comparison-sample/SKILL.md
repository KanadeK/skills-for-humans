## 中文

当你有已匹配的普通纱线和一对合用织针，想**实际织一片能看到正针与反针差别的小样**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是若干行仍在针上的原创练习布，前段每行织正针、后段正反两面交替；不是毛衣，也还未收针。正针与反针是同一小样的两类动作，不分成两份标题。针尖远离眼睛和儿童活动区，坐姿不稳或手疼就停。

### 准备与输入

用光线充足、椅子稳定的桌面，拿已知护理标签的线和两根相同尺寸、针头完整的普通织针。先选少量例如 8–12 个线圈与短行数，实际按手部能力调整；这不是成衣图样或标准密度。把线团放在不易滚远处，先找一个中途暂停时能把活线圈安全留在针上的位置。

### 执行

1. **少量起针并点数。** 在左针打滑结作第一个线圈，用右针从前向后穿过该圈，绕工作线、拉出新圈，再把新圈放回左针；重复到你选的少量圈数。圈要能沿针滑但不自行掉落。太紧就重新放松起针，不用硬推伤手。
2. **织一段每行都正针。** 左手持有圈的针，工作线在后；右针从前向后穿过左针第一圈，绕工作线、拉新圈到右针，再放下旧圈。整行逐圈做，转过针、下一行仍正针，重复几行。每行末点数，掉圈就停在原位。
3. **再织正反交替的短段。** 保留同样针数，做一行正针；转面后把工作线置于前，右针由右向左、朝自己方向穿入左针第一圈，绕线拉新圈，放下旧圈，整行反针。再交替正行、反行几次。换动作时把工作线从两针之间移前/后，不从针外绕出意外加针。
4. **对照并安全暂停。** 看前段两面凸纹与后段一面较平 V 形、另一面凸粒的差别；后段边缘卷曲可记录，不等于整件失败。点数、记已完成行与下一行是正或反，把活圈留在针上并安全收起，不让线团拉跑针。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一片仍在针上的小样有可指出的两段纹理，线圈数量可数且下一行明确；收针/藏线属于后续结果。只起针未织行，或不知下一行方向，尚未完成此样片。

- **线圈紧到推不动：** 不强拉，放松重试或换本线适用针径。
- **行末多了一圈：** 查是否换正/反时线从针外绕出，回到那行按可靠教程改或重新小样。
- **少了一圈、出现垂直梯状线：** 先挂住掉圈，转掉针恢复流程，别继续织让它跑长。
- **后段边缘卷曲：** 记录平针面常见表现，不靠猛拉改；比较中心纹理即可。
- **手、眼或坐姿疲劳：** 分次完成，用浅色粗线、放大照明和可信现场协助数圈；疼痛时停。

### 假设与边界

这里采用普通手织的正反针，线在针后的正针和线在针前的反针须依你持针方式确认。它是一片个人学习小样，不复制付费图样或声称尺寸可穿。

## English

Load this Skill when matched ordinary yarn and needles are ready and you want to **knit a small piece that visibly compares knit and purl texture**. Finish with original practice fabric still on the needle: a knit-every-row section followed by alternating knit and purl rows. It is no sweater and not yet bound off. Knit and purl are two moves in one sampler, not two counted titles. Keep tips away from eyes and children's activity; pause for unstable seating or painful hands.

### Preparation and inputs

Sit with good light at a stable chair and use labelled yarn and a sound equal-sized pair of ordinary needles. Choose a small number such as 8–12 loops and short rows, adjusting to your hands. This is not a garment pattern or gauge standard. Keep the yarn ball from rolling away and find a safe place to leave live loops on a needle at a break.

### Execution

1. **Cast on a few and count.** Make a slip knot on the left needle as the first loop. Put the right needle through it front to back, wrap working yarn, draw a new loop, and place that loop back on the left needle. Repeat to your chosen small count. Loops should slide but not fall off. Restart more loosely rather than forcing tight loops painfully.
2. **Knit every row for one section.** With loops on the left needle and working yarn behind, put right needle through the front of the first left loop toward the back, wrap the yarn, pull a new loop to the right needle, and let the old loop off. Work across, turn the needles, knit the next row too, and repeat several rows. Count at row end and pause immediately on a dropped loop.
3. **Make a short knit/purl alternating section.** Keep the same loop count and knit one row. Turn, bring working yarn to the front, insert right needle into the first left loop from right to left toward you, wrap and pull a new loop through, and release the old loop; purl across. Repeat alternating knit and purl rows a few times. Move yarn between needles when switching front and back, not around the outside where it may create an extra stitch.
4. **Compare and pause safely.** Identify bumpy ridges on both faces of the first section and a flatter V side with bumpier reverse on the second. Record any curling instead of calling the whole work failed. Count stitches, note last row and whether the next is knit or purl, and leave live loops secured on the needle without yarn tugging them off.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The small piece still on a needle has two identifiable textures, countable loops, and a clear next row. Binding off and weaving tails are later outcomes. Cast-on loops without rows or an unknown row direction do not complete the sampler.

- **Loops are too tight to slide:** Do not force them; restart loosely or use a needle suitable for this yarn.
- **An extra loop appears:** Check whether yarn travelled outside the needles when switching knit and purl; return to that row under a reliable tutorial or restart the small sample.
- **A loop disappears with a ladder below:** Catch it and use the dropped-stitch recovery rather than knitting onward.
- **Second section curls:** Record a common stockinette response and compare the central texture without forcing it flat.
- **Hand, eye, or seating fatigue:** Split sessions, use light bulky yarn, magnified lighting, or trusted help counting; stop for pain.

### Assumptions and limits

This uses ordinary hand-knit knit and purl moves. Confirm yarn-back knit and yarn-front purl against your handedness and chosen tutorial. It is a personal learning piece, not a copied paid pattern or a wearable size claim.
