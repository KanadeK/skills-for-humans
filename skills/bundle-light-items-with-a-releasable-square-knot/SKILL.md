## 中文

当几件普通轻量小物在桌面或架上需要暂时放在一起，而它们不靠绳子承重或保障安全时加载。你要用**能轻松解开的方结/平结维持一个不压坏物品的轻束，或改用盒子**。绝不用于搬运载荷、车辆捆扎、吊挂、攀登、牵引、固定人或动物、约束运行中的电线/设备，或任何脱落会伤人的用途。

### 准备与输入

先问：这束放在稳定表面上时，即使绳子松开，物品也不会掉下砸人或造成危险吗？若否，改用合适容器或专业固定方法，不使用本 Skill。检查物品有无易碎、易变形或怕压边缘，绳/带是否柔软、完好、足够长且两端材质/粗细相近；看不到或握不住时请愿意的人协助。不要用带尖金属丝缠绕可受损表面。

### 执行

1. **先把物件摆稳。** 在桌面或架面把同类轻物件排成不易滚开的组，必要时垫一层不会伤物的普通材料。不要将承重、危险品、带电/运行设备或会被压坏的物件塞入束中。
2. **松松绕一圈。** 用柔软绳带从组的外侧环绕，两端在容易取到的位置相遇；留出不压弯、不勒出印子的余量。若轻束本身仍会散开或绳会切入表面，直接换盒/套，不靠越勒越紧解决。
3. **打两个相反的半结。** 左右手各握一端；先右端跨过左端并从下穿回，再让此时在左手的那端跨过右手端并从下穿回。轻拉四段使结平整。这是方结，不是两次同方向交叉形成的歪结；必要时对照来源图练习。
4. **只做轻量检查。** 把物件放回稳定表面，看绳结是否平整、两端能找到、没有勒伤或使物件翘起。轻碰束边检查是否会立即散开即可；不提起、拖拽或加重量“测试强度”。若松滑，重系或改容器，不能升级为安全固定。
5. **给未来解开留出口。** 留可握的短绳尾并让使用者知道从哪儿解，避免长环垂到通道。取用时在平面托住物件，轻推结体并逐端解开；不要拿刀在物品旁切绳。

### 完成与停止

轻物件仍由桌/架承托，绳结仅帮助整理，松开时不会造成危险；结平、物品不被压坏、能徒手解开。绳结不稳、束后会滚落、物件受压、任务需要真正承重或安全保障时停止捆扎，换适合的容器/固定方案。打成结不等于它有承载等级。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两次交叉同方向，结歪且滑：** 解开后按“第一右跨左、第二反向”重做，对照图看结是否平整。
- **越勒越紧才不散：** 松开并换盒或不同分组；绳印说明方法不适合该物件。
- **绳尾短到难解：** 在平面上重系并留可抓尾端，不用剪刀贴物品强拆。
- **把轻束当提手：** 放回平面，用合适容器拿取，绳结不承担搬运。
- **绳材毛糙或沾污：** 换软且完好的材料，不让摩擦/污渍直接伤物。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只用于稳定桌/架上的普通轻物件，按Scouting America的明确提醒，方结不用于重载。不同材质、形状和环境会影响是否适合扎束；不是每件东西都应绑起来。握持、触觉或视力不便时可由可信者用不同颜色/触感的两端帮助回读结形，或直接使用盒/袋。副作用是一束可以解开的东西，不是一项新的承重工程。

## English

Load this Skill when a few ordinary light items need temporary organisation on a desk or shelf, with no weight or safety duty placed on the cord. You will make **a gentle bundle held by a square or reef knot that you can undo, or switch to a container**. Never use this for carrying a load, vehicle cargo, hanging, climbing, towing, restraining a person or animal, bundling live or running equipment, or any use where slipping could injure someone.

### Preparation and inputs

Ask whether the items would still be safe on their stable surface if the cord loosened. If they might fall, hurt someone or cause another hazard, choose an appropriate container or specialist securing method instead. Check for fragile, deformable or pressure-sensitive edges and use soft, sound cord or tape long enough for two ends of similar size. Ask willing help if seeing or gripping is hard. Avoid sharp wire against a finish that could be damaged.

### Execution

1. **Set the items down securely.** Arrange similar light objects on a desk or shelf so they are not ready to roll apart. Add an ordinary soft barrier if needed. Do not include load-bearing, dangerous, powered or running equipment, or objects the loop would crush.
2. **Wrap with slack.** Loop soft cord outside the group, bringing both ends together where you can reach them. Leave enough room to avoid bending or marking objects. If the bundle still falls apart or the cord cuts into a surface, use a box or sleeve instead of tightening harder.
3. **Tie two reversed half knots.** Hold one end in each hand. Pass the right end over and under the left; then pass the end now in your left hand over and under the right. Gently pull the four segments until the knot lies flat. This is a square knot, not two same-direction crossings that make a crooked knot. Practise against the source illustration if needed.
4. **Check only a light bundle.** Set items back on their supporting surface. Look for a flat knot, findable ends, no pressure marks and no lifted item. A light touch at the side can show whether it immediately comes apart. Do not lift, drag or add weight to “test strength.” Retie or use a container if it slips; it does not graduate to safety restraint.
5. **Leave a release path.** Keep short grippable tails and make the release point obvious to the person who will open it. Avoid a long loop hanging into a passage. To open, support items on a surface, push the knot body gently together and undo each end. Do not cut beside the goods.

### Success and stop

The desk or shelf still carries the weight; the cord only organises. If it loosens, nothing hazardous happens. The knot is flat, items are uncrushed and a person can undo it by hand. Stop and use a better container or securing method if the knot slips, items could roll off, the bundle crushes them or actual load or safety support is needed. A knot has no load rating merely because it is tied.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Both crossings went the same way, leaving a crooked slippery knot:** Untie and reverse the second crossing; compare the resulting flat shape with the illustration.
- **It only stays together when tightly cinched:** Loosen it and use a box or smaller group. Pressure marks show this method does not suit the item.
- **The tails are too short to release:** Retie on the surface with grippable ends instead of cutting close to goods.
- **The bundle is used as a carrying handle:** Set it down and use a proper container. The knot is not a transport support.
- **Cord is rough or dirty:** Replace it with clean, sound, soft material before friction or dirt damages the items.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary light objects resting on a stable desk or shelf. Scouting America explicitly says this knot is not for heavy loads. Material, shape and setting change whether bundling helps; not every item should be tied. If sight, touch or grip is limited, a trusted helper can distinguish the ends by colour or texture and read the knot back, or you can use a box or bag. The side effect is a bundle you can open, not a new load-bearing project.
