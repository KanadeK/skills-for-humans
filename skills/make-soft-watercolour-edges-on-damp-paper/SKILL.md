## 中文

当你想用**湿纸与湿色相遇**做一小块有柔软边缘的原创色形时，加载这份 Skill。结果是晾干后颜色在限定区内扩散，边界比干纸笔触更软。与 E006 平涂/渐变对照的连续色带不同，本次控制的是一处色形的扩散边缘；H271 的铅笔轮廓不会替你完成这一步。

### 准备与输入

准备标签允许的普通水彩、适合湿用的纸、清水、刷子、吸水纸和不怕水的台面。用铅笔轻标一小块练习范围，旁留干纸区作比较。杯子安稳放好，不在餐食或电子设备旁涂；难以控制纸张时可请人按住自己的垫板边。

### 执行

1. **把目标区湿到有光而非积水。** 清水轻刷小块纸，观察均匀湿润；如果有流动水珠，先用近干笔或吸水纸边轻取，不把纸泡透到变软。
2. **点入少量颜料。** 用笔蘸少量调好的颜色，在湿区内落下一两笔，看它向外散开；不要对同一点反复摩擦。若颜色马上冲出目标区，停加水，让纸吸一会儿或下次缩小湿区。
3. **保留一个干纸对照。** 在相邻干纸上用同色轻画一笔，观察边缘差别。想加第二色时先少量试在湿区一角，不把所有颜色搅成泥。
4. **平放晾干再判定。** 自然干燥后看湿区是否留下软边、干区是否较清晰，写下纸湿度与笔水量的真实观察；若水痕重也记录，不去刮湿纸。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

同纸有一处干后的湿纸软边色形及干纸对照，你能指出扩散发生在哪里。纸尚湿、颜色仍在流时不能宣称最终形状已定。

- **颜色冲出范围：** 停止加颜料，用吸水纸边轻吸多余水；下一次先缩小湿区。
- **完全不扩散：** 纸可能已干，下次在仍微湿时点色，不在半干纸面用力搓。
- **两色搅浑：** 少碰几次、等干后再决定是否叠层；不以更多水清洗画面。
- **纸起毛或破：** 立刻停刷，让它干，换能承受湿笔的纸。
- **视觉或手部控制受限：** 大一号练习框、对比色和稳定垫板能降低难度；由你确认软/硬边区别。

### 假设与边界

湿纸效果依赖纸、颜料和干燥环境，无法保证某个特定云朵或名家效果。只做少量水基材料的纸面练习，不用喷雾、盐、酒精或热风制造额外效果。

## English

Load this Skill when you want a small original colour shape with a **soft edge made where wet paint meets damp paper**. Finish after drying with controlled spread in a chosen area and an edge softer than a dry-paper stroke. Unlike E006's flat-and-graded continuous wash comparison, this tests diffusion around one shape. H271's pencil outline cannot do this pigment-and-water work for you.

### Preparation and inputs

Set out ordinary labelled watercolour, paper suitable for damp work, water, brush, absorbent paper, and a wipeable surface. Lightly mark a small practice area and leave a dry-paper comparison space. Keep the cup stable and away from food or electronics. Trusted help may hold your own backing board if control is difficult.

### Execution

1. **Dampen, without flooding.** Brush clean water over the small chosen area until evenly damp with a sheen. If droplets move across it, lift excess gently with an almost dry brush or paper edge; do not soak the sheet until weak.
2. **Place a little pigment.** Add one or two strokes of mixed colour inside the damp area and watch it spread. Do not scrub the same point. If it races beyond your area, stop adding water and wait briefly, or make a smaller damp area next time.
3. **Keep a dry comparison stroke.** Make a light stroke of the same colour on neighbouring dry paper and compare edges. If adding a second colour, try a little in one damp corner rather than stirring every colour together.
4. **Dry flat before judging.** After natural drying, check whether the damp area has a soft edge and the dry stroke a firmer one. Note actual paper wetness and brush load; record tide marks instead of scraping wet paper.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One dry sheet shows a damp-paper soft-edged shape beside a dry-paper stroke, and you can point to where diffusion occurred. While paint is still moving, the final shape is not known.

- **Colour escapes the area:** Stop adding paint and lightly touch excess water with an absorbent edge. Make the damp patch smaller next time.
- **No spread occurs:** The paper may have dried; add pigment while it remains damp next time, without scrubbing partly dry paper.
- **Two colours become muddy:** Touch fewer times and wait until dry before deciding on a later layer; do not wash the image with more water.
- **Paper pills or tears:** Stop brushing, dry it, and switch to paper that tolerates a damp brush.
- **Sight or hand control is limited:** A larger test area, contrasting colour, and stable board can help; you confirm soft versus firm edges.

### Assumptions and limits

Wet-paper effects vary with paper, pigment, and drying conditions; no named cloud or famous look is guaranteed. This is a small water-based paper exercise without spray, salt, alcohol, or hot air effects.
