## 中文

当你已有电磁炉，准备买一口普通锅，却只看到“适用电磁炉”和锅口直径时，加载这份 Skill。目标是购前确认锅的**有效磁性底面**与实际要用的炉区相配，底面平整，且不会覆盖控制区。这里不接通、测试或维修炉具，也不判断燃气灶使用。

### 准备与输入

记下电磁炉的准确型号、打算使用的炉区，以及该型号说明书写的可识别锅底范围或特殊限制。找候选锅的锅底材料、真正接触炉面的平面直径、磁性区域直径与底部形状；包装只写“锅口 28 厘米”时，锅底尺寸仍未知。准备尺子；若商家允许，可以用小磁铁辅助判断锅底是否有磁性。

### 执行

1. **读炉区条件。** 从准确型号说明书找计划使用炉区的最小可识别底径、适用材料和任何控制区/边缘限制。不同炉区可能不同，不能拿其他炉具的通用数字套用。
2. **看真正加热的底面。** 读候选锅底材料与平整度，确认磁性区域覆盖多大。复合锅底可能只有中心一小块有磁性；锅口直径、装饰圈和外沿不能替代有效底径。
3. **交叉核对。** 将有效磁性底径与目标炉区说明书比较，同时确认锅底能平稳接触炉面、不挡控制区。磁铁能吸住是有用线索，但单独不能证明磁性面积够大或实际加热均匀。
4. **决定用途是否成立。** 写下“炉区型号/条件 → 锅底材料和有效直径 → 平整/控制区条件 → 通过、不通过或未确认”。若你要用它处理特定份量，还应确认锅自身的可用深度与份量；不要用炉区适配替代容量判断。
5. **缺信息就停。** 没有有效底径、底部照片或准确炉区要求时，向卖家或制造商索取；仍不明确就换有清楚规格的候选，不在商店通电试热来填空。

### 成功与停止

成功是有来源的静态适配判断，足以决定是否继续考虑这口锅；不是实际烹饪或性能保证。若炉具损坏、说明书要求专用附件，或判断需要拆机、改线、热锅试验，就停止本 Skill，交给制造商支持或专业人员。别因“磁铁吸住”忽略型号限制。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把 28 厘米锅口当锅底：** 查锅底平面及磁性圈的实际直径；无资料就标未确认。
- **磁铁只吸住中间一点：** 不能宣告通过；按磁性区域的有效尺寸重查，或换底部资料完整的锅。
- **合适锅底却挡住控制键：** 用炉面图和锅的外形重查位置；换小一号或另一个允许的炉区，不让锅压到控制区。
- **细字或底部不便查看：** 放大产品图、请店员展示底面或读出规格；无需举起重锅或接触热炉面。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程假设你的设备确为电磁炉，且可获得准确型号说明书；其他加热方式不能照搬。厘米与英寸先统一单位。没有磁铁时，可以使用制造商明确写出的材料与适配表，但缺少有效底径仍不能确认。现实副作用是你可能放下一口“全炉通用”的锅，因为通用二字没有标出底面积。

## English

Load this Skill when you already have an induction hob and are considering an everyday pan whose listing mainly says “induction compatible” and gives a rim diameter. Your pre-purchase result is a check of the **effective magnetic base** against the zone you intend to use, plus flatness and control clearance. This does not power on, test, or repair a hob, or assess gas cooking.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the exact hob model, intended cooking zone, and that model's stated detectable base range or special restrictions. Find the candidate's base material, flat contact diameter, magnetic-area diameter, and base shape. A “28 cm pan” may describe its rim rather than the base. Have a ruler; if the seller permits, a small magnet may help identify a magnetic base.

### Execution

1. **Read the zone requirements.** Use the exact model manual for the zone's minimum detectable base size, suitable material, and any edge or control restrictions. Zones can differ; a number from another hob is not your specification.
2. **Inspect the heating surface.** Find out what the candidate's flat base is made of and how wide its magnetic area actually is. A composite base may have only a small magnetic centre. Rim diameter and decorative rings do not enlarge that area.
3. **Compare both sides.** Match the effective magnetic diameter to the chosen zone's requirements, and confirm that the base can sit flat without covering controls. A magnet sticking is useful evidence of magnetism, but alone does not prove that the area is large enough or that heating will be even.
4. **Record a use decision.** Write “hob model/zone requirement → base material and effective diameter → flatness/control condition → pass, fail, or unconfirmed.” If a particular amount of food must fit, check the pan's own usable depth and quantity as a separate requirement; zone fit does not establish capacity.
5. **Stop on missing specifications.** Ask the seller or maker for the base diameter, underside image, or zone requirement. If it stays unclear, choose a candidate with usable data. Do not energise a shop hob to fill an information gap.

### Success and stop conditions

Success is a sourced static compatibility decision that lets you continue or reject the purchase, not a guarantee of cooking performance. Stop this Skill if the hob is damaged, its manual requires a special accessory, or the decision would require dismantling, rewiring, or a hot-pan test. Use manufacturer support or an appropriate professional. Magnetism does not override model-specific limits.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The 28 cm rim was used as base size:** Find the actual flat and magnetic base diameters; otherwise mark them unconfirmed.
- **A magnet sticks only in the centre:** Do not mark the pan compatible; recheck the effective magnetic area or choose a better-documented base.
- **The base fits but the pan covers controls:** Compare the hob layout with the whole pan and choose another allowed zone or pan size.
- **The print or underside is inaccessible:** Enlarge a product image or ask staff to show and describe the base. No heavy lifting or contact with a hot surface is required.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes your appliance is an induction hob and you can get its exact manual; other heating methods require different rules. Convert centimetres and inches before comparing. Without a magnet, use the maker's explicit material and compatibility information, but an unknown effective base size remains unknown. You may leave a “universal” pan behind because that word omits the area doing the work.
