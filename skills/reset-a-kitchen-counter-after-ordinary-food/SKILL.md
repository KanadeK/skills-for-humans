## 中文

当你做完一顿普通餐食，台面上留下**面包屑、蔬果碎屑、酱汁或一般食物油印**，且没有生肉、禽、海鲜汁液或危险污染接触时，用这份 Skill 把可接近的工作面恢复到可继续使用的状态。这里不清洁灶台、电器内部、排水管或整间房；若发现生动物性食品汁液碰过台面，转到专门的生食接触复位流程。

### 准备与输入

确认台面的真实材质与表面维护说明，尤其是石材、木、涂层、接缝或密封面。找适用于该台面及食物接触面的清洁产品标签、清水、干净布/海绵、收集残渣的容器。移开已完成且仍要吃的食物、可移动电器和不耐水物品；水、电源与热表面相邻时先停止并按设备安全说明处理，不能边清洁边让湿布碰插座。

### 执行

1. **先确认污染只在普通范围。** 看碎屑和液体到达了哪些台面区域、边缘与接缝；有生肉汁、污水、体液、霉害、未知化学品或电器内漏时退出本篇，按对应安全/专业流程处理。不让一块干净布先走过不明污染再擦即食区。
2. **把食物残渣先移走。** 用适用纸/软工具把固体碎屑和粘着物收进符合当地规则的垃圾或厨余去向，不刮伤台面，也不把大块推到水槽管道。可食剩菜是否保留走食品保存判断，不能为了台面整齐把它留在室温。
3. **选一个相容的清洁路径。** 按台面厂家与清洁产品说明，用适用肥皂/洗剂和水擦洗污区及可见扩散边界；易刮/易渗表面不能套用硬刷、强酸或不明粉剂。只用产品标签规定的用量/接触方式，不把两瓶清洁剂混在一起。
4. **处理产品残留与水。** 按产品和台面说明用干净水/布擦去需要去除的洗剂，换干净面继续，不把脏水推回食物准备区。台面、接缝和靠边处干燥到可按其用途使用；湿布按其清洁与干燥方式处理，别团在台面上。
5. **按可观察结果收尾。** 台面无可见食物碎屑、油印或洗剂残留，触手可及的工作区干净且干燥，备餐用品回到不污染食物的位置。若表面受损、污物进入无法接近的结构或洗剂不适合该台面，停止并查厂商/专业指导。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

普通可见食物残渣已去除，台面按其材质和产品说明洗净、必要时擦净洗剂并干燥，下一次可在合适区域准备食物；停止于超出范围的污染或材料损伤也是完整决定。这里不宣称做过灭菌或专业消毒验收。

- **水把碎屑推到接缝里：** 暂停湿擦，先用适用软工具取出可见残渣，再按材料说明处理。
- **擦完仍滑腻或有明显泡沫：** 用干净布/水按产品说明去除残留，必要时换水，不叠加另一清洁剂。
- **清洁剂让台面变色：** 立刻停止使用，保存产品名和台面资料，向厂家/专业人员询问。
- **发现其实有生肉汁液：** 隔离即食食品与用过的布，改用专门生食接触流程，不把普通擦拭结果当合格。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇仅处理厨房台面上可识别的普通餐后残渣，不处理霉害、化学品、体液、高处或电气/燃气维修。材质和封边各有护理差异，避免统一“喷一遍即可”。视力或手部操作受限时用明亮光线、可及高度与可信协助；不能安全控制水靠近电器时先移开设备或停止。水和用过的布需要后续处理。

## English

Use this Skill after an ordinary meal leaves **crumbs, fruit or vegetable pieces, sauce, or ordinary food grease** on a kitchen counter, with no contact from raw meat, poultry, seafood juice, or hazardous contamination. Restore the accessible worktop for later use. This does not clean a stovetop, appliance internals, plumbing, or the whole room. Switch to the raw-food-contact route if raw animal juices touched the counter.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the actual counter material and its care directions, especially stone, wood, coating, joints, and seals. Read a cleaning product label suitable for the surface and food-preparation area. Have water, a clean cloth or sponge, and a scrap container. Move finished food you will still eat, movable appliances, and water-sensitive items. Stop where water would reach electricity or a hot surface until appliance safety directions make the area safe; do not wipe a socket with a wet cloth.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the ordinary contamination boundary.** See how far crumbs or liquid reached across the counter, edge, and seam. Leave this Skill for raw meat juice, sewage, bodily fluid, mold, unknown chemical, or an appliance leak; use its safety or professional route. Do not drag one cloth through unknown matter into a ready-to-eat area.
2. **Remove food pieces first.** Use suitable soft paper or a tool to collect solids and stuck food into a waste or food-scrap destination under local rules. Do not scratch the counter or push large pieces into a drain. Whether edible leftovers stay is a food-storage decision; a clear counter is not permission to leave them at room temperature.
3. **Choose one compatible cleaning route.** Under counter-maker and product directions, use appropriate soap or detergent and water on soil and its visible spread. A scratch- or soak-sensitive surface does not inherit hard brushes, strong acid, or mystery powder. Follow this one product's amount and contact instructions and do not mix cleaners.
4. **Remove product and water as directed.** Use clean water or cloth to remove detergent where required by product and counter instructions. Turn to a clean section instead of spreading dirty water back into the prep area. Dry the surface, seams, and edges to a state suitable for use. Clean and dry the used cloth by its own care route rather than leaving it balled up on the worktop.
5. **Close out by observation.** No visible crumbs, food grease, or product remain; the reachable work area is clean and dry and supplies are placed without contaminating food. Stop and ask the maker or a professional if the surface is damaged, soil enters inaccessible construction, or the product is incompatible.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Ordinary visible food is gone, the counter was washed under its material and product directions, detergent was removed where needed, and the surface is dry for the next preparation. A stop for out-of-scope contamination or damage is also complete. This is not a sterilisation or professional sanitising claim.

- **Water pushes crumbs into a joint:** Pause wet wiping. Lift accessible residue with a permitted soft tool, then use the material's instructions.
- **The counter still feels slippery or foamy:** Remove product using clean cloth or water as directed, changing water if needed. Do not layer another cleaner.
- **The product discolours the surface:** Stop it immediately and keep product and counter details for maker or professional advice.
- **It was actually touched by raw meat juice:** Separate ready-to-eat food and the used cloth and switch to the raw-contact route. Ordinary wiping is not its endpoint.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers accessible ordinary food residue on a kitchen counter, not mold, chemicals, bodily fluids, heights, or gas/electrical repair. Materials and edge seals differ, so one spray is not universal. Use bright light, reachable surfaces, or trusted help for vision or grip limits. Move appliances safely or stop if water cannot be controlled near electricity. Water and used cloth still need handling.
