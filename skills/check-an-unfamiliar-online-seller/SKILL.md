## 中文

当你已看中一件普通日用品，但第一次遇到这个网店或平台内卖家时，加载这份 Skill。你要决定是否**从这位卖家下单**，不是替商品做规格检验，也不是判定对方一定合法或违法。只处理常规消费；高价投资、医疗用品、账户凭据处理和买后争议不在这里。

### 准备与输入

打开你实际会付款的商品页，记下平台名称、商品名称、标出的销售方与发货方、你需要它的时间和能接受的总费用。准备一个能独立搜索卖家名称的渠道；不要因为页面有漂亮徽章就把这个步骤省掉。

### 执行

1. **找出交易对象。** 分清平台、页面上的店铺、实际销售方与发货方。名称不一致时，记录分别是谁；找不到销售方，先不付款。
2. **核对能否找到对方。** 查看当前卖家的联系方式、页面或平台提供的联系入口、所在地信息和经营记录。独立搜索准确名称及常见问题反馈，特别留意是否有同名不同站点。单条好评或差评都不足以给卖家定性。
3. **读这笔订单的实际条件。** 看商品是谁交付、预计何时可得、运费与其他费用、退回条件、以及出问题时应先联系卖家还是平台。只记录页面对这笔商品写明的规则，不从别人的订单推断你的权益；不解释法定权利。
4. **看付款会去哪里。** 结算入口应与这笔交易的卖家及平台安排一致。若对方在平台订单外私信催你转账、索要验证码或改去陌生链接，停止，不提交付款或凭据。锁形图标或 HTTPS 只能说明连接方式，不能单独证明卖家可靠。
5. **做三态决定。** **继续：** 卖家身份、取得方式、总费用和处理问题的入口都可核实，且符合你的边界。**换卖家：** 商品仍想买，但这位卖家无法满足条件。**暂停：** 关键信息缺失或相互矛盾；先询问并保留书面答复，或离开。即使选择继续，也不是“零风险”证明。

### 成功条件

你能指明实际销售方、发货方、可联系的渠道、交付与退回条件和最终选择。还没付款就决定离开，也是完成；订单按钮不是倒计时考试。

### 常见报错、停止与补救

- **平台很熟，卖家却陌生：** 回到这条商品页核对销售方，不把平台名自动当成卖家名。
- **只找到店铺自己的五星评论：** 到独立来源核对近期具体经历；仍无足够资料就暂停，不用星数代替身份。
- **页面说法互相冲突：** 记录冲突处，要求可保存的澄清；不确定交付或总费用时不结算。
- **链接或付款路径突然改变：** 停止，从你自己保存或手动找到的正规平台入口重新核对；不要在聊天里发送验证码、密码或付款资料。
- **已经付款才发现问题：** 保存订单与沟通记录，转用平台和商家的实际售后入口；这份买前 Skill 在此结束。

### 假设、可及替代与现实副作用

卖家披露、退回规则和平台责任会随地区与交易类型改变，必须以当前页面为准。这份 Skill 不给退款保证或法律判断。看不清页面时放大或使用屏幕阅读；听力或电话沟通不方便时优先找可留存的文字渠道；页面不可访问或缺少文字条件时可以换卖家。不要为完成核验而注册不需要的账户。

副作用是可能多花十分钟，或把一次不确定的购买留到下次。

## English

Load this Skill when you have chosen an ordinary household item but have not bought from this online shop or marketplace seller before. Decide whether to **order from this seller**. You are not verifying every product specification or proving that a business is lawful or fraudulent. Investment, medical products, account-security work, and disputes after payment are outside this everyday task.

### Preparation and inputs

Open the actual listing you would pay for. Record the marketplace, item, named seller and fulfiller, when you need the item, and the total cost you can accept. Have an independent way to search the seller's exact name. A polished badge on the listing does not complete the check.

### Execution

1. **Identify who is in the transaction.** Separate the platform, storefront, actual seller, and fulfiller. Write down different names rather than treating them as one company. If you cannot identify the seller, do not pay yet.
2. **Check whether the seller can be reached.** Look for current contact details, a platform contact route, location information, and selling history. Search the exact name independently for concrete recent experiences, noting similarly named but different sites. One positive or negative comment does not settle the matter.
3. **Read the terms for this item.** Find who will deliver it, the stated arrival window, shipping and other charges, return conditions, and whether a problem goes first to the seller or platform. Record the rules shown for this transaction; another buyer's order does not define yours. Do not infer legal rights from this checklist.
4. **Check the path to payment.** Checkout should match this seller and the platform's published transaction flow. If someone messages you to pay outside the order, requests a one-time code, or moves you to an unfamiliar link, stop without submitting payment or credentials. A padlock or HTTPS describes the connection, not the seller's trustworthiness.
5. **Choose one of three outcomes.** **Proceed:** seller identity, delivery, total cost, and a route for problems are clear and meet your limits. **Choose another seller:** you still want the item, but this seller does not meet them. **Pause:** critical information is missing or contradictory; request a written answer you can keep, or leave. Proceeding is never proof of zero risk.

### Success

You can name the actual seller, fulfiller, contact route, delivery and return conditions, and your decision. Leaving before payment is a complete outcome. The order button is not a timed exam.

### Common errors, stop points, and recovery

- **The platform is familiar but the seller is not:** Return to this listing and identify the actual seller rather than borrowing the platform's reputation.
- **Only the shop's own five-star comments are available:** Look independently for recent, concrete experiences. If evidence stays thin, pause instead of using stars as identity documents.
- **The listing contradicts itself:** Record the conflict and seek a written clarification. Do not check out while delivery or total cost is unclear.
- **The link or payment route suddenly changes:** Stop and recheck from a platform entry you found yourself. Do not send codes, passwords, or payment details in chat.
- **You discover a problem after paying:** Keep the order and messages, then use the seller's and platform's actual support routes. This pre-purchase Skill ends here.

### Assumptions, accessible alternatives, and side effects

Seller disclosure, return rules, and platform responsibility vary by region and transaction type; check the current listing. This Skill gives no refund guarantee or legal interpretation. Magnify or use a screen reader if the page is hard to read; choose a written contact route if phone calls are inaccessible. If the terms are not readable or the site is inaccessible, you may choose another seller. You do not need to create an unnecessary account to complete the check.

The side effect may be ten more minutes or a purchase deferred.
