## 中文

当你的一双**已清洁且全干的普通光面皮鞋**需要按厂家说明做薄层皮面保养时，加载这份 Skill。结果是小试兼容、薄涂均匀、按产品等待并轻拭后的皮面；它与去污清洗鞋面不同，也不等于修复开裂或给鞋做防水认证。麂皮、反绒、漆皮、混合涂层和材质不明鞋不走这条光面皮路线。

### 准备与输入

再核鞋面材质、鞋厂与保养剂标签是否相容，保护台面，备少量产品和两块干净软布。鞋若湿、明显掉色、开胶或有深裂先暂停并处理原问题。取下鞋带避免沾剂，不把护皮喷雾、家用油、强溶剂或与本鞋不兼容的抛光剂当默认替代。

### 执行

1. **先隐蔽小试。** 在鞋跟内侧或不明显处用布点少量保养剂，按产品要求等候，看是否变色、发粘、起白或伤涂层。出现这些迹象就停，不把不适用产品涂满鞋。
2. **少量薄涂。** 小试无异常且标签允许时，用另一块软布在光面皮区域均匀薄擦，避开鞋底、鞋带孔内和不同材质装饰。不要把皮面涂到积油、把鞋底弄滑。
3. **按本品说明等待和轻拭。** 在通风安全处放足标签要求的时间，再用干净软布轻拭/抛光，去掉多余残留。无需追求品牌广告光泽；用量和间隔不凭感觉翻倍。
4. **查干爽与可穿性。** 皮面不粘手、颜色无异常、外底无保养剂残留后再穿回鞋带；若油感、掉色或裂纹加重，停穿并查厂家/合适维修者。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

小试通过、光面皮薄涂且按说明处理，表面干爽，外底干净；材质不符时明确不涂也是真实正确收口。未等产品吸收、把厚剂留在鞋底，不算保养完成。

- **小试明显变色或发粘：** 停用产品，按标签处理测试处并找兼容方案。
- **保养剂涂到鞋底：** 按外底材料说明清掉残留并干燥，未清前不穿。
- **皮面仍油腻：** 用干净软布轻拭，多等；不叠加第二层。
- **发现是麂皮/漆皮/涂层皮：** 停止，转该材质的专门说明。
- **手眼不便：** 用小剂量、稳定垫板及可信协助检查边界，由你确认不沾底和无异常变色。

### 假设与边界

不同光面皮及表面涂层也会对产品有不同反应；Clarks 的步骤说明了原则，真实鞋款和保养剂标签优先。此法不恢复开裂鞋面的结构，也不承诺防水、防滑或耐用年限。

## English

Load this Skill when **your ordinary smooth-leather shoes are already clean and fully dry** and their maker allows a thin conditioning treatment. Finish with a compatible patch test, even light coat, product wait, and gentle buff. This differs from removing dirt and does not repair cracked structure or certify waterproofing. Do not use this smooth-leather route on suede, nubuck, patent, mixed coated, or unknown uppers.

### Preparation and inputs

Recheck material and both shoe-maker and conditioner labels for compatibility. Protect the surface and have a small product amount plus two clean soft cloths. Pause for wet shoes, colour loss, loose bonding, or deep cracks. Remove laces to keep product off them. Do not substitute aerosol protector, household oil, strong solvent, or an incompatible polish by default.

### Execution

1. **Patch-test out of sight.** Dab a little conditioner on an inconspicuous heel or inner spot and wait under product directions. Check for colour change, tack, white residue, or coating damage. Stop rather than treating the full upper if it reacts.
2. **Apply a light even coat.** When the test is sound and labels permit, use another soft cloth to spread a thin amount over smooth leather only, avoiding outsole, lace holes, and different trim. Do not make the leather oily or the sole slippery.
3. **Wait and buff by product directions.** Leave it in a safe airy place for the specified time, then lightly buff or wipe away excess with a clean soft cloth. An advertised high gloss is not a necessary result; do not double product amount or interval by guess.
4. **Check dry feel and wear readiness.** Refit laces only once leather is non-tacky, colour looks sound, and no conditioner remains on the outsole. Stop wear and ask the maker or repairer if oiliness, colour loss, or cracking worsens.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The patch test passed, the smooth upper received a thin label-following treatment and feels dry, and the outsole is clean. Correctly declining treatment when material is unsuitable is also a real finish. A thick coat still on the sole before product waiting is unfinished.

- **Patch darkens or becomes sticky:** Stop the product, handle the test spot under its label, and seek a compatible route.
- **Conditioner reaches outsole:** Remove residue under outsole directions and dry it before wear.
- **Leather remains greasy:** Buff lightly with clean soft cloth and wait longer, without a second coat.
- **Upper is suede, patent, or coated:** Stop and use that material's own guidance.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Small measured amounts, stable board, and trusted boundary inspection can help while you verify no sole contamination or colour change.

### Assumptions and limits

Smooth leathers and coatings still differ. Clarks provides principles, while actual shoe and product labels control use. This cannot rebuild cracked leather or promise waterproofing, traction, or years of service.
