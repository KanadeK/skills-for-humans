## 中文

当你觉得一批普通衣物已经自然晾好、准备收下折叠或穿用时，用这份 Skill 确认**不只表面干，厚处也干**。这里检查的是正常晾干的结束点；若湿衣曾被忘在封闭桶里或有异常滞留史，先走湿衣滞留判断，不用本篇直接宣布干净可用。衣物干燥标签仍决定允许的晾法。

### 准备与输入

回看衣物本次晾的时间和位置、标签规定的平铺/悬挂/阴干方式，检查室内是否有明显冷凝、架下滴水或湿毛巾。准备干净可及的台面，逐件查看；不要把整批一次塞进柜子以后再靠气味找漏网湿衣。若衣物有不明污染、明显霉斑或气味变化，停止普通收纳，改走适用的湿衣/专业处理流程。

### 执行

1. **一件一件从支撑处取下检查。** 先摸或看外层，再翻到允许查看的里侧、厚接缝、袖口、腰头、口袋边与折叠处。室温下“摸着凉”不必然等于湿，“外面不滴水”也不证明厚处干；结合触感、表面状态及可见湿痕判断，无法判断就让可信的人协助。
2. **按实际湿处决定是否继续。** 厚处仍潮时，保持衣物标签允许的平铺或悬挂方式，松开重叠部分、改变接触面或换掉已湿的支撑毛巾；保留通风与滴水去向。阴干衣物仍应阴干，不能因为赶时间挂到直射日光或靠在高温热源上。
3. **检查晾处是否妨碍继续。** 衣物之间紧贴、架子下积水、窗面凝结或房间越来越潮时，减少同处衣物量、恢复安全空气流动、清理积水或换地点。若改变地点会违反衣物标签，暂停并找合适支撑，不把潮湿衣物压进柜里。
4. **确认可结束的衣物。** 里外和较厚部位都不再有可辨认的湿感或水痕，且没有新变形/掉色时，按衣物用途折叠、挂起或穿用；这些后续收存方法另按衣物特性安排。湿的部分继续晾并记下下一次检查，不拿整个批次的平均状态决定单件去向。
5. **收尾现场。** 晾衣架、毛巾、托盘或地面如果还湿，擦干或继续晾干，再恢复公共通道。衣物若长时间仍湿且原因不明，停止反复“再等等”，转湿衣滞留或环境潮湿排查；气味正常不能证明无问题。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

每件衣物有可观察的“可收下 / 继续按标签晾 / 因异常停止”结果；确认可收下的衣物包括厚接缝和折处也干，湿支架和地面已处理。没有通用晾晒小时数代替检查。

- **表面干、袖口仍潮：** 单独展开袖口继续按标签晾，稍后复查，不用热风强吹代替证据。
- **平铺毛巾比衣服还湿：** 换干毛巾或允许的通风支撑，保持衣物原形。
- **收下后发现口袋边潮湿：** 取出重新晾，检查相邻柜中是否有水汽；下一轮先查厚处。
- **连续检查都潮且房间凝水：** 减少湿衣负荷、改善安全通风或换晾处；若环境问题无法处理，停止把更多湿衣放进去。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只覆盖正常自然晾干的普通衣物，不诊断霉菌、不处理危险污染或专业护理织物。厚度、空气湿度、布料与支撑方式不同，不能给所有衣物一个“晾 X 小时”。视觉或触觉受限时用固定光源、标出待复查件、可信现场协助；行动不便时把架放在可及高度逐件检查。延后收纳会继续占用位置，但把湿衣塞进柜中不是替代方案。

## English

Use this Skill when ordinary clothes seem air-dried and you are about to take them down to fold or wear. Confirm that **thick parts, not only the surface, are dry**. This checks the endpoint of a normal air-drying process. If wet clothes were forgotten in a closed washer or have an abnormal holding history, use the wet-laundry-delay decision first; this Skill cannot certify them ready. Their drying labels still govern placement.

### Preparation and inputs

Recall when and where this batch was hung and each label's flat, line, or shade requirement. Look for window condensation, drips under the rack, or a soaked support towel. Have a clean reachable surface for one-item checks. Do not put an entire load away and later use smell to locate damp misses. For unknown contamination, visible mold, or unusual odour change, stop ordinary storage and use the relevant wet-laundry or professional route.

### Execution

1. **Check one item off its support at a time.** Inspect or feel the outer fabric, then the accessible inside, thick seams, cuffs, waistbands, pocket edges, and folded areas. Cool fabric at room temperature is not automatically wet, and “no surface drip” does not prove that the thick part is dry. Use feel, surface condition, and visible damp marks together, or trusted help if you cannot judge them.
2. **Respond to actual damp areas.** If a thick part is still damp, keep the garment on label-permitted flat or hanging support. Open overlaps, change the contact surface, or replace a wet supporting towel; maintain airflow and a drip destination. Shade-dry clothing stays in shade, even when you are in a hurry. Do not put it against a high-heat source.
3. **Check whether the site is slowing it down.** If garments touch all along the rack, water pools below, a window condenses, or the room grows damp, reduce the load, restore safe air movement, clean up water, or change place. If a new place conflicts with the garment label, pause and find an allowed support rather than packing it damp into a cupboard.
4. **Release items that actually finish.** When the outside and thick places show no identifiable moisture or water marks and no new shape or colour damage, fold, hang, or wear the item under its own garment needs. Later storage choices belong to the clothing-storage task. Keep damp pieces drying and note when to check again; a batch average does not decide a single garment's destination.
5. **Finish the site too.** Dry or continue airing a wet rack, towel, tray, or floor before reopening a shared path. If an item stays damp across checks without a clear reason, stop simply waiting and assess delayed wet laundry or the room's moisture. Normal odour alone cannot certify it.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Every garment has an observable result: put away, continue label-compatible air drying, or stop for an abnormal condition. Items released are dry at thick seams and folds, and wet supports and flooring were handled. No universal number of drying hours replaces this check.

- **The surface is dry but a cuff remains damp:** Open that cuff and air-dry it under the label, then recheck. Do not substitute intense hot air for evidence.
- **The flat-dry towel is wetter than the garment:** Replace it or use permitted ventilated support while keeping the garment's shape.
- **A pocket edge is damp after putting it away:** Take it out to dry again and check nearby stored items for moisture; start with thick places next time.
- **Repeated checks find dampness and room condensation:** Reduce wet load, improve safe ventilation, or change location. Stop adding damp garments if the room problem cannot be addressed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for normal air drying of ordinary clothing, not mold diagnosis, hazardous contamination, or professional-care fabric. Thickness, humidity, cloth, and support vary, so one “dry for X hours” rule cannot cover all garments. For limited vision or touch, use steady light, mark items due for recheck, or ask trusted help. For limited mobility, lower the rack and check one item at a time. Delayed storage occupies space, but putting wet clothes into a cupboard is not a substitute.
