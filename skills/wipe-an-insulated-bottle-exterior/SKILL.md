## 中文

当你的一只普通保温杯、随行水瓶或带涂层的饮具**外壳**沾了手印、饮料滴痕或轻微灰尘，想在下一次拿取前整理外面时，用这份 Skill 按准确产品的表面许可轻擦、去残留并干燥。目标只限外表面：饮口、壶内、吸管与密封圈是否干净，必须按各自产品说明另行确认；外壳发亮不是“可以直接喝”的证明。

### 准备与输入

确认杯/瓶型号与外层是裸不锈钢、喷涂、印花、贴饰还是有软底座，查看制造商是否允许浸水、机洗、某种洗剂或拆底座。若刚盛热饮，先放到能安全拿取的温度，不在烫手状态翻动或猛摇。准备产品和表面允许的软布/海绵、少量温和手洗剂（若许可）、洁净水及干布，放在稳定清洁的台面。对不知材质或装饰已翘起的杯，先停查制造商，不用刷具试出涂层有多牢。

若饮口、内壁或盖里有异味、残留、可疑霉斑或清洁史不明，把杯标为暂不饮用；本次只做外面不能替代内部清洁。不要自配强酸、漂白剂或把外部涂层用钢丝球磨亮。

### 执行

1. **划定外表面的可擦范围。** 只认杯身、外把手、底部外侧等实际可见且制造商许可的区域；饮口、螺纹、盖内、吸管和密封圈属不同清洁对象。握稳杯，不让水或洗剂顺着未确认的接缝、装饰贴片或底座结构积住。
2. **先在小处试。** 用产品允许的轻湿软布或温和洗剂在不显眼处试擦，观察是否褪色、掉漆、粘手或留下擦痕；有变化就停。不同品牌即使都叫“保温杯”，外涂层和可机洗许可仍不同，不把别家标签贴到这只杯上。
3. **轻擦普通外部污迹。** 用小量软布按允许方向清掉手印和滴痕，避免硬刷和长时间浸泡；如果标签允许整体手洗，也只按其边界处理。顽固残留若需要强力磨、溶剂或拆件才可到达，停下查该杯的专用方法。
4. **去洗剂并干燥。** 按产品说明用干净湿布或可控的清水去除外壳洗剂，再用干布擦干可见表面，让缝边和底部按说明干燥。不要把仍湿的涂层或软底座紧包进封闭袋。回到饮用前，另核对饮口、盖与内部已按制造商路线洗净并干燥；没有这一步就不要宣称整杯清洁完成。

### 成功条件

已知外壳材质的杯身普通手印/滴痕被适用软工具去除，无新脱漆或划痕，清洁品残留被去掉且外部干燥；饮用相关部位的状态被明确留作独立检查。若产品说明缺失或外壳损伤，停止外壳清洁并查制造商同样是完成判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **外面亮了就直接喝：** 停下核对饮口、盖、吸管和内部的清洁状态，外壳任务没有完成这些部位。
- **用钢丝球擦彩色涂层或贴饰：** 立即停，改查该产品软工具许可；掉漆不靠继续磨恢复。
- **别家同类杯可机洗，就把这只也机洗：** 看准确杯底/说明和盖子组合，不能凭外形推断许可。
- **热饮还烫就反转杯身清洁：** 放稳等可安全拿取，不为几枚指纹冒烫伤或洒漏风险。
- **软底/接缝持续潮湿：** 按制造商方式充分干燥并检查是否存水；无法清到/干到的部分别被本次外擦判成完成。
- **外表有裂纹、涂层剥落或异常气味：** 暂停使用/清洁，联系制造商或换杯，外擦不修复结构或卫生风险。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理普通水杯/瓶**外壳日常轻污**，不处理饮口、壶内、可拆密封圈、霉害或消毒。品牌与盖型对浸水、洗碗机、涂层和软底部件的规则不同，以手中产品说明为准。视力或握力不便时用明亮稳定台面、小量软布和可信协助；不能稳住热杯时等冷却。外部清洁的结果很局部，须诚实保持与饮用卫生的界线。

## English

Use this Skill when the **outside** of an ordinary insulated bottle, travel tumbler, or coated drink vessel has fingerprints, beverage drips, or light dust. Wipe, remove product residue, and dry the exterior within the exact model's finish rules. The result is only an outside surface. Drinking rim, interior, straw, and gaskets need their own maker-directed cleaning checks. A shiny shell does not establish that the vessel is ready to drink from.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify bottle model and whether the outside is bare steel, powder coat, print, decal, or a soft base, and check maker permission for immersion, dishwasher use, soap, and disassembly. If it held a hot drink, wait until handling is safe rather than turning or shaking a scalding vessel. Have a maker-compatible soft cloth or sponge, a little mild hand-dish soap if permitted, clean water, a dry cloth, and a stable clean counter. Stop for unknown finish or lifting decoration until the maker's care is known.

If the drinking edge, interior, or cap has odour, residue, suspected mould, or unknown history, mark the item not ready for drinking. Outside wiping cannot replace interior cleaning. Do not improvise acid or bleach mixtures or scour a coating with steel wool.

### Execution

1. **Bound the exterior area.** Work only on visible and maker-approved body, outside handle, and outer base surfaces. Rim, threads, cap interior, straw, and gasket are separate tasks. Steady the vessel. Do not leave cleaner pooled in an unverified seam, decal edge, or base structure.
2. **Trial out of sight.** Use an allowed lightly damp soft cloth or mild soap at an inconspicuous area. Stop for colour loss, lifted finish, sticky feel, or scratching. Two similarly named insulated bottles may have different exterior and machine-wash permissions; another brand's label is not yours.
3. **Wipe ordinary outside marks gently.** Remove fingerprints and drips with a small amount of soft material in the permitted direction. Avoid hard brushing and prolonged soaking. If the actual label permits whole-item hand washing, stay within that permission. Stop and find a product-specific method for residue that needs force, solvent, or disassembly.
4. **Remove cleaner and dry.** Under maker directions, remove exterior soap with clean damp cloth or controlled water, then dry visible areas and allow seams and base to dry as instructed. Do not wrap a damp finish or soft base in a closed bag. Before drinking again, separately confirm rim, cap, and interior were cleaned and dried under their model-specific route; this outside job alone does not finish the entire vessel.

### Success

An identified finish has less ordinary outside soil, no new scratch or peeling, no remaining cleaner film, and a dry outer surface. Drinking-contact parts are explicitly left for their separate checks. When care is unknown or the finish is damaged, stopping for maker guidance is also a complete decision.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A shiny outside becomes permission to drink:** Check rim, cap, straw, and interior separately. This task did not clean them.
- **Steel wool attacks colour or decals:** Stop and check this model's soft-tool permission. More abrasion cannot restore lost finish.
- **Another brand permits dishwashing, so this one goes in too:** Check the exact base marking, instructions, and cap combination, not resemblance.
- **A hot beverage vessel is turned over to remove fingerprints:** Park it until it can be handled safely; spills and burns cost more than marks.
- **A soft base or seam stays wet:** Dry under maker guidance and assess trapped water. Unreachable wet structure is not certified by an outside wipe.
- **Crack, flaking finish, or unusual odour appears:** Stop use and consult the maker or replace it; exterior wiping does not repair structure or hygiene.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers **light outside soil** on an ordinary insulated or travel drink vessel, not rim, interior, gaskets, mould, or sanitizing. Brands and lids have different rules for soaking, dishwashers, coatings, and soft bases; use the product in your hand. Use a bright stable counter, small soft cloth, or trusted help for limited sight or grip. Wait if a hot vessel cannot be held safely. Exterior cleanliness is a narrow outcome and must not be confused with drinking hygiene.
