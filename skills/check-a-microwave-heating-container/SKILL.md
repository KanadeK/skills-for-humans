## 中文

当你准备把普通食物连容器放进微波炉，**容器、外包装、盖或衬膜是否适用尚不确定**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是“这套容器与开口方式按本机和食品说明可用”，或者在加热前换到已确认适用的容器/停止。这里不靠“看起来像玻璃/塑料”猜耐热，也不维修门封、转盘或电气部件；婴幼儿食品与危险化学品不在范围内。

### 准备与输入

读食品包装是否写明需拆外盒、刺孔、移除膜、转盘放置或换盘；读容器、盖、保鲜膜/袋子的制造商标示及确切微波炉手册。确认食物本身、份量和后续加热/搅拌/静置要求，容器放在转盘或指定位置能正常转动，门能完整关合。若门弯曲、关不紧或运行中门开仍继续工作，停止使用并联系合格支持；不做“再试一次”验证。

### 执行步骤

1. **按确切标示判容器。** 只用制造商或食品包装明确允许在该微波模式/食品情境使用的容器；玻璃、陶瓷和塑料也需看具体制品。未标明的外卖盒、普通餐盒、塑料袋或一次性盒不因过去没融化就自动合格。怀疑变形、破损或标签缺失时换已确认适用的器具。
2. **检查金属与包装层。** 普通微波模式通常不放金属盘、金属边或铝箔；只有确切微波炉和食品说明都明确允许的特殊用法才遵守其限定。先拆掉与加热无关的包装、订书钉或不适用封口，不靠“只有一点点金属”作安全实验。
3. **给蒸汽留说明允许的出口。** 食品说明要求刺孔、揭角或使用带排气的微波适用盖时照做；不能把密闭罐、旋紧盖或完全封住的膜当作普通加热容器。保鲜膜只有产品和食品说明都允许时才用，并按要求避免与食物接触。
4. **核对尺寸、转盘和可取出性。** 容器放入后不碰门、顶壁或不该触碰的部件，转盘能按机型设计转动，盖能留空间，结束后有合适隔热工具和耐热落点。若太大、太重或会倾倒，改小份与合适容器，不关门硬挤。
5. **开机前给出明确结论。** 容器/盖/包装都适用、蒸汽出口符合说明、炉门正常关闭且你能安全取出，才进入加热。缺任一关键标示就换容器或停止，不用食品当材料测试样品。

### 完成与停止

你能指出**哪个标示或说明允许本次容器、盖与包装在这台微波炉里按何种方式使用**，并有排气与取出路径，就完成检查。材料不明、门受损、容器变形、转盘被卡或盖子无法排气时先不加热。若运行中有火花、烟或异常声响，关机并退出此流程，不伸手进正在运行的炉腔排查。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看材质名就放进去：** 查确切制品是否标示微波适用；没有就改明确适用的容器。
- **包装写“直接加热”，却没读开口步骤：** 重新读刺孔/揭膜/拆外盒要求，确认整份包装与当前份量匹配。
- **盖子完全密封：** 改用说明允许的排气方式，不能确认就停，不用压力顶开盖子。
- **小金属边或订书钉被忽略：** 按食品和本机说明移除/换盘，不赌金属小就没事。
- **容器标着可微波但取出烫手：** 食物可把容器加热，预备合适隔热工具和稳定落点，不徒手测试。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只处理普通家用微波加热前的容器边界，不把微波适用等同于所有模式、所有温度或所有包装状态都安全。不同型号有不同金属、转盘和容器限制；确切设备、容器与食品说明优先。看不清小字可放大清晰照片、用可访问文字说明或请可信的人读回；没有证据就换明确合格的容器。现实副作用是多洗一只盘，却不让外卖盒承担未经申请的材料测试。

## English

Load this Skill before ordinary food and its dish enter a microwave when **container, outer package, lid, or film suitability is still unclear**. Finish with “this exact container and venting arrangement is supported by the food and oven directions,” or change to a confirmed container or stop before heating. Do not infer heat suitability from looking like glass or plastic. Door-seal, turntable, or electrical repair, infant food, and hazardous chemicals are outside this task.

### Preparation and inputs

Read food-pack instructions for removing outer boxes, puncturing or peeling a corner, removing film, turntable placement, or transferring to another dish. Read the maker's markings for container, lid, film, or bag and the exact microwave manual. Know the food, amount, and later heating, stirring, and standing instructions. Check that the vessel sits where the turntable can operate and the door closes fully. Stop using an oven whose door is bent, will not close securely, or continues running open; use qualified support rather than “one more test.”

### Execution steps

1. **Judge the exact vessel's marking.** Use a container clearly supported by its maker or food packaging for this microwave mode and food context. Glass, ceramic, and plastic still depend on the actual product. An unmarked takeaway tub, ordinary lunch box, bag, or disposable tray is not approved by surviving a previous heating. Replace a warped, damaged, or unverified vessel.
2. **Check metal and packaging layers.** Ordinary microwave mode generally excludes metal pans, metal trim, and foil. Only follow a special exception when both exact oven and food directions permit it with their limits. Remove irrelevant packaging, staples, or unsuitable seals. “Just a small bit of metal” is not a safety test.
3. **Leave the steam outlet directions require.** Puncture, peel a corner, or use a vented microwave-safe cover when the package directs. Do not make a sealed jar, screwed lid, or completely closed film your ordinary heating vessel. Use plastic wrap only when the product and food directions allow it, keeping it off food where required.
4. **Check fit, turntable, and removal.** The vessel must clear door and cavity surfaces, let the turntable operate as designed, leave cover room, and have a heat-safe removal tool and landing place. Divide an oversized, heavy, or tipping portion instead of forcing the door closed.
5. **Make a supported start decision.** Heat only when dish, cover, and package are suitable; the required vent works; the door closes normally; and you can remove the vessel safely. Missing decisive markings mean a confirmed replacement or a stop, not a food-based material experiment.

### Success and stop

You can identify **which marking or direction supports this dish, cover, and package in this exact oven and how it must vent**, with a removal route. Do not heat with unknown materials, a damaged door, warped dish, blocked turntable, or unvented required cover. Sparks, smoke, or abnormal noise during operation means switch off and leave this flow; do not reach inside a running cavity to investigate.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Material name alone is treated as approval:** Check that exact product's microwave marking; use a clearly approved vessel when it is absent.
- **“Heat in pack” is read without its opening step:** Recheck puncture, peel, outer-box, and amount directions for this exact package.
- **The lid seals completely:** Use the approved vent route, or stop if uncertain. Do not let pressure lift it for you.
- **A metal edge or staple is overlooked:** Remove or transfer under food and oven directions instead of betting that small metal is harmless.
- **Microwave-safe is read as cool to touch:** Hot food can heat its dish. Have suitable heat protection and a stable landing place rather than testing by hand.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This only checks container boundaries before domestic microwave heating. Microwave suitability does not mean every mode, temperature, or package state is approved. Models vary in metal, turntable, and vessel rules; exact equipment, container, and food directions take priority. Magnify a clear photo, use accessible text, or ask a trusted person to read small print. With no evidence, transfer to a clearly suitable dish. You may wash one extra plate instead of running an unapproved material test on a takeaway box.
