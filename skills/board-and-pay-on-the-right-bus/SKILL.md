## 中文

当你已在有效站点等候，目标公交车靠近，需要**核对这辆车、依当地规则从合适车门上车并确认车费有效**时加载。线路与下车站的选择已在此前完成；这里处理的是眼前车辆和上车这一刻。下车、换乘及坐过站另行处理。票价、支付方式、上下车门和是否需下车刷卡都以当期运营方规则为准。

### 准备与输入

看一眼你已确认的线路、方向/终点及可能的支线或短线标记，拿出这次实际能用的票、卡或当地接受的支付方式。先从运营方正式渠道核对支付规则：是否上车前买票/激活、从哪个门进、在何处验证、凭证是否要保留。别把另一座城市的刷卡声当作本车的支付说明。

### 执行

1. **读眼前车辆，不只读线路号。** 在安全候车区比较车头/侧面线路、终点、支线或临时服务标识与计划。号码相同而终点、支线或“只到某站”不同，就暂不上车；车辆停稳后可向司机或工作人员简短确认，但不站在车道里拦车。
2. **按现场规则表示要乘车。** 某些运营方需要你在站牌附近示意，另一些有固定排队或车门指引；在安全位置依实际规则操作。等车辆完全停稳，让下车乘客先走。车满、门未开放或司机要求等下一班时退回候车区，不强行挤上。
3. **走允许的门和上车方式。** 看当地告示与车上标识；有的服务通常前门上，有的允许持指定票卡者从任一门上，现金则须前门。需要坡道或协助时在安全位置告知司机，等设备和下车动线准备好，不自行拉动设备或卡在门边。
4. **按本车有效方式付费或验证。** 在规定时点刷卡、出示或激活票，留意读卡器成功提示或保存纸票/转乘凭证。若提示不清、失败或设备故障，先移开门口，向司机/正式渠道确认可用方法；不反复盲刷造成重复扣费，也不把“没人查票”当作许可。TfL 伦敦公交只需上车触碰一次；其他系统可能不同，不凭本例决定下车时是否再刷。
5. **进车并确认本轮完成。** 在车内安全位置站稳或坐好，保持过道、车门和优先空间畅通。确认自己已登上计划中的服务且票证/支付按当地规则有效，接下来再按下车计划留意站名。

### 完成与停止

只有**车辆方向/支线吻合、按允许方式进入，并且本次车费已按规则验证**，才算完成这次上车；无法核对或付款时就先留在安全候车区或按司机的正式指引退出，不声称已经乘上正确车。不要为赶车跑入车道、拦门或绕过当地票务规则。

### 常见报错与补救

- **同线路号，终点不同：** 看支线、短线和实时变更，等与你的计划相符的车；不确定时向停稳车辆的司机确认。
- **扫码或刷卡没明确成功：** 按读卡器与司机指引检查一次，再用运营方允许的替代方式；不要不断重复扣费。
- **票要预先激活：** 若当地确有此规则，安全地完成后再上车；手机无电或网络不可用时查官方可用的实体票/其他方式，不伪造凭证。
- **车满、坡道暂不可用或门没开：** 退回安全位置，问下一班或正式替代安排；需要无障碍服务时找运营方可用支持，不保证同一辆车一定能载你。
- **上车才发现方向错：** 不在行驶中要求开门或下车；按车上安全指引到允许的站下，再使用后续恢复流程。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通固定线路公交，不覆盖铁路闸机、航空、跨境票务或优惠资格认定。城市、支付介质、费用与可用车门不断变化，均须读你乘坐的运营方当期资料。视觉、听觉、行动或语言不便时，可先用官方文字/播报、工作人员或可信陪同核对车号、提示和上车支持；不要交出支付账户控制权。副作用是错过一辆不符条件的车，通常比坐反方向更可恢复。

## English

Load this Skill when you are waiting at an active stop and a bus arrives. Your job is to **check this vehicle, use its permitted door and confirm a valid fare under the local operator's rules**. You have already chosen the route, direction and intended exit stop. Alighting, transfer and missed-stop recovery belong to later steps. Fares, doors, payment methods and any tap-out rule are current operator facts, not city-independent instructions.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep your checked route, direction or destination, and any branch or short-working marker in view. Have a ticket, card or other method actually accepted for this trip ready. Check official operator information for whether to buy or activate before boarding, which door to use, where to validate and whether to retain proof. A beep you remember from another city is not this bus's fare rule.

### Execution

1. **Read the vehicle, not only its route number.** From a safe waiting place, compare its front or side display, destination, branch and temporary service marker with your plan. If the number matches but the destination, branch or short-working limit does not, wait. You can ask the driver of a stopped bus briefly; do not step into a lane to flag it down.
2. **Signal and approach according to local instructions.** Some operators ask you to signal near the stop; others use a fixed queue or door instructions. Act from a safe place. Wait until the vehicle has fully stopped and let people get off first. If it is full, the door stays shut or the driver asks you to wait for the next service, step back to the waiting area.
3. **Use the permitted door and boarding aid.** Read the sign or vehicle marking. Some services normally board at the front; others allow certain ticket holders at any door while cash riders use the front. If you need a ramp or assistance, tell the driver from a safe place and wait for the device and exit path; do not operate equipment yourself or block a door.
4. **Pay or validate the fare for this bus.** Tap, show or activate at the required point, check the reader's success signal or keep the paper ticket or transfer. If the result is unclear, payment fails or equipment is down, move clear of the doorway and ask the driver or official channel about a permitted option. Do not tap blindly several times or treat lack of inspection as permission. A London bus requires one touch on entry; that example does not decide whether another system needs a tap on exit.
5. **Settle safely and confirm this round.** Hold on or sit, keeping aisles, doors and priority space clear. Confirm that you are on the planned service and your fare or proof is valid under its rules, then follow your separate exit-stop plan.

### Success and stop

You have boarded successfully only when the vehicle's direction and branch fit your plan, you entered as permitted and this trip's fare was validated as required. If you cannot verify the vehicle or payment, remain at the safe stop or leave according to the driver's official instructions; do not claim the correct bus was taken. Do not run into traffic, hold doors or bypass the fare rule to catch it.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Same route number, different destination:** Check branch, short working and current service change; wait for the matching bus or ask the driver after it stops.
- **No clear payment success:** Check the reader and driver's instruction once, then use an operator-approved alternative. Avoid repeated charges from blind retries.
- **Ticket must be activated first:** If that is the local rule, do it safely before boarding. If a phone is flat or offline, check official physical-ticket or other accepted options; do not invent proof.
- **Bus is full, ramp unavailable or door remains shut:** Step back, ask about the next service or official alternative. Accessibility support matters, but this particular vehicle is not guaranteed.
- **You notice the wrong direction after boarding:** Do not demand a door opening in motion. Follow on-board safety instructions, get off at a permitted stop and use the recovery task.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers ordinary fixed-route buses, not rail gates, flights, cross-border ticketing or eligibility for discounts. Cities, fare media, costs and usable doors change; consult the operator you are actually riding. If sight, hearing, mobility or language makes checking harder, use official text or announcements, staff or a trusted companion to verify the display, payment signal and available boarding help without handing over control of a payment account. Letting a mismatched bus go is usually easier to recover from than riding in the wrong direction.
