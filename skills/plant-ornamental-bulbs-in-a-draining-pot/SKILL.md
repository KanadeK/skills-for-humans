## 中文

当你手上是一包已识别、允许在本地室外盆栽的**观赏开花球根**，想把它们真正种下时，加载这份 Skill。这里处理休眠球根的方向、深度、间距与排水，不把 E002 的种子或 E003 的带根幼苗换个名字重种一次。

### 准备与输入

读本包的品种、适合季节、种植深度、间距、上下方向、日照和首次浇水说明；袋子混装或标签缺失时先停下辨认。确认你有权使用此处容器，盆稳定、有排水孔，介质适用于该种球根。观赏球根不是食物，若儿童或宠物可能接触而安全性未知，先改放无法触及的位置并查该品种资料。大型重盆需要协助搬运。

### 执行

1. **检验种植资格。** 对照当地当前季节与品种说明，剔除明显软烂或严重发霉而无法判断的球根，必要时询问供货方或园艺服务。不要按“所有球根都是秋种、三倍深”概括。
2. **准备尺寸足够的有孔盆。** 按包装所需深度和间距核对盆的实际宽深；放不下就减少本次球根或换合适容器。检查底孔通畅、摆放处能接住或安全排走水，填入本品种适用的盆栽介质并留浇水余量。石子铺底不能替代孔；不把盆放在会漏水损坏电器的地方。
3. **按实物说明排布。** 在介质上先摆好数量与间距，核对每颗的上下方向；形状不清就查该品种图例。只做一个品种或一套说明明确的组合，不临时发明混层规则。
4. **覆土与标记。** 按包装允许的深度覆介质，轻压稳定，写品种和日期。覆土后你仍能说出每颗所在位置，不靠明年猜。
5. **按说明第一次浇水。** 浇到介质与球根接触并让多余水从底孔排出，或服从该品种更具体的初次给水要求。避免长期积水，记下下次观察发芽和介质的时机；发芽不是当天验收项。

### 成功条件

盆器稳定且排水通、球根身份明确、深度与间距来自该包说明、标签可读，首次给水及下一次复看有依据。品种或方向不明、盆不排水、季节不合或球根可疑时，明确停止并保留包装资料。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把球根与可食洋葱混放：** 立即分离并清楚标识；不要尝味辨别观赏球根。
- **看不清尖端与根盘：** 查本品种图例或问供应者，不硬按一个通用朝向。
- **盆底没有孔：** 改用有孔容器；铺石子不能让封闭盆自行排水。
- **种完第二天没芽：** 查该品种正常时程和真实介质状态，不追加大量水或挖出检查。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只做小型、普通、已识别且当地允许种植的观赏开花球根；不教食用球茎、野生采集、检疫、药剂浸泡或不明有毒植物处理。握力或弯腰困难时用腰高稳固盆台并请人搬土；视觉受限时请人读包装并核对球根方向。副作用是一个可能数周仍看似没变化、却需要按说明照看的盆。

## English

Load this Skill when you have an identified packet of ordinary **ornamental flowering bulbs** that may be grown outdoors in your area and want to plant them in a pot. Dormant bulb orientation, depth, spacing, and drainage are the work here. A seed or already rooted seedling uses another process.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this packet's species, planting season, depth, spacing, orientation, sun, and first-watering directions. If contents are mixed or the label is missing, identify them before work. Confirm permission to use the container and a stable pot with drainage holes and suitable growing medium. Ornamental bulbs are not food. If children or pets might reach a species whose safety is unknown, keep it inaccessible and seek species-specific information. Arrange help for a heavy pot.

### Execution

1. **Confirm planting suitability.** Compare the current local season with species directions. Set aside a bulb that is clearly soft, rotten, or seriously mouldy until a supplier or horticultural source can advise. Do not assume all bulbs are autumn-planted at one universal depth.
2. **Prepare a large enough draining pot.** Compare its real width and depth with the packet's spacing and planting-depth needs; plant fewer bulbs or choose a suitable pot if they do not fit. Check that holes are open, runoff has a safe route, and the medium suits the species, leaving headroom for water. Gravel at the base does not replace holes. Keep runoff away from equipment.
3. **Lay out the actual bulbs.** Arrange number and spacing on the surface first. Verify top and root end from this species' guide; if unclear, ask rather than force a guessed orientation. Use one species or a combination with explicit compatible instructions, not an invented layered recipe.
4. **Cover and label.** Add medium to the supported depth and firm gently. Mark species and date. You should still know where the bulbs sit without excavation next year.
5. **Water as directed.** Settle the medium and let any permitted excess leave through the holes, or follow a more specific first-watering instruction for this species. Avoid prolonged waterlogging and set a sprout/medium check. Germination is not expected that day.

### Success

The pot is stable and draining; identity, depth, and spacing come from the actual packet; the label is readable; first watering and next observation have a basis. Stop with the packet retained if species or orientation is unresolved, the pot cannot drain, season is unsuitable, or a bulb is suspect.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Ornamental bulbs are mixed with food onions:** Separate and identify them clearly. Never taste an ornamental bulb for identification.
- **You cannot see tip or root plate:** Check a species illustration or ask the supplier rather than impose a universal direction.
- **The pot has no hole:** Use a draining container; gravel cannot make a sealed pot drain.
- **Nothing sprouts the next day:** Check the species timeline and real medium state; do not add large amounts of water or dig bulbs up to inspect.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is for small, ordinary, identified, locally permitted ornamental flowering bulbs. It is not edible bulb cultivation, wild collection, plant quarantine, chemical soaking, or handling an unknown toxic species. Use a stable waist-high surface and lifting help if needed. Have someone read the packet and confirm orientation if vision is limited. The result may look like an empty pot for weeks and still requires care.
