## 中文

当一件可重复使用的普通家居物品“不好用了”，你准备把替换品写进购物清单时，加载这份 Skill。你要判断这次用途究竟需要**继续用、安排合适的维修、确认借用/租用，还是购买替换品**。这里从已有物品的失效开始，不负责整理多项模糊补货需求，也不替代付款前对已选商品的查重。不要用本篇尝试电气、燃气、承重结构、防护用品或其他安全关键物品的维修或试用。

### 准备与输入

写下原物必须完成的一件具体工作、下一次使用时间、现在的故障表现，以及你手上能核实的说明书、型号或服务信息。另列家里已拥有的同功能物品和可能的借用/租用渠道。别把“看着旧”自动翻译成“功能失效”；也别把“看着还能用”当成安全证明。

只做无需拆机、攀高、通电排故或承受危险载荷的外观与功能核对。若检查本身可能伤人，停止使用并按制造商或合格人员的要求处理；本篇只记录替换需求，不教维修。看不清或搬不动时，请可信的人描述可见状况，或使用已有可及工具；不要为了证明它坏了而重复危险动作。

### 执行

1. **把“坏了”写成观察。** 记录哪项工作做不到、从何时开始、是否只是外观磨损或缺一个可单独取得的普通部件。你需要的是该工作在截止前有人或物能完成，不是给旧物颁布退役证书。
2. **确认现物能否继续承担这次用途。** 只依据可安全核实的状态、原说明和实际使用限制判断。能安全完成就暂不列购买；状况不明或使用会带来风险，就停用并标“待核实”，不要试到它彻底坏掉。
3. **查不用买新物的可行路线。** 先看已有的同功能物品是否可取用且未被预留；再查制造商或可靠服务方是否提供适用的普通维修，能否在使用前完成。维修费用、时间或结果不明就记为待确认，不能把“可以修”写成“已经修好”。本篇不提供维修步骤。
4. **只把已确认的借用算作覆盖。** 对短时、低频用途，先找到可能的借用、共享或租用渠道。物品能否完成工作、对方是否同意、何时能取还都需实际落实；未获同意或预约前，它只是候选，不是可用存量。
5. **按使用期限作决定。** 原物可安全用，选“继续用”；可安排的维修或已确认取还的借用能及时满足用途，选相应路线并写下一步；上述路线都不能可靠满足已确认用途，选“需替换”；仍有时间查清关键未知，选“待确认”。安全状况不明时停止使用原物，别用这份清单批准一次试验。
6. **只把真正的替换缺口交给采购。** 写一行`用途和期限｜原物为何不可用｜已确认的替代为何不够｜需替换/待确认｜下一步`。型号、尺寸、包装、价格和商家规则在选购时分别核对；本篇不宣布哪件新商品一定合适。

### 完成与停止

你能说清“继续用 / 安排维修 / 已确认借用或租用 / 需替换 / 待确认”中的一个结果、它依据的观察，以及下一步由谁在何时做。没有真实使用机会时，可以不买；没有安全依据时，先停用原物。维修未完成、借用未获同意或新物尚未核对规格，都不算已经解决用途。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只因外观旧就想换：** 回到那件具体工作；若它仍安全可用，把“想升级”与“必须替换”分开记录。
- **维修方案只有一句“应该能修”：** 核对负责方、可适用的说明、完成时间和真实费用；未确认前保留待确认，不拆开尝试。
- **朋友说“有空借你”：** 取得明确同意、可取得时间和归还约定后才计为可用；否则先查另一条路线。
- **备用物也被别人预留：** 不重复计算同一件物品，重新核对对方的用途和权限。
- **期限到了但没有安全可用方案：** 停用有疑虑的原物，改变这次普通任务的安排；不要用不合适的替代品硬做。

### 假设、替代与已知副作用

这里仅处理低风险、可重复使用的普通家居物品，以及**是否要买**的决定；不替代维修、退换、保修权利或专业安全判断。维修服务、借用关系、交通、费用与时间在各地不同，按你实际能确认的条件填写。可能的副作用是少买一件物品，却多了一次询问或送修；下一步未落实前，缺口仍存在。

## English

Load this Skill when a reusable, ordinary household item “doesn't work well anymore” and you are about to put its replacement on a shopping list. Decide whether this use calls for **continued use, an appropriate repair arrangement, confirmed borrowing or rental, or a replacement purchase**. This starts with an owned item's failure; it does not organize several vague replenishment requests or replace a checkout duplicate check for an item already chosen. Do not use this Skill to repair or test electrical, gas, load-bearing, protective, or other safety-critical items.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down the one job this item must do, the next time you need it, what failure you observed, and any manual, model, or service information you can verify. List equivalent items you already own and possible borrowing or rental routes. “Looks old” does not automatically mean “cannot do the job”; “looks fine” does not certify safety either.

Only inspect what you can check without taking equipment apart, climbing, troubleshooting powered equipment, or imposing a dangerous load. If checking could hurt someone, stop using the item and follow the maker's or a qualified person's instructions. This Skill only records a replacement need; it teaches no repair. If you cannot see or lift it, use an existing accessible aid or ask someone you trust to describe what is visible. Do not repeat a hazardous action to prove failure.

### Execution

1. **Turn “broken” into an observation.** Record which job fails, since when, and whether the problem is only appearance or a separately obtainable ordinary part. Your goal is to have that job covered by the deadline, not to issue a retirement certificate to the old item.
2. **Check whether the owned item can still serve this use.** Rely only on safely verifiable condition, its instructions, and the actual use limits. If it can do the job safely, do not add a purchase yet. If its condition is unknown or use may be risky, stop using it and mark it “confirm”; do not test it until it fails completely.
3. **Check routes that avoid a new purchase.** First see whether an equivalent item already owned is available and not reserved. Then check whether the maker or a reliable service provider offers an appropriate ordinary repair that can finish before the use. Unknown repair cost, time, or result stays “confirm”; “repairable” is not “repaired.” This Skill gives no repair instructions.
4. **Count a loan only after confirmation.** For a short or infrequent use, find a possible borrowing, sharing, or rental route. Whether the item can do the job, the owner's agreement, and workable collection and return times all need real confirmation. Before agreement or booking, it is only an option, not usable stock.
5. **Decide against the use deadline.** Choose “keep using” if the original item is safe and adequate. Choose an arranged repair or a loan with confirmed collection and return if it will cover the use in time, and write the next action. Choose “replace” if none of those routes can reliably cover a confirmed use. Choose “confirm” if there is still time to resolve a material unknown. Stop using an item of uncertain safety; this list does not authorize a test run.
6. **Pass only a real replacement gap to shopping.** Write one line: `Use and deadline | why the old item is unavailable | why confirmed alternatives do not cover it | replace/confirm | next action`. Check model, size, pack, price, and seller rules during selection. This Skill does not claim that a particular new product will fit.

### Success and stop conditions

You can name one result—“keep using / arrange repair / confirmed loan or rental / replace / confirm”—its observed basis, and who will do what next and when. With no real occasion to use it, you may buy nothing. Without safety evidence, stop using the original item. An unfinished repair, an unagreed loan, or a replacement whose specifications have not been checked has not solved the use.

### Common errors and recovery

- **It only looks old:** Return to the specific job. If it remains safe and usable, record “would like an upgrade” separately from “must replace.”
- **The repair plan is only “it should be fixable”:** Confirm the responsible party, applicable instructions, completion time, and real cost. Keep it pending until then; do not disassemble it to find out.
- **A friend said “you can borrow it sometime”:** Count it only after clear agreement, an available pickup time, and a return arrangement. Otherwise check another route.
- **The spare is reserved by someone else:** Do not count one item twice; confirm the other person's use and permission.
- **The deadline arrives without a safe option:** Stop using the doubtful item and change the ordinary task plan. Do not force an unsuitable substitute to do the job.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers only low-risk reusable household items and the **purchase-need decision**. It does not perform repair, returns, warranty claims, or professional safety assessment. Repair services, borrowing relationships, travel, costs, and timing vary; use only conditions you can actually confirm. You may buy one fewer item and gain a question or repair errand instead. Until that next step happens, the use remains uncovered.
