## 中文

当你想在公开公园等普通场地和少量同行者短时吃点东西时，用这份 Skill 先核**场地是否允许、食物能否在整个往返中保持安全、收工如何清场**，再实际野餐。结果是一顿在允许地点完成、食物处理有据、没有把垃圾或剩食留给场地的普通野餐。这里不教烧烤、现场生肉处理、保温烹饪或过敏事故处置。

### 准备与输入

从场地管理方当期页面和现场标识核开放时段、能否在选定区域吃东西、桌位是否需预约、人数/宠物/玻璃/火源和垃圾规则；别把另一公园的许可套过来。选可达、能按时离开的地点，确认饮水、洗手、厕所和可及路线是否真可用。优先选包装说明允许常温携带的未切完整水果、干粮等，避免为野餐引入复杂冷链；切开的水果、冷餐肉、熟食沙拉等易腐食品须按标签和食品安全要求冷藏。与同行人分享前核他们是否能吃这些食物，不猜过敏信息。需要保冷而没有冷藏箱/冷源与能核温度的方式时，改选常温安全食品；不把凉手感当温度计。

### 执行

1. **把地点和退出点定下来。** 选正式允许的普通小区域，不占已预约桌、不堵通道、不靠近禁止进的水边或敏感地带。写清最晚离开时间和垃圾去处；若规则不明先问管理方，无法确认就换地点。
2. **按食物性质分装。** 常温食物遵包装说明保持干净、密闭；易腐冷食预先冷透，放在有冰/冷源和温度计的保冷容器中，保持约 4°C（40°F）或更低，尽量少开盖，避免阳光直晒。若无法持续确认冷藏条件，就不带易腐品。不要把生肉或需现场烹调的食品加入这份无烹饪野餐。
3. **准备干净的取食与回收。** 带饮水、清洁双手的可用方式、分食器具及垃圾袋；在没有可用洗手设施时，选无需现场处理的预包装食物。食物、清洁用具与垃圾分开放，别把用过的餐具塞回干净食物袋。
4. **现场只取要吃的一份。** 按场地规则坐下，先清洁双手，再从保冷容器取小份冷食，其余继续保持冷藏。易腐食物离开安全温控的累计时间不要超过 2 小时；室外温度高于 90°F（约 32°C）时限为 1 小时。时间从离开安全温控开始累计，不因重新盖盒而清零；无法判断已暴露多久或冷藏是否保持，就丢弃而不留作下餐。不要喂野生动物。
5. **按真实状态清场。** 把垃圾和可重复容器分别收好，按场地要求投放或带走，检查桌面/地面、同行人的物品和离场路线。易腐剩食只有仍符合安全温控与时间条件才按既有剩菜流程尽快冷藏，否则丢弃；本文不重新教剩菜保存。现场无合适垃圾点就把密封垃圾带到允许的收集处，不留在公园。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你在正式允许的地点吃完普通短时野餐，能说明食品常温资格或实际冷藏/暴露时间，且人、物和垃圾都清楚收尾。只有铺开毯子却违反场地规则，或食物安全状态未知仍打包带回，不算完成。

- **桌位被预约或场地禁止野餐：** 换管理方允许的区域或改日，不占用他人的预约。
- **保冷箱不够冷/温度无法确认：** 不提供易腐食品；改用适合常温的现成食物，不用气味尝试判断安全。
- **活动拖长，冷食已超时：** 按上面的 FDA 时间界限丢弃，不为了减少浪费留作下餐。
- **垃圾桶满或没有：** 密封带走到允许的收集点，不堆在桶边或投进自然环境。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

不同地区场地许可、费用、食品标签和废物分类会变，以当期管理方及产品说明为准。食品安全温度与时间依据下方 FDA/USDA 的公开口径；易感人群、食物过敏、病症与儿童特殊饮食需要适用的专业建议，不能靠本篇兜底。行动、手部、感官或经济条件无法支持冷链时选更短、更易退出的地点和常温安全食品，而非冒险保留易腐品。场地可能要求将所有废物带走。

## English

Use this Skill for a short ordinary picnic with a few companions in a public park or similar place. First check **whether the place permits it, whether food can stay safe for the whole trip, and how you will clear the area**. Then eat there. The result is a permitted picnic with an evidenced food plan and no waste or leftovers abandoned on site. This does not teach grilling, handling raw meat outdoors, hot holding, or response to an allergic reaction.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the site manager's current page and signs to check hours, where eating is allowed, table bookings, group or pet rules, glass or flame rules, and waste handling. Another park's permission does not apply. Choose an accessible place you can leave on time and check whether water, handwashing, restrooms, and the route are actually available. Prefer foods labelled for room-temperature carriage, such as uncut whole fruit or dry packaged food, rather than adding a difficult cold chain. Cut fruit, deli meat, prepared salads, and other perishables need safe cold handling under label and food-safety rules. Check with companions before sharing; do not guess their allergies. Without a cooler, cold source, and a way to verify temperature, choose shelf-stable food instead of perishable food. A cool feel is not a thermometer.

### Execution

1. **Choose place and exit.** Use an area formally open to a small ordinary picnic without occupying a reserved table, blocking a route, or entering restricted waterfront or habitat. Know latest departure and waste route. Ask the manager when a rule is unclear; change location if you cannot establish permission.
2. **Pack by food type.** Keep shelf-stable items clean and closed as their packaging directs. Chill perishable cold food beforehand and keep it in a cooler with ice or other cold source and a thermometer, at about 40°F (4°C) or below. Open it sparingly and keep it out of direct sun. Do not bring perishables when safe cold conditions cannot be confirmed. Raw meat and items needing on-site cooking are outside this no-cook picnic.
3. **Prepare clean service and collection.** Bring water, an available way to clean hands, serving utensils, and a waste bag. Without usable handwashing, choose ready-to-eat packaged food that needs no on-site preparation. Keep food, clean utensils, and waste separate; do not return used utensils to a clean-food bag.
4. **Serve only a small amount.** Sit under venue rules, clean hands, take a small portion of cold food, and keep the rest cold. Perishable food's cumulative time outside safe temperature control must not exceed 2 hours, or 1 hour when outdoor temperature is above 90°F (about 32°C). Count from leaving safe temperature control; closing a box does not reset the clock. If time or safe cold status is unknown, discard instead of saving it for another meal. Do not feed wildlife.
5. **Clear the real site.** Separate waste and reusable items, use the venue's allowed disposal or take them away, and check table, ground, companions' belongings, and exit. Only perishable leftovers still meeting safe time and temperature conditions may go promptly into the existing leftover-storage workflow; otherwise discard. This Skill does not reteach leftover storage. If no suitable bin exists, carry sealed waste to an allowed collection point rather than leave it in the park.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You ate a short ordinary picnic in an authorised place, can explain shelf-stable status or verified cold and exposure time, and left people, items, and waste accounted for. Spreading a blanket in a prohibited place or taking home perishable food of unknown safety is not completion.

- **A table is reserved or picnics are prohibited:** Use an allowed area or another day, not someone else's booking.
- **The cooler is not cold enough or temperature cannot be checked:** Do not serve perishables. Use suitable shelf-stable food, not smell as a safety test.
- **The outing runs long and cold food exceeds its limit:** Discard under the FDA time rule above rather than save it to avoid waste.
- **The bin is full or absent:** Carry sealed waste to an allowed collection point; do not pile it beside a bin or leave it outdoors.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Site permission, charges, food labels, and waste sorting vary by region and time; check actual manager and product instructions. Food time and temperature rules above follow public FDA/USDA guidance. People with special vulnerability, allergies, illness, or a child's special diet need suitable expert guidance rather than this general picnic Skill. If mobility, hand, sensory, or money constraints make cold storage unreliable, choose a shorter easy-exit site and shelf-stable food instead of taking unsafe perishables. The venue may require you to carry all waste out.
