## 中文

当你想在**一个获准、安全、固定的观鸟点**专心看一小段时间，而不是只记某一只鸟时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张带起止时间、地点粗范围、观察方式、每类鸟实际最高同时数量与未知项的私人清单。它不同于单鸟目视记录；也不是自动提交给科学平台的“完整”数据。H297 的不打扰原则仍适用，不能为了清单靠近巢、诱叫或离开开放路径。

### 准备与输入

选许可固定点、清楚开始和计划结束时刻，坐/站得稳；清单可纸写，不需注册 eBird。记录观鸟是否为这段时间主要目的，是否始终在同一点，能否看到/听到、天气及视觉/听觉限制；中途电话或离开要标实际中断。不看太阳、不举镜行走。地方保护规则或隐私敏感时只记粗地点。

### 执行

1. **写起点再开始。** 记日期、本地时区、开始时间和粗生境，在原地观察，不走动到别的热点还称“固定点”。看见鸟先按自己可靠线索记种或类群、遇到不确定记“未识别”，不要凭应用建议直接记种名。
2. **按实际观察累计。** 同一时间看见三只可计三只，过一会儿又见两只可能是旧鸟时不要简单加成五只；用这段里能确认的最高同时数或明确独立个体证据。听到但看不见也标“听到”，并保留声音 ID 的不确定性。
3. **标出努力与缺口。** 记下观察中断、视野遮挡、只能看一侧或因不熟悉某类鸟而未判名。若有鸟故意略过或不是持续专心观鸟，不把清单叫作“本段所有鸟都记录”；个人完整度与 eBird 平台正式协议可再分别核对。
4. **到时停并整理。** 记结束时间/实际时长，核每条的观测方式、计数依据与未知项，存为私人记录；日后若要公开上传，先按平台当前规则和敏感地点保护另作决定，不默认本篇授权上传。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一张固定点个人清单有真实起止时间、粗地点、鸟/类群与数目或未知、目视/听声及努力中断说明；没有见到鸟也可留下真实零观察记录。只写“三只好看的鸟”而无时长/类别，不算有努力口径的清单。

- **忘记起始时间：** 记“起点未知”，这次只作普通观感记录，下一次再做正式计时。
- **中途移动到另一地点：** 分为两段或改行进记录，别合并成同一固定点。
- **鸟来回出现可能重复：** 用可证的同时最大数或标数量不确定，不累加猜数。
- **软件给出未核种名：** 留未知或类群，不自动提交物种数。
- **视听/行动不便：** 无障碍固定座位、可信协助读鸟位或记时，可保留谁看见/谁描述。

### 假设与边界

Cornell eBird 固定点协议重视真实开始、时长与观察努力；本篇做**私人**清单，不自动满足公开科研数据要求。两次记录数差异也不能在这里解释成数量涨跌。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to watch birds **from one permitted safe fixed spot for a chosen time**, beyond one individual field note. Finish with a private checklist carrying real start/end, coarse location, observation mode, each type's highest simultaneous count or separately proven individuals, and unknowns. This is not automatically a “complete” scientific-platform submission. H297 non-disturbance still applies: no nest approach, playback, or closed-path chase for list length.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a permitted fixed spot with stable seating/footing and a clear start and planned end; paper notes need no eBird account. Record whether birds are the session's main focus, whether you stay fixed, sight/hearing limits, weather, and later interruptions. Use a coarse location where protection or privacy matters. Do not point optics at sun or walk with them raised.

### Execution

1. **Write the start before watching.** Note date, local time zone, start, and broad habitat, staying in place rather than walking to another hotspot under a fixed label. Record a bird by reliable species or group clues; keep uncertain sightings unidentified rather than accepting an app's top name.
2. **Count what is actually supported.** Three birds seen together can count as three. Two later birds may be the same individuals, so do not simply add to five. Use a supported maximum seen at once or distinct-individual evidence. Mark heard-only detections and retain uncertainty in sound IDs.
3. **Mark effort and gaps.** Write phone interruptions, blocked view, one-sided access, or groups you could not identify. With deliberate omissions or casual off-and-on watching, do not label it every bird in the session. Personal completeness and eBird's formal protocol need separate checks.
4. **Stop at the time and close.** Record end and actual duration. Check each line for mode, count basis, and unknowns, then save privately. If later uploading publicly, review current platform rules and sensitive-site handling separately; this Skill grants no automatic posting permission.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A private fixed-place list has real start/end, coarse place, birds/groups and defensible counts or unknowns, sight/sound modes, and effort interruptions. A true zero-observation session may be recorded. “Three nice birds” without time or identity limits is not an effort-aware list.

- **Start time was forgotten:** Mark it unknown, treat this as casual notes, and time a later session.
- **You moved to another site:** Split sessions or use a travel mode, not one stationary list.
- **Birds return and may be repeats:** Use supported simultaneous maximum or mark count uncertain rather than sum guesses.
- **Software gives an unverified name:** Keep group/unknown, not an auto-submitted species count.
- **Sight, hearing, or mobility is limited:** Accessible fixed seating and trusted pointing/time help can retain who actually saw and who described.

### Assumptions and limits

Cornell eBird stationary protocol values true start, duration, and focused effort. This **private** list does not automatically qualify for public research submission. Differences between two lists cannot yet be called population trends.
