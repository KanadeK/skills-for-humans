## 中文

当你要组装厂家明确允许普通人处理的小型非承重平板用品，例如不承担人的重量、防倾倒或防护功能的普通整理件时加载。目标是**按准确型号说明完成整件低风险顺序，或在不匹配时停在可核对的步骤**。这不是家具、建筑或安全结构验收；需要墙固、防倾倒、专业安装、危险工具或电气/机械/燃气操作的产品不进入此流程。

### 准备与输入

确认准确型号及说明版本，先浏览整份说明中人数、工具、方向、材料和最终使用限制。全部板件与紧固件已按说明核对，工作面平稳且不挡通道，说明要求的普通手工具完好可用。需要两人而只有一人、需钻孔/上墙/承重固定、或你的身体条件不允许稳定操作时暂停，不能自行删掉步骤来降低难度。

### 执行

1. **先辨每块的方向。** 用准确图示的孔位、边缘、成品面和左右/上下标记对照实物，把当前步骤所需零件单独摆好。图示看不清、零件编号不符时停；不要以“装上再看”试错。
2. **按一个说明步骤做一个动作组。** 在第一个连接前看清它用哪个零件号、连接方向及是否要求暂时松留。只使用说明允许的手工具；紧固、插入或转动细节服从准确型号说明，不根据相似家具的记忆补步骤。
3. **每步完成就核对。** 让眼前连接、数量和朝向与该步结束图一致，确认没有明显裂开、翘起、卡住或多余零件。需要后面才完全锁紧的地方仍照说明保持其状态，不提前猛拧。
4. **不匹配就停在这一页。** 连接不上、孔位错开、需要明显用力才能挤入、出现损坏或发现缺件时，记步骤号、两边零件编号和可见差异；仅按厂家允许的办法退回或修正。不要靠电钻、敲击、胶或替代螺丝把问题藏进去。
5. **按完整说明核对普通完成点。** 所有适用的用户级步骤、必要连接与厂商终检均已做完，外观与预期方向一致，才能进行说明允许的普通低风险使用。若图中有防倾倒/承重/防护要求，别用“主体看起来站得住”跳过它；交相应有资质的人并在完成前不使用。

### 完成与停止

准确说明允许由使用者完成的普通非承重步骤都能在实物上逐项指认，零件和方向一致，无已知强装、损坏或遗漏；这不是对结构承载的保证。步骤看不懂、需要额外人数/危险工具/固定方式、零件不符或稳定性有疑问时保持未完成，不作普通使用。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两块板看起来一样却孔位相反：** 回到编号和边缘/孔位提示，纠正前不插入连接件。
- **一步多出螺丝：** 对照本步与整套零件清单，停下找差异，不把多余件当厂家赠品。
- **连接件卡住：** 不加长杠杆或敲进去；核对朝向和准确说明，必要时联系供应方。
- **一个人做不了说明的双人步骤：** 等愿意且有能力的人协助，或安排专业服务；不靠墙角托住危险部件。
- **说明中有墙固或防倾倒图标：** 退出本低风险组装范围，请合适人员处理；主体拼好也不代表可安全使用。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只整合厂家本来允许的普通非结构性用户步骤。品牌、地区、材料和说明版本不同，不存在通用拧紧力矩或各型号共同的零件顺序。看不清图示或握持不稳定时，用放大、语音读回或有能力的协助者，并让双方逐步确认；不能完成就停。副作用是你可能比短视频慢，但至少眼前的“第 7 步”真的是说明上的第 7 步。

## English

Load this Skill for a small flat-pack item the maker explicitly permits an ordinary person to assemble, such as an organiser that does not bear a person's weight or serve an anti-tip or protective function. The result is **the complete low-risk sequence under the exact model guide, or a stop at a checkable step when reality differs**. This is not furniture, building or safety-structure acceptance. A product requiring wall fixing, anti-tip work, specialist installation, hazardous tools or electrical, mechanical or gas work is outside this flow.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the exact model and guide revision. Skim the full guide for people, tools, orientation, materials and final-use limits. All boards and fasteners were checked against it, the work area is stable and clear of routes, and permitted ordinary hand tools are sound. Pause if the guide requires two people and only one is available, drilling, wall or load-bearing fixing, or work you cannot physically keep stable. Do not delete a step to make it appear easier.

### Execution

1. **Identify each piece's orientation first.** Match the exact drawing's holes, edges, finished face and left/right or up/down marks to the item. Lay out only the current step's parts. Stop if the drawing or ID is unclear; “fit it and see” is not an identification method.
2. **Do one guide step as one action group.** Before the first joint, read its part ID, joining direction and whether it must remain loose for later steps. Use only tools permitted by the guide. Fastening, insertion and turning details follow the exact model, not memory from another kit.
3. **Check reality after each step.** Compare actual joint, count and orientation with the end-of-step drawing. Look for visible splitting, lifting, binding or unexplained extra parts. Leave a connection loose until the guide says to finish it; do not overtighten early.
4. **Stop on the page that does not match.** When holes do not line up, a joint needs obvious force, damage appears or a part is missing, record step number, both part IDs and visible difference. Reverse or correct only by a maker-permitted method. Do not hide the mismatch with a power drill, hammer, glue or substitute screw.
5. **Check the whole ordinary endpoint.** All applicable user steps, required connections and maker final checks must be complete and orientation correct before ordinary low-risk use under the guide. If the guide shows anti-tip, load-bearing or protective work, “the main body stands” does not waive it. Leave the item unused and seek suitably qualified help.

### Success and stop

Each ordinary non-structural step the exact guide permits you to perform can be identified on the real item, with matching parts and orientation and no known forced fit, damage or omission. This is not a load-capacity guarantee. Keep it unfinished when a step is unclear, another person or hazardous tool/fixing is required, parts differ or stability is in doubt; do not use it yet.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Two panels look alike but their holes face opposite ways:** Return to IDs and edge/hole cues before inserting connectors.
- **A screw is left over at a step:** Compare this step and the overall parts count; pause rather than calling it a free spare.
- **A connector binds:** Do not add a lever or hammer. Recheck orientation and the exact guide, then contact the supplier if needed.
- **A two-person step has one worker:** Wait for willing capable help or arrange a professional; do not prop an unsafe piece against a wall.
- **The guide has a wall-fixing or anti-tip symbol:** Exit this low-risk workflow for suitable help. An assembled body is not necessarily safe to use.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This coordinates only manufacturer-permitted ordinary non-structural user steps. Brand, region, material and guide revision differ; there is no universal tightening torque or common part order. Enlarge diagrams, use spoken readback or ask capable help when sight or grip is limited, checking each step together. Stop if it cannot be done safely. The process may be slower than a video, but the real “Step 7” will at least match the guide's Step 7.
