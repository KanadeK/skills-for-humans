## 中文

当普通面对面、电话或文字对话将近结束，或你确实需要离开，却担心一走了之或无限找理由时加载。目标是**说出正在结束、交代确实存在的下一动作，并真正退出这一轮互动**。不要求对方满意、给最后一个回复或继续聊天；有具体请求要拒绝时用拒绝/边界流程，威胁、骚扰和危机不在这里处理。

### 准备与输入

回想这段谈话是否已经产生真实约定：例如你答应发一份普通信息、对方给出一个待确认时间、还是仅仅聊过一会儿。你不必为想结束的事实编一项紧急任务；只需要知道自己是否还欠一个实际交接。若对方正在说重要且未完的一句话，在可行时等到短暂停顿；有时你需要明确打断才能离开，也可以简短说明。

### 执行

1. **给对话一个听得懂的结束信号。** 说“我现在要先结束这段聊天/得走了，谢谢今天聊这些。” 文字沟通可写“我先离线了，今天先到这里。” 不必用一串理由证明自己有资格离开。
2. **只概括确实需要带走的事。** 有实际下一步时，用“我会在__前发__”“我们仍待确认__”之一说清负责人和状态。若没有约定，就不要为了显得亲切自动承诺“明天一定联系你”。对方的观点可以被听见，不等于你必须说同意。
3. **给一次普通回合的空间。** 对方若还有与这次交接直接相关的一句提醒，听完并核关键事实；若开了全新话题，可以说“这个我现在接不上，之后若有需要再单独说”。对方不回应也不妨碍你结束，不需要再发三条“真的走了”。
4. **按说过的话退出。** 面对面从开放的普通路线离开，电话说完再挂，文字则停止当前回合的连续回复。留下的承诺只按你实际答应的范围处理；若需要以后再谈，双方可另约真实时间，不把无限在线当礼貌。

### 完成与停止

你已经让对方知道本轮谈话结束，真实待办有明确归属或被标为未定，随后实际离开/停止回复，本轮完成。**对方未回“再见”不代表你没完成，对方不高兴也不自动证明你做错。** 对话中若出现人身威胁、反复越界或需要专业支持的情形，退出本日常收口流程，使用相应安全/正式支持，不靠礼貌话术维持风险互动。

### 常见报错与补救

- **说“先这样”但有事尚待你发送：** 补一句你确实会做什么、什么时候；不能做到就改成待确认。
- **为了走掉说“改天一定见”，其实没有打算：** 改成真实的“今天先到这里”，不制造下一份空约定。
- **解释越说越长，谈话又重开：** 缩到一句结束信号和一项真实待办，然后按自己说的退出。
- **对方另起新话题：** 承认听到了，但可明确本轮不继续；不需要帮每个新话题找到终点才能离开。
- **文字消息被已读不回：** 你已经清楚收口就停止补发，不把回执当作需要再辩论的邀请。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于自愿、可退出的普通沟通，不包含危机、骚扰、工作岗位的强制交接或医疗/法律专业会谈。不同关系、文化与沟通媒介没有统一的“正确结束句”或眼神接触义务。听觉、视觉、语言或注意力条件不同，可用简短文字、手势/提示或双方可理解的复述，保留真实约定而不必模拟一种社交表演。副作用是本次聊天结束，下一次是否存在要由真实情况决定。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary in-person, phone or text conversation is winding down or you need to leave, but worry about vanishing or inventing endless reasons. Your result is to **signal that this round is ending, hand off any real next action and actually exit the interaction**. The other person need not approve, send the last reply or keep talking. Use the boundary task to decline a specific request; threats, harassment and crisis are outside this ordinary flow.

### Preparation and inputs

Recall whether this talk created a real commitment: perhaps you promised one ordinary piece of information, they suggested a time still to be confirmed, or you simply chatted. You do not need a fabricated emergency to justify ending. Know whether one real handoff remains. Where possible, let an important unfinished sentence reach a short pause. Sometimes you must clearly interrupt to leave, and a brief explanation is enough.

### Execution

1. **Give a recognisable closing signal.** Say “I need to end this chat or leave now. Thanks for talking today.” In text: “I am going offline; let's stop here for today.” A chain of reasons is not required to earn permission to leave.
2. **Carry forward only what actually exists.** For a real action, say “I will send __ by __” or “we still need to confirm __,” with owner and state. Without one, do not promise “I'll definitely contact you tomorrow” merely to sound friendly. Hearing a view does not require pretending to agree.
3. **Leave room for one ordinary handoff turn.** Hear a directly relevant reminder and confirm its key fact if needed. For a new topic, “I cannot take this up now; we can discuss it separately if needed” is enough. Silence does not block your exit, and you need not send three more “really going now” messages.
4. **Exit as stated.** Leave by the normal open route, end the phone call after your closing, or stop the current back-and-forth in text. Handle only commitments you actually made. For a later conversation, agree on a real time if useful rather than treating infinite availability as politeness.

### Success and stop

The other person knows this round ended, genuine open work has an owner or is marked undecided, and you have actually left or stopped replying. **No “goodbye” response is required; their unhappiness alone does not prove your end was wrong.** For threats, repeated boundary violations or specialist support needs, leave this everyday closing task for appropriate safety or formal support rather than staying in a risky exchange for politeness.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Let's leave it there” hides something you promised to send:** State what you truly can do and when. If you cannot, mark it unconfirmed.
- **You say “we must meet again” only to leave:** Use a truthful “I will stop here today” instead of inventing a future commitment.
- **Your explanation grows until the conversation restarts:** Return to one closing line and one real pending action, then follow through on leaving.
- **They start a new topic:** Acknowledge it but end this round. You need not solve every new topic before leaving.
- **A text is read but not answered:** If your closing was clear, stop sending follow-ups. A read receipt is not a fresh invitation to debate.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for voluntary ordinary interaction you can leave, not crisis, harassment, mandatory workplace handoff or medical or legal consultation. Relationship, culture and medium do not give one universal closing phrase or eye-contact duty. For hearing, sight, language or attention needs, use brief text, a gesture or mutually understood readback while preserving real commitments without staging a social performance. This round ends; another conversation exists only if someone actually chooses it.
