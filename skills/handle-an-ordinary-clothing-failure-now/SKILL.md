## 中文

当你即将出门或已在普通活动中，突然发现衣服**破了、扣不上、弄脏、湿透或不再合身**，却没有时间按正常流程修理时，用这份 Skill 决定换衣、用安全外层遮挡、改时间或退出。目标是让你能继续活动或诚实停止，而不是让临时补丁成为新事故。真正缝扣、修开线、处理拉链、去渍与试穿，分别留给对应任务。

### 准备与输入

先找能停下并有隐私的地方，确认哪里失效、还有多少时间、活动真实需要什么：基本遮蔽、可自由活动、天气适用及主办方明确要求。看看手边是否有自己的备用衣物、可用外层、可借且对方同意的衣物，以及能否通知需等你的人。防护装备、工作安全服、寒冷/恶劣天气关键保暖层或身体伤害不属普通外观补救；停止相关活动并找适用的安全替代或专业帮助。

### 执行

1. **先决定是否还能正常穿。** 检查破口/湿处会不会使基本遮蔽、走路坐下、抬手、呼吸或必要的天气防护失效；在私密处做实际动作检查，不只站着看镜子。失效会随动作扩大、露出不愿公开的部位，或影响安全时，不靠硬撑继续。
2. **按现实可用性选第一替代。** 有合身、符合活动要求的备用衣物就换；没有时看一个干净合适的外层能否自然遮住普通外观问题，同时保持活动和体温舒适。借衣前征得对方同意并确认能及时归还；不假定别人必须提供。
3. **不要为了赶时间制造危险修理。** 不用热熨斗、溶剂、不明胶或靠近皮肤的尖锐固定物现场补一个会受力的破口；也不把损坏的鞋底、防护衣或重要扣具视为可用外套掩盖。普通小外观缺点若你接受且不妨碍活动，可以如实穿着，不需向所有人解释。
4. **替代达不到要求就降级计划。** 改晚出发、换一个可参与的活动安排、提前告诉相关人你会迟到或不能参加；只说必要信息，不被迫公布身体或衣物细节。若已在现场，找安全、私密的退出路线或可信工作人员协助，不为了避免尴尬留在不适合的状态。
5. **事后再走正式处理。** 把失效衣物单独放好，记下需缝、去渍、洗干或换码的真实问题。下次可在出行前试穿与检查；这次没有时间，并不授权忽略衣物标签或未来维修边界。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你已有经实际动作检查、愿意穿且符合这次活动基本要求的衣物方案，或已明确改期/退出并通知必要的人；没有危险临时固定、未经同意借衣或让外观焦虑凌驾于行动安全。停止参加也可以是完整结果。

- **外层站着能遮，坐下就露出破口：** 回到私密处复查，换更稳妥的衣物或调整/退出活动。
- **备用衣物也湿或不合身：** 不把它当合格替代，选择其他可用方案或延后。
- **要迟到却怕解释原因：** 只告知新的到达估计或不能到场，衣物细节由你决定是否说。
- **活动需要特定安全/防护衣物：** 暂停该活动，使用经认可的替代；普通遮挡不能恢复防护功能。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通日常服装失效后的即时决定，不处理医疗、职业防护、极端天气或法律着装权利。文化、场所要求、身体能力和隐私需求各不相同；可以在可及洗手间或私人空间检查，用镜子、照片或可信且经你同意的人协助，但决定穿什么属于你。更换衣物或离场可能耽误安排，故用真实时间和必要通知管理后续。

## English

Use this Skill just before leaving or during an ordinary activity when clothing has **torn, stopped fastening, become stained or soaked, or no longer fits**, and there is no time for a proper repair. Choose a replacement, a safe outer cover, a changed time, or an exit. The aim is a workable activity or an honest stop, not a makeshift patch that creates another problem. Sewing a button or seam, dealing with a zip, stain removal, and fitting have their own tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Find a place to pause with privacy. Identify the failure, time left, and what this activity actually needs: basic coverage, free movement, weather suitability, and any stated host requirement. Check for your own spare, a useful outer layer, clothing someone explicitly agrees to lend, and a way to notify people waiting for you. Protective gear, safety-critical workwear, essential clothing for severe cold or weather, or bodily injury leave ordinary appearance recovery. Stop the affected activity and obtain an appropriate safe alternative or professional help.

### Execution

1. **Check whether the item is still wearable.** Test whether the tear or wet area compromises coverage, walking, sitting, reaching, breathing, or necessary weather protection. Try the actual movements in private rather than only standing in front of a mirror. Do not push through if the failure expands with movement, exposes an area you want covered, or affects safety.
2. **Choose the first real alternative.** Change into a fitting spare that meets this activity's actual needs if you have one. Otherwise test whether a clean suitable outer layer naturally covers an ordinary appearance issue while preserving movement and comfort. Ask before borrowing and agree on return; another person does not owe you clothing.
3. **Do not create a risky quick repair to save minutes.** Avoid hot irons, solvents, unknown glue, or sharp fasteners near skin to patch a stressed tear on the spot. An outer layer does not make a damaged shoe sole, protective garment, or critical closure functional. An ordinary minor appearance flaw may be worn as it is when you accept it and it does not interfere with the activity; you need not explain it to everyone.
4. **Downgrade the plan if no clothing option meets the need.** Leave later, choose a different feasible participation plan, or tell affected people you will be late or absent. Share only necessary information; you do not owe body or clothing details. If already on site, use a safe private exit route or trusted staff help rather than staying in a condition that does not work for you.
5. **Handle the garment properly afterward.** Set the failed item aside and note whether it needs sewing, stain removal, washing and drying, or a different size. Check and try on clothes before a later departure where possible. Running short today does not override care labels or the boundary of a later repair.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have an outfit you have movement-tested, accept wearing, and that meets this activity's basic needs, or you have explicitly delayed or left and notified necessary people. No hazardous quick fastening, unconsented borrowing, or appearance anxiety has overridden safe movement. Leaving the event can be a complete result.

- **A layer covers the tear standing but not sitting:** Return to privacy, retest, change to a more reliable garment, or alter or leave the activity.
- **The spare is also wet or does not fit:** Do not count it as a solution; use another real option or delay.
- **You may be late but do not want to explain why:** Give the new arrival estimate or say you cannot attend; you decide whether to share clothing details.
- **The activity needs specific protective clothing:** Pause it and use an approved substitute. Ordinary concealment cannot restore protective function.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an immediate choice for ordinary everyday clothing, not medical care, occupational protection, extreme weather, or dress-code legal rights. Culture, venue, mobility, and privacy vary. Check in an accessible bathroom or private place; use a mirror, photo, or trusted help with your consent. You decide what to wear. Changing or leaving can delay plans, so use a real time estimate and necessary notice.
