## 中文

当你想在熟悉城市里散散步，却不想走到不知如何返回的远处时，用这份 Skill 选并走一段**公开、短时、容易退出的公园路线**。结果是你按自己的时间和通行条件到一个预定转返点，再沿可行的出口离开；缩短路线或现场停下也可以是正确决定。自然步道难度选择、恶劣天气改期与长途探索归其他任务。

### 准备与输入

先写你实际可用的时间、愿意走的路面/坡度、需要的座椅或厕所/无障碍入口，以及最晚返回时点；这不是运动处方，不设步数或速度。查看公园管理方当日开放范围、入口、地图和临时封闭，最好选可见路标且靠近城市交通或原入口的短路。厕所、饮水和座椅可能有独立开放时间，不能因地图有图标就当一定可用。按普通天气穿合适衣物并带自需的水/联系设备；遇到危险天气、显著封路或身体不适时不开始。本篇不进入水边活动、陡坡攀爬或野外独走。

### 执行

1. **画一个短往返或小环。** 在官方/现场图上标起点、一个可辨认的转返点、返回出口；优先选择公共维护路径，别把看不清的社交小径当正式路线。确定“走到____就转身/沿____出口走”，无需追求整园覆盖。
2. **核算退出余量。** 从总可用时间扣到达、休息、回程和离园所需的余量。如果回程或通行条件不明确，就缩到更近的转返点。想用厕所或座椅时查其当日可用状态，并准备不依赖它的备选退出。
3. **在入口对照现实。** 看开园告示、路面、坡道/台阶与人车共用情况是否符合你的条件；地图和现场不同以现场封闭与工作人员指示为准。通行不适合就改另一条明确开放路径或取消，不穿越围栏。
4. **按路标走并检查返程。** 沿所选公共路径前进，到预定点或用时边界即返回。每次岔路先确认下一段和出口；若失去方向，回到最近的已确认标识或向公园工作人员求助，不靠猜去更远处“碰碰运气”。不要边走边盯屏幕跨路。
5. **在实际出口结束。** 走回原入口或另一处预先确认的出口，核时间和交通/同行安排。记一句“今天走了____到____；下次可调____”，不把未走完的地图全线说成已完成。若中途因环境或能力改变而停止，在可安全退出的地点结束并记录原因。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你选出符合自身时间和可及条件的**起点—转返点—出口**，并实际按开放路径走到预定或缩短的结束点，知道从哪里离开。强行走满预想距离不是成功条件。

- **地图标了厕所但现场关闭：** 用已知出口结束或调整路线，别为找厕所进入不开放区域。
- **路径比想象的坡/碎石多：** 回到适合的维护路径或缩短，别以“就这一段”强行通过。
- **路口太多开始不确定：** 回到最后一个确认过的路标，查现场图或问工作人员，再决定继续/退出。
- **公园临时关闭或天气变化：** 按公告离开或改室内休闲；复杂天气决策另做，不在本篇冒险续行。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

城市公园开放时段、路面、设施和无障碍信息随管理方、季节和维护变化；示例公园的路线不适用于你所在城市。行动、视觉、听觉或感官负担不同时可选更短的平整路线、固定座椅周围的小段、可信同伴或工作人员指引，但仍要核真实入口和出口。本文不提供体能训练、医学判断、野外导航或紧急救援指导。散步的实际体验由你决定。

## English

Use this Skill when you want an ordinary walk in a city park without going so far that the way back becomes unclear. Choose and follow a **public, short, easy-to-exit park route**. The result is reaching a planned turnaround point within your own time and access limits, then leaving by a feasible exit. Shortening or stopping on site can be the right decision. Trail difficulty, severe-weather changes, and longer exploration are separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Write your real time available, suitable surface and slope, any need for a bench, restroom or accessible entrance, and latest return time. This is not an exercise prescription and sets no step count or pace. Check the park manager's current open areas, entrances, map, and temporary closures. Prefer a short marked route near transit or your original entrance. Toilets, water, and seating may have separate hours; an icon is not a guarantee. Dress for ordinary conditions and carry water or a contact device as you need. Do not start during hazardous weather, substantial closures, or personal discomfort. Water activities, steep scrambling, and remote solo trails are outside this Skill.

### Execution

1. **Mark a short out-and-back or small loop.** On an official or on-site map, identify start, recognisable turnaround, and exit. Prefer maintained public paths; an unclear informal track is not a formal route. Write “at ____ I turn / follow ____ exit.” Covering the whole park is unnecessary.
2. **Leave time to exit.** Subtract arrival, break, return, and departure from your total time. If return time or path condition is uncertain, move the turnaround closer. Check current restroom or seat availability if you depend on it and know an exit that does not rely on it.
3. **Compare map with the entrance.** Read opening notices, actual surface, ramps or steps, and shared pedestrian/cycle conditions. Follow real closures and staff directions when a map differs. Choose another clearly open route or cancel if the surface does not fit; do not cross a barrier.
4. **Follow markers and check the return.** Walk the chosen public path and turn at the planned point or time boundary. At each fork, confirm the next segment and exit. If unsure, return to the last confirmed marker or ask park staff rather than walking farther on a guess. Do not stare at a screen while crossing a path or road.
5. **Finish at a real exit.** Return to the entry or another exit you confirmed in advance, checking time and transit or companion arrangements. Note “today I walked ____ to ____; next time I could change ____.” Do not claim you covered a full map route you did not walk. If environment or capacity changes mid-way, end at a safe exit and record why.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You chose a **start—turnaround—exit** that fits your time and access needs and actually followed open paths to the planned or shortened end, knowing how to leave. Forcing the originally imagined distance is not a requirement.

- **A mapped restroom is closed:** Use the known exit or shorten the walk rather than enter restricted space searching for another.
- **A path is steeper or rougher than expected:** Return to a suitable maintained route or shorten rather than force one more section.
- **Too many forks make the way unclear:** Return to the last confirmed sign, check the on-site map, or ask staff, then continue or exit.
- **Park closes or weather changes:** Follow notices and leave or switch to indoor leisure. Complex weather decisions belong elsewhere; do not push on here.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Park hours, surface, amenities, and access information vary by manager, season, and maintenance; example parks do not set your city's route. For mobility, sight, hearing, or sensory needs, choose a shorter smooth path, a small section around a permitted seat, a trusted companion, or staff guidance while still verifying start and exit. This Skill does not prescribe fitness training, diagnose health, navigate wilderness, or teach emergency rescue. You judge the walk's experience.
