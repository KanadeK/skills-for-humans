## 中文

当现场戏剧、音乐会等**已经开场**，你才到达或中途必须离席后想再入场时，用这份 Skill 向前台确认迟到/再入场规则，选择等待、在允许时由人引座，或按规则退出。结果不是“我有票所以立刻进去”，而是你在不打断演出与观众的前提下得到一个实际可执行的入场决定。普通按时凭票入座是另一流程。

### 准备与输入

先确认票面确属这个日期、剧场和场次，找到正式入口或前台，不跟着正在入场的人钻进暗场。现场规则可能规定只在某段落、休息或完全不允许迟到入座；票面、官网与工作人员最新说明优先。准备说明你是刚到还是临时离席、原座位在哪里，以及你能等到何时。行动、听觉或视觉支持需要时直接告诉工作人员，别自行摸黑走楼梯。此处不处理票价退换争议。

### 执行

1. **停在允许的等候处。** 找前台/引座员出示自己的票和场次，问“现在能入座吗？如果不能，下一次允许的入座时点或其他选择是什么？”保持通道畅通，不在厅门口开手机灯找座。
2. **确认规则与可及安排。** 听清是等候一段、在指定休息时间进、由工作人员引座到临时位置，还是本场无法再入。座位可能与原票不同，接受前问是否仍适合你的行动/感官需要；不同意就继续等或退出，不自行挑空座。
3. **在允许时跟随引座。** 把手机、票和物品准备好但避免屏幕强光与交谈，按工作人员路线与时机进去；若指示先等就等，绝不自行推门或穿过观众排。坐下后遵守本场拍摄、手机和离席规则。
4. **无法入座时作真实选择。** 若等待超过你的可用时间或场馆拒绝当场再入，可在允许区域等、离开，或向票务柜台询问实际可用安排；不要在这里保证改签/退款。把“错过开头/未入座”作为真实结果，不把站在门口听到声音当完成观看。
5. **确认离场路径。** 如果选择退出，按前台指引离开，别逆向穿过演出空间；若已入座但必须再走，再问能否重进，不假定上次放行会重复。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你得到工作人员或馆方规则确认的**允许入座时间/位置，或明确不能入座的替代决定**，并遵守它。迟到本身不使票自动失效，但也不保证随时可进；实际票务结果依场馆。

- **票的日期/场地搞错：** 停在前台核验，按正式票务渠道处理，不进错厅。
- **工作人员说“等下一个间隙”：** 问大致在哪里等、如何被叫到；别把等待误解为立刻入座许可。
- **原座位无法再用或需要可及座：** 让引座员提供正式安排；不与已入座观众换位。
- **听不清指示、现场很暗：** 在明亮允许区域请工作人员重复或书写关键指引，不自行摸黑前行。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

此流程只适用于某些现场演出的迟到/中途再入；电影院、放映活动和宽松场次可能规则不同，应按其实际场馆说明。正式前台有决定权，票面并非打断演出的许可。你可能错过节目片段或需改日，记录事实即可；费用、退款与法律责任不在本篇承诺范围。需要无障碍路径时不必为了减少打扰而冒险。

## English

Use this Skill when a live play or concert **has already started** before you arrive, or you need to re-enter after leaving mid-show. Check late or re-entry rules with front-of-house staff and choose to wait, be seated when permitted, or leave under venue rules. A ticket does not mean “walk in now.” The result is an actual permitted admission decision that does not interrupt the show or audience. Normal on-time seating is another workflow.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm your ticket is for this date, venue, and performance. Find the formal entrance or front-of-house desk instead of slipping after other patrons into a dark auditorium. Rules may allow seating only at a scene break, interval, or not at all. Current ticket, venue notice, and staff directions govern. Be ready to say whether you just arrived or stepped out, where your original seat is, and how long you can wait. Tell staff about mobility, hearing, or vision support needs rather than feeling your way up dark stairs. Ticket price and refund disputes are outside this task.

### Execution

1. **Stop in an allowed waiting area.** Show your own ticket to an usher or front-of-house member. Ask, “May I be seated now? If not, when and where may I wait, and what are my alternatives?” Keep passageways clear; do not light up the door with your phone to hunt for a seat.
2. **Confirm rules and access arrangements.** Learn whether you must wait, enter at an interval, accept a staff-directed temporary seat, or cannot enter this show. A seat may differ from the one printed. Ask whether it meets your mobility or sensory needs before accepting. You may wait or leave rather than choose an unapproved empty seat.
3. **Follow the usher when admitted.** Prepare ticket and belongings without bright screens or conversation. Use the directed route and timing. If told to wait, wait; do not push a door or cross a row yourself. Once seated, follow this show's phone, recording, and exit rules.
4. **Make an honest choice if seating is unavailable.** If the wait exceeds your time or the venue denies re-entry, wait in an allowed place, leave, or ask the box office about actual options. This Skill promises no exchange or refund. “Missed the beginning” or “not seated” is a real result; hearing sound from the doorway is not attending from a seat.
5. **Confirm your exit route.** If leaving, follow staff directions rather than crossing the performance space. If you were seated and need to leave again, ask whether re-entry remains possible; one admission does not promise another.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have a staff- or venue-confirmed **permitted seating time and place or a clear alternative when seating is impossible**, and you follow that decision. Late arrival does not by itself prove a ticket invalid, but it also never guarantees immediate entry. The actual ticket outcome depends on the venue.

- **Wrong date or venue:** Stop at the desk for validation and use formal ticketing channels rather than enter another show.
- **Staff says “next suitable break”:** Ask where to wait and how you will be called. Waiting is not immediate permission to enter.
- **Original seat is unavailable or you need an accessible one:** Ask the usher for an authorised arrangement; do not trade seats with someone already watching.
- **Instructions are inaudible or the space is dark:** Ask staff in a lit allowed area to repeat or write key directions. Do not move blindly through the auditorium.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for late or repeated entry to certain live performances. Cinema screenings and relaxed shows may differ; use the actual venue's rules. Front-of-house staff control admission, and a ticket is not permission to interrupt a show. You may miss a segment or need another day. This Skill does not promise fee, refund, or legal outcomes. Do not endanger your access route merely to avoid disturbing others.
