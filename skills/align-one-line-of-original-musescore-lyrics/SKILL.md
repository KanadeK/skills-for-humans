## 中文

你为自己的短旋律写了一句歌词，想让读谱者看清每个字或音节在哪个音发出时，逐音录入，而不是把整句作为普通文本压在谱下。成果是一行歌词与目标音符对齐，跨音节或拖长处表达清楚。歌词完全由你原创；对别人的唱词改几个字不算授权。

### 准备与输入

保存谱面，先把歌词按这段旋律的音数划分字/音节，标明连字符和需要一个音节唱多个音的位置。确认所选乐器或声部是实际要唱的旋律；如果字数与音数根本不合，先改创作计划，不用排版掩盖。

### 执行

1. 选歌词起始音，进入 Lyrics 输入，录入第一个字或音节。
2. 按音节逐步前进，使用空格、连字符或延长线表达下一个字、拆词或拖腔，不一次粘贴整句。
3. 退出输入模式，对着谱面逐音朗读歌词与音高，核字没有跳到休止或下一乐句。
4. 正常观看尺寸核文字不碰连奏线或下一行，保存重开；若字音对应错，改该音节的推进位置。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

自写歌词一行按计划逐音对齐，读谱可顺唱，错字与错位已排除。

- **整句挤一个音：** 撤销，按字音逐步输入。
- **拖腔没表示：** 在相应音节后用延长线，并核终点。
- **文字撞记号：** 先核锚点，再做小范围布局调整。

### 假设与边界

只处理一行原创歌词，不翻译、校对语言押韵、授权他人文本或声乐可唱性。

## English

Use this when a self-written lyric line needs to align to your original melody, note by note, rather than sitting below the staff as plain text. Finish with readable syllable-to-note mapping and clear treatment of split words or a held vowel. The lyric must be your own or explicitly permitted; small edits to another writer's words are not permission.

### Preparation and inputs

Save the score and divide the lyric by syllables against the melody's note count, marking split words and any one-syllable passage spanning several notes. Select the vocal melody staff. If the mapping itself fails, revise the creative plan before forcing layout.

### Execution

1. Select the first sung note, enter Lyrics mode and type the first syllable.
2. Advance syllable by syllable, using space, hyphen or melisma extension as needed rather than pasting the whole sentence as one block.
3. Exit input mode and read the lyric along each note, checking no syllable landed on a rest or next phrase.
4. Inspect at normal size for collisions with slurs or the next system, save and reopen; fix any wrong syllable advance at its source.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One original lyric line aligns note by note with the intended melody and can be sung from the score without displaced syllables.

- **Sentence on one note:** Undo and enter syllables one at a time.
- **Melisma missing:** Add an extension after the held syllable and inspect its end.
- **Text collision:** Confirm lyric anchoring before local spacing changes.

### Assumptions and limits

This aligns one original lyric line. It does not translate, assess rhyme, license another person's text or certify vocal singability.
