## 中文

当你在普通家用厨房发现**生肉、禽或海鲜及其汁液直接碰过台面**，而之后要处理熟食或即食食物时，用这份 Skill 先限制接触范围，清洁可见污物，再按台面和适用产品说明完成进一步处理。它不同于餐后的普通碎屑擦拭；看不见汁液不等于没有接触。若是污水、体液、危险化学品或大面积无法接近的污染，停止普通家用流程并寻求对应专业指导。

### 准备与输入

确认生食何时、在哪块台面、哪些边沿或近旁接触点可能被手/包装/汁液碰过。把即食食物、干净器具移离范围；若它们已直接接触生食汁液，不继续供应，按该食品的安全处置指导判断，不靠擦台面补救。读台面材质说明与拟用产品**是否允许食品接触面使用**、接触时长、通风、需否再用清水去残留、干燥和手部防护。没有合适产品或说明时，先停止在这块区域制作即食食物，不凭自配比例启动。

### 执行

1. **隔离并洗手。** 暂停即食备餐，清楚划出被污染的台面与触碰区，处理生食/包装后按适用指导洗手；生食用过的刀、砧板和水槽走各自清洁路径，不用同一块布把汁液拖到别处。
2. **先除可见污物。** 用适用的一次性材料或专用清洁布从边界向受污区收集可见汁液和残渣，按当地规则丢弃/待洗；避免强力喷洒使液体扩散。脏布不回到干净食物区，台面缝隙无法接近或材质损坏时先停求助。
3. **先清洁，再按许可处理。** 按台面材质用适用肥皂/洗剂和水清洗整个实际接触范围，按产品要求去除洗剂残留。只有在已完成清洁后，才按**适用于该食品接触面**的现成产品或所在地权威认可的明确说明进行消毒/杀菌步骤；照该产品标签的用量、覆盖、接触时间、通风、清水去残留与干燥要求执行，不自创全球通用漂白剂配比。
4. **禁止混配和越界。** 漂白剂、酸、氨和其他清洁剂不能混在一起；没有与台面和用途相容的产品时继续暂停即食备餐，查制造商/当地食品安全机构说明或改用另一个已核实干净的工作区。若出现刺激性气味、皮肤/眼不适或异常反应，离开暴露区并按产品和当地紧急指导求助。
5. **检查再恢复。** 台面无可见残渣、用过的布/纸已移出、产品标签规定的接触及去残留/干燥步骤已完成，才把新的干净器具与食物放回。这个结果是按适用说明完成处理，不宣称肉眼能验证微生物为零。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

生食接触范围与即食区已分开，台面先去污再按相容产品/当地权威说明处理，使用过的工具和受影响食物有独立去向；缺少材料或无法清理时，明确暂停即食用途也是完整结果。

- **只喷产品但台面上还有生食渣：** 停止并先按材质清洁，脏污会妨碍后续处理。
- **产品没有写可用于食品接触面：** 不把“家用消毒”四个字当许可，换合适产品或改用已核实的工作区。
- **刀和砧板也沾了汁：** 不因台面复位就宣布器具可用，分别走刀/砧板安全清洁流程。
- **台面接缝、开裂区渗入汁液：** 暂停这块区域供即食备餐，向台面制造商/专业人员询问可清洁性。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇面向普通家庭可识别台面的生动物性食品接触，不处理食物中毒诊断、商业厨房法规、管道/电器维修或危险污染。产品配方、台面材料与地方指导会改变步骤；只按手中准确标签/权威说明执行。视觉或行动不便时用明亮标识、可及清洁区和可信协助，避免让未处理区域与已处理区域再次交叉。即食备餐可能延后，材料不足时应诚实停用该区域。

## English

Use this Skill when **raw meat, poultry, or seafood or its juices directly touched a household kitchen counter** and you next need to handle cooked or ready-to-eat food. Isolate the contact area, remove visible soil, then use a further route supported by the surface and product. This is different from wiping ordinary crumbs after a meal. An invisible juice trail can still be a contact. Stop ordinary home cleaning and seek relevant professional guidance for sewage, bodily fluids, hazardous chemicals, or widespread inaccessible contamination.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify when and where the raw food, packaging, hands, or juice touched the counter and any edge or nearby contact point. Move ready-to-eat food and clean tools away. If they directly contacted raw juices, do not serve them; use that food's safety guidance rather than treating a wiped counter as a rescue. Read the surface care and a proposed product's **food-contact-surface permission**, contact time, ventilation, post-use rinse, drying, and hand-protection directions. Without an applicable product or clear directions, pause ready-to-eat preparation on this surface rather than inventing a dilution ratio.

### Execution

1. **Isolate and wash hands.** Pause ready-food preparation, mark the affected counter and touch area, and wash hands under applicable guidance after raw food or packaging. Knives, boards, and sink touched by raw food have their own cleaning routes. One cloth must not drag juice into a clean zone.
2. **Remove visible soil first.** Use suitable disposable material or a dedicated washable cloth to collect visible juice and debris from the boundary toward the soiled area. Discard or wash it under local rules; do not spray forcefully and spread liquid. Do not return the dirty cloth to ready food. Stop for inaccessible contaminated seams or a damaged surface.
3. **Clean first, then use a permitted further step.** Wash the actual contact area with surface-compatible soap or detergent and water; remove cleaner residue where directed. Only after cleaning, use a ready product **labelled for this food-contact surface** or a clear locally authorised sanitising method. Follow that product's amount, coverage, contact time, ventilation, rinse, and drying directions. Do not invent one worldwide bleach ratio.
4. **Do not mix or exceed the scope.** Never combine bleach, acid, ammonia, or other cleaners. Without a product suitable for this surface and use, keep ready-food prep paused, seek surface-maker or local food authority directions, or use another verified clean work area. For irritating fumes, skin or eye effects, or an unexpected reaction, leave exposure and use product and local emergency guidance.
5. **Check before resuming.** Return new clean tools and food only once visible soil is gone, used cloth or paper is removed, and the product's labelled contact, rinse, and dry steps are complete. This records a process under applicable guidance; your eyes cannot certify zero microbes.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Raw-contact and ready-food areas were separated, the counter was cleaned before a compatible labelled or local-authority treatment, and used tools and affected food have separate destinations. Stopping ready-food use when the method or material is missing is also a complete outcome.

- **A spray was used while raw residue remains:** Stop and clean under surface care first; soil obstructs the later step.
- **The product never says food-contact surface:** “Household disinfectant” alone is not permission. Find a suitable product or work on another verified clean area.
- **A knife and board also took raw juice:** The counter result does not clear those tools. Use their own safety-cleaning route.
- **Juice entered a seam or crack:** Pause ready-food preparation there and ask the counter maker or a professional about cleanability.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary household raw-animal-food contact on an identifiable counter, not diagnosis of food illness, commercial-kitchen regulation, plumbing or appliance repair, or hazardous cleanup. Products, surfaces, and jurisdictions change the method; follow the actual label and authority. Use good light, an accessible work area, or trusted help when vision or movement is limited, without recrossing processed and unprocessed areas. Preparation may be delayed, and without materials this counter stays out of ready-food service.
