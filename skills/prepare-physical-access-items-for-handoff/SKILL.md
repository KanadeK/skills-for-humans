## 中文

当你将离开旧住处，需要向有权接收的人交还实体钥匙、门禁卡、遥控器或类似物时加载。你要**逐件核对实际持有物、用途、交接对象和结果**，并把缺件或尚需使用的物件如实标出。这里不指导复制钥匙、读取/更改密码、修改锁具、判断合同义务或归责遗失。

### 准备与输入

先从实际接收方的说明确认由谁收、在哪里、何时、如何交；不同住处和地区不一样。与各持有人一起找出住处、邮箱、储物处、停车处等实际发放或临时借用的实体物件；只记用途和数量，别把门禁码或可复用的敏感标识写进公开清单。

### 执行

1. **按持有人摊开清点。** 在有权人可见的地方逐件列“用途/位置 → 物件类型 → 当前持有人 → 状态”。同一用途有多把也数实物；别人持有的先请求对方确认，不写成自己手中已有。
2. **与接收方要求核对。** 查看实际交付清单或约定，标出需归还、明确允许另处处理、目前找不到、仍需最后一次进出的物件。对未知项问接收方；不要靠一份外国范本推定所有钥匙都要以相同方式交。
3. **安排最后进出与交付顺序。** 保证有权的人在离开前仍能完成必要进出，并说清最后一把由谁持有和何时交。不要把钥匙随普通搬家箱交运或无记录地留在门边；按接收方确认的安全方式送达。
4. **当面或按约定交付并回读。** 对有权接收者逐件对数量与用途，在纸质或双方接受的记录里写实际时间、地点/方式、接收人及收到的数量。对方只确认部分时，未收到的仍标未交，不把预计交付算完成。
5. **处理缺件和争议事实。** 找不到物件、数量不合或交付方式变动时，记录最后已知持有人和查找结果，及时问实际接收方下一步；不擅自复制、停用、编程门禁或承诺费用结论。

### 完成与停止

每件已知实体访问物都有实际状态：已交并可核对接收、待最后进出、缺件待问或由接收方明确另行处理。接收人不明、交付方法未经确认或尚有未完成的合法进出需求时，不把整串钥匙写成“已全部交接”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一把钥匙不知开哪儿：** 不在公共场所逐门试锁；问发放者或有权接收方，将用途先标未知。
- **室友还持有一张卡：** 记实际持有人和约定归还点，等对方确认，不能以自己交出钥匙圈代替其交付。
- **临走前还需一次开门：** 指定最后持有人与明确交付时间，不提前交到无法再取回的地方。
- **交付地点无人接收：** 按对方现行说明联系并记录未交状态；不把物件塞进未经确认的信箱或门缝。
- **交付后清单数量与对方不同：** 当场逐件复点、保留双方记录，未解决项明确待核，不把分歧改成一致。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只管理普通实体访问物的事实交接，不处理数字账号、秘密代码、押金或法律责任。实际场所对卡、遥控器、复制件和归还凭据有自己的流程。看不清小物或难以到场时，可请双方认可的可信代理核对，使用大字或语音清单；交接人的授权仍要按当地实际规则确认。副作用是一个钥匙圈不再被当作准确的资产数据库。

## English

Load this Skill when you are leaving an old place and physical keys, access cards, remotes or similar items must go to an authorised receiver. You will **account for each item, its purpose, real recipient and outcome**, while leaving missing or still-needed items explicit. This does not teach copying keys, reading or changing codes, altering locks, interpreting a contract or assigning blame for a loss.

### Preparation and inputs

Ask the real recipient who accepts items, where, when and by which method. Places and regions differ. With other holders, find physical items issued or temporarily borrowed for the home, mailbox, storage or parking areas. Record purposes and counts only; do not publish access codes or reusable sensitive identifiers on a shared list.

### Execution

1. **Count items by current holder.** In a space visible to authorised people, list “purpose/place → item type → current holder → state.” Count actual copies even if several share a purpose. Ask another holder to confirm their item; do not write it as already in your hand.
2. **Compare with this receiver's request.** Read the actual return list or agreement and mark items to return, those explicitly handled another way, missing items and items still needed for final access. Ask the receiver about unknowns; a foreign template does not establish one return method for every key.
3. **Plan final access and handoff order.** Keep a lawful way for authorised people to make the last necessary exit, naming who holds the final item and when it will be returned. Do not ship keys in an ordinary moving carton or leave them unrecorded by a door; use the recipient's confirmed secure route.
4. **Hand over and read back.** With the authorised receiver, match purposes and counts. On paper or another method both sides accept, record actual time, place or channel, receiver and number received. If only part is accepted, mark the rest unreturned; expected delivery is not completion.
5. **Record missing or disputed facts.** If an item cannot be found, counts differ or the method changes, note the last known holder and search result and ask the real recipient for next steps. Do not copy, disable or program access equipment, or promise a cost outcome.

### Success and stop

Each known physical access item has an actual state: received with a checkable handoff, held for final access, missing pending an answer, or explicitly handled another way by the receiver. If you do not know who receives it, the method is unconfirmed or lawful final access is still needed, do not mark the entire set “returned.”

### Common errors and recovery

- **A key's use is unknown:** Do not test it on random public doors. Ask its issuer or authorised receiver and mark the purpose unknown meanwhile.
- **A housemate still has one card:** Record its actual holder and agreed return point, then wait for confirmation. Your own key ring cannot count as their handoff.
- **One last entry is still needed:** Name the last holder and return time rather than leaving the key somewhere inaccessible too early.
- **Nobody is at the return point:** Contact the receiver under its current instructions and keep the state unreturned. Do not put the item through an unapproved letterbox or door gap.
- **The receiver's count differs after handoff:** Recount together and preserve both records. Leave unresolved items under review instead of rewriting disagreement as agreement.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a factual handoff of ordinary physical access items, not digital-account, secret-code, deposit or legal advice. The actual site sets the process for cards, remotes, copies and receipt records. If small items are hard to see or travel is difficult, use a trusted proxy accepted by both sides and a large-print or audio list; confirm that person's authority under the real local process. The side effect is that a key ring is no longer mistaken for an accurate asset database.
