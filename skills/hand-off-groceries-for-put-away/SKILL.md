## 中文

当你把普通食品买回家，但接下来由另一个人归置时，加载这份 Skill。它让对方知道**哪些要先处理、商品要求什么、实际经过多久、谁现在负责**。具体放进冰箱哪个位置由[买菜回家后收纳](../put-away-groceries/SKILL.md)处理；发送一句“东西到了”或把袋放在门口，不算完成交接。

### 准备与输入

看一眼所有食品和包装，找出标签要求冷藏/冷冻的、即食的和可能渗漏的生肉海鲜。保留可读的商品标签、购买/到家时间和你知道的运输保冷情况；不知道的地方标“未知”。确认下一位处理者是否真的在场、能否及时接手，以及家中有没有可靠的合适储存位置。看不清小字时放大照片或请人读出；需要协助搬动时先安排真实可用的帮助，不默认接收人能提重物。

### 执行

1. **先找需要马上控制温度的食品。** 按手中标签和当地食品安全指导处理冷藏、冷冻等易腐物；不要等干货点清、收据拍完才说冷食在哪里。若接收人尚未在场，由当前持有人先确保食品处在符合要求的条件，或找确实能立即接手的人。购物袋、门口和一条未读消息都不是可靠冷藏设备。
2. **做一次异常与分隔检查。** 包装渗漏、破损、解冻迹象或时间/温度史不明的食品单独指出，不把它口头升级成“安全没问题”。生食可能滴漏时与即食食品分开；条件无法确认的部分停止按普通食品交接，依标签和当地指导保守处理。
3. **把关键事实压成一张短交接单。** 至少写或说：品名/数量、具体包装上的保存要求、何时买到和到家、现在放在哪里、包装或保冷异常、下一步谁先处理。不要把未核实的时刻、标签或温度补成整齐的猜测。
4. **指定并确认接手。** 向一位确定的人明确说“这几样需要先按标签放好；其余在这里；这包曾有/没有已知异常”，给他看包装或清晰照片，请其复述先处理的物品或直接接过执行。对方明确接手后，你再退出；没有回复的短信、群里一句话或“他应该看到了”仍是交接待定。
5. **接收人不在场时先保安全，再留信息。** 当前持有人能按标签可靠归置的，先完成必要的低温处理并记录具体位置与时间，再给下一位留下可找到的说明。不能安全归置也没人能立即接管时，不把易腐食品无人看管地留作以后；按当地食品安全指导决定下一步，明确记录“未完成交接”。
6. **逐项关单。** 每件需要及时处理的食品都有符合条件的当前状态和一位知道自己责任的人；一般常温食品也有明确位置或待处理说明。若有争议或未知，把它单独标出并停止承诺以后食用；商家漏货和退款另走售后流程。

### 完成条件

下一位处理者明确接管了需及时处理的食品和关键信息，或在其未到场时食品已被当前持有人可靠处理且交接状态标为**待确认**。冷食没有因为“责任已经转过去”而仍留在室温袋里；每个未知都仍然是未知。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只发了一条消息，对方没有回复：** 继续由当前持有人负责必要的保存或安排已确认的替代接手；不要把发送时间当接收时间。
- **不知道食品买后多久、是否一直保冷：** 不编一个时间线。把该项隔离，查可得记录、包装和当地官方指导；无法建立安全条件时不要标成“明天可以吃”。
- **生肉袋渗漏或冷冻品明显解冻：** 先与其他食品分开，说明实际异常；不要让下一位只收到“都放冰箱”四个字。能否保留按具体食品和当地指导决定。
- **接收人看不清标签或不能搬重物：** 提供大字照片、语音读出或可信协助；必要处理无人能安全完成时明确停止，不用含糊责任转移代替行动。
- **交接单写“尽快处理”却没写哪样：** 补上品名、位置和标签要求，让接收者能立刻找到并执行。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 只处理普通家庭的食品信息与责任交接，不做过敏处置、医疗判断、罐藏/发酵或商家配送投诉。食品标签、地区安全规则和家庭分工随情况而变；具体要求以手中包装和所在地权威为准。实时口头交接、电话、清晰照片或文字都可使用，但接手是否发生要靠对方确认及食品实际状态。副作用是一张短记录；它比冰箱门口的集体失忆便宜。

## English

Load this Skill when you bring ordinary groceries home but someone else will put them away. Give that person **the urgent items, this product's requirements, the real time passed, and the current owner of the task**. [Put Away Groceries](../put-away-groceries/SKILL.md) covers the detailed placement. Saying “the bags are here” or leaving them at a doorway is not a handoff.

### Preparation and inputs

Look over the food and labels. Identify items marked for chilling or freezing, ready-to-eat food, and raw meat or seafood that could leak. Keep the readable label, purchase and arrival times, and what you actually know about cooling during transport. Mark anything else “unknown.” Confirm that the next handler is present and can act promptly, with suitable reliable storage available. Enlarge a label photo or ask someone to read it if necessary; arrange real help for heavy items rather than assuming the receiver can lift them.

### Execution

1. **Find food whose temperature needs attention now.** Follow the labels and local food-safety guidance for chilled, frozen, and other perishables. Do not finish counting tins or photographing receipts before identifying the cold food. If the receiver is absent, the current holder must first keep the food under its required conditions or find someone who can take it immediately. A shopping bag, doorstep, and unread message are not refrigeration equipment.
2. **Check exceptions and separation.** Point out leaks, damage, signs of thawing, or unknown time and temperature history. Do not describe an uncertain item as “definitely safe.” Keep leaking raw food away from ready-to-eat food. Stop the ordinary handoff for any item whose conditions cannot be established and handle it conservatively under its label and local guidance.
3. **Make one short handoff note.** Write or say at least: item and amount, storage instruction on the actual package, when it was bought and arrived, where it is now, any package or cooling exception, and who must act next. Do not complete an unknown clock, label, or temperature with a tidy guess.
4. **Name and confirm the new handler.** Tell one specific person which items must be stored first under their labels and where the rest are. Show the packages or clear photos; ask them to repeat the priority or take the items and act. Step out only after they explicitly accept. A sent text, a group message, or “they probably saw it” leaves the handoff pending.
5. **If the receiver is away, make the food safe before leaving a note.** If you can store the urgent food reliably as labelled, do so and record the real location and time before sending the next person a findable note. If you cannot and no one can take over now, do not leave perishables unattended for later; follow local food-safety guidance and mark the handoff incomplete.
6. **Account for every item.** Each food needing prompt action has a condition it can safely remain in and a person who knows they are responsible. Ordinary ambient goods have a clear place or pending instruction too. Isolate a dispute or unknown instead of promising later use. Missing goods and refunds belong to a separate seller process.

### Success

The next handler has explicitly accepted the time-sensitive food and its critical information. If they are absent, the current holder has kept the food under reliable conditions and marked the handoff **pending**. Cold food does not stay in an ambient bag just because someone believes responsibility transferred. Unknowns stay unknown.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You sent a message and got no answer:** The current holder keeps responsibility for necessary storage or arranges a confirmed alternative. The send timestamp is not the receipt timestamp.
- **Purchase time or cooling history is unclear:** Do not invent a timeline. Isolate the item and check available records, packaging, and local official guidance. If safe conditions cannot be established, do not mark it “fine for tomorrow.”
- **Raw-food packaging leaks or frozen food has clearly thawed:** Separate it and pass the actual exception, not merely “put it all in the fridge.” Whether it can be kept depends on the food and local guidance.
- **The receiver cannot read the label or lift the load:** Offer an enlarged photo, spoken directions, or trusted assistance. If no one can complete a necessary action safely, stop explicitly rather than transferring a vague responsibility.
- **The note says only “handle soon”:** Add the item, location, and label requirement so the receiver can find and act on it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary household food and responsibility handoff, not allergy care, medical assessment, canning, fermentation, or a delivery complaint. Labels, local rules, and household roles vary. Follow the package in hand and the relevant authority. A live conversation, call, clear photo, or text can carry the information, but the person's confirmation and the food's real state establish whether the handoff happened. You may make one short record; it costs less than collective amnesia at the refrigerator door.
