## 中文

当你完成一次**普通、干燥**外拍，镜头外表面或机身有轻微灰尘，想在收纳前做型号允许的外部护理时，加载这份 Skill。结果是松散灰尘被安全处理、镜头加盖、器材存入干燥处；有沙粒、潮气、液体进入或凝露时停止常规擦拭并查本机说明书/服务建议。这里护理器材，不评价照片效果。

### 准备与输入

在干净、稳定、没有扬尘和来往碰撞的位置操作。先确认相机已完成写卡，按型号说明关机；手和清洁布保持干净。找到机身/镜头说明书对外部清洁、涂层、湿气和存放的规定。只处理能够安全接触的外部镜片与外壳；不要打开机身或触碰图像传感器。

### 执行

1. **先检查再碰镜片。** 在斜光下看是否只有松灰，还是有沙粒、盐、水迹或凝露。若湿、进水、明显沙粒嵌附或内部起雾，先停止；不要强擦、加热或拆开镜头。
2. **清除松散颗粒。** 按本机说明，用干净手动吹气球轻吹外部镜片和可及缝隙的松灰，吹嘴不碰镜片。不要用嘴吹出水滴；不要先用布把颗粒拖过镀膜。
3. **仅在适合时轻拭。** 仍有可擦指印且说明书允许时，使用干净、适合光学镜片的软布按型号说明轻拭；发现阻力、颗粒或划痕风险立即停。不要用家用溶剂、纸巾、强力压缩气、未获准的清洁液或内部传感器清洁法代替。
4. **加盖并收纳。** 外部干燥且没有可见松灰后装上相应盖，放到凉爽、干燥、通风、不会被压碰的位置；按本机说明处理电池和长期存放。用一句话记录是否发现异常，避免下次把已存在的痕迹归因于新使用。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

镜头外表面没有可见松散颗粒、盖子就位、器材处于干燥安全的存放位置；或异常情况被记录并停止常规清洁。光学镀膜划痕和内部水汽不能靠本篇判断已经修复。

- **灰尘吹不走或有砂粒：** 不增加擦拭力，按型号说明联系合适的清洁服务。
- **镜片有凝露：** 暂停使用和擦拭，按本机说明处理温差/潮气；不要热风烘。
- **布不确定是否干净：** 不使用它，先获得合适清洁材料。
- **清洁后仍有污迹：** 不反复摩擦；记录位置并查型号说明或服务支持。
- **需要拆镜头或清传感器：** 超出本篇，停止并用型号专门流程。

### 假设与边界

仅适用于日常干燥环境后的外部轻微灰尘。具体镜片镀膜、清洁布/液、镜头拆装和电池长期存放有型号差异，本机说明书优先。内进水、冲击、盐雾、沙尘大量侵入、霉菌或电气故障不在本篇范围。

## English

Load this Skill after an **ordinary dry** outing when an existing camera's exterior lens or body has light dust and needs model-allowed care before storage. Finish with loose dust safely handled, caps fitted, and the camera in dry storage. If sand, liquid entry, or condensation is present, stop ordinary wiping and follow the model's manual or service advice. This cares for the tool; it does not judge a photo.

### Preparation and inputs

Work on a clean, stable surface away from drifting dust and collisions. Confirm the camera has finished writing to the card and turn it off as the model directs. Keep hands and cloth clean. Find the camera/lens manual's exterior cleaning, coating, moisture, and storage guidance. Touch only safe exterior surfaces; do not open the body or touch the image sensor.

### Execution

1. **Inspect before touching glass.** In angled light, decide whether you see loose dust or sand, salt, water marks, or condensation. Stop if wet, water entered, grit is embedded, or the inside is fogged. Do not force a wipe, heat the device, or disassemble a lens.
2. **Remove loose particles first.** As the manual allows, use a clean hand blower on exterior glass and reachable seams without touching the glass with its nozzle. Do not blow mouth moisture or drag particles under a cloth.
3. **Wipe only when suitable.** If a removable fingerprint remains and the manual permits, use a clean soft cloth suitable for optical glass with gentle model-directed strokes. Stop when you feel resistance or see particles. Avoid household solvents, tissues, high-pressure compressed air, unapproved fluids, and improvised sensor cleaning.
4. **Cap and store.** When the exterior is dry and visibly free of loose dust, fit the proper caps and place the equipment somewhere cool, dry, ventilated, and protected from pressure or knocks. Follow this model's battery and long-storage instructions. Record any anomaly for the next outing.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

No visible loose particles remain on the exterior glass, caps are fitted, and the equipment rests in a dry safe place; alternatively, an anomaly is recorded and ordinary cleaning has stopped. This Skill cannot certify that a coating scratch or internal moisture is repaired.

- **Dust will not blow away or grit remains:** Do not rub harder; consult the model's cleaning guidance or appropriate service.
- **Glass has condensation:** Stop use and wiping; follow the manual's temperature/moisture steps, never hot-air dry it.
- **Cloth cleanliness is uncertain:** Do not use it until you have suitable clean material.
- **Marks remain after gentle cleaning:** Do not keep rubbing; record them and consult model guidance or service.
- **Sensor work or lens disassembly seems needed:** Stop; those require a separate model-specific process.

### Assumptions and limits

This covers light exterior dust after ordinary dry conditions. Coatings, fluids, mount handling, and long-term battery storage vary by model; its manual governs. Water entry, impact, salt spray, heavy sand intrusion, mould, and electrical faults are outside scope.
