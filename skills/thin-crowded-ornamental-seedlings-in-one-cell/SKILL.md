## 中文

当你的一格室内普通观赏种子发出**多株幼苗**，本品种说明要求此阶段留一株，且几株已经挤在一起时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按说明留下可继续生长的一株，**不把其它幼苗从共同介质里拔出来拖伤根**。只在已辨品种、阶段明确时做；E001 成年盆栽换盆不是间苗，本篇也不处理食用幼苗收获。

### 准备与输入

核每格标签、种子包的建议株数、间苗时点和是否有可辨“真叶”等条件；一时无法分辨幼苗还是杂草/另一种子时不要剪。找稳定桌面、能看清茎基部的光/放大方式、清洁小剪刀和可收拾的落点。手部精细动作困难可请可信的人协助，先讲清**只剪指定过密株**；不为省事整盘抓拔。

### 执行

1. **确认现在确需间苗。** 不是每格见两点绿就马上剪；按本品种说明核它是否应保留多株、是否到了适宜阶段。若全部看起来很弱或品种身份不明，先记录并请可靠园艺来源核，不强制选“最高的”。
2. **选要保留的一株。** 看健康、位置和本包的栽培要求，选一株不明显受损、能在格内留出空间的幼苗。不要凭颜色/高度单一信号保证未来长势；只是本次可观察的选择。
3. **只剪掉多余茎，不拉根。** 用清洁小剪刀在介质表面附近剪断指定多余幼苗，轻拿走剪下部分；剪刀不碰保留株茎和新叶。拔起邻株可能牵扯共同根系，除非有该品种另行明确移栽做法，本篇不把它当标准间苗。
4. **核保留株与格位。** 看留下的一株是否仍稳、根区介质未被大幅掀动、身份标签还在。之后按既有小苗光/水条件继续，不为补偿被剪苗而加倍浇水或肥料。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

本格按品种要求留下适宜株数，保留幼苗茎叶没有被剪伤、根区未被拔动，格位仍可辨。暂时太小或信息不清而选择等待，也须留下待核点，不乱剪成“一株”。

- **剪刀太大、看不清基部：** 停下换小工具、放大或请现场可信协助；不盲剪。
- **误剪掉想留下的苗：** 不把它粘回去；标记本格真实余苗状态，查是否还有健康苗可留，不保证补救成功。
- **多株根已纠缠：** 不通过硬拔分离；按可靠品种/园艺指导再判是否可整体移栽。
- **包装要求多株一起长：** 不按本篇强制留一株，服从包装的株数条件。
- **剪后保留株摇晃：** 轻稳介质并减少扰动，记录状态；不靠猛压土或灌水固定。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

少量普通观赏小苗可按包上阶段与留株数处理；稀有、高价值或需要特殊移栽的品种应咨询生产商/专业园艺。剪下的苗是本次间苗结果，不代表可食用或适合移植。精细动作可用放大和协助替代，但“剪而非拔”的根系保护判断不变。

## English

Load this Skill when **several seedlings occupy one indoor ornamental cell**, this variety's directions say to keep fewer at this stage, and crowding is now real. Keep a seedling that can continue, **without pulling neighbours and dragging its roots**. Work only with identified plants and a known stage. This is not E001's mature houseplant repotting or an edible-sprout harvest.

### Preparation and inputs

Check cell label, packet's intended plant count and thinning stage, including any true-leaf cue. Do not cut when you cannot distinguish intended seedlings from another seed or weed. Use a stable table, adequate view or magnification of stems at the medium surface, clean small scissors, and a place for removed tops. If fine hand work is difficult, ask trusted help after identifying **only the crowded plants to cut**. Do not pull a whole tray by hand for convenience.

### Execution

1. **Confirm thinning is due now.** Two green points do not automatically mean cut today. Check whether this variety should retain several plants and whether it has reached the suitable stage. If identity is unknown or every seedling appears weak, record the uncertainty and ask a reliable horticultural source rather than choosing “the tallest” by reflex.
2. **Choose the retained seedling.** Use visible health, position, and packet needs to select one without obvious damage and with room in the cell. Colour or height alone cannot guarantee future growth; this is a present observable choice.
3. **Cut surplus stems without pulling roots.** With clean small scissors, snip designated extras near the medium surface and lift away the tops. Keep blades off the retained stem and new leaves. Pulling a neighbour can disturb intertwined roots. Unless this variety has a separate supported transplant method, this Skill does not use uprooting as its standard thinning route.
4. **Check the retained plant and label.** It should remain steady, the root-area medium should not be torn up, and identity remains legible. Continue the existing seedling light and water routine afterward. Do not double water or fertiliser to “compensate” for removed seedlings.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The cell has this variety's intended count, retained stems and leaves were not cut, roots were not pulled, and the cell remains identifiable. If plants are too young or facts insufficient, waiting with a recorded question is also safer than cutting to an invented count.

- **Scissors are too large or stems too hard to see:** Stop for smaller tools, magnification, or trusted in-person help; do not cut blindly.
- **You cut the intended plant:** It cannot be glued back. Mark what remains, check whether a healthy seedling can stay, and do not promise recovery.
- **Roots appear intertwined:** Do not separate by force. Use reliable variety or horticultural guidance before any whole-root transplant.
- **The packet wants several plants together:** Do not force this one-plant route. Follow its actual count.
- **The retained seedling wobbles:** Steady medium gently with minimal disturbance and record it; do not compact hard or flood to hold it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

A few ordinary ornamental seedlings can be thinned under the packet's stage and count. Rare, valuable, or specialist transplant varieties need maker or qualified advice. Removed tops are a thinning result, not automatically food or transplant material. Magnification and help can replace fine motor work while the cut-not-pull root protection remains.
