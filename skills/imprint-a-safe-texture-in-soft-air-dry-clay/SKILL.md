## 中文

当你已有一块**仍软的风干黏土表面**，想用自己有权使用的普通物件压出可见凹凸时，加载这份 Skill。结果是黏土上的原创新纹理和完好的压纹物。H273 的纸拓印把凹凸转成纸上的色痕；本篇是在泥面留下凹形，并要防止把泥壁压穿。文物、借用艺术品、危险尖物、受保护图案和不宜沾泥的物件都不试。

### 准备与输入

确认黏土尚软，产品允许手压纹理且作品厚度有余量。选干净、坚固、无锐边的自有纹理物，例如普通压纹板；先检查它不会掉色、碎裂或被黏土污染。用可清洗的垫板承托泥件，不在餐具或食物台面操作。无法确认物件安全或许可时换物件。

### 执行

1. **在边角轻试。** 用同种泥的小碎块或作品不显眼处先压一次，观察凹痕、黏连和泥的变形。若粘住难取、物件掉屑或泥太薄，停止该图案。
2. **支撑泥壁再按。** 一手或垫板托住背面，在选定区域均匀轻按纹理物；力只到能看见凹凸，不把底、壁压穿。曲面一次压一小段，不强迫硬板贴满弯曲表面。
3. **直起取走，检查两面。** 缓慢取出纹理物，看泥纹是否可辨、物件是否干净完好、背面是否仍完整。若边缘被拉坏，用少量同泥轻整回，不用尖刀重新刻。
4. **决定保留和晾干。** 保留自己选择的纹理，不再用不同物件反复按到泥软塌；将作品置于安全垫板，后续按产品干燥说明检查。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

软泥表面出现可指认的压入纹理、支撑壁未穿、来源物无损，作品可进入晾干。只在泥面用颜料画几条线，不是物件压纹。

- **物件粘泥拔不出：** 别猛拉；按材料说明轻松动，必要时牺牲这块试验泥而保全原物。
- **纹理太浅：** 换同泥碎块小试更合适的压力，不在成品上用力补压到底。
- **泥壁裂或穿：** 停止继续压，先修复/重新塑形；不要把破口藏到纹理里。
- **原物掉色或掉屑：** 立即停用并检查泥件，换自有稳固物件。
- **抓握/视觉不便：** 用较大钝纹理板、稳定垫板和可信协助；仍由你核查泥壁与原物。

### 假设与边界

压纹不会神奇保留到干后完全相同，黏土收缩可能改变细节。只压普通自有纹理，非复制文化特定花纹；此作仍非食品、水或耐热器具。

## English

Load this Skill when a **soft air-dry clay surface** is ready and you want an imprint from an everyday object you own or may use. Finish with original relief in the clay and an intact source object. H273's paper rubbing turns relief into marks on paper; this presses a negative into clay and must protect its wall. Do not use artifacts, borrowed art, sharp objects, protected designs, or objects that cannot be soiled safely.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the clay is still soft, the product permits imprinting, and the piece has enough wall thickness. Choose a clean firm blunt owned texture such as a plain texture board, checking for loose colour or fragments and whether clay might harm it. Support the piece on a washable board, away from dishes and food surfaces. Change objects when permission or safety is unclear.

### Execution

1. **Test at an edge.** Press once into a scrap of the same clay or an unobtrusive part and look for relief, sticking, and distortion. Stop if it bonds, sheds, or makes a thin wall unsafe.
2. **Support the wall and press.** Hold the back or use a board while pressing the textured object evenly into the chosen area. Use only enough force for visible relief, not to punch through. On a curve, press a small part rather than forcing a rigid board around it.
3. **Lift straight and inspect both sides.** Remove slowly and check a readable imprint, a clean intact source, and unbroken clay backing. If an edge pulled, ease it with a little of the same clay instead of carving with a sharp knife.
4. **Keep the result and dry later.** Leave the texture you chose rather than repeatedly pressing new objects until clay collapses. Rest the piece safely on its board for drying under its product instructions.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The soft clay bears identifiable impressed relief, its supporting wall is unpunctured, the source object is unharmed, and the piece can dry. Painting a few lines on clay without an imprint is a different action.

- **Object sticks:** Do not yank. Loosen gently under material directions, sacrificing practice clay if needed to protect the original object.
- **Relief is too shallow:** Test slightly different pressure on scrap instead of forcing an almost finished wall.
- **Wall splits or punctures:** Stop stamping and repair or reshape first; do not hide the hole in texture.
- **Source sheds colour or chips:** Stop and inspect the clay; choose a firm owned replacement.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Use a larger blunt texture, stable board, and trusted help while you inspect both wall and source.

### Assumptions and limits

Drying shrinkage may change fine detail. Only ordinary owned textures are used, not copied cultural motifs. The result is not a food, water, or heat-safe object.
