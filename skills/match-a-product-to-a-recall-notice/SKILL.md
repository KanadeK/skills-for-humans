## 中文

当你已看到一则与拟购商品有关的召回或安全警示，必须确认手中这件是否属于公告范围时加载。这里只决定买前“匹配 / 不匹配这则公告 / 无法确认”，不处理已购商品的退换、拆解、危险物处置或医疗问题。

### 准备与输入

打开销售地区相关主管机关的**原始公告**，查看更新日期和完整受影响范围。准备实物或卖家提供的准确品牌、商品名、型号、规格、批号、序列号、生产日期、条码及图片中公告要求的字段；并非每则公告都用全部字段。若标识在不可及位置，可请店员展示或要求准确商品照片。不要拆机、撕毁封口或冒险触碰来找代码。

### 执行

1. **找到原始公告。** 从主管机关的召回检索入口进入具体公告；社交截图或卖家转述只作线索。确认公告日期、是否有扩展范围或更正。
2. **提取这则公告的匹配条件。** 写下品牌、名称、型号、颜色/尺寸、日期或批次范围、识别位置以及任何例外。公告照片用于辅助识别，不代替数字代码。
3. **逐项对照当前这件。** 比较商品本体、包装和订单页；型号相似、同品牌或购买时间大致相同都不等于精确匹配。若公告要求某字段，而你读不到它，就标“无法确认”。
4. **决定买前动作。** 明确匹配：不买，也不试用。明确不匹配：只说明“未落入**这则**公告”，继续做普通标签与用途检查，不据此宣称商品全面安全。无法确认：暂停购买，向卖家或公告列出的官方渠道核实准确代码，或者选另一件。
5. **保存最少证据。** 记下公告链接及更新日期、商品关键代码和结论；不用上传个人订单或联系方式来完成买前判断。

### 成功与停止

你能指出公告中哪一行与哪一个商品代码相符或不符；未取得必需代码时保持“无法确认”。若你已经持有或使用疑似召回物，立即停止本购买流程，按公告里的安全指示处理并转到售后任务；不要依这份 Skill 试运行、修理或处置。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只对品牌和外观：** 继续查型号、日期、批次等公告指定字段；查不到就暂停。
- **看旧截图：** 回到原始公告查看当前范围与更新，不沿用一张过期截屏。
- **卖家说“不是那批”但不给代码：** 要求可核对的准确标识，或换商品。
- **代码与公告格式不一致：** 确认抄写、前导零和代码位置；不要自行推断“差一点也算”或“差一点就安全”。

### 假设、替代与副作用

召回主管机关和公告字段因地区、品类而异。这里假设有可访问的官方公告与商品标识；网络、视觉、语言或动作受限时请现场人员展示标识、使用放大/朗读，或等到能核实后再决定。你可能失去一件折扣品；折扣不改变公告范围。

## English

Load this Skill when you have seen a recall or safety alert relevant to an item you are about to buy and need to know whether the exact item is covered. It produces “matches / does not match this notice / unconfirmed” before purchase. Returns, dismantling, hazardous disposal, and medical matters are separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Open the **original notice** from the relevant authority for the selling region, including its update date and full affected range. Have the actual item or seller's exact brand, name, model, size, lot, serial number, manufacture date, barcode, and the fields required by that notice; a notice may use only some of these. If an identifier is inaccessible, ask staff to show it or provide an exact-item photo. Do not dismantle a device, break a seal, or touch a hazardous part to find a code.

### Execution

1. **Find the primary notice.** Start from the authority's recall search and open the specific notice. A social screenshot or seller summary is only a lead. Check the notice date, later expansion, and corrections.
2. **Extract its matching conditions.** Record affected brand, name, model, colour or size, date or lot range, identifier location, and exceptions. Photos help recognition but cannot replace required codes.
3. **Compare this item field by field.** Check the product, package, and listing. A similar model, shared brand, or roughly matching purchase period is not an exact match. If a required field cannot be read, mark unconfirmed.
4. **Choose the pre-purchase action.** Clear match: do not buy or try it. Clear non-match: conclude only that this item is outside **this notice**, then continue ordinary label and purpose checks; do not declare it universally safe. Unconfirmed: pause, verify the exact code with the seller or the notice's official contact, or choose another item.
5. **Keep minimal evidence.** Note the notice link and update date, critical item codes, and your result. Personal order details are unnecessary for this pre-purchase decision.

### Success and stop

You can point to the notice condition and the corresponding item identifier. A missing required code remains unconfirmed. If you already own or use a possibly recalled item, stop this buying workflow and follow the notice's safety instructions and the separate after-sales route. Do not use this Skill to test, repair, or dispose of it.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Matching only the brand or appearance:** Find the model, date, and lot fields specified in the notice; otherwise pause.
- **Using an old screenshot:** Reopen the original notice for current scope and updates.
- **The seller says “different batch” without a code:** Ask for the checkable identifier or choose another item.
- **The code format looks different:** Recheck transcription, leading zeros, and code location. Do not infer that “almost the same” either matches or guarantees safety.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Recall authorities and notice fields vary by region and product. This assumes an accessible official notice and item identifier. If network, vision, language, reach, or handling prevents a check, ask staff to show the mark, use magnification or read-aloud help, or wait until verification is possible. You may lose a discount; it cannot change the recall boundary.

- [US FDA: FDA 101 Product Recalls](https://www.fda.gov/ForConsumers/ConsumerUpdates/ucm049070.htm) — notices may identify exact packaging, UPC, lot, and date fields that must be compared.
