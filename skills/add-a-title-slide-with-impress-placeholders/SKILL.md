## 中文

你已有一份自己的非敏感 Impress 演示稿，开头需要一张清楚说明主题的标题页时使用本篇。成果是第一页用预设标题/副标题占位框承载准确文字，后续内容页顺序不变；不靠手画两个松散文本框模仿标题布局。标题只说清主题与场合，不做未经证实的成果宣传。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 工作副本，写好一行主标题和可选的短副标题，记录原来第一页和总张数。确认现有模板或母版里有 Title Slide 一类布局；若已存在封面，先判断是替换文字还是新增，不重复制造两张封面。

### 执行

1. 在第一张前或原封面处选择标题页布局，核标题与副标题占位框已出现。
2. 把自己的短文字分别填进正确占位框，不覆盖母版共同元素。
3. 在普通编辑视图和放映预览中检查文字不截断、对比清楚，副标题不抢主标题层级。
4. 核后续内容页顺序与总张数的预期变化，保存重开标题页。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

首页准确表达主题，预设占位框可编辑，后续页不乱序；若已有封面且不应新增，则只完成原页修订。

- **标题被截：** 缩短文字或调整已有布局，不盲缩到难读。
- **多出两张封面：** 撤销重复新增，保留一张。
- **改到母版：** 退出母版视图，在普通页占位框编辑。

### 假设与边界

只做一张普通标题页，不设计品牌模板或保证舞台投影效果。你负责文字真实性和模板使用许可；字体显示因设备而异。

## English

Use this Skill when your own non-sensitive Impress deck needs a first slide naming its subject. Finish with accurate title and subtitle in built-in placeholders on slide one while later content order stays intact. Do not emulate a title layout with two loose text boxes. The wording names topic and setting without unsupported achievement claims.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP working copy, draft one title line and optional short subtitle, and note original first slide and total count. Locate a Title Slide layout in the existing template/master. If a cover already exists, decide whether to edit it instead of adding a second one.

### Execution

1. At the beginning or existing cover choose a title-slide layout and verify title/subtitle placeholders appear.
2. Enter your short wording in the appropriate placeholders without overwriting shared master elements.
3. Check in edit and show preview for clipping, readable contrast and a subordinate subtitle.
4. Confirm later-slide order and expected count change, then save and reopen the title slide.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Slide one names the topic accurately in editable placeholders and later slides retain order; if a cover already existed, only its intended edit counts.

- **Title clipped:** Shorten or adjust layout without shrinking to unreadable type.
- **Duplicate covers:** Undo the extra title slide.
- **Master edited:** Exit master view and edit normal-slide placeholders.

### Assumptions and limits

This makes one ordinary title slide, not a brand template or live-projection guarantee. You own wording and template permission; fonts may render differently elsewhere.
