## 中文

当一项**普通社交约定已经到点而你没有做到**，例如没按说好的时间到场、忘记带约好的普通物品或没有做答应的小事，加载这份 Skill。目标是说明事实、承认影响并提出你确实能完成的补救；对方可以拒绝。本篇不处理工作人事、欠款、合同、法律义务、欺骗或持续操控。约定尚未到期而需要主动取消，属于事前调整，不用本篇假装失败已发生。

### 准备与输入

把原约定写成一句话：谁、什么事、何时要完成；再写现在能核实的事实、谁受影响、是否有必须马上停止的后续安排。不要先编出一个听起来更有说服力的原因。确认自己能提供的补救时间、物品或方式；做不到的新承诺不能用来覆盖旧承诺。

### 执行

1. **尽快联系受影响的人。** 用双方平时能收到的渠道说“我答应在四点到，现在没到；这是我没做到。抱歉。”若影响了对方等候或准备，可指出这个具体影响，但不要替对方宣布他已经原谅或一定生气。
2. **给必要事实，不做借口长篇。** 你可以说“我忘了”或“我估错了时间”，同时保留责任。不要把手机、天气或其他人写成自动免责按钮，也不要求对方听完一场自责演讲。
3. **提出一个能交付的选择。** “如果还来得及，我六点前能把那本书送到；如果已经不需要，我就不再打扰。”时间、地点和可用资源都要真实。若已经无法补回这次机会，明确说“这次我补不回来”，只提出仍有意义的后续动作。
4. **把是否接受留给对方。** 对方接受就复述新安排、记下并完成；拒绝或说需要时间就尊重，不反复请求“给我一次机会”。没有回复时，保持待确认，不把沉默写成新的同意。
5. **处理自己造成的其他影响。** 若有人已经按旧约等你或调整计划，只把更正传给真正受影响的人。需要归还普通借物时确认实际交接；涉及正式费用、权利或敏感责任时停止本日常流程，使用适用的专业/正式渠道。

### 完成条件

你已向受影响的人说明原约定与实际落差，做了必要的道歉，提出可执行补救或坦白无法补回，并准确记录“接受 / 拒绝 / 未回复”及你下一步能做什么。真正的新约定只有在对方同意后才成立。对方必须原谅、信任恢复或给你第二次邀请，不是完成条件。

### 常见报错与补救

- **怕尴尬而一直不联系：** 先发一条简短事实，不用等到写出完美解释。
- **补救比旧承诺更大但仍做不到：** 缩回到有时间、资源和边界支持的一项动作；新承诺不是情绪止痛药。
- **对方说“不用了”：** 接受，停止推销补救；保留你能独立完成的必要归还或更正。
- **没人回复，你就默认改期成功：** 标为未确认，别在新时间突然到场或替对方安排。
- **发现这是正式工作/财务责任：** 停止用普通社交话术处理，转向实际规定、记录与合适的专业渠道。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份 Skill 只覆盖自愿、低风险的普通社交承诺。关系、地区与文化不会把一次失约自动变成可用礼物补偿的公式；按这次实际影响和对方意愿处理。对方可能需要文字、语音或更长时间回应，使用双方可及的渠道即可。你控制的是如实说明与后续行动，不控制他人的情绪或决定。

现实副作用是约定可能真的结束，或你需要按新的小承诺做事。诚实不保证关系立即复原，但能让账面停止虚构。

## English

Load this Skill after an **ordinary social commitment was due and you did not keep it**: you missed the agreed time, forgot an everyday item you promised to bring, or did not do a small favour. State the fact, acknowledge the effect, and offer a repair you can actually deliver. The other person may decline. Work/HR duties, debts, contracts, legal obligations, deception, and repeated manipulation are outside this Skill. If the commitment is still in the future, change or cancel it beforehand rather than treating failure as complete.

### Preparation and inputs

Write the original promise in one sentence: who, what, and when. Note facts you can verify now, who was affected, and whether further action based on the old plan must stop. Do not first build a more persuasive excuse. Check the real time, item, or method available for repair. A new promise you cannot keep cannot cover the old one.

### Execution

1. **Contact affected people promptly.** On a channel they usually receive: “I said I would arrive at four, and I did not. I failed to do that. I'm sorry.” You may identify their waiting or preparation as a concrete effect, without declaring that they must be angry or have forgiven you.
2. **Give the needed fact without a long defence.** “I forgot” or “I misjudged the time” can be said while keeping responsibility. Do not make the phone, weather, or another person an automatic exemption, or require an audience for a performance of self-blame.
3. **Offer one deliverable option.** “If it still helps, I can bring the book by six; if not, I won't keep bothering you.” Time, place, and resources must be real. If this opportunity cannot be restored, say “I cannot give this occasion back” and offer only a later action that still matters.
4. **Leave acceptance to them.** If they accept, repeat the new plan, record it, and do it. If they decline or need time, respect that instead of repeatedly asking for another chance. Without a reply, mark the result pending; silence is not agreement to your revised plan.
5. **Correct other effects you caused.** If others waited or changed plans for the old commitment, tell only those actually affected. Confirm a practical handoff for an ordinary borrowed item. When formal costs, rights, or sensitive responsibilities are involved, stop this everyday flow and use the applicable formal or professional channel.

### Success

You told affected people the original commitment and actual gap, apologised where needed, offered a feasible repair or said honestly that the opportunity cannot be restored, and recorded “accepted / declined / no reply” with your next action. A new commitment exists only after the other person agrees. Forgiveness, restored trust, or a second invitation is not a required result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You avoid contact from embarrassment:** Send one brief factual message before trying to write a perfect explanation.
- **Your new offer is bigger than the old promise but still impossible:** Reduce it to one action your time, resources, and boundaries can support. A new promise is not pain relief.
- **They say “no need”:** Accept it and stop selling the repair. Finish only any necessary return or correction you can do independently.
- **No one replies, so you assume a new date is agreed:** Mark it unconfirmed; do not arrive uninvited at the new time or schedule for them.
- **It turns out to be formal work or financial responsibility:** Stop using ordinary social wording and follow the actual rules, records, and suitable professional channel.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers voluntary, low-risk social promises only. Relationship, region, and culture do not make a gift an automatic formula for repairing a missed plan. Respond to actual impact and the other person's choice. They may need text, voice, or time to answer; use a channel both can access. You control factual disclosure and future actions, not their feelings or decisions.

The commitment may end, or you may have one new small action to complete. Honesty does not instantly restore a relationship, but it stops the record from being fictional.
