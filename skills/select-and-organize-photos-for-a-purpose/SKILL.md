## 中文

当相册里有很多普通照片，而你需要一组用于回看、打印参考、给自己做项目或以后再挑选时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**按这一次真实用途选出能代表内容的一组照片，并在不丢原件的情况下找回它们**。筛选不等于删除相似照片，也不等于公开分享。

只整理你有权管理、低敏感的照片。含私人材料、他人不愿公开的画面或项目机密的照片，先按实际同意与保存规则处理。这个流程不帮你判断发布许可、摄影版权、云端备份或永久删除。

### 准备与输入

把用途说具体：“从周末活动里选 8–12 张自己回看”比“整理所有照片”更可执行。选一个日期/事件/文件夹范围和你能看完的一小批。先确认你是在原相库、本地文件夹还是同步云相册里操作；不知道应用会如何处理删除、移出相册和编辑时，本轮只做不删原件的选择。

### 执行

1. **定义入选标准。** 按本次用途选最需要的画面、人物或步骤；质量检查包括能否看清主体、是否明显失焦、遮挡、过曝或缺少关键部分。好看不一定有用；唯一记录了重要内容的普通照片也可能要留。
2. **逐组打开，不只看缩略图。** 相似连拍至少放大几张，看焦点、表情、文字或动作差异。挑代表照进入“候选”，其余保持原样；不因文件名、拍摄时间或系统推荐的“最佳”单独决定删除原件。
3. **对照用途再缩小候选。** 看整组是否有开头、关键内容与必要结尾，是否重复太多或遗漏某个真实需要的画面。可有一张最清楚的主照和少量补充；不为了凑固定数量而加入无关照片。
4. **放入可找回的精选位置。** 在你已经使用的照片应用里给这一组建一个按用途命名的普通相册/集合，或在文件管理器里保存一个清晰的精选清单。不要把“共享相册”误当私人的普通相册；不需要分享就不要打开共享入口。
5. **核对原件关系。** 回到原相库或原文件夹确认所选照片仍能在那里找到；相册/集合通常只是组织视图，不是备份证明。若应用只允许移动或导出，先看实际提示与存储位置，不覆盖、删除或替换原图。编辑后的版本若有单独存放，也要认清哪个是原图。
6. **从精选位置重新打开。** 核对张数、顺序、主体与用途。看是否有漏选、误选或只有低分辨率预览。结果能用之后再结束本批；额外的备份、发送或删除留给各自流程。

### 完成条件

一组照片能通过用途/事件名称找回，打开后确实包含需要的画面；你知道原件所在的相库或文件夹，且本轮没有用删除来假装筛选完成。对尚未确认价值的相似照片保留待判断，不按“精选组里没有”推断它们可丢弃。

### 常见报错与补救

- **精选组里都是同一瞬间：** 放大比较连拍，留清楚且有实际差异的少数张；其余先留在原相库，不急着删。
- **从相册移除后担心原图不见了：** 先在原相库搜索并打开；若界面显示要从所有设备删除，取消并检查应用规则。
- **准备发给别人时才发现敏感画面：** 停止分享，另行按收件对象、版本和权限流程核对；本地精选不自动授权外传。
- **不知道原图是在手机还是云端：** 查看当前应用显示的实际位置和下载状态；无法确认时把“原件位置”标待查，不把精选相册当备份。
- **照片太多看不完：** 缩小到一个日期或事件，完成这一批后再继续；不要让一次筛选变成对整个相库的突击删除。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

不同照片应用对相册、收藏、归档、编辑版本和同步的实现不同。Apple 与 Google 的普通相册中移除项目通常不会删除图库原件，但具体操作前仍看实际提示；删除图库照片可能同步影响其他设备。可用放大、朗读文件信息、键盘或可信协助者检查，视觉缩略图不是唯一入口。你可能发现拍了 42 张同一片云；这不要求立刻审判它们的存在价值。

## English

Load this Skill when your library contains many ordinary photos and you need a set for personal review, a print reference, a project, or later selection. **Choose photos for this real use and make the selection findable without losing the originals.** Curation is not deletion of similar frames and does not authorize public sharing.

Organize only ordinary low-sensitivity photos you may manage. If images contain private material, people who did not agree to publication, or project confidential content, follow actual consent and retention rules first. This Skill does not decide publication rights, copyright, cloud backup, or permanent deletion.

### Preparation and inputs

Make the use specific: “choose 8–12 photos from the weekend for my own review” is more workable than “organize every photo.” Pick one date, event, or folder and a batch you can actually inspect. Check whether you are in the source library, a local folder, or a synced cloud album. If you do not know how the app treats deletion, removal from albums, or edits, make only a selection that preserves originals this time.

### Execution

1. **Define what earns a place.** Choose needed scenes, people, or steps for this use. Check whether the subject is visible and whether focus, obstruction, exposure, or a missing edge matters. A pretty image may not serve the purpose; an ordinary one may be the only record of something needed.
2. **Open groups, not thumbnails alone.** Zoom into several similar burst shots and compare focus, expression, text, or action. Mark a representative image as a candidate and leave the others in the source library. Do not delete an original based only on name, capture time, or an app's suggested “best” label.
3. **Reduce candidates against the purpose.** See whether the set includes its start, important content, and any needed ending. Remove needless repetition from the *selection* and fill genuine gaps. One clear main image and a few supporting frames may be enough; do not pad to a fixed number.
4. **Use a findable selection location.** Make an ordinary purpose-named album or collection in the photo app you already use, or a clear selection list in a file manager. Do not confuse a shared album with a private ordinary album. Avoid the Share route when sharing is not needed.
5. **Check where originals remain.** Return to the source library or folder and find the selected originals. An album or collection is generally an organizing view, not backup proof. If the app offers only move or export, inspect its prompt and destination first; do not overwrite, delete, or replace originals. Know which image is original when edited versions are stored separately.
6. **Reopen from the selection.** Check count, order, subject, and purpose. Notice an omitted shot, an accidental one, or a low-resolution preview. Finish this batch when the set works; backup, sending, and deletion have their own workflows.

### Success

You can find the set by its purpose or event name, open it, and see the images needed. You know where the originals live and did not use deletion to simulate curation. Keep uncertain similar shots for later judgment. Absence from the selected set does not make an original disposable.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The selection is the same instant repeated:** Zoom into burst shots and retain a few clear, genuinely different frames in the selection. Leave the others in the source library for now.
- **Removing from an album makes you worry the original vanished:** Search and open it in the source library. Cancel any prompt that says it will delete from all devices and check the app's rule.
- **A private image appears just before sharing:** Stop the share and use a separate recipient, version, and permissions check. A local selection does not grant permission to distribute it.
- **You do not know whether the original is on phone or cloud:** Inspect the actual location and download status shown by the app. Mark the original location pending if unclear; a selected album is not a backup.
- **There are too many photos to inspect:** Reduce scope to one date or event, finish that batch, then return later. Curation need not turn into a mass purge.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Photo apps differ in albums, favorites, archives, edited versions, and syncing. In Apple and Google ordinary albums, removing an item usually leaves the library original, but still read the actual prompt before acting. Deleting a library photo can propagate to other devices. Zoom, spoken file information, keyboard controls, or a trusted helper can support review; thumbnails are not the only route. You may discover 42 photos of one cloud. They do not all require an immediate verdict on their right to exist.
