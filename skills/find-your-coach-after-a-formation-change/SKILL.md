## 中文

你的列车和站台已经确认，但显示**车厢顺序、停靠分区或预订车厢与原计划不同**时，用这份 Skill核对今天这班的实际编组，在安全的开放站台分区等候或请工作人员处理找不到的车厢。它不替你决定座位权利、改签或补偿，也不因为站台号没变就假定车厢位置没变。整班车改站台是另一种任务，先核对新站台再找车厢。

### 准备与输入

保留车次号/出发时间、站台、票或预订上的车厢号、座位号、等级/无障碍需求。查该班当前的站台编组屏、分区标识、运营方官方信息或人员说明；车厢可能换序、缩编、取消或实际停靠点偏移。旧车厢示意图不能充当到站车辆的编号；同站台另一班的车厢图也不能混用。无法安全沿长站台移动时先找站方可及帮助，不为座位跨越边缘或封闭区。

### 执行

1. **确认仍是同一车次与站台。** 在当前出发屏核对车次、时间、终点及站台。若整个站台已改，先按改台公告修正，再处理车厢；单独拿一张编组图无法解决站台错误。
2. **读当前编组与分区。** 查这班车实际车厢号排列、是否有你预订的车厢、其预计停靠的站台字母/区段；注意图示的更新时间和“预计”性质。若无法读懂图、系统显示不可用或多个来源冲突，问该班工作人员，不沿人流盲走。
3. **在安全开放区段就位。** 按站方标识走到该车厢预计停靠的开放分区，保持在站台安全线后并留出上下车通道。别跑到列车另一端、穿越轨道或拦车门求一个座位。列车实际停稳后再看车身车厢号和类别，确认与票/实际安排一致；预计分区不是车厢已经到位的证据。
4. **车厢不存在时读回下一步。** 预订车厢取消、编号与票不符、无障碍车厢位置变化或列车长度不足时，在不阻碍上下车的安全处向列车/站方人员出示必要预订信息，询问本班实际允许的车厢、座位与协助方式。不要自行占别人的预订席，也不推断原票自动可用于另一车次。

### 成功条件

你已在正确站台按当前编组找到预订/可乘车厢的开放候车区，列车到后再次核对车身编号；或车厢缺失/信息冲突已向站方确认替代与下一步。站台分区字母相同不等于车厢号一定相同。

### 常见报错与补救

- **旧截图显示车厢在另一端：** 看该班当前编组和信息时间，到车后再核对实物编号。
- **站台上有两列或相邻班次：** 先确认车次和屏幕来源，别用另一班的编组图。
- **预订车厢不存在：** 不自动换成任何空座；问工作人员实际座位/票务方案并保留其说明。
- **站台分区太远或无障碍路线中断：** 在可达位置联系站方协助，不冒险跑过拥挤平台。
- **车厢号与分区屏冲突：** 以当前列车实物、车次和站方人员核实，别堵门反复对旧地图。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 只处理普通列车的同班次编组/停靠分区变化，车厢号、等级、预订与可及设施由实际运营方决定。德国 DB 的编组图和其他车站的站台显示是实例，不是所有铁路都提供完整实时编组。没有手机可看站台牌或问工作人员；有移动、视觉或听觉限制时提前请求该班真实协助。更改候车分区可能多走路，但抢车厢不应破坏站台安全。

## English

Use this Skill when the service and platform are confirmed but its **coach order, stopping section, or booked coach differs from the original plan**. Check today's formation, wait in a safe open section, or ask staff what to do if a coach is absent. This does not decide seat rights, rebooking, or compensation. An unchanged platform number does not guarantee an unchanged coach position. If the whole train moved platform, verify that change first.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep train ID/time, platform, and the ticket or booking's coach, seat, class, and accessibility needs. Read this run's current platform formation board, section signs, official operator information, or staff guidance. Order may reverse, shorten, omit a coach, or shift its stopping position. An old diagram is not the vehicle number; do not borrow an adjacent train's formation. If a long platform is hard to traverse, ask for actual accessible help rather than approaching the edge or a closed section.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the same service and platform.** Check current train ID, time, terminus, and platform. If the whole train changed platforms, resolve that first. A formation diagram cannot repair a wrong-platform decision.
2. **Read current formation and section.** Find the actual coach-number sequence, whether your coach appears, and its expected platform letter/section. Note the information time and that a diagram may be a forecast. If it is unavailable, unreadable, or conflicts with another source, ask staff for this specific train instead of following a crowd.
3. **Wait in an open safe section.** Follow station signs to the expected coach area, staying behind the platform safety marking and keeping doors and paths clear. Do not run to the far end, cross tracks, or block a door for a seat. Once the train stops, read the vehicle's actual coach number and class before boarding; an expected section is not proof that a coach arrived there.
4. **Read back an absent-coach outcome.** For a cancelled coach, number mismatch, changed accessible coach, or shortened train, show necessary booking details to train or station staff from a safe nonblocking spot. Ask which coach, seat, and assistance are actually permitted on this run. Do not occupy another reservation on your own or assume the ticket is valid on a different train.

### Success

On the correct platform you found the open waiting section for the currently indicated booked or permitted coach and will recheck its number on arrival. An absent or conflicting coach with a confirmed staff alternative/next step is also complete. The same section letter is not proof of the same coach number.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Old screenshot places the coach at the other end:** Use this run's current formation and information time, then check the physical coach number.
- **Two trains share a platform or depart close together:** Verify train ID and which formation display you read.
- **Booked coach is absent:** Ask staff for the actual seat or ticket path; do not claim any empty seat by assumption.
- **Section is too far or accessible route fails:** Ask station staff for help at a reachable point rather than rushing along a crowded platform.
- **Physical coach conflicts with the section screen:** Cross-check this train and ask staff, keeping clear of the doorway and ignoring stale diagrams.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a same-run coach formation or stopping-section change. Actual operator rules control coach, class, booking, and accessible facilities. German DB formation data and other station displays are examples; not every railway has a complete live diagram. Use platform signs or staff without a phone. Arrange help early for movement, sight, or hearing needs. A longer walk is possible, but reaching a coach never justifies unsafe platform behavior.
