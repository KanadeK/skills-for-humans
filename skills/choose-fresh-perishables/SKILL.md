## 中文

当你站在蔬果、鸡蛋、肉或海鲜前，想判断“买哪一个，还是都不买”时加载这份 Skill。它帮助你找拒收线索，不提供肉眼微生物扫描，也不把“闻起来还行”升级成安全证书。

### 准备与输入

确认食材种类、散装或包装、你打算哪天吃、回家路程和保冷条件。包装食品还要看储存方式、日期、封口和卖场温度。

如果你看不清标签、无法闻气味、不方便触碰或拿取食材，可以拍清晰照片放大、请同行者描述、请店员展示标签，或优先选择信息完整的包装品。不要把某一种感官当成唯一入口。

### 执行：从环境到食材

1. **先看摊位或冷柜。** 选择来源可追溯、陈列清洁的卖家。需要冷藏的食物应处在正常工作的冷柜里；冷冻品应保持硬冻。
2. **检查包装与标签。** 封口应完整，无鼓胀、渗漏、严重破损；储存条件和日期要与你计划的使用时间相容。包装在漏，产品已经主动提交了拒绝申请。
3. **蔬果看“是否适合用途”。** 明显霉变、流汁腐烂、严重损伤或大面积碰伤可以直接排除。外形不标准但结实、无腐败的食材未必有问题；不要把审美当食品安全。
4. **鸡蛋先排除裂漏。** 不买外壳开裂、渗漏或包装污染的蛋。回家前仍要避免碰撞与长时间受热。
5. **鲜肉和冷藏食品不靠颜色单判。** 排除渗漏、鼓包、破损、过期、明显异味或没有按标签温度保存的产品。颜色会受包装与氧气影响，不能单独盖章。
6. **海鲜使用多条线索。** 鲜鱼和贝类应来自可靠冷藏陈列；排除酸败、氨味等明显异常气味、干枯边缘、严重损伤。活贝类对敲击无闭合反应是负面信号。不要为了“再确认一下”生尝。
7. **冷冻海鲜检查冻态。** 包装破裂、食物已经柔软弯曲，或出现大量可能提示融化再冻的霜和冰晶时，选择别的产品。
8. **把回程算进决定。** 只买你能及时冷藏或放进保冷袋的量。生肉和海鲜放入独立防漏袋，远离熟食、即食食品和日用品。

### 成功条件

你能给每项输出“买 / 不买 / 先问清楚”，并说出依据、仍不确定的地方、回家运输方式和预计使用时间。正常外观只能让它继续候选，不能宣告绝对安全。

### 常见报错与补救

- **线索互相矛盾：** 选保守结果，不买，或要求更可追溯的信息；价格优惠不会修复储存历史。
- **标签看不懂：** 拍照放大、查当地官方标签说明或问店员。猜一个日期格式可能把月份和日期交换。
- **回家时间突然变长：** 先不买易腐品，改买耐储存选项，或补充可靠保冷条件。
- **买后发现渗漏、异味或曾受热：** 隔离它，避免污染其他食物；不要用清洗、再冷冻或重口味烹调掩盖未知安全史，按当地官方建议处理。
- **吃后不适或出现过敏反应：** 停止食用并寻求适当医疗帮助。这份 Skill 到此立即停止，不参与诊断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

季节、品种、售卖方式和文化偏好会改变“好看”的标准，因此这里只使用明显拒收线索和标签条件。你的嗅觉、视觉、握力或语言能力不应成为购买门槛；换一种可访问的检查方式，或选择信息更完整的产品。

现实副作用是挑选会慢一点，而且你可能对“特价”产生新的日志记录。少买一个可疑商品通常比回家后说服自己更便宜。

## English

Load this Skill when you are standing in front of produce, eggs, meat, or seafood and need to decide “this one, another one, or none.” It helps you find rejection clues. It does not provide visible-spectrum microbiology or turn “smells fine” into a safety certificate.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the food, loose or packaged state, when you expect to use it, travel time, and cooling available. For packaged food, also read storage instructions, dates, seals, and the display temperature.

If labels are hard to see, smell is unavailable, or handling/reaching is difficult, enlarge a clear photo, ask a companion to describe what they observe, ask staff to show the label, or choose a product with clearer packaging. No single sense is a required interface.

### Execution: environment before appearance

1. **Check the seller and display.** Prefer traceable sellers and clean displays. Food sold chilled should be in a working cold case; frozen food should remain hard-frozen.
2. **Inspect package and label.** Reject leaking, swollen, badly damaged, open, or unsuitable-dated packaging. If the package is leaking, it has already filed its own rejection ticket.
3. **Judge produce for its intended use.** Reject obvious mould, leaking decay, serious damage, or bruising that defeats storage or use. Unusual shape is not the same as spoilage; aesthetics is not food safety.
4. **Reject cracked or leaking eggs.** Keep accepted eggs protected from impact and prolonged warmth on the trip home.
5. **Do not use meat colour alone.** Reject leaks, swelling, damage, expired dates, obvious off odours, or storage that conflicts with the label. Packaging and oxygen can change colour.
6. **Use several seafood clues.** Buy from reliable chilled displays. Reject sour, rancid, ammonia-like, or otherwise clearly abnormal odours, dried edges, and serious damage. Live shellfish that do not close when tapped provide a negative signal. Do not taste raw food to “make sure.”
7. **Check frozen seafood state.** Choose another package if it is torn, the product bends instead of staying hard-frozen, or heavy frost and ice suggest thawing and refreezing.
8. **Include transport in the decision.** Buy only what you can chill promptly or carry in a cold bag. Put raw meat and seafood in separate leak-resistant bags away from cooked, ready-to-eat, and household products.

### Success

For each item you can output buy, reject, or ask first, with the reason, remaining uncertainty, transport plan, and expected use date. Normal appearance only keeps an item under consideration; it never proves absolute safety.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Clues disagree:** Choose the conservative result, reject it, or obtain traceable information. A discount does not patch unknown storage history.
- **The label is unclear:** Enlarge a photo, use official local label guidance, or ask staff. Guessing a date format can swap month and day.
- **Travel suddenly becomes longer:** Delay the perishable purchase, select a shelf-stable alternative, or add reliable cooling.
- **You discover leaks, abnormal odour, or warming after purchase:** Isolate the item from other food. Washing, refreezing, or strong seasoning cannot repair an unknown safety history; follow local authority advice.
- **You feel ill or have an allergic reaction after eating:** Stop eating and seek appropriate medical help. This Skill stops here and does not diagnose.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Season, variety, market style, and cultural preference change what “good-looking” means, so this Skill relies on clear rejection clues and labelled conditions. Visual, smell, grip, reach, or language access should not be a gate; use another accessible observation or choose a better-documented product.

The known side effect is a slightly slower shop and a new log entry for the word “special.” Rejecting one doubtful item is usually cheaper than negotiating with it at home.
