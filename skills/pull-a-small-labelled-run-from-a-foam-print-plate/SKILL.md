## 中文

当你的**自有泡棉版已有一张可接受试印**，想再做几张可逐张检查的同版纸印时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一个数量真实、顺序可辨、每张都干燥的个人小组作品；它与只做第一张试印或只印一次的独版不同。没有许可的图案、个人资料或标志不因“只印三张”就变成可公开传播。

### 准备与输入

确认版未破、试印显示凹线和方向符合你的目标；先决定一个小且可完成的张数，准备同类纸、少量水基可洗画材、护台垫和彼此不碰的晾纸位置。材料不够就缩小张数，不临时换多种颜色把本次对照弄乱。需要图案对位时在废纸标版角和纸角，不在成品正面画定位线。

### 执行

1. **从第一张开始重新上色。** 按首次试印已经可用的墨量和微湿条件轻上色，确认凹线仍空；把纸与版对好，均匀手压，慢慢揭起并放到单独晾位。
2. **每张独立复上色。** 版面需要清理时按画材说明轻擦并晾至可用，再按相近方法给下一张上色；不是拿第一张残色硬压出后几张还称“同等印数”。每次查看版是否损坏，坏了就停。
3. **保留真实顺序和差别。** 在纸边写 1、2、3 等制作次序与同一版号，不滥用“限量版”暗示专业认证。看每张是否足够可辨；过淡可保留为失败样、另补一张，但记录总尝试数与选入组的张数。
4. **全干后整理。** 平放直到无湿颜料会互粘，分开核数量、方向和每张的线条。工具按材料清洗，版留作下一轮；不把湿纸直接叠压。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

同一版至少两张可辨且已干的纸印，实际顺序与选入组的数量诚实标清，每张仍可看出同一设计。把几张湿纸贴在一起、数不清成功与失败，不算完成。

- **后几张越来越淡：** 每张重上合用颜色，按试印条件查水分，不靠残墨假装一致。
- **纸移动造成双影：** 调整手压/角标，下一张重新试；不把失败张计入整齐小组。
- **泡棉版开始裂：** 停印、记录实际张数，不强撑目标数量。
- **没有足够晾纸空间：** 分两次做或缩小张数，不叠湿纸。
- **手眼操作不便：** 大纸、清楚角标和可信协助固定，由你核查每张是否入组。

### 假设与边界

每张印会略有差别，手工小组不等于商业版次、版权许可或价值担保。版能重用才有这项任务；若你想要一次性的独特转印，应走独版稿。

## English

Load this Skill when **your owned foam plate has an acceptable proof** and you want a few individually checked impressions from the same plate. Finish with an honest small set whose order and count are clear and whose sheets are dry. This differs from a first proof or one-off monotype. An unlicensed design, personal information, or mark is not suddenly public-shareable because only three copies were made.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm an intact plate and proof showing the desired recesses and direction. Decide a small feasible count. Prepare similar owned paper, a little washable water-based colour, protected board, and separate drying spaces. Reduce the count if supplies are short rather than switching colours mid-run without a reason. For alignment, mark plate and paper corners on scrap instead of drawing registration marks across finished fronts.

### Execution

1. **Re-colour for the first run sheet.** Apply the proof-tested thin colour load and slight moisture condition, keeping recesses clear. Align paper and plate, press evenly by hand, lift slowly, and place in its own drying space.
2. **Re-colour separately for every sheet.** Clean lightly and dry the plate when the product needs it, then colour the next impression similarly. Do not press several later sheets from only residual colour and call their count equivalent. Check for plate damage each time and stop on a tear.
3. **Preserve real order and differences.** Number production order 1, 2, 3 with the same plate identifier, without using “limited edition” to imply professional certification. Check legibility. Keep a faint attempt as a failed sample or replace it, recording actual attempts and selected sheets honestly.
4. **Sort only when dry.** Let sheets lie apart until pigment will not stick, then verify count, direction, and recognisable shared design. Clean tools under their product directions and keep the plate. Do not stack damp prints.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

At least two dry recognisable impressions from one plate have true order and selected count, each still showing the shared design. Several stuck wet sheets with unclear successes and failures do not form a finished run.

- **Later impressions fade:** Re-colour each and check water against the proof, not merely use leftover ink.
- **Sheet slides and makes a double image:** Adjust pressure and alignment marks next time; do not count a failed sheet in the neat set.
- **Foam begins to tear:** Stop and record the actual number rather than forcing a target count.
- **Drying space is insufficient:** Work in stages or reduce count, never stack wet sheets.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Use larger paper, clear corner marks, and trusted holding help while you decide each sheet's inclusion.

### Assumptions and limits

Hand impressions differ slightly. A small personal run is not a commercial edition, copyright licence, or value guarantee. It requires a reusable plate; a one-off transfer belongs to the monotype path.
