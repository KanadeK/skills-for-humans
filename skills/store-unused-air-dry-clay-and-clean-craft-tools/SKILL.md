## 中文

当你结束一次小型风干黏土制作，**还有干净未用的开封材料和沾泥工具**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按本品说明密封的可用余料、干净工具和未被泥染坏的工作面。这比 H280 一般“创作收工”具体：风干材料敞口会变硬，且某产品明确不把加过水的泥放回存料袋。

### 准备与输入

先读本包装关于开封、加水余料、密封和清洗的说明；不同品牌不强行共用储存法。备原可复封袋或干净密闭容器、清水、软布/纸和可放干工具的位置。把尚未完成的湿作品与未用余料分开；不要将沾色、沾灰或接触食物的泥混回干净原料。

### 执行

1. **分清干净余料与工作泥。** 未用、没有加水和杂物的材料按包装可复存条件留用；已另加水或被杂物污染的泥遵照本品说明另行处理，不为了省材料塞回主包。
2. **及时封好可存部分。** 把干净余料放回可复封包装或适合的密闭容器，挤出明显多余空气并关严，标材料名和开封日期。容器若漏气，换合用容器，别让泥裸露在桌上。
3. **清工具与护台垫。** 按工具和泥的说明先擦走大块，再用合适水量清洗普通钝工具；擦干并放回固定位置。水彩笔或特殊材质工具按各自说明，不把整件电动设备浸水。
4. **检查桌面与手。** 取走垫板，查桌面有无染色/残泥，按产品允许的清理法处理并洗手。湿作品留在安全晾干位置，不与已密封余料混放。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

可复存的干净余料已封严、工具清洁干燥、桌面无本次残泥，湿作品另有安全位置。盖子轻搭、泥仍敞口，不算完成。

- **余料已加水或混入灰尘：** 不放回干净原包，按产品说明决定能否短时另存或丢弃。
- **密闭袋漏气：** 换合用容器并查封口，标明里面是什么。
- **泥已经硬了：** 不默认加水就能恢复，先查厂家；不能用的按当地普通处理。
- **工具留厚泥难洗：** 尽早按说明擦去，再温和清洗，不用尖刀刮坏工具。
- **台面/衣物被染：** 按材质与厂家污渍指引处理，别用未经确认的强溶剂。

### 假设与边界

Crayola 说明开封未用泥可密封，且其说明书提醒加过水的泥不要作为普通余料储存；这不是对所有品牌的普遍规则。工具清洁也以材质标签为准。本篇不处理危险涂料或公共共享设备维护。

## English

Load this Skill when a small air-dry clay session ends with **clean unused opened material and clay-covered ordinary tools**. Finish with reusable remainder sealed under its own directions, clean tools, and a work surface free of this session's clay. This is more specific than H280's general creative close: opened air-dry material hardens, and a product may exclude water-added clay from storage with the clean remainder.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this package's opened-clay, added-water, sealing, and cleaning rules; brands need not share one storage method. Set out its resealable bag or a clean airtight container, water, soft cloth or paper, and a place to dry tools. Separate unfinished wet art from unused stock. Do not mix coloured, dusty, or food-contact clay back into clean material.

### Execution

1. **Separate clean stock from worked clay.** Keep only unused material free of added water and debris under the package's reusable conditions. Handle water-added or contaminated clay separately under the product's directions instead of stuffing it back into the main pack to save a scrap.
2. **Seal reusable material promptly.** Use the resealable original bag or suitable airtight container, remove obvious excess air, close it fully, and label clay and opening date. Replace a leaking container rather than leaving clay bare on the table.
3. **Clean tools and board.** Under tool and clay directions, wipe away bulk material before washing ordinary blunt tools with suitable water. Dry and return them to a findable place. Follow their own instructions for watercolour brushes or special material; do not immerse an electrical tool.
4. **Check surface and hands.** Lift the protective board, check for marks or residue and clean by material directions, then wash hands. Leave unfinished wet work in its safe drying place, apart from sealed unused stock.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Clean reusable remainder is sealed, tools are clean and dry, the surface is clear, and wet art has a separate safe place. A loosely resting lid over still-exposed clay is unfinished.

- **Remainder has added water or dust:** Do not return it to clean stock; use product directions for separate short-term handling or disposal.
- **Sealing bag leaks air:** Change to a suitable container and check its closure and label.
- **Clay has hardened:** Do not assume added water revives it. Check the maker and dispose of unusable material under local ordinary rules.
- **Tools retain thick clay:** Wipe promptly then wash gently under directions instead of scraping tools with a sharp blade.
- **Surface or clothing stains:** Follow material and maker stain guidance rather than an unverified strong solvent.

### Assumptions and limits

Crayola directs airtight storage for unopened-use remainder and its guide says not to store water-added clay as ordinary leftovers; these are not universal rules for all brands. Tool cleaning follows each material label. Hazardous paints and shared industrial equipment are outside scope.
