## 中文

当橱柜、冰箱或冷冻室里有几件**未开封**的包装食品，你想按日期安排先用顺序时，加载这份 Skill。你要识别标签在该食品适用地区和条件下表示安全截止、品质建议，还是根本不是给消费者读的期限；然后只对仍可按可靠依据使用的食品排序。本篇不为食物发明统一保质期，不判断已经开封的食品，也不把整理顺序当成“过期也要吃”的命令。婴幼儿配方、医疗饮食和需要专门监管的食品不在此普通流程内。

### 准备与输入

准备食品原包装或清晰照片，逐件确认名称、日期前的**完整词语**、日期格式、产品适用的地区说明、储存要求，以及你是否知道实际保存条件。语言本身不能证明地区规则；进口商品也可能另有适用的本地标签。看不清的小字可以放大、用读屏或请可信的人读出原文，但不要只记录孤立的数字。

包装破损、保存温度或时间史不明，先标“条件未证实”。食品已开封时，印在未开封包装上的日期可能不再是充分依据，应转到开封后的保存要求。以下顺序只处理标签和保存条件均可核对的未开封食品。

### 执行

1. **抄对日期的身份。** 把“use by / 此日期或之前食用”“best before / 此日期前最佳”“Best if Used By”、生产日、批次码等按包装原文分开记。批次码或生产日不能自动当食用截止日。日月次序不明时先查该包装或生产商的解释，不自行交换月份和日期。
2. **按适用地区解释，不靠翻译猜。** 香港食物安全中心说明其 `use by` 关乎安全，过了这一天不应食用；`best before` 主要关乎品质。美国 FDA 则说明其包装日期用语没有统一通行的定义，并支持把 `Best if Used By` 用作最佳风味和品质日期。因此，在美国看到 `Use By` 或其他词时，不把香港的安全结论直接套过去；查这件商品的生产商说明与当地适用指引。
3. **先验证日期成立的条件。** 逐件核对包装规定的冷藏、冷冻、常温及其他保存要求是否得到满足。香港的 `use by` 判断也以正确保存为前提；当前标签日期还没到，不能修复已知失温、受潮或破损。条件不明时先停止对该件排序，按保存信息不明的流程处理。
4. **分成四种结果。** 对仍符合条件且有明确安全截止的食品，排在截止前要处理的“优先”组；已超过适用安全截止的食品列“停止”，不进入轮换。只有品质日期的食品列入“品质优先核对”组，日期过去也不自动等于安全或不安全，仍须按包装、状态及当地可靠指导判断。词语或格式无法解释的列“待核”，不猜日期。
5. **摆放但不覆盖原信息。** 同类且仍合适使用的食品，把需要先处理的放到看得见、拿得到的位置；新批次放后面。保留名称、原日期、保存说明和批次信息，不把几个不同批次倒进一个无从追溯的容器。若需要制定餐食或决定冷冻，交给各自的任务。
6. **留下下一次检查点。** 记下要先用的具体食品、依据和待核的问题。按实际购物与使用节奏重查，不把一次排序当成永久安全证书。

### 完成条件

你能给每件食品标出“安全截止前优先 / 超过适用安全截止而停止 / 品质日期需核对 / 待核”，说清依据来自哪一句标签、哪个地区和哪些已确认的保存条件。可使用的食品有可执行的先后顺序；无法解释的日期和保存史没有被猜成安全。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看数字，不看前面的词：** 重新读完整标签；生产日、销售展示日和品质日期不能互换。
- **美国包装写 `Use By`，就套香港规则：** 停止跨地区推断，查实际产品及当地说明。英文相同不等于规则相同。
- **香港 `use by` 已过，但闻着正常：** 不用气味覆盖安全截止；按当地指引停止食用。
- **`best before` 已过，就自动吃或自动丢：** 它不是独立的安全判定。先看包装状态、真实保存条件与当地适用指导，信息不足时待核。
- **开封日期忘了或包装无字可读：** 不补造日期；开封品转开封后决策，缺标签/保存史者单独隔离待核。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

日期词语、写法和监管含义因地区与产品而异；这里只给出香港和美国两种已核实例子，不把中文或英文当成产地。视觉、记忆或可及性受限时，可记录包装照片、用大字或朗读方式保存原文；他人帮助读标签时，你仍要保留可追溯依据。排序可以减少遗忘，却不能延长任何食品的安全条件。

副作用是旧批次可能终于被看见，也可能出现需要向生产商询问的一项。冰箱不会替你解释缩写。

## English

Load this Skill when several **unopened** packaged foods in a cupboard, fridge, or freezer need a use-first order. Identify whether each date, under the product's applicable region and conditions, is a safety deadline, a quality guide, or not a consumer expiry date at all. Then order only food with enough reliable information to use. This Skill invents no universal shelf life, does not assess opened food, and never turns rotation into an order to eat unsafe food. Infant formula, medical diets, and specially regulated foods are outside this everyday flow.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the original packs or clear photos. For each item, confirm its name, the **complete words before the date**, date format, region-specific product information, storage instructions, and what you know of its actual storage. Language alone does not establish the applicable rules; imported food may have another local label. Enlarge print, use reading access, or ask a trusted person to read the exact wording, but do not record only the digits.

Mark damaged packaging or uncertain temperature or storage history as “condition unverified.” If a product has been opened, its printed unopened date may no longer be enough; use the after-opening instructions. The order below is for unopened food whose label and storage conditions can be checked.

### Execution

1. **Identify what the date is.** Record “use by,” “best before,” “Best if Used By,” production dates, and batch codes exactly as printed. A batch code or production date is not automatically a last day to eat. If day/month order is unclear, check the package or producer instead of swapping the two by guesswork.
2. **Interpret under the applicable region.** Hong Kong's Centre for Food Safety says its `use by` is about safety and food should not be eaten after that date; `best before` mainly concerns quality. The US FDA says date wording is not uniform across US packages and supports `Best if Used By` as a best-flavour-and-quality phrase. Do not transfer the Hong Kong safety rule to a US `Use By` label from the English words alone; check the actual producer and local guidance.
3. **Confirm the conditions that make a date useful.** Check whether each item's refrigeration, freezing, ambient, and other package requirements were met. The Hong Kong `use by` guidance depends on correct storage; a date still in the future cannot undo known warming, dampness, or damage. When conditions are unknown, stop ranking that item and use the separate unknown-storage-history decision.
4. **Give each item one of four outcomes.** Put food with a verified safety deadline and valid conditions into “use before deadline”; put food beyond an applicable safety deadline into “stop,” not back into rotation. Food with only a quality date goes into “check quality and conditions” — passing that date alone neither certifies nor condemns it. Put an unreadable or uninterpretable phrase/format in “confirm,” without guessing.
5. **Arrange without covering the original evidence.** Among similar food still eligible for use, place items needing earlier attention where you can see and reach them, with newer batches behind. Keep name, original date, storage directions, and batch information. Do not empty different batches into an untraceable container. Meal planning and deciding whether to freeze are separate tasks.
6. **Set the next check.** Record the specific items to use first, why, and any questions to resolve. Recheck as your shopping and use changes; one sorting session is not a permanent food-safety certificate.

### Success

For each item you can say “use before a safety deadline / stop after an applicable safety deadline / check a quality date / confirm,” with the exact package phrase, applicable region, and verified storage conditions behind it. Eligible foods have a usable order; unclear dates or histories have not been guessed into safety.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You read only digits:** Return to the full label. Production, display, and quality dates do different jobs.
- **A US pack says `Use By`, so you apply Hong Kong's rule:** Stop the cross-region inference and check this product and local guidance. The same English words do not prove the same rule.
- **A Hong Kong `use by` date passed but it smells fine:** Smell does not override that safety deadline. Stop eating it under the applicable guidance.
- **A `best before` date passed, so you automatically eat or discard:** It is not a stand-alone safety test. Check package condition, real storage, and local guidance; leave it unresolved if evidence is missing.
- **You forgot when a pack was opened or its print is unreadable:** Do not invent a date. Send opened food to the after-opening decision and isolate missing-label or unknown-history items for review.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Date phrases, formats, and regulatory meanings vary by region and product. Hong Kong and the US are verified examples here; Chinese or English text is not a country code. For vision, memory, or access needs, retain a package photo or large/accessible reading of the original wording. Help with reading does not replace traceable evidence. Rotation can reduce forgotten stock but cannot extend any safety condition.

Older batches may finally become visible, and one item may require a question to its producer. The refrigerator will not explain abbreviations for you.
