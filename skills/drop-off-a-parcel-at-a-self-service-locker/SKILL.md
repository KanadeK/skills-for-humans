## 中文

你已经完成普通包裹的准入、包装与寄送资料准备，并拿到该服务允许的**自助柜交寄方式**时，用这份 Skill 完成一件包裹的现场投递和凭证核对。与“从柜里取走”不同，你要判断柜格是否合适、包裹是否确实放入、柜门是否关闭，以及系统有没有登记交寄。寄送准备归 H131，商家退货授权归退货 Skill；本稿不指导寄危险品或给每个柜机套一套按钮。

### 准备与输入

从承运方/商家的官方说明核对当前包裹、服务、柜址或允许的柜机范围、有效二维码/码/标签、包装及柜格尺寸限制，并确认交寄尚未过期或被取消。有的服务允许多个柜址，有的取件只能在指定柜址，不能混淆。检查箱体与标签、带走所需凭证；若内容受限、不明或包装未完成，回 H131 或服务方规则处理，不先投柜。包裹太重、过大或你无法安全够到柜格时，找官方可及替代，不硬塞。

### 执行

1. **确认柜机接受这件包裹。** 到现场核对运营方、该服务可用的柜机、交寄码仍有效且有合适空间。不要把普通取件码当交寄码，也不要因为屏幕有“投递”字样就跳过商家或承运方授权。
2. **按官方屏幕/应用打开一个柜格。** 扫官方码或按服务说明输入交寄资料，只操作系统分配的柜门。打开后先看柜格是否空、干净且尺寸足以放入，里面若有别人的件则停止并报官方，不叠放。不要碰锁住的邻格或用第三方工具开门。
3. **放入一件并关门。** 核对自己手里的包裹标签/编号与本次操作一致。让包裹完整进入、关门时不会夹住或挤压；放好后关严柜门，按设备提示完成流程。箱子放不进时不要强压：保持包裹在自己手里，按该运营方“尺寸不符”或改柜格/取消流程处理。不要让系统显示投递完成却仍抱着包裹离开。
4. **取得交寄记录再离开。** 在屏幕、官方 App、短信或邮件中找该服务提供的交寄确认/凭证，记下包裹编号和时间。柜门没关好、确认未出现、柜内原有物品或发现投错柜时，留在安全处按运营方故障渠道报告，提供柜址、时间、编号，别试图自己撬柜取回。交寄确认是服务点收存记录，不是承运商已完成运输或收件人已收到。

### 成功条件

正确的一件普通包裹进入该服务授权且合适的空柜格，门已关，官方系统给出可追溯交寄记录；或因设备/尺寸/授权问题，包裹仍在你手里且你有明确下一步。包裹离手但没有确认时不能宣布成功，需按运营方渠道核实。

### 常见报错与补救

- **扫码失败：** 核对这是不是该服务支持的柜机、码是否仍有效；用官方备用输入方式或客服，不借陌生人账号。
- **没有合适柜格：** 不把箱子硬塞或放在柜外；按该服务允许的其他柜机、柜台或改期办理，费用和期限以实际规则为准。
- **柜格里已有包裹：** 不叠放、不拿走别人的件；保留自己的包裹并报告柜址/编号。
- **关门后没有确认：** 检查官方 App/邮件/短信是否有记录，仍没有就按故障入口报柜址、时间、包裹编号；不重复投另一柜造成双重记录。
- **把错误包裹放进柜里：** 不尝试撬门；立即联系运营方说明实际编号、柜址与时间，按其正式处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一件已获准交寄的普通包裹，不是危险品；包装、标签、费用及商家授权已在投柜前确认。柜机屏幕与码规则随运营方、地区及寄件/退货渠道变化。无法读屏、够门或携带包裹时，使用该方支持的可及柜格、柜台或可信协助，码只按正式授权分享。交寄后应保存凭证并查后续追踪；设备登记不等于最终送达。

## English

Use this Skill after an ordinary parcel has passed item, packing, and address preparation and you have a **locker drop-off method that the service accepts**. Deposit one parcel and verify the result on site. Unlike collecting a parcel, this task checks space, actual placement, closed door, and a registered drop-off. H131 owns shipping preparation; a merchant return requires its own authorization first. This file neither ships dangerous goods nor pretends every locker has identical buttons.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the carrier or merchant's official rules for this parcel, service, allowed locker site or network, valid code/QR/label, packing, and compartment-size limits. Confirm the authorization has not expired or been cancelled. Some services allow multiple drop-off sites while collections use a designated locker; do not mix these rules. Check the closed package and label and keep the details needed for proof. If contents are restricted, uncertain, or unpacked, return to H131 or provider instructions before depositing. For a parcel you cannot lift or a compartment you cannot reach, seek an official accessible alternative rather than forcing it.

### Execution

1. **Confirm this locker accepts the parcel.** Match operator, supported service, live drop-off credential, and suitable available space. A collection code is not automatically a sending code. A “drop-off” screen does not substitute for carrier or merchant authorization.
2. **Open one compartment by official instructions.** Scan the official code or enter drop-off details as instructed, touching only the door allocated by the system. Check that it is empty, usable, and large enough. If another parcel is inside, stop and report it; do not stack yours. Do not pull other locked doors or use a third-party opener.
3. **Deposit one parcel and close.** Compare its label/reference with this transaction. Place it fully inside without trapping or crushing it when the door closes. Close the door firmly and finish the on-screen process. If it does not fit, keep the parcel in your hands and follow that operator's too-small/alternative-compartment/cancellation route. Do not leave with a “deposited” screen while still carrying the parcel.
4. **Find the drop-off record before leaving.** Look for the operator's confirmation or receipt on the screen, official app, text, or email, and keep parcel reference and time. If the door did not close, confirmation is missing, the compartment held another item, or you deposited the wrong parcel, report site, time, and reference through the operator's fault channel. Do not pry the door to retrieve it. A drop-off record is evidence of point handover, not completed transport or recipient delivery.

### Success

The correct ordinary parcel is in one authorized, suitable empty compartment; its door is closed; and the official system supplies a traceable drop-off record. A clear stop with the parcel still in your hands after a size, device, or authorization issue is also valid. If the parcel has left your hands but no confirmation appears, seek operator verification rather than claiming success.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The QR will not scan:** Verify the locker supports this service and the code remains valid; use only the operator's official fallback or support, not another person's account.
- **No suitable compartment:** Do not squeeze the parcel in or leave it outside. Use another permitted locker, staffed point, or later time under that service's real cost and deadline rules.
- **Another parcel is in the opened space:** Do not stack or remove it. Keep yours and report the compartment and site.
- **The door closes without confirmation:** Check the official app, text, or email. If still absent, report site, time, and parcel reference rather than depositing a second time.
- **Wrong parcel was deposited:** Do not pry the door; contact the operator immediately with the actual reference, site, and time.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is one already authorized ordinary parcel, with packing, labels, price, and merchant authorization checked before the locker visit. Screen and code rules differ by operator, region, and sending/return route. If reading the screen, reaching the door, or carrying the parcel is difficult, use a provider-supported accessible compartment, counter, or trusted assistance. Share the credential only as formally permitted. Keep proof and follow later tracking; device registration is not final delivery.
