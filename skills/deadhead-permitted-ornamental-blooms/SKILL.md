## 中文

当一株普通观赏植物的花朵已凋谢，而你有权管理它且不打算保留这些花的种子或果实时，加载这份 Skill。你要完成的是**去掉真正的残花，不切掉未开的花苞或保留用途的种荚**。E001 的受损室内叶片修剪以坏叶为目标，判断和切口不同。

### 准备与输入

核品种资料是否适合摘残花，以及这株是否正在留种、提供鸟类食物或由他人管理。比较至少一朵已开败的花与一枚花苞，确定差异。若植株有刺、刺激性汁液、病斑或位置难以安全触及，暂停并请适当人员处理；准备清洁小剪刀和收集容器。

### 执行

1. **确定目标。** 找褪色、萎缩或已脱落大部分花瓣的花，再看花柄上未开的新芽。若目标可能是保留的种荚或你认不出花苞，先问负责者或查品种图例。
2. **选择该植物允许的方式。** 柔软细小残花可按说明用指尖摘；较硬的花柄用清洁小剪刀。查这一品种应在花头下、下一个芽上方或整根花梗连接处的哪个位置处理，不给所有植物一条万能切线。
3. **只做有限一轮。** 托住花梗，摘/剪目标残花，避开新芽和健康叶。对尚有开放花的小花序只移除已败部分；不能确认时保留，不为整齐一次剪光。
4. **收集并复看。** 将花屑收入容器，依据本地规则及是否有病虫迹象处理，不默认可堆肥。看是否误碰芽或留下明显破损，并记录下次复看时间；不会立刻再开花也不等于操作失败。

### 成功条件

已明确选中的残花被移除，花苞和有意保留的种荚仍在，植株无明显新损伤，废料得到去向。若品种、目标或使用权不明确，保留花朵并停止；这是比误剪更好的完成状态。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把绿小球当残花：** 可能是花苞或留种果实，先辨认，不摘。
- **剪刀碰到新芽：** 停止本轮，记录位置并观察；不继续修成更大的切口。
- **整株花都有斑点：** 暂停普通摘残花，记录并查病虫/环境，不用多剪掩盖。
- **有人想保留种子：** 先取得负责者的决定，留种路径不执行本 Skill 的摘除步骤。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里仅在获准位置处理容易触及的普通观赏花，不做带刺灌木重剪、野外采摘、病原鉴定或农药使用。握力受限可请人用清洁剪刀操作；视力受限可让人指出已败花和新芽。现实副作用是少量花屑，且有些花卉本来就不因摘残花重复开花。

## English

Load this Skill when flowers on a permitted ordinary ornamental plant have faded and you do not intend to keep their seeds or fruit. **Remove actual spent blooms without cutting unopened buds or seedheads that have a purpose.** E001's damaged indoor foliage trim targets a different plant part and decision.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether this species benefits from deadheading and whether the owner is saving seed, leaving fruit for birds, or managing the plant themselves. Compare one spent bloom with one bud. Pause for thorns, irritating sap, disease-like signs, or a location you cannot reach safely. Prepare clean small scissors and a collection container.

### Execution

1. **Identify the target.** Find faded, shrivelled flowers or those losing most petals; look for unopened growth on the same stalk. If the target may be a valued seedhead or you cannot tell a bud from it, ask the person responsible or check species illustrations.
2. **Choose a permitted method.** Pinch a soft small spent bloom if the species allows, or use clean scissors on a firmer stalk. Check whether this species needs a cut below the head, above the next bud, or at the flower-stalk junction. There is no one cut line for all plants.
3. **Do one bounded pass.** Support the stalk and remove only selected spent blooms, avoiding buds and healthy leaves. On a cluster still holding open flowers, remove only spent parts; keep what you cannot identify rather than cutting the whole display for tidiness.
4. **Collect and review.** Put debris in a container and use local and pest/disease-aware disposal rules; compost is not automatic. Check for an accidentally touched bud or torn tissue and set a later review. A new flower need not appear immediately for the operation to have been correct.

### Success

The chosen spent flowers are gone, buds and deliberately retained seedheads remain, the plant has no obvious new damage, and debris has a route. If species, target, or permission is uncertain, leave the flower in place and stop; that is better than a mistaken cut.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A small green structure looks like a spent flower:** It may be a bud or seedpod; identify before pinching.
- **Scissors touch a new bud:** Stop, record the place, and observe instead of expanding the cut.
- **Many flowers show spots:** Pause routine deadheading and investigate environment or pests; more cutting does not establish a cause.
- **Someone wants to collect seed:** Obtain their decision and leave the relevant seedheads.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is for a reachable ordinary ornamental in a permitted location, not heavy pruning of thorny shrubs, wild collecting, pathogen diagnosis, or pesticides. Ask for clean-scissor help if grip is limited and for a bud/spent-flower description if vision is limited. Some debris remains, and some plants do not rebloom after deadheading.
