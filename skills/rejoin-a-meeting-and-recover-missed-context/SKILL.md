## 中文

当你已从普通线上会议**完全掉出**，后来能重新加入，且担心错过决定、任务或提问时，加载这份 Skill。结果是确认自己实际在场的时间和错过的关键内容，再继续参与或安排后续。连接还在但断续时，用本域的“换可用通道”Skill；这里不做网络维修。

### 准备与输入

保留原会议邀请和已认可的联络方式，别从陌生即时消息里的新链接仓促重入。核对会议标题、时间和组织者。看应用是否显示“重新加入”；若原邀请有电话拨入选项，只在该会议、地区和费用条件允许时使用。若有组织的会议信息或隐私规则，按其要求处理纪要与聊天内容。

### 执行

1. **确认你真的离开了。** 查看会议界面状态，记下大致掉线时间。若只是画面卡顿但仍能收发，用在会内降级流程；不要反复开多个相同会议连接。
2. **从可信原入口重入。** 用原邀请或应用内会议列表，确认当前会议身份。若要换设备，先弄清旧设备是否仍在会议中，避免两路音频回声。需要主持人批准时等待实际批准，不把等待室写成已回会。
3. **验证重入能双向沟通。** 向主持人或会议文字发一句“我刚才掉线，现在回来了；能听到/看到第 X 项吗？”收到实际回应后再按当前允许方式参与。重入失败时用已约定的独立渠道通知并决定改用文字、电话或不参加余下部分。
4. **补回最小必要事实。** 只问断线期间是否形成与你有关的决定、分派、截止点或需要你回答的问题；可先看有权访问的会议文字或纪要，再向一位相关人核对。不要要求所有人从头重开，也不要把聊天流的部分片段当完整结论。
5. **校正自己的记录。** 在笔记里标明“掉线时段未在场”，对听来的决定注明来自谁或哪个许可记录。确有你负责的动作时复述负责人、下一步和时间，请相关人确认；若无法核实，标“待确认”而不是擅自执行。
6. **结束未解决部分。** 若会议已结束或你仍无法稳定重入，向主持人约定一个可行的简短补录渠道。任何没有核对的同意、任务或截止都不要从沉默中推定。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你已从原入口重入并能双向沟通，且把掉线时段涉及自己的关键决定补到可核对状态；或你已通知无法重入并约定补录方式。重入图标亮了但没人能听到你，不算完成。

- **反复进入等待室：** 看主持人是否仍在线并通过原渠道联系；不要连续点陌生替代链接。
- **两台设备同时发声：** 静音或离开多余的一路，只留一条音频。
- **聊天里有一句似乎交给你：** 问清是谁决定、内容和期限；片段不是完整的任务交接。
- **会议已结束：** 不假装参加了后半段，询问许可的纪要或相关人的简要核对。
- **无法访问字幕、纪要或听力不便：** 请求可访问文字或口头补录，并明确哪些信息还未确认；不要为了补全而未经允许录制会议。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

重新加入、等待室、电话拨入和会议记录随软件及组织权限而异。你只处理自己有权看的普通会议信息；没有记录权限就问相关人员，不绕过限制。诚实标记缺席片段，可能多花几分钟，却能避免把猜测写成决定。

## English

Load this Skill after you have **fully dropped out** of an ordinary online meeting and can later rejoin, but may have missed a decision, assignment, or question. Confirm when you were present and recover the key missing context before continuing or acting. If you remain in a choppy meeting, use the separate working-channel Skill. This does not repair the network.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the original invitation and an agreed contact route. Do not rush into a new link from an unfamiliar instant message. Check meeting title, time, and organiser. See whether the app offers Rejoin. Use a phone option from the original invitation only if that meeting, region, and possible charges allow it. Follow any organisation rules for meeting notes and chat.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the full drop.** Check the meeting state and note roughly when you disconnected. If only video froze while you can still send and receive, use the in-meeting fallback instead of opening repeated connections.
2. **Rejoin from a trusted original entry.** Use the original invitation or in-app meeting list and verify meeting identity. When switching device, find out whether the old device remains connected so you do not create two audio paths. Wait for actual host approval if a lobby appears; a waiting room is not rejoining.
3. **Prove two-way contact.** Tell the host or meeting chat, “I dropped out and am back; can you hear/see me on item X?” Continue through a permitted channel only after a real response. If rejoin fails, notify them through the agreed independent route and decide on text, phone, or leaving the rest.
4. **Recover only needed facts.** Ask whether the gap contained a decision, assignment, deadline, or question that involves you. Read permitted chat or notes first if available, then confirm with one relevant person. Do not make everyone restart, and do not treat one chat fragment as a full conclusion.
5. **Correct your own record.** Mark the gap “not present” in your notes. Attribute second-hand decisions to the person or permitted record that supplied them. If an action is yours, repeat the owner, next step, and timing for confirmation. Mark anything unconfirmed “to confirm” instead of acting on a guess.
6. **Close an unresolved gap.** If the meeting ended or rejoining remains unstable, agree with the host on a short accessible follow-up. Never infer consent, an assignment, or a deadline from silence during your absence.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You rejoined through the original route, regained two-way communication, and checked the decisions that affected you during the gap; or you told the host rejoining failed and arranged a follow-up. A glowing Rejoin icon without working communication is insufficient.

- **You repeatedly reach a lobby:** Check whether the host is still present and contact them through the original route. Do not click a series of unfamiliar replacement links.
- **Two devices play meeting audio:** Mute or leave the extra path so only one audio path remains.
- **Chat seems to assign you something:** Confirm who decided, the action, and its timing. A fragment is not a complete handoff.
- **The meeting has ended:** Do not claim attendance for the missed portion; request a permitted record or a brief check with a relevant person.
- **Captions or notes are inaccessible, or hearing is difficult:** Request accessible text or a spoken recap and mark remaining unknowns. Do not record the meeting without permission just to fill the gap.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Rejoin, lobby, phone, and meeting records depend on the app and organisation. Handle only ordinary meeting information you may access. Ask the relevant person when a record is unavailable rather than bypassing a restriction. Marking your absence honestly takes minutes but keeps guessed decisions out of the record.
