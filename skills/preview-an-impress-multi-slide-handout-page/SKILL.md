## 中文

你想把短 Impress 演示稿作为纸质讲义准备，先检查每张纸排几张幻灯片是否仍能读清时使用本篇。成果是一页多张的打印预览，页序、标题和关键图表仍可辨，没有意外打印讲者备注或隐藏页。这里停在预览，不擅自使用打印机或宣称纸本已交付。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 副本，先决定每页放两张还是更多，记下预期可见幻灯片数与首末标题。检查备注、批注和隐藏页是否不该出现在讲义中。若图片或小字本来就难读，合排可能应减少每纸页数。

### 执行

1. 在 Handout/Print 设置选择讲义类型与每纸页数，不选择 Notes 类型。
2. 打开预览，核顺序、首末页、标题和一处最小关键图表仍可读。
3. 检查预览边缘没有被裁、备注和不该出现的隐藏页不在纸面；不合适就调少每纸页数。
4. 保留预览设置或记录选择，退出打印前核并未实际下发打印任务。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

多张合排预览顺序正确、关键内容可读、无不该出现的备注，未执行实际打印。

- **字太小：** 减少每纸页数而不是继续压缩。
- **备注混入：** 核类型是否误选 Notes。
- **最后一张漏：** 核打印范围与隐藏状态。

### 假设与边界

仅验证软件预览，不保证实际纸张、打印机或观众阅读效果。你决定披露内容和纸张使用；没有现场验收。

## English

Use this Skill when preparing a short Impress deck as a paper handout and you need to see whether several slides per sheet remain readable. Finish with a multi-slide print preview whose order, titles and key figures are discernible and that does not unexpectedly print speaker notes or hidden slides. Stop at preview; do not claim paper was printed or delivered.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP copy, choose an initial two-or-more slides per sheet, and note expected visible count and first/last titles. Inspect Notes, comments and hidden slides for exclusion. If images or type are already small, use fewer slides per sheet.

### Execution

1. Choose Handout in print settings with intended slides per sheet, not Notes output.
2. Open preview and check order, first/last slides, titles and smallest essential chart.
3. Check edges, Notes and unintended hidden slides; reduce slides per sheet when unreadable.
4. Keep or note settings, exit before printing and confirm no actual print job was sent.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Multi-slide preview has correct order, readable essentials, no unwanted notes and no actual print job.

- **Text too small:** Use fewer slides per sheet.
- **Notes leak:** Check output type is Handout.
- **Last slide missing:** Check range and hidden state.

### Assumptions and limits

This checks software preview only, not physical paper, printer or audience readability. You choose disclosure and paper use; no live acceptance is claimed.
