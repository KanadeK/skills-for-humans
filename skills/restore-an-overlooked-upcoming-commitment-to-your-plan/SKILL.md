## 中文

当你发现**已经答应的普通安排还未开始，却漏在自己的日历或待办里**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是从原约定核真实时间、地点、对象和自己要准备的事，补回个人计划，并检查遗漏是否已经影响他人。若时间已经过去，归 H268；若与另一项真实承诺撞时间，先按本域“双订冲突”Skill处理。

### 准备与输入

找最初答应的消息、邀请或组织方更新，别凭“我好像记得周三”补日历。确认这项确实已成立，还是只是对方提出、你尚未答复；仅邀请时按对应邀约任务处理。核当前所用日历账号和是否藏了某个分组，免得因视图没显示就新建重复条目。只处理普通非紧急安排，不决定服务罚款、合同或人事后果。

### 执行

1. **从原约定重建事实。** 写日期、当地时间/时区、地点或线上入口、时长、谁负责确认和你答应带/做什么；有更新就按可追的最新有效通知。缺核心信息时向相应联系人问，不编填空。
2. **先找是否真的漏记。** 在你用的相关日历/清单按日期、关键词和被隐藏的分组查看一次；若已经在另一账号或日历，恢复可见性或只保留一处权威个人记录，不叠加两个提醒制造混淆。
3. **补一条准确的个人记录。** 在自己可访问的计划工具里记原约定的时间地点和来源，设置一项对自己有用的回看点。只改自己的记录；不要误以为新建个人日历项会通知组织者或自动替别人更新邀请。需要共享信息时另走有权的正式渠道。
4. **核遗漏造成的实际影响。** 看这个时段现在是否另排了事、准备材料是否来得及、是否有人因你未记录而等不到答复。无影响时不必为了“完成流程”再发一条多余确认；若出现冲突，走双订处理；若准备不能按时完成，及时准确告知受影响的人并提出可行选项。
5. **完成一次可用性检查。** 从个人计划里重新找到该安排，核时间、地点、来源和下一步；该安排真正发生或变更前，按后续实际消息更新，而非让旧条目冒充当前确认。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你有一条从原约定核过、此刻能找回的个人安排，并清楚知道是否需要另行告知受影响的人。只把“有个事”写进提醒、却没日期和联系人，不算恢复安排。

- **另一个日历里已存在：** 显示正确分组或指定它为个人真源，不因没看见就复制。
- **原通知的时间已变：** 查组织方最新有效更新，在个人记录标来源，不靠旧截图。
- **对方只是邀约未得到你的答复：** 不当成已承诺，先答复邀请；“加入日历”不能替代同意。
- **一补日历就发现重叠：** 不把第二项悄悄删掉，核两边状态并用双订冲突流程通知。
- **视觉/记忆或提醒形式不适合：** 用可读屏、语音或纸面方式记同样的时间与来源，并设自己可用的回看点。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

不同日历会按账号、订阅和响应设置显示或隐藏事件；个人记录不是他人已收到确认的证据。提醒时间按你的交通与准备情况设，不规定通用提前几分钟。遗漏本身可能没有影响，也可能暴露真实冲突；先核事实再沟通，免得用多余消息制造新混乱。

## English

Load this Skill when you realise an **ordinary commitment you already accepted has not begun but is missing from your own calendar or task plan**. Check the original agreement for time, place, people, and preparation, restore it to your personal plan, and see whether the omission affected anyone. A time already passed belongs to H268. A real overlap with another promise uses the separate double-booking Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the original acceptance, invitation, or organiser update. Do not fill the calendar from “I think it was Wednesday.” Check that the plan was agreed rather than merely offered without your reply. For an invitation only, use the invitation-response task. Check your current calendar account and hidden groups so you do not create a duplicate because one view did not show it. This is for ordinary nonurgent plans, not fee, contract, or employment decisions.

### Execution

1. **Reconstruct facts from the agreement.** Record date, local time or time zone, place or online entry, duration, who confirms details, and what you agreed to bring or do. Use a traceable latest valid update. Ask the relevant person for missing essentials instead of guessing.
2. **Check whether it was truly omitted.** Search relevant calendars or lists once by date, keyword, and hidden group. If an item exists in another account or calendar, make that view visible or designate one personal source instead of stacking duplicate reminders.
3. **Add one accurate personal record.** In a planning tool you can access, record the agreed time and place and source, and set a useful return point. Change only your own record. A new personal event does not automatically tell the organiser or update everyone's invitation. Share changes separately through an authorised route when needed.
4. **Check the real effect of the omission.** See whether another task now occupies the time, preparation is still feasible, or anyone waited for your response because your plan was missing. If nothing was affected, do not send a needless confirmation just to complete a procedure. For a clash, use double-booking recovery. If preparation cannot be done in time, tell affected people accurately and offer feasible options.
5. **Check retrievability.** Find the event again in your personal plan and verify time, place, source, and next step. Update it from later real messages before it happens or changes, rather than treating an old entry as current confirmation.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You have one personal event checked against the agreement that you can find now, and know whether someone needs a separate notice. A reminder saying only “something happening” without a time or contact has not restored the plan.

- **It already exists in another calendar:** Show the correct group or make it your personal source; do not copy just because it was hidden.
- **The original time changed:** Check the organiser's latest valid update and note its source rather than using an old screenshot.
- **It was only an invitation, not your acceptance:** Reply first. Adding it to your calendar is not consent.
- **Adding it reveals an overlap:** Do not silently delete the other item. Check both states and notify through the double-booking process.
- **Visual, memory, or reminder format does not fit:** Use accessible calendar text, speech, or paper with the same time and source and a return point you can use.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Calendar account, subscription, and RSVP settings change which events are shown. Your personal record is not proof another person received confirmation. Choose reminder timing from your own travel and preparation, not a universal number of minutes. The omission may have no external effect or reveal a real clash. Check facts before adding messages that create confusion.
