## 中文

当你的一件普通衣物表面粘着**松散的外来浮毛、纸屑或轻微绒屑**，想在穿前或收存前整理外观时，用这份 Skill 轻轻取走它们。目标是表面不再明显粘毛，原有纤维、绒面和装饰没有被带走。衣物自身纤维结成、附着在布面的毛球不是“多滚几次就会走”的浮毛；那是另一种修整任务。

### 准备与输入

确认衣物干燥、洗护标签或制造商说明允许表面刷理/粘取，并看看它是平滑梭织、绒面、松针织还是有刺绣/印花。用手指轻触一小处：浮毛能轻移开，还是与原纤维连着？准备干净粘毛滚、适用的柔软衣刷或有说明可用于该面料的胶带，放在光线足够、能平放衣服的位置。贵重、极松散、特殊涂层或工具适用性不明时先停查制造商；不在正穿着的衣物上用强粘物。

### 执行

1. **先排除其他问题。** 看见的是可移动的外来毛/纸屑，还是团在原布上的小球、粉状洗剂残留、颜色变化、破丝或洞？只有松散浮毛继续。粉末/色变属于洗后外观问题，连接着的毛球走 H064 的另一 Skill，洞或勾丝要先停，不能用滚筒把损伤扩大。
2. **在不显眼处试工具。** 衣物平放、不拉紧，用适合这块面料的柔软刷或粘毛滚轻触一个小角。观察工具是否带走原布纤维、留下胶或拉起印花；有任何一项就停止并换更温和、经制造商允许的方法。
3. **轻取表面浮毛。** 一手稳住衣物，用小范围、轻压力向同一方向刷或滚，抬起工具再换位置；避开松装饰、线头和易勾处。粘纸失效就换干净一层，不用力来补偿旧粘纸。没有适用工具时，只拣能轻轻拿起的较大纸屑，不用刀片刮。
4. **停在合理外观。** 对着光看最显眼的区域是否改善；仍有与布纤维连着的颗粒不继续猛滚。收走浮毛并按工具说明处理废粘纸或刷子。若同一衣物每次洗后都大量粘毛，可另查分桶与洗后问题，不把今天的滚筒当洗衣流程修复。

### 成功条件

表面松散浮毛明显减少，衣物原有纤维和装饰没有被拉坏，工具无胶残留或新勾丝。若面料不允许/不耐受工具，识别并停止同样是完成判断；不为了“零毛”牺牲衣物。

### 常见报错与补救

- **滚筒带走了原布的绒：** 立即停用；确认这是不是面料本来的表面，而不是外来浮毛，查制造商方法。
- **胶带或滚筒留下粘痕：** 停止再叠胶，不用另一种清洁剂直接擦；按面料和工具说明或请专业护理处理。
- **纸屑和毛球混在一起：** 先轻取可移动的部分；附着毛球按可剪修纤维的任务另行判断。
- **越刷越多线头：** 停止摩擦，检查勾丝或松边；表面破损不是继续刷掉的对象。
- **今天取完明天又满是毛：** 记录可能的掉毛来源与洗涤搭配，转分桶/洗后问题；不要把持续产毛误判为这次没滚够。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理普通衣物上松散、可机械轻取的浮毛，不教去污、重新水洗或切削毛球。绒面、长毛、涂层和装饰面可能天然不适合粘毛滚，工具的制造商说明与衣物材质优先。视觉受限时可用明亮灯、放大镜或可信的人确认是否真的改善；握力受限时衣物平放、小面积滚动。粘纸产生少量垃圾，但过度粘取可能丢掉本来属于衣物的纤维。

## English

Use this Skill when **loose outside lint, paper bits, or light fuzz** sit on an ordinary garment and you want to tidy it before wearing or storing. Lift the loose material gently. The result is a visibly cleaner surface without removing original fibres, nap, or trim. Pills made from the garment's own fibres and attached to it are a different surface task; repeated rolling does not turn them into loose lint.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the garment is dry, its care or maker guidance permits surface brushing or adhesive lifting, and whether the face is smooth woven, napped, loosely knitted, printed, or embroidered. Touch a small spot: does the fuzz move freely, or is it connected to the fabric? Have a clean lint roller, suitable soft garment brush, or maker-approved tape and a well-lit place to lay the item flat. Stop for valuable, very loose, coated, or tool-uncertain materials until the maker's advice is known. Do not press strong adhesive on a garment being worn.

### Execution

1. **Rule out another problem.** Is this movable outside fuzz or paper, an attached fibre ball, detergent powder, discolouration, a pulled thread, or a hole? Continue only for loose lint. Powder or colour belongs to post-wash appearance work; attached pills take H064's other Skill. Stop for a snag or hole instead of making it larger with a roller.
2. **Trial the tool out of sight.** With the garment supported flat, lightly touch a small inconspicuous area using a compatible soft brush or roller. Watch for original fibres lifting, adhesive residue, or raised print. If any occurs, stop and seek a gentler method allowed by the maker.
3. **Lift loose material lightly.** Steady the cloth with one hand. Brush or roll small areas with light pressure in one direction, lifting the tool before moving to the next area. Avoid loose trim, threads, and snag-prone places. Replace a spent adhesive sheet instead of pressing harder. Without a suitable tool, pick up only large loose paper bits by hand; do not scrape with a blade.
4. **Stop at a useful appearance.** Check the most visible areas under light. Do not keep rolling particles still attached to the cloth. Dispose of collected lint and spent sheets or clean the brush as its maker directs. If the same item comes back covered after every wash, investigate sorting and post-wash causes separately; today's roller is not a repair to the laundering process.

### Success

Loose surface lint is visibly reduced without pulling original fibres or trim, leaving glue, or adding a snag. Identifying an unsuitable fabric and stopping is also complete. Zero visible fluff is not worth damaging the garment.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The roller lifts the garment's own nap:** Stop at once. Determine whether the surface is the original fabric rather than outside lint, and check the maker's method.
- **Tape or roller leaves sticky residue:** Do not layer more adhesive or wipe on an unrelated cleaner. Use fabric and tool guidance or professional care.
- **Paper bits and pills are mixed:** Lift movable bits first; assess attached pills under the separate fibre-trimming task.
- **Brushing raises more loose threads:** Stop friction and inspect a snag or weak edge. Damage is not something to keep brushing away.
- **Lint returns after the next wash:** Record possible shedders and wash pairing, then use sorting or post-wash guidance rather than more pressure today.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers loose material on ordinary clothing that can be lifted gently; it does not teach stain treatment, rewashing, or cutting attached pills. Nap, long fibres, coatings, and trim may rule out a roller; garment and tool maker instructions control the choice. Use strong light, magnification, or trusted help if sight makes it hard to tell what moved. Work in small flat areas if grip is limited. A used adhesive sheet creates waste, while over-lifting can remove fibres that belonged to the garment.
