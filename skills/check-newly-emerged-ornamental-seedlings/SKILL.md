## 中文

当你的一盘室内观赏种子**已经出现第一批幼苗**，原来的发芽遮盖、摆放和给水状态可能不再合适时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按本品种说明核幼苗实际的光、空气与介质湿度，并留下下一次检查点。它不是 E001 已成形盆栽的浇水，也不诊断病害、施药或保证小苗活下去。

### 准备与输入

找回播种标签和包装，确认品种、播种日期、发芽后要求与本次可用光源。看有多少格已出苗、是否还盖着透明/遮光罩、介质表面和格底水分、托盘是否积水；只看最湿表层容易漏掉内部干燥。若设备、幼苗或水靠近不安全的电气部件，先停并安全隔离，不进行设备改装。

### 执行

1. **按品种核罩盖阶段。** 包装或可靠同品种说明若要求出苗后移除/逐步开启发芽罩，就照说明操作并标未出苗格另待观察；罩上大量凝水或小苗碰罩时，不继续把它当永久温室。未知时先查来源，不用一掀一盖反复试运气。
2. **给可持续而合适的光。** 核这类幼苗的光需求和当前真实位置；已装好的安全灯或自然光可以按说明使用，但不在本篇安装电气灯具或猜通用照射小时。幼苗明显朝一侧拉长、发白或光无法提供时，改到合规可用位置或请园艺支持，不把它和成熟盆栽摆放规则混同。
3. **查介质而非日历。** 用手指、可用小工具或盆重在不伤根的位置核介质是否该补水；根据品种和介质说明轻柔给水，不冲倒细茎。保证孔仍能排水、托盘积水倒掉；表面一层雾湿不代表根区得到水，泡水也不等于照顾更周到。
4. **记录这轮的小苗状态。** 标已出苗格、仍待发芽格、光/罩/水调整和下次查看时点；若成片倒伏、根部异常或霉状物明显，先把盘与其他苗分开并向可靠推广/园艺机构核原因，不自己喷药或大幅加热补救。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你知道这盘哪些格真正出苗，并按本品种条件处理罩盖、光、介质水分与排水，留下下一次检查。幼苗之后继续长高才可评价长期效果；今天调整完不能宣称全盘健康保证。

- **罩还密闭且苗已顶到它：** 核包装并按获准方式打开/移除，避免一直压着新苗。
- **只喷表面、格底仍干：** 核更深处和排水，再按说明补到介质可用状态。
- **托盘积水：** 倒掉余水，检查孔与给水量，不让细根长期泡着。
- **小苗细长或倒伏：** 留记录并核光/水/空气；严重或不明原因找可靠园艺支持，不猜化学处理。
- **看不清小苗或难以移动托盘：** 用放大、桌面高度、轻盘或可信现场协助，身份和根区状态仍需核。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一盘少量非食用观赏植物的幼苗，品种及包装可查。不同种子出苗时间、光照和湿度要求不同，不用统一天数/水量/灯距。既有安全光源不足时可以暂停扩大播种或请合格园艺人员建议，不在本篇接线装灯。一次观察可能发现要调整，也可能只记录“保持原状”。

## English

Load this Skill once an indoor ornamental seed tray **has its first visible seedlings** and its germination cover, placement, or water may need changing. Check actual light, air, and medium moisture against this variety's guidance and leave a next review point. This is not E001's established houseplant watering, nor disease diagnosis, pesticide use, or survival guarantee.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the sowing label and packet for variety, date, and post-emergence directions, plus the light actually available. Note which cells emerged, whether a clear or dark cover remains, medium surface and lower moisture, and tray runoff. A wet top can hide dry lower medium. If water and seedlings sit near unsafe electrical parts, stop and separate safely rather than modify equipment.

### Execution

1. **Check the cover stage for this variety.** When packet or reliable same-variety guidance says to remove or progressively open a germination cover after emergence, do so, while marking cells still to emerge. Heavy condensation or seedlings pressing a lid means it is no longer a permanent greenhouse. If directions are unknown, verify instead of repeatedly lifting and replacing it blindly.
2. **Supply sustainable suitable light.** Check seedling light needs against the real spot. Existing safely installed light or natural light may be used under its guidance, without wiring a lamp or guessing universal hours. For obvious leaning, stretching, paleness, or inadequate available light, move to an allowed usable place or ask horticultural support; mature-houseplant placement rules do not automatically fit seedlings.
3. **Check medium, not the calendar.** Use a finger, suitable small tool, or pot weight away from fragile roots to judge whether water is due under variety and medium directions. Water gently so stems are not washed down. Keep holes draining and empty tray runoff. A surface mist is not root moisture, and a puddle is not extra care.
4. **Record the tray state.** Mark emerged and pending cells, changes to light, cover, or moisture, and when to inspect next. If many seedlings collapse or roots or mould look abnormal, separate the tray from others and consult reliable extension or horticultural guidance. Do not spray a product or add strong heat on a guess.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You know which cells actually emerged and have handled cover, light, medium moisture, and drainage under this variety's guidance, with a next check. Later growth is needed to judge long-term effect. One adjustment today cannot certify the whole tray healthy.

- **Seedlings touch a closed cover:** Check packet and open or remove it by the permitted route instead of pressing new growth under it.
- **Only the surface is misted while the cell stays dry:** Check deeper medium and drainage, then water as directed.
- **The tray holds water:** Empty it and check holes and amount rather than soaking fine roots.
- **Seedlings stretch or collapse:** Record and check light, water, and air; ask reliable horticultural support for serious or unknown causes instead of guessing chemical treatment.
- **Small seedlings or tray lifting are inaccessible:** Use magnification, table-height work, a light tray, or trusted help while still checking identity and root-area state.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a small tray of non-food ornamentals with known packet identity. Emergence timing, light, and moisture vary; no universal days, volume, or lamp distance applies. If existing safe light is insufficient, pause more sowing or ask qualified horticultural help rather than install electrical equipment here. A check may call for adjustment or simply “keep as is.”
