## 中文

你想检验一个原创小形状能否从姿态 A 变到姿态 B，而不是立刻做完整短片时，先做两帧动作试片。结果是时间线上能明确点到两个不同姿态，播放顺序与计划一致，背景和草图不被替换。两帧只测试动作方向，不能代表流畅动画或成品视频。

### 准备与输入

先保存 .kra，切换 Animation 工作区，准备透明的动画绘画层和与它分开的背景层。写下姿态 A、B 最明显的区别和想要的先后时点；如果当前层是不透明白底，先另建透明层以便以后使用洋葱皮。

### 执行

1. 在时间线目标起点创建第一帧，画只含必要轮廓的原创姿态 A。
2. 到第二个时点，按需要创建空白帧或复制帧，再明确改变形状位置或姿态成为 B；核 A 没被覆盖。
3. 来回点击两帧看顺序，使用预览播放确认变化方向和停留时间不是意外跳到空白。
4. 保存并重开 .kra，再点选两个帧格核姿态都能找回；若复制后没变化，回 B 帧继续画。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

同一动画层上存在两个可分辨的姿态帧，顺序正确，背景稳定且原稿重开仍保留时间线。

- **第二帧空白：** 检查建的是空白还是复制帧，按计划补画 B。
- **第一帧被改掉：** 撤销或从保存稿恢复 A，再确认当前时点。
- **背景跟着跳：** 把背景放非动画层，检查其可见性。

### 假设与边界

这只是本地两帧练习，不包含音频、长片、跨软件渲染或公开发布。帧速率和具体时长按本次视觉目标设置，没有通用流畅度承诺。

## English

Use this when a simple original shape needs a first motion check from pose A to pose B, before making a full animation. Finish with two distinct poses you can select on the timeline in the intended order, while background and sketches remain intact. Two frames test direction, not smooth motion or a finished movie.

### Preparation and inputs

Save the .kra, switch to Animation workspace and prepare a transparent animated paint layer separate from background. State the visible difference between pose A and B and their order. If the active layer has an opaque white background, create a transparent drawing layer so onion skin can work later.

### Execution

1. Create the first frame at the intended start in the timeline and draw a minimal original pose A.
2. At the second time point create a blank or duplicated frame as appropriate, then visibly change position or pose into B while confirming A remains intact.
3. Click between frames and play preview to confirm the motion direction and hold timing, avoiding an accidental blank interval.
4. Save and reopen the .kra, revisit both frame cells to confirm their poses persist; if a duplicated B never changed, revise B.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The same animation layer contains two distinguishable poses in the intended order, with stable background and timeline retained after reopen.

- **Second frame blank:** Check blank versus duplicated frame and draw B as planned.
- **A overwritten:** Undo or restore A from the saved master and verify current time.
- **Background jumps:** Keep background on a nonanimated layer and inspect visibility.

### Assumptions and limits

This is a local two-frame exercise, without audio, long-form production, cross-app rendering or public release. Frame rate and hold length depend on this tiny visual goal; smoothness is not promised.
