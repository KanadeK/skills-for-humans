## 中文

当你按制造商说明清洗并晾干可重复使用餐盒后，发现**密封槽仍潮、有可见残留/异味，或盖与密封圈不能正常贴合**时，用这份 Skill 判断是可再次按说明处理、需要原厂部件，还是暂停食物用途。它从普通清洗**没有得到可用状态**时开始，不把餐盒的气味、颜色或“盖上去的手感”当微生物/防漏认证。

### 准备与输入

找到餐盒准确系列与部件图，核对它本来声称适合干食、液体、冷藏、微波还是仅临时携带；看盒身、盖、圈、槽和卡扣的清洁/更换说明。准备干净明亮台面，记录当前水滴、食物残留、染色、变形、裂缝或缺件。明显霉斑、化学品/污水污染或无法辨认材质时停止普通家用补救，按当地/专业与厂家建议处理。

### 执行

1. **先分清残留与材料变化。** 可见食物碎屑、油膜、洗剂泡沫或槽中水需要进一步清洁/干燥；单纯已洗净的旧色印不自动说明有食物残留，但也不靠颜色宣布安全。查洗前照片和厂家说明，无法判断就暂不装食物。
2. **逐件检查贴合。** 在全部部件已经干燥后，按厂家图确认密封圈方向、卡槽位置、盖板与锁扣动作。圈伸出槽、扭曲、裂开或缺失，盖体翘曲/裂纹、锁扣无法自然闭合时停止把它当原本的防漏/密闭容器；不能靠硬压或胶水临时“修”食品接触结构。
3. **只采用厂家支持的下一步。** 对可清洁的残留按盒/圈各自材料说明再洗/干燥；对允许更换的圈或盖，取得适配该型号的部件；对持久异味、染色或材料劣化，询问厂家是否有专用处理与适用边界。不要把不同强力去渍剂、漂白剂和酸混用，或用超出耐热的热水试图“杀掉味道”。
4. **有限验证可用范围。** 只有该型号原本宣称可装液体且厂家说明/结构允许时，复装后可在水槽上用清水做一次可控制的短暂闭合检查；漏水即退出液体携带用途。一次不漏也不证明卫生、长期密封或食品安全时间；不宣称原本只装干食的盒子突然防漏。
5. **决定归队或退出。** 残留已去、各件干燥完好并按原方向就位且用途与说明相符，才可进入下一次装食物计划；继续渗水、无法清洁的槽或明显破损时隔离并查厂家更换/当地回收。食物保存时长仍由食品本身和冷链决定，不由一只盖子的合上声决定。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你能说清是可洗/可干的残留、可能无法逆转的材料变化，还是圈/盖实际损伤，并已选择受支持的再次处理、原厂部件或暂停食品用途。异常盒子没有因盖得上就混回安全存放容器。

- **槽里还有水，却急着扣盖：** 拆开按说明继续干燥，避免把水封在看不到的位置。
- **盖子一角总是翘起：** 核对圈方向与型号；不强压、不对装液体作防漏承诺。
- **洗后仍有颜色但摸不到油膜：** 先查材料与厂家说明，不加第二种清洁剂碰运气，也不以淡色/深色独立判断可食用性。
- **水槽清水试验漏了：** 停止装液体，查适配部件或更换，不能用外袋包住就当修好密封圈。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只针对普通可重复使用的食品盒后处理，不诊断霉菌、食源性疾病，也不修理保温罐/电热饭盒。不同厂家“防漏”声明只适用于对应型号和完好装配；含水试验只查眼前漏水，不替代食品安全或长期密闭证明。看不清槽与小圈可用放大照片/可信协助，手部力量不便时不以强扣证明正常。停用可能意味着需要另找适用容器。

## English

Use this Skill after cleaning and drying a reusable food box by its maker's directions when **a groove remains wet, visible residue or odour persists, or the lid and gasket do not seat normally**. Decide whether another directed cleaning, a correct maker part, or a pause from food use is needed. It begins when routine cleaning has **not** yielded a ready container. Smell, colour, or the feel of a closed lid is not a microbiological or leak certificate.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the exact collection and parts diagram. Check whether it was meant for dry food, liquid, refrigeration, microwave use, or only short carrying. Read body, lid, gasket, channel, and latch care or replacement directions. Use a clean bright surface and record water, food residue, staining, distortion, cracks, or missing parts. For visible mold, chemical or sewage contact, or an unidentifiable material, stop ordinary home recovery and use local, professional, or maker guidance.

### Execution

1. **Separate residue from material change.** Visible crumbs, grease film, soap suds, or pooled groove water need more cleaning or drying. An old colour mark on a cleaned box is not automatically food residue, but colour alone cannot certify safety. Compare earlier photos and maker directions and hold food if you cannot decide.
2. **Inspect fit part by part.** Once all parts are dry, use the maker diagram for gasket orientation, channel, lid, and latches. A seal outside its groove, twisted, cracked, or missing, a warped or split lid, or a latch that cannot close normally means it cannot be treated as its original leak-resistant or closed container. Do not force it or use glue to patch a food-contact structure.
3. **Use only a maker-supported next step.** Reclean and dry accessible residue by the body and gasket material directions. Obtain a correct part for this model if replacement is supported. Ask the maker whether a persistent odour, colour, or material deterioration has a suitable treatment. Do not mix strong removers, bleach, and acids or exceed heat limits to “kill a smell.”
4. **Verify only the permitted use.** Only if this model originally claims liquid use and its instructions or construction permit it, you may briefly test the assembled lid with clean water over a sink. A leak removes liquid-carrying use. One pass proves neither hygiene, long-term sealing, nor food-safe holding time. A dry-food-only box does not become leakproof from a test.
5. **Return or retire the box.** Reuse for food only when residue is removed, parts are dry and intact, assembly follows the maker, and use matches its design. Isolate a leaking, uncleanable, or visibly damaged box and ask about maker parts, replacement, or local recycling. Food storage life still depends on food and cold-chain handling, not on the sound of a latch.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can identify treatable residue or dampness, possible lasting material change, or actual lid or gasket damage and have chosen directed recleaning, a correct part, or stopping food use. An abnormal box did not return to service merely because the lid clicks.

- **Water remains in the channel and you want to latch it:** Separate and dry under the directions instead of sealing hidden moisture in.
- **One corner of the lid stays lifted:** Check gasket orientation and model fit. Do not force it or promise no leaks with liquids.
- **Colour remains but there is no obvious grease:** Consult material and maker directions rather than trying a second chemical or using colour alone to decide food use.
- **A clean-water sink test leaks:** Stop liquid carrying, seek the correct part or replacement, and do not treat an outer bag as gasket repair.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers post-clean assessment of ordinary reusable food boxes, not mold or food-illness diagnosis or insulated/electrical container repair. A maker's leak claim is model- and assembly-specific. A water test checks an immediate leak only; it is not a food-safety or long-term-seal test. Magnify a groove or ask trusted help for a small gasket; do not treat a hard-to-close latch as normal by applying force. Retirement may require another appropriate container.
