## 中文

当你有一双**暂时不穿的普通鞋**，准备收起来几周或一个季节时，加载这份 Skill。结果是鞋已清洁、内外全干、形状有适度支撑、在凉爽干燥处成对可找回；这与 H068 衣物收存的材料和空间条件不同。湿鞋、发霉鞋或鞋底开胶先不密封入库。

### 准备与输入

核鞋和鞋垫完全干、无可见土/盐和持续异味；有问题先分别清理、晾干或求专业处理。读材质和品牌储存建议，备干净无染色纸、尺寸合适的鞋撑（可选）、鞋盒或透气鞋袋，以及避光、远离暖气和潮湿墙角的储位。不要把潮湿鞋直接装密封塑料袋，也不强塞过大鞋撑。

### 执行

1. **先查是否真的可收。** 打开鞋口，触看鞋头内、鞋垫底和鞋底，确认干燥、无霉、无松胶。若仍潮，退回自然晾干；若坏，记录待修/停穿而非藏进盒里。
2. **轻撑形状并配对。** 在鞋头放足以托形的干净无染色纸，或放合适鞋撑；不能把鞋面撑大、压花饰。鞋带轻收好但别死勒，左右鞋放一起，不让重物压在上面。
3. **放在可访问储位。** 依材质选清洁鞋盒/透气袋，放凉爽干燥、避直晒和直热处，标鞋款、尺码与收存日期；留一点通风余地，按容器说明。别把鞋堆在会渗水的地面或闷湿储物柜。
4. **设复查与取用点。** 记录何处能找到，之后检查储位潮气或异味；取出时再次查鞋底、鞋面和鞋垫状态，必要时先通风，不直接把久放损坏的鞋穿去长途。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一双完整干鞋有适度支撑、成对入合适储位并可找到；不确定干燥则保持待收，不靠袋子遮住湿气。把鞋扔进潮柜并忘记位置，不算完成。

- **收前摸到鞋垫潮：** 取出晾干鞋和垫，干后再收。
- **鞋撑使鞋面鼓起：** 换小一号或用少量纸支撑，不强撑。
- **储处闷湿/有霉味：** 换干燥通风处，检查鞋是否受损；有霉不直接穿。
- **不同材质相互染色：** 分开护纸/袋，避免紧贴。
- **高处储位不便取：** 选低位可及空间或可信协助，不为收鞋搬重箱爬高。

### 假设与边界

Clarks 对麂皮建议避光避热、凉爽干燥储存；其它鞋也以自己标签为准。鞋撑和纸只辅助保持形状，不能修复已变形鞋或保证永不发霉。收存不等于下一季仍安全穿着，需重新检查。

## English

Load this Skill when an **ordinary pair you own will not be worn for weeks or a season** and needs storage. Finish with shoes clean, dry inside and out, lightly supported, paired, and findable in a cool dry place. This has material and space requirements different from H068 clothing storage. Do not seal wet, moldy, or delaminating shoes away.

### Preparation and inputs

Check shoes and removable insoles are fully dry and free of visible grit, salt, and persistent odor. Clean, dry, or seek suitable help first if not. Read material and maker storage advice. Have clean non-bleeding paper, optional correctly sized shoe supports, a box or breathable bag, and a shaded dry storage spot away from radiators and damp walls. Do not seal damp shoes in plastic or force oversized supports into them.

### Execution

1. **Check actual readiness.** Open the shoes and inspect toe lining, insole underside, outsole, mold, and loose bonding. Return a damp pair to air drying. Mark a damaged pair for repair or no wear instead of hiding it in a box.
2. **Support shape and pair them.** Use enough clean non-bleeding paper in toes or suitable shoe trees to keep form without stretching uppers or pressing decoration. Gather laces loosely; keep left and right together and avoid heavy objects on top.
3. **Place in an accessible spot.** Choose a clean box or breathable bag appropriate to the material and store cool, dry, away from direct sun and heat. Label model, size, and storage date. Preserve the container's intended airflow and avoid a damp floor or sealed humid cupboard.
4. **Set a recheck and retrieval route.** Note where the pair lives and check later for humidity or odor. On removal, inspect outsole, upper, and inserts again and air if needed instead of taking a damaged long-stored pair on a long trip immediately.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A sound dry pair is lightly supported, stored together appropriately, and retrievable. When dryness is uncertain, leave it in a drying state rather than hiding moisture in a bag. Shoes thrown into a damp cupboard without a findable place are unfinished.

- **Insole is damp before boxing:** Remove and air shoe and insert fully first.
- **Support bulges the upper:** Use a smaller support or less paper without stretching.
- **Storage smells damp or musty:** Move to a dry aired place and inspect for damage; do not wear moldy shoes directly.
- **Materials transfer colour:** Separate with clean paper or bags rather than squeezing different pairs together.
- **High storage is hard to reach:** Choose a low accessible position or trusted help; do not climb with heavy boxes just to store shoes.

### Assumptions and limits

Clarks advises shade, low heat, cool dry storage for suede; each other model's label still controls. Paper and shoe trees support shape but cannot repair distortion or guarantee no mold. Storage does not certify safe wear next season; recheck the pair.
