## 中文

当可靠配方或包装说明要求你在**普通敞口锅**里用水煮或小火煮，却还没开火时，用这份 Skill 决定锅、水量和操作空间。结果是一口能稳定放在灶上、食物加入后仍有搅拌和冒泡余量、你能安全结束并取出食物的锅。这里不处理高压锅、油炸、罐藏、燃气或电器维修，也不替配方发明烹饪时间。

### 准备与输入

先读这次具体食物的可靠说明：液体量、食物量、是否先烧水再下料、盖不盖盖、是否会起泡或膨胀，以及何时结束。找一口适合你灶台和本次量的锅，确认锅底平稳、把手牢固、锅与盖没有明显损坏。准备能取出食物的勺、漏勺、耐热篮或夹具；动作和器具要适合你的握力、站立和视听条件。别等水滚了才研究怎样把整锅端去水槽。

### 执行步骤

1. **先按说明估算锅里会有多少。** 把水、食物占的空间和加热时的冒泡/可能起泡一起考虑。这里没有适用于所有锅的“装到某条线”数字；若食物加入后接近锅沿、无处搅拌，改用更大的稳定锅或按可靠说明分批做，不靠手扶住溢出的锅。
2. **把空锅放稳再加水。** 锅底与可用炉头匹配，不摇晃、不挡住邻近操作；把手朝向内侧，避免路过时碰翻。需要大量水而你搬不动时，用量杯或壶把水加进已在灶上的锅，而不是先装满再搬。
3. **按实际下料时刻加水和食物。** 配方若要求先沸后下料，现在只加所需水；若要求冷水同煮，就按该顺序。不要为了“保险”擅自把水加到锅沿，也不要用不适用的密封盖把普通锅当高压锅。
4. **留下可执行的终点。** 食物煮好后是用漏勺捞、提起稳定篮具，还是等水冷却后处理锅？先让所选工具放在手边，并确认你不必端着一锅滚水穿过厨房。无法可靠取出食物时，换锅/工具或有人现场协助后再开火。
5. **开火前最后检查。** 锅稳、液体量与说明相符、把手不伸向通道、食物加入后的空间够、你能持续看守热源，才开始加热。不能看守就先不开火；锅还没有提出加班申请。

### 完成与停止

你能指出锅为何容得下这次食物和液体、怎样安全下料与取出、何时需要按配方观察，就完成了准备。若锅不稳、无余量、把手或盖有问题、你无法处理热水重量，先停止并改锅、减量或找可用协助；不要用更高火力解决容量问题。

### 常见报错与补救

- **食物还没入锅就快满了：** 关火或先不要开火，换更大锅或分批；热锅里不要临时舀出大量滚水来“修容量”。
- **锅装好后太重：** 让它留在稳定炉头上，用量具补水；结束时用合适篮具或漏勺取食物，水冷却后再处理。
- **锅盖过紧或器具说明不清：** 只使用适合普通敞口煮制的锅盖方式；机型专用限制以说明书为准，不试探压力。
- **水、泡沫或蒸汽已冲向锅沿：** 在安全可达时降热或关热，等待稳定；若有烟、明火、燃气异味或设备异常，退出此 Skill，按当地紧急指导处理。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设是家用普通灶台和水基煮制，不涉及压力、热油或维修。锅大小、食物膨胀、炉头限制与人的力量没有统一默认值；以配方、器具说明和你的实际能力为准。看不清刻度可用量杯或可读说明，握持或搬运困难可在炉上用壶加水、使用稳定篮具或获得现场协助。现实副作用是你可能先洗一口更大的锅，却避免用厨房地板接住汤水。

## English

Load this Skill **before turning on the heat** when a reliable recipe or pack directs you to boil or simmer food in an ordinary **open pot** of water. Set up a pot that stands securely on your hob, leaves room to add and move the food while the liquid bubbles, and lets you finish without carrying an unsafe load. This does not cover pressure cookers, deep frying, canning, gas or electrical repair, or an invented cooking time.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the directions for this exact food: water and food amounts, whether water boils before adding food, lid use, likely foaming or expansion, and the specified endpoint. Choose a pot that fits the hob and planned amount. Check that its base is stable, handles are secure, and the pot and lid have no obvious damage. Have a spoon, slotted spoon, heat-appropriate basket, or tongs that you can actually use with your grip, standing, sight, and hearing. A boiling pot is a poor place to start planning how to carry it to the sink.

### Execution steps

1. **Estimate what will occupy the pot.** Allow for water, the food's space, bubbling, and any expected foam. There is no single fill line for every pot. If food would come close to the rim or leave no stirring room, choose a larger stable pot or divide the job according to reliable directions. Do not plan to steady an overflowing pot with your hand.
2. **Stabilise the empty pot before adding water.** Match its base to a usable burner, keep it from rocking, and avoid obstructing nearby work. Turn the handle in from the edge so a passing hand cannot tip it. If a full pot would be too heavy for you, use a jug to add water to the pot already on the hob.
3. **Follow the real loading order.** If the recipe says to add food after water boils, add only the required water now. If it starts food in cold water, keep that order. Do not fill to the rim for “insurance,” or use an unsuitable sealed lid as if an open pot were a pressure cooker.
4. **Plan the endpoint.** Will you lift food with a slotted spoon, raise a stable basket, or handle the pot only after its water cools? Put the chosen tool within reach and avoid needing to walk across the kitchen with a pot of boiling water. If no workable extraction method exists, change the pot or tool or arrange in-person help before heating.
5. **Make a final preheat check.** Start only when the pot is stable, the liquid matches the directions, the handle is away from traffic, food will fit with working space, and you can attend the heat. If you cannot stay with the task, leave the burner off. The pot has not requested overtime.

### Success and stop

You can explain why the pot holds the food and liquid, how you will add and remove the food, and when the recipe asks you to observe it. Stop before heating if the pot rocks, has no headroom, has a faulty handle or lid, or would leave you managing more hot water than you can safely handle. Change the pot, reduce the batch, or arrange suitable help. More heat cannot repair a size problem.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Almost full before food goes in:** Leave the heat off or turn it off when safely possible. Choose a larger pot or divide the batch; do not try to ladle a large amount of boiling water out of a hot pot as a capacity repair.
- **The filled pot is too heavy:** Keep it on a stable hob and use a jug for additions. Remove food with a suitable basket or slotted spoon later; handle the water after cooling.
- **The lid seals tightly or its directions are unclear:** Use only a lid arrangement suited to ordinary open-pot cooking. Follow device-specific instructions rather than testing pressure.
- **Water, foam, or steam reaches the rim:** Lower or turn off heat if safely reachable and wait for control. Smoke, flame, gas odour, or equipment failure ends this Skill; follow local emergency guidance.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary domestic hob and water-based cooking, without pressure, hot oil, or repair. Pot size, food expansion, hob limits, and your strength have no universal default. Follow the recipe, pot and appliance directions, and your actual capacity. Use a measuring jug or readable directions if markings are inaccessible. Fill on the hob, use a stable basket, or seek in-person assistance if carrying or gripping is difficult. You may wash a larger pot but spare the floor a serving of boiling liquid.
