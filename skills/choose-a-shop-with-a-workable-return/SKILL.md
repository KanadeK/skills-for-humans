## 中文

当你已经知道这次需要什么，却还没决定去哪家店，且**买完后的返程**会改变选择时，加载这份 Skill。它只选能取得所需物品、进入店内、并把预计负担带回家的购物地点；不决定购买量、不按单位价排名，也不替你执行配送订单。多家店的先后顺序是另一个任务。

### 准备与输入

列出本次必须取得的物品或用途、预计整包大小和重量，以及最多三家可能去的店。找各店当日营业时间、是否有目标商品的信息、入口和店内可达条件、到店与返家方式。对你来说关键的细节可能是台阶、电梯是否运行、通道宽度、取货协助、可坐下休息的位置，或是否要空手操作车票和扶栏；不用套用别人的“无障碍”标准。冷藏或冷冻食品还要有从付款到家可满足标签要求的计划。

库存、无障碍设施或交通规则在线上说不清时，联系店方或运营方核实；仍未知就标为未知。地图上的地点标记不会保证货架有货、门能进去或末班车还在。

### 执行

1. **先定义一次出行的最低完成结果。** 写下本次非买不可的用途，和哪些物品缺一个就无法完成它。可选商品不能用来证明一个本来失败的目的地“也算有收获”。
2. **逐店核对取得条件。** 看该店是否真的出售所需类别、当日是否开放、关键商品的库存能否确认。只看到旧网页或陌生人说“应该有”时，保留不确定；你可以接受一次探索性空手往返，但不能把未知写成已经取得。
3. **检验从门口到商品再到收银处。** 对照你实际需要的入口、通道、伸手/取货帮助、休息与付款方式。必要的协助只有店方确认可提供，才算入选条件。某店距离近，却无法进入、取到物品或完成付款，就先剔除；不用以身体硬闯来验证网页。
4. **倒着走一遍满载返程。** 从该店收银台到家，核对袋子或推车、可用的手、步行/轮行距离、台阶、换乘、交通运营方规则和营业/班次时间。用预计**买完后的**负担算，不用空手去程的轻松感替代。需要冷链时，查具体标签、保冷能力和直返时间；不成立就不把需冷商品列为本次可取得。
5. **在可行店之间作选择。** 优先能完成必需用途且返家可行的店；若不止一家合格，再用你现场可核实的时间、总费用和个人偏好选择。没有合格店时，改成更小的一趟、延期，或另行核实可行取得方式，不把“先到门口看看”当作已完成购物计划。
6. **留下一个失败出口。** 写下选中店、预计购买组、返家方式；若库存或入口信息未确认，再写一个可接受的空手回家或替代店方案。到店发现条件改变时，买冷食前重算，不带着已购易腐品临时展开漫长的第二家店探索。

### 完成条件

你能指出**一家**此次可用的店和买完后的返程：必需商品有可信的可得依据或明确的现场确认点，入口与店内流程适合你，满载和需冷食品都可回家。若关键条件查不到，诚实输出“仍需确认”或“不出发”，而不是选一个看起来最近的地址。

### 常见报错与补救

- **最近的店走得到却进不去：** 改查真实可用入口、店内协助或下一家店；入口不成立就不把该店算可行。
- **去程能搭车，回程推车或大袋不确定能否上车：** 先查运营方当期规则和替代返程；查不到就降低本次负担或换店。
- **商品库存信息过期：** 视为未知，联系商店；若仍未知，出发前决定是否接受空手返家，不在现场编造第二家店的库存。
- **店内可买冷食，返程却无法保冷：** 不买该类食品，或改选能满足标签条件的店与返程；不把保冷袋当作无限时冰箱。
- **到店后必需项缺货：** 先作缺货采购决策；要去另一家时重新检查实际负重、时间与冷链。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这份 Skill 不假定你开车、能久站、能读小字，或任一国家的公交及商店规则相同。店方、运营方和商品标签的当期信息优先；电话、清晰照片、语音阅读或可信同行者可替代小字地图。若所有店都不可行，选延期或其他取得方式是有效结果。副作用可能是放弃一条地图上最短、现实中却走不完的路线。

## English

Load this Skill when you know what you need but have not chosen a shop, and **getting home with the purchase** may change the answer. It selects a place where you can obtain the essentials, reach them inside the shop, and return with the expected load. It does not choose pack quantities, rank unit prices, place a delivery order, or sequence several shops.

### Preparation and inputs

Name the essential items or purpose, their expected full-pack size and weight, and up to three candidate shops. Find today's hours, information on the needed goods, the entrance and interior access, and the outbound and return journey. What matters to you may be steps, a working lift, aisle width, help reaching goods, somewhere to rest, or a free hand for a ticket or rail. You do not need to adopt someone else's standard of “accessible.” For chilled or frozen food, plan how the time from paying to home can meet the item's labelled condition.

If stock, access, or transit rules are unclear online, confirm them with the shop or operator. Mark what remains unknown. A pin on a map does not guarantee stock, an entrance you can use, or a return service.

### Execution

1. **Name the minimum result for this outing.** Write the essential use and which items must come together to complete it. Optional goods cannot turn a failed essential trip into a completed one.
2. **Check what each shop can supply.** Does it sell the needed type, open today, and have credible current stock information for the essential item? Treat an old webpage or “probably” as uncertainty. You may choose to risk an exploratory empty-handed trip, but do not record unknown goods as obtained.
3. **Check the path from the door to the goods and checkout.** Match the entrance, aisles, reaching help, rest, and payment to what you need. Count required assistance only if the shop confirms it is available. A nearby shop that you cannot enter, use, or pay in is not a feasible choice. Do not use your body to test a webpage's promise.
4. **Work backward through the loaded return.** From checkout to home, check bags or cart, free hands, walking or wheeling distance, steps, transfers, operator rules, and timing. Use the **expected purchased load**, not how easy the empty-handed outbound journey feels. If cold food is planned, check its actual label, cooling available, and direct return. Leave it out when that cannot work.
5. **Choose among feasible shops.** Prefer a shop that completes the essential use and permits a workable return. If several qualify, compare time, total costs you can verify now, and your own preferences. If none qualifies, reduce this trip, defer it, or separately verify another way to obtain the goods. Arriving at a door is not the same as having a shopping plan that works.
6. **Record an exit.** Note the chosen shop, expected purchase group, and way home. If stock or entrance details remain uncertain, decide whether an empty-handed return or verified alternative shop is acceptable. If conditions change on arrival, recalculate before buying cold food; do not take perishables on an improvised long search for another store.

### Success

You can name **one** feasible shop and loaded return: essentials have a credible availability basis or a clear on-site check, the entrance and interior work for you, and the expected load and cold items can get home. If a critical condition cannot be established, “confirm first” or “do not set out” is an honest result, not a failure to choose the nearest address.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The nearest shop is reachable but not usable:** Check a real entrance, available help, or another shop. Do not count it as feasible while the entrance fails.
- **Transit works outbound but a cart or large bag may not board on return:** Check current operator rules and another return method. If unconfirmed, reduce the load or choose another shop.
- **Stock information is old:** Mark it unknown and contact the shop. If it remains unknown, decide whether an empty-handed return is acceptable instead of inventing a second shop's stock.
- **Cold food is available but the return cannot keep it cold:** Leave that category out or choose a shop and route that meet the label. A cool bag is not an unlimited refrigerator.
- **An essential item is out of stock on arrival:** Make the stockout purchase decision first. Before another shop, recalculate your actual load, time, and cooling.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This Skill assumes neither a car, long standing, small-print vision, nor identical transit and store rules across regions. Current shop and operator information and the product label govern. A call, enlarged photo, audio reading, or trusted companion can replace a small-print map. When every shop fails, deferring or using another acquisition method is valid. You may give up the shortest route on the map because it would not work in practice.
