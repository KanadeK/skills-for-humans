## 中文

当可靠配方写着“煮沸”“保持滚沸”“转小火煮”或“微沸”，而你已经把普通水基煮锅安全安置好时，加载这份 Skill。你要输出**当前锅液符合该步骤要求的运动状态，并按正确起点计时，直到配方规定的结束信号**。泡泡只能告诉你液体状态，不能单独证明食物安全熟透；动物性食品的熟度按适用的[熟度核对](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)处理。

### 准备与输入

在[开火前安置煮锅](../set-up-a-pot-for-boiling/SKILL.md)后，标出配方当前要求的是**滚沸、普通沸腾、轻微持续小火**，还是“先沸后小火”；并记下何时开始计时、何时加料、是否盖盖、液面应怎样维持、用什么信号结束。食物量和锅具变化会改变达到目标状态所需的火力，不能把别人的旋钮档位当作通用温度。准备可看、可听或可振动的计时方式，并能一直留意锅。

### 执行步骤

1. **先找目标，不先把火力拉满。** 按配方加热。滚沸表现为大泡持续从锅底涌出、表面不断翻动；小火煮是较细小、较温和而持续的气泡。两者都不是“完全没动静”。若配方只说“煮至熟”却没有适用的食物完成判断，先找可靠说明，不靠泡泡补上安全条件。
2. **需要先沸后小火时，按顺序转换。** 等出现配方要求的沸腾状态，再逐步调低到稳定的小火；观察片刻确认气泡仍持续。只是关小旋钮不代表液体立刻变温和，盖盖与锅大小也会改变响应。
3. **按配方规定的事件开始计时。** 有的步骤从下料开始，有的从水重新沸腾开始。冷食入锅后泡泡可能暂时停下；不要沿用原来空锅的滚沸状态，也不要自行延长/缩短有安全意义的规定时间。配方没说清时，先核实该食物的可靠说明。
4. **维持状态而非追求大火。** 留意气泡、液面、锅沿和配方要求的搅拌/盖盖。气泡明显减弱时小幅加热，过于猛烈或逼近锅沿时在安全可达时降热；每次调整后等锅液回应。水分低于配方要求、食物贴锅或出现异味时先降热/停热，查原因再继续。
5. **按食物的结束条件停火。** 到达配方规定的时间或可观察状态后，执行其指定检查；对需要安全核心温度的食物用正确测量，不以液体沸腾替代。完成便关热并按计划取出食物。食物未达要求时继续受控加热和复查，不靠尝未熟的动物性食品确认。

### 完成与停止

你能说出这一段要求的气泡状态、实际计时起点、液面是否受控，以及配方或适用食品安全要求如何确认结束，就完成了。液体一直冲向锅沿、锅底将干、器具故障或无法看守时停止加热；烟、明火、热油失控和燃气异味退出此日常流程，按当地紧急指导处理。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把微小边缘气泡当滚沸：** 等到配方要求的连续大泡，或确认它实际上只需小火；重新标定计时起点。
- **“转小火”后不再冒泡：** 小幅调高并等待液体恢复轻微持续气泡；不要用锅壁的一次小泡当成稳定状态。
- **下料后水不再沸腾：** 按配方判断是否需等重新沸腾才开始计时；不要静默跳过这个事件。
- **盖盖后猛烈翻涌：** 在安全可达时降热，并按配方调整盖法；别在蒸汽正冲脸时掀盖。
- **时间到了但食物条件未达：** 按适用可靠说明继续并复查；时间和表面泡泡不是动物性食品熟度证明。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只处理普通敞口锅的水基煮制，不处理压力锅、热油、罐藏或配方外的危险操作。灶具、海拔、锅盖、液量和食物会改变气泡与计时表现；按具体配方、设备与当地食品安全指导判断。看不清气泡时可听受控的声音、用可访问的计时器或请现场可信的人描述，但不把声音当作熟度温度计；保持身体远离蒸汽。现实副作用是你要多看几眼锅，却可能少做一锅被大火强迫表演的食物。

## English

Load this Skill when a reliable recipe says “boil,” “keep at a rolling boil,” “reduce to a simmer,” or “barely simmer,” and an ordinary water-based pot is already set up safely. Your output is **the liquid motion that step calls for, a timer started at the right event, and a stop at the recipe's stated endpoint**. Bubbles describe the liquid, not whether food is safely cooked through. Check animal foods against the applicable [doneness guidance](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md).

### Preparation and inputs

After [setting up the pot](../set-up-a-pot-for-boiling/SKILL.md), identify whether this step requires a **rolling boil, ordinary boil, steady gentle simmer, or a boil followed by simmering**. Note when to add food and start timing, lid use, required liquid level, and the actual endpoint. Food amount and equipment change the dial position needed; someone else's burner setting is not a universal temperature. Have a timer you can see, hear, or feel and be able to attend the pot.

### Execution steps

1. **Find the target before setting maximum heat.** Heat as directed. A rolling boil has large bubbles rising continuously and disturbing the surface. A simmer has smaller, gentler but continuing bubbles. Neither means a perfectly still surface. If a recipe only says “until cooked” without a food-specific completion check, find reliable directions rather than inventing a safety condition from bubbles.
2. **For “boil, then simmer,” make the transition in order.** Wait for the specified boiling state, then lower heat gradually until the gentle bubbles remain steady. Watch long enough to confirm it. Turning the dial does not instantly calm the liquid, and the lid and pot size affect its response.
3. **Start timing at the event the recipe names.** Some steps count from adding food; others count after the water returns to a boil. Cold food can interrupt bubbling. Do not treat the empty pot's earlier boil as if it continued unchanged, or silently alter a time that matters for safety. Clarify an ambiguous instruction with reliable food-specific guidance.
4. **Maintain the state, not maximum power.** Watch bubbles, liquid level, pot rim, and any instructed stirring or lid position. Increase heat a little when bubbling fades; lower it safely when agitation becomes too strong or nears the rim. Wait for the pot to respond after each change. If liquid drops below what the recipe needs, food sticks, or an abnormal smell appears, reduce or stop heat and investigate before continuing.
5. **Stop on the food's endpoint.** At the stated time or observable result, perform the recipe's check. For food requiring a safe core temperature, measure properly rather than substituting a boiling liquid. Turn off heat and remove the food as planned when done. If a requirement is unmet, continue controlled heating and recheck; do not taste undercooked animal food as a test.

### Success and stop

You can name the requested bubble state, real timer start, controlled liquid level, and the recipe or applicable food-safety evidence that ends this step. Stop heating if liquid repeatedly approaches the rim, the pot risks drying out, equipment fails, or you cannot attend it. Smoke, flame, uncontrolled hot oil, or gas odour exits this everyday flow; follow local emergency guidance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A few edge bubbles are called a rolling boil:** Wait for sustained large bubbles if that is required, or confirm the recipe actually wants a simmer; reset the timing event.
- **The simmer disappears after lowering heat:** Raise heat a little and wait for gentle continuous bubbling. One bubble at the wall is not a stable state.
- **Adding food stops the boil:** Check whether timing starts when boiling returns. Do not silently skip that event.
- **A covered pot surges:** Lower heat when safely reachable and adjust the lid as the recipe directs. Do not lift a lid into a burst of steam toward your face.
- **The timer ends before the food is done:** Continue and recheck against reliable food-specific directions. Time and surface bubbles do not prove animal-food doneness.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary open-pot water-based cooking, not pressure cooking, hot oil, canning, or dangerous operations outside the recipe. Hob, altitude, lid, liquid amount, and food change bubbling and timing. Use the exact recipe, equipment directions, and local food-safety guidance. If bubbles are hard to see, listen from a safe distance, use an accessible timer, or ask a trusted person present to describe the surface; sound is not a core-temperature reading. Stay clear of steam. You may watch the pot more often and spare the food a performance at full burner power.
