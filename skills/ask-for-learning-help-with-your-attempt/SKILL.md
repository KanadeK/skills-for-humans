## 中文

当你已读过或试做一项普通学习任务，仍卡在一个可指出的位置，准备向老师、助教或同伴请教时，加载这份 Skill。目标是发起一项**对方能回答且符合课程规则**的求助，并用回应检查自己下一步能否做；对方必须立即回复不是完成条件。准备独立练习归 H255，自己定位错因归 H256，组织共同学习归 H259。

### 准备与输入

保留题目/材料的可定位出处和自己的原始尝试，先核课程对合作、外部帮助、答案分享和办公时间的规定。只分享对方有权看的必要片段；不要把同学的作业、付费整章或私人学习记录贴到公开群。没有试过时，先完成一个合理的小尝试；若题意根本不清，可明确写“我连 X 这个词的要求都不能确定”，不编造尝试。

### 执行

1. **缩成一个可回答的问题。** 写“我以为题目要 A；做到第 B 步时出现 C；我想确认 D”，或指出具体段落、页码/标题和不懂的关系。尽量问“为何这一步这样选/该先检查哪个条件”，而非“把所有题做给我”。
2. **选能回答的人和合适渠道。** 概念或评分标准优先找教师/助教的指定办公室时间或课程渠道；普通练习过程也可问允许协作的同伴。看对方是否愿意、有时间和访问材料权限；不要求同伴承担老师的正式裁决。
3. **带上已试过什么。** 只附自己允许分享的一小段原过程、用过的资料和已经排除的猜测；口头可翻到卡点，文字可用简短编号。不要只发一张没有上下文的答案截图，也不要为了展示努力贴整份受保护材料。
4. **发出或当面问，并确认对方听懂问题。** 用一句请求开头，如“能帮我看第 B 步为什么不适用吗？我试过 X。” 留给对方拒绝、改时间或转另一渠道的空间。没有回覆就记录待答，并在课程允许的时间后做一次合理跟进；不连环催促。
5. **把回应变成自己的下一步。** 对方给提示后，用自己的话复述关键条件，在另一项小练习或原题未做的下一步尝试。若仍不明白，问一个新的具体追问并记录；若对方只是给了完整答案而你仍不会解释，标“答案收到、理解未核”，不假称掌握。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你通过合适渠道提出含原尝试、具体卡点和可回答问题的请求，并记下回应/待答状态；若有回应，你还做了一个自己的检查动作。对方不在、不同意或不能马上解答，不使一次诚实求助失效。

- **问题是“我都不会”：** 选一个最早卡住的词或步骤，附一项已试过的办法。
- **对方直接给答案：** 请其解释一个关键判断，再自己尝试相近题；复制答案不算理解。
- **老师/同伴没有空：** 查正式办公时间或约定另一渠道，尊重其边界，不反复打断。
- **课程不允许互给答案：** 只按规则问概念、题意或可公开的例子，必要时找教师确认边界。
- **口头/文字形式不可及：** 选择课程允许的邮件、书面、语音或辅助手段；只需让对方能准确定位问题。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

学校、课程和学习社区的帮助渠道各不相同；教师、助教和同伴也有不同的可回答范围。对方的提示可能仍需验证，尤其当它与正式材料冲突时。你获得的是一次可核问题与下一步，不保证对方时间、分数或长期掌握。

## English

Load this Skill when you have read or tried an ordinary learning task, remain stuck at a locatable point, and are ready to ask a teacher, tutor, or peer. Make **one answerable request within course rules**, then use any response to check your own next step. The other person replying immediately is not a completion condition. The independent first attempt belongs to H255, self-diagnosis to H256, and organising group study to H259.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep a location for the prompt or material and your original attempt. Check course rules on collaboration, outside help, answer sharing, and office hours. Share only the necessary excerpt that the person may see. Do not post classmates' work, an entire paid chapter, or private study records to a public group. If you have not attempted anything, make one reasonable small attempt first. If even the wording is unclear, say precisely “I cannot tell what X asks” instead of inventing an attempt.

### Execution

1. **Shrink the question.** Write, “I thought the task asked A. At step B I got C. I want to check D,” or identify the exact passage, page or heading, and relation you cannot explain. Ask why a step fits or which condition to check, rather than “do every problem for me.”
2. **Choose the person and channel.** For a concept or official criterion, use the teacher or tutor's designated office hours or course route. A peer may help with an ordinary practice process when collaboration is allowed. Respect their time, willingness, and access to the material; a peer need not issue a teacher's formal decision.
3. **Bring what you tried.** Show only a small shareable part of your own process, resources used, and guesses already ruled out. In person, point to the sticking point; in writing, use short numbered context. A bare answer screenshot is hard to diagnose, while a whole protected source is too much to share.
4. **Ask and check that the question landed.** Start, “Can you help me see why step B doesn't apply? I tried X.” Allow refusal, a different time, or another route. With no reply, mark pending and follow up once after a reasonable course-permitted interval rather than repeatedly pinging.
5. **Turn a response into your own next step.** Paraphrase the key condition and try a small different exercise or the next uncompleted step yourself. If still unclear, ask one new precise follow-up and record it. If someone supplies a complete answer without an explanation you can use, mark “answer received, understanding unchecked,” not “mastered.”

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You sent or asked through an appropriate route with an original attempt, locatable block, and answerable question, and recorded answered or pending. With a response, you also made one check of your own. An unavailable person, refusal, or delayed answer does not invalidate an honest request.

- **Your question is “I understand nothing”:** Pick the earliest term or step that blocks you and add one thing you tried.
- **Someone gives only the answer:** Ask for one reasoning decision, then try a comparable task yourself. Copying an answer is not understanding.
- **The teacher or peer is busy:** Check formal office hours or agree on another route and respect their boundary.
- **The course forbids answer exchange:** Ask only about concepts, prompt meaning, or allowed examples and confirm the boundary with the teacher if needed.
- **Spoken or written interaction is inaccessible:** Use course-permitted email, text, audio, or assistive means; the person needs a way to locate the question.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Schools, courses, and learning communities provide different help routes. Teachers, tutors, and peers have different authority and available time. A hint may still need verification, especially when it conflicts with official material. This produces one checkable question and next step, not guaranteed response time, grades, or lasting mastery.
