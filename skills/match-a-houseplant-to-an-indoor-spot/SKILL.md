## 中文

当你要给一盆新来的或搬过位置的普通室内盆栽找长期位置时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一处**有品种依据、实际能放、可复查**的位置；摆好并不保证它以后不会出现问题。

### 准备与输入

确认植物名称或至少拿到卖家/栽培者针对这盆植物的可靠资料，重点看光照、温度和是否怕直晒。看不清标签时可拍照放大或请人读；认不出品种时，先不要把“喜阴”“耐晒”当成事实。只考虑能稳放花盆、不会堵通道、能接住排水、不会让人被沉重盆器砸到的位置。如果儿童或宠物可能接触而植物安全性不明，先选他们接触不到的地方并查该品种的可信安全资料。

### 执行

1. **列出两个实际候选位置。** 在早、中、晚各看一次光线，区分直射、明亮散射与持续偏暗；隔着窗帘或建筑遮挡的光不等于室外同朝向的光。
2. **对照这盆植物的要求。** 以品种资料判断哪处光照较接近，检查暖气、冷风、空调出风口和紧贴玻璃的冷热变化。不要用“所有绿叶都要散射光”替代品种说明。
3. **检查可用性。** 花盆与接水托盘要稳，排水不浸湿家具或插座；你能定期摸到盆土、看见叶片并安全移动它。大盆或高处位置需要他人协助时先安排协助，不冒险搬举。
4. **选一处并记下基线。** 记位置、日期、当前叶色/姿态和你依据的光照要求。只改变这一项，给后续观察留可解释的起点。
5. **按品种资料复看。** 若出现新焦斑、明显徒长、持续萎蔫或大范围变色，检查光线、浇水和病虫害等可能因素，不凭一个症状断定原因。资料或安全条件不足时请教可靠园艺服务，而不是连续搬来搬去。

### 完成与停止

完成时，你能说出这盆植物需要什么光、所选位置实际有什么光和温度条件、何时复看，以及哪里接住排水。没有可用位置或品种要求不明，就记录缺口并暂缓永久摆放；这也是诚实的结束。

### 常见报错与补救

- **窗边有太阳，植物却晒伤：** 核对直射时段及品种资料，移到满足其要求的散射光位置；先记录变化，不把焦斑当成缺水证据。
- **只在夜间看过房间：** 白天再观察；灯亮不证明自然光足够。
- **盆会滴到家具或靠近电源：** 调整托盘和位置；不要把托盘长期灌满水来保护家具。
- **位置对你不可及：** 选较低且稳定的位置或约定可靠协助；不在高处强行端重盆。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里处理一盆普通观叶或开花盆栽，不辨认野生植物、不给食用植物定产量，也不处理疑似中毒。不同品种和季节的光照需求会变。视力受限时请人描述直射时段或使用可读的记录；行动受限时优先选择能坐着检查的位置。你会获得一处需要定期复看的植物位置，而不是一次性永久配置。

## English

Load this Skill when a new or moved ordinary potted houseplant needs a lasting indoor position. The result is a **species-informed, physically usable, reviewable** spot. Putting a pot there does not guarantee future health.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the plant's identity, or obtain reliable care guidance for this actual plant from its grower or seller. Look for light, temperature, and direct-sun limits. Enlarge an unreadable label or ask someone to read it. If the plant is unidentified, do not invent a shade or sun preference. Consider only places where the pot is stable, paths stay clear, drainage can be caught, and a heavy container cannot fall onto someone. If children or pets might reach a plant whose safety is unknown, keep it out of reach and seek trustworthy species-specific safety information.

### Execution

1. **List two real candidate spots.** Observe them in the morning, midday, and later in the day. Distinguish direct sun, bright indirect light, and persistent low light. A curtained or shaded window is not equivalent to outdoor light in the same direction.
2. **Match the actual plant.** Use its guidance to judge which spot is closer to its needs. Check radiators, cold draughts, air-conditioning outlets, and temperature changes against glass. Do not apply one rule to every leafy plant.
3. **Check usability.** Place the pot and drip tray securely, away from sockets and surfaces that runoff could damage. You should be able to inspect leaves and growing medium and move the pot safely. Arrange help for a heavy pot or high shelf before moving it.
4. **Choose and record a baseline.** Note the spot, date, current leaves and posture, and the light requirement used. Change this one condition so later observations have a meaningful starting point.
5. **Review using the plant's guidance.** New scorch, stretched growth, persistent wilt, or widespread discoloration may involve light, water, or pests. One symptom does not identify a cause. If the guidance or safe placement is missing, consult a reliable horticultural source instead of moving the plant repeatedly.

### Success and stop

You can explain the plant's light need, the chosen spot's actual light and temperature, the next review, and where runoff goes. If no spot fits or the plant's needs are unknown, record that gap and defer permanent placement.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A sunny window scorches leaves:** Recheck hours of direct sun and species guidance; move to a suitable indirect-light spot. Record the change instead of treating scorch as proof of thirst.
- **You saw the room only at night:** Observe it in daylight. An illuminated room is not evidence of adequate natural light.
- **The pot drips onto furniture or near power:** Change the tray or spot. Do not leave the pot sitting in a permanently full saucer.
- **The spot is inaccessible:** Choose a lower stable surface or arrange help. Do not lift a heavy pot onto a high shelf alone.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers an ordinary potted foliage or flowering plant, not wild-plant identification, edible crop yields, or a suspected poisoning. Species and seasons change the relevant light conditions. If you cannot see direct-sun periods, ask for a description or keep an accessible observation record. If moving is difficult, prefer a spot you can check while seated. The side effect is an occupied space that still needs review.
