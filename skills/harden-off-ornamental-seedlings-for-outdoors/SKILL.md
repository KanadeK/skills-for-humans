## 中文

当你培育的普通观赏幼苗已在室内长到准备外移的阶段，**但还没直接栽到户外**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是按品种与当地实际天气让幼苗逐步接触户外光、风和温度变化，并作出“可按后续计划移栽/继续适应/天气不合适暂缓”的决定。这里不完成移栽，不保证植物耐霜或存活；已成形盆栽的外出摆放也不自动套用这套幼苗流程。

### 准备与输入

核种子包或同品种可靠说明的户外时点、耐寒条件、当前幼苗状态以及真正的栽种位置。看**当地现在和接下来一段的天气**，尤其夜间低温、强风和烈日；别把别的地区最后霜期当你的日期。找一处你有权用、稳固、遮风且可从阴影逐步调整光照的户外位置，能每天把盘安全拿回。没有可访问的搬运方法、有人会绊倒的公共位置或天气风险无法核时，先停。

### 执行

1. **从短时、受保护的户外接触开始。** 选本品种和当天条件允许的温和时段，先放在有遮蔽的明亮阴影处而非骤然正午全日照；给本盘留出真正回收时间与联系/提醒方式。种子包若有更具体步骤则优先。
2. **在一次次安全机会中逐步增加。** 观察叶片是否明显灼伤、倒伏、干透或持续萎蔫；状态尚可才按可靠说明逐步增加户外时间、光照与风的接触。土壤仍按幼苗需要保持合适湿度，托盘有余水就倒掉，不因在室外便自动多浇一倍。
3. **按实际天气及时收回或暂停。** 夜间低温、霜冻可能、强风、暴雨或幼苗明显受压时，按品种条件提前带回安全处并记录影响；**逐步适应不会把怕霜植物变成耐霜植物**。没有把握就延期，而不是为赶原日历硬放整夜。
4. **对照未来去处做结束判断。** 经一段按品种/条件逐步接触后，幼苗能在预定时间段保持正常状态、未来位置天气也适用，才标“已适应，可进入另一个移栽步骤”；仍虚弱或外部条件不稳则继续短时阶段或推迟。把日期、每次暴露和异常留给下一动作。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你有一份多次接触的真实记录，并据幼苗反应、品种和当前天气作出**继续、暂缓或进入移栽**的决定。把盘放在户外一天却未查回收和夜温，不算完成适应。

- **第一天就放烈日或大风：** 立即移回安全处，核叶片状态，从允许的遮蔽短时条件重来。
- **白天暖就忘了夜间霜冻：** 查当地实际夜况，怕霜幼苗带回/延期，不靠“已经硬化”免疫。
- **盘土在外面干得更快：** 核介质而非仅按日历，按品种轻柔补水并保持排水。
- **无法每天安全搬运：** 减少盘量、用稳固可访问位置或可信协助；无人负责回收时不要开始需要收回的阶段。
- **叶片受损或持续萎蔫：** 停止增加暴露，记录并找可靠园艺资料/人员核；不自己喷药救场。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这适合少量普通非食用观赏幼苗按本品种说明准备外移；各地天气、容器与家庭可用位置不同，没有跨地区固定两周日程。读屏/语音天气、轻盘和现场协助可帮助判断与搬运；光照/风/温度的实际证据仍要核。适应完成只是外移前一关，不是户外移栽或长期成活保证。

## English

Load this Skill when ordinary ornamental seedlings grown indoors are ready for an outdoor phase **but have not yet been planted outside**. Under variety and actual local weather, expose them gradually to light, wind, and temperature changes, then decide **ready for a separately planned transplant / keep acclimating / defer for unsuitable weather**. This does not transplant them or guarantee frost tolerance or survival. Established houseplants moving outdoors do not automatically follow the seedling route.

### Preparation and inputs

Check packet or reliable same-variety guidance for outdoor timing and cold tolerance, current seedling condition, and the real destination. Check **current local weather and the next period**, especially nights, wind, and intense sun. A frost date from another region is not yours. Have a stable permitted sheltered outdoor place where shade can be adjusted, plus a way to bring the tray back each day. Pause if safe lifting, a non-obstructive location, or relevant weather information is unavailable.

### Execution

1. **Start with short sheltered exposure.** Choose a mild time allowed for this variety and day, place the tray in bright shade protected from wind, not sudden all-day midday sun. Set a real return time and accessible reminder or contact. More exact packet directions take priority.
2. **Increase gradually across safe opportunities.** Check for scorched leaves, collapse, dry medium, or sustained wilting. Only when conditions remain suitable, lengthen outdoor time and light or wind exposure under reliable guidance. Keep medium moisture appropriate for seedlings and empty tray runoff; outside does not imply doubling water by default.
3. **Bring in or pause for actual weather.** For cold nights, possible frost, high wind, heavy rain, or obvious seedling stress, return the tray to a safe place under variety limits and record the effect. **Acclimation cannot make a frost-sensitive plant frost-hardy.** Delay instead of forcing an overnight stay to honour an old calendar.
4. **Judge against the future place.** After repeated suitable exposures, when seedlings remain in reasonable condition and intended placement weather fits, mark “acclimated; ready for a separate transplant step.” For weak plants or unstable outside conditions, continue shorter exposure or defer. Keep dates, exposures, and abnormalities for the next decision.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You have a real record across exposures and a **continue, defer, or proceed-to-transplant** decision from plant response, variety, and current weather. Leaving the tray out for one full day without a return or night check is not completed hardening off.

- **The first exposure is full sun or strong wind:** Move it to shelter, inspect leaves, and restart with a permitted short protected phase.
- **Warm daytime hides a frosty night:** Check actual local night conditions and bring in or delay tender seedlings. “Hardened” is no exemption.
- **Medium dries faster outside:** Check medium rather than calendar; water gently for variety needs while retaining drainage.
- **Daily safe lifting is unavailable:** Use a smaller tray, accessible stable spot, or trusted help. Do not start a take-back stage without someone responsible for bringing it in.
- **Leaves are damaged or wilting persists:** Stop increasing exposure, record it, and use reliable horticultural guidance or people rather than guessing a spray treatment.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This prepares a few ordinary non-food ornamental seedlings for outdoor placement under their variety guidance. Weather, containers, and home access differ; no cross-region fixed two-week schedule is promised. Accessible weather reading, a light tray, and in-person help can support the work while actual light, wind, and temperature still need checking. Acclimation is one checkpoint before transplant, not proof of later survival.
