## 中文

当一份普通工作、学习或志愿协作成果已经接近完成，准备交给指定接收人，但你还没确认**内容、正确文件和对方可使用性**时加载。目标是得到“可提交”或“还有这些未解决”的诚实状态。早期需求澄清、任务拆步、交接未完成工作与交付后的反馈各有流程；这份自查不代替专业行业审查，也不能证明对方已经验收。

### 准备与输入

打开最初/最新的真实任务说明、约定产出、必要范围、格式、截止信息和接收人。找出实际准备发送的那一份文件、链接或物件，不对桌面上所有名为“最终”的副本同时检查。若任务要求矛盾或重要部分未知，先把问题记成待确认，而不是自己想象一个验收标准。

### 执行

1. **逐项对照真实要求。** 把接收人明确要的内容/数量/用途与眼前成果对上；缺项、占位文字、未核实的事实和只做到一半的段落单列。别把“我花了很多时间”写进完成判据，时间不能替代内容。
2. **用接收人的视角打开一遍。** 真正打开将要发的文件或可交付版本，看首页/第一页、关键中段、最后一页，确认文本、图、链接/附件和可读顺序。若要导出 PDF 或其他格式，查导出后的实际文件，而非只看编辑画布；视力/设备条件不同可用可访问的另一查看方式或可信审阅协助。
3. **检查交付位置和权限。** 核文件名、版本日期或唯一标识，以及附件/链接确实指向刚查看的版本。对方是否能按任务指定的方式打开、下载或取用，要用你有权查看的共享设置/说明核对；不为“测试权限”把机密发给陌生人。若对方需要账号、密码或特殊软件而任务未说明，标未准备好并问接收人。
4. **减去不应随附的东西。** 看是否误含他人的私人信息、内部批注、无关草稿、隐藏页或错的收件对象；按实际授权和任务要求移除/询问，不为美观删掉必须保留的证据。纸质成果检查页序、份数与物件状态。
5. **给自己一个准确结论。** 写“可提交：__（版本/位置）”；或“暂不提交：__缺项，由__补/确认，预计__再查”。如成果只能部分交付，向接收人明确标未完成的范围并走未完工作交接，而非把封面写‘全部完成’。

### 完成与停止

你已用**本次实际要求**核对内容和可用性，定位唯一拟交版本，知道对方应如何取得它，没有未说明的隐私/权限/格式问题，才算自查完成且可提交；否则有具体未解决清单。**自查通过不是已发送，更不是接收人接受或现实使用成功。** 专业安全、医学、法律、财务或行业判断必须交相应合格人，不能被这张普通清单盖章。

### 常见报错与补救

- **检查的是编辑稿，发出去的是旧导出文件：** 只对实际要发的版本做最后查看，清楚标唯一文件。
- **自己能开链接，对方没权限：** 查受邀对象和共享方式是否符合任务，不公开扩权碰运气。
- **第一页漂亮，最后一页缺内容：** 至少看关键中段和收尾，按要求逐项核对。
- **留着未完成标记/内部批注或多余私人资料：** 该补的补、该问的问、该删除的按授权删除；不要悄悄把不确定变成事实。
- **截止快到还缺关键项：** 及时把可交与不可交的边界告诉接收人，按实际权限请求决定，不虚报完成。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于低风险普通协作成果，不评价人事绩效或专业作品的行业合规性。文件格式、分享设置和阅读辅助工具按实际任务而定，没有某一 App 是前置。视觉、听觉、语言或操作条件不同，可用屏幕朗读、纸面核对或可信授权者按同一要求协助查看，不向无关人泄露材料。副作用是发现一份旧文件、坏链接或误附批注，在它抵达接收人之前处理更省事。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary work, study or volunteer output is nearly finished and you plan to give it to a named recipient but have not checked **content, the actual file and the recipient's ability to use it**. The result is an honest “ready” or “these points remain.” Early requirement clarification, splitting the work, handing off incomplete work and feedback after delivery have separate tasks. This check is not professional domain review or proof of recipient acceptance.

### Preparation and inputs

Open the real original and latest assignment, agreed output, required scope, format, due point and recipient. Locate the one file, link or object you actually plan to submit rather than reviewing every desktop file named “final.” For conflicting instructions or a material unknown, mark a question for confirmation instead of inventing an acceptance criterion.

### Execution

1. **Compare against the actual request.** Match each explicitly needed item, amount or use to the present output. List missing pieces, placeholders, unchecked facts and half-finished sections. Hours spent are not a substitute for content in a done criterion.
2. **Open it from the recipient's point of view.** Really open the version to be sent and inspect its first page, key middle and last page for text, images, links, attachments and readable order. For a PDF or other export, inspect the exported file, not just the editor canvas. Use another accessible viewer or trusted authorised review help if sight or device use differs.
3. **Check delivery location and access.** Verify filename, version date or unique identifier, and that the attachment or link points to the version just inspected. Use sharing settings or instructions you are allowed to check to see whether the recipient can open, download or receive it as requested. Do not send confidential material to a stranger to “test access.” For an unexpected account, password or special software requirement, mark not ready and ask the recipient.
4. **Remove what should not travel with it.** Look for someone else's private information, internal comments, unrelated drafts, hidden pages or wrong recipients. Remove or ask under your real authority without deleting evidence the task requires. For paper, check page order, number of copies and physical condition.
5. **Give an accurate conclusion.** Write “ready: __ (version and place)” or “not ready: __ missing, supplied or confirmed by __, check again __.” For partial delivery, tell the recipient exactly what is incomplete and use the unfinished-work handoff, not a cover labelled fully done.

### Success and stop

You have checked content and usability against **this actual assignment**, selected one version, know how the recipient should obtain it and have no unexplained privacy, permission or format issue, so the pre-submit check is complete and it is ready. Otherwise, you have a concrete open list. **Self-check is not sending, recipient acceptance or real-world success.** Qualified people must make specialist safety, medical, legal, financial or domain judgments; this ordinary list cannot certify them.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You checked an editor draft but send an older export:** Inspect only the actual outbound version at the end and name the unique file.
- **You can open a link but the recipient cannot:** Check intended access and sharing without making it public on a guess.
- **The first page looks polished but the last is missing:** Inspect the key middle and ending and compare each real requirement.
- **An unfinished marker, internal comment or unnecessary personal data remains:** Fill, ask or remove under authority; do not silently convert uncertainty into fact.
- **A deadline is close and a key part is missing:** State the deliverable and missing boundary promptly and ask the owner for a decision; do not report complete.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers low-risk ordinary collaboration, not personnel appraisal or industry compliance. Format, sharing and assistive viewing follow the actual task; no app is required. For sight, hearing, language or device needs, use screen reading, paper checks or a trusted authorised reviewer following the same request, without disclosing material to unrelated people. You may catch an old file, broken link or stray comment before the recipient has to.
