## 中文

你有一张放在 Impress 页上的获准图片，主体只占小部分，想去掉无关边缘让读者看清主题时使用本篇。成果是可见画面聚焦目标、比例不被拉坏、图片说明仍真实。裁切只是隐藏边缘，不等于删除敏感图像内容；若要隐私脱敏不能靠裁切后发送原文件。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 副本，说出必须保留的主体和一项不能裁掉的背景线索。确认图片允许使用且原文件可找回。选正确图像而不是整个页面；若图中含不应披露内容，先处理来源许可与脱敏问题，不做普通裁图。

### 执行

1. 进入 Format > Image > Crop 或图像工具栏裁切模式，观察四角与四边标记。
2. 从无关边缘小幅收进，保持主体和必要上下文；不要拖普通缩放柄把人物或物体拉变形。
3. 退出裁切后在放映预览看主体大小、比例与旁边文字，核图注没有因裁切而变假。
4. 不满意时用原图/Original Size 恢复再裁，保存重开核结果。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

主体和必要线索仍可见，边缘减少、比例不变，原图可恢复。

- **主体被切掉：** 恢复原图，缩小裁切幅度。
- **图被拉长：** 撤销普通缩放，使用裁切控制。
- **隐藏处仍敏感：** 不要把裁切当脱敏，处理来源。

### 假设与边界

只调整一张普通获准图的画面，不做图像修复、真正脱敏或版权裁决。你负责图注真实性与对外分享边界。

## English

Use this Skill when a permitted image on one Impress slide has distracting margins and its subject needs emphasis. Finish with a visible frame centered on the target, undistorted proportions and truthful caption. Cropping hides edges rather than securely deleting sensitive image data; do not send a source file as privacy redaction.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP copy and name the subject plus one background clue that must stay. Confirm permission and recoverable original. Select the image, not whole slide. If it contains material that cannot be disclosed, resolve source/privacy issues before ordinary cropping.

### Execution

1. Enter Format > Image > Crop or image-toolbar crop mode and inspect corner/edge handles.
2. Trim distracting edges in small moves while keeping subject/context, avoiding stretch handles that distort objects.
3. Preview subject size, proportion and adjacent text; check caption remains accurate.
4. If worse, restore original extent/Original Size and retry, then save and reopen.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Subject and needed context remain visible, distracting edges shrink, proportions hold and original is recoverable.

- **Subject clipped:** Restore source and crop less.
- **Image stretched:** Undo resizing and use crop controls.
- **Hidden area sensitive:** Do not treat cropping as redaction.

### Assumptions and limits

This frames one permitted image, not image repair, secure redaction or rights adjudication. You own caption truth and sharing limits.
