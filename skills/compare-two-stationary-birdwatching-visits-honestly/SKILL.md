## 中文

当你已有**同一安全固定点的两次真实观鸟清单**，想弄清这两次“我记录到了什么不同”时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张把物种/类群、数量、听见/看见、时长和未知分开的对照；不是野生鸟类数量真实升降的结论。H296 的植物记录和 E018 的单次清单不完成跨两次口径比较。

### 准备与输入

确认两张清单的地点范围、日期、当地时区、开始/结束、时长、固定或行进模式、是否有中断。若地点不同、一个只路过或起始时间忘记，仍可比较“我写了什么”，但不能当同口径观察努力。保留原记录，不为了使数量相等而修改旧数；物种 ID 不确定就保留类群，敏感地点不公开。

### 执行

1. **先列可比口径。** 并排写两次季节/日期、时段、天气、观察时长、视野与是否用镜、是否专心，标哪项不同。这些差异可能改变看见/听见机会，不能在差异未写明时比较裸数。
2. **逐条对齐身份等级。** 将确有证据的相同物种对齐，较粗类群单独放，不把第一次的“未识别小鸟”强合到第二次某物种。只看见/只听见分栏，回放/软件首选未经复核不填确定种。
3. **写观测差别而不写种群结论。** 例如“第一次记录 2 只同见、第二次记录 1 只，第二次只看了更短时间”；日期、时间、视野条件解释可比性，不推断真的减少。缺少数据时明确“无法比较”。
4. **留下下一次更可比的行动。** 若你想继续个人观察，可自愿在同一获准地点、类似时段和时长再做记录，同时保持鸟不受扰；不把两次短观察升级成生态调查或稀有鸟定位发布。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一张两次观测结果与努力条件并列的个人对照、确认项和未知项不混，并明确不作群体趋势断言。只写“鸟变少了”而不核两次时长/季节，不算完成。

- **两次地点或方式不同：** 标不可直接比数量，保留分别的观察。
- **一张缺时长/中断：** 写缺口，只做质性回忆，不算同努力比较。
- **物种 ID 之后被改：** 保存原观察与更新依据，在两表按同一证据等级重对齐。
- **数量可能重复计入：** 回到每张最高同时数/独立个体证据，不相加猜数。
- **视觉/阅读受限：** 大字并列表、颜色以外的符号与可信协助读表，保留来源区分。

### 假设与边界

同一地点的两次私人清单只能比较记录，不足以估算真实鸟类种群。季节迁徙、天气和观察者技能都会改变结果；本文不做生态统计或公开保护物种位置。

## English

Load this Skill when **two real bird lists from the same safe fixed viewpoint** exist and you want to know how your records differ. Finish with birds/groups, counts, heard/seen, duration, and unknowns compared alongside conditions. This is not a verdict that the actual wild population rose or fell. H296 plant notes and E018's one-session list do not make this cross-visit comparison.

### Preparation and inputs

Check both lists for place, date, local time zone, start/end, duration, stationary or travelling mode, and interruptions. Different sites, one incidental pass, or forgotten start still permit “what I wrote” comparison, not equal-effort counts. Keep originals; do not edit earlier numbers to match. Preserve uncertain IDs at group level and keep sensitive places private.

### Execution

1. **Place effort side by side first.** Note both season/date, time of day, weather, duration, view, optics, and focus, marking differences. These affect chances of detecting birds, so raw numbers alone are not the first comparison.
2. **Align identity at the same evidence level.** Pair a genuinely supported same species. Keep broader groups separate. Do not convert visit one's “unknown small bird” into visit two's named species. Separate seen from heard, and do not fill confirmed names from unverified app results.
3. **Describe recorded differences without population claims.** For example, “two together on visit one, one on visit two, and the second watch was shorter.” Condition differences bound comparison. Write “cannot compare” when needed rather than “birds declined.”
4. **Leave a more comparable next action.** If continuing personally, return voluntarily to the same permitted place at similar time and duration without disturbing birds. Two casual visits are not an ecological survey or permission to publish rare-site details.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A private side-by-side note includes observation results and effort, keeps confirmed and unknown apart, and avoids population trend claims. “There are fewer birds now” with no duration or season check is not completion.

- **Locations or modes differ:** Mark counts not directly comparable and preserve each record.
- **One list lacks time or interruption notes:** Record the gap and limit to qualitative recollection.
- **A species ID is revised later:** Keep original sight and new reason, aligning both tables at the same evidence grade.
- **Counts may duplicate individuals:** Return to each session's simultaneous maximum or independent-individual proof, not guessed sums.
- **Reading is inaccessible:** Large side-by-side table, non-colour symbols, and trusted reading help can keep sources distinct.

### Assumptions and limits

Two private fixed-site visits compare notes only, not actual bird population size. Migration, weather, and observer skill change detection. This is neither ecological statistics nor protected-species location sharing.
