## 中文

当共享冰箱里已有一件没标明归属的食物，而你想取用、挪动或为新食物腾地方时，加载这份 Skill。完成可以是确认物主和权限，也可以是明确“还不知道、先不碰、下一步找谁”；对方必须回复不是完成条件。这里不凭外观判断食物安全，也不教你私自丢掉别人东西。

### 准备与输入

先看这台冰箱已有的家庭约定、分区图或学校/单位规则。记下物品所在位置、能从外面辨认的包装和是否已有侧面标签，不打开别人的盒子寻找线索，也不在公共群发送可能暴露隐私的完整包装或个人信息。若漏液、门关不上或食品安全可能受影响，先保护冷藏条件并按场地及食安流程处理风险；归属问题不能让食物在室温下等待群聊。

### 执行

1. **先排查已经约定的归属线索。** 看这件是否在某人的专属区域、是否有不显眼但完整的标识、或最近的交接说明。分区与标签只是证据；公共架层或没有字，并不等于大家获准取用。
2. **问一个能答的问题。** 用约定的消息、当面或纸条渠道描述位置和外观，例如“冰箱中层左侧的蓝盖盒是你的吗？我想知道能否挪到同一冰箱的右侧，暂不打开或取用。”若已有清理时间，写明真正的时间和负责执行的人，不发明一个你无权设定的期限。
3. **按回复的范围处理。** 物主确认后，只在获准范围内取用或移动；“是我的”不等于“可以吃”。请物主补上双方认可的归属/权限标识，或在他们同意后由你补写，仍保留食品原来的标签和日期信息。
4. **无人回复时保留未知状态。** 不把沉默当成共享、放弃或可丢弃。若不妨碍冷藏，保持原位并记录问题；需要空间时先找另一处不影响安全的已许可位置。若场地有正式清理负责人和已公布的规则，交给其流程，不自行借用该规则处理私人冰箱。
5. **确有即时物理风险时先止损。** 漏液或卡门等问题先按食品安全和场地规则隔离、通知负责人；只有在你有权限且能维持适用温度时才做最小必要的移动，并记录移动前后位置。日期或温度史仍不明的食品单独进入保存信息不明的判断，归属确认不能替它补全安全证据。

### 完成条件

你获得了明确的物主及取用/移动权限，或留下“物主未确认、未取用或擅自丢弃、已使用何种联系渠道、下一步由谁处理”的记录。冰箱的安全条件没有因等待回复被破坏。若必须由场地管理员决定，完成的是有效交接，不是擅自宣布食品归你。

### 常见报错与补救

- **没标签就当公共食品：** 停止取用，核对分区与约定；无人能确认就保持未知。
- **群里没人回就准备扔：** 沉默不是授权。只有实际适用的家庭协议或机构流程能决定谁在何时处理，且仍要遵守食品安全要求。
- **物主说“是我的”但没说能否移动：** 再问这一次具体动作，别把身份确认扩大为所有权限。
- **搬动时发现日期或气味不确定：** 不试吃、不靠闻味判断；按适用食品安全指引和保存史另作决定。
- **发出的照片包含他人姓名或隐私：** 改用位置和一般外观描述；需要图片时只分享完成辨认所必需的部分。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

家庭协议、合租习惯与学校/单位公用冰箱的清理政策不同。Weill Cornell Housing 提议事先约定归属和分区，NYU 一处公用冰箱有特定标识与清理制度；后者不能变成你家冰箱的自动丢弃规则。不方便打字或看屏幕时用口头、语音或纸条提问，并以各方都能理解的方式记录答复。一个人拒绝回复时，你只能管理自己的动作，不能替他同意。

可能的副作用是新食物要暂找别处放，以及一条清楚但稍显无聊的消息。它比冰箱考古后的误食更容易收尾。

## English

Load this Skill when food already in a shared refrigerator has no clear owner mark and you want to eat it, move it, or make space for something new. A valid finish may be confirmed owner and permission, or a clear “still unknown; leave it alone; here is the next contact.” Another person's reply is not a required success condition. This Skill does not certify safety by appearance or authorize you to discard someone else's food by guesswork.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the household agreement, zone map, or school/workplace rule that actually governs this fridge. Note the item's location, packaging visible from outside, and any label on another side. Do not open someone else's box to investigate or post a full package image that reveals unnecessary personal information. If a leak, blocked door, or food-safety problem is possible, protect cold storage and follow the facility and food-safety response first. A chat cannot keep food cold while you wait.

### Execution

1. **Check agreed ownership clues.** See whether the item sits in a named area, has a less visible complete label, or appears in a recent handoff note. These are clues, not universal rules. A common shelf or absent writing does not grant use permission.
2. **Ask an answerable question.** Use the agreed message, in-person, or paper channel and describe location and appearance: “Is the blue-lid box on the left of the middle shelf yours? May I move it to the right of the same fridge? I won't open or use it.” If a clean-out is already scheduled, state its real time and responsible person; do not invent a deadline you cannot set.
3. **Act only within the reply.** Once the owner confirms, use or move food only as permitted. “It is mine” does not mean “you may eat it.” Ask the owner to add an agreed ownership/permission mark, or add it with their consent, while retaining original food labels and dates.
4. **Keep unknown as unknown without a reply.** Silence is not sharing, abandonment, or permission to discard. Leave the item if cold storage is not impaired and record the question. Look for another permitted safe space if you need room. If a venue has a designated manager and published clean-out rule, hand the issue to that process; do not borrow it as a rule for a private household.
5. **Handle immediate physical risk first.** For a leak or blocked door, isolate the risk and notify the responsible person under food-safety and venue rules. Make only the smallest move that your authority allows and that maintains the required temperature, then record the old and new locations. Unknown dates or temperature history still need a separate preservation-evidence decision; identifying an owner cannot create missing safety evidence.

### Success

You have an identified owner and explicit permission for the action, or a record saying the owner is unconfirmed, you did not use or discard the food without authority, what contact was tried, and who handles the next step. Waiting has not broken the fridge's safety conditions. If a venue manager must decide, a proper handoff is completion, not a claim that the food became yours.

### Common errors and recovery

- **No label means communal food:** Stop using it and check zones and agreements. Keep it unknown if nobody can confirm.
- **No one replies, so you plan to bin it:** Silence is not authority. An actually applicable household agreement or venue process decides who may act and when, subject to food safety.
- **The owner says “mine” but not whether it can move:** Ask for permission for this specific move; identity is not every permission.
- **A move exposes an uncertain date or smell:** Do not taste it or treat smell as a safety test. Use actual storage history and applicable guidance separately.
- **Your photo shows someone else's name or private details:** Describe the location and general appearance instead; share only the part needed to identify it if a photo is necessary.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

Household agreements, roommate customs, and institutional clean-out policies differ. Weill Cornell Housing suggests agreeing ownership and zones; one NYU communal fridge has its own label and clearing rules. The latter is not an automatic disposal rule for your home. If typing or reading a screen is difficult, ask by voice, in person, or on paper and record the answer accessibly. When someone declines to reply, you control your own actions, not their consent.

You may need another safe spot for new food and one clear, slightly boring message. Both are easier to resolve than eating the wrong person's mystery lunch.
