## 中文

当你有**自有或明确获准的庭院**，想真正确定并放置一个普通小堆肥桶，但还没有投料时，加载这份 Skill。结果是桶有可达、排水、可管理的固定位置，或因条件不合而延后；不是一张任意角落的购买清单。共用、租住房屋或地方回收方案的规定优先；没有获准户外空间可改用当地合规收集服务，不能擅放公共区域。

### 准备与输入

核权利人/场地规则是否允许庭院堆肥、允许哪些桶及有无距离/动物管理要求。准备已有或拟用的普通有盖桶的实际尺寸，检查盖、通气、底部/排水及能否清理；不在本篇 DIY 开孔或使用电动工具。按自身行动能力评估全年走得到、水可取得、不会挡门/通道；重桶请人协助，不独自搬装满的桶。

### 执行

1. **先排除不合位置。** 不把桶紧贴围栏、墙体或邻居门窗，不放低洼积水、排水口、食物处理处、陡坡或会被人绊倒的位置。看地面能否让空桶稳定站住，并考虑雨后和夜间能否安全走到。
2. **试放空桶并开盖。** 选获准、排水适当且可从上方安全投料的位置；空桶先放下，实测盖能完全开闭、取出成熟料的门能打开、四周有维护站位。防动物设计依本地条件和桶说明核对，不宣称一个盖子能挡所有动物。
3. **检查水与干料路径。** 找到可安全取水的位置、能干存落叶/普通干纸的地方，以及厨房暂存桶到这里的可行路线。若需要越过不安全台阶或靠蛮力搬重料，缩小方案或换位置。
4. **作实际选址结论。** 位置和桶均获准、站稳、可进出和维护，才标记“可开始建堆”；否则写明许可/排水/可及性哪项未满足并停止投料，选择已开放的当地回收路径。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

空桶在获准位置稳固，盖/维护口可用，排水和通行可行；或一项门槛未满足而明确暂缓。把桶买回却放在无法维护的角落，不算合适选址。

- **场地许可不明：** 先向权利人/管理者核实，不把规则猜成默许。
- **雨后积水或桶脚陷入：** 换排水更合适的获准位置，不先投湿料试运气。
- **盖打不开、取料门贴墙：** 移动空桶重试，留维护空间。
- **动物能轻易进入：** 查桶及当地管理方案，无法控制就暂不投厨余。
- **行动或搬运受限：** 使用更小空桶、短路径和可信协助；无法安全维护则选当地收集服务。

### 假设与边界

EPA 的庭院选址条件支持可达、排水、水源和与围栏留空间；具体禁放区、邻里和动物规则因地而异。此 Skill 只放空桶，不保证后续堆肥无味、无害虫或可安全用于食用作物。

## English

Load this Skill when you have an **owned or explicitly permitted yard** and need to place an ordinary small compost bin before adding material. Finish with a reachable, draining, manageable fixed position or a clear defer decision. This is more than a shopping list. Shared, rented, and local collection rules take priority. Without permitted outdoor space, use an accepted local collection route rather than placing a bin on public land.

### Preparation and inputs

Check owner and site rules for composting, bin type, spacing, and animal control. Know the actual dimensions of an existing or intended ordinary lidded bin and inspect lid, airflow, base or drainage, and cleanout access. This Skill does not drill DIY holes or use power tools. Consider year-round walking access, nearby water, and keeping paths clear. Ask help with a heavy bin rather than carrying it full alone.

### Execution

1. **Rule out poor spots.** Avoid placing directly against fences, walls, or a neighbour's opening, and avoid standing water, drains, food-preparation areas, steep ground, and trip hazards. Check the empty bin can stand on the ground and that rain or darkness will not make access unsafe.
2. **Trial-place and operate the empty bin.** On an approved draining spot, open and close its lid fully, open any finished-material hatch, and leave room for a person to maintain it. Check animal barriers under local conditions and bin directions; a lid alone is not a promise against every animal.
3. **Check water and dry-material routes.** Identify safe water access, a dry place for leaves or plain paper, and a workable route from kitchen scrap container. Shrink the plan or move the bin if it requires unsafe steps or hauling heavy material by force.
4. **Record a real siting decision.** Mark “ready to start” only when permission, stability, access, and maintenance work. Otherwise note the missing permission, drainage, or access condition and defer feeding, using an available local collection service instead.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

An empty bin stands at a permitted place, lid and hatch work, and drainage and access are feasible; or a missing gate is named and setup pauses. Buying a bin and leaving it in an unserviceable corner is not proper siting.

- **Permission is unclear:** Ask the owner or manager before using the space.
- **Rain leaves standing water or base sinks:** Move to another permitted draining site rather than testing with wet scraps.
- **Lid cannot open or hatch faces a wall:** Reposition the empty bin and leave work room.
- **Animals can easily enter:** Check bin and local control options. Defer kitchen scraps if access cannot be managed.
- **Walking or lifting is limited:** Use a smaller empty bin, short route, and trusted help; choose local collection if maintenance is unsafe.

### Assumptions and limits

EPA siting guidance supports access, drainage, water, and spacing from a fence. Exact exclusion zones, neighbour rules, and animals vary locally. Placing an empty bin does not guarantee odor-free, pest-free, or food-crop-safe compost.
