## 中文

当你考虑给一盆普通室内植物补充肥料时，加载这份 Skill。先决定**现在是否需要、产品是否适合、实际用量是多少**，才执行。叶片变黄不自动等于缺肥。

### 准备与输入

确认品种、当前是否在生长期、上次施肥时间、最近是否换过含缓释肥的新介质，以及产品标签的适用植物、稀释和间隔说明。标签看不清可放大或请人读。没有可信品种资料或完整产品说明，就先不施肥。把肥料放在儿童和宠物接触不到的地方，按标签处理皮肤接触与储存。

### 执行

1. **核对需要。** 从品种和当地季节/室内光照判断是否活跃生长；休眠、近期刚施过肥、介质已含缓释肥或植株明显受损时先暂停，查明确指导。不要从单一黄叶推断缺肥。
2. **核对产品。** 读实际瓶/袋的适用范围、浓度、计量单位、稀释方法及使用间隔。不同液体、颗粒和缓释产品不能直接互换量；过期、无标签或不能辨认的产品不使用。
3. **决定可量的剂量。** 以品种与产品都允许的方案为界，按标签用量具量取；不要追加“保险量”，也不要和其他产品混用来解决不明症状。若要求稀释，先按标签在安全容器里完成。
4. **按说明投入。** 核查盆土是否符合产品对湿度的要求；RHS 提醒不要给干透介质直接施肥，以免伤根。把产品施在标签指定位置，不溅到人、家具或电器，结束后清理量具和双手。
5. **记录与复查。** 记产品、剂量、日期和下次最早复核时间。后续异常先核对水、光、盐分和病虫等可能因素，停止追加肥料并求可信园艺建议。

### 成功与停止

成功可以是“证据表明现在不施肥”，也可以是“品种与产品都允许，按量执行并记录”。如果产品标签、植物身份、上次用量或介质状态不明，停止；把不确定事实列出来，不猜一个低剂量。

### 常见报错与补救

- **叶子黄了就想加肥：** 先查水、光和品种资料；缺肥不是唯一解释。
- **新盆土已含养分：** 查介质包装的有效说明，再决定是否需要额外产品。
- **剂量没有合适量具：** 暂缓并找能读清单位的量具；厨房餐具若会接触食物，不作肥料量具。
- **刚施完才发现重复：** 不再叠加，记录实际已施量并请可靠园艺服务指导；不要用猜测的“大量冲水”覆盖。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里处理普通非食用室内盆栽与合法零售植物肥料，不解释农药、人体暴露治疗或食用作物施肥。需要协助量取时，请人读出产品标签并复核单位；手部操作困难可选小包装或请可信的人协助。现实副作用是一个真实的施肥记录和可能要收好的剩余产品。

## English

Load this Skill when you are considering fertiliser for an ordinary indoor plant. Decide **whether it needs feeding now, whether the product fits, and what dose the label supports** before applying anything. A yellow leaf is not automatic proof of nutrient deficiency.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the species, whether it is actively growing, its last feeding, whether new potting mix already contains slow-release fertiliser, and the product label's plant scope, dilution, and interval. Enlarge an unreadable label or ask someone to read it. If species guidance or the complete product directions are unavailable, defer feeding. Keep fertiliser away from children and pets and follow its storage and contact instructions.

### Execution

1. **Check need.** Use species guidance, season, and actual indoor light to judge active growth. Pause if dormant, recently fed, already in fertilised medium, or visibly stressed until guidance is clear. One yellow leaf is not a nutrient diagnosis.
2. **Check the actual product.** Read its plant scope, concentration, unit, dilution method, and interval. Liquid, granular, and slow-release quantities cannot be interchanged. Do not use an unlabelled, unreadable, or expired product.
3. **Measure a supported dose.** Stay within both species and product directions. Use a measuring tool with the right units; add no “insurance” extra and do not combine products to fix an unknown symptom. Dilute exactly as the label says when required.
4. **Apply as directed.** Check the medium's required moisture state. RHS warns against feeding completely dry compost because roots may be damaged. Apply only where the label directs, away from people, furniture, and electrical equipment. Clean the tool and your hands afterward.
5. **Record and review.** Write down product, dose, date, and earliest next review. If the plant later looks abnormal, also consider water, light, salts, and pests. Stop adding fertiliser and seek reliable horticultural guidance.

### Success and stop

“Not fed because the evidence says wait” is a valid success, as is “plant and label allow it, measured dose applied and recorded.” Stop if product identity, species, previous dose, or medium state is uncertain; list the missing facts instead of guessing a smaller dose.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Leaves yellow, so you reach for fertiliser:** Check water, light, and species guidance first. Deficiency is only one possible cause.
- **New potting mix contains nutrients:** Check its package before adding another product.
- **No readable measuring tool:** Wait for a tool with the correct unit. Do not use food-contact kitchenware for fertiliser.
- **You discover a duplicate dose afterward:** Do not add more. Record what was actually used and seek reliable horticultural guidance rather than flooding the pot on a guess.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers ordinary non-edible houseplants and lawful retail plant fertiliser, not pesticides, treatment of human exposure, or edible-crop feeding. Ask someone to read and confirm units if dosing is inaccessible; use a small package or arrange help if grip is difficult. A genuine feeding record and a stored leftover product remain afterward.
