## 中文

当一次普通退换、维修更换或供应方换新已经实际发生，旧凭证指向的物品与眼前新实物不再完全相同时加载。你要保存**原购买 → 实际处理记录 → 当前物品**的可找回事实链。这里不办理退换、不判断新物品承接何种保修或法定权利，也不接管账号或暴露完整序列号。

### 准备与输入

取出原购买凭证与先前物品索引、商家/厂商真正给出的换货收据、服务确认、案件编号或交付单，以及眼前新物品的正常可见型号/变体。缺哪一环就记缺，不编造“应该给”的单据。原件仍由有权者保管，订单和序列号细节不写公开表。

### 执行

1. **区分旧与新身份。** 用中性“原物品/当前物品”两行记各自厂家、完整型号/变体与安全可读的区分标识。新件外观相同也不写成旧件本身；读不到新件标识就标待确认，不拆机找号码。
2. **把实际事件放在中间。** 记发生的日期、谁接收旧物/交出新物、纸上写的服务或换货引用号、新件实际交付状态。申请中、承诺寄出和实际收到分别记录；尚未收到就不要把未来物品写进当前持有。
3. **在私人索引里连接三处资料。** 原购买凭证位置、换货/服务文件位置与当前实物对应在同一行或相互指向的两行。纸本与电子邮件按原来源保留，避免把旧件记录覆盖成新件而丢失中间证据。
4. **核对新的书面条款而不推断权利。** 若供应方给了适用于新件的文字、起止日或限制，按其原文记录来源；没给就标“新件条款待确认”，向实际提供方问，不把原件期限自动重新起算或直接结束。
5. **做一次从新物品反查。** 从当前物品的中性名称找到事件记录和原购买凭证，再由事件记录确认新件确实已到手。找不到某环就标待问/待收，保存已知的真实材料，不为了让链条好看编新凭证。

### 完成与停止

授权者能从眼前新物品找到原购买记录及实际替换事件，并知道哪些书面条件尚未确认；这只是来源链，不证明保修延长或退款资格。服务只承诺未履行、新旧型号不符、文书矛盾或属于法律/财务争议时保留未知并问实际供应方/合适专业渠道。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把旧序列号直接写到新物品行：** 分开旧/新身份，必要细节只放私人资料，标明未知的新标识。
- **只有“已申请更换”的消息：** 记申请状态，等新件实见才建当前物品对应关系。
- **换货凭据丢了但还有案件编号：** 保留编号、日期和沟通渠道，向实际供应方索取可核对记录；不伪造单据。
- **新件型号后缀不同：** 如实记两个型号和事件，向供应方确认实际适用书面资料。
- **旧凭证被扔掉：** 查原邮件/订单或供应方记录，缺失处标缺，不宣称原购买证据仍在。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于已发生的普通商品更换，不解释保修转移、期限、退款或消费者法律。各厂商处理流程不同，Apple 的案件编号只是一个记录示例，不代表每家都会给相同格式。视力、阅读或整理困难时请获授权者私下读回，避免把完整序列号、地址或付款资料传到公共服务。副作用是新物品有自己的身份，不必冒充旧序列号。

### 来源

- [美国 FTC：Warranties](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/warranties) — 购买凭证与书面资料共同保存可维持初始记录；不采用其美国法律结论。

## English

Load this Skill after an ordinary return, exchange, repair replacement or supplier swap has actually occurred and the item named by your old proof is not exactly the item now in front of you. Preserve a retrievable fact chain from **original purchase → actual service or exchange record → current physical item**. This does not perform the exchange, decide warranty transfer or legal rights, take over an account or expose full serial numbers.

### Preparation and inputs

Get the original purchase proof and prior item index, actual exchange receipt, service confirmation, case reference or delivery record supplied by the business, and the new item's normally visible model or variant. Mark a missing link as missing; do not invent a document that “should” exist. The authorised holder keeps originals. Order and serial details stay off a public index.

### Execution

1. **Separate old from new identity.** Use neutral “original item / current item” rows with each maker, full model or variant and a safely readable distinguishing mark. A visually identical replacement is not the same physical item. If you cannot read its mark normally, keep it unconfirmed rather than opening the housing.
2. **Place the real event between them.** Note date, who accepted the old item or handed over the new one, the written service or exchange reference, and actual delivery state. Applied, promised to ship and physically received are different states. Do not index a future item as presently owned.
3. **Connect all three locations in one private index.** Point from current item to original proof and exchange or service record, or use linked rows. Keep paper and email at their actual source locations. Do not overwrite the old item row with a new identity and erase the middle event.
4. **Read any new written terms without inferring rights.** Record only terms, dates or limits the provider actually issued for the replacement, with their source. If absent, write “new-item terms to confirm” and ask the real provider. Do not automatically restart or end the old period yourself.
5. **Test lookup from the current item.** From its neutral name, find the event and original purchase proof; from the event, confirm the replacement physically arrived. If any link fails, mark it pending or missing while retaining real material rather than creating a pleasing but false chain.

### Success and stop

An authorised holder can get from the current item to original purchase and the actual replacement event, knowing which written conditions remain unknown. This is provenance, not a promise of extended coverage or refund. Leave unfulfilled service, differing models, conflicting papers or legal and financial disputes open with the actual provider or suitable professional channel.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Old serial is copied into the current item's row:** Keep old and new identities separate; private details stay in controlled records and the new mark may remain unknown.
- **Only an “exchange requested” message exists:** Record the request and wait for physical arrival before linking a current item.
- **The exchange slip is missing but a case ID exists:** Keep ID, date and channel and ask the provider for a checkable record. Do not fabricate the slip.
- **The new item has a different model suffix:** Record both identifiers and event and ask the provider which written information actually applies.
- **Original proof was discarded:** Search original email, order or provider records and mark a missing link as missing, not present.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a record chain after an ordinary product replacement, not warranty transfer, duration, refunds or consumer-law advice. Providers differ; an Apple case number is only one example of a service record, not a format every business supplies. An authorised person can read the chain back privately when sight, reading or sorting is difficult. Keep full serial, address and payment detail out of public services. The new item gets its own identity instead of impersonating the old serial.
