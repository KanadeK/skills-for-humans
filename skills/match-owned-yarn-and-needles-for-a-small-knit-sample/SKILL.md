## 中文

当你已有一团普通纱线，想在**不先买齐整套装备**的情况下挑一对可用于小样片的织针时，加载这份 Skill。结果是记录了纤维、粗细、护理规则、建议针径与现有针的匹配结果，或明确暂停；还没有织物。H003 核商品购买用途，本篇从已有材料组织一次实际手织练习。

### 准备与输入

拿原纱线标签、现有织针包装或针身毫米标记、可记字的纸。读纤维成分、粗细/weight、建议针径、标示密度和洗护符号；“纱线重量”分类不是这一团的克数。针号字母/国家数字会不同，以包装毫米数比对。若纱线身份不明、标签缺失或你对纤维敏感，先查厂商，不猜湿洗或身体接触安全。

### 执行

1. **抄下本团的真实信息。** 记录品牌/批次（若有）、纤维、粗细、长度、建议针径和洗护限制，特别把“建议”与“必须”分开。没有相关栏目就记“未标”，不从另一团的标签借数据。
2. **比对现有两根针。** 找到同尺寸、表面光滑、针尖和连接处无裂的合用织针；先按本团建议毫米范围选择一个起点。两根型号不同或针尖会勾纱时暂停更换，不硬织。
3. **做最小试握与线圈。** 在桌上用一段线轻绕针，确认手能稳拿、线圈可滑动而不掉落；这只是起步可行性，不代表织密度已匹配。视力或手部不便可用浅色粗纱、较易握的针柄或可信协助照明。
4. **标练习边界。** 把纱线、针径和洗护标签放一起，写“小样片，先织再测密度”；缺匹配针或护理信息时记具体缺口，不直接开始衣物制作。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一组已知材料和合用针的练习组合，标签要点可回查，或无匹配时明确停下。只看纱线颜色、猜针号，不能算配材完成。

- **针上只有美制/本地号：** 查该针厂毫米对照，不凭相同数字认定同径。
- **标签没写建议针径：** 查原厂资料或先仅做小样试针；不承诺成衣尺寸。
- **针尖勾丝或连接处松：** 换完好针，不拿坏针硬试。
- **试圈太松/太紧：** 记录并在样片后测密度，必要时调整针径，不在配材阶段保证结果。
- **色觉/抓握不便：** 用大号清晰标签、浅色线和可信协助读字，仍由你选可控材料。

### 假设与边界

纱线标签推荐针径是起点，实际手劲和目标图样会改变密度。不能仅凭标签推断染色牢度、过敏安全或洗后尺寸；后续护理仍按本品真实标识。

## English

Load this Skill when you own ordinary yarn and want to choose a needle pair for a small practice swatch **without buying a full kit first**. Finish with fibre, weight category, care, suggested needle size, and available match recorded, or a clear hold. No fabric exists yet. H003 checks a purchase against use; this organises material already in hand for actual knitting.

### Preparation and inputs

Have the original yarn label, available needle marks or packages, and paper for notes. Read fibre, yarn weight category, suggested needle diameter, stated gauge, and care symbols. The weight category is not the skein's physical grams. Country and letter/number needle sizes differ, so compare labelled millimetres. For missing identity or a fibre you cannot safely handle, check the maker instead of guessing wet care or contact suitability.

### Execution

1. **Record this skein's actual facts.** Note brand or lot if present, fibre, weight category, length, suggested needle diameter, and care limits, distinguishing a recommendation from a requirement. Mark missing fields rather than borrowing another skein's label.
2. **Compare two available needles.** Select sound, smooth needles of matching size with no split tips or loose joins, using this yarn's suggested millimetres as a starting point. Pause if the pair differs or catches the yarn instead of forcing a trial.
3. **Test basic handling.** Make a light test loop around a needle at a table: can you hold it, and can a loop move without falling? This shows starting feasibility, not achieved gauge. For grip or vision needs, try a lighter smooth yarn, easier handle, light, or trusted assistance.
4. **Mark the practice boundary.** Keep yarn, needle size, and care label together and note “small swatch; measure gauge after knitting.” If no suitable needles or care data exist, record that gap rather than starting a garment.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A known yarn and usable matched needle pair are ready with label facts traceable, or an exact missing input stops work. Choosing on colour alone and guessing needle size does not complete preparation.

- **Needle shows only a national or letter number:** Check that maker's millimetre conversion; identical numbers across systems need not match.
- **Label omits needle recommendation:** Consult the yarn maker or make only a small trial, without promising garment dimensions.
- **Tip snags or join is loose:** Use a sound needle instead of forcing damaged equipment.
- **Test loop is too loose or tight:** Record it, then measure a knitted sample and adjust diameter if needed.
- **Colour or grip access is limited:** Large readable labels, light yarn, and trusted reading help support your material choice.

### Assumptions and limits

A suggested needle size begins an experiment; hand tension and pattern change gauge. A label alone cannot prove colourfastness, allergy safety, or washed dimensions. Later care follows the actual yarn's instructions.
