## 中文

当你将短期离家，而一只普通室内猫的日常食水、猫砂和观察必须由别人接续时，加载这份 Skill。结果是**真实照料者确认过的时间、进入方式、日常步骤、异常联系与替补**；只写一张纸而无人答应，不算交接。

### 准备与输入

先有这只猫现行的主人/兽医护理安排、食物和水位置、猫砂盆与废物去向、提笼位置，以及你的离家/返家时刻。Cats Protection 建议普通居家猫由可靠照料者定期到访，并以至少每日两次为其一般建议；年龄、健康和真实兽医计划可能需要更多。药物、特殊饮食或疾病监测必须按兽医书面计划由具资格的人接手，本 Skill 不制定剂量。

### 执行

1. **先找到愿意且能到场的人。** 明确日期、每次到访窗口、猫是否完全室内饲养、是否能安全接近它及其熟悉路线。面对面走一次现场；有偿服务按真实协议和当地规则确认，不假定谁可进入你的家。
2. **给出准确日常资料。** 写现有食物种类/份量与时间（只照抄已定计划）、更换清水、砂盆位置与清理方法、猫正常反应和需要观察的变化。把用品放在可找位置，清楚标注；不在公开消息里放门禁码、钥匙藏处或账户密码。
3. **明确异常路线。** 记录你能及时接通的方式、备份联系人、猫的兽医与提笼位置。照料者发现明显病痛、无法进屋或猫找不到时应按事先约定迅速联系你/兽医或合适的当地服务，不自行猜药和剂量。
4. **请对方复述并确认。** 让照料者指出食水、砂盆、废物去向和联系方式，确认每次实际到访会留下简短事实更新。需要交钥匙时通过约定的安全渠道单独处理，不贴在清单上。
5. **设替补和返家核对。** 主照料者无法到访时有已同意的替补或正式服务；没有可行替补就调整离家安排。回来后核实际到访、食水和猫状态，收回或调整访问权限。

### 成功条件

主照料者已经明确同意并掌握每次到访、日常操作、异常和替补路线；猫所需用品真实在位，返家交接有记录。无人确认、特殊护理无人合格接手或无法合法安全进屋时，短期外出照料计划尚未成立。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只说“有空帮我看一眼”：** 改为具体日期、到访次数、步骤和确认回执。
- **临时照料者不知道猫砂怎么处理：** 现场走一遍真实工具与本地废物路线，别让它堆在家里。
- **猫需要药而照料者不会给：** 与兽医和合适照料者单独建立书面计划，不用这份普通交接猜给药。
- **主照料者取消：** 启用已同意的替补或改变行程，不能把猫独留当默认降级。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只整理一只已由你照料的普通室内猫的非医疗短期交接，不授权陌生人入屋，不指导用药、疾病治疗或宠物寄养合同。若你无法书写，可以用私密语音或可读大字，但接收者必须确认。真实副作用是另一个人获得任务与必要的有限访问权限；这要在返家后核销。

## English

Load this Skill when you will be away briefly and another person must continue an ordinary indoor cat's food, water, litter, and observation routine. Finish with a **real sitter who has confirmed dates, access, tasks, exception contact and backup**. A written list that nobody accepted is not a handoff.

### Preparation and inputs

Have the cat's existing owner/veterinary care plan, food and water locations, litter station and waste route, carrier location, and your departure/return times. Cats Protection generally advises reliable home visits and suggests at least twice daily for an ordinary cat; age, health, and an actual veterinary plan may need more. Medication, therapeutic diets, or disease monitoring require a separate veterinary-written plan and a capable caretaker. This Skill sets no dose.

### Execution

1. **Find a willing, available person.** Agree dates, visit windows, indoor-only rule, ability to approach this cat safely, and how they will enter. Walk the home once together. For paid care, confirm the actual agreement and local rules; no one gains entry just because a list names them.
2. **Give the existing routine accurately.** Copy current food type, quantity, and timing from the established plan; include fresh water, litter location and cleaning, the cat's usual behavior, and changes to note. Put supplies where they can be found and label them. Never put door codes, hidden keys, or account passwords in a widely shared checklist.
3. **Set an exception route.** Give your reachable contact, a backup contact, veterinarian, and carrier location. If the cat appears unwell, cannot be found, or the sitter cannot enter, they should promptly use the agreed owner/vet/local help route, not invent medicine or doses.
4. **Ask for readback and confirmation.** Have the sitter locate food/water, litter, waste route, and contacts, and agree to leave a short factual update after each actual visit. Transfer keys by a separately agreed secure method, not in the general note.
5. **Arrange backup and return review.** Identify a consenting backup or formal service if the primary sitter cancels. Without one, change the absence plan. On return, check actual visits and cat condition and close or adjust access permissions.

### Success

The primary sitter accepted and can carry out visits, routine, exceptions, and backup; supplies are physically in place and return review has an owner. If nobody confirms, specialist care has no qualified receiver, or lawful safe access is missing, the absence-care plan is not established.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Look in when you can” is the whole request:** Replace it with dates, visit frequency, tasks, and explicit acceptance.
- **The sitter cannot find the litter disposal route:** Walk the actual tools and local waste route before leaving.
- **The cat needs medication the sitter cannot give:** Establish a separate written veterinary plan with a suitable person, not a guessed dose here.
- **The primary sitter cancels:** Use the consenting backup or change travel; leaving the cat unattended is not a default fallback.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a nonmedical short-term handoff for one indoor cat in your care. It grants no automatic home access and gives no drug, illness-treatment, or boarding-contract advice. Use private audio or large-print instructions if writing is inaccessible, then get readback. A second human now holds real tasks and limited access that should be closed after return.
