## 中文

当普通敞口锅里的**水基食物或水**突然翻涌、起泡，已逼近锅沿但尚无火、烟、燃气异味或失控热油时，加载这份 Skill。目标是让锅液停止越沿趋势，决定能否在更温和的状态继续。它处理的是**正在发生的煮制控制问题**，不保证食物质量或修理灶具；油炸、火灾与设备故障立即退出。

### 准备与输入

先看你是否能**安全够到**该锅的火力开关而不伸手越过热锅，锅是否稳，是否有溢出的液体流向电源/燃气控制或有人站在热液路径上。观察即可，不把脸凑到蒸汽上方。若有烟、明火、热油失控、燃气异味、触电迹象或开关无法安全触及，离开危险并按当地紧急指导求助；不要把本流程当消防或设备维修教程。

### 执行步骤

1. **先让热输入减少。** 在安全可达时把热源调低或关掉；人和旁人避开锅沿与溢出路径。不要抓着锅柄摇晃、端起一锅沸液穿越厨房，或把身体挡在锅与墙之间。
2. **等翻涌真正下降。** 热源关小后液体不会瞬间平静。维持安全距离观察；锅盖若加重翻涌，只在能避开蒸汽、锅仍稳定时按器具说明移开或错开。拿不稳盖子就让锅先停热稳定，别试图用手按住泡沫。
3. **重新估算锅容量。** 液体仍靠近锅沿、食物膨胀或起泡量明显超过余量时，不直接开大火重试。待能安全操作后，按配方选择更大稳定的锅、分批煮，或终止这锅；若只是火力过高，恢复到配方要求的较温和气泡并持续看守。
4. **核对继续烹饪的依据。** 停热期间计时或液面改变了，就按该食物的可靠配方重新确定计时与液体要求。食物安全完成条件未知时不试吃、也不把“刚才很沸”当熟度证明；用适用的[熟度核对](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)。
5. **溢出物等安全后再处理。** 热液已流入炉头、电器或地面时，不站在滑湿或灼热区域继续煮；只在安全时关热、隔离旁人，让设备与液体冷却并依器具/当地安全指导处理。电气或燃气异常交专业人员，不在本 Skill 内拆机。

### 完成与停止

锅液已退回有余量的受控气泡状态，热源、锅和周边仍可安全使用，你知道配方下一步，才算可继续。若锅不稳、热源不能安全控制、液体进入设备、有人被烫伤、出现烟火或燃气异味，就停止日常流程并求助；不以“还能吃”覆盖人身与设备风险。

### 常见报错与补救

- **越溢越把盖子压紧：** 先减热；不稳的盖子或喷出的蒸汽会增加烫伤风险，不用身体压泡沫。
- **关小火后立刻端锅：** 等翻涌停止、确认器具可安全搬动；优先让锅留在原位，不端滚水过屋。
- **锅太小却只调低火：** 停止并换大锅或减小批量；火力无法创造锅内容量。
- **时间线乱了：** 停热和重新沸腾是否影响配方，按食物特定说明确认；没有依据就不宣称已完成。
- **地面或设备被热液溅到：** 先避开、阻止其他人踩进来；冷却前不为保住食物去擦电器或继续操作。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只覆盖可控的水基锅液翻涌，不覆盖油、压力、火灾或设备维修。食物的淀粉、蛋白、锅盖和加料量会改变起泡，无法给一个通用的“恢复几秒”数字。看不清锅面时可用安全距离的声音或现场可信的人描述，但仍须能安全控制热源；动作或站立能力不足时先关热、让锅保持原位并寻求现场协助。副作用是清理工作可能要等冷却，但热液没有获得继续扩张的许可。

## English

Load this Skill when **water or ordinary water-based food** in an open pot surges or foams toward the rim, but there is no flame, smoke, gas odour, or uncontrolled hot oil. Regain a contained liquid level and decide whether cooking can resume more gently. This handles an **active pot-control problem**. It does not repair equipment or guarantee the food's quality. Oil frying, fire, and equipment failure leave this flow immediately.

### Preparation and inputs

First judge whether you can reach the heat control **safely without leaning over the pot**, whether the pot stands firm, and whether spilled liquid threatens electrical or gas controls or anyone nearby. Look from a safe position rather than putting your face over steam. If there is smoke, flame, uncontrolled oil, gas odour, electrical danger, or no safe access to the switch, leave the hazard and follow local emergency guidance. This is neither a firefighting nor an appliance-repair procedure.

### Execution steps

1. **Reduce heat input first.** Lower or turn off the burner if safely reachable, and keep yourself and others clear of the rim and spill path. Do not rock the pot by its handle, carry a boiling load across the kitchen, or trap yourself between the pot and a wall.
2. **Wait for the surge to subside.** A lower setting does not calm a pot instantly. Watch from a safe distance. If a lid worsens the rise, move or offset it only when you can avoid steam, the pot is stable, and the equipment allows it. If the lid cannot be handled safely, keep heat off and let the pot settle. Do not hold foam down with your hand.
3. **Reassess capacity.** If liquid still nears the rim or the food expands or foams beyond the available room, do not restart at high heat. Once it is safe to work, follow the recipe using a larger stable pot, a smaller batch, or a stop. If excess heat alone caused the surge, return to the gentler bubble state the recipe needs and keep attending the pot.
4. **Re-establish a basis for cooking.** If the pause altered timing or liquid level, use reliable food-specific directions to decide the next step. Do not taste food whose safe completion is unknown or treat the earlier vigorous boil as proof of doneness. Use applicable [doneness guidance](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md).
5. **Handle spills only after safety comes first.** If hot liquid entered a burner, appliance, or walking area, do not continue over a slick or scalding surface. Turn off heat only if safe, keep people away, and let equipment and liquid cool under its directions and local safety guidance. Gas or electrical faults need qualified help, not disassembly here.

### Success and stop

Resume only when the liquid has room under a controlled bubble pattern, heat, pot, and surroundings remain safe to use, and you know the recipe's next step. Stop the everyday procedure if the pot is unstable, heat cannot be controlled safely, liquid entered equipment, someone is scalded, or smoke, fire, or gas odour appears. “The food might still be edible” does not override people and equipment.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Pressing down the lid as foam rises:** Reduce heat first. An unstable lid and escaping steam can scald; do not use your body to contain foam.
- **Carrying the pot as soon as heat is lowered:** Wait for bubbling to stop and handling to be safe. Prefer leaving the pot in place rather than carrying boiling water across a room.
- **The pot is too small but you only reduce heat:** Stop and use a larger pot or smaller batch. Dial settings do not create volume inside a pot.
- **The timeline is lost:** Check food-specific directions for the effect of the pause and return to boiling. Do not claim a finished result without evidence.
- **Hot liquid reaches the floor or equipment:** Keep clear and prevent others stepping into it. Do not wipe electrical controls or keep cooking over the spill before cooling and safety checks.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a controllable water-based boil-over, not hot oil, pressure, fire, or equipment repair. Starch, proteins, lids, and batch size affect foam; no universal “wait a few seconds” rule applies. If you cannot see the surface, listen from a safe distance or ask someone present to describe it, but you must still have safe heat control. If movement or standing is difficult, turn heat off, leave the pot in place, and get in-person help. Cleanup may wait until cooling; the liquid does not get permission to keep expanding.
