## 中文

当你本来要在普通视频会议中开摄像头，但预览变黑、冻结或显示了错误摄像头时，用这份 Skill 先确认**实际发出去的画面**，再做有限的界面检查。若你本来就不想开镜头，不需要修；保留关闭即可。这里不拆设备、不改系统深层设置，也不处理账号安全事件。

### 准备与输入

确认会议允许不开摄像头，以及你是否仍能用语音或文字参加。不要只凭自己的小窗判断别人是否能看到：有些应用会隐藏本地预览。先看会议里的摄像头开启状态和应用预览；有需要时问一句“现在能看到我的画面吗？”只在信任该会议及参与者时开启镜头，检查背景中没有不愿分享的人或物。

### 执行

1. **分清预览与发送。** 若会议明确显示镜头关闭，且你想开启，先用会议中的摄像头开关；若本地小窗不见但参与者看得到，查本应用的“显示自己”设置即可，不把它当故障。
2. **检查眼前的遮挡与选择。** 看镜头遮盖是否关闭、摄像头是否接好，并在会议的视频设置里确认所选设备是你打算用的那一个。切换设备后等片刻看预览，别边切边猜。
3. **只核本应用的授权。** 如果应用提示无权限，查看本机或浏览器给**这个会议应用/站点**的摄像头权限；按自己的隐私决定是否允许。受管理设备没有权限时请管理员处理，不绕开限制。不要为一场会关闭安全软件或开放所有网站的摄像头权限。
4. **做一次简单排除。** 若别的普通应用正在占用镜头，先正常关闭那个应用，再回会议看预览；若仍黑屏，可在本机自带相机应用里测试摄像头是否有画面。关闭测试应用后回会议，不同时争用。别在重要会议中反复重启、卸装或改驱动。
5. **核对结果并选降级。** 画面恢复后看预览、向参与者确认一次即可；未恢复时关闭摄像头并说明“镜头暂不可用，我用语音/文字继续”。若视觉展示是本次唯一必要结果，向主持人确认是否延期或改用已允许的材料，不把黑画面当已交付。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你已确认画面正常且对象正确，或明确告诉会议参与者镜头不可用并通过允许的渠道继续/改期。这两个都是完整结果，不要求把硬件修好。

- **自我预览不见：** 先问参与者能否看到，再找应用的本地预览设置；不要立刻打开系统权限。
- **选错外接镜头：** 在应用里选正确设备并看预览；遮住不打算使用的镜头，而不是把错误画面继续发送。
- **预览黑但本机相机能用：** 核本应用权限、镜头选择与其他占用应用；仍失败就降级并记录现象供之后求助。
- **镜头画面里出现私人空间或他人：** 立即关镜头，调整位置和背景，经自己核对后再决定是否开启。已被看到的内容不能靠关镜头撤回。
- **身体、光线或感官条件不适合开镜头：** 用语音、文字或会议认可的静态说明；本篇不把“必须露脸”当成功条件。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

按钮名称和系统权限位置随平台变化；先使用会议内可见的开关、设备选择和预览，不照搬某个平台菜单。摄像头故障若伴随设备损坏、组织限制或账号异常，停在已可用的沟通方式并找相应支持。你可能仍以黑屏以外的形式参与；这不会证明摄像头已经修复。

## English

Use this Skill when you intended to use a camera in an ordinary video meeting but its preview is black, frozen, or showing the wrong camera. Confirm **what is actually being sent** before making a few interface checks. If you prefer the camera off, leave it off. This does not involve opening hardware, deep system changes, or account security incidents.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether the meeting allows camera-off participation and whether voice or text still works for you. Your own thumbnail alone may not prove what others see: some apps hide local self-view. Check the meeting's camera state and preview, and if needed ask, “Can you see my video now?” Turn on the camera only when you trust the meeting and its audience, and check the background for people or objects you do not want to show.

### Execution

1. **Distinguish preview from transmission.** If the meeting says the camera is off and you want it on, use its camera control. If only your local thumbnail is missing while participants see you, check the app's self-view setting instead of treating it as a camera failure.
2. **Check the visible cover and device choice.** Check that the lens cover is open and the camera connected. In meeting video settings select the camera you intend to use. Wait for the preview after switching instead of cycling blindly.
3. **Check permission for this app only.** If prompted about access, inspect camera permission for **this meeting app or site** in your device or browser. Decide according to your privacy preference. On a managed device, ask its administrator rather than bypassing a restriction. Do not disable security software or permit every site to use the camera for one meeting.
4. **Make one simple isolation check.** If another ordinary app may be using the camera, close it normally and recheck the meeting. If still black, test the camera in the device's built-in camera app, close that test, then return to the meeting. Avoid repeated restarts, reinstalls, or driver changes during an important call.
5. **Verify or downgrade.** Once the preview returns, check the intended image and confirm with a participant once. If it does not return, keep the camera off and say, “My camera is unavailable; I'll continue by voice/text.” If a visual demonstration is the only required outcome, agree with the host on postponing or another permitted material. A black picture is not a delivered demonstration.

### Completion, common problems, and recovery

You either confirmed a normal view from the intended camera or told participants the camera is unavailable and continued or rescheduled through an allowed channel. Both are complete outcomes; hardware repair is not required.

- **Your self-view disappeared:** Ask whether others can see you, then check local self-view settings before changing device permissions.
- **The wrong external camera was selected:** Choose the intended device and check its preview. Do not keep broadcasting the unwanted angle.
- **The preview is black but the built-in camera app works:** Check meeting-app permission, device selection, and competing apps. If it persists, downgrade and record the symptom for later support.
- **Your view shows a private space or another person:** Turn the camera off immediately, adjust the setting, and check before deciding to turn it back on. Switching off cannot retract what was already seen.
- **Appearance, lighting, or sensory needs make video unsuitable:** Use voice, text, or a meeting-approved static explanation. Showing your face is not this Skill's success condition.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Button labels and permission locations differ by platform. Start with visible meeting controls, camera selection, and preview rather than copying one product's menu. If the problem involves device damage, organisational controls, or an account issue, stop at a usable communication mode and contact appropriate support. Participating another way does not prove the camera has been repaired.
