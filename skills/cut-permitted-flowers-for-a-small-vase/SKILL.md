## 中文

当你想从自己或经明确许可的**栽培观赏花**中剪几枝放进室内花瓶时，加载这份 Skill。结果是选对开放程度、干净剪取、及时入水并稳妥摆放的一小瓶花。摘残花处理已谢的花；这里剪的是还可供观赏的枝条，两者不会互相替代。

### 准备与输入

确认有权剪取、植物已识别且适合做切花；不从公园、路边或他人花坛采。查品种有无刺激性汁液、尖刺或接触风险，未知就不剪并问可靠园艺来源。准备干净锋利的小剪刀、清水桶/花瓶和不会压坏家具的稳定位置。视力或握力受限时请人协助，不在高处冒险剪。

### 执行

1. **先决定保留哪些花。** 选少量品种允许剪的花枝，留足母株继续生长和原本要给人、传粉者或留种的花。花苞太嫩、已谢残花和不明品种都不在本次采切里。
2. **准备水再下剪刀。** 清洗容器并装清水，放在可安全取用处。按品种说明选择剪取时段和位置，用清洁剪刀作平整切口，不硬扯枝条或挤伤邻芽。
3. **去掉会浸水的叶。** 按品种处理下部叶片，及时把花枝放入水中；需要特殊处理的空心、木质或有汁液茎先查对应资料，不套用一般枝条手法。
4. **摆放并记维护入口。** 花瓶放稳、远离食物处理面、强热源、儿童宠物可接触的不明植物；记下检查水位、换水和移除腐败花枝的时间。水浑或花明显衰败时及时收尾，按本地规则处理材料。

### 成功条件

你能指出这几枝的来源许可、品种与剪取位置；花枝在干净水中，花瓶稳固，维护日期明确。若有接触风险、无许可、容器不洁或不知这种花怎么切，就停止，改用不需采切的展示方式。

### 常见报错与补救

- **先剪后找花瓶：** 下次先备水；已剪枝条尽快按品种指导入清水，不晒在台面上。
- **水里泡了很多叶片：** 去掉可安全去除的水线下叶片并换清水，避免腐败。
- **花瓶摇晃或会漏水：** 换稳固容器和位置，不把美观压过安全。
- **拿不准是否能采：** 向所有者问明，未经许可不剪。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只做少量普通栽培观赏花的获准剪取和基础瓶插，不处理野花采集、鲜花商业加工、植物毒性鉴定或特殊花材药剂。切花有品种差异和有限寿命；AI 指令不能保证开花时长。需要协助剪切时让帮忙的人先确认保留枝条和切口。副作用是一处需要换水的室内摆放。

## English

Load this Skill when you want a few **cultivated ornamental flowers** from plants you own or have explicit permission to cut for a small indoor vase. Finish with suitable blooms cleanly cut, promptly in water, and placed stably. Deadheading removes faded blooms; this harvests stems still worth displaying.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission, species identity, and suitability as cut flowers. Do not collect from parks, roadsides, or someone else's bed. Check species guidance for irritating sap, thorns, or contact concerns; leave an unknown plant and seek reliable advice. Prepare clean sharp small scissors, clean water and a stable vase location that will not damage furniture. Arrange help if vision, grip, or reach makes cutting unsafe.

### Execution

1. **Decide what stays.** Choose only a few species-permitted stems. Leave enough for parent growth and flowers intended for others, pollinators, or seed. Very immature buds, spent blooms, and unidentified species are not this harvest.
2. **Have water ready before cutting.** Clean the container and fill with water in a safe accessible spot. Follow species guidance for timing and cut point, making a neat cut with clean scissors rather than tearing stems or neighboring buds.
3. **Keep leaves out of the water.** Remove permitted lower leaves and place stems in water promptly. Hollow, woody, or sap-bearing stems may need special species directions; do not impose the ordinary method on them.
4. **Display with a maintenance route.** Keep the vase stable, away from food preparation, strong heat, and children's or pets' access to a plant of unknown safety. Set a check for water level, water change, and failing stems. If water turns cloudy or flowers decay, clear them using local waste rules.

### Success

You can identify permission, species, and cut point; stems are in clean water, the vase is stable, and the next water check is set. Stop and use an uncut display route if contact safety, permission, clean water, or species method is unresolved.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You cut before finding a vase:** Prepare water first next time; put existing cut stems into clean water promptly according to species guidance.
- **Many leaves sit below the waterline:** Remove what can safely be removed and use fresh water to avoid decay.
- **The vase rocks or leaks:** Choose a stable vessel and site; appearance does not overrule safety.
- **Permission is unclear:** Ask the owner. Do not cut on assumption.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers a small permitted harvest of ordinary cultivated ornamentals, not wildflower collection, commercial floristry, toxicity identification, or specialised preservatives. Cut flowers differ by species and have a limited life; instructions cannot guarantee vase days. A helper should confirm stems to leave and cut points before assisting. The side effect is an indoor vase requiring water changes.
