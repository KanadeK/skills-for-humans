## 中文

当你打算带一只已由你照料、平时能够牵引的普通狗外出，准备扣上现有装备时，加载这份 Skill。终点是**项圈/胸背和牵引绳实际完好、贴合、不妨碍正常动作，并由能控制它的人拿住**；这不教反应性犬训练或处理危险犬。

### 准备与输入

核对这只狗的既有兽医/训练限制、当地实际佩戴和身份标识要求，以及项圈/胸背品牌的尺码与扣法。狗激烈抗拒、受伤、呼吸异常或你无法稳妥控制时取消普通出行并联系兽医或合格训练人员。不要把英国来源中的身份牌法律条文当全世界规则。

### 执行

1. **看实物损耗。** 查织带、缝线、扣、牵引绳和金属连接点有无裂损、卡滞或磨穿；损坏就换合适完好物，不用胶带或绳结临时修复受力部位。
2. **按这只狗与产品贴合。** 依据产品说明检查松紧、狗能正常呼吸、张口、走动与如厕，装备不摩擦伤口，也不容易从头或肩滑脱。RSPCA 的指宽示例针对普通项圈，不直接套到各种胸背结构。
3. **确认接点和控制者。** 在安全室内扣好牵引绳，轻轻核实卡扣已锁且不会碰开；牵引者能用该长度和握法保持狗在可管理范围，不把绳缠在手腕或儿童身上。
4. **核本地外出前提。** 看狗可进入的地点、牵绳和身份标识的本地要求，确认有清理排泄物的用品；路线与当下天气另交路线 Skill 判断。
5. **作放行/停止决定。** 可用时记下需要后续复查的磨损点；若贴合或控制条件不明，今天不要用该装备外出，找合适替换或合格帮助。

### 成功条件

你能指出装备实际完好、扣接有效、贴合与狗正常动作、谁持绳及当地规则来源，或明确止步。不能把“以前没挣脱过”当作今天扣具完好的证据。

### 常见报错与补救

- **卡扣松或裂：** 直接停用并换合适装备；现场打结不是安全认证。
- **胸背勒住腋下或狗缩躲：** 调整到产品允许的贴合，无法解决就暂停并找合格指导。
- **狗一退就可能脱出：** 在安全室内停下重新核尺码和结构，不拿公路试。
- **牵引者拉不住这只狗：** 改请具能力的人或选择别的照料安排，不以更短的绳硬补。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只狗已习惯基本牵引且没有攻击/严重恐惧问题；本 Skill 不教咬伤处置、强制约束、受伤犬带出或训练新犬。手部握力或行动受限时要有能够真正控制此犬的替代牵引者；视力受限时请人检查扣具裂损和现场环境。副作用是可能因检查不合格而取消原计划。

## English

Load this Skill before clipping on the usual equipment for an ordinary dog in your care that you know and can normally handle. Finish with **an intact, dog-fitting collar or harness and lead that allow normal movement, held by a capable person**. This is not reactive-dog training or dangerous-dog handling.

### Preparation and inputs

Check any existing veterinary/training limits, actual local ID and lead rules, and the maker's sizing and fastening directions. Cancel an ordinary outing and seek veterinary or qualified training advice if the dog strongly resists, is injured, has unusual breathing, or cannot be controlled by this handler. A UK source's ID-tag law is not a worldwide rule.

### Execution

1. **Inspect actual wear.** Check webbing, seams, buckles, lead, and metal attachment for cracks, jams, or fraying. Replace a damaged load-bearing part; tape or a new knot does not certify it.
2. **Fit to this dog and product.** Use maker directions for snugness, normal breathing, opening the mouth, walking, and toileting. Avoid rubbing a wound or an easy slip over head or shoulders. RSPCA's finger-width example is for a standard collar, not every harness geometry.
3. **Confirm connection and handler.** Clip the lead in a safe indoor area and gently check it latched and cannot pop open. The handler must manage this dog with that lead length and grip. Do not wrap the lead around a wrist or child.
4. **Check local prerequisites.** Confirm the actual dog-access, lead, and ID rules and have waste pickup supplies. The route and present weather are a separate decision.
5. **Make a go/hold decision.** Note any wear to recheck if ready. If fit or control is uncertain, do not use this setup outside today; find a suitable replacement or qualified help.

### Success

You can identify sound equipment, working connection, fit and normal movement, actual handler, and local rule source, or state the reason for holding. “It has never slipped before” is not evidence that today's clip works.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The clip is loose or cracked:** Stop using it and replace it; a field knot is not certification.
- **A harness rubs under an arm or the dog shrinks away:** Adjust under the product instructions; if unresolved, pause for qualified fitting advice.
- **A backward step may slip the harness:** Recheck size and construction indoors, not on a road.
- **The handler cannot hold this dog:** Use a capable handler or another care arrangement, not an even shorter lead as a guess.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This dog already tolerates ordinary lead use and has no aggression or severe fear issue. This Skill does not teach bite response, forced restraint, walking an injured dog, or first-time training. If grip or mobility is limited, a genuinely capable alternate handler is necessary. Ask someone to inspect defects and surroundings if vision is limited. The plan may be cancelled when gear fails.
