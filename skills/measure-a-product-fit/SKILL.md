## 中文

当你准备买需要经过门、走廊或楼梯，并在固定位置使用的家具或家电时，先加载这份 Skill。结果是“这个准确型号在现有空间有可核对的进入和使用余量”，或“暂不下单并列出缺失尺寸”。它不负责搬运、安装、燃气或电气接线。

### 准备与输入

准备卷尺、纸或手机记录。记下准确型号、商品完成安装后的宽高深、把手或突起、门和抽屉打开时所占范围，以及包装尺寸。包装与商品尺寸用途不同：前者用于核对进入通道，后者用于核对摆放和操作。需要通风、靠墙距离或固定方式的产品，以该型号说明书为准，不套用别的型号的“标准留缝”。

### 执行

1. **画终点。** 量目标位置在前后、左右、上下实际可用的最窄处；把踢脚线、把手、管线、暖气片、斜墙和不平整位置标出来。只量墙到墙的一次读数不够。
2. **画使用状态。** 按候选型号资料标出开门、拉抽屉、翻盖、伸出脚踏或清洁时需要的空间。确认你仍能到达常用物品；“能塞进去”和“能用”是两个结果。
3. **核对必要余量。** 将商品外形和说明书要求的通风、开门、安装余量一起与终点比较。没有该型号的必要数据，就写“未确认”，不要把零余量算作通过。
4. **倒走进入路线。** 从目标位置回到入口，量每道门的实际净宽和净高、走廊转角、楼梯平台及电梯开口。可拆包装的尺寸也必须来自卖家或制造商确认；不要自行假设能拆门、拆机或斜着硬挤。
5. **做一次逐项裁决。** 分别写下“包装能进”“商品能摆”“使用时能打开”“型号要求的余量够”。四项都有证据才进入购买决定；其中一项不明就先索取准确尺寸或换候选。

### 成功与停止

完成时，你有带单位的现场读数、准确型号的尺寸来源和四项通过/不通过/未确认结论。任一处需要危险搬运、建筑改造、燃气或电气操作时停止在选购阶段，交给相应专业人员判断。此处的“通过”是纸面尺寸核对，不是安装或搬运保证。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看商品正面宽度：** 补量深度、把手、门或抽屉完全打开的范围；换一种开门方向也要重新核对。
- **摆得下，包装进不去：** 用包装外尺寸重查最窄入口和转角；向商家确认可交付方式，不擅自安排拆卸。
- **页面只有约数或系列尺寸：** 找准确型号的规格/安装图；仍缺关键值，就暂停购买并保存待问清单。
- **卷尺读数或方向难辨认：** 请可信的人代量并读回数值，或拍带刻度的照片放大核对；无法取得可靠读数时不要用猜测补洞。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程假设你能合法查看自己的目标位置，并能取得准确型号资料。租住、共享空间或行动不便时，可请有权限的人提供照片和实测数据；无法进入某段通道就保留“未确认”。商品页面和地区常用单位不同，先统一单位再比较。你可能发现原先选的款式不合适；一张草图比买后讨论物理学便宜。

## English

Load this Skill before buying bulky furniture or an appliance that must pass through an entrance and work in a fixed spot. Your outcome is either evidence that the exact model can enter, stand, and operate with the required room, or a short list of missing measurements and a paused purchase. Moving, installing, and gas or electrical work are outside this task.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring a tape measure and a place to record numbers. Identify the exact model, its installed width, height and depth, handles or projections, open-door and open-drawer dimensions, and package dimensions. Package dimensions answer the route question; product dimensions answer the placement and use questions. Take ventilation, wall clearance, and fixing requirements from the exact model's instructions, not a generic rule.

### Execution

1. **Measure the destination.** Record the narrowest usable width, height, and depth at several points. Mark skirting boards, handles, pipes, radiators, sloped walls, and uneven areas. One wall-to-wall number is rarely the whole opening.
2. **Measure the working state.** Use the candidate's specifications to map fully opened doors, drawers, lids, footrests, and cleaning access. Check that you can still reach what you need. Fitting the footprint is only one part of use.
3. **Check required clearance.** Compare the installed product plus its model-specific ventilation, door, and installation allowances with the measured space. If those requirements are unavailable, record “unconfirmed”; zero spare space is not evidence of fit.
4. **Trace the route backward.** From the destination to the entrance, measure clear door openings, corridor turns, stair landings, and lift openings. Use package dimensions for this check. Only count unpacking or removable parts if the seller or manufacturer confirms the delivery method; do not assume you may remove a building door or force an angle.
5. **Record four decisions.** Mark package entry, final placement, full operation, and required model clearance as pass, fail, or unconfirmed. Continue the purchase decision only when all four have evidence. Otherwise get the missing exact dimensions or choose another candidate.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish with site measurements, units, an exact-model specification source, and four explicit decisions. Stop at the shopping stage if the plan would require unsafe moving, building changes, or gas or electrical work; the relevant professional must assess that work. A paper fit check is not a guarantee of delivery or installation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the front width was checked:** Add depth, handles, and the fully open door or drawer range. Recheck if the opening direction changes.
- **It fits the spot but the package cannot enter:** Recheck the narrowest route and turns against package dimensions; ask the seller about available delivery options rather than planning your own disassembly.
- **The listing gives a range or series average:** Find the exact model's specification or installation drawing. If a critical value remains missing, pause and keep a question list.
- **The tape or orientation is difficult to read:** Ask a trusted person to measure and read numbers back, or enlarge a photo that includes the scale. An unreliable reading stays unconfirmed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes you may inspect the destination and can obtain exact-model information. In rented or shared spaces, or when access is difficult, ask someone with access for photos and measurements; mark inaccessible segments unconfirmed. Convert units before comparing regional listings. You may discard a favourite model. The sketch has a lower storage cost than an unusable purchase.
