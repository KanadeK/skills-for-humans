## 中文

你发现自己赶不上普通包裹的原定送达、有人值守点领取或自助柜取件时间，而包裹还可能调整时，用这份 Skill 查该件**真正开放的选项**，选一个自己能履行的交接方案，并核实服务方是否接受。目标是取得新的已确认安排、所需行动和期限；若不能更改，也要知道包裹目前去向与下一步。H133/H134 负责真正领取与设备操作，H136 处理收到后的错件/损伤。本文不承诺改投成功或免费。

### 准备与输入

用承运商或商家官方追踪入口读取包裹编号、当前状态、原地址/取件点、原日期与保留期；找出你确实可用的新日期、可到的服务点或愿意并**被允许**代收的人。分清是你收件、你寄出的件、商家控制的订单，谁有权申请更改可能不同。只从官方入口操作，不在陌生短信链接提交登录密码或一次性验证码。选项可能因寄件方限制、签收条件、物品类别和配送阶段而消失；费用、期限和身份证明以该件当期信息为准。

### 执行

1. **先查还来不来得及。** 看当前状态与最后可变更时间：包裹在运输中、已派送、已暂存、已过领取期限，能做的事不同。原通知仍在手机里不代表箱子还在原柜。先读该件的官方“管理配送/重新投递/暂存”选项；没有选项时询问承运商，必要时找有控制权的寄件方。
2. **比较可用的交接结果。** 按该件实际许可，在改送日期、获准的服务点暂存、官方允许的其他地址、获准代收者之间选一个。核对新地点是否真能到、营业或柜机可用时间、保留期限、是否仍要求本人签名/身份证明，以及额外费用。不要未经邻居同意把包裹改给他，也不要把需当面签收的件擅自改为“随处放”。
3. **提交一项准确请求。** 在官方渠道核对包裹号和新日期/地点/接收人，再确认一次费用与规则后提交。必要的个人信息只给服务方正式渠道；不分享账户验证码给自称快递员的陌生联系人。屏幕显示“已提交”时先保留申请编号，不把它当成“已生效”。
4. **读回确认并交接给未来的自己。** 等服务方新的追踪或确认消息，核对它实际接受的日期、地点、截止、取件凭证和费用。设一个自己会看到的提醒，在新期限前完成 H133/H134 的领取或在家收件。若请求被拒、状态不变或包裹已退回，记录拒绝原因/最新去向，再用正式客服或寄件方选择下一步；不继续按旧安排跑错地点。

### 成功条件

新交接安排已由实际服务方确认，包裹号、日期/地点、领取人条件、费用与下一步能核对；或者你确认该件不可变更，知道最新去向与需要联系谁。仅按下“提交”或获得一个预计时间，不算服务方已接受。

### 常见报错与补救

- **改送按钮不存在：** 查看当前状态和寄件方限制，向官方客服或有权寄件方询问，不用修改账号地址冒充该件改派。
- **旧取件码过期：** 查实时追踪与是否已退回/转点，再联系运营方；不反复在柜上试失效码。
- **新地点营业时间也赶不上：** 提交前换真正可用的日期/地点或获准的代收方案，不把问题移动到下一排队窗口。
- **请求显示已提交但没有变化：** 保留申请编号，在官方追踪中核实是否被接受；原期限临近时尽快联系服务方。
- **费用或身份条件无法接受：** 不确认未经理解的收费或交接方式，选择该件仍允许的其他方案或让其按正式退回/后续流程处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

此 Skill 只处理普通包裹的配送/暂存时间冲突，不修改法律责任、退款、保险或特殊货物手续。UPS、DHL 等不同地区服务的选项与费用可能相反，必须查你的具体包裹。没有智能手机可用承运方正式客服或柜台；行动不便时优先确认可及点或规则许可的代领。新安排可能更晚或收费；状态需要服务方确认后才变。

## English

Use this Skill when you cannot meet an ordinary parcel's planned delivery, staffed-point pickup, or locker collection time and a change may still be possible. Check the **options actually open for this shipment**, choose a handover you can meet, and verify the provider accepted it. The result is a confirmed new plan with duties and deadline, or a clear current location and next action when change is unavailable. H133/H134 handle actual collection and device use; H136 handles wrong or damaged goods after receipt. A change is neither guaranteed nor necessarily free.

### Preparation and inputs

From official carrier or merchant tracking, note parcel reference, current state, original address or point, planned date, and hold deadline. Identify a new date or place you can really use, or a willing **permitted** recipient. The recipient, sender, and merchant can have different rights to request changes. Use an official entry point, not an unfamiliar text link asking for passwords or one-time login codes. Shipper controls, signatures, contents, and journey stage may remove options. Fees, timing, and ID requirements follow the actual shipment.

### Execution

1. **Check whether a change can still happen.** In transit, out for delivery, held for collection, and past the deadline are different states. An old notice does not prove the parcel remains at its original locker. Read this shipment's official manage-delivery, redelivery, or hold choices. If none appear, ask the provider or the sender who controls the request.
2. **Compare attainable handovers.** Among options this parcel permits, consider a new date, approved collection point, approved other address, or a willing authorized receiver. Check real opening/access hours, hold deadline, personal-signature or ID requirement, and any fee. Do not nominate a neighbour without consent or turn an in-person signature shipment into an unattended drop on your own authority.
3. **Submit one accurate request.** Match parcel reference and new date, site, or recipient through the official channel. Confirm any fee and rule before submitting. Share only necessary personal data through that channel; do not forward account login codes to an unknown claimed courier. Keep a request number when it says “submitted”; this alone is not acceptance.
4. **Read back the confirmation.** Wait for updated official tracking or a confirmation message and check the accepted date, site, deadline, proof for pickup, and fee. Set a reminder for the new deadline and use H133/H134 or the agreed home handover. If declined, unchanged, or returned, record the reason and latest route, then ask official support or the sender about next steps. Do not travel under a stale plan.

### Success

The provider has confirmed the new plan, and you can verify parcel reference, date/site, authorized recipient conditions, fee, and next action. A confirmed refusal with latest parcel location and responsible contact is also a valid stop. A click on “submit” or an estimated date is not proof of an accepted change.

### Common errors and recovery

- **No change button appears:** Check state and shipper restrictions, then ask official support or the controlling sender. Editing your account's default address does not necessarily redirect this parcel.
- **The old locker code expired:** Check live tracking for return or transfer before contacting the operator; do not repeatedly try an expired code.
- **The new site's hours still conflict:** Before submitting, choose a date or site you can actually use, or a permitted willing receiver.
- **Submitted request has no status change:** Keep its reference and verify in official tracking; contact the provider promptly if the original deadline is near.
- **Fee or identity condition is unacceptable:** Do not approve an unexplained charge or handover. Consider only other options still offered for this parcel or its formal return path.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers scheduling conflict for an ordinary parcel, not liability, refunds, insurance, or special-goods procedures. UPS and DHL options and charges differ by service and region; check your shipment. Without a smartphone, use official telephone or counter support. For mobility barriers, ask about accessible points or permitted third-party pickup. A new arrangement may arrive later or cost money; it is real only when the provider confirms it.
