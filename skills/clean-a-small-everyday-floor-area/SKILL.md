## 中文

当你家一小片**普通、可识别**的居室地面有灰尘、砂粒或轻微日常污迹，想恢复到能正常、安全走过的状态时，用这份 Skill 先干取颗粒，再按该地板许可决定是否轻微湿擦。目标是看得见的普通脏污减少、地面没有积水或滑腻清洁膜，其他人可重新通行；不承诺把旧划痕或受潮变形擦回出厂状态。厨房食物接触区和明显危险污染另有范围。

### 准备与输入

认清这片是瓷砖/石材、木、强化地板还是其他材料，并找准确产品或安装方的护理说明；重点核对能否用水、允许的工具与清洁品、边缝是否怕积水。看地面实际是可干扫的砂尘、轻微黏迹，还是玻璃碎片、未知化学品、体液/霉、大片积水或暴露电线。准备干净软扫帚、干式拖布或厂家允许的吸尘工具，若说明允许湿擦再备相容产品、少量水与干布。安排家人/宠物绕行的路线，不让清洁水成为新滑倒点。

发现尖锐碎片、湿水接触电器、未知化学品、霉害或无法安全绕开的通道时停止普通地面清洁，按相应专业/当地指导处理。水不会让危险污染自动降级为“轻污”。

### 执行

1. **先划定小范围和通行路。** 不搬无法安全搬动的重物，只移开可轻拿的小件，保留稳定的干燥绕行路线。通知同住者这片暂不能踩，行动辅助设备需要的宽度也算在通行条件内；没有安全绕行路就分更小段或稍后再做。
2. **先干取松散颗粒。** 按地板说明用软扫、干式拖布或允许的吸尘方式，把砂粒和灰尘从小片区域向一个可收集位置带走，避免硬颗粒在湿布下变成磨料。产品禁止的旋转刷、硬毛、金属边工具不因“灰很厚”而获许可。砂屑另按普通垃圾路径收起。
3. **只在有许可时试湿擦。** 若仍有轻微黏迹且该地板/接缝允许湿式护理，按准确地板和清洁品标签选择方法，在不显眼处用少量、不过湿的布或拖头试擦；检查是否变色、发黏、翘边或留下滑膜。有任何变化就停止湿擦。若材料或产品不明，干式除尘已完成，污迹留待确认，不把木/强化板当瓷砖大量浇水。
4. **分小段去污并去残留。** 仅在许可区域用相容工具轻擦，按产品要求更换脏布/脏水、去掉洗剂残留；不把一盆越来越浑的水拖满全屋。石材、瓷砖及板材有不同的缝与涂层要求，不混用酸、漂白剂、研磨粉或蒸汽设备。需要多次强刷或污迹沿缝深入时停下查材料专法。
5. **干燥后再开放行走。** 用厂家允许的方式让清洁区与缝边干燥，保持绕行提醒，收走湿拖具但不让其挡路；检查没有可见积水、洗剂膜、松动地垫或遗留砂粒。只有安全可走时才把轻物件放回和恢复通行。不用脚在未干地面上试滑不滑。

### 成功条件

一小片普通地面的松散砂尘已清除，许可范围内的轻污得到处理，清洁品残留和水迹已按材质去除，地面可正常通行且绕行提示撤回。材质/污染/干燥条件不明时停止湿擦并保留安全通道，也是完成正确判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **未知材质直接上湿拖或蒸汽：** 停下查准确地板说明；先完成干式除尘，不把别家“防水”广告当本地板许可。
- **砂粒没扫就拖，留下新划痕：** 停止摩擦，干取残余颗粒；划痕不是加力拖地能修复的。
- **脏水继续拖，地面变成灰膜：** 换干净布/水，按产品说明去掉膜并充分干燥；不要叠加更多洗剂。
- **地面湿滑仍让人走：** 保持绕行/提醒，先按材料方法干燥；无安全绕路就缩小施工段。
- **木/强化板边缝开始翘或积水：** 停止加水、处理可见水，查制造商与专业维修；不继续用热风或蒸汽“烘平”。
- **清洁后工具和线缆挡路：** 移到安全位置，确保地面干且通道无绊物再结束。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理普通居室**小范围、可识别的日常砂尘与轻污**，不处理霉、体液、危险化学品、大面积积水、破玻璃或电气接触。地板产品对湿擦甚至蒸汽的许可存在真实差异，以准确品牌/安装说明为准；本篇不给固定水量或化学配方。视力、弯腰或移动不便时用可及高度的长柄软工具、较小区块和可信协助，不能建立安全绕行路就不同时拖全宽通道。地面需要真实干燥时间，家人要暂时换路。

## English

Use this Skill for a **small identifiable ordinary room-floor area** with dust, grit, or light everyday soil. Remove loose particles dry first, then decide whether the actual flooring permits light damp cleaning. The result is less visible ordinary soil and a normally walkable surface without puddles or slippery cleaner film. This does not rub old scratches or moisture swelling back to factory condition. Kitchen food-contact zones and hazardous contamination have other boundaries.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify tile or stone, wood, laminate, or another material and find the exact product or installer care guidance. Check whether water, particular tools and products, and moisture at joints are allowed. Distinguish dry dust and grit or a light sticky mark from broken glass, unknown chemicals, bodily fluids or mould, broad standing water, or exposed electrical contact. Have a clean soft broom, dry mop, or permitted vacuum tool. Only if damp work is allowed, prepare a compatible product, small water amount, and dry cloth. Arrange a route for people and pets around the patch rather than creating a new slippery route.

Stop ordinary cleaning for sharp fragments, water near electricity, unknown chemicals, mould, or a passage that cannot be safely bypassed. Water does not downgrade a hazardous material into light soil.

### Execution

1. **Bound the area and a passable path.** Do not move furniture too heavy to move safely. Lift only small easy items and keep a stable dry detour. Tell others this patch is temporarily unavailable; room for an assistive device is part of the path. If no safe bypass exists, work a smaller section or defer the job.
2. **Lift dry particles first.** Under floor guidance, use a soft broom, dry mop, or permitted vacuum setting to draw grit and dust toward a collectable point. A wet cloth over sand becomes an abrasive. A forbidden beater brush, hard bristle, or metal edge does not become permitted because soil is thick. Send ordinary grit to the appropriate waste route.
3. **Trial damp work only with permission.** If light stickiness remains and this floor and its joints permit damp care, follow exact floor and cleaner labels. Try a small amount on an inconspicuous spot with a cloth or mop that is damp rather than wet. Stop for colour change, tackiness, raised edges, or slippery film. When material or product is unknown, dry dusting can be complete; leave the spot for confirmation rather than flooding wood or laminate as if it were tile.
4. **Work in small sections and remove residue.** Only in permitted zones, wipe gently with a compatible tool. Refresh dirty cloth or water and remove product residue as its label directs. Do not carry an increasingly dirty bucket across the room. Stone, tile, and planks have different joint and finish needs; do not improvise acid, bleach, abrasive powder, or steam. Stop for repeated hard scrubbing or soil penetrating a seam and find material-specific care.
5. **Open the area only after drying.** Let the patch and joints dry under maker guidance, keeping the detour notice. Put wet tools away from the passage and check for no visible puddle, cleaner film, bunched mat, or remaining grit. Return light objects and reopen walking only after the area is safe. Do not test slipperiness with a foot on a floor still wet.

### Success

Loose dust and grit are removed from a small ordinary patch. Light soil within the allowed method is treated; residue and moisture are removed under material guidance. Walking resumes with the detour withdrawn. Stopping damp work when floor, soil, or drying conditions are uncertain while retaining a safe route is also a correct decision.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An unknown floor receives a wet or steam mop:** Stop and check its exact care. Complete dry dusting first; another brand's waterproof claim is not this floor's permission.
- **Sand is mopped into new scratches:** Stop friction and remove remaining dry particles. More pressure cannot clean away a scratch.
- **Dirty water leaves a grey film:** Change cloth and water, remove film under the product label, and dry. Do not add more cleaner on top.
- **Others walk through a wet patch:** Keep the detour or notice until material-safe drying is complete. With no bypass, shrink the section.
- **Wood or laminate seams swell or hold water:** Stop adding water, remove visible liquid, and ask maker or professional repair. Hot air or steam is not a flattening fix.
- **Tools or cords block the finished route:** Move them safely and check both a dry floor and unobstructed passage before closing the task.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers **small, identifiable everyday dust/grit and light soil** in ordinary rooms. It excludes mould, bodily fluids, hazardous chemicals, broad flooding, broken glass, and electrical contact. Real floors differ in damp and even steam permissions; the exact product or installer guidance controls. This Skill supplies no universal water volume or chemical mixture. For limited sight, bending, or movement, use a reachable long-handled soft tool, smaller sections, or trusted help. If no safe bypass exists, do not clean the whole passage at once. Drying takes time and sends others around the patch briefly.
