## 中文

当你已在照料一只普通室内猫，需要新设或调整一处猫砂盆位置时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**让猫能安静、安全到达并转身使用的真实设施**，而不是仅选一个人类闻不到的角落。清理已用猫砂是另一项任务。

### 准备与输入

观察这只猫目前接受的砂盆和猫砂、体型、行动能力，以及家里安静且能定期清理的位置。不同猫有自己的偏好；原盆仍可用时不突然撤掉它。若猫近期突然不用盆、排尿困难或显得痛苦，尽快联系兽医，不把换位置当诊断。家中儿童不能接触猫砂和废物。

### 执行

1. **筛位置。** 选猫能随时到达、通道不易被门或人堵住、噪声少且不紧贴食水或睡处的地方。不要把盆藏到你以后根本没法清理的角落。
2. **核盆本身。** 猫要能进出、转身和掩埋；年老或行动不便的猫可能需要低入口。查本猫过往是否接受带盖盆，不能为了遮味强加会让它退避的门帘。
3. **按熟悉条件设置。** 放稳砂盆，加入它原本能接受或品种/照料资料允许的猫砂和深度。要更换砂种、盆型或位置时逐步过渡，别把所有条件一次改完。
4. **现场试通路。** 确认猫能从常活动区独立走到盆，没有被其他动物堵住；你能拿出清理工具、把废物安全交到当地规定路线并洗手。
5. **留下复看点。** 记盆的位置、猫是否实际使用、清理负责人与下一次检查；布置完成不等于猫已接受。出现持续避用或异常行为时请兽医或合格行为人员判断。

### 成功条件

猫有一处合适、稳定、可到达的砂盆；你能指出为什么选此处以及如何清理与复查。如果位置、入口、猫砂偏好或使用情况不能确认，就保留已有可用盆并继续观察，不把新盆摆好当作验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **洗衣机旁一开机猫就跑：** 改找更安静且可到达的位置。
- **高边盆让猫跨不过去：** 选符合这只猫行动能力的入口，不要求它先“学会”跨越。
- **更换全部猫砂后拒用：** 依猫的习惯和可靠指南逐步调整，先保留可用选择。
- **多人家中盆前常被堵：** 重新安排位置与数量，必要时咨询专业建议；并排摆多个盆不一定形成独立选择。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份 Skill只处理已由你照料的普通室内猫及一处非医疗设施，不解释排泄异常的病因，也不处理流浪猫。多个猫、已有慢性问题或特殊行动限制需要结合兽医和可信猫行为资料。你可以用可读/触觉标记记录清理物品位置，行动不便时选能安全触及的砂盆；猫仍须能使用。现实副作用是增加一处持续维护任务。

## English

Load this Skill when you already care for an ordinary indoor cat and need to establish or adjust a litter station. Finish with a **real quiet, safe, reachable tray the cat can turn around in**, not merely a corner people cannot smell. Cleaning a used tray is a separate task.

### Preparation and inputs

Observe this cat's accepted tray and litter, size, mobility, and quiet places you can actually clean. Individual preferences differ; keep an existing usable tray while trying a change. If the cat suddenly avoids the tray, has trouble urinating, or appears distressed, contact a veterinarian promptly rather than treating a location change as diagnosis. Keep children away from litter and waste.

### Execution

1. **Screen locations.** Choose a place the cat can reach at all times, not blocked by doors or people, with little noise and apart from food, water, and sleeping spots. Do not hide the tray so thoroughly that you cannot service it.
2. **Check the tray.** The cat should enter, turn, and dig comfortably. An older or mobility-limited cat may need a low entry. Respect its reaction to covered trays; a flap that traps smell for people may discourage use.
3. **Start with familiar conditions.** Set the tray stably with litter and depth the cat already accepts or reliable care guidance supports. Change litter, tray, or location gradually instead of changing every condition at once.
4. **Walk the actual route.** Confirm the cat can reach it from normal spaces without another animal blocking it. Confirm you can reach cleaning supplies, use a local waste route, and wash your hands afterward.
5. **Set a review.** Record location, actual use, who cleans, and next check. Placement alone is not acceptance. Persistent avoidance or unusual behavior belongs with a veterinarian or qualified behavior professional.

### Success

The cat has a suitable stable reachable tray, and you can explain its location and cleaning/review route. If access, entrance, litter preference, or use remains uncertain, keep the existing usable tray and observe rather than claiming the new setup is accepted.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A washing machine starts and the cat flees:** Find a quieter accessible spot.
- **The high side is hard to cross:** Choose an entry suited to this cat's mobility instead of expecting it to adapt by force.
- **An abrupt complete litter change leads to avoidance:** Use a gradual transition guided by the cat and reliable advice; retain a usable option.
- **Several animals block the route:** Reconsider location and resource count and seek qualified advice if needed. Trays side by side may not provide independent choices.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is one ordinary nonmedical household station for a cat already in your care, not a diagnosis of toileting problems or stray-cat handling. Multi-cat homes, chronic problems, and special mobility needs may require veterinary or qualified behavior advice. Keep cleaning supplies marked in an accessible format and place the tray where you can safely reach it, while preserving the cat's access. One continuing maintenance task remains.
