## 中文

当你只为一次普通任务需要一件低风险用品，家里没有，而借用或租用似乎比购买合适时，加载这份 Skill。完成结果是**真实可执行的借用安排，或明确知道这条路暂时不成立**；“朋友可能有”不算现货。它不处理专业工具培训、危险设备、身份凭据、租赁法律争议或已经借到手后的归还台账。

### 准备与输入

先写下这次要完成的工作、物品必须满足的少数条件、使用日期与预计归还日期。列出你愿意联系的人或当地正规借用服务；不要默认某位熟人必须出借。馆方/平台可能有预约、开放时段、费用、押金、年龄或使用限制，逐项查当期说明；不同地方的规则不能互抄。

如果物品本身需要专门培训、安装或安全检查，先按提供方的要求确认资格与条件。无法安全判断它适不适合时，停止这条普通借用路线，不靠“借来的就不用看说明”绕过限制。行动、阅读或交通不便时，先核对取还是否可及，必要时选其他渠道。

### 执行

1. **限定一次用途。** 写成“周六下午需要一个能放下这些文件的展示板，周日归还”一类的工作和时间，不写“以后可能会用”。规格只写足以判断能否完成这次工作的条件；尺寸或兼容性复杂时另行核对。
2. **查渠道是否真实可用。** 对熟人，先问是否有合适物品且那段时间愿意借；对借用服务，查当前目录、可预约日期、取还地点、费用与条件。网页显示“有”不等于你已预约，熟人说“我看看”也不是同意。
3. **给对方一个容易拒绝的问题。** 说清用途、借用和归还时间、会如何取还，允许对方拒绝或提出条件。别把“你周六又不用”当成出借许可。收到明确同意或服务预约确认后，记下其范围；不需要把对方的私人信息复制到公开清单。
4. **核对取还和物品状态。** 在取得前确认地点、开放时间、携带负担、使用限制与应有附件；取得时按对方或服务要求核对明显损坏、缺件和可用性。状态异常先说明，别自行修理或默默带走。归还日期、地点和要求留在你能看到的记录里。
5. **设一个等待截止点。** 若对方未答复、预约未确认、取件赶不上使用、条件或费用不可接受，把借用标“未成立”，再回到购买缺口或改变这次任务。不要因为已经问了三个人，就在表格里提前写“已解决”。
6. **完成交接。** 借用成立时，写一行`用途｜物品与提供方｜确认依据｜取件与归还时间地点｜限制/费用｜下一步`。借入后再按实际状态记录归属和归还；这里的完成不等于物品已在你手里，也不等于任务已完成。

### 成功与停止

你能指向明确同意或预约、能满足用途的物品、可行取还时间和实际要遵守的条件，才把“可借”作为本次购买的替代路线；领取前仍标“待取得”。任何关键条件未确认，就保留采购缺口或改期。若物品有安全疑虑、来源要求你做不具备资格的操作，停止借用和使用；这份清单不能发许可证。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“有空借你”没有日期：** 补问这次具体使用与归还时间；没有明确答复就不从采购缺口中删掉。
- **网站显示库存但预约失败：** 以实际确认页/通知为准，查另一时段或另一渠道；别按网页快照出发。
- **取还路程或开放时间不合适：** 在付款或答应前重新计算能否到达、搬运和归还；不适合就改渠道或停止。
- **到手才发现缺件或损坏：** 按提供方要求记录并告知，不自行修理；用途不能安全完成时不使用，并启用备用计划。
- **对方拒绝或没有回复：** 礼貌结束，这只是借用路线不成立，不是对方欠你一个物品。

### 假设、替代与已知副作用

这适合可退出的普通借用；服务是否存在、费用、押金、规则和人与人之间的信任安排因地而异。没有借用渠道时，不编造一个；可以延后任务、找其他合适方式或保留有依据的购买需求。副作用是你可能少买一件，也多出明确的取还义务；取得后要把归还安排继续保留。

## English

Load this Skill when you need a low-risk item for one ordinary task, do not have it at home, and borrowing or renting seems more suitable than buying. Finish with **a workable, confirmed borrowing arrangement or a clear finding that this route is not available yet**. “A friend might have one” is not stock on hand. This does not teach specialist-tool use, hazardous equipment handling, credential sharing, rental-law disputes, or the return record after you take possession.

### Preparation and inputs

Write the job, the few conditions the item must meet, the use date, and the expected return date. List people you are comfortable asking or a formal local lending service; no acquaintance is obliged to lend. A service may set reservations, opening hours, fees, deposits, age limits, or use restrictions. Check its current terms rather than importing another location's rules.

If the item requires training, installation, or safety checks, verify the provider's requirements first. If you cannot safely tell whether it suits the job, stop this ordinary borrowing route; an item being borrowed does not remove its instructions. Check whether collection and return are accessible for your mobility, reading, or transport needs, and choose another route if they are not.

### Execution

1. **Define one use.** Write a job and time, such as “a display board that holds these papers on Saturday afternoon, returned Sunday,” rather than “might be useful someday.” List only the conditions needed to judge this job; check complex sizing or compatibility separately.
2. **Check whether the route is real.** Ask a person whether a suitable item is available for those dates and whether they are willing to lend it. For a service, check its current catalog, reservable dates, collection and return places, fees, and terms. “Available” on a page is not your reservation; “I'll check” is not consent.
3. **Make refusal easy.** State your use, borrowing and return times, and how collection and return would work. Allow the owner to decline or set conditions. “You won't use it on Saturday” is not permission. After clear agreement or a service booking, record its scope without copying someone's private details into a public list.
4. **Check collection, return, and condition.** Before collection, confirm place, opening hours, carrying burden, permitted use, and expected accessories. At collection, follow the provider's process to note visible damage, missing parts, and whether it can do the job. Report a problem before taking or using it; do not repair it yourself. Keep the return date, place, and conditions where you can see them.
5. **Set a reply cutoff.** If the person does not answer, the booking is not confirmed, pickup misses the use, or terms and cost do not work for you, mark the borrowing route “not arranged” and revisit the purchase gap or change the task. Asking three people has not yet filled the gap.
6. **Leave a handoff record.** For a confirmed route, write `Use | item and provider | confirmation | collection and return times/places | conditions/cost | next action`. After you borrow it, track ownership and return against what actually happened. Finishing this check does not mean the item is in your hands or the job is done.

### Success and stop conditions

Only treat borrowing as an alternative to this purchase when you have clear agreement or a booking, a suitable item, workable collection and return times, and the actual conditions. Until collection, mark it “awaiting pickup.” If a material condition remains unknown, keep the purchase gap or reschedule. Stop if the item has a safety concern or the provider requires an operation you are not qualified to perform. This list cannot issue a licence.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Borrow it sometime” has no dates:** Ask about this specific use and return period. Without a clear answer, do not remove the purchase gap.
- **The website shows stock but booking fails:** Rely on the actual confirmation page or notice; try another time or route rather than travelling from a screenshot.
- **Travel or opening hours do not work:** Before paying or agreeing, check whether you can collect, carry, and return it. Change routes or stop if you cannot.
- **Parts are missing or damage appears at collection:** Record and report it as the provider requires; do not repair it yourself. If the job cannot be done safely, do not use it and take your backup route.
- **The person declines or does not reply:** Close politely. This route failed; the other person does not owe you an item.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers ordinary borrowing you can leave or change. Services, fees, deposits, rules, and person-to-person trust vary by location. If no route exists, do not invent one: delay the task, use another suitable route, or retain an evidenced purchase need. You may buy one fewer item and gain a clear collection-and-return obligation. Keep the return plan after you collect it.
