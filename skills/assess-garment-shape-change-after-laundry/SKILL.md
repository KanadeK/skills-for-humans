## 中文

当你洗涤或烘干后发现一件普通衣物**缩水、拉长、扭斜或局部变形**，用这份 Skill 保存证据，判断是否有标签允许的温和整理，还是停止并接受变化/求助。外来串色和原色褪失另有流程；开线或附件损坏归普通缝补范围。不要将“缩水衣服泡某种东西再猛拉”当通用修复配方，有些尺寸变化不可逆。

### 准备与输入

读衣物洗涤、脱水、滚筒或平铺晾干标签，记录实际机型程序、水温与烘干经历。找洗前照片、尺寸记录或同款未洗参照；在稳定干净平面摆好衣物，记肩宽、衣长或最明显的变形位置。若是羊毛、丝绸、皮革、贵重结构服装或安全功能衣物，停下通用处理，按制造商/专业护理方案。

### 执行

1. **先停止继续施加热与拉力。** 不再给这件衣物追加热烘、热水或强行拉扯；如仍湿，双手托住衣身放到标签允许的支撑上。只靠领口、袖子或细肩带吊起，会把本来可能可整理的湿衣继续拉长。
2. **确认变的是哪一维。** 与洗前记录/未受影响部位比对长度、宽度、缝线方向和印花位置；皱褶造成的暂时不平整、湿重下垂、真正缩水或接缝扭斜不自动同治。没有可靠参照时记“疑似”，不要宣布尺寸已精确减少多少。
3. **核对可能触发条件。** 查本次温度、脱水、烘干与支撑是否超出标签或制造商限制；这些是线索，不等于已经证明唯一根因。若还有开线、撕裂或装饰松脱，先停整理，转对应修补/专业判断。
4. **仅在仍湿且允许时轻柔整理。** 标签允许平铺且衣物尚湿、无损坏时，在干净平面托住主体，按原缝线和合理轮廓轻轻摆平，不用力拉到洗前数字，也不使用无来源的浸泡/化学品。保持标签要求的支撑晾干，复查是否稳定；一有纤维或缝线受力异常立即停止。
5. **作现实去向决定。** 干后仍明显不合身或结构变形时，不承诺“再洗一次会恢复”；保留标签、程序、照片，向制造商/专业护理询问是否有适用办法，或根据穿用需求接受、改用、退役。若涉及退换，按商家政策另行处理，本篇不判断法律权利。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你已记录真实形变和本次处理条件，知道可否做一次标签允许的轻柔整理，或已经停止并选专业/接受/停用去向；衣物没有因暴力拉伸再添破损。完成不以恢复原尺寸为必要条件。

- **湿衣越拽越长：** 立刻停止拉扯，在允许的平面上托住重量，保持原有缝线方向。
- **已干且比原来短：** 不靠更热的水、蒸汽或酸碱“激活恢复”，保留证据并咨询适用专业方法。
- **原来只是皱，误判缩水：** 对照同一位置、自然状态和衣物标签；整理褶皱按该衣物许可方式进行。
- **缝线扭斜或出现小破口：** 停止用力塑形，按普通修补/专业护理范围处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只涉及普通衣物洗后形状，不提供纺织修复承诺或特殊功能装备维修。不同纤维、织法、成衣结构的尺寸稳定性不同；标签与实际测量比“棉/化纤都一样”可靠。测量或伸手困难时可在桌面标记参照点、用大字照片或可信现场协助。温和整理可能只改善一部分外观，也可能不能改变缩水。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary garment looks **shrunken, stretched, twisted, or locally distorted after washing or drying**. Preserve evidence and decide whether gentle reshaping under its label is justified or whether to stop and accept the change or seek help. Incoming dye and original colour loss have other routes; open seams and broken trim belong to repair. There is no universal soak-and-pull formula for shrunken clothing. Some size changes are irreversible.

### Preparation and inputs

Read wash, spin, tumble, and flat-dry care instructions and note the actual washer cycle, water temperature, and dryer history. Find a prewash photo, measurements, or an unwashed matching reference. Lay the item on a stable clean surface and note shoulder width, length, or the clearest distorted point. For wool, silk, leather, valuable structured clothing, or protective-function wear, stop this generic route and use maker or professional care.

### Execution

1. **Stop adding heat and pull.** Do not apply another hot tumble, hot water, or forceful stretching. If still wet, support the garment body with both hands on a label-allowed surface. Lifting only by collar, sleeve, or thin strap can extend the distortion.
2. **Find which dimension changed.** Compare length, width, seam direction, and print placement with prewash records or an unaffected area. Temporary wrinkling, sag under wet weight, true shrinkage, and twisted seams do not share one fix. Without a reliable reference, record “suspected,” not an invented exact loss.
3. **Review possible triggers.** Compare the actual heat, spin, drying, and support with the label and maker limits. These are clues, not proof of one cause. Stop reshaping if a seam has opened, fabric has torn, or trim has come loose; use an appropriate repair or professional route.
4. **Reshape gently only while damp and allowed.** If flat drying is permitted, the item is still damp, and there is no damage, support the body on a clean flat surface and arrange it along the original seam and reasonable outline. Do not pull it to a prewash number or add an unverified chemical soak. Keep the labelled support until dry and check whether shape holds. Stop at any strain on fibres or seams.
5. **Choose a realistic destination.** If it remains badly misshapen or no longer fits when dry, do not promise another wash will restore it. Keep label, cycle, and photos and ask the maker or professional cleaner about any suitable option, or accept, repurpose, or retire it according to how you use it. Merchant returns follow the actual shop policy; this Skill does not decide legal rights.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You recorded actual shape change and treatment conditions, know whether one label-compatible gentle arrangement was possible, or stopped with a professional, acceptance, or retirement route. Forceful stretching has not added tears. Restoring original dimensions is not required for a complete decision.

- **The wet garment lengthens as you pull:** Stop immediately and support its weight flat when permitted, preserving seam direction.
- **It is dry and shorter than before:** Do not use hotter water, steam, acid, or alkali as a guessed “restore” trigger. Preserve evidence and ask about an applicable professional method.
- **It was wrinkled rather than shrunken:** Compare the same points in a natural position under its label. Handle wrinkles only by a permitted route.
- **A seam twists or a small tear appears:** Stop shape pulling and route to ordinary repair or professional care.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers the shape of ordinary clothing after laundry, without textile-restoration promises or protective-gear repair. Fibre, knit, weave, and construction have different dimensional stability. Labels and actual reference measurements are stronger than “all cotton/synthetics behave alike.” Use tabletop markers, enlarged photos, or trusted in-person help if measuring or reaching is difficult. Gentle arrangement may help appearance only partly or not reverse shrinkage.
