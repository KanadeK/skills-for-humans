## 中文

当一份大包装超过你自己的实际用量，但可能与熟人的需求合在一起时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**付款前决定买几份、每份归谁，或取消共购**。这里只适合可按原包装分开的普通商品：例如一箱独立标注的密封食品，或能逐件点清的普通日用品。需要拆开食物、转装化学品、共享药品或处理陌生人交易时停止；本 Skill 不处理买后的保存、配送和争议。

### 准备与输入

读现售包装的实际件数、每件规格、适用日期或说明、现场总价和额外费用。先写你自己在合理使用窗口内确实能用的件数，以及你最多愿意先付多少。对食品，只有独立密封且每份保留必要标签与日期的包装才进入共购判断；不要把开封后重新分袋算作“原包装”。

列出你愿意询问的人和能双方接受的领取方式、最晚答复时间。你不需要收集多余的地址或个人资料，也不应把朋友的沉默当作同意。能否携带整包、在交接前按标签要求暂放并按约定交接，也是购买前必须确认的输入；没有可靠条件就不共购需要该条件的商品。

### 执行

1. **先看自己要多少。** 例：一包 12 件，你确定只会用 4 件，就把自己的份额写成 4，而不是因为包装有 12 件就把需求改成 12。商品不适用或日期赶不上时，直接退出这个选项。
2. **发出具体、可拒绝的邀请。** 说明商品、每份数量、按当前价格算出的个人应付金额及可能的额外费用、领取方式和你何时需要答复。例如“这包有 12 件，我拿 4 件；你愿意按实际价格拿 4 件，并在周末领取吗？不方便完全没关系。”普通费用分摊先说清，不事后补一项。
3. **只登记明确承诺。** 对方确认数量、费用和领取方式后，才把份额写进分配表。“也许”“下次看看”和表情回应都不是可计数的购物单位。没人及时回复，就按自己的量重新选择。
4. **对齐包装件数。** 你与已确认者的份额之和不能超过实际件数；有剩余时，只有你自己在适用窗口内能用且负担得起，才可记为你的份额。不能把未分配件数放到“以后找人”栏。若分配不齐，选较小包装、减少购买量或不共购。
5. **最后核对付款与交接能力。** 以结账前真实价格重算每人金额和你要先付的总额，确认你能携带、按标签条件暂放并按约定交接。价格变化或有人退出时，回到第 3 步重新告知并确认份额；未重新确认前不要付款。

### 成功条件

结账前你有一张简单分配表：**包装共几件、每人已确认几件、每人应付多少、怎样领取、是否有你能用掉的剩余**。总数吻合且你承担得起，才作出“可以买”的决定；否则“改买自己的量或取消”同样是完成。别把对方未来一定来领取当作已经验收的事实。

### 常见报错、补救与停止

- **ERR_INTEREST_ONLY：** 对方说“听起来不错”但没有确认份额或领取方式。发一次具体澄清；到你设定的购买时间仍无明确答复，就不计入需求。
- **ERR_COUNT_MISMATCH：** 承诺合计 10 件，包装却有 12 件。剩余 2 件只有你能真实用掉才留下；否则换规格或取消。不要把差额派给最客气的人。
- **ERR_LATE_WITHDRAWAL：** 付款前有人退出。重新合计和确认费用；条件不再成立就不买这包。
- **ERR_LABEL_LOST：** 食品分开后有人拿不到必要标签、日期或原密封。停止该食品共购，选各自可独立购买的包装。
- **ERR_UPFRONT_COST：** 分摊后看似便宜，但你今天付不起整包或无法搬运。选个人可承担的数量；共购不是透支许可。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里假设参与者可以自由拒绝、能沟通具体数量与普通费用，并有双方接受的交接方式。价格、费用、支付习惯和食品标签要求因地区而异，以现场与当地信息为准。说话或听力不方便时用文字；不想提供住址时约定双方接受的公共交接点或不共购。任何人不愿意承担费用、取货或保存责任，都不需要解释理由。

现实副作用是购买前要多等一次回复；这比先买 12 件再试着给余下 8 件寻找主人更容易收尾。

## English

Load this Skill when a large pack exceeds what you would use yourself but could match the confirmed needs of people you know. The result is a **pre-purchase decision about how many packs to buy and who would take each share**, or a decision not to share a pack. Use it only for ordinary goods that can be separated as sold, such as individually sealed and labelled food packs or countable household items. Stop if sharing requires opening food, transferring chemicals, sharing medicines, or trading with strangers. Storage, delivery, and disputes after purchase are separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the current number of units, amount per unit, relevant dates or instructions, total shelf price, and any extra charges. Write down how many units you yourself can realistically use within the relevant window and how much you can pay up front. Food only qualifies when each person's share stays factory-sealed and retains the necessary label and date; repacking opened food is not the same thing.

Choose whom you are comfortable asking, a handover method both sides can accept, and a latest reply time. You need no unnecessary address or personal details, and silence is not consent. Confirm before buying that you can carry the whole pack, hold it under its label's conditions until handover, and manage the planned pickup. If you cannot meet a required condition, do not share-buy that product.

### Execution

1. **Count your own need first.** If a pack holds 12 units and you will use 4, record your share as 4. Do not redefine your need as 12 because the box does. Remove an unsuitable product or one whose date will not work.
2. **Make a specific invitation that allows a no.** State the product, units per share, each person's cost based on the current price, any extra charges, the proposed pickup, and when you need an answer. For example: “There are 12 units; I will take 4. Would you like 4 at the actual shared price and pick them up this weekend? No problem if not.” Agree on ordinary cost sharing before purchase.
3. **Record only explicit commitments.** A share counts only after the person confirms the quantity, cost, and pickup plan. “Maybe,” “next time,” and an interested emoji are not countable units. If nobody answers in time, choose again using only your own need.
4. **Match the pack count.** Your share and all confirmed shares cannot exceed the actual count. Keep any remainder only if you can use it within its applicable window and afford it yourself. Do not reserve unassigned units for hypothetical future takers. If the allocation fails, choose a smaller pack, buy less, or cancel the shared purchase.
5. **Recheck payment and handover capacity.** Use the real price before checkout to recalculate each person's share and the full amount you must first pay. Confirm that you can carry, hold the pack under its label's conditions, and hand it over as agreed. If the price changes or someone withdraws, tell everyone affected and reconfirm their shares from step 3; do not pay on an obsolete allocation.

### Success

Before checkout you have a simple allocation: **actual units in the pack, units each person confirmed, each share's cost, how it will be collected, and any remainder you can personally use**. If the count and your capacity work, the decision may be “buy.” Otherwise “buy only my own quantity” or “cancel” completes the task. Another person's future pickup is still a plan, not a completed handover.

### Common errors, recovery, and stop points

- **ERR_INTEREST_ONLY:** Someone likes the idea but has not confirmed units or pickup. Ask once for the missing detail. If the reply deadline passes, exclude that share.
- **ERR_COUNT_MISMATCH:** Confirmed shares total 10 but the pack holds 12. Keep the other 2 only if you will really use them; otherwise change pack or cancel. Do not assign the difference to the politest person.
- **ERR_LATE_WITHDRAWAL:** Someone withdraws before payment. Recount and confirm costs again; if the conditions fail, do not buy this pack.
- **ERR_LABEL_LOST:** Someone would receive food without its necessary label, date, or factory seal. Stop this food-sharing plan and use separately purchasable packs.
- **ERR_UPFRONT_COST:** Shares look cheap, but you cannot pay for or carry the entire pack today. Choose an amount you can manage yourself; a group plan is not permission to overextend.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes everyone can freely decline, discuss exact quantities and ordinary costs, and agree on a practical handover. Prices, charges, payment customs, and food-labelling rules vary by place; use the actual offer and local information. Use text rather than speech when it is easier to access. If sharing an address is unwelcome, agree on an acceptable public pickup place or skip the group purchase. No one owes an explanation for declining the cost, pickup, or storage responsibility.

The purchase may wait for one more reply. That is easier to resolve than buying 12 units first and searching for owners of the other 8.
