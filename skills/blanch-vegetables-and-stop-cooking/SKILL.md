## 中文

当可靠配方要求你把**普通蔬菜短时放入沸水、随即取出，并在需要时迅速冷却以停止继续加热**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是按该蔬菜与用途规定的起点、时长和停止动作完成一次焯煮。它不是“所有蔬菜两分钟”的表，也不处理罐藏、冷冻保存计划、婴幼儿食品或不明状态的食材。

### 准备与输入

读清你这次要用的蔬菜、切块大小、数量、配方规定的沸水量/入锅方式、计时起点、可观察完成信号，以及要不要立即冷却。先把蔬菜按可靠说明清洗、整理；食材状态可疑时不以焯水恢复安全。准备[稳妥的煮锅](../set-up-a-pot-for-boiling/SKILL.md)、能一次捞起或分批捞起的篮具/漏勺、可访问的计时器，以及配方要求的冷水或冰水。盛热水的锅不应成为你临时寻找漏勺的提醒器。

### 执行步骤

1. **先建立配方要求的沸腾。** 水、锅和蔬菜量按这次可靠说明来；锅内留食物加入后仍能冒泡和操作的余量。盖子或篮具只按锅与配方的方式使用，不封闭加压。
2. **以可控批量下料。** 用合适工具让蔬菜靠近水面缓慢入锅，不从高处扔进滚水。放入过多导致锅快溢、长时间不能恢复要求的沸腾时，先减小批量并按配方重设这一批；不能用“多煮一会儿”猜一个通用补偿时间。
3. **按说明指定的事件计时。** 有些流程在下料后计时，有些在水重新沸腾时计时；照当前蔬菜的可靠说明执行。下料后水暂时不翻滚，就观察是否已恢复，不拿空锅先前的沸腾冒充本批的计时起点。
4. **到点及时取出。** 结合配方的时长与适用的颜色、质地或其他结束线索，用篮具或漏勺把蔬菜移出。若配方要求停止余热，立刻转入备好的冷水/冰水，轻轻翻动后按说明沥干；不要让蔬菜一直泡着，或者把盖好的热锅当作“已停止”。
5. **进入下一步前核对用途。** 若这是为即食配菜、再炒或另一道菜做的预处理，只按该配方继续；有些蔬菜和用途还需要后续烹调。用于冷冻保存的焯煮时长与冷却要求必须取自对应保存指导，不能把这份普通成餐流程当保存安全证明。

### 完成与停止

你能说出这批蔬菜何时下锅、何时开始计时、为何现在取出、是否按配方停止余热，以及接下来是上桌还是继续烹调，就完成了。配方没有可用时长/停止线索、蔬菜状态不可靠、取出工具或冷却安排缺失时先停下准备；不要在沸水已开后现场猜测。

### 常见报错与补救

- **水加料后很久不再沸腾：** 这一批可能太大；安全取出或等恢复后按可靠说明调整下一批，不私自借用冷冻保存表的时间。
- **忘记什么时候开始计时：** 不把模糊时间写成精确值。若配方完成条件无法可靠判断，停止把这一批当作已按要求焯好，重新按可靠说明处理。
- **蔬菜取出后仍继续变软：** 确认配方是否要求快速冷却；有要求而未做时立即按该流程处理，不让热锅继续加热。
- **锅太满或蒸汽冲向你：** 在安全可达时降热/关热，等锅稳定再分批；身体不要越过沸水寻找食物。
- **打算存入冰箱或冷冻库：** 转交适用的保存流程；焯过不等于已具备安全保存时长。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 假设普通家庭水基焯菜，时间与终点由**这次蔬菜和用途**的可靠配方提供；不同切块、数量、设备与海拔会改变进程。这里不复制一张跨蔬菜通用时间表。看不清锅面可用安全距离的声音、可访问计时器或现场可信的人描述，但不要把听到水声当成食物完成证据。握锅和搬运不方便时，使用稳定篮具/漏勺让热水留在灶上，或先取得协助。副作用是你可能要准备一盆冷水，却少得到一盆被余热持续加工的蔬菜。

## English

Load this Skill when a reliable recipe asks you to **put ordinary vegetables briefly into boiling water, remove them, and cool them quickly when needed to stop further cooking**. Finish one blanch using the timing event, duration, and stop action for this vegetable and purpose. This is not a “two minutes for every vegetable” chart and does not handle canning, a freezer-preservation plan, infant food, or questionable ingredients.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the vegetable, piece size, amount, prescribed water and loading method, timing start, observable endpoint, and whether immediate cooling is required. Wash and prepare it according to reliable directions. Blanching does not restore food whose condition is doubtful. Prepare a [stable boiling pot](../set-up-a-pot-for-boiling/SKILL.md), a basket or slotted spoon that can lift this batch, an accessible timer, and the cold or iced water required by the recipe. A hot pot should not be your reminder to find a slotted spoon.

### Execution steps

1. **Establish the boil the recipe requires.** Use the water, pot, and vegetable amounts directed for this job, leaving room for the added food to bubble and for you to work. Use lid or basket only as the pot and recipe permit; do not seal an ordinary pot under pressure.
2. **Load a controllable batch.** Lower vegetables near the water surface with a suitable tool rather than dropping them from a height. If the batch threatens to overflow or the boil cannot return as directed, reduce the next batch and reset it according to reliable directions. “Just cook longer” is not a universal correction.
3. **Time from the specified event.** Some directions start when food enters; others start when the water returns to a boil. Follow those for this vegetable. If bubbling pauses after loading, observe its return rather than using the empty pot's earlier boil as this batch's clock.
4. **Remove promptly at the endpoint.** Use the stated duration and applicable colour, texture, or other finish cue, then lift the vegetables with a basket or slotted spoon. If the recipe calls for stopping residual heat, transfer them straight into prepared cold or iced water, move them gently, and drain as directed. Do not leave them soaking indefinitely or treat a covered hot pot as “stopped.”
5. **Check the next use.** For an immediate side dish, later stir-fry, or another meal component, continue only as that recipe says; some uses require further cooking. Blanching times and cooling requirements for freezing must come from the applicable preservation guidance. This ordinary meal step is not proof of safe storage.

### Success and stop

You can say when this batch went in, when timing began, why it came out now, whether residual heat was stopped as directed, and whether it will be served or cooked further. If reliable timing or stop cues, food condition, a removal tool, or a required cooling setup is missing, pause preparation. Do not begin boiling and then guess.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The water takes a long time to boil again after loading:** The batch may be too large. Remove safely or wait for recovery and adjust the next batch under reliable directions; do not borrow a preservation chart's time for an unrelated meal.
- **You lost the timer start:** Do not turn an uncertain interval into a precise one. If the recipe's completion condition cannot be established, stop treating this batch as correctly blanched and use reliable directions to decide the next step.
- **Vegetables keep softening after removal:** Check whether the recipe requires rapid cooling. If it does, follow that stop step promptly instead of letting the hot pot continue the process.
- **The pot is too full or steam blows toward you:** Lower or turn off heat if safely reachable. Let it settle and divide the batch; do not lean over boiling water to hunt for food.
- **You intend to refrigerate or freeze the result:** Use the applicable storage procedure. Blanching alone does not establish a safe storage period.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary domestic water blanching. The **exact vegetable and use** supply its timing and endpoint; cut size, quantity, equipment, and altitude affect the process. No universal blanching table is copied here. If the surface is hard to see, listen from a safe distance, use an accessible timer, or ask a trusted person present to describe it; boiling sounds do not prove food completion. A stable basket or slotted spoon can keep hot water on the hob when lifting is difficult, or arrange help first. You may prepare a bowl of cold water and avoid a bowl of vegetables that keeps cooking from stored heat.
