## 中文

当一块**自有小手绣正面已完成**，但背面还可见待处理线尾、工作绷尚夹着布时，加载这份 Skill。结果是每条线尾被固定、针具清点收盒、布从工作绷轻取出可平放保存；它不负责后来是否清洗或陈列。H280 的一般收工不会代替背面线尾与针具检查。未完成的活线迹先停在缝制流程，不提前拆绷。

### 准备与输入

在好光线桌上检查正面轮廓、填色和背面的旧/新色尾线，确认没有未完针脚。备合用针、软布、针盒和可平放干净布的地方；剪刀只在最后修短。先数自己这次取出的针和可见针具，针尖不留在沙发或地上。若布要按材质清洗，保留足够边距和标签，不先装框。

### 执行

1. **每条尾分开固定。** 将针带到布背，把一条尾穿过邻近牢固针迹下方几次、改变走向并轻拉检查不松；再处理下一条。避免在正面结大疙瘩或把多个颜色拖过会透出的浅色区。
2. **复查针与背面。** 背面没有长松线、未剪的突出尾或会勾住的圈，正面没有被拉皱；确认绣针、毛线针和剪刀都在可见位置且针回针盒，才修短多余线尾。若尾太短无法固定，标“待处理”而不装作收好了。
3. **逐步松开工作绷。** 松螺丝，沿周边轻抬外圈而不是拽图案中心；布慢慢离开内圈，检查压痕和纤维。工作绷边有毛刺时不要硬滑过针迹。
4. **平放并标下一步。** 将布正面朝上置于干净干燥平面，写明是否还需按材料去导线/清理或放进展示绷。不要折在凸起线迹上，也不把仍潮的布密闭收存。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

正反线迹稳、所有针具清点入盒、工作绷已松开而布可平放；后续清洁/展示状态明确。针藏在布背或尾线一扯就开，不算完成。

- **尾线太短穿不到稳固针迹：** 先停修剪，找该纱线适用固定法，不靠一个外露松结。
- **背线透到浅色正面：** 退回改走同色附近的牢固针迹。
- **绷圈夹住刺绣：** 先松螺丝并分段移圈，不拽针脚硬脱。
- **数不清针或怀疑落针：** 停移动布，先找齐工具，避免遗留尖端。
- **视力/手部不便：** 放大灯、平桌和可信协助持圈，由你核对针数与布面。

### 假设与边界

DMC 以背面穿过现有针迹固定线尾，工作绷久夹可能留下折痕；具体布和线的护理仍以标签为准。此处只是安全收尾，不等于档案级保存或衣物耐磨保证。

## English

Load this Skill when the **front of your owned small embroidery is done** but reverse-side tails still need checking and a working hoop still grips the cloth. Finish with each tail secured, all needles accounted for and cased, and cloth released to lie flat. Later cleaning or display is a separate decision. H280's general creative close does not replace reverse-thread and needle checks. Do not remove the hoop from unfinished live stitching prematurely.

### Preparation and inputs

Under good light at a table, inspect front outline and fill plus every old/new-colour tail on the back. Confirm no unfinished stitches. Have suitable needle, soft cloth, case, and a clean flat place. Cut surplus only at the end. Count the needles and tools you brought out; leave no point on couch or floor. Keep cloth margin and labels if a later permitted wash is needed, without framing first.

### Execution

1. **Secure each tail separately.** On the reverse, pass one tail under nearby sound stitches several times, change direction, and tug gently to check hold before moving to the next. Avoid a big front knot or dragging dark floss across pale areas where it will show.
2. **Check needles and reverse.** Look for long loose strands, untrimmed tails, snagging loops, or new front puckering. Ensure embroidery and tapestry needles and scissors are visible and needles returned to a case before trimming excess. Mark a too-short unsecure tail pending instead of claiming it complete.
3. **Release the working hoop gradually.** Loosen the screw and lift the outer ring around its edge without pulling at the motif centre. Ease cloth off the inner ring, checking hoop dents and fibres. Do not scrape stitches across a rough hoop edge.
4. **Lay flat and name the next step.** Place fabric front-up on a clean dry surface, noting whether guide removal/cleaning or a display hoop remains. Avoid folding on raised stitches or sealing damp cloth in storage.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Front and back threads hold, every needle is counted into its case, the working hoop is released, cloth lies flat, and later care/display state is explicit. A hidden needle or tail that opens under a gentle pull is unfinished.

- **Tail is too short for sound stitches:** Stop trimming and seek a fibre-specific method rather than one loose external knot.
- **Reverse dark tail shows through pale front:** Undo and route it under same-colour nearby stitches.
- **Hoop grips the stitching:** Loosen first and move around the edge rather than yanking stitches out.
- **Needle count is uncertain:** Stop moving the cloth and find all points before continuing.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Magnification, flat table, and trusted ring-holding help can support your needle count and fabric check.

### Assumptions and limits

DMC secures thread by weaving under existing reverse stitches, and a work hoop left on too long may crease. Actual cloth and thread care still follow labels. This is safe finishing, not archive conservation or garment-wear certification.
