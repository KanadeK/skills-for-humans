## 中文

当你有一件获准携带的普通小型易碎物，要和衣物/其他行李一同短途移动，担心**挤压、碰撞或内部乱晃**时加载。目标是给它一个有支撑的包内位置，经过轻微移动检查仍不明显碰撞；不保证任何物品绝对不碎。物品本身是否可上飞机/车、危险电池、保价或索赔不在这里判断；潮湿/漏液物另行隔离。

### 准备与输入

检查这件物品是否已经有裂纹、松动部件、尖锐外露或受湿风险；已有损伤先记真实状态，别把包装说成修好。看实际承运方和随身/托运规则是否允许此物、容器与行李大小。选结实、尺寸贴合的内盒或保护套及不会掉屑/染色的缓冲物，留意包里重物、硬角与水瓶位置。极贵、无法替代或一碰就坏的东西，先判断是否值得这趟带；本 Skill 不承诺普通软包能承受粗暴托运。

### 执行

1. **确认物件能安全接触包装。** 如有可拆、易晃的普通配件，按制造商说明固定或分开；不要自行拆修设备。液体、未知粉末、危险电池或尖锐破裂物不能借“裹一层”进入日常行李流程。
2. **建立独立内层。** 用尺寸合适的保护套/内盒包住物件，在空隙处放缓冲物，让硬边、玻璃或陶瓷表面不直接碰行李拉链、金属、另一件硬物。对于形状不规则的物件，先支撑薄弱处，别只把最厚的地方包得很大。
3. **选包内不受重压的位置。** 避开包底硬角、会反复折叠/受压的边缘和可能漏液的物品；在可控行李中放稳，并让外部重物不直接压在物件上。若装载顺序/途中取用与其他物品冲突，按真正更重要的风险重排，别把易碎物当填缝材料。
4. **做温和的移动检查。** 合上内层和行李，在安全平面轻轻倾斜或提起，听/摸是否有明显滑动、敲碰或压迫；有就补支撑或换更稳的容器，再检查一次。不要用摔包来“测试强度”；测试通过只说明眼前轻微移动未发现问题。
5. **按实际运输规则携带并抵达复查。** 自己可控制时尽量保持包稳定，存放位置服从承运方规定。到达后取出物件，核对是否有新裂纹、松动或外层受压；损坏就保留实际状态和服务凭证，用服务方正式流程询问后续，不从本 Skill 推断赔偿。

### 完成与停止

物件获准携带、被有支撑的独立内层固定，与硬角/重压/湿物分隔，温和移动时无明显碰撞，且你知道到达后要检查，就完成普通保护。**这仍不是防碎保证，也不等于承运方接受任何特殊物品。** 无合适容器、物件已不安全、重量/尺寸超规则或无法避免强压时，别强塞进包；改用制造商/承运方认可的方式或不携带。

### 常见报错与补救

- **物件包了很多层仍能晃动：** 缩小内盒空隙、补在受力点并重做轻微移动检查，不只增加外层体积。
- **上方放了沉重物或硬角直接碰物件：** 调整包内位置与隔层，必要时减少要带的东西。
- **内盒挤得过紧：** 不让薄弱部位承担压紧力，换合适尺寸或支撑结构。
- **旁边水瓶可能漏：** 先用湿物隔离流程处理，不让缓冲布成为吸漏材料。
- **到达后发现裂纹：** 记录真实受损位置和此前状态，找实际承运/保管方了解流程，不把包装通过轻测写成“必定是对方造成”。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通、可携带的小型易碎物，不提供艺术品保管、危险电池、航空禁带、保价或赔偿建议。每种材料、运输方式和行李受力不同；软包/硬盒都只能降低可观察的普通碰撞风险。行动、视觉或手部操作不便时可请可信协助做温和检查和装放，不交给陌生人托管。副作用是会占一些空间，甚至让你决定这次不带它。

## English

Load this Skill when one permitted small ordinary fragile object must travel in a bag with clothes or other items and you worry about **crushing, knocks or movement inside**. Your result is a supported position that shows no obvious contact during a gentle movement check, not a promise that nothing can break. Permission for an aircraft or train, dangerous batteries, declared value or claims are separate matters. Damp or leaking things use the moisture-isolation task.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the object for existing cracks, loose parts, exposed sharp edges or moisture vulnerability; record any prior damage rather than saying wrapping repaired it. Check the actual operator's carry or checked-bag rule and bag dimensions. Choose a sound, closely fitting inner case or sleeve and padding that will not shed or stain, and notice heavy objects, hard corners and bottles in the bag. For an irreplaceable or unusually delicate object, decide first whether this trip is worth the risk; an ordinary soft bag is not guaranteed to survive rough handling.

### Execution

1. **Check that wrapping is appropriate.** Secure or separate ordinary loose parts as the maker directs; do not repair or dismantle equipment yourself. A leaking liquid, unknown powder, hazardous battery or sharp broken object does not become ordinary luggage by adding a wrapper.
2. **Create an independent inner layer.** Put the object in a suitable case or inner box and fill space so hard edges, glass or ceramic do not contact zips, metal or another hard item. For an uneven shape, support its weak areas instead of simply adding bulk around the strongest part.
3. **Choose a position away from crushing.** Avoid the hard bottom corner, a folding or repeatedly pressed edge and anything that might leak. Keep the inner layer stable in luggage you can manage, with heavy things not pressing directly on it. If access to other items conflicts, reorder by the actual higher risk; a fragile object is not gap filler.
4. **Make a gentle movement check.** Close case and bag. On a safe surface, tilt or lift slightly and listen or feel for sliding, knocks or pressure. Add support or change the case and check again. Do not drop the bag to “test its strength.” Passing a gentle check says only that no problem was obvious in that small movement.
5. **Carry under the real service rule and inspect on arrival.** Keep the bag stable where you control it and follow the operator's storage rules. At arrival, inspect for a new crack, looseness or pressure on the wrapper. If damaged, retain the real state and service record and ask the provider about its process; this Skill does not infer compensation.

### Success and stop

The item is permitted, supported in an independent inner layer, separated from hard corners, weight and wet things, has no obvious knocks in a gentle check, and has an arrival inspection planned. **This is no breakage guarantee or acceptance of any special object by a carrier.** Without a suitable case, for an unsafe object, outside actual limits or under unavoidable crush, do not force it into the bag. Use a maker- or operator-approved option or leave it behind.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Many layers but it still moves:** Reduce empty space in the inner case, support contact points and gently check again; do not just increase outside bulk.
- **A heavy item rests on it or a hard corner touches:** Reposition and separate, or carry less.
- **The inner case is too tight:** Do not put compression through a weak point; use a suitable size or support.
- **A nearby bottle might leak:** Use the moisture-isolation task first; padding cloth is not a leak plan.
- **A crack appears on arrival:** Record actual damage and prior state and ask the real carrier or custodian about its process. A gentle test does not prove who caused damage.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for small ordinary permitted fragile things, not specialist art handling, hazardous batteries, aircraft prohibitions, insurance or claims. Material, carrier and forces vary; soft and rigid cases can only reduce observable ordinary contact risk. For mobility, sight or hand-use needs, ask trusted help for a gentle packing and movement check without giving the item to an unknown custodian. The protection uses space and may lead you to leave the item behind.
