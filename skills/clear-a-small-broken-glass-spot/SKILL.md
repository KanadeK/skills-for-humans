## 中文

一个普通杯子或小玻璃摆件在室内**可触及的硬表面**摔碎，碎片集中在你能看清并隔离的小范围内时，用这份 Skill 先阻止踩踏，再用工具收集、封装和复查。它不处理大型窗玻璃或镜片、带电设备、灯泡、化学品、体液、受伤者，或散进地毯、缝隙而无法确认边界的碎片。别用“已经扫过一次”代替安全判断。

### 准备与输入

先让人和宠物离开碎片区域，并告诉可能经过的人暂时绕行。保持区域可见；需要的话打开固定照明，不在碎玻璃旁触碰有受损风险的电器。穿能包住脚趾的鞋。准备小扫帚和簸箕，或能控制住大片的夹具；再备一个不会被碎片轻易刺穿、能封好并写上“碎玻璃 / Broken glass”的容器，例如结实纸盒或合适的硬质容器。仅有薄塑料袋、裸手或一双普通手套时，先隔离并找工具，不直接开捡。手套也不是用手抓碎片的许可。

如果有人割伤、碎片沾到体液或不明液体，或玻璃来自灯泡、电子设备、药品/化学品容器、大窗板或松动的固定玻璃，停止本流程，隔离现场并按相应专业或当地服务处理。需要急救的情况先求助；这里不提供伤口处理步骤。

### 执行

1. **划清碎片区。** 从外围观察掉落点和可能弹到的边缘。移开人和宠物，暂时挡住通行；别赤脚进入，也别让孩子负责寻找小片。无法保持别人不进入时，继续隔离并找人协助。
2. **用工具收大片。** 从外侧向中心工作。用夹具夹取明显大片，或用刷子与簸箕收集，缓慢放进敞开的坚固容器。不要用裸手或戴手套的手捏、压、整理碎片，也别将碎片从一个软袋倒到另一个软袋。
3. **扫小片，再处理残余。** 用刷子和簸箕收可见小片。硬表面上剩下细小片时，可用夹具夹住一小片微湿的一次性纸巾轻带起，把纸巾也用工具送进容器；不把手掌压在纸巾上找碎片。这里不安排吸尘器或可重复使用的海绵清理：如果只靠它们才能处理，看作工具不足并继续隔离。
4. **复查可及处。** 从不同方向用灯光看原点、近旁地面和可见的家具脚周围；视力不便时请能看清的人协助，或用可控的灯光和放大方式。只复查不用伸手探的地方。还有可见碎片就重复工具收集；碎片进入深缝、地毯或够不到的区域时，维持隔离并找合适帮助，不宣布已清净。
5. **封装并恢复通行。** 盖好/贴牢容器，外面标明有碎玻璃，放在不会被儿童、宠物或他人误碰的位置。查你所在地对这种玻璃和包装的收运规则再弃置；普通饮料瓶的回收箱未必接收碎饮用杯、镜子或窗玻璃。检查扫帚、簸箕上是否仍挂着可见碎片，用工具送入容器。只有可见及可及范围已复查、容器已封好且通道没有可见碎片，才解除临时隔离。

### 成功条件

人和宠物没有再踩入作业区；可触及硬表面上的可见碎片被工具收进封闭、标记的坚固容器；通道复查后再开放，容器按当地规则待弃置。看不见的微小碎片不等于被证明不存在；无法复查或仍有可见碎片时，隔离仍是本次正确的结束状态。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有薄垃圾袋：** 不把尖片直接塞进去。先挡住通行，找能封口的结实纸盒或合适硬容器，再收集。
- **手套戴上就想徒手捡：** 停下，换夹具、刷子和簸箕；普通手套不能替工具承受锋利边缘。
- **扫了一次便让人走过：** 保持隔离，沿边缘和家具脚重新用斜光查看，处理还可见的碎片。
- **碎片滚进地毯或深缝：** 不伸手摸索，也不凭一次表面清扫宣称安全；留下提醒并找能处理该材料和位置的人。
- **容器或弃置规则不明：** 暂放在安全处并标明内容，查当地收运方的具体要求，不把所有玻璃都投进瓶罐回收。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一处小型、普通、未污染的室内破损，硬表面能安全走近，且可暂时封闭。行动、握力或视力不便时，先阻断通行，请能安全操作工具的人协助；不以弯腰、跪进碎片区或伸手试探换取“自己完成”。你会暂时占用通道，并留下一个需要按当地规定处置的容器。英国康沃尔与美国纽约的弃置规定只是不同地方的实例，不能直接替代你所在地规则。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary drinking glass or small glass ornament breaks across a **small, reachable indoor hard surface**, and you can see and keep people out of the affected area. Isolate first, collect with tools, seal the pieces, and check the route before reopening it. Large window or mirror panes, electrical equipment, bulbs, chemicals, body fluids, injury care, and shards hidden in carpet or deep gaps are outside this procedure. One pass with a broom is not proof that a route is clear.

### Preparation and inputs

Move people and pets away and tell anyone approaching to use another route. Make the area visible with fixed lighting if needed; do not touch damaged electrical equipment near the glass. Wear closed-toe shoes. Get a small broom and dustpan, or controllable tongs for large pieces. Have a sturdy container that sharp pieces will not readily puncture and that can be closed and marked “Broken glass,” such as a strong cardboard box or suitable rigid container. If you have only a thin bag, bare hands, or ordinary gloves, keep the area closed and get tools first. Gloves do not make grasping shards by hand safe.

Stop this procedure if someone is cut, fragments touch body fluids or unknown liquid, or the source is a bulb, electronic device, medicine or chemical container, large window pane, or loose installed glass. Keep others out and use appropriate local or professional help. Seek help for an injury first; this file does not give wound treatment.

### Execution

1. **Mark the affected patch.** From its perimeter, look at the fall point and places pieces may have bounced. Keep people and pets away and block the route temporarily. Do not enter barefoot or ask a child to find small pieces. If you cannot keep others out, maintain the barrier and ask for help.
2. **Collect larger pieces with tools.** Work from outside toward the center. Lift obvious pieces with tongs or sweep them into a dustpan and move them slowly into the open sturdy container. Never pinch, press, or arrange shards with bare or gloved fingers. Do not transfer them between soft bags.
3. **Sweep smaller pieces and pick up residue.** Brush visible smaller pieces into the dustpan. On a hard surface, you can grip a small, slightly damp disposable paper towel with tongs to lift fine remnants; put the towel in the container with the tool. Do not press a palm into a towel to hunt for glass. This procedure does not rely on a vacuum or reusable sponge. If these are your only remaining options, keep the area closed and get suitable help or tools.
4. **Inspect the reachable area.** Use angled light from more than one direction to check the fall point, nearby floor, and visible spaces around furniture feet. If vision makes this hard, ask someone who can see the fragments or use controlled light and magnification. Do not reach blindly under furniture. Repeat tool collection for visible pieces. If glass is lodged in carpet, a deep gap, or an unreachable spot, keep the area isolated and find appropriate help rather than declaring it clear.
5. **Seal and reopen.** Close or tape the sturdy container, label it as broken glass, and keep it where children, pets, and others will not mistake it for ordinary rubbish. Check local collection rules for this glass and its packaging before disposal; bottle recycling may reject drinking glasses, mirrors, or window glass. Check the broom and dustpan for visible attached pieces and transfer them with tools. Reopen the route only after the reachable area has been inspected, no visible pieces remain on the route, and the container is closed.

### Success

People and pets have not walked through the affected area. Visible shards from the reachable hard surface are collected with tools in a closed, labeled, sturdy container. The route is inspected before reopening, and disposal follows local rules. Invisible microscopic fragments have not been proved absent. If the patch cannot be checked or visible pieces remain, keeping it isolated is a valid stopping result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a thin rubbish bag is available:** Do not put sharp pieces straight into it. Block the route and find a sturdy box or suitable rigid container first.
- **Gloves seem enough to pick by hand:** Stop and switch to tongs, brush, and dustpan. Ordinary gloves are not a replacement for tools at a sharp edge.
- **The route reopens after one sweep:** Keep it closed and check the perimeter and furniture feet with angled light; collect what is still visible.
- **Shards enter carpet or a deep gap:** Do not feel around by hand or claim surface sweeping cleared it. Leave a warning and find someone equipped for that location and material.
- **The container or disposal rule is unclear:** Store it securely and labeled while checking your local collector's instructions. Do not assume all glass belongs in bottle recycling.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers one small ordinary, uncontaminated indoor breakage on a hard surface you can safely approach and temporarily close. If mobility, grip, or sight makes tool use difficult, block access and ask someone who can handle the tools safely. You need not bend, kneel among shards, or probe by hand to finish alone. The route stays closed for a while, and a labeled container remains until locally compliant disposal. Cornwall and New York disposal rules are examples from different places, not rules for your location.
