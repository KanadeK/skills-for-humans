## 中文

当第一轮分送后，清单上某只普通搬家箱没有被实际看到，或交接方说交了而你尚未找到时加载。你要**核对最后一个可靠状态、把具体差异交给实际负责方并留下下一次复查点**。这里不直接宣布丢失、不处理索赔、保险、赔偿或危险拆箱。

### 准备与输入

拿到自己保管的箱 ID/第一站表、实见到货记录，以及实际服务方的正式清单或自运交接记录。个人分送表与承运方清单是两份资料，编号可能不同；先找两者的对应。确认谁在起点最后亲眼见到这只箱，谁在终点负责接收，及联系方式。不要把“清单上有”当成“已装车”的证据。

### 执行

1. **复查实际到达区而不乱改状态。** 按箱 ID、外观和可能的第一站，在获准且安全的普通落位区找一次；也问是否被误送另一房间。找到时由有权接收者确认外标和清单对应，再改为已见并按正确目的地处理。不要擅自打开私人箱件来猜身份。
2. **写出最后已确认事件。** 分开记“装箱完成”“起点交出”“服务方确认装载”“终点实际见到”各有哪些证据。缺某一步就标未知，别把未见直接写成永久丢失。
3. **向实际责任链提出一个可回答的问题。** 给承运方、司机、装箱者或接收人相应的箱 ID、可公开描述、最后确认时点和当前未见状态，问他们记录中的最新位置/下一步核查人。涉及私人内容只给有权者，不在公开群里贴详细清单。
4. **把答复写成状态与下一次动作。** 可用“仍在途中／已送但落位待找／位置不明待核／已见并改送”等状态；注明是谁、何时、依据什么答复，下一次何时找谁复查。对方未答时写“待回”，不是“已解决”。
5. **同步实际交接文件。** 若服务方要求在它的正式交付清单上标差异，按其真实流程如实记录并保留副本；本文不解释签署效果或当地索赔期限。若箱内缺项影响今日基本活动，另做眼前可行的临时生活安排，不等追踪完成才吃饭或休息。

### 完成与停止

这只箱要么被有权者实际辨认并归入正确第一站，要么有一条具体、可复查的未收状态，含最后确认事件、责任联系人和下一次核对点。服务方记录与个人表冲突、物主/权限不明或需进入危险区域时保持待核并转实际负责人；不以推测关闭记录。

### 常见报错与补救

- **箱在错房间：** 核外标和唯一 ID，改为实际已见并请有能力的人按正确路线分送；修正两处记录。
- **服务方说“都交了”但没有对应编号：** 请对方指出其正式记录中的对应项和交付位置；仍不清楚就保留未收差异。
- **只有箱内物品名称，没有外箱号：** 用有权者可提供的装箱记录、照片和外观缩小范围；不能确认就不认领相似箱。
- **口头答复后没有下文：** 记答复人和时点，按约定再问；“正在查”不是最终状态。
- **箱内是今天急用物：** 并行安排已确认可用的临时替代，追踪线继续保持开放。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通搬迁中的事实追踪，不解释运输合同、赔偿或索赔流程；不同服务方的正式单据和反馈渠道各异。看不清箱标、无法走遍区域时，请获准的可信者读回或找箱；不要强迫任何人翻动重物。副作用是“应该在某个角落”变成有联系人和下次时间的待查项。

## English

Load this Skill when a normal moving box on your list was not actually seen in the first receiving pass, or someone says it was handed over but you cannot locate it. You will **check the last reliable state, give a specific discrepancy to the real responsible party and set a next check**. Do not declare a permanent loss by guesswork. Claims, insurance, compensation and hazardous unpacking are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Get your own box ID and first-stop list, the observed-arrival record, and the actual provider's formal inventory or a self-move handoff note. Your routing list and the carrier's list are separate and may use different IDs; establish a correspondence. Identify who last actually saw the box at origin, who receives it at destination, and how to reach them. An item on a list is not proof it was loaded.

### Execution

1. **Recheck arrived areas without changing state by hope.** Search permitted, safe ordinary drop areas by box ID, appearance and possible first stop; ask whether it went to a wrong room. If found, have an authorised receiver match outside mark to one list row, then mark it seen and route correctly. Do not open a private box to guess its identity.
2. **Write the last confirmed event.** Separate evidence for “packed,” “released at origin,” “carrier confirms loaded” and “physically seen at destination.” Mark any missing step unknown rather than translating not seen into permanently lost.
3. **Ask one answerable question along the actual chain.** Give the relevant mover, driver, packer or receiver the box ID, safe public description, last confirmed time and current unseen state. Ask whose record shows its latest location and who checks next. Share private contents only with authorised people, not in a public group.
4. **Turn the response into a state and next action.** Use “still in transit / delivered but location to find / location unknown pending check / seen and re-routed” as applicable. Note who answered, when, on what basis and whom to ask next when. No reply means awaiting reply, not solved.
5. **Keep real handoff records aligned.** If the provider requires a difference on its formal delivery record, record the facts through its actual process and keep your copy. This does not interpret signing effects or local claims deadlines. If the box contains something needed today, make a separately confirmed temporary living plan; do not defer food or rest until tracing is done.

### Success and stop

Either an authorised person physically identifies this box at the right first stop, or there is a specific revisit-able unreceived state with last confirmed event, responsible contact and next check. Keep a conflict between carrier and personal records, unknown ownership or unsafe search area pending with the actual responsible party. Do not close it with an inference.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The box is in the wrong room:** Match outside mark and unique ID, mark actual arrival and ask capable help to route it correctly; correct both records.
- **The provider says “everything delivered” without a matching ID:** Ask for the corresponding formal entry and handoff location. Keep the discrepancy open if unclear.
- **You know contents but no outside box number:** Use authorised packing notes, photos and appearance to narrow candidates. Do not claim a similar carton without confirmation.
- **A verbal answer has no follow-up:** Record the person and time, then ask again at the agreed point. “We're checking” is not final.
- **The box holds today's needed item:** Arrange a confirmed usable substitute in parallel; keep the trace open.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This tracks facts in an ordinary move, not transport contracts, compensation or claims procedure. Providers have different formal forms and response channels. If you cannot read labels or visit the drop areas, ask an authorised trusted person to read back or search; nobody must shift heavy goods for this list. “It should be somewhere” becomes an open item with a contact and next time.
