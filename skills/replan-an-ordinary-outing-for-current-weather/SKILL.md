## 中文

当你已安排公园散步、普通野餐或类似短时休闲，临近出门或现场发现天气和原计划不合时，用这份 Skill 根据**当期权威信息、场地状态和真实退出时间**在缩短、改室内、延期/取消中作一次决定。这里不教危险天气求生；一旦出现雷声、当地危险天气警报或场地封闭，停止户外休闲并按当地主管部门/现场人员指引离开。

### 准备与输入

写原定地点、时段、同行人、必须条件（如平整路面、能坐、有通行服务）和最近的退出方式。查看所在地官方气象服务的最新预报、预警/提示及场地管理方开闭公告，记查看时间；不同地区的预警名称与严重程度不同，不靠旧截图。看你能不能在天气变差前结束并实际离开，不把“我们已经出门”当继续理由。若雷雨可能影响时段，不以露天亭、树下或短雨停当安全备选；按正式信息调整。

### 执行

1. **先看是否已越出普通范围。** 有当前危险天气警报、雷声/闪电、管理方封闭或你不能安全返回时，取消/结束该户外活动；已经在外时去正式可避险的封闭建筑或按当地主管部门指示行动，不在本篇编造现场救援技巧。普通天气变化才继续下一步选择。
2. **只用仍成立的条件重算。** 对照预计变化时段、路面可能变湿/能见度、温度、场地开闭与你/同行人的实际限制；决定最迟离开点。预报不确定、退出时间不足或关键通道不明，就不要用“短一点”掩盖未知，改室内或取消。
3. **从三个结果中选一个。** 条件仍适合且出口近，可缩成一个明确短段；有确认开放且可达的室内场所，可改在那里做低风险休闲；两者都不成立就取消/改日。室内备选要核开放、费用/预约与可及性，不能只说“找个地方躲雨”。
4. **发出一条明确改动。** 告知同行人/相关场所“原定____；根据____时查看的信息，改为____；集合/结束点____；如再变则____”。如果已有正式预约，按主办方流程变更或取消；不替它承诺免费退款。
5. **现场再次核对并执行。** 出门前或到场时看实际天气与管理方公告；与决定不同则采用更保守的退出或取消。若突然打雷，不等投票继续活动，马上进入适当的封闭避险地点并遵从官方指引。离开后确认同行人已接到变更，不把“消息发出”当对方已安全到达。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你能指出当期信息、原计划不成立的条件、**缩短/室内/取消**的实际选择以及通知与退出位置；若已经在外，实际按新决定结束或转移。冒险维持原计划不算“计划弹性”。

- **不同天气 App 显示不一致：** 优先所在地官方预报/警报与场地公告，说明不确定并选更易退出方案，不找最乐观的一张图。
- **同行人想“再试十分钟”：** 给出已知天气与退出条件；对自己不接受的风险直接退出，不把多数意见当安全证明。
- **室内备选也关闭/需预约：** 改日或取消，不把地下通道、亭子或树下当活动场地。
- **途中网络断了：** 以出发前保存的正式公告和现场标识/工作人员为准；无法确认当前状态时退出到熟悉的安全公共路线，不继续探索。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通休闲的日程选择，不做雷暴、洪水、火灾、极端温度中的生存指导，也不规定个人体能或医学阈值。气象与公园通知的渠道因地区而异，需选当地权威来源。可用电话、广播、可及文字/语音或可信协助获取信息；同行通知可用约定方式。取消可能不方便，但不能用沉没成本代替天气判断；必要时按实际票务规则处理。

## English

Use this Skill when you planned a short ordinary park walk, picnic, or similar outing but the weather no longer fits shortly before leaving or while there. Use **current authoritative weather, venue status, and real exit time** to shorten it, move indoors, postpone, or cancel. This is not severe-weather survival advice. On thunder, a local hazardous-weather alert, or venue closure, end the outdoor leisure activity and follow local authorities and site staff.

### Preparation and inputs

Write the planned place and time, companions, requirements such as smooth access or seating, and nearest exit. Check your area's official weather service for current forecast, watches or warnings, and the site manager's opening notices; record when checked. Alert names and severity differ by region, so an old screenshot is not enough. Can you finish and actually leave before conditions worsen? Being already on the way is no reason to continue. When thunderstorms may affect the period, an open pavilion, tree, or short break in rain is not a safety plan; use official information.

### Execution

1. **Check whether this is beyond ordinary scope.** With a current hazardous alert, thunder or lightning, a manager closure, or no safe return, cancel or end the outdoor activity. If already outside, go to an appropriate enclosed shelter or follow local official instructions; this Skill invents no field rescue technique. Continue planning only for ordinary changes.
2. **Recalculate with conditions that still hold.** Compare timing, possible wet surfaces and visibility, temperature, site opening, and your or companions' real limits. Set a latest exit. If the forecast is uncertain, exit time too short, or a key route unknown, do not disguise uncertainty as “just a shorter walk.” Move indoors or cancel.
3. **Choose one of three outcomes.** If conditions remain suitable with a nearby exit, shorten to a named small segment. If a verified open accessible indoor place exists, use it for low-risk leisure. Otherwise cancel or reschedule. Check an indoor option's hours, charges or booking, and access; “find somewhere to hide from rain” is not a plan.
4. **Send one clear change.** Tell companions or organiser “planned ____; as of ____ official check, changed to ____; meeting or end point ____; if it changes again ____.” Alter a formal reservation through the organiser's route when needed. Do not promise a free refund on its behalf.
5. **Recheck and act.** Check real weather and site notices before departure or on arrival. If they differ, use the more conservative exit or cancellation. For new thunder, do not wait for a group vote; move promptly to suitable enclosed shelter and follow official instructions. Confirm companions received the change; sending a message is not proof they reached a safe place.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can point to current information, the condition that invalidated the old plan, the real **shorter / indoor / cancelled** choice, and a notified exit or meeting point. If already outside, you actually ended or moved according to it. Preserving the original plan at avoidable risk is not flexibility.

- **Weather apps disagree:** Prefer the local official forecast or alert and venue notice. State uncertainty and choose the easier exit instead of finding the most optimistic graphic.
- **Companions want “ten more minutes”:** Explain known weather and exit facts. Leave if you do not accept the risk; a majority vote is not a safety finding.
- **The indoor option is closed or needs booking:** Postpone or cancel. A passageway, open pavilion, or tree is not an indoor activity site.
- **Connection fails en route:** Use saved official notices and on-site signs or staff. With no way to establish current conditions, exit by a familiar public route rather than explore farther.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This chooses an ordinary leisure schedule; it does not teach survival in storms, floods, fire, or extreme temperatures, or set personal fitness or medical thresholds. Weather and park notice channels differ by region; use your local authority. Phone, radio, accessible text or audio, or trusted help can provide information, and use an agreed method to update companions. Cancelling may be inconvenient, but sunk cost is not a weather assessment. Follow actual ticket rules when relevant.
