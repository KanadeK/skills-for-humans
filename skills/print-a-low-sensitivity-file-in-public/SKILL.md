## 中文

当你有一份普通、低敏感电子文件，要用图书馆或其他公开打印服务拿到纸张时加载。结果是**正确页数的纸被本人取走、公共设备和可见任务按场所支持的方式收口**。不把身份证明、财务、病例、法律争议或其他高敏感文件上传到你不了解留存方式的系统；若必须处理这些材料，先找可控的专用渠道。实体纸稿复印是另一份流程。

### 准备与输入

从场所正式网站、柜台或机器确认今天是否可打印、接受的文件/提交渠道、纸张规格、单双面/颜色、每页费用与取件位置。某些图书馆用官网上传或第三方打印站，另一些用馆内电脑、邮箱、USB 或取件码；不要凭陌生二维码推断是官方入口。查看是否会存储文件或有删除说明；没有说明就把后端留存标“未知”。先确认你确有权打印此内容，别用本 Skill 绕过馆藏或版权限制。

### 执行

1. **只准备本次要出的文件。** 在自己设备核对文件名、页面顺序、最后一页和你真正需要的份数；普通文件可用便于预览的格式。去掉不必要的私人内容。别把一个包含其他敏感附件的文件夹整体上传。
2. **用实际场所入口建立任务。** 在官网给的服务、馆内设备或工作人员确认的渠道选择文件；需要临时电脑时只在你控制的界面操作，不保存账号密码或让旁人代登录。若服务把文件交给外部提供方，先按你的隐私接受度决定是否继续。
3. **付款前预览与核价。** 对照页数、页序、份数、纸型、单双面、彩色/黑白与总价；不确定先打一张不敏感测试页或问工作人员。预览错位/空白就返回修正，不连续重复点打印以免多收费、多出纸。
4. **看任务状态并到正确打印机取件。** 场所若采用取件码，保管号码并等官方界面显示可取；有的系统直接从馆内电脑送到公开取纸点。到场按本服务方式释放任务，核对自己文件的第一页、末页和总张数，把全部输出立即取走，别翻看或拿走别人的纸。
5. **清理你可控制的遗留。** 按服务提供的功能取消多余/失败任务、退出公共账号或临时电脑、移除 USB，并删除自己留在该设备的本地副本/下载记录（若允许且确为本人文件）。检查进纸/出纸口和台面无你的纸张。**界面上看不到不等于后台数据已擦除**；对保留政策有要求就向服务提供方确认。

### 完成与停止

你拿到内容、页数和份数正确的纸，已收好自己的设备/取件码，且已退出可见会话、取消多余任务并移走可控的本地文件，本轮完成。若打印机卡纸、任务失败或页数少了，先查官方任务状态并请工作人员处理，不盲目反复提交。不能确认公开系统的隐私处理时，停止上传该文件，改用可控渠道。

### 常见报错与补救

- **纸出来了却有空白/裁切：** 对照预览、纸型与方向，先修正一页测试，不直接再印整份。
- **提交成功但机器没有纸：** 核对取件码、可取状态与正确设备；任务若仍排队或失败，问工作人员，不重复付费碰运气。
- **多次误点产生重复任务：** 在官方界面取消未打印任务；已出纸的按场所规则处理，不把他人的纸一并拿走。
- **公共电脑还显示文件/账号：** 退出并清理本人可见的本地副本，拔走 USB；无法删除时请工作人员协助，不声称已远程清除。
- **低敏感判断变了：** 文件含证件、账户或私人记录时停止使用未知留存的公开渠道，不因已经排队就继续上传。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通低敏感材料，不提供版权/法律判断、费用争议建议或对第三方服务器的删除保证。场所可用性、文件格式、费用、取件码和任务保留期各不相同。视觉、行动、听觉或设备条件不同，可用场所正式柜台辅助、可及机器或可信陪同，但不把本人账号密码交给陌生人。副作用是纸张和可能的服务方留存；后者只能按真实政策求证。

## English

Load this Skill when you have an ordinary, low-sensitivity digital file and need paper from a library or other public print service. The result is **the correct pages collected by you and visible device or job traces closed where the service allows**. Do not upload identity, financial, medical, legal-dispute or similarly sensitive records into a system whose retention you do not understand; use a more controlled service for such material. Photocopying an existing paper original is another task.

### Preparation and inputs

From the venue's official site, desk or machine, check that printing works today, accepted files and submission route, paper size, single or double sides, colour, cost and collection point. One library may use official upload or an external print provider; another may use its computer, email, USB or a release code. An unfamiliar QR sign is not proof of an official route. Read any retention or deletion notice; if absent, mark backend retention unknown. Confirm you may print the content; this Skill does not bypass collection or copyright rules.

### Execution

1. **Prepare only this job's file.** On a device you control, check name, page order, last page and number of copies. A format with a usable preview helps for ordinary material. Remove unnecessary personal content. Do not upload a folder that also contains unrelated sensitive attachments.
2. **Create the job through the venue's real route.** Select the file through its official service, local machine or staff-confirmed channel. At a shared computer, use only a session you control; do not save credentials or let a stranger log in for you. If an outside provider receives the file, decide whether that privacy tradeoff works before proceeding.
3. **Preview and check the charge first.** Compare pages, order, copies, size, sides, colour or black and white, and total cost. For uncertainty, print one harmless test page or ask staff. Fix a blank or clipped preview instead of clicking Print repeatedly and producing extra charges and pages.
4. **Check job state and collect at the correct printer.** If the venue uses a release code, keep it and wait for its official “ready” state. Another system may send directly from a library computer to an output point. Release the job by that service's method. Check your first and last page and total sheet count, and take all output immediately without reading or taking someone else's pages.
5. **Clear traces you control.** Use service features to cancel extra or failed jobs, sign out of the public account or computer, remove your USB and delete your own local downloads or copies from that device where allowed. Check input or output trays and the desk for your pages. **A job disappearing from view is not proof its server copy was erased.** Ask the provider if retention matters to you.

### Success and stop

You have the correct content, pages and copies, your device or release code is back with you, and you have signed out, cancelled extra jobs and removed local files you control. For jams, failed jobs or missing pages, inspect official job state and ask staff rather than blindly resubmitting. If the public system's privacy handling cannot be confirmed for this file, stop the upload and choose a controlled route.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Output is blank or clipped:** Recheck preview, size and orientation, then test one page before reprinting all.
- **Submission succeeded but no paper appears:** Verify release code, ready state and device. Ask staff for queued or failed jobs instead of paying twice on a guess.
- **Several accidental jobs were submitted:** Cancel unprinted jobs through the official interface; handle printed pages under venue rules and leave others' paper untouched.
- **A public computer still shows your file or account:** Sign out, remove your own visible local copy and USB. Ask staff if you cannot clear it; do not claim remote erasure.
- **The material turns out sensitive:** Stop using an unknown-retention public system for IDs, accounts or private records even if you have already waited in line.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers ordinary low-sensitivity material, not copyright or legal advice, billing disputes or guaranteed deletion from a third-party server. Venue availability, formats, fees, release codes and retention vary. For sight, mobility, hearing or device needs, use the venue's real desk assistance, accessible equipment or a trusted companion without giving passwords to strangers. You gain paper and may leave a provider-side record; only its actual policy can clarify the latter.
