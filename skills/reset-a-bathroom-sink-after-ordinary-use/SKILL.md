## 中文

当你家普通洗手台的盆、龙头或邻近可及台面有**牙膏点、皂液、日常水渍和少量头发**，需要恢复到下次可用状态时，用这份 Skill。它只处理可识别普通污垢，不拆龙头/下水、修漏水或清理马桶、霉害、体液和不明化学品。不同龙头表面与石材台面可能不接受同一种清洁剂，先确认材质。

### 准备与输入

查看盆体、台面、龙头的厂家护理信息和想用的清洁剂标签，确认适用表面、用量、接触时间、手部防护、通风与是否需清水擦净。移开牙刷、口杯和其他会碰到清洁剂的物品；电动牙刷底座或插座附近有水时，不用湿布接触通电部位，退出并按设备安全说明/专业帮助处理。通风不足、刺激性气味或旧产品残留不明时先停，不叠加第二种产品。

### 执行

1. **先界定普通范围。** 只处理可见牙膏、皂液、少量毛发和水印。排水持续堵塞、漏水从墙/柜内来、可见霉斑、体液或化学刺激物另走适用专业/安全路径；不靠更强清洁剂掩盖结构问题。
2. **清出表面物件与松散污物。** 从台面取走可移动物，干净的保留洁净去向；用纸/软布收集可见头发和牙膏块，按当地垃圾规则处理，不把一团毛发推入排水口。
3. **逐个材料用许可方式清洁。** 对盆体与台面，按材质和产品说明以适用洗剂/水轻擦，先小范围试再扩大；对龙头饰面，若厂家只支持清水和软布，就只用那种方法，不拿盆体产品喷在五金上。不要用尖锐刮片、强磨蚀垫或酸性去垢剂直接试未知天然石面。
4. **按标签去残留并干燥。** 清洁产品要求清水冲/擦时照做，换干净布面，避免把污物带回杯子和牙刷。擦干盆边、龙头底座及可见台面接缝，不把水长留在封边；洗完的布按材质洗净摊开晾干。
5. **恢复可使用的洗手台。** 表面无可见牙膏/皂液与明显清洁剂残留，放回干净个人用品；地面若被水打湿需继续隔离/按地材流程处理。若小处试验后褪色、台面蚀痕或五金失光，停止并记产品与材质问厂家。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

普通可见污垢在不伤材料的情况下移除，洗手台及邻近区域按标签去产品残留并干燥，物品安全归位；材料/产品不明或出现损伤时停止是完整结果。本篇不声明表面无菌。

- **龙头表面被强力产品擦得发暗：** 停止该产品，按龙头厂商说明清水处理并求助，不用另一种强剂“抛亮”。
- **接缝还湿：** 用干净布吸干可接近处，漏水持续则停用并找维修，而不是往缝内喷化学品。
- **牙膏/皂膜只在一处没掉：** 查该面材质和产品许可，先小块复试或求厂家建议，不整片硬刷。
- **洗手台旁有湿的电器插座：** 不碰湿通电部位，阻止旁人靠近并找合格电气帮助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只管普通可及洗手台，不做高处、下水拆修、霉害或危险化学清理。浴室窗、排风与产品气味因空间不同，产品不允许或通风不足时停而非勉强用；也可按材质先用被允许的清水/温和方法。看不清细缝时用光线和放大照片，行动不便时用可及高度软布或可信协助。布和毛发垃圾有后续去向。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary bathroom basin, faucet, or nearby reachable counter has **toothpaste spots, soap, everyday water marks, or a little hair** and you need it ready for next use. It covers identifiable ordinary soil, not faucet or drain dismantling, leak repair, toilet cleaning, mold, bodily fluids, or unknown chemicals. Faucet finish and stone may reject a product suitable for the basin, so identify each material first.

### Preparation and inputs

Read basin, counter, and faucet maker care and the proposed product label for compatible surface, amount, contact time, hand protection, ventilation, and rinse requirements. Move toothbrushes, cups, and other things that must not meet cleaner. If water reached an electric brush base or outlet, do not wipe energised wet parts; leave and follow appliance safety or qualified help. Stop for poor ventilation, irritating odour, or unknown old product residue instead of layering a second cleaner.

### Execution

1. **Set an ordinary boundary.** Handle visible toothpaste, soap, a few hairs, and water marks only. A recurring blocked drain, leak from wall or cabinet, visible mold, bodily fluid, or irritating chemical takes its own safety or professional route. Stronger cleaner does not solve construction trouble.
2. **Clear objects and loose soil.** Move personal items to a clean place. Lift visible hair and lumps of toothpaste with paper or a soft cloth for local trash, not a ball pushed down the drain.
3. **Use one permitted method per material.** Clean basin and counter with a compatible product and water under their care, testing a small spot before scaling. If the faucet maker permits only water and a soft cloth, do that rather than spray basin cleaner onto its finish. Avoid sharp scrapers, harsh scouring pads, or acid scale remover on unidentified natural stone.
4. **Remove product and dry as directed.** Rinse or wipe cleaner off where its label requires, using a clean cloth section instead of moving soil back to cups and brushes. Dry basin edges, faucet base, and visible counter seams; do not leave water pooling in a seal. Wash and spread the used cloth to dry under its care.
5. **Restore a usable sink.** No visible toothpaste or soap and no obvious cleaner residue remain; return clean personal items. Keep any newly wet floor isolated for the floor material process. For fading, etching, or dull metal after a small test, stop and retain product and material details for the maker.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Ordinary visible soil is removed without material damage, cleaner residue is handled by the label, sink and nearby surface are dry, and items are returned safely. A stop for unknown care or new damage is complete. This does not claim a sterile surface.

- **A strong product dulls the faucet:** Stop it. Use only the faucet maker's permitted water care and seek advice, not another strong polish.
- **A seam remains wet:** Dry accessible water with a clean cloth. A continuing leak needs repair, not chemical spray into the seam.
- **One soap patch remains:** Check that material and product permission for a small retry or ask the maker; do not scrub the whole basin harder.
- **An outlet near the sink is wet:** Do not touch wet energised parts. Keep others away and get qualified electrical help.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for an ordinary reachable sink, not heights, drain repair, mold, or hazardous chemistry. Windows, fans, and product fumes vary. If product or ventilation is unsuitable, stop or use a gentler water route the material allows. Use light, magnification, a reachable soft cloth, or trusted help for vision and mobility limits. Used cloth and hair waste need handling.
