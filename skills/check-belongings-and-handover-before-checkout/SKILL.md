## 中文

准备离开普通酒店、旅舍或短租房间，想在**还能合法进入且有人负责交接时**把自己的物品带齐、住宿方物品留好、钥匙按本次规则交回，就用这份 Skill。结果是房间最后一轮已查区域、自己的包件数和交接状态都清楚。它不处理退房后才发现遗失物的追查，也不判断损坏、费用或租赁责任。

### 准备与输入

打开本次预订的实际退房时间和最新指引，确认是否须到前台、放回钥匙、关窗关灯、处理垃圾或发离开消息；不要把其他住宿的习惯套来。数一遍自己的包、钥匙/房卡以及共同出行者。留出从房间到交接点的时间。把最后还要用的手机、外套或眼镜放在一个明确的“最后拿”位置，避免检查完又重新散开。

### 执行

1. **先按去向分开。** 自己带走的东西放进随身包或待搬区；住宿方提供的毛巾、衣架、遥控器、钥匙等留在指引指定的位置；不确定归属的物品先问同行者或住宿方，不顺手当赠品装走。寄存或借来的物品按实际交接记录单独核对。
2. **逐区做最后一轮实看。** 从床与床边、床下可见处、桌面/抽屉、插座、衣柜/保险柜、浴室、冰箱、门后和共享空间中你实际使用过的地方，按固定方向只走一遍。每查完一处就关上或做自己的标记，避免“我好像看过”循环。只检查你被允许进入且能安全够到的区域；高处或他人私人物品请负责人协助。
3. **封包后做数量和近期取用核对。** 把每位同行者的随身包件数与仍在房间的东西比一遍；钱包、手机、钥匙、车票等下一段要用的个人物品确认在可控制的位置，不把它们随暂存行李离手。走到门口，再回望已查区域和充电处；一旦重新拿出东西，重新核对那个区域。
4. **按本次指引完成退房动作。** 在规定时点前做住宿方确实要求的关窗、关灯、关门、钥匙/房卡归还或前台/平台通知。只做明确要求的普通整理；有损坏、费用或特别清洁争议时记录事实并向住宿方询问，不自行维修或推断责任。把自己的物品全部带过门槛后再锁门或交出最后一把钥匙；未获允许不要把包留在房间当寄存。
5. **确认交接状态。** 前台或房东实际收到钥匙/退房消息时，记下已完成的交接方式；自助钥匙投递则按本次指引放到指定处，不写成“工作人员已确认收到”。若要寄存行李，核对对方确实接受、取回凭据和时段；它与退房是两次交接。

### 成功条件

你能说出已检查和没法检查的区域、带走的包件数、仍由住宿方保管或待取的东西，以及本次钥匙与离开通知的实际状态。全部个人物品已带离已查空间，住宿方物品未混入包，要求的退房动作在实际时限内完成。没有收到确认的自助投递，只记“按指引投递”，不虚构对方已验收。

### 常见报错与补救

- **刚检查完浴室又拿出洗漱物：** 重查浴室、插座和放置处，再封包；最后使用会使先前的勾选失效。
- **多人都以为对方拿了充电器或钥匙：** 每件由实际持有人确认；无法确定就看最后使用位置，不用猜测完成清单。
- **发现包里混入住宿方物品：** 在交出钥匙前放回指定处或交给负责人；不确定归属先问。
- **退房时间快到但某区域够不到：** 告诉住宿方和同行者具体未查位置，请可用帮助；不要冒险攀爬或把未查写成已查。
- **门已锁、钥匙已交才想起物品：** 走住宿方的正式联系渠道请求协助，不私自返回或尝试别人的钥匙；这已转为遗失物追查。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 只适用于普通短住的物品和约定交接。前台退卡、自助钥匙盒、数字退房和额外整理要求随实际住宿而变；Airbnb 的平台规则不是全球酒店规则。视觉不便可用触摸与可信同行者分区复核；行动受限时请工作人员处理够不到的位置。没有收纳袋也可以按固定待搬区和包件数核对。最后的几分钟可能找到一只袜子，但不会自动证明住宿方已经验收房间。

## English

Use this Skill while you can still enter your ordinary hotel, hostel, or short-stay room and complete its handover. Bring your belongings, leave the property's items, and return keys under **this stay's** rules. Finish knowing which areas were checked, how many of your bags left, and what actually happened at handover. This does not trace an item noticed missing after departure or decide damage, fees, or tenancy responsibility.

### Preparation and inputs

Open this reservation's checkout time and latest instructions. Check whether it requires a desk visit, key return, lights or windows left a certain way, rubbish handling, or a departure message. Do not import another property's habits. Count your bags, keys or cards, and companions. Allow time to reach the handover point. Put the phone, coat, glasses, or other last-use item in one named “take last” spot so the sweep does not scatter again.

### Execution

1. **Separate by destination first.** Put your possessions in bags or a carry-out area. Leave property towels, hangers, remotes, and keys where this stay directs. Ask a companion or the property about uncertain ownership; do not silently pack a mystery object. Reconcile anything borrowed or formally stored against its own handover record.
2. **Make one final visible sweep by area.** Check bedside and visible under-bed space, desk and drawers, outlets, wardrobe or safe, bathroom, fridge, behind the door, and the shared areas you actually used. Move in one direction, closing or marking each area when done, so “I think I checked” does not loop forever. Check only places you may enter and safely reach; ask for help with high spaces or another person's belongings.
3. **Count bags and next-use items after closing them.** Match each traveler's bag count to anything still in the room. Put your wallet, phone, keys, tickets, and other next-leg items where you control them, not in luggage about to be stored. At the door, look back at the swept areas and charging points. If someone uses an item after its area was checked, recheck that area.
4. **Do this stay's actual checkout actions.** Before its deadline, follow the stated ordinary instructions for windows, lights, doors, keys or cards, desk visit, or platform message. Do only the normal tidying actually requested. If damage, fees, or unusual cleaning becomes disputed, record what you can see and ask the property; do not repair anything or assign blame. Move all your items across the threshold before locking up or giving away the last key. Never use the room as unapproved post-checkout storage.
5. **Mark the real handover state.** If staff or host actually receives the key or departure notice, note the method. If you use a self-service drop, record “deposited as instructed,” not “staff confirmed receipt.” For bag storage, confirm acceptance, retrieval proof, and hours. Bag storage and checkout are separate handovers.

### Success

You can identify checked and unreachable areas, the bag count taken away, anything formally held for later retrieval, and the actual key and departure-message state. Your belongings have left the checked space, property items have not entered your bags, and required checkout actions met the real deadline. A self-service drop without a receipt remains a recorded drop, not an invented acceptance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You use toiletries after checking the bathroom:** Recheck the bathroom, outlet, and shelf before closing bags; last use cancels the earlier tick.
- **Each traveler thinks someone else took a charger or key:** Get confirmation from the person actually carrying it. If uncertain, inspect its last-use spot.
- **A property item turns up in your bag:** Return it to the instructed place or hand it to staff before giving up the key; ask if ownership is unclear.
- **Checkout is near and an area is unreachable:** Tell staff and companions the exact unchecked place and ask for usable help. Do not climb or mark it checked.
- **The door is locked and the key returned when you remember an item:** Use the property's official contact to request help; do not force entry or try another person's key. This has become a lost-item follow-up.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary short-stay belongings and agreed handover. Desk card return, a key box, digital checkout, and tidying requests vary by property; Airbnb's rules are not universal hotel rules. If sight is limited, use touch and a trusted companion's area check. If movement is limited, ask staff to handle an unreachable place. A fixed carry-out area and bag count work without organizer pouches. The last minutes may reveal a missing sock, but they cannot prove that the property has accepted the room.
