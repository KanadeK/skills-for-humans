## 中文

当你做完普通家常菜，一口**已安全冷却的锅**上有附着食物、但没有明显严重焦黑或涂层损坏时，用这份 Skill 按锅的真实材质松开残留并清洗。它不同于一轮普通杯盘碗的清洗顺序：锅表面可能是不粘层、珐琅、裸露不锈钢或需保养的铸铁，处理不能互抄。锅还在冒烟、食物大片焦结或表面破损时先停止这条普通残留流程。

### 准备与输入

确认锅具品牌/型号或至少可核实的表面类型，查看锅和制造商说明对洗剂、浸泡、刷具、洗碗机及干燥的限制。让锅按说明降温，不用冷水冲高温锅来赶工。备好适用手洗剂、清水、柔软海绵/尼龙刷或厂家允许的刮片和干净擦布。材料不明、涂层剥落、珐琅崩口、锅体裂缝或严重变形时停止继续做食品接触清洁，先询问制造商/专业建议。

### 执行

1. **先移走松散部分。** 用不伤该表面的工具把可松动食物移到垃圾或适用厨余路径，不把大块残渣直接塞进下水口。确认锅已冷到可安全触碰，不用裸手抓锋利或烫的边缘。
2. **按材质选一个允许的松脱方式。** 厂家许可的非粘、珐琅或不锈钢锅，可先用其说明允许的温水和温和洗剂软化，再用合适海绵轻洗；只按该型号允许的时长与工具。养护过的铸铁/碳钢若厂家反对长时间浸泡，就用其允许的短时清洗、合适刮片和随后干燥/薄油步骤。**不要把一款锅的浸泡或钢丝刷做法复制到另一种表面。**
3. **小范围检查效果再继续。** 选一块不显眼残留轻试，食物开始脱离且表面不被刮花，才继续清理其他区域；越刷越粗糙、涂层脱落或露出不明底材时立刻停。无需用很大力量证明锅还能用。
4. **按说明洗净、漂净与干燥。** 清水去除洗剂和食物残留，按厂家要求彻底干燥；需养护表面的锅依本型号说明处理，不给所有锅抹油。清洁完成后再看食品接触面是否完整、无可见残渣。
5. **仍粘住就记录并降级。** 如果一次温和且符合说明的处理未松动，查该锅的专门顽固污渍做法或请厂家/专业人员建议；不要升级为不明粉末、酸、漂白剂混合或尖锐金属刮擦。若发现是焦结层，转焦底损伤判断。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

普通附着食物已在不损伤表面的条件下移除，锅按其材质说明洗净和干燥，食品接触面无新损伤；若材质/护理信息不足或出现损伤，停下并隔离待确认也是完整结果。

- **软化时想直接整锅泡一夜：** 先查该型号；养护铸铁等可能不允许长泡，改走其专门短时清洁。
- **非粘或珐琅被刮出痕：** 停止使用更硬工具，保留照片和型号咨询厂家，不以继续刷平当修复。
- **锅还烫，水一浇就剧烈冒汽：** 停止加水，安全放下并按说明冷却后重来。
- **洗后仍有黑硬层：** 当作焦结/可能损伤另行判断，不把扩大力气当下一步骤。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只处理普通可手洗锅具的非严重附着物，不处理油锅起火、电气/燃气设备维修或危险化学清洁。厂家说明可因涂层和系列而不同；“不锈钢”“铸铁”字样不等于掌握了整锅结构。握力不便可把锅稳放在水槽中用小面积软工具处理，重锅请可信的人协助；看不清表面时用强光、放大照片，不靠指尖摸未知破损。清理后仍有刷具、抹布和台面要洗。

## English

Use this Skill for **a safely cooled pan** with ordinary adhered food after home cooking, when there is no severe black scorch or visible coating damage. Loosen and clean it under the pan's actual material and care instructions. This differs from a batch of everyday cups and plates: the surface may be nonstick, enamel, stainless steel, or maintained cast iron, and one method cannot be copied across them. Stop this ordinary-residue route for smoke, widespread char, or a damaged cooking surface.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the brand and model or at least a verifiable cooking surface, then read its maker guidance for soap, soaking, brushes, dishwasher use, and drying. Let it cool by those instructions; do not quench a hot pan with cold water to hurry. Have suitable dish soap, water, a soft sponge or nylon brush or maker-approved scraper, and a clean drying cloth. Stop food-contact cleaning and seek maker or professional advice if the material is unknown, coating flakes, enamel chips, the body cracks, or the pan is badly warped.

### Execution

1. **Remove loose matter first.** Use a tool safe for this surface to move loose food to a suitable waste route rather than blocking the drain with large pieces. Confirm the pan is cool enough to handle, and keep bare hands away from sharp or hot edges.
2. **Choose one permitted loosening method for this material.** Where the maker allows, nonstick, enamel, or stainless cookware may soften residue with its specified warm water and mild soap, then a suitable soft sponge. Follow this product's allowed contact time and tools. If a seasoned cast-iron or carbon-steel maker warns against long soaking, use its shorter cleaning, suitable scraper, then its drying and thin-oil process. **Do not copy a soak or steel-wool rule from one surface to another.**
3. **Test a small area before scaling.** Gently try one inconspicuous patch of residue. Continue only if food loosens without scratching. Stop immediately if the surface roughens, coating lifts, or unknown base material appears. Force is not proof that a pan is reusable.
4. **Wash, rinse, and dry by the manual.** Remove soap and food with water, dry thoroughly as the maker directs, and care for a seasoned surface only under its own instructions. Do not oil every pan. Inspect the food-contact surface for integrity and visible remaining residue afterward.
5. **Record and downgrade if it remains stuck.** If one mild supported pass fails, seek this pan's stubborn-residue instructions or maker or professional advice. Do not escalate to mystery powders, mixed acids and bleach, or sharp metal scraping. A hard char layer belongs to the scorched-pan assessment.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Ordinary adhered food came off without new surface damage, the pan was cleaned and dried by its material instructions, and the food-contact area has no visible residue. A clear stop for missing care information or new damage is also complete.

- **You want to leave the entire pan soaking overnight:** Check this model first. Seasoned cast iron may forbid long soaking; use its shorter method.
- **A nonstick or enamel surface scratches:** Stop the harder tool and keep photos and model for the maker. More scraping does not repair it.
- **The pan is hot and water flashes to steam:** Stop adding water, set it safely down, and cool it under instructions before resuming.
- **A hard black layer remains:** Assess char and possible damage separately instead of simply increasing force.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary hand-washable cookware and nonsevere stuck food, not oil fires, gas or electrical repair, or hazardous chemicals. Maker directions vary by coating and collection. “Stainless” or “cast iron” alone does not identify every part of a pan. Work on a stable surface with small soft tools or trusted help for a heavy pan if grip is limited. Use bright light or magnification for a hard-to-see surface, not fingertips on unknown damage. Brushes, cloths, and counter still need cleaning afterward.
