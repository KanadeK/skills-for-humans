## 中文

当你想去诗歌开放麦、社区分享夜或类似活动**只作为观众**，又担心被误当成登台者、被拍进宣传或现场被点名时，用这份 Skill 先辨认主办方的互动规则，再选择自己接受的参与程度。完成不是必须登台或被主持人喜欢，而是你在已知边界内观看、回应或选择离开。普通按座位看电影/戏剧归另一任务。

### 准备与输入

在主办方当期公告核时间、地点、费用/报名、观众是否可只听、表演者是否需单独报名或抽签、年龄/内容提示、摄影/录音及可及支持。不同活动差异很大：有的开放麦明确欢迎“来朗读或只听”，有的会拍摄，有的禁止观众录音。选一个你愿意的边界，如“只听和鼓掌，不登台、不露脸”；若场地规则无法满足，活动前就可不去。不要靠偷拍/录音留纪念，也不要带别人受保护作品上台。

### 执行

1. **到场先向主办方确认身份。** 说“我今天作为观众来，不报名表演”，问观众与表演者入口/名单是否不同，哪里可以坐或站、何时允许离开。若活动要求所有人必须表演或同意你不能接受的录制，选择退出，不靠临场“应该没事”押注。
2. **认清互动信号。** 听主持人是否有自愿上台、举手回应、观众提问或临时邀请的规则；掌声、起立、发言和登台不是同一件事。有人把报名表递来时可问清用途再决定，不因尴尬自动签名。
3. **按自己的边界参与。** 愿意时用主办方允许的掌声、简短回应或问答表达支持；不愿互动时可说“谢谢，我今天只想听”，或向主持人示意跳过。若主持/表演者继续逼迫，离开到安全的公开区域并向工作人员说明；不把拒绝升级为争吵。
4. **保护自己与别人。** 按主办方规则处理拍摄/录音；若活动可能拍摄而你不愿被拍，使用主办方提供的正式避拍方式或换不入镜位置。没有可行避拍路线就离开；不要假设入场一定可阻止摄像。也不公开上传他人的朗读、脸或私密话语。
5. **在可行时安静收工。** 按场地出口和节目段落离开，不穿过台前打断表演；记一句你实际听到或喜欢的内容，若想之后作为表演者参加，再单独核报名、时间和作品许可，不把观众身份自动升级。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你知道活动实际的观众/表演边界和拍摄规则，按自己愿意的程度真实观看或回应，并在越界时明确拒绝或退出。没人点你上台也可成功；参加者登记、被邀请和正式报名不能混为一谈。

- **有人误以为你报名了：** 及时说明“我只作为观众”，请主办方从表演名单移除，别等到被叫上台才硬撑。
- **活动公告没写能否只旁听：** 先问主办方，若必须登台而你不愿意就不参加。
- **镜头对着你但没有清楚的避拍办法：** 请工作人员解释实际选择；不能满足就离开，不拿偷拍反制。
- **内容或互动强度超出预期：** 选择现场允许的退出路线，不需要当众解释私人理由。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

开放麦可在图书馆、酒吧、学校或社区场地举行，年龄、费用、影像、噪声和互动约定各异，主办方现行规则优先。行动、视听或语言需求可提前问可及位置、字幕或工作人员帮助；若支持不足，离开也是有效选择。本文不教登台表演、公开发表受保护作品、酒精消费或骚扰应对中的危机干预。你可以享受活动而不成为节目的一部分。

## English

Use this Skill when you want to attend a poetry open mic, community sharing night, or similar event **as an audience member**, but worry about being treated as a performer, filmed for promotion, or called on. Learn the organiser's interaction rules first and choose how much to join. Success does not require taking the stage or winning the host's approval. You watch, respond, or leave within known boundaries. Ordinary seated cinema or theatre attendance is another task.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the current organiser listing for time, location, charges or registration, whether listening only is allowed, whether performers sign up or enter a draw, age and content notices, filming or recording, and access support. Events differ: some welcome people “to read or listen,” some film, and some ban audience recordings. Choose a boundary such as “listen and applaud, no stage and no camera.” If venue conditions cannot meet it, you can decline before going. Do not secretly record others as a keepsake or bring protected work onto a stage.

### Execution

1. **State your role on arrival.** Tell the organiser, “I am here to listen, not sign up to perform.” Ask whether audience and performer entrance or lists differ, where you may sit or stand, and when you may leave. If everyone must perform or accept filming you do not want, exit rather than bet that it will be fine.
2. **Recognise interaction signals.** Listen for voluntary stage slots, show-of-hands responses, audience questions, or invitations. Applause, standing, speaking, and performing are not one action. If offered a sign-up sheet, ask what it commits you to before writing your name.
3. **Join at your chosen level.** If willing, applaud or briefly respond through the allowed route. If not, say “Thanks, I am only listening today,” or signal the host to skip you. If pressure continues, move to a safe public area and tell staff; refusal need not become an argument.
4. **Protect your own and others' boundaries.** Follow organiser filming or audio rules. If cameras may include you and you object, use the organiser's actual opt-out method or an out-of-frame place. If neither is possible, leave; admission alone cannot be assumed to stop filming. Do not upload someone else's reading, face, or private words.
5. **Finish quietly when feasible.** Use the venue exit and a suitable pause rather than crossing in front of a performer. Note one thing you actually heard or enjoyed. If later interested in performing, separately check sign-up, time, and work permissions; an audience place does not become a stage slot by default.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You know the real audience-versus-performer and camera rules, watched or responded at a level you chose, and declined or left if the boundary failed. Nobody calling you on stage is compatible with success. Attendance, invitation, and formal performance sign-up are distinct.

- **Someone thinks you signed up:** Say you are audience-only and ask to be removed from the performer list before a stage call.
- **The listing does not say whether you may only listen:** Ask the organiser first. If performing is required and you do not want that, skip the event.
- **A camera points at you with no clear opt-out:** Ask staff about actual choices. Leave if they cannot meet your boundary; do not retaliate by filming them.
- **Content or interaction becomes too intense:** Use the allowed exit without a public explanation of private reasons.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

An open mic may be in a library, bar, school, or community venue, with different ages, charges, image rules, sound levels, and interaction customs. Current organiser rules govern. Ask ahead about accessible positions, captions, or staff help for mobility, hearing, sight, or language needs; leaving is valid when support fails. This Skill does not teach stage performance, publication of protected work, alcohol use, or crisis intervention for harassment. You may enjoy a show without becoming part of it.
