## 中文

当物品基本搬出、你要向有权接收的人说明旧住处当时可见状态时加载。你会得到一份**有时间、区域和观察依据的事实记录**，对看不到或判断不了的地方明确留白。这里不判断损坏原因、责任、押金、清洁标准，也不修理设施。

### 准备与输入

看实际接收方是否提供验收表或要求特定交接方式；把它作为需核对的本地要求，不把别的地区范本当规则。确认你可以进入和拍摄的区域，尊重同住者、邻居和私人信息。准备日期、时间与区域清单；相机不可用或不允许拍照时，文字记录也可做事实备忘。

### 执行

1. **在交接前记录时点。** 写日期、当地时间、地址或可区分的空间标识，以及谁在场；不把晚些时候补拍的画面冒充同一时刻。
2. **逐区写可观察事实。** 对有权查看的地面、墙面、固定设施外观和空置区域，写“看见什么、在哪里”。用“厨房柜门左下角有可见划痕”这种描述，避免“某人弄坏”或“正常磨损”这类未核实结论。
3. **按权限补充影像。** 若允许拍，先做能识别区域的全景，再拍必要局部，并将照片与文字中的区域对应。避免录入人脸、私人文件、门禁码或他人财物细节；需要时经许可遮挡。保留原始拍摄时点和一份你能找到的记录。
4. **把未知和异常单列。** 看不到、无法正常接近、需操作设备才知道的状态写“未查看/未测试”，不要填“完好”。遇到漏水、裸线、异常气味或其他可能危险的状态，停止使用受影响处，报告有权处理的人；不试图查因或维修。
5. **按实际流程交给接收者。** 只向有权者提供所需的事实记录和待确认项，记下交付方式与对方是否收到。若对观察不同意，分别记双方说法或请对方更正具体事实；不替任何一方裁定费用或责任。

### 完成与停止

记录能回答何时、何处、你实际看到了什么、哪儿未查看，并且私人信息未无谓公开；接收者拿到的是事实和问题。拍照未获许可、条件危险或需要法律/维修判断时，改用获准方式记录或转给适当负责人，不扩大访问权限。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有一张没有方位的近照：** 补区域名称和允许拍摄的上下文照片；不能补拍时如实说明定位不足。
- **写“全部完好”但某房没进去：** 改为已观察的区域和未查看区域，不用总评覆盖缺口。
- **照片含他人私人信息：** 停止传播，按实际权限处理副本；后续只拍必要位置或先获同意。
- **双方对一个划痕意见不同：** 保留可观察位置、照片和各自原话，交由实际流程处理，不推断谁造成。
- **设备表现异常：** 记能观察到的症状并通知有权人员，不拆卸或调试水电燃气装置。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通交接的事实整理，不提供押金、租约、权利或赔偿意见。不同住处和地区的表格、拍摄许可、保存方式不同，以实际规则为准。看不清、无法进入或使用相机不便时，可由获准的人描述、拍摄并读回；“他人代看”应在记录中说明。副作用是模糊记忆变成一张有出处的清单，但它本身不裁定争议。

## English

Load this Skill after most goods are out and before you describe the old place to someone authorised to receive it. You will make **a factual record with a time, area and basis for each visible observation**, leaving unseen or uncertain conditions explicit. This does not assign the cause of damage, liability, deposits or cleaning standards, and does not repair facilities.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether the real receiver supplies an inspection form or requires a particular handoff. Treat that as a local requirement, not a rule imported from another region. Confirm which areas you may enter and photograph while respecting housemates, neighbours and private information. Prepare date, time and area headings. Written notes can still record facts when a camera is unavailable or photography is not permitted.

### Execution

1. **Record the handoff-stage time.** Note date, local time, an address or distinct space label, and who is present. Do not present images taken later as if they captured the same moment.
2. **Write observable facts by area.** For accessible floors, walls, visible fixed fittings and emptied areas, note what you can see and where. “Visible scratch at the lower left of the kitchen cupboard door” is a fact description; “someone broke it” or “normal wear” needs a judgment you may not have.
3. **Add images within your permission.** If photography is allowed, make an area-identifying view and necessary close-ups, linked to the written area entry. Avoid faces, private documents, access codes and details of another person's goods; obscure them with permission where needed. Keep original capture times and a copy you can locate.
4. **List unknowns and abnormalities separately.** Mark an unseen, unreachable or untested fixture “not viewed / not tested,” not “fine.” For a leak, exposed wiring, unusual smell or another potentially hazardous condition, stop using the affected area and tell the responsible person. Do not diagnose or repair it.
5. **Use the actual handoff channel.** Give necessary facts and open questions only to the authorised recipient; note how you sent them and whether they were received. If another person disagrees, record each account or ask which specific observation they challenge. Do not decide cost or responsibility for either side.

### Success and stop

The record shows when and where you looked, what you really observed and what you did not inspect, without needless exposure of private information. The recipient gets facts and questions. If photos are not permitted, an area is unsafe or a legal or repair decision is needed, use an allowed record method or hand the matter to an appropriate authority rather than expanding your access.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only an unlocated close-up exists:** Add the area name and a permitted context image. If it cannot be retaken, state that location is uncertain.
- **“Everything is fine” hides an unopened room:** Replace the blanket statement with observed areas and an explicit unviewed area.
- **A photo contains someone else's private information:** Stop sharing it and handle copies under the real permissions. Photograph only the necessary place next time or ask first.
- **People disagree about a scratch:** Preserve its observable position, image and each account. Let the real process handle cause and responsibility.
- **A fixture behaves abnormally:** Note the observable symptom and tell the responsible person. Do not dismantle or test water, electrical or gas systems beyond ordinary observation.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is factual preparation for an ordinary handoff, not advice about deposits, leases, rights or compensation. Forms, photography permission and retention differ by place; use the actual rules. If you cannot see, enter or operate a camera, an authorised person can describe or photograph and read back; say when another person made the observation. The side effect is a traceable account in place of a vague memory, not an automatic decision in any dispute.
