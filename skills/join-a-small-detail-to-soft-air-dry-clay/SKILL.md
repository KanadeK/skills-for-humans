## 中文

当一件**仍软、未干的普通风干黏土作品**需要接上一个小而轻的装饰细节时，加载这份 Skill。结果是接缝暂时牢靠并有晾干后复查入口，不是能承重的结构连接。只用同种材料和其说明允许的少量水；已干硬件、金属挂钩、食品接触结构不在本篇。

### 准备与输入

查黏土包装关于接合与用水的说明，备同一种仍可塑的主体和小配件、钝头塑形工具、一点清水、保护台面的垫板。主体若太薄会被接件拉裂，先减小配件或放弃。没有钝头工具时可信协助者可准备；不用刀片或尖金属针。

### 执行

1. **试摆和定接触面。** 将小配件放到主体预计位置，看重量是否会让主体塌或偏；先调整形状与支撑，不急于粘。
2. **在两面轻轻划粗。** 用钝头工具在配件背面和主体对应处刻浅交叉线，只粗化表面，不划穿壁。局部粘土已经硬到碎裂，就停止本湿接路径。
3. **少水贴合并抹顺。** 按产品指示点极少水让接触面略湿，压合两块，再用手指或钝工具把边缘轻抹入主体。不能往缝里灌水、用湿泥厚堆成支柱。
4. **静放并安排复查。** 轻松开手，看配件能否保持位置；将作品放安全垫板上自然晾干，之后再看接缝是否开裂或脱落。湿时一碰没掉不等于干后保证牢固。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

小配件位置正确，主体未被戳穿，湿接缝暂能保持且已安排干后检查。配件持续往下滑、主体开裂，应停而不是继续加水。

- **配件太重：** 换小配件或不接，不能把装饰连接当挂重物的承力点。
- **粗化时划穿壁：** 停、回补主体或重新塑形，别把破口藏在配件下。
- **加水后变糊滑落：** 暂停让多余水按说明挥发，再评估；不追加更多湿泥。
- **干后接缝开裂：** 记录失败，按本品的修裂/干后粘接说明选择安全修复或重做，不宣称原接缝成功。
- **精细动作困难：** 用大一些的主体、更轻配件和可信协助定位，仍由你核验接缝与停止条件。

### 假设与边界

不同品牌接合方法不同；Crayola 示例的刻纹加少量水只支持其产品，不自动适用于所有风干材料。即使接成，装饰物仍不耐水、热或载重。

## English

Load this Skill when a **still-soft ordinary air-dry clay object** needs one small light decorative detail attached. Finish with a seam that holds for drying and a planned dry-state check, not a load-bearing joint. Use the same clay and only the little water its directions permit. Dry hard pieces, metal hooks, and food-contact structures are outside scope.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the clay package on joining and water. Set out a soft base and small detail of the same material, a blunt craft tool, a little water, and protected board. If the base is so thin that the detail will tear it, reduce the detail or stop. Use trusted help to prepare a blunt tool if needed, not blades or pointed metal needles.

### Execution

1. **Place the detail first.** Try its planned position and check whether its weight sags or tips the base. Adjust form and support before wetting a seam.
2. **Roughen both contact faces gently.** Make shallow crossing marks with a blunt tool on detail and base, without cutting through the wall. Stop this soft-joining route if clay has hardened enough to crumble.
3. **Moisten lightly, press, and blend.** Under product directions, dab very little water onto contact faces and press together. Smooth the edge softly into the base with fingers or blunt tool. Do not pour water into the seam or pile wet clay into a thick structural prop.
4. **Rest and plan a recheck.** Release your hand lightly and check that the detail stays positioned. Leave the piece on a safe board to air dry and check later for splitting or detachment. Holding for a moment when wet does not guarantee dry strength.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The light detail sits where planned, the base has not been punctured, the damp seam holds for now, and a dry check is pending. Persistent slipping or cracking calls for a stop, not more water.

- **Detail is too heavy:** Use a smaller detail or omit it; this decorative joint cannot carry hanging weight.
- **Scoring punctures a wall:** Stop and rebuild or repair the base rather than hiding the hole beneath the detail.
- **Too much water makes a slippery mush:** Pause to let moisture leave under product directions; do not add more wet clay.
- **Seam cracks after drying:** Record failure and use the product's safe crack or dry-attachment directions, or remake. Do not claim the original seam succeeded.
- **Fine movement is hard:** Use a larger base, lighter detail, and trusted positioning help while you confirm the seam and stop conditions.

### Assumptions and limits

Brands vary. The Crayola example of scoring and a little water supports its product, not every air-dry material. A joined decoration remains unsuitable for water, heat, or loads.
