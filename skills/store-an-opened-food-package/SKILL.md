## 中文

当一件普通食品**已经拆开食品密封层**，这次用完后还有余量时，加载这份 Skill。它决定眼前的余量能否按具体说明继续保存，并实际覆盖、放置和记录；不是给所有食品规定一个统一开封天数。若食品仍未拆封，可先做[开封前的余量检查](../check-before-opening-a-food-package/SKILL.md)。

### 准备与输入

拿出食品和原包装或清晰照片，确认准确品名、哪一层密封何时拆开、从拆开至今怎样放置、是否有渗漏或异常，以及标签上的开封后温度、容器、使用期限和其他条件。核对设备是否可靠、位置是否有空间、原包装能否按说明重新密封；需要换容器时准备干净、食品适用且适合该用途的有盖容器。开封日期忘了、离开所需温度多久不明，就写“未知”，不要填今天以制造新鲜纪录。

看不清标签或不方便搬动时，放大照片、语音读取、请可信的人读出关键条件或协助取放。无法确认安全关键条件，先不要把余量归入“以后吃”。

### 执行

1. **先核对能否继续保存。** 对照实际开封时间、之后的温度/放置历史、包装完整性与这件商品的开封后说明。包装未开封时的日期不能单独延长开封余量的寿命；外观和气味正常也不能补回缺失的时间或温度记录。曾明显失去要求的保存条件、渗漏污染或关键历史无法查明时，隔离并按当地食品安全指导处理，不执行后面的“贴标签继续放”。
2. **抄下开封后的具体条件。** 标出它要求冷藏、冷冻还是其他方式，是否要在包装内、重新密封或转入合适容器，以及何时用完。具体日期词汇和适用安全限制因地区而异；标签与本地指导冲突或不清楚时，先查制造商对应产品及所在地权威，不从别的食物借一个天数。
3. **实际放进符合条件的位置。** 需要低温时及时放入可靠且有空间的设备，不让余量在台面等你整理其他东西。原包装能按说明关闭就继续使用；否则按食品及容器说明选已确认合适的干净有盖容器，把原标签信息留在旁边或拍照留存。生肉、禽肉、海鲜等要防漏，避免滴到即食食品；不把清洁用品和食物放在一起。
4. **写真实记录。** 在仍能辨认的包装或容器上写品名和实际开封日期；有开封后使用条件时，把原文或可查的位置一并保留。若产品本身有多个独立密封小份，只给真正拆开的那份写开封记录，不把外盒打开日冒充每份的日期。
5. **做一次结束检查。** 食品在标签要求的位置、被妥善覆盖或密封、不污染其他食物，且你下次能读到品名、实际开封时间和具体条件。若没有可靠冷藏、合适容器或可确认的保存历史，不承诺以后食用；按当地指导决定是否需丢弃，不能用反复冷冻或调味掩盖未知史。

### 完成条件

对于仍可保存的余量，**条件、位置、容器、真实开封信息**四项都可指出；不符合条件的余量被隔离并得到明确停止/处理决定。仅在容器上写“今天”或闻起来正常，都不等于完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **常温售卖，开封后标签却要求冷藏：** 立即按该包装要求处理；没有可靠冷藏空间时不把余量留作以后。货架位置是未开封状态的信息。
- **只看原来的最佳食用期或使用期限：** 补读开封后说明和当地日期含义，记录真实开封时间；不要把原日期直接抄成余量到期日。
- **已转进容器，原标签找不到：** 先找到原包装、购买记录或制造商对应说明；关键储存条件仍未知时停止保留，不能给无名容器凭空指定天数。
- **冰箱满了或温度不可靠：** 不把食品挤到门关不上，也不以室温当临时冷藏。能否使用其他可靠设备、是否必须丢弃，按这件食品和当地安全指导决定。
- **忘记开封多久：** 不重新写今天冒充开封日，不靠气味判安全；对无法建立安全条件的易腐余量保守处理。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 仅处理普通家庭食品，不处理婴幼儿食品、罐藏、发酵、严重过敏处置或医疗饮食。包装工艺、日期词义和储存规则因产品和地区不同；你手中的说明与适用权威是事实来源。容器材质匹配、冷藏空间重排与长期日期轮换是邻近任务，此处只验证本次余量能实际安全存放。可能的副作用是一个需清洗的容器和一条可读的标签；这比假装冰箱有记忆更便宜。

## English

Load this Skill when the food seal of an ordinary product is **already broken** and some food remains after this use. Decide whether the real remainder can still be kept under its own directions, then cover, place, and record it. This is not a universal number of days after opening. If the food is still sealed, start with [the before-opening remainder check](../check-before-opening-a-food-package/SKILL.md).

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the food and its original package or a clear photo in view. Confirm the exact product, which seal broke and when, how it has been held since then, any leak or damage, and the label's temperature, container, use period, and other after-opening conditions. Check that the storage equipment works, space exists, and the original pack can be closed as directed. If transfer is needed, have a clean, food-suitable covered container confirmed for that use. If you do not know the opening date or time away from required conditions, write “unknown”; today's date cannot repair the history.

Enlarge a label, use audio reading, or ask a trusted person to read a critical instruction or help you reach a shelf. If a safety-critical condition cannot be confirmed, do not put the remainder into the “eat later” category.

### Execution

1. **Check whether keeping it is still possible.** Compare the real opening time, subsequent temperature and handling history, package condition, and this product's after-opening directions. The unopened date alone cannot extend an opened remainder's life. Normal appearance or smell cannot recover a missing time or temperature record. If required storage clearly failed, food leaked onto something else, or critical history cannot be established, isolate it and follow local food-safety guidance rather than proceeding to a fresh label.
2. **Read the specific after-opening conditions.** Record whether this item needs refrigeration, freezing, or another method; whether it must stay in its package, be resealed, or move to a suitable container; and when it must be used. Date wording and safety limits vary by region. If the label and local guidance conflict or are unclear, check the maker's information for this product and your local authority. Do not borrow another food's number of days.
3. **Put it in a place that meets those conditions.** Refrigerate promptly in reliable equipment with room when required. Do not leave it on the counter while you organize other things. Keep the original pack if it closes as directed. Otherwise use a clean covered container already confirmed as suitable for the food and purpose, and keep the original label or a photo accessible. Contain leaks from raw meat, poultry, and seafood away from ready-to-eat food; keep cleaners away from food.
4. **Record what really happened.** Mark the name and actual opening date on a legible package or container. Preserve the original after-opening instruction or where it can be found. If the product contains individually sealed portions, mark only the portion you actually opened; the outer box's opening date is not automatically each portion's date.
5. **Check before finishing.** The food is in its required location, covered or sealed appropriately, cannot contaminate other foods, and you can read its name, actual opening time, and product-specific condition next time. Without reliable cooling, a suitable container, or a confirmable storage history, do not promise future use. Use local guidance to decide whether it must be discarded; refreezing or heavy seasoning cannot erase an unknown history.

### Success

For a remainder you can still keep, you can identify its **condition, location, container, and real opening information**. A remainder that fails has an explicit isolate-and-stop decision. Writing “today” on a container or detecting a normal smell is not completion.

### Common errors and recovery

- **It was sold on an ambient shelf but now says refrigerate after opening:** Follow this package promptly. Without reliable cold space, do not keep the remainder for later. The shelf position described its unopened state.
- **You rely only on the original best-before or use-by date:** Read the after-opening directions and local meaning of the date; record when the seal actually broke. Do not copy the printed date as the remainder's automatic deadline.
- **The food moved to a container and the label is lost:** Find the original package, purchase record, or maker's directions for the exact item. If critical storage conditions remain unknown, stop keeping it rather than inventing a number of days for an unnamed container.
- **The fridge is full or its temperature unreliable:** Do not force food into a space whose door will not close or treat room temperature as temporary refrigeration. Whether another reliable appliance works or the food must be discarded depends on the actual product and local safety guidance.
- **You forgot when it was opened:** Do not rewrite today as the opening date or use smell as a safety test. Handle a perishable remainder conservatively when safe conditions cannot be established.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary home food, excluding infant food, canning, fermentation, serious allergy handling, and medical diets. Packaging, date words, and storage rules vary by product and region; the item in your hand and applicable authority provide the facts. Detailed container matching, refrigerator rearrangement, and long-term date rotation are neighboring tasks. Here you establish that this actual remainder can be stored as required. You may gain one container to wash and a readable label; that is cheaper than pretending the refrigerator has memory.
