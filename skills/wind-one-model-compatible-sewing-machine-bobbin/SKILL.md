## 中文

当你有**型号与说明书可查、运转正常的家用缝纫机**，需要为废布练习绕好一个正确型号的空梭芯时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一枚松紧均匀、不过满、能按本机说明使用的梭芯，绕线机构已退回；此时尚未装入梭壳或缝布。梭芯透明/金属、尺寸与绕法随机型，不能因“差不多能卡进去”混用。

### 准备与输入

从本机铭牌/说明书确定具体型号、梭芯样式和绕线图；只有外形近似而无兼容证据就停。备空且无裂/缺口的梭芯、标签适用的普通线、稳定台面和合脚控制。无说明书、绕线器损坏、针可能意外运动或电源线破损时不要运行；儿童按设备要求监护。手指、衣袖和长发远离转轴与针。

### 执行

1. **依本机图定位线轴和导线。** 在设备安全待机状态放上线轴，沿说明书的绕梭芯专用导线钩和张力盘走线，使线真正进入盘间；不直接把线轴连到空梭芯绕过张力盘。
2. **安装空梭芯并进入绕线模式。** 按本机规定方向让线头通过孔或绕几圈，梭芯坐到绕线轴上并锁到指定位置；型号有离合/针停止设置时按说明操作。轻拉确认梭芯不歪，手离开运动范围才启动。
3. **低速观察绕线。** 依本机脚踏或按键缓慢绕，初始线尾按说明处理，观察线层平整而不是一侧堆山或松散飞圈。机器达到其自动停止点或接近标示满度就停，不硬灌更多线。
4. **退出并核成品。** 关闭运动、按说明把绕线轴退回正常位置，取下梭芯并剪断连接线，检查是否过满、变形、裂损、绕线松散。异常梭芯按说明重绕，不装机碰运气。记录线种和梭芯型号以备穿线。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

正确样式空梭芯被均匀绕到本机允许范围，取出后绕线器正常退位、手和线远离运动件；未完成装梭/上线。一个过满、侧堆或有缺口的梭芯不算可用成品。

- **线绕得很松或偏一侧：** 停机，查专用张力盘/导线走法，按本机图重绕。
- **梭芯样式不确定：** 停止，查型号或厂家，不试不同材质/尺寸替代。
- **绕线时针也意外运动：** 立刻停，查本机绕线模式/离合或请维修者。
- **梭芯裂、满到卡死：** 不装入机器，换兼容完好空芯。
- **手眼/行动限制：** 大字型号图、明亮台面和熟悉该型号的可信协助，可帮助你确认退位与绕线层。

### 假设与边界

Singer 明确同品牌机器也有不同梭芯和绕线结构；这篇只给检查顺序，实际导线方向、按键和离合完全以本机手册为准。不调整内部张力螺钉或修电机。

## English

Load this Skill when a **sound identified home sewing machine with its manual** needs one correct empty bobbin wound for scrap practice. Finish with a compatible evenly wound, not overfilled bobbin and winding mode disengaged. The bobbin is not yet installed or sewing fabric. Clear/metal bobbins, size, and winding path vary by model; a near fit is not compatibility.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the machine plate/manual for exact model, bobbin class, and winding diagram. Hold if compatibility is based only on appearance. Have a sound empty bobbin without chips, suitable ordinary thread, stable table, and controllable pedal or button. Do not run a machine with no manual, damaged winder, unexpected needle movement, or frayed power cord; follow supervision needs for children. Keep fingers, sleeves, and hair away from moving spindle and needle.

### Execution

1. **Route spool under the exact model diagram.** In a safe idle state, place thread spool and pass thread through the bobbin-winding guides and tension disc so it seats between discs. Do not lead spool straight to bobbin while skipping winding tension.
2. **Mount empty bobbin and engage winding mode.** Feed a tail through its hole or make starting wraps as this manual directs, seat and lock the bobbin on its spindle, and use the model's clutch/needle-stopping control if present. Check it does not wobble, then clear hands before motion.
3. **Wind slowly while watching.** Use the maker's pedal or button gently, handling starting tail only under its directions. Watch for even layers, not a one-sided mound or loose flying loops. Stop at automatic stop or marked safe fullness; do not force more thread on.
4. **Disengage and inspect.** Stop movement, return spindle/mode to normal under instructions, remove bobbin and cut its connecting thread. Check for overfill, warping, damage, and loose winding. Rewind an abnormal bobbin before installation. Note thread and bobbin class for threading.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One correct bobbin is wound evenly within model limits and removed with winder reset and hands clear. Upper and lower threading remain later work. An overfull, one-sided, or chipped bobbin is not usable completion.

- **Thread winds loose or one-sided:** Stop and check winding disc and path against this manual, then rewind.
- **Bobbin type uncertain:** Hold for model or maker confirmation, not a guessed material or size.
- **Needle moves unexpectedly during winding:** Stop at once and check winding/clutch mode or use service.
- **Bobbin cracks or jams from overfill:** Do not install; replace with a sound compatible empty bobbin.
- **Grip, sight, or movement is limited:** Large model diagrams, good light, and trusted model-aware help can support checking wound layers and disengagement.

### Assumptions and limits

Singer notes even its own machines differ in bobbin type and winder. This supplies a check sequence; the actual guides, direction, control, and clutch come from the exact manual. No internal tension-screw or motor repair is taught.
