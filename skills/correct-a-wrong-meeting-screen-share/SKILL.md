## 中文

当会议里共享的是错误窗口、空白画面、过期内容，或你怀疑看到了无关私人区域时，用这份 Skill **先停共享，再重新选择**。目标是让参与者看到这一轮确实需要的普通内容，并确认对方也看得到；这不是敏感信息泄露响应流程。若已展示敏感或组织受管信息，先停止共享，再按适用的组织规则联系负责人员。

### 准备与输入

在脑中或纸上说清要展示的**一个**页面、文件或窗口，确认你有权给这些参与者看。准备一项不共享的替代方式，例如口头说明或会后通过已认可渠道发送，避免为了救一张幻灯片把整个桌面暴露出去。不同软件的“窗口”“标签页”“整屏”选项不同；分享整屏会带上随后切出的窗口和通知风险。

### 执行

1. **立即止住错误画面。** 点会议里的“停止共享/Stop sharing”；若看到共享指示仍亮着，重复确认当前确实不再发送。不要一边继续展示错误画面一边翻找正确文件。已经看见的画面不能靠停止共享撤回。
2. **核对目标和无关内容。** 打开要展示的正确版本，看标题、页码或标识；把不该露出的聊天、个人文件和通知处理好。只有不涉及敏感泄露且拥有权限的普通材料才继续本篇。
3. **重新选择最小范围。** 在会议共享选择器里，优先选目标窗口或浏览器标签页；确需跨多个窗口才考虑整屏。查看选择器预览或应用的共享边框，核对选中的是当前目标。若选项因平台、浏览器或权限不可用，不猜按，先用可见界面提示或主办方协助。
4. **请对方确认一项可见细节。** 开始共享后问“能看到标题 X 或第 Y 页吗？”你本机预览正常不等于所有参与者收到。如果看不到，停止共享，检查是否选错窗口、窗口被关闭、内容仍在加载或主持人限制共享；只做一次有针对性的重选。
5. **收口。** 画面正确时继续讲解，结束后主动停止共享并看指示消失。仍不行则说明“共享暂不可用”，转口头、会议允许的文字/文件渠道或改期；发送文件前重新核对象与版本，避免把 H225 的核对省掉。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

目标参与者确认看到了正确的普通内容，且你结束时已关闭共享；或你已清楚通知共享不可用并改用对方同意的渠道。只有本机出现预览，不算他人已收到。

- **共享的是旧版本：** 停止，回文件核标题/日期，再重新选择；不要在直播画面中翻私人目录。
- **共享画面一直空白：** 停止并核选项；窗口共享不行时，可用平台允许且隐私可控的文件/口头替代，不自动扩大到整屏。
- **参与者说“看不到”：** 请其说是全黑、冻结还是错误页，再只改对应选择；仍失败时降级，别让会议等待无限重试。
- **误露私人或受管信息：** 立即停止共享；已见内容无法撤回，按所在组织或场合的实际报告路径处理。本篇不教隐藏、掩盖或自行评估泄露后果。
- **看不清共享边框或预览：** 使用应用的可访问共享列表、读屏名称或可信协助；无法确认目标时不开始共享。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只处理你有权展示的低敏感普通内容。会议主持权限、浏览器、系统和版本可能改变可用共享方式；用当前应用提供的选项和反馈为准。停止共享会短暂打断讨论，却比持续展示错误画面更可控；已暴露内容不能撤回。

## English

Use this Skill when a meeting shows the wrong window, a blank image, outdated content, or an unrelated private area. **Stop sharing before selecting again.** The goal is to show the intended ordinary content to the intended participants and confirm they can see it. This is not an incident response procedure. If sensitive or organisation-managed information was shown, stop sharing first and contact the responsible people under applicable rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Name the **one** page, file, or window you intend to show, and confirm you may show it to these participants. Have an alternative such as spoken explanation or later sharing through an approved channel. Do not expose a whole desktop merely to rescue one slide. Apps differ in window, tab, and whole-screen options; whole-screen sharing can expose later windows and notifications.

### Execution

1. **Stop the wrong picture immediately.** Use the meeting's Stop sharing control and check that the sharing indicator goes away. Do not search for the right file while the wrong view remains live. Stopping cannot retract what others already saw.
2. **Check the target and unrelated content.** Open the correct version and check its title, page, or identifier. Deal with chats, personal files, and notifications you do not intend to expose. Continue here only for ordinary material you are permitted to show and when no sensitive disclosure needs handling.
3. **Select the smallest scope again.** Prefer the intended window or browser tab in the meeting's share picker. Consider the whole screen only when moving between windows is necessary. Inspect the picker preview or visible share border to confirm the target. If a platform or permission does not offer the needed option, use its visible guidance or ask the host instead of guessing.
4. **Ask someone to confirm a visible detail.** After sharing, ask, “Can you see title X or page Y?” A good local preview does not prove delivery to everyone. If they cannot see it, stop and check whether the wrong window was selected, it closed, content is still loading, or the host restricts sharing. Make one targeted retry.
5. **Close the loop.** When the view is correct, continue and actively stop sharing at the end; check the indicator disappears. If it still fails, say sharing is unavailable and use spoken, meeting-approved text/file delivery, or reschedule. Check the recipient and version before sending a file rather than skipping H225's task.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The intended participants confirmed the correct ordinary content, and sharing ended when you finished; or you clearly told them sharing was unavailable and moved to a channel they accepted. A local preview alone is not proof of receipt.

- **The shared file is an old version:** Stop, verify its title or date outside the live view, then select again. Do not browse personal folders on air.
- **The shared image stays blank:** Stop and check the selection. If window sharing fails, use a permitted file or spoken alternative with controlled privacy rather than automatically expanding to the whole screen.
- **A participant says “I can't see it”:** Ask whether it is black, frozen, or the wrong page, then correct that condition once. Downgrade if it still fails instead of keeping the meeting in endless retries.
- **Private or managed information was shown:** Stop sharing immediately. What was seen cannot be withdrawn; follow the actual organisation or event reporting route. This Skill does not teach concealment or self-assessment of disclosure impact.
- **The share border or preview is inaccessible:** Use the app's accessible share list, spoken item names, or trusted help. Do not start sharing when you cannot confirm the target.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers only low-sensitivity ordinary content you may show. Host controls, browser, system, and version can change sharing options; rely on current app choices and feedback. A short interruption is a controlled outcome. It cannot undo material already exposed.
