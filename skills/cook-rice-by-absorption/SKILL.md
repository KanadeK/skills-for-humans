## 中文

当你手里是一款**适合吸水法**的米，想按包装或适用饭锅说明把米和预定量的水一起煮成一锅米饭时，用这份 Skill。这里的水应被米吸收，和“用大量水煮好再沥掉”的办法不同。它不提供所有米种通用的水米比或时长，也不替压力锅、陌生设备发明按钮。

### 准备与输入

读这包米关于是否淘洗、米与水的量取单位、加热与停火/静置的说明；饭锅则读其准确型号和所用量杯/水位线。不同米品、加工方式与设备要求不能互抄。准备可饮用水、干净量具、容量足够且能安全盖好的锅或正常工作的饭锅、计时方式和防烫工具。锅、盖或电线损坏，或你看不清关键水位时先停，换可读说明或可靠协助。

### 执行

1. **固定这一包米的基线。** 决定本次做多少，按同一套包装/设备单位量米和水。某些饭锅自带量杯并配专属刻度，不能把它与普通杯的水位线随意混用；说明缺失时找此米或设备的可靠做法，不从另一个米种猜比例。
2. **给吸水和膨胀留空间。** 按说明处理米（需要时淘洗或浸泡），放入适用容器并加准确水量。确认锅容量、盖法及设备最高/最低装载要求，不能把米水装到溢出边缘。
3. **启动相符的加热。** 普通带盖锅按米品说明经历加热、加盖和后续小火/静置阶段；饭锅按制造商的米类设置和说明操作。蒸汽和锅盖很烫，开盖前避开脸与手；不要为了焦虑反复揭盖，使吸水条件不断变化。
4. **按信号检查，而非只盯钟。** 到米品或设备提示的检查点，确认液体被吸收、米粒达到该品类要求的质地；若仍硬或还有明显未吸收的水，按该产品的补救说明继续，不靠一段凭空新增的固定分钟数。若闻到焦糊或设备异常，先安全停热，不用加水掩盖故障。
5. **按说明结束。** 需要静置的先完成静置，再用安全的器具松饭、取用。不要用生肉用过的勺或盘盛米饭。准备当餐可吃的量；不马上吃的熟饭要按当地食品安全指导及时保温或冷却并保存，不留在室温等下一顿。

### 成功条件

这锅米按手中产品及设备说明完成，米粒达到可接受质地、预定水量已按机制吸收或已按可信说明处理，能安全盛出；若包装/设备条件不明或设备异常，明确停止是完整结果。熟饭的后续安全去向也已确定。

### 常见报错与补救

- **套用另一款米的水米比：** 退回这一包和设备说明，重新量取；如果已经开煮，不凭一张通用表直接加大量水。
- **饭锅专用杯和普通杯混用：** 确认水位线对应的量具与米类，必要时改用包装给出的同单位做法。
- **到时间仍硬或锅里仍有水：** 按该米品的可信结束/补救提示继续观察；计时器只提醒你检查，不能单独宣布熟了。
- **饭做好却没有马上吃：** 立即选择按当地要求安全保温，或及时冷却并冷藏；室温台面不是已安排的保存方式。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这针对普通家用米饭吸水法，不是某个米种、品牌或营养治疗配方。地区、海拔、锅具和米加工方式会改变水量与时间；具体包装和设备说明优先。视觉、握力或端热锅困难时可用大字水位、稳定台面、易握量具或可信协助；不能安全操作蒸汽与热锅就停止。需要清洗锅具，且熟饭不能因看似干爽就长期室温放置。

## English

Use this Skill with an identified rice product **suited to the absorption method** when you want a measured amount of water and rice to cook together and become one pot of rice. The water is intended to be absorbed, unlike a method that boils grain in excess water and drains it. This Skill gives no universal water ratio or minutes for every rice, and invents no controls for pressure cookers or unfamiliar appliances.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this pack's directions for rinsing, rice and water units, heating, stopping, and resting. For a rice cooker, read the exact model and identify its supplied cup and water lines. Rice processing and appliance differences matter. Have drinking water, clean measuring tools, a suitable roomy lidded pot or working rice cooker, an accessible timer, and protection for hot parts. Stop if a pot, lid, or cable is damaged or a critical water line cannot be read; find readable instructions or trusted help.

### Execution

1. **Fix this product's baseline.** Decide how much to make, then measure rice and water in one consistent system from the package or appliance. Some cookers pair their special cup with matching marks; do not casually mix those with an ordinary cup. If instructions are missing, find a reliable method for this rice and equipment instead of borrowing a ratio from another grain.
2. **Allow for absorption and expansion.** Prepare the rice only as its directions require, including rinsing or soaking if specified. Put it and the measured water into a suitable container. Check pan capacity, lid use, and the appliance's load limits; do not fill it to a spill-prone rim.
3. **Use the supported heating route.** A plain lidded pan follows the rice's heating, covered simmer, and rest directions. A rice cooker follows its maker's rice setting and instructions. Steam and lids are hot; keep face and hands clear when checking. Repeatedly lifting the lid changes the absorption conditions rather than fixing uncertainty.
4. **Check signals, not only minutes.** At the product or device's check point, see whether liquid has been absorbed and the grain reaches the intended texture. If it remains hard or visibly wet, use guidance for this rice to continue. Do not add an invented universal number of minutes. For scorching smells or abnormal equipment behavior, stop the heat safely instead of masking a fault with water.
5. **Finish as directed.** Complete a specified rest, then loosen and serve with a safe utensil. Do not use a spoon or dish that just held raw meat. Make what you can use now; rice not eaten promptly needs suitable hot holding or prompt cooling and cold storage under local food-safety guidance, not a stay on the counter until the next meal.

### Success

The rice follows this pack and appliance's method, reaches an acceptable texture, and its measured water is absorbed or handled under reliable directions. You can serve it safely. A clear stop for unknown settings or faulty equipment is also complete. Cooked rice has a safe next destination.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You copy another rice's water ratio:** Return to this pack and appliance. If already heating, do not dump in a large guessed amount from a general chart.
- **A cooker cup and ordinary cup are mixed:** Match the water line to the supplied measure and rice type, or use a package method with consistent units.
- **The timer ends but rice is hard or water remains:** Follow trustworthy finish or recovery cues for this rice. A timer calls for a check; it does not certify doneness.
- **The rice is done but not being eaten:** Choose locally suitable safe hot holding or prompt cooling and refrigeration now. Room-temperature counter space is not a storage plan.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary home rice by absorption, not a rice-variety matrix or medical nutrition formula. Location, altitude, pan, and processing change water and time; the exact pack and appliance govern. Use large-print water marks, a stable surface, easier-to-grip measures, or trusted help if vision, grip, or a hot pan is difficult. Stop if steam and heat cannot be handled safely. The pot needs cleaning, and cooked rice is not safe for prolonged room-temperature keeping just because it looks dry.
