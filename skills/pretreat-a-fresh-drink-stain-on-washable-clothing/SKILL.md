## 中文

当你**刚把已知的普通非油性饮料**洒到可在家水洗的衣物上，且衣物与洗涤剂标签都可读时，用这份 Skill 在正常洗涤前处理局部。奶油、油脂、颜料、体液、腐蚀性液体或不知道来源的痕迹不走这条路；有安全疑问先停止。目标是减轻污渍并避免扩散，不承诺一次洗净。

### 准备与输入

确认饮料是什么、何时洒上，衣物能否水洗、允许水温、颜色/印花/镶边限制，以及适用洗衣剂的预处理说明。准备干净白布、可控制的清水和接水位置。先在不显眼处按产品说明试验；颜色转移、面料起毛或涂层变化时停止。贵重、结构复杂或只准专业护理的衣物交专业处理。

### 执行

1. **先轻按表面。** 用干净白布从污渍边缘向内轻按，吸走未渗入的饮料；换干净面继续，不用力横向擦。若液体可能是危险化学品，停止触碰并退出普通洗衣流程。
2. **只在标签允许时用水。** 将衣物背面朝向可控制的清水流，让水从污渍背面向外带走部分液体；无法安全翻到背面或装饰不耐水时先停，查制造商说明。不要把水量开到喷溅别处，也不使用超出标签上限的温度。
3. **按一个产品说明预处理。** 若衣物与洗衣剂都允许，在小处测试无变化后，将适用液体洗衣剂或预洗产品按其说明用于污渍；接触时长服从产品与衣物，不发明“一定泡一小时”。不同时叠加漂白剂、氨或其他清洁品。
4. **按原衣物洗法洗。** 把这件衣服交给其标签允许的机洗或手洗流程，选项和剂量按实际洗衣机/洗衣剂说明。普通污渍不替你重新定义整桶程序，也不保证多加洗剂有效。
5. **干燥前复查。** 在充足光线下看同一位置；仍有明显痕迹时先不要烘干或熨烫，按残留污渍的流程重新判断。若布色、图案或表面结构已经变化，停止继续去渍并记录，这可能不再是可洗掉的污物。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

饮料余量已被轻按或允许的清水带走，所用产品与衣物相容，衣物按标签洗过且干燥前已复查；若处理会伤衣或仍未确认条件，停止也是完整结果。

- **白布沾上衣服原本的颜色：** 立即停止加水和摩擦，保留衣物标签与产品记录，向制造商或专业护理咨询。
- **饮料含奶油或油层：** 停止当作纯水性饮料处理；按已知油脂污渍流程和标签重新判断。
- **越擦越大：** 改为轻按、不断换布面；不要用力刷来证明污渍存在。
- **洗完还有影子：** 暂缓热烘与熨烫，先判断是真污渍还是染色/面料变化，再按适用方法处理或停下。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理普通、已知、非油性的日常饮料和可水洗衣物；不同饮料的染料与面料固色不同，没有一个通用去渍温度或成功保证。握力或视力受限时可在稳定盆上操作、用大字标签和可信现场协助；无法安全控水时停止背面冲洗。局部处理会产生湿布和待晾的衣物，并可能需要再洗一次。

## English

Use this Skill when you have **just spilled a known ordinary non-oily drink** on clothing approved for home water washing and can read both garment and detergent labels. Treat the spot before an ordinary wash. Cream, oil, paint, bodily fluids, corrosive liquids, and marks of unknown origin need another route; stop first if safety is uncertain. The goal is to reduce the mark without spreading it, not to promise one-wash removal.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the drink and spill time, whether the garment allows water washing, its temperature limit, colour, print and trim warnings, and the laundry product's pretreatment directions. Have a clean white cloth, controlled water, and a place for runoff. Test the product in a hidden area as directed. Stop if garment dye transfers, fibres roughen, or a coating changes. Valuable, complex, or professional-care-only clothing needs professional advice.

### Execution

1. **Blot the surface first.** Press a clean white cloth lightly from the edge toward the centre to pick up loose drink. Use a fresh part of the cloth as needed; do not scrub across the fabric. If the liquid could be hazardous, stop contact and leave ordinary laundry.
2. **Use water only if the label permits it.** Face the back of the stain into a controllable water flow so some liquid moves out through the original entry side. If you cannot reach the back safely or the trim cannot take water, stop and check the maker's directions. Avoid spraying other items and do not exceed the garment's water-temperature limit.
3. **Pretreat under one product's directions.** If both garment and laundry product allow it and a hidden test shows no change, apply a suitable liquid laundry detergent or prewash product as directed. Its contact time follows product and fabric limits, not an invented hour-long soak. Do not layer bleach, ammonia, or other cleaners onto it.
4. **Launder by the garment's method.** Send it to the machine or hand-wash route its label allows, with settings and dose from the actual washer and detergent instructions. One spot does not redefine the whole load's program, and extra detergent is not guaranteed to help.
5. **Check before drying.** Inspect the same spot in good light. If a visible mark remains, do not tumble-dry or iron it yet; reassess under the residual-stain workflow. If dye, print, or surface texture has changed, stop stain treatment and record it. The change may no longer be removable soil.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Loose drink was blotted or moved with allowed water, the product was compatible, the garment followed its care wash, and you checked before heat drying. Stopping when treatment could damage the garment or conditions are unknown is complete.

- **The white cloth picks up the garment's original dye:** Stop water and rubbing immediately; keep garment and product details for the maker or professional cleaner.
- **The drink contained cream or an oil layer:** Stop treating it as a purely watery drink. Reassess as a known grease stain under the labels.
- **The mark grows as you rub:** Blot with clean sections of cloth instead of brushing harder.
- **A shadow remains after washing:** Hold heat drying and ironing; decide whether it is residue or a dye/fabric change, then treat by a supported route or stop.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers everyday known non-oily drinks on ordinary washable garments. Drink dyes and fabric colourfastness differ, so there is no universal stain temperature or success guarantee. Work over a stable basin, enlarge labels, or use trusted in-person assistance for grip or vision limits. Stop back-flushing if you cannot control water safely. You will have wet cloths and clothing to dry and may need another wash.
