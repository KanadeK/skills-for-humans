## 中文

当你已有**获准、排水可用的小庭院堆肥桶**，想从空桶开始建一堆普通植物性材料时，加载这份 Skill。结果是干湿料均有、不过湿、表层覆盖的首堆，而不是立刻得到熟肥。H080 的厨房垃圾收尾不会替你建堆。本篇排除肉鱼、骨、奶脂、宠物粪、患病植株、处理木材、亮面纸及当地不接受的“可堆肥”塑料/袋。

### 准备与输入

先核场地和本地收集/堆肥规则允许所选材料。准备足量干落叶、未上蜡无胶无彩的纸板/纸或未处理小枝等“干料”，少量果蔬边角、咖啡渣等获准“湿料”，以及清水、手套和可安全取放的钝工具。EPA 用按体积约 2–3 份干料对 1 份湿料解释一个庭院起点，但实际材料和气候不同，不拿它当硬性法律或保证。

### 执行

1. **检查空桶和投料资格。** 底部可排水、盖能合，动物不易直接取料。逐项剔除贴纸、塑胶、胶带、肉奶油脂、宠物废物、病株和不明受化学处理材料；无法确认就不投入。
2. **用粗干料铺底。** 放一层能留空气缝的普通小枝或干叶，别把底压成密不透气的湿纸饼。再把少量合格湿料与更多干料分层/混合，较大植物块可手动安全分小，不用粉碎机。
3. **调到微湿并盖住食物。** 材料太干可少量补水，目标接近拧过的海绵而非滴水；把果蔬边角埋入并在最上层盖足干料，减少动物接触。盖好桶，但保留桶原有通气设计，不用塑料膜死封。
4. **留下下一次检查。** 记录开堆日期、投入类别和当前湿度，设置几天后查看气味、水分与盖的入口。手套脱下后洗手，工具按材料清洁。写“已建堆，待分解”，不称成熟肥。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

获准容器内有通气底层、合格干湿料、适宜水分和覆盖的食物，盖/通气正常；一堆果皮裸露在桶顶不算安全起堆。

- **缺干料：** 暂不倒入湿厨余，先补足合格干料或用当地收集服务。
- **发现不合格材料：** 在可安全处理时取走并按当地规则处置，不靠厚盖掩埋肉奶/宠物废物。
- **底部积水或料一捏滴水：** 先加干料并查排水，停止继续加湿料。
- **动物能碰到表面食物：** 停止投料，补干料与合用盖/屏障；无法控制则转当地收集。
- **体力或感官不便：** 小批投料、轻工具、可信协助观察气味和湿度；由你确认可管理范围。

### 假设与边界

庭院堆肥需要时间，外观或自热都不证明灭菌。EPA 原始材料表与当地接收清单可能不同；本篇采用两者都允许的普通材料，不含虫箱或食用作物安全承诺。

## English

Load this Skill when you already have a **permitted, draining small backyard compost bin** and want to make its first ordinary plant-material pile. Finish with both brown and green material, controlled dampness, and a covered top, not finished compost. H080's kitchen waste close does not start this pile. Exclude meat, fish, bones, dairy, fats, pet waste, diseased plants, treated wood, glossy paper, and locally unaccepted compostable plastics or bags.

### Preparation and inputs

Check site and local compost rules for each material. Have enough dry leaves, plain unwaxed unglued uncoloured paper/cardboard or small untreated twigs as browns, and a little permitted fruit/vegetable scrap or coffee grounds as greens, plus water, gloves, and a safe blunt tool. EPA gives roughly two to three volumes of browns per volume of greens as a backyard starting guide; materials and climate vary, and this is neither a legal rule nor a guarantee.

### Execution

1. **Check bin and eligibility.** Base drains, lid closes, and animals cannot readily reach contents. Remove stickers, plastic, tape, meat, dairy, fats, pet waste, diseased material, and uncertain chemical-treated material. Leave anything unconfirmed out.
2. **Lay a bulky brown base.** Put in small ordinary twigs or dry leaves with air spaces rather than pressing a wet paper mat against the floor. Add a small amount of suitable greens with more browns in layers or mixed. Break large plant pieces safely by hand if useful, without powered shredders.
3. **Dampen and cover food.** Add a little water only if too dry, aiming for wrung-out-sponge damp rather than drips. Bury fruit and vegetable scraps and cover the top with ample dry browns to reduce animal access. Close the lid but preserve the bin's designed airflow instead of sealing it in plastic film.
4. **Leave a check point.** Note start date, material types, and current dampness, with a later check for odor, water, and lid access. Remove gloves, wash hands, and clean tools as appropriate. Label “pile started, decomposing,” not finished compost.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The permitted bin has an airy base, eligible browns and greens, appropriate dampness, covered food, and working lid/airflow. A heap of exposed peels on top is not a sound start.

- **Not enough browns:** Delay kitchen scraps until suitable dry material is available or use local collection.
- **An ineligible item appears:** Remove safely and dispose under local rules, not just hide meat, dairy, or pet waste under leaves.
- **Base pools water or material drips when squeezed:** Add browns, check drainage, and stop adding wet feed.
- **Animals reach exposed food:** Stop feeding, add cover and a suitable lid/barrier; use local collection if access cannot be controlled.
- **Stamina or senses limit work:** Add in small batches, use light tools or trusted help for smell and moisture checks, while you decide manageable scope.

### Assumptions and limits

Backyard compost takes time; looks and warmth cannot prove sanitation. EPA ingredient lists and local collection lists may differ. This uses ordinary material permitted by both, not worms or a food-crop safety promise.
