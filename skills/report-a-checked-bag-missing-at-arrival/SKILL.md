## 中文

你已抵达本次托运行李应交给你的机场，按显示的提取带等候后仍不见箱子时，用这份 Skill核对是否找对提取点，向**实际负责的航司行李服务**报延误并保存案件编号。完成是航司接受报告、你的行李标签与联系方式录入正确且知道从哪里追踪；不是箱子已找回，也不保证赔付。箱子到手但破损或少内件，走另一份损伤/少件记录流程。

### 准备与输入

保留所有航段/实际运营航司、抵达机场和航班号、托运时给你的行李标签/收据、行李外观描述，以及能安全接收行李的联系方式。看机场官方行李提取屏/航司指示，确认这班的转盘或特殊行李提取点；不要因看到相似箱子就带走，也不要从别人行李牌上找自己的编号。若中转时标签目的地并非这里，先核托运凭证和航司答复，不把计划中的终点等同实际行李交付点。

### 执行

1. **先排除找错提取位置。** 核对到达航班及其官方转盘状态、行李是否仍在卸载、超大件/特殊件是否在另处领取。若航司已发“延误行李”通知，读通知中的真正案件编号与需补资料；通知不等于已核对你所有联系信息。
2. **通过该航司的正式入口报障。** 依机场现场指示找行李服务柜台、官方扫码入口或航司网站，最好在离开机场前报告；如果现场无法办理，用运营方公布的离场后渠道和真实期限。提供行李标签号、航班/票务参考、箱子颜色/大小/特征、可联系及送达的地址或取件方式。个人地址只输给确认的正式渠道，不贴在机场公共屏或社交群。
3. **把两种编号分开记。** 留下托运行李标签/收据原件，另记航司新建的延误案件编号、受理时间、服务渠道和下一次追踪入口。向人员读回标签号、行李描述、联系电话/地址是否准确；“查不到箱子”与“没有给你建报告”是两件事。
4. **按案件追踪并更新。** 在航司官方行李追踪系统查状态，联系方式/所在地点变化时按其规则更新。若有个人追踪器，其位置链接仅在该航司正式支持且你愿意时分享；它不是航空公司已找到行李或允许你自行进入后台寻找的证据。必要物品临时缺少时向航司问本票可用的正式帮助，费用与赔付不在本文预设。

### 成功条件

你确认了实际提取点，正式提交延误行李报告并获得可追踪案件编号，标签号、箱貌和联系/送达信息得到读回；或者发现箱子仍在正确的另一个官方提取位置。只问一句“箱子在哪里”而没有报告或编号，不足以完成报障。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一直在错误转盘等：** 看本次到达航班的机场显示与特殊件指示，再决定是否报延误。
- **只有行李条编号，没案件编号：** 向航司正式行李服务申请受理并留新编号，两者用途不同。
- **出了机场才发现未报：** 立即查该航司当期离场后报告入口和时限，如实说明，不能套用别家期限。
- **联程由多家航司执飞：** 以实际行李标签和本票航司/机场行李服务规则确认谁接报告，别在不同客服间无编号地转圈。
- **收到相似箱子但标签不是你的：** 不打开或带走他人行李，交机场/航司人员，继续报自己的箱子。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一件普通托运行李延误/未出现在应提取机场的服务报告，不解释行李责任、海关手续、保险或法定赔偿。Delta、American、British Airways 的报告入口和期限各异，必须以实际运营航司及机场当期流程为准。无手机可找行李柜台或官方电话；语言、听觉、移动障碍可要求航司可及服务并保留纸面信息。案件建立后仍可能要等待寻找与配送。

## English

Use this Skill when you have reached the airport where your checked bag is meant to be handed over, waited at the displayed claim, and it has not appeared. Confirm the correct reclaim point, file a delay report with the **responsible operating airline baggage service**, and keep its case number. Finish when the airline has accepted a report with correct tag and contact details and you know where to track it, not when the bag is recovered or compensation paid. A bag that arrives damaged or short of contents has a different reporting path.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep all legs/operating airlines, arrival airport and flight, the checked-bag tag/receipt from first handover, a visual bag description, and contact details where you can actually receive it. Use official airport screens and airline signs for this flight's carousel and any oversized/special-item point. Do not take a lookalike case or search another passenger's tag. If the printed bag-tag destination is not this airport, check the receipt and airline response before treating your planned final city as the bag's current handover point.

### Execution

1. **Rule out the wrong claim point.** Check this arrival's official carousel/status, whether unloading continues, and any separate special-bag collection. If the airline sent a delayed-bag notice, read its real case number and required updates; a notification is not proof all your delivery details are correct.
2. **Report through the airline's official route.** Use the airport baggage service desk, an official scan option, or airline site as directed, preferably before leaving. If that is not possible, use its published post-airport channel and actual deadline. Give bag-tag number, flight/booking reference, colour/size/distinguishing marks, and a usable contact/delivery or collection route. Put private addresses only into verified official channels, never on a public airport screen or social post.
3. **Keep the two references distinct.** Retain the original bag tag/receipt and separately record the new delayed-bag case ID, acceptance time, service channel, and status route. Read back tag, bag description, phone, and address with staff. “Bag not found” and “no report was opened” are different states.
4. **Track and update the case.** Use the airline's official baggage portal, updating location/contact under its rules. Share a personal tracker link only if the airline supports it and you choose to; a tracker point is not proof of airline recovery or permission to enter staff areas. For essential items temporarily absent, ask the airline about actual available help without assuming a payment result here.

### Success

You confirmed the real claim point, filed a formal delayed-bag report, got a traceable case ID, and read back tag, appearance, contact, and handover information. Finding the bag at another officially designated claim point also resolves the apparent absence. An unrecorded “where is my bag?” question is incomplete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Waiting at the wrong carousel:** Recheck the airport's display for this arrival and special-bag signs before reporting a delay.
- **Bag tag exists but no case number:** Ask official baggage service to open a case and give a new reference; they serve different purposes.
- **Already left without reporting:** Promptly check this airline's current post-airport route and deadline and explain facts honestly; another airline's window does not apply.
- **Several airlines on the itinerary:** Use actual tag and this ticket's airline/airport process to identify the responsible reporting desk rather than bouncing among support without a case.
- **A lookalike bag has another tag:** Leave it unopened with airport/airline staff and continue the report for yours.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary checked-bag delay/missing-at-claim report, not liability, customs, insurance, or statutory compensation advice. Delta, American, and British Airways differ on routes and deadlines; use this actual carrier and airport. Without a smartphone, use the baggage desk or official phone. Ask for accessible language, hearing, or mobility support and keep a paper reference. The case may still require waiting for tracing and delivery.
