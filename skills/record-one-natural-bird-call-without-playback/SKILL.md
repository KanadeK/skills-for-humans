## 中文

当你在安全获准地点**自然听到野鸟叫声**，想留下声音证据而不引诱它回应时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一段带时间/粗地点的可回放录音，或有节奏高低描述的手写笔记；不要求当场认种。它不同于目视外形记录，也不能把 Merlin 等软件自动建议当确认。**不播放鸟叫引诱、不靠近鸟巢、不为收音离开安全路径。**

### 准备与输入

站稳、避道路和私人谈话区，打开现有手机的本地录音工具或纸笔；不必下载应用、注册账号或购买麦克风。检查电量/空间与当地录音、隐私和保护地规则。若目标声来自巢、鸟警戒或人群隐私不能避开，就不录，退远或只作模糊个人笔记。

### 执行

1. **先安静听并找方向。** 不模仿、不播放音频，不让鸟因你靠近而改变叫声；写下大致方位、声是短促/连续、高低或重复模式。听不到稳定声也不用追进灌木。
2. **可安全时短录。** 手机麦克风朝向声源、身体保持静止，不跨道路追逐；让录音含几次自然发声与一点背景。手机若会记录私人对话，改位置或停录。无法录时写节奏描述和时间即可。
3. **核保存与元信息。** 停止录音、回放一点确认文件真的保存且目标声可听，标日期、粗地点、生境、环境噪声与是否同时看见鸟；不公开准确巢位。录音失败就标“仅文字”，不把空文件当证据。
4. **身份另作审慎判断。** 以后可与机构示例声及本地季节比较，但软件声音 ID 只是候选，需独立复核；未见鸟/音质差就保持“未知鸟声”，不要直接写进公开清单为确定物种。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

有一段真实自然声文件或可读的节奏/高低文字，连同时间和不敏感地点与不确定性；鸟未受诱叫惊扰。只是手机显示了一个自动物种名却没保存声/观察，不算完成。

- **录音背景车声太大：** 标限制，改为文字或下次在安全处自然等待，不追鸟。
- **手机没存文件/没权限：** 检查本地保存或只写笔记，不上传空证据。
- **应用建议多个种或误认：** 保留候选而不报告确种，查听到的实际模式。
- **鸟显示警戒/巢附近：** 立刻停录并退远。
- **听力/行动受限：** 可访问波形、可信协助描述，仍保留谁听/谁记录的来源；不能安全到场就不强行参与。

### 假设与边界

野鸟声音因环境和个体变化，短录音不保证可识别。Cornell 的声音识别帮助学习但明确需人工复核；录音私用不自动授权发布他人对话或敏感野生动物位置。

## English

Load this Skill when you **naturally hear a wild bird from a safe permitted place** and want sound evidence without drawing a response. Finish with a short saved recording tagged by time and coarse place, or a written rhythm/pitch description; no on-the-spot species ID is required. This differs from visual field marks and Merlin-like automatic suggestions are not confirmation. **Do not play bird sounds to lure, approach a nest, or leave safe paths to record.**

### Preparation and inputs

Stand still away from roads and private conversations. Use an existing phone's local recorder or paper notes; no download, account, or microphone purchase is needed. Check battery/storage and site rules for recording, privacy, and protected habitat. Near a nest, alarmed bird, or unavoidable private speech, do not record; retreat or keep only a vague personal note.

### Execution

1. **Listen quietly and locate broadly.** Do not mimic or play sounds and avoid approaching until the bird changes behaviour. Note coarse direction and whether the call is short, continuous, high/low, or repeated. A fading call is not a reason to chase into brush.
2. **Record briefly if safe.** Point phone mic toward sound and keep still without crossing a road. Include a few natural phrases and background context. Change position or stop if private talk would be captured. Without recording, write rhythm and time instead.
3. **Check storage and metadata.** Stop, replay a little to prove file is saved and target audible, then note date, coarse location, habitat, noise, and whether the caller was seen. Do not publish exact nest sites. Mark failed audio “written only” rather than cite an empty file.
4. **Keep identity separate.** Later compare actual sound with institutional examples and local season. App Sound ID is a candidate requiring independent verification. Without sight or good audio, keep “unknown bird sound,” not a confident species on a public checklist.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A genuine natural sound file or readable rhythm/pitch note has time, non-sensitive location, and uncertainty, without playback disturbance. A displayed automatic name with no saved sound or observation is not completed evidence.

- **Traffic overwhelms audio:** Record the limitation, switch to notes, or wait naturally at a safe place another time.
- **Phone fails to save or lacks permission:** Check local save or make written notes without uploading empty evidence.
- **App suggests several or a wrong species:** Keep candidates, compare the actual heard pattern, do not publish a forced ID.
- **Bird alarms or a nest is nearby:** Stop and retreat.
- **Hearing or mobility is limited:** Accessible waveforms and trusted description can help if observer and recorder are credited; do not force an unsafe visit.

### Assumptions and limits

Bird calls vary with setting and individual, and a short recording may not identify them. Cornell's sound tools aid learning but demand independent review. Private recording does not authorize publishing other people's speech or sensitive wildlife sites.
