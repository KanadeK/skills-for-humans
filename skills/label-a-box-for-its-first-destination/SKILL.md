## 中文

当一个普通搬家箱已经装好、即将交给搬运者，而接收者需要**不拆箱就知道它先送到哪儿**时加载。你要给这个实际箱件一个唯一标识和第一站，并在私人清单中留一条对应记录。这里不决定物品是否搬、如何装箱、谁能安全提举，也不把你写的标签当作承运方的正式货物清单。

### 准备与输入

拿到已确认的第一站区域及收件人能认出的名称，例如“新住处卧室 A 门边”，而不只是“新家”。核对箱内最终物品组、会接收这只箱的人、可用的纸笔或标签，以及实际搬运方对标识的要求。细节有隐私时，外侧只写足够分送的信息；完整内容记在由有权接收者保管的清单里。

### 执行

1. **给实际箱件一个不重复的号。** 在这次搬迁的清单中查重，再给箱子一个短 ID，例如 `B01`。它只标识这个箱，不把同一号码复用给两个箱。若已存在搬运方编号，先问对方怎样并列记录，别覆盖其标签。
2. **写出第一站。** 在箱外写同一个 ID 和接收者能辨认的目标区域。颜色可辅助分组，但还要有文字、可读符号或双方约定的可访问标记；只凭颜色，接收者可能连猜的入口都没有。
3. **做一条对应清单行。** 记 `箱 ID → 第一站 → 经授权的人可见的内容摘要 → 待确认项`。若某物的第一站还没有人确认，就把这箱标为“待分送”，不要编一个房间。你的清单不是承运方正式清单；两者各按实际要求核对。
4. **站在收件者的位置读一次。** 不翻箱内物品，只看箱外标签，能否找到清单中的唯一一行并指出第一站？标签不要被封箱胶带、堆叠方向或旧地址遮掉；旧标签与新标签冲突时，先去掉或清楚作废旧信息。
5. **交接信息。** 将接收者需要的 ID 与目的地表用双方能读的方式交给有权接收的人。公开搬运者只需第一站，不自动获得箱内私人明细。之后目的地改变时，同步修改箱外和清单，并通知实际接收者。

### 完成与停止

只看箱外的接收者能找到唯一清单行并说出正确第一站；受保护的内容没有被公开写在箱上。ID 重复、目的地无人确认、标签不可读或旧新标识冲突时，这箱停在待分送，不让猜测进入下一站。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两箱都叫 `B01`：** 停止交出，给其中一箱换新号，同时改清单；不能只在心里记哪个是“真的 B01”。
- **箱内最后多放了一件：** 更新有权者可见的清单行；若第一站变了，箱外也要一起改。
- **旧标签仍很醒目：** 明确作废旧地址/旧号码，再让收件者读回新标签。
- **标签掉落或字迹模糊：** 对照由装箱者确认的内容重新贴；不能确认身份时先放待分送，不凭相似箱型认领。
- **收件者看不清字：** 共同选用大字、高对比、语音、触觉或可信的人读回等方式；只增加颜色不一定有用。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通低风险搬迁和已获同意的接收者。不同服务方的标识、正式清单与特殊物品规则不同，按实际服务方执行；易碎字样不保证安全搬运。你可以坐着按照片和装箱者的确认制作清单，不必为贴标签搬动重箱。副作用是每只箱多一条可核对的名字，神秘箱数量下降。

## English

Load this Skill after one ordinary moving box is packed and before handoff, when a receiver needs to know **its first destination without opening it**. You will give that physical box a unique ID and first stop, with one matching entry on a list you control. This does not decide what moves, how to pack, or who can safely lift it. Your label is not the carrier's formal inventory.

### Preparation and inputs

Know the confirmed first-stop area in terms your receiver understands, such as “by bedroom A's door” rather than just “new home.” Check the final contents group, who will receive this box, your paper or label, and the actual provider's marking requirements. Keep private content details on a list held by an authorised receiver; put only enough routing information on the outside.

### Execution

1. **Give the physical box one unused ID.** Check your list before assigning a short identifier such as `B01`. Do not reuse it for another box in this move. If a mover has its own identifier, ask how to record both; do not cover its label.
2. **Write the first stop.** Put the same ID and a recognisable destination area on the box. Colour may help, but include text, a readable symbol or another accessible mark agreed with the receiver. A colour-only code can leave a person with no way to decode the route.
3. **Make one matching list row.** Record `box ID → first stop → contents summary visible only to authorised people → open questions`. If nobody has confirmed the destination, mark the box “hold for routing” rather than inventing a room. Your personal list and a carrier's formal inventory must each be checked under their own actual requirements.
4. **Read it as a receiver would.** Without opening the box, can you find exactly one list row and point to its first stop? Keep the label visible beyond sealing tape, stacking and old address marks. Remove or clearly cancel an old label that contradicts the new one.
5. **Hand over the routing information.** Give an authorised receiver the ID and destination list in a format they can use. A public handler needs the first stop, not automatic access to private contents. If the destination changes later, change both box and list, then tell the actual receiver.

### Success and stop

Using the outside alone, your receiver can find one matching list row and name the correct first stop; private detail is not posted publicly. Hold this box for routing if its ID is duplicated, destination unconfirmed, label unreadable, or old and new markings conflict. Do not turn a guess into the next stop.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Two boxes both say `B01`:** Stop handoff, renumber one and correct the list. A mental note about the “real B01” will not travel with both boxes.
- **One more item went in at the end:** Update the authorised list row. Change the outside too if the first stop changed.
- **The old label still stands out:** Clearly cancel its address or ID, then have the receiver read the current one back.
- **The label falls off or blurs:** Re-create it against contents confirmed by the packer. If identity cannot be confirmed, hold the box rather than identifying it by its shape.
- **The receiver cannot read the print:** Agree on large high-contrast print, audio, tactile marking or a trusted readback. More colour alone may not help.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers an ordinary low-risk move with an authorised receiver. Provider labelling, formal inventory and special-goods rules differ; follow the actual service. Writing “fragile” does not guarantee safe handling. You can make the list while seated from photographs and the packer's confirmation; labelling does not require you to lift a heavy box. The side effect is one checkable name per carton and fewer mystery boxes.
