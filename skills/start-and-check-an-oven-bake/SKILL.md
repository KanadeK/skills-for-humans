## 中文

当你已[匹配烤箱模式与层位](../match-oven-mode-and-rack-to-a-recipe/SKILL.md)、[留出烤盘热风通路](../space-bakeware-for-oven-airflow/SKILL.md)，准备把普通食物送入按配方要求预热的烤箱时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**从正确事件开始计时，在配方规定的检查点观察并保持烤箱可控**；它不保证食物在某个统一分钟数自动完成。

### 准备与输入

读出配方的模式、温度、预热要求、烤盘层位、开始计时事件、何时检查/转盘、怎样判完成。确认这台烤箱的预热提示在确切机型说明里意味着什么，食物和适用容器已经备好；若有独立烤箱温度计，按其说明使用而不把它当设备维修。准备可访问计时器、干燥隔热工具和近处耐热落点，能在必要时安全关热。

### 执行步骤

1. **确定预热条件已满足。** 配方要求预热时，等烤箱在对应模式下真正进入说明所称的就绪状态；不要把开机时间直接算作食物烘烤时间。配方若明确从冷箱开始，则按该配方执行，不强迫它预热。
2. **稳妥放入烤盘并关门。** 使用适用隔热工具，身体避开开门热气，把盘放在确认过的架位，不碰壁、门或热元件；门能正常关严。难以拿稳时先停热求助或改轻盘/小批，不在热箱门口试握力。
3. **按配方指定的事件启动计时。** 多数普通预热烘烤从食物入炉/关门时开始，但具体配方可能不同；记录真实起点。若同时两盘或分批，每一批记录自己的入炉时间，空炉预热和上一批时长不借给下一批。
4. **按约定检查，不频繁窥探。** 可用炉灯/观察窗或配方指定的时点查看颜色和状态；开门会改变炉内热状态，有些脆弱食物过早移动会塌陷。只在配方、机型或实际异常需要时用隔热工具开门/转盘，并记录那次改变。
5. **出现偏差先定位，再决定下一步。** 配方检查点到时若上色不均，可按[不均匀上色的处理](../respond-to-uneven-oven-browning/SKILL.md)核对摆放与可靠调整；若烟、火、燃气异味或设备异常，关热并退出日常流程。到达预计终点时转入食物特定的成品判断，而不是因铃响直接上桌。

### 完成与停止

食物已按正确预热/入炉事件计时，关键检查点与任何开门/转盘有记录，烤箱仍可安全运行，就完成过程控制。预热状态无法确认、门关不上、烤盘不稳、你无法安全看守或记录起点时先暂停；食物是否完成必须另按配方和食品安全要求检查。

### 常见报错与补救

- **从按下预热键就给食物计时：** 分清空炉与食物实际入炉的时间；已失去起点时按可靠配方/食物结果重新判断，不编出精确分钟数。
- **烤盘入炉后门关不严：** 停热，安全时取出改盘/架位；不能靠加时间补门缝。
- **每隔几分钟都开门：** 回到配方检查点；若已多次中断，不把原预计时间当绝对结束证据。
- **两盘进入时间不同却共享铃声：** 分别记下时间与完成检查，不让早入炉的盘替后入炉的一盘报到。
- **烤箱警示或温度明显异常：** 关热并按机型说明或专业支持处理，不自行修改温控器。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这份 Skill 只跟踪普通烘烤过程，模式、预热显示和开门响应依机型与配方变化。烤箱温度可能有热区，不能由一个显示数字推断每个角落完全一致。视觉、听力、站立或握持不便时可用炉灯、大字/震动计时、轻烤盘和现场可信协助；没有安全开门取盘路径就改变批次或方法。副作用是你要记录入炉时间，换来计时器不再为一只空烤箱加班。

## English

Load this Skill after [matching oven mode and rack](../match-oven-mode-and-rack-to-a-recipe/SKILL.md) and [leaving airflow around pans](../space-bakeware-for-oven-airflow/SKILL.md), when you are ready to load ordinary food into an oven preheated as the recipe requires. **Start timing at the right event, observe at recipe checkpoints, and keep the oven under control.** No universal minute count is a completion guarantee.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the mode, temperature, preheat requirement, rack, timer-start event, check or rotation point, and finish test in the recipe. Confirm what the preheat signal means in this exact oven manual. Have food and approved bakeware ready. An independent oven thermometer may help under its instructions but is not equipment repair. Have an accessible timer, dry heat protection, a nearby heat-safe landing place, and a safe way to switch off heat.

### Execution steps

1. **Establish any required preheat.** If the recipe requires it, wait until the oven reaches the ready state the manual actually describes in the matching mode. The empty oven's startup time is not food baking time. If the reliable recipe explicitly begins in a cold oven, follow that route instead.
2. **Load the pan securely and close the door.** Use suitable heat protection and keep clear of the rush of hot air. Place the pan on its checked rack without touching wall, door, or element. The door must close normally. If you cannot hold the pan steadily, stop heat for help or use lighter approved bakeware or a smaller batch rather than testing grip in a hot doorway.
3. **Start at the recipe's event.** Many ordinary preheated bakes count from food loading or closing the door, but the actual recipe governs. Record the real start. With two pans or separate batches, give each its own entry time. Empty-oven preheat and an earlier batch cannot donate minutes to a later one.
4. **Check by plan, not constant peeking.** Use the oven light or window and the recipe's checkpoint when possible. Opening changes oven heat, and some fragile food can collapse if moved early. Open or rotate with protection only when recipe, model, or a real abnormality calls for it, and record the change.
5. **Localise a deviation before responding.** Uneven colour at a checkpoint can use [Respond to Uneven Oven Browning](../respond-to-uneven-oven-browning/SKILL.md) to check layout and supported adjustments. Smoke, fire, gas odour, or equipment faults stop the everyday flow. At the expected endpoint, use a food-specific finish check rather than serving on the bell alone.

### Success and stop

Food was timed from its correct preheat and loading event, checks and any door or pan change are recorded, and the oven remains safe to run. Pause when preheat cannot be confirmed, the door will not close, a pan is unstable, you cannot attend, or the timer start is lost. Food completion still requires recipe and food-safety evidence.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Timing food from pressing the preheat button:** Separate empty-oven time from real food exposure. When the start is lost, return to reliable recipe and food checks rather than inventing exact minutes.
- **The door stays ajar around a pan:** Stop heat and change pan or rack when safe. Extra time does not close a door gap.
- **You open every few minutes:** Return to recipe checkpoints. Repeated interruptions make the original expected time weaker as completion evidence.
- **Two pans enter at different times but share one bell:** Note separate starts and endpoints; the earlier pan cannot check in for the later one.
- **The oven warns or heat is clearly abnormal:** Turn it off and use this model's directions or qualified support, not a home thermostat repair.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This tracks an ordinary bake. Modes, preheat displays, and door response vary by oven and recipe. Hot spots mean one displayed temperature is not proof of identical heat everywhere. For visual, hearing, standing, or grip limits, use the oven light, a large or vibrating timer, lighter pans, or in-person help. Change the batch or method when loading and removal cannot be safe. You may write down the food-entry time so the timer no longer works overtime for an empty oven.
