## 中文

当你在普通公交上发现计划的下车站已经过去，或因临时不停靠而被带到后面的站，甚至已在后面的站下车时加载。目标是**在允许的下一站安全下车（若仍在车上）、核对实际位置，然后选出可走、可乘或可暂停的一条真实路径**。不要要求司机在非站点停车、突然开门或倒车。原定下车程序、常规换乘和“因此要迟到”的通知分别由其他流程处理。

### 准备与输入

记下原计划站名、车上显示或播报的下一站、当前线路与方向。你需要运营方当前站序/实时公告、可用步行路线或反向班次信息；没有手机时看车内/站牌图、询问司机（不干扰驾驶）或站务人员。还要考虑你实际的行动能力、天气、照明、道路过街条件和继续乘车的费用；这些不能由“只多坐了一站”自动推断。

### 执行

1. **先稳住车内动作；已下车就跳到第 2 步。** 仍在车上时，用本车规定的按钮、拉绳或其他方式请求下一处可停站；有些运营方需要提前示意。抓好扶手或坐稳，等车完全停妥再起身和下车。若车辆改道或下一站无法停，听实际通知或在安全时向司机问最近可下车处，不要求中途放人。
2. **以实际站点重置地图。** 下车后在安全位置读站牌名称/编号、线路方向和附近正式信息，对照原目的站；分清“仅多一站”“已转到支线/环线”“原站临时取消”。不要因为手机地图的定位点漂移就在路边盲走。没有网络时先把站牌和能询问的正式渠道作为起点。
3. **比较三种真实路径。** A：若有安全、可及的步行路线，查看正式道路或可靠地图信息，确认过街点、距离及个人能否走；B：查运营方当前反向或其他可到达的公交，确认上车站、方向、下一班与票务条件；C：两者都不明或不可行时留在安全位置，联系运营方工作人员或可信接应人，改变目的地或等待更合适的服务。票价、换乘有效性与末班车以这次运营方规则为准。
4. **选并写下一步。** 记“我现在在__站；要到__；选__；下一动作是__；若__发生就暂停/重选”。要乘回去时按新的站序核对车与方向；要步行时只走你已确认的合法可达路线。若行程因此影响约定，另按迟到通知流程告知相关人，不把赶时间当作危险过街的理由。
5. **到第一个核对点再验证。** 抵达预定过街点、反向站位或接应位置时核对它确实存在且可用；若发现站点关闭、道路不安全、班次已过或费用条件不同，停下重选，不继续执行一张已经失效的路线。

### 完成与停止

你已经在安全位置确认实际站点，选出有根据的返回/替代方式与第一个核对点，并知道什么条件下要停下或求助，本轮恢复决策完成。真正到达目的地还需执行所选路线；不能把“决定往回走”写成“已到”。若没有可核验的安全过街或可行班次，就先暂停并使用现场正式支持或可信接应，而不是沿车流追车。

### 常见报错与补救

- **按铃太晚或已过目标站：** 申请下一处允许停靠的位置；不反复拍门或要求司机在行车道停车。
- **“下一站就在对面”但道路不好过：** 找合法且个人可用的过街方式；没有就选公交或接应方案，不翻护栏。
- **同号反方向车不确定：** 查现时站序、终点或支线，必要时问停稳车辆的司机；线路号相同不是返回证明。
- **反向车已收班、手机没电或没有网：** 使用站牌、纸图或运营方工作人员；信息仍不足就保留安全位置，联络可信接应，不猜末班时间。
- **以为原车票一定能用：** 查该运营方当期换乘/重新上车规则和实际票证；未经确认不宣称免费。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通城市公交的非紧急失误恢复，不提供逐街导航、交通执法解释或保证抵达。城市站距、道路、夜间照明、路线、无障碍设施与票务差异很大；行动、视觉、听觉或语言不便时，以运营方无障碍信息、车内播报/显示、站务人员和可信陪同确认实际选择。若处境不再是普通可退出出行问题，使用当地适当的即时帮助。副作用是时间与可能的车费增加，安全不因原计划被打乱而降级。

## English

Load this Skill when you realise you have ridden past your planned stop on an ordinary bus, including when a temporary skip has carried you onward or you have already alighted at a later stop. Your result is to **leave at the next permitted safe stop (if still aboard), establish where you really are and choose a verified walk-back, return service or pause**. Do not ask the driver to open doors between stops, reverse or make an unsafe unscheduled stop. Planned alighting, normal transfer and telling someone you will be late are separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the planned stop name, the next stop shown or announced on board, and the current route and direction. You need the operator's current stop sequence and alerts plus usable walking or return-service information. Without a phone, use vehicle and stop maps or ask the driver when it does not interfere with driving, or staffed service. Consider your actual mobility, weather, lighting, road crossing and possible extra fare. “Only one stop too far” settles none of these.

### Execution

1. **Move safely on the vehicle first; skip to step 2 if already off.** If still aboard, request the next stop using this bus's button, cord or other stated method; some operators need advance notice. Stay seated or hold on, and get up or leave only after it stops completely. If a diversion or closure prevents the next stop, follow the real announcement or ask about the nearest permitted exit when safe. Do not ask to be let out mid-road.
2. **Reset your map at the actual stop.** From a safe place after alighting, read the stop name or ID, route direction and nearby official information against your intended stop. Separate “one stop farther” from a branch, loop or temporarily closed intended stop. Do not walk from an uncertain phone location dot. Without data, start from signs and available staffed information.
3. **Compare three feasible paths.** A: for a safe accessible walking route, check a legitimate street or reliable map route, crossing points, distance and your ability to use it. B: check the operator's current opposite-direction or other suitable service, its boarding stop, direction, next departure and fare conditions. C: when neither can be verified or used, stay somewhere safe and ask staff or a trusted person for a pickup or a changed destination. Fares, transfer validity and last service depend on the operator you are using now.
4. **Choose and write the next action.** Note “I am at __ stop; I need __; I choose __; my next action is __; I pause or replan if __.” For a return bus, check its actual sequence and direction again. For walking, use only a confirmed legal, accessible route. If the delay affects an appointment, notify people through the separate late-arrival task. A clock does not make a hazardous crossing acceptable.
5. **Verify the first checkpoint.** At the planned crossing, opposite-direction stop or pickup point, confirm it exists and is usable. If the stop is closed, road unsafe, service gone or fare terms different, stop and replan instead of continuing on a stale route.

### Success and stop

This recovery decision is complete when you know your actual safe location, have chosen a grounded return or alternative with a first checkpoint, and know what would make you stop or get help. Reaching the destination still requires taking that path; “I will walk back” is not “I arrived.” If no safe crossing or feasible service can be verified, pause and use official on-site support or a trusted pickup instead of chasing traffic.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You requested the stop too late or already passed it:** Ask for the next permitted stop, not a door opening in the road.
- **The opposite stop looks close but crossing is unsafe:** Find a legal route you can use or choose transit/pickup; do not climb a barrier.
- **A same-number return bus may not be heading back:** Check current stop sequence, terminus and branch; ask a stopped driver if needed.
- **The return service has ended, phone is flat or data unavailable:** Use posted information, paper maps or operator staff. If that is still insufficient, stay somewhere safe and contact a trusted person rather than guessing a last departure.
- **You assume the original fare covers a return:** Check the operator's current transfer and reboarding rules and your actual fare media; do not promise a free ride.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers an ordinary, non-emergency city bus mistake. It does not provide turn-by-turn street navigation, interpret traffic law or guarantee arrival. Stop spacing, roads, nighttime lighting, route, accessibility and fares vary. If mobility, sight, hearing or language affects your options, use operator accessibility information, on-board announcements or displays, staff or a trusted companion to confirm the real path. If the situation is no longer an ordinary trip you can safely exit, seek appropriate immediate local help. The detour may cost time and money; safety does not become optional because the original plan changed.
