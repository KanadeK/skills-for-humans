## 中文

当你在普通居室里打翻少量、**确知是什么的日常液体**（例如水或普通非危险饮料），它开始向地面、桌面或软质家具扩散时，用这份 Skill **先让人和电器安全、再控制扩散**。完成这里只表示小泼洒不再扩大、通道暂时可管理；随后还要按地板、家具或纺织物材质做专门清洁。热油、沸水、体液、污水、危险清洁剂、化学品、未知液体、广泛浸透和电器进水立即退出本篇。

### 准备与输入

站在干燥可稳住的位置，辨认液体来源、预计量、流向及碰到的地面/家具/地毯；看是否接近插座、插头、延长线、已通电设备或人的行走路线。准备吸水布/纸、能阻止别人进入湿滑区的明显提示或现场说明。无法从安全位置辨认液体、液体仍不断流出、有人受伤或电器接触已发生时先停止普通清洁并求合适专业/紧急帮助。

### 执行

1. **先护住人。** 让旁人绕开湿区，必要时用不会绊倒人的可见提示标出范围；不要跨过滑地去拿清洁用品。滑倒或电气危险不是必须由你先擦完才能报告的事。
2. **看电器边界再决定能否靠近。** 液体若碰到插座、线缆、通电设备或你得站在湿处操作电源，**不要触碰设备、插头或湿处开关**；让人远离并联系有资格的管理者/电工或当地紧急帮助。只有电器和你始终在干燥安全区、且来源可安全扶正时，才止住继续泼出的杯/瓶；这不是电气维修步骤。
3. **限制小泼洒扩散。** 在没有上述停止条件时，从液体扩散边界开始用干净吸水材料轻压、逐步向中心收集，换干净面，不把液体拖向更大的地面或未受影响的布料。对软质表面先吸表面，不强擦进纤维；浸入垫层、墙缝或无法接近处时停并转材料/物业指导。
4. **给每个接触面去向。** 硬地面仍滑的继续按该材料清洁/干燥流程，家具和软垫按标签与各自局部清洁流程；厨房食品接触面另走厨房台面流程。不要拿一瓶万能清洁剂跨表面、混配化学品或把吸水布再用于食物准备区。
5. **确认控制结果并交接。** 可见流动已停止，通道上的游离液体已吸走；未干区域继续标示/隔开直到材质流程确认可用。记录液体实际到达的表面和需要后续清洁、干燥或专业评估的部分。用过的吸水材料按其用途清洗/弃置，处理完普通污物后清洁双手。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你从干燥安全位置限制了小量已知液体的扩散，让旁人避开湿滑区，并为每个实际接触面确定后续材料处理；没有触碰湿的通电设备或宣称“擦过表面”就处理了垫层。安全条件不满足时明确隔离求助也是完整结果。

- **地面还亮着水光，旁人要走过：** 继续阻断通行并吸干/转对应地面流程，不撤提示。
- **饮料渗进沙发缝或地板下：** 停止靠表面用力擦来假装内部已干，查家具/地材说明或求物业/专业人员。
- **液体碰了插线板：** 不站在湿处拔插头或试开机，隔离并找专业电气处理。
- **闻到不明刺激味才发现不是饮料：** 离开普通泼洒流程，避免接触并按产品或当地紧急指导求助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理小范围、已知且非危险的日常液体。不同地板、涂层、纺织物对水和清洁剂的容忍不同；这里不提供跨材质消毒或深层除霉。行动、视觉或搬移困难时先用可见/可听提示隔离，使用可及吸水工具或可信协助；不能安全靠近就不要逞强。吸水后仍可能有一件湿布和一个待清理的接触面。

## English

Use this Skill for a small spill of a **known ordinary household liquid**, such as water or a nonhazardous drink, when it starts moving across a floor, table, or soft furnishing. **Protect people and electrical equipment first, then contain the spread.** Finishing here means the small spill no longer spreads and the route is managed; floors, furniture, and fabrics still need their own material care. Hot oil, boiling water, bodily fluid, sewage, hazardous cleaner, chemical, unknown liquid, broad saturation, or water inside an appliance leave this Skill immediately.

### Preparation and inputs

Stand on a dry stable surface. Identify the source, approximate amount, direction of travel, and floor, furniture, or carpet it reached. Check nearby outlets, plugs, extension leads, energised appliances, and walking routes. Have absorbent cloth or paper and a visible way to warn or keep others out of a wet area. Stop ordinary cleaning and seek appropriate professional or emergency help if you cannot identify the liquid from safety, it keeps flowing, someone is injured, or electrical contact occurred.

### Execution

1. **Protect people first.** Route others around the wet area and visibly mark it where needed without creating another trip hazard. Do not cross a slippery floor for supplies. A fall or electrical danger does not wait for you to finish wiping before it can be reported.
2. **Check the electrical boundary before approaching.** If liquid touched an outlet, cord, powered appliance, or you would have to stand wet to work a switch, **do not touch the equipment, plug, or wet-area controls**. Keep others clear and call a qualified manager or electrician or local emergency help. Only if you and electrical items remain safely dry, and the source can be righted without risk, stop a cup or bottle pouring more. This is not electrical repair.
3. **Contain the small spill.** Without a stop condition, press clean absorbent material at the spreading edge and work toward the centre, changing to a clean part instead of dragging liquid across more floor or unsoiled fabric. On soft material, blot its surface rather than rubbing liquid deeper. Stop for a soaked pad, wall gap, or inaccessible space and use material or property guidance.
4. **Give each contacted surface a route.** A hard floor that remains slippery follows its material cleaning and drying route; furniture and upholstery follow their labels and spot-care routes. Food-contact kitchen worktops have a separate kitchen process. Do not apply one all-purpose cleaner across every material, mix chemicals, or return a spill cloth to food preparation.
5. **Confirm containment and hand off.** Visible flow has stopped and free liquid is off the walking path. Keep any damp patch marked or isolated until its material route says it is usable. Record what the liquid reached and what still needs cleaning, drying, or professional assessment. Wash or discard used absorbents by their use, and clean hands after ordinary soil handling.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

From a dry stable place, you contained a small known spill, kept others from the slippery area, and assigned each contacted material a next action. You did not touch wet energised equipment or claim a wiped surface proves a cushion is dry inside. Isolating and calling for help when conditions fail is a complete result.

- **The floor still shines and someone needs to pass:** Keep the route blocked, absorb more, and use the floor's cleaning process before removing the warning.
- **Drink reached a sofa seam or under flooring:** Stop pushing on the visible surface as if it dried the inside; consult fabric or flooring instructions or property help.
- **Liquid reached a power strip:** Do not stand in wetness to unplug or test it. Isolate and seek qualified electrical help.
- **An irritating unfamiliar odour reveals it was not a drink:** Leave ordinary spill cleanup, avoid contact, and use product or local emergency guidance.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers small, known, nonhazardous household liquid spills only. Flooring, finishes, and fabrics differ in water and product limits. It offers neither cross-material sanitation nor deep mold removal. If movement, vision, or lifting is difficult, isolate with visible or audible warning and use reachable absorbents or trusted help. Do not force approach when unsafe. After containment, a wet cloth and an affected surface may remain.
