## 中文

你的一段本地演示需要让以后查看的自己知道这段在做什么，可以在 OBS 场景里加一句原创短标题。成果是文字在目标分辨率可读、不遮挡应用操作，也不写姓名、地址、账户或虚假成果。OBS 标题是录制画面内容，保存到输出文件后不能靠改源追回已录版本。

### 准备与输入

写不超过一行的说明性标题，核不含敏感项目名或他人品牌。确认场景里可用字体许可及输出画布比例，先挑不会出现关键菜单和弹窗的区域；不要用外部网页或文件动态文字源承载隐私。

### 执行

1. 添加 Text Source，直接输入原创短标题并选择易读字号与颜色。
2. 放到不遮操作的位置，核它在源顺序中位于窗口上方且没有挡住错误提示。
3. 按最终录制尺寸看文字是否完整、对比足够，不在只看放大预览时定稿。
4. 录几秒并重开文件，核标题出现且没有泄露信息；不合就调整后重录。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

真实测试录像里标题清楚且不妨碍演示，内容为本人授权文字。

- **标题不显示：** 核源可见性与堆叠顺序。
- **标题挡弹窗：** 移到不会出现提示的区域或缩短文字。
- **字太小：** 按输出文件尺寸提高字号并重测。

### 假设与边界

只加一句私有演示标题，不提供字幕、品牌授权、视觉设计验收或可及性标准认证。

## English

Use this when a private local demo needs one short self-written label to identify its topic later. Place a readable title at the output resolution without obscuring application actions or including names, addresses, accounts or unsupported claims. An OBS title is baked into that recorded version; changing the source later does not edit a previous file.

### Preparation and inputs

Draft a one-line descriptive title without sensitive project names or borrowed marks. Check that the font is available for this use and choose an area away from menus and dialogs at the output aspect ratio. Avoid external web or file-fed text for this simple private title.

### Execution

1. Add a Text Source, type the original short title directly and choose readable size and color.
2. Position it away from actions, above the window source in stack order, without blocking error messages.
3. Inspect at final recording size for complete words and sufficient contrast rather than judging only enlarged preview.
4. Record and reopen a few seconds, confirming the title appears without private information; adjust and re-record if needed.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The actual test recording shows a clear title that does not obstruct the demo and contains only authorized wording.

- **Title missing:** Check source visibility and stacking.
- **Dialog covered:** Move or shorten the title.
- **Text too small:** Increase type size against actual output and retest.

### Assumptions and limits

This adds one private-demo title, not captions, brand rights, design approval or accessibility certification.
