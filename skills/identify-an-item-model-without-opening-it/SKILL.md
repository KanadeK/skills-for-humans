## 中文

当你手边有一件普通用品，准备找它的准确使用/组装说明，却只有商品名、相似照片或一摞不知版本的纸时加载。你要**从实物或可追溯资料读出厂家与完整型号/货号，明确仍缺的后缀或版本**。这里不拆外壳、不翻重物、不触碰内部电气/燃气/机械部位，也不教维修。

### 准备与输入

把实物放在能安全查看的位置，确认它无需继续运行即可正常查看外侧、可正常打开的门框或原包装标签。厂家的型号、序列号、批号、零件号常是不同信息；只为找说明记录必要的型号/货号和可能的版本/地区后缀，序列号等敏感识别信息不要发到公开渠道。若准确标签位置未知，先查该厂商针对用品类别的官方“找型号”说明，不照搬别家标签位置。

### 执行

1. **从最容易验证的地方找。** 看原包装、订单中的准确货号、用品可见的厂家标签或随附纸质说明封面，记信息来自哪里。名称或外观可帮助缩小范围，但不是最终型号证据。
2. **读全标识而不截尾。** 连同字母、数字、斜线、横杠与变体后缀抄下厂家和完整型号/货号。不要把相似的 `0/O`、`1/I` 自行替换；不确定就拍许可范围内的清晰局部图放大核对，或请可信者读回。
3. **分清不同编号的作用。** 对照标签文字和厂商说明找 `Model`、`Product/Article` 等字段；`Serial`、批次或一个小零件编号不自动等于整件型号。必要时将它们分栏保留给实际客服，不公开分享全部标签。
4. **核对实物与纸面。** 原包装/订单和用品标签一致时可记“已核对”；只查到其中一处或号码不一致时写明冲突，不选看起来像的某一个。赠品、同系列新旧款、地区型号不能靠颜色合并。
5. **确定可继续还是停。** 得到可回溯的完整型号/货号以及未知后缀，交给找说明的下一步。标签在必须拆壳、翻重物或触及危险位置才看得到时暂停，问厂家正规支持渠道；不为了查说明去做维修动作。

### 完成与停止

你有厂家与实物支持的完整型号/货号、来源、必要的后缀及未确认处；这只是身份识别，不表示某份说明已经匹配。只有外观相似、标签有冲突或需要危险接近时保持“型号未确认”，不要使用相邻型号的操作步骤。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只记商品系列名：** 补读实物标签上的完整型号/货号和变体后缀。
- **订单写一个号，标签是另一个：** 保留两者与来源，问供应方或厂商，不用猜谁是新版。
- **把序列号当型号输入搜索：** 回到标签字段和厂家查找说明；序列号只在真实服务方需要时私下提供。
- **标签太小：** 在权限允许下放大局部照片、改善光线或请可信者读回，不去拆外壳。
- **只有难以安全够到的铭牌：** 暂停查实物，先用包装/订单和厂商支持核对，不翻重物。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通用品的只读身份核对。厂家、地区和年代会改变标签位置、编号格式和文件类型；GE 网址只是一个有类别定位器的例子。行动、视力或阅读不便时由获准者查看正常可见标签并读回，你仍能确认写下的字符；不要把完整序列号发到公共讨论。副作用是“长得差不多”正式失去型号资格。

## English

Load this Skill when you have an ordinary item and need its exact user or assembly guide, but only know a product name, a similar picture or a pile of uncertain papers. You will **read the maker and complete model or article identifier from the item or traceable records, with any uncertain suffix explicit**. Do not open the housing, turn over a heavy item, reach electrical, gas or mechanical internals, or perform repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Set the item where normally accessible labels can be viewed safely without keeping it operating. A maker's model, serial, batch and part IDs can mean different things. Record only the model or article number and any revision or regional suffix needed to find a guide; do not post a serial or other sensitive identifier publicly. If you do not know where the label belongs, use the maker's own locator for that product class, not a different brand's placement rule.

### Execution

1. **Start with easy-to-verify sources.** Check original packaging, exact item number on an order, a normally visible maker label or the supplied paper guide's cover, noting where each value came from. Name and appearance narrow a search but do not prove a model.
2. **Transcribe the whole identifier.** Copy letters, digits, slashes, hyphens and variant suffixes with the maker. Do not silently swap similar `0/O` or `1/I`; enlarge a permitted close-up image or ask a trusted readback if unsure.
3. **Separate the number types.** Use label wording and maker guidance to find `Model`, `Product` or `Article`. `Serial`, batch or small-part number is not automatically the whole item's model. Keep other fields in separate columns for actual private support if needed, not a public post of the whole label.
4. **Compare object with paperwork.** Mark it confirmed when package or order and item label agree. When you have only one source or values conflict, record that instead of picking the one that looks familiar. Similar colour, same series and regional variants are not interchangeable evidence.
5. **Continue or stop.** Pass the traceable full model or article ID and remaining unknown suffix to the manual lookup. If the label requires opening a housing, turning over a heavy item or reaching a dangerous location, pause and ask the maker's proper support channel. Looking for a manual is not permission to repair.

### Success and stop

You have an item-supported maker and full model or article ID, its source, relevant suffix and named unknowns. Identity alone does not prove any guide applies. Appearance-only match, conflicting labels or unsafe access remains “model unconfirmed.” Do not follow a neighbouring model's steps.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a family name was copied:** Read the complete identifier and variant suffix from the real item label.
- **Order and label have different values:** Keep both and their sources and ask supplier or maker; do not guess which is newer.
- **A serial is entered as the model:** Check label fields and the maker locator again. Share serial only privately when the actual service needs it.
- **Print is too small:** Enlarge a permitted close-up, improve light or ask a trusted readback. Do not open the housing.
- **The only tag is hard to reach safely:** Use packaging, order and maker support first rather than flipping a heavy item.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is read-only identification of an ordinary item. Maker, region and age change label location, code format and document type; GE is merely an example of a product-class locator. A permitted helper can view a normally accessible tag and read it back when mobility, sight or reading is limited. Do not publish the full serial in a public discussion. “Looks the same” officially loses model-number status.
