## 中文

当一项普通、非专业事项已经有发放机构确认的到期/续办/回覆时点，而要用的纸张还分散在不同地方时加载。你要在**不自行解释法定期限**的前提下，确认来源、列出当期材料、按取得难度安排准备与复看。这里不交表、不付款、不做法律、税务、财务或医疗资格判断。

### 准备与输入

找收到的通知或机构自己的页面/账号信息，确认事项名称、适用对象、日期或时间窗、时区/办理方式若它有写，以及目前真正要求的材料。纸面印出的日期与机构当前确认的日期分栏记录；不确定就问实际机构。只在本人可控的日程或纸表记录中性事项和下一步，不把账号、证件号码或私密内容放进共享提醒。

### 执行

1. **先锁住事实来源。** 写“谁发的、哪个事项、哪天哪时按机构确认、在哪儿核对到”。旧邮件、转发截图与当前官方说明不一致时标待问，不能挑一个最宽松的日期当成官方延期。
2. **把材料逐项列出来。** 按机构当期要求记中性材料名、形式（原件/副本/信息等，若明示）、从哪个现有文件夹找、是否需向机构/本人取得。未写的材料不自行加，资格和填写内容另问有权者或专业渠道。
3. **按实际取得路径安排先后。** 将已在手、要找回、要等待他人/机构确认、确实缺少分开；需要多步才能取得的先启动。给每项一个负责人和下次检查时点，不用统一“到期前三天”假装所有材料都来得及。
4. **设置有内容的复看提示。** 提醒里只写“核对事项 X 的材料 A/B 和机构答复”，而非单独一个日期；有权限的人才能看到具体纸张。机构通知有变化时更新当前记录并保留旧消息为历史，不让两个日期都当真源。
5. **在关键时点如实收束。** 逐件检查材料是否实际可取、形式是否已确认；缺必需项或机构未答时联系其官方渠道询问可行安排或是否需改期，不编造延期/替代资格。材料齐了也只表示准备完成，实际办理结果另记。

### 完成与停止

这项到期事项的当前来源与时点可追溯，每项材料有已在手、寻找中、待答或缺少状态及复看负责人；未确认日期或缺件不会被写成“全备”。遇到法定期限、税务/财务/医疗资格、费用或身份认证判断时退出本普通流程，向实际机构或合适专业人士核实。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看见通知上的日期：** 向实际发放方核对当前状态，区分“纸上写”与“已确认”。
- **日历只写“下周到期”：** 加上要核对的材料和答复、责任人及下一次检查点。
- **一项资料等别人提供：** 明确谁联系谁和何时再问，没收到前标待答，不把请求当在手。
- **材料齐了但格式不明：** 问机构原件/副本/提交渠道要求，不能因拿到纸就宣布符合。
- **时点改变或已经过去：** 保留事实与当前不确定处，联系实际机构询问下一步；本 Skill 不给法律宽限期结论。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只为普通到期事项做材料准备，不设统一保存年限、法定宽限、费用或资格规则。不同机构和地区会改变通知与办理方式；纽约公共图书馆卡到期只是一个机构日期示例，不能替其它事项定要求。行动、阅读或日程工具可及性不同，纸表、大字或经授权的可信者回读均可；敏感材料仍本地保管。副作用是日历提醒终于会提示准备什么，而不只是对你喊日期。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary non-specialist task has an issuer-confirmed expiry, renewal or response point but its paper materials are scattered. **Trace the source, list current requirements and stage the materials and review points according to how they can actually be obtained**, without calculating a legal deadline. This does not submit, pay or decide legal, tax, financial or medical eligibility.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the notice or institution's own page or account record and confirm task name, intended person, date or window, time zone or channel if stated, and the materials currently required. Keep the date printed on an old notice separate from what the issuer currently confirms; ask when uncertain. Use a private calendar or paper note with a neutral task and next step, not account, ID or private detail in a shared reminder.

### Execution

1. **Fix the factual source first.** Write who issued it, which matter, the institution-confirmed time and where you verified that. If an old email, forwarded image and current official page disagree, mark ask-first rather than choosing the most generous date as an extension.
2. **List only actual materials.** From current issuer guidance, note a neutral name, stated original/copy/information format, existing folder location and any need to obtain it from yourself or the institution. Do not add unstated items. An authorised or qualified person handles eligibility and answer content separately.
3. **Order work by real acquisition path.** Separate already in hand, to retrieve, waiting on another person or institution, and missing. Start multi-step items early. Give each an owner and next check. A universal “three days before” rule cannot make every material available.
4. **Set a reminder with a task in it.** Write “check item A/B and issuer reply for matter X,” rather than a date alone, showing detailed papers only to authorised people. If the issuer changes information, update the current record and keep the older message as history instead of two competing current dates.
5. **Close the preparation state honestly at the check point.** Verify the item is physically reachable and its format confirmed. Ask the official issuer about a feasible alternative or rescheduling when a required item or answer is missing; do not invent a grace period or accepted substitute. Even a complete packet is preparation, not the finished transaction.

### Success and stop

The due item's current source and time are traceable, and each material has an in-hand, searching, awaiting answer or missing state with a review owner. An unconfirmed date or absent item does not become “all ready.” Exit for statutory timing, tax, financial or medical eligibility, fees or identity-authentication decisions and check with the real institution or suitable qualified person.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the notice's printed date was used:** Verify current state with the real issuer and separate printed from confirmed.
- **Calendar says only “due next week”:** Add material and reply checks, owner and next review.
- **Another person must provide a page:** Name who contacts whom and when to ask again; awaiting a request is not in hand.
- **Pages exist but format is unclear:** Ask about original, copy and channel instead of claiming compliance merely because paper exists.
- **Time changes or has passed:** Retain facts and current unknowns and ask the issuer about the next step; this Skill gives no legal grace-period answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This prepares materials for ordinary due items. It sets no universal retention period, statutory grace, fee or eligibility rule. Institution and region change notices and processes. A New York Public Library card expiry is only one example of a checkable issuer date, not another task's requirements. Paper notes, large print or trusted authorised readback can support access while sensitive records remain local. Your reminder now knows what to prepare instead of merely shouting a date.
