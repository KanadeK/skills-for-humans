## 中文

你已有一张普通流程图，步骤顺序清楚，却看不出“谁负责做”时使用本篇。成果是两条带角色标签的泳道，每个现有节点只落在一个主要责任区，跨道箭头仍连到正确对象。它可视化已知分工，不替组织作新的权限决定。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODG 副本，先按原资料列出两种角色和每个节点真实执行者；权限不清的步骤先标未知，不能靠图画出确定性。选择横向或纵向泳道并留跨道线空间。若只有一角色，泳道不会增加价值。

### 执行

1. 在背景区域画两条清楚边界的长泳道，各放一条短角色标签。
2. 逐个把已有节点移进对应主要责任泳道，不改节点文字。
3. 检查跨道连接器仍接原起终点、箭头方向与主流程顺序一致。
4. 沿流程逐步读角色；一节点归属不明就标待核而非硬塞到某道，保存重开。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

两角色泳道可辨，已知节点归属正确，跨道关系完整；未知权限被明说。

- **箭头跨道脱开：** 重接 gluepoint 后再核。
- **泳道压住节点：** 调整背景层与透明度/边界。
- **角色其实不确定：** 标未知，停止假分工。

### 假设与边界

只画两种已同意的普通角色，不作为正式岗位或权限文件。你负责从原资料核分工。

## English

Use this Skill when a small ordinary process has clear order but readers cannot see who acts. Finish with two labelled role lanes, each existing node assigned one primary owner, and cross-lane connectors still attached correctly. It visualizes an agreed division, not a new authority decision for an organization.

### Preparation and inputs

Save ODG copy. From source notes list two roles and true owner of each node; mark uncertain authority unknown rather than diagramming certainty. Choose horizontal or vertical lanes with room for cross-lane lines. One-role processes do not benefit from lanes.

### Execution

1. Draw two simple background lanes with short role labels.
2. Move each existing node to its verified owner lane without rewriting the action.
3. Check cross-lane connectors retain original endpoints, arrows and process order.
4. Read roles along flow; flag an uncertain node rather than forcing assignment, then save/reopen.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Two roles are readable, known nodes correctly assigned, cross-lane links intact and unknown ownership explicit.

- **Cross-lane arrow detaches:** Reconnect gluepoint and recheck.
- **Lane covers node:** Fix background order and boundary.
- **Owner uncertain:** Mark unknown rather than invent.

### Assumptions and limits

This draws two agreed ordinary roles, not a formal responsibility or access policy. You verify assignments from source.
