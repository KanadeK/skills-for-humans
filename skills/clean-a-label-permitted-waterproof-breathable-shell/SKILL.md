## 中文

当你的一件**确认带防水透气膜、表布有耐久拒水处理的普通外层**因脏污或表面不再成珠而需要清洁，且准确衣标和制造商说明允许家庭护理时，加载这份 Skill。目标是洗去影响表面拒水的残留，按许可干燥/处理后再核表布成珠状态。它不同于羽绒要恢复填充蓬松，也不同于羊毛湿态防拉伸。**外露膜/永久成珠表面等特例、带羽绒的复合外套、破损膜和防护用途装备不在本篇内。**

### 准备与输入

查衣标、型号和准确面料/制造商护理页，确认是否确为可家庭洗的常规表布+膜结构，是否有“不可烘干/不可熨/无需或禁止 DWR”的特殊说明。GORE-TEX 某些外露膜产品明确禁止滚筒、熨烫和 DWR，因此只看“GORE-TEX”字样不够。准备说明允许的清洁剂、机器程序和干燥位置；洗衣机有柔顺剂或粉末残留时按制造商建议先处理。不能辨认结构或关键热限制时先停，问制造商/合格护理服务。

### 执行

1. **先决定本款是否可以照此洗。** 对照衣标的洗、漂、烘、熨限制与本款制造商说明；取更严格的一方。需要专业护理、外层结构特殊、内外层已脱胶或漏水原因不明时，不套标准外层流程，也不靠热量“修好膜”。
2. **按说明整理外层。** 清口袋，处理主拉链、口袋和腋下拉链、贴片与抽绳；不同款的开合细节听本款说明。与可能勾挂、重污或需要柔顺剂的负载分开。确认机器容量、程序和温度在衣标允许范围内。
3. **洗去残留并充分漂净。** 只使用本款允许的液体或技术织物清洁剂与其剂量；GORE-TEX 指南反对粉末洗剂、柔顺剂、漂白剂和去渍剂，但仍以本款要求为准。选获允许的程序和脱水强度；制造商要求额外漂洗时执行。不要用更高温度或更强清洁品代替彻底漂净。
4. **按衣标干燥，再判断热处理资格。** 先让外层按获允许方式完全干燥。只有**衣标与本款指南都允许**滚筒或隔布低温熨烫时，才按其方法对表布拒水做热激活；不要把另一款“烘 20 分钟”写成通用指令。若禁止热或结构特殊，停在已清洁干燥状态，询问制造商下一步。
5. **做一个有限的表面检查。** 在干净、干燥且允许接触少量水的表布上，滴/轻喷一点水，看是否成珠滚开，再擦干；这只反映表布拒水，不证明膜、接缝或整件装备的防水完整性。若仍明显浸润，记录位置并查本款官方 DWR 处理或服务路径；本篇不自行购买、混配或喷涂涂层。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

衣物洗净、洗剂漂净、按衣标完全干燥，表布成珠状态有实际观察；若本款不允许热/家庭护理或拒水仍未恢复，也明确留下“已清洁、需进一步确认/处理”状态。表面成珠不等于能用于安全关键场景。

- **洗剂或柔顺剂残留：** 按衣标与机器说明再做允许的漂洗，不用漂白剂冲掉残留。
- **只看到品牌标志，找不到具体型号：** 先核制造商和衣标，尤其是否是外露膜/永久成珠特例；不猜热处理。
- **表布湿透感仍在：** 停止把它当完全恢复；核清洁、干燥、许可热处理及本款 DWR 服务，别宣称膜必坏。
- **衣标禁止滚筒或熨烫：** 不以通用文章覆盖本款禁令；干燥后向制造商确认下一步。
- **涂层剥离、缝线异常或装备用于防护：** 停止家庭恢复尝试，交制造商/合格服务；本篇不维修结构或认证性能。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通可家庭护理的常规防水透气外层，不适用于外露膜、带羽绒复合结构或职业/安全防护装备。站立、提湿衣或观察水珠不便时，可用可访问标签朗读和可信协助；关键条件仍须以本款说明核实。没有获允许的机器/热处理并不意味着应强行洗涤，合格护理服务是完整替代。干净、拒水状态和真正防水性能是不同层级的证据。

## English

Load this Skill for an **ordinary verified waterproof-breathable shell with a face-fabric durable water-repellent finish** when dirt or poor surface beading calls for cleaning and the exact label and maker permit home care. Remove residue affecting the outer face, dry or treat only as allowed, and check face-fabric beading. Down needs fill loft restored; wool needs support against wet stretching. **Exposed-membrane or persistent-beading exceptions, down-insulated composites, damaged membranes, and protective equipment are outside this Skill.**

### Preparation and inputs

Check care label, model, and matching fabric or maker guidance. Confirm this is a conventional face-fabric-plus-membrane structure that permits home washing, and whether special instructions forbid tumble drying, ironing, or DWR. Some GORE-TEX exposed-membrane products explicitly forbid all three; the brand name alone is insufficient. Prepare only the permitted cleaner, machine program, and drying route. Handle existing softener or powder residue as the maker directs. Stop for the maker or qualified care service when structure or heat limits cannot be identified.

### Execution

1. **Decide whether this model can follow the route.** Compare wash, bleach, dry, and iron symbols with exact maker guidance; use the stricter limit. Professional-care-only, special outer surfaces, delaminating layers, or unknown leakage causes do not fit a standard shell procedure. Heat cannot simply “fix the membrane.”
2. **Prepare the shell as instructed.** Empty pockets and handle main, pocket, and pit zips, tabs, and drawcords for this model. Keep it apart from snagging, heavily soiled loads or ones that need softener. Check washer capacity, program, and temperature against the label.
3. **Wash away residue and rinse thoroughly.** Use only a model-permitted liquid or technical-fabric cleaner at its dose. GORE-TEX guidance advises against powder detergent, softener, bleach, and stain remover, while the actual garment still governs. Use permitted program and spin, including any extra rinse the maker requires. More heat or stronger chemistry is not a substitute for rinsing.
4. **Dry by label, then decide on heat eligibility.** First dry fully through an allowed route. Apply maker-approved tumble or low-heat covered ironing for face-fabric DWR only when **both label and model guidance permit it**. Another product's “20 minutes” is not a universal instruction. With heat forbidden or a special surface, stop at clean and dry and ask the maker about next steps.
5. **Make one limited surface check.** On clean, dry face fabric that tolerates a little water, drip or lightly spray a small amount, observe whether it beads and rolls, then dry the spot. This reflects face-fabric repellency only, not membrane, seam, or whole-garment waterproof integrity. If the face still wets out, note where and check this model's official DWR treatment or service route. This Skill does not buy, mix, or spray a finish on its own.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The shell is clean, cleaner rinsed out, and fully dry within label limits, with an observed face-fabric beading state. When heat or home care is forbidden or beading still fails, leave an honest “cleaned; needs further guidance or treatment” state. Beading is not approval for safety-critical use.

- **Cleaner or softener remains:** Use another permitted rinse under garment and washer instructions, not bleach.
- **Only a brand logo is known:** Find model and label, especially whether this is an exposed-membrane or persistent-beading exception. Do not guess the heat step.
- **The face still wets out:** Do not call performance fully restored. Check cleaning, drying, permitted heat, and model-specific DWR service without declaring the membrane failed.
- **Label forbids tumble or ironing:** Do not override it with a general article. Ask the maker about the next step after drying.
- **Coating peels, seams fail, or equipment is protective:** Stop home recovery and use the maker or qualified service. No structural repair or performance certification is offered here.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary home-care-permitted conventional shell, not exposed membrane, down composite, or occupational or safety protective gear. When standing, lifting wet clothing, or seeing beads is difficult, use accessible label reading and trusted help; critical conditions still come from the exact product. Lack of an allowed machine or heat route is a reason to choose qualified care, not force washing. Cleanliness, surface repellency, and real waterproof performance are different evidence levels.
