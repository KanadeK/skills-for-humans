## 中文

当你在公寓、宿舍或公共洗衣房**获准使用一台空的共用洗衣机或烘干机**时，用这份 Skill 从占用前核对到结束交接。目标是完成自己的负载、取回所有个人物品，并把设备按现场规则留给下一人；衣物分组、准确机型程序和污渍处理沿用各自的任务。本篇不替现场决定付款、预约、开放时间或他人衣物处置权。

### 准备与输入

读现场张贴的使用资格、预约/排队、机器编号、付款方式、产品限制、关门/清理和遗留物规则；规则不明先问管理者。准备已按标签分好的衣物、适用洗剂及其剂量、收衣篮、可在周期结束时返回的提醒和后续晾/烘空间。核对机器完全空、可用、无漏水/警报/损坏，附近没有他人的私人物品；不要把空着但标明已预约或维护的机器当成无主机器。

### 执行

1. **确认此刻可开始。** 核对预约、实际剩余开放时间和自己的完整洗涤/烘干周期；若结束时无法回来或没有湿衣去向，改约而不是让下一人替你收。费用/支付条件按现场公示，不猜一条全球统一收费规则。
2. **只放自己的合格衣物。** 机器内有别人的衣物或物品时先停止并走“设备被占用”流程；不拿出来“帮忙”。按衣物标签、准确机型说明和洗剂标签装载/设定，别超过负载或把不明产品倒进共用机器。烘干机按本机说明核对绒毛过滤网在位和可用。
3. **设一个跟实际周期对应的取衣提醒。** 记录机器编号、启动时间和屏幕/说明显示的结束事件，提醒自己及时回来；不要把软件估计分钟数当机器已经停稳或所有衣物已干。场所要求留在附近时照做，不把衣物长期无人照看。
4. **结束后只拿自己的负载。** 等机器完全停稳、按说明开门，数清个人衣物、洗剂、篮子及可能掉落的小物件。洗好的湿衣立即进入标签允许的晾晒/烘干去向；烘完逐件查干燥状态，不能把湿衣留机内给下一位处理。
5. **把设备和空间交接出去。** 按现场及本机要求处理可清理的绒毛、洗剂洒漏与自己留下的垃圾，必要时按张贴要求留门通风；不拆部件或自行清洗未知内部。机器/排水异常、收费未正确完成或遗留他人财物时保留机器编号并向管理者报告。清出个人物品，确认下一人能按规则接手。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你的衣物与用品已取回并有合适下一步，机器已停、空、无自己造成的可见洒漏/垃圾，遵守现场清理与交接规则。即使设备故障导致中断，明确停用、保管自己的衣物并向管理者报告也是完成的降级。

- **机器空着但有预约标记：** 不占用，核对现场队列或另选可用机器。
- **付费后机器未启动：** 不重复输入个人凭据或反复付费；记录机器编号和交易状态，按现场渠道找管理者。
- **回来发现别人的衣物与自己的混在一起：** 先停止移动、区分个人所有物并找管理者，别替别人作丢弃或赔偿决定。
- **结束时绒毛网损坏或排风警示：** 停用并报告，不用更长一轮来验证设备。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这仅处理你获准使用的普通共享洗衣设备；各场所的时间、费用、预约和可移动他人衣物的规定不同，只有现场规则对该地点有效。视力、听力、行动或数字支付不便时使用现场支持的可及说明、提醒方式或可信协助；无可用支付/操作路径时与管理者沟通，不要求交出密码。多洗一轮占用公共资源，应诚实排队。

## English

Use this Skill when you are **allowed to use an empty shared washer or dryer** in an apartment, residence hall, or laundromat. Check before occupying it, finish your own load, retrieve your belongings, and leave the machine under site rules for the next person. Sorting, exact-model cycles, and stains keep their own workflows. This file does not invent payment, booking, opening hours, or authority over another person's laundry.

### Preparation and inputs

Read posted eligibility, reservations and queues, machine number, payment, product restrictions, door and cleaning expectations, and abandoned-item rules. Ask site staff when a rule is unclear. Have a label-sorted load, suitable detergent and its dose, basket, a return reminder, and a drying destination. Confirm the machine is fully empty, available, and without leak, alert, or damage, and that another person's property is not nearby. An empty machine marked reserved or under maintenance is not unclaimed.

### Execution

1. **Confirm you can start now.** Check bookings, remaining opening time, and the full wash or dry cycle. Reschedule if you cannot return or have nowhere to put wet clothes; do not turn the next user into your pickup service. Follow this site's posted price and payment route instead of a universal fee assumption.
2. **Load only your qualified items.** If someone else's clothes or property are inside, stop and use the occupied-machine route; do not remove them as a favour. Load and set the machine by garment, exact-model, and detergent instructions without exceeding its capacity or pouring unknown products into shared equipment. For dryers, check the fitted usable lint filter by this model's manual.
3. **Set a pickup reminder tied to the real cycle.** Record machine number, start, and the finish event shown by its display or manual. Return promptly. An app's estimated minutes do not prove the drum has stopped or that every garment is dry. Stay nearby when the site requires it.
4. **Retrieve only your own load at the end.** Wait for a full stop and open the door under the manual. Count your clothing, detergent, basket, and any small items that may have fallen. Give washed wet clothing its label-compatible air or tumble route immediately. Inspect dried items one by one; do not leave damp clothing in the machine for the next person.
5. **Handoff machine and room.** Follow site and model directions for user-accessible lint, your detergent spills, and your trash; leave the door for ventilation if posted. Do not dismantle parts or clean unknown internals. Record the machine number and report abnormal operation, drainage, payment status, or another person's found property to the manager. Remove your own belongings and leave the next user a usable space.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Your clothes and supplies are retrieved with a next destination, the machine is stopped and empty, and your visible spill or trash is cleared under posted handoff rules. For a fault that interrupted the cycle, stopping use, securing your own load, and reporting it to the manager is a complete downgrade.

- **The empty machine has a reservation marker:** Do not occupy it; check the site queue or another available machine.
- **Payment completed but the machine did not start:** Do not repeatedly enter credentials or pay again by guesswork. Note machine number and transaction status and use the posted support route.
- **Another person's clothes appear mixed with yours:** Stop moving things, separate what you know is yours, and ask staff. Do not decide disposal or compensation for them.
- **A lint filter is damaged or vent warning appears:** Stop and report it; a longer cycle is not an equipment test.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary shared equipment you are authorised to use. Site schedules, fees, reservations, and rules on moving others' laundry vary; only the local rule governs that place. Use accessible posted directions, reminders, or trusted help for vision, hearing, movement, or payment access. If there is no usable payment or control path, ask the manager; do not hand over passwords. An additional load occupies shared capacity, so queue honestly.
