## 中文

当一只普通室内猫未来有**可提前准备的常规外出**，而它尚未熟悉提笼时，加载这份 Skill。任务是让提笼成为可自愿探索的熟悉空间，并检查笼体能安全使用；不是今天把猫强塞进去或保证它以后不紧张。紧急就医要按兽医指导处理，不为训练耽误。

### 准备与输入

核对提笼尺寸、门与锁扣、通风、底部稳固和可清洁性；一只猫一只适配的提笼。查看猫是否有过强烈恐惧、攻击或身体不适，有则向兽医或合格行为人员求指导。准备安静、猫可自行离开的房间和一块熟悉、可洗的垫物；不把人用香水或未核对的镇静产品当作训练替代。

### 执行

1. **先检查提笼。** 门、扣、提手和底座完好，猫能自然进入并转身；坏扣不靠胶带修。清洁后确认无刺激残留，放入熟悉垫物。
2. **让笼子常驻一处安静位置。** 打开并固定门，避免突然合上。让猫按自己的节奏靠近、闻、走入或离开；不要追捕、推入或堵住出口。
3. **在猫仍放松时做短练习。** 可把它熟悉、且已获主人/兽医允许的玩具或原有食物放在笼旁/笼内；不改变食谱或加喂量。若猫退开，就停在当前阶段，下次再试。
4. **逐步增加步骤。** 只有猫能平静自愿进出时，才在可靠指导下短暂碰门、关门并很快重开；确认没有夹伤或惊慌。时间与次数以这只猫的反应为准，不设必须完成的训练天数。
5. **结束与收好。** 记录它能接受到哪一步，保持提笼清洁可用；每次练习后让猫能离开。正式出行的门窗关闭、固定笼体和交通规则属于实际旅程另行核对。

### 成功条件

提笼结构可用、放在猫可自愿探索的位置，猫实际观察或进入的阶段有记录，下一步不超过它能承受的范围。若猫恐惧、笼损坏或本次需要紧急交通，停止训练并转专业指导；强行关门不算成功。

### 常见报错与补救

- **一拿出提笼猫就躲：** 保持安静与开放，把目标退到“能在同一空间看到提笼”，不追猫。
- **猫进去了就立刻关门：** 先恢复自愿进出，之后再尝试更短、可退出的关门步骤。
- **门扣不牢：** 换合适提笼；运输安全不能靠熟悉程度弥补。
- **计划出行时间很近：** 向兽医询问实际可行的安全转运建议，不用训练进度承诺准时。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一只已由你照料的普通室内猫的**非紧急、非强制**提笼熟悉练习，不教药物、约束术、紧急救援或汽车固定法。行动或听觉/视觉受限时请可信的人帮忙检查门扣和猫的身体语言；猫的选择与安全边界仍重要。提笼会暂时占据一处室内空间。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary indoor cat has **planned, non-urgent travel** in the future but is unfamiliar with its carrier. Make the carrier a space it can explore voluntarily and confirm the structure is usable. This does not force-load the cat today or promise stress-free travel. Follow veterinary direction for urgent care; do not delay it for practice.

### Preparation and inputs

Check carrier size, door and latches, ventilation, stable base, and cleanability; each cat needs a suitable separate carrier. If the cat has severe fear, aggression, or illness, seek veterinary or qualified behavior advice. Choose a quiet room the cat can leave and familiar washable bedding. Human perfume or an unchecked calming product is not a training substitute.

### Execution

1. **Inspect the carrier first.** Door, latch, handle, and base must work, and the cat should enter and turn naturally. Tape is not a repair for a faulty latch. Clean it without irritating residue and add familiar bedding.
2. **Leave it open in a quiet place.** Secure the door against accidental closure. Let the cat approach, sniff, enter, and leave on its own timetable. Do not chase, push, or block the exit.
3. **Keep early sessions brief and relaxed.** You may place a familiar permitted toy or existing food near or inside the carrier, without changing the cat's diet or total amount. If it withdraws, end at that stage and try another day.
4. **Add steps gradually.** Only after calm voluntary entry and exit, use reliable guidance to briefly touch the door, close it, and open it again. Check for trapping or panic. Let this cat's reaction determine duration; no mandatory training-day count.
5. **Close the practice.** Record which step it tolerated and keep the carrier clean and available. Let the cat leave after every practice. Actual travel requires separate checks of doors/windows, securing the carrier, and transport rules.

### Success

The carrier is structurally sound, accessible for voluntary exploration, and the cat's observed stage and next small step are recorded. Stop and seek qualified help for panic, a damaged carrier, or urgent travel. Forcing the door shut is not a success.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The cat hides when the carrier appears:** Leave it quiet and open. Reduce the aim to seeing it in the same space instead of chasing the cat.
- **You close the door as soon as it enters:** Restore voluntary entry and exit before a shorter, reversible door step.
- **The latch is unreliable:** Replace the carrier. Familiarity cannot compensate for transport failure.
- **The appointment is soon:** Ask the veterinarian for practical safe-transport guidance; do not promise training will finish on time.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is **non-urgent, voluntary** familiarization for a cat in your care, not medication, restraint technique, rescue, or vehicle securing. Ask a trusted helper to check latches and body language when movement, hearing, or vision makes that difficult. The cat's choice and safety still control the session. The carrier occupies a home spot.
