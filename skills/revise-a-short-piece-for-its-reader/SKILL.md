## 中文

当你已有一篇**从头到尾完整的自有短稿**，却怀疑读者看不出重点、顺序不顺或句子还未收紧时，用这份 Skill 先修结构、再修句子和错字，得到能用于你原定低风险用途的一版。没有全文时先完成起稿；出版合同、法律声明、医疗/财务建议或未经授权的他人作品不靠这份普通修订来“变安全”。

### 准备与输入

找出原来的目标读者、目的、使用场合和篇幅限制；保留未改原稿或一个可返回的版本，再复制一份供本轮修改。准备正文中事实、引文、图片/人物授权的来源记录。没有这些记录时明确待核，修订后也不能直接对外当事实。若找朋友阅读，先征得同意并只分享对方有权接触的内容；拒绝反馈不影响你完成自审。

### 执行

1. **离开作者视角看一次全文。** 不看大纲先读，写一行“读者看完会得到什么”；若它与原任务句不一致，决定改文本重点或明确改目的，不同时假装两者都是唯一主线。
2. **为每段写一句实际作用。** 只写这段现在让读者知道/感到/继续做什么；没有作用的删或移，次序让必要信息先出现、变化与结尾能被理解。读者需要的背景补到首次使用之前，而不是埋在末段。
3. **先处理大改，再修表面。** 优先补缺失步骤或关键场景、调整段落顺序、删重复、修不成立的结尾；结构稳定后才改词句、语气和节奏。不要把一段不相关的文字磨到完美后仍不舍得删。
4. **核查事实、素材和读者边界。** 每个客观结论找可靠原始来源或标清你在推断；引文/图片/真实人物细节核对许可与隐私。无法核实的删、改成诚实不确定，或暂停对外使用；不因句子流畅就发布来源缺口。
5. **用一次读者路线检查。** 自己大声读或用可及朗读工具从头到尾听，标出需回读、指代不明、跳步和结尾突兀之处；可请一名愿意的读者回答“哪里第一次看不懂”，但不要求对方保证喜欢。依据具体反馈修，不把所有个人口味都视为必须同时满足。
6. **最后才校对并定版。** 检查错字、重复词、名称/数字一致性和实际使用需要的格式；对照目标读者与开头/结尾再读一遍。留下一个可识别的新版本，作品文件整理与下次入口交 H280；若事实/权限还没解决，标注“未准备公开”而非按发送键。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你有一版与读者/目的匹配、从开头到结尾能跟随的短稿，结构问题先于错字解决；未核事实与素材权限已查清或明确阻止发布。完成不要求所有读者赞同或喜欢。

- **全文最重要的一点直到结尾才出现：** 考虑把它提前，再重排支持它的段落，不只在第一句加粗。
- **同一事实出现两个数字：** 回到来源核对，暂不发布；字体统一不能修事实冲突。
- **反馈互相矛盾：** 先看目标读者与实际误解点，选一项可验证的修改；不同口味不必投票决定全文。
- **只修错字却仍看不懂：** 返回段落作用和信息顺序，复制保留旧版本后再做大改。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理本人有权修改的普通短篇文字，不做版权法律判断、学术诚信认证或现实专业建议。纸稿可用另页重写，数字稿可先复制/保留可撤回版本；不用 AI 才能完成。视力、阅读或语言习惯不同，朗读、较大字体、打印和可信同意的读者反馈都可用。删改会改变原稿，保留旧版让决定可回滚。

## English

Use this Skill when you have a **complete short draft you own** but suspect the reader may miss its point, struggle with order, or meet loose sentences. Revise structure before lines and typos to make a version fit your intended ordinary use. Finish a whole draft first if needed. A publishing contract, legal notice, medical or financial advice, or someone else's unlicensed work cannot be made safe by ordinary prose revision.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the intended reader, purpose, use, and length limit. Preserve the original or a returnable version, then work on a copy. Gather sources for factual claims, quotes, images, and details about real people. Mark missing evidence clearly; flowing prose does not permit publishing unchecked claims. Ask before inviting a friend to read, and share only material they may receive. A declined invitation does not block your own review.

### Execution

1. **Read once outside the writer's head.** Without the outline, state in one line what the reader gets from the piece. If it differs from your original task sentence, decide whether the text or purpose changes rather than pretending two main points are one.
2. **Name each paragraph's actual job.** State what this paragraph currently helps the reader know, feel, or do next. Cut or move one without a role; order information so necessary context arrives before use, a change makes sense, and the end can be understood.
3. **Make large changes before surface polish.** Add a missing action or scene, reorder paragraphs, cut repetition, and repair a weak ending first. Once structure holds, adjust words, voice, and rhythm. A beautiful paragraph outside this piece does not earn permanent residence by being polished.
4. **Check facts, materials, and reader boundary.** Seek reliable primary sources for objective claims or say explicitly when you infer. Check permission and privacy for quotes, images, and real-person details. Remove or qualify an unverified claim, or hold publication. A smooth sentence cannot fill a source gap.
5. **Test one reader route.** Read aloud or use an accessible read-aloud tool from start to finish. Mark places that require rereading, have an unclear referent, skip a step, or end abruptly. A willing reader may answer “where did you first get lost,” without promising to like it. Revise specific misunderstanding rather than obey every preference at once.
6. **Proofread and set the version last.** Check typos, repeated words, names, numbers, and any format needed for actual use. Read opening and ending against the reader goal again. Keep a recognisable new version; H280 owns later file closeout and the next creative session. If facts or rights remain unresolved, mark it not ready for public sharing rather than press Send.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have a short version that fits its reader and purpose and can be followed from opening to ending. Structural gaps were handled before typos. Factual and material permissions were checked or explicitly prevent publication. Success does not require every reader's agreement or approval.

- **The main point appears only at the end:** Consider moving it forward and reorder its support, not merely bold the first sentence.
- **One fact has two different numbers:** Return to the source and hold publication. Matching fonts do not fix conflicting facts.
- **Feedback conflicts:** Use the intended reader and actual confusion to choose a testable edit. Preferences need not vote on the whole piece.
- **Typos are fixed but it still confuses:** Return to paragraph roles and information order, keeping an old copy before large edits.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary short writing you have the right to change, not copyright-law advice, academic-integrity certification, or professional real-world guidance. Rewrite a paper draft on another page or copy a digital file to make changes reversible. AI is not required. Read-aloud, larger type, printing, and a trusted consenting reader help different visual, reading, or language needs. Editing changes the original, so preserve a route back.
