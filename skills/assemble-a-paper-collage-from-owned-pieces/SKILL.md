## 中文

当你已有几张**自己做的或获准使用的纸片**和一张底纸，想把它们装成一幅小型平面拼贴时，用这份 Skill 先干排、再以适用纸胶固定、等干后验收。这与一张纸不靠胶自立的折纸不同：这里的关键是多片重叠次序、粘接材料与干燥。它不教锋利裁切、喷胶、强溶剂、热熔胶，也不准未经许可把别人的图像当成可公开作品。

### 准备与输入

确认每片素材的来源和是否可用于这次个人/公开用途；看纸张厚薄、涂层与底纸能否配合所选产品。准备制造商明确适用于纸/纸板的低风险胶水或胶棒、干净压纸布/垫板、可平放至干的桌面；读胶的涂布、接合和干燥说明。先在废纸角落测试是否洇色、起皱或粘不住；若产品标签不清、刺激性气味强或需危险刀具/化学品，暂停换材料。

### 执行

1. **先不涂胶摆一遍。** 将纸片放在底纸上，决定哪张在后、哪张在前，确认主体、留白和边缘不会被最后一片遮住；这一步沿用你自己的构图选择，H272 负责前期构图规划。可拍一张仅供自己记位置的照片，不含未经同意的人或隐私材料。
2. **分批连接，先底层后上层。** 按胶产品标签在一片干净干燥纸片背面或底纸指定区薄薄施胶，避免胶流到正面；对齐后轻压到说明要求，才做下一片。每次只拿几片，防止整幅在未干时不断移动。
3. **控制纸的水分与接触。** 纸起皱、颜色渗出或边缘卷起时停止再加胶，检查测试与产品用量，不靠更多水/胶“熨平”。用手或干净布按压时别把未干颜色擦到另一个图形，手部沾胶按产品安全说明清洗。
4. **平放等待真实干燥。** 保持底纸平整并按胶产品说明等干，远离食物、火源和会碰到的通道。产品说明没有授权的热风、烤箱或化学固定剂不能作为加速按钮。干燥中若要移动，先用稳定托板而不是只拎一角。
5. **干后验收与有限补救。** 纸片没有明显翘起、相互盖住非预期内容，作品能平放或按原用途展示；一角松动时只按同一产品说明小范围重贴并再等干。整片仍脱落或底纸变形，就保留观察，换底纸/胶材做新版本或停止，不把失败稿宣称耐久。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

一张底纸上的多片自有/获准纸材按你选择的前后关系固定，胶按标签干燥，边缘和主要内容可见；若材料不相容，停止并记录原因也能结束本次试作。成品是小型平面拼贴，不承重、不耐水，也不因完成就获得他人作品的转载权。

- **贴下去才发现主角被挡：** 在产品允许的未干阶段小心调整；已粘牢则为下一版改层序，不硬撕整幅。
- **胶越多越皱：** 停止继续涂，按产品说明晾干，下一版先在废纸测更薄的一层。
- **边角一干就起：** 检查纸面是否有涂层/灰、胶是否适用，按相同产品小范围重贴或换材料。
- **找到的图像版权不明：** 暂不公开或改用自己绘制/合法授权的素材，不靠改颜色声称原创。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只用普通纸、底纸与标签允许的低风险胶，不做喷雾、刀具、热熔、灯具/承重结构。儿童参与需按产品年龄标示与现场成人安排；本文不代替照看。握力不便时用大纸片、可及桌面与可信协助，视力不便可用高对比摆位和触觉轻定位；不要求精细的毫米对齐。胶水和底纸可能消耗，桌面在干燥前不能腾空。

## English

Use this Skill when you have several **paper pieces you made or have permission to use** and one backing sheet and want to assemble a small flat collage. Dry-arrange first, attach with compatible paper adhesive, and judge only after drying. Unlike a one-sheet fold that stands without glue, this job depends on several layers, adhesive material, and drying. It does not teach sharp cutting, aerosol glue, strong solvent, hot glue, or public reuse of unlicensed images.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the source of each piece and whether your intended private or public use is permitted. Check paper thickness, coating, and backing against the adhesive. Have a low-risk paper or card glue with maker directions, clean pressing cloth or board, and a table that can stay flat until dry. Read application, joining, and drying instructions. Test a scrap corner for bleed, wrinkle, or poor adhesion. Pause for unclear labels, strong irritating fumes, or material that needs a blade or hazardous chemical.

### Execution

1. **Arrange without glue first.** Put pieces on backing, choose what sits behind or in front, and check that your subject, open space, and edges survive the last layer. Your own visual composition leads; H272 handles earlier paper composition planning. A private photo may help remember placement without including non-consenting people or private material.
2. **Join in small groups, back to front.** Under the glue label, apply a thin amount to the back of one clean dry piece or its marked backing area. Align and press as directed before moving to the next. Handle a few pieces at a time so the whole work does not keep shifting while wet.
3. **Control moisture and contact.** Stop adding glue for wrinkling, bleeding colour, or curling edges. Recheck the scrap test and product amount rather than add water or more glue to “iron” it flat. Press with clean hands or cloth without transferring wet colour to another piece. Clean adhesive from hands by its safety label.
4. **Keep it flat for real drying.** Leave backing supported for the product's drying period, away from food, flame, and foot traffic. Unapproved hot air, an oven, or chemical fixative is not a speed control. If it must move while drying, support the whole piece on a firm board rather than lift one corner.
5. **Inspect dry and repair only within limits.** Pieces stay attached, intended foreground is visible, and the work can lie flat or display as planned. Reattach one loose edge only by the same product's directions and dry it again. For repeated detachment or warped backing, record what happened, use new support or glue for another version, or stop; do not call this trial durable.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Several owned or permitted pieces sit in chosen front-to-back relation on one sheet, the glue dried by label, and edges and main content remain visible. Stopping for incompatible material with a recorded reason is a complete trial. This small flat collage bears no load or water and completing it grants no rights to someone else's work.

- **You discover the subject was covered after sticking:** Adjust gently only if still wet and permitted. If set, change layer order in a new version rather than tear the whole sheet.
- **More glue creates more wrinkles:** Stop coating, dry by label, and test a thinner scrap application next time.
- **An edge lifts when dry:** Check coating, dust, and glue fit; retry one small edge with the same product or change material.
- **An image's rights are unknown:** Hold public sharing or use your own drawing or licensed material. Recolouring does not make copied art original.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Use ordinary paper, backing, and label-compatible low-risk glue only, not spray, blades, hot melt, lighting, or load-bearing structures. Children need product age guidance and a present adult arrangement; this file is not supervision. Use larger shapes, a reachable table, and trusted help for limited grip, or high-contrast placement and tactile light guides for limited vision. Millimetre-perfect alignment is unnecessary. Glue and backing are consumed and the table stays occupied until dry.
