## 中文

当你吃完盒中普通食物，要清洗一个**设计为重复使用的餐盒**，且盒盖可能带可拆密封圈时，用这份 Skill 按实际型号判断哪些部件能拆、能机洗或只能手洗，再洗净、彻底干燥和复装。它不把一次性外卖盒自动改造成长期容器，也不负责判断里面剩菜能否再吃；后者用食品保存流程。材质、结构或制造商说明不清时先停止强拆。

### 准备与输入

确认品牌/型号或可核实的产品说明，分别看盒身、盖、密封圈、卡扣及通气阀的拆卸、洗涤、最高温/洗碗机和干燥限制；**一件可机洗不代表其他件也可机洗**。把食物残余按安全与本地废弃流程移走，检查盒体是否裂、盖是否变形、圈是否松脱或有明显污物进入不可接近处。准备干净水、适用手洗剂、软布/厂家允许的小刷和可分件晾干的位置。化学品、污水或严重不明污染退出普通清洗。

### 执行

1. **照说明拆可拆件。** 只拆制造商明确允许用户取出的圈、阀或内片，按说明顺序并放在不会滚失的干净托盘上；固定封边、胶接件或需要工具撬的结构不强拆。记下圈的原方向与卡槽，失去说明时不靠用力试出形状。
2. **先去普通食物残留。** 用合适软工具移走盒身、盖内侧和允许接近的槽沟中可见碎屑或油膜；不拿尖刀刮塑料或硅胶。若食物进入无法接近的固定缝或胶层，暂停使用该盒存食物并询问厂家。
3. **每件按自己的许可洗。** 手洗件用适用洗剂和水轻洗、按产品要求漂去洗剂；允许机洗的件按其标示架位及准确机器说明安放，不用盒身的许可代替盖/圈许可。避免把小圈随意丢进会冲走或卡住的机器；需要专用篮具时按本机说明使用。
4. **拆开晾到真正干。** 盒身、盖、圈和槽沟分别沥水/晾干，检查边角和密封槽没有积水或泡沫；不在还湿时直接扣紧收进柜。过热烘干、直晒或其他加速方式只有产品说明允许时才用。
5. **按原方向复装并检查。** 所有部件已干且完好后，按制造商步骤把密封圈/阀放回对应槽位，盖能自然闭合、无圈被夹出或部件缺失。不能正确装回、盖变形、圈裂开时暂停再装食品，按厂家零件/更换说明处理；清洁完成不等于餐盒必然防漏。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

餐盒可接近的食品接触面、盖与获准拆卸的密封件已按各自说明洗净、无明显残留且干透；完整且正确装回或明确停用待确认。若不能安全拆洗某部位，停止强行复用比把食物封在不明槽沟里更完整。

- **硅胶圈拉不出来：** 查这款是否真的可拆；不可拆或说明缺失就停，不用刀尖撬。
- **盒身可机洗、盖只有手洗说明：** 分成不同路线，盖不随盒身进机。
- **槽沟仍有水滴：** 继续分开放干，不能先扣紧让水留在密封区。
- **洗后盖关不严：** 查圈方向、缺件与变形；不要硬压到卡扣断裂。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只管普通可重复使用的食品盒，不含真空电器、保温罐内部结构、婴幼儿食品容器或罐藏密封。不同型号圈拆法与耐热不同，说明书比外观可靠。看不清小槽可放大照片，用可及高度的托盘防零件丢失或请可信的人协助；无安全拆卸工具/方法时先停。分件晾干占空间，盒子下一次装食物前仍需依据食品安全与该容器用途判断。

## English

Use this Skill after ordinary food is gone from a **reusable food box** whose lid may contain a removable gasket. Decide by exact model which parts may be removed, dishwashed, or hand washed, then clean, fully dry, and reassemble. A disposable takeout tub is not automatically a long-term container. Whether remaining food was safe to keep belongs to the food-storage workflow. Stop forcing parts when material, construction, or maker directions are unclear.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the brand/model or reliable care directions. Check body, lid, gasket, latch, and vent separately for user disassembly, washing, heat, dishwasher rack, and drying limits. **Permission for one part does not cover the others.** Route leftover food by its safety and local waste processes. Check for body cracks, warped lid, loose gasket, or food in an inaccessible place. Have water, suitable dish soap, a soft cloth or maker-allowed small brush, and separate clean drying space. Chemical, sewage, or serious unknown contamination leaves ordinary cleaning.

### Execution

1. **Remove only maker-removable pieces.** Take out a gasket, valve, or insert only when the maker permits it and follow the directed sequence on a clean tray where parts will not disappear. Do not pry fixed seams, glued joints, or parts needing force. Note the gasket's original orientation and groove. When directions are missing, force is not an identification method.
2. **Remove ordinary food first.** Lift visible crumbs and grease from body, lid underside, and accessible grooves with a suitable soft tool. Do not scrape plastic or silicone with a sharp knife. Stop storing food in this box and ask the maker if food entered a fixed inaccessible joint or adhesive layer.
3. **Wash each part by its own permission.** Hand-wash pieces with suitable soap and water and remove product residue as directed. Put approved machine parts on their specified rack under the exact dishwasher manual; the body's permission does not transfer to lid or seal. Do not leave a small ring loose where the machine could wash it away or jam. Use an approved small-parts basket if this model requires one.
4. **Dry fully while apart.** Let body, lid, gasket, and groove drain and dry separately. Check corners and the sealing channel for pooled water or suds; do not close a still-wet box for cupboard storage. Heat, direct sun, or other speed-ups need explicit maker approval.
5. **Reassemble and check.** Only after every part is dry and intact, reinstall gasket and vent in the maker's orientation. The lid closes normally with no seal pinched out or part missing. Hold the box out of food use and check replacement directions for a warped lid, split seal, or part that will not seat. Cleaning alone does not prove leak resistance.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Accessible food-contact faces, lid, and permitted removable seals were cleaned by their own directions, show no obvious residue, and are dry. Parts are correctly reassembled or held for confirmation. Stopping when a joint cannot be safely cleaned is more complete than sealing food against an unknown groove.

- **A silicone ring will not come out:** Verify this model actually permits removal. Do not use a knife point when it does not or instructions are missing.
- **The body is dishwasher safe but the lid says hand wash:** Use separate routes.
- **Water remains in a groove:** Keep pieces apart to dry; do not latch water into the seal.
- **The lid no longer closes evenly:** Check orientation, missing parts, and distortion rather than forcing a latch to break.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary reusable food containers, not vacuum-powered equipment, insulated-vessel internals, infant-food containers, or canning seals. Gasket removal and heat tolerance depend on exact model, not appearance. Magnify a small groove, use a reachable tray to hold parts, or ask trusted help. Stop when no safe disassembly route exists. Separate drying takes space, and later food use still needs a food-safety and container-purpose decision.
