## 中文

一次普通的聚餐、户外活动或小型聚会已经约好，后来天气、场地开放状态或设施变化，使原安排可能无法使用时，加载这份 Skill。你的目标是**选出可实行的替代、延期或取消，并让所有相关人知道同一个决定**。这份 Skill 不负责预测天气，也不代替官方灾害预警、撤离指令或场地方决定。

### 准备与输入

找出原定时间地点、活动真正需要的条件、已确认参与者、谁负责预订或交通，以及最后能改决定的时间。核对具体变化：是预报可能下雨，现场已不适合，还是场地方明确关闭？记录信息来自何处、何时更新；别把“有人说可能关门”写成“已经取消”。

如有实际官方警报、封闭或主办方安全限制，照其指示行事；若正在发生紧急危险，停止普通重排流程并联系适用的应急渠道。只在没有这类紧急情形时继续。

### 执行

1. **找出原方案失效的条件。** 例如露天活动需要干燥地面，场地不再开放，厕所或无障碍通道不可用。轻微天气变化未必需要重排；关键条件失效则别用“应该没事”覆盖事实。
2. **列最多两个真实可用的选项。** 可以保留原计划并用已有遮蔽、改到可用室内地点、改时间，或取消。逐项查地点是否确实开放、容纳人数、通行与基本设施、参与者的交通时间、预订和费用。查询中的空位不算已经订到。
3. **问必须同意的人。** 场地主人或服务方要确认可用；新地点或时间显著改变参与者的花费、交通或可及性时，给受影响者拒绝或退出的选择。不能替别人决定“走远一点没关系”。人数或座位也变化时，重新核对实际容量。
4. **设一个决定截止时间。** 在活动开始前留出够大家收到消息并改变行程的时间。若替代地点、交通或必要同意到截止仍未确认，延期或取消；不要让人按两个地址出发。
5. **先落实，再发唯一决定。** 和实际运营方确认新的开放状态、预订或取消条件；必要时调整交通和用品。随后发：“原计划 [时间/地点] 因 [已确认变化] 不能按原样进行。现在决定 [新时间/地点/取消]；需要你在 [截止时间] 回复 [具体问题]。未确认项是 [项目]，我会在 [时间] 更新。”若替代仍待确认，就明确写“暂缓出发”，不要写成已改定。
6. **核对通知覆盖面。** 给参与者、场地主人、司机或负责订单的人发其需要的变更。检查谁没收到或没回；关键交接不能只依赖一个可能被静音的群聊。对方拒绝新安排时，接受退出并按实际预订条款处理后续。

### 完成条件

所有需要行动的人知道**一个**最终版本：保留、换场、延期或取消；该版本的场地、时间、容量与必要同意已经确认。若关键条件尚不确定，大家收到“不要按原计划出发”及明确的下次更新时间。你不需要保证每个人满意，也不能把“消息已发送”当成“所有人已读”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **预报反复变化：** 约定一次决策时间和触发条件，例如场地能否使用；在截止前只在关键事实变化时更新，不每十分钟重投票。
- **新地点看起来可用，但还没确认：** 先问运营方并核对无障碍入口、实际容量和预订；得到答复前保留“待确认”状态。
- **有人已经出发：** 直接用能联系到对方的渠道告知新地点或取消；不要仅在群聊底部补一句。无人接听时留下简短消息，按实际安全与活动规则处理，别假定已通知成功。
- **替代方案让部分人无法参加：** 给退出、分组或改期的选择；不把可及性或额外费用当作小题大做。
- **场地费用或票不能退：** 查本次实际条款，联系有决定权的服务方；把已确定与待确认的费用分开告诉承担者，不保证退款。
- **有人仍拿旧地址：** 再发一条有时间标记的最终决定给受影响者，必要时直接联系正在路上的人；明确旧地址作废。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里处理自愿、普通、小型活动。天气风险、营业状态、交通与退款条件因时间和地区变化；使用当时实际可靠的信息和官方或运营方要求。你可能多付改订费用、失去原场地，或让一部分人无法来。把这些影响说清楚，允许他们不接受新安排。群聊不会自动把旧地图链接撤回。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary meal, outdoor activity, or small gathering was already agreed, but weather, venue availability, or facilities may make the original plan unusable. Your goal is to **choose a workable alternative, postpone, or cancel, and get everyone affected onto the same decision**. This Skill does not forecast weather or replace official hazard alerts, evacuation orders, or a venue's decision.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the original time and place, the conditions the activity actually needs, confirmed participants, who controls bookings or transport, and the last useful decision time. Check what changed: a forecast suggests rain, the site is already unusable, or the operator has closed it. Note where the information came from and when it was updated. “Someone says it might close” is not “it has been cancelled.”

Follow any actual official warning, closure, or organizer safety rule. If there is immediate danger, stop ordinary replanning and contact the applicable emergency channel. Continue only for an ordinary nonemergency change.

### Execution

1. **Identify the condition that failed.** An outdoor event may need dry ground; a venue may be closed; a toilet or accessible entrance may no longer work. A small change need not force a new plan, but do not wave away a critical failed condition.
2. **List at most two genuinely usable options.** Keep the plan with available shelter, move to an open indoor place, change the time, or cancel. For each, check actual opening, group capacity, access and basic facilities, travel time, reservations, and cost. A queried vacancy is not a confirmed booking.
3. **Ask the people whose agreement matters.** The host or operator must confirm availability. Give participants a choice when a new place or time materially changes their cost, travel, or access. Do not decide for them that “a bit farther” is fine. Recheck capacity if the group size or seating changes.
4. **Set a decision deadline.** Leave enough time before the event for people to receive the message and change their journey. If the alternative place, transport, or necessary agreement is still missing at that point, postpone or cancel. Do not send people toward two addresses.
5. **Confirm, then send one decision.** Check the new availability and booking or cancellation terms with the actual provider; adjust transport and supplies where needed. Then send: “The original [time/place] cannot work because [confirmed change]. The decision is [new time/place/cancel]. Please reply by [deadline] about [specific question]. [Item] remains pending; I will update at [time].” If the alternative is still unconfirmed, say “hold off on leaving,” not “moved.”
6. **Check who needs the update.** Tell participants, the host, drivers, and whoever owns an order what changed for them. Check who did not receive or answer it; a muted group chat is not a reliable critical handoff. If someone declines the new arrangement, accept the withdrawal and handle any booking consequence under the real terms.

### Success

Everyone who must act knows **one** final version: keep, move, postpone, or cancel. Its place, time, capacity, and necessary agreements are confirmed. If a key condition remains uncertain, people receive “do not leave under the old plan” and a clear next update time. You do not need to make everyone happy, and “sent” does not mean “read.”

### Common errors and recovery

- **The forecast keeps shifting:** Choose one decision time and trigger, such as whether the place can be used. Update before then only when a decisive fact changes rather than polling the group every ten minutes.
- **The new place seems available but is not confirmed:** Ask the operator and check its accessible entrance, actual capacity, and booking. Keep it pending until you get an answer.
- **Someone has already left:** Use a channel that may reach them directly to send the new place or cancellation; do not only add a line at the bottom of the group chat. Leave a short message if they do not answer, follow actual safety and event rules, and do not assume delivery succeeded.
- **Some people cannot use the alternative:** Offer withdrawal, a split group, or a later date. Access and extra cost are real constraints.
- **The venue fee or tickets may be nonrefundable:** Read the actual terms and contact the authorized provider. Separate known from pending costs when telling the people who bear them; do not promise a refund.
- **Someone still has the old address:** Send a time-stamped final decision to affected people again, and contact anyone already travelling directly if possible. Mark the old address obsolete.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This covers a voluntary ordinary small gathering. Weather risk, opening hours, transport, and refunds vary by place and time; use reliable information available then and the relevant official or operator rule. You may pay a change fee, lose the original place, or leave some people unable to attend. State these effects and allow them to decline. A group chat does not retract an old map link by itself.
