## 中文

当准确用品说明允许你完成一项小型、非结构性的普通手工具任务，而你还没开始动手时加载。目标是**物件稳、表面受保护、零件与工具能找到、其他人仍可通行**的工作面。这里只布置环境，不教拧螺丝、组装、切割、钻孔或设备维修。

### 准备与输入

先读准确任务说明的空间、人数、工具和物件放置要求。确认任务确实是小型普通手工，不需危险刀具、动力工具、带电作业、承重/防护固定或专业安装。准备现有纸张、托盘、小盒、布垫等合适保护材料；不必为了做一次活购买专用磁垫。

### 执行

1. **选稳固且可到达的面。** 物件和手工具放上去不会晃或滑到边缘，你能按说明从所需方向操作。桌、地面或其他位置要结合实际身体能力；不要用摇晃椅面、通道中央或需危险伸手的位置代替。
2. **保护周边又保留原有用途。** 按物件与表面说明铺不易滑的普通保护层，留出门、走道、其他人正在用的空间。不要用未经确认会掉色、导电或易粘住的材料接触敏感表面；有疑问先查准确说明。
3. **把一项任务分成几个可见位置。** 当前步骤的零件放在最容易够到处，后面才用的零件仍按原袋/编号分开；给手工具一个不会滚落的固定落点。用纸上步骤号或小盒标记，不必把多种紧固件倒成一堆。
4. **在动手前做路径与旁人检查。** 人能从入口到需要的区域走通，工具和零件不伸出边缘，不让其他人误把工作中的半成品当可用物。需要中途暂停时，预留一个能安全放下手工具和未完成件的位置。
5. **读回开工状态。** 指出说明、当前步骤、工具落点、零件分区及离开后收拾责任人。东西放不下、看不清、拿不到或路径被堵，就换面或缩小一次要做的范围，再开始具体任务。

### 完成与停止

低风险任务有稳定可用的工作面，周边得到适当保护，当前与后续零件不混，工具有固定安全落点，通路仍能使用。需要危险动作、场地不稳、材料不相容或无法防止小件丢失时先调整工作条件；台面准备好不证明用品已经完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **螺丝滚到边缘：** 换小盒/纸分格并移回内侧，别把地板当零件托盘。
- **保护布会滑，物件跟着走：** 换说明允许且稳定的面或保护层，不能靠手持续按住危险位置作业。
- **不同步骤零件混在一起：** 依准确编号重新分格，身份不明的暂停对应步骤。
- **台面堵住别人通行：** 调整位置或时段，保留走道；不把短任务变成共用区长期占用。
- **光线或身体姿态不适合核对：** 换能看清和够到的位置、放大说明或请愿意的协助者；不要边猜边动工具。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只为厂家允许的普通手工具操作布置环境，不替代用品说明、危险作业许可或完整组装。地区、住房空间、身体能力和工具不同，可用纸、原包装、小盒等已有物品替代专用托盘。需要更多人或更大面积而现场没有时暂停任务。副作用是小螺丝终于有固定座位，不必马上去地板探险。

## English

Load this Skill before one small non-structural task that the exact item instructions permit you to do with ordinary hand tools. You will make **a stable, protected, reachable surface where parts and tools stay findable and other people can still pass**. This arranges the area; it does not teach screwdriving, assembly, cutting, drilling or equipment repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the exact task's requirements for space, people, tools and item position. Confirm it is an ordinary small hand job, with no hazardous blade, powered tool, live electrical work, load-bearing or protective fixing, or specialist installation. Gather suitable existing paper, tray, small box or cloth protector. One task does not require buying a branded magnetic mat.

### Execution

1. **Choose a stable reachable surface.** The item and tools should not wobble or slide off an edge, and you should be able to work from the directions the guide requires. Choose a table, floor or other place for your actual mobility; a shaky chair, middle of a passage or hazardous reach is not a substitute.
2. **Protect the surroundings without taking over their use.** Use a non-slip ordinary layer compatible with the item and surface instructions. Keep doors, routes and space other people are using available. Do not put an unverified dyeing, conductive or sticky material against a sensitive finish; check the exact guidance if uncertain.
3. **Give one job visible zones.** Put only the current step's parts close by, leaving later parts in original bags or labelled groups. Give each hand tool a fixed landing spot away from a rolling edge. A paper step number or small box can do the job; pouring all fasteners into one pile cannot.
4. **Check paths and other users before work.** People can pass from entry to areas they need, and tools or parts do not project over edges. Do not let somebody mistake a partial item for a usable one. Reserve a safe place to set tools and the unfinished piece if you pause.
5. **Read back the start state.** Point to guide, current step, tool landing spot, part groups and who will restore the space afterward. If you lack room, light, reach or a clear path, change the surface or shrink the work scope before starting the actual operation.

### Success and stop

The low-risk task has a stable surface, suitable protection, distinct current and later parts, a tool landing spot and usable routes. Fix unstable space, incompatible materials, hazardous requirements or a setup that loses small parts before work. A ready work area does not mean the item itself is finished.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Screws roll toward the edge:** Use a small box or paper compartments and move them inward. The floor is not a parts tray.
- **The protective cloth slides with the item:** Choose a stable, maker-permitted surface or layer; do not constantly grip a dangerous position to compensate.
- **Parts from different steps are mixed:** Separate by exact ID again. Hold the dependent step when identity stays unknown.
- **The area blocks another person's route:** Move it or change time and keep the passage available. A short task is not a permanent occupation of shared space.
- **Light or posture prevents a reliable check:** Use a clearer, reachable position, enlarge the guide or ask willing help instead of using a tool while guessing.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This prepares the environment for maker-permitted ordinary hand work; it does not replace the item's guide, authorise hazardous work or complete assembly. Region, home space, ability and tools differ. Existing paper, packaging or boxes can replace a special tray. Pause if the real job needs more people or area than you have. Small screws finally get assigned seats instead of immediate trips to the floor.
