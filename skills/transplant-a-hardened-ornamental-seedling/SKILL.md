## 中文

当一株普通**非食用**观赏幼苗已按可靠资料完成逐步炼苗、准备真正移到室外时，加载这份 Skill。E002 的炼苗只判断户外适应程度；你在这里核实地点与天气，完成一次实际移栽或明示暂缓。

### 准备与输入

确认种袋/品种的光照、株距、移栽深度和耐寒条件；查看当地当前官方天气预报及短期霜冻风险，别用固定月份替代当地事实。只在你获准使用、无需深挖或碰触地下设施的小花坛或有排水的室外容器操作。幼苗尚未炼好、病弱、根团太脆或位置不合适时暂停。手部、弯腰或搬土困难可用腰高容器或请人协助。

### 执行

1. **检查开工条件。** 对照种袋和实际地点核光照、空间、介质排水和天气。炼苗不等于耐霜；预报或资料不允许时保留幼苗在安全位置并设下次检查。
2. **标出一个合适位置。** 按种袋株距避开已长成植物及通道。先给土壤或盆栽介质适度湿润，移开表面小石块和普通杂草；不翻动不明管线、危险废物或大型根系。
3. **保护根团脱盘。** 从育苗格底部轻推，托住叶片或根团，别抓细茎硬拔。根团散开、幼苗倒伏或容器不能安全脱离时停止并求合适帮助。
4. **按原深度放入。** 做与根团相配的小洞，把幼苗置于种袋允许的深度，轻填边缘并固定；不要把叶心或茎基深埋来“增加稳定性”。给它一枚可读标签。
5. **浇定根水并留复看点。** 按品种和天气说明浇水，使周围介质贴合根团而不长期积水。记日期、位置、预报和下次检查入口；后续看干湿与新生长，不以刚直立证明已成活。

### 成功条件

你能指出这株幼苗的位置、标签、种袋株距/深度依据、当日天气判断和下一次检查。根团已安放、周围介质稳定，或因实际霜冻/位置/苗况不合格而明确暂缓。没有“先埋下去再看”默认路径。

### 常见报错与补救

- **预报出现霜冻：** 依品种说明暂缓；炼苗不能给植物升级耐寒等级。
- **细茎快被拔断：** 停手，从格底松开并托根团；不拉茎。
- **花坛被占或无授权：** 改找获准、有排水且符合品种的容器，不侵占公共绿地。
- **移栽后萎蔫：** 记录土壤和天气、按品种护理复查；持续恶化时求园艺指导，不把每次下垂都归咎缺水。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理一株普通小型观赏幼苗，不种食用作物、树木，也不教大型土方、化学处理或用电设备安装。位置与耐寒必须以当地实际天气和品种资料判断。行动受限时用可及容器，视觉受限时请人读种袋和确认栽植深度。现实副作用是多一个需要回看的室外位置。

## English

Load this Skill when one ordinary **non-edible** ornamental seedling has completed gradual hardening under reliable guidance and is ready to move outdoors. E002's hardening ends at readiness; here you verify the site and weather, then physically transplant or explicitly defer.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm packet or species light, spacing, planting depth, and cold tolerance. Check the current official local forecast and short-term frost risk rather than a fixed calendar month. Use only a permitted small bed or draining outdoor container that needs no deep digging near unknown utilities. Defer if hardening is incomplete, the seedling is weak, the root ball is fragile, or the site does not fit. A waist-height container or lifting help can replace bending and hauling.

### Execution

1. **Check the start conditions.** Compare packet needs with actual light, room to grow, drainage, and weather. Hardening does not make a tender plant frost hardy. If the forecast or guidance rules it out, hold the seedling safely and set another check.
2. **Mark one suitable position.** Use packet spacing, existing plants, and clear paths. Moisten the soil or potting medium as appropriate and remove loose stones and ordinary small weeds. Do not disturb unknown utilities, hazardous debris, or large roots.
3. **Release the root ball gently.** Push from the cell base and support leaves or root ball, not the thin stem. Stop and seek suitable help if the root ball falls apart or the seedling cannot be freed safely.
4. **Plant at the supported depth.** Make a small hole matching the root ball, set the seedling at the packet-supported depth, fill gently, and steady it. Do not bury the growing point for stability. Add a readable plant label.
5. **Water in and set a review.** Use species and weather guidance to settle medium around roots without leaving it waterlogged. Note date, position, forecast, and next check. Standing upright immediately does not prove establishment.

### Success

You can point to the seedling's place and label and explain packet spacing/depth, today's weather decision, and next check. Its root ball is placed in stable medium, or an actual frost, site, or seedling condition has caused an explicit hold. “Plant first, decide later” is not the default.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Frost appears in the forecast:** Defer according to the species. Hardening is not a hardiness upgrade.
- **The thin stem is about to snap:** Stop; loosen from underneath and support the root ball, not the stem.
- **The bed is occupied or unauthorised:** Use a permitted, draining, species-suitable container instead of public ground.
- **The transplant wilts:** Record soil and weather and review species care. Seek horticultural advice if decline continues rather than assuming every droop needs more water.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This handles one small ornamental seedling, not edible crops, trees, large earthworks, chemicals, or electrical equipment. Location and cold tolerance depend on real local weather and species guidance. Use an accessible container if mobility is limited; ask for packet reading and depth confirmation when vision is limited. One outdoor spot now needs review.
