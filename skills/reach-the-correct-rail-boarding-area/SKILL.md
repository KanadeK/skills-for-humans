## 中文

你的普通铁路车次、出发站和当前站台已与站方信息对上，或站台仍待公布时，用这份 Skill 按**该车站真正的门禁、检票、候车与开放通知**到达可上车位置。不同铁路可能是闸机刷卡、电子票扫条码、工作人员检票或候车区等广播叫号；不能把一个地方的“进站”等同另一个地方的“已经能上车”。完成是站在允许的正确候车区域、仍能核对列车身份并安全等待；不含票务权利判断或硬闯关闭通道。

### 准备与输入

先看刚核对的车次、日期、站台及信息更新时间，带着实际票、预订或该运营方要求的凭证。查出发站当前的入口、闸机/人工查验、安检或候车区规则，以及是否有“尚未开放/等候广播”的限制。数字票要按运营方认可的 App、文件或纸票形式出示，不假设截图可用；荷兰 NS 的电子票与卡片刷入就不是同一动作。行李、轮椅或其他协助需要时，使用站方公布的可及路径与服务，别把普通楼梯当唯一通路。

### 执行

1. **在入口再读一次车次状态。** 对照站内实时出发屏、公告与自己的车次/站台。若站台尚未公布、车次取消或现场与旧截图冲突，留在允许的公共候车处问工作人员或等正式通知，不进未知站台。
2. **只走该服务允许的入站方式。** 有闸机就按票/卡和运营方说明通过指定入口；人工检票或候车叫号就等工作人员开放。扫码失败、票面不符或闸机不开时停在入口侧联系工作人员，不尾随别人冲闸，也不反复把不明凭证靠到不同机器上。
3. **按站方标识到正确开放区域。** 沿允许的指示牌、开放楼梯/电梯或工作人员路径去当前站台或等候区。车站可能有同名站台的不同区域、分段候车或受控登车门；看清站台号、分区与车辆停靠信息。若标识和实时屏冲突，先在安全位置核对，不跟人群挤进封闭通道。
4. **安全等车并保持复核。** 在站台按当地地面安全线和站方提示留出边缘距离，不把行李或推车放在边缘，避免堵住通道。注意广播、屏幕和工作人员：延误、站台或车厢变更可能发生。只有列车实际到来、显示的服务身份与你的票/计划一致且站方允许登车时，才进入下一步上车；不要因为另一列车先停同站台就跳上去。
5. **变化时回到信息点。** 站台迟迟不公布、入口关闭、可及通路失效或车次变更时，查最新站方信息并找工作人员给出可到达的新路线；不穿越轨道、维修区或封闭门求快。保留更新后的车次—站台—分区记录，再继续等待。

### 成功条件

你按该站当前规则通过所需入口，抵达与已核对车次相符且开放的候车区/站台，在安全位置等待并持续检查变化；或因站台/入口未开放而合法留在公共区域，知道下一次从何处查公告。仅通过闸机不证明找对列车，站上旧站台也不算完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **手机有票但闸机不认截图：** 查运营方接受的原始电子票/App 或可打印版本，请工作人员协助；不借别人的票或尾随过闸。
- **车次号对，站台分区不对：** 对照屏幕、分区标识和车厢停靠信息，在开放通道内修正；不跨越轨道。
- **站台仍待公布：** 等官方显示/广播，别默认昨天的站台或先排在一个看似热闹的入口。
- **车站安排先在候车室等叫号：** 留在允许候车区，按该班实际叫号进场；不把开放大厅当已开放登车口。
- **需要电梯/协助而路径不可用：** 在可返回的位置联系站方，确认真实替代与所需时间，不用风险路线硬赶原车。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本文处理普通、可安全等待的铁路乘车准备；门禁、验票、安检、候车叫号、票面形式及可及设施按车站和运营方当前规则，不提供普遍时间/票价/补票保证。英国 Network Rail、荷兰 NS 和美国 Amtrak 的资料显示这些机制并不一致。无手机可用纸票、站内屏/广播、人工服务，具体仍看本次服务是否接受。可能需要多等或多走一段开放通路，安全与正确性优先于追随人流。

## English

Use this Skill after matching an ordinary rail service, departure station, and current platform, or while the platform remains unannounced. Follow **this station's real gate, ticket check, waiting, and boarding-call rules** to reach an area from which you can board. Systems may use card gates, e-ticket barcodes, staff checks, or a lounge until boarding is called. Entering a station is not automatically permission to board. Finish at the correct open waiting area, still able to verify the train and wait safely; no fare-rights ruling or closed-door shortcut.

### Preparation and inputs

Read back the matched train, date, platform, and information time, with the actual ticket, booking, or operator-required proof. Check this station's current entrance, gate/staff check, security or waiting area, and any “not open yet / await announcement” restriction. Present a digital ticket in a format its operator accepts, rather than assuming a screenshot works. NS e-tickets and card check-in are different actions, for example. Use the published accessible route or assistance for luggage or mobility needs rather than treating stairs as the only way.

### Execution

1. **Recheck the train at the entrance.** Compare the station live board and notices with your service/platform. For an unannounced platform, cancellation, or conflict with an old screenshot, remain in a permitted public area and ask staff or await an official update rather than entering an unknown platform.
2. **Use only this service's permitted access method.** At gates, present the ticket/card as instructed at the designated entry. Where staff check tickets or call passengers from a waiting area, wait for access to open. If a scan fails, the ticket conflicts, or a gate remains closed, stop on the entrance side and ask staff. Do not tailgate or probe random readers with an unclear credential.
3. **Reach the correct open area.** Follow permitted signs, open stairs/lifts, or staff directions to the current platform or waiting area. A station may have platform sections, coach positions, or controlled boarding doors. Read platform, section, and coach positioning. If signs disagree with live information, pause somewhere safe and resolve it instead of pushing into a closed passage with a crowd.
4. **Wait safely and keep checking.** Stay behind the local platform edge marking under station instructions. Keep bags and strollers away from the edge and paths clear. Follow announcements, screens, and staff for delays, platform or coach changes. Only move to board when the train actually arrives, its displayed service matches the ticket/plan, and station staff permit boarding. An earlier train at the same platform may be someone else's.
5. **Return to an information point when conditions change.** For an unannounced platform, closed entrance, failed accessible route, or changed service, use the latest station information and ask staff for a reachable alternative. Do not cross tracks, work areas, or locked doors for speed. Update the service-platform-section record and continue waiting.

### Success

Under this station's current rules you pass any required access point and reach an open waiting area or platform for the matched service, stand safely, and keep watching for change. If access or platform is not announced, waiting legally in a public area with a known official recheck route is a valid pause. Passing a gate alone does not identify the train; standing on an old platform is incomplete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A phone ticket is not accepted as a screenshot:** Find the operator-approved original app ticket or printable form and ask staff. Do not borrow a ticket or follow someone through a gate.
- **Right train number, wrong platform section:** Compare the live board, section signs, and coach position; correct only through open routes, never across tracks.
- **Platform is pending:** Wait for an official screen or announcement, not yesterday's platform or the busiest-looking gate.
- **This station calls boarding from a lounge:** Stay in the permitted area until this service is called. An open hall is not an open boarding gate.
- **Lift or assistance route is unavailable:** Ask staff at a reachable position for a real alternative and required time. Do not force an unsafe path to save this train.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary, safely interruptible rail boarding preparation. Access gates, ticket checks, security, boarding calls, ticket formats, and accessible facilities follow the current station and operator, with no universal timing, fare, or catch-up guarantee. Network Rail, NS, and Amtrak illustrate different mechanisms. A paper ticket, departure board, announcement, or staff can replace a phone when accepted for this service. A longer wait or open-route detour is preferable to following a crowd to the wrong place.
