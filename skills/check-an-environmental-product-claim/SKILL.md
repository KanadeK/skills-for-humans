## 中文

当“可回收”“可堆肥”“再生成分”或一个环保徽章是你想多花钱或换商品的主要理由时，付款前加载。你的任务是确认**宣称的对象、范围和实际条件**，不是给整件商品做生命周期评估、认证审计或当地垃圾分类执法解释。

### 准备与输入

取得这件准确商品的包装正反面、宣称旁边的小字、制造商给出的依据，以及你所在地实际可用的收集方式。先说清你在乎的是商品本体、包装，还是用后处理；不同问题不能共用一个绿色勾号。看不清小字时拍照放大、用朗读工具或请人读出完整限定词；若需查当地服务，可等联网后再买。

### 执行

1. **翻译成可核对的一句话。** 例如“外盒含一定比例再生材料”，不要改写成“整件商品环保”。“绿色”“对地球友好”等没有具体对象和指标时，只把它记为待证宣称。
2. **圈出范围和条件。** 是商品还是包装？全部还是某一部件？再生成分比例多少？“可堆肥”需家庭还是工业设施？标签没有说，就不要替它补写。
3. **找可追溯依据。** 查看制造商解释、明确标准或认证机构的公开范围。徽章若无颁发者、标准和适用产品，不能当作完整证据；有第三方标志也只证明其明确评价的属性。
4. **核对当地实际路径。** 对“可回收/可堆肥”，查所在地收集机构是否接收这种材质和形态，以及是否要拆分部件。产品在理论上可处理，不等于你所在区域会收。
5. **作购买决定。** 写“已确认的具体属性 / 宣称未确认 / 与我当地条件不相容”。若环保属性是必要条件，后两种就暂缓或换商品；若只是加分项，不给未确认的宣称加分。

### 成功与停止

你可以用一句准确的话说出**哪个部件、哪项属性、什么条件下**成立，并指出依据。找不到范围或当地接收信息时停在“未确认”；不要因包装颜色或树叶图案宣布它有环境净收益。购买以外的实际分类与处置仍按当地正式指引执行。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把外盒声明套到内装物：** 重新找明确对象；不能定位就不计入理由。
- **看见三角箭头就默认本地回收：** 查当地机构接收规则，必要时把材质和形态问清。
- **把“可堆肥”当成随处可丢：** 核对家庭/工业条件与当地去向；不能实现就不按该属性购买。
- **看到认证章但没有范围：** 查认证者与标准，找不到就保留“未确认”。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 假设你只是作普通消费选择，不要求你证明整条供应链的环境影响。环保宣称和收集系统因地区变化；不能上网、无法读小字或没有对应收集设施时可选择包装更少、使用寿命更符合需求的商品，或直接不为该宣称付溢价。贴纸本身不参与回收运输。

### 来源

- [美国 FTC：消费者环保宣称指南](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/eco-friendly-and-green-marketing-claims) — 区分商品与包装、再生材料比例、认证含义，以及本地回收可得性；美国规则不作跨境法律结论。
- 当地实际接收范围须以你所在地收集机构的现行说明为准。

## English

Load this Skill before paying when “recyclable,” “compostable,” “recycled content,” or an environmental seal is a main reason to pay more or switch products. Your task is to identify the **object, scope, and usable conditions** of the claim. It is not a life-cycle assessment, certification audit, or legal interpretation of local waste rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Get the exact item's front and back, fine print beside the claim, the maker's explanation, and collection options where you live. Decide whether you care about the product, its package, or end-of-use handling. One green check mark cannot answer all three. Enlarge the text, use read-aloud help, or have someone read every qualifier. If local services need an online check, wait and buy later.

### Execution

1. **Rewrite the claim as a testable sentence.** “The outer carton contains a stated share of recycled material” is narrower than “the entire product is green.” Treat “earth-friendly” without a defined object and measure as unverified.
2. **Mark scope and conditions.** Product or package? Whole item or one component? What recycled-content share? Home or industrial composting? Do not supply qualifiers the label omitted.
3. **Find traceable support.** Look for the maker's explanation, a named standard, or the certifier's public scope. A badge without an issuer, standard, and covered product is incomplete evidence; a genuine third-party mark supports only the attributes it actually evaluated.
4. **Check the local route.** For recyclable or compostable claims, ask your local collection service whether it accepts this material and form and whether parts must be separated. A theoretical route is not your area's route.
5. **Decide before buying.** Record “specific attribute supported / claim unconfirmed / incompatible with my local route.” If the attribute is essential, wait or choose another item for either of the latter outcomes. If it is only a bonus, give no bonus for an unconfirmed claim.

### Success and stop

You can say **which part has which attribute under which conditions**, and point to support. If scope or local access remains unknown, stop at unconfirmed. Package colour and leaf artwork do not establish a net environmental benefit. Follow current local instructions for actual disposal after use.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Applying a carton claim to the contents:** Find the named object; otherwise do not count the claim.
- **Assuming a recycling triangle means local acceptance:** Check the local collector's material and form rules.
- **Reading “compostable” as “leave it anywhere”:** Confirm home or industrial conditions and a real destination. If unavailable, do not buy for that attribute.
- **Seeing a seal without its scope:** Check the issuer and standard; keep unconfirmed if you cannot find them.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary purchase choice, not proof of a supply chain's total impact. Claims and collection systems vary by region. Without internet, readable fine print, or a suitable local service, you can choose less packaging, a product that will meet your actual use, or simply decline to pay a premium for the claim. The sticker will not carry itself to a collection site.

### Sources

- [US FTC: Eco-Friendly and Green Marketing Claims](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/eco-friendly-and-green-marketing-claims) — product versus package, recycled-content scope, seals, and local recycling access; US rules are not asserted in other regions.
- For actual acceptance, use the current instructions of your local collection service.
