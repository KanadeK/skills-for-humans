## 中文

当你已经核对过一盆普通室内植物的基本养护问题，决定不再继续养它时，加载这份 Skill。任务是**移走植株和旧介质、避免把疑似问题带到其他盆、清理位置，并明确花盆下一步去向**。一片黄叶不构成“植物已死”的证明。

### 准备与输入

确认你有处置这盆植物的决定权，以及当地普通植物、盆土和盆器的实际收集规则。若只是短暂萎蔫、不知原因或别人仍在负责这盆植物，先停下沟通或查可信园艺资料。疑似病虫时先保持与其他植物隔离；未知毒性、尖刺、大型重盆和人体接触问题交有能力者处理。准备容器和稳定工作面。

### 执行

1. **确认处置状态。** 记录为何决定移走、是否看到虫或病害迹象；不把普通养护失败和确诊病害混为一谈。若有人希望保留且你无处置权，先协调，不直接扔。
2. **限制散落。** 将小盆移到可清理的地方，用接土容器接住植株和旧介质；不抖到其他盆、食物台面或风口。疑似虫害材料先封存，按当地废弃规则决定去向，别默认能进入家庭堆肥。
3. **分别处理物料。** 植株、介质、塑料或陶制盆器的收集路线可能不同。依据本地规则分流；无法确认时先把材料装好留待核实，不把所有东西一股脑放进“可回收”。
4. **清理盆器与位置。** 如果只是普通无异常旧盆，先清除残土，用温水和肥皂清洗并晾干；是否再栽新植物要另作决定。若原株有疑似病虫或盆器破裂，不把简单冲洗当作消毒证明，查园艺及当地处置指导。
5. **结束并复看邻盆。** 清理台面和手，把邻近植物看一遍，记录需要继续观察的迹象。空位和干净盆不证明问题根因已解决。

### 成功与停止

成功时，植株和旧介质已按已核实的路线处理或封存待核，位置无散落，盆器已清理或明确弃置，邻盆需要复查的事项有记录。若仍无法决定是否移走、材料可能有特殊风险或当地收集方法不明，先停在安全收纳状态。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只是叶子下垂就准备丢：** 核对盆土、光照、品种和最近变化；先完成真实决定。
- **虫害盆土撒到其他植物：** 停止移动材料，清理污染处并检查邻盆；保持问题盆隔离。
- **旧盆洗过就马上栽新株：** 疑似病虫时进一步核对可靠的清洁/弃置指导，不把肥皂水视为万能消毒。
- **不知道本地接不接受盆土：** 分开封存并查本地收集说明，不靠猜的回收标志处理。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理你有权处置的普通室内小盆栽，不做病原鉴定、农药清除或危险植物处理。需要搬举协助时请人帮忙；看不清细小虫体可用放大照片或请人观察。结果是空间恢复和一只待决定是否再用的盆；保留邻盆观察任务。

## English

Load this Skill after you have reviewed the basic care problems of an ordinary houseplant and decided to stop keeping it. **Remove plant and old medium, avoid transferring a suspect problem, clear the spot, and give the pot a next state.** One yellow leaf is not proof that a plant is dead.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm your authority to dispose of this plant and the actual local collection rules for vegetation, medium, and container. Pause to discuss or seek reliable horticultural guidance if it is merely wilted, the cause is unknown, or someone else is responsible for it. Keep a suspect pest or disease problem separate from other plants. Unknown toxicity, spines, a large heavy pot, or human exposure needs capable help. Prepare a debris container and stable surface.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the decision state.** Record why you are removing it and whether insects or disease-like signs were seen. Do not equate failed care with a confirmed disease. If you lack authority or someone wants to keep it, coordinate first.
2. **Contain loose material.** Move a small pot to a cleanable area and catch plant and old medium in a container. Do not shake material onto other pots, a food surface, or an air vent. Contain suspect infested material first and use local waste guidance; home compost is not a default.
3. **Separate material routes.** The plant, medium, and plastic or ceramic pot may use different local collection routes. Follow confirmed instructions. If unclear, keep material contained until you verify the route instead of dropping everything into recycling.
4. **Clear pot and spot.** For an ordinary pot without abnormal signs, remove old medium, wash with warm soapy water, and dry. Decide on reuse separately. If pests or disease were suspected, or the pot is broken, ordinary washing is not proof of disinfection; seek reliable horticultural and local disposal guidance.
5. **Close and review neighbors.** Clean the surface and hands, look over nearby plants, and note any signs needing later review. An empty spot and clean pot do not explain the original problem.

### Success and stop

The plant and medium have a verified disposal or contained pending route; the area has no loose debris; the pot has been cleaned or clearly set for disposal; nearby review needs are recorded. Stop in a safe contained state if the removal decision, material risk, or local route remains unresolved.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A drooping leaf prompts disposal:** Recheck medium, light, species, and recent changes before finalising the decision.
- **Suspect medium spills near other plants:** Stop moving it, clear the affected area, inspect neighbors, and keep the suspect plant isolated.
- **A washed pot is immediately replanted:** If pests or disease were suspected, check reliable cleaning or disposal guidance. Soap and water are not a universal disinfection certificate.
- **Local soil collection is unknown:** Contain it separately and check local rules; a guessed recycling symbol is not a disposal plan.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is for an ordinary small houseplant you have the right to remove. It does not diagnose pathogens, clear pesticides, or handle hazardous plants. Arrange help for lifting. Magnify a photo or ask someone to inspect tiny signs when needed. The physical result is restored space and a pot with an unresolved or completed reuse decision; neighboring plants may still need watching.
