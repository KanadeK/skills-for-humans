## 中文

当一堆衣服正试图以“我们都差不多”进入同一桶时，加载这份 Skill。你要做的不是按颜色完成一张漂亮照片，而是找出不能共用同一温度、力度、漂白和干燥条件的东西。

### 准备与输入

把衣物摊到干燥、光线足够的位置。先确认：

- 每件衣物的洗涤、漂白、干燥、熨烫和专业护理符号或文字；
- 颜色、面料、是否第一次洗、是否曾经掉色；
- 普通脏污、明显污渍或需要特殊处理的污染；
- 你能使用的洗涤与干燥方式；
- 贵重、结构复杂、装饰多、破损或没有可读标签的衣物。

看不清小标签时拍照放大、使用可靠的符号指南或请人读出内容。色觉差异时不要只说“深色浅色”，用衣物名称、位置或可触摸标签标记各组。

### 执行

1. **先读限制，再谈分几桶。** 被划掉的处理不能用；温度、线条和圆点通常表示上限或处理强度。符号体系因地区可能不同，不确定时查与你标签对应的官方图例。
2. **先移出不适合普通机洗的衣物。** 禁止水洗、只允许专业护理、标签不可读、贵重且结构复杂的衣物单独放置。它们不是“之后偷偷塞进去”队列。
3. **按不兼容条件拆分。**
   - 白色/浅色与可能掉色的新深色分开；
   - 羊毛、精细面料与结实棉织物分开；
   - 重污与普通衣物分开；
   - 温度、搅动、脱水、漂白或干燥上限不同且无法共同满足时分开；
   - 防水、超大或高吸水物品若机器说明要求专用程序或单洗，就另组。
4. **严重污染先停止。** 体液、危险化学品、虫害或其他不明污染不进入普通家洗流程。按照当地卫生或专业处理建议处置。
5. **普通污渍先看两个标签。** 衣物标签和去渍产品标签都允许时才预处理，并先在不显眼处测试。绝不把含氯漂白剂与氨、酸或其他清洁剂混合。
6. **用最严格的共同限制命名每桶。** 例如“浅色、冷水、轻柔、不漂白、不可烘干”。同桶里任何一件衣物的更严格上限都会成为整桶上限。
7. **做口袋与结构检查。** 清空纸巾、硬币和电子设备；按衣物说明处理拉链、扣件和翻面。纸巾进入洗衣机后会进行一次非常成功的水平扩展。
8. **为每件衣物分配去向。** 机洗某桶、手洗、专业护理、或等待更多信息。没有“先放旁边然后忘记”的未定义状态。

### 成功条件

每桶都有清楚的衣物清单、控制整桶的最严格标签、不能加入的项目和原因、去渍前置条件与干燥边界。你能拿起任意一件衣物，指出它下一步去哪儿。

### 常见报错与补救

- **ERR_ONE_RED_SOCK：** 新的或已知掉色的鲜艳衣物单洗、与同类深色洗，或先按标签做可靠掉色测试；不要让全桶替你收集证据。
- **标签缺失或模糊：** 暂停，查品牌/材质信息或选择专业护理。最温和程序也不是所有未知衣物的通用恢复模式。
- **桶太小不值得洗：** 等待同类衣物、手洗（标签允许时），或接受一次较小负载；不要为了装满而合并不兼容限制。
- **已经混进去才发现：** 在启动前取出；若已启动且安全可暂停，按机器说明暂停处理。不要把手伸进仍在运动或锁定的设备。
- **误用了不兼容化学品：** 离开暴露区域、保持通风并按产品标签/当地应急建议处理；不要加入另一种化学品“中和”。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里假设衣物属于普通家庭洗护范围，且你能获得对应地区的符号说明。手部操作、站立或弯腰困难时，可在桌面坐着分拣、使用轻质篮或请人搬运；判断仍由可读标签而不是体力决定。

现实副作用是房间短暂出现几堆衣物，以及你可能第一次发现某件衣服一直在请求“专业护理”而你从未查看日志。

## English

Load this Skill when a pile of clothes is attempting to enter one machine under the theory that “we are all basically fabric.” Your job is not to produce an attractive colour arrangement. It is to find items that cannot share temperature, movement, bleach, or drying limits.

### Preparation and inputs

Spread the clothing on a dry, well-lit surface. Confirm:

- washing, bleaching, drying, ironing, and professional-care symbols or text;
- colour, fabric, first-wash status, and known dye transfer;
- ordinary soil, visible stains, or unusual contamination;
- washing and drying methods available to you;
- valuable, structured, embellished, damaged, or unreadable-label items.

Enlarge a label photo, use the matching official symbol guide, or ask someone to read it when needed. If colour vision makes “dark versus light” unreliable, identify groups by garment name, location, or tactile markers instead.

### Execution

1. **Read limits before counting loads.** A crossed-out process is prohibited; temperatures, bars, and dots commonly express a ceiling or treatment strength. Symbol systems vary, so use the official guide that matches the label.
2. **Remove items that do not belong in an ordinary machine load.** Hold anything that forbids washing, requires professional care, has no readable label, or is valuable and structurally complex. This is not a queue for secretly adding later.
3. **Separate incompatible conditions.**
   - whites/lights from new or known colour bleeders;
   - wool/delicates from sturdy cottons;
   - heavily soiled from ordinary clothing;
   - incompatible temperature, agitation, spin, bleach, or drying limits;
   - waterproof, oversized, or highly absorbent items when the machine manual requires a special cycle or separate load.
4. **Stop on serious contamination.** Bodily fluids, hazardous chemicals, pests, or unknown contamination do not enter this ordinary home-laundry flow. Use appropriate local health or professional guidance.
5. **Read both labels before stain treatment.** Proceed only when the garment and product allow it, and test an inconspicuous area. Never mix chlorine bleach with ammonia, acids, or other cleaners.
6. **Name each load by its strictest shared limit.** For example: “lights, cold, gentle, no bleach, no tumble dry.” One stricter garment controls the whole load.
7. **Check pockets and fasteners.** Remove tissues, coins, and electronics; handle zips, hooks, and turning inside-out according to the garment guidance. A tissue in the washer performs extremely successful horizontal scaling.
8. **Assign every item a destination.** Machine load, hand wash, professional care, or hold for more information. There is no undefined “put it over there and forget” state.

### Success

Each load has an item list, the strictest controlling label, exclusions with reasons, stain-treatment conditions, and a drying boundary. You can pick up any garment and name its next destination.

### Common errors and recovery

- **ERR_ONE_RED_SOCK:** Wash a new or known bleeder separately, with compatible darks, or use a label-safe reliable colourfastness check. Do not make the entire load gather evidence.
- **The label is absent or unclear:** Stop and check brand/material information or choose professional care. The gentlest cycle is not a universal recovery mode for unknown garments.
- **A compatible load is very small:** Wait for similar items, hand-wash if permitted, or accept a small load. Do not merge incompatible limits to fill the drum.
- **You notice an item after loading:** Remove it before starting. If the cycle has started and the manual permits safe pausing, follow the machine instructions. Never reach into moving or locked equipment.
- **Incompatible chemicals were mixed:** Leave the exposure area, ventilate, and follow product labels and local emergency guidance. Do not add another chemical to “neutralize” it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This assumes ordinary household clothing and access to the symbol system used on its labels. If gripping, standing, or bending is difficult, sort while seated at a table, use light baskets, or ask for lifting help. Readable limits, not physical strength, own the decision.

Known side effects include temporary clothing clusters and discovering that one garment has requested professional care for years without receiving a response.
