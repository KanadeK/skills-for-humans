## 中文

当你在普通自有手绣小样里**刚发现一两段回针错位或拉线过紧起皱**，想在继续绣前退回正确位置时，加载这份 Skill。结果是有问题的短段被松开/重绣，布未被挑破；或确认线已固定太牢、布受损，停止而不扩伤。它是即时针脚恢复，不替代 H062 的衣服破口修补，也不使用刀片挑历史织物。

### 准备与输入

停针，将唯一在用的针安全放到可见针垫/针盒；在光线下看正面错位与背面线走向，区分是布绷松、线拉太紧还是针落错孔。仅处理近期、可辨的一小段普通绣线；成片旧绣、贵重布、过密多层线、缝在衣物上的针脚不按此简法硬拆。

### 执行

1. **先定要保留的最后一针。** 指出图上正确线条和出错第一针，在背面找出同一根线的路径，别把另一色的固定线当目标。若路径不清，停止并请可信熟手看，不乱剪。
2. **逆向松开最近线迹。** 在还未最终锁尾的情况下，用钝针尖或手指轻提最近线圈，按做针相反顺序让线退回几针；一手轻托布，不从一个孔猛扯整段。布纤维开始被牵起时即停。
3. **按根因恢复张力或位置。** 若绷圈松，先均匀重绷；若线太紧，退到布平整，再用较轻张力重做短针；若落点错，回到导线正确位置。不靠在正面叠一层厚线盖错针。
4. **核对两面和针数。** 正面轮廓或填色回到目标，布不再明显皱、背面无新长松线；针回针盒，确认没有断针头留在布里。无法安全复原时记录停止状态，保留布样作为练习证据。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

短段错误被定位并安全退/重绣，布和邻近线迹完好；或受损风险明确停下。只把皱布压平、线仍勒住，不算修正。

- **退线时布纱被勾起：** 立即停，不用尖刀追线。
- **背面线走向无法分辨：** 放大、换光或请熟手看，不能剪掉一整团。
- **绷圈松导致整片都皱：** 先调绷圈，再只退近期受影响线，不硬拉布边。
- **线已经短到无法再绣：** 先按厂商方法固定旧线，另起兼容线继续。
- **视觉/手部不便：** 大图案、放大灯和可信协助轻托布，由你决定停止点。

### 假设与边界

近期未锁尾线迹较容易逆向退出，已经固定或历史/珍贵织物需更专门的方法。DMC 强调过紧针脚会让布起皱；这里以不伤布为最高边界，不保证完全无痕。

## English

Load this Skill when you have **just noticed one or two short misplaced back-stitch segments or tension puckering** in an owned ordinary hand-embroidery sample. Undo to the sound point before stitching farther. Finish with the segment eased or reworked without tearing cloth, or a clear stop when thread is too secure or fabric is at risk. This is immediate stitch recovery, not H062 clothing-hole repair or blade removal from a historic textile.

### Preparation and inputs

Stop and put the one active needle in a visible holder or case. Under good light inspect the front misplacement and reverse thread path. Decide whether the hoop loosened, thread was pulled too tight, or needle entered the wrong hole. Only a recent traceable short section of ordinary floss is in scope. Dense old work, valuable cloth, multilayer stitching, or a worn garment needs another route.

### Execution

1. **Find the last stitch to keep.** Point to the correct design line and first wrong stitch and follow that thread on the back. Do not mistake another colour's secured thread for the target. If path is unclear, stop for trusted experienced help instead of cutting at random.
2. **Ease recent stitches in reverse.** Before the tail has been finally secured, use a blunt needle tip or fingers to lift the latest loop and withdraw a few stitches in the reverse order of making them. Support cloth with one hand instead of yanking the whole line through one hole. Stop as soon as fibres lift.
3. **Correct the cause.** Retension an uneven hoop before reworking. For a too-tight thread, undo until cloth lies flat and restitch with lighter tension. For a wrong hole, use the original guide line. Do not pile thick front thread over the error.
4. **Inspect both sides and account for needle.** The front outline or fill follows the intended spot, cloth no longer puckers obviously, and the back has no new long loose loops. Return needle to case and confirm no broken point remains in fabric. If safe recovery fails, record the stop and keep the sample as honest practice evidence.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A short error is located and safely undone or reworked with cloth and neighbouring stitches sound, or a material-risk stop is explicit. Merely pressing puckered cloth flat while tight thread remains is not correction.

- **Cloth fibres lift while unpicking:** Stop immediately without a sharp blade chase.
- **Reverse thread route is unclear:** Magnify, change light, or ask a skilled person; do not cut a whole cluster.
- **Loose hoop puckers the whole patch:** Retension first, then undo only recent affected stitches instead of tugging cloth edges.
- **Tail has become too short to restitch:** Secure old thread by maker guidance and begin compatible new floss.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Larger art, magnified lamp, and trusted gentle cloth support help you decide when to stop.

### Assumptions and limits

Recent unsecured stitches may back out gently; secured old work or valuable textiles need specific expertise. DMC notes too-tight stitches can pucker cloth. Protecting the fabric comes first, with no invisible-repair promise.
