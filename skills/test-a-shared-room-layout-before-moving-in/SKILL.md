## 中文

当几件普通家具要一起进入同一房间，而你准备购买、搬入或决定先摆哪里时加载。你要检查**合起来的占地、开合范围和真实使用者的通行**，得到一张能交给搬入者的可行布置图，或保留不合适/未确认的方案。这里只做纸面预检，不教搬动重物、安装、结构修改或长期收纳。

### 准备与输入

取得房间可进入区域的实测宽、长与明显高度限制，记录门、窗、固定设备、踢脚线、散热器、斜墙及已有家具。对每件拟放入的物品，用你手中的实物或准确型号资料确认宽深、突出部件、打开门/抽屉的范围和其说明书要求的使用余量。还要问实际使用者怎样经过、坐下、取物或使用辅助用品；不要套一条对每个家庭都成立的“标准走道宽”。

### 执行

1. **画同一比例的房间与物品。** 用纸格、可访问绘图工具或地面上经许可的轻量标记，画出固定障碍和数件物品的占地。尺寸单位统一，并标出哪些数字是估计或未量到的。
2. **加上每件的工作状态。** 将椅子拉出、柜门或抽屉打开、床边日常使用、清洁取用等实际动作需要的范围画进去；遵守准确产品说明。几件物品都“能放下”并不代表这些动作可以同时发生。
3. **检查人的路径。** 从入口走到每个必须到达的位置，按实际成员和必要辅助方式检查能否通过、转身或取物。开着的门与拉出的抽屉也算占地。不要为了塞下家具遮挡要使用的门或必须保持通畅的通道；建筑或无障碍要求以当地适用规定和有权人员判断。
4. **试至少一种替代摆法。** 若两件互相挡住，调整位置、减少同时进入的物品或改变一件的选择，再从使用状态和路径重查。不要把未经确认的拆卸、固定或“斜着硬塞”画成已解决。
5. **给搬入者一张可读结果。** 标物品名称、第一摆放位置、朝向、门/抽屉方向，以及仍需现场复核的尺寸。请实际使用者或有权代表读回关键路径；纸面通过只表示值得进行现场核对，不保证运输或安装。

### 完成与停止

至少有一份同尺寸依据的组合布置，数件物品在正常使用状态下没有已知互相冲突，实际使用者需要的路径有证据可通行。某一关键尺寸未知、通道被挡或需要改造/危险搬运时，把方案标为未确认或不可行，不先买或搬再赌空间会自己长大。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只把占地矩形排满：** 补画门、抽屉、椅子和人的实际动作范围；重排后再测。
- **两件各自放得下，合起来过不了人：** 按实际使用者的路线删减或换位，不用只看空房照片下结论。
- **商品页面只有近似尺寸：** 找准确型号、实物或服务方资料；关键值仍缺时留“未确认”。
- **某位使用者看不懂草图：** 用可触摸轮廓、大字、口头方向或可信的人按实际路径读回，让其参与判断。
- **搬入者不知道最终朝向：** 在图上补出门/抽屉朝向及临时落位，抵达时再按实际条件确认。

### 假设、替代与副作用

你有权限量房、讨论布置；租住或共享空间可能需先取得相应许可。厂商余量、建筑通道要求和每个人的行动方式不同，本文没有通用厘米数。行动或感官不便时可由愿意且有权限的人量取，你在可访问草图上决策；不靠现场推重物试错。副作用是纸上可能先失去一件“刚好塞得下”的家具。

## English

Load this Skill when several ordinary furniture pieces will share one room and you are about to buy, move or choose their first positions. You will check **their combined footprints, working sweeps and the real occupants' routes**, ending with a usable placement sketch or a clearly rejected or unconfirmed plan. This is a paper check, not a lesson in heavy moving, installation, structural changes or permanent storage.

### Preparation and inputs

Measure the room's usable width and length and any important height limits. Record doors, windows, fixed equipment, skirting boards, heaters, sloped walls and furniture already there. For each proposed piece, use the real item or exact-model information for width, depth, projections, door or drawer movement and any required use clearance. Ask how actual occupants walk through, sit, reach things and use access equipment. There is no single corridor-width number for every home.

### Execution

1. **Draw room and pieces to one scale.** Use gridded paper, an accessible drawing tool or permitted lightweight floor markers. Include fixed obstacles and all proposed footprints. Use consistent units and mark estimated or missing measurements.
2. **Add each working state.** Show a pulled-out chair, open cupboard or drawer, bedside access and ordinary cleaning or retrieval. Follow each product's actual instructions. Several pieces may fit as rectangles but prevent each other from being used.
3. **Test people's routes.** Trace the path from the entrance to every necessary point for the real occupants and any needed aids, including turning and reaching. An open door or drawer also uses space. Do not block a door that must work or a route that must remain clear just to fit an item. Ask the relevant authority about local building or access requirements.
4. **Try at least one alternative.** If pieces collide, move a position, reduce the set entering together or change a candidate, then recheck working states and routes. Unconfirmed disassembly, fixing or forcing an angle is not a solved layout.
5. **Make the result readable to movers.** Mark each piece's name, first position, orientation, door or drawer direction and measurements still needing an on-site check. Have actual occupants or an authorised representative read back key paths. A paper pass warrants an on-site check; it does not guarantee transport or installation.

### Success and stop

You have at least one combined layout with measured inputs. The pieces have no known conflict in ordinary use, and the routes required by actual occupants have evidence that they work. If a critical dimension is missing, a route is blocked or the plan requires building changes or unsafe moving, mark it unconfirmed or infeasible. Do not assume the room will grow after purchase.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only footprint rectangles were packed in:** Add door, drawer, chair and human movement sweeps, then rearrange and test again.
- **Pieces each fit alone but block the occupant together:** Remove or move a piece against the real person's route, rather than deciding from an empty-room photograph.
- **A listing gives approximate dimensions:** Get the exact model, real item or provider data. Leave the plan unconfirmed if a necessary value remains unknown.
- **An occupant cannot use the drawing:** Try tactile outlines, large print, spoken directions or a trusted path readback and include their judgment.
- **Movers cannot tell the intended orientation:** Add the door or drawer direction and temporary placement to the sketch; confirm against the real room on arrival.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

You have permission to measure and discuss the room; rental or shared spaces may require approval. Product clearances, building routes and personal mobility differ, so there is no universal centimetre rule here. If movement or sensory access is limited, a willing authorised person can take measurements while you decide from an accessible sketch. Do not trial-push heavy furniture to find out. The side effect is that a “just fits” piece may leave the plan while it is still only on paper.
