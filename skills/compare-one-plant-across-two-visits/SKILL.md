## 中文

当你**已有一次植物现场记录**，想知道隔一段时间后它是否可见地改变，用这份 Skill 回到同一获准位置，核对是否同一株/同一片，再比较**一项实际可见特征**。结果可以是变化、没有观察到变化或无法确认；这与初次写观察记录的起点不同。本文不推断物种、是否生病、可否食用或变化的因果。

### 准备与输入

带上旧记录的日期、路径、固定参照物、原来能看见的叶/花/果/颜色等特征。查地点现在仍开放，路面和普通天气能否安全到达；不为寻找旧株跨栏、踩花坛、进入私地或摸植物。选择一个旧记录里**确实写过**的比较点，例如“上次没有看见花，今天能否从同一处看见”；旧笔记没记的东西不能补成过去的“无”。若你无法再到原位置，宁可记“视角变了/无法比较”，也不假造连续性。

### 执行

1. **先确认对象。** 在允许的原路径上用固定参照物和旧记录判断是不是同一株；若多株无法区分，明确把单位改成“这片植物”，并只比较旧记录也覆盖的那一片。不要贴私人标签、绑绳或改动自然物来“确保”识别。
2. **核对两次观察条件。** 记今天日期、时段、视角、光线与周围环境有何明显不同；这些会影响你看不看得见细节。并排看旧记录和眼前对象，别靠记忆把上次的颜色说得更鲜艳。
3. **只比较一个特征。** 写“上次____，这次____”，例如可见花、叶色或裸露枝条；明确是“看见/没看见/看不清”，不把没看清当缺失。若有照片，只用你有权使用的旧/新照片并按同一部位比较，不上传敏感地理信息。
4. **把结果分类。** 若两次条件足够接近且特征不同，写“观察到差异”；看起来一样写“本次未见差异”；对象或视角不确定写“无法确认”。这不说明天气或某个原因导致变化，更不能证明植物健康/危险。
5. **留可继续的入口并离开。** 写一句“下次从____位置再看____”，也允许不再跟踪。沿允许路线离开，不采集样本。若现场关闭或已被移走，记录“本次无法观察”，不去封闭区补拍。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有两次带日期、能说明对象/视角关系的记录，围绕**同一个可见特征**给出差异、未见差异或无法确认之一，并诚实写出限制。只换季节就猜“这株一定开过花”不是比较。

- **找不到同一株：** 用参照物重新核对；仍不确定就改记片区或标无法比较，不偷换对象。
- **旧记录只有照片没有日期/位置：** 可以记今天观察，但旧照不能形成可靠时间对照；先从这次建立新基线。
- **第二次光线/角度完全不同：** 标明条件差别，优先比较仍可辨的大特征；其余留“无法确认”。
- **发现虫、封闭或不稳路面：** 停止并从允许路线离开，观察任务不要求贴近/触碰。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

可重复观察依赖你能再次安全、合法地到达同一位置；季节和维护可能改变现场。视觉不便时可用官方可及路径、放大或可信协助，并标对照依据来自谁。一次前后差别不足以断言原因，甚至“没有看见花”也不等于该株从未开花。此笔记是个人观察，不声称符合正式物候项目的物种鉴定或提交标准。

## English

Use this Skill when you **already have a field note for one plant** and want to see whether anything visible has changed after another visit. Return to the same permitted viewpoint, verify the same individual or patch, and compare **one actually visible feature**. The result may be a change, no observed change, or uncertainty. That starting point differs from making an initial note. This Skill does not infer species, illness, edibility, or the cause of change.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring the old date, path and landmark, and a leaf, flower, fruit, or colour feature that was actually recorded. Check the site is still open and the path and ordinary weather allow safe access. Do not cross a barrier, trample a bed, enter private land, or touch the plant to recover the old view. Pick one feature **actually noted before**, such as “I saw no flower from this viewpoint; can I see one now?” A missing note is not a past “no.” If you cannot reach the original position, record “viewpoint changed / comparison unavailable” rather than invent continuity.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the subject.** From the allowed path use landmark and old note to judge whether this is the same individual. If several cannot be separated, explicitly use “this patch” only when the old record covered the same patch. Do not tag, tie, or alter a plant to make it identifiable.
2. **Check both viewing conditions.** Note today's date, time, viewpoint, light, and obvious setting differences. These affect what details can be seen. Put the old note beside the real plant rather than improve the remembered colour in your head.
3. **Compare one feature.** Write “before ____; now ____” for a visible flower, leaf colour, bare branch, or similar point. Distinguish seen, not seen, and unclear; unclear does not mean absent. If using photos, compare your authorised old and new images of the same part without uploading sensitive location details.
4. **Classify the result.** With sufficiently comparable conditions and different features, write “observed difference.” If they look alike, “no difference seen this time.” For uncertain plant or viewpoint, “cannot confirm.” None proves weather or another cause, or a health or hazard verdict about the plant.
5. **Leave a next path and exit.** Write “next time look at ____ from ____,” or decide not to follow further. Leave by the permitted route and collect nothing. For a closed site or a removed plant, record “not observable this visit” rather than enter a restricted area for a photo.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have two dated records whose subject and viewpoint relation you can explain and one **same visible feature** classified as changed, not changed as observed, or uncertain, with limits noted. Guessing that a plant must have flowered because seasons changed is not a comparison.

- **You cannot find the same plant:** Recheck landmarks. If still uncertain, record a patch or mark no valid comparison rather than substitute another.
- **The old record is a photo without date or location:** You may note today's observation, but it is not a reliable timed comparison; treat today as a fresh baseline.
- **Light or angle differs strongly:** Mark the difference and compare only a still-legible broad feature, leaving the rest uncertain.
- **Insects, closure, or unstable ground appear:** Stop and leave by the allowed path. Observation requires no close approach or touch.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Repeated observation depends on returning legally and safely to the same place; seasons and maintenance can change it. For sight access, use an official accessible path, magnification, or trusted help and label whose evidence was used. A before/after difference alone does not establish cause. Even “no flower seen” does not mean the plant never flowered. This is a personal note, not species identification or an approved submission to a formal phenology project.
