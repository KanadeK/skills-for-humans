## 中文

当你已有**一条不惊扰鸟而写下的真实可见特征记录**，现在想用适用本地与季节的图鉴核物种时，加载这份 Skill。结果是有明确支持和反证的物种候选，或诚实标“未能判种”；不是重新去追鸟。E018 的目视记录是原始证据，本篇才进行解释。图鉴照片、自动识别建议、地点常见度都不能单独把不清楚的鸟变成确定物种。

### 准备与输入

打开自己的笔记：体形、大小、色纹、喙/尾、行为、生境、日期和光线限制。选当地可用、对应季节的可靠图鉴/鸟类机构资料，注意雌雄、幼鸟、换羽形态可能不同；别拿异国常见种硬套。资料可为纸图鉴或后台公开网页；无网时保持笔记，不需注册新账号。敏感巢位/稀有鸟具体地点不上传公开平台。

### 执行

1. **先按四类线索缩小组别。** 用体形与大小、整体色纹、行为、生境筛出少数可能组别，不从单一亮色直接选种名。与日期/季节和范围图相冲突的候选标疑点。
2. **逐个比“能排除”的细节。** 对两个近似候选查喙形、尾长、翼斑/眼圈等笔记里**确实看见**的细节；没有看见时不能把图鉴那枚花纹填进原记录。反证重要：图鉴说应有的明显标记，你确定没有看见才可谨慎排除。
3. **把结论分级。** 证据充分且没有重要冲突时记录“较有把握”候选、所用图鉴和匹配线索；若多个候选同样可能，留到类群或“未识别”，别用自动应用首位建议当最终事实。
4. **保存原记录与复核入口。** 原始所见与后加的推断分栏存，写还缺什么线索及以后可在合法安全地点自然观察；不回现场诱叫、靠近巢或追踪同一鸟补照片。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

笔记中能看出原证据、候选/未知的理由和图鉴来源；无把握仍保留类群是完整判断。只写一个没有核对线索的物种名称，不算复核。

- **图鉴候选地区不符：** 查本地与季节资料，不能靠颜色像就强认。
- **两种鸟都很像且缺关键斑：** 标“未确定”，下一次自然观察再找，不追鸟。
- **照片颜色受逆光影响：** 回到体形/行为与原光线限制。
- **软件给出与笔记矛盾的首选：** 软件只是候选，人工核实际线索，不照单上报。
- **视觉/语言不便：** 可访问图鉴、可信协助读描述，保留谁观察、谁解释的来源。

### 假设与边界

鸟类种群分布和羽色随地点、季节、年龄变化，图鉴仍是证据辅助。本文不鉴定保护等级、不公开敏感地点，也不把一次观测用于科学种群结论。

## English

Load this Skill when a **real non-disturbing visual field note already exists** and you want to compare it with a guide suitable for your region and season. Finish with a species candidate whose supporting and contrary clues are explicit, or an honest unresolved ID. This interprets E018's original observation; it is not a reason to chase the bird again. A photo, app suggestion, or local popularity alone cannot make an unclear view certain.

### Preparation and inputs

Open your note for size, shape, pattern, bill/tail, behaviour, habitat, date, and light limits. Choose a credible guide for the actual region and season, allowing differences in sex, age, and moult. Do not impose a common bird from another country. A paper guide or public webpage works; offline notes need no new account. Do not post exact nest or rare-bird sites publicly.

### Execution

1. **Narrow the group with four clue types.** Use form/size, overall pattern, behaviour, and habitat to select a few groups rather than a name from one bright colour. Mark range/season conflicts as doubts.
2. **Compare discriminating details.** For two lookalikes, use bill, tail, wingbar, eyering, or other clues you **actually recorded**. Do not fill unseen marks into the original note from a guide. A major expected mark can exclude a candidate only when you truly observed its absence.
3. **Grade the result.** With sufficient matching evidence and no serious conflict, record a reasonably supported candidate with the guide and matching clues. When multiple remain plausible, stop at a group or “unidentified.” A first app suggestion is not independent proof.
4. **Preserve source note and a later entry.** Keep original sight and later inference in separate fields, noting the missing clue for a future natural observation from a lawful safe spot. Do not return to lure, approach a nest, or pursue the individual for a photo.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The record distinguishes original evidence, candidate/unknown reason, and guide source. Keeping an uncertain group is a complete judgment. A species label with no compared clue is not verification.

- **Candidate's range does not fit:** Check local seasonal material, not colour resemblance alone.
- **Two lookalikes need a missing mark:** Record “uncertain” and wait for later natural evidence without pursuit.
- **Photo colours are distorted by backlight:** Return to form, behaviour, and recorded light limits.
- **Software's top result contradicts your note:** Treat it as a lead, verify independently, and do not auto-report.
- **Vision or language is limited:** Accessible guides and trusted description help can retain who observed versus who interpreted.

### Assumptions and limits

Range and plumage vary with place, season, and age, so guides assist evidence rather than guarantee ID. This does not decide conservation status, publish sensitive sites, or infer population from one sighting.
