## 中文

当你已有一套模块化衣柜或置物系统，想加抽屉、篮筐、门或层板时，加载这份 Skill。目标是购前形成**与已有框架准确规格相容、且关键配件齐全**的清单；它不组装、不钻墙，也不把“同品牌同系列”当作全部兼容证据。

### 准备与输入

先从现有柜体铭牌、订单或说明书取得准确系统、型号/年代版本、柜框宽度、深度和可用高度，确认你有权查看但不拆卸现有柜体。写出要增加的具体功能，例如“加一只可拉出的篮筐”。准备制造商针对该框架的配件兼容表、每件拟买部件的完整货号和“包装包含/另售”清单。型号或尺寸不明时先标未知。

### 执行

1. **确认宿主框架。** 只在准确的系列、版本、宽度和深度下查配件；另一代或更深的同系列框架可能使用不同尺寸。先核现有柜体，不先把网店里的配件加满购物车。
2. **查想要的动作。** 例如篮筐要能拉出、门要有允许的开合范围。列出完成该动作所需的主体、导轨、支架、门铰等部件；某些产品页只卖主体，轨道另售。
3. **逐件核兼容表。** 将每件完整货号与框架的宽深高及相邻部件核对；门、抽屉可能互相占用同一位置。不要用宣传组合图代替准确型号的“适用框架”字段。
4. **核包装与数量。** 阅读每件到底含几个部件、是否左右成对、固定件是否随包。缺失配件要写成“另需确认/另需购买”，不默认照片里的东西全在盒内。负载和防倾倒条件仍按准确系统说明执行；无法满足的安装前提会使该组合暂不成立。
5. **输出一份有证据的清单。** 写“已有框架 → 要完成的动作 → 相容主体与必需配件货号/数量 → 包装包含与缺口 → 可考虑、不相容或未确认”。有一个关键连接件无法确认，就不宣称组合已齐全。

### 成功与停止

你能指出每一件必要部件与现有框架的适配依据、数量和取得方式，才完成购前核对。墙体固定、重载、儿童防倾倒或改造旧柜需要按制造商说明和合适专业支持判断；本 Skill 在购物清单处停止，不指导安装。没有准确框架资料时可暂不买，而不做“差不多”拼装。

### 常见报错与补救

- **同系列就认为都能装：** 回到框架宽度、深度、版本和每件适用清单，不用品牌名替代尺寸。
- **篮筐买了，轨道缺席：** 从“包装包含”与“另售”逐项补齐；未能确认轨道型号时暂停该候选。
- **门和抽屉单独都合适，组合后互相挡：** 查看厂家组合图、预留位置和开合条件；换布局或删减部件。
- **高处柜体标识看不清或不便测量：** 使用已有订单/说明书、放大照片或请有权限的人代量；不拆固定件找型号。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程只处理普通模块家具的购买组合，不代表安装安全验收。品牌可能改版，地区名称/尺寸单位也可能不同，按你实际框架和当地在售货号核对。若厂商不再提供可靠适配表，向其支持渠道核对或换独立于旧系统的方案。你可能少买一只暂时无法使用的抽屉。

## English

Load this Skill when you already own a modular wardrobe or storage frame and want to add a drawer, basket, door, or shelf. The outcome is a **complete, frame-compatible list of required parts** before purchase. It does not assemble or drill anything, and “same brand and series” is not sufficient evidence of fit.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the existing frame's label, order, or manual to record its exact system, model or generation, width, depth, and usable height. Inspect only what you may access without dismantling fixed furniture. Name the desired function, such as “one pull-out basket.” Get the maker's fit chart for that frame, every candidate's full part number, and its included-versus-sold-separately list. Keep an unidentified frame or dimension unknown.

### Execution

1. **Identify the host frame.** Search add-ons under the exact series, generation, width, and depth. Another era or deeper frame with the same series name may use different parts. Establish the existing frame before filling an online cart.
2. **Map the desired action.** A basket must pull out; a door must open in its available space. List the main part and any rails, brackets, or hinges required for that action. A product page can sell the basket alone while its rails are separate.
3. **Check each component.** Compare every full part number with the frame's width, depth, height, and neighbouring parts. Doors and drawers may compete for the same space. A staged product photo is not an exact “fits this frame” table.
4. **Check contents and quantity.** Read how many pieces each package contains, whether left/right parts come as a pair, and which fixings are included. Mark missing parts “must confirm/get separately” rather than assuming everything pictured is boxed. Follow exact-system load and anti-tip conditions; an unavailable required installation condition leaves the combination unconfirmed.
5. **Output an evidenced list.** Record “existing frame → desired action → compatible main and supporting part numbers/quantities → included and missing pieces → consider, incompatible, or unconfirmed.” One unknown essential connector prevents a claim that the set is complete.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when every required part has a documented match to your frame, a count, and a way to obtain it. Wall fixing, heavy loads, child tip-over protection, or modifying an old frame must follow maker instructions and appropriate professional support. This Skill stops at the shopping list and does not install. Without exact frame evidence, waiting is a valid result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The same series name was treated as a universal fit:** Return to frame width, depth, generation, and each part's fit table.
- **The basket arrived without rails:** Rebuild the list from included and sold-separately items. Pause if the rail's exact part number remains unknown.
- **A door and drawer fit separately but collide together:** Check the maker's combination drawing and opening space; change layout or remove a part.
- **A high frame's mark is unreadable or measuring is difficult:** Use an old order/manual, enlarge a photo, or ask someone with access to measure. Do not dismantle fixed pieces just to find a number.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary modular-furniture buying check, not installation safety acceptance. Brands change generations and markets use different names or units; verify your real frame and local current part numbers. If the maker no longer publishes a dependable fit chart, ask support or choose an option independent of the old system. You may buy one fewer drawer that would otherwise remain an inert box.
