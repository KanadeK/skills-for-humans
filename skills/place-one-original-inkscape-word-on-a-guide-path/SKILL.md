## 中文

你的一枚原创图标需要很短的自写词沿上方弧线走，而不是用普通横排文字挡住主体时，可把文字放到导向路径上。完成后字母顺序、方向和小尺寸可读性都能核；路径本身可以不显示，但不能随手删除。字体使用权与跨设备缺字问题要另行确认。

### 准备与输入

保存 SVG，写一个简短原创词，确认不冒用品牌或认证文字。选择本机有使用权的字体和简单曲线，先在页面上估计文字长度能否容纳；如果目标图标非常小，文字可能根本不适合加入。

### 执行

1. 画一条导向路径，在路径附近输入短词，先检查字形大小和方向。
2. 选中文字与路径，执行 Text > Put on Path，核字基线跟随计划曲线。
3. 调文字对齐和路径形状使字间不拥挤；若不想看到导向线，设其填色/描边为无而保留对象。
4. 缩到目标尺寸读完整词，保存重开；若跨设备字体不稳，另做审阅副本并保留可编辑原稿。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

原创短词沿曲线位置可读，导向路径和文字仍可分别编辑，字体限制已标明。

- **文字反向或倒置：** 核路径方向并调整，不靠逐字旋转。
- **路径删除后字跳回：** 恢复导向路径，仅隐藏其描边。
- **小尺寸读不清：** 删去文字或增大图标用途尺寸，不硬缩字。

### 假设与边界

只处理一条原创短词的路径排布，不保证字体嵌入、商标许可、阅读可及性或专业标志设计。

## English

Use this when a short word of your own should follow a gentle curve above an original icon instead of a straight label crowding the body. Finish with correct letter order, orientation and target-size readability. The guide path may be invisible but must remain. Font rights and cross-device availability require separate checking.

### Preparation and inputs

Save and draft one short original word without claiming a brand or certification. Use a font you are allowed to use and a simple curve. Estimate whether the word fits the available width; tiny icons may be better without text.

### Execution

1. Draw the guide path and type the word near it, checking type size and direction.
2. Select text and path, use Text > Put on Path and inspect the baseline.
3. Adjust text alignment and path shape to avoid crowded letters; hide guide fill/stroke rather than deleting the path.
4. Read the full word at target size, save and reopen; if font substitution is a concern, make a review copy while preserving the editable master.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The original short word remains readable along the curve, with editable text and path and stated font limits.

- **Text reversed:** Inspect and reverse path direction rather than rotating letters individually.
- **Text falls off:** Restore the guide path and hide its stroke only.
- **Unreadable small:** Remove text or use a larger icon placement instead of shrinking type further.

### Assumptions and limits

This places one original word on a path, not font embedding, trademark clearance, accessibility certification or professional logo design.
