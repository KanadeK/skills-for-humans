## 中文

当你和一两位或几位同伴愿意共同学习，但目前只有“有空一起学”这句话时，加载这份 Skill。结果是**一次**经参与者同意、符合课程规则的学习会安排：做什么、怎么做、各自准备什么、何时何地结束。真正运行学习会归本域另一份 Skill；安排发出去不证明内容已学会。

### 准备与输入

先查课程/教师对共同做题、共享答案和作业独立性的说明；不清楚就问正式渠道。选一项大家都真实需要的普通学习范围，准备一项能在会后检查的产出，如“各自先解两道允许合作的练习，会上比较思路”和“列出三处需要问教师的概念”。征询参与者的时间、场地、交通、听力/视觉与线上可及条件，不假设所有人能用同一软件。

### 执行

1. **把模糊愿望缩成一次产出。** 写“这次结束时，每人能留下什么”而非“复习整门课”。区分允许共同讨论的练习与必须独立提交的作业；有规则疑点先隔离，不把聚会变成答案交换。
2. **选一个适当规模与时段。** 只邀真正愿意参加、能有发言与独立练习机会的人；若太多人，拆成小组。给开始、结束和中间核心练习留现实时间，必要时短休息；人数不是参与质量的指标。
3. **定学习方式与准备责任。** 约定带哪段可分享材料、每人会前先尝试什么、会上是独立作答后比较、互相解释，还是整理待核问题。分配轻量角色：谁发地点/链接、谁看时间、谁记未解问题；可以轮换，不让一个人负责全部。
4. **发送一条完整邀请并收实际答复。** 包含目标、允许活动、时间/地点或获准线上入口、需要带的材料、可及替代和退出办法。对方未回复就是待确认，不把邀请人数当到场人数；若关键参与者不同意时间或方式，就调整一次或取消，不强迫。
5. **留一张会前小卡。** 记已确认参与者、议程、每人准备事项、课程合作边界及结束后要核的产出。若只剩你一人，可以改为个人学习，但不称为“已组织小组”。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

至少两人确认了一次明确目标、时间/地点、允许方式、准备责任和停点的安排；或你已诚实标出邀请未成行并改用个人学习。群聊里有“哈哈可以”但无时间与任务，不算约好。

- **目标是“把课全复习完”：** 缩到一个章节问题、几道允许的练习或一张待核清单。
- **课程不允许合作当前作业：** 改用允许的旧题、概念解释或自编练习，并问清边界。
- **大家只想边聊边看资料：** 保留社交部分，但给学习环节一项可观察结果；若没有，就如实称聚会。
- **有人不能到同一地点/使用同一平台：** 换可访问地点、许可的远程方式或异步准备；参与者同意后更新安排。
- **确认人数不足：** 不假装成行，发取消/改期通知并保留可独做的准备。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

课程政策、场地与参与者时间各不相同；本篇不指定统一人数、时长或软件。纸质卡、可访问消息或语音安排都可用，前提是参与者能核到实际时间、地点、任务与合作边界。一个清楚的邀约会产生答复和可能的改期，不产生已掌握知识的证明。

## English

Load this Skill when you and a few peers want to study together but have only said, “Let's study sometime.” Produce **one** agreed session plan within course rules: what to do, how, what each person prepares, and when and where to stop. Actually running the session is a separate Skill. Sending an invitation does not prove learning happened.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the course or teacher's rules for joint problem work, answer sharing, and independent assignments; ask through the official channel if unclear. Pick one ordinary scope the participants genuinely need. Choose a checkable output such as “each person tries two collaboration-permitted problems before comparing approaches” or “list three concepts to ask the teacher about.” Ask about time, place, transport, hearing, vision, and online access needs rather than assuming everyone can use one app.

### Execution

1. **Turn a wish into one output.** State what each person could leave with rather than “review the whole course.” Separate permitted discussion from assignments that must be submitted independently. Isolate policy uncertainty before it becomes answer exchange.
2. **Choose workable size and time.** Invite willing people who can each speak and practise; split an oversized group. Set start, end, core practice time, and a break if useful. Headcount is not a measure of learning quality.
3. **Choose method and preparation duties.** Agree which shareable material to bring, what each person tries beforehand, and whether the session will use independent attempts then comparison, explanation, or unresolved-question listing. Assign light roles: who sends the place or link, who watches time, who records open questions. Rotate rather than making one person carry everything.
4. **Send a complete invitation and wait for real replies.** Include goal, permitted activity, time and place or approved online entry, needed materials, access alternative, and a way to decline. No reply is pending, not attendance. If essential participants cannot accept the time or method, adjust once or cancel without pressure.
5. **Leave a short pre-session card.** Record confirmed people, agenda, preparations, collaboration boundary, and the output to check at the end. If only you remain, convert it to individual study rather than calling a group organised.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

At least two people confirmed a session with a concrete goal, time/place, permitted method, preparation roles, and stopping point, or you honestly marked that no group formed and switched to solo study. “Sure, maybe” in a chat without a task or time is not a confirmed session.

- **The goal is “review everything”:** Narrow it to one chapter question, a few permitted exercises, or a list of unknowns.
- **The course forbids sharing work on the current assignment:** Use permitted old questions, concept explanations, or self-made practice and confirm the boundary.
- **Everyone wants to chat while browsing notes:** Social time can remain, but give the study portion one observable result. Without it, call the gathering what it is.
- **Someone cannot use the venue or platform:** Choose an accessible place, permitted remote method, or asynchronous preparation and update only with consent.
- **Not enough people confirm:** Do not claim the group is happening. Notify invitees of cancellation or change and keep any solo preparation.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Course rules, venues, and schedules vary. This does not impose one group size, duration, or app. Paper, accessible messages, or spoken planning work when participants can verify actual time, place, tasks, and collaboration limits. A clear invitation produces replies and perhaps rescheduling, not proof of mastery.
