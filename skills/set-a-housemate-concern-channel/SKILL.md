## 中文

当你与同住者正在制定共用区域约定，或发现现有约定缺少提出问题和修改的方法时加载。你们要确认**普通问题怎样提出、怎样知道对方收到、何时复议和何时求助适当第三方**。这不要求任何人同意一项具体安排，不处理骚扰、威胁、紧急危险、法律争议或设备维修。

### 准备与输入

请所有受影响的同住者选择自己实际会看的沟通方式，确认何时适合谈、哪些事需要私下而非群聊、是否有管理方或双方都接受的协调者。不要假设每个人都能用同一 App、语言或声音通话。现有设施使用、清洁约定可作讨论对象，但这里不重写那些具体分工。

### 执行

1. **区分普通问题和需立即转交的情况。** 普通问题可以是某个安排执行不了、共用区域持续被占或约定文字有歧义。明显危险设施、威胁或紧急情况走实际管理或当地适用帮助路径，不在室友群里等投票。
2. **约好提出方法。** 选每人能接收且能在合适时机使用的渠道；用“观察到什么 → 影响哪个使用 → 想讨论哪个改变”开头。避免公开私人细节或在对方无法回应的群里给人贴标签。
3. **把收到与同意分开。** 约定一个现实的确认方式和双方可讨论的时间窗；“我看到了”只表示收到，不表示接受提案。未读、无法沟通或缺席的参与者保留待确认，不把沉默解释成赞成。
4. **记录小范围决定。** 讨论后写下具体试行变更、涉及区域、何时开始、谁受影响、何时复看；若没有共识，记录各自说法和暂行不改变的部分。新规则不能悄悄覆盖没参与的人。
5. **设复议与求助出口。** 到约定日期问受影响者新方式是否可执行；若仍无法达成或有人无法安全沟通，按实际住宅条件联系双方认可的协调者或管理方，不在公共渠道升级羞辱。

### 完成与停止

同住者知道用什么渠道讲具体普通问题、对方怎样确认收到、哪些改变需要明示同意、何时复看以及哪里求助；未解决项保持未解决。出现危险、威胁、严重隐私侵害或正式权利争议时退出本 Skill，寻求适当当地或专业支持。

### 常见报错与补救

- **群里发了消息没人答：** 核对渠道是否真能到达相关人，换其接受的方式；沉默仍是未确认。
- **一方说“收到”却被记成“同意”：** 更正记录为收到，单独问具体提案能否接受。
- **讨论变成评价某个人：** 返回一次可观察事件、实际影响与具体待改点，换一个能平静交流的时间。
- **有人不便口头或文字沟通：** 用对方可接受的翻译、语音、文字或可信协调者，给足回读机会。
- **只有两个人决定了所有人的用法：** 先只试行不影响缺席者的部分，并找其确认其余内容。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这用于普通、可安全讨论的共住安排，不充当法律、心理危机或人身安全处理路径。沟通速度、文化、语言、障碍和住宅规则不同，不制定统一的回复小时数。纸面、面对面或数字渠道都可以，关键是相关人实际收到且能表达不同意。副作用是冰箱门不必背负匿名纸条的治理职责。

## English

Load this Skill when making common-area arrangements with housemates or when an existing arrangement has no way to raise and change a problem. You will agree on **how ordinary concerns are raised, how receipt is confirmed, when to review and when suitable outside help is sought**. Nobody has to accept a particular proposal. Harassment, threats, immediate danger, legal disputes and equipment repair are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Ask every affected housemate which channel they actually check, when a conversation is workable, which topics should be private rather than in a group, and whether a building contact or mutually accepted facilitator exists. Do not assume the same app, language or voice call works for everyone. Existing use and cleaning agreements can supply examples; this Skill does not silently rewrite their duties.

### Execution

1. **Separate ordinary concerns from urgent handoff.** An ordinary concern might be an infeasible routine, repeated common-area blockage or ambiguous wording. A dangerous facility, threat or immediate situation goes to the actual building or applicable local help channel, not a housemate vote.
2. **Choose how to raise it.** Agree on a channel each person can receive and use at a suitable time. Start with “what I observed → which use it affects → which change I want to discuss.” Do not expose private detail or label someone in a group they cannot answer in.
3. **Separate receipt from consent.** Agree on a realistic acknowledgement and discussion window. “I saw it” means received, not accepted. An unread message, inaccessible channel or absent participant remains unconfirmed; silence is not approval.
4. **Record a limited decision.** After talking, write the trial change, affected area, start, people affected and review date. Without agreement, record each account and which existing parts remain for now. A new rule must not quietly bind somebody who did not join.
5. **Revisit and name a help route.** On the agreed date, ask affected people whether the trial works. If disagreement remains or safe discussion is not possible, use an appropriate facilitator or management contact under the real housing arrangement rather than public shaming.

### Success and stop

Housemates know which channel carries a specific ordinary concern, how to confirm it was received, which changes need explicit agreement, when to review and where to seek help. Unresolved stays unresolved. Exit this Skill for danger, threats, serious privacy harm or formal rights disputes and seek appropriate local or professional support.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A group message gets no answer:** Check whether it actually reaches the people concerned and use a channel they accept. Silence stays unconfirmed.
- **“Received” is recorded as “agreed”:** Correct the record to receipt and ask separately about the specific proposal.
- **The discussion becomes a judgment of someone:** Return to one observable event, its effect and a concrete change to discuss at a calmer time.
- **Someone cannot use speech or text as proposed:** Use an agreed translation, audio, text or trusted facilitator, with a chance for them to read back.
- **Two people decide a rule for everyone:** Try only the part that does not bind absent residents and ask for their input on the rest.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is for ordinary shared-living matters that can be discussed safely, not legal, mental-health-crisis or personal-safety guidance. Response pace, culture, language, disability and building rules differ; no universal reply-hour limit is imposed. Paper, face-to-face and digital channels can all work when affected people actually receive them and can disagree. The refrigerator door retires from its second job as an anonymous complaint board.
