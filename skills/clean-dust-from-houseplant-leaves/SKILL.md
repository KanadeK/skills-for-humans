## 中文

当一盆普通室内植物的叶片落了可见灰尘，而你要做的是**清洁叶面**时，加载这份 Skill。结果是适合擦拭的叶片去掉普通灰尘、叶片没有被折损，清洁工具不把疑似问题带到下一盆。虫害处理不由这份 Skill完成。

### 准备与输入

确认品种资料是否允许湿布擦拭，叶面是否光滑、坚实；带绒毛、蜡粉、脆弱表面的叶片可能不适合直接湿擦。先查看是否有虫体、蛛丝、黏液、扩散斑点或其他不明问题；出现这些迹象，先转入隔离和辨认，不跨盆共用抹布。大盆或高处盆器先移到可及且稳定的位置，必要时请人协助，避免水流到插座。

### 执行

1. **先分清普通灰尘与异常。** 轻看叶正反面；若只是松散灰尘，按品种允许方式清洁。疑似虫卵、霉斑或黏性分泌物不靠反复擦拭“确认已治好”。
2. **准备轻湿而不滴水的软布。** 用清水，不加洗洁精、酒精、牛奶或叶面上光剂。先在一片不显眼且许可的叶上轻试，确认不留下损伤。
3. **托住叶片轻擦。** 从叶柄向叶尖清洁表面，需要时轻看叶背；换到干净布面。不要用力拽叶或把水冲进盆土、家具和电器。
4. **一盆一套干净接触面。** 擦完后将布清洗并晾干；要清下一盆时换干净布，尤其不能把刚接触异常叶面的布带过去。
5. **复核与收尾。** 看是否已去除普通灰尘、叶片仍完整、没有积水。遇到不明斑点或清洁后迅速再出现黏性痕迹，停止日常擦拭并转入观察/求助。

### 成功与停止

你能指出清洁前后的普通灰尘差别，叶片无明显新损伤，布和水已收好。若品种不适合湿擦或出现疑似病虫，留下观察记录并停止，不强行取得“亮叶”效果。

### 常见报错与补救

- **叶片带绒或表面粉层：** 查品种的清洁说明，不套用湿布；找适用的软刷或专业建议。
- **擦到黏性物或会动的小点：** 停止跨盆清洁，记录并隔离疑似盆栽；不要用同一块布继续工作。
- **叶被拉弯：** 改为一手轻托、一手擦；太脆弱就停止。
- **布很脏还继续擦：** 换洁净面或新布，结束后按布料说明清洗。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里清理普通室内盆栽的可擦叶片，不处理植物病害或喷药。明尼苏达大学推广服务建议用清水和软布去尘，并避免工具把虫害传给别的植物。手部操作困难可请人代擦、用稳固低位工作台；视力受限可请人确认叶面是否有异常。副作用是一块需要洗的布，而不是植物健康证明。

## English

Load this Skill when ordinary dust is visible on an indoor plant and your task is to **clean suitable leaves**. Finish with dust removed without breaking foliage or carrying a suspect problem to the next plant. Pest treatment is a different task.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether species guidance permits damp-cloth cleaning and whether leaves are smooth and sturdy. Fuzzy, waxy, powder-coated, or delicate leaves may need another method. First look for insects, webbing, sticky traces, spreading spots, or another unknown issue. If present, move to isolation and identification; do not share a cloth across plants. Bring a high or heavy pot to a safe accessible surface with help when needed, away from sockets.

### Execution

1. **Separate dust from an abnormal sign.** Inspect both sides gently. Proceed when it is loose ordinary dust and the species permits cleaning. Repeated wiping does not prove that eggs, mould, or sticky secretions were cured.
2. **Dampen a soft cloth lightly.** Use plain water, not detergent, alcohol, milk, or leaf-shine product. Test gently on one inconspicuous permitted leaf and stop if it marks or damages the surface.
3. **Support each leaf while wiping.** Work from stem toward tip; inspect undersides if appropriate and change to a clean part of the cloth. Do not tug leaves or spill water into equipment, furniture, or the pot.
4. **Use a clean contact surface per plant.** Wash and dry the used cloth. Start another plant with a clean cloth, especially after touching a leaf with any abnormal sign.
5. **Review and close.** Check that ordinary dust is gone, leaves remain intact, and no water is pooled. Stop routine wiping and record or seek advice if unexplained spots remain or sticky traces promptly return.

### Success and stop

You can see the difference in ordinary dust, with no obvious new leaf damage, and the cloth and water are put away. If the species cannot be wiped or a possible pest/disease sign appears, stop with a record instead of forcing a glossy-leaf result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Leaves are fuzzy or powder-coated:** Check species instructions; do not apply a damp cloth by default. Seek a suitable soft-brush method or advice.
- **You find sticky material or moving specks:** Stop cross-plant cleaning, record the sign, and isolate the suspect pot. Do not keep using the same cloth.
- **A leaf bends under pressure:** Support it lightly with one hand, or stop if too fragile.
- **The cloth is carrying dust between leaves:** Switch to a clean surface or cloth, then launder it as its fabric permits.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This removes ordinary dust from wipeable indoor foliage; it does not diagnose disease or apply pesticides. University of Minnesota Extension recommends plain water and a soft cloth and warns against transferring insects between plants. If grip is difficult, work on a stable low surface or ask for help. If vision limits inspection, have someone describe unusual marks. The side effect is a cloth to wash, not proof of plant health.
