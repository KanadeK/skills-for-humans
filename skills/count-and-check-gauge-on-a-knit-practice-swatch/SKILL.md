## 中文

当你有一片**已经织出来的手织样片**，想核针数、行数和它与一个明确密度目标的差别时，加载这份 Skill。结果是可回算的测量记录及“继续用原针/换针再织样/样片太小无法判断”之一。它不凭标签推荐针号保证成衣大小，也不是 H066 的实际衣物试穿。

### 准备与输入

确认目标来自你自己的学习设定或实际获准图样，写明“每多宽有几针、几行”，单位不要混淆；如果只有针号而无密度目标，就只记录实测不判匹配。样片应有足够宽、行数和较平稳的中央区域，避开卷边与起收针边；8–12 针的微小教学片通常不足以准确套用 10 厘米/4 英寸图样目标。备尺、可读光线和纱线/针径标签。

### 执行

1. **先复查活针数。** 若样还在针上，逐圈点数，标记缺/多的具体位置；有掉针先止住，不继续拿错误行推算密度。已收针样片可从中央数可辨的 V 形针柱与行。
2. **选中央测量跨度。** 将样片自然平放，不拉伸到理想尺寸；在足够宽的中部量一个明确长度，数其内针柱与行数，写下跨度和单位。边缘卷曲或纱线太蓬看不清时记录限制，放大/请可信协助，不自造整数。
3. **只和同口径目标比。** 把实测按比例换算到目标长度，区分“针/10 厘米”和“行/10 厘米”；若目标规定洗后密度，而样片未按标签洗整，标记当前只是洗前预测。不同针法也不直接相比。
4. **选下一次动作。** 实测针数过多（太密）可用较大针再织新样，过少（太松）可试较小针；仅在纱线和目标允许时做。差距不确定、样片太小或材质变化待洗整时先补证据，不在原样上硬拉出合格数字。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

记录含纱线/针径、针法、实际跨度、针/行数、洗前或洗后状态和下一步判断；没有足够样片时明确不可判也成立。只抄包装密度不量自己的样，不算完成。

- **把 4 英寸和 10 厘米混用：** 重写单位并按同一跨度换算，别直接比较两个数字。
- **只数边缘针：** 换中央较平区域或织更大的样。
- **样片卷曲严重：** 先按纱线标签整理并等干后重测，不能靠拉伸伪造密度。
- **数针时发现掉圈：** 先固定/恢复掉针，再重数受影响的区。
- **视力/手指辨圈不便：** 大号浅色纱、放大与可信协助指认，由你核对记录及单位。

### 假设与边界

密度取决于纱、针、针法、个人松紧及护理状态。针号调整方向只是一种新样片试验，不是成衣合身或产品性能保证。

## English

Load this Skill when you have a **real knitted swatch** and need to count stitches and rows against an explicit gauge target. Finish with traceable measurements and one decision: keep the needle, remake with a different needle, or admit the sample is too small to judge. A yarn-label recommendation does not guarantee garment size, and this is not H066's clothing fit check.

### Preparation and inputs

Write the target from your own study or a permitted real pattern as stitches and rows per stated width, keeping units explicit. Without a gauge target, record only your measurement, not a match. The swatch needs a wide enough fairly stable centre away from cast-on, bind-off, and curling edges. An 8–12-stitch teaching strip is usually too small for a reliable 10 cm/4 in pattern gauge. Have ruler, readable light, and yarn/needle record.

### Execution

1. **Check live stitch count.** On a needle, count loops one by one and note a missing or extra location. Secure a dropped stitch before using that row for gauge. On a bound sample, count visible central V columns and rows.
2. **Measure a central span.** Lay the sample naturally flat without stretching toward a desired answer. In a large enough centre, mark a clear measured length and count stitches and rows within it. Record span and units. If curling or fluffy yarn prevents counting, note the limitation, magnify or ask trusted help instead of inventing an integer.
3. **Compare only like with like.** Scale your measurement to the target span, distinguishing stitches per 10 cm from rows per 10 cm. If target gauge is after washing but your swatch is untreated, label this only a pre-wash prediction. Do not compare different stitch textures directly.
4. **Choose the next trial.** Too many stitches across the target width (too tight) can call for a larger needle on a new swatch; too few (too loose) can call for a smaller one, only when yarn and target allow. For weak evidence, small samples, or care-dependent change, gather more before claiming a match; do not pull the old swatch into an invented number.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The record includes yarn/needle, stitch pattern, actual span, stitch and row counts, before/after-care state, and a next decision. An honest “cannot judge from this sample” is valid. Copying package gauge without measuring your hands is unfinished.

- **4 in and 10 cm are mixed:** Rewrite units and scale to one common span instead of comparing raw numbers.
- **Only edge stitches were counted:** Use a stable centre or knit a larger sample.
- **Severe curling obscures size:** Care for the swatch under its label and remeasure dry, not stretched to a target.
- **A dropped loop appears during counting:** Catch or recover it before recounting the affected area.
- **Stitches are hard to see or feel:** Light bulky yarn, magnification, or trusted pointing help can support your unit and count review.

### Assumptions and limits

Gauge changes with yarn, needles, stitch, hand tension, and care. Needle adjustment is a fresh swatch experiment, not a promise that a garment will fit or perform.
