## 中文

当航司正式通知航班延误、取消或改动，或原行程已不能按计划执行，需要**查清当前预订/替代安排并协调受影响的人**时加载。仅换了登机口、航班本身仍按原安排时用候机与登机口流程；一次普通“会迟到”的礼貌通知也有既有 Skill。这里不判断退款、赔偿、签证或入境权利，不把航司展示的备选班次自动当作你已确认的座位。

### 准备与输入

找出原预订编号、出发日期/机场、实际承运航司、各段航班与同行旅客。只通过该航司官方 App/官网、机场正式信息或柜台核对；短信链接和社交媒体转述先当提醒，不在陌生页面输入密码、验证码或付款资料。把航班“预计晚点”“确认取消”“新班次已提供但待接受”“新班次已确认”分开记录，注明查看时点与来源。

### 执行

1. **先确认变化影响哪一段。** 以本次日期/航班号核对实际状态、原机场/航站楼及已知新时间；再看受影响的后续段、转机与接应安排。显示的预计时间仍可再变，旧登机牌和原接机时间不能继续自动使用。
2. **查看航司给出的当前安排。** 在预订详情中逐名/逐段核对是否已有新班次、出发日期与时区、机场/航站楼、目的地和连接；看它是“可选方案”“系统已安排”“待你确认”还是最终确认。若不合适或没有方案，通过航司正式自助入口或工作人员问可用选项及下一步；看清任何显示的费用/条件再决定，不凭本 Skill 推断免费改签或退款资格。
3. **把后续节点重新挂到确认的行程。** 新班次若已确认，查看是否需要重新值机/拿新登机牌、托运行李现在由谁处理、哪一段行李或协助安排需重新确认、下一次实际到机场/到口截止。被取消的航班上原来托运的包，不因 App 显示新航班就可以凭想象断定去向；向航司询问这只包在**你这件事**上的交接。材料、机场中转与登机流程按各自专门说明处理。
4. **发一条有事实边界的更新。** 告诉确实需要知道的同行人、接机者或约定对象：“原__航班现为__；我已确认/尚未确认__；新预计到达__（来源/查看时刻）；下次核对__；有变化我再通知。”只发他们行动所需的信息，不群发预订编号、证件图或完整票据；对方无需替航司证明座位已确认。
5. **设复查点并保持一个当前版本。** 在航司给出新确认、临近下一办理时点或状态再变时，回到官方预订/航班状态核查一次，并把新时间、门位和已通知的人更新到同一记录。旧截图留作历史，不同时流传两个互相矛盾的“最终安排”。

### 完成与停止

你能说清现在到底是原航班延误、取消后待安排，还是新行程已获实际确认；下一段办理、行李与受影响的人都有负责人和下一复查点，本轮协调完成。**这不代表已乘机、行李已转到新机或任何赔付已获准。** 若信息冲突、无可接受替代、航司要求专业资格判断或费用/权利有争议，保留官方记录并向实际承运航司或合格渠道求证，不在这里宣称解决。

### 常见报错与补救

- **App 推送说“改签”，预订页还没确认：** 记录为待核实，用官方预订详情或柜台确认旅客、航段和状态。
- **新班次到了不同机场或跨日：** 对照出发机场当地日期/时区、转机和接应人安排，别只改一串航班号。
- **同行人不在同一预订：** 分别查各自实际预订；你的确认不能代表他人的座位。
- **托运行李已交出去：** 向航司问现在由谁处理、跟哪段走、何处查询/领取，记录实际答复，不说“应该会自动过去”。
- **机场屏、App 和短信时间不同：** 核对同一航班/日期、各来源更新时间，向航司正式人员问当前有效信息；通知相关人时标“待确认”。
- **手机或网络不可用：** 用机场航司柜台、正式告示或电话，记下工作人员实际答复，不让陌生代办接管账号。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通旅客面对已发生航班变化的事实协调，不是航空法律、赔偿或金融建议。航司的重排能力、票证、行李及接应政策因行程和地区不同；只引用你这次实际确认的信息。听觉、视觉、行动或语言不便时可请正式柜台提供可理解的文字/口头信息或请可信授权同行者协助记录，但不要交出本人账号控制权。副作用是你需要把旧行程明确降为历史版本，否则大家会按不同时间来接你。

## English

Load this Skill when the airline has actually announced a delay, cancellation or flight change, or the original itinerary can no longer be used as planned, and you need to **establish the current booking or replacement and coordinate people affected by it**. A gate-only move with the original flight intact belongs to gate monitoring. A simple courtesy note that you are late has its own Skill. This does not decide refunds, compensation, visas or entry rights, or treat a displayed flight option as a confirmed seat.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the original booking reference, departure date and airport, operating airline, every affected leg and travelling companion. Check the carrier's official app or site, airport information or desk. Treat a text link or social post as a prompt to verify, not a place to enter passwords, one-time codes or full payment details. Record “estimated delay,” “confirmed cancellation,” “replacement offered but not accepted” and “new flight confirmed” separately with time and source.

### Execution

1. **Identify the affected leg first.** Match the current status to this date and flight number, original airport or terminal and any new time. Then check onward flights, connections and pickup. Estimates may move again; an old pass and original pickup time do not remain automatically usable.
2. **Read the airline's current arrangement.** In the booking, check each person and leg for a new service, departure date and time zone, airport or terminal, destination and connection. Distinguish “available option,” “assigned by the system,” “needs your confirmation” and final confirmation. If it does not fit or none is shown, use official self-service or staff to ask what can actually be taken next. Check any displayed charges or conditions before choosing; this Skill does not infer a free change or refund right.
3. **Reattach later tasks to the confirmed itinerary.** If a new flight is confirmed, check whether fresh check-in or a new pass is needed, who now handles checked baggage, what assistance needs reconfirmation, and the actual airport or gate deadline. An app showing a new flight does not prove where a bag handed over for the cancelled flight is now. Ask the airline about **this bag in this case**. Use the dedicated material, transfer and boarding procedures for those tasks.
4. **Send one fact-bounded update.** Tell companions, a pickup person or another affected contact what they need to act: “original __ flight now __; I have/have not confirmed __; new estimated arrival __ (source and check time); next check __; I will update if it changes.” Do not broadcast booking references, document pictures or complete tickets. A contact cannot confirm a seat for the airline.
5. **Set a recheck point and keep one current version.** When the airline confirms a change, the next processing time approaches or status changes again, recheck the official booking and flight status. Update new time, gate and people informed in one record. Keep an old screenshot as history rather than circulating two conflicting “final” itineraries.

### Success and stop

You can state whether the original flight is delayed, cancelled and waiting for an option, or replaced by an actually confirmed itinerary. Later processing, baggage and affected people have owners and a next check point. **This is not a completed flight, rerouted bag or approved compensation.** If sources conflict, there is no acceptable option, the airline asks for specialist eligibility decisions, or money or rights are disputed, keep the official record and ask the operating airline or appropriate qualified channel rather than claiming resolution here.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A push alert says “rebooked” but the booking has no confirmation:** Mark pending and check the official booking or desk for each traveller, leg and state.
- **The new flight uses another airport or day:** Recheck local departure date and time zone, connection and pickup rather than changing only the flight number.
- **Companions have separate bookings:** Verify each real booking separately; your confirmation does not confirm their seats.
- **A checked bag was already handed over:** Ask the airline who has it, which leg it follows and how or where to trace or collect it; record its actual answer.
- **Airport board, app and message disagree:** Match flight and date, note update times and ask airline staff for current effective information. Tell contacts “unconfirmed” meanwhile.
- **Phone or data is unavailable:** Use an airline airport desk, official display or phone and record the actual reply, without giving account control to an unknown fixer.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is factual coordination for an ordinary passenger after a real flight change, not aviation law, compensation or financial advice. Rebooking, tickets, bags and pickups vary by itinerary and region. Use only what is confirmed for this trip. If sight, hearing, mobility or language affects access, ask the official desk for usable written or spoken information or a trusted authorised companion to help record it without handing over account control. The old itinerary becomes history; otherwise different people may come to meet you at different times.
