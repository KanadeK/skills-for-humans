## 中文

当普通文字文档的应用意外退出、断电或崩溃，而最近文字可能还没保存时，加载这份 Skill。先用**原应用可见的恢复功能**找到候选，再另存副本并核内容；不装恢复软件、不扫系统目录，也不把旧稿直接覆盖掉。若涉及工作受管或机密文件，按组织规则处理位置和权限。

### 准备与输入

记下原文件名、最近写的两三处独特句子、大约出问题时间和原保存位置。先看原文件是否还在；不要为测试恢复而在它上面继续输入或点“恢复此版本”。准备一个你有权使用的安全保存位置，给恢复副本不同的文件名，如“原名-待核恢复稿”。可用读屏、大字或可信协助查看列表，不要求逐字视觉比对。

### 执行

1. **从原应用正常打开。** 看是否出现“文档恢复/自动恢复/未保存文件”列表。不要急着关闭列表或点“清除”；有些应用关闭后会删除可恢复候选。没有列表时，只查看该应用明确提供的“恢复未保存文档”入口；不通过陌生脚本或系统深挖碰运气。
2. **逐项预览候选。** 若有多份，按文件名、时间和你记得的独特句子比较；时间最新只是线索，不保证内容最完整。不能预览时不按日期盲目覆盖现稿。
3. **先保存一个独立副本。** 在候选内容打开且仍可读时，用“另存为/保存副本”给它新文件名和清楚位置；避免“恢复/替换当前文件”按钮直接覆盖原件。若应用只有覆盖式操作且你无法确认影响，停下并寻求该应用或组织支持。
4. **核实副本真的可用。** 从保存位置重新打开副本，查标题、段落开头、最近两三处文字和结尾。与原文件并排或逐段比对，把缺失部分和不确定的顺序标出来。恢复时间戳与完整内容不是同一件事。
5. **决定下一步。** 完整或足够的副本可继续编辑，但保留原件直到确认；若只恢复一部分，把它当线索，结合自己的笔记重写缺口。没有候选则记“应用未提供可核恢复稿”，停止本轮，不宣称文件永久无可能或全部可找回。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你有一个已重新打开、内容可核且不覆盖原件的恢复副本；或明确记录原应用未提供可用候选并保留原文件现状。救回一个文件名但打不开，不算完成恢复。

- **恢复列表有多个同名项：** 对照时间和独特句子，必要时分别另存并标记，别只选最大或最新。
- **误点“关闭并删除恢复稿”的确认：** 取消，先预览和另存；已确认删除时不要再给出必定能恢复的承诺。
- **候选内容比原件旧：** 保留两者，查差异后再决定合并哪几段；不自动用旧稿替换新稿。
- **云端状态一直显示“正在保存”：** 若文字仍可见，先按应用允许的方法保存副本；不要刷新导致未上传文字消失。
- **保存处无权限或空间不足：** 选一个你有权使用且安全的可用位置，不把受管材料转到私人云盘或公共服务。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

自动恢复是否开启、保存间隔、版本列表和“保存副本”名称因应用而异。Word 的恢复窗格只是一个例子，不是所有编辑器都具备。恢复稿可能缺最后几句；另存和重开能验证手中这份，却不能证明丢失部分永远找不回。此 Skill 只处理普通文字，不涵盖损坏磁盘取证或系统维修。

## English

Load this Skill when a text document app unexpectedly quits, loses power, or crashes before recent writing may have saved. First inspect recovery offered **inside the original app**, then save a separate copy and verify its content. Do not install recovery software, search system directories, or overwrite the old document in haste. For managed or confidential work, follow your organisation's location and permission rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Recall the original filename, two or three distinctive recent phrases, roughly when the problem occurred, and where the original was saved. Check whether the original still exists. Do not type into it or press “Restore this version” merely to test recovery. Choose a safe location you may use and a different name such as “original-name-recovered-to-check.” Screen reading, large print, or trusted help can make the list accessible without requiring visual line-by-line comparison.

### Execution

1. **Open the original app normally.** Look for Document Recovery, AutoRecover, or Unsaved Documents. Do not close or clear the list immediately; some apps remove recoverable candidates after dismissal. If no pane appears, use only a recovery entry visibly provided by that app. Do not run unfamiliar scripts or dig through system folders.
2. **Preview candidates.** If there are several, compare name, time, and your distinctive phrases. The newest time is a clue, not proof of the fullest content. If you cannot preview, do not overwrite the current copy by date alone.
3. **Save a separate copy first.** While a candidate is open and readable, use Save As or Save a Copy with a new name and clear location. Avoid Restore or Replace actions that overwrite the original. If the app only offers replacement and you cannot verify its effect, stop and use app or organisation support.
4. **Verify that the copy works.** Reopen it from its saved location. Check the title, opening paragraphs, recent phrases, and ending. Compare it with the original side by side or section by section, marking missing text and uncertain order. A recovery timestamp is not the same as complete content.
5. **Choose the next action.** Edit the new copy when it is complete enough, while retaining the original until confirmed. If only part returned, use it as a clue and rewrite gaps from your own notes. If no candidate exists, record “no usable in-app recovery candidate found” and stop this round, without claiming all recovery is impossible or guaranteed.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You have reopened and checked a recovered copy that did not overwrite the original, or clearly recorded that the app offered no usable candidate while preserving the original state. Recovering a filename that cannot open is not recovered text.

- **Several items share a name:** Compare times and distinctive phrases; save separate labelled copies if needed instead of selecting only the largest or newest.
- **A close-and-delete prompt appears:** Cancel it until candidates are previewed and saved. If already confirmed, do not promise guaranteed recovery.
- **The candidate is older than the original:** Keep both, examine differences, and only then decide which passages to combine. Do not automatically replace newer work.
- **Cloud status remains “Saving”:** If text is still visible, use an app-supported method to save a copy before refreshing away unsynced text.
- **No permission or space at the destination:** Choose another authorised safe location. Do not move managed material to a personal cloud or public service.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

AutoRecovery settings, intervals, version lists, and Save a Copy labels differ by app. Word's recovery pane is an example, not a universal feature. A candidate may miss the last sentences. Saving and reopening verifies this copy, not that lost content is permanently unrecoverable. This covers ordinary text, not damaged-drive forensics or system repair.
