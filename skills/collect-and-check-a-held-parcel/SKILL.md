## 中文

你的普通包裹显示由承运商或有人值守的服务点暂存、准备领取时，用这份 Skill 确认**实际可领状态、地点、截止与授权资料**，到场按规则领取，并在离开前核对外包装。它不教绕过身份验证，也不替别人登录账户；自助快递柜操作归 H134，错件、少件或损伤后的交涉归 H136。领取不是包裹内物品已完好或商家履约的证明。

### 准备与输入

从承运商或商家的官方追踪页、可信通知中核对追踪号/通知编号、状态是否明确“可领取”、服务点的具体地址、营业时间、保留期限与领取人条件。包裹可能已经到附近设施，却尚未处理成可领取；此时先等官方状态或联系服务方，不白跑。按该服务和地区当期规定带身份证明、取件码、到件通知或代领授权中**实际要求**的材料；不是每家都用同一组合。把取件码只向授权柜台或服务系统出示，不截图公开发布。需要代领时，先查该件是否允许以及双方需带什么；不通过借用他人证件或账号绕规则。

若通知地址不一致、来源可疑、要求你在陌生链接输入密码/验证码，先从承运商官方入口独立核对。行动不便、排队或时间冲突时，查看服务方允许的可及服务或合法代领方式；超过保留期可能发生退回，不能假设柜台会无限期代存。

### 执行

1. **出门前复核可领。** 看实际包裹的官方状态、服务点、可办理时段与截止时间；确认是有人值守的取件点，不把附近仓库或快递柜当柜台。准备追踪/通知资料和该处所需的身份证明。来源不明时用官方客服或官网核实，而非用消息里的登录链接。
2. **在授权柜台提出领取。** 向工作人员说明是领取暂存包裹，按其规则出示必要资料。仅提供该次交件需要的信息；不要把密码、登录验证码或身份证照片留给未经确认的个人。若资料不符，询问缺少哪项官方要求、能否补足或走认可的代领/改约程序，不强求工作人员跳过验证。
3. **在交接处核对“这是我的包裹”。** 对照你手中的追踪号或通知与外包装可见收件人、件数及服务方标识；注意同名不同件。未经确认不要把别人的包裹带走。外箱明显开口、湿损、严重挤压或标签错位时，在现场向工作人员指出并按该服务规则记录可见状态；不擅自拆开别人的或未完成交接的包裹。
4. **取得可继续处理的结果。** 若号码、件数和外观均符合预期，按该点规定完成签收/领取并保留追踪或收件凭证，带包裹离开。若件号不符、明显损伤或无法释放，记录时间、地点、服务方给出的状态和下一步，必要时保留不含旁人信息的外观照片，转 H136 或服务方正式渠道；不在柜台争论法律赔偿。现场签名的含义与后续申诉规则以该服务为准，不把签名当作内件完好结论。

### 成功条件

你按选定服务方当前规则从**正确地点**领取到与通知匹配的普通包裹，并在离开前看过件数和外部状态；或你拿到缺少何种资料、包裹未准备好、已转点或发现异常的明确记录和下一步。知道为什么没领到，也比把别人的包裹带回家更接近完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **追踪显示到站但柜台找不到：** 先确认状态是不是“可领取”以及服务点是否相同；请工作人员或官方客服按编号查，不在附近服务点逐个试运气。
- **证件地址/姓名与面单不同：** 按该服务允许的补充证明或授权方式处理；不能提供时先保留包裹并询问正式方案，不借别人的证件凑数。
- **取件码缺失或过期：** 在承运商/商家官方渠道核对或重取；不要把账号验证码转发给自称代领的人。
- **包裹外部受损或标签不对：** 在柜台指出可见事实并请求按现场规则记录。后续保留包装和必要证据，转 H136 处理，不保证现场立刻赔付。
- **已过服务点保留期限：** 以当前追踪和客服答复查包裹实际去向，不因旧通知仍在手机里就认定包裹仍在原点。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里处理普通国内包裹在有人值守的领取点；不同服务、地区和特殊商品的身份证明、代领、保留期与签收规则不同。UPS、FedEx、USPS 的美国页面只是实例，不能当作你当地的统一规定。文字通知不可读或无法到场时，可用该方正式客服、可及服务或许可的代领方案。拿证件出门与排队会占时间；隐私信息只给有权办理的渠道。本文不保证箱内物品与订单一致。

## English

Use this Skill when an ordinary parcel is held by a carrier or staffed collection point and appears ready for you. Confirm the **actual ready status, location, deadline, and authorization requirements**, collect it under that provider's rules, and check its exterior before leaving. This does not bypass identity checks or log into another person's account. Locker operation belongs to H134; wrong, missing, or damaged-item follow-up belongs to H136. Collecting a parcel does not prove the contents are intact or match a merchant order.

### Preparation and inputs

Use official tracking or a trusted notice to confirm the tracking/notice number, an explicit ready-for-collection status, the exact point, opening hours, hold deadline, and who may collect. A package can be at a nearby facility but not yet processed for release; wait for official readiness or ask the provider rather than traveling on an arrival scan. Bring the ID, release code, notice, or third-party authorization **actually required** for this service and region. Providers do not use one universal combination. Show a release code only to the authorized counter or system; do not post its screenshot. For third-party pickup, check whether this item allows it and what each person must present. Do not borrow someone else's identity or account to get around the rule.

If a notice has an inconsistent address, an uncertain origin, or asks you to enter a password or one-time sign-in code at an unfamiliar link, verify independently through the carrier's official entry point. For mobility, queue, or timing barriers, check the provider's accessible or permitted collection alternatives. A hold may expire; do not assume unlimited storage.

### Execution

1. **Confirm readiness before travel.** Check the actual parcel's official status, staffed location, service hours, and deadline. Do not confuse a nearby depot or locker with a counter. Have its tracking or notice details and the required identity proof. If the notice source is uncertain, use official support or site navigation rather than its login link.
2. **Request release at the authorized counter.** Tell staff you are collecting a held parcel and present only what that service requires. Do not leave passwords, login codes, or ID photos with an unverified person. If the proof does not match, ask which official requirement is missing and whether a permitted third-party or rescheduling route applies. Do not press staff to skip verification.
3. **Check that it is your parcel at handover.** Compare your tracking or notice with visible recipient, parcel count, and service markings. A shared name does not mean a shared shipment. Do not take a mismatched parcel. For a visibly open, wet, heavily crushed, or mislabeled outer package, point out the observable condition on site and have it recorded under that service's process. Do not open someone else's or an unreleased parcel.
4. **Leave with a traceable outcome.** When number, count, and outer condition align, complete the provider's collection/signature step, retain available tracking or receipt, and take the parcel. If the identifier is wrong, damage is visible, or release fails, note time, place, provider status, and next step. Keep a minimal exterior photo without bystanders' details if useful, then use H136 or the provider's formal channel. Do not argue compensation law at the counter. The meaning of a signature and later claim rules follow that service; signing is not proof of the inside condition.

### Success

You collected a matching ordinary parcel from the **correct location** under the chosen provider's current rules and checked count and exterior before leaving. An equally valid stop is a clear record of missing proof, not-yet-ready status, relocation, or an anomaly with a next action. Knowing why it was not released beats carrying home another person's parcel.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Tracking shows arrival but the counter finds nothing:** Check whether the status actually says ready for collection and whether it names this point. Ask staff or official support to locate it by number instead of trying random nearby points.
- **ID address or name differs from the label:** Follow this service's permitted extra-proof or authorization path. If unavailable, leave the parcel held and ask for an official option. Do not borrow someone else's ID.
- **Release code is missing or expired:** Retrieve or verify through the carrier or merchant's official channel. Do not forward account sign-in codes to a supposed collector.
- **The exterior is damaged or the label is wrong:** Show observable facts to counter staff and request a record under on-site rules. Preserve packaging and necessary evidence for H136; immediate compensation is not promised.
- **The hold deadline passed:** Use current tracking and support to learn where the parcel actually is. A stale phone notice does not mean it remains at the original counter.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This concerns an ordinary domestic parcel at a staffed point. ID, third-party pickup, hold periods, and signatures vary by service, region, and special item. US pages from UPS, FedEx, and USPS are examples, not universal local rules. If you cannot read the notice or attend, use official support, accessible service, or a permitted collector. Bringing ID and queuing take time; share private details only with authorized channels. This file does not guarantee the contents match the order.
