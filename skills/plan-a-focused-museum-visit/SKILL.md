## 中文

当你想去博物馆或展览，却不想把“所有展厅都走完”当任务时，用这份 Skill 依据**一个真实兴趣、可用时间和场馆当日限制**选一个主目标及备选。完成物是可执行的参观计划；这不代表已经入场、看到了某件作品或完成观展体验。

### 准备与输入

写一句你想看什么，例如“看两件以纸为材料的作品”或“了解一个展览如何讲某段历史”，不以馆名热度替代兴趣。确认你能留给参观的时间、交通与回程、费用上限、行动/感官需要和是否愿意排队。只用场馆官网、官方公告或馆方人员核对日期、开放时间、展览是否正在展出、入口、票/预约、摄影与随身物规则、可及服务；价格、展期和限制会变，不套用别的馆的数字。

### 执行

1. **缩成一个主目标。** 从当日真的开放的展览/展区中选一个与你兴趣相符的目标。网页展示馆藏图片不等于原件当天在展；展厅关闭、轮换或需另约就改目标，不把“网站上有”当现场保证。
2. **算出可用现场时间。** 从总时段扣除到达、安检/入场、馆内移动、休息和按时离开的缓冲；不确定排队时留余量。若只剩短时，选一小区或几件作品，不把“逛全馆”挤进半小时。
3. **核实入场和可及路径。** 看主目标在什么楼/区域、用哪个入口、是否需票或预约、费用是否在你范围、实际可用的无障碍/语言/安静空间支持。找不到关键信息时问场馆，不把“不知道”自动填成“可行”。
4. **选一个备选与停点。** 如主展厅临时关闭，选同馆一个仍符合兴趣的替代区域；如果费用、时间或可及条件不成立，就改日、换馆或在线查看馆方资料。给计划写“到____时离开/休息”，参观不靠硬撑完成。
5. **写成四行。** 记录“日期与馆方核对时间—主目标—入场/路径/预算—备选与退出点”。出发前再核当天更新；正式购票或预约依你实际决定，计划本身不等于购买。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你能解释为什么选这个展区，并有经当前馆方信息核对的日期、入场条件、可行时间与备选。只有收藏一张宣传海报，没有核展期/开放和实际路线，计划仍不完整。

- **想看的作品未展出：** 改成同主题现展，或接受这次只能看其他内容；不向工作人员索要闭馆藏品。
- **入场或特殊展票条件不明：** 暂停承诺并问馆方，不能靠旧攻略推断免费或可随到随进。
- **行程过满：** 删到一个主目标，保留休息和离开时间，不把疲惫当失败。
- **现场政策突然改变：** 以当日公告为准，启用备选或改期；不拿旧截图与工作人员争执。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

场馆、国家、票务、安检和可及服务差异大，本篇是核对顺序而非通用规章。可由可信同伴协助阅读官网或联系场馆；你仍决定费用和是否前往。人多、感官负担、行动时间或语言支持不足时，可把目标缩小或选择官方线上内容。规划并不产生参观权，也不保证某件藏品上墙。

## English

Use this Skill when you want to visit a museum or exhibition without making “see every gallery” the mission. Choose one main target and fallback using **your real interest, available time, and current venue conditions**. The result is a workable plan, not proof that you entered, saw an object, or experienced the exhibition.

### Preparation and inputs

Write one interest, such as “see two works made with paper” or “learn how one exhibition tells a period of history.” A popular museum name alone is not a purpose. Know your visit time, travel and return route, spending limit, mobility or sensory needs, and tolerance for a queue. Use the venue's official site, current notices, or staff to check date, hours, whether an exhibition is on view, entrance, ticket or booking, photography and bag rules, and access services. Prices, dates, and restrictions change; another museum's numbers do not apply.

### Execution

1. **Set one main target.** Choose an exhibition or area genuinely open that day and relevant to your interest. A collection image online does not guarantee that original is on view. If galleries rotate, close, or require another booking, change the target instead of treating “on the website” as a promise.
2. **Calculate usable on-site time.** Subtract arrival, security or admission, movement inside, a break, and a buffer to leave on time. Allow for an uncertain queue. With little time, choose one small area or a few works rather than compress an entire museum into half an hour.
3. **Check admission and access route.** Locate the target floor or area and entrance, any ticket or booking, whether cost fits, and actual mobility, language, or quiet-space support. Ask the venue when a necessary detail is missing; do not turn “unknown” into “feasible” by default.
4. **Choose a fallback and stop point.** If the main gallery closes, pick another open area in the same venue that still fits your interest. If cost, time, or access cannot work, reschedule, choose another place, or use official online material. Write a rest or departure time; exhausting yourself is not the success condition.
5. **Make a four-line plan.** Record “date and time of venue check—main target—admission/route/budget—fallback and exit.” Recheck day-of updates before travelling. Buying or booking remains your decision; the plan itself is not a purchase.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can explain the chosen area and have a date, admission condition, workable time, and fallback checked against current venue information. Saving one publicity image without verifying dates, opening, or route is not a complete plan.

- **The desired object is off view:** Choose a currently displayed related area or accept different content today; do not demand a stored object from staff.
- **Ticket or special-exhibition conditions are unclear:** Pause the commitment and ask the venue rather than infer free or walk-in access from an old travel post.
- **The route is overfull:** Cut to one main target, keeping rest and departure time. Fatigue is not failure.
- **A policy changes on site:** Follow the current notice and use the fallback or reschedule instead of treating an old screenshot as authority.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Venues, countries, ticketing, security, and access services vary. This is a check order, not a universal rulebook. Trusted help may read the site or contact the venue while you decide on cost and travel. For crowds, sensory load, mobility limits, or language barriers, shrink the goal or use official online content. Planning creates no entry right and guarantees no particular collection object on the wall.
