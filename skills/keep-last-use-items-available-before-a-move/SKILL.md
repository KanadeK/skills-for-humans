## 中文

当大部分物品开始封箱，但你还要在旧住处生活到离开时，加载这份 Skill。你要留下一小组**仍能完成离开前实际日常活动的物品**，并知道每件在最后一次使用后交到哪里。它不制定长期去留、搬运箱子、处理租赁交接或布置新住处。

### 准备与输入

确认最可能的离开时刻、这段时间还有几次睡眠、洗漱、换衣、进食或设备充电等实际活动，以及参与者各自必要的可及用品。根据真实情况写，不照抄“每人一套”的固定清单。找一个不阻挡通道、别人知道不能当普通箱装走的暂放区；如果有人代为打包，先告诉对方这一区域的边界。

### 执行

1. **从剩余活动反推依赖。** 对每项还会发生的活动，写出最少需要哪件普通物品、谁用、最后一次大概何时用。已有可用替代品就写清楚；未知的离开日期要保留足够的实际使用路径，不能凭预计时间全封。
2. **把最后使用组从普通装箱流中取出。** 在暂放区或仍需使用的位置放好，并作双方能读的“暂不装箱”标记。带个人隐私或特殊储存要求的东西只由有权者管理；具体保管规则按物品说明，不在这里给医疗或设备处理建议。
3. **指定最后一次使用后的去向。** 每件写成“活动完成 → 放入本人随行包／交给指定的人／按服务方规则另行处理”。若它也要供到达后的第一段时间使用，接到单独的可达必需物包；不要在旧家用完后随手混入最早封走的箱。
4. **做一次实际可行性回读。** 请会执行的人照剩余日程口述：今晚怎么用、明早最后一次用后放哪儿？如果某件物品两处同时需要，而目前只有一件，就安排借用、替代、错开或更改计划，不写一个不存在的副本。
5. **离开前逐项清点。** 只在最后一次实际使用之后，把暂留物按所记路线移交。暂放区有未说明物品时先确认主人和去向，不为了腾空而塞进任意箱。

### 完成与停止

到离开为止的实际基本活动有可取用的物品或已确认替代；暂留物在最后使用后有明确接手位置/人。离开时刻改变、替代品不存在、代打包者不知暂放区时，先修正计划，不提前封走关键物品。

### 常见报错与补救

- **明早还要用，今晚却封了箱：** 若安全且有权限，找回并恢复到暂留区；找不到就先落实替代，而不是假设到时能开箱。
- **两个人都以为对方会带走：** 给每件暂留物写一个实际接手人，并在交出时回读。
- **离开时间延后：** 重新数剩余活动和用品，不沿用原本只够一晚的计划。
- **暂留物挡路或难拿：** 换一个不妨碍通行、使用者能接近的位置；必要时请愿意且有能力的人协助取放。
- **打包者把暂留区也封了：** 立刻指出有标记的范围和实际需要，恢复关键物品，再核对剩余箱件。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通搬迁的日常使用连续性计划，不是紧急撤离、医疗护理、危险品保管或租赁法律建议。家庭成员、辅助用品、宠物用品和当地服务条件会改变具体清单，按真实使用者确认。阅读、行走或握持不便时可用大字、语音、照片和可信协助者共同清点；不要求你搬动重箱。副作用是暂留区会抵抗“既然收拾了就全封掉”的冲动。

## English

Load this Skill when most belongings are being boxed up but you still need to live at the old place until departure. You will keep a small set of **items needed for actual routines before you leave** and know where each goes after its last use. This does not decide permanent ownership, carry boxes, manage tenancy handover or set up the new place.

### Preparation and inputs

Know your likely departure time and the remaining sleep, washing, clothing, meals or device-charging activities that will really happen. Include each person's actual access needs. Build the list from those activities, not a fixed “one set per person” formula. Choose a visible holding area that does not block a route and that packers know is outside the normal boxing flow. Tell a packing helper exactly what it covers.

### Execution

1. **Work backward from remaining routines.** For each activity still to come, name the minimum ordinary item, who uses it and roughly when it is last needed. Note a genuine available substitute. If departure time is uncertain, preserve a usable path rather than sealing everything on a guessed date.
2. **Keep the last-use set out of normal packing.** Put it in the holding area or where it remains in use, with a “do not box yet” mark both sides can understand. Private items or those with special storage needs stay under an authorised person's care and their actual instructions; this Skill gives no medical or equipment-handling directions.
3. **Assign the route after final use.** For each item, write “activity complete → personal carry bag / named handoff person / separate provider-approved route.” If it is needed just after arrival too, connect it to a reachable essentials kit. Do not let it fall into the first ordinary box closed after use.
4. **Read back a feasible day.** Have the people doing the work say how they will use each item tonight or tomorrow and where it will go afterward. If the same single item is needed in two places at once, arrange a real loan, substitute, sequence or changed plan. Do not list a duplicate that does not exist.
5. **Check each item before leaving.** Transfer it only after its actual final use. If something remains in the holding area without a known owner or route, confirm those first rather than putting it into any box to clear the space.

### Success and stop

Each real basic activity until departure has an available item or confirmed alternative, and each held item has a destination or person after last use. If departure changes, a substitute is missing or packers do not know the holding-area boundary, correct the plan before boxing a key item.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Tomorrow's item was boxed tonight:** If safe and authorised, retrieve it and return it to the holding area. If it cannot be found, secure a real substitute rather than assuming the box can be reopened later.
- **Two people expect the other to take it:** Name one actual recipient for the item and read back the handoff.
- **Departure is delayed:** Recount the routines and supplies still needed; a plan that covered one night may no longer cover the gap.
- **Held items block the route or are hard to reach:** Use a reachable spot away from the path, with willing capable help if needed.
- **A helper boxes the holding area:** Point out the marked boundary and real need immediately, recover key items, then check the remaining boxes.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This keeps ordinary daily use possible during a move. It is not emergency evacuation, medical care, hazardous-goods storage or tenancy advice. Household members, assistive or pet supplies, and local provider rules change the actual list; confirm it with the real users. If reading, walking or grip is difficult, use large print, audio, photographs and trusted help for the count. You do not have to move heavy boxes. The holding area may resist the satisfying but premature act of sealing every last object.
