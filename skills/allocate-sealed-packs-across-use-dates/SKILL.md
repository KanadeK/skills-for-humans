## 中文

当你已经知道某种普通食品或日用品要在**相隔较远的几个日期**使用，而打开一只容器后必须在更短时间内用完时，加载这份 Skill。这里决定今天买多少个**独立密封单元**、每个单元对应哪些日期，以及是否必须等以后再买一部分。它不负责到家保存、开封操作、食品治疗或日期标签的法律解释。若所有用途本来都落在同一个可用窗口，普通包装量判断就够了。

### 准备与输入

只列已经确定的日期和每次实际用量，另列家中已确认可用的同款库存。读取候选商品的未开封日期、存放条件、开封后期限和**真正独立的密封单元数**。外盒有六份，不代表里面六份都能各自重新获得一个开封期限；须看实际封口和适用说明。再确认你今天的付款、携带、储位，以及以后是否真的能在下一次用途前补货。

标签看不清时放大照片、查该商品可访问说明或请店员读出原话；仍无法确认单元是否独立密封或适用期限，就不要凭外盒图片分配日期。食品的 *use-by* 与 *best-before* 在英国分别关联安全与品质；其他地区按当地权威和实际商品标签，不把外语词汇当全球规则。

### 执行

1. **把用途排到日历上。** 逐项写“日期—要用多少”，不把暂定聚会、可能想用或未知数量算作已确认需求。例：本周一和下周一各需要 100 毫升同一种适用商品。
2. **画出每个封口的窗口。** 从你计划第一次打开该单元的日期开始，按该单元标签上的开封后要求判断能覆盖到哪天；同时核对其未开封日期和储存条件。按适用的安全期限与开封条件取更严格的范围，不因外盒的远期日期而延长一只已打开的瓶子。*Best-before* 关乎品质，不自动把过了该日的食品判为不安全；预期品质是否符合用途仍要如实决定。
3. **将日期分配给密封单元。** 每个日期只能分给届时仍符合其实际标签条件、且数量足够的单元。同一单元用过后，后续日期仍必须落在它的开封窗口内。例：若候选商品明确要求开封后三天内用完，周一打开的一只 250 毫升瓶不能自动负责八天后的 100 毫升；若两只各 120 毫升的小瓶确实分别密封、日期合适，就可以各配一个周一。
4. **分清今天买与以后买。** 今天能买到的独立单元若可覆盖所有已确认日期，且你有真实储位、预算和运输能力，就记录今天的件数与预计余量。若只适合先买第一段，就写下下一次用途前**确实可行**的补货日和剩余件数。没有可行补货且没有适用的独立密封选项时，停止这项购买计划，不把未来买到货当作既成事实。
5. **在付款前复核实物。** 检查手中实际单元数、每单元数量、日期与最后总价，和刚才的日期分配一致。包装或促销组合变了就重排；详细单位价格比较交给价格任务。

### 成功条件

你有一张短表：**每个确定用途日期由哪只尚可用的密封单元覆盖、今天买几只、以后何时补几只、预计剩多少**。或者你明确输出“没有安全且可执行的组合，暂不按此计划买”。只知道合计毫升数却无法解释第二次使用时哪只仍可用，不算完成。

### 常见报错、补救与停止

- **ERR_OUTER_DATE_ONLY：** 外盒日期很远，却忽略开封后的更短要求。按每个实际打开的单元重排；已开的一只不会在下周自动恢复未开封状态。
- **ERR_INNER_NOT_SEALED：** 看似多份装，里面却共用一个封口或开封说明不清。按一个共同开封窗口计算，或改选能核实独立封口的商品；不能凭分隔线猜。
- **ERR_LATER_SHOP_FICTION：** 方案靠“下周再买”，但店不在可达路径、配送时间未确认或你无法再次付款。把该段标为未覆盖，寻找另一适用包装或改变计划，不假装采购已完成。
- **ERR_ONE_UNIT_TOO_SMALL：** 一个单元不足它被分配的那次用途。按同日真实需求增加适用单元，或重新选规格；不能把另一日期的余量跨越不合规的开封窗口挪过来。
- **ERR_INTERRUPTED：** 中途离开货架。保留日期表，回来后重新读手中现货的封口、日期、数量和价格再结账。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份 Skill 假设使用日期可事先确认，商品用途和限制已另行核实。不同地区的日期标签、运输和店内库存不相同，所有窗口都以当前商品及当地规则为准。视力、阅读或握持受限时，请店员展示独立封口与标签、提供文字说明，或选标示更明确的适用商品；无法验证关键期限就停止。食品开封后的实际保存属于入户后的任务，不能由购物计划代替。

现实副作用是你可能少买一只大瓶、多安排一次补货，或选单独密封的小份。日历不会替包装补上一道封口。

## English

Load this Skill when you know an ordinary food or household consumable will be used on **separated dates**, but opening one container starts a shorter usable period. You are deciding how many **independently sealed units** to buy today, which dates each unit can cover, and whether some units must be bought later. Storage after arrival, the act of opening, medical food choices, and legal interpretation of dates are separate tasks. If all uses already fit one opening window, an ordinary package-size decision is enough.

### Preparation and inputs

List only confirmed use dates and the real amount for each, plus any stock you know is usable. Read each candidate's unopened date, storage conditions, after-opening instructions, and **actual number of independently sealed units**. Six portions printed on a box do not mean six separate opening windows; check the seals and applicable directions. Confirm what you can pay for, carry, and place today, and whether another purchase before a later use is genuinely possible.

If print is hard to read, enlarge a clear photo, use accessible product information, or ask staff to read the wording. If you cannot confirm whether portions are separately sealed or what limits apply, do not allocate them from a box picture. In the UK, *use-by* and *best-before* relate to safety and quality respectively; elsewhere follow the actual label and local authority rather than treating English words as universal rules.

### Execution

1. **Put the uses on a calendar.** Write “date—amount needed” for each. Do not count a tentative gathering, a possible use, or an unknown amount as confirmed demand. For example, you need 100 mL of one suitable product this Monday and another 100 mL next Monday.
2. **Draw a window for each seal.** Starting from when you would first open that unit, apply its own after-opening instructions; also check its unopened date and storage conditions. Use the stricter applicable safety and opening limit. A distant date on the outer box does not extend the life of a bottle already opened. *Best-before* concerns quality and does not automatically make food unsafe after that date; decide honestly whether expected quality suits your use.
3. **Assign dates to sealed units.** Each use must be assigned to a unit that will still meet its actual label conditions and hold enough product then. Later uses assigned to the same unit must still fall within its opening window. If a candidate explicitly says to finish within three days of opening, one 250 mL bottle opened on Monday cannot automatically cover another 100 mL eight days later. Two genuinely separate 120 mL bottles with suitable dates could cover one Monday each.
4. **Separate today's purchase from a later one.** If independently sealed units sold today cover every confirmed date and you can pay for, carry, and place them, record the count and likely remainder. If only the first period can be covered now, write a **feasible** restock date and remaining count before the next use. When there is no workable restock and no suitable sealed option, stop this purchase plan; an imagined future shop is not completed supply.
5. **Recheck the item before paying.** Compare the actual unit count, amount per unit, dates, and final price with your allocation. Replan if the pack or offer differs. Detailed unit-price comparison belongs to the price task.

### Success

You have a small table showing **which still-usable sealed unit covers each confirmed date, how many units to buy today, when and how many to buy later, and the expected remainder**. Or you explicitly conclude that no safe, feasible arrangement supports the plan. A combined millilitre total without an answer for the second use is not completion.

### Common errors, recovery, and stop points

- **ERR_OUTER_DATE_ONLY:** The outer date looks distant, but a shorter after-opening instruction was ignored. Reallocate each actually opened unit; it does not become unopened again next week.
- **ERR_INNER_NOT_SEALED:** The box looks portioned, but the contents share a seal or the instructions are unclear. Treat them as one opening window or choose a product whose separate seals can be verified. A printed divider is not a seal.
- **ERR_LATER_SHOP_FICTION:** The plan assumes another shop next week, but the store is inaccessible, delivery is unconfirmed, or you cannot pay again. Mark that use uncovered and find another suitable pack or change the plan; do not claim the later purchase happened.
- **ERR_ONE_UNIT_TOO_SMALL:** A unit cannot cover its assigned use. Add suitable units for that date or choose another size. Do not move another date's remainder across an invalid opening window.
- **ERR_INTERRUPTED:** If you leave the shelf, keep your date table. On returning, reread the seals, dates, amounts, and price of the item actually in hand before checkout.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes use dates can be confirmed and the product's suitability and restrictions have already been checked. Date labels, transport, and stock vary by place; each window follows the current product and local rules. If seeing, reading, or holding the pack is hard, ask staff to show the separate seals and wording, request an accessible description, or choose a suitable product with clearer information. Stop if a critical limit cannot be verified. How food is actually stored after opening is a later task; a shopping calendar cannot perform it.

You may buy one less large bottle, schedule another shop, or choose separately sealed portions. A calendar cannot add a seal to a package.
