## 中文

你已按好一个清楚和弦，想让扫弦手按**固定下上方向和拍点**持续一小段，并有意留一个不碰弦的空拍时使用本篇。完成时，连续两组四拍的动作与所数拍点一致，空拍是计划中的安静而非失误。它只管一只手的节奏，不教新和弦或双和弦成曲。

### 准备与输入

用指腹、食指或本琴允许的柔软拨片轻扫，手腕保持放松；不用硬拨片刮尼龙弦。选舒适慢速，先口数四拍。把一组定为“第 1 拍下扫、第 2 拍上扫、第 3 拍下扫、第 4 拍空过”，动作方向持续交替；你可以按本琴和手部条件调整速度，别追求音量。

### 执行

1. **先做无声动作。** 不接触琴弦，口数 1–4，并让手在预定方向下、上、下、上移动；第四拍虽向上移动，但只在弦外划过。
2. **加一个清楚和弦。** 按好已经会的和弦，依数拍轻扫前三拍；第四拍抬离琴弦，无声完成同一上行动作。肩膀与手腕放松。
3. **核对脉冲。** 再做一组，听声响是否落在 1、2、3，第四拍确实安静，下一组第一拍仍如期下扫。若错拍，只减慢并重数，不靠加速冲过去。
4. **收口并记录。** 做到两组连续可数再停；若手指刮痛或琴弦受损，立即停。记下一处最容易漏掉的静音拍供下次复习。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

两组四拍里下、上、下有声，第四拍有意无声，手仍按上行动作过拍；或者你记下错在哪拍。别把随意停手说成有计划的休止。

- **第四拍总响：** 将扫弦路径移离琴弦，保持计数。
- **上下方向乱：** 先空手慢数，再加琴弦。
- **和弦忽然闷：** 先检查按弦手，节奏练习暂缓。
- **手腕僵或痛：** 休息，减小动作和音量。

### 假设与边界

假设一个和弦已清楚响起；本篇的四拍是原创短练习，不是某首歌的扫弦谱。你听拍点验收；节拍器只是可选辅助。

## English

Use this Skill with one chord that already rings clearly to keep a **down/up hand motion on a steady count** while deliberately skipping contact on one beat. Finish with two four-count groups whose audible strikes match your count; the quiet beat is planned, not an accidental miss. This teaches one hand's rhythm, not a new chord or a two-chord piece.

### Preparation and inputs

Strum gently with thumb, index or a soft model-suitable pick; do not scrape nylon strings with a hard pick. Choose a comfortable slow count. Set an original four-beat group: down on 1, up on 2, down on 3, and a silent up motion past the strings on 4. Adjust speed for your instrument and hand; volume is not the goal.

### Execution

1. **Move silently first.** Away from strings, count 1–4 aloud as your hand moves down, up, down, up. On count four, pass outside the strings without touching them.
2. **Add one clear chord.** Fret the known chord and gently sound counts 1–3. On 4, move upward outside the strings. Keep shoulder and wrist relaxed.
3. **Check the pulse.** Repeat the group. Hear notes on 1, 2, 3, true silence on 4, and a punctual next downstroke on 1. If the count slips, slow down and restart rather than rushing through.
4. **Close and note a miss.** Stop after two consecutive countable groups. Stop sooner if the hand hurts or strings are damaged. Name the quiet beat you tend to miss for later practice.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Across two four-beat groups, down/up/down sound, count four stays silent, and the hand still makes its up motion; or you identify the missed count. Merely stopping the hand at random is not a planned rest.

- **Count four sounds:** Move the strum path off the strings while counting.
- **Directions tangle:** Rehearse silently at slower speed first.
- **Chord mutes:** Check the fretting hand before the rhythm exercise.
- **Wrist stiff or painful:** Rest, reduce movement and volume.

### Assumptions and limits

One chord already rings clearly. The four-count pattern is an original tiny exercise, not a copied song strum. You judge the pulse; a metronome is optional.
