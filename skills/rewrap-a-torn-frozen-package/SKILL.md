## 中文

当一件**原本已安全入冰格**的普通冻品包装在冰格内刮破、封口松开或外层标签将脱落，而食品身份与持续低温的历史仍能确认时，用这份 Skill 恢复包装与信息。这里修的是可确认冻品的保护层；不判断停电后是否仍安全，也不把身份不明的冰块升级成“今天刚买”。

### 准备与输入

在打开冰格后先找出原食品名称、原包装和必要的储存/烹调说明、已知的入冻日期，以及破口位置。确认食品仍按适用条件保持冻态，包装损坏不是由于长时间室温放置或冰格失效；与其他食物、尤其即食食品之间没有可疑接触。备好食品适用的冷冻袋、包裹材料或能封严的盒和标签。任一关键事实无法确认时，先隔离并转向保存信息不明的判断，不用新标签覆盖问题。

### 执行

1. **先分清破损与变温。** 只有包装在可靠冷冻期间受损、食品仍可识别且状态可确认，才继续。发现明显解冻、汁液渗出到其他食品、异物进入或原始身份丢失时，停止直接重包；按具体食品与当地安全指导处理。
2. **尽量保留原包装。** 如果只是外层裂口、内层仍包住食品，可在外面加一层适合冷冻的包裹，减少食品暴露与空气进入。若原包已打开但食品仍能安全处理，则用干净、食品适用的冷冻包装重新装好；生动物性食品远离即食食品和共用工具。不要为了拍照让它在室温里排队。
3. **把原信息一起保住。** 原标签还能读，就保留在新包里可见的位置或拍清楚，另写准确食品名称、原先已知的入冻日期及今天的**重包日期**，各自标明含义。需要按原包装烹调、解冻或追溯批次的信息也要留得可找回。未知日期写“未知”，不把重包日写成初次冷冻日。
4. **封严并检查隔离。** 按包装说明排出可安全排出的多余空气、封好破口或新包，确认没有漏出、原料汁液不会接触别的食品，冰格能正常关闭和维持适用低温。冻结烧伤主要是品质损失；若食品的安全史不明，它不能替你证明安全。
5. **决定下一次怎么用。** 放回后记录这件包仍需按原食品与当地说明决定使用；包装变新不重置任何原日期或安全期限。若已有需要优先核查的问题，把它显眼标成“待核实”，而非放回冰格深处等待遗忘。

### 成功条件

冻品重新封在合适包装中，原身份、入冻日期及必要说明仍可区分，重包日期没有冒充原始日期，且没有可疑接触或失控温度问题。条件不足时，完整结果是明确隔离并停止“修复成功”宣告。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只写今天日期：** 补上“今天重包”和原入冻日期各自的含义；原日期不知就写未知，并转交不明保存信息判断。
- **薄袋再次破裂或空气很多：** 换明确适合冷冻食品的包装，按说明封严；反复漏气会损伤品质。
- **生食破包并碰到即食食品：** 停止把两者一起重包，隔离受影响物，按当地交叉污染指导处理；清洁工具与表面。
- **包装破时冰格也曾停电：** 不用“重新包好”来判断可否继续保存；先处理实际时间与温度史。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

美国 FSIS 的破包说明针对肉与禽类且前提是仍在冰格内；不同食品、包装和地区应服从其具体标签与本地权威。本 Skill 不教解冻、罐藏、发酵或过敏处理。看不清原标签时可放大、拍照或请可信的人读出；若需要很大握力才能包紧，用容易封合的合适容器或请人协助，不用带裂口的袋继续将就。重包增加包装材料，不能恢复已经失去的安全或品质。

## English

Use this Skill when packaging around ordinary food **already stored properly in a freezer** tears, opens, or starts losing its label, while its identity and continuous cold history are still known. You are repairing protection and information for confirmed frozen food. This Skill does not decide safety after a power failure or rename an unidentified block “bought today.”

### Preparation and inputs

Find the original food name, packaging, essential storage or cooking directions, known original freezing date, and the location of the tear. Confirm it has stayed frozen as required, rather than sitting at room temperature or in a failed appliance. Check for questionable contact with other food, particularly ready-to-eat food. Have food-suitable freezer wrap, a bag or tight container, and a marker. If a critical fact is missing, isolate the item and use the separate unknown-storage-information decision instead of covering the gap with a new label.

### Execution

1. **Separate a torn wrapper from a warm item.** Continue only if the packaging was damaged during reliable freezing and the food and its condition can still be identified. If it has visibly thawed, raw juices have reached other food, foreign material has entered, or the identity is gone, stop the routine rewrap and follow guidance for the actual food and location.
2. **Keep the original pack when possible.** If only the outer layer is torn and another layer still contains the food, add suitable freezer wrapping outside it to reduce air exposure. If the pack is open but handling remains safe, move the food into clean freezer-suitable packaging. Keep raw animal food away from ready-to-eat food and shared tools. Do not queue the item at room temperature for a photo shoot.
3. **Carry the original information across.** Keep the readable original label visible or photograph it clearly. Mark the exact food, original freezing date if known, and **today's rewrap date**, naming what each date means. Preserve essential cooking, thawing, and lot information when it matters. Write “unknown” for an unknown original date; a rewrap date cannot become an invented freezing date.
4. **Seal and separate.** Remove excess air as the package permits, close the tear or new pack, and check for leaks and raw-food contact with anything else. The freezer must close and maintain the food's applicable cold condition. Freezer burn mainly reduces quality; it cannot prove safety when the item's history is unknown.
5. **Keep the original use conditions.** Put the item back with any unresolved question plainly marked. Later use still follows the original food and local guidance. New wrapping does not restart an original date or safety period.

### Success

The frozen food is protected by suitable packaging; its identity, original freezing date, essential directions, and rewrap date remain distinct. No unexplained temperature or contact problem has been glossed over. If those conditions fail, isolation and a clear stop are the complete result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only today's date is written:** State “rewrapped today” and identify the actual original freezing date separately. If it is unknown, mark it unknown and pass the history question to the relevant workflow.
- **A thin bag tears again or leaves much air:** Use packaging identified for freezer food and close it as directed. Repeated air leaks damage quality.
- **Raw food touched ready-to-eat food:** Stop packing both together. Isolate affected items and use local cross-contamination guidance; clean tools and surfaces.
- **The freezer lost power too:** A new wrapper cannot decide whether the food can still be kept. Resolve its actual time and temperature history first.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

The US FSIS torn-pack advice concerns meat and poultry that stayed in a freezer; follow the exact product and local authority for other foods and circumstances. This Skill does not teach thawing, canning, fermentation, or allergy handling. Enlarge or photograph a small original label, or ask someone trusted to read it. If tight wrapping is hard on your hands, use a suitable easy-closing container or trusted help rather than accepting a split bag. Rewrapping uses material and cannot restore lost safety or quality.
