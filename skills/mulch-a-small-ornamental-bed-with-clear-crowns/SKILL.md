## 中文

当你有一小块已种植、获准养护的**非食用观赏花坛**，打算按植物和材料说明加一层覆材时，加载这份 Skill。结果是适用的土面被覆盖，但株心、茎基和行走路径仍清楚，不把“厚”当作越多越好。

### 准备与输入

确认你对花坛有操作许可、主要植物身份和对土壤湿度/排水的要求，读覆材种类、用途和厚度说明。不要使用无法辨认来源、可能含污染物或未经确认适合该植物的材料。看不清低矮株心时先标记或请人指出。工作面应可安全触及，不在斜坡、高台或隐藏电缆/管线处挖掘。

### 执行

1. **判断此时是否适合。** 查土壤是否已适度湿润、没有冻结或积水，品种是否耐受该覆材。RHS 指出有些低矮地被或喜干植物不宜被湿覆材埋住；不合适就不铺。
2. **先清理土面。** 只移除看得清且允许处理的普通杂草和散落物，不伤新芽或表层根。疑似病害、尖锐废物或不明材料先停下另行处理。
3. **划出留白。** 在每株茎基、株心和低垂叶片周围保留清楚空隙，避免覆材紧贴。通道、排水口和邻居的植物也不覆盖。
4. **均匀铺合适厚度。** 按材料和园艺指导把覆材摊在裸露土面，不堆成小山；拿不准厚度时先查，不凭一袋必须用完的压力加厚。
5. **复核并收尾。** 看株心可见、茎基不闷、路径不滑不堵，剩余材料按标签存放。记覆材、日期和需要之后检查的下陷、散失或过湿现象。

### 成功条件

实际土面有与植物相容的一层覆材；你能指出未被埋住的株心、茎基与排水/行走边界，并说明材料与厚度依据。若植物或覆材不明、土壤冻结/积水或无法安全触及，记录原因后不铺。

### 常见报错与补救

- **覆材紧贴茎：** 轻轻往外拨，露出茎基；贴茎的湿覆材会增加软烂风险。
- **一株低矮植物快被盖没：** 停下，先确认它的株心和品种需要；必要时该处不覆。
- **剩余半袋想全倒上去：** 保存剩余材料，不为清空袋子改变指导厚度。
- **路径洒了碎屑：** 清回花坛或按本地规则处理，避免滑倒及堵排水。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只在已获准的小型普通观赏花坛表面铺覆材，不设计新花坛、进行土壤改造、化学除草或处理大型树木。坐姿可及的高床或请人运送袋装材料可替代长时间弯腰；视力受限时请人指出株心和边界。副作用是土面与水分变化，需要之后观察，不是从此免浇水的证明。

## English

Load this Skill when you may care for an existing small **non-edible ornamental bed** and want a species- and material-appropriate mulch layer. Finish with suitable soil covered while crowns, stem bases, and walking space remain clear. More mulch is not automatically better.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission for the bed, the main plants and their moisture and drainage needs, and the mulch material's purpose and depth directions. Do not use material of unknown origin, possible contamination, or unknown plant compatibility. Mark low crowns or ask someone to point them out if hard to see. Work only where you can reach safely; do not dig near hidden utilities or on an unsafe slope.

### Execution

1. **Decide if today fits.** Check that soil is appropriately moist, not frozen or waterlogged, and that the plants tolerate this material. RHS notes some low-growing or dry-site plants should not be buried under damp mulch. Hold when unsuitable.
2. **Clear the surface.** Remove only visible ordinary weeds and loose debris you may handle, without injuring new shoots or surface roots. Stop for suspect disease, sharp waste, or unknown material.
3. **Mark clear space.** Keep a visible gap around every crown, stem base, and low leaf. Leave paths, drains, and neighbors' plants uncovered.
4. **Spread the supported layer.** Follow material and horticultural depth guidance across exposed soil. Do not heap it around a plant. If depth is unclear, check before adding more; an open bag is not a quota.
5. **Review and close.** Confirm crowns and stems remain visible, paths are not slippery or blocked, and remaining material is stored as its label says. Record material, date, and later signs to check, such as dispersal or excess wetness.

### Success

There is a plant-compatible layer on the actual soil; you can point to clear crowns, stem bases, drainage and walking boundaries and explain material and depth. If plants or mulch are unknown, soil is frozen/waterlogged, or safe access is missing, record the reason and do not spread it.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Mulch touches stems:** Pull it back gently. Persistent damp contact raises softening and disease risk.
- **A low plant is nearly buried:** Stop and confirm its crown and species need; leave that area bare if appropriate.
- **Half a bag remains:** Store it instead of exceeding the supported depth.
- **Material spills on a path:** Clear it back to the bed or use the local waste route to prevent slips or blocked drains.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a surface task in a permitted small ordinary ornamental bed, not new-bed construction, soil engineering, chemical weed treatment, or work on large trees. A reachable raised bed or lifting help can replace sustained bending and hauling. Ask someone to identify crowns and edges if vision is limited. Soil and moisture conditions change afterward and need review; mulch does not cancel watering.

### Sources

- [RHS: Non-chemical Weed Control](https://www.rhs.org.uk/prevention-protection/weeds-non-chemical-control) — keep mulch clear of stems and perennial crowns.
