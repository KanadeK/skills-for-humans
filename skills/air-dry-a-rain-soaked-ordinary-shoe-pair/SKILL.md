## 中文

当一双**普通鞋被干净雨水湿透或明显进水**，你需要保护形状、让里外自然干时，加载这份 Skill。结果是鞋内、鞋垫和外层按鞋款说明干透，或明确未干继续等待。它与仅刷掉污物、洗可拆鞋垫的起点不同。污水、化学液、血液等污染不按本普通雨水流程处理；电热鞋、专业防护鞋先看厂家。

### 准备与输入

确认来源是普通雨水、鞋材和护理说明允许的晾法。准备吸水垫、可替换的无印刷染色纸或干布、室内通风位；别把鞋直接放在易染家具上。若有人要马上穿，先另找干鞋，不能用吹风机、暖气、烘干机或直晒硬赶速度。手眼/体力不便可请人协助移鞋。

### 执行

1. **打开能打开的部分。** 松鞋带、魔术贴或搭扣，轻拿出厂家允许拆的鞋垫；不撬粘住的部件。倒掉可安全倒出的积水，软布按压外层，不扭鞋身。
2. **支撑原有形状。** 在鞋头轻放干净吸水纸/布到能撑起塌下的部位即可，别塞得撑大鞋；纸湿透时更换。左右鞋分开放，让空气能从开口进入，鞋垫另摊晾。
3. **在室温通风处等。** 远离高热、明火、直晒和儿童/宠物通道；按鞋款说明可能需要比一夜更久。偶尔查鞋头、鞋跟、内层和鞋垫底面，湿就继续通风，不因外层先干就密闭收纳。
4. **干后判断能否重新穿。** 取出支撑纸，检查无潮湿、异味、开胶、变形或硬裂，再把干鞋垫与鞋带装回。若已开胶、发霉或明显变形，停穿并问厂家/维修者；本 Skill 不承诺恢复原性能。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

鞋内外和可拆件已干，形状基本稳定、无新明显损坏，才重新收纳或穿；仍湿时明确留在晾干区。只摸鞋面干、内里潮，却宣称完成，不算。

- **吸水纸把鞋头撑变形：** 立刻减少填充，恢复轻支撑。
- **晾了一晚仍湿：** 继续通风、换湿纸，不上直热。
- **麂皮出现水痕：** 先全干，再按本材质专用护理说明，不湿刷更多水。
- **闻到霉味或见开胶：** 停穿并求厂家/合适维修，不靠香粉遮盖。
- **位置不够或移动困难：** 分开摆在可管理通风处，请可信协助移鞋，不把湿鞋密封进袋。

### 假设与边界

鞋型、内衬和天气改变干燥时间。Clarks 的湿鞋指导用于其鞋类说明；实际鞋款若不同，以本鞋标签优先。自然晾干不等于防水或抓地性能检测。

## English

Load this Skill when **an ordinary pair is wet through from clean rainwater or clearly holds water** and you need to support its shape and dry inside and out naturally. Finish when shoe lining, removable insoles, and upper are dry under the model's directions, or honestly keep waiting. This differs from brushing dirt or washing detached insoles. Contaminated floodwater, chemicals, or blood are outside an ordinary rain route; powered or protective footwear needs maker guidance.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm ordinary rainwater and the model's material and drying guidance. Have an absorbent protective mat, replaceable plain non-bleeding paper or dry cloth, and an airy indoor spot. Do not set shoes on stain-sensitive furniture. If they must be worn immediately, choose a different dry pair rather than rushing with a hair dryer, radiator, tumble dryer, or intense sun. Ask help moving shoes if grip or stamina is limited.

### Execution

1. **Open allowed parts.** Loosen laces, hook-and-loop closures, or buckles. Gently remove only maker-approved detachable insoles; do not pry bonded ones. Tip out water that can safely drain and blot the outside with soft cloth rather than twisting the shoe.
2. **Support original shape.** Put enough clean absorbent paper or cloth in the toe to support a collapse, without stretching it. Replace wet stuffing. Separate left and right shoes so air reaches openings and spread removable insoles out separately.
3. **Wait with room-temperature airflow.** Keep away from concentrated heat, flame, harsh sun, and a child or pet traffic path. Actual models can take longer than one night. Check toe, heel, lining, and insole underside from time to time; keep airing while any remains wet rather than closing it into storage when only the upper is dry.
4. **Decide whether to wear again.** Remove stuffing and check for remaining dampness, odor, separating glue, distortion, or stiff cracks. Reinsert dry insoles and laces only when sound. Stop wear and ask maker or repairer about separation, mold, or major shape change; no original-performance promise is made.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Upper, inside, and removable parts are dry with broadly preserved shape and no obvious new damage before storage or wear, or the still-wet pair clearly remains in drying. A dry-feeling upper over damp lining is not completion.

- **Stuffing stretches the toe:** Remove some immediately and restore light support.
- **Still wet next morning:** Continue airflow and replace damp paper, without direct heat.
- **Suede develops a water mark:** Dry fully first, then use suede-specific care rather than more wet brushing.
- **Mold smell or loose bonding appears:** Stop wear and ask maker or repairer instead of covering smell with powder.
- **Space or moving is difficult:** Keep shoes apart in a manageable aired spot and use trusted moving help, not a sealed wet bag.

### Assumptions and limits

Construction, lining, and weather change drying time. Clarks gives a wet-shoe method for its shoes; actual model labels take priority. Air drying is not a waterproof or traction performance test.
