## 中文

当一件普通大件的尺寸与目标位置已经核对过，交付或搬入即将发生时加载。你要确认**今天的通道确实可用、进入权限已安排、服务方的交付终点与你的计划一致**，然后交给实际搬运者判断安全操作。静态尺寸通过不代表通道今天没有被箱子、访客或楼宇安排挡住。这里不教提举、拆门、拆家具、修改建筑或解释服务合同权利。

### 准备与输入

拿到先前的物品与包装尺寸、最窄通道的测量记录和预计进入路线。确认实际交付服务是到门口还是到指定房间、到达时间、谁有权开放入口或协调公共区域，以及第一落位。看实际服务方现行的楼梯、电梯、包装和房间交付条件；不要套用别家规则。关键测量缺失时，先补上或请服务方核实，不用今天的目测替代。

### 执行

1. **从交付点走一遍今天的路线。** 不搬大件，只看入口、门扇实际可开范围、走廊转角、楼梯平台、电梯和目标房间。对照已量好的净空间，记录新出现的普通障碍、暂放箱、关闭的门或改变的入口安排。
2. **处理你有权且能安全处理的小障碍。** 将自己的轻量杂物移离通道，或联系有权人员处理公共物品；不要阻塞其他人的通行、触动消防设施、擅自移门或为通路拆卸承重/电气设备。暂时清空后还要确认不会在到货前再被占用。
3. **核对实际权限和交付终点。** 确定到货时谁开门、是否需要预约电梯/允许使用该区域、服务方承诺到门口还是房内、第一落位有无空间。不能进入某段或无权作决定，就向管理者或服务方问清，而不是把“尺寸够”当作许可。
4. **把变化交给搬运方。** 告诉实际负责人路线、最窄处、临时障碍、目的地区域和仍未知的条件。若包装方式、物品状态或路线不同于先前的尺寸记录，请对方重新判断；你不指挥危险转角或强行穿越。
5. **做一个开工前裁决。** 写“今天可进入并有接收位置 / 等待清障或许可 / 请服务方改交付方案”。只在当前路线、权限和交付终点都已确认时让交付按计划开始；事实变化就重新核对。

### 完成与停止

交付者与接收者有同一条今天可用的路线、已确认的进入安排及第一落位，剩余风险交给有资质的实际搬运者判断。通道尺寸不明、公共区未获许可、设备或人的安全受影响、交付范围不同于预期时暂停并协调或改期，不用试搬来验证纸面假设。

### 常见报错与补救

- **上周量过，今天门口堆了箱：** 安全且有权时移开轻量物品，或请负责人清障；清不掉就通知交付方调整。
- **房内交付以为已购买，实际只有门口交付：** 查真实订单/服务说明，安排合法可行的后续接手或修改服务，不让物品滞留在共用通道。
- **电梯或入口今天不能用：** 问管理方和服务方可用时间/替代交付点；不私自决定楼梯搬运。
- **实物包装与记录不一致：** 重新取得准确尺寸和服务方确认，原“能过”结论暂停。
- **你无法亲自走完整条路：** 请获准进入的人按照片、尺寸和障碍读回；无法查到的路段继续标为未知。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通搬入和已有静态尺寸记录。楼宇规定、地区、服务等级和个人行动条件差异很大；本文不设通用通道数值或许诺搬运一定成功。不能行走、测量或移动障碍时，请愿意且有权限的人代查，并让实际搬运方独立决定方法。副作用是“临时”堆在通道的箱子需要真正找个去处。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary large item's dimensions and destination were already checked and its delivery or move-in is close. You will confirm **that today's path is open, access is arranged and the provider's handoff point matches your plan**, then leave safe handling to the actual movers. A static fit check does not prove that today's corridor is clear of cartons, people or building restrictions. This does not teach lifting, removing doors, dismantling furniture, altering the building or interpreting contract rights.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the earlier item and package dimensions, measurements at the route's narrow points and the intended path. Confirm whether the actual service delivers to the entrance or a chosen room, the arrival time, who can open the entry or coordinate shared areas, and the first placement point. Read the real provider's current stairs, lift, packaging and in-room conditions; another company may differ. If critical measurements are missing, obtain them or ask the provider to assess rather than replacing them with a quick glance today.

### Execution

1. **Walk today's path from handoff to destination.** Do not move the large item. Check the entry, actual door swing, corridor corners, landings, lift and destination room. Compare with measured clear openings, and record any new ordinary obstruction, staged carton, closed door or changed access arrangement.
2. **Address small obstacles you may safely handle.** Move your own light clutter out of the route or ask the authorised person to handle shared items. Do not block others, touch fire equipment, remove doors on your own or dismantle load-bearing or electrical equipment. Check that a cleared route will stay clear until arrival.
3. **Confirm access and the agreed endpoint.** Name who opens the entrance, whether a lift booking or area permission is needed, whether the provider stops at the doorstep or enters the room, and whether there is a first placement spot. Ask building management or the provider about inaccessible or unauthorised segments; dimensions alone do not grant permission.
4. **Tell the movers what changed.** Give the actual lead the route, narrow points, temporary obstacles, destination area and remaining unknowns. If package form, item condition or path differs from the recorded measurements, have them reassess. Do not direct an unsafe turn or force passage.
5. **Decide before work starts.** Record “current entry and receiving spot confirmed / waiting for clearance or permission / ask provider for another delivery arrangement.” Start the planned delivery only when today's path, access and endpoint are confirmed; repeat the check if facts change.

### Success and stop

The provider and receiver share one currently usable path, confirmed access arrangement and first placement point; the real qualified handlers still judge the physical move. Pause and coordinate or reschedule if route dimensions are unknown, shared access is unauthorised, equipment or people would be endangered, or service scope differs from the plan. Do not trial-carry a large object to test a paper assumption.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Measured last week; boxes block the entrance today:** Remove light items when safe and permitted or ask their owner to clear them. Tell the provider if they cannot be cleared.
- **Expected in-room delivery, bought doorstep service:** Read the real order and service scope; arrange a lawful feasible handoff or change service rather than leaving the object in a shared passage.
- **Lift or entrance unavailable today:** Ask management and provider about another time or endpoint. Do not decide on stair carrying yourself.
- **Actual packaging differs from the record:** Get its accurate dimensions and provider confirmation. Suspend the previous fit conclusion.
- **You cannot inspect the whole route:** Ask someone authorised to enter to read back photographs, measurements and obstructions. Mark unseen segments unknown.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers an ordinary move-in after a static dimension check. Building rules, region, service level and personal mobility vary; there is no universal passage number or delivery guarantee here. If you cannot walk, measure or clear an obstacle, ask a willing authorised person to inspect, and let the actual mover choose its own handling method. The side effect is that a “temporary” corridor carton must find a real home.
