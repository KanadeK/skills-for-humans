## 中文

当一株普通、仍可支撑的观赏多年生草本因花重或普通风雨向旁边倒，压到邻株或通道时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**给它相称、可调整的支撑**；已经折断的茎不会因为绑回去就愈合。

### 准备与输入

确认地块获准管理、植物身份、茎是否仍完整，以及适用支架类型。选能承受该株实际高度和花重的轻型支架或软绳，避免粗硬线直接勒茎。只在你能确认地下无设施且不需深打桩的小范围操作；大型树木、承重结构、尖刺或未知刺激性植物不在这里。若植株处于公共通道，先保证通道安全再处理。

### 执行

1. **看清是倾斜还是断裂。** 完整但倾倒的茎可尝试支持；折裂、腐软或有明显病害的茎先停下查品种护理，不能硬绑成“已修好”。
2. **选位置与支架。** 在不压邻株、不堵行走路、不碰地下设施的位置放一个与株形相称的支撑。春季提前支撑较容易；现在补救时动作要更轻，避免折损已长成的枝叶。
3. **逐段轻扶。** 把茎慢慢引到自然可承受的位置，不强迫完全笔直。需绑单根茎时，按品种与支架说明让软带在茎与支架之间交叉成松的 8 字，打结留出生长和随风轻动的空间；不要把茎勒死在杆上。
4. **检查固定和入口。** 轻看支架是否独立稳固、绑带没有切进茎、通道不绊脚。高风风险或支架无法稳固时停止并求适当园艺帮助，不加高危险尖杆凑合。
5. **留下调整点。** 记下日期和需要检查的绑带松紧、支架倾斜、茎生长；随着植物长高适时调节，暂时直立不是永久合格证明。

### 成功条件

植株得到相称支撑，仍能轻微自然活动；绑带不勒茎，支架不堵通道且下次调整时间明确。若茎已断裂、支撑不稳或地面情况不明，留下真实状态并求助，不声称已修复。

### 常见报错与补救

- **细线勒出一道沟：** 改用软绑带并放松，记录损伤；不让伤口继续受力。
- **强拉到完全直立时茎发响：** 立刻停止，回到自然可承受角度。
- **支架成了绊脚点：** 移出通道或换低矮安全方案，不把别人绕路当作接受。
- **支架随风一起倒：** 停止继续加绑，重新评估大小与锚固资格，必要时求助。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只是小型普通草本观赏植物的**非结构性**支撑，不做树木修剪、土建固定或公共设施安装。握力或弯腰受限时请人协助绑扎或在可及高床操作；看不清茎伤口时请人描述。支撑物会留在原处，需要复查，不给植物存活保证。

## English

Load this Skill when a small ordinary herbaceous ornamental is leaning after flower weight or normal weather and is pressing on a neighbor or path while its stems are still supportable. Finish with **suitable adjustable support**. A snapped stem does not heal because it has been tied upright.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission to manage the bed, plant identity, whether stems remain intact, and suitable support type. Choose a light support and soft tie sized for actual height and flower weight; avoid hard wire against stems. Work only where you know there are no buried services and no deep driven posts are needed. Trees, load-bearing work, spines, and unknown irritating plants are outside scope. If a public path is affected, secure access before plant work.

### Execution

1. **Distinguish lean from break.** An intact leaning stem may be supported. A snapped, rotten, or disease-like stem needs species guidance first; binding it is not repair.
2. **Choose support and position.** Place a structure suited to the plant without crushing neighbors, blocking a path, or disturbing underground services. Early-season support is easier; emergency support around mature growth needs gentler movement.
3. **Guide in small steps.** Ease stems toward a position they can naturally tolerate, not mandatory perfect vertical. For one stem, follow species and support directions to cross a soft tie between stem and support in a loose figure eight, leaving space for growth and wind movement. Do not cinch a stem to the stake.
4. **Check stability and access.** Confirm the support stands independently, ties do not cut stems, and nobody will trip over it. Stop and seek suitable horticultural help if high wind or poor anchoring makes the setup unreliable.
5. **Set an adjustment check.** Record date and what to inspect: tie tightness, leaning support, and new growth. Adjust as the plant grows. Upright for one moment is not a permanent certificate.

### Success

The plant has proportionate support and some natural movement; ties do not constrict stems, paths stay open, and adjustment has a review point. If stems are broken, support unstable, or ground conditions unknown, record the state and seek help rather than claiming repair.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A thin tie cuts a groove:** Switch to a soft loose tie and record injury; stop further load on the wound.
- **A stem creaks while forced vertical:** Stop and let it return to a tolerable angle.
- **The support trips pedestrians:** Move it clear or choose a lower safe option. Others detouring around it is not acceptance.
- **The support falls with the plant in wind:** Stop adding ties; reassess size and anchoring or seek help.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is **non-structural** support for a small ordinary herbaceous ornamental, not tree work, building anchorage, or public-fixture installation. Arrange help for ties if grip or bending is difficult; work in a reachable raised bed when possible. Ask for a stem-damage description if vision is limited. The support stays and must be checked; survival is not promised.
