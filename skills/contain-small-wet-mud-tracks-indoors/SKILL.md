## 中文

当你把**确知是普通室外土壤的小量湿泥**带进门，鞋底或门垫仍在继续印出潮脚印时，用这份 Skill 先停止扩散、取走松散泥块，并把地板/地毯交给各自材质的后续清理。它与干沙不同：湿泥和水会传播与打滑，不能直接按干砂扫，也不能在布料里用力揉出一大片泥。污水、动物粪便、农药/化学品、尖锐物、大范围浸透或未知来源退出本篇。

### 准备与输入

从干燥稳定处看湿泥已到哪里、鞋/门垫是否还滴泥、地面是木、乙烯基、瓷砖还是地毯。找本地材质与门垫护理说明，准备干净白布/吸水纸、不尖锐的取泥工具、接泥容器和可暂放鞋的地方。先告诉同住者绕开湿滑路；靠近通电设备、插座或需要站在水里清理时停止并求合格帮助。地材已起翘、缝下积水或垫层湿透也不属于小范围控制。

### 执行

1. **截断鞋与门垫的继续传播。** 在安全处脱下或暂放湿泥鞋，让人绕行；按门垫材质安排吸泥/后续晾干，不把它在室内大力抖开。不能安全脱鞋或移动门垫时请可信的人协助，不走过湿区去取工具。
2. **先取泥块，再吸自由水。** 用软工具轻挑可见半固体泥块入容器，不把砂砾压进地材；对白布/纸轻按游离水和表面泥迹，换干净面，避免来回强擦。地毯/布垫不把湿泥刷进纤维和背衬。周围仍滑时持续提示/隔离。
3. **按材质分配后续时机。** 硬地面颗粒移走后，按地材厂家许可去剩余泥膜并干燥；对地毯上少量仍附着但不再流动的普通泥，先读地毯/产品说明，允许时待松散固体干后用合适吸尘方式处理，再决定是否做材料适用的小处点清。不能将一块地板清洁产品直接喷上地毯或未封闭木地板。
4. **检查边缘和鞋垫去向。** 看门缝、踢脚边、鞋底纹路和门垫背面是否仍会带出泥水；可接近的残留按各自材料说明处理，无法接近或持续渗水时停问物业/专业人员。用过的布/工具分开洗干，别带到食品接触区。
5. **恢复通行或标示待干。** 游离泥水不再扩散、不会有人踩到滑点时，按地材说明让地面完全干再撤提示；如果地毯/垫层仍湿，保留隔离和后续检查，不宣称表面看起来干就代表内部干。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

小量已知湿泥的来源被截住，松散泥块和自由水安全移出，行走路径被隔离直到地材许可下可再次通行，鞋/门垫/地面各有后续去向；无法安全到达或渗入结构时停下也是完整结果。

- **越擦脚印越大：** 停止摩擦，先轻取块状泥、换干净吸水面，再查地材说明。
- **湿泥刷进地毯：** 停止刷洗，吸走表面水并按地毯护理判断下一步，不自创强力去渍。
- **木地板缝里有水：** 不灌更多水“冲干净”，先隔离并按该地材/物业建议处理。
- **鞋底还在滴泥：** 暂不让鞋穿回室内，单独按鞋材清理/晾干。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理小范围、已知普通室外湿土，不处理洪水淤泥、污水、动物排泄物、危险粉尘或化学品。不同地材与地毯背衬对水和工具的容忍不同，不用一份“万能拖地剂”。行动/视力受限时先隔离路径、用可及轻工具或可信协助；不能看见渗入范围就不声称干透。清理后仍可能有需晾干的鞋垫、布和地毯。

## English

Use this Skill when a small amount of **known ordinary outdoor soil is wet and tracked inside**, with shoes or a mat still making damp prints. Stop spreading, lift loose clumps, and hand flooring or carpet to its material care. Unlike dry grit, wet mud and water move and slip; do not simply dry-sweep it or scrub it widely into fabric. Sewage, animal waste, pesticide or chemical, sharps, broad saturation, or unknown source leave this route.

### Preparation and inputs

From a dry stable point, see the mud path and whether shoe or mat still drips. Identify wood, vinyl, tile, or carpet. Find floor and mat care. Have clean white cloth or paper, a blunt lifting tool, a mud container, and a place for shoes. Ask others to go around. Stop and seek qualified help near powered equipment or outlets or where cleaning requires standing in water. Lifted flooring, water beneath seams, or a soaked pad is beyond a small track.

### Execution

1. **Cut off shoe and mat spread.** Remove or hold muddy footwear at a safe point and route people around it. Blot or later dry a mat under its care rather than shake it hard indoors. Ask trusted help if shoes or mat cannot be moved safely. Do not cross a wet patch for tools.
2. **Lift clumps before blotting free water.** Take semisolid mud with a soft tool into a container without pressing grit into a finish. Blot free water and surface traces with clean white paper or cloth, turning to clean sections instead of scrubbing back and forth. Do not brush wet mud deeper into carpet fibres or backing. Keep a warning while the path slips.
3. **Route later work by material and timing.** Once grains leave a hard floor, use maker-permitted care for the mud film and dry it. For small attached but no-longer-flowing ordinary mud on carpet, read carpet and product directions: where allowed, let loose solids dry enough to vacuum suitably, then decide whether a tested material-compatible spot treatment is needed. Do not put one vinyl cleaner on carpet or unfinished wood.
4. **Check edges, shoe, and mat.** See whether threshold, skirting, shoe tread, and mat back still transfer moisture or grit. Handle accessible parts by their own care. Stop for inaccessible spaces or continuing seepage and ask property or qualified help. Clean and dry used cloth and tools separately from food-contact use.
5. **Reopen or mark for drying.** Free mudwater no longer spreads. Keep the path isolated until the flooring is dry under its care. A wet carpet or pad remains held for later checking; a surface that looks dry does not certify its interior.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

The source of a small known wet trail is stopped; clumps and free water have been lifted, the route stays isolated until material-safe use, and shoe, mat, and floor have destinations. Stopping for unsafe reach or moisture in construction is a complete result.

- **Wiping grows the footprint:** Stop rubbing. Lift mud clumps, change blotting material, and use floor care.
- **Wet mud is brushed into carpet:** Stop brushing, blot surface water, and check the carpet's actual directions before another treatment.
- **Water enters wood-floor joints:** Do not add more water to “rinse”; isolate and follow that floor or property guidance.
- **The shoe still drips:** Keep it out of indoor traffic and clean or dry under its material care.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a small known ordinary outdoor mud trail, not flood silt, sewage, animal waste, hazardous dust, or chemicals. Flooring and carpet backing differ in water and tool limits; no universal mopping mix applies. For limited reach or vision, isolate first and use a light accessible tool or trusted help. Do not claim deep drying when the extent cannot be checked. Shoes, mat, cloth, or carpet may still need drying.
