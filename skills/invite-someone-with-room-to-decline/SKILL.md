## 中文

你想邀请熟人参加一次普通、可选择的活动，但又不想让对方先猜活动内容、承受默认答应的压力时，用这份 Skill。目标是**发出对方能据此判断、也能轻松拒绝的邀请**；发出去本身可以完成这一步，对方答应不是你的可控成功条件。接受、拒绝、改期或多人协调分别由后续任务处理。

### 准备与输入

先选一项你真的会参加的活动，比如喝咖啡、一起逛展或短时间散步。写下大致地点/形式、时长、费用或需预约的要点，以及你真正有空的一个或两个时间窗。地点尚未定也可以明确说“地点再商量”；成本或交通负担若明显，别把它藏在“随便聚聚”里。想清楚是否必须在某时前得到答复；只有活动有真实预订或安排期限才提出这个时间点。

### 执行

1. **先说目的和最小条件。** 在合适的私下渠道发一条短消息：活动、可行时间范围、地点或线上形式、预计时长。不要让对方先回答“你有空吗”才揭晓活动；同意空闲不等于同意参加。
2. **给出真实退路。** 直接写“如果不方便，直接说没关系”，并允许对方不解释原因。措辞要与行动一致：不能一边写“没压力”，一边连发催促或要求对方证明为什么不来。若你只接受某个时段，也诚实说清；不要伪装成无限可改。
3. **把待确认部分标为待确认。** 可用一句话收尾：“如果你有兴趣，我们再确认具体地点。” 不在对方答复前订一个不能取消的名额、把对方加入群聊或发日历邀请。若要先订位且需要回应期限，说明真实期限和未回复时你会自行处理，不把沉默当同意。
4. **发送后留出空间。** 用对方通常能接收的方式发一次；若活动临近且确有协调必要，可做一次简短核对。没有回复，就按未接受处理并安排自己的选择；拒绝后不改用其他渠道追问。收到有条件的答复时，复述该条件并交给“确认安排”的下一步，而不是直接写“约好了”。

可以改写成你自己的话，例如：“这周六下午想去看〔展览〕，大约一小时，门票约〔金额〕。你有兴趣一起吗？不方便也完全没关系；如果想去，我们再定具体时间。” 数字、地点和费用必须替换成真实信息；这不是固定社交咒语。

### 成功条件

一条邀请已经通过合适渠道发出，对方能从中知道要做什么、何时大致进行、有哪些显著成本或限制、能否拒绝；你记录状态为“待回复 / 接受待确认细节 / 拒绝 / 无回复”，而非默认成行。无回复、拒绝和有条件答复都是有效结果，不需要把对方说服到同意才算此步完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只问“周末有空吗”：** 补上活动、时间范围与明显成本，让对方知道自己在回答什么。
- **为了显得随和，给出你做不到的五个时段：** 收回不真实的选项，留下你确实能安排的时间。
- **“没关系”后又反复催答：** 停止连续提醒；除非有真实截止，否则给对方空间并自己安排替代。
- **对方说“也许”，你已经订了两张票：** 把“也许”保留为未确认，说明实际订票期限；未经同意不要替对方承担费用或占位。
- **对方拒绝：** 简单确认收到即可，不要求解释，也不把拒绝写成待争取状态。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里假设双方可以自由选择的普通非工作强制活动。权力关系、宗教或文化礼节、费用能力和个人界限会改变合适的措辞与渠道；你要按实际关系调整，而不是套统一“高情商”句式。无法使用文字渠道时可当面或通过双方常用的可访问方式表达同样信息，但仍给对方退出空间。明确的拒绝权有时会让活动落空；这比建立一个只有你知道的约定更诚实。

## English

Use this Skill when you want to invite an acquaintance to an ordinary optional activity without making them guess the activity or feel that a first reply commits them. Finish with **an invitation they can assess and comfortably decline**. Sending a usable invitation completes this step; their agreement is not under your control. Accepting, declining, rescheduling, and coordinating a group are separate next tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose something you would genuinely attend, such as coffee, an exhibition, or a short walk. Note the approximate place or format, duration, notable cost or booking need, and one or two time windows you can actually make. It is fine to say “we can choose the exact place later.” Do not hide a significant ticket or travel cost behind “just hanging out.” Decide whether a response deadline is truly necessary; use one only when a real booking or planning cutoff exists.

### Execution

1. **State the purpose and minimum conditions first.** In a suitable private channel, send one short message with the activity, workable time range, place or online format, and approximate duration. Do not ask “Are you free?” and reveal the activity only after they answer. Being free is not agreeing to attend.
2. **Offer a real way out.** Say directly that declining is fine and they need not explain. Make your actions match the phrase: “no pressure” followed by repeated nudges or a demand for reasons is pressure. If you can only do one slot, be honest rather than pretending every day works.
3. **Label what is still undecided.** You can close with “If you are interested, we can confirm the exact place.” Do not make a non-refundable booking for them, add them to a group chat, or send a calendar event before they respond. If a place must be booked, explain the real reply deadline and what you will do without a reply. Silence is not consent.
4. **Leave room after sending.** Send once through a channel they normally receive. If the activity is near and coordination genuinely needs it, make one short check. With no reply, treat it as not accepted and choose your own next step. After a refusal, do not switch channels to pursue an answer. For a conditional reply, restate the condition and move to confirming details; do not mark the outing “booked.”

In your own words, for example: “I am thinking of seeing [exhibition] on Saturday afternoon, around an hour, with tickets about [amount]. Would you like to come? If it does not work for you, no worries. If it does, we can settle the exact time.” Replace the amount, place, and time with real facts; this is an example, not a social incantation.

### Success

One invitation has reached a suitable channel. The other person can tell what it is, roughly when it would happen, any significant cost or constraint, and that declining is allowed. Record the reply honestly as “pending / interested, details pending / declined / no reply,” not an assumed plan. No reply, a refusal, or a conditional answer are all valid outcomes. You do not need to persuade the person into yes to complete this step.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “Are you free this weekend?” was sent:** Add the activity, workable window, and significant cost so they know what they are answering.
- **You offered five slots you cannot make:** Correct the options and keep only real ones.
- **“No worries” is followed by repeated reminders:** Stop the nudge chain. Unless there is a real cutoff, give space and make your own alternative plans.
- **They said “maybe,” but you bought two tickets:** Keep “maybe” unconfirmed and explain any real ticket deadline. Do not commit them to cost or a place without agreement.
- **They decline:** Acknowledge simply; do not demand a reason or label the refusal “still persuadable.”

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes an ordinary optional activity between people free to choose. Power differences, religious or cultural customs, money, and personal boundaries can change the right wording and channel; adjust to your actual relationship rather than a universal “polite” script. If text is inaccessible, speak in person or use another channel both of you normally use, with the same clear information and exit option. A genuine option to decline may leave the outing unfilled; that is more honest than a plan only you know exists.
