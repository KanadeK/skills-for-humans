## 中文

当你的一只**已稳定淡水缸按既有鱼种方案到了部分换水时间**，水处理剂和专用工具都备齐时，加载这份 Skill。结果是计划比例的旧水安全移出、处理过且温度适配的新水慢慢补入、设备和鱼状态复查；不是整缸换空。具体比例/频率由本缸方案决定，RSPCA 与 OATA 的示例值不一致也不能被当成世界统一量。新缸、异常水质急救或漏电不在本普通流程。

### 准备与输入

读原方案和试剂结果，量当前水量与允许更换量，备**只给鱼缸使用**的干净桶、可手动启动不需口吸的虹吸器、合用除氯/氯胺剂和温度计。按产品说明先处理替换水并核温度与本缸鱼种范围相容；单纯静置不一定去除氯胺。水和电器相邻时按设备说明安全处理，出现漏电、破线、湿插头直接停并找合格人员。覆盖手上伤口，事后洗手。

### 执行

1. **先稳住鱼与设备。** 看鱼没有明显危急、设备正常，按制造商安全说明处理会在低水位暴露的设备；不自行拆电热/过滤结构。鱼通常留缸以减少搬动，但若本缸专业方案不同，依其规定。
2. **只移走计划部分。** 将鱼缸专用桶放稳，启动不需口吸的虹吸器，缓慢从目标水层/底物表面带走旧水与可见残屑，避开鱼和饰物；达到本次已定量即停。**绝不嘴吸软管。** 水量或鱼受惊失控时停下，回到安全状态。
3. **核新水再缓慢补入。** 再确认水处理剂按这桶水的真实体积用量、温度和本缸计划相容，避免直接倒未处理自来水。沿缸壁/合用导流方式缓加，不冲散底砂、不骤变水温。若处理剂说明不清，暂停补水并求水族专业帮助。
4. **复查与记录。** 水位回到计划位置，按设备说明恢复运行并看流量/温度与鱼行为；移走专用桶中的旧水按当地和环境规则处理，不放生水族生物/植物。记时间、更换量、处理剂/温度和异常；按计划安排后续水质检测。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

计划部分水已被处理新水替换，温度/设备/鱼状态复核并记录，器具仍为鱼缸专用；缺水处理剂、温度不符或电气危险时暂停交专业人员。只换了看得见的清水、未核处理剂，不算完成。

- **处理剂不足或说明不清：** 停换水，先备合用产品并求可信水族指导。
- **新水温度不合范围：** 暂不补入，按计划安全调整/等待，不向鱼身直接冲水。
- **虹吸器需要嘴吸：** 换无需口吸的专用器具，绝不以口启动。
- **设备接触水或电线异常：** 保持距离并交合格人员，不带水拆修。
- **体力/视力不足以控制桶和水位：** 减小单次操作或请可信且熟悉本缸方案的人协助，不能用猜的体积。

### 假设与边界

普通部分换水应服从鱼种、缸体、既有测试与设备说明。厂商/福利机构示例百分比在不同阶段不同，本文不设万能换水比例，也不处理药物、急救或新缸循环。

## English

Load this Skill when an **established freshwater tank is due for a partial change under its existing species plan** and suitable conditioner and dedicated tools are ready. Finish with the planned portion removed and replaced slowly with treated temperature-compatible water, then equipment and fish checked. Never empty the whole tank by default. Exact volume/frequency belong to this tank's plan; different RSPCA and OATA examples are not one global rule. New tanks, abnormal-water emergency response, and electrical faults are outside this routine.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the existing plan and test results; know present volume and approved replacement amount. Have a clean **aquarium-only** bucket, siphon started without a mouth, suitable chlorine/chloramine conditioner, and thermometer. Treat replacement water by its product directions and compare its temperature with the species range; simply standing tap water may not neutralise chloramine. Follow device safety for electricity near water. Stop for leakage, broken wires, or a wet plug and seek qualified help instead of service work. Cover hand cuts and wash hands afterwards.

### Execution

1. **Stabilise fish and equipment first.** Check fish are not clearly distressed and equipment operates. Follow maker safety steps for devices that could be exposed as water drops, without dismantling heater or filter. Fish ordinarily remain in the tank to avoid handling, unless this tank's professional plan directs otherwise.
2. **Remove only the planned portion.** Steady the aquarium-only bucket and start a no-mouth siphon. Move slowly through the selected water/substrate surface to lift old water and visible waste away from fish and decor. Stop at the amount set by the plan. **Never suck a hose by mouth.** Pause if volume control or fish response becomes unsafe.
3. **Verify and add replacement gently.** Recheck conditioner amount for this bucket's real water volume and replacement temperature against the plan. Do not pour untreated tap water straight in. Add slowly by wall or suitable diffuser without blasting substrate or causing abrupt temperature change. Hold and seek aquatics help if the conditioner directions are unclear.
4. **Recheck and record.** Restore planned water level, run equipment again under its directions, and observe flow, temperature, and fish. Dispose of old aquarium water under local and environmental rules without releasing aquarium plants or animals. Record time, volume, conditioner/temperature, anomalies, and next planned test.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The planned portion was replaced by treated water, temperature/equipment/fish were checked and recorded, and equipment remains dedicated to the tank. Missing conditioner, wrong temperature, or electrical danger creates a hold and expert route. Clear-looking replacement poured without checking treatment is unfinished.

- **Conditioner missing or unclear:** Stop and obtain a suitable product and trusted aquatics advice first.
- **Replacement temperature outside range:** Do not add it yet; adjust or wait safely under the plan, without directing water at fish.
- **Siphon requires mouth suction:** Use a no-mouth aquarium device, never your mouth.
- **Equipment meets water or cords are abnormal:** Stay back and use qualified help, not wet electrical repair.
- **Stamina or sight cannot control bucket/level:** Reduce operation size or ask a trusted person who knows this tank's plan; do not guess volume.

### Assumptions and limits

Routine partial changes follow species, tank, existing tests, and device instructions. Example percentages vary by stage and source, so there is no universal change fraction here. Medicine, emergencies, and new cycling remain outside scope.
