## 中文

当你有一张**与泡棉版对应的真实试印**，其中某处问题确实来自版上的凹线或方向，而非只是墨量/纸湿度时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一处有目的的安全改版和第二张可比较试印；若凹线无法撤销，明确重做，不把换色/重压伪称为修版。它与首次试印的开头不同，依据是已有纸面证据。

### 准备与输入

把干燥的第一张试印与印版按镜像方向并排，找一处具体问题：应该留白的线太浅、线被遗漏、构图边界需要加深。若问题只是颜料太多、太少或纸滑动，先修印法而非版。备钝笔、废纸、少量可洗水基画材和可保护的台面；不使用锐利刀具。文字方向错误常需新板，不能把旧凹线“擦掉”。

### 执行

1. **把纸上问题映回版上。** 试印和版左右对照，轻指想改的那一段，在背面或纸旁记具体位置。确认是版的物理线条问题；否则停止本修版任务并改做印刷参数试验。
2. **只改一个可逆范围的小目标。** 用钝笔在对应处轻补浅线或加一段必需凹线，先边角校准压力，避免扎穿。原来错误的深凹线不能恢复成完整凸面；需去掉整条时换新泡棉版。
3. **清理并第二次试印。** 若上次颜料残留，按画材说明清洁/晾干版，再用同类可洗水基画材和相近纸按首次试印法取第二张。给纸标“修版后试印 2”，平放晾干。
4. **对照结论。** 比较两张的目标位置，记录改善、未变或变坏；版受损时停止继续压。若问题消失但新问题是墨量，保留版并在印法上另查，不无限改版。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

你能指出第一张的问题、一处实际改版、第二张对应变化；或确认旧凹线不可撤销而换版。只说“第二张看着更好”却不知道改了哪里，不算证据驱动修版。

- **试印与版左右对不上：** 先标镜像和版角，再动钝笔。
- **越补越深把版刺穿：** 停止，重做版；不用胶带盖破口假作完整凸面。
- **原本要删除的线已经压深：** 不能按橡皮擦方式复原，设计新版本或重做。
- **第二张仍浅但版凹线清楚：** 排查上色/压力/湿度，不继续压深版。
- **手眼不便：** 大试印、对角编号和可信描述可帮助映射，由你决定修点和停点。

### 假设与边界

泡棉修版主要能加深/增加凹痕，不能可靠删除旧痕。小批试印迭代是印版工作方式，但本文不教专业版画认证、锐利刻刀或版权判断。

## English

Load this Skill when a **real first proof matches your foam plate** and one problem is actually in its recessed line or orientation, not merely pigment load or paper dampness. Finish with one purposeful safe plate change and a comparable second proof, or remake when an unwanted indentation cannot be erased. Changing colour or pressing harder is not a plate revision. A paper proof supplies the entry evidence.

### Preparation and inputs

Put the dry first proof beside the plate in mirrored orientation and locate one concrete defect: a missing or faint uninked line or a boundary that needs more definition. If the only issue is excess ink, too little ink, or paper movement, adjust printing instead of the plate. Have a dull pencil, scrap, a little washable water-based colour, and protected surface, without sharp carving tools. Incorrect text direction often needs a new plate because old dents do not rub out.

### Execution

1. **Map proof defect back to plate.** Compare left and right reversal and identify the target segment. Note its location on paper or the plate back. Confirm this is a physical plate-line issue; otherwise stop this revision and test print settings.
2. **Make one limited change.** With a dull tip, reinforce a faint line or add one necessary short recess, calibrating pressure at a margin without puncturing. A wrongly deep old recess cannot become raised foam again; use a new plate if it must disappear.
3. **Clean and pull a second proof.** If last pigment remains, clean and dry the plate under material directions. Make another impression with similar washable colour and paper using the first-proof steps. Label it “revised plate proof 2” and let it dry flat.
4. **Compare actual outcomes.** Check the target in both proofs and record improvement, no change, or a worse result. Stop pressing a damaged plate. If the line is now sound but ink load fails, keep the plate and investigate the printing process rather than over-revising.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You can point to a defect on proof 1, one physical plate change, and the corresponding difference on proof 2, or state that an unwanted old recess requires a new plate. “The second one looks better” without knowing what changed is not evidenced revision.

- **Proof and plate do not align:** Mark reversal and plate corners before touching the pencil.
- **Repeated pressing punctures foam:** Stop and remake; tape over a hole is not an intact raised printing surface.
- **A line must be removed but is already deeply pressed:** It cannot be erased like graphite; redesign or remake.
- **Proof 2 is still faint though recess is clear:** Inspect colour load, pressure, and moisture, not more denting.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Large proofs, corner numbers, and trusted description can help map locations while you decide the change and stop.

### Assumptions and limits

Foam revision can mostly deepen or add recesses, not reliably delete them. Proof-led iteration is ordinary print practice. This does not teach professional edition certification, sharp carving, or copyright adjudication.
