## 中文

当你准备为一个普通房间或地下室区域买**便携式除湿机**，却把“水箱几升”当作机器能力时，加载这份 Skill。结果是候选的每日除湿额定量是否与面积、潮湿程度和你能做到的倒水方式相符。它不寻找或修复漏水根源，不处理整屋管道式系统，也不保证霉菌或健康问题消失。

### 准备与输入

量目标空间的大致地面面积，记录门窗和邻近空间是否通常关闭、平时温度，以及潮湿是偶尔、持续还是已有渗水。若有可靠湿度计，记读数与观察时间；没有就只写你实际看见和闻到的情况，不编精确湿度。找候选准确型号的**单位时间除水量**（常见为每天品脱或升）及测试条件、水箱容积、满箱停机/倒水方式和低温适用说明。

### 执行

1. **先划定服务区域。** 量机器实际要处理的房间或可连通区域，记下门窗能否按说明保持适当关闭。整屋面积不能直接代替一台便携机的服务范围。
2. **记录湿负荷。** 把普通潮湿、持续明显潮湿与水从墙地渗入分开。已知漏水、积水或不断发展的潮斑先处理来源；买更大机器不等于解决进水。
3. **读正确的容量。** 用适用于当前地区/测试方法的制造商或可信机构选型表，把空间大小与潮湿程度对应到每日除水能力。水箱容量只决定多久可能需要倒一次，不能代替每天能从空气中取出的水量；不同测试条件的额定值先查口径。
4. **核对实际运行限制。** 看计划放置点的气流空间、该型号允许的工作温度，以及满水停机后你能否安全倒水。若想连续排水，先确认有合适且经说明书允许的排水安排；本 Skill 不教接水管或靠近电路布线。
5. **写有限结论。** 记录“空间与潮湿线索 → 每日除水额定量及其条件 → 水箱/排水可行性 → 可考虑、偏小、条件不符或未确认”。缺面积、测试口径或水处理方案时暂停该候选。

### 成功与停止

你能解释为什么准确型号的每日除湿量适合这个空间，且温度、气流和倒水条件可满足，才完成购前核对。正在渗水、大片霉菌、结构损坏或电气受潮时停止普通选购，联系有能力处理房屋和安全问题的人；机器额定值不保证任何医学或建筑修复结果。

### 常见报错与补救

- **水箱大就判定除湿快：** 找“每天可去除的水量”及测试条件，另记倒水频率。
- **去年一台机器不够用，就只买更大：** 先查潮湿来源、门窗状态和原机实际运行/倒水情况；不能用新容量掩盖持续进水。
- **房间偏冷但候选没有低温资料：** 查准确型号允许温度及低温性能；缺资料就不承诺它会按标称除水。
- **倒水费力或说明书难读：** 先确认可安全提取水箱，或请可信的人协助查看可读说明；没有可行倒水/排水路径就换方案。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本流程只处理普通便携机的选购。不同地区用升/天或品脱/天，且旧新测试条件可能不同；先统一单位与口径再比较。现场湿度随天气和活动变化，单次感觉不能充当长期实测。你可能买到容量适中但更容易倒水的型号，也可能决定先处理漏水来源。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a **portable dehumidifier** for an ordinary room or basement area when a bucket size is being mistaken for machine capacity. Your result is whether the model's daily water-removal rating fits the space, dampness, and water-emptying route you can actually manage. It does not find or repair a leak, size a ducted whole-home system, or promise to resolve mould or a health condition.

### Preparation and inputs

Measure the approximate floor area and note whether doors and windows to nearby spaces normally stay closed, the usual temperature, and whether dampness is occasional, persistent, or accompanied by seepage. If you have a reliable humidity meter, record its reading and time; otherwise describe only what you can observe. Get the exact model's **water removed per unit time** (often pints or litres per day) and test conditions, bucket size, full-bucket shutoff/emptying method, and low-temperature guidance.

### Execution

1. **Define the served space.** Measure the room or connected area the portable unit would actually serve and whether its doors and windows can meet the product's operating guidance. Whole-house area is not a single portable unit's service area.
2. **Record the moisture load.** Distinguish ordinary dampness, consistently heavy dampness, and water entering from a wall or floor. Handle known leaks, standing water, or a growing wet patch at their source; a larger appliance does not repair incoming water.
3. **Read the right capacity.** Use maker or credible guidance that applies to the market and test method to match space size and dampness to a daily removal rating. Bucket volume predicts how often you may need to empty it, not how much water the unit can remove in a day. Check the basis before comparing different ratings.
4. **Check operating limits.** Confirm air space at the planned position, the exact model's permitted temperature range, and whether you can empty a full bucket safely. If continuous drainage is desired, first establish that a suitable, documented drain route exists; this Skill does not install a hose or route water near electrical circuits.
5. **Write a limited decision.** Record “space and dampness → daily removal rating and its conditions → feasible bucket/drain handling → consider, undersized, unsuitable conditions, or unconfirmed.” Pause a candidate if area, rating basis, or water-handling plan is missing.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can explain why the exact model's daily removal fits this space and its temperature, airflow, and emptying requirements can be met. Stop ordinary shopping for active seepage, extensive mould, structural damage, or wet electrical equipment; contact someone qualified to address the building and safety issue. A machine rating guarantees neither medical nor building remediation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A large bucket was read as fast removal:** Find water removed per day and its test conditions; record bucket-emptying separately.
- **Last year's unit struggled, so only capacity was increased:** First check the moisture source, doors/windows, and how the old unit actually ran and was emptied. New capacity cannot cover a persistent leak.
- **The space is cold but the candidate lacks low-temperature guidance:** Check the exact model's operating limits and cold-condition performance; otherwise do not promise rated removal.
- **Emptying is difficult or the guide unreadable:** Confirm that the bucket can be handled safely or ask a trusted person to help read accessible instructions. Choose another solution if no workable emptying or drain route exists.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary portable-unit selection. Regions use litres/day or pints/day, and old versus new ratings can use different tests; align units and conditions before comparing. Humidity changes with weather and activity, so one impression is not a long-term measurement. You may choose a moderate unit with a manageable bucket, or decide to address a water source first.
