## 中文

当一组普通物品已确定要随这次搬迁走，但还没装箱，而你要判断手边某个箱、袋或容器是否适合这组物品时加载。结果是**选定有依据的容器与必要分组，或停止等待合适容器/专业方案**。这里不决定哪些东西搬、不教实际提举与绑扎，也不包装危险品、承重家具或需专业拆装的设备。

### 准备与输入

拿到这组物品的数量、体积、易碎和普通使用状态；估计总负载时以可查的商品或实际资料为准，不靠“箱子看起来没装满”猜轻重。查看候选容器的完好程度、适用尺寸、制造商或提供方标明的承载条件，以及你实际使用的搬运服务是否接受它。路线与谁搬还需另核对，本 Skill 不代替那个决定。

### 执行

1. **先看容器本身。** 有明显破损、潮湿、软底、失效把手或关不上等问题的容器不作为这组的正常选择。记录可见的额定限制和使用说明；找不到必要限制就不自行发明承重数字。
2. **按物品条件匹配。** 核对物品是否放得下、能按提供方建议留出保护空间，且不会因为挤压、相互碰撞或漏出而失去可用性。易碎小件可考虑较小且有适当保护的独立单元；具体包法服从容器和承运者说明。
3. **把过重或过杂的组拆小。** 发现一组虽能塞进大箱，但负载或拿取条件无法确认时，改成较小、能说明内容的单元或请实际承运者判断。不要为了省箱子把一组压到超过容器的标示条件。
4. **查服务方例外。** 若有搬运公司或共享运输工具，核对它对容器、液体、易腐、特殊物品的现行要求。被拒收或条件未知的东西另列待确认，不藏进普通箱；危险内容交专业渠道。
5. **写一行选择。** 记“物品组 → 选用容器与适用依据 → 需分开的部分 → 尚待承运/搬运条件”。若合适容器不存在或仍不能关闭/保护，先不要装，换容器或请求专业帮助。

### 成功与停止

这一组有可核对的容器选择或拆分决定，容器状态和提供方条件没有已知冲突；搬运者仍需单独确认自己能否安全处理该负载。没有合适容器、限制不明或物品需要危险处理时停在未装状态，不用胶带把问题封起来。

### 常见报错与补救

- **大箱装得下但负载不明：** 拆成较小单元并重新核对容器条件；别把容量当成可安全搬动的证明。
- **易碎物品周围空隙太多：** 查准确容器/承运方的保护要求，换小容器或专业包装，不让物品在箱内自由旅行。
- **旧箱底部变软：** 换完好容器；额外绕几圈胶带不等于取得原额定能力。
- **承运方不接收该类内容：** 从普通容器方案退出，问实际服务方可行路线。
- **说明字太小或看不清箱底：** 请可信的人读回或用照片放大；状态仍不明就不选该箱。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 只选择低风险普通容器，不制定统一的每箱公斤上限、提供搬举姿势或替承运商承担责任。个人能力、台阶和通道必须另核对；品牌与地区的容器说明不同。握持、视力或行走不便时在桌面高度查说明，由合适的人协助看箱底，不靠试提来发现已超能力。副作用是大箱子不再自动赢得所有沉重物品的投票。

## English

Load this Skill when one ordinary group is already selected for a move, no box is filled yet, and you must decide whether an available carton, bag or container suits that group. Your result is **a supported container choice and any needed split, or a pause for a suitable container or professional route**. You do not decide move scope, teach lifting or securing, or pack hazardous goods, load-bearing furniture or equipment needing specialist disassembly.

### Preparation and inputs

Know the group's count, size, fragility and ordinary condition. Use available product or actual information to estimate the load; an unfilled-looking carton is not a weight measure. Check the candidate container's condition, dimensions, stated rating or instructions, and whether the actual transport provider accepts it. Route and handler capability still need separate checking.

### Execution

1. **Inspect the container itself.** A torn, damp, soft-bottomed or damaged container, one with a failed handle or one that will not close, is not this group's normal choice. Record any stated rating and use conditions. Do not invent a load limit when a necessary one is missing.
2. **Match the contents.** Check that items fit with protection space as the provider advises and will not become unusable through crushing, collision or leakage. Fragile small pieces may need smaller protected units. Follow the actual container and carrier instructions for packing details.
3. **Split an overly heavy or mixed group.** A group that fits in a large box may still have an unknown load or poor handling conditions. Make smaller named units or ask the actual provider. Do not exceed a container's stated conditions just to use fewer boxes.
4. **Check provider exceptions.** If a moving company or shared transport service is involved, read its current requirements for the container, liquids, perishables and special goods. Hold refused or unclear items separately instead of hiding them in a normal box; specialist channels handle hazardous content.
5. **Write one decision row.** Record “item group → container and source of fit → separated parts → carrier/handling unknowns.” When no suitable container exists or it cannot close or protect the contents, do not pack yet; choose another or request expert help.

### Success and stop

This group has a checkable container or split decision, with no known conflict in condition or provider instructions. A handler must still check whether they can manage the resulting load. Stop unpacked when no container fits, necessary limits are unclear or handling would be hazardous. Tape is not a solution to an unknown rating.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A large box fits but load is unknown:** Split into smaller units and check container conditions again. Volume does not prove safe handling.
- **Fragile pieces have space to move freely:** Check the actual provider's protection instructions, use a smaller container or request specialist packing.
- **An old box has a soft bottom:** Replace it. More tape does not restore a verified rating.
- **The carrier refuses this contents category:** Leave the normal-container route and ask the actual provider for a feasible one.
- **Instructions or box bottom are unreadable:** Ask a trusted person to read them back or enlarge a photo. Do not choose the box if its condition stays uncertain.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This chooses ordinary low-risk containers; it gives no universal kilograms-per-box limit, lifting technique or carrier guarantee. Personal ability, stairs and route need their own check. Container instructions differ by brand and region. If grip, sight or walking is difficult, inspect at table height and use suitable help to see the box bottom rather than trial-lifting to discover a problem. The side effect is that the largest box no longer wins every heavy item by default.
