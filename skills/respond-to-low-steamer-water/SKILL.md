## 中文

当蒸制途中**蒸汽明显消失、水位可能过低、听见异常干响或闻到焦味**时，加载这份 Skill。目标是先结束可能的干烧，再决定器具和食物是否仍有可靠条件继续。它不维修炉灶或电蒸锅，不用额外时间猜食物已经安全熟透；有烟、明火、燃气异味或电气危险时直接退出并求助。

### 准备与输入

确认是否能在不越过热锅的情况下安全关闭热源；记下蒸制多久、配方何时开始计时，以及蒸汽从何时变弱。电蒸锅的缺水提示、自动断电和重新加水方法因型号而异，必须读取确切设备说明；不能把某一个品牌的“会自动停”当作你手上机器的保证。让旁人远离可能烫人的锅、盖与蒸汽。

### 执行步骤

1. **先停止热输入。** 在安全可达时关灶或按设备说明停机；不要为了看水还有多少而把脸或手放到蒸汽出口。若已经有烟、火、燃气异味或电气异常，离开危险并按当地紧急指导求助，别继续诊断锅底。
2. **让器具回到可安全检查状态。** 热锅底和少量剩水可能仍很烫。按锅或设备说明等热与蒸汽减弱，用合适隔热工具再查看水位；不把冷水猛倒进异常发热的干锅，除非该确切设备说明明确要求可这样补水。盖子向远离身体方向打开。
3. **分清低水位与已干烧。** 若仍有水、器具稳固无焦损，查清配方所需水量与补水方式；按说明恢复到食物上方有蒸汽、食物不浸水的状态。若锅底明显烧焦、容器变形、把手松动或设备行为异常，停止使用并交给合适支持渠道，不在本 Skill 内修复。
4. **单独判断食物。** 蒸汽中断多久或食物中心是否符合安全要求不清楚时，不拿“补蒸几分钟”或外层热气做保证。按具体食物的可靠配方和[熟度要求](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)判断能否继续；没有依据就暂停食用并按适用食品安全指导处理。
5. **只有两边都成立才恢复。** 器具与水位可安全恢复、食物时间/温度史有可靠解释时，按该配方重新建立蒸汽和计时节点；记下本次停止原因，下一次用合适水量、批量和可观察提示。缺其中之一则结束这一锅。

### 完成与停止

热源已停，锅具状态与食物状态分别得出“可按说明恢复”或“停止并处理”的可解释结论，就是完成。设备故障、干烧损伤、食物安全史不清或无法安全查看时不重新开火。这里的停止可能损失一份食物，但不要求你通过试吃来确认风险。

### 常见报错与补救

- **电蒸锅没提示，就认为一定有水：** 核对确切型号的低水位/自动停机说明；未知功能不算保护。
- **蒸汽没了立刻倒冷水：** 先停机并按说明等待、检查；热干锅或发热底座不是试水池。
- **锅底已焦仍继续蒸：** 停止，待安全后按厂家支持处理，不把焦味当调味。
- **时间忘了，便多蒸五分钟：** 食物特定安全与完成条件未建立时暂停；随意加时不能补写缺失的时间线。
- **水加多后接触食物：** 按器具说明修正或停止这次蒸制，不把水煮误报为持续蒸制。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本流程只负责普通蒸制的停热与是否可恢复，不替用户维修、处理火灾或给出通用冷却分钟数。锅、炉、电蒸锅和食物不同，实际设备说明与当地食品安全规则优先。看不清水位时可用器具标记、大字说明或现场可信协助；身体无法接近热源时先安全退出并求助。副作用是你可能少完成一份蒸菜，但多保留一口完整的锅。

## English

Load this Skill if **steam visibly fades, water may be low, the base makes an unusual dry sound, or a scorched smell appears** during steaming. End possible dry heating first, then decide separately whether the equipment and food still have a reliable route to completion. This does not repair a hob or electric steamer or guess food safe from extra minutes. Smoke, flame, gas odour, or electrical danger leaves this flow immediately for local emergency help.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether you can stop heat without reaching over the hot vessel. Note elapsed time, the recipe's timing start, and when steam weakened. Electric steamers differ in alerts, automatic shutdown, and refill instructions: read the exact model's manual. One brand's automatic stop is not a promise about your equipment. Keep others clear of hot pot, lid, and steam.

### Execution steps

1. **Stop heat input first.** Turn off the hob or stop the device under its directions when safely reachable. Do not put your face or hand over a steam outlet to inspect water. If there is smoke, fire, gas odour, or electrical abnormality, leave danger and follow local emergency guidance rather than diagnosing a pot base.
2. **Wait for an inspectable state.** A hot base and small water remainder can still scald. Follow pot or device directions, let heat and steam subside, and use suitable heat protection to check water. Do not pour cold water quickly into an abnormally hot dry pot unless the exact equipment directions explicitly permit that refill method. Lift a lid away from your body.
3. **Distinguish low water from running dry.** If water remains and the equipment is stable and undamaged, check the recipe's required amount and approved refill route. Restore steam with food still above water under those directions. If the base is scorched, a container warped, a handle loosened, or equipment behaves abnormally, stop using it and seek suitable support instead of repairing it here.
4. **Judge food separately.** If the steam interruption duration or food centre is unknown, “a few more minutes” and outside vapour cannot certify it. Use reliable directions for the exact food and applicable [doneness rules](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md). Without enough evidence, hold off eating and follow the applicable food-safety guidance.
5. **Resume only when both sides work.** If equipment and water can safely be restored and the food's time and temperature history can be explained, re-establish steam and the timing event under its recipe. Record what stopped this batch and use an appropriate water amount, load, and observable cue next time. Otherwise end this attempt.

### Success and stop

The heat is off and equipment and food each have an explainable “resume under directions” or “stop and handle separately” result. Do not restart with equipment faults, dry-heat damage, unknown food safety history, or no safe way to inspect. Stopping may cost a portion of food; tasting it is not the risk test.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The electric steamer did not warn, so you assume it has water:** Verify the exact model's low-water and auto-stop instructions. An unknown feature is not protection.
- **Steam fades and you immediately pour cold water in:** Stop and inspect under directions first. A hot dry base is not a test basin.
- **The base is scorched but steaming continues:** Stop and follow the maker's support route when safe. A burnt smell is not seasoning.
- **Timing was lost, so you add five minutes:** Hold food until its specific completion and safety conditions can be established. An arbitrary extension cannot recreate missing history.
- **Refilling immerses the food:** Correct the level under equipment directions or stop; do not report water boiling as continuous steaming.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This only handles heat shutdown and possible recovery in ordinary steaming. It is not appliance repair, firefighting, or a universal cool-down timer. Pots, hobs, electric steamers, and foods vary; exact manuals and local food-safety rules take priority. Use equipment marks, readable instructions, or trusted help when water is hard to see. If your body cannot safely reach the heat control, leave danger and get help. You may lose a steaming batch and keep an intact pot.
