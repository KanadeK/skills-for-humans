## 中文

当一段旅程真实延误、提前、改道或改期，使你**下一项普通参观、预约或住宿前活动**的原时间不再可靠时加载。目标是根据真实到达、从到达点到场所的路程、该场所**本次**开放/最后入场/预约规则，决定保留、缩短、改期或跳过。公交/铁路下一班的改选、航班变动和一句礼貌迟到通知已有其他流程；这里不解释退款、租赁或保险权利，也不安排整队集体活动。

### 准备与输入

记当前实际位置、最近一次由交通/服务方给出的到达时间及其是否只是预计；找下一项活动的正确场所、日期、预订/入场方式、从新到达点到它的可行路线与自己实际所需的步行/休息时间。查场所官网、票面或正式联系渠道的当天开放、最后入场、预约时段、可否迟到或改期。**“有定时票”不等于有统一宽限；“关门时间”也不一定是最后入场时间。**

### 执行

1. **把旧衔接标失效。** 更新实际或当前预计到达、转乘/步行需求，不再使用原截图中已经不可能的时间。预计仍可能再变，写明查看时刻与来源；不把地图上的直线距离当真实到门时间。
2. **只核下一项活动的关键条件。** 对照本次票/预约、今天的开放与最后入场、活动是否准时开始、场所是否要求重新登记，以及你到门前还需的排队/步行时间。正式页面和实际预订说法不同就联系场所确认；别把另一个博物馆、另一日的规则拿来填空。
3. **选一个现实结果。** 到达加上路程/排队仍可用，便保留并标下次复核点；只剩很短时间则确认是否值得缩短；预约窗口已失效或无法安全抵达，查场所是否允许改到其他时段，或明确跳过。费用、票证与是否可改期只按本次场所规则问清，不猜一定免费或可退。
4. **把必要协调发给真正受影响的人。** 若场所需要确认，问“我现在预计__到，原预约__；按这次规则可否在__入场/更改到__？需我做什么？” 向接应/同行人只发已确认或仍预计的到达与新的会合安排，避免散发预订码或私人行程。对方未回复就记未确认，不把自己的备选当双方新约定。
5. **写一个当前版本并复查。** 记“下一项__；决定保留/缩短/改期/跳过__；场所实际答复__；何时从哪里出发__；若到时仍不行则__”。临出发再查交通与场所当天状态；变了只重算受影响的下一段，别同时传播旧新两个‘最终计划’。

### 完成与停止

下一项活动有**可核实且你能赶到的现行安排**，或已清楚决定改期/跳过、必要对象得到事实更新，仍待确认的事有负责人和复查点，本轮完成。**重排不是实际到场、获准入内或取得退款。** 规则未知、距离/可及路径不明、场所已关或费用权利有争议时停下向正式场所/服务方求证，不为守旧计划走危险捷径。

### 常见报错与补救

- **按原行程截图计算还能赶上：** 从新实际位置和当前到达时间重算步行、排队和最后入场。
- **把闭馆时刻当最后可入场时刻：** 查该场所本日最后入场/预约细则，两者可能不同。
- **以为所有定时票都能迟到：** 看本次票与场所规则；有的允许、有的限制，不能跨场馆移植。
- **交通预计又变：** 标成新的预计，通知受影响的人并只更新实际受影响的下一段。
- **场所未回改期请求：** 保留原确认与已发问时间，别在未得到答复时宣称新时段获准。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通、可退出的单人或少数同行旅程衔接，不提供场馆专门参观、多人活动主持、租赁/签证/保险/赔偿建议。场所规则、开放日、可及路线与费用因预订不同。行动、视觉、听觉或语言不便时，可用场所正式文字/电话、可及地图或可信同行协助，给真实到门时间留余量。副作用是可能放弃一项活动；已经变动的时间表不会因为你不改日历而恢复。

## English

Load this Skill when a real journey delay, earlier arrival, diversion or reschedule makes the old time for your **next ordinary visit, booking or pre-lodging activity** unreliable. Decide whether to keep, shorten, move or skip it using actual arrival, travel from there to the venue and **this venue's current** opening, last-entry and reservation rules. Changing onward buses or trains, a changed flight and a simple “I will be late” note have separate tasks. This does not decide refunds, tenancy or insurance rights or run a whole group event.

### Preparation and inputs

Record your actual location and latest arrival given by the transport or service provider, marking any estimate as an estimate. Find the next venue, date, booking or entry method, a feasible route from the new arrival point, and your real walking or rest time. Check the venue's official page, pass or contact for today's opening, last entry, booked slot and whether late arrival or change is allowed. **A timed pass has no universal grace period, and closing time need not equal last entry.**

### Execution

1. **Mark the old connection invalid.** Update actual or current expected arrival and onward walk or transfer, and stop using a screenshot whose timing is now impossible. Estimates can move again; note their source and check time. Map distance in a straight line is not time to the door.
2. **Check the next activity's critical terms only.** Compare this pass or booking with today's hours, last entry, scheduled start, any new registration and your time to walk and queue. If an official page and booking disagree, ask the venue. Another museum's policy or an old event page does not fill a gap here.
3. **Choose one realistic result.** Keep it with a recheck point if arrival plus travel is feasible. If little time remains, decide whether a shortened visit is worthwhile and allowed. For a lost booking window or unsafe route, ask whether the venue permits another slot or mark the activity skipped. Ask about fees, ticket use and changes under this booking's rules rather than assuming they are free or refundable.
4. **Update people who actually need to act.** Ask the venue when confirmation matters: “I now expect to arrive __ for my original __ booking; under this booking can I enter at __ or move to __, and what must I do?” Give a pickup person or companion only confirmed or clearly estimated arrival and a changed meeting plan. Do not spread booking codes or private itinerary details. No reply means unconfirmed, not a jointly agreed new plan.
5. **Keep one current version and recheck.** Note “next activity __; keep/shorten/move/skip __; venue reply __; leave from __ at __; if it fails __.” Check current transport and venue status before moving. Recalculate the affected next leg rather than distributing old and new “final” plans together.

### Success and stop

The next activity has a **verifiable current arrangement you can reach**, or you have explicitly moved or skipped it and updated people affected, with owners and recheck points for unknowns. **Replanning is not attending, admission or a refund.** For unknown rules, route or access, closed venue or disputed money or rights, ask the actual provider officially instead of taking an unsafe shortcut to preserve the old plan.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An original screenshot still looks achievable:** Recalculate from actual location and new arrival through walk, queue and last entry.
- **Closing is treated as last entry:** Check today's venue-specific entry cutoff or booking rule; they may differ.
- **Every timed pass is assumed to allow late arrival:** Read this pass and venue; policies differ even between museums.
- **The travel estimate changes again:** Mark a new estimate, update affected people and revise only the next steps it changes.
- **The venue has not answered a change request:** Keep original confirmation and the time you asked. Do not claim a new slot is approved before a reply.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is an ordinary, exit-able solo or small-companion travel connection, not specialist venue touring, group-event leadership, tenancy, visa, insurance or compensation advice. Venue policy, date, accessible path and costs vary by booking. For mobility, sight, hearing or language needs, use official text or phone, an accessible map or a trusted companion, allowing your real time to the door. You may drop an activity; an altered itinerary does not heal because the calendar stays unchanged.
