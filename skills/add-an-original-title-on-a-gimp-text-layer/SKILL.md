## 中文

你想把自己写的一句短标题放在自有图片上做私人卡片或练习图时使用本篇。成果是标题在独立文字层、位置和对比可读、原图像层未被涂改。这里加入原创文字，不复制歌词、广告标语或他人的签名，也不把标签当作照片原本包含的内容。

### 准备与输入

保存 XCF 主本，写好少量字，找图像里不会遮住主体与必要线索的留白。确定文字与背景亮暗有足够对比，先不做强阴影或描边补救。若图像是证据或需要原貌呈现，不加字。

### 执行

1. 选择 Text 工具，在留白区建文字框，输入原创短标题。
2. 在 Layers 面板确认新文字层独立于照片层，命名并保持可编辑。
3. 在目标输出尺寸看字是否可读、是否遮主体；若不合适移动文字层而非改原图。
4. 保存 XCF 并导出预览重开，核文字没有截断且原图层仍可隐藏文字后单独查看。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

短标题清楚、可单独编辑或隐藏，原图层完整，输出预览不截字。

- **字被裁：** 扩大文字框或调整位置。
- **盖住主体：** 移到留白而非硬缩。
- **文字层被栅格化：** 从 XCF 恢复可编辑层。

### 假设与边界

只添加原创短标题，不保证商用字体授权或公开传播许可。你负责内容和输出场景。

## English

Use this Skill for a self-written short title on an owned image as a private card or practice graphic. Finish with an editable text layer, readable placement/contrast and untouched image layer. Add original wording, not copied lyrics, slogans or signatures, and do not imply the title was part of the captured scene.

### Preparation and inputs

Save XCF master, prepare a few words and locate whitespace away from subject and key context. Aim for readable text/background contrast before adding heavy shadows or strokes. Do not add text to evidence or faithful-original images.

### Execution

1. Choose Text tool, create a box in whitespace and enter original short title.
2. Confirm a separate text layer in Layers panel, name it and keep it editable.
3. Inspect at target output size for legibility and subject obstruction; move text layer rather than edit source pixels.
4. Save XCF and reopen preview export for clipping; verify image layer remains viewable with text hidden.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Short title reads clearly, remains separately editable/hideable, source layer is intact and export preview has no clipping.

- **Text clipped:** Expand box or move it.
- **Subject covered:** Move into whitespace instead of shrinking blindly.
- **Text rasterized:** Restore editable layer from XCF.

### Assumptions and limits

This adds original short text, not a guarantee of commercial font rights or public sharing. You own wording and use context.
