## 中文

当你已有电影或现场演出的票，想从“票在手机里”走到**正确场次的合适座位**，再按场馆规则结束离开时，用这份 Skill 核对日期、入口、厅/场、座位与离场方式。结果是合法入场、在允许位置观看并清楚怎样离开；它不包括买票、票务争议或迟到后强行入场。

### 准备与输入

从官方票面或场馆/售票方消息核对日期、开始时间、场馆地址、厅号/剧场、票数、座位或自由座规则、入口/核票方式。各馆票务、年龄、随身物、食品、拍摄、字幕/辅助听力与无障碍安排不同，出发前查目标场馆当日说明，不能用另一家影院的常识代替。保留能出示的票或馆方允许的纸质替代，设备低电量时提前使用正式补票/柜台渠道；不把票码公开给别人。计划开场前留找入口、安检和座位的时间，回程或离场路径也要可行。

### 执行

1. **到对场馆和入口。** 对照票面、现场标识及工作人员指引，别只看电影/演出名称；同名不同日期、场次或影厅不是同一张票。若需身份证明或特殊票种核验，以馆方实际要求为准；不符合就先找票务人员处理。
2. **完成正式核票。** 让工作人员或官方设备确认票有效并指向正确厅/门；扫码失败或屏幕损坏时到柜台，不钻过闸口。记录现场提供的座位区域或入场指示，不自行拿别人票上的编号找座。
3. **按座位规则就座。** 指定座位逐项核排/号/区，免费座按现场开放规则选；保留通道和无障碍位置的实际安排。座位被占或不适用时请工作人员核对，不和其他观众抢座或自行交换需许可的位置。
4. **按本场规则观看。** 开场前调好设备和个人需要，遵守馆方对手机灯光、交谈、食品、照片/录音及中途出入的规则。影院与现场演出的迟到/再入场不同；要离席、需要安静或辅助服务时先找工作人员的可行路线，不影响他人观看。
5. **按开放路线离场。** 节目结束或你需要提前离开时听现场指示，用允许出口慢慢走，核随身物和同行人；不逆行穿过舞台、后台或封闭通道。若有身体不适或安全指示，跟随场馆人员的实时安排，本篇不处理紧急救援。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

票已被正式接受、你位于该场次允许的座位或区域、按当场规则观看并知道正式离场路径。只打开手机票面而进错厅、坐错位，不满足本篇。

- **同名影片/演出有多个场次：** 重新核日期、时间、厅/场与票码，找工作人员指路，不猜最近的门。
- **有人坐了你的指定位置：** 先核自己票，再请前台/引座员处理，不争执或占用另一处指定座。
- **票码或手机无法显示：** 用官方柜台或场馆允许的替代方式核验，未经确认不跟别人一同入场。
- **标志、声音或路线不适合你的可及需要：** 提前向场馆询问合法安排；若当天无法安全就座，可退出并按馆方实际票务渠道处理，不许诺退款。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通观众流程，不涉及后台、设备操作、票务法律争议或紧急疏散教学。影院和现场演出共享票面—核票—座位—离场的检查链，但手机、拍摄、中途出入与辅助服务以本场馆为准。语言、感官或行动条件不同可用馆方前台、引座或正式辅助服务；“首次/独自”不改变票和座位的规则。节目体验由你判断，不是本篇的自动验收。

## English

Use this Skill when you already have a cinema or live-performance ticket and want to get from “the ticket is on my phone” to **the correct show and permitted seat**, then leave under venue rules. Check date, entrance, auditorium, seat, and exit route. The result is approved entry and viewing from an allowed place, not ticket purchase, a ticketing dispute, or forcing your way in after a late arrival.

### Preparation and inputs

From the official ticket or venue/seller notice, check date, start time, address, screen or theatre, ticket count, assigned or open-seating rule, and admission method. Age, bag, food, photography, captions, assistive listening, and access rules vary. Read today's rules for this venue, not another cinema's habits. Keep a ticket you can show or an officially accepted paper alternative. With low battery, arrange a formal box-office or replacement route before arrival; do not expose your code publicly. Allow time before start for entrance, any security, and seating, and know a feasible return or exit route.

### Execution

1. **Reach the right venue and entrance.** Compare ticket, signs, and staff directions, not only the film or show's name. Another date, showing, or auditorium is not your ticket. If identification or a special ticket type must be checked, follow the actual rule and ask ticket staff if there is a mismatch.
2. **Complete official admission.** Have staff or the approved machine verify the ticket and direct you to the right door. For a failed scan or broken screen, use the box office rather than slip past a barrier. Note the area or seat instruction actually given; do not use someone else's ticket number.
3. **Take an allowed seat.** For assigned seats compare section, row, and number; for open seating follow the area rules. Keep aisles clear and respect access-seat arrangements. If your seat is occupied or unusable, ask an usher to check rather than argue or take another reserved seat.
4. **Follow this show's house rules.** Set devices and personal support before the show. Follow local rules for phone light, conversation, food, photography or recording, and leaving and returning. Cinema and live-performance late seating differ. Ask staff for a suitable route if you must leave, need quiet, or need an assistive service.
5. **Leave by an open route.** At the end or if you leave early, follow venue directions, move without blocking others, and check your belongings and companions. Do not go through a stage, backstage, or restricted corridor. For illness or safety instructions, follow current venue staff direction; this Skill does not teach emergency response.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

The ticket was officially accepted, you viewed from the permitted seat or area for that showing, and you know the formal way out. Merely opening a mobile ticket while entering the wrong auditorium or seat is not success.

- **Several shows share a name:** Recheck date, time, auditorium, and code and ask staff instead of choosing the nearest door.
- **Someone occupies your assigned seat:** Verify your ticket first, then ask the front-of-house team or usher. Do not fight or take another reserved place.
- **Code or phone cannot be displayed:** Use an official box-office or venue-approved alternative; do not follow another patron through without validation.
- **Signs, sound, or route do not meet your access needs:** Ask about formal arrangements. If you cannot safely use the seat today, leave and follow the real ticketing route without assuming a refund.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary audience flow, not backstage access, equipment operation, ticketing law, or emergency evacuation instruction. Cinema and live shows share ticket, admission, seat, and exit checks, while phones, recording, re-entry, and assistive services follow the actual venue. Language, sensory, or mobility needs may use ushers and formal support. First-time or solo status does not change ticket and seat rules. You judge the experience; it is not an automatic acceptance test.
