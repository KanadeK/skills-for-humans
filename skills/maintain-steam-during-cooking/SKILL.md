## 中文

当普通非压力蒸锅已经[核对器具](../check-an-open-pot-steamer-setup/SKILL.md)、[摆好食物](../arrange-food-for-even-steaming/SKILL.md)，并准备按可靠配方开始加热时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**按配方规定的事件计时，持续提供蒸汽，同时水不淹食物也不把锅烧干**；完成仍以该食物的可靠结束条件为准。电蒸锅如有专用计时、水位或自动保温机制，应使用确切型号说明，不借普通锅的步骤猜按钮。

### 准备与输入

再确认锅底水量、食物批量、盖子放法和食物的时间/熟度要求；计时器应可看、可听或可振动。识别你是否能从器具允许的位置安全观察水位、蒸汽和热源；如果必须掀盖才能检查，先读器具说明的安全方法。需要核心温度的食物，准备可靠温度计与当地食物特定规则；外面的蒸汽不能替中心完成认证。

### 执行步骤

1. **按器具和配方建立蒸汽。** 加热锅底水，确认蒸汽按配方到达食物，再放篮和盖或按说明先装好再加热。不要把篮底浸进水里，也不要把普通盖子压成密封压力装置。
2. **在正确事件启动计时。** 配方可能从水沸、篮放入或盖盖开始算；只按它实际写的起点。冷篮与食物可能暂时降低蒸汽，观察恢复情况，不把准备阶段的空锅时间算进食物受热时间。
3. **维持而不追求最大火力。** 按器具要求让水持续产生蒸汽，同时留意锅边是否过度翻涌、盖是否稳定、是否有正常蒸汽出口。火力过猛导致水冲上篮或锅沿时，在安全可达时降热；蒸汽明显消失时先查水量与热源，别凭空延长计时。
4. **处理水位变化。** 按配方和器具可安全检查的方式确认还有足够水完成这一段。水位低到可能干烧、闻到焦味、出现烟或设备异常时关热并[按低水位停点处理](../respond-to-low-steamer-water/SKILL.md)。未经器具说明，不向正在异常加热的锅底猛倒冷水。
5. **按食物证据结束。** 到达指定时间后先关热，再用安全的开盖方式取出代表性食物按配方检查。若涉及动物性食品或其他需安全温度的食物，用适用的[熟度核对](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)，不以“蒸汽很大”替代。未达条件时，只有水量、器具和可靠说明都仍成立才继续。

### 完成与停止

这段蒸制在配方规定起点计时、蒸汽持续且锅水受控，并由食物特定结束证据确认完成。水干、器具不稳、未知的计时起点、食物熟度无法核验或热源不能看守时停止；有烟、火、燃气异味或电气异常按当地紧急指导退出日常流程。

### 常见报错与补救

- **盖上后就忘了水位：** 先关热，按器具安全方式查看；水量未知时不继续以“可能还够”计时。
- **蒸汽断断续续：** 查热源和水位，再按配方确认计时是否需要重置；不要只把分钟数往后加。
- **盖子被水汽顶得摇晃：** 在安全可达时降热并停下，核对盖、篮和水位；不按住盖子模拟压力锅。
- **时间到了但厚块中心未达要求：** 先按食物特定规则复查，再决定能否安全延长；外部水汽不算内部读数。
- **水触到食物：** 结束该次“蒸”的比较口径，停热后按器具说明修正水位或选择真正的水煮流程，不暗中换技法。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只写普通敞口锅的蒸汽控制，不给任何食物统一分钟数，也不处理压力锅、罐藏或设备维修。锅、篮、盖、炉头和海拔会改变蒸汽表现；可靠配方与器具说明优先。若看不清蒸汽或水位，使用器具已有可读标记、可访问计时器、现场可信的人描述或先停热安全查看；不要将手伸进蒸汽验证。现实副作用是你需多次确认水仍在锅里，而锅底也会因此保持锅底的身份。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary non-pressure steamer has [passed equipment checks](../check-an-open-pot-steamer-setup/SKILL.md), food has been [arranged for steam access](../arrange-food-for-even-steaming/SKILL.md), and a reliable recipe is ready to begin. Time from the **event the recipe specifies**, keep steam available without submerging food or running the pot dry, and stop on food-specific evidence. An electric steamer's timer, water marks, or warming mode must follow the exact model's instructions, not guessed open-pot buttons.

### Preparation and inputs

Reconfirm water, batch size, lid position, and the food's timing and doneness requirement. Have a timer you can see, hear, or feel. Identify a safe way, permitted by the equipment, to observe steam, water, and heat. If checking requires lifting a lid, read how to do so safely first. For food requiring a safe centre temperature, have a reliable thermometer and applicable local food-specific rule. Steam outside is not a certificate for the centre.

### Execution steps

1. **Establish steam under the equipment and recipe directions.** Heat the water and confirm steam reaches the food, then add basket and lid, or assemble before heating if directed. Do not submerge the basket base or press an ordinary lid into a pressure seal.
2. **Start timing at the right event.** A recipe may start when water boils, the basket goes in, or the lid closes. Use its actual event. A cold basket and food can interrupt steam briefly; observe its return instead of crediting empty-pot heating time to the food.
3. **Maintain steam rather than maximum power.** Keep water producing steam while watching for an overactive boil at the rim, lid stability, and the normal steam outlet. Lower heat safely if water surges toward the basket or rim. If steam disappears, check water and heat instead of simply adding guessed minutes.
4. **Manage falling water.** Use the recipe and equipment's safe check method to confirm enough water remains. If water may be running dry, a scorched smell appears, smoke rises, or equipment behaves abnormally, turn off heat and use [Respond to Low Steamer Water](../respond-to-low-steamer-water/SKILL.md). Do not pour cold water quickly onto an abnormally heated base without its instructions.
5. **Finish on food evidence.** At the stated time, turn off heat and use a safe opening method to remove a representative piece for the recipe's check. For animal foods or another item with a required safe temperature, use the applicable [doneness process](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md), not the volume of visible steam. Continue only if water, equipment, and reliable directions still support it.

### Success and stop

This steaming period used the recipe's real timing start, steady steam, controlled water, and food-specific completion evidence. Stop if the water dries, equipment becomes unstable, timing is unknown, doneness cannot be checked, or you cannot attend the heat. Smoke, flame, gas odour, or electrical failure leaves the everyday flow under local emergency guidance.

### Common errors and recovery

- **You forgot the water under a closed lid:** Turn off heat and inspect by the equipment's safe method. Do not keep timing on “probably enough.”
- **Steam starts and stops:** Check heat and water, then whether the recipe requires timing to restart. Do not merely add minutes by guesswork.
- **The lid rocks under active steam:** Lower heat safely and stop to check lid, basket, and water. Do not hold it down like a pressure cooker.
- **Time ends but a thick centre is not done:** Recheck against food-specific rules before deciding on a safe extension. Outside vapour is not an internal reading.
- **Water touches the food:** This is no longer the same steaming setup. Stop heat and correct the level under the equipment instructions, or deliberately use a water-boiling method rather than silently changing technique.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary open-pot steam control. It supplies no universal minutes and does not cover pressure cooking, canning, or appliance repair. Pot, basket, lid, hob, and altitude alter steam; the reliable recipe and exact equipment manual take priority. If steam or water is hard to see, use accessible marks, an accessible timer, a trusted person's description, or turn off heat for a safe check. Do not put your hand into steam to test it. You may check water more often, allowing the pot bottom to remain a pot bottom.
