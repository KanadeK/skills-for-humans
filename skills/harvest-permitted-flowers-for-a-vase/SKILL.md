## 中文

当你想从**自有或明确获准**的已知普通观赏植株取几枝花，供自己瓶插时，加载这份 Skill。结果是花枝已入水，来源和植株状态都可核；它不同于剪掉已谢残花来养护植株。野外、公园、邻居花圃和公共绿地未经许可不采。身份不明、可能有毒或刺激性汁液的植物先跳过；本篇不教可食用或药用采收。

### 准备与输入

确认你能采、准备少量所需枝数，先读该花种的可靠采收/修剪说明；不同花开放程度与可剪位置不同。拿干净、适合茎粗且你会安全握持的剪具、可戴的手套和已有清水的桶。若剪具不稳、够不着、枝上有刺或汁液风险不明，请可信的人协助或换花，不为一束花爬高或徒手硬折。

### 执行

1. **挑能用的花枝。** 选健康、未严重萎蔫、开放阶段符合该花种说明的少数枝；看清是否有未开放的邻近芽和植株可留的花量。天气很热、植株明显缺水时改日或等凉爽时，不把枯萎花当成采收。
2. **先决定切点和去向。** 按该花种说明找不伤主干、芽或邻枝的切点；保留足够瓶插茎长，却不为求长把整株剪秃。水桶放在近处，让剪下的茎马上入水。
3. **逐枝干净剪取。** 在稳固姿势下用干净合用剪具剪一枝，放进桶，再看植株与需求是否仍允许下一枝。不要拉扯撕开茎皮；发现伤口异常、虫害密集或无法辨认可剪点时停。
4. **核对并收尾。** 清点枝数与来源，检查植株没有被过量剪去、剪具已安全收好。将花枝移到凉爽处待后续整理；临时入水是这一步的结束，不能声称已经完成瓶插作品。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

少量合适的已知花枝在清水中，采收有许可，原植株没有明显过剪或拉伤。只剪掉枯败花头、没有取得可瓶插花枝，不是本 Skill 的完成。

- **花种或许可不清：** 不采；先向权利人或可靠花种资料确认。
- **切下后找不到水：** 先停剪，取得可用水桶后再继续；不要让新剪枝堆在日晒桌上。
- **茎太粗、够不到或握持不稳：** 换可安全到达的枝，或请有经验的人操作；不升级到危险工具。
- **采一枝后发现植株受损：** 停止继续采，记录情况；不靠多剪把损伤“修平”。
- **手眼或体力受限：** 可让可信协助者持桶或剪枝，由你确认许可、选择和结束状态。

### 假设与边界

这是一小束私人观赏花，不保证花期、不识别野生植物，也不替代某物种的修剪指南。摘花本身会减少植株上的花；在生长缓慢或珍贵植株上，少采或不采同样是正确结果。

## English

Load this Skill when you want a few **known, ordinary ornamental stems** from your own or an explicitly permitted plant for a vase. Finish with cut stems in water and an intact source plant. This is different from deadheading faded blooms to care for a plant. Do not collect from parks, wild areas, neighbours' beds, or public land without permission. Skip unidentified plants or ones with uncertain toxicity or irritating sap. This is not edible or medicinal harvesting.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm permission and decide a small number of stems. Check reliable guidance for that flower species because opening stage and cut position vary. Set out clean shears suited to the stem and your grip, gloves if suitable, and a bucket already holding clean water. If the tool is unstable, the flower is out of reach, thorns are present, or sap risk is unclear, seek trusted help or choose another flower; do not climb or snap stems by force for a bouquet.

### Execution

1. **Choose usable stems.** Look for healthy stems that are not badly wilted and are at the stage recommended for that species. Notice nearby buds and how many blooms the plant can keep. Defer during strong heat or obvious water stress; faded heads are not a harvest.
2. **Choose a cut point and destination.** Follow species guidance for a cut that avoids damaging the main stem, buds, or neighbouring growth. Leave enough length for a vase without stripping the plant for length. Put the water bucket close so each stem can enter water promptly.
3. **Cut one at a time.** From a stable position, use clean suitable shears and put each stem in the bucket. Recheck the plant and your actual need before cutting another. Do not pull and tear bark. Stop if a cut wound looks abnormal, pests are dense, or you cannot locate a suitable cut point.
4. **Check and finish.** Count stems, name their source, inspect the plant for overcutting or tearing, and put tools away safely. Move the bucket to a cool place for later preparation. Stems in temporary water end this task; they are not yet a finished display.

### Success, problems, and recovery

A few suitable known stems stand in clean water with permission recorded mentally or on a note, and the source plant is not visibly overcut or torn. Removing only spent heads without a usable stem does not complete this harvest.

- **Species or permission is unclear:** Do not cut; ask the owner or check reliable species guidance.
- **No water after a cut:** Pause further cutting and get a water bucket before continuing instead of piling stems in sun.
- **Stem is too thick, out of reach, or hard to control:** Choose an accessible stem or experienced help, without escalating to dangerous tools.
- **The plant looks damaged after one cut:** Stop, note what happened, and do not cut more to disguise the mark.
- **Grip, sight, or stamina is limited:** Trusted help may hold the bucket or make the cut while you confirm permission, choice, and completion.

### Assumptions and limits

This is a small private ornamental harvest. It does not promise vase life, identify wild plants, or replace species pruning advice. Picking removes blooms from the plant; taking fewer or none can be the right result for slow-growing or valued plants.
