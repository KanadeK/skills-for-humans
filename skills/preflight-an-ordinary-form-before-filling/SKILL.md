## 中文

当你准备填写一份普通、低风险表格，但不确定每个字段要什么、材料是否在手时加载。你要得到**字段与发放机构说明的对照表，以及已知、缺少、待问三种准备状态**，在此阶段不填写敏感答案。这里不处理法律、税务、医疗、财务资格，不代填账号或身份认证信息，也不提交表格。

### 准备与输入

确认表格来自实际机构、用途、版本/日期及对应说明；纸本或网页截图不完整时先找全。准备本人有权查看的相关材料，保持原件私密。只在本地记录字段名和资料来源，不把证件号、账户号或详细私事抄到共享清单。特殊资格或法定声明字段直接转实际机构/专业人士解释。

### 执行

1. **先读全表的目的和结构。** 看标题、发放者、适用人、填写说明、签名/提交部分和附件提示，核对是不是你要办的那一版。相似旧表不能因为题目差不多就直接复用。
2. **逐项记录真实字段要求。** 只记字段原文、帮助文字、明确的必填/选填标记、条件分支及要求的格式。没有标明的项目不能擅自认作选填，也不能把某网站使用星号的习惯写成所有表格规则。
3. **把字段连到事实来源。** 每行写“从哪份你有权用的资料核对/由谁本人确认 → 资料已在手或缺少”。需要别人同意或他们的资料时先询问本人，不因你在准备表格就拥有他人的信息。
4. **把未知单列。** 字词含糊、资料互相冲突、表格缺页、格式/范围不清时标“待机构确认”，不在草稿里随手填 `N/A`、零或猜测日期。只有表格自己允许“未知/不适用”时才按其指示考虑。
5. **给下一步一个可查清单。** 写“可按来源回答的字段 / 需取得材料 / 需问机构的具体字段”，并标这份表的版本与问询入口。填表由你在真实说明和权限下另行完成；预检通过只表示准备好，不表示内容已经正确或被接收。

### 完成与停止

准确版本、字段、格式、条件与材料来源能逐项核对，未知处没有被猜填；敏感信息仍在受控位置。表格不完整、发放者不明、涉及法律/税务/医疗/财务判断或必须填写你无权提供的他人资料时暂停对应内容，向实际机构或合适专业渠道询问。

### 常见报错与补救

- **用了去年的同名表：** 查实际机构当期版本，按新字段重新预检。
- **看不到后半页说明：** 找完整表单或请机构提供可读版本，缺页字段保留未知。
- **某格没有星号就当选填：** 查表单自己的标记和说明；没有结论就问机构。
- **两个资料写了不同日期：** 并列来源和冲突，不挑一个“看起来顺眼”的填入。
- **问题牵涉他人私密信息：** 先确认其意愿与机构要求，不将信息复制到公共核对表。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只做普通表格的前置事实核对，不是填表或资格建议。不同机构对“选填”“不知道”“不适用”的表示不同；英国 GOV.UK 的设计惯例不自动适用于手中表格。视力、识字或语言障碍时可用官方可访问版本、翻译或经授权的可信读回，但警示和条件必须保留。副作用是空白格在掌握事实前会保持空白。

## English

Load this Skill before filling one ordinary low-risk form when you do not yet know what every field asks for or whether source materials are available. Make **a map of the exact fields and issuer guidance, with known, missing and ask-first preparation states**, without entering sensitive answers at this stage. This does not decide legal, tax, medical or financial eligibility, fill account or identity-authentication details, or submit the form.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the real issuer, purpose, version or date and matching instructions. Get the full form when paper or a screenshot is incomplete. Use materials you may access and keep originals private. Put only field names and sources in local notes, not ID or account numbers or private detail in a shared sheet. Send specialist eligibility or formal declaration questions to the real issuer or qualified person.

### Execution

1. **Read purpose and structure first.** Check title, issuer, intended person, instructions, signature or submission sections and attachment notices against your task. A same-named older version is not automatically reusable.
2. **Record what each field actually requires.** Note the exact wording, help text, explicit required or optional mark, conditional branch and stated format. Do not decide an unmarked question is optional, or export one website's asterisk habit to every form.
3. **Link fields to factual sources.** Write “which document you may use or which person must confirm → source in hand or missing.” Ask another person before using their information; form preparation does not grant access to their private details.
4. **Keep unknowns separate.** Mark ambiguous words, conflicting records, absent pages or unclear format or scope “ask the issuer.” Do not casually enter `N/A`, zero or a guessed date in a draft. Consider “unknown/not applicable” only when this form actually permits it.
5. **Leave a checkable next-step list.** Separate fields answerable from a source, materials to obtain and exact questions for the issuer, with form version and contact route. You fill later under the real guidance and your authority. A successful preflight is not a correct or accepted submission.

### Success and stop

Exact version, fields, formats, conditions and factual sources are mapped, with no guessed answer for an unknown; sensitive information remains controlled. Pause an incomplete form, unclear issuer, specialist legal, tax, medical or financial question, or somebody else's information you are not authorised to provide, and ask the real institution or suitable qualified channel.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Last year's same-named form was used:** Find the actual issuer's current version and recheck every field.
- **The second half of the instructions is unseen:** Obtain a complete or accessible form; leave affected fields unknown.
- **No asterisk is treated as optional:** Read this form's own markers and guidance or ask its issuer.
- **Two sources have different dates:** List both and their origins; do not pick the more convenient date.
- **A field requests another person's private detail:** Check consent and issuer instructions before use and keep it out of a public checklist.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is factual preparation for an ordinary form, not filling or eligibility advice. Optional, unknown and not-applicable conventions differ; UK GOV.UK design patterns do not govern your form automatically. Official accessible formats, translation and authorised readback can help when sight, literacy or language differs, preserving warnings and conditions. Blank boxes stay blank until there is a real basis.

### Sources

- [GOV.UK Design System: Question Pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/) — optional and “I do not know” responses are form-specific design choices, not options to invent on another form.
