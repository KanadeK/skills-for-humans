## 中文

你已经把现有尤克里里调好，想学会用按弦手在**一根弦的一个品位**发出清楚的音时使用本篇。完成时，你能轻拨同一根弦，听到稳定不闷、不持续打品的声音，并通过松开按弦手指让它停住。它只训练单音接触，不要求会读和弦图或换和弦。疼痛、麻木或琴颈结构异常时立即停。

### 准备与输入

坐稳，琴身轻靠身体，手腕保持能自在移动。选一根容易够到的弦和靠近琴头的普通品位，例如第一弦第二品；先拨空弦作为声音对照。看清金属品丝：手指按在目标品丝**靠琴头的一侧近处**，不直接压在金属线上。选什么品位以你手部舒适为准。

### 执行

1. **放一根指尖。** 用指尖轻放在所选弦、目标品丝后方，其他手指不要误碰该弦；只用能发声的压力，不把手腕弯成难受角度。
2. **轻拨并听。** 另一只手只拨这一根弦。把按弦音与空弦的清晰度比较：若闷，检查有无别的手指碰弦；若打品，微移到更接近目标品丝而不压在品丝上。
3. **只改接触点重试。** 每次只小调手指位置或压力，拨一次便听一次；若始终打品且空弦也异常，停止把问题归咎于手法，记录给懂琴的人检查。
4. **主动收音。** 稳定响起后轻松开按弦手指，让音停止，再按原位置重来一次。给手休息；发痛就结束，不以用力更猛作为通过条件。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

同一目标弦能发出至少两次清楚、可主动停止的按弦音；或你记录了空弦也异常的设备疑点。手指压得越痛不代表音越对。

- **声音闷：** 看指肚是否碰邻弦或按点太靠中间。
- **打品：** 把指尖移近目标品丝后方，仍保留舒适压力。
- **空弦也嗡响：** 暂停技术练习，检查琴是否需专业调校。
- **手痛或麻：** 立刻停、休息；不强迫重复。

### 假设与边界

假设已调音且琴体完好。你决定舒适按点；本篇不诊断手部健康，不调整弦高或琴颈，也不把一次单音等同于完成整首曲子。

## English

Use this Skill after tuning your existing ukulele when you want **one clear note at one fret on one string**. Finish by plucking the same string twice with a stable, unmuted, nonbuzzing sound and deliberately stopping it by releasing the fretting finger. This is a single-note contact exercise, not a chord or chord-change lesson. Stop immediately for pain, numbness or an abnormal neck.

### Preparation and inputs

Sit steadily with the body lightly against you and a freely moving wrist. Choose an accessible string and an ordinary fret near the head, for example second fret on the first string. Pluck it open as a sound reference. Notice the metal fret: put your fingertip close behind the target fret on the headstock side, not directly on the metal. Choose a comfortable fret for your hand.

### Execution

1. **Place one fingertip.** Touch only the chosen string behind its target fret. Keep other fingers off that string. Use just enough pressure for a note without bending the wrist painfully.
2. **Pluck and listen.** Sound only that string with the other hand. Compare clarity with the open string. A dull note may mean accidental touching; a buzz may mean the fingertip sits too far behind the fret.
3. **Change one contact detail.** Adjust position or pressure slightly, then pluck once and listen. If even the open string buzzes or the note stays abnormal, stop blaming technique and record a question for someone who can inspect the instrument.
4. **Stop the note intentionally.** Once it rings clearly, relax the fretting finger to end the sound and repeat the original placement once. Rest your hand. Pain ends the trial; harder force is not the passing condition.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You sound and intentionally stop the chosen clear fretted note at least twice, or record an equipment concern including an abnormal open string. Painful pressure does not prove correctness.

- **Dull sound:** Check if a finger pad touches a neighbour or the target string improperly.
- **Buzz:** Move nearer just behind the target fret with comfortable pressure.
- **Open string buzzes too:** Pause and seek suitable setup inspection.
- **Pain or numbness:** Stop and rest rather than force repetitions.

### Assumptions and limits

The ukulele is tuned and intact. You choose the comfortable contact point. This is no health diagnosis, string-height or neck adjustment, or claim of playing a complete song.
