## 中文

你已按一班铁路车次到原站台或正在过去，却收到这班车**改从另一站台/轨道出发**的通知时，用这份 Skill 验证“改的是同一班”，找站方开放路线重新就位。结果是在新站台正确且安全的位置等这班车，或明确无法确认/无法及时到达并找运营方后续安排。它处理已发生的站台变化；初次找车次站台、普通进站候车和站内换乘是不同任务。人群突然跑起来不是站方公告。

### 准备与输入

保留原票/车次编号、日期、出发时间、运营方、显示终点和你的目标经停站，以及先前站台。读新公告的来源和时间：本站实时出发屏/站台屏、广播、工作人员或运营方实时渠道。多班同终点车可能相邻，不能只凭“往同一个城市”认出同一班。站内可及通路、电梯/闸机限制和实际开口由本车站决定；行动不便时尽早找站方，不为了追列车跑进封闭区域。

### 执行

1. **证明通知指向你的车。** 将新的站台公告和原计划按车次号或运营方、计划/更新后出发时间、终点与停靠站交叉核对。若广播听不清、App 与屏幕冲突，停在安全处看最新站方屏幕并问工作人员；先不离开已知安全区域。
2. **更新状态，不只改一个数字。** 看车是否仍运行、延误多久、新站台是否已经开放、是否仍经过你要下的站。旧站台截图不能作为继续等待依据，新站台号码也不能证明票务和班次没有变化。记录“同一车次—新站台—信息时间”。
3. **按开放通道重走。** 用站内当前导向和工作人员说明走到新站台；发现封闭门、需另一次闸机操作或可及电梯不可用时，停止猜路并询问站方允许的替代。不要跨越轨道、逆行冲闸或推挤人群，也不为准点承诺奔跑穿楼梯。
4. **到达后再核一次。** 在新站台屏幕核对这班的车次、终点、停靠和“可上车/请勿上车”提示，等站方放行。若公告再次变化，重复核对并更新路线；确认赶不上时向运营方询问下一班与该票具体适用规则，不自行断言可以免费改乘。

### 成功条件

同一班次的改台信息有可核验来源，你经开放、安全、可及的路线抵达新站台并在正确候车区等候；或因信息冲突、站台未开放、通路不可及/时间不足而安全停下，知道下一步问谁。旧站台无人并不能单独证明新站台正确。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只听到一个新站台号：** 查车次、时间和终点，防止跟着另一班去错地方。
- **App 与现场屏幕不同：** 查更新时间和该站最新显示，仍冲突就问站方；不要在站台间来回狂奔。
- **新通路关闭或电梯不可用：** 在安全位置联系工作人员确认可达路径和是否需要协助，不跨禁区求近。
- **车已开或下一站台太远：** 停止追车，保留通知与车次，按该运营方规则询问后续可行车次/票务，不保证原票有效。
- **新站台又改变：** 重新读这班的当前站方信息，不能把第二条旧截图当最终版。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通、可安全等待的铁路改台，不教跨轨、票务法律或赔偿规则。英国 National Rail、德国 DB 和荷兰 NS 的信息渠道是地区实例。无手机可用站内屏、广播或工作人员；听觉、视力、行动需求应使用站方实际可及服务。更改位置可能多花时间，安全停下并核对比跟随人流上错车更接近任务目标。

## English

Use this Skill when you had matched a rail service to one platform or were heading there and are told **the same train will depart from another platform or track**. Verify that the change refers to your service, then use the station's open route to reposition. The result is safely waiting at the new correct platform, or a clear stop if the change cannot be confirmed or reached, with an operator next step. This begins after initial service/platform matching; a crowd running is not an announcement.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep the original ticket/train ID, date, departure time, operator, displayed terminus, your calling point, and earlier platform. Read the source and time of the new notice: live station or platform board, announcement, staff, or official live channel. Several trains can share a terminus, so “toward the same city” does not identify one run. The current station controls open paths, lifts, and gates. Ask early for real assistance if moving between platforms is difficult; do not enter a closed area to chase a train.

### Execution

1. **Prove the notice names your train.** Cross-check the new platform against train ID or operator, planned/revised departure time, terminus, and calling points. For an unclear announcement or app/board conflict, stay safe, check the latest station display, and ask staff before leaving the known area.
2. **Update more than one number.** Check that the service still runs, its delay, whether the new platform is open, and whether it still calls at your stop. An old screenshot is no reason to remain at the old platform; a new platform number alone does not prove the train and ticket conditions are unchanged. Record “same service — new platform — information time.”
3. **Use an open station route.** Follow current signs and staff to the new platform. For a closed gate, another ticket check, or a failed accessible lift, stop and ask for the permitted alternative. Never cross tracks, tailgate, or push through a crowd; no scheduled minute justifies running blindly on stairs.
4. **Recheck on arrival.** On the new platform, confirm train ID, terminus, calls, and any board/announcement saying whether boarding is permitted. If the platform changes again, repeat. If you cannot catch it, ask the operator about the next service and your specific ticket; do not assume a free change of train.

### Success

The change was verified for the same run, you used an open safe accessible path, and you wait in the correct new boarding area. A safe stop for conflicting notices, unopened platform, inaccessible route, or insufficient time with a known staff/operator next step also counts. An empty old platform alone does not identify the right new one.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a new platform number was heard:** Verify train ID, time, and terminus to avoid following another departure.
- **App and station board disagree:** Check update times and the latest station notice; ask staff if unresolved rather than shuttling between platforms.
- **New path is closed or lift fails:** From a safe point ask staff for an accessible allowed route; do not enter restricted space.
- **Train has left or new platform is too far:** Stop chasing, retain notice and reference, ask the operator about available later trains and ticket rules; validity is not presumed.
- **Platform changes a second time:** Read this train's current official station information again, not the second old screenshot.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary safely interruptible platform change, not track crossing or a legal compensation guide. UK National Rail, German DB, and Dutch NS are regional examples. Station screens, announcements, and staff can replace a phone. Use actual accessible help for hearing, sight, or mobility needs. Moving may cost time; a verified safe stop is preferable to blindly following the crowd onto a wrong train.
