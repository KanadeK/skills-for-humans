## 中文

你已有一张适合本琴调弦的**单个和弦图**，想将其变成可听的和弦时使用本篇。完成时，图上应响的弦逐根检查后，在一次轻扫中共同发声；标了 X 的弦不误弹。它处理“读图到一个和弦”，后续换位和节奏各有独立结果。示例可以用公有或自画的简单图，不复制付费乐谱。

### 准备与输入

确认本琴为图所用的调弦，四根空弦已调好。观察图上的四条竖线、品位横线、圆点/指号，以及 O（空弦）或 X（不弹）；图的朝向依来源标记核对，不能假定所有截图都同向。选手能舒适够到的简单和弦，不要求特定手指必须迁就一张模糊图。

### 执行

1. **把图映射到真琴。** 逐根指出 G、C、E、A 的位置和需要按的品位；若图没有标弦名或调弦，与可信图例核对后才继续。
2. **慢放按弦手指。** 在品丝靠琴头的一侧近处放指尖，留出邻弦空间；标 O 的弦不按，标 X 的弦准备避开。手腕或指关节疼痛时停下。
3. **一弦一弦试音。** 对照图逐根轻拨要响的弦；闷或打品时只调整该处手指，重新检查其它弦没有因移动被遮住。无需追求大声。
4. **扫一次并复核。** 轻扫图中允许的弦，听它们能否一起响；若 X 弦被碰到，缩小扫弦范围重来。记下图的名称及仍未清楚的弦，不把视觉上像手型当作成功。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

所有应响弦单独清楚、禁弹弦避开，一次扫弦有完整和弦声；或者明确记录哪根失败。

- **图朝向看不懂：** 找有弦名/指号的原图例，别猜。
- **一根弦闷：** 看邻指是否压住它，先试单弦再扫。
- **手指痛：** 降低压力、休息或选更合适的简单图；不硬撑。
- **本琴非标准调弦：** 用匹配图，不移植这张图的音名。

### 假设与边界

本篇假设完整且可辨认的图来自获准来源；不同调弦、左手图和变调夹位置需另行核对。你听音验收，本篇不提供版权歌曲谱或和弦库承诺。

## English

Use this Skill with **one permitted chord diagram** that matches your ukulele tuning. Finish when every required string rings individually and together in one gentle strum while any X-marked string is avoided. This turns a chart into one chord; changes and rhythm have separate results. Use an owned, permitted or self-drawn simple diagram rather than copying a paid song sheet.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the instrument's tuning against the diagram and tune its four open strings. Identify four vertical strings, fret lines, dots/finger numbers, O for open and X for not played. Confirm chart orientation from its labels, as screenshots may differ. Choose a simple shape your hand can reach comfortably; do not force it around an unreadable chart.

### Execution

1. **Map chart to instrument.** Point to G, C, E and A and the frets marked for each. If the diagram omits string names or tuning, check a reliable legend before proceeding.
2. **Place fingertips slowly.** Press just behind frets on the headstock side, leaving adjacent strings room. Leave O strings open and prepare to avoid X strings. Stop for wrist or joint pain.
3. **Test strings one by one.** Gently pluck each required string. Adjust only the finger causing a mute or buzz, then recheck the others. Volume is not the goal.
4. **Strum once and verify.** Sweep only allowed strings and listen for a complete chord. Narrow the sweep if an X string sounds. Record the diagram name and any string still unclear; a visual hand shape alone does not pass.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

All required strings ring singly and together, excluded strings stay out, or you identify the failed string honestly.

- **Chart orientation unclear:** Return to a labelled original legend.
- **One string muted:** Check whether a neighbouring finger touches it, one string at a time.
- **Hand hurts:** Ease pressure, rest or choose a more reachable simple shape.
- **Nonstandard tuning:** Find a matching chart instead of transplanting note names.

### Assumptions and limits

The diagram is complete, legible and permitted. Alternate tunings, left-handed charts and capo placement require their own mapping. You judge the sound; this provides no copyrighted song chart or complete chord library.
