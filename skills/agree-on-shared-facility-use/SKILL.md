## 中文

当几位同住者经常需要同一处普通设施，例如浴室、厨房操作区或洗衣机，而时间冲突开始靠猜时加载。你要与实际使用者定下**普通使用、冲突和临时变化的可执行办法**。这里只谈使用权的日常协调，不替别人决定私人用品可否共享、不修设备，也不解释租约或公共设施管理规定。

### 准备与输入

请会使用设施的人参与或明确留下待确认项。先了解实际作息、最长难以中断的使用段、可否预约、谁有行动或沟通方面的需要，以及房屋或管理方已有的规则。只收安排所需信息，不要求别人公开隐私原因。若只有一部分人到场，可以讨论草案，但不能把未参与者写成已同意。

### 执行

1. **说清设施与冲突时刻。** 指定一处设施及最常撞车的时间，而不是用“大家互相体谅”代替可操作问题。区分短时必需使用和可错开的普通使用，不按身份自动排优先级。
2. **提出普通安排。** 让各人说需要的大致时段、提前告知方式和是否接受共享日历/门上标记。用大家实际能做到的简短约定，例如忙时先声明预计用多久，超出再通知；不规定未经同意的通用分钟数。
3. **约好碰撞时怎么办。** 两人都需要时，明确谁询问谁、怎样交换或延后、无法等时去哪个已确认的替代点。特殊使用条件由相关人自行说明需要的安排，不要求公开个人原因。
4. **确认临时变化和边界。** 来客、设备不能用、某人时间临时改变时，由谁向受影响的人发出通知，原安排何时恢复。实际房屋或管理方规则优先；任何人没同意的新独占安排不能悄悄写进记录。
5. **回读并留一条可改的记录。** 让各参与者用自己的话说普通时段、碰撞方法和如何通知变化。写明从何时试行、何时复看；未参与者的部分标待确认，不把沉默等同于许可。

### 完成与停止

参与者能说清这处设施正常怎么轮用、同时需要时怎么协调、谁会通知变动；未同意或无法执行的部分明确未定。持续冲突、设施本身异常或管理规则不明时暂停争议部分，改查实际规则或求合适协调；本 Skill 不教修理或强制他人让用。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“先到先用”每天仍撞车：** 找真实高峰时段，补上提前告知和冲突交换方法。
- **有位室友没参与：** 只记已参与者同意的试行范围，联系其确认，不代签。
- **一方需要更长时间但不想解释原因：** 只谈可行时段、通知和替代，不追问隐私。
- **群聊没人看：** 换成各人确实能接收的短消息、纸面标记或口头确认；重要变动要被对方收到。
- **实际设施坏了：** 停止按正常预约执行，联系有权处理者，另定可行替代；不要自行维修。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通共住关系和有权使用的设施，不规定法定时段、噪声标准或费用分摊。文化、轮班、障碍和家庭安排会改变可行时段；没有智能手机也能用纸表或当面回读。副作用是“这个点谁在用”从猜谜变成可以礼貌核对的事实。

## English

Load this Skill when several housemates repeatedly need one ordinary facility, such as a bathroom, kitchen work area or washer, and access is being guessed. You will agree with actual users on **a workable normal pattern, collision rule and way to handle changes**. This coordinates everyday use; it does not grant access to private goods, repair equipment or interpret tenancy or building rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Invite the people who will use the facility or mark their input pending. Learn real schedules, any hard-to-interrupt use periods, whether booking is possible, access or communication needs, and existing building or management rules. Ask only what the arrangement needs; nobody must disclose a private reason. If some people are absent, discuss a draft but do not record them as agreeing.

### Execution

1. **Name the facility and collision.** Identify one facility and the time demand actually overlaps. “Be considerate” alone cannot route two people at once. Distinguish brief essential use from flexible use without assigning automatic priority by identity.
2. **Propose a normal pattern.** Each person states a rough time need, notice method and whether a shared calendar or door mark works. Choose a short arrangement everybody can follow, such as stating an expected finish during busy periods and updating when it slips. Do not impose one universal minute limit without agreement.
3. **Decide what happens when demand collides.** Name who checks with whom, how to swap or delay, and any confirmed alternative if waiting does not work. The person concerned can state an access need without disclosing why it exists.
4. **Handle changes and existing limits.** Decide who tells affected users about a visitor, unavailable equipment or changed schedule, and when normal use resumes. Real building rules take precedence; do not quietly write a new exclusive right nobody accepted.
5. **Read back a revisable record.** Have participants restate normal access, the collision method and change notice. Record when to try it and when to revisit it. Mark an absent person's part unconfirmed; silence is not consent.

### Success and stop

Participants can explain normal use, what they do when both need access, and who announces changes. Unagreed or infeasible parts remain explicitly open. Pause a contested part if collisions continue, the facility malfunctions or building rules are unknown; check the real rules or seek suitable coordination. This Skill neither repairs equipment nor forces another person to yield.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“First come, first served” still causes daily clashes:** Identify the real busy period and add notice plus a swap method.
- **One housemate did not participate:** Record only the participants' trial scope and ask the other person; do not sign for them.
- **Someone needs more time but will not explain why:** Discuss workable windows, notice and alternatives without pressing for private reasons.
- **Nobody reads the group chat:** Use a message each person receives, a paper marker or spoken confirmation. An important change needs an actual recipient.
- **The facility itself fails:** Stop using the ordinary booking arrangement, tell the responsible party and find a feasible alternative. Do not repair it yourself.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary shared living and facilities the participants may use. It sets no legal time, noise or cost-allocation rule. Culture, shift work, disability and household rhythms affect a workable schedule. Paper or spoken readback can replace smartphones. The side effect is that “who is using it now?” becomes a polite, checkable question instead of a guessing game.
