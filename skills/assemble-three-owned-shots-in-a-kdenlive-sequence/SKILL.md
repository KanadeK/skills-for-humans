## 中文

你已从自有短视频里选出三个用途不同的镜头，想做一段最小可观看的顺序时使用本篇。成果是三镜头在时间线上按约定先后衔接、每段至少有一个清楚视觉信息，播放时不出现意外黑帧或覆盖。它组织顺序，不靠花哨转场掩盖三段内容其实相同。

### 准备与输入

保存工程，分别写下镜头一、二、三的短目的和原文件名，确认许可与声音边界。检查 Timeline 目标视频轨为空或有足够空间，避免拖入时压住已有内容。若某镜头与另一段实质重复，就合并选择而非硬凑三段。

### 执行

1. 按预定顺序把三个项目箱素材放入 Timeline 相邻安全位置。
2. 在 Project Monitor 从头到尾看一遍，核每段开头结尾可辨，未误叠/留黑洞。
3. 核每镜头对应原文件且音轨没有突然混入不该听的声音。
4. 顺序不通就只调整镜头次序，保存重开再次短看。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

三段各有不同作用、顺序明确、预览连续，源文件仍在。

- **黑帧空隙：** 缩短间隙或明确留白目的。
- **片段覆盖旧内容：** 撤销并找安全轨位。
- **三段同义：** 删冗余镜头，不靠数目凑。

### 假设与边界

只组一个私下三镜头序列，不证明故事好看或观众理解。你负责内容和许可。

## English

Use this Skill when three permitted short shots each serve a different purpose and need a minimal watchable order. Finish with three clips in intended sequence, one clear visual point per shot and no accidental black frame or overwrite on playback. This assembles order rather than hiding redundant shots behind transitions.

### Preparation and inputs

Save project and note each shot's purpose/source name, rights and audio boundary. Check target video track has safe space so a drop does not overwrite existing clips. If a shot repeats another's outcome, reduce rather than forcing three.

### Execution

1. Place three bin clips in intended order on safe adjacent Timeline positions.
2. Watch in Project Monitor from first to last, checking discernible edges, no unintended overlap or blank gap.
3. Verify each source identity and no unintended audio enters mix.
4. For poor narrative order, rearrange shots rather than effects, then save/reopen and preview.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Three shots have distinct roles, ordered continuous preview and source files remain.

- **Black gap:** Close gap or justify pause.
- **Old clip overwritten:** Undo and use free track space.
- **Shots redundant:** Remove duplicate purpose.

### Assumptions and limits

This assembles a private three-shot sequence, not audience comprehension or quality. You own content and rights.
