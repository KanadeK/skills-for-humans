## 中文

当你的一片**小织样已收针藏线**，想按这团纱线的真实护理规则处理，再量干后尺寸时，加载这份 Skill。结果是洗护方式有依据、样片干燥且尺寸状态明确，或标签缺失/禁水而暂停湿处理。它与只在针上数针的密度检查不同；尤其羊毛、混纺或染色纱不能用一个万能浸泡时间。

### 准备与输入

找回原标签的纤维与洗护符号，确认样片只有该种纱或能同时遵守各标签。先量未处理长宽并标“洗前”；准备标签允许的水温、温和用品、干净毛巾与平整通风位。标签禁止水洗、缺失、会严重掉色或纤维身份不明时，不试湿整理，记录限制并向厂家核实。无需图样指定尺寸时只自然铺平，不强拉拉伸。

### 执行

1. **按标签决定洗或不洗。** 可手洗者才按标签用允许温度和合用用品轻轻浸润/冲净，避免搓揉、拧绞导致变形或缩毡；需要干洗/特殊护理的只交给其合适途径，不在家用通用方法替代。
2. **温和去水并铺平。** 将湿样托起放毛巾间轻压吸水，不扭成绳；在平整透气处按原织形轻理边，不拉到目标数字。所用线若标签要求其他晾法，以标签为准；远离直热，防止湿样染坏家具。
3. **等全干才复量。** 摸中心、边和背面无潮后，自然平放量长宽；有足够中央区域再按密度检查法数针/行。把洗前/洗后数字并列写下，出现缩、伸或卷边如实记录。
4. **收存或重做决策。** 全干且无变色/破损再收好，保留标签与记录；若与目标图样密度不符，下一次换针/线做新样，不靠再拉伸旧样制造合格。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

样片的处理严格符合纱线标签、全干，洗前与洗后或“未湿处理”的状态可回查；材料不适合时正确暂停同样算完成判断。仍潮时量到好看的数并称最终密度，不算。

- **标签缺失或禁水：** 停湿处理，核厂家/专业护理；只保留干态记录。
- **水中明显掉色：** 停止继续混洗，按标签单独处理，不能宣称原色不变。
- **样片被搓缩/变形：** 不强力拉回，记录实际结果并为下次调整材料/护理。
- **内层仍潮：** 延长平放通风，未干不密封收存或复量。
- **手眼/搬运不便：** 用小样、低位平面、放大尺及可信协助托湿织物，由你核对标签和最终记录。

### 假设与边界

洗护会改变不同纤维的尺寸与手感，未知产品不能套用某教程的洗整配方。样片密度只能帮助规划下一次试织，不保证实际成衣合身或达到标签外的卫生效果。

## English

Load this Skill when a **small swatch has been bound off and tails woven** and you want to care for it under this yarn's actual label and measure after drying. Finish with a justified care route, dry sample, and clear post-care dimensions, or hold wet treatment for missing/prohibiting instructions. This differs from counting a sample still on needles. Wool, blends, and dyes do not share one soaking time.

### Preparation and inputs

Find original fibre and care symbols. If the sample mixes yarns, check that all labels can be obeyed together. Record untreated width and length as “before care.” Have only label-permitted water temperature and mild product, clean towels, and a flat aired spot. For no-wash, missing, unknown-fibre, or obvious dye-transfer warnings, defer home wet care and ask the maker. Without a pattern size target, lay it naturally rather than stretching to a number.

### Execution

1. **Choose wash or no wash from the label.** Hand-wash only if allowed, with its temperature and compatible product, wetting and rinsing gently without rubbing or wringing that might distort or felt the fabric. Dry-clean-only or specialist care goes to its proper route rather than a generic home bath.
2. **Remove water gently and lay flat.** Support the wet swatch between towels and press lightly; do not twist it into a rope. Arrange its edges in natural knitted shape on an aired flat surface without pulling to a target gauge. Follow any different maker drying rule, avoid strong heat, and protect furniture from wet dye.
3. **Measure only fully dry.** Check centre, edge, and reverse for remaining dampness, then measure length and width naturally. On a sufficiently wide centre, count stitches/rows using the gauge procedure if needed. Record before and after side by side, including shrinkage, growth, or curl.
4. **Store or decide on a fresh swatch.** Keep it with label and record only once dry and sound. If it misses a pattern gauge, change needle or yarn for a new swatch instead of stretching the old one into a claimed match.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The care route follows the real yarn label, the swatch is fully dry, and before/after or “not wet-treated” state is traceable. Correctly withholding wet care for unsuitable material is a valid decision. Measuring a damp swatch at a flattering size and calling it final gauge is not.

- **Label missing or forbids water:** Pause wet work, check the maker or specialist care, and retain dry measurements only.
- **Water shows strong colour bleeding:** Stop mixing it with other items and follow label-specific care; do not promise unchanged colour.
- **Rubbing shrinks or distorts:** Do not force it back; record the outcome and adjust materials or care next time.
- **Centre remains damp:** Continue flat air drying before closed storage or final measurement.
- **Handling or measuring is difficult:** Work with a small piece, low surface, enlarged ruler, and trusted support lifting wet fabric while you confirm label and record.

### Assumptions and limits

Fibre care changes size and feel. An unknown product cannot use another tutorial's universal wash/block recipe. Gauge can guide a later trial, not guarantee garment fit or any hygiene performance beyond its label.
