## 中文

当你想知道一件普通包装食品按你**实际计划用量**会提供多少标签所列的某项数值，而包装正面只写“一份”或“每 100 克”时加载。本 Skill 只换算标签数字，不能决定你该摄入多少；医疗饮食、严重过敏、婴幼儿喂养和保健功效不在此范围。

### 准备与输入

找到准确商品的完整营养表、它采用的基准（每份、每 100 g/mL、整包或冲调后）、包装总量，以及你预计使用的同单位数量。先选**一个**你要核对的标示项目，例如糖或钠，而非把整表都重新排版。若包装与网上图片版本不同，以实物和适用地区的正式标签为准。小字不便阅读时放大清晰照片、使用读屏或请人读出数字与单位；关键数字仍要复核。

### 执行

1. **标记数字的分母。** 美国 Nutrition Facts 常按每份列出，包装可能有多份或另列整包一栏；欧盟标签以每 100 g 或 100 mL 为共同基准，也可另列每份。标签“一份”是计算基准，不是你必须吃的建议量。
2. **把计划用量换到同一单位。** 例如标签每 50 g 含糖 12 g，而你计划用 100 g，就有 2 个标签份量；按标示数值估算为 24 g。若你只用 25 g，则是半份、约 6 g。保留“约”，因为标签值可能经过取整。
3. **核对食用状态。** 读营养表指未冲调产品、按指定方法制备后的产品，还是整包。你准备加入别的材料时，不能把原商品的表格当成完成品总值；如果不知道冲调比例或取用量，就标“无法换算”。
4. **比较时统一分母。** 两件商品只有在同样的食用状态、同一营养项目和可换算的用量下才比较数字。一个“每份”与另一个“每 100 g”不能直接比大小。
5. **输出有限结论。** 记录商品版本、项目、标签基准、你的用量、算式和结果。若你事先有普通购买阈值，可据此判断是否符合；阈值本身不由这份 Skill 发明。

### 成功与停止

你能说出“这件商品按我计划使用的量，标签估算的某项目约为多少”，并指出原表的分母。标签缺项目、单位不一致且无法换算、配制条件不明或计划用量没确定时停在“未知”，不要用正面的大号数字代填。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把一包当一份：** 读每包份数和份量；若整包也有专栏，先确认你看的那一栏。
- **把每份与每 100 g 直接比：** 用同一实际用量重算两件商品。
- **冲调前后混用：** 找到对应状态和比例；找不到就不要排名。
- **算到一半被打断：** 留下“标签分母、计划用量、项目”三个数字，回头再算；不用记住一个悬空的乘数。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里只处理普通购物时的算术，不解释营养目标或健康效果。地区的营养表格式、单位和取整规则不同，按实物标签及当地官方说明读。无法称量时可用包装标明的件数或容量作近似，并明确误差；没有可信数量就保持未知。副作用是你可能发现一小包食品在标签上拥有不止一个身份。

## English

Load this Skill when you want the amount of one listed value in an ordinary packaged food at the quantity you **actually plan to use**, while the front says only “per serving” or “per 100 g.” This is label arithmetic, not a recommendation for how much you should consume. Medical diets, severe allergies, infant feeding, and health-effect claims are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the complete panel for the exact product, its basis (per serving, per 100 g/mL, whole pack, or prepared), the pack total, and your planned quantity in a compatible unit. Choose **one** listed item, such as sugar or sodium, rather than recopying the entire panel. If an online image differs from the pack, use the physical label and local official guidance. Enlarge small print, use read-aloud tools, or have someone read the number and unit; check critical figures again.

### Execution

1. **Mark the denominator.** US Nutrition Facts often list values per serving; a pack can contain several servings or include a separate whole-pack column. EU panels use a common per-100 g or per-100 mL basis and may add per-portion values. A label serving is a calculation basis, not an order to eat that amount.
2. **Convert your planned amount to that basis.** If 50 g is labelled as containing 12 g sugar and you plan to use 100 g, that is two label servings: approximately 24 g by the declared value. For 25 g, it is half a serving, approximately 6 g. Keep “approximately” because declared values may be rounded.
3. **Check the food's state.** Does the panel describe the product as sold, prepared by a stated method, or the whole pack? Added ingredients are not included in the original product's values. If the mixing ratio or amount you will use is unknown, mark the calculation unconfirmed.
4. **Use one basis for comparison.** Compare two items only for the same nutrient, comparable prepared state, and a quantity you can convert. A “per serving” number cannot be ranked against a “per 100 g” number without conversion.
5. **Make a limited record.** Note the exact version, listed item, label basis, your amount, arithmetic, and result. If you already set an ordinary buying threshold, you can compare with it; this Skill does not invent the threshold.

### Success and stop

You can say “At my planned amount, this label implies approximately this much of the listed item,” and name the label's denominator. If the item is absent, units cannot be reconciled, preparation is unclear, or your amount is unknown, stop at unknown. A large front-panel number cannot fill a missing input.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Treating one pack as one serving:** Read servings per container and serving size; confirm which column you used if a pack column also appears.
- **Comparing per-serving with per-100 g directly:** Recalculate both at the same actual amount.
- **Mixing as-sold and prepared values:** Find the corresponding state and ratio; if unavailable, do not rank them.
- **Interrupted calculation:** Save the label basis, planned amount, and listed item, then calculate later instead of remembering a stray multiplier.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is arithmetic for ordinary shopping, not interpretation of dietary targets or health outcomes. Panel formats, units, and rounding differ by region; follow the actual pack and local official guidance. If you cannot weigh an amount, a labelled count or volume may give an explicit approximation; without a trustworthy quantity, keep unknown. One small pack may turn out to have several label identities.
