## 中文

当可靠的**当前本地预报**提示夜间可能霜冻，而一盆普通观赏植物可能不耐受时，加载这份 Skill。你要按品种和现场条件决定移到无霜安全位置、采用适用的临时覆盖，或明确无法保证保护并求助。炼苗不能使不耐霜植物变耐霜。

### 准备与输入

核对植物品种/标签的耐寒说明、预报覆盖的地点与夜间时段，以及盆器能否安全移动。只处理你有权照料、可安全搬动的小盆；大盆、破裂盆、尖刺或未知刺激性植物请人协助。提前确认临时位置不会堵门、压坏地面、靠近热源或让儿童宠物接触不明植物。

### 执行

1. **把风险对齐。** 对照当地官方最新预报、低温时段和该品种耐受范围；不以“去年同日没霜”或地区平均最后霜日替代今晚预报。微地形和盆栽暴露会改变风险，资料不足就保守地求园艺建议。
2. **优先选可行的移动路线。** 若适合，把小盆移至获准、通风并符合品种最低温度与光照要求的临时无霜位置；室内搬入前查看可见虫害，避免紧贴其他植物。稳定放置并接住排水。
3. **无法搬动时才评估覆盖。** 只用与品种、盆器和天气条件相适合的园艺覆盖物，按可靠说明固定且不压折植株、不堵通道。RHS 明示覆盖物提供的是**有限**保护；预报低于其能力时，覆盖并不是安全保证，应另找帮助或接受无法保护的状态。
4. **安排撤除与复查。** 记植物原位置、临时位置/覆盖方式、预报时间和下一次查看。天气转暖或覆材内过湿时按资料适时移除并复核植物状态；不让临时覆盖长期闷住植株。

### 成功条件

你能指出本地预报、品种耐寒依据、盆实际去向或覆盖条件，以及何时复查和撤除。若没有符合条件的移动/覆盖方式，明确记录剩余风险并求助，不能把“盖上了”写成“已防霜成功”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有历年霜冻日期：** 查最新当地预报；历史均值不能替今晚做决定。
- **把薄布当万能保温层：** 查材料实测用途与预报强度；必要时移动或求助，别承诺不会冻伤。
- **重盆搬起后失去平衡：** 放回安全处并找协助，不用力挪过台阶。
- **天气暖了仍密封包着：** 按资料撤除或调整并检查潮湿、受压部位。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里处理一盆普通可移动非食用观赏植物的临时天气反应，不建温室、不装电加热、不教大型植株包裹或预测天气结果。行动受限时请人搬运；看不清预报时用可读版本或请人读出地点、时间和数值。天气可能变，所有办法都要复查，且没有存活保证。

## English

Load this Skill when a reliable **current local forecast** flags possible overnight frost and one ordinary ornamental pot may be tender. Use species and actual site conditions to move it to a safe frost-free place, use a supported temporary cover, or acknowledge that protection cannot be assured and seek help. Hardening does not turn a tender plant frost hardy.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the species label's cold tolerance, the forecast location and overnight interval, and whether the pot can move safely. Work only on a small pot you may tend and lift safely; ask for help with heavy, cracked, spiny, or unidentified irritating plants. Confirm the temporary spot will not block an exit, damage a floor, sit beside a heat source, or put an unknown plant within reach of children or pets.

### Execution

1. **Align risk with evidence.** Compare the latest official local forecast and cold interval with species tolerance. Last year's same date or an average last-frost date is not tonight's forecast. Local pockets and container exposure may differ; seek horticultural advice when key facts are missing.
2. **Prefer a workable move.** When suitable, move the small pot to a permitted, ventilated temporary frost-free spot meeting species temperature and light needs. Check visible pests before bringing it near other indoor plants. Set it stably and manage runoff.
3. **Assess a cover only when moving is unavailable.** Use horticultural material supported for the species, pot, and forecast; secure it without crushing growth or blocking access. RHS describes **limited** protection from covers. If forecast cold exceeds that protection, a cover is not a safety guarantee; seek another route or record the unresolved risk.
4. **Set removal and review.** Note original and temporary position or cover, forecast interval, and next check. Remove or adjust the covering when warmth or trapped moisture makes that appropriate under reliable guidance, then observe the plant. A temporary cover is not a permanent sealed home.

### Success

You can state the local forecast, species hardiness source, actual move or cover conditions, and removal/review time. If neither route fits, document remaining risk and seek help; “covered” is not equivalent to “frost damage prevented.”

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only an average frost date is available:** Check a current local forecast; history cannot decide tonight.
- **A thin cloth is treated as universal insulation:** Check its tested use against the forecast. Move the plant or seek help instead of promising survival.
- **A heavy pot becomes unstable while lifting:** Set it down safely and arrange help rather than carrying it across steps.
- **The weather warms but the plant stays sealed:** Adjust or remove the cover as guidance allows and check damp or crushed areas.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a temporary weather response for one movable ordinary non-edible ornamental. It does not build a greenhouse, install electrical heat, wrap a large plant, or predict the weather. Arrange lifting help when mobility is limited and a readable forecast or spoken location/time/value when vision is limited. Conditions can change; recheck them. No survival guarantee follows.
