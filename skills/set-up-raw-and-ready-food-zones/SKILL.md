## 中文

当这顿饭既有生肉、禽、海鲜等**生动物性食品**，也有洗好后即食的菜、熟食或装盘配料时，在动刀前用这份 Skill 设置两条不会互相污染的路径。目标是工作面、工具、手和盛装容器各有明确归属；它不教具体切法、烹调温度或事后清洁事故。

### 准备与输入

列出哪些食物之后还会充分烹调，哪些会在当前步骤后直接入口。检查可用台面、砧板、刀、夹/勺、容器、洗手处和可信清洁条件。若家里只一小块台面或一套工具，先决定能否**分时**使用并按当地食品安全要求在中间彻底清洁；不能时删去会造成交叉接触的做法或暂缓。颜色标签可以帮助辨认，但不代替清洁。

### 执行

1. **标出两条线。** 给生动物性食品设放置、切配、暂存、用过工具的区域；给即食/熟食设另一处干净、不会被生汁液滴到的位置。用容器或清晰记号辅助，不让原料包装跨过即食操作面。
2. **把器具也分开。** 生食与即食或熟食分别有可识别的刀、板、夹、盘；尝味和拌食工具不与生食品工具共用。已经接触生食的盘不能直接接回熟食。若必须复用，安排符合当地指导的完整清洗步骤和再次使用前的明确检查点。
3. **安排手与水槽的转换。** 在接触生食之前确认能方便洗手，生食之后、接触即食食物之前再洗。不要为了“洗得更干净”例行冲洗生肉或禽肉，水花可能把污染带到周围；产品确有特别说明时先核对相关安全指导。
4. **小空间按顺序做，不同时铺满。** 如果只有一个可用工作面，可以先处理即食食品并盖好移到安全位置，再按适用方法清洁工作面和工具，最后处理生食；若之后还要回到即食食品，必须再次完成所需清洁和洗手。时间顺序是用来维持隔离的，不是一张自动消毒卡。
5. **预留成品的干净出口。** 提前放好装熟食/即食食品的干净容器与夹具，确定它们不会经过生食台面或使用过的原料盘。检查刀、板、手、盘和水槽的转换点是否说得清；没有实际可做的路径就先停下，不在开火后再试错。

### 成功条件

你能指出生食和即食食品各在哪处理、用哪些工具、何时洗手和清洁，以及成品将放进哪个干净容器；没有一个未清洁工具被要求在两条线上同时工作。空间或清洁能力不足时，改菜单/步骤或停止，才是可接受的预检结果。

### 常见报错与补救

- **拿两块不同颜色却都没清洁的板：** 先按适用方式处理工具；颜色只标识身份，不证明卫生。
- **生肉盘拿来装熟食：** 换干净的盛装容器；不要因为外观没血迹就宣布安全。
- **即食配菜放在水槽旁等生肉冲洗：** 把即食食品盖好移开；不要例行冲洗生肉禽类造成飞溅。
- **一套工具、没有可清洁的时间或水源：** 这套双路径无法安全运行，先删改菜单或取得可用工具与清洁条件。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 处理普通家庭备餐，不涉及生食动物性食物的食用建议、医疗饮食或危险化学消毒操作。厨房面积、文化菜式和行动能力不同；可用桌面高度的稳定操作区、大字标签、不同触感的合适工具或可信协助，但仍需真实可达的洗手与清洁路径。分区可能多占一点台面并多洗一套工具，这是防交叉接触的实际成本。

## English

Use this Skill before cutting when one ordinary meal includes both **raw animal food** such as meat, poultry, or seafood and foods that will be eaten ready-to-eat, such as washed produce or cooked components. Set two paths for surfaces, tools, hands, and containers so one does not contaminate the other. This is not a lesson in cutting, cooking temperatures, or cleaning up an accident after it occurs.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify what will be thoroughly cooked later and what will be eaten after this preparation step. Check worktops, boards, knives, tongs or spoons, containers, handwashing access, and a reliable way to clean. In a small kitchen with one surface or tool set, decide whether it can be used **in sequence** with thorough cleaning between uses under local guidance. If that cannot be done, change the method or wait. Colour coding helps recognition but does not replace washing.

### Execution

1. **Mark two paths.** Give raw animal food places for unpacking, preparing, holding, and used tools. Give ready-to-eat or cooked food another clean place protected from raw drips. Containers or clear labels may help; do not carry a raw package across the ready-food area.
2. **Separate the tools too.** Give raw and ready or cooked food identifiable knives, boards, tongs, and plates. Do not use a tool that tasted or mixed raw food for ready food. A plate that held raw food does not become a cooked-food plate. If tools must be reused, plan a full cleaning process supported by local guidance and an explicit check before switching.
3. **Plan hand and sink transitions.** Make handwashing accessible before raw work and again after raw food, before touching ready-to-eat items. Do not routinely rinse raw meat or poultry in an attempt to clean it; splashes can carry contamination around the sink. Check food-specific safety directions if a product has an unusual requirement.
4. **Sequence a small worktop instead of filling it.** With one usable surface, prepare ready-to-eat food first, cover and move it to a protected location, then clean surfaces and tools as required before raw work. If you return to ready-to-eat food later, repeat the required cleaning and handwashing. An order of work is a way to maintain separation, not automatic disinfection.
5. **Reserve a clean exit for cooked food.** Place clean serving or ready-food containers and utensils before starting. Keep their path away from the raw area and the original raw-food plate. Check that you can describe each transition for hands, knives, boards, dishes, and sink. If no workable path exists, stop before heating rather than improvising later.

### Success

You can point to where raw and ready foods are handled, which tools each uses, when hands and surfaces will be cleaned, and which clean container receives the finished food. No uncleaned tool is scheduled for both paths at once. With insufficient space or cleaning capacity, changing the menu or stopping is a valid preflight result.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Two different-coloured boards are both dirty:** Clean them appropriately first. Colour identifies a role; it does not prove hygiene.
- **The raw-meat plate is used for cooked food:** Choose a clean serving dish. A plate without visible blood is not proven clean.
- **A ready-to-eat side waits beside a sink where raw meat is rinsed:** Cover and move the side. Do not routinely rinse raw meat or poultry and spread splashes.
- **One tool set, but no time, water, or way to clean it:** The two-path process cannot safely run. Change the menu or obtain workable tools and cleaning conditions first.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary home preparation, not advice to eat raw animal food, a medical diet, or hazardous chemical disinfection. Kitchen size, dishes, and mobility vary. A stable reachable surface, large-print labels, suitable tools with different tactile cues, or trusted help may improve access; handwashing and cleaning still need a real route. Two paths may take space and an extra set of tools to wash. That is the actual cost of preventing cross-contact.
