## 中文

当你需要**现在吃一顿不加热的普通餐食**，手边也没有能可靠维持冷藏的设备，却有几项**未开封、常温可存放且明确可直接食用**的食品时，加载这份 Skill。关键结果是**当场组合并吃掉可安全使用的份量，不把开封剩余物默认留到下一顿**。这里不处理停电应急计划、婴幼儿食品、医疗饮食或罐藏制作。

### 准备与输入

逐件确认包装写明未开封可常温保存、这件食品可以**不再烹调直接食用**，且现有保存史、日期与包装状态可靠。常温货架上有些罐/袋装食品只是“可存放”，仍可能要求加热；用[即食状态核对](../confirm-ready-to-eat-food-for-a-no-heat-meal/SKILL.md)逐件筛。备好干净手、可用开罐/开袋工具、盘或直接食用方式，确认没有要用不安全水清洗的原料。选择这顿能吃完的包装量；没有冷藏条件就不能先承诺开封后留存。

### 执行步骤

1. **先排除不合格的“常温食品”。** “Keep Refrigerated”、需加热/煮熟、密封破损/鼓胀/漏液、开封已久或保存史不明的东西都不进入本餐。不要靠闻味或看颜色替代标签，家制罐头与发酵食品不在这里处理。
2. **从即食且可开封的东西定本餐量。** 按实际人数与食欲挑一项能构成本次主要部分的合格食品，再加可直接取用的伴食；不套营养治疗比例。尽量用小包装或大家能当场吃完的份量，避免需要冷藏但你没有的剩余物。
3. **开封前准备清洁路径。** 洗手，清洁开罐器、盘和餐具；无法安全清洁时选能卫生地直接开启食用的合格食品或暂缓。普通果蔬若需清洗但没有安全流动水，换包装明确预洗即食或别的食品，不用湿巾/清洁剂擦成“已洗”。
4. **等要吃时再开封组装。** 按包装使用方式打开，防止锋利边缘或泄漏；用干净工具只处理本餐份量。不同食品的汁液、包装和餐具不要把生食污染带进即食食物。简洁地组合成现在可吃的一份，不用炉具或额外处理来补全隐藏条件。
5. **关掉“剩余自动常温保存”假设。** 开封后读每件包装要求；需要冷藏的剩余物在没有可靠冷藏时不能放回橱柜等以后吃。可行时当场合理分食完；做不到就按当地食品安全与垃圾处理指导处置，不为减少浪费编一个安全时限。

### 完成与停止

你得到一份现时可直接吃、材料均有明确常温未开封与即食证据、清洁操作可行、开封剩余物有去向的餐食，就完成了。若关键食品只是常温储存却须加热、包装异常、没有安全开封/清洁手段或份量必然留下需冷藏剩余物且无法处理，缩小或改选，不把冰箱容量写成未来的承诺。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“货架上卖”就当成可冷吃：** 看是否标明需加热；需要的就退出本餐。
- **大罐只吃一半，剩下留在桌上：** 读开封后条件；无冷藏时先选小份，已开封剩余物按当地指导处理。
- **罐头鼓胀或严重凹损：** 不打开试味，停止使用并按保守食品安全指导处理。
- **想加入未洗果蔬但没有安全水：** 换明确预洗即食且保存合格的东西，别用肥皂或湿巾清洗食物。
- **开罐器不洁或手不便安全开封：** 清洁工具、选安全易开包装或获得现场协助，不让锋利边缘成为餐具。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里的“常温可存放”仅指**未开封且按该产品标签保存**，不等于开封后仍可常温放置，也不证明某件食品可不煮就吃。地区日期与产品规则不同，依实际标签和当地官方指南。握力、视觉或行动不便时可用易开、单人份、可读标签的产品和现场可信协助；不能清洁工具或安全开启就换食品。现实副作用是你可能多用一个小包装，却不必替一台不存在的冰箱保管晚餐。

## English

Load this Skill when you need **one ordinary meal now without heat**, have no reliable chilling, and can use **unopened, shelf-stable foods clearly ready to eat without cooking**. Finish by combining and eating a suitable amount **now**, without assuming opened remains may sit for another meal. This is not a power-outage plan, infant-food instruction, medical diet, or home-canning method.

### Preparation and inputs

For every item, verify its intact unopened package permits shelf storage, it is **ready to eat without further cooking**, and date and storage history are reliable. Some shelf cans and pouches are only shelf-stable, not necessarily approved to eat unheated; screen them using [Confirm Ready-to-Eat Food](../confirm-ready-to-eat-food-for-a-no-heat-meal/SKILL.md). Have clean hands, an opening tool, dish or direct-eating method, and no item needing unsafe wash water. Choose a pack amount this meal can use. No fridge means you cannot pre-approve opened leftovers.

### Execution steps

1. **Reject unqualified “pantry foods.”** Anything saying Keep Refrigerated, cook or heat before eating, with a broken, swollen, or leaking package, opened long ago, or with unknown storage does not enter this meal. Smell and colour do not replace a label. Home-canned and fermented food are outside this task.
2. **Size the meal from ready foods you can open.** Choose one suitable component for the actual people and appetite, then add ready sides as wanted. No therapeutic nutrition ratio is imposed. Prefer a small pack or an amount that will be eaten now, avoiding opened food needing refrigeration you cannot provide.
3. **Prepare a clean opening route.** Wash hands and clean can opener, dishes, and utensils. If cleaning cannot be done safely, use genuinely ready foods in packaging you can open hygienically or wait. Ordinary produce needing washing cannot be safely substituted with wipes or detergent when running water is unavailable; choose clearly pre-washed, properly stored produce or another item.
4. **Open and assemble when eating begins.** Open as the package directs, avoiding sharp edges and leaks. Use clean tools for this meal's amount. Do not bring raw-food contamination from other packaging or tools onto ready food. Assemble a portion you can eat now without a hidden cooker or preparation step.
5. **Close the “leftovers can return to the shelf” assumption.** Read after-opening directions. Opened food requiring refrigeration cannot go back into a cupboard when chilling is unavailable. Share a reasonable portion now when feasible; otherwise handle remaining food under local food-safety and waste guidance, not an invented safety period.

### Success and stop

You have an immediately edible portion with evidence each item was shelf-stable unopened and ready to eat, a workable clean opening method, and an explicit path for any remains. Choose less or another food if an item merely stores on a shelf but requires heating, its pack is abnormal, opening or cleaning is unsafe, or it inevitably leaves opened food needing unavailable refrigeration.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Sold on a shelf” is read as “fine to eat cold”:** Check for a heat-before-eating instruction. Exclude food that needs it.
- **Half a large can is left on the table:** Read after-opening conditions. Choose a smaller pack next time; handle already opened remains under local guidance when there is no fridge.
- **A can is swollen or severely damaged:** Do not open it for a taste test. Exclude and follow conservative food-safety guidance.
- **Unwashed produce is added without safe water:** Use clearly pre-washed ready food with valid storage or another item; soap or wipes are not produce washing.
- **Can opener is dirty or cannot be gripped safely:** Clean it, use a safely easy-open pack, or get in-person help. A sharp rim is not an eating utensil.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

“Shelf-stable” here applies **unopened under that product's labelled storage**, not after opening, and does not itself prove ready-to-eat status. Regional dates and product rules vary; use actual labels and local official guidance. Easy-open single portions, readable labels, and trusted in-person help can support limited grip, sight, or mobility. Choose another food when opening or tool cleaning is unsafe. A small extra package may avoid making a non-existent refrigerator responsible for dinner.
