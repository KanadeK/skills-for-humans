## 中文

当完成一件普通任务**必须使用的设备或应用不可用**，而纸面或另一现成工具无法按原约定交付时，加载这份 Skill。你要尽早给相关人一个准确的状态、可行替代或延期提议，再按对方真实回复行动；不宣称任务已经上传、发送或被接受。若任务其实可在安全载体上继续，先用本域的离线桥接 Skill。

### 准备与输入

核原约定的交付内容、对象、时间和为什么该设备真是必需：是否只是你习惯用它，还是内容、权限、格式或最终提交入口都依赖它。列出目前**已完成**和**未完成**的部分，保留现有稿件状态；别为“证明努力”反复重启、拆机、改系统或用未经授权的私人账号。选已经约定且实际能用的联系渠道。

### 执行

1. **确认阻塞范围。** 做一次界面级检查：原文件是否可在已授权设备/本地副本打开、正式提交入口是否可达。在线占位文件或未预备离线的云文档可能打不开；无证据时不能把它们写成可用替代。停止在可见界面，不进入系统维修。
2. **提出两个现实选项以内。** 例如“现在交可读的普通纸面摘要，正式文件在设备恢复后补”或“把原交付推迟到明天某个具体时间”。只有你真能做到、对方有权接收的选项才提出；如果没有可靠恢复时间，给下一次更新的时间而不是编造完成时间。
3. **及时发送可核消息。** 通过对方已接受的通道简短说：“原定 X，已完成 A，尚缺 B；设备/应用当前无法完成 B。我能改为 Y，或在 Z 前再给状态。你希望选哪种？” 有必要就附可分享的非敏感部分，但不把未核草稿当正式完成。
4. **看是否实际送达与得到决定。** 查消息发送状态；若频道不可用，按已约定备选渠道联系。未回覆就是“待确认”，不自动把你提出的新时间视为已同意；原约定是否仍有效要看双方条件。若对方接受替代，复述这次要交的内容、渠道和时间。
5. **留恢复后的关口。** 记下真实状态、对方答复、下一次更新点和现有稿件位置。原设备恢复后先核已存在的最新版和未同步内容，再执行正式交付；只在目标渠道确实显示提交/收到时更新为“已交”。无法在可接受时间恢复，就按实际约定再次沟通，而非静默失踪。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你已通过可核的渠道发出真实阻塞、完成程度和可行的替代/更新时间，并记录对方答复或“待确认”。对方必须同意新方案不是你的成功条件；最终文件送达仍是另一个实际动作。

- **只说“设备坏了”：** 补已完成/未完成、需要对方决定的选项和下次更新点。
- **你给了无法保证的恢复时间：** 改说何时提供下一次状态，不将预计修好时间当事实。
- **消息未送出或对方没读：** 核送达状态并用原约定备选方式联系；不把打字框里的文字当通知。
- **纸面摘要没有所需数据：** 不从记忆补虚构数字；说明哪些输入不可访问，暂缓正式交付。
- **受管文件只能经原系统提交：** 遵守权限，向负责人员说明限制；不传个人云盘或绕过组织渠道。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

设备、应用、组织和截止规则因场景而异；本篇是普通协作沟通，不处理合同、法律或紧急安全时限。离线文件是否可打开取决于之前是否可用，本 Skill 不教启用或绕过访问控制。对方可能提出第三个办法或不同期限，你按真实回复协商。清楚说出未交付状态，比安静等待设备“自愈”更便于安排。

## English

Load this Skill when an **essential device or app is unavailable** and an ordinary task cannot be delivered as agreed through paper or another already usable tool. Tell the relevant person the true status early, propose a workable alternative or changed time, and act on their actual reply. Do not claim the task was uploaded, sent, or accepted. If the task can safely continue elsewhere, use the separate offline-bridge Skill first.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the promised result, recipient, and time, and why this device is truly necessary: is it habit, or are the content, permission, format, or final submission bound to it? Separate what is **done** from what remains. Preserve existing work; do not repeatedly restart, open hardware, change system settings, or use an unauthorised personal account to “prove effort.” Choose an agreed contact route that works now.

### Execution

1. **Confirm the blocked part.** Make one interface-level check: can an existing authorised device or local copy open the source, and can the formal submission route be reached? Online-only placeholders or cloud documents without prior offline access may not open. Without evidence, do not present them as an available fallback. Stop at visible controls, without device repair.
2. **Offer no more than two real options.** For example, “I can give a readable ordinary paper summary now and the formal file after the device returns,” or “Move the original delivery to a specific time tomorrow.” Offer only what you can do and the recipient may receive. If repair time is unknown, offer a time for your next update rather than inventing a completion time.
3. **Send a checkable notice promptly.** Through an accepted channel say briefly, “We planned X. A is done; B is not. The device/app cannot complete B now. I can do Y or update you by Z. Which works?” Attach a shareable non-sensitive partial result if useful, without calling an unchecked draft final.
4. **Check delivery and the actual decision.** Verify the notice was sent. If that channel fails, use the agreed alternative. No reply means “awaiting confirmation,” not automatic agreement to your new time; whether the old plan still applies depends on both sides' actual conditions. If they accept an option, repeat content, route, and timing.
5. **Leave a recovery checkpoint.** Record true status, their reply, next update, and where existing work sits. After the device returns, inspect the latest existing version and unsynced work before formal delivery. Change status to “delivered” only when the target channel actually shows submission or receipt. If recovery misses an acceptable time, communicate again under the real agreement rather than disappearing.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You sent a checkable notice of the real block, progress, and feasible alternative or update time through an agreed channel, and recorded their reply or “awaiting confirmation.” Their agreement is not a condition for completing this communication; final delivery still requires a separate real action.

- **You only say “my device broke”:** Add completed and incomplete work, options requiring their choice, and the next update point.
- **You promise an unverified repair time:** State when you will give another status instead of treating an estimate as fact.
- **The notice did not send or remains unread:** Check delivery state and use an agreed alternative. Words in a composer are not a notification.
- **A paper summary lacks required data:** Do not invent figures from memory. Identify inaccessible inputs and delay formal delivery.
- **Managed work can only be submitted in its original system:** Respect access and tell responsible people. Do not move it through personal cloud storage or around organisation controls.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Device, app, organisation, and deadline rules vary. This is ordinary collaboration, not contract, legal, or emergency timing advice. Offline availability depends on prior setup; this Skill does not enable or bypass access control. The recipient may offer a third method or timing; negotiate based on their actual reply. A clear “not delivered yet” status is more useful than silently waiting for a device to recover.
