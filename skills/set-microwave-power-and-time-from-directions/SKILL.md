## 中文

当[容器与摆放已核对](../arrange-food-for-even-microwaving/SKILL.md)，食品包装或可靠配方给出了微波功率、时间范围、中途动作和静置要求，你需要在**这台实际微波炉**上启动第一段加热时，加载这份 Skill。结果是**可解释的第一段功率与时长、明确的中途/结束检查点**，不是直接宣告食物熟透。未知功率或份量不靠随手编一分钟数填空。

### 准备与输入

记录食品确切名称、数量、冷藏/冷冻或室温状态、包装是否要求拆封/刺孔/转盘、设备额定输出功率或相应档位说明。找到食品指示适用的功率、时长或范围、何时搅动/转向、静置多久、怎样验收。微波炉控制面板“高/中/解冻”的含义随型号不同；要用机型说明定位，不把烤箱温度单位当作微波功率。

### 执行步骤

1. **先确认指示适用这份食物。** 份量、初始温度、包装形式和炉具类型要与食品说明吻合。配方只适用于另一种份量或功率而没有调整方法时，不自行按比例倍增时间，先查该食品或制造商的可靠说明；生动物性食品缺安全做法时停止这条微波路线。
2. **在本机找到要求的档位。** 食品写具体功率或某种百分比时，按确切型号说明选择相应功能；找不到相当档位就暂停，不用最接近的按钮猜。容器和转盘必须适合该功能；机器门或旋盘异常时不启动。
3. **为第一段设保守的说明内时长。** 若食品给一个允许范围，在其条件匹配时从范围较短一端开始，保留中途检查与后续补足的空间；若只有明确单值就照原文执行，不擅改关键安全时长。时间从实际启动这份食物算，预先翻手册的分钟不计入。
4. **写下中途与静置的事件。** 食品要求“半程搅拌”“旋转”“加热后静置”时，先设提醒或用纸记住，不把这些动作删成纯加热时间。到点后仍需按食品与当地安全指导确认中心与冷点。
5. **启动前检查停止路径。** 门关合、容器可排气、能安全暂停与开盖、可看守异常声/火花/烟，才按启动。设备提示不明或需要你离开无人看守时先不开机。

### 完成与停止

你能说出此食品在这台炉上的**功率、第一段时长、中途动作、静置与最终检查**的来源，就是完成设置。设备功率/模式不明、食品份量不符且无可靠调整、包装限制冲突或安全完成条件缺失时停止启动；不要靠“平时这样热”补全未知。

### 常见报错与补救

- **看到“高火”却不知本机档位：** 查确切机型说明；不能确认就不用这组指示。
- **把双倍食物直接设双倍时间：** 先查该食品可靠份量调整；没有就分批或换有明确说明的方法。
- **时间范围一律取最大：** 在条件匹配时先按可靠来源允许的短端启动，仍需中途动作、静置与终点检查。
- **包装写需刺孔但已经设好时间：** 先完成包装/容器要求再启动，定时器不修密封包装。
- **响铃就以为可食：** 继续执行搅拌、静置和食品特定熟度检查，铃声只是阶段结束。

### 假设、替代与副作用

普通微波炉的输出功率、模式、转盘和食品份量差异很大，文中不给跨型号的“每瓦几分钟”换算。医疗饮食、婴幼儿食品和缺食品安全指导的生动物性食物不在本流程。看不清小字或屏幕时可用制造商可读说明、大字/语音计时或现场可信协助；不能确认档位和终点就选择其他可靠加热法。现实副作用是“再来两分钟”失去默认管理权限，冷点也少了一层制度保护。

## English

Load this Skill after the [vessel and food layout have been checked](../arrange-food-for-even-microwaving/SKILL.md) when the exact food package or reliable recipe gives microwave power, a time or range, mid-cook actions, and standing. Start the first period on **this actual oven** with **an explainable power, duration, and next check**. Starting is not proof of doneness. Unknown power or portion size cannot be repaired by invented minutes.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the exact food, amount, refrigerated/frozen/room-temperature state, packaging puncture or removal, turntable instruction, and this microwave's rated output or setting meanings. Find food-specific power, time or range, stirring or turning event, standing period, and finish check. “High,” “medium,” and “defrost” differ by model; use its manual. Conventional oven degrees are not microwave power settings.

### Execution steps

1. **Confirm the directions fit this portion.** Amount, start temperature, package, and oven type should match. Do not simply double minutes for a different quantity or power without a reliable food or maker adjustment. Stop using this microwave route for raw animal food without a validated safe process.
2. **Find the requested setting on this model.** Use the exact manual for a specified power or percentage. Pause if no equivalent can be established rather than guessing the closest button. Dish and turntable must suit that mode; do not start a microwave with faulty door or turntable.
3. **Choose the first directed interval.** When a range applies under matching conditions, start at its shorter permitted end and leave room for checks and further safe heating. Follow a food-specific single value as stated rather than casually changing a safety-critical time. Start the clock when this food really starts, not while you are reading instructions.
4. **Mark mid-cook and stand events.** Set a reminder or note for a required halfway stir, rotation, or standing period. Those steps are part of the process, not optional minutes to delete. The food and local safety guidance still decide its centre and cold spots after heating.
5. **Check the start and stop path.** Begin only when the door closes, vessel vents, you can pause and open safely, and you can attend for unusual sound, sparks, or smoke. An unclear alert or need to leave is a reason to wait.

### Success and stop

You can cite the source for this food's **power, first duration, midpoint action, standing, and final check on this model**. Stop before starting when mode or output is unknown, amount differs without a reliable adjustment, package rules conflict, or safe endpoint is absent. “I usually do this” is not the missing instruction.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The pack says “high” but the button meaning is unclear:** Read the exact model manual; do not use the instruction until its setting can be established.
- **Twice the food is given twice the time:** Find a validated portion adjustment, split batches, or use another instructed method.
- **The longest end of every range is chosen by default:** Under matching conditions start at the supported shorter end, then keep the midpoint, stand, and endpoint checks.
- **The pack requires puncturing after the timer is set:** Complete package or vessel instructions before starting; a timer does not vent a seal.
- **The bell sounds and food is called ready:** Perform required stirring, standing, and food-specific doneness checks. It ended a stage.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Domestic microwave output, mode, turntable, and portion size vary widely. This supplies no universal minutes-per-watt conversion. Medical diets, infant food, and raw animal foods without food-specific safety directions are outside it. Use readable maker instructions, a large or speaking timer, or trusted in-person help for inaccessible print or controls. Choose another reliable heat method when setting or endpoint cannot be confirmed. “Two more minutes” loses its default administrative rights and cold spots lose an excuse.
