## 中文

当承运商、商家或普通售后已接下你的事项，并说会做一项具体动作或在某时答复，而你需要知道事情是否真的推进时加载。你要**把承诺、官方状态和眼前可观察的结果分开，适时提出一次具体跟进问题**。这里不重复初次报错，不判断退款/赔偿法律权利，不替服务方完成动作，也不把“系统显示已处理”自动当作实物到手。

### 准备与输入

找回先前的真实联系记录、订单/运单/工单号、对方说过的具体动作与时间条件；不清楚就标“未说明”，别替其加期限。只在本人可控的笔记记录必要的识别信息，账号密码或完整付款资料不发在公开群或交给第三方查询者。不同服务有自己的官方状态入口，没有入口就按其确认的联系方式询问。

### 执行

1. **把承诺写成可查的一行。** 记“谁、何时、在哪个渠道，说要做什么、何时给下一次消息、在什么条件下”。“我们会处理”若没有具体动作，就先问是回电、补发、上门还是调查以及下一次回复点，不自行选结果。
2. **在约定或合理的复看点查官方记录。** 看实际服务方提供的案件状态、物流跟踪或原会话答复；注明查看时刻和来源。预计、处理中、已安排、已交接和自己亲眼确认的完成是不同状态；网页没有更新也不证明服务方一定没做。
3. **与可观察结果对一次。** 若承诺寄来的普通物件还没实际收到、约定回电没发生或预约未被确认，写“承诺内容／官方声称状态／我实际看见的差异”。需要账款或资格判断的部分只记服务方说法，不在本 Skill 核算或裁定。
4. **发一个有边界的跟进。** 用原工单或官方渠道报编号、承诺与当前差异，问“目前确认到哪一步、谁负责下一步、何时再核对？”保存对方实际回答或无人答复状态，不每隔几分钟重复发送同一催问。
5. **更新下一步而不提前关单。** 对方完成了具体可观察动作时记完成及依据；仍待服务方、你自己补材料或双方改变方案时分别写负责人和下一复看点。服务方明说无法继续或路径失效，转相应收口流程，不在这里伪造解决。

### 完成与停止

一项承诺有可追溯的内容和进度，当前状态基于实际答复/观察，未完成项有下一负责人和核对时点；或者明确无可用状态入口。这个结果是跟进记录，不保证服务成功、退款到账或索赔成立。问题进入专业权利、财务争议、危险事项或对方明确无法继续时退出这份普通跟进。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只记“客服答应了”：** 补问具体动作、条件与下一次答复时点，记录说话者或渠道。
- **工单写“完成”，实物/回电未发生：** 保留两种事实，向官方渠道指出差异，不直接改成自己满意的状态。
- **没有工单号：** 用原订单/运单及联系日期让服务方找记录，别凭记忆编一个编号。
- **预计时间一次次延后：** 记录每次由谁给出的新信息，比较当前承诺与实际状态，不把旧预计写成最终期限。
- **客服没有回复：** 留已发送内容与时点，按其实际服务渠道再核；“无人答”不是“已解决”。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通物流或商家服务的一项承诺跟进，不提供法定时限、赔偿、退款或金融核算。商家和地区的状态名称不同，以真实渠道为准；Apple 的案件编号只是一种示例。视力、听力或语言不便时可以让可信授权者读回官方答复并保留可访问记录，不交出账号控制权。副作用是“会帮你处理”必须落实为可以复查的一件事。

## English

Load this Skill when a carrier, seller or ordinary service provider already has your case and has said it will take one action or reply at a stated point. **Separate the promise, official status and what you can actually observe, then ask one precise follow-up at a suitable time.** This does not repeat the first problem report, decide refund or compensation rights, do the provider's work or turn “system says resolved” into “item received.”

### Preparation and inputs

Find real earlier contact, order or tracking or case reference, the provider's stated action and its timing conditions. Mark an unstated point “not stated” instead of making up a deadline. Keep only necessary references in notes you control; do not post passwords or full payment details publicly or give account control to a third-party checker. Providers may have official status pages or only a confirmed contact channel.

### Execution

1. **Write one checkable promise row.** Record “who, when, through which channel, what they said they would do, next update point and any condition.” If it was only “we'll handle it,” ask whether the next action is a call, shipment, visit or investigation and when you should hear back. Do not choose an outcome for them.
2. **Check at the agreed or sensible review point.** Use the real provider's case page, tracking or original thread and note time and source. Estimated, in progress, arranged, handed over and physically confirmed by you are different states. A page that has not refreshed is not proof no work happened.
3. **Compare one observable result.** If an ordinary replacement has not arrived, a promised callback did not happen or an appointment was not confirmed, note “promised / official reported / actually seen” separately. For money or eligibility, record only what the provider said; this Skill does not calculate or judge it.
4. **Make a bounded follow-up.** In the original case or official channel, give the reference, promise and current difference, and ask “what step is confirmed, who owns the next one and when should I check again?” Save its answer or no-answer state. Do not send the same chase every few minutes.
5. **Update the next step without closing early.** Mark a specific observed action complete with its basis. Distinguish waiting on provider, your own missing material and a changed plan, each with a person and next review. If the provider says it cannot continue or the route fails, pass to the closeout task rather than claiming resolution here.

### Success and stop

One promise has traceable wording and progress, current state grounded in a real reply or observation, and open work has a next owner and check time; or lack of a status channel is explicit. This is a follow-up record, not guaranteed service, posted money or a successful claim. Exit for specialist rights, financial dispute, hazardous work or an explicitly dead service route.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “support promised” was recorded:** Ask for the action, condition and next reply point, with person or channel noted.
- **Case says “done” but the item or callback is absent:** Keep both facts and ask officially about the mismatch rather than changing observed state.
- **There is no case number:** Use original order or tracking and contact date for the provider to locate it; do not invent a number.
- **The estimate moves repeatedly:** Record who gave each update and compare the current promise with reality. An older estimate is not the final deadline.
- **Support does not reply:** Keep what you sent and when and check through its real route. No answer is not solved.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This follows one ordinary logistics or seller promise, not a statutory time limit, compensation, refund or accounting. Status names vary by company and region; Apple case IDs are one example only. A trusted authorised helper can read an official reply back accessibly when sight, hearing or language differs without taking over your account. “We'll handle it” now has to name something you can check.
