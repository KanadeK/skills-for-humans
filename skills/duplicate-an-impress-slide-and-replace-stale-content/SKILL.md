## 中文

你需要一张与已有页**结构相同但内容不同**的新页时使用本篇。成果是复用布局后的新页写明自己的标题和资料，旧页仍在，旧文字、图片、备注或动画的陈旧信息没有误留。复制后仅改标题会造成假新页，不能算完成。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 副本，列一份原页对象清单：标题、要点、图片、图表、讲者备注、链接和动画。另写新页独立目的与可用素材，不使用未经许可图片。记录原张数和原页位置，决定副本应插在其后还是稍后移动。

### 执行

1. 选准原页，用 Slide > Duplicate Slide 建一张副本，核原页还在且总数多一。
2. 逐对象替换新页文字、图像、图表数据或删除不适用对象，并核备注/链接/动画没有旧信息。
3. 放映预览原页与新页，确认版式一致但各自信息独立、无错误重复。
4. 保存重开，按清单逐项打勾；未能核实新内容时保留草稿标识而非交付。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

原页保留，新页只复用结构、内容与目的独立，旧资料没有误留。

- **旧备注留着：** 切到 Notes 查并改。
- **旧图仍在：** 核使用许可与内容，替换或删除。
- **复制出两张：** 撤销多余副本并核张数。

### 假设与边界

只复用一页布局，不复制它的事实作为新成果。你负责新素材真伪与许可；复杂动画可删除不用而非硬改。

## English

Use this Skill when a new slide needs the **same structure but different content** from an existing one. Finish with a new title and source-checked facts in the reused layout, old slide retained, and no stale text, image, notes or animation claim left over. Changing only the title would create a false new slide.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP copy and inventory original title, points, image/chart, speaker notes, links and animation. Define a distinct new purpose and permitted assets. Note old count and original slide position, deciding where the duplicate should end up.

### Execution

1. Select source and use Slide > Duplicate Slide; verify original remains and count rises by one.
2. Replace or remove each unsuitable text, image, chart, note, link and animation on the new slide.
3. Preview original and new slide; structure can match while each claim and visual belongs to its own topic.
4. Save and reopen, checking every inventory item; leave draft status when new facts cannot be verified.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Original remains, new slide reuses structure only, has its own purpose and contains no stale old claims.

- **Old notes remain:** Check Notes view and replace.
- **Old image remains:** Verify relevance/permission, replace or remove.
- **Too many duplicates:** Remove excess copy and check count.

### Assumptions and limits

This reuses one slide's structure, not its factual claims. You own new asset accuracy and rights; complex animations can be removed instead of forced into new content.
