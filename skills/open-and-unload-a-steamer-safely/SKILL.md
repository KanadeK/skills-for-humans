## 中文

当普通蒸锅已到配方规定的检查或取出时刻，锅盖和篮具里仍存有热蒸汽时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**安全停止热源、让蒸汽避开身体、确认食物完成并移到近处耐热位置**。这里不把“时间到了”自动等同食物安全熟透，也不教处理烫伤、火灾或设备维修。

### 准备与输入

确认具体食物的配方结束线索和任何适用安全温度；需要时备好已清洁的温度计。找一个近处稳固、可耐热的容器或垫面，准备干燥且适合器具的隔热手套、夹具或提篮把手。读确切蒸具说明，尤其是电蒸锅可能会在计时结束后进入保温模式；灯灭或铃响不保证底座和蒸汽已经冷却。握力、站立、视力或听力不足以安全开盖时先让热源停止并取得现场协助。

### 执行步骤

1. **先按说明停止加热。** 在安全可达时关炉或停电蒸锅，并确认是否还有自动保温。食物需要立刻取出还是需按配方稍停，由具体说明决定；不要让热篮长时间敞放在不稳位置。
2. **从蒸汽路径外开盖。** 站在可操作的一侧，使用干燥隔热工具握稳盖，先让靠身体的一边保持遮挡、把蒸汽导向远离脸和手的方向。按器具说明让盖上的热凝水尽量滴回锅里或落在耐热位置，别让它流向手臂、地面或电器。看不清蒸汽不代表它不存在。
3. **等最强的蒸汽散开再取食。** 不把手伸过仍在冒汽的篮口。篮具在设计负重和你的实际握力内，缓慢提起、在锅上方让水滴回去，再移动到近处耐热落点；太重就改用合适夹具分次取食，不端整套锅跨厨房。
4. **按食物证据判完成。** 从本批合适位置取样，依据配方的质地、时间和适用食品安全要求检查。需要核心温度的食物用[熟度核对](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)，不以盖里有蒸汽替代。未达要求时，只有器具、水量与可靠说明仍成立才继续蒸；未知时间/温度史先停止宣称可食。
5. **给热锅安排余程。** 剩余水与篮具按确切器具说明原位冷却后再清空/清洁，告诉旁人它仍烫。食品的暂存、剩菜与入户保存另用对应流程，不让热食无限期停在台面。

### 完成与停止

食物已由配方/安全条件核实，热源停止，盖和篮安全落位，余水明确等待冷却，才完成这一段。盖无法握稳、凝水会流向电器或人、篮具太重、食物未完成且无法安全继续、出现烟火/燃气异味时停止日常操作并求助。

### 常见报错与补救

- **计时器响就徒手掀盖：** 先停热、用合适干燥隔热工具，从侧面把蒸汽导离身体；铃声不是冷却信号。
- **盖上水珠流向手：** 先稳住盖并让凝水按器具说明滴回锅里或耐热位置，别横跨身体举盖。
- **篮具装满提不动：** 放稳，使用夹具小批取食或现场协助；下次在冷态减小批量。
- **食物表面冒汽，中心不确定：** 按食物特定完成条件复查；需要温度证据时不要跳过。
- **电蒸锅进入保温：** 按该型号操作真正停止，别把“蒸制计时结束”当底座无热。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通非压力蒸具的收尾，具体开盖、断电、提篮和冷却方式以设备说明为准。不同食物的熟度和储存条件不同。视觉、听觉或握力受限时使用可访问计时器、清晰标记、稳固提手或可信的现场协助；无法获得安全落点就先停热等待，不匆忙端锅。现实副作用是盖子和锅水还要冷却一会儿，而你的手仍属于你。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary steamer reaches the recipe's check or removal point but hot steam remains under the lid and around the basket. **Stop heat safely, direct steam away from your body, confirm the food's endpoint, and move it to a nearby heat-safe place.** A finished timer does not certify safe doneness. Burn treatment, fire response, and equipment repair are outside this procedure.

### Preparation and inputs

Know the exact food's recipe endpoint and any applicable safe temperature. Have a cleaned thermometer if needed. Set a stable heat-safe dish or surface nearby. Use dry heat protection, tongs, or the basket handle suited to this appliance. Read the exact steamer manual; an electric model may enter a warming mode when its timer ends. A silent bell or unlit light does not prove the base and steam are cool. If grip, standing, sight, or hearing will not allow a safe opening, stop heat and arrange in-person help first.

### Execution steps

1. **Stop heating under the manual.** Turn off the hob or electric steamer when safely reachable, and check for an automatic warming mode. Whether food should leave immediately or rest briefly follows the exact recipe. Do not leave a hot loaded basket on an unstable surface.
2. **Open from outside the steam path.** Stand to a workable side and hold the lid with dry heat protection. Keep the near side shielding you while directing escaping steam away from face and hands. Under the equipment directions, let hot condensation drip back into the steamer or onto a heat-safe surface rather than onto arms, floor, or electrical parts. Steam still exists when it is hard to see.
3. **Let the strongest plume pass before removal.** Do not reach across a steaming basket. Lift it slowly only within its rated load and your real grip capacity, let water drip into the pot, and move it the short distance to the heat-safe landing place. If it is too heavy, settle it and remove food in small portions with suitable tools; do not carry the entire steamer across the kitchen.
4. **Check the food's own evidence.** Sample an appropriate part of the batch under its recipe's texture, time, and food-safety conditions. Use the [doneness check](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md) when safe core temperature matters; steam under a lid is not a centre reading. Continue only if equipment, water, and reliable directions still support it. If time and temperature history are unknown, do not claim it is ready to eat.
5. **Plan the hot equipment's remainder.** Leave water and basket to cool under the actual manual before emptying or cleaning, and tell others they are hot. Food storage and leftovers belong to their applicable process; do not leave perishable food indefinitely on a counter.

### Success and stop

The food meets its recipe and safety condition, heat is off, lid and basket rest safely, and water has a cooling path. Stop everyday handling if the lid cannot be held, condensation would land on a person or electrical part, the basket is too heavy, food is not done and safe continuation is impossible, or smoke, fire, or gas odour appears. Get appropriate help.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The timer sounds, so you lift the lid bare-handed:** Stop heat, use suitable dry protection, and direct steam away. A bell is not a cooling signal.
- **Condensation runs toward your hand:** Stabilise the lid and let droplets go back into the pot or onto a heat-safe place under the manual, not across your body.
- **A full basket is too heavy:** Settle it and remove smaller portions with tongs or in-person help. Use a smaller cold-loaded batch next time.
- **The outside steams but the centre is unknown:** Check the food-specific endpoint and any required core temperature.
- **An electric steamer switched to warm:** Use that model's real off control; “steaming timer ended” need not mean “base is cold.”

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This ends an ordinary non-pressure steam task. Lid, unplugging, basket removal, and cooling follow the exact equipment manual. Food types also differ in doneness and storage. For visual, hearing, or grip limits, use accessible timers, clear marks, stable handles, or trusted in-person help. If no safe landing place exists, stop heat and wait instead of rushing with a hot pot. The lid and water will still need time to cool; your hands remain your own.
