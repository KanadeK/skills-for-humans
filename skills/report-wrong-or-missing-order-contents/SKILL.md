## 中文

你收到**确实寄给自己的普通订单包裹**，但打开后物品型号、数量或配件与该包裹的发货明细不符时，用这份 Skill 先排除分箱发货或正常替代，再向负责订单的商家说清差异与希望的处理。目标是对方受理并给出可追踪的下一步；不预设必然退款或补发。若整件包裹外标签属于他人，先用误投包裹 Skill；若封口破损、受潮或内件疑因运输损坏，先保留现场并用损伤记录 Skill。

### 准备与输入

找到订单号、购买商品/数量、该包裹的发货明细、追踪号与收货日期。把实际收到的商品、型号/尺寸/颜色、数量与配件放在一处，留着外箱、填充、标签和发货单。只拍说明差异所需的物品与外箱信息；遮住家庭地址、完整付款信息和无关人脸。若物品明显有危险破损、漏液、电池鼓胀或未知物，不继续翻找，隔离并联系服务方；本文不处理危险现场。

### 执行

1. **先核对“这箱本来该有什么”。** 在订单官方页面看是否被拆成多个包裹、另一个追踪号是否仍在路上、是否已批准替代或缺货取消。不要把整个订单清单等同于眼前一箱的装箱单。对照同名但型号/规格不同的商品，记录准确差异。
2. **列一张事实清单。** 写明订单/这一包裹编号、应有与实有的商品及数量、收货时间，以及“未收到”“收到不对的型号”“缺一项配件”等事实。照片限于能看清商品标识、数量、包装状态的几张；别为证据摆拍拆坏物品或泄露住址。
3. **走负责订单的官方渠道。** 在商家订单页的“缺少/错误商品”等入口提交，或使用商家公开客服；第三方卖家按平台提供的卖家联系规则。用一句具体请求说明你希望补发正确商品、补齐缺少件、得到退换说明或其他该商家允许的处理。不要自行把错件寄到聊天里出现的私人地址，也别先丢弃错件和包装。
4. **记录回复和下一步。** 保存受理编号、对方实际答复、是否要退回错件、谁给标签、时限和后续跟进时间。若查出另一个包裹仍在途，按其追踪重新判断，不重复报“少件”。若对方要求更多照片，只提供必要且不泄露无关个人信息的证据。

### 成功条件

差异经过分箱/替代/取消核对后仍成立，商家正式渠道已收到清楚事实并给出编号或明确处理路径；或你发现所缺件另有在途包裹并能追踪到它。是否补发或退款由真实政策和答复决定，提交不等于获准。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看总订单就报少件：** 查每个包裹的发货明细和追踪，再决定这一箱是否短少。
- **相似型号当成正确商品：** 对照订单中的型号、规格与商品标签，保留不一致的事实照片。
- **急着用掉或退走错件：** 先留在原状态并等商家正式处置说明；退货按商家认可的地址和标签办理。
- **客服要完整身份证或银行卡照片：** 核对是否真为正式渠道且为该问题必要；无关敏感资料不发，改用官方支持询问替代核验。
- **外包装有明显破损：** 同时记录外观，转损伤证据流程，不仅按仓库配货错误猜测原因。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

此 Skill 是普通消费订单的事实核对与服务请求，不判断法律权利、欺诈、退款资格或高额争议。各商家对分箱、替代、退回和补发的政策不同；Walmart、Target 只是当前官方流程的实例。无法读细小型号可放大或请可信的人协助，个人资料尽量留在自己设备和官方渠道。包装和错件会临时占地方，服务方回复可能需要等待。

## English

Use this Skill when a parcel **addressed to you for an ordinary order** contains a wrong model, quantity, or part compared with that shipment's actual contents list. Rule out split shipping or an approved substitution, then tell the responsible seller what differs and what resolution you want. The result is a traceable response path, not a guaranteed refund or replacement. For a parcel addressed to someone else, use the misdelivery route first. For a broken seal, wet package, or possible transit damage, preserve it and use the damage-record route.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the order number, purchased item and quantity, this parcel's shipment breakdown, tracking reference, and receipt date. Keep the actual items, model/size/colour, quantity, and accessories together with the outer box, fill, label, and packing slip. Photograph only what shows the discrepancy, hiding home address, full payment details, and bystanders. For an unsafe break, leak, swollen battery, or unknown contents, stop searching inside and isolate it; this file does not handle hazards.

### Execution

1. **Check what this box was meant to contain.** On the official order page, look for split parcels, another tracking number still moving, approved substitution, or cancelled shortage. The whole order list is not necessarily this box's packing list. Compare lookalike items by exact model/specification and record the actual difference.
2. **Make a short factual inventory.** Note order and parcel references, expected versus received item/quantity, receipt time, and a precise statement such as “missing one part” or “wrong model.” Use a few necessary photos of identifiers, count, and package state. Do not damage an item for a staged picture or expose your address.
3. **Use the seller's official order channel.** Choose its missing/wrong-item route in the order page or published customer support. Follow the platform's seller-contact route for a marketplace seller. State one desired outcome: the correct item, missing piece, return instructions, or another resolution the seller offers. Do not send an item to a private address found only in chat; keep the wrong item and packing until instructed.
4. **Record the reply and next action.** Keep case number, actual reply, any approved return, who supplies a label, deadline, and follow-up date. If another parcel is still in transit, follow that parcel instead of repeating a shortage report. Share only needed evidence if support asks for more.

### Success

After checking splits, substitutions, and cancellations, a real discrepancy is reported with clear facts through the seller's official channel and has a case or next step. Discovering a separately tracked parcel is also a valid resolution of the apparent shortage. Replacement or refund depends on the actual policy and reply; submission is not approval.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Reporting from the whole-order list:** Check each parcel's shipment detail and tracking before deciding this box is short.
- **A similar model looks correct:** Compare exact model/specification on order and product label and keep a factual photo.
- **Using or returning the wrong item immediately:** Keep it as received and await the seller's official return or disposal direction. Use an approved address and label.
- **Support asks for a full ID or bank-card picture:** Verify the channel and necessity; do not send unrelated sensitive data. Ask official support for another verification route.
- **Outer packaging is visibly damaged:** Record its condition and use the damage-evidence route rather than assuming a packing error.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is factual order reconciliation and a service request, not a ruling on legal rights, fraud, refund eligibility, or high-value disputes. Seller rules for split shipments, substitutions, returns, and replacements differ. Walmart and Target pages are examples, not your seller's policy. Magnify small model text or ask trusted help while keeping personal information on your device and in official channels. Packaging and the wrong item may take space while support replies.
