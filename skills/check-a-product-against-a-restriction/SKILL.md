## 中文

当你已有一条明确的普通购买限制，例如“不买带香味的这类用品”或“只接受标明某种材质的版本”，却面对正面宣传和背面小字时，加载这份 Skill。它负责付款前核对这一条限制，不帮你制定医疗饮食、严重过敏或危险物质的安全方案。

### 准备与输入

先把限制写成能判定的一句话：要排除什么、什么表述算通过、哪些未知不能接受。确认你拿的是准确品牌、型号、口味或香型、包装版本；同系列照片不是这件商品的证据。准备读包装背面、吊牌或制造商对应版本说明。看不清时可拍照放大、使用屏幕朗读，或请现场人员逐字读出相关行；口头概括不能代替关键原文。

### 执行

1. **找信息来源。** 先看完整成分或材质表、适用范围、警示与限制，再看正面的“无某物”宣传。醒目的圆章不自动覆盖小字。
2. **对准对象。** 核对声明讲的是商品、包装、某一部件，还是某个旧版；把限定词如“部分”“添加”“外层”一起读完。
3. **逐项比对。** 出现明确冲突就判为“不买”。文字明确满足你的要求，且没有相反警示，才记为“这一条通过”；这不等于商品整体安全或适合所有用途。
4. **处理空白和矛盾。** 信息缺失、列表不完整、网络页面与实物不一致，或限制所需的条件没有写明时，记为“未确认”。向制造商或店员索取准确版本的书面资料；无法核实就换信息更完整的商品或暂缓购买。
5. **留下简短依据。** 若这条限制很重要，记下准确版本、看到的原文、来源和查看日期，避免下次把相似包装误认成同一个结果。

### 完成与停止

你能给这件准确商品写出“这条限制通过 / 不通过 / 未确认”以及对应原文。付款只在你接受所有必要限制的结果后进行；“未确认”不靠猜测自动变成通过。严重过敏、医疗限制或危险化学品选择应停在既有专业方案与产品正式说明，不使用本 Skill 自行宣布安全。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看正面：** 翻到成分、材质和警示；如果包装遮住关键行，先索取完整信息。
- **把未列出当成不存在：** 确认列表是否完整、对应哪个部件；仍不清楚就保留“未确认”。
- **商品换了版本：** 重新核对当前实物；旧截图的结论不继承到新配方或新包装。
- **现场无法读取：** 拍清晰照片回家核对或改选标签可访问的商品；不用在货架前即时猜答案。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设你已经知道自己的普通偏好或采购规则；语言、标签格式和要求披露的信息因地区与品类而异。店员可以帮助找到信息，但其即兴保证与准确版本的正式说明不是同一种证据。你可能少买一件商品，购物袋的空位不会提交申诉。

## English

Load this Skill when you already have one ordinary buying restriction, such as “no scented version of this household item” or “only a version that states this material,” and the front claim competes with small print. It checks that restriction before payment. It does not create a medical diet, a severe-allergy plan, or a hazardous-substance safety rule.

### Preparation and inputs

Write a testable sentence first: what you exclude, what wording would satisfy you, and which unknowns are unacceptable. Confirm the exact brand, model, flavour or scent, and package version in your hand. A photo of a related product is not evidence for this one. Get the full package, tag, or manufacturer's information for that version. If print is inaccessible, enlarge a photo, use a screen reader, or have someone read the relevant lines verbatim; a spoken summary is insufficient for a critical restriction.

### Execution

1. **Find the governing text.** Read the complete ingredients or materials, intended scope, warnings, and restrictions before trusting a large front-panel claim. A confident badge does not erase the small print.
2. **Match the object.** Check whether a statement describes the product, its packaging, one component, or an earlier version. Keep qualifiers such as “partly,” “added,” and “outer layer.”
3. **Compare line by line.** An explicit conflict means reject. Clear text meeting your rule, with no contrary warning, means only that this one restriction passes; it does not certify the whole product as safe or suitable.
4. **Handle gaps and conflicts.** Missing information, an incomplete list, a web page that disagrees with the item, or an unstated condition means unconfirmed. Request written information for the exact version from the maker or seller. If you cannot verify it, choose a better-documented item or wait.
5. **Keep a short trace.** If the restriction matters later, note the exact version, relevant wording, source, and date checked so a similar package does not inherit the result.

### Success and stop

You can mark the exact item “passes this restriction / fails / unconfirmed” and point to the wording. Pay only after every necessary restriction has an outcome you accept. Unconfirmed never silently becomes passes. For severe allergy, medical limits, or hazardous chemical choices, stop at your established professional plan and official product information; do not use this Skill to declare safety.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Reading only the front:** Inspect ingredients, materials, and warnings. Request the full information if packaging hides the relevant line.
- **Treating absent wording as proof of absence:** Confirm whether the list is complete and which component it covers. Otherwise keep unconfirmed.
- **The version changed:** Recheck the physical item. An old screenshot does not approve a new formula or package.
- **You cannot read it in store:** Take a clear photo for later review or choose an accessible label instead of guessing at the shelf.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes you already know your ordinary preference or buying rule. Languages, label forms, and required disclosures differ by region and category. Staff can help locate information, but an improvised assurance is different from an exact-version statement. You may buy one fewer item; the free space in your bag will cope.
