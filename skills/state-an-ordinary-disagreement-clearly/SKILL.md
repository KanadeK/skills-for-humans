## 中文

当别人就普通安排、偏好或低风险事实说出看法，而你确实不同意，却不想靠沉默假装赞同或把谈话变成输赢时加载。目标是**说清具体哪一点不同、你的理由或不确定性，并给对方回应或停在不同意见的空间**。对方同意不是成功条件；这也不处理威胁、骚扰、权力胁迫或需要专业裁决的医疗、法律、财务问题。

### 准备与输入

先复述在脑中听到的对方原话，确定你不同意的是哪一项，而不是给对方整个人贴标签。分清这是事实判断、个人喜好还是共同决定：事实若可核，需要来源和不确定性；偏好可以不同而不需要辩证对方错。想好你此次只是表达、希望协商一个小决定，还是须由明确负责人决定。选双方可用的时间/渠道，不在对方无法回应的公开场合突然逼其表态。

### 执行

1. **对准一项真实差异。** 可以先说“我听到你更倾向__，对吗？我在__这点想法不同。” 若你没理解，先请求澄清，不先反驳一个自己想象的版本。别用“你总是”或揣测动机扩大范围。
2. **用自己的话说明看法。** 说“我更倾向__，因为__”或“我查到的资料是__，但还要核对__”。让理由与这件事有关，避免一次堆满多年前的旧账或未经核实的事实。既不必先道歉自己的存在，也不把个人口味说成唯一真理。
3. **留出普通回应空间。** 问“你是怎么看这点的？”或“我们要不要先核这个事实？”；对方解释时听其实际回答，不把沉默、点头或礼貌结束算成同意。你也可以说暂时不用继续讨论。
4. **按决策归属收口。** 不必共同决定时可停在“我们看法不同”；若要一起做一件事，确认是否有都能接受的小测试/替代方案、谁去核资料、何时再决定。若决定权明确在对方或场所，表达你的信息和边界后尊重其合法普通选择；这不是让你承诺不愿做的事。
5. **停止无增量的重复。** 同一点各自已说清、没有新事实或对方不想谈，就记“未达成一致/暂不决定/按__方案执行”中的真实状态，结束这轮。需要拒绝具体请求或设个人界限时转对应流程，不继续用分歧话术绕圈。

### 完成与停止

你准确表达一个与对方真实观点不同的看法，说明支持它的普通理由或未知，并知道下一步是核实、协商、由负责人决定还是保留不同意见，本轮完成。**成功不需要对方被说服、道歉或改变人格。** 一旦出现威胁、操控、骚扰或高度专业/高风险争议，退出这份日常对话流程，选择合适支持或正式渠道。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只说“你错了”：** 指出哪项事实或选择与你不同，再说你自己的依据/偏好。
- **把对方意思猜错：** 先复述具体句子请其确认，修正目标后再表达不同意。
- **一口气给十条理由：** 选最相关的一两条；对方愿意再深入才补资料。
- **对方说“随便”就当同意：** 询问是否真的要共同决定，或明确暂不定案，不替其写赞成票。
- **表达后仍不停重复：** 若没有新信息就停下，保留不同意见或约实际复查点。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于自愿、低风险的普通分歧，不是心理治疗、冲突危机处置、谈判操控或法律/医疗/财务裁定。关系、文化、语言和表达习惯不同，没有必须保持眼神接触或必须接受某套固定句式。听觉、视觉、语言或处理速度不适合即时口头谈话时，可用简短文字或稍后再说，并给双方阅读/回复空间。副作用是你们可能仍不同意，但彼此不必猜测对方是否假装赞同。

## English

Load this Skill when someone states an ordinary plan, preference or low-risk factual view and you genuinely disagree, but do not want to pretend through silence or turn the conversation into winning. Your result is to **identify the exact difference, give your reason or uncertainty, and leave room for a reply or for views to remain different**. Their agreement is not success. This does not handle threats, harassment, coercion or specialist medical, legal or financial disputes.

### Preparation and inputs

Recall what they actually said and identify which point differs from your view instead of labelling the whole person. Distinguish a factual claim, a personal preference and a shared decision. A checkable fact may need a source and uncertainty; different preferences need no proof that the other person is wrong. Decide whether you only need to state your view, negotiate one small choice or leave a decision to an agreed owner. Choose a usable time or channel rather than forcing a public response they cannot comfortably give.

### Execution

1. **Target one real difference.** “I heard that you prefer __. Is that right? I see __ differently.” If you did not understand, clarify before disagreeing with an imagined version. Avoid “you always” or guessing motives.
2. **State your view in your own words.** “I prefer __ because __,” or “The information I found says __, though I still need to check __.” Keep reasons tied to this matter rather than unloading old grievances or unchecked facts. You need not apologise for existing, and a taste is not a universal truth.
3. **Leave ordinary room to respond.** Ask “How do you see that part?” or “Should we check that fact first?” Listen to the real answer rather than treating silence, a nod or a polite exit as consent. Either of you may decide not to continue now.
4. **Close according to who decides.** If no joint action is needed, “we see it differently” may be enough. For a shared action, see whether a small trial or alternative works for both, who checks information and when to decide. If the ordinary decision belongs to a named person or venue, share your view and boundaries, then respect their actual choice. This does not require agreeing to do what you will not do.
5. **Stop repeating without new input.** When both views are clear and no new fact appears or the other person wants to stop, record the actual status: “not agreed,” “decision pending,” or “following __ plan.” For declining a concrete request or setting a personal limit, use the separate boundary task rather than cycling through disagreement wording.

### Success and stop

You accurately expressed one difference from their real view, gave a relevant ordinary reason or unknown, and know whether the next step is checking, negotiating, a named decision maker or simply remaining different. **Success needs no conversion, apology or personality change from them.** For threats, manipulation, harassment or high-stakes specialist disputes, leave this ordinary conversation and use appropriate support or formal channels.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “you're wrong” is said:** Name the actual fact or choice you differ on and your own basis or preference.
- **You guessed their position:** Read back the specific statement for confirmation and correct the target before disagreeing.
- **Ten reasons arrive at once:** Use one or two relevant ones, then add sources only if they want to go deeper.
- **“Whatever” is treated as agreement:** Ask whether a joint decision is still needed or leave it pending, not a vote you cast for them.
- **The point is repeated without new information:** Stop, keep the difference or set a real review point.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is voluntary low-risk disagreement, not therapy, crisis response, manipulative negotiation or a legal, medical or financial ruling. Relationship, culture, language and expression vary. There is no universal eye-contact requirement or magic phrase. If hearing, sight, language or processing pace makes an immediate talk hard, use brief text or return later and give each person room to read and respond. You may still disagree, but neither person has to guess whether the other is pretending.

### Sources

- Original synthesis. Target one real difference, state your reason, permit reply and close under the actual decision owner; persuasion or one cultural script is not the result.
