## 中文

当你已经入住实际房间，发现这次明确包含的普通设施/用品不在、不能正常使用，或一项日常必需条件与确认信息不符时加载。目标是**核对承诺与眼前事实，向真正负责的住宿方提出一项具体、可跟进的请求**。出发前设施确认、办理入住、退房交接各有独立流程；这份 Skill 不自行拆修电器、水路或燃气，也不推断退款/赔偿权利。

### 准备与输入

找到本次预订确认、具体房型/房源说明、可用的设施列表及住宿方正式前台、房东或平台消息渠道。区分“本房型明确列出”“品牌或图片只作示例”“我希望额外提供”三种情况；同品牌各房间可能不同。记房号/入住日期等必要识别信息，在公开评论或群聊不贴完整预订、证件、付款资料。若设施涉及明显危险、漏水、电气异常或安全问题，先停止使用并及时按场所正式安全流程报告，不做试修。

### 执行

1. **对一项问题做非侵入式核对。** 比较预订原文与实际位置、提供数量或可见状态；能按客人正常使用方式确认的才确认。找不到吹风机与设备有电气异响不是同一种事实，别因“不工作”拆开插座或机器。只拍能说明差异的必要照片，避开他人、个人文件和无关房间画面。
2. **决定可接受的现实下一步。** 例如请住宿方说明设施实际位置、补送缺的普通用品、安排其人员查看，或提供可用的替代；优先说本次入住需要什么，而不先要求一个未经核实的费用结果。若该设施对你的通行或基本住宿确实不可缺，明确告诉对方使用条件与时间需要，不靠身份标签推断。
3. **通过正式渠道发一条短请求。** 模板：“我在__号房/本次预订，确认信息写__；现在看到/试到__；我需要__。请问谁可以在何时处理，若不能处理有什么本次可用方案？” 留下发送时点和真实答复。酒店可先问前台；短租可按实际平台/房东渠道联系，具体入口随服务不同。
4. **核对答复的可观察部分。** 对方给出位置、补物、人员上门或换用方案时，记负责人、时间、进入房间是否需你在场以及实际是否发生；“会处理”没有动作或时点时再问一次具体信息。尚未发生就写待办，不把客服回复当设施已经可用。
5. **没有解决时有界收口。** 保存原承诺、现状与沟通记录；按本服务正式渠道问下一负责人或可接受的替代，并决定当前能否继续住/暂时停用该设施。对费用、预订变更或权益有争议时另走平台/住宿方正式及合格渠道，不在这里裁定。

### 完成与停止

你有一项准确事实、一项向负责方发出的具体请求和实际结果：设施/用品可用了，或明确谁继续处理、何时复查、什么仍未知，本轮完成。**发出消息不等于问题解决，也不保证得到指定替代。** 明显危险设施立即停止使用并报告；对方要求你自己维修或用不安全方式继续使用时拒绝该动作，转正式人员。

### 常见报错与补救

- **品牌网站说有，房型确认没有：** 以这次预订和房源细节核对，向住宿方问是否可提供，不把通用品牌宣传写成承诺。
- **用品可能在别处：** 问实际存放位置，别翻动他人或员工区域。
- **设备不能用但原因不明：** 只描述客人正常操作后的可见结果，停止反复试错，不拆修。
- **对方说“稍后”但无下一动作：** 问谁处理、预计何时以及若不能处理的替代，再记真实答复。
- **房东或前台无人回复：** 保留已发送内容和时间，按预订服务的正式帮助入口继续；不向陌生号码发送个人资料。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通住宿的非紧急设施/缺物沟通，不含租赁法律、赔偿、医疗、电气、燃气或水路维修建议。住宿类型、可用渠道、房型、服务时段与是否有人上门各不同，按本次实际说明为准。行动、视觉、听觉或语言不便时可用正式文字渠道、前台可用协助或可信授权同行者记录你的具体使用需要，不交出账号控制权。副作用是有一条诚实的待办记录，直到现实物品或服务真正出现。

## English

Load this Skill after entering your actual room when an ordinary amenity or supply explicitly included for this stay is missing, unusable in normal use, or a necessary everyday condition differs from the confirmation. Your result is to **compare the real promise with the observed state and make one specific, trackable request to the responsible lodging provider**. Checking amenities before travel, check-in and checkout have their own procedures. Do not dismantle electrical, plumbing or gas equipment or infer a refund or compensation right here.

### Preparation and inputs

Find this booking confirmation, room or listing description, included facilities and the property's official front desk, host or platform message channel. Distinguish “listed for this room,” “brand or photograph only suggests it,” and “I would like an extra.” Rooms under one brand can differ. Keep necessary booking and room identifiers private; do not post full reservation, ID or payment details publicly. For obvious danger, leaks, abnormal electrical behaviour or another safety issue, stop using the facility and report through the venue's real safety route rather than trying a repair.

### Execution

1. **Check one issue without invading or repairing.** Compare the booking wording with actual place, quantity or visible state. Confirm only what normal guest use permits. A hair dryer not found and an appliance making an electrical noise are different facts; do not open an outlet or machine to investigate. Take only necessary evidence photos without other guests, personal papers or unrelated room scenes.
2. **Choose a practical acceptable next step.** Ask where the amenity is, for a missing ordinary item to be supplied, for venue staff to inspect it, or for an available alternative. State what you need during this stay before demanding an unverified monetary outcome. If a condition is essential to your access or basic stay, describe the specific requirement and timing rather than expecting a label to explain it.
3. **Send one short request through the real channel.** Template: “I am in room __ on this booking; the confirmation says __; I now see or experience __; I need __. Who can handle this and when, or what usable option is available if it cannot be fixed?” Keep the sent time and actual reply. A hotel front desk or a short-stay host or platform may have different official routes.
4. **Check what the reply actually changes.** For a location, delivery, staff visit or alternative, note owner, time, whether you need to be present, and whether it happened. “We will handle it” without action or timing deserves one precise question. Until it happens, mark pending rather than saying the room works.
5. **Close this round with a boundary if still unresolved.** Keep the original promise, observed state and contacts. Ask the service's official next owner or available substitute and decide whether you can continue this stay while leaving the amenity unused. Take money, booking changes or rights disputes to the provider or platform's actual qualified process, not to this Skill for a verdict.

### Success and stop

You have one accurate fact, one specific request to the responsible provider and a real result: the item works or is present, or you know who owns the next action, when to check and what remains unknown. **Sending a message is not resolution or a guarantee of your preferred substitute.** Stop using an obviously dangerous facility and report it. Decline instructions to repair it yourself or keep using it unsafely and ask for formal staff handling.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The brand site lists an amenity but this room confirmation does not:** Check the actual booking or listing and ask whether it can be supplied, without presenting generic branding as a promise.
- **An item may be stored elsewhere:** Ask its real location rather than searching staff or another guest's area.
- **A device does not work but why is unclear:** Describe only what normal guest use showed; stop repeated experiments and do not repair it.
- **Staff say “later” with no action:** Ask who, when and what substitute exists if they cannot handle it, then record the answer.
- **Host or desk does not reply:** Keep what you sent and when, then use the booking service's official help route, not an unfamiliar number asking for personal data.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for an ordinary non-emergency lodging amenity or missing supply, not tenancy law, compensation, medical advice or electrical, gas or plumbing repair. Lodging type, channel, room, service hours and visits vary; use the actual booking. For mobility, sight, hearing or language needs, use the real written channel, front-desk assistance or a trusted authorised companion to record your specific requirement without giving over account control. A pending record remains honest until the item or service appears.
