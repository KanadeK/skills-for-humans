## 中文

你要在车站、图书馆或其他普通公开场所把一件物品**短时放到正式寄存处或自助柜，并稍后取回**时，用这份 Skill在交出物品前核对可寄物品、位置、费用、开放与取回条件。目标是物品获服务方接收、你有真实可用的凭证和取回计划，最后按规则取回同一件；不保证所有“行李寄存”都接收大箱、贵重物或需要跨天保管。

### 准备与输入

从场所管理方官网/现场牌/正式服务台确认服务确实由谁运营、在哪、今天何时关闭、是否要预约、价格/超时费、可存大小/类型及到期处理。普通柜与有人值守衣帽间可能有完全不同的开门次数和取回方式；例如有的柜一次开启就结束租用，不能当随取随放。先从自己物品中取出马上要用、不能按该服务规则寄存或不愿离手的证件、付款工具、药品等；是否接受具体物品以该场所规则为准，不能靠“箱子放得进”推断允许。

### 执行

1. **先算能否按时取回。** 对照自己的离开时间、场所关闭时间、柜机服务时间和所需移动时间；问清超时、夜间、丢票/失码、设备故障时的正式处理渠道。不能在开放时段回来，就先选明确支持长时间保管的服务或不寄。
2. **核对物品范围与实际费用。** 查该服务允许的大小、重量、物品种类、是否需由你上锁/封合、是否有人值守，以及何时付费。大型行李、贵重物、电池/危险品或易腐物不可凭猜测放进去；规则不明时向工作人员问或改用其他服务。不要向陌生二维码付款或把密码交路人代办。
3. **办理一次清楚的交接。** 按本柜/衣帽间的现场指示放入或交给正式人员，确认门已按说明锁好、物品/件数被接收，取得票据、钥匙、二维码或柜号等本服务实际凭证。记录寄存点位置、服务商、取回截止与费用；凭证自己保管，不把可开柜二维码公开发给朋友或社交群。
4. **按原方式取回并验件。** 在可办理时间返回同一运营点，按正式凭证/身份要求开柜或交还票据。核对取回的是自己的件数与物品；门不开、凭证丢失或东西不符时，先在现场找运营方正式人员，保留受理编号与原凭证，不撬锁或拿相似物。需补费时按事先核实的渠道处理。

### 成功条件

一件符合本服务规则的物品已按正式流程暂存，你知道取回地点、时限、凭证和异常求助入口，并在计划时段取回相同物品；或者寄前发现范围/时间/费用不合而改用有依据的选择。只把物品关进柜里却不知凭证如何取回，不算安全完成交接。

### 常见报错与补救

- **柜子够大但场馆不收大行李：** 先看场所物品政策与独立寄存服务要求，不能只看物理尺寸。
- **衣帽间今天早关门：** 在寄前核当天开放时段；已寄则及时联系场所正式人员，不指望普通入口开放就能取件。
- **票据/二维码遗失：** 按运营方官方丢失凭证流程，准备其要求的身份与寄存事实，不试开多柜。
- **一次性开柜后还想重新放回：** 先查本柜是否支持多次开启；不能就重新办理合法服务，别把门虚掩。
- **取回件数不对或柜门故障：** 在现场停止离开并找运营方登记事实；不凭自己猜测收费或赔偿。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里处理普通短时储物，不提供保管责任、赔偿、失窃调查或危险品指导。英国图书馆、荷兰 NS 与 Rijksmuseum 的物品范围、票据、费用和开放时间都不同，不能互相套用。没有手机可选该服务明确支持的纸票/人工方式；行动或读码困难时问正式服务点的可及方案。寄存会让物品暂时离手，可信的取回计划比一时少拎一个包更重要。

## English

Use this Skill to leave one ordinary item **temporarily with an official cloakroom or self-service locker and reclaim it later** at a station, library, or other public venue. Before handover, check accepted item type, site, price, opening hours, and retrieval conditions. Finish with the service accepting the item, a real usable claim credential and collection plan, then the same item reclaimed under its rules. “Luggage storage” does not always accept large cases, valuables, or overnight use.

### Preparation and inputs

From the venue manager's site, on-site sign, or formal desk identify who runs the service, where it is, today's closing time, booking need, price/overtime fee, allowed size/type, and expiry treatment. A staffed cloakroom and locker can differ in how often they may be opened or how contents are reclaimed. Some lockers end a rental at first opening, so do not assume repeated access. Remove documents, payment methods, medicines, or other things you need soon or cannot leave under that service's rules. Physical fit is not proof of permission for an item.

### Execution

1. **Check you can return in time.** Compare your own departure, venue closing, locker hours, and movement time. Ask about overtime, overnight storage, lost ticket/code, and official fault support. If you cannot return during access hours, choose a service that clearly supports the longer period or keep the item.
2. **Check accepted contents and real price.** Read size, weight, item-type, lock/seal and staffing conditions, and when payment occurs. Do not guess that a large case, valuable, battery/dangerous, or perishable item is accepted. Ask staff or choose another provider when unclear. Do not pay through a suspicious QR or give a code to a stranger for help.
3. **Make an identifiable handover.** Follow this locker/cloakroom's instructions, confirm the door is locked or formal staff accepted the piece/count, and obtain the ticket, key, QR, or locker number that this service actually uses. Keep the provider/site, retrieval deadline, and price with your record. Protect the credential; a public QR photo could open the locker for someone else.
4. **Reclaim and compare.** Return during the service window and use its credential or ID method at the same operator point. Check the piece and count. For a door fault, missing ticket, or wrong item, stay on site and ask the operator's formal staff to record it, keeping the case reference and original credential. Do not pry a lock or take a lookalike. Pay any legitimate additional fee through the verified channel.

### Success

One permitted item went through the formal storage process, you know the pickup place/time, credential, and fault channel, and you reclaimed the same piece within the plan. Discovering a size, time, or fee mismatch before deposit and choosing a documented alternative also counts. Closing a locker without knowing how to reclaim its contents is incomplete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Case fits but the venue rejects large luggage:** Read the item policy and separate provider rules, not physical dimensions alone.
- **Cloakroom closes earlier today:** Check today's hours before deposit. If already stored, promptly contact formal staff; an open building entrance is not proof collection is open.
- **Ticket or QR is lost:** Follow the operator's official lost-credential process with the required ID and deposit facts, not trial openings of many lockers.
- **A one-open locker is opened and you want to put items back:** Check whether multiple openings are supported; otherwise start a new permitted rental rather than leaving the door ajar.
- **Count differs or door fails at collection:** Record facts with the operator before leaving; do not assume a fee or compensation outcome.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary short storage, not liability, compensation, theft investigation, or dangerous-goods guidance. British Library, NS, and Rijksmuseum have different accepted items, receipts, fees, and hours. Without a phone, use a paper ticket or staffed option if this service supports one. Ask its official accessibility route for movement or code-reading needs. Storage puts the item out of your reach; a reliable collection plan matters more than a few hands-free hours.
