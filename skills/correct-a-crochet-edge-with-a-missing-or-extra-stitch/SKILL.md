## 中文

当你的**平排美式短针小样**新近出现一侧越钩越窄或越钩越宽，想在继续加行前找出首个漏针/多针处时，加载这份 Skill。结果是准确找到受影响行并退回重钩，或承认样片太复杂需新开。它不是靠拉伸把斜边拽平，也不适用于有故意加减针的图样。

### 准备与输入

取起始目标针数及上次正确行数，停止手上的新行，用夹子固定钩上活圈并在亮处摊平样片。美式短针行的转向锁针通常不算一枚短针；先查本图样实际定义，不能把美式规则套到另一术语体系。复杂镂空、圆周或断线需对应方法。

### 执行

1. **从最近行向下数。** 每行从第一枚短针到末枚逐个数，标哪一行首次与目标不同；只看外观倾斜不能证明哪针出错。对照两侧边柱，查看转面时是否跳过第一枚或漏掉最后一枚。
2. **区分变窄与变宽。** 少针常因行首/行末漏钩或中段跳针；多针可能因把转向锁针算进去或同一孔钩两次。找到具体位置后记录“第几行、哪边、少/多几针”，不是泛称“我手不稳”。
3. **只退受影响的短行。** 保留活圈控制，缓慢拆回第一次错误前一行，再按原短针法重做：转向锁针只是抬高，第一枚短针钩入上一行第一枚针顶两股线，末针也要钩到。每重做一行就数，不能一次拆到线团乱成结。
4. **复核再续钩。** 修后的几行针数一致、两侧边缘不再继续漂移，再记录问题原因与下一行方向。若无法分辨首个错误或纱线已毛裂，做小样重启/请可信熟手，不造一个隐藏加针来凑数。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

第一处错行与漏/多针原因可指出，短行被重做且行针数复归；或确认需要重启。只在下一行故意多/少钩一针让外观暂平，不算定位根因。

- **首末针看不清：** 用可移针目标两边，放大光线再数，不靠手感猜。
- **拆太快活圈滑走：** 先夹住当前圈与近处线，放慢一次拆一行。
- **转向锁针被算进普通短针：** 回到该行并按本图样规则重钩。
- **本来图样就有加减针：** 停止通用纠偏，按原图样核目标行数。
- **视力/手部不便：** 粗浅色线、逐行照片与可信协助计数，由你判定何处退回。

### 假设与边界

只修近期普通短针矩形，没有装饰加减针。错行越久、纱线越毛，拆回的成本越高；允许诚实重做小样，而非保证无痕。

## English

Load this Skill when a **plain US single-crochet rectangle** has recently begun narrowing or widening at an edge and you want the first missing or extra stitch before adding more rows. Finish by finding and reworking the affected short rows, or honestly restarting when too complex. Do not stretch a sloping edge into a supposed fix. Intended pattern increases and decreases are outside this general correction.

### Preparation and inputs

Have the original target stitch count and last known sound row. Stop the new row, secure the live hook loop with a clip, and spread the sample in good light. A US single-crochet turning chain usually does not count as a single crochet; verify this pattern's terms and do not impose US terminology elsewhere. Openwork, rounds, and broken yarn need their own method.

### Execution

1. **Count backward from recent rows.** Count each row from first through final actual single crochet and mark the first row differing from the target. A slant alone does not locate the error. Compare both edge columns for a skipped first or last stitch after turning.
2. **Distinguish narrow from wide.** Missing stitches often come from an edge skip or an unworked inner stitch; extra stitches may come from counting the turning chain or doubling a hole. Record row, edge, and number missing or extra instead of blaming vague hand tension.
3. **Undo only affected short rows.** Control the live loop and unravel slowly to before the first wrong row. Rework using the original single-crochet route: turning chain raises height, the first stitch enters the previous row's first stitch under top loops, and the last must be worked too. Count after each remade row rather than ripping a tangle back to the ball.
4. **Check before more rows.** With consistent stitch counts and edges no longer drifting, note the cause and next row direction. If the first error cannot be found or yarn is frayed, restart this small sample or seek trusted technique help instead of hiding a compensating stitch.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You can point to the first wrong row and missing/extra cause, and reworked rows restore the count, or clearly choose a restart. Inserting an opposite error later to make an edge look straight is not root-cause correction.

- **First or last stitch is hard to see:** Mark both edges removably and count under magnified light instead of guessing.
- **Live loop slips during undo:** Catch it and nearby yarn, then unravel one row at a time.
- **Turning chain was counted as a stitch:** Return to that row and follow the actual pattern's count rule.
- **The pattern intentionally changes width:** Stop generic correction and use its row target.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Bulky light yarn, row photos, and trusted counting help support your undo point.

### Assumptions and limits

This is for recent plain single-crochet rectangles with no decorative shaping. Older errors and fuzzy yarn cost more to undo. An honest restart is valid; no invisible-fix promise is made.
