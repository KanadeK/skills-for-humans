## 中文

你要安排一次普通、自愿的小群体活动，已经知道要做什么，却需要找到**必要参与者都确实能参加**的一段时间时，用这份 Skill。结果是具体时间和每人的实际答复，或明确没有可行交集并决定下一步。它不代替单人邀约、跨时区换算、创建日历邀请或已经成行后的突发恢复。

### 准备与输入

先说清活动约需多久、是否有地点或场馆开放限制、哪些人必须在场，哪些人可以选择参加。不要在没有得到同意时把一个人的空闲日历当作对方答应。挑两三段你自己真的可行且足够长的候选时间；如果时间跨地区，写明每段日期、起止时间和明确时区，必要时先做专门的时区核对。若有人不便公开完整日程，只需要他们对候选段给“可以 / 不行 / 暂不确定”。

### 执行

1. **把问题缩到可回答。** 发给相关人同一版活动、预计时长和两三段具体候选时间，标注必要参与者与实际场馆约束。给一个与真实预订需求相符的回复时点；没有截止就别制造“今天必须回”的压力。允许回答“都不行”和“还不能确定”，不要把沉默当空闲。
2. **按人、按时段记录原答复。** 为每段记录必要参与者的可以/不行/未知，可选参与者另列。只依据他们实际回复，不从“我周末一般有空”推断某个周六可到。若有人只说“下午”，问清该活动需要的起止范围；如果提议受交通或可及条件影响，也请他们说出会改变是否能来的具体限制，不要求公开全部私人原因。
3. **找真正的交集。** 先淘汰任一必要参与者明确不能到的候选，再看其余候选是否足够活动全程和准备/移动时间。若两个都可行，选一个并说明选择理由（例如场馆开放或让更多可选参与者参加）；别用多数票把必要参与者投票出局。
4. **提出最终版本让各人确认。** 发出完整日期、起止时间、地点或线上方式、时区、活动和当前参加名单，请必要参与者确认该版本。有人回答“应该行”仍标暂定；场馆预订或收费未明确同意前不代大家付款。只在各关键人确认同一版本后写“已定”，然后再用普通日历邀请流程发事件。
5. **没有交集就明确结束这一轮。** 说“目前这几个时段没有共同可行时间”，给出真实的下一选项：换候选窗、缩短/改变活动、在所有人同意下调整必要参与者，或暂不安排。不要把无人可行的时间发成邀请让系统替你解决人类日程。

### 成功条件

要么必要参与者对同一具体时间及参与方式做了明确确认，活动时长和场馆条件可行，下一步由谁发邀请或订位清楚；要么没有交集被如实记录，并有下一轮或停止决定。收齐“可用时间”不等于大家同意最终选中的那段；日历邀请已发送也不等于每个人已接受。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只问“大家什么时候有空”：** 收回无边界的问题，给少数真实候选和活动时长。
- **一个必要参加者没回复，却按其他人的票数定了：** 保持该时段暂定，单独询问必要参加者或调整目标与名单，不能替他答应。
- **有人说“下午都行”，活动要两小时：** 问清最早开始与最晚结束，确认整段是否可用。
- **投票结束后才发现地点当天不开：** 核实具体场馆的本次日期/时段，重新筛候选并说明变更；不把已投的时间沿用为新事实。
- **每人都能参加不同的一段：** 明确“无交集”，再讨论改变条件，不把三份单独的“能去”拼成一场集体活动。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

适用于小型、普通、自愿活动；大型会议排期、劳动安排、已开始活动的事故和强迫参加不在本文。可用时间属于个人信息，只收这个活动需要的窗口和限制，不要求公开完整日历。不能用投票工具时，一个简短表格或逐人消息足够；视觉、听觉或文字不便时用各人可访问的渠道读回结果。几个人的自由时间未必有交集；诚实说没有，胜过制造一次预定好的缺席。

## English

Use this Skill when a small voluntary group knows the ordinary activity but still needs **one window its essential attendees can actually use**. Finish with a specific time and everyone's real response, or an honest finding that no workable overlap exists and a next decision. This does not replace a one-person invitation, timezone conversion, calendar invite creation, or recovery after an event is underway.

### Preparation and inputs

State the activity's approximate duration, any venue-hour constraint, who must attend, and who may join optionally. Access to someone's free/busy calendar is not agreement to attend. Choose two or three windows that really work for you and are long enough. For different regions, state date, start and end times, and an explicit time zone for each; use a dedicated timezone check where needed. Someone who does not want to share a full schedule need only answer “yes / no / uncertain” for these options.

### Execution

1. **Make the question answerable.** Send everyone the same activity, duration, and two or three concrete windows, with essential attendees and actual venue limits clear. Give a reply point only when a real booking or planning need calls for one; do not invent a “reply today” emergency. Accept “none work” or “not sure yet.” Silence is unknown, not availability.
2. **Record each person's answer for each window.** Mark essential attendees yes, no, or unknown for every option; list optional people separately. Use their actual response, not “I am usually free on weekends” as a yes for a particular Saturday. If someone says only “afternoon,” ask for the start and end range the activity needs. Ask about transport or access limits that change their attendance without requesting every private reason.
3. **Find real overlap.** Drop any window an essential attendee definitely cannot make. Check that the remainder covers the whole activity plus needed preparation or travel. If more than one works, select one with a stated reason such as venue hours or more optional attendees. Do not use a majority vote to exclude someone defined as essential.
4. **Propose one final version for confirmation.** Send the full calendar date, start and end, place or online mode, time zone, activity, and current attendee list; ask essential people to confirm that version. “Probably” remains tentative. Do not pay for a venue or ticket for others before agreement. Mark it settled only after key people confirm the same version; then use the ordinary calendar-invite task.
5. **If there is no overlap, close this round clearly.** Say that the current options do not share a workable time. Offer a real next choice: new windows, a shorter or changed activity, a changed essential group with everyone's agreement, or no event for now. Do not send an invitation for an impossible window and expect software to solve human schedules.

### Success

Either essential people explicitly confirmed the same specific time and participation mode, with duration and venue conditions workable and a named owner for the invite or booking; or the lack of overlap and the next or stop decision are clear. Collecting availability is not the same as consent to the final selected slot. A sent calendar invitation is not proof each person accepted.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “When is everyone free?” was sent:** Replace the unbounded question with a few real windows and the activity duration.
- **An essential person has not replied, but the others voted:** Keep the option tentative, ask that person, or change the objective and attendee list with consent. Do not answer for them.
- **“Any afternoon” may not cover a two-hour activity:** Ask for earliest start and latest end, then check the full window.
- **The venue is closed on the chosen day:** Verify that date and time with the actual venue, rescreen options, and explain the change. Old votes are not new facts.
- **Each person can do a different slot:** State “no overlap” and discuss a change in conditions, not a group outing assembled from separate individual yeses.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a small ordinary voluntary activity, not large conference scheduling, employment rosters, an event already disrupted, or coerced attendance. Availability is personal information; collect only the windows and limits this activity needs, not full calendars. A short table or individual messages work without a polling app. For sight, hearing, or text barriers, use channels people can access and read back the result. Several free people may have no shared free time; saying so beats scheduling a predictable absence.
