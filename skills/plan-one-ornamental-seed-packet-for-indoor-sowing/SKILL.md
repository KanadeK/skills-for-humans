## 中文

当你已有一包**普通非食用观赏植物种子**，想在室内开始，却还没确认此时是否合适时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张针对**这包种子**的“现在播/等到某个条件再播/信息不足先停”决定卡；实际装盆与播种属于后续 Skill。不要把别的花种的周数、当地最后霜期或电热设备要求复制过来。

### 准备与输入

读包装上的植物名、室内或室外播种方式、建议时间、播深、发芽光照/温度、预计育苗阶段和最后去处。若包装缺关键说明，查同品种生产商或本地大学推广/园艺机构；不能确认品种或它是否适合家庭可及环境时先停。准备实际可用的采光与能排水的容器计划，不为了这次任务安装未知电气加热/照明。儿童或宠物可接触且植物安全性未知时，先核可靠品种信息与摆放边界。

### 执行

1. **先判断本包是否适合室内起苗。** 包装要求直接室外播、特殊低温处理或专业条件，而你没有可信说明/条件时，不把它当通用室内种子。可以改选适合室内启动的另一包，但那是另一个输入。
2. **从真实去处倒推时间。** 若将来要搬到室外，查所在地适用于这类植物的可栽时段、霜冻风险与包上的室内生长期，预留逐步适应室外的阶段。不同地区的月份不能互抄；没有可靠当地时点，就记待核，不从气候地图随手猜一天。
3. **核室内资源是否撑得住。** 问自己能否在整段育苗期间提供包装要求的可用光、位置、排水与日常查看，不只是播种当晚放得下一只杯子。只看见窗户不代表足够光，设备或能源需求不可满足时可以推迟或选别的种子。
4. **写一张一包一计划的小卡。** 记品种和来源、建议播种窗口、播法关键条件、预计转阶段/移栽窗口、需要准备的容器/位置与两个待核信息。到计划时再核包装和本地实际条件；这张卡不保证发芽或户外成活。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你能说明这包观赏种子**现在播、稍后播，或因缺说明暂停**的依据，且知道下一项必需准备。只把“春天”或社交媒体的日期写进日历，没有核包装和本地条件，不算完成。

- **包装只写模糊季节：** 查同品种可靠说明和本地计划；没证据就不发明具体星期。
- **已经错过推荐室内窗口：** 不把幼苗压缩生长期以追日程，核是否还有其它合规路线或留到下一季。
- **没有后续栽种地点：** 先决定幼苗将留盆还是移往室外，不让一盘苗在室内无限排队。
- **需要专用温控/光源但手头没有：** 推迟或换适合现有条件的品种，不自装或改装电器。
- **说明难读或语言不通：** 放大、读屏、请人朗读或查生产商可访问版本；种子名和关键条件仍须核对。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只规划少量普通观赏种子，不提供食用作物栽培、农药、植物毒性或野外采种建议。季节、光和户外条件随地区与品种不同；官方资料是判断方法，不是读者所在地的固定日期。计划可能得出“这次不播”，那也是完整而可用的结果。

## English

Load this Skill when you have **one packet of ordinary non-food ornamental seeds** and want to start indoors but have not checked whether now fits that packet. Produce a packet-specific card: **sow now, wait for a stated condition, or pause for missing information**. Preparing cells and sowing are later tasks. Do not import another flower's weeks, a different region's frost date, or an electrical device requirement.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the packet's plant name, indoor or outdoor method, suggested timing, depth, light and temperature for germination, expected seedling phase, and final destination. For missing essentials, check the same variety's maker or local university or horticultural guidance. Pause if identity or fit for your accessible home setting remains unknown. Plan for real available light and a draining container; do not install unfamiliar heat or lighting equipment for this task. If children or pets can reach a plant whose safety is unknown, verify species and placement with a reliable source first.

### Execution

1. **Check whether this packet belongs indoors.** If it requires direct outdoor sowing, special cold treatment, or specialist conditions for which you have no reliable instructions or means, do not treat it as a generic indoor seed. A different suitable packet is a different input.
2. **Work backward from a real destination.** For a later outdoor move, check the local plant-appropriate planting window, frost risk, packet's indoor growth period, and time for gradual outdoor acclimation. Months from another region are not yours. Without a reliable local window, mark it to verify rather than guessing a date from a map.
3. **Check that indoor resources last through the phase.** Can you provide the packet's usable light, space, drainage, and regular checks for the whole seedling period, not only fit a cup on sowing night? A visible window does not prove adequate light. If necessary equipment is unavailable, defer or choose an appropriate seed instead.
4. **Write one packet, one plan.** Note variety and source, sowing window, critical sowing conditions, likely next-stage or outdoor window, needed container and place, and two open questions. Recheck packet and local conditions when the date arrives. A plan cannot guarantee germination or outdoor survival.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

You can explain why this ornamental packet is **ready now, better later, or paused for missing facts**, and name the next prerequisite. “Spring” or a social-media date in a calendar without packet and local checks is insufficient.

- **The packet only says a vague season:** Find same-variety reliable guidance and a local plan; do not invent a week.
- **The recommended indoor window passed:** Do not compress seedling development to chase a date. Check another allowed route or save it for a later season.
- **There is no later planting place:** Decide whether seedlings stay in pots or move outdoors before leaving a tray waiting indefinitely.
- **Special heat or light gear is needed but absent:** Defer or change to seeds that fit current conditions; do not wire or modify equipment.
- **Instructions are tiny or inaccessible:** Magnify, screen-read, ask someone to read, or find a maker's accessible version, still checking identity and limits.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This plans a few ordinary ornamental seeds, not edible crop production, pesticides, toxicity decisions, or wild seed collection. Season, light, and outdoor conditions differ by location and variety. Institutional sources provide the decision method, not a universal local date. “Do not sow this time” can be a complete and useful result.
