## 中文

当你想外出拍照，却一路看见很多东西、最后说不出拍了什么时，用这份 Skill 在一段**短而允许拍摄的路线**上找同一视觉主题的不同例子。结果是少量由你拍下、能指出共同点的照片；不是按景点打卡，也不在这里挑选展出成片（那属于照片整理任务）。

### 准备与输入

选你可安全到达、允许普通手持拍摄的公开或获准地点，先看现场标识与工作人员说明；规定不明就询问或换位置，不推断“户外一定能拍”。决定一个肉眼可找的主题，如重复形状、光影边缘或相似颜色，写一句“我要找____在不同地方怎样出现”。手机或相机有可用电量和空间即可，不必买器材。路线留有返回点，尊重你的行动能力、天气和光线；可走很短、坐在获准位置观察，不以步数作为摄影成绩。默认拍物体、纹理和风景，避开可识别的人、私密窗内、车牌和敏感标识；有人进入画面时先放下相机或改构图。

### 执行

1. **先看，不急着按快门。** 到第一个安全停点，环顾主题是否真的出现。找不到就把主题缩宽一档，例如从“红色门”改成“红色细节”，不要为完成清单闯入封闭区域。
2. **为每张图选一个明确主体。** 问自己“这张中哪一块是主题证据？”让主体与背景有可见区别，移动相机取景或等杂乱的人流离开；不要挡路、踩车道或跨越栏杆争角度。按快门前再看边缘有没有无关人脸、地址或私人信息。
3. **找两个不同实例。** 在路线允许范围内拍 2–3 处不同主体，不靠同一物体的十连拍凑数。每拍完一处，简短记“地点或次序—主题怎么出现”；不需要记录精确位置或别人的身份。
4. **现场检查拍摄是否成立。** 在安全位置看缩略图，确认至少两张可辨认、主体确属同一主题且不是意外拍入私人细节。模糊则稳住设备重拍；隐私不合适则停止使用该张、在设备上删除或重新避开拍摄。不要边过路口边看屏幕。
5. **按路线收工。** 到预定时间或返回点即结束。记一句“今天主题在____和____出现”，留待另一次照片挑选；此处不需要上传、发给别人或做完整作品集。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有至少两张在允许场景拍摄、清楚显示**不同主体共享一个主题**的照片，并能用一句话说明共同点。只有大量照片但无主题，不满足本篇结果。

- **主题太窄找不到第二处：** 在原路线内放宽到上一级可观察特征；仍找不到，就记录未完成并另选时间/路线。
- **人流不断入画：** 等待、转向空景或换可拍的静物，不把陌生人当作必须获得的素材。
- **电量或天气不允许继续：** 安全返回，保留已拍结果；少于两张就诚实标作未完成。
- **现场限制不清或明确禁止：** 停止拍摄并遵守现场要求，改去允许地点；本篇不解释当地法律。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

拍摄与分享规则随地点、地区和对象而变，现场规则与相关人的意愿优先。行动或视觉条件不适合较长步行时，选无障碍短路、固定位置的不同安全取景点或可信同伴协助；你仍要能确认主题和照片。文件可能带位置资料，之后整理或分享前再核对设备设置；本篇完成无需分享。取景方式是创作选择，没有唯一“正确角度”。

## English

Use this Skill when you want to photograph outside but keep seeing everything and return without knowing what you captured. Along a **short route where photography is permitted**, find different examples of one visual theme. The result is a few photos you took and can connect by one shared feature, not a checklist of landmarks or a final exhibition selection. Selecting finished photos is another task.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose a public or authorised place you can reach safely and where ordinary handheld photography is allowed. Read signs and staff instructions; ask or change location if unsure rather than assume outdoors always means permission. Pick a visible theme such as repeated shapes, edges of light and shadow, or a colour, and write “I will look for how ____ appears in different places.” A working camera or phone with battery and space is enough; no gear purchase is needed. Plan a short route and return point suited to your mobility, weather, and light. A very short route or observation from an allowed seated place counts; step count is not the achievement. Prefer objects, textures, and landscapes. Avoid identifiable people, private windows, plates, and sensitive signs. Lower the camera or reframe when someone enters.

### Execution

1. **Look before shooting.** At a safe stopping point, see whether the theme actually appears. If not, widen it one level, such as “red details” instead of “red doors.” Do not enter a restricted place to complete a list.
2. **Choose a clear subject for each frame.** Ask what in the picture shows the theme. Separate it visibly from the background by repositioning the camera or waiting for clutter to clear. Do not block a path, step into traffic, or cross a barrier for an angle. Check frame edges for unrelated faces, addresses, or private information before pressing the shutter.
3. **Find different examples.** Photograph 2–3 distinct subjects along the permitted route, rather than ten bursts of one object. After each, note “stop or order—how the theme appears”; exact coordinates or other people's identities are unnecessary.
4. **Check on site.** From a safe place, inspect thumbnails. Make sure at least two frames are legible, show the intended shared theme, and have no accidental private details. Steady the device and retry a blurred view. Stop using, delete on the device, or retake a frame that intrudes on privacy. Do not inspect a screen while crossing a road.
5. **Finish at the planned boundary.** Stop at the return point or time. Write “today the theme appeared in ____ and ____” for later photo selection. No upload, message, or portfolio is required here.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have at least two legible photos from permitted settings of **different subjects sharing one visible theme**, and can state the shared feature in a sentence. A large unthemed camera roll does not meet this finish line.

- **The theme is too narrow for a second example:** Widen to an observable parent feature within the same route. If none appears, mark this attempt incomplete and choose another time or route.
- **People continually enter the frame:** Wait, turn toward an empty scene, or choose a still object. Strangers are not required material.
- **Battery or weather prevents continuation:** Return safely and keep what you have. Fewer than two qualifying frames means the task remains incomplete.
- **Restrictions are unclear or photography is prohibited:** Stop and follow the site's instructions. Move to a permitted place; this Skill does not interpret local law.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Photography and sharing rules differ by place, region, and subject; site rules and people's wishes govern your choice. If a longer walk or visual scan is unsuitable, use an accessible short route, different safe viewpoints from one allowed place, or trusted help. You still need to verify the theme and frames. Files may contain location data, so check device settings before any later organisation or sharing. Sharing is unnecessary for this task. Composition is a creative choice, with no single correct angle.
