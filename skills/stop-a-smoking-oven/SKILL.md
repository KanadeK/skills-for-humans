## 中文

当普通烘烤出现**配方未预期的烟、明显焦味或烤箱异常提示**，而尚无可见火苗时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**停热、让人离开烟与高温路径，待安全后分别判断食物和器具**。一旦有火、浓烟继续加重、燃气异味、电气危险或无法安全触及控制，立即离开并按当地紧急指导求助；本 Skill 不教灭火或维修。

### 准备与输入

先确认你能否从安全位置关闭烤箱，门是否已关好，旁人是否靠近排气或门口。只从外部判断，不为找烟源立刻开门或伸手取盘。食物烤了多久、烤盘/衬垫是什么、有没有已知滴落，可在安全后回看；眼下不要求你诊断是食物、纸还是电器。

### 执行步骤

1. **在安全可达时关闭热源。** 保持烤箱门关闭，让自己和其他人远离门口与排气路径。不要拉出冒烟热盘穿过厨房，也不要往箱内泼水。出现火或控制不可安全触及时，离开危险按当地消防/紧急指导处理，不继续本日常步骤。
2. **观察是否在停热后降级。** 不站在门前吸入烟或等一个自定分钟数。持续变浓的烟、异常声响、设备错误、燃气异味或电气问题都需要退出与专业支持。只有在烟停止、设备冷却且确切机型说明允许时才进一步打开检查。
3. **安全后分开检查来源。** 烤纸/容器是否不适用、食物是否滴落或烧焦、烤盘是否挡住风口、门是否异常，可按配方和手册回顾；机型故障、损坏电线/元件或可疑气味交制造商/合格人员，不拆设备。不要因为烤盘看似完整就立刻高温重启。
4. **对食物做保守判断。** 严重焦黑、材料异常或时间温度史不清的食物不靠刮黑处、再加热或强调味保证安全。若食物和容器均可确认仍适用，按食物特定[完成条件](../check-an-oven-bakes-endpoint/SKILL.md)决定是否还有可靠继续路径；不能确认就停止食用并按当地安全指导处理。
5. **只在原因和条件都清楚时安排下一次。** 纠正适用烤盘、衬垫、层位或份量后按配方与机型说明重做。若原因不明、仍有烟或设备受损，这次烘烤结束，寻求支持而非提高或降低温度试错。

### 完成与停止

热源已停、烟无继续升级、人员安全、设备冷却后有明确“可按证据重做 / 设备停用求助 / 食物弃置或另行核对”之一，就是完成。火、持续浓烟、燃气味与电气危险立刻退出日常流程；保住这一盘不是成功条件。

### 常见报错与补救

- **见烟立刻开门找原因：** 先关热并保持门关，观察是否加重；有火时开门可能让危险扩大。
- **把水泼进烤箱：** 不这样做；退出并按当地紧急指导处理火灾，设备冷却后交合格支持。
- **烟停了就继续烤同一盘：** 先核对食物、容器、风口和设备状态；原因不明就取消这次烘烤。
- **觉得只是焦了一边：** 烟是热异常；上色不匀的普通调整只适用于**没有烟和故障**的情况。
- **烤箱有错误码却只重启：** 记录型号与故障信息，按制造商或合格支持处理，不在本 Skill 内维修。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里仅处理普通烘烤中**尚无火的早期异常停止**，不做火灾应对、烫伤治疗或燃气/电气维修。烤箱、烤具、食物与烟源不同，气味不能可靠诊断原因。视听受限时使用设备可及提示、现场可信人的描述或烟雾报警器；无法安全确认时退出求助，不为了记录原因靠近烟。副作用是一盘食物可能损失，但烤箱无需用烟来报进度。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary bake develops **unexpected smoke, a clear scorched odour, or an abnormal oven alert**, before any visible flame. **Stop heat, keep people out of smoke and hot-air paths, and judge food and equipment separately once safe.** Flame, growing dense smoke, gas odour, electrical danger, or an unreachable control means leaving danger and following local emergency guidance. This is neither firefighting nor repair.

### Preparation and inputs

Check whether you can switch the oven off from a safe position, whether the door is shut, and whether anyone stands by the vent or door. Observe from outside; do not open the oven or reach for a pan merely to locate smoke. Elapsed baking time, pan and liner, and possible drips can be reviewed when safe. You need not diagnose food, paper, or appliance immediately.

### Execution steps

1. **Switch heat off when safely reachable.** Keep the oven door closed and people clear of the door and vent. Do not carry a smoking hot pan across the kitchen or pour water into the cavity. If flame appears or the control is unsafe to reach, leave and use local fire or emergency guidance rather than continuing this everyday flow.
2. **Watch for reduction after heat stops.** Do not stand in smoke waiting for a made-up number of minutes. Smoke that grows or persists, odd sounds, equipment alerts, gas odour, or an electrical fault require exit and qualified support. Open for later inspection only after smoke has stopped, the oven has cooled, and the exact model's directions allow it.
3. **Inspect possible sources separately once safe.** Check recipe and manual against a liner or vessel, drips or scorching, blocked vents, and door condition. Send equipment faults, damaged wiring or elements, and suspect odours to maker or qualified support rather than disassembling anything. An intact-looking pan does not justify an immediate hot restart.
4. **Judge food conservatively.** Scraping off black areas, reheating, or strong seasoning cannot certify food that is severely charred, exposed to an unsuitable material, or missing a reliable time and temperature history. If food and vessel are clearly still suitable, use the food-specific [endpoint check](../check-an-oven-bakes-endpoint/SKILL.md) to decide whether any reliable continuation exists. Otherwise stop eating plans and follow local food-safety guidance.
5. **Plan another attempt only with a known cause and restored conditions.** Correct approved bakeware, liner, rack, or quantity and restart under recipe and model directions. Unknown cause, continuing smoke, or damaged equipment ends this bake for qualified support, not hot/cold dial experimentation.

### Success and stop

Heat is off, smoke no longer escalates, people are safe, and once the oven cools you have a clear “retry from evidence, stop equipment for support, or discard/check food” result. Fire, persistent heavy smoke, gas odour, and electrical danger leave the everyday flow immediately. Saving the tray is not success.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Opening at the first sign of smoke to find its source:** Stop heat and keep the door shut while judging whether danger grows. Opening during a fire can worsen it.
- **Throwing water into the oven:** Do not do so. Use local emergency guidance for fire and qualified support after cooling.
- **Smoke stopped, so the same bake continues:** Check food, container, vents, and oven state first. Unknown cause ends this attempt.
- **Calling it only one dark edge:** Smoke is a heat abnormality. Ordinary uneven-browning adjustments apply only without smoke or faults.
- **An error code is cleared by repeated restart:** Record model and alert and use maker or qualified support, not repair under this Skill.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This only stops an **early pre-flame abnormality** in ordinary baking. It is not fire response, scald treatment, or gas/electrical repair. Oven, vessel, food, and source of smoke vary; odour cannot diagnose the cause. If sight or hearing is limited, use accessible oven alerts, description from someone present, or smoke alarms. Leave for help when it cannot be assessed safely rather than moving closer to document it. A tray may be lost; the oven need not report progress in smoke.
