## 中文

当你按已确认的程序烘了一批普通衣物，机器**报结束或自动停止**时，用这份 Skill 核对衣物真实干燥状态，再决定收下、只继续适用部分、转自然晾干或停止。它不把感应器或计时器当作每件衣物的合格证，也不教拆机修排风。衣物标签仍决定能否继续受热。

### 准备与输入

记录这批衣物和已运行的程序、机型说明、衣物干燥上限；按本机允许的方法等机器完全停稳并开门。准备干净台面/篮子与可自然晾晒的位置。若有焦味、烟、火花、排风警报或异常高热，停止触碰设备并按说明和当地紧急/专业指导处理；只有安全时才检查衣物。热衣物取出时注意烫手，给它降到可判断的状态。

### 执行

1. **不要只摸最外面一件。** 在安全停机后抽取厚与薄、靠门与内侧等不同位置的衣物，检查里外、接缝、袖口、腰头和重叠处。发热状态会影响触感，必要时放在干净处稍降温再判断；“铃响了”只表示程序结束。
2. **把干与未干分开。** 真正干、无明显新缩水/掉色/焦痕的一件按标签收下；仍潮的另放，不把一件潮衣混入整篮干衣。新缩水、串色或表面损伤交洗后外观变化任务，停止继续加热该件。
3. **为潮衣选择允许的后续。** 复查衣物标签和准确机型说明：若都允许，按该型号的干燥等级、较小负载或适用追加程序继续，并再次检查；或转标签允许的自然晾干。不能以所有衣物再次高热一整轮来修一条厚缝，也不复制另一型号的“再烘十分钟”。
4. **识别设备问题。** 若整批反复潮湿、过滤网被绒毛堵、显示排风警告或机器异常，按说明处理使用者可清理的绒毛过滤网；警告持续、排风不畅或有焦味时停用并联系管理者/专业维修，不自行拆卸风道。为湿衣安排合适晾晒/滞留处理，不能堆在密闭滚筒里等待修机。
5. **结束并清理。** 按本机说明处理绒毛过滤网、清理散落绒毛和安全放好干衣；未干衣物有下一次检查的具体去向。衣物是否可入柜取决于实物干燥和护理状态，不取决于机器屏幕写的百分比。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

这批每件衣物被归为可收、需按标签继续干燥、外观异常停止或设备问题停止；干衣没有和潮衣混存，机器无被忽略的警报。结束检查可以得出“还没干”，不要求为了省事让所有衣物过烘。

- **一件袖口潮，别的都干：** 只处理潮的那件，按其标签和机型决定继续或晾干。
- **取出时很热，不确定是否干：** 放到干净安全处降到可判断状态再检查厚处，不靠高热感觉推断。
- **第二轮后大部分仍潮：** 停止连续加时，核对负载、过滤网与设备警示，必要时停用并求助。
- **出现缩小或表面硬化：** 停止对该件继续加热，保留标签与所选程序记录，转洗后外观变化判断。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只针对普通衣物与安全停稳的家用烘干机，不涉及机器维修或危险污染。热泵、排风式和洗烘一体的结束提示不同，以确切机型为准。触觉或视力受限时用稳定光线、衣物原尺寸记录和可信现场协助；无法安全开门或处理热衣时停止。可能还需占用自然晾晒位置、再运行部分负载或联系管理者。

## English

Use this Skill when a documented cycle for ordinary clothes **signals completion or stops automatically**. Check actual garments before putting them away, continuing only permitted pieces, air drying, or stopping. A timer or sensor is not a pass certificate for every item, and this Skill does not teach vent repair. Garment labels still control further heat.

### Preparation and inputs

Note the load, cycle used, exact model guide, and each garment's dry limits. Let the machine stop fully and open it as its manual permits. Have a clean surface or basket and an air-dry option ready. For burning odour, smoke, sparks, vent alert, or abnormal heat, stop contact and follow the manual and local professional or emergency guidance; inspect garments only when safe. Let hot fabric cool to a state you can judge without burning your hands.

### Execution

1. **Check more than the outermost garment.** After a safe stop, sample thick and thin items and positions near the door and deeper in the load. Check inside, seams, cuffs, waistbands, and overlaps. Heat can affect what dry fabric feels like; let an item cool on a clean surface if needed. A chime proves only that a cycle ended.
2. **Separate dry from damp.** Put away an item only when it is really dry with no obvious new shrinking, dye transfer, or scorching. Keep damp pieces separate rather than mixing one into a basket of dry clothes. Route new shape, colour, or surface damage to the post-wash appearance task and stop heating that item.
3. **Choose an allowed next step for damp items.** Check the garment labels and this model's manual again. If both permit it, use an appropriate smaller load, dryness control, or documented additional cycle, then inspect again; alternatively use label-permitted air drying. Do not give the entire dry load another high-heat cycle for one damp cuff, or copy “ten more minutes” from another machine.
4. **Recognise a possible appliance problem.** If most items remain damp repeatedly, the lint filter is blocked, a vent warning appears, or operation is abnormal, follow the manual for user-accessible lint-filter care. Stop and contact the manager or qualified service for a persistent warning, restricted venting, or burning smell; do not dismantle ducts. Give wet garments a suitable air-dry or delay plan rather than leaving them closed in the drum waiting for repair.
5. **Finish and clean up.** Handle the lint filter under this manual, clear loose lint, and place dry items safely. Damp pieces have a real destination and next check. Cupboard readiness follows garment condition, not a percentage on a screen.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Every item has a result: ready, continue label-compatible drying, stop for appearance damage, or stop for equipment trouble. Dry and damp items are separate and no appliance warning is ignored. A valid end check can say “still damp”; it need not overdry a whole load for convenience.

- **One cuff is damp while the rest is dry:** Handle only that item, by its label and this model's available options.
- **An item is hot and hard to judge:** Let it cool safely on a clean surface, then inspect the thick places. Do not infer dryness from heat.
- **Most items remain damp after a second attempt:** Stop repeatedly adding time; check load, filter, and alerts and get help or stop use as needed.
- **The item shrinks or hardens:** Stop heating it, retain the label and cycle record, and use the post-wash appearance-change decision.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary clothing in a safely stopped home dryer, not appliance repair or hazardous contamination. Heat-pump, vented, and combined machines can signal completion differently; follow the exact model. If touch or vision is limited, use steady light, original-size records, and trusted in-person help. Stop if you cannot open or handle hot clothing safely. Another partial cycle, air-dry space, or manager contact may be needed.
