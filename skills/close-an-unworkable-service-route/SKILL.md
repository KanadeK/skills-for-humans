## 中文

当普通包裹、退换或售后事项**已经遇到明确障碍**时加载：服务方说无法继续、官方流程显示缺条件或已结束，或者原联系渠道失效。你的目标是分清能补的条件、该找的负责人和真正无路可走的部分，留下一张有下一步、未知项和停止点的收口记录。仍有具体承诺和复查时间时，先按原工单跟进；第一次报问题也不从这里开始。

### 准备与判断

取出本人可用的订单、运单或案件编号和最后一次真实答复，写下查看日期与官方渠道。保留原件或原始消息，在私人记录里只摘必要事实。先确认是谁说“不能继续”、原话与原因；网页没更新、一次无人回复、自己猜测过期，都不足以证明最终拒绝。不同商家、承运商、地区的规则不同，只核对**你这件事**适用的官方页面或原工单，尤其是实际条件和时限；不要把别家的规定搬来。

### 执行

1. **给障碍定状态，不替服务方补判词。** 分成“还缺我能提供的资料或联系信息”“须由寄件人/商家等另一方处理”“仍在处理但暂时无答复”“服务方明确终止或该渠道已失效”。记下来源和日期。某些 UPS 美国理赔状态的“未批准”其实提示补充商品描述或收件人联系方式；有寄件人限制时须联系寄件人。它们只是识别状态的例子，不能用于判定别家案件。
2. **只问一次能改变路径的问题。** 在原工单或服务方公布的正式渠道说明编号和当前障碍，问：“具体缺什么或为何无法继续？谁有资格做下一步？是否有本事项适用的替代官方入口、条件或日期？”把答复照实记下。没有回复就写“截至何时未答”，不填想象中的拒绝理由；无法使用电话时找该服务方实际提供的文字或无障碍渠道。
3. **选一个现实下一步。** 能补资料的，确认资料从哪里取得、交给谁和是否值得继续，再按官方方法提交；需寄件人或商家操作的，给对方必要编号和服务方原话，先确认对方愿意且能够接手；有另一正式入口的，核对适用条件、费用或时限后再决定是否使用。没有可行路径时，记录“当前停止联系这个渠道”；这不是承认问题已解决，也不代替专业权利判断。
4. **写四行收口卡。** ①最后确认的事实与依据；②仍未知的事，不写成事实；③下一动作、负责人和实际适用的复看点，或“暂无可行动作”；④停止点：什么已尝试、何种新事实出现才重开。保存官方答复和必要凭证在自己可控的位置。无需把密码、完整付款信息或私人地址发进公开评论。
5. **按状态结束本轮。** 已找到可行接手人或官方入口，就把事项标成“转交/待办”并保留复看点；服务方明确结束且无现实下一步，就标“未解决、当前暂停”。若后来有实物到达、服务方新答复或适用政策信息，按新事实重新判断，而不是无限重复同一条催问。

### 完成与停止

收口卡能回答“现在实际知道什么、谁能做下一步、什么仍不知道、何时停止或重开”，本轮就完成。**完成的是决策记录，不是包裹送达、维修完成或退款获准。** 若需要解释法律权利、索赔、付款争议或涉及危险物品、紧急安全问题，停下这份日常流程，使用相应官方或合格专业渠道；不要让这张卡变成维权期限指南。

### 常见报错与补救

- **状态写“未批准”就认定永久结束：** 先读服务方给出的具体原因和可补条件；有的状态仍需要额外信息。
- **寄件人才能操作：** 不用他人账号代办。把必要事实交给愿意接手的寄件人/商家，并记谁负责回告；无人愿意接手则如实记为未知或受阻。
- **调查或搜索到期，没有找到物件：** 记“该流程没有找到”，不改写成“已送达”或“物件一定不存在”。例如 USPS 的搜索过期仅表示该搜索未匹配到物件。
- **反复无人答复：** 核对是否用对正式渠道、是否还有公开的替代入口和适用时限；仍无结果时停下重复发送，记录最后询问与未答状态。
- **怕暂停会错过机会：** 先查看本人订单/服务规则中实际适用的条件或时限；不确定就向官方或合格专业渠道求证，不凭本 Skill 发明期限。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通物流、商家退换和一般售后；不保证任何商家提供申诉入口、补发、退款或赔偿。渠道和费用因地区、服务与订单而异。行动、视觉、听觉或语言不便时，可用服务方现有的文字/无障碍渠道，或请可信且获授权的人协助读回状态与记录，不交出账号控制权。副作用是案子可能暂时仍未解决，但不会被“已关闭”三个字悄悄改造成“已解决”。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary parcel, return or after-sales matter has hit a **specific obstacle**: the provider says it cannot continue, its official process says a condition is missing or the case has ended, or your original contact route has failed. Your result is a short closeout record that separates a fixable condition, the person who can act and a genuine stopping point. If a concrete promise still has a review time, follow that live promise first. This is not the first problem report.

### Preparation and decision

Gather your order, tracking or case reference and the last real reply. Note the date and official channel you checked. Keep original messages or documents and copy only necessary facts into private notes. Identify who said the route cannot continue, their actual words and reason. A stale webpage, one unanswered message or your guess about expiry does not prove final refusal. Rules vary by seller, carrier and region; check the official page or case route that applies to **your case**, especially actual conditions and dates.

### Execution

1. **Name the obstacle without deciding for the provider.** Sort it as “information or contact details I can supply,” “only the sender, seller or another party can act,” “still in progress but unanswered for now,” or “provider explicitly ended this route.” Record source and date. In UPS's US process, some “not approved” claim statuses ask for a better item description or receiver contact details; a shipper restriction points to the shipper. These are status examples, not rules for every company.
2. **Ask one question that could change the route.** In the existing case or published official channel, give your reference and obstacle. Ask: “What exactly is missing or why can this not continue? Who can take the next step? Is there another official route, condition or date that applies to this case?” Record the answer as given. If nobody replies, write “no answer by [date],” not an invented reason. If calling is inaccessible, use a real written or accessible option the provider offers.
3. **Choose one feasible next action.** If you can supply information, identify where to get it, who receives it and whether you want to continue, then use the provider's method. If only the sender or seller can act, give them the necessary reference and provider wording, and confirm they are willing and able to take it on. If another official route exists, check its conditions, cost and applicable dates before choosing it. If none is feasible, record “stop contacting this route for now.” That does not say the matter is solved or decide specialist rights.
4. **Make a four-line closeout card.** Write (1) last confirmed fact and evidence, (2) what remains unknown, (3) next action, owner and applicable review point or “no action available now,” and (4) what was tried and what new fact would reopen this route. Keep replies and necessary records somewhere you control. Do not post passwords, full payment details or private addresses in public comments.
5. **End this round according to its state.** A workable owner or official route means “handed over / pending” with a review point. An explicit end with no feasible next action means “unresolved / paused.” Reconsider if the parcel arrives, the provider supplies new information or you find applicable policy details. Do not repeat an identical chase indefinitely.

### Success and stop

You are done with this round when the card says what is actually known, who can act, what is unknown and when to stop or reopen. **That is completion of a decision record, not delivery, repair or approval of a refund.** Stop this everyday procedure for legal rights, claims, payment disputes, hazardous items or urgent safety matters, and use the relevant official or qualified specialist route. This card is not a rights or deadline guide.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Not approved” is treated as permanently final:** Read the provider's specific reason and any missing condition first; some statuses still need information.
- **Only the sender can act:** Do not operate their account. Give necessary facts to a willing sender or seller and note who will report back. If no one takes it, record that as blocked or unknown.
- **A search expires without finding the parcel:** Write “this search did not find it,” not “it was delivered” or “it cannot exist.” A USPS expired search, for example, only states that its search found no match.
- **Repeated silence:** Check that you used the real official channel, whether another published route exists and any applicable dates. If there is still no answer, stop sending the same message and record the last question and unanswered state.
- **Pausing feels risky:** Check the actual conditions and dates for your order or service first. Ask the provider or qualified help if unclear rather than inventing a deadline from this Skill.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers ordinary logistics, seller returns and everyday after-sales contact. It does not guarantee an appeal route, replacement, refund or compensation. Channels and costs depend on region, service and order. If mobility, sight, hearing or language makes one channel difficult, use an offered written or accessible route or a trusted authorised helper to read and record status without handing over account control. The matter may remain unresolved; an administrative “closed” does not quietly become “solved.”
