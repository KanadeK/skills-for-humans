## 中文

当你刚洗完普通衣物，发现**原本没有的颜色出现在另一件衣物上**，用这份 Skill 在烘干前判断是否串色、哪些件受影响，以及是否有衣物与产品都允许的有限尝试。它不把原衣服自身褪色或洗涤剂残留也叫“外来染色”；这两种情况走别的判断。结果可以是停止，不承诺把每件衣服恢复原色。

### 准备与输入

按洗前照片、颜色、衣物位置和已知掉色史找可能的来源，但不要仅凭同桶深色衣物就给它定罪。读每件受影响衣物的洗、漂白、干燥标签以及任何想用的去色产品说明。准备干净可分开放湿衣的位置。体液、危险化学品或不明污染不属本篇；先停止普通处理。

### 执行

1. **先暂停热干燥并分开。** 把疑似掉色来源、出现新颜色的受影响件、没有变化的件分开放，记录新颜色的位置和程度；不要让潮衣互相压在一起。尚未确认能否继续处理前，不把受影响件烘干或熨烫。
2. **区分外来色与自身变色。** 新颜色若集中在原本浅色/白色衣物、与另一件脱色方向相符，才把串色列为可能；如果是原布色变浅、印花掉落或白色洗剂粉末，转相应褪色/残留流程。无法区分时拍照保留证据并暂停新产品。
3. **为每件评估允许的路径。** 衣物和产品标签都允许、面料与装饰相容时，先在不显眼处按说明试验；对纯白且确认可水洗的普通衣物，可以核对正规包装去色产品是否明确适用，并严格照其说明做一次有界处理。彩色、印花、复杂或贵重衣物不能照搬白布方案；条件不明时联系制造商/专业护理。
4. **只复洗经核实的受影响件。** 若选择产品方案，按衣物/产品说明与准确洗衣设备可支持的流程单独处理，完成后在安全光线下复查；不要把多种去色剂、漂白剂、醋、氨等混用，也不靠提高温度超过标签上限。明显未改善或布色开始受损时停止继续试错。
5. **给各组去向。** 已恢复且标签允许的衣物按正确干燥方式继续；仍串色的记录结果，选择接受外观变化或求专业意见。疑似掉色来源今后按洗前分组流程单独处理，不把本次结果当成它永远不会再掉色的证明。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

受影响件已识别并在热干燥前隔离；你能说明为何尝试某个衣物与产品都支持的方法，或为何停止。没有把可能受损的彩色衣物一起投入白布去色实验。

- **整桶看着泛灰但没有新色块：** 先查洗剂残留、脏污转移和光线；不马上投放去色产品。
- **不显眼测试处也掉色：** 停止该产品，留存照片和说明，咨询专业护理。
- **产品说明只针对纯白：** 不用于彩色或印花件；按其实际材质寻找别的可信方案或停止。
- **复洗后仍有颜色：** 接受可能无法恢复的现实，先别加第二种化学品。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理普通洗后外来染色，不处理医疗防护、专业护理或危险污染。颜色识别会受照明和色觉影响；用洗前照片、衣物标记及可信现场协助，不凭肉眼强行断定来源。重新处理会耗水、时间，也可能进一步影响原布色；保留证据和停止选择都具有实际价值。

## English

Use this Skill just after washing ordinary clothes when **a colour that was not there appears on another garment**. Before drying, decide whether dye transferred, which items were affected, and whether both garment and product labels support a limited attempt. Original fabric fading or detergent residue is not the same mechanism. Stopping can be the right result; not every item can return to its original colour.

### Preparation and inputs

Use prewash photos, colours, garment positions, and known bleeding history to find a possible source, but do not blame a dark item merely because it shared the drum. Read each affected garment's wash, bleach, and dry labels and any proposed colour-remover directions. Have clean separate places for wet items. Bodily fluids, hazardous chemicals, or unknown contamination leave this ordinary workflow.

### Execution

1. **Pause heat drying and separate.** Put a suspected bleeding source, newly coloured affected items, and unchanged items in separate places. Record where and how much new colour appears. Do not press damp garments together. Do not tumble-dry or iron affected pieces before deciding whether treatment is allowed.
2. **Separate incoming dye from original colour loss.** A new hue on a formerly light or white item, consistent with another item's loss, supports possible transfer. Original colour fading, lost print, or white powder residue calls for the fading or residue route. If uncertain, photograph it and hold new products.
3. **Check the allowed route item by item.** If both garment and product labels permit treatment and fabric and trim fit, test inconspicuously as directed. For plain white ordinary washable fabric, check whether a packaged colour remover explicitly fits and make one bounded attempt by its full directions. Do not transfer a white-cloth method to coloured, printed, complex, or valuable garments. Ask the maker or professional cleaner when conditions are unclear.
4. **Rewash only verified affected items.** If you select a product route, treat separately under garment and product directions and a washer method your exact equipment supports. Reinspect in good light. Do not mix colour removers, bleaches, vinegar, ammonia, or other cleaners, or exceed the garment temperature limit. Stop further trials if the mark does not improve or the original dye is damaged.
5. **Give each group a destination.** Properly dry recovered clothes under their labels. Record any remaining transfer and accept an appearance change or seek professional advice. Sort the suspected bleeding item separately next time; this wash did not prove it will never bleed again.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Affected items were identified and separated before heat drying. You can explain one label-supported attempt or a clear stop. Coloured garments were not placed in a white-fabric colour-remover experiment.

- **The whole load looks grey without a new colour patch:** Check detergent residue, soil transfer, and lighting before using a colour remover.
- **The hidden test patch loses colour too:** Stop the product and keep photos and directions for professional advice.
- **The product is marked for plain white only:** Do not use it on coloured or printed items; find an actual compatible route or stop.
- **Colour remains after rewashing:** It may be permanent; do not add another chemical by guesswork.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers newly transferred dye after ordinary washing, not protective gear, professional-care fabric, or hazardous contamination. Lighting and colour vision affect identification. Use a prewash photo, garment identifiers, or trusted in-person help rather than forcing a visual verdict. Another treatment costs water and time and may harm original dye; evidence and a stop decision have practical value.
