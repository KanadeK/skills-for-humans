## 中文

当这顿饭已选好，却在**开工前**发现原做法需要的火口、锅、工作面、容器或可靠冷藏比你实际拥有的多时，用这份 Skill 改成现实可行的菜单与做法。它不是把同一菜单挤进一张更密的时间表；也不教陌生电器操作、失控火源或食品安全的“省略版”。

### 准备与输入

列出这顿饭原本每道菜要完成的关键功能、各自可信做法与设备要求、已确定人数和时间。实际清点能正常工作的加热源、锅具、盖子、刀板、水、可清洁工作面、冷藏与盛装容器。对于有生动物性食品、需冷藏或需要特定设备的食品，读它的包装和当地食品安全指导。坏了或不懂怎么用的设备不算“可用”；不要因为缺锅就拿未核实的容器加热。

### 执行

1. **把缺口写成具体步骤。** 不是“厨房太小”，而是“这两道同时要求唯一的锅”“这款产品要求目前没有的设备”或“预煮份没有可用冷藏位”。先确认是否只是 H023 可解决的顺序冲突；若只是先后安排，改排程即可，不改菜单。
2. **标出不能省的安全依赖。** 生食与即食食物的分开处理、产品要求的彻底加热、洗手清洁以及需低温保存的条件，都不能被“设备有限”取消。某食品必须用特定安全方法而你做不到，就把它从本餐候选中移出，不通过猜时间或摸锅壁补救。
3. **为每道菜找已经验证可行的做法。** 看原配方、包装或可信烹饪资料是否提供适合你现有设备的版本。能在一只锅内按已知安全步骤完成的，留；需要你没有的火口、附件或锅容量的，改用可信做法、分成可完成批次，或删掉这道菜。不能把烤箱做法直接复制成微波炉按钮。
4. **用最小餐食重新组装。** 保留能完成这一餐主要用途的食物与份量，把装饰或重复菜排后。若选择不加热的食物，先确认它本来就可按标签即食，不能把未熟原料改叫冷盘。人数和份量若因此变化，回到份量规划任务重新核对。
5. **再次检查资源全程可用。** 新方法所需的锅、工具、清洁空间、盛装容器和下一步如何安全检查熟度，都要能实际指到。若某道先完成，需要按食品和当地规则安全等待；没有这种能力就调晚它的开始或删去。改变的方法会改变时间，交给多道菜顺序任务重排。
6. **给出开工决定。** 写下新的菜名或食物组合、每项采用的可信做法、实际设备、不能省的检查点以及明确取消的原计划步骤。任何关键依赖仍未满足时，输出“这顿原方案不可行”，选择更简单的确实可完成餐食或暂缓。

### 成功条件

新方案使用的每件设备、容器和食品条件都真实可用，能按可靠做法完成和检查；原菜单无法执行的步骤已被替换、删去或分批，且不会以跳过食品安全作为代价。若没有合格方案，明确停止比开火后发现没有锅更完整。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只是同一锅排不开，却把整份菜单推倒：** 先检查能否顺序做；能安全等待的步骤可重排，真正做不到才改做法或删菜。
- **“微波炉也能加热”被当作原配方等价：** 查该产品和设备说明有没有可信的新做法及安全检查；没有就不做这个转换。
- **省了砧板，生食与即食食品共用脏工具：** 先安排能执行的分开或清洁路径；做不到就选不需要该冲突的餐食。
- **锅能装下但没有保存额外份的冷藏空间：** 缩回本餐量或选择能按实际条件处理的做法，不把做多了留在台面上等下次。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通家庭的开工前方案重排，不提供医疗饮食、过敏处置、危险刀工或电气/燃气维修。产品与设备说明、地区食品安全规则和个人可及性优先。视觉、握力、站立或同时看顾多处困难时，可选更少步骤、可触及工作面、能读的说明与可信协助；不应以体力硬撑替代缺设备。菜单可能变简单、开饭变晚或取消一道菜，这是新约束下诚实的结果。

## English

Use this Skill **before cooking** when a chosen meal requires more working burners, pans, preparation space, containers, or reliable cold storage than you actually have. Change the meal and method so they can run in your kitchen. This is not merely squeezing the same menu into a denser timeline, and it does not teach an unfamiliar appliance, uncontrolled flame, or a shortcut around food safety.

### Preparation and inputs

List the real job of each planned dish, its reliable method and equipment, confirmed diners, and serving time. Count working heat sources, pans, lids, knives and boards, water, cleanable surfaces, cold storage, and serving containers. For raw animal foods or products requiring cooling or a specific appliance, read the package and local food-safety guidance. A broken or unfamiliar appliance is not available equipment; do not heat food in an unverified container because a pan is missing.

### Execution

1. **Name the missing step.** Replace “my kitchen is small” with “two dishes need the only pan at once,” “this product requires equipment I lack,” or “extra cooked food has no cold space.” First check whether H023's simple sequencing can resolve the conflict. If an order change is enough, keep the menu and reschedule.
2. **Mark safety dependencies you cannot delete.** Separation of raw and ready-to-eat food, product-directed thorough cooking, hand and tool cleaning, and required cold storage do not disappear with limited equipment. Remove a food from this meal if its required safe method cannot be performed; do not improvise time or use touch as proof.
3. **Seek methods already supported for your equipment.** Check the recipe, package, or trusted cooking source for a route that fits your real tools. Keep a dish when a known safe method works in one pan. For a dish requiring absent burners, attachments, or capacity, use a supported alternate method, split into manageable batches, or remove it. Oven instructions are not automatically microwave settings.
4. **Rebuild the smallest working meal.** Keep foods and amounts that achieve the meal's main use. Move decoration or duplicate sides later. A no-heat option must truly be ready-to-eat under its label; uncooked raw food does not become a cold plate by renaming it. If people or amounts change, recheck the separate portion-planning task.
5. **Verify the whole route.** Point to every pan, tool, cleaning area, serving container, and appropriate doneness check needed by the new method. Food finishing early still needs locally suitable safe holding; if unavailable, start it later or remove it. A changed method changes timing, so revise the multi-dish sequence.
6. **Decide whether to start.** Record the new dishes or combination, supported method, actual equipment, non-negotiable checks, and cancelled steps from the old plan. If an essential dependency is still missing, output “the original meal cannot run here,” choose a simpler genuinely feasible meal, or defer.

### Success

Every piece of equipment, container, and food condition in the revised meal is actually available and supports a reliable completion check. Impossible steps have been replaced, removed, or made into safe batches without trading away food safety. When no valid route exists, a clear stop is more complete than discovering the missing pan after heating starts.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only one pan is double-booked, but you discard the whole meal:** Try sequencing first. Reorder steps that can wait safely; change methods or remove a dish only when necessary.
- **“A microwave heats things” becomes “it is equivalent to this recipe”:** Check whether the product and appliance provide a supported method and safety check. Without one, do not make that conversion.
- **One fewer board leads to raw and ready food sharing dirty tools:** Plan a workable separation or cleaning route. If that cannot be done, choose food without this conflict.
- **The pan holds the food, but there is no cold space for extra servings:** Return to today's amount or a method your facilities can handle. Do not store surplus on the counter for next time.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a pre-cooking redesign for an ordinary household meal, not medical nutrition, allergy handling, hazardous knife work, or gas/electrical repair. Exact product and appliance directions, local food-safety guidance, and access needs govern. If seeing instructions, gripping, standing, or watching concurrent tasks is difficult, choose fewer steps, a reachable surface, readable directions, or trusted help. Physical strain does not replace missing equipment. A simpler menu, later serving, or one cancelled dish is an honest result of the new constraint.
