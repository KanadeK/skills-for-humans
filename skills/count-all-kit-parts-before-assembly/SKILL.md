## 中文

当普通非承重用品的包装已经送到、你准备按厂家说明做用户级组装，但还没开始拧或插入零件时加载。你要得到**按准确说明核对的零件数量表，知道哪些齐全、哪些尚未找到、哪些确实缺失或不符**。这里只清点，不使用工具组装、不决定代用零件，也不安装承重、防倾倒或其他防护部件。

### 准备与输入

确认用品的准确型号、版本或包装标签与手中的说明一致；不对版就先找正确说明。找齐交付的全部箱包，核对外箱上的“第几箱/共几箱”等信息。零件可能在小袋、板件夹层或另一箱里，但只能在获准且安全的包装范围检查；大件需要危险搬动时请有能力的人。将原袋和标签保留，别把两种相似小件倒成一堆。

### 执行

1. **先列应有项。** 从准确说明的零件页记名称/编号、每种应有数量及相关包装；图只是示意时以标明的编号和数字为准。说明中若有必须由专业人员处理的承重或防倾倒项，明确另列，不把“先不装”当成可安全使用。
2. **按箱逐项找。** 对照箱号打开已到包装和配件袋，查看厂家标明或安全可见的夹层与小盒。每找到一种就在清单旁记实际位置、编号/可见特征与数量，暂不把未知件归进相似项。
3. **比较数量与状态。** 对每行写“实见且匹配／仍有包装未查／实见但型号不符或损坏／已查全部仍缺”。不要因一只袋子还没找到就立刻宣称少件，也不要因总数恰好对上就忽略两种件混淆。
4. **让下一步停在对的地方。** 某项关键件缺失、破损或身份不明时，保留原包装和具体差异，不强行开始依赖它的步骤；询问实际供应方的渠道由后续处理。其他独立且说明允许的步骤是否可做，以准确说明为准。
5. **交出核对表。** 让将要组装的人读回每种件的编号、数量、放在哪里、是否仍待确认。把小件继续按原袋/清楚标签分开，避免清点完反而制造新的失踪事件。

### 完成与停止

全部已交付包装已查，每种应有件有实见数量和状态；“齐全”只指这份准确说明下的零件核对，不证明组装或结构安全。包装未齐、说明不对应、关键件缺损或专业固定要求未解决时，保持未准备好，不以其他尺寸相近的件代替。

### 常见报错与补救

- **总数对但某编号少、另一编号多：** 分开记录两行，重查袋标签，不用总数抵消错误。
- **以为缺件，实际上另有一箱未开：** 先对外箱数量和已到状态，再检查实际能安全查看的箱。
- **说明图片看起来很像，编号读不清：** 放大准确说明或请可信的人读回，不把相似物硬配。
- **板件在底部、独自搬不动：** 暂记未查，请合适的人协助；不要为清点冒险翻大件。
- **有不在清单上的零件：** 单独存放并记录来源，向厂家确认，别直接叫“备用”塞进某步。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只适用于厂家允许的普通低风险非结构性组装前清点。不同品牌的袋号、图示比例和包装拆分方式各异，以准确说明为准；某些防倾倒/墙面固定要求不可由本 Skill 豁免。视力、阅读或握持不便时可用大字、照片放大和可信协助者逐件读回，不要求本人搬动重箱。副作用是“多出一颗螺丝”会先成为待核事实，而不是默认赠品。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary non-load-bearing kit has arrived and you are about to start manufacturer-permitted assembly, before driving or inserting parts. You will make **a count against the exact guide that separates present, not-yet-found, genuinely missing and mismatched parts**. This counts only. It does not assemble with tools, authorise substitute parts or fit load-bearing, anti-tip or other protective components.

### Preparation and inputs

Check that the exact item model, revision or package label matches your instructions; find the right guide if not. Gather every delivered package and compare any “box n of total” marks. Parts may be in small bags, folds or another carton, but inspect only places you may reach safely and ask capable help for a large piece. Keep original bags and labels; do not pour lookalike hardware into one pile.

### Execution

1. **List what should exist.** From the exact guide's parts page, write the part name or number, required count and relevant package. Where a picture is illustrative, rely on stated identifiers and quantities. Keep any load-bearing or anti-tip work requiring a professional separate; “not installed yet” does not mean the item is safe to use.
2. **Search package by package.** Compare carton labels and inspect arrived boxes, hardware bags and the manufacturer's marked or safely visible compartments. For each find, record actual location, number or visible feature and count. Leave unidentified pieces out of a lookalike category.
3. **Compare count and condition.** For each row mark “seen and matched / package still unsearched / seen but wrong or damaged / all checked and still absent.” A missing bag is not proof of a missing part before the boxes are checked, and matching total count does not resolve two mixed-up part types.
4. **Stop the affected next step.** Keep the original packaging and exact difference when a key part is absent, damaged or unidentified. Do not force an assembly step that depends on it. Later supplier contact uses the real service route; whether an independent step may continue depends on the exact instructions.
5. **Hand over a usable count.** Have the person assembling read back each part ID, quantity, location and open question. Keep small items in original bags or clear separated labels so the count itself does not create a new disappearance.

### Success and stop

All delivered packages were checked, and each expected part has an observed count and state. “Complete” means complete against this exact guide only, not assembled or structurally safe. A package not delivered, wrong guide, damaged or missing key part, or unresolved professional fixing requirement keeps preparation incomplete. Do not substitute a similarly sized piece.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Total count matches but one ID is short and another extra:** Keep separate rows and recheck bag labels. A total cannot cancel a mismatch.
- **A part seems missing but another carton is closed:** Check the external package count and arrival status, then inspect cartons you can reach safely.
- **Pictures look alike but IDs are unreadable:** Enlarge the exact guide or ask a trusted readback; do not force a similar object into a step.
- **A board lies under a heavy stack:** Mark not checked and ask suitable help. Do not flip large pieces alone for a count.
- **A part is not in the list:** Store it separately with its source noted and ask the maker. Do not assume it is a spare for an arbitrary step.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is a pre-count for ordinary low-risk, non-structural assembly permitted by the manufacturer. Bag IDs, picture scale and multi-carton packing differ by brand; follow the exact guide. This Skill cannot waive anti-tip or wall-fixing requirements. For limited sight, reading or grip, use large print, enlarged photos and a trusted person to read parts back; you need not move heavy cartons. An “extra screw” becomes a fact to check instead of a presumed bonus.
