## 中文

当普通用品的外表面到了厂家建议的护理时点，或出现可按**准确型号和材料说明**处理的轻微普通使用痕迹时加载。你要在不拆开的前提下做一轮获准的外部保养，观察是否仍能按原用途使用；不把它当作修理、消毒危险污染或恢复结构安全的承诺。普通房间台面清洁另按该表面的流程，本 Skill 只处理用品本身与它的专属护理要求。

### 准备与输入

核对实物型号、不同材质/饰面和厂家用户护理章节；同一用品的织物、金属、木面或塑料可能要不同方法。把说明允许的用品、数量、使用位置、是否需等待/干燥和禁用条件逐项记下。看不到准确说明或状态需要打开外壳、进入内部、处理带电/燃气/机械部位时停在查资料/专业支持，不凭通用清洁口诀操作。

### 执行

1. **先分区域看实物状态。** 按准确说明区分各外表面和接缝，记普通灰尘、浅痕以及可见裂开、松动、异常气味或材料破损。后几项不是“多擦几遍”能证实修好的；需要安全判断时暂停使用并联系厂家/专业人员。
2. **只选对这一面获准的方法。** 对照材料护理页和用品标签，确认清洁材料、是否可接触水、怎样施加以及周边另一材质是否必须避开。不混用化学品，也不把某品牌给塑料的配方搬到木面或另一型号。
3. **按说明做小范围外部处理。** 只触及正常可见、可安全够到的外面；需要先试在隐蔽处时遵循厂家允许条件。不要把液体喷进缝、按钮、线口或内部，也不拆下未知罩盖。按准确说明轻处理、必要时移去残留，不用硬刷/溶剂追顽固痕迹。
4. **达到说明要求的结束状态。** 依材料说明擦干、等待或保持通风，直到允许重新使用；没有给出条件时不自行发明“过几分钟就行”。检查表面没有新增变色、发粘、起皮或可见功能异常，有这些变化就停并记录所用方法向厂商求助。
5. **留下一条下次可用的记录。** 记用品型号、处理的材料区、实际使用的方法、日期、仍未解决的痕迹或需专业查看的状态。若同一用品还有别的材质，它们要按各自章节另核对；一次外观护理不代表内部或结构已安全。

### 完成与停止

获准的外表面按准确材料说明处理并达到可使用的干燥/等待状态，没有可见新损害；异常与未知仍被记录并转合适渠道。没有匹配说明、需拆开或触电/燃气/机械风险、顽固或危险污染、裂件/松动安全疑虑时停止，不用更强清洁剂或自行维修升级任务。

### 常见报错与补救

- **同一把椅子的不同材质被喷了同一种东西：** 停下，查每块材料的准确护理页；已有变化时拍必要事实并问厂家，不再叠加新药剂。
- **顽固印子擦不掉：** 保留它并问产品允许的专门办法，不靠磨砂或强溶剂试运气。
- **有水渗进接缝或部件异常：** 停止使用受影响物，按厂家说明联系支持，不打开外壳烘干或维修。
- **不知道用品具体饰面：** 先确认型号、材料/饰面名称，未知就不做可能损伤的处理。
- **表面看起来干了但说明要求更久：** 依准确说明继续等待，不以触摸感觉代替规定条件。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只做厂家允许的普通外部护理，不能代替 D09 的居室表面清洁、H114 的防刮垫或任何维修。具体地区产品配方、材料和护理版本不同，不提供通用清洁剂比例。视力、阅读或握持不便时可由可信者读回材料与说明，使用合适协助而不是危险翻动。副作用是“全都喷同一瓶”可能被准确说明驳回，用品不必承担那次实验。

## English

Load this Skill when you reach an ordinary item's maker-recommended exterior care point or notice a minor everyday mark that its **exact model and material guide** says you may address. You will do one permitted exterior care pass without opening the item and observe whether it remains usable for its ordinary purpose. This is not a promise of repair, hazardous decontamination or structural safety. Generic room-surface cleaning has its own task; this follows the item's own finish instructions.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the actual model, its distinct materials or finishes and the maker's user care section. Fabric, metal, wood and plastic on one item may need different treatment. Note allowed supplies, amount, location, waiting or drying and prohibitions. If the exact guide is unavailable or the condition calls for opening a housing or reaching electrical, gas or mechanical internals, stop for information or qualified support instead of applying a generic cleaning rule.

### Execution

1. **Map real exterior surfaces and condition.** Under the exact guide, separate material areas and seams. Note ordinary dust or light marks, but also visible cracking, looseness, unusual smell or material damage. More wiping cannot prove the latter repaired. Pause use and ask maker or qualified help when safety is uncertain.
2. **Choose only a method approved for that surface.** Compare the material care page and product label for cleaner, water contact, application and protection of an adjacent finish. Do not mix chemicals or carry a recipe from one maker's plastic finish to another model's wood.
3. **Treat a small ordinary exterior area as directed.** Touch only normally visible, safely reachable exterior. If the maker permits a hidden-spot trial, follow that condition. Do not spray into seams, buttons, cable openings or internals, and do not remove unknown covers. Treat gently under the exact guide, clearing residue where directed rather than scrubbing a stubborn mark with solvent or abrasion.
4. **Reach the guide's finish state.** Dry, wait or ventilate as the material guide directs until use is allowed. Do not invent a “few minutes” rule when conditions are missing. Check for new discolouration, tackiness, peeling or visible abnormal function. Stop and tell the maker what was used if any appears.
5. **Leave a retrievable care note.** Record model, material area, actual method, date and any unresolved mark or condition needing a qualified look. Another finish on the same item still needs its own section; exterior care does not certify internal or structural safety.

### Success and stop

Permitted exterior areas were treated under the exact material guide and reached their use-ready drying or waiting state without visible new damage. Unknowns and abnormalities remain recorded for appropriate help. Stop rather than escalating cleaners or attempting repair when guidance is unmatched, opening or live/gas/mechanical access is needed, contamination is hazardous, or cracks or looseness suggest a safety issue.

### Common errors and recovery

- **One spray was used across several materials on a chair:** Stop and read each material's exact care section. If anything changed, record facts and ask the maker rather than adding another chemical.
- **A stubborn mark remains:** Leave it and ask about the permitted specific method; do not gamble on abrasion or stronger solvent.
- **Liquid entered a seam or a part behaves abnormally:** Stop using the affected item and contact maker support under its guidance. Do not open a housing to dry or repair it.
- **The exact finish is unknown:** Confirm model and material or finish name before a treatment that could damage it.
- **It feels dry but the guide requires longer:** Wait under the exact instruction; touch is not a substitute for its condition.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Only maker-permitted ordinary exterior care belongs here. This does not replace room-surface cleaning, a passive floor pad or repair. Product formulae, materials and guide versions differ by region; there is no universal cleaner ratio. A trusted person can read materials and directions back or help without a dangerous flip when sight, reading or grip is limited. “One spray for everything” may be rejected by the real guide, sparing the item that experiment.
