## 中文

当几个人会同时在厨房处理生肉、禽、海鲜或生蛋，以及无需再烹调即可入口的食物时，在碰食材前加载。你的任务是分清**谁负责哪个工作区、器具何时可交给下一人、谁确认清洁完成**。这不是教切肉、熟度或消毒液配制；无法安全分开与清洁时，先改计划，不靠一句“小心点”继续。

### 准备与输入

根据已定菜单，圈出生食接触任务和即食/已熟食物任务。确认谁会处理它们、可用的砧板、刀、盘、台面和洗手/洗涤位置，以及哪种工具必须共用。身体或感官条件影响谁能安全搬动和清洗时，参与者自行说明可承担部分，不指定一个人永久做“脏活”。

### 执行

1. **画两个边界。** 给生食接触区和即食/已熟食物区分别标出工具、台面和负责人。用两个实物区域或清楚的先后时段均可，但不能让同一未清洁工具在两区来回借用。
2. **标出手与物的转换点。** 写明谁在接触生肉、禽、海鲜、生蛋或其汁液后先洗手，再碰即食物品；谁把用过的器具和盘子送去清洗。不要把生食盘直接递给负责上桌的人。
3. **指定清理和验收人。** 对每个会改作即食用途的共用台面、板、刀和盘，写“前一负责人停止使用 → 按适用指导彻底洗净 → 下一负责人确认可用”。洗过的事实要可观察，不凭擦一眼或换个方向继续。
4. **检查一套工具的情况。** 只有一套工具时，先安排生食与即食任务不重叠，再确保清洗条件和时间确实存在；不能建立可靠转换，就改用独立工具、调整菜单或暂停即食处理。
5. **给开工口令。** 两位负责人各自说出自己的区域、不能跨过的边界和交接信号；任何人发现生食汁液进入即食区，先停那一区的工作，按当地适用食品安全指导处理，不由本 Skill 宣告食物安全。

### 成功与停止

开工前你有一张“区域/工具 → 负责人 → 清洁转换 → 接手确认”的图或文字。没有洗手和洗涤条件、无人承担转换，或无法确认污染范围时，停止即食任务并重新设计流程。表格是防错约定，不是对已做食物的安全证明。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两人都拿同一块板：** 立即暂停共用，把板的当前用途标清；安排另一块或完成适用清洗后再转用途。
- **工具看起来干净：** 视觉状态不代替清洗记录；负责清理的人复述已完成的步骤。
- **生食区的盘子被拿去上桌：** 停止使用该盘，另用干净器具；涉及食物是否还能用，按当地权威指导判断。
- **有人看不清区域标记：** 用位置、文字、触感或口头回读双重标识；区分仍不可靠就改为不同时处理。

### 假设、替代与副作用

假设这是普通家庭备餐，有可用的洗手和器具清洗条件；当地食品安全机构和具体食材说明仍决定实际处理要求。空间小、颜色辨认困难、握持或站立不方便时，优先采用物理分区、明确标记或不重叠时段，并让合适的人负责搬动，不用单靠颜色暗号。副作用是清洗和等待被写进计划，而不是被假装不存在。

## English

Load this Skill **before food handling** when several people will work around raw meat, poultry, seafood or eggs and food that will be eaten without further cooking. Your endpoint is clear ownership of **each prep area, when tools can change hands, and who confirms cleaning**. This does not teach knife work, doneness or mixing sanitiser. If separation and cleaning cannot be made reliable, change the plan instead of saying “just be careful.”

### Preparation and inputs

Use the chosen menu to mark tasks touching raw food and tasks touching ready-to-eat or cooked food. Identify the people, boards, knives, plates, counters and handwashing/utensil-washing access available, plus any tool that must be shared. If mobility or sensory access affects lifting or cleaning, have each participant state what they can safely do. Do not assign one person every “dirty” task by default.

### Execution

1. **Draw two boundaries.** Give raw-contact and ready-to-eat/cooked areas their tools, surfaces and owners. Two physical areas or clearly separated time blocks can work, but an uncleaned tool cannot travel back and forth between them.
2. **Mark hand and object transitions.** Name who washes hands after raw meat, poultry, seafood, eggs or their juices before touching ready-to-eat items, and who takes used tools and plates for washing. A raw-food plate must not be passed straight to the serving owner.
3. **Own cleaning and acceptance.** For a shared counter, board, knife or plate that will change to ready-to-eat use, write “previous owner stops → wash thoroughly under applicable guidance → next owner confirms usable.” Completion must be observable; a quick look or turning the board over is not a handoff.
4. **Check the one-tool case.** With a single set of tools, arrange raw and ready-to-eat work in non-overlapping periods and ensure actual washing access and time. If that transition cannot be reliable, use separate tools, change the menu or pause ready-to-eat work.
5. **Say the start cue.** Each owner states their area, boundary and handoff signal. If anyone notices raw juices entering the ready-to-eat area, pause that area's work and follow applicable local food-safety guidance. This Skill does not certify the affected food as safe.

### Success and stop

Before cooking, you have a map of “area/tools → owner → cleaning transition → receiving check.” Stop ready-to-eat work and redesign when handwashing/cleaning access is absent, nobody owns the transition, or the extent of contact cannot be determined. The map is an error-prevention agreement, not a certificate for food already prepared.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Both cooks reach for the same board:** Pause shared use and mark its current role. Get another board or complete applicable cleaning before switching use.
- **A tool looks clean:** Appearance is not a washing record. Have the cleaning owner state what was done.
- **A raw-food plate reaches the serving area:** Stop using that plate and use clean equipment. Use local authority guidance to judge any food that may have been affected.
- **Someone cannot distinguish area markers:** Add position, text, touch or a spoken readback. If separation remains unreliable, use non-overlapping work periods.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes ordinary home prep with handwashing and utensil-washing access. Local food-safety authorities and the actual ingredients still govern handling. In a small kitchen, or where colour, grip or standing is difficult, prefer physical spacing, clear multi-sense labels or separated times; an appropriate person handles lifting. The side effect is that washing and waiting appear in the plan instead of pretending to be free.
