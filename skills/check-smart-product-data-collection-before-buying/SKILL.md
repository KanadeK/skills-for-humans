## 中文

当你考虑买普通联网家用品，却看不出商品和配套 App 会收集哪些家庭或个人信息时，付款前加载。任务是得到**针对你在意的功能，哪些数据必需、哪些可选、会共享给谁、哪些仍不清楚**的简短记录。这里不登录测试、不输入任何人的资料，也不审计企业是否符合法律。儿童用品、医疗用途、安防监控及需要专业隐私评估的情形不在范围内。

### 准备与输入

确认准确型号、销售地区、正式 App 名称和你要用的功能，例如“定时开关一盏灯”。列出你特别在意的资料类型，如位置、声音、家中活动、联系人或使用记录。若你不愿提供某类资料，先写下来；不用先同意权限才能提问。

### 执行

1. **找到对应的正式说明。** 查制造商产品页、隐私说明及配套 App 的资料收集摘要，核对商品、App 和发布日期。只覆盖品牌集团的总政策而没有说明这款商品时，继续问具体范围。
2. **分开商品与手机。** 写下商品自身的传感器或记录，和 App 想访问的手机权限；“手机有麦克风”不等于这款商品必然记录声音，也不代表它绝不记录。
3. **按功能区分必需与可选。** 问“要完成〔功能〕必须收集什么？哪些只是个性化、分析或额外服务？”查看官方文字是否支持不启用可选项；没有说明就标为未知，不凭一个开关的存在推断后台行为。
4. **核对共享与控制。** 找到资料可能交给哪些机构或服务、用于什么目的，以及店方明确提供的关闭或删除路径。只记录查到的选项；本次不创建账号、不上传样本、不现场改设置。
5. **写一页购前结论。** 列“功能 → 必需资料 → 可选资料 → 已说明的共享对象/目的 → 可用控制 → 未知”。若关键资料或控制方式仍不明，而这会改变你的决定，就暂停购买或向制造商正式渠道索取解释。

### 成功与停止

你能说出这款商品为了你选定的普通功能会要求哪些信息、哪些分享可选择，以及哪些尚无依据；或停在“未说明”。这不是隐私安全认证，也不保证产品未来不会改变政策。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只找到另一款 App 的说明：** 先核对正式 App 名称和版本；不能对应就标未知。
- **销售人员只说“很安全”：** 请其指出具体收集、共享和控制说明；形容词不能替代资料。
- **隐私页把所有产品写在一起：** 问制造商哪些条目适用这款型号及你要用的功能。
- **要安装并同意才能看到详情：** 先查公开正式说明或问制造商；不为了获取购前信息提前交出数据。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 是普通消费前的信息整理，不涉及法律合规判断、渗透测试或账号操作。政策和配套 App 会变，记录查询日期；英国信息专员办公室的材料也注明正在审阅，不能当作其他地区的法律。阅读、听说或联网不方便时，请商家提供可读文字、纸本或可信同伴协助，但关键条款仍需与你买的型号对应。副作用是一个小小的数据清单，可能比商品盒子长。

## English

Load this Skill before paying for an ordinary connected household product when you cannot tell what household or personal information the item and its companion app will collect. Your endpoint is a short record of **what data your chosen function needs, what is optional, who it may be shared with, and what remains unclear**. You do not sign in, provide anyone's data, or audit legal compliance. Children's products, medical uses, security surveillance, and cases needing specialist privacy assessment are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the exact model, sales region, official companion app, and an ordinary function you want, such as scheduling one lamp. List information categories you care about, such as location, sound, activity at home, contacts, or usage history. Note any category you are unwilling to share before looking at consent screens; agreeing to permissions is not a prerequisite for asking.

### Execution

1. **Find the matching formal information.** Read the maker's product page, privacy notice, and app data-collection summary. Check the product, app, and publication date. If a company-wide policy does not say which terms cover this item, ask for the specific scope.
2. **Separate product from phone.** List sensors or records on the item separately from phone permissions requested by its app. A phone having a microphone does not prove this product records sound, or prove that it never does.
3. **Separate necessary and optional by feature.** Ask “What data must be collected for [function], and what is for personalisation, analysis, or extra services?” Look for formal support for declining optional collection. If it is not explained, mark it unknown; a visible toggle alone does not prove what happens behind it.
4. **Check sharing and controls.** Find the named organisations or services that may receive data, their stated purposes, and any documented shutoff or deletion route. Record only what you can inspect. Do not make an account, upload a sample, or change settings for this pre-purchase check.
5. **Keep one-page results.** Write “function → necessary data → optional data → stated recipients/purposes → available controls → unknowns.” If a decisive collection or control remains unclear, defer or ask the manufacturer through an official channel.

### Success and stop

You can state what this item asks to collect for your chosen ordinary function, what sharing is optional, and what lacks evidence; or stop at “not explained.” This is not a privacy-security certificate and cannot promise the policy will never change.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only another app's notice appears:** Match the official app name and edition. Keep this one unknown if they differ.
- **Sales staff say only “it's secure”:** Request the specific collection, sharing, and control description. An adjective is not a data map.
- **One policy covers every product in a brand:** Ask which entries apply to this model and function.
- **Details appear only after installation and consent:** Seek public formal information or ask the maker first. Do not surrender data merely to obtain pre-purchase details.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This organises ordinary pre-purchase information; it does not determine legal compliance, test the product's security, or operate an account. Policies and companion apps change, so record the date. The UK Information Commissioner's page says it is under review and is not law for other regions. If reading, hearing, speaking, or internet access is difficult, request accessible written or paper information or help from a trusted companion while still matching the exact model. A side effect is a small data list that may be longer than the box.
