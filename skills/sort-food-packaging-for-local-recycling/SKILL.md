## 中文

当你吃完或做完一餐，手上有**食品包装、纸盒、瓶罐或外卖容器**，要决定哪些进入回收桶时，用这份 Skill 按**实际收运方**当前清单判断并处理食物残留。塑料底部树脂编号、回收箭头或“环保”字样不证明本地项目会收；食品碎屑与剩菜先走厨余/垃圾和保存流程。本篇不处理电池、药品、危险化学容器或锋利破损玻璃。

### 准备与输入

查市政、物业、楼宇或收运方的实际区域、投放模式和**当前**准入清单，包括材质、盖、纸盒涂层、食品污染、是否需压平/散放、袋子能否一起投入。确认包装不是退还押金/商家回收等另一个路径。准备可放确定回收、明确垃圾和待查三个位置，必要时用可安全控制的清水去食物残留。规则查不到时保留待查，不让不确定材料污染整桶。

### 执行

1. **先把食物与包装分开。** 可安全保留食物按既有食品流程，不可食碎屑按当地厨余/垃圾规则；包装尽量清空。不要将满是汤汁的盒子倒进纸类或回收桶。
2. **按当地项目核对整件。** 分别看主体、盖、薄膜、纸标签、复合材料是否都被接收；只有明确接受的部分按规则投放。树脂编号只识别材料，不保证收运。某地接受带油披萨盒不代表另一地也接受；以现场清单为准。
3. **达到该项目要求的清洁状态。** 对被接收的瓶罐/容器，按当地说明刮净或适量冲去残留，再沥干；纸类按当地要求去食物并保持干燥。无需为了一个不可接收物把它洗到闪亮，也不能用喷洗把食物汁液溅到干净餐具。
4. **处理不接收与危险例外。** 本地拒收或仍不明的普通包装走当地垃圾/待确认路径，不能靠“我希望它被回收”入桶；电池、电子产品、药品、清洁剂瓶中残液、尖锐破损玻璃等查专门收集途径，不按普通包装处理。
5. **做投放前最后检查。** 依当地要求散放、压平或分类，不擅自把整包回收物套在不允许的塑料袋中；检查没有剩食、液体外漏或明显混进垃圾。封好/安放收集单元，之后由垃圾移出任务交给真正的收集点。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

进入回收单元的都是实际本地项目明确接受、处理到它要求状态的材料；拒收/未确认/危险例外有另一个明确去向。需要查规则而暂不投放，是比错误投放更完整的结果。

- **包装上有回收三角就直接丢桶：** 查材质和本地接受表，标志只帮你识别，不替收运方承诺。
- **纸盒沾满食物和油水：** 先按当地纸类规则判断；不能清理到被接受状态就不混入干纸回收。
- **塑料盖和瓶身不是同一种材质：** 按当地规则分别判断，不能让瓶身资格自动覆盖盖子。
- **回收袋底部流出汤汁：** 暂停移出，处理泄漏及受污染材料，重建干净、不漏的投放方式。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只处理普通餐后包装，不判断地方行政处罚或商业回收合同。各地收什么、是否洗/压平/分袋会改变，中文或英文不等于任何国家规则。读不清包装标识时可拍照放大、查本地收运方可及说明；手部力量有限时分小批处理锋利罐边，不能安全拿破玻璃就停。可能多留一小组待查物品，下一步要真实交给收运点。

## English

Use this Skill when a meal leaves **food packages, cartons, bottles, cans, or takeout containers** and you must decide what enters recycling. Use the **actual local collector's** current list and food-residue rules. A plastic resin number, arrow logo, or “eco” claim does not prove the program accepts an item. Scraps and edible leftovers have their own food routes. This does not process batteries, medicine, hazardous chemical containers, or broken sharp glass.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the current municipality, property, or hauler list for this area and collection method: materials, caps, coated paper, food soil, flattening, loose collection, and allowed bags. Check whether the package follows a deposit-return or store take-back route instead. Make three spaces for confirmed recycling, ordinary trash, and uncertain items. Use only controllable water for food residue when needed. If you cannot find rules, hold the item for checking rather than contaminate a whole bin with guesses.

### Execution

1. **Separate food from package first.** Keep food with a verified safe use under the existing food workflow; route inedible scraps by local organics or trash rules. Empty packaging as far as practical. Do not pour a soup-filled box into paper recycling.
2. **Check the whole item under the local program.** Evaluate body, cap, film, paper label, and composites separately. Place only clearly accepted parts as directed. A resin number identifies material, not collection eligibility. Acceptance of a greasy pizza box in one area is not permission in another.
3. **Meet the program's actual cleanliness rule.** For accepted bottles or containers, scrape or rinse food residue as instructed and let liquid drain. Remove food and keep paper dry as required locally. Do not use water to polish an unaccepted object, or spray food liquid onto clean dishes.
4. **Route rejections and hazardous exceptions.** Ordinary packages rejected or still unclear go to local trash or hold-for-check, not into recycling on hope. Batteries, electronics, medicine, leftover cleaner, and broken sharp glass need their own collection guidance rather than the ordinary package bin.
5. **Make a final pre-drop check.** Follow local loose, flattened, or separate requirements. Do not tie recyclables inside an unaccepted plastic bag. Check for remaining food, leaking liquid, or obvious trash. Close or stage the unit; the later waste-handoff task takes it to an actual collection point.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Every item in recycling is explicitly accepted by the actual local service and prepared to its standards. Rejected, uncertain, and hazardous exceptions have other destinations. Holding an item to check rules is more complete than a wrong deposit.

- **A recycling triangle sends an item straight into the bin:** Check material and local acceptance. The mark identifies; the collector decides.
- **A carton is soaked in food and grease:** Use the actual paper rule. Do not mix it with dry paper if it cannot reach the accepted condition.
- **Bottle and cap are different materials:** Assess both under local rules; body permission does not transfer to the cap.
- **Soup leaks from the recycling bag:** Pause collection, handle the leak and contaminated material, and use a clean nonleaking route.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary post-meal packaging, not fines or commercial recycling contracts. Collection, rinsing, flattening, and bag rules vary by locality; Chinese or English does not identify one country. Enlarge small marks or use an accessible local hauler guide. Work in small batches around sharp can edges if grip is limited; stop if broken glass cannot be handled safely. A hold-for-check group may remain and the accepted items still need actual collection.
