## 中文

当你已把可安全同烘的一组普通湿衣放进**确切型号已知的烘干机**，需要选择程序、热量等级和目标干燥状态时，用这份 Skill。它不替代入机前筛选，也不把面板上的“棉”“轻柔”“智能”当成所有品牌等价命令。没有准确说明书或衣物标签仍不明，就暂停开机。

### 准备与输入

确认组内最严格的滚筒热处理限制、轻柔要求、最大负载与该批厚薄差异。找到本机完整型号、对应程序表、自动感应/定时模式定义、可调干燥等级以及暂停/结束方法；公共机器同时读现场规则。确认过滤网在位、机器无异常警示，电源/排风状态按本机正常使用说明。需要无障碍帮助时先找到可读面板、说明书或可信现场协助。

### 执行

1. **把最严格衣物限制映射到本机程序。** 阅读本机程序表，找明确允许该组面料与烘干等级的选项。衣物标签禁止的热量、滚筒或力度不能因程序名好听而启用；找不到共同许可时拆成更小组或改走标签允许的自然晾干。
2. **选有依据的干燥目标。** 若本机提供感应干燥、干燥程度或定时模式，按说明选择适合这组衣物及打算穿用/收存的目标；不能把某机器的“Normal/More Dry”换算成另一台的固定分钟数。标签或设备没有足够信息时不要猜统一高温。
3. **再核对负载和门。** 衣物能按本机要求自由翻动，门关好，过滤网已正确装回；过大、厚薄极不均或夹带不可烘物时返回筛选。看不到关键设置时不靠记忆摸索高热按钮。
4. **启动并留意正常信号。** 按说明启动，确认显示/旋钮进入预期程序且无异常声音、烟、焦味或排风警示。出现异常立刻按说明停用并联系管理者/专业人员；火灾或明显危险按当地紧急指导撤离求助，不拆机或继续试跑。
5. **准备真实结束检查。** 记录本机的结束提醒方式，结束后实际摸查厚处与各类衣物；传感器或铃声只提示检查。未干与过热风险分别判断，不能让机器自行替你决定全部衣物可直接进柜。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

所选程序和干燥目标能从**这批最严格衣物标签与这台机器说明**逐项解释，机器正常启动且有结束检查计划。没有两边都支持的设置时，停止开机并改组或晾干也算完成判断。

- **程序叫 Delicate 但说明热量未知：** 查准确型号的程序表，不能从英文名猜温度。
- **只有一件衣物限制更低：** 取出另组或自然晾，不让它靠少数服从多数。
- **定时结束就认为必干：** 进入实际衣物检查，未干时按机型和标签判断下一步。
- **警示灯或焦味出现：** 停用并求助，不能增加分钟数、清零报警或拆开排风部件尝试“修好”。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇假设设备完好、正确安装且说明书可得；某型号示例不能外推到热泵、排风式、洗烘一体或公共设备。视力、听力、触觉或操作能力不同，可用大字手册、手机放大、厂商允许的可及界面或可信现场协助；若无法确认关键设定就停止。耗电、机器占用和之后逐件检查是真实成本。

## English

Use this Skill after a compatible group of ordinary wet clothes is screened and loaded into a dryer with a **known exact model**. Choose a documented cycle, heat severity, and dryness target. This does not replace the pre-load screen, and panel names such as Cotton, Delicate, or Smart do not mean the same thing on every brand. Pause if the exact manual or a garment limit is still unknown.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the group's strictest tumble heat and gentle-action limits, load maximum, and differences in thickness. Find the full model, its cycle guide, automatic/sensor and timed-mode definitions, adjustable dryness levels, and safe pause/end process. Read site rules for shared machines. Confirm a fitted lint filter, no unresolved alert, and normal power and vent status under this manual. Arrange accessible panel or manual reading or trusted in-person help before starting if needed.

### Execution

1. **Map the strictest label to this model's cycles.** Read its cycle guide for an option explicitly suited to the fabrics and severity limit. A friendly program name does not override prohibited tumble, heat, or action. If no one setting fits, split the load or use label-allowed air drying.
2. **Choose a supported dryness target.** If the model offers sensor drying, dryness levels, or timed mode, use its definitions for this group and your intended wear or storage. Another machine's Normal or More Dry does not convert into a fixed number of minutes here. Do not guess one high-heat setting when label or manual evidence is missing.
3. **Recheck load and door.** Clothing has room to tumble by this model's directions, the door is closed, and its lint filter is reinstalled correctly. Return to screening for bulk, mixed thickness, or a no-tumble item. Do not feel around for an unfamiliar high-heat control when a critical display is inaccessible.
4. **Start and observe normal operation.** Follow the manual, confirm the intended cycle started, and watch for unusual noise, smoke, burning odour, or vent alert. Stop and contact the site manager or qualified service for an abnormal machine. For fire or immediate danger, follow local emergency instructions and leave safely; do not dismantle or keep test-running it.
5. **Plan a real finish check.** Note how this model signals completion, then inspect the actual thick areas and different garments. A sensor or chime calls for a check, not permission to put everything straight into a cupboard. Remaining dampness and heat damage need separate decisions.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You can explain the selected cycle and dryness target from **this group's strictest labels and this exact manual**. The machine starts normally and a physical end check is planned. When no setting satisfies both, stopping and regrouping or air drying is complete.

- **Delicate is named but its heat is undocumented:** Find the exact model's cycle guide; the English name is not a temperature measurement.
- **One item has a lower limit:** Remove it for another group or natural drying rather than putting it to a vote.
- **The timed cycle ends, so you assume all are dry:** Inspect the garments. Decide on continuation only under this model and the labels.
- **An alert or burning smell appears:** Stop and get appropriate help. Do not add minutes, clear alerts without cause, or dismantle vent parts to “fix” it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes a properly installed undamaged machine with its exact manual. One model's example does not govern heat-pump, vented, combined washer-dryer, or shared equipment. Use large-print manuals, phone magnification, manufacturer-supported accessible controls, or trusted in-person help when displays, sound, touch, or controls are difficult. Stop if a critical setting cannot be confirmed. Energy use, machine occupancy, and later item-by-item checks are real costs.
