## 中文

当**已有室内兔厕**按日常计划需要清走湿脏垫料并恢复可用时，加载这份 Skill。结果是兔有可随时到达的干净厕所、原有安全非结团垫料正确补回，实际粪便变化有记录。它与 E005 猫砂盆不共用猫砂：结团、膨胀、香味或泥土样猫砂可能不适合兔。兔若停止吃或不排新粪，立即联系兽医，不把清盆当治疗。

### 准备与输入

核原主人/兽医给这只兔的厕位、托盘数量与垫料类型；多兔应有足够能分别使用的入口，清一盆期间不要让所有兔无厕可用。备手套、垫料、清洁袋/桶、托盘可用清洁用品和水；清洁剂须能彻底冲净、托盘干后再铺。兔有异常软便、血迹、尾部脏污或无新粪时记录并立即/及时咨询兽医，不自行判断病因。

### 执行

1. **先看粪与可用性。** 在原安全区对照平常排便间隔看是否继续有新粪，再看湿垫料和可到达的另一厕所；较其平常停止排新粪就立即联系兽医，不能继续只做普通清理。兔不因你清理被强抱到危险地面。
2. **移走湿脏料而不扬尘。** 戴手套轻清便尿污染的纸/获准垫料和旧草，按当地普通废物规定装袋；不要把兔粪投入 E009 的普通庭院堆肥。若容器可安全整体拿出，先让另一合用托盘继续开放。
3. **按本托盘说明洗、冲、干。** 用兔/容器允许的温和清洁方式去残留，彻底冲净并擦/晾干，避免强烈香味或未冲净化学剂。裂边、尖角或不能干的托盘不直接放回。
4. **按原布局恢复厕位。** 铺回原方案允许的纸质非结团/不膨胀垫料、可取用干草保持在平常位置；重新放到兔熟悉且离休息处合适的地方，检查没有堵入口。脱手套洗手，记清理时间和观察到的粪便状态。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

兔始终有可用厕所入口，旧湿脏料已移走，新料安全、托盘干净干燥、兔可达；异常粪/食欲有兽医转介。只换了表层纸、尿湿深层和强清洁剂仍在，不算。

- **只有这一盆且兔马上要用：** 先备获准临时替代，缩短离位时间，不把厕所封闭数小时。
- **误拿结团/香味猫砂：** 不用，核兔方案和安全替代。
- **清洁剂闻得到或盆未干：** 冲洗/干燥后再铺料。
- **兔不吃、没有新粪或出现血/严重污尾：** 立即联系兽医，记录末次正常状态。
- **手眼/搬运受限：** 小份清理、低位托盘和可信协助洗盆，保留兔的可及性与实际记录。

### 假设与边界

兔的厕所习惯、伴兔数量与安全垫料随机体和既有环境而定。RSPCA 与 PDSA 的材料清单是可选例，不代替原兽医计划；这篇不是医疗分诊，也不承诺清盆即可防病。

## English

Load this Skill when an **established indoor rabbit toilet** needs routine removal of wet litter and return to service. Finish with a clean constantly accessible tray, approved non-clumping material restored, and actual dropping changes recorded. It is not interchangeable with E005 cat litter: clumping, expanding, scented, or clay cat products may not suit rabbits. A rabbit that stops eating or produces no new droppings needs an immediate vet call, not litter cleaning as treatment.

### Preparation and inputs

Check this rabbit's owner/vet plan for toilet place, tray count, and material. For a group, preserve enough usable toilet access while one is cleaned. Have gloves, approved litter, waste bag/container, and vessel-compatible cleaner and water. Rinse cleaner entirely and dry tray before new litter. For unusual loose stool, blood, soiled rear, or no new droppings, record and promptly/urgently contact a vet without self-diagnosis.

### Execution

1. **Check droppings and access first.** In the familiar safe area, compare fresh droppings with this rabbit's usual interval, note wet material, and check another toilet is reachable. Stopped production of new droppings compared with normal goes to an immediate vet call, not merely ordinary cleaning. Do not force-hold the rabbit onto an unsafe floor.
2. **Remove wet litter without dust.** With gloves, lift urine/faeces-soiled paper or approved litter and old hay to local ordinary disposal. Do not add rabbit waste to E009's ordinary home compost. If lifting the whole tray safely, keep another suitable toilet available.
3. **Wash, rinse, and dry under tray directions.** Use rabbit- and vessel-compatible gentle cleaner, rinse fully and dry before refilling, avoiding strong fragrance or chemical residue. Do not put back a cracked sharp-edged or still-wet tray.
4. **Restore the familiar toilet.** Add the existing-plan safe paper-based non-clumping/non-expanding litter and accessible hay where normally offered. Replace the tray in its familiar suitable site away from sleeping area, with entrance clear. Remove gloves, wash hands, and note cleaning time and actual droppings.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Rabbits retained toilet access, wet material left, replacement is safe, tray clean/dry and accessible, and abnormal appetite or droppings have a vet route. Changing only the visible top while urine-soaked layers or cleaner remain is unfinished.

- **Only tray is needed immediately:** Arrange a permitted temporary alternative and shorten the unavailable period, rather than closing the toilet for hours.
- **Clumping or perfumed cat litter was brought:** Do not use; check the rabbit plan and suitable replacement.
- **Cleaner smell remains or tray is damp:** Rinse and dry before filling.
- **Rabbit stops eating, has no new droppings, or blood/severe soiling appears:** Contact a vet immediately and give last normal time.
- **Grip, sight, or lifting is limited:** Small portions, low tray, and trusted washing help can preserve access and accurate record.

### Assumptions and limits

Toilet habits, companions, and safe litter differ with the animal and established environment. RSPCA/PDSA materials are options, not a substitute for a vet plan. Cleaning is not diagnosis or guaranteed disease prevention.
