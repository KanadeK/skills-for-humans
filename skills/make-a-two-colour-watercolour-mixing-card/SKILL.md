## 中文

当你想知道手头**两种实际水彩**混在一起会得到什么，而不是凭色名猜，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张干后仍有色名、原色和两种混合比例可回看的小卡。它不是 H271 的实物明暗观察，也不是一幅完整画。只用标签允许的普通水基美术颜料，不把画笔和调色水用于饮食。

### 准备与输入

选两色现有水彩，记下包装上可辨的名称；不需要买新色。用自己的水彩纸画四个小格并留书写处，备清水、稳定杯、画笔和可擦拭台面。若颜料标签、接触警示或使用年龄条件不清，换可确认材料；色觉辨别不便可请人描述干后颜色，同时保留标签和格位。

### 执行

1. **先留两个原色基准。** 分别在两格涂等面积小样，冲净笔后再碰下一色，写下颜料名。水量只要能涂开，不拿湿色当最终颜色判断。
2. **调两种可区分比例。** 在调色处先以第一色较多、第二色较少混一小滴，涂第三格；洗笔后反过来配较多第二色，涂第四格。用“甲多/乙多”等真实记录，不假装称量出精确百分比。
3. **让卡平放干。** 避免水杯泼到卡上，不用吹风机或熨斗催干。干后看两个原色和两个混色格是否仍可区分，把实际观察的颜色词写在旁边；若两格近似也如实记录。
4. **存成可回查卡。** 标日期、纸种或本次所用颜料系列，放到干燥可找回处。下次换纸或换颜料时另做卡，不把这张当全品牌通用色谱。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一张干燥、有两原色、两比例混色与真实标签的卡能被重新辨认。只有湿调色盘里一摊颜料，没有纸面记录，不算参考卡。

- **第二格混入第一色：** 洗净画笔和调色处，重做受污染的小格。
- **格子互相流入：** 先让相邻格干或留更宽空白，不用手指抹开。
- **干后颜色和湿时不同：** 按干后观察改记，不修改颜料名称。
- **两种比例看起来一样：** 记录相同或近似，下一次再拉大比例差；不虚构第三色。
- **手眼或色觉可及性不足：** 用大格、清晰编号和可信颜色描述，判定仍以可回查实物为准。

### 假设与边界

混色卡是个人材料参考，不保证颜色复制、显示器匹配或永久耐光。水彩透明度、纸和干燥会改变结果；品牌示范的格数和模板不作为必需规格。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to see what **two watercolours you actually own** make together instead of guessing from their names. Finish with a dry small card showing both original colours and two distinguishable mix ratios with labels. This is not H271's observed light-and-dark drawing or a complete painting. Use ordinary labelled water-based art paint under its own warnings, and keep brush and paint water away from food.

### Preparation and inputs

Pick two existing labelled colours; no new purchase is needed. Draw four small spaces with writing room on owned watercolour paper. Set out water, a stable cup, brush, palette, and wipeable surface. Replace paint if label, contact warning, or age condition is unclear. If colour discrimination is difficult, trusted description of dry swatches can help while labels and positions stay fixed.

### Execution

1. **Make two original-colour references.** Paint one small equal-sized swatch of each colour, rinsing the brush before touching the next, and write their names. Use enough water to spread paint; do not judge final colour from wet swatches.
2. **Mix two clearly different ratios.** On the palette, use more of colour A than B for the third space. Clean the brush and reverse the balance for the fourth. Record “more A” and “more B” honestly rather than claiming measured percentages.
3. **Dry the card flat.** Keep the water cup from spilling on it; do not rush with a hair dryer or iron. Compare both originals and mixes once dry, writing the colour you actually observe. If two spaces look similar, record that too.
4. **Keep a reusable record.** Date the card, note paper or paint range, and store it somewhere dry and findable. If you change paper or pigment later, make another card rather than treating this one as a universal manufacturer chart.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A dry card has two original swatches, two differently proportioned mixes, and truthful labels you can reread. A puddle only on a wet palette is not a reference card.

- **Second swatch picks up the first pigment:** Wash brush and palette, then repaint the contaminated space.
- **Spaces bleed into one another:** Let neighbouring spaces dry or leave more space instead of rubbing with a finger.
- **Dry colour differs from wet colour:** Update the observation after drying without changing the paint's real name.
- **Two ratios look the same:** Record similar or equal, then try a wider ratio difference another time; do not invent a third colour.
- **Grip, sight, or colour access is limited:** Use larger numbered spaces and trusted colour description while keeping the physical result traceable.

### Assumptions and limits

This is a personal material reference, not a promise of colour replication, display matching, or lightfastness. Paper, transparency, and drying change the result. A brand's template size is not a required specification.
