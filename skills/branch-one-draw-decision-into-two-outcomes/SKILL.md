## 中文

你有一个普通且真正只有两种处理结果的判断，例如“资料齐全吗”，想在 Draw 图上显示两条后续路时使用本篇。成果是一个判断菱形连到两个不同结果节点，路径不交错、没有看似单一路径的误导。这里先建立分叉结构，下一步再把两条线分别标清。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODG 副本，把条件写成可回答的问题，分别写出成立与不成立的可观察下一步。若现实有第三种“未知”状态，不要硬塞进二选一；先改流程合约。留出两个结果节点与连接线的空间。

### 执行

1. 画一个判断形状并写清问题，确认它不是普通动作节点。
2. 在两个方向放不同结果节点，用各一条附着连接器从菱形接出。
3. 逐条追线，核起点都是判断、终点各不相同，线不穿过其它文字。
4. 轻移判断节点核两条线仍跟随；保存重开，缺任一路就不收口。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一个可答问题有两条附着且不同的结果路径，无第三路被误藏。

- **两路指向同节点：** 按实际结果拆成两个终点。
- **一条线脱开：** 重新接到 gluepoint。
- **条件其实不二元：** 停止画图并改判断定义。

### 假设与边界

这里只画普通流程二分结构，不声称业务规则完整。你负责判断条件与结果，不用于危险操作。

## English

Use this Skill for a genuinely two-outcome ordinary decision such as 'Are materials complete?' that needs two next paths in Draw. Finish with one decision diamond connected to two different outcome nodes, paths not confused or falsely shown as one. This builds branch structure; a later check labels each exit.

### Preparation and inputs

Save ODG copy, phrase condition as answerable question, and write observable next actions for true and false. If an unknown third state is real, do not force it into a binary diagram; revise process scope. Reserve space for two destinations and lines.

### Execution

1. Draw one decision shape with the question, distinct from action nodes.
2. Place separate outcome nodes and connect each from the diamond with its own attached connector.
3. Trace each line: both start at decision, reach distinct outcomes and avoid text.
4. Move decision slightly to verify both links follow, then save/reopen. Do not close with one route absent.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One answerable decision has two attached distinct outcome paths, with no hidden third condition.

- **Both reach same node:** Use distinct outcome destinations.
- **One line detaches:** Reconnect its gluepoint.
- **Not binary:** Stop and redefine decision.

### Assumptions and limits

This draws a binary ordinary-process structure, not a complete policy. You own condition and outcomes; exclude hazardous procedures.
