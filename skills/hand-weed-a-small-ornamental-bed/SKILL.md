## 中文

当你在获准照料的小型**非食用观赏花坛**里看见几株已确认不想保留的杂草，需要现在把它们移走时，加载这份 Skill。结果是目标杂草被清走，想留的幼苗和邻株根系未被连带拔出。覆材能抑制之后的杂草，但不能替你核认眼前哪株能拔。

### 准备与输入

先确认地块归属、需要保留的种植清单或标记，以及杂草的可辨特征。认不出幼苗与杂草时先拍照、比对可靠园艺资料或问地块负责人，不靠“长在不对的位置”推断。只用轻型手工具处理可及的小范围；避开尖锐垃圾、未知刺激植物、大型根系和可能有地下管线的区域。准备手套和收集容器。

### 执行

1. **圈定一小片和明确目标。** 指出要留下的株、要移除的株以及两者根区是否交错。若目标紧贴珍贵幼苗且身份不清，今天不拔。
2. **选择土壤状态。** 土略湿时通常较容易带根起出；冻结、积水或特别板结时暂缓，避免大块翻土伤邻株。不要为了拔草灌满整片花坛。
3. **轻松根区。** 用手或小叉在确认的目标旁边松土，托住邻近观赏植株；尽量将目标根部完整取出，不切碎蔓延性根系或拉着邻株一起走。碰到难缠、深根或大片蔓延杂草时记录并求相应园艺办法，不升级成大挖。
4. **收集并复核。** 将拔出的材料放入容器，按当地规则及是否带种子、病虫决定去向，不把可疑材料丢回花坛。核对想保留的幼苗还在、土面稳固，记下需要再观察的根段或新芽。

### 成功条件

这一小片已确认的目标杂草实际移走，保留植株未明显受伤，材料离开花坛或明确暂存，遗漏根段有复查点。身份、使用权或邻株根系风险不明时停止并留记录，不能通过误拔来完成配额。

### 常见报错与补救

- **像杂草的嫩苗没有标签：** 先比对品种或问负责人，暂时保留。
- **两株根缠在一起：** 停止硬拉，改用小叉局部松土或请懂品种的人处理。
- **断根留在土里：** 记录位置复查；不为追一段根大范围挖坏花坛。
- **看见种子已经成熟：** 小心收集，按本地规则处理，别边走边抖散。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一小块获准管理的普通观赏地，不教化学除草、入侵物种控制、食用菜地卫生或大型土方。需要坐姿工作可使用可及的高床或短时分段完成；视力受限时请人指出留与拔的对象。结果是局部清理，其他杂草以后仍可能长出。

## English

Load this Skill when a few **identified unwanted plants** are growing in a permitted small non-edible ornamental bed and you want to remove them now. Finish with the target weeds gone while desired seedlings and nearby roots remain. Mulch may suppress later weeds; it does not identify today's target for you.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the bed's owner, desired planting list or labels, and distinguishing signs of the unwanted plants. If you cannot distinguish a volunteer seedling from a weed, photograph it, check reliable horticultural guidance, or ask the caretaker. Position alone is not identification. Use only light hand tools in a reachable patch, away from sharp waste, unknown irritating plants, large roots, and possible buried utilities. Prepare gloves and a debris container.

### Execution

1. **Bound the patch and targets.** Point out plants to keep and remove, and whether their roots overlap. If a target sits beside a valued seedling and identity is uncertain, do not pull it today.
2. **Check soil condition.** Slightly moist soil usually releases roots more readily; defer frozen, waterlogged, or severely compacted ground to avoid tearing a large area. Do not flood the bed to make weeding easier.
3. **Loosen near the target.** Use your hand or small fork beside a confirmed weed while steadying nearby ornamentals. Lift as much of its root as practical without chopping spreading roots or dragging desired plants out. Record deep, entangled, or extensive weeds and seek appropriate horticultural advice instead of enlarging this into major digging.
4. **Collect and review.** Put removed growth in a container. Follow local rules and consider seeds or pest signs rather than leaving suspect material in the bed. Confirm desired seedlings remain and soil is stable; mark any root fragments or regrowth to review.

### Success

The identified weeds in this small patch are physically removed, desired plants show no obvious damage, debris has left or is safely contained, and remaining roots have a review point. Stop and record uncertainty if identity, authority, or root overlap cannot be resolved; mistaken pulling is not a quota.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An unlabelled seedling looks like a weed:** Compare the species or ask the caretaker and leave it meanwhile.
- **Roots of two plants are intertwined:** Stop pulling; loosen locally with a small fork or seek species-aware help.
- **A root breaks off:** Mark the spot for review. Do not excavate the entire bed for one fragment.
- **Seedheads are already mature:** Contain them carefully under local rules rather than shaking them across the bed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is a small permitted ornamental patch, not chemical weed control, invasive-species management, food-garden sanitation, or earthworks. Use an accessible raised bed or short sessions if bending is difficult; ask someone to identify keep/remove plants if vision is limited. Local cleanup does not prevent all future weeds.
