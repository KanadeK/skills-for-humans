## 中文

你已经有一条公交或铁路行程，但能否完成它取决于**具体站点、车厢/车辆、通道、信息方式或人员协助**时，用这份 Skill 提前核对每一段的真实可用条件，并留出设备变化时的替代。目标不是给自己贴一个“无障碍路线”标签，而是确认“我需要的动作在出发、换乘、到达每一处都有可行方式”，或及时换成可证实的其他行程。普通选车次、票价、步行定位和医疗评估不在这里。

### 准备与输入

先用动作写下你的需求，而非诊断标签，例如：全程无台阶、可坐电梯、不走长距离、上车需要坡板/人员协助、能读到文字变更、能听到报站、需要低位柜台或额外换乘时间。列出行程的出发站/站牌、所有换乘点、到达点、预计时间、各段运营方和实际列车/公交服务。查运营方官方路线、站点可及性页、设备状态与协助规则。只看到一个站名标“可及”不足以证明从入口到站台、上车、下车、出口全链路都可用。

### 执行

1. **把行程拆成可核对的节点。** 对每段标出进入站点/站台、上车、下车、换乘通道、最终出口这些动作。把哪一处必须有电梯、缓坡、低地板、可读/可听信息或人员帮助写在节点旁；另列你能接受的替代方式。别按身份类别假定所有人需要相同设施。
2. **查静态许可和当前状态。** 在每家运营方官方渠道确认该站与服务是否提供所需设备、车辆/车厢或协助，再查临行的电梯停运、站口关闭、改站台、服务绕行。站方地图通常说明布局，实时状态才说明今天可能用不用得上；一处关键电梯失效时，整条所谓“无台阶”路线可能断掉。
3. **需要人员协助就完成双向确认。** 按运营方该服务的当前规则说明日期、班次、出发/换乘/到达节点、所需具体动作与辅助设备；核对是否要预约、会面地点/时间、有人确认的编号或消息。只提交申请而未收到确认，不能把协助记成已安排。无法使用 App 时查电话或现场正式渠道，不让陌生人接管账号或公开个人医疗资料。
4. **算出可退出的替代。** 若某个必要条件未确认或设备临时不可用，先问运营方有没有经过核实的替代站/通道/车辆/协助，并重新查每个节点及换乘时间。不要用楼梯、跨轨、关闭入口或未经确认的“好心人抬一下”作为默认兜底。无法安全走通就停止这条行程、改时或换服务。
5. **临行与途中再读回。** 出发前看一次运营方最新状态，记录关键设备/协助的确认时间、联系渠道和备选路线。到换乘点若实际情况不同，停在安全、可联系工作人员的位置重新核对，不靠原截图强行继续。真正上车、检票或换乘仍按当地运营程序。

### 成功条件

你的具体行程在出发、每次换乘和到达处，都有已核实的必要通道、车辆条件、信息形式与已确认的协助安排；知道何时再查状态及哪项失效时改走哪里。若关键条件无可靠证据，明确停止或换成同样核实过的路线，也是本次正确结果。“规划器显示可及”但没有核对今天的关键节点，不算完成。

### 常见报错与补救

- **路线页面写无台阶，但所需电梯停运：** 不把静态标签当现场保证，查运营方当期替代路线/人员帮助；没有安全方案就改行程。
- **只查出发站，忘了换乘或到达出口：** 补齐全链路节点；任一必须动作不可行，整趟仍待确认。
- **已提交协助但没有会面确认：** 联系运营方核对请求状态、地点、时间与编号，未确认就不要依赖这项协助。
- **车厢/站台临时更动：** 在安全位置读新公告并向站方确认所需通道或协助是否跟着改；不沿原图去封闭区。
- **App 不可读或听不清广播：** 使用运营方提供的文字、音频、人工或其他可及渠道交叉确认，不让一种信息形式成为唯一失败点。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是一趟普通公共交通的可行性核对，不保证设备永不故障，也不判断医疗资格、残障认定、法定权利或票务赔偿。运营方、日期、车站、具体车次/车辆、辅助设备和协助机制必须按实际路线核实。TfL、National Rail、NS 的官方工具只是可参考的核对实例；无智能手机时用运营方电话、站内公告或人员渠道。充分核对可能让你多花时间或改线，但不用不可靠设施冒险。

## English

Use this Skill when a bus or rail itinerary exists but completing it depends on **specific stops, vehicles, lifts, information formats, or staff help**. Verify the actual workable conditions at each leg and keep a route change for equipment failure. The aim is not to label you “accessible”; it is to prove the actions you need are feasible at departure, every transfer, and arrival, or choose another verified journey. Selecting trains, fares, street navigation, and medical assessment are outside this task.

### Preparation and inputs

Write needs as actions rather than a diagnosis: no steps, working lift, limited walking, ramp or staff boarding help, text service changes, audible stops, lower counter, or extra transfer time. List origin stop/station, every interchange, destination, time, operators, and actual bus or train services. Read each operator's route, stop accessibility, equipment status, and assistance rules. A station tagged “accessible” is not proof the path from entrance to platform, vehicle, transfer, and exit works for your need today.

### Execution

1. **Turn the journey into checkable nodes.** For each leg list entering the stop/platform, boarding, alighting, interchange route, and final exit. Put required lift, ramp, low-floor vehicle, readable/audible information, or staff help next to the node where needed. List acceptable alternatives separately. A disability label does not specify the same facilities for everyone.
2. **Check capability and live state.** In each operator's official channel confirm that the stop/service has the needed equipment, vehicle/coach, or help. Then check close-to-travel lift outages, closed entrances, platform changes, and diversions. Maps describe layout; live status shows whether a critical link is usable now. A failed lift can break a whole supposed step-free route.
3. **Get a confirmed help arrangement where needed.** Under that operator's current rules provide travel date, train/route, departure/transfer/arrival nodes, exact help actions, and any aid relevant to safe boarding. Verify whether booking is required, meeting place/time, and an accepted reference or message. A submitted request without confirmation is not arranged assistance. Use official phone or staffed channels if an app is inaccessible; do not give a stranger control of your account or publish medical details.
4. **Keep an exit and alternative.** If a necessary condition is unknown or equipment fails, ask the operator for a verified alternate stop, path, vehicle, or help, then recheck all nodes and transfer time. Stairs, tracks, a closed entrance, or an unconfirmed stranger lifting you are not default backups. If no safe complete path exists, stop that itinerary, change time, or choose another service.
5. **Read back before and during travel.** Before leaving, check current operator status again. Record critical equipment/help confirmation time, contact channel, and alternate plan. At an interchange, if reality differs, pause somewhere safe with staff contact and recheck rather than forcing an old screenshot. Actual ticket gates, boarding, and transfer follow their local procedures.

### Success

For the specific itinerary, departure, every transfer, and arrival have verified necessary paths, vehicle conditions, information forms, and confirmed assistance. You know when to recheck and what to do if a critical link fails. An unknown critical condition ends in a clear stop or another equally verified route. A planner's “accessible” tag without today's node checks is incomplete.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Step-free page but required lift is out:** Do not treat a static label as a live guarantee. Check operator alternatives or staff help; change the trip if no safe route is confirmed.
- **Origin checked but transfer or exit missed:** Fill in all nodes. One impossible required action leaves the whole trip unverified.
- **Help requested but no meeting confirmation:** Ask the operator for accepted status, place, time, and reference. Do not depend on unconfirmed help.
- **Coach or platform changes:** From a safe place read the new notice and confirm required path/help still applies. Do not follow an old map into a closed route.
- **App unreadable or announcements inaudible:** Cross-check through operator text, audio, staffed, or other accessible channels; avoid a single fragile information format.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This checks feasibility for one ordinary public-transport journey. Equipment can still fail, and it does not rule on medical eligibility, disability status, legal rights, or fare compensation. Verify real operator, date, stop, service, aid, and assistance mechanism. TfL, National Rail, and NS tools illustrate checks, not a guarantee for your location. Official telephone, station notices, or staff can replace a smartphone. A well-checked route may take longer or change, without requiring you to gamble on broken facilities.
