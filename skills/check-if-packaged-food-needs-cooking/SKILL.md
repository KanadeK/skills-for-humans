## 中文

当你准备买包装食品，打算开封即食、简单加热或按包装烹调，却不确定它**是否已可直接食用**时加载。这里仅做买前状态与所需条件核对；不教授烹饪技法、熟度判断、婴幼儿食品或病后饮食，也不把外观当作食品安全证明。

### 准备与输入

拿到准确商品的正反面、烹调与保存说明，确认你原本打算怎样吃，以及届时可用的设备和时间。留意“生的”“未完全熟制”“必须充分烹调”“ready to cook”“oven ready”“ready to eat”等原文，不自行用相似的中文译名代替。小字看不清可拍照放大、请店员展示完整包装或让人逐字读出；网页商品图须与实物版本一致。

### 执行

1. **先查状态，不看颜色。** 在包装正面、背面和烹调说明中寻找是否明确为即食、已完全熟制，或相反地标为生/未熟/需要烹调。裹粉、烤色或微波炉图片都不能推翻“未即食”字样。
2. **核对具体方法。** 若必须烹调，读标签指定的设备、从冷冻还是解冻状态开始、是否需要温度计及其它不能省略的条件。这里只判断你是否具备这些条件，不在货架前发明替代烹法。
3. **与计划吃法比较。** 你打算不烹调就吃，标签却要求烹调：这件不符合计划。标签只允许烤箱、你只有微波炉：除非准确包装明确提供可用的微波路线，否则不要按微波炉推定可行。
4. **处理矛盾或缺失。** 正面像即食、背面却写“cook thoroughly”，以明确安全说明作为停止信号；包装缺少必要说明或两处条件矛盾时，记“未确认”，向制造商核对准确版本或选另一件。
5. **输出买前结论。** 写“与预定吃法相容 / 明确不相容 / 未确认”，并记下决定性的原文及你实际拥有的设备。相容只说明买前所需条件可安排，不保证实际烹调已经完成。

### 成功与停止

你能说出这件食品是明示即食还是需要完成哪种烹调，以及你的计划和设备是否符合标签。无法确认状态、缺指定设备、没有时间遵守说明或标签互相矛盾时先不买。购买后的实际熟度与保存属于其它 Skill；食用后不适立即停止此购物流程并按当地医疗指导求助。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“已经上色所以肯定熟”：** 回到生/未即食字样和烹调说明，别靠照片投票。
- **“oven ready”被读成“ready to eat”：** 保留完整英文及本地说明，重新判断是否仍需烹调。
- **设备只有一种：** 只采用此商品明确提供且你能执行的方式；没有就换商品或调整计划。
- **购物中断：** 留下状态词、要求的设备和准确商品版本，回来再决定，不凭印象续签。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设你能取得准确包装、能够决定普通食物的购买，并会在买后遵守正式烹调说明。不同地区使用的标签词和设备说明不完全相同；以下美国资料是误读风险的实例，不是全球统一温度表。若你读不清、听不见店员、不能使用指定设备或无法取得说明，选择明确即食且符合其它要求的商品，或延后购买。外观很有信心，细菌并未签字。

## English

Load this Skill before buying packaged food when you plan to eat it as opened, merely warm it, or cook it by the package, but cannot tell **whether it is already ready to eat**. This is a pre-purchase status and feasibility check. It does not teach cooking, doneness testing, infant feeding, or a medical diet, and appearance is not a food-safety certificate.

### Preparation and inputs

Get the exact item's front, back, cooking, and storage directions. State how you intended to eat it and what equipment and time you will really have. Look for the original wording: raw, uncooked, cook thoroughly, ready to cook, oven ready, or ready to eat. Do not replace it with a loose translation. Enlarge small print, ask staff to show the full pack, or have someone read it verbatim. An online photo must match this version.

### Execution

1. **Read status before appearance.** Find an explicit ready-to-eat, fully cooked, raw, uncooked, or must-cook statement across the front, back, and directions. Browning, breading, or a microwave picture cannot override “not ready to eat.”
2. **Read the required method.** If cooking is required, note the specified appliance, frozen or thawed starting state, thermometer requirement, and any condition you cannot skip. At the shelf, you only decide whether you can meet those conditions; do not invent an alternative method.
3. **Compare with your plan.** If you plan to eat it without cooking but the label requires cooking, it fails the plan. If the label specifies an oven and you only have a microwave, do not assume the microwave will work unless that exact pack provides a suitable route.
4. **Handle gaps or contradictions.** A ready-looking front paired with “cook thoroughly” on the back is a stop signal, not an invitation to trust the picture. Missing essential directions or conflicting wording means unconfirmed. Ask the maker about the exact version or choose another item.
5. **Give a pre-purchase result.** Mark “fits intended use / clearly does not fit / unconfirmed,” with the decisive wording and your available equipment. Fits means you can arrange the required conditions before buying, not that cooking has happened.

### Success and stop

You can state whether the item is explicitly ready to eat or requires a stated cooking route, and whether your plan and equipment match. Pause the purchase if status is unknown, equipment or time is missing, or directions conflict. Actual cooking, doneness, and storage belong to other Skills. If someone feels unwell after eating, stop this shopping workflow and seek appropriate local medical help.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“It looks browned, so it must be cooked”:** Return to raw/not-ready wording and the directions; a photo does not get a vote.
- **Reading “oven ready” as “ready to eat”:** Keep the complete phrase and local directions; check whether cooking is still required.
- **You have only one appliance:** Use only a method explicitly provided for this exact item that you can follow; otherwise change item or plan.
- **Interrupted shopping:** Save the status phrase, required appliance, and exact version, then decide later rather than relying on memory.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes you can get the exact package, make an ordinary food purchase, and later follow its actual cooking instructions. Label terms and appliance guidance vary by market. The US examples below illustrate a misreading risk, not a universal temperature chart. If print, staff speech, equipment, or instructions are inaccessible, choose an explicitly ready-to-eat option that passes your other checks, or delay the purchase. Appearance can be confident; bacteria have signed nothing.
