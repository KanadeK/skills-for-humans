## 中文

当你已有这次出行要带的普通衣物，却不确定如何放进包里才能少起不必要的硬折痕、避免厚针织或有版型的衣物被压变形时，用这份 Skill 按**真实结构和可用空间**安排一次装包。目标是包能正常关闭，目的地需要的衣物仍可用；不承诺每件打开后像刚熨好。选哪些衣物、行李规则、航班限制和行李遗失都不在这里解决。

### 准备与输入

把要带的衣物放到干净平面，确认全部已干燥、没有未处理的湿污；记下哪些要在到达后马上穿、哪些容易出现可见折线、哪些厚重或有肩线/领形，哪一件不能承受紧压。查看包内实际可用空间、已有鞋和硬物的位置，以及有没有干净分隔袋、装衣夹或可保护衣物的薄袋。附件要现有且能用，不为了“完美折叠”先购买一套收纳系统。

特殊功能服、防护用品、需要制造商专用包装的衣物不按普通折卷处理。长途存放和换季防虫归 H068；本 Skill 只管这次出行到达前的短期形状保护。

### 执行

1. **按损伤风险分组，而不是按颜色排照片。** 将衣物分成轻便、可接受松卷的休闲件；易出现硬折痕或有领形/结构的件；厚重、松针织或易被拉变形的件。翻看护理标签和手感，别因为同名“衬衣”就假设都可以紧卷。先把到达后立刻要用的那件标出来。
2. **先放不怕压力且较重的物品。** 按包的开合方向安放鞋、硬物和相对耐压的衣物，让它们不直接顶着易皱面料或压坏衣领；脏鞋或可能漏的液体与干净衣物隔开。不要用易变形针织衫填所有尖角，让它替行李承担缓冲职责。
3. **按结构折或卷。** 轻便耐皱件可以展平、顺着本来衣身松卷，卷完不需勒成硬筒；易皱或有版型的衣物展平、抚平大褶后少做折线，放进现有装衣夹/平整隔层或置于不会被重物压的位置。厚重针织先平折，托住整件，不为了省一小格空间硬卷或悬挂拉长。具体做法以衣物和包的实际形状为准，没有万能“所有衣服都卷”的定律。
4. **试关包并检查压力。** 轻合包口，不用坐在包上、强拉拉链或让鞋底压住需要当天穿的衣物。包盖关不上、衣领被折成尖角、针织衣身被扯长时，重新分层、换可用包或减件；更小体积不值得换一件到达后不能穿的衣服。
5. **把先用的衣物做成可找回状态。** 把到达后即用的那件放在能先取到、不必翻乱整包的位置；其他件按干净/已穿分开。到达后如条件允许及时取出易皱或厚针织件，按其结构平放或适用地悬挂，让衣物不再长期受包内挤压。若仍有褶皱，按 H065 的标签/工具边界再判断，不把每道折线当装包失败。

### 成功条件

包按正常方式关闭，没有硬物或强压明显损害衣物结构，急需的衣物可先取到，干净衣物与湿脏物分隔，到达后知道如何尽快取出。发现空间不够并减件/换包也是完整决定；不能以拉坏包或压坏衣服换取“全部塞进去了”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **所有衣物都紧卷，衬衣和厚毛衣一起挤：** 拿出结构敏感件，改为平整折放或更宽松分层；厚针织未必更省空间。
- **重鞋压在刚熨的衣服上：** 调整重物位置并隔开鞋面/鞋底，先检查衣物有无被压出硬折痕。
- **包要靠身体坐上去才关：** 停止压缩，减件或换包；闭合困难不是衣服同意被挤压。
- **湿衣与干净衣物混放：** 停止合包，按实际条件先干燥/隔离；别让装包把清洗任务倒回干净区。
- **到达后急需衣物被压在最底层：** 按使用顺序重排，不为取一件翻乱全部。
- **衣物原本就皱，却怪装包方法：** 分开记录出发前与到达后的状态；可用标签允许的穿前整理，但不宣称折法会修复既有褶皱。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理普通短途/中途旅行衣物的形状和可取用性，衣物已经选好，包可用。不同布料的褶皱、厚度和肩/领结构不同，折与卷都不提供零皱承诺；包、空间和你能携带的重量也是真实限制。视力或手部操作不便时用大平面、少量分组、语音/触觉标记或可信帮助，不要求完成某个“完美折法”。保护结构可能多占空间或减少可带衣物，属于诚实取舍。

## English

Use this Skill when you have already selected ordinary clothes for a trip but need to fit them into a bag without unnecessary hard creases or crushing thick knits and shaped garments. Arrange **actual structures within actual space**. The result is a bag that closes normally and clothes that remain usable at the destination, not a promise that every item emerges freshly ironed. Choosing what to bring, baggage rules, flight limits, and lost luggage belong elsewhere.

### Preparation and inputs

Lay selected clothes on a clean flat surface. Confirm they are dry and do not have unresolved wet soil. Note what you need soon after arrival, what creases visibly, what is bulky or has shaped shoulders/collars, and what cannot tolerate tight pressure. Inspect usable bag space and where shoes and hard objects will sit. See whether you already have clean dividers, a garment folder, or a simple protective bag. None is a purchase requirement for a “perfect fold.”

Special-function clothing, protective equipment, or items requiring maker-specific packing do not follow ordinary folding. Long seasonal storage is H068's concern. This Skill protects shape only during this trip before unpacking.

### Execution

1. **Group by damage risk rather than colour.** Separate light casual pieces that can tolerate a loose roll; garments that show hard fold lines or have collars and structure; and bulky loose knits or pieces that stretch under pulling. Read care and feel the actual item; two things called “shirts” need not tolerate the same compression. Mark what must be worn right after arrival.
2. **Place heavier, pressure-tolerant items first.** Put shoes, hard objects, and sturdier clothes in the bag's real orientation so they do not point into wrinkle-prone cloth or crush a collar. Keep dirty shoes or potentially leaking liquids away from clean clothes. A shape-sensitive sweater is not universal padding for every sharp corner.
3. **Fold or roll for the structure.** Smooth a light wrinkle-tolerant item and roll it loosely along its body; it need not become a hard cylinder. Smooth a wrinkle-prone or shaped item, use fewer hard folds, and place it in an existing folder, flat section, or area clear of heavy pressure. Fold bulky knitwear flat and support the whole piece instead of forcing a tight roll or hanging it until stretched. The garment and bag determine the method; there is no law that every item must be rolled.
4. **Trial closure and inspect pressure.** Close the bag gently. Do not sit on it, force the zipper, or let a shoe sole crush what you need to wear today. If the lid will not close, a collar folds sharply, or a knit is pulled long, rearrange, choose another available bag, or take fewer items. A smaller volume is not useful if the garment becomes unusable.
5. **Make the first-needed item retrievable.** Place what you need on arrival where it can be reached without disordering everything. Keep clean from worn items. At the destination, promptly unpack wrinkle-prone and bulky pieces if circumstances permit, laying or hanging them as their structure allows rather than leaving them crushed. If wrinkles remain, use H065's care-label/tool boundary later; every fold is not evidence of packing failure.

### Success

The bag closes normally, hard items and force do not visibly damage garment shape, immediate-use clothing is reachable, clean clothes are apart from wet or dirty things, and you know what to unpack promptly. Choosing fewer items or another bag when space fails is a complete decision. A broken bag zipper or crushed garment is not a successful “everything fit.”

### Common errors and recovery

- **Everything is tightly rolled, including a shirt and thick sweater:** Take shaped items out and use flatter, looser placement. Thick knitwear may not save space when rolled.
- **Heavy shoes sit on freshly pressed clothes:** Move and separate them; inspect for hard pressure creases.
- **The bag only closes under your body weight:** Stop compressing, remove an item, or use another bag. Difficult closure is not the cloth's consent to crushing.
- **Wet clothes enter the clean pile:** Pause closure and dry or isolate them under actual conditions. Packing must not reverse cleaning work.
- **The arrival item is at the bottom:** Reorder by real use so retrieving one piece does not scatter all the others.
- **A garment was wrinkled before packing:** Record departure and arrival states separately. Use permitted garment care later; a fold cannot repair an existing crease.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This handles shape and access for ordinary short-trip clothing already selected for a usable bag. Fabrics vary in creasing, bulk, and shoulder/collar structure. Neither fold nor roll guarantees zero wrinkles. Bag space and weight you can carry are real constraints. If sight or hand use is limited, work on a large surface in small groups with spoken or tactile labels or trusted help; no exact “perfect fold” is required. Protecting shape may use more space or mean carrying one fewer piece. That is the honest tradeoff.
