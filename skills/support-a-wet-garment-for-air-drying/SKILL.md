## 中文

当你已经给洗好的普通衣物找到安全晾晒位置，却还需要决定**这一件湿衣服怎样被支撑**时，用这份 Skill。目标是在标签允许的平铺、悬挂、滴干与阴干条件下放好衣物，湿重不会一直拉一个领口或夹点。这里不重新洗衣、不操作烘干机；羊毛针织物若已有专用说明，优先照其方法。

### 准备与输入

读这件衣物的干燥标签和制造商说明；分清“平铺”“悬挂”“滴干”和“阴干”，查与你标签相符的符号图例。看衣物湿重、长度、是否有容易变形的针织、弹力、装饰或接缝，以及晾处的平面/横杆是否能承重且清洁。准备干净干燥的吸水毛巾或适用晾衣网/衣架，确认滴水不会落到滑道或电器。标签缺失或与面料状态矛盾时先停止猜测。

### 执行

1. **选标签许可的支撑方向。** 指定平铺就把整件主体放在干净、能通风的平面或晾衣网上；指定悬挂才用稳固横杆/衣架。滴干的衣物保留标签要求的姿态与滴水去处；阴干时避开直射日光。不能用一种衣架替所有符号作翻译。
2. **托住湿重再移动。** 双手或干净毛巾托住衣身，不只拎领口、袖口、细肩带或一小块湿布。衣物过重时分步搬到较近、较低的架上，或请可信的人协助；无法安全托住就先停。
3. **按支撑面整理形状。** 平铺时让肩、身、袖自然展开但不强拉回“理想尺寸”，避免叠成多层；若用毛巾，毛巾湿透后按衣物允许的方法更换或给衣物更透气的支撑。悬挂时让重量落在标签允许的区域，夹子或衣架不能留下明显拉扯/压痕；必要时改用更宽的支持或回到允许的平铺法。
4. **留出水与空气的路径。** 相邻衣物不要紧贴，滴水不落在别人的衣物或通道。检查衣服没有碰脏墙、地面或热源，架没有因重量倾斜。衣物若明显下坠、肩部拉长或夹点变形，趁湿及时托起、重新分配重量，不靠干后熨烫补救形变。
5. **交给晾干阶段。** 确认这件衣服姿态符合标签、重量得到支撑、仍有气流；记住晾晒位置以便稍后检查真正干透。没有可用支撑时暂停晾这一件并寻找适用方法，不把它团在密闭桶内等一天。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

衣物在允许的平铺/悬挂姿态上稳定展开，湿重没有集中在易拉伸的单点，滴水和气流有去处。你已完成放置，衣物是否干透还需之后检查。

- **挂上后肩膀被拉长：** 立即托起改用允许的宽支撑或平铺，别等完全干了再扯回去。
- **平铺的毛巾一直湿着：** 按衣物允许方式换干毛巾或更透气的平面；不要把衣物夹在两层湿布里。
- **夹子留下很深的印：** 放松夹持，换更合适的接触位置/支撑；若标签不允许悬挂，改回平铺。
- **标签无法判明：** 保留湿衣的安全支撑并查制造商/专业护理，停止使用会持续拉伸的猜测方法。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这针对可自然晾干的普通衣物，不包括防护装备、皮革、复杂结构服装或无法确认护理方式的贵重织物。各符号地区版本和衣物实际构造不同，不以“针织一定平铺”等口号覆盖标签。手部或站立受限时用低架、小件逐个搬、易握支撑与可信协助；视觉受限时靠可读标签和触摸稳定支架，不在不明污染上徒手操作。支撑物本身可能变湿，须及时处理。

## English

Use this Skill once you have a safe drying place for a freshly washed ordinary garment but still need to decide **how this wet item is supported**. Place it flat, on a line, dripping, or in shade as its label requires, without wet weight pulling continuously on a collar or clip. This does not wash the garment again or run a dryer. Follow any specific wool-knitwear guidance first.

### Preparation and inputs

Read this item's drying label and maker directions. Distinguish flat, line, drip, and shade using a symbol guide matching the label. Notice wet weight, length, stretch-prone knit or elastic, trim and seams, and whether the chosen surface or rail is clean and load-bearing. Have a clean dry absorbent towel or suitable mesh, rack, or hanger. Confirm drips miss slippery routes and electrical items. Stop guessing if the label is missing or conflicts with the garment's condition.

### Execution

1. **Choose an allowed support direction.** For a flat-dry instruction, lay the whole garment on a clean, ventilated surface or drying mesh. Use a secure rail or hanger only when hanging is allowed. Keep the required orientation and runoff for drip drying; keep shade-dry items out of direct sun. One hanger is not a translation for every symbol.
2. **Carry the wet weight.** Support the body with both hands or a clean towel rather than lifting only by the collar, cuff, thin strap, or one point. Move a heavy item in stages to a closer lower rack or ask someone trusted for help. Stop if you cannot support it safely.
3. **Arrange the shape on its support.** For flat drying, spread shoulders, body, and sleeves naturally without stretching to an imagined size or stacking folds. Replace a saturated towel or use more breathable support as the garment permits. For hanging, let weight rest where the label permits; a clip or hanger must not produce visible pulls or deep pressure marks. Use broader support or return to allowed flat drying if needed.
4. **Leave paths for water and air.** Separate neighbouring garments and direct drips away from other clothes and walkways. Check that the item misses dirty walls, floor, and heat sources and that the rack stays level. If it sags or a shoulder or clip point distorts, support and redistribute the wet weight now rather than relying on ironing later.
5. **Hand off to drying.** Confirm label-compatible orientation, supported weight, and airflow. Remember the site for a later true-dryness check. If no support works, pause this item and find an allowed method; do not ball it up in a closed tub for a day.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

The garment is stable in its allowed flat or hanging orientation, wet weight is not concentrated on one stretch-prone point, and air and drips have routes. Placement is complete; dryness still needs a later check.

- **The shoulders stretch after hanging:** Support the item immediately and use an allowed broad support or flat method. Do not wait until dry to tug it back.
- **The flat towel stays wet:** Replace it or use a more breathable permitted surface; do not sandwich the garment between wet layers.
- **Clips leave deep marks:** Reduce pressure or change contact point and support. Return to flat drying when the label does not allow hanging.
- **You cannot interpret the label:** Keep the wet item safely supported and ask the maker or a professional cleaner. Stop a guessed method that keeps stretching it.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers ordinary garments approved for natural drying, not protective gear, leather, complex construction, or valuable fabric with unknown care. Symbol systems and garment construction vary; “all knits lie flat” does not override a label. For limited grip or standing, use a low rack, move one small item at a time, and ask for trusted help. If vision is limited, rely on readable labels and a stable rack; do not handle unknown contamination bare-handed. A support towel may itself become damp and need attention.
