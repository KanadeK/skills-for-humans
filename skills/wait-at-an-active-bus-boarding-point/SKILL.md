## 中文

你已确定应在哪一侧、哪个站乘坐普通公交，但到了现场还要找到**真正启用、可以安全候车的位置**时加载。这里不重新规划线路方向，也不处理上车刷卡；完成信号是你站在有效站牌或指定候车区，知道队伍与车辆会从哪里接人。不同运营方的站牌、排队和临时迁站规则不一样。

### 准备与输入

保留你已核对的线路号、方向/终点、站名或站点编号，并看现场运营方站牌、站台编号、临时告示及实时服务公告。手机没网时可用站牌、现场正式信息或工作人员；如果看不清或听不到，尽量找有文字、播报或可协助确认的正式渠道。不要把人群聚集处当作站牌。

### 执行

1. **确认此处仍有效。** 对上已选的站名/编号和线路，读站牌是否标出该线路与方向，是否有遮盖、迁站或临时停靠通知。多个候车岛或车位时，按运营方的站位编号找，不根据“那里人最多”猜。
2. **把临时变化先解决。** 站点关闭或路线临时不停靠，就读现场告示或运营方实时公告中的替代站点与步行方式，重新核对该站确实服务你的方向。说明不清时在安全位置向工作人员或官方渠道问；不要穿车道、翻护栏或追逐驶离的车。
3. **在安全候车范围内定位。** 站在站牌旁或指定候车区、路缘后方，保持车辆靠站和车门/坡道区域畅通。有队伍就按现场规则排队；没有明显队伍也给先到者与要下车的人留位置。TransLink 的“站牌杆旁或后方、离路缘保持距离并排队”是温哥华的具体示例，不是每城的统一站位图。
4. **让车辆能看到你，同时保持退路。** 在运营方允许的候车范围内留意来车，不躲在停靠视线之外；需要示意时等车辆靠近再从安全位置示意。别为了抢一步跨进机动车道。候车时间与告示相差很大时，核对站点编号和运营方实时信息，再决定继续等或重选正式站点。

### 完成与停止

你能指出有效站牌/候车区、对应线路与方向，以及本处安全排队和车辆靠站位置，就完成了候车准备；下一步才是核对实际来车与上车。若站点关闭、身份不明或没有安全可达的候车位置，停在安全处核对运营方的替代安排，不以追车或随队穿行当作补救。

### 常见报错与补救

- **站牌有线路号，却方向不一致：** 返回已选的线路/方向计划核对，不凭同一号码直接候车。
- **好几条队伍或站位：** 看站位编号和现场引导；问工作人员或队首是否为你那一线路，但以官方信息确认。
- **站牌被遮盖或写着临停：** 查当期告示的临时站点与实际停靠方向，再安全前往；不假设下一根站牌一定可用。
- **车没停或等候信息不一致：** 核对自己是否在有效站位且足够可见，查运营方公告。别在车后追赶。
- **坡道、手推车或行李需要空间：** 留出车门旁通道，按现场正式指引或向工作人员询问可用候车位置；别堵住别人下车。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通、有明确站点的公交；招手即停、预约接送或临时接驳须以该运营方指引为准。步行、视力、听力或语言条件不同，可先查询运营方无障碍信息，利用文字/播报/工作人员或可信陪同确认站位，不要求冒险跨越道路。副作用是你可能需要在正式替代站多走一段，无法从这份 Skill 得到所有城市通用的站台编号。

## English

Load this Skill after you have chosen the correct boarding-side stop and direction but still need the **active, safe place to wait within that stop**. This does not plan the route again or pay a fare. You finish when you are at the operating sign or designated waiting area and know where the line and vehicle boarding point are. Signs, queues and temporary relocations vary by operator.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep your checked route number, direction or destination, and stop name or ID. Read the operator's sign, bay number, temporary notices and current service alerts. Without mobile data, use posted or staffed official information. If visual or audio information is hard to use, look for the operator's written, announced or assisted option. A crowd is not a stop sign.

### Execution

1. **Check that this point is active.** Match the chosen stop name or ID and route to the sign. Look for a cover, relocation notice or temporary boarding instruction. At a station with several islands or bays, use the operator's bay number rather than guessing from the largest crowd.
2. **Resolve a temporary change first.** If the stop is closed or your route no longer serves it, read the on-site notice or live operator alert for the replacement point and walking route. Confirm the replacement serves your direction. If unclear, ask staff or an official channel while you remain somewhere safe. Do not cross a vehicle lane, climb a barrier or chase a departing bus.
3. **Take a safe waiting position.** Stand beside the sign or in the designated area, back from the curb, leaving room for the bus, doors and ramp. Join the local line if there is one; otherwise leave room for earlier arrivals and people getting off. TransLink's advice to stand beside or behind its stop pole, back from the roadside and in a line is a Vancouver example, not a universal stop map.
4. **Stay visible with a way back.** Within the permitted waiting area, watch approaching vehicles rather than hiding from the stopping driver's view. Signal from safety if that operator asks riders to do so. Do not step into traffic to gain a place. If arrivals differ greatly from the notice, recheck the stop ID and current operator information before choosing to wait or move to a verified alternative.

### Success and stop

You can point to the active sign or waiting area, the matching route and direction, and a safe place for the queue and approaching bus. Only then move to checking and boarding the actual vehicle. If the stop is closed, its identity is unclear or there is no safe accessible waiting position, stop in a safe place and verify the operator's alternative; chasing a bus is not recovery.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The route number matches but the direction does not:** Recheck your route/direction plan before waiting for the same number.
- **Several queues or bays appear:** Read bay numbers and on-site guidance. You may ask staff or the queue, then confirm with official information.
- **The sign is covered or says temporary closure:** Verify the temporary point and direction from current notices, then reach it safely. The next pole is not automatically correct.
- **A bus passes or the wait estimate looks wrong:** Check the active bay, visibility and operator alerts. Do not run behind it.
- **A ramp, stroller or luggage needs room:** Keep the door area clear and use staff or posted guidance for an accessible waiting position.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for ordinary fixed-stop buses. Hail-and-ride, booked shuttles and temporary replacements follow their own operator's instructions. Mobility, sight, hearing or language needs may call for operator accessibility information, written or announced guidance, staff or a trusted companion. None require an unsafe road crossing. You may need to move to a verified replacement stop; this Skill cannot supply a universal bay number.
