## 中文

当你要进行一次可退出的普通短途旅行，尚未装包，却不确定**实际该带什么、哪只包能由你一路带走**时加载。目标是把具体活动、住宿真实提供物与交通携物规则交叉，写出能执行的“必带/条件带/不带”清单。这不是航空行李限额、签证、保险、药物或危险物品指南；物品装载顺序与湿物/易碎物包装属于后续流程。

### 准备与输入

写下出发与返回日期、住宿夜数、白天活动、可能的衣着/天气需求、需到达的交通方式和你会亲自搬运的路段。查看实际住宿预订或该物业官方设施说明，不从品牌名推断一定有毛巾、吹风机、洗衣或存包；交通行李数量、尺寸、重量、是否能托运/寄存要以你这趟服务的当期资料为准。你自己的固定必要物品由你据日常真实需要列出；本 Skill 不决定医疗用法或证件法律要求。

### 执行

1. **用活动而非想象清单起草。** 按天写“去哪里、做什么、要穿/用什么、能否重复使用或就地取得”。把当次确实要用的普通衣物、清洁、设备与充电物归到相应活动；没有对应活动的东西先放候选栏。
2. **逐项核实服务会提供什么。** 从实际房型/预订确认寝具、洗护、洗衣、充电、储物等与你清单有关的设施或使用条件；只写已确认的事实，未知则标“待问”。交通上是否允许手提大包或提供托运，同样查本次服务及出发/到达站，而不是套用同品牌另一条线路。
3. **分三个口袋做减法。** 必带：缺了会使已安排活动无法完成，且不能合理从已确认服务取得；条件带：天气/活动有实际条件才需要；不带：重复、无对应活动或由服务明确提供。对不确定天气保留有用的少量余量，不为每个想象场景各放一套装备。
4. **用你的携带能力和实际交通规则做最后筛选。** 选一只你能在本次路程中提、推、抬或安排正式协助的包，并按运营方具体限制核对。试着在安全环境按本次预计装量提/移动一下；如果明显难以独自控制、放不进服务允许空间或需要多一件未获准行李，先删减/改包或问正式服务安排，不把痛苦当作打包算法。
5. **留下可核对的结果。** 写“必带__；条件__（触发条件__）；不带__；已确认提供__；待核__；选用__包”。在装包前只复查待核项，不从头再打开一份网上万能清单。真正摆放、途中取用和回程拆包交后续流程。

### 完成与停止

你有一份与**本次活动、实际服务和个人携带能力**相符的清单与包型，未知服务有明确待核点，就完成范围决定。**清单不是已经装好，也不能保证住宿/承运服务会临时兑现未确认设施。** 如果必要物品涉及航空、处方、危险品、法律证件或超出你能安全搬运的情况，停下并查对应官方/合格流程，不由这份通用清单硬判。

### 常见报错与补救

- **从网络周末清单照抄了很多物件：** 每件回到实际活动或已确认条件；没有对应场景就移到“不带”。
- **订的是连锁品牌，就假设每间房相同：** 查这次具体物业/房型或直接问接待，未确认不从包里删掉必要替代。
- **火车/巴士行李规则被当作飞机规则：** 回到这趟实际承运方页面；航空专属行李使用航空流程。
- **包能装下却提不动：** 改成自己能控制的包、减去条件物品或核实正式行李协助，不强行搬运。
- **天气或行程变动：** 只调整被改变的条件栏与携带余量，不重写所有物件。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通短途住宿或往返旅行，不以天数、性别、城市或背包品牌复制清单。设施、交通规则、费用和可及协助随具体预订变化。行动、视觉、听觉或设备条件不同，可以用纸质活动表、可信协助与可控的拉箱/更轻行李，但不把包交给陌生人代管。副作用是可能放弃“以防万一”的多余物，目的地仍可能出现需要现场小额购买的普通用品。

## English

Load this Skill before packing for an ordinary short trip you can leave, when you are unsure **what this trip truly needs and which bag you can manage throughout it**. The result is a usable “must bring / conditional / leave behind” list based on real activities, actual lodging provisions and transport baggage rules. This is not an airline allowance, visa, insurance, medication or hazardous-goods guide. Loading order and protecting wet or fragile things come later.

### Preparation and inputs

Write departure and return dates, nights away, daytime activities, clothing or weather conditions, transport and the parts where you personally handle the bag. Check the real lodging booking or that property's official amenities, rather than assuming a brand always supplies towels, a hair dryer, laundry or storage. Quantity, size, weight and any checked-bag or storage option depend on this transport service and stations. List your fixed personal necessities from your own life; this Skill does not set medical use or legal document requirements.

### Execution

1. **Start from activities, not an imagined master list.** For each day, note where you go, what you do, what you wear or use and what can be reused or obtained from a confirmed service. Attach ordinary clothing, cleaning or device and charging items to actual activities. Put anything without one in a candidate column.
2. **Check what the real services supply.** From the booked room or property, confirm bedding, wash facilities, laundry, charging or storage that matters to your list, including any conditions. Record confirmed facts and mark unknowns “ask.” For transport, check whether a large hand bag or checked item is accepted on this exact service and at its origin and destination, not on another route of the same brand.
3. **Make three buckets by subtracting.** Must bring: a planned activity fails without it and no confirmed service can reasonably supply it. Conditional: needed only for a real weather or activity condition. Leave behind: duplicate, no linked activity or confirmed supplied by a service. Keep a small useful margin for uncertain weather, rather than one outfit for every imagined scenario.
4. **Filter against your carrying ability and the actual operator rule.** Choose a bag you can lift, roll, carry or arrange official help for along this route, and check its specific transport limits. In a safe place, test moving roughly what you plan to carry. If it is clearly hard to control, does not fit the permitted space or creates an unapproved extra item, remove or change things or ask the provider about legitimate assistance. Pain is not a packing algorithm.
5. **Leave a checkable result.** Note “must bring __; conditional __ when __; leave __; confirmed supplied __; still verify __; use __ bag.” Before packing, recheck only unknowns instead of restarting from an online universal list. Actual placement, access en route and unpacking are later jobs.

### Success and stop

You have a list and bag choice fitted to **this activity plan, real services and your carrying capacity**, with unresolved services named. **A list is not a packed bag, and an unconfirmed amenity is not a promise.** For airline, prescription, hazardous, legal-document or unsafe lifting issues, stop and use the relevant official or qualified route rather than forcing an answer from this general list.

### Common errors and recovery

- **An online weekend list adds too many items:** Tie each to an actual activity or confirmed condition; move the rest to “leave.”
- **A chain brand is treated as identical rooms:** Check this property and booked room or ask reception. Keep a necessary alternative until confirmed.
- **Train or coach rules are taken as flight rules:** Check the carrier for this leg. Airline baggage has its own procedure.
- **The bag holds it all but you cannot carry it:** Switch to a controllable bag, remove conditional items or verify formal baggage assistance.
- **Weather or plan changes:** Adjust the affected conditional items and margin, not every item from scratch.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This is for an ordinary short trip, not a list multiplied by trip length, gender, city or bag brand. Amenities, transport rules, costs and access help depend on the booking. For mobility, sight, hearing or device needs, use a paper activity table, trusted assistance and a manageable rolling or lighter bag without handing belongings to an unknown person. You may leave “just in case” items behind and need to buy an ordinary small item later.
