## 中文

当这次要搬的普通物品已经确定，但还没选箱子或开始装载时，加载这份 Skill。你要把它们整理成**目的地和交接负责人都清楚、受限或不能混走的物品已隔离**的预装箱分组。这里不教用什么箱、装多重、怎样搬，也不替代箱外标签和新居第一晚必需物安排。

### 准备与输入

拿出已经同意的本次搬运清单，而不是重新决定谁的东西能搬。确认新住处的粗略目的地区域、实际运输方式、谁会在起点与终点交接。找所用搬运方当前的接收或特殊处理说明；无搬运方时按自己的实际运输限制标注未知，不猜所有物品都能普通装箱。

### 执行

1. **按到达后的第一站分组。** 给每组一个人能理解的目标位置，如“新住处厨房低柜附近”而不是只写“杂物”。不能确定目的地的物品单列待确认，不把未来收纳细节硬定在此处。
2. **每组指定交接人。** 写明谁在起点确认这一组实际交出，谁在终点知道它该去的区域。一个人可以承担两端，但不能用“搬家公司会知道”代替你自己的收件指向。
3. **先找不能混入普通组的例外。** 看实际承运方是否对某些物品有限制或要求另外处理；食品、液体、易碎或需保密的物品不按名称自动套用统一禁运表。无法确认就独立标“问服务方/另行决定”，不和普通组混在一起。危险物品处理交给适用专业渠道。
4. **把不相容的用途拆开。** 即使同去一个房间，也不要把一件物品的关键配件放进无人知晓的另一组；需要相互找到的部件在清单上建立对应关系。隐私物品只向有权交接的人说明，不把内容写在公开箱外。
5. **做预交接回读。** 起终点负责人各自看组名、目标、例外和待确认项，确认不会凭印象漏下一组。实际箱子、重量与标识留给后续步骤；分组表若被装箱结果改变，及时修正。

### 成功与停止

每组已确定要搬的物品都有可解释的目的地和起终点负责人；特殊处理、承运限制及去向未知的东西没有悄悄进入普通组。没人有权接收、服务方要求不明或需要危险操作时，停止该组装箱，先取回准确信息。

### 常见报错与补救

- **所有东西都写成“新家”：** 补出能让到场者辨认的第一站区域，不强制决定长期收纳位置。
- **关键配件与主体分组后失联：** 在两组记录互相指向的名称/编号；实际是否同箱再按合适包装决定。
- **某件物品被服务方拒收：** 从普通组撤出，请实际服务方说明可行路线；不藏在其他箱里。
- **起点有人同意、终点没人知道：** 先指定接手人或可信代收路径；不能确认就暂停该组。
- **看不清清单或无法搬动：** 用大字、语音或图示让有权限的人回读，分组不要求你移动重物。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 在装箱前做信息分组，不是承运合同、保险或危险品处理建议。不同公司与地区的限制不同，以实际服务方的现行说明为准。行走、握持或阅读不方便时可在座位上根据照片和他人经许可的描述分组，把“未亲见”保留为未知。副作用是“杂物一箱”被改成几个能交接的名字。

## English

Load this Skill when the ordinary goods for this move are already chosen but no boxes or loading methods have been selected. You will make **pre-pack groups with a clear destination and handoff owner, while restricted or incompatible items are held apart**. This does not choose a box, set a safe load, carry anything, label the final boxes, or prepare first-night essentials.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the agreed shipment list; do not reopen ownership or move/stay decisions. Identify rough destination areas, the actual transport route, and who will hand groups over at origin and receive them at destination. Read the chosen mover's current acceptance and special-handling guidance. Without a mover, note the actual transport constraints and unknowns rather than assuming every item fits ordinary packing.

### Execution

1. **Group by first arrival point.** Give each group a destination a person can understand, such as “near the new kitchen's low cupboard” instead of “miscellaneous.” Put uncertain destinations in a confirm-first group; this is not the time to dictate permanent storage.
2. **Name a handoff person per group.** Record who confirms the group is handed over at origin and who knows its destination area at arrival. One person may do both, but “the movers will know” is not your receiving instruction.
3. **Find exceptions before normal groups swallow them.** Check whether the actual carrier restricts or separately handles any item. Food, liquids, fragile goods or private items do not all obey one universal forbidden list. Mark an unclear item “ask provider / decide separately” rather than mixing it into normal groups. Leave hazardous handling to an appropriate professional channel.
4. **Keep related pieces findable.** Even when two pieces go to the same room, do not let a crucial accessory disappear into another unnamed group. Cross-reference their group names or IDs; decide physical co-packing later. Tell only authorised receivers about private contents, not a public box exterior.
5. **Read the plan back before packing.** Origin and destination handlers each review group name, first stop, exceptions and open questions. Correct the group plan when actual packing changes it. Box choice, weight and final label come later.

### Success and stop

Every agreed moving group has an understandable first destination and people responsible at both ends; special handling, carrier restrictions and unknown destinations have not slipped into a normal group. Stop packing an affected group if nobody can receive it, the provider's rule is unclear, or safe handling would require specialist action.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Everything is labelled “new home”:** Name the first area an arriving person can identify without deciding permanent storage.
- **A main item and crucial part lose contact across groups:** Cross-reference their names or IDs. Choose whether they can share a box only during packaging planning.
- **The provider refuses an item:** Remove it from normal groups and request the actual provider's feasible route. Do not hide it in another carton.
- **The origin agrees but nobody can receive it:** Name a willing receiver or authorised alternative before releasing that group.
- **The list is unreadable or lifting is difficult:** Use large text, audio or a diagram and ask an authorised person to read it back. Grouping does not require you to move heavy items.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is information grouping before packing, not carrier-contract, insurance or hazardous-material guidance. Restrictions vary by mover and region; use the actual service's current terms. If walking, gripping or reading is difficult, group from a seated position using photos and descriptions from someone permitted to inspect, while preserving “not seen” as unknown. The side effect is replacing “one box of miscellaneous things” with a few names a receiver can use.
