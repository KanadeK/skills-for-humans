## 中文

当你要给现有普通家用设备买墨盒、碳粉、集尘袋等可更换耗材，却面对一排相似货号时，加载这份 Skill。任务到“准确设备型号与候选耗材有可追溯的适配依据”为止；不拆修设备、不处理过滤饮水或医疗用途，也不把“看着能装”当兼容证明。

### 准备与输入

找到设备铭牌、原说明书、购买记录或设备内可读取的型号显示，抄下**完整设备型号**，包括后缀。再确认要换的是哪一种耗材，例如墨盒、碳粉还是成像鼓；它们可能位于同一货架，但不是同一个部件。准备制造商针对该型号的说明书、支持页或适配查询，以及候选包装的完整货号、版本和销售地区。

### 执行

1. **先确认主机。** 用设备本体或可信记录复核完整型号，不只凭外壳颜色、系列名或旧订单标题。旧耗材可作为线索，但如果旧件本来就买错了，它不能成为权威来源。
2. **确定耗材角色。** 对照设备说明书看需要替换的类别、颜色、数量和适用位置；标准容量与高容量可能有不同货号，仍须逐一核对，也不要把另一个部件当替代品。
3. **在准确型号下查适配表。** 优先用制造商对该设备型号的支持页或说明书，记录列出的完整耗材货号。若资料按地区、设备制造地、版本或日期分支，就先核对你的设备与购买地；不要把别处的列表直接复制到本地购物车。
4. **逐字比对候选。** 读包装或商品页的完整货号、耗材类别和明确支持的主机型号。只说“适用某系列”或只靠外观相似，不足以确认细小后缀。第三方卖家的兼容声明要记录为卖家声明，不冒充制造商适配表。
5. **写一条购买判定。** 记录“主机型号 → 耗材类别 → 可核对货号 → 候选货号 → 地区/版本条件 → 来源”。全部一致才标为确认；任一项不明，暂停该候选并询问厂商或卖家具体信息。

### 成功与停止

你能指出准确设备和部件，并找到支持候选完整货号的具体来源，才算完成购前核对。若资料冲突、型号铭牌无法辨认、设备已改装，或更换动作涉及维修、高压、危险化学品，就停止自行推断并交给合适的支持渠道。本 Skill 不保证产品质量，也不执行更换。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只记了系列名：** 回到设备本体或购买凭据找完整型号和后缀；找不到就带照片向制造商支持确认。
- **旧包装与官方表不一致：** 不按旧包装复购；确认是否拿错设备、地区或耗材类别，并保存相互矛盾的编号。
- **卖家写“通用兼容”，没有逐型号清单：** 向卖家索取书面支持型号和货号；仍无法核实时选择有明确依据的候选或不买。
- **小字无法辨认：** 用手机放大铭牌和包装，请可信的人读回货号，或使用制造商的无障碍支持渠道；相似字符必须逐个核对。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这份流程假设你有权查看自己的设备型号，且制造商仍公开适配资料。各地区耗材编号可能不同，语言或地区不是自动推断的；按照设备和销售地资料查。没有网络时可用随设备提供的纸质说明书，或带完整型号到店让店员查证。你可能多花十分钟，也可能少拥有一盒形状正确但毫无用处的塑料件。

## English

Load this Skill before buying ink, toner, vacuum bags, or another replaceable supply for an everyday device when several part numbers look almost identical. The task ends with traceable evidence linking your exact device model to the candidate supply. It does not repair the device, cover drinking-water or medical filters, or declare a part compatible because it looks as if it will fit.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the device label, original manual, purchase record, or model display and record the **full model number**, including suffixes. Name the supply role: an ink cartridge, toner cartridge, imaging drum, or bag may share a shelf but cannot automatically replace one another. Have the manufacturer's model-specific manual, support page, or lookup table, plus the candidate's full part number, version, and sales region.

### Execution

1. **Identify the host first.** Verify the complete device model against the device or a reliable record, rather than colour, series name, or an old order title. An old supply is a clue, not authority if it may itself have been wrong.
2. **Identify the supply role.** Check the manual for the type, colour, number, and position that need replacement. Standard and high-yield options may have different part numbers; check each one and do not substitute another component.
3. **Use the exact model's compatibility source.** Find the complete supported part numbers in the maker's manual or support page. If its list varies by region, manufacture location, revision, or date, match those conditions to your device and place of purchase before selecting a number.
4. **Compare the candidate character by character.** Read its full part number, supply type, and explicitly supported device models. A generic “for this series” label or similar shape does not establish a suffix match. Record a third-party seller's claim as that seller's claim, rather than presenting it as the maker's table.
5. **Write the decision trail.** Record “device model → supply role → supported part number → candidate part number → region/revision condition → source.” Mark confirmed only when all relevant fields agree. Otherwise pause this candidate and ask for specific information.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can name the exact device and component and point to a source supporting the candidate's complete part number. Stop guessing when sources conflict, the model label is unreadable, the device has been modified, or replacement involves repair, high voltage, or hazardous chemicals. This Skill does not guarantee quality or perform installation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the series is known:** Find the full model and suffix on the device or purchase record. If unavailable, show a label photo to the maker's support team.
- **An old package disagrees with the maker's list:** Do not reorder from memory. Check the host, region, and supply type, and save both conflicting numbers.
- **A seller says “universal” without a model list:** Ask for written device-model and part-number support. If it remains unverified, choose a documented candidate or defer the purchase.
- **Small print is inaccessible:** Enlarge a photo, ask a trusted person to read the number back, or use the maker's accessible support channel. Check lookalike characters individually.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes you may inspect your own device and that the maker still publishes compatibility information. Supply numbers can differ by region; neither language nor location automatically establishes a match. Without internet access, use the paper manual or bring the full model number to a retailer for verification. Ten minutes of checking may prevent a box of perfectly shaped but useless plastic.
