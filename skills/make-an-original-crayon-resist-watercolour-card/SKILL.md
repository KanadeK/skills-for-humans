## 中文

当你想在**自己的小张纸**上利用蜡笔线条抗水彩，做一张原创图像或图案时，加载这份 Skill。结果是干后仍看得出蜡线与水彩区的区别。它不是 H273 的纹理拓印：这里不从物件转印表面凹凸，而是先画自有蜡线，再让颜料越过它。不要临摹受保护作品或把博物馆示范人物换色复制。

### 准备与输入

用包装允许的普通蜡笔、水彩和自己的水彩纸，备清水、稳定杯、画笔、可擦拭垫板。先在边角试一小段蜡线能否留在纸上且水彩不会完全盖掉；若是水溶性画材而非普通蜡笔，效果可能不同，换合用材料。颜料和画笔不接触食物，儿童按产品标识监护。

### 执行

1. **定一个小型原创形状。** 画自己的简单轮廓、路线或纹样，留一点纸面让色彩流过。不需要复制名家图像，也不用大量细线把纸磨破。
2. **用蜡笔画可见线。** 在干纸上以稳定但不过猛的压力画线；边角先试的蜡线能抗水彩时再继续。若线很淡，可在未上水前补一次，不拿尖物刻纸。
3. **刷水彩越过线。** 调少量普通水彩，轻扫跨过蜡线及旁边纸面；看颜料在无蜡区着色、在蜡线处较难附着。只刷到能观察差异，不用反复摩擦让纸起毛。
4. **自然晾干并确认。** 平放干燥后从合适光线查看蜡线是否仍与彩色背景分离，写下你自选的主题和材料。若线局部被遮，也如实保留；作品是你的原始组合，不要求和范例相像。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

小卡干燥、能指出至少一段蜡笔线抵住水彩，且图像是你自己决定的。只画蜡笔未刷水彩，或只刷色未留可辨抵抗线，都不算完成该混合材料练习。

- **水彩盖住所有线：** 先确认用的是蜡笔而非水溶性笔，在废纸重试较清楚的蜡线和较薄水彩。
- **纸起毛或太湿：** 停刷、平放干，下次减少回刷或换能湿用的纸。
- **蜡线太粗遮住主题：** 下一张卡减少线量；本张可继续作为材料试验，不刮掉蜡层。
- **颜料在不该留白处扩散：** 等干后决定是否接受或另做，不靠化学漂白回退。
- **手眼不便：** 用大轮廓、粗蜡笔、稳定垫板或可信协助固定；由你设计和核对抵抗效果。

### 假设与边界

蜡笔品牌、纸吸水和颜料浓度改变抵抗强弱，没有保证完全留白。只做普通私人水基纸艺，不使用喷涂、熔蜡、热风、溶剂或模仿现有受保护作品。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to use **wax-crayon lines resisting watercolour** on your own small sheet to make an original image or pattern. Finish with a dry card where wax lines and painted paper behave visibly differently. This is not H273's texture rubbing: no relief is transferred from an object. You draw your own wax line before washing paint over it. Do not recolour a protected artwork or copy the museum's example figure.

### Preparation and inputs

Use ordinary wax crayon, watercolour, and owned watercolour paper allowed by their labels, plus water, a stable cup, brush, and wipeable backing. Test a short wax line at a margin: it should remain and keep some paint off. A water-soluble drawing tool may behave differently; change to suitable material if needed. Keep pigment and brushes away from food. Follow product supervision labels for children.

### Execution

1. **Choose a small original shape.** Plan your own outline, route, or pattern with room for colour to cross it. A famous picture is unnecessary; avoid so many fine marks that the sheet is abraded.
2. **Make visible wax lines.** Draw on dry paper with steady but moderate pressure. Continue after the margin test shows resistance. Reinforce a faint line before adding water if needed; do not score paper with a point.
3. **Wash paint over the lines.** Mix a little ordinary watercolour and brush lightly across wax and bare paper. Watch pigment settle on unwaxed paper and adhere less to wax. Stop once the contrast is visible instead of scrubbing paper fibres.
4. **Dry naturally and check.** Let the card dry flat, then find at least one wax line separated from the painted background in suitable light. Note your chosen subject and materials. A partially covered line can stay as a truthful result; resemblance to an example is irrelevant.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The card is dry, at least part of a wax line visibly resists the wash, and you chose the image yourself. Crayon alone without paint or paint alone without a discernible resist does not complete the mixed-material exercise.

- **Paint covers every line:** Confirm the tool is wax rather than water-soluble, then retest a firmer line and thinner wash on scrap.
- **Paper pills or floods:** Stop brushing and dry flat; use fewer passes or paper suited to wet work next time.
- **Wax line is so wide it hides the idea:** Use fewer lines on the next card. Keep this one as a material test rather than scraping wax off.
- **Paint spreads into unwanted areas:** Judge after drying and accept or try another card, without chemical bleaching.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Use a broad shape, thick crayon, stable backing, or trusted help holding it while you design and verify the resist.

### Assumptions and limits

Crayon brand, paper absorption, and paint concentration change resistance; complete white retention is not promised. This is ordinary private water-based paper art without spraying, melting wax, heat guns, solvents, or copying protected work.
