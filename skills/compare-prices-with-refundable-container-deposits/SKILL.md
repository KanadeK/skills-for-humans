## 中文

当你已决定购买**相同总量、同样符合用途的普通饮品**，但候选包装的容器押金不同，或一边有可退押金、一边没有时，加载这份 Skill。它分别回答：**今天实际要付多少**，以及**满足本地退还条件并真的退回容器之后，净支出会是多少**。第二个数字是条件情境，不是今天已得到的退款；容器是否收、返还点能否使用必须按你所在地和这一件包装核实。

### 准备与输入

先锁定同样饮品用途和总量，记下每个候选这次确实可买到的包装数、内容净量、货币和当前结算价；规格/数量尚不等价时先做[同单位价格比较](../compare-unit-prices/SKILL.md)和购买量判断。对每项看押金是否已**包含**在标价/结算总额内，还是另列一行；区分容器押金、不可退服务费/税费以及促销折扣。找实际适用的官方/商家退还说明，核该种容器是否合格、需要保留何种状态、返还点与每件可获多少；不把另一地区或另一容器的数字套过来。

### 执行

1. **先算同量的今天应付。** 对每个方案以结算预览或可确认标价为基础，加上未包含在其中的押金和其他本次必付额；已经含押金就不再加一次。若结算页尚未显示完整费用，记“应付未确认”，不要把广告价当付款数。
2. **核一条可兑现的退款条件。** 押金若确为可退、容器在此地/此店的规则内，记录每件可返还金额和必要条件。若标签、地区、购买地点、容器状态或返还渠道不清，把退款标“未知”，不从总额里扣。退还仍需你以后实际完成，今天不能假装已发生。
3. **并排列两种口径。** “今天应付”就是步骤 1 的总数；“合格且全部实际退回后的净支出”＝今天应付－可核的容器返还总额。例：两种方案都得到 2 升普通饮品，A 今天付 6（其中已含 0.4 押金），按本地规则全部合格退回后净 5.6；B 今天付 5.8、无押金。今天 B 少付 0.2，成功退回后 A 少支出 0.2；退不回时 A 仍付 6。这些数只是列式示例，不是当前商店报价或本地押金标准。
4. **只在同口径下排名。** 比今天现金负担就用“今天应付”；比退回后条件净额就用两边同样可兑现的退还条件。若排名反转，明确写出两种结果，不给一个无条件“更便宜”。返还路程、储存和行动负担归取得方式判断；押金金额本身不能证明你有实际退还能力或意愿。
5. **付款前复核并停在事实处。** 看最终结算是否把押金另列、优惠和数量是否仍相同。实际价格或押金变了就重算；返还资格不明时只能确认今天应付比较。购买后保留需要的凭据/容器标识，是否真的退回属于以后的独立行动，不计入这次已完成事实。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

你能对同量候选分别说出**已核今天应付**和**有条件的成功退回后净额**，列清容器资格与尚未发生的返还。只从广告价直接扣“可能会退”的押金，不算完成比较。

- **标价已经含押金又在结算里加一次：** 对照账单列项，只数本次真正要支付的押金一次。
- **所有瓶罐都被当可退：** 查这件包装的标志、所在地和当前返还规则；不合格或未知就不扣。
- **只算退后价，忘了今天现金：** 两列都保留；购买时必须先付的金额不能从口袋里提前消失。
- **有资格却不方便退：** 把净额保留为条件情境，按今天应付和你真实可行的取得/退还路径作决定。
- **一边包含不同饮品量：** 先锁同总量或改为同单位比较，别拿一瓶对两瓶的押金差当饮品价格差。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇只比较普通饮品包装在当前观察到的价格与明示容器返还规则；不解释法律权利，不处理酒精、药物、医疗食品或保健功效。地区、商家、容器和返还方式不同，官方范例不能跨地区移植。读屏、放大、纸笔或向店员询问可替代小字；关键数字缺失就停止条件净额比较。你可能选择今天少付，也可能选择可退后净额较低的方案；两者各有真实前提。

## English

Load this Skill after deciding on the **same total amount of equally suitable ordinary beverage** when candidate packages have different refundable container deposits or one has none. Answer separately: **what you pay today** and **what your net spend would be after the specific eligible containers are actually returned under local rules**. The second figure is conditional, not a refund already in your hand. Check eligibility and a usable return route for this packaging and region.

### Preparation and inputs

Fix the same beverage purpose and total amount. Record each option's available pack count, net contents, currency, and current checkout price. If specifications or amounts differ, first use [Compare Unit Prices](../compare-unit-prices/SKILL.md) and the separate quantity decision. Check whether the deposit is **included** in the shelf or checkout total or shown as an extra line. Separate refundable deposit from nonrefundable fees, taxes, and discounts. Find the actual applicable official or seller return terms: eligible container, required condition, return point, and amount per item. Do not import another region's or package's figure.

### Execution

1. **Calculate today's same-quantity amount first.** For each option, start from confirmed checkout or shelf terms and add any deposit and other mandatory charge not already included. Do not add an included deposit twice. If checkout still hides a required charge, mark today's total unconfirmed rather than using the headline price as cash due.
2. **Check one redeemable condition.** If the deposit is genuinely refundable and this container qualifies here, record the per-container amount and conditions. For unclear label, region, purchase source, container condition, or return route, mark the refund unknown and do not subtract it. Returning it is still a future action, not something done today.
3. **Write both price bases side by side.** “Pay today” is step 1. “Net after every eligible container is actually returned” equals today's amount minus the verified total redemption. For example, both options provide 2 litres. A costs 6 today including a 0.4 deposit and would net 5.6 after successful eligible return. B costs 5.8 today with no deposit. B requires 0.2 less cash now, while A would cost 0.2 less after the return. If no return happens, A remains 6. These are arithmetic examples, not current prices or local deposit standards.
4. **Rank like with like.** Use pay-today amounts for present cash needs, or conditional net amounts under equally credible return conditions. If rankings reverse, show both instead of one unconditional “cheaper.” Travel, storage, and mobility costs of returning containers belong to the acquisition decision; a stated deposit does not prove you can or will redeem it.
5. **Recheck at payment and stop at fact.** Confirm the final checkout's deposit line, offer, and quantity. Recalculate when price or deposit changes. Without known redemption eligibility, compare only confirmed pay-today amounts. Keep any needed receipt or package marking after purchase; actual later redemption is another action, not a fact completed by this comparison.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

For equal amounts, you can state a **confirmed pay-today amount** and a **conditional net after successful return** for each option, with eligibility and the still-unperformed return explicit. Subtracting a “maybe refundable” deposit from an advertisement does not complete a comparison.

- **An included deposit is added again:** Read the checkout lines and count each actual deposit only once.
- **Every bottle or can is assumed redeemable:** Check this exact package marking and the current local program. Unknown or ineligible means no deduction.
- **Only the later net is shown:** Keep both columns. Cash due at purchase cannot disappear from your wallet early.
- **Eligible, but returning is impractical:** Leave net as a condition and decide using pay-today and your actual feasible acquisition and return route.
- **The beverage amounts differ:** Fix the same total amount or return to unit-price comparison. A one-bottle versus two-bottle deposit difference is not automatically a beverage-price difference.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This compares ordinary beverage packaging using observed current prices and stated container-return terms. It does not interpret legal rights or cover alcohol, medicines, medical foods, or supplement claims. Region, seller, package, and return method differ; official examples are not portable local amounts. Screen reading, magnification, paper, or staff explanation can replace tiny print; missing key numbers stop the conditional-net comparison. You may choose the lower cash need now or a potentially lower later net, each with real conditions.
