## 中文

当你要买一件会长期使用的普通家用商品，而标签没有说明日后能否取得常见替换部件或正式维修渠道时加载。结果是**现在可查的支持路径与限制**，或明确未知。这里不预测未来库存、不解释保修权利，也不指导你拆修电器、燃气设备或危险部件。

### 准备与输入

记下候选的完整型号、版本和销售地区。先说清你关心的是哪类支持：例如可单独购买的普通部件、制造商受理渠道，还是是否有公开的维护说明。不要把“零件”笼统写成所有故障都能自行修好；有些工作只能由合适的维修人员处理。

### 执行

1. **查制造商对准确型号的资料。** 找官方支持页、部件目录、说明书或正式服务说明，记录页面日期与地区。系列首页和几年前的论坛回答只能提供线索。
2. **分开当前事实与未来承诺。** 问店员或制造商：当前有哪些部件/服务可查，谁能取得，是否必须通过授权渠道；若写有支持年限，再记录它从何时计算、适用于哪些型号和地区。今天有货不等于将来一直有货。
3. **核对你的关键缺口。** 如你的购买前提是能更换一个普通易损件，就查它是否列在准确型号下，以及店方说的是可购买、可申请维修，还是仅有一个通用客服电话。这三种不是同一结果。
4. **留下来源和时间。** 写“型号／地区／当前可用路径／正式限制／仍未知”。若询问费用或等待时间，只记录同一时点的实际报价和条件，不把它推广为未来固定费用。
5. **作购前决定。** 需要的支持路径有具体证据才继续考虑；没有资料就暂缓、问制造商或选更透明的型号。不要让“应该有配件”替代零件号或服务说明。

### 成功与停止

你能指出当前正式支持资料、谁提供哪些部件或服务，以及它明确没回答什么；或标为“无资料/未知”后停止。此结果不保证未来维修成功或任何地区的法定权利。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有同系列另一个型号的目录：** 回到准确型号和地区；仍没有就保留未知。
- **客服把耗材、维修件和整机置换混在一起：** 请其逐项说明渠道，不把三者合成“可维修”。
- **口头说“至少十年”：** 找到写明起算时间、地区及产品范围的正式说明；没有就只记录口头说法。
- **资料被撤下或无法打开：** 请制造商提供当前版本或书面答复；无法确认时暂停依赖这项支持的购买。

### 假设、替代与副作用

法律、制造商责任和维修资源随国家、商品与时间变化；本 Skill 只采集购前产品信息，不判断法律义务。没有网络、看不清目录或不便致电时，可带完整型号请店员展示纸本、使用官方文字客服或请可信同伴协助。副作用是购买可能延后，但不会凭一条通用热线想象出不存在的零件仓库。

## English

Load this Skill before buying an ordinary durable household product when its label says little about future access to common replacement parts or a formal repair channel. Your result is the **support route and limits documented today**, or an explicit unknown. This does not predict future stock, interpret warranty rights, or teach repairs to electrical, gas, or hazardous components.

### Preparation and inputs

Record the candidate's full model, revision, and sales region. Name the type of support that matters: an ordinary replaceable part, a maker's service channel, or published maintenance information. “Parts available” does not mean every fault can be fixed at home. Some work belongs with qualified repair staff.

### Execution

1. **Find exact-model maker information.** Look for its official support page, parts catalogue, manual, or service statement. Note the page date and region. A series homepage or years-old forum answer is only a lead.
2. **Separate present facts from future promises.** Ask the shop or maker which parts or services are listed now, who may obtain them, and whether an authorised channel is required. If a support period is stated, record when it starts and which model and region it covers. Stock today does not promise stock forever.
3. **Check your decisive gap.** If access to one ordinary wear part matters to your purchase, see whether it is listed for this model. Distinguish a part for sale, a repair request route, and a generic support phone number; they are different results.
4. **Keep source and time.** Write “model / region / current route / stated limits / unknowns.” If you ask about cost or waiting time, keep the actual dated quote and its conditions rather than turning it into a permanent price.
5. **Decide before buying.** Continue considering the item only when a required support route has concrete information. Otherwise defer, ask the maker, or choose a more transparent model. “There should be parts” is not a part number or a service statement.

### Success and stop

You can point to current formal support information, who provides which parts or services, and what it leaves unanswered; or you mark the route unavailable or unknown and stop. This cannot guarantee a future repair or establish rights in every region.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A catalogue covers only another model in the series:** Return to the full model and region; keep this one unknown if it is absent.
- **Support mixes consumables, repair parts, and whole-unit exchange:** Ask for each channel separately instead of recording all three as “repairable.”
- **Someone says “at least ten years”:** Request the formal statement with start date, region, and covered models. Otherwise record it as a spoken claim only.
- **The page is gone or will not open:** Ask the maker for the current edition or a written answer. Defer a purchase that depends on this support if it stays unclear.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Law, maker duties, and repair resources vary by country, product, and time. This Skill gathers pre-purchase product information; it does not decide legal obligations. Without internet, readable catalogues, or phone access, bring the complete model to a shop for paper information, use official text support, or ask a trusted companion. A purchase may wait, but a generic hotline no longer has to impersonate a parts warehouse.
