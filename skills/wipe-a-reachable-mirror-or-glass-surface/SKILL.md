## 中文

当你家一小块**完整、可从稳定地面触及**的镜面或普通玻璃上有手印、水点和少量灰尘，想恢复清楚的表面时，用这份 Skill 按实际镀膜与边缘结构选择轻擦、去残留和擦干。目标是视线/反射更清楚，没有新划痕或镜边积液；不承诺擦掉镜背已发黑的损伤。外墙窗户、高处玻璃、裂片和电子屏需要别的安全与材料规则。

### 准备与输入

确认玻璃没有裂纹、松动、锋利崩边；站在地面能稳定够到全部要擦的区域，不需要椅子、梯子或身体越过窗外。识别是镜子、普通玻璃、镀膜/磨砂/印花玻璃，查制造商或安装方允许的清洁品；镜子的背面与边缘不是普通正面玻璃。准备两块干净、无砂粒的柔软不掉毛布，一块轻湿擦、一块干燥，及明确相容的小量产品。移开近处容易被液体碰到的电器或物品；无法隔开电气接触风险就停。

看到裂纹、松镜片、黑边扩散或特殊镀膜说明不清时，不用手摸边试硬度；停止日常清洁并找制造商/专业人员。本文不处理碎玻璃和外墙窗。

### 执行

1. **先划出可触及且可擦的区域。** 只处理正面完整玻璃；镜框、密封边、背面、电气面板或特殊涂层按各自说明限制。即使一只手够得到上角，只要脚要离地或身体需探到窗外，就缩小范围，不冒高处风险。
2. **先轻取松散颗粒。** 用干净软布轻轻带走可见灰尘和砂点，让颗粒不被湿布压在玻璃上划出细痕；布沾砂就换干净面。禁止用刀片、硬毛刷或磨料把颗粒往下刮。
3. **在小处试相容湿擦。** 按实际玻璃/镜子说明，将少量允许的产品放在软布上，布是湿润而非滴液；先试不显眼的小片，看是否留膜、褪色、损伤镀层。镜子不向面上直接大量喷液，避免清洁剂沿边缘进到背面。某些普通窗玻璃可用的产品未必适合镜背或特殊镀膜。
4. **小范围擦净并擦干。** 用轻压力处理手印和水点，换洁净布面去掉产品残留，再用干布擦干可触及的正面及镜子边缘附近。不要让液体在下边缘、框缝或背面聚集。对着光检查还有没有普通污迹；若只是擦痕，用干净布复查，不靠加更强溶剂解决。
5. **决定何时停。** 普通污迹已减少且表面干燥，就收好布与产品。若污迹需要强酸、研磨、金属刮刀，或发现黑边/镀膜斑点来自材料内部，停止本次轻清洁并查该产品专法；外表干净不等于玻璃或镜背结构已修复。

### 成功条件

可触及的完整正面普通污迹已按材料许可减少，清洁品和水迹被去除，镜边无明显积液，原玻璃没有新刮痕或涂层损伤；或你识别高处、破裂、镜背/特殊镀膜问题并停止。无需把危险角落擦成“零指纹”。

### 常见报错与补救

- **镜子直接喷到边缘滴水：** 停止喷洒，用允许的干净布处理可见水迹，查镜子说明；持续黑边不是再加清洁剂能洗掉的。
- **布里夹砂仍继续擦：** 换洁净布面，先移走颗粒；加大压力会把小砂变磨料。
- **窗玻璃产品直接用在镜背或镀膜面：** 先核对真实产品许可，在小片测试；不清楚就停。
- **要踩凳子才能够到上方：** 留下高处区域交有合适设备与能力的人处理，本 Skill 只做站在稳定地面可及处。
- **玻璃已有裂纹或松动：** 隔离并停止触碰/加液，转相应破损处理；擦洗不能恢复结构安全。
- **多擦后仍有条纹：** 检查布是否沾了旧清洁品、换干净干布轻复擦，不叠加强酸或溶剂。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理室内小范围、完整、可触及的普通镜面与玻璃轻污；磨砂、印花、自洁或安全膜玻璃应以其制造商说明为准。视觉受限可用斜光、放大镜或可信帮助确认是否还有污迹；握力或移动受限时可缩小区域、使用可控制的短柄软布或请人协助，不靠站高处。少量可见指纹可以保留，镜边受液损坏的代价更大。

## English

Use this Skill for light fingerprints, water spots, and dust on a **small intact mirror or ordinary glass surface reachable from stable floor level**. Check the actual coating and edges, wipe lightly with compatible material, remove residue, and dry. The result is clearer reflection or view without new scratches or pooled liquid at a mirror edge. Existing dark damage behind a mirror cannot be washed away from the front. Exterior windows, high glass, broken pieces, and electronic screens have different rules.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm no crack, loose panel, or sharp chipped edge. You can reach the entire intended patch while standing on a stable floor, with no chair, ladder, or reach outside a window. Identify mirror versus plain, coated, etched, or printed glass, and check maker or installer cleaner permissions. Mirror backing and edges are not simply front-face glass. Have two clean soft lint-free cloths free of grit, one for light damp work and one for drying, plus a little explicitly compatible product. Move nearby items that could be harmed by liquid; stop if electrical contact cannot be kept away.

Stop routine wiping for cracks, loose mirrors, spreading dark edges, or unclear special-coating guidance. Do not feel a sharp edge with a hand to test it. This Skill does not handle broken glass or outside high windows.

### Execution

1. **Bound the reachable, wipeable patch.** Work only on the intact front face. Frame, seal, backing, electronics, and special coating have their own restrictions. If reaching a top corner would lift your feet or lean your body outside, shrink the task. This is ground-level work.
2. **Lift loose particles first.** Gently remove visible dust and grit with a clean soft cloth so a later damp cloth cannot grind them across glass. Switch to a clean cloth face if it catches sand. Do not drag a blade, hard brush, or abrasive over the particles.
3. **Trial compatible damp wiping.** Under actual mirror or glass care, place a small allowed product amount on a soft cloth that is damp but not dripping. Try an inconspicuous small spot for film, fading, or coating change. Do not spray a mirror generously; cleaner can run around an edge to its backing. A product suitable for plain window glass may not suit backing or specialist coatings.
4. **Wipe small areas and dry.** Work light hand marks and spots with modest pressure. Use a clean cloth face to remove cleaner residue, then a dry cloth for the accessible front and near mirror edges. Do not pool liquid along a bottom edge, frame gap, or backing. Check against light for ordinary marks. If only streaks remain, retry with a clean cloth rather than escalating to solvent.
5. **Know when the light clean is over.** Stop when ordinary marks are reduced and the surface dry; put cloth and product away. If a stain seems to require acid, abrasive, metal scraping, or a dark backing or coating patch emerges, stop and find exact product guidance. A clear front cannot repair glass or backing structure.

### Success

Light ordinary marks on the reachable intact face are reduced within material limits, cleaner and moisture removed, and no puddle remains at a mirror edge. No new scratch or coating damage appears. Recognizing height, breakage, backing, or special-coating limits and stopping is also complete. Zero fingerprints at a hazardous corner is not required.

### Common errors and recovery

- **A mirror is sprayed until liquid runs into its edge:** Stop spraying, dry visible permitted moisture, and check its care. More product cannot wash away a persistent dark edge.
- **A gritty cloth keeps wiping:** Change to clean material and lift particles first. More pressure turns sand into an abrasive.
- **Window product is applied to backing or coated glass:** Check the actual maker permission and trial a small area. Stop if uncertain.
- **The upper part needs a stool:** Leave high work for someone with appropriate equipment and ability. This Skill covers only stable ground-level reach.
- **Glass is cracked or loose:** Isolate and stop touching or wetting it; use the breakage route. Cleaning cannot restore structural safety.
- **Streaks survive several passes:** Check cloth for old product and use a clean dry face. Do not layer acids or solvents.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers light soil on intact, reachable indoor ordinary glass or mirrors. Etched, printed, self-cleaning, and safety-film glass follow their maker instructions. Use angled light, magnification, or trusted help when sight makes marks hard to see. If grip or movement is limited, reduce the area, use a controllable short-handled soft cloth, or ask for help rather than climbing. A little visible fingerprint is cheaper than liquid damage to a mirror edge.
