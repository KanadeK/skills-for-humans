## 中文

你的一枚原创图标有三条刻度或三颗小点，手动摆放让间距一大一小时，使用等距分布。完成后首尾标记位置不被意外改掉，中间标记落在均衡位置，缩小时节奏仍清楚。这与“对齐成同一线”是两种不同结果；需要先确定三个标记的外侧边界。

### 准备与输入

保存 SVG，确认三件标记的大小是否一致以及草图要求按中心距还是空白间隙等分。先把两端放在正确位置，单选这三个对象；若它们都在一个组里，先进入组或暂时取消分组。

### 执行

1. 在 Align and Distribute 中选择与草图匹配的水平或垂直分布方式，先核方向和端点。
2. 执行一次等间距分布，检查中间标记是否落在两端之间而不是整体漂移。
3. 缩小到目标尺寸看节奏与可辨性，若三点黏成一团，扩大两端间距再分布。
4. 保存重开，核三件仍可独立选择、大小未因分布改变。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

三处重复标记沿预期轴等间距，首尾位置和对象尺寸仍符合图标设计。

- **整体漂移：** 撤销，先摆好两端再选正确分布按钮。
- **按错方向：** 撤销，核水平或垂直设置。
- **间距虽等仍糊：** 按小尺寸阅读需要调整整体跨度。

### 假设与边界

只处理一组三个简单矢量标记，不推导复杂图案规律或多页排版网格。

## English

Use this when an original icon has three ticks or dots with visibly uneven gaps. Distribute them evenly while preserving deliberately placed first and last marks. The middle mark should settle into a balanced interval that still reads when reduced. Equal spacing is a different outcome from placing all parts on one alignment line.

### Preparation and inputs

Save and check whether the three marks share a size and whether the sketch calls for equal center distances or equal blank gaps. Position outer marks first and select only the three objects. If they form one group, enter it or ungroup temporarily.

### Execution

1. In Align and Distribute choose horizontal or vertical distribution that matches the sketch, confirming axis and end marks.
2. Apply distribution once and inspect the middle mark between the outer anchors rather than a shifted whole set.
3. Zoom to target size; if marks merge visually, move outer anchors apart and redistribute.
4. Save and reopen, confirming each mark remains separately selectable and keeps its dimensions.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The three marks have equal planned spacing with outer positions and object sizes preserved.

- **Whole set moved:** Undo, place endpoints and choose the right distribution action.
- **Wrong direction:** Undo and check the chosen axis.
- **Equal but crowded:** Increase overall span for small-size legibility.

### Assumptions and limits

This spaces three simple vector marks, not a complex pattern system or multi-page layout grid.
