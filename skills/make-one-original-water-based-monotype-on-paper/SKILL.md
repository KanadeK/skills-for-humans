## 中文

当你想在**自己的可洗平滑板上现画一幅原创小图**，趁水基颜料尚可转移时只压出一张纸印，加载这份 Skill。结果是一张独特的纸面转印；它与可重复压同样凹线的泡棉版小组不同。板材须能按产品说明清洗，不能用借来的屏幕、文物玻璃、餐具或怕染色的家具作板。

### 准备与输入

备自有适合水基可洗颜料的平滑塑料/亚克力板或其它厂商准许板、少量普通水基可洗颜料、自己的纸、合用笔和护台垫。先在板边小试涂色与清洗，确认不会永久染色、板边不锐；未确认就换板。决定一个无需复制任何画作的小图；字和方向会镜像，没把握就不用字。油性油墨、溶剂及压机不在本篇。

### 执行

1. **在板上画单次图像。** 用薄而可转印的湿色画几条或几块原创形状，留部分板面空白；颜料厚到能流淌就吸减，不在板上等到完全干才压。
2. **纸板对位并手压。** 将纸轻放在湿图像上，稳住一角，用平掌或合用光滑工具从中心向外温和压过，不横向滑纸。板若不稳，停下加防滑垫或请人稳住，而非提高压力。
3. **一次揭开并晾平。** 从一边缓慢揭起，放在清洁干燥位置，观察图像的镜像、缺印、意外混色。它可能只有一张充分转印；残留的较淡第二印是可选实验，不伪称与首张完全一致的版次。
4. **标记与清板。** 纸边标日期与“独版试作”，让纸干透；按颜料/板说明清洗平板和笔，检查台面。若首张失败，清板重画新图，记录是另一次试作。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

至少一张从湿板转来的原创纸面图像正在安全晾干，方向/缺印能被说明，板材可清洁。只在板上绘画未转到纸，不算独版完成。

- **颜料在压纸前干了：** 按产品条件重新润湿或清板重画，不用溶剂强洗。
- **图像糊成大片：** 下一次减色量、减少水和纸滑动；本次诚实保留为试作。
- **纸没有完整接触板：** 小面积逐区手压，别猛压易碎板或用家具压机。
- **字反向：** 重画镜像字或不使用字，不能把纸上补字称原板转印。
- **手眼/行动受限：** 小板、大形状、定位角标和可信协助稳板，由你决定是否接受转印。

### 假设与边界

独版图像每次重画，不能像泡棉凹线版那样保证下一张图案重复。NGA 示例列多种媒体和板，本篇只选普通可洗水基材料；洗净板不代表可重新用于食品。

## English

Load this Skill when you want to **paint a small original image on your own washable smooth plate** and press one paper impression while water-based paint can still transfer. Finish with a unique paper monotype. This differs from a foam plate whose fixed recesses support a small repeated run. The plate must be washable under its maker's directions; do not use a borrowed screen, artifact glass, dish, or stain-sensitive furniture as a plate.

### Preparation and inputs

Have an owned smooth plastic/acrylic or other maker-approved plate, a little ordinary washable water-based paint, owned paper, suitable brush, and protected surface. Test painting and cleaning at a plate edge, checking for permanent stain or sharp sides; change plates when uncertain. Choose an original simple image without copying an artwork. Text and direction reverse, so omit text if unsure. Oil ink, solvents, and printing presses are outside scope.

### Execution

1. **Paint a one-time plate image.** Make a few original lines or fields with a thin transferable wet layer and open plate space. Blot paint thick enough to run. Do not wait until it fully dries on the plate.
2. **Align paper and press by hand.** Place paper gently over the wet image, hold one corner, and press from centre outward with flat hand or a suitable smooth tool without sliding. If the plate slips, add a non-slip backing or trusted holding help rather than more force.
3. **Lift once and dry flat.** Peel from one side, put the sheet in a clean drying spot, and observe reversal, gaps, and unexpected colour blends. There may be only one strong impression; a lighter residue print is optional and should not be called an identical edition copy.
4. **Label and clean plate.** Mark date and “monotype trial” on the paper margin and dry it completely. Wash plate and brush under their paint directions and check the table. If the first transfer fails, clean and repaint a new design, recording a new attempt.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

At least one original image transferred from the wet plate to paper is drying safely; its orientation and missed areas can be described, and the plate can be cleaned. Painting only on the plate without paper transfer is not a finished monotype.

- **Paint dries before contact:** Rewet only as the product allows or clean and repaint, without solvent force.
- **Image smears into a patch:** Use less paint/water and prevent paper sliding next time; keep this sheet honestly as a trial.
- **Paper misses part of the plate:** Press small areas evenly without crushing a fragile plate or using furniture as a press.
- **Text reverses:** Repaint mirrored text or omit it; writing over the paper is not plate transfer.
- **Grip, sight, or movement is limited:** Use a small plate, broad shapes, corner alignment marks, and trusted help holding it while you accept or reject transfer.

### Assumptions and limits

A monotype image is repainted for each attempt; it cannot promise the repeatability of a recessed foam plate. NGA lists several media and plate materials, while this beginner route uses only washable water-based ones and original art. A cleaned art plate does not become food equipment.
