## 中文

你有一张标题和要点已写好的内容页，文字大小或缩进让读者分不清主次时使用本篇。成果是在放映预览中标题先被识别，正文要点按一两级阅读且没有截字；不把每一行都做成同样粗大的标题。这里收敛现有内容层级，不重写主题结论。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODP 副本，写下标题、一级要点与确有从属关系的少数二级要点。先在普通编辑视图看是否用了布局占位框，是否有文字框超出画布。若字数太多，考虑拆页或精简而非无限缩字号。

### 执行

1. 把主标题留在标题占位框，正文放正文框，按语义调整大纲级别。
2. 只保留必要的二级缩进，统一同级字形与行距，不用重色块代替层级。
3. 在放映预览看标题、最长要点、最后一行，检查边缘和对象之间无覆盖。
4. 若仍难读，撤销局部缩小并拆分内容；保存重开后再预览。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一张页标题与要点主次可辨，最长和末尾文字完整，放映预览可读。

- **正文缩到太小：** 减少文字或拆页，不靠微字通过。
- **二级比一级更突出：** 核大纲级别与样式。
- **末行被截：** 调整框高或内容量后重预览。

### 假设与边界

本篇只核一页的层级与可读性，不证明观点正确或现场投影效果。你决定语义顺序；不同屏幕与字体会影响实际观看。

## English

Use this Skill when a slide's existing title and points have confusing size or indentation. Finish with a title recognizable first, body points readable at one or two levels, and no clipped text in show preview. Do not make every line an equally large heading. This clarifies existing hierarchy without rewriting the topic's conclusions.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODP copy and list title, primary points and genuinely subordinate secondary points. Check normal view for layout placeholders and off-canvas text. If too much text exists, consider reducing or splitting content instead of endless font shrinking.

### Execution

1. Keep main title in the layout's title area and body in its content area, setting outline levels by meaning.
2. Retain only needed second-level indentation with consistent type and spacing, not heavy blocks as hierarchy substitutes.
3. In show preview inspect title, longest bullet and last line for clipping or overlap.
4. If still hard to read, undo tiny type and split content; save, reopen and preview again.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Title and point hierarchy are apparent, longest and last text remain complete, and show preview is readable.

- **Body too small:** Reduce or split content rather than using tiny type.
- **Subpoint dominates:** Inspect outline level and styling.
- **Last line clipped:** Adjust box or content amount and re-preview.

### Assumptions and limits

This checks one slide's hierarchy and readability, not factual correctness or live projection. You decide semantic order; display and fonts affect viewing.
