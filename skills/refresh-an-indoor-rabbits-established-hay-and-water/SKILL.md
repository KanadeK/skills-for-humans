## 中文

当你照看**已有饮食方案、平常吃草喝水的室内兔或稳定兔群**，要补足原方案中的干草和清洁饮水时，加载这份 Skill。结果是兔在原可及位置能取到质量合格的草和水，脏湿容器被清理，并记录食欲；不是重新开营养处方或随意改颗粒粮/蔬菜量。若兔停止进食或没有新粪便，立即联系兽医，不把“再放一把草”当治疗。

### 准备与输入

看原主人/兽医指定草类、水碗/水瓶位置与多兔资源数量，查上次补充记录。备干燥无霉味的本兔已用草、清水、可洗容器、合用清洁用品；不同饮食阶段或病兔按兽医方案。草发潮、发霉、误掺陌生植物、饮水器破裂或不能确认食欲基线时先停并核实。不要让兔在清理时失去所有饮水入口。

### 执行

1. **先看兔和旧供应。** 看兔是否主动进草、是否有新粪便，草架/草区是否还有干净草，水量、水色和容器是否被排泄物污染。停止吃或不排新粪为立即兽医联系，不排在本次普通补料后再观察一夜。
2. **去掉不合格草并补原草。** 轻移走湿脏、霉变或掉进厕所的旧草，按原计划在兔平常可取的位置添干净草；多兔时核每只都能实际接近，不让一只挡住全部供应。把草和厕所关系维持原有布局，不临时改住处。
3. **清容器并换水。** 留另一个可用饮水点或按原方案短时处理，倒旧水、按容器说明清洗并彻底冲净清洁剂，再加适合本兔饮用的清水。碗稳固不易翻，水瓶按说明确认出水；不把未冲净肥皂水给兔。
4. **核可及并记录。** 回看草、水都可自由取得、容器不漏、兔仍有原活动/躲藏路；记补水/草时间及实际摄食观察。异常联系兽医或原负责人，颗粒/蔬菜另按原方案而非本篇加量。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

合格草与清洁水在原计划位置可及，多兔没有被单点争抢完全阻断，旧湿脏供应被移除，饮食观察有记录；停止吃/无新粪便已立即转兽医。只添草而水碗空/脏，不算完成。

- **兔没吃或无新粪便：** 立即联系兽医，说明最后一次正常进食/排便，不等下一次巡视。
- **草发霉或混陌生植物：** 不投，换原方案合格草。
- **水瓶不出水或碗易翻：** 暂换原方案允许的可达水源并求修，不让兔断水。
- **清洁剂味仍在：** 继续冲净/换容器，不能直接加水盖住残留。
- **抓握/视觉不便：** 较轻水容器、清楚标签和可信协助检查出水，由你确认兔可达。

### 假设与边界

兔需要持续取得适宜干草和饮水，但具体品种、年龄、特殊饮食与多兔资源由现有方案决定。PDSA 对普通健康兔的一般比例不能替代这只兔的兽医计划；本文不诊断厌食或给药。

## English

Load this Skill when **already-owned indoor rabbits with an established diet normally eat hay and drink water**, and you must refresh that existing supply. Finish with sound hay and clean water reachable in their usual places, soiled vessels cleaned, and appetite recorded. Do not redesign nutrition or change pellet/vegetable amounts. If a rabbit stops eating or produces no new droppings, contact a vet immediately; more hay is not treatment.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the owner's/vet's hay type, bowl/bottle place, and resource plan for multiple rabbits. Check last refill. Have dry nonmoldy approved hay, clean water, washable vessels, and compatible cleaner. Different ages or illness follow vet directions. Stop and verify for damp/moldy hay, unknown plants, broken drinkers, or no reliable appetite baseline. Keep some drinking route available while a vessel is being washed.

### Execution

1. **Look at rabbits and existing supply.** Check whether they approach hay, whether fresh droppings exist, how much clean dry hay remains, and water level/contamination. Not eating or no new droppings goes to immediate vet contact, not an overnight wait after this refill.
2. **Remove bad hay and restore the known kind.** Lift wet, dirty, moldy, or toileted hay and put fresh approved hay in usual reachable places. For multiple rabbits, verify each can actually reach it without one blocking all supply. Keep their established hay/toilet layout rather than rebuilding housing without a plan.
3. **Clean vessel and replace water.** Preserve another usable drinking point or follow a short existing procedure. Empty old water, clean and thoroughly rinse the vessel under its instructions, then refill with water suitable under this rabbit's plan. Check bowl stability or bottle delivery; soap residue is not drinking water.
4. **Confirm access and record.** Verify free access, no leaks, and routes to usual activity/hides. Record time and actual eating observation. Route abnormality to vet or owner; pellets/greens stay under their separate established plan.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Approved hay and clean water are reachable in planned locations, no rabbit is fully blocked by single-point competition, old soiled material is removed, and intake is recorded. A non-eating/no-droppings rabbit has immediate vet escalation. Hay refilled beside an empty dirty water bowl is unfinished.

- **Rabbit has not eaten or produced new droppings:** Call a vet immediately with last known normal times, not next-round monitoring.
- **Hay is moldy or mixed with unknown plants:** Do not offer it; use sound plan-approved hay.
- **Bottle fails or bowl overturns:** Provide an accessible plan-approved water route and seek repair without leaving rabbits dry.
- **Cleaner smell remains:** Rinse or change vessel rather than hiding residue under water.
- **Grip or sight is limited:** Light vessels, clear labels, and trusted water-delivery checks can support your access decision.

### Assumptions and limits

Rabbits need continuing appropriate hay and water, while breed, age, special diet, and multi-rabbit resource details belong to the actual plan. General PDSA proportions do not replace this rabbit's vet advice. No appetite diagnosis or drug is offered.
