## 中文

当普通小件的非承重平板组装被打断，你不确定上次做到哪一步、某些连接件是否已拧到位时加载。目标是**用准确说明和眼前实物找回可靠的继续位置，或保持暂停并说明未知**。这里不修复损坏件、不猜测承重安全，也不替代缺件交涉、专业墙固或电器维修。

### 准备与输入

先确认仍是准确型号和版本的说明，找齐剩余原袋、已做步骤记录与实物。检查部分组装体是否稳定到可以安全观察；会倒、需有人持续支撑或有锐利破损时先停止并让合适的人处理，不靠手扶着猜。使用者无需凭记忆承认某步已经完成。

### 执行

1. **把当前状态作为未知起点。** 从说明第 1 步开始，只用实际看得见的板件朝向、孔位、连接件和对应数量判断每一步是否确实完成。找不到实物证据就写“不确定”，不要跨到后面页码。
2. **核对未固定与暂时松留。** 分清说明要求本阶段仍松的连接、应已锁紧的普通连接及还没加入的零件；不按“摸着有点晃”统一拧紧。需要触碰危险或承重部位才能判断时退出本低风险流程。
3. **找最早不确定或不一致的一步。** 记步骤号、实物与图示差别、剩余同编号零件数量。若说明明确允许倒回，且实物完好并能安全操作，可以按它的顺序退回到已核实状态；否则停下问供应方或合适专业人员。
4. **重新确认继续条件。** 需要的人数、工具、工作面与未完成步骤的零件仍齐全，板件没有因放置中断而弯曲、损坏或换位；缺任何一项就保持暂停，不用替代螺丝或强行对孔。
5. **从确认点继续并做短记录。** 在第一步未完成/需重做的地方按准确说明继续，每完成一页与实物回读一次。若还会中断，记最后确实完成的步骤、需保持松的连接和剩余零件位置；别把预计进度写成已完成。

### 完成与停止

你能指出一个有实物证据支持的最后完成步骤、下一步、松留状态与剩余零件，才可继续普通非承重流程；或者保留具体未知并停在安全状态。零件损坏、方向无法辨认、部分结构不稳、说明含墙固/防倾倒或需要危险操作时不复工、不使用成品。

### 常见报错与补救

- **记忆说第 7 步，实物只到第 5 步：** 以实物和准确说明为准，从最早未确认处重查。
- **上次把应暂时松留的件拧死：** 查说明是否允许安全松回；不允许或材料已受损就停，不硬掰板件。
- **剩余螺丝数与预期不符：** 复核所有原袋及已装位置，无法对应时不拿相似件补。
- **部分用品竖着会晃：** 按说明允许的安全姿态放置并取得需要的帮助；不能稳定时不边扶边继续。
- **没有前次照片或记录：** 仍可逐步实物核对；看不到的连接状态不能靠猜补全。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只恢复厂家允许的普通小件非结构性组装。不同产品说明对暂紧、翻转与倒回的要求不同，不用通用口诀代替。阅读或视力困难时由可信协助者按页读回、你核实实物；部分结构重到不能安全处理时交有能力的人或专业服务。副作用是“我记得做到这了”变成能指认的实物证据。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary small non-load-bearing flat-pack build was interrupted and you are unsure which step was finished or which connection was left loose. Your outcome is **a verified restart point from the exact guide and real item, or an honest paused state with named unknowns**. This does not repair damage, guess load safety, manage supplier shortages, fit walls or anti-tip devices, or repair equipment.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the guide still matches the exact model and revision. Gather the remaining original bags, prior step notes and partial item. First check that the assembly is stable enough to observe safely. If it may topple, needs someone to hold it continuously or has sharp damage, stop and seek suitable help. Memory is not proof a step was done.

### Execution

1. **Treat the present state as an unknown start.** From Step 1, use only visible panel orientation, holes, connectors and matching counts to decide whether each step was really completed. Mark a step uncertain when the physical evidence is missing; do not skip forward by page number.
2. **Separate unfinished from intentionally loose.** Distinguish joints the guide says should still be loose, ordinary joints already meant to be secured, and parts not yet introduced. Do not tighten everything because it “feels a little wobbly.” Exit this low-risk flow if checking requires touching hazardous or load-bearing parts.
3. **Find the earliest uncertain or conflicting step.** Note its number, difference between real item and drawing, and remaining pieces of that ID. Reverse to a verified state only when the maker explicitly allows it and the item is sound and safe to handle. Otherwise ask the supplier or suitable qualified help.
4. **Recheck conditions for continuing.** Required people, tools, work area and remaining parts are still available, and panels were not bent, damaged or moved into a new wrong position during the pause. If one is missing, remain paused; do not substitute a screw or force holes together.
5. **Continue from the verified point and record it.** Follow the exact guide at the first undone or maker-permitted redo step, comparing each finished page with the item. If stopping again, note the last step physically confirmed, joints that should remain loose and where remaining parts are. Do not record planned progress as completed.

### Success and stop

Continue ordinary non-structural assembly only when you can identify a last completed step with physical evidence, the next step, intentionally loose states and remaining parts; otherwise retain a specific unknown and stop safely. Do not restart or use the item with damaged parts, unreadable orientation, unstable partial structure, required wall or anti-tip fixing, or a hazardous operation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Memory says Step 7; the item only supports Step 5:** Use the physical item and exact guide, restarting at the earliest unconfirmed step.
- **A joint meant to stay loose was tightened early:** Read whether the maker allows safely loosening it. Stop if not or material is already damaged; do not bend the panel to compensate.
- **Remaining screw count differs:** Recheck original bags and installed positions. Do not fill a gap with a lookalike.
- **The partial item wobbles upright:** Use only a stable orientation the guide permits and required help. Do not keep assembling while holding it up by hand.
- **No earlier photo or note exists:** Work through the real steps anyway; a hidden joint state cannot be completed by guessing.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This resumes only maker-permitted ordinary small non-structural assembly. Each guide has its own loose-tight, flipping and reversal rules; no generic shortcut overrides them. A trusted person can read pages aloud when reading or sight is difficult while you verify reality. Ask capable or professional help if the partial item is too heavy to handle safely. “I think we reached this step” now requires physical evidence.
