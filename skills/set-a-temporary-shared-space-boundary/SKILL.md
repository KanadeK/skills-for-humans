## 中文

当你想为一次来访、普通活动或工作临时占用共用区域，且结束后其他人还要照常使用时加载。你要在开始前确认**哪一块可用、哪些通路与用途必须保留、何时恢复以及谁负责复原**。这不决定长期房间使用权、结束访客社交关系或搬动重型家具。

### 准备与输入

先核对实际房屋、管理方或既有共住约定是否允许这次使用。问受影响者原本在该时段要怎样使用空间，是否需要通行、取物或安静。确认要放入的普通轻量物品、可移动范围和清理用品；他人私物不能因为活动需要就自动挪动。若要拍原状照片，先确认拍摄权限并避开私人信息。

### 执行

1. **画可用与保留区。** 用能被相关人读懂的简图或描述，写“活动用哪块 → 入口、门、通路和他人必需使用点继续可用”。人数、坐席、普通器材超过空间实际能力时缩小活动或换地方，不靠临时堵门解决。
2. **说清开始和真正结束。** 约定布置开始、活动结束、取走物品与恢复完成各自的大致时点。恢复需要时间，就把它算进占用，而不是活动一结束就宣布空间已还。
3. **逐项确认临时变化。** 哪些轻量家具或物品获准移动，原先在哪里、谁会移动、由谁恢复；不能安全移动或未经物主同意的保留原位。装饰、用电或噪声等依实际场所规则，不在本 Skill 自创许可。
4. **指定一个恢复负责人和可见终点。** 负责人可以协调多人，但要知道将普通垃圾、自己的物件和获准移动的东西送回哪里，并确认下一个使用者能进入。必要时记录许可范围内的原状文字或照片，给复原作对照。
5. **让受影响者回读。** 谁还会使用哪些位置、临时占用何时结束、谁收到变动通知？未获同意或关键通路仍会被挡时停在草案，不把没人反对当成许可。

### 完成与停止

实际相关的人知道可用范围、保留路线、时间、允许的改动、恢复负责人和判断空间已可再用的信号。场所不允许、受影响者未确认、通路或紧急出口被堵、需要危险搬运时不要按原方案布置，换小范围或另择地点。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只说“今晚用客厅”：** 补出具体面积、他人仍要走的路和恢复完成时点。
- **活动结束时间没有算清理：** 与相关人重新确认延长后的占用或缩短活动，让恢复不挤占下一位使用者。
- **物品属于室友但主人不在：** 不擅自移动；另选布局或等对方明确同意。
- **大家以为另一个人负责收尾：** 写唯一协调人和实际执行者，开始前读回。
- **照片包含私人文件或人：** 不拍或在权限允许下只记物品位置和简图；复原不要求公开隐私。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通一次性使用和住户有权讨论的共用区；机构预约或租赁条件以该场所现行规则为准。活动、文化、行动能力和住户作息各异，没有通用结束时间。无法搬动或看清布局时，可请愿意且获准的人做轻量布置，用大字、触觉或语音回读边界；不以“恢复”理由让人搬重物。副作用是“就借一下”终于有可核对的边界。

## English

Load this Skill before temporarily using a common area for a visit, ordinary activity or work when others will need the space again afterward. Agree **which part you may use, which paths and purposes stay available, when normal use returns and who coordinates the reset**. This does not create a permanent room right, end a visit socially or teach heavy furniture moving.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the real housing, management or existing housemate rules for this use. Ask affected people what they need from the space at that time, including passage, access to goods and quiet. Identify ordinary light items, permissible moves and cleanup supplies. Another person's private goods are not automatically movable for an activity. Confirm photography permission before recording the original layout and avoid private information.

### Execution

1. **Mark use and reserved areas.** With a sketch or description affected people can understand, note “activity area → entrance, doors, routes and others' necessary points that stay usable.” Reduce the plan or choose another place if people, seats or ordinary equipment exceed the space rather than blocking a door.
2. **Separate activity end from reset end.** Agree on approximate setup, activity, item removal and completed-restoration times. Count reset time inside your occupation; the space is not returned merely because the event ended.
3. **Confirm temporary changes one by one.** Which light furnishings may move, where they started, who moves them and who returns them? Leave a heavy or unapproved item in place. Follow actual site rules for decoration, power and noise rather than inventing permission here.
4. **Name one reset coordinator and a visible finish.** The coordinator may involve others but must know where ordinary waste, your goods and approved moved items go, and how the next user can enter. When permitted, note the original arrangement in words or a privacy-aware image for comparison.
5. **Read back with affected people.** Who still uses which position, when does temporary use end and who receives change notices? If consent is missing or a necessary path stays blocked, keep this as a draft. No objection is not permission.

### Success and stop

The affected people know the allowed area, protected route, time, permitted changes, reset coordinator and signal that the space works again. Do not set up as planned when site rules forbid it, people have not confirmed, a route or exit would be blocked, or unsafe moving is required. Shrink the plan or use another place.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“We'll use the living room tonight” is all the plan says:** Add its exact part, the path others still need and reset-complete time.
- **The end time excludes cleanup:** Agree on the longer occupation or shorten the activity so reset does not consume the next person's time.
- **A housemate owns an item but is absent:** Do not move it. Change layout or wait for permission.
- **Everybody assumes someone else will finish:** Name one coordinator and the people doing each part, then read back before use.
- **A photo includes faces or private documents:** Do not take or share it. With permission, use only item positions or a simple sketch; resetting does not require exposed private information.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers an ordinary one-off use of a common area residents may discuss; the actual site's booking or housing rules govern. Activity, culture, mobility and schedules vary, so there is no universal end time. If moving or seeing the layout is difficult, ask willing authorised people to handle light setup and use large-print, tactile or spoken readback. A reset is no reason to force a heavy lift. “Just for a bit” now has a checkable boundary.
