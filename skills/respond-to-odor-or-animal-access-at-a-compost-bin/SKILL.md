## 中文

当你的普通庭院堆肥桶**出现异常腐臭、裸露食物或动物进入迹象**，加载这份 Skill。结果是停止续投，安全排查并修正可控的干料/通气/盖问题，或退出这条家庭流程交当地服务处理。它不教捕鼠、喷药、徒手接触野生动物或保证“无害虫”。有动物正在桶内、咬伤风险或大量污染时不要开盖翻找，退开并联系当地合适服务。

### 准备与输入

从安全距离看盖是否开裂/没合、食物是否露在表面、周围有无掉落物、积水或脚印；戴手套，避免扬尘，备合用干叶/普通干纸和钝工具。先准备本批厨余的替代处置，不让它在厨房无限堆放。当地动物/废物管理规则优先；不能判断是什么动物时不靠近确认。

### 执行

1. **暂停喂料并分辨风险。** 不再往桶里加湿厨余。若动物仍在、桶被咬穿/翻倒、出现大量粪污或尖锐物，保持距离、隔离人和宠物，按当地服务/管理者安排，不在本篇继续动手。
2. **只在可安全接近时查原因。** 看水是否积、材料是否黏湿、食物是否裸露、盖/屏障能否闭合；翻阅上次投料是否有肉奶油脂或不合格包装。强腐臭常与过湿或缺空气有关，不能仅用香味掩盖。
3. **做有限可逆修正。** 若只是湿臭且无动物在场，补合格干料、轻松动一小部分、查排水；若只是食物暴露，埋回并用足量干料盖住，修好合用盖。不能安全修盖或已反复招动物，就暂停该桶并改用获准收集服务，不放毒饵或化学驱避剂。
4. **清理周围并复查。** 不徒手捡可疑污染物；按当地规则清理自己掉落的普通材料，脱手套洗手，记录问题、修正和下一次查看点。只有气味、动物通路与盖都确实改善后才考虑恢复小批投料。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

续投已停，风险分级清楚，普通可控问题得到修正并留复查，或桶被停用并交合适服务。给臭堆再倒香料/大量厨余，不能算处理。

- **加干料后仍腐臭：** 继续暂停投料，查积水、压实与不合格料；无法安全处理就退出家庭桶。
- **动物反复掀盖：** 不与动物接触，改善合用屏障或联系场地/当地管理者。
- **发现肉、奶、油、宠物粪：** 不在桶里继续埋深；按当地安全清理途径处置，必要时由合适人员处理。
- **自己不能安全翻动：** 不强行搬重桶，寻可信帮助或转收集服务。
- **嗅觉不便：** 可信协助者可报告气味，仍结合可见湿度、盖与暴露食物判断。

### 假设与边界

EPA 说明家庭堆肥的臭味可能由湿或缺氧引起，盖和埋食物有助控制动物；实际动物与场地风险不同。一次改善不证明桶无病原体、无鼠或适合食用作物。高风险污染与动物接触超出本篇。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary backyard compost bin has **abnormal foul odor, exposed food, or signs of animal entry**. Finish by pausing feed, safely correcting controllable brown/air/lid problems, or retiring this home route for local service. It does not teach trapping, pesticides, bare-hand wildlife handling, or a pest-free guarantee. If an animal is inside, there is a bite risk, or contamination is extensive, do not open and dig through it; step back and contact the appropriate local service.

### Preparation and inputs

From a safe distance inspect lid closure and damage, exposed food, spilled scraps, water, and tracks. Use gloves, avoid raising dust, and have accepted dry leaves or plain paper and a blunt tool. Prepare an alternate route for today's scraps instead of letting a kitchen bucket sit indefinitely. Local animal and waste rules come first. Do not approach to identify an uncertain animal.

### Execution

1. **Stop feed and grade risk.** Add no more wet kitchen scraps. If an animal remains, the bin is bitten through or overturned, or there is heavy fecal or sharp contamination, stay back, keep people and pets away, and follow local service or site-manager directions rather than working this routine.
2. **Inspect only when approach is safe.** Look for pooled water, sticky wet material, visible food, and whether lid and barrier work. Review recent additions for meat, dairy, fats, or ineligible packaging. Strong rot smell often involves wetness or low air; fragrance does not solve it.
3. **Make limited reversible corrections.** For merely wet odor without an animal present, add suitable browns, loosen a small area, and check drainage. For exposed food, bury and cover it well and repair an appropriate lid. If a lid cannot be made safe or animals repeatedly get in, stop using this bin and choose permitted collection, without poison bait or chemical repellent.
4. **Clean nearby and recheck.** Do not pick suspicious contamination up bare-handed. Clear ordinary scraps you caused under local rules, remove gloves, wash hands, and note problem, correction, and a later check. Resume small feeds only when odor, animal route, and lid have actually improved.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Feed has paused, risk is understood, an ordinary controllable problem has a correction and recheck, or the bin is stopped and routed to suitable service. Adding fragrance or more kitchen waste to a stinking pile is not a fix.

- **Odor persists after browns:** Keep feed paused and check pooling, compaction, and ineligible material; leave home-bin handling if unsafe.
- **Animals repeatedly lift lid:** Avoid contact, improve a suitable barrier, or involve the site manager or local service.
- **Meat, dairy, oils, or pet waste is found:** Do not bury it deeper as a solution; use local safe removal, by appropriate personnel if needed.
- **You cannot turn safely:** Do not force a heavy bin; seek trusted help or a collection service.
- **Smell is inaccessible:** Trusted help may report odor while visible wetness, lid, and food exposure still guide the decision.

### Assumptions and limits

EPA notes home-bin odor can reflect excess moisture or little air, and covered scraps plus a working lid help limit animal access. Actual animals and site risks vary. A single improvement cannot prove no pathogens, no rodents, or food-crop suitability. High-risk contamination and animal contact are outside this Skill.
