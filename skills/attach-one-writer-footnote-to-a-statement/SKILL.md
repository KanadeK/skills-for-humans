## 中文

你有一处具体事实或术语需要在页底交代来源或短背景，又不想打断正文时使用本篇。成果是标记紧跟对应句子、页底有同号脚注，读者能找到可信来源或解释。它不是给整段随意加一个无关链接，也不替你核实事实真伪。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODT 副本，圈定要注释的准确句末，准备自己可核的来源信息或简短解释。先看文稿现有脚注样式与编号，避免手敲上标数字。若来源缺失，先保留待核，不用编造出处。

### 执行

1. 将光标放在对应句末标点之后，使用 Insert > Footnote and Endnote > Footnote。
2. 在跳到的脚注区域输入精简、可核内容，保留足够识别来源的信息。
3. 回正文核标记位置及脚注号对应，再看页底没有截断或跟到错误句。
4. 保存重开，抽查分页变化后标记和脚注仍成对；错位时撤销重插，不手改编号。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一处正文标记、一条页底脚注准确配对，内容可核，正文没有无关插入。

- **标在错句：** 撤销并在准确句末重插。
- **脚注没有来源：** 写明待核而非虚构引文。
- **两处数字不同：** 用真正脚注字段，不手敲上标。

### 假设与边界

本篇只添加一条普通脚注；学术引文格式、法律效力与来源真实性仍由你按相应规范核查。

## English

Use this Skill when one specific fact or term needs a short source or context note at page bottom without interrupting prose. Finish with a marker immediately after the relevant statement and a matching numbered footnote with trustworthy source or explanation. This is not a random link for a whole paragraph and does not itself prove the fact.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODT copy, identify the exact statement end and prepare a verifiable source or brief explanation. Inspect existing footnote numbering and style. Do not type a superscript numeral manually. If source is missing, mark it for review rather than inventing attribution.

### Execution

1. Place cursor just after the statement punctuation and use Insert > Footnote and Endnote > Footnote.
2. Enter concise verifiable content in the footnote area, enough to identify a source.
3. Return to body and check marker placement and matching number, then inspect page bottom for clipping or wrong association.
4. Save and reopen, checking marker and note stay paired after pagination. Undo and reinsert for a misplaced note, not typed numbering.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

One body marker and one page-bottom footnote pair correctly, note content is checkable and prose has no unrelated insertion.

- **Marker on wrong statement:** Undo and reinsert at exact end.
- **Note lacks source:** Mark for checking instead of inventing citation.
- **Numbers differ:** Use actual footnote, not typed superscript.

### Assumptions and limits

This adds one ordinary footnote. Scholarly citation style, legal force and source truth still require your relevant checks.
