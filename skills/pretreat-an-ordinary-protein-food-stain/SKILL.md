## 中文

当一件**普通可水洗衣物**刚沾上或留有可确认的蛋、奶、酸奶等**普通食物蛋白污渍**，你准备在整件洗涤前处理它时，加载这份 Skill。关键判断是衣物与产品能否承受低温冲洗及合适的蛋白污渍预处理；这和轻油脂污渍的去油机制不同。血液、体液、未知污染、羊毛/丝绸等特殊蛋白纤维、专业护理衣物和严重敏感材料不在本篇内。

### 准备与输入

先核污渍确为日常食物，不靠颜色猜；混有油、色素或不明成分时可用 H055 的未知污渍判断流程。读衣标洗涤和干燥限制；没有可读标签或衣物易掉色、装饰脆弱时停下查制造商或专业护理。拿普通凉水、干净吸水材料，以及**衣标和产品都允许**的液体洗衣剂/针对食物蛋白的预处理品。酶类产品虽可用于某些蛋白污渍，但羊毛制造商明确不建议含酶洗剂，不能把“蛋白污渍需要酶”推广到所有面料。

### 执行

1. **先去表面余物，不把它压进纤维。** 用干净纸巾或钝边轻轻抬走残余食物；从外向内轻按，不来回擦大。污渍下垫可吸水布，保持其他干净衣物分开。
2. **按衣标做凉水初冲。** 衣标允许水洗时，从污渍背面用适用的凉水轻冲，让表面残留向外走；不要先用热水或高热烘干试图“消毒掉”普通食物印记。衣标对水温更严格就按衣标，手和感官条件不便时用浅盆/现场协助。
3. **先核产品再小范围试。** 选产品标示能处理这类食物污渍，且**明确适合该衣物材质/颜色**的预处理方式；按产品说明在不显眼处测褪色或损伤，只有测试无异常才在污渍处按其用量和接触方式处理。不要自己混用漂白剂、溶剂或多种去渍剂。
4. **按衣标完成整件洗涤。** 预处理并非已经洗净；按衣标和机器/手洗条件清洗，与不兼容衣物分开。若产品要求漂净或等待一段时间，以其说明和衣标为准，不造统一分钟数。
5. **湿态先查再决定干燥。** 洗后在合适光线/可及方式下核原污渍位置；若仍明显，先不要用烘干热固定痕迹，按衣物/产品允许再处理或停下求助。若颜色已经改变、纤维受损或来源仍不确定，不反复加产品硬救。

### 完成、常见问题与补救

普通蛋奶残留被按相容方式移除或处理到可以继续标签洗涤，洗后位置经检查，未超衣物/产品限制。仍有印记时明确记“未去净，先不热干”，不把泡沫或产品气味当污渍消失的证据。

- **污渍有油和颜色，不只是蛋奶：** 停止把它当单一蛋白污渍，先查真正成分和衣标；需要时用未知污渍流程。
- **产品只写“强力”，没写材质/用法：** 不凭广告语上衣；查完整产品说明或停下。
- **隐蔽试处掉色：** 不在明显处重复使用，改求制造商/专业护理建议。
- **衣物是羊毛或丝绸：** 不使用本篇含酶路线；按该件专属标签和纤维护理说明处理。
- **洗后仍有污渍：** 先保持不烘干，核是残留还是颜色变化，按允许方法再处理；不保证一次洗净。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本篇仅处理来源已知、普通食物量的可水洗衣物，不提供体液、危险化学品或高级面料的处理。衣标、产品说明、染色牢度和实际水质不同；“凉水先冲”仍服从更严格的衣物限制。使用桌面高度水盆、放大标签、读屏或请人协助搬运可替代弯腰和小字，不改变产品相容性判断。

## English

Load this Skill for a **known ordinary egg or dairy food-protein stain** on **ordinary washable clothing** before laundering the whole garment. Check whether garment and cleaner allow a cool rinse and appropriate protein-stain pretreatment. This differs from lifting light grease. Blood, body fluids, unknown contamination, wool and silk protein fibres, professional-care garments, and fragile special materials are outside this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm that the source is ordinary food rather than guessing from colour. For added oil, dyes, or unknown ingredients, use H055's separate unknown-stain decision. Read wash and dry limits. With an unreadable label, unstable colour, or fragile decoration, stop for maker or qualified care advice. Prepare ordinary cool water, clean absorbent material, and a liquid laundry product or protein-food pretreatment **permitted by both garment and product labels**. Enzymes can target some protein stains, but wool guidance warns against enzyme detergent; “protein stain” does not make enzymes safe for every fabric.

### Execution

1. **Lift excess without pushing it inward.** Gently lift food residue with a clean absorbent sheet or blunt edge; press from outside inward rather than rubbing it larger. Place absorbent material beneath and keep clean garments apart.
2. **Make an allowed cool first rinse.** When the care label allows water, gently flush from the back with suitable cool water so residue moves out. Do not start with hot water or high dryer heat to “sanitise away” an ordinary food mark. Follow any stricter garment temperature limit. Use a shallow supported basin or in-person help when hand or sensory access requires it.
3. **Check product, then test a small hidden place.** Choose a product labelled for this sort of food stain and **explicitly suitable for this garment's fabric and colour**. Follow its directions for a hidden colourfastness or damage test. Only if unchanged, use its stated amount and contact method on the mark. Do not mix bleach, solvents, or several stain removers yourself.
4. **Wash the whole item by label.** Pretreatment is not completed laundering. Wash within garment and washer or hand-wash directions, separate from incompatible items. Follow product and label instructions for rinsing or contact time rather than inventing a universal number.
5. **Inspect while damp before drying.** Check the original place under usable light or an accessible method. If a visible mark remains, avoid setting it with dryer heat; repeat only an allowed method or seek help. If colour or fibres changed or the source is still uncertain, do not keep stacking products.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The ordinary egg or dairy residue was removed or pretreated compatibly for label washing, and its location was inspected afterward without crossing garment or product limits. If a mark remains, record “not clear; no heated drying yet.” Foam or product scent is not proof the stain is gone.

- **Oil and colour are present too:** Stop calling this a single protein stain. Check the actual mixture and label, using the unknown-stain route if necessary.
- **The bottle says only “powerful” without fabric or use conditions:** Read complete directions or stop rather than applying marketing copy to clothing.
- **The hidden test fades colour:** Do not repeat on the visible mark; seek maker or qualified care guidance.
- **The garment is wool or silk:** Do not use this enzyme route. Follow its own label and fibre-care guidance.
- **A mark remains after washing:** Keep it out of heated drying and check residue versus colour change before another allowed treatment. One wash is not a guarantee.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a known ordinary food amount on water-washable clothing, not body fluids, hazardous chemicals, or valuable specialist fabrics. Label, product, dye stability, and water vary. Even a cool rinse yields to a stricter garment limit. A table-height basin, magnified label, screen reading, or help lifting can replace bending or tiny text without changing the compatibility decision.
