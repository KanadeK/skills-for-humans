## 中文

当你已进入正确飞机、手上有获准带入客舱的普通随身物，需要找到座位并**按该航班机组指示放好东西**时加载。这里不重新判断行李尺寸/禁带资格、不教航空安全或处理紧急情况。登机口扫码归前一步；本步的完成信号是你坐到正确位置、物品安放经机组允许且通道不被占住。

### 准备与输入

看本段登机牌或机组给出的座位排号/字母，区分可放头顶行李架的客舱包与需要放在座位下或机组指定位置的小件；具体件数、尺寸、特殊物品与座位限制以当次航司/机组规则为准。进机前或队伍停顿时把稍后要用的普通小件放在可取得处，避免站到过道中央才大规模翻包。需要协助找座位或处理物件时直接问机组，不假设旁边旅客有义务搬运。

### 执行

1. **按实际座位号找对排。** 看座椅上方/附近的排号和字母，与本段登机牌或机组最新安排核对；座位显示不一致、有人已坐或机型变化时请机组处理，不自行要求别人让出未核对的位置。
2. **在不挡过道的位置准备物件。** 保管好证件、手机和这段旅程需要在身边的小物；不要把所有东西摊在座位或地板上。先问/看机组对本座位的可用存放处，紧急出口排、隔板座或不同机型可能与普通座位不同。
3. **按机组认可的位置安放。** 将适合的小件放在座位下或指定位置，较大的获准客舱包按机组指示放入可用行李架；别占住过道、舱门或别人的座位，不硬塞超出空间的包，也不擅自搬动他人物品。举不起或找不到合适空间就明确向机组询问可用方案。
4. **行李架满或被要求改为托运时停下核对。** 听登机口或机组对这只包的实际处置、标签/凭证和抵达后领取位置；哪些东西必须留身边按航司对该物件的规则由机组确认，不在过道自行拆装危险或不明物品。记录必要标签信息，不把“交给工作人员”误写成“包会在座位上方”。
5. **坐定并接受后续机组指示。** 再看一次座位号，收好自己的随身小件，让过道与其他乘客可用；后续机组关于座椅、带扣和起飞准备的正式说明按现场执行。这份 Skill 到此收口，不代替机组安全演示。

### 完成与停止

你在经确认的座位，获准随身物已放到本航班允许的位置，过道畅通；若有临时交运物，知道凭证与领取安排，本轮完成。**坐好不代表航班已起飞，也不保证行李一定在头顶。** 座位、行李位置或交运指示不明确时先请机组核实，不抢占他人空间或继续堵在过道争论。

### 常见报错与补救

- **找不到排号或座位被占：** 出示本段座位信息向机组核对，别把上一段航班座位号沿用过来。
- **头顶行李架已满：** 向机组问可用位置或是否需正式交运；不强塞、不替别人重排。
- **手机/证件被放进够不到的包：** 在不挡过道、机组允许的时机整理必要小件，不在飞机移动或机组要求就坐时起身翻架。
- **无法自行举包或存放位置不适用：** 向机组说明具体需要，由其告知可行安排；不假设某一座位下方总有空间。
- **包被临时收走却没记领取方式：** 立即向交接人员确认标签、凭证和到达后的领取位置；按真实答复记录。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通航班已获准入舱的随身物。机型、座位、机组与航司规则会改变可用空间；行李许可资格与特殊物品由专门行李规则和机组决定。行动、视觉、听觉或语言不便时，请机组用可理解方式确认排号、存放位置和协助安排，不交给陌生人代管证件或物品。副作用是行李可能被正式交运，领取位置要另记。

## English

Load this Skill after entering the correct aircraft with ordinary belongings approved for the cabin, when you need your seat and must **place items under this flight's crew directions**. It does not re-decide baggage size or prohibited-item eligibility, teach aircraft safety or handle emergencies. Gate scanning was the previous task. This one finishes when you are in the correct seat, items are placed as the crew permits and the aisle is clear.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the row and letter on this leg's boarding pass or the crew's current assignment. Distinguish a permitted cabin bag from a small item meant for under-seat or another crew-designated place; counts, sizes, special items and seat restrictions depend on this airline and crew. Before entering or while the queue pauses, put ordinary things you will need later somewhere reachable instead of unpacking a whole bag in the aisle. Ask crew for seat or item help when needed; a nearby passenger is not automatically your porter.

### Execution

1. **Find the actual seat row.** Compare row and letter signs near the seats with this leg's pass or crew update. If the display differs, the place is occupied or the aircraft changed, ask crew instead of demanding a seat you have not verified.
2. **Prepare items without blocking the aisle.** Keep documents, phone and small items you need on this leg under your control. Do not spread belongings across a seat or floor. Check the crew's available storage for this seat; exit rows, bulkheads and different aircraft may differ from an ordinary row.
3. **Use a place the crew permits.** Put suitable small items under the seat or in the directed place, and a larger permitted cabin bag in an available overhead bin as crew instructs. Keep the aisle, doors and other people's seats clear. Do not force a bag into a small space or rearrange another person's property. If you cannot lift it or find a place, ask crew for a permitted option.
4. **Pause if bins are full or a bag must be checked.** Confirm how this particular bag will be handled, its tag or receipt and where it will be returned after arrival. Ask crew which items must stay with you under the airline's rules; do not unpack an uncertain or hazardous item in the aisle. Record necessary tag details. “Given to staff” does not mean “in the bin above me.”
5. **Sit and follow the next crew directions.** Check the seat once more, keep small belongings controlled and leave the aisle usable. Follow the crew's subsequent formal instructions for seating, restraint and departure preparation. This Skill ends here; it does not replace their safety demonstration.

### Success and stop

You are in a confirmed seat, approved cabin belongings are in this flight's permitted positions and the aisle is clear. If an item was taken for gate check, you know its receipt and collection arrangement. **Being seated is not takeoff, and a bin over your head is not guaranteed.** If the seat, storage or gate-check direction is unclear, ask crew before taking someone else's space or continuing to block the aisle.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Row cannot be found or seat is occupied:** Ask crew using this leg's seat details; do not reuse the seat number from a previous flight.
- **Overhead bins are full:** Ask crew for an available location or formal gate-check route. Do not force a bag in or rearrange others' luggage.
- **Phone or documents are inside an unreachable bag:** Retrieve ordinary essentials when crew permit and without blocking others. Do not get up to rummage when the aircraft is moving or crew ask you to remain seated.
- **You cannot lift the bag or the usual space is unavailable:** Tell crew what help you need; the same under-seat space does not exist at every seat.
- **A bag is taken but the return point is unknown:** Ask the person accepting it for tag, receipt and arrival collection location, and record the actual answer.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers belongings already permitted in an ordinary aircraft cabin. Aircraft, seat, crew and airline rules affect space; a separate baggage check and crew decide permitted items and special cases. For mobility, sight, hearing or language needs, ask crew to confirm row, storage and assistance in a way you can use. Do not give documents or belongings to a stranger to hold. The bag may be formally checked, and you will need its actual collection point.
