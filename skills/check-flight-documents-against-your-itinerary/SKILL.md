## 中文

你已订普通航班，出发前要确认**每一位旅客、每一段航程实际需要随身提供什么证件和旅程材料**时，用这份 Skill从运营航司、出发机场安检机构以及相关国家/地区官方渠道建立核对清单。目标是把“已拿到且形式符合说明”“还需官方确认”“明显缺少”分清，并在去机场前处理缺口。这不是签证、移民、入境或海关资格判断；一张机票也不是可登机或可入境的证明。

### 准备与输入

拿出实际订位/行程，逐位记录姓名的订位写法、航班日期、各段出发/中转/到达地点、运营航司及必要的确认编号。找航司当前旅行文件页面、机场安检方当前身份/登机牌说明，以及涉及出入境/中转时相应官方机关的当期要求入口。只记录核对所需的证件种类、签发与到期信息，不把完整护照、身份证号或付款信息贴进公开笔记或陌生“文件检查”网站。未成年人、代订、跨境和代码共享航班可能有另外的官方条件，不能按成人国内单程模板猜。

### 执行

1. **先把航程拆成逐人逐段。** 对每位旅客列出实际运营航司、出发安检点、中转与最终目的地；核对订位姓名与将要出示的官方身份文件写法。拼写/名字疑点在航司正式渠道解决，不在柜台临时自己涂改证件或猜“差一点也行”。
2. **向各负责方取当期清单。** 航司查值机/登机要求，机场安检方查该出发地可接受身份形式与登机牌要求；国际/中转涉及的入境、过境或其他材料向有关政府/官方机构核实。航司的辅助查询工具可以指路，但不能替代有权机关的最终决定。不同国家、年龄和航段的要求可能不一样。
3. **逐项核对手中实物与形式。** 对清单中的每项标“原件/官方认可电子形式已备”“需补办/找回”“规则或有效性待确认”。检查文件姓名、有效期、订位/航班日期及官方接受的纸质/电子出示方式；只做可观察的比对，不自行裁断复杂有效期、国籍或入境资格。登机牌尚未生成时记为值机后取得，不把订位邮件写成已持登机牌。
4. **把疑点交给有权渠道。** 文件缺失、姓名不一致、形式不被确认、跨境/中转规则不清或手机无法稳定出示电子文件时，在出发前联系航司和相应安检/目的地官方渠道，记下答复、日期及下一动作。不要购买非官方“保证过关”服务，也不复制他人的证件/二维码凑数。
5. **装成可现场取用的小清单。** 将已确认需要的原件或官方认可形式与航程确认资料放在本人能控制且办理手续时能取到的位置，另存官方联系入口和未解决项。临行前再看航司/机场通知是否改了航段或值机方式；有关键项仍未确认就先处理或调整行程，不把“到机场试试”当计划。

### 成功条件

对每位旅客、每段航程，运营航司、安检与相关官方清单要求已逐项对照实际文件，需携带项能取到，订位身份与文件没有未解决的明显冲突，电子/纸质形式有依据；或缺失/资格疑点被明确标记并交给有权渠道处理。本文只能证明你完成了材料核对流程，不能保证值机、安检、登机或入境获准。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只有订位邮件，没有登机牌：** 将登机牌列为值机后待获得项目，按航司值机流程办理，不把订单号出示给安检当已值机。
- **身份证件名字与订位不一致：** 留下准确差异，联系运营航司确认可用修改方式；不要自行判断哪种拼写差异一定获准。
- **同一行程含国内、跨境和中转段：** 分段核官方要求，别用第一段可用的证件证明整程有效。
- **清单要求的文件只在托运行李里：** 在交运行李前按航司/安检说明重新安排可取用方式，不到检查点才找不着。
- **文件到期或电子版形式不确定：** 查签发机关、航司和实际负责方的当期规则；无法确认就先停止依赖该文件，准备官方认可的替代或改期。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这是普通旅客整理与事实核对，不解释签证、国籍、移民、过境、海关法律，也不指导规避安检。美国 TSA、加拿大 CATSA 与 Delta 页面仅是不同职责的实例；真正清单随出发地、航司、目的地、年龄和日期变化。没有智能手机可询问航司/机场可接受的纸质或人工方式；视听/阅读限制可用官方可及客服或可信协助，但凭证仍由你控制。核对可能暴露需改订位或补材料的现实成本，越早发现越能处理。

## English

Use this Skill after booking an ordinary flight to check **what each traveller must be able to present on each leg**. Build a current checklist from the operating airline, departure airport screening authority, and relevant official destination/transit sources. Distinguish “in hand and accepted in this format,” “needs official confirmation,” and “missing” before going to the airport. This is not a visa, immigration, entry, or customs eligibility decision. A ticket alone proves neither boarding nor entry.

### Preparation and inputs

Take the actual booking/itinerary. For each traveller note the booked spelling, flight dates, each origin/transit/destination, operating airline, and needed confirmation reference. Find the airline's current travel-document page, the departing checkpoint's ID/boarding-pass guidance, and relevant official authorities for cross-border or transit requirements. Record only document types, issuing and expiry facts needed for comparison; do not place full passport/ID numbers or payment data in a public note or unfamiliar document-check website. Children, third-party bookings, cross-border legs, and codeshares may have different official requirements, not an adult domestic one-way template.

### Execution

1. **Split the itinerary by traveller and leg.** For each person list actual operating airline, departure checkpoint, transfers, and destination. Compare booking name with the official identity document intended for the trip. Bring spelling questions to the airline's formal channel; do not alter a document or assume “close enough” at the counter.
2. **Get current lists from responsible sources.** Ask the airline about check-in/boarding documents and the departure screening authority about acceptable ID and boarding-pass form. For international/transit entry or other materials, consult the relevant government or authorized official agency. An airline helper tool can point to rules but does not replace a competent authority's decision. Country, age, and segment conditions differ.
3. **Compare actual items and accepted forms.** Mark each listed item “original or officially accepted digital form ready,” “missing/to retrieve,” or “rule/validity unresolved.” Check visible names, dates, booking/flight dates, and official paper/digital presentation rules. This is observable comparison, not your own ruling on complex validity, nationality, or entry. A boarding pass not yet issued belongs in “obtain after check-in”; a booking email is not a boarding pass.
4. **Send unknowns to the authority.** For a missing document, name conflict, uncertain format, cross-border/transit rule, or unreliable mobile display, contact the airline and applicable screening/destination official channel before travel. Record the answer, date, and next action. Do not buy an unofficial “guaranteed clearance” service or borrow another person's ID/QR code.
5. **Make a retrievable carry set.** Keep confirmed originals or accepted forms and journey references under your control and reachable when the process asks for them. Retain official contact entry points and unresolved items. Recheck airline/airport notices for a changed leg or check-in method before departure. Resolve a critical unknown or adjust the trip rather than treating “try at the airport” as a plan.

### Success

For each traveller and leg, you compared the operating airline, checkpoint, and relevant official list with actual documents. Required items are retrievable; visible booking/ID conflicts and form questions have no unresolved assumption, or are clearly referred to an authorized channel. This proves completion of a document-check workflow only, not guaranteed check-in, screening, boarding, or entry.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Booking email but no boarding pass:** Keep “obtain after check-in” on the list and use the airline's check-in route; an order reference is not an issued boarding pass.
- **Name differs between booking and ID:** Record the exact discrepancy and ask the operating airline about its correction path; do not decide which variation must be accepted.
- **Domestic, international, and transit legs coexist:** Check official rules per leg. An ID accepted for the first does not establish the whole trip.
- **Needed document is inside checked baggage:** Before handing the bag over, arrange a retrievable form under airline/screening instructions. Do not discover the gap at the checkpoint.
- **Expiry or digital format is uncertain:** Ask the issuer, airline, and responsible authority for current rules. Stop relying on that item until an official alternative or changed trip is established.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is ordinary passenger preparation and fact comparison, not visa, nationality, immigration, transit, customs, or screening-bypass advice. US TSA, Canadian CATSA, and Delta illustrate different responsibilities. Actual lists vary by departure, airline, destination, age, and date. Without a smartphone, ask about officially accepted paper or staffed options. Use accessible official support or trusted help for reading/hearing limits while retaining control of credentials. An early check may reveal a booking correction or document cost, which is a real planning result.
