## 中文

当普通烘烤接近配方预计时间，你需要决定**现在取出、再烤一段还是停止这份做法**时，加载这份 Skill。你要获得与**这类食物、这份配方及适用食品安全要求**相符的完成证据；铃声和表面颜色只是提醒/线索，不是通用熟度证明。保存、剩菜和买后食品处置不由这里完成。

### 准备与输入

先确认食物和所用烤盘/模式是否仍与配方相符、实际入炉时间、开门或换位记录、配方写的成品测试，以及此食物是否需要当地规则中的安全核心温度/静置。准备干燥隔热工具和近处耐热落点。使用洁净、适合该食物的测试签或温度计，先知道应在哪个位置检查；未熟肉、禽、海鲜不能用尝味来决定。

### 执行步骤

1. **把预计时间当检查点。** 到配方给出的观察时刻，在安全可达时按器具说明开门，用隔热工具稳住烤盘；不把“多烤了 X 分钟”替代实际证据。若时间起点遗失，按更可靠的食物特定完成判断，不重建一条虚构时间线。
2. **用这份配方对应的线索。** 某些蛋糕配方会用中心轻触回弹、测试签从中心取出后的状态或边缘变化；烤蔬菜、面包和焗菜各有别的目标。只采用配方或可靠食物说明明确适用的测试，不把“牙签干净”强加给所有食物。测试位置也需对应最可能未完成处。
3. **有食品安全要求时加上正确测量。** 生肉、禽、海鲜等需要内部条件的食物按当地权威与[已有熟度核对](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)测中心和必要静置；外层金黄或中心冒汽不足以替代适用温度/时间组合。温度计读数不可信时先解决仪器/位置问题，不能把猜测写成合格。
4. **给三种明确结果。** “达到配方和安全条件，取出并按说明静置/上桌”；“未达到、烤箱和食物状态仍可控，按可靠说明继续并在下个检查点复测”；或“食物/烤箱条件不明，停止声称完成并寻求可靠指导”。不把未完成和不安全合并成一句“再烤久一点”。
5. **取出后给余热一个位置。** 按配方把热盘放在稳固耐热处，必要时按说明冷却或静置；不要在滚烫盘边让儿童、宠物或衣袖路过。剩菜保存另走已有流程，热食不会因离炉就自动安全储存。

### 完成与停止

你有一条能说明**哪项配方线索、哪项适用安全证据、在哪个位置检查、为何现在停止或继续**的记录，就是完成判断。缺配方完成条件、温度计失效、食物安全史不明、烤箱故障或持续烟味时停止宣称“已烤好”，不靠一层焦色说服自己。

### 常见报错与补救

- **计时器响就直接上桌：** 回到配方完成测试和食物特定安全要求，铃声只让你开始检查。
- **表面深色、中心仍湿/不足：** 按该配方的可靠调整继续或停止；不自行移到最高层、加大火力来掩盖内部问题。
- **蛋糕测试签只插到边缘：** 按配方要求在中心或适用位置复测，避开只看最先完成的部分。
- **肉外表上色但中心未测：** 用干净温度计按当地规则检查，不能用菜谱图片代替中心读数。
- **烤盘不稳或烤箱冒烟：** 先处理器具/热源安全，食品完成判断让位于停止边界。

### 假设、替代与副作用

此流程不提供跨食物的统一分钟数或温度，也不把某一种蛋糕测试扩展到所有焗烤品。高海拔、烤盘材料、模式与实际炉温会改变时间和外观；用可靠配方、本机说明、当地食品安全指导。视觉不便时可用可访问温度计、可靠的触感/测试签信息或现场可信协助，但某一感官不能取代必须测量的安全指标。副作用是计时器不再替你裁判，反而更接近它真实的工作范围。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary bake approaches its expected recipe time and you must decide **remove it now, continue under directions, or stop this attempt**. Get completion evidence that belongs to **this food, this recipe, and any applicable food-safety requirement**. A bell and surface colour are reminders or clues, not universal proof of doneness. Preservation and leftover storage are separate tasks.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm food, pan, and mode against the recipe, actual loading time, door or rotation changes, the recipe's finish test, and whether local rules require a safe core temperature or resting period. Have dry heat protection and a nearby heat-safe landing place. Use a clean tester or thermometer suited to the food, with a known correct measurement location. Do not taste undercooked meat, poultry, or seafood as a test.

### Execution steps

1. **Treat the expected time as a checkpoint.** At the recipe's observation point, open only when safe under the oven manual and steady the pan with heat protection. “It had X extra minutes” is not a substitute for evidence. When the timing start is lost, use stronger food-specific completion tests rather than reconstructing a fictional clock.
2. **Use cues for this particular recipe.** Some cake recipes use a gentle centre spring-back, a tester from the middle, or edge changes. Vegetables, bread, and casseroles have different targets. Use only tests supported by a reliable recipe or food-specific guidance. Do not impose a “clean toothpick” rule on every baked food. Check the location most likely to lag.
3. **Add a proper safety measurement when required.** Meat, poultry, seafood, and other foods with internal conditions follow local authority guidance and the existing [doneness check](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md) for centre readings and any rest. A golden outside or steam at the centre does not replace an applicable temperature-and-time combination. Resolve bad probe placement or an unreliable device before claiming success.
4. **Choose one of three results.** “Recipe and safety conditions met: remove and rest or serve as directed”; “not yet met but oven and food remain controlled: continue under reliable directions and recheck”; or “food or oven condition unknown: do not claim completion and seek reliable guidance.” “Bake a little longer” does not cover both unfinished and unsafe states.
5. **Give residual heat a landing place.** Put the hot pan on a stable heat-safe surface and follow recipe cooling or resting directions. Keep children, pets, and loose sleeves away. Leaving the oven does not give perishable food a storage plan; use the separate leftover process.

### Success and stop

You can name **which recipe cue, which applicable safety evidence, where it was checked, and why you stop or continue**. Do not claim “done” with missing recipe criteria, faulty temperature measurement, unknown food-safety history, oven failure, or persistent smoke. A dark crust is not an affidavit for the centre.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The timer rings and food is served:** Return to the recipe's finish test and food-specific safety rule. The bell begins the check.
- **Outside is dark while the centre is wet or unfinished:** Continue or stop under reliable recipe adjustments. Do not move to the highest rack or turn heat up to disguise an interior problem.
- **A cake tester only reaches an edge:** Check the centre or recipe's intended point; do not sample only the part most likely done first.
- **Meat is browned but its centre was not measured:** Use a clean thermometer under local rules; a recipe photo is not a core reading.
- **The pan wobbles or the oven smokes:** Set equipment and heat safety first. Food finish judgment yields to the stop boundary.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This supplies no universal minutes or temperatures across foods, nor does one cake test apply to every bake. Altitude, pan material, mode, and actual oven heat alter time and appearance. Use reliable food directions, the exact oven manual, and local safety guidance. With limited vision, use an accessible thermometer, suitable tactile or tester evidence, or trusted in-person help, but no single sense replaces a required measurement. The timer loses its role as judge and returns to a job it can actually perform.
