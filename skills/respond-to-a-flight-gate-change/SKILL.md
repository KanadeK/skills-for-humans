## 中文

你已经按一班普通航班在某登机口候机，随后机场屏幕、航司通知或工作人员说**这班改了口**时，用这份 Skill先证实通知针对同一班，再沿机场开放路线重新到位。目标是在新口核对同一航班/状态，或在时间、区域通行、信息冲突时得到官方下一步。它不是普通候机，也不解释安检/入境资格或自动改签权利。附近的人跑了，并不能证明你也该跑。

### 准备与输入

保留航班号、日期、实际运营航司、目的地、原口、登机牌及最新状态。看变更来源和时间：机场当前出发屏、航司官方 App/网站、广播/文字公告或机场/航司人员。代码共享、多班同目的地、同一航司相邻航班容易认错。准备使用机场当前导向或可及地图；新口若跨航站楼或控制区，不假定可以自行穿过去，先查机场正式路线和是否还需某种手续。

### 执行

1. **证明是这班的变更。** 用航班号或实际运营方、日期、出发时间、目的地核对新口公告。只听到一个“B12”或看见队伍移动时，先看正式屏幕；App 与屏幕冲突则比较更新时间并问工作人员。
2. **读出完整新状态。** 不只改口号，还核对航站楼/区域、登机开始或结束状态、延误/取消信息。旧登机牌口号与旧截图退为历史证据；未分配新口则留在允许候机区继续看官方公告，不抢先猜。
3. **按开放路线重新到位。** 依机场指示、官方地图或工作人员建议走向新口，预留实际距离与可及设施时间。若需要跨区域、再过安检或权限不清，问机场/航司有权人员，不尾随他人穿门或逆向冲检查点。走不到、身体条件不允许赶路或登机已临近结束时，尽快请航司确认可行安排，而不是盲目冲刺。
4. **到口后再次核对。** 看口边屏与工作人员信息是否仍是这班，核登机状态及必要材料。又改口则重复“同班—新口—开放路线”三步；若已停止登机或取消，向航司正式渠道问接下来处理，不自行断言下一班可免费乘坐。

### 成功条件

新口通知由机场/运营航司可核验来源指向同一航班，你按允许且可及路线到了当前新口，并确认当前登机状态；或因新口未公布、信息冲突/不可达/时间不足而安全停下并得到官方求助路径。跟着人群到一扇门，不能替代航班身份核对。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只听见新口号：** 用航班号、日期、目的地交叉核对，不跟同航司另一班走。
- **App 与机场屏幕不同：** 比较最新时间，找机场或航司人员核当前公告，不在两口之间反复狂奔。
- **新口在另一航站楼：** 查机场正式可通行路线、实际所需手续与时间；不要走关闭/受控通道求近。
- **新口也没显示或再次变化：** 保留在安全候机区等待并复核本班实时公告，别把第二张旧截图当最终版。
- **到口才知登机已结束：** 保留变更通知、航班信息，向运营航司询问该票实际后续选择；不保证改签或赔付。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这只处理普通航班的登机口变动与位置修正，不代替机场安全/边检规则、旅客权益判断或复杂跨境中转。伦敦机场和 Delta 的官方资料是信息渠道实例，具体航班实时状态必须由本机场和运营航司核对。无手机可用出发屏、文字/语音广播或工作人员；行动、视觉、听觉限制下应及时请求该机场实际协助，不能把危险冲刺当默认替代。移动到新口可能多花时间。

## English

Use this Skill when you were waiting at a gate for a matched ordinary flight and an airport display, airline alert, or staff member says **that flight has moved gates**. Prove the change is for the same flight, then use an open airport route to reposition. Finish with the same flight and current boarding state verified at the new gate, or an official next step for timing, controlled-area access, or conflicting information. This is not initial waiting or a ruling on security, entry, or automatic rebooking. A nearby crowd running is not a notice.

### Preparation and inputs

Keep flight number, date, operating airline, destination, old gate, boarding pass, and last status. Note source and time of the change: live airport departures, official airline app/site, audible or text notice, or airport/airline staff. Codeshares and neighbouring same-destination flights are easy to confuse. Use current airport signs or accessible maps. If the new gate appears across terminals or controlled zones, verify an official route and any required process before moving.

### Execution

1. **Prove it changed for your flight.** Match new-gate notice to flight number or operating airline, date, time, and destination. If you heard only “B12” or saw a moving queue, read the official display. For an app/display conflict, compare update time and ask staff.
2. **Read the whole new state.** Check terminal/zone, boarding start or closure, delay, or cancellation as well as the gate number. The old printed gate and screenshot are historical. If the new gate is not yet assigned, stay in a permitted departure area and monitor official information rather than guessing.
3. **Reposition by an open route.** Use airport directions, official maps, or staff guidance, allowing actual distance and accessible-equipment time. For a terminal or controlled-area crossing, renewed screening, or unclear permission, ask authorized airport/airline staff. Do not tailgate or go backward through a checkpoint. If the path is unreachable, movement is difficult, or boarding may close soon, ask the airline about an actual option instead of sprinting blindly.
4. **Read back at the new gate.** Verify gate-side display and staff still show this flight, its boarding status, and needed documents. For another change, repeat same-flight/new-gate/open-route checks. For closed boarding or cancellation, seek the airline's formal next step; free carriage on another flight is not assumed.

### Success

A verifiable airport/operating-airline source names a new gate for the same flight, and you reach its current gate through a permitted accessible route with boarding state confirmed. An unassigned, contradictory, unreachable, or too-late gate with a safe stop and official help path is also valid. Reaching a door with a crowd does not identify your flight.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only a gate number was heard:** Cross-check flight, date, and destination before moving with another same-airline service.
- **App and airport display differ:** Compare current update times and ask staff rather than running repeatedly between gates.
- **New gate is in another terminal:** Verify the airport's permitted route, any process, and time. Do not shortcut through a closed or controlled passage.
- **New gate is pending or changes again:** Wait in a safe allowed area and recheck the actual flight's live notice, not another stale screenshot.
- **Boarding ended when you arrive:** Keep the gate-change notice and flight details and ask the operating airline about this ticket's real next options; rebooking or payment is not promised.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary flight gate-position correction, not airport security/immigration procedure, passenger-rights ruling, or complex international transfer. London airport and Delta pages illustrate information channels; the real airport and airline control your flight's live status. Screens, text/audio notices, or staff can replace a phone. For mobility, sight, or hearing barriers, ask for actual airport assistance early rather than treating a risky dash as the default. A new gate may take longer to reach.
