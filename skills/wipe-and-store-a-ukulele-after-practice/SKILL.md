## 中文

你刚在普通干燥室内弹完现有尤克里里，想做**不拆弦的轻度收尾**时使用本篇。结果是手碰过的弦和可安全触及的外表面轻拭过，琴放进合适琴盒或稳定琴架，远离温度剧变、潮气和挤压。这里不做上油、抛光、品丝打磨、换弦或修理。若琴被雨淋、表面有凝露、开裂或松动，先停止普通收纳并按制造商建议处理。

### 准备与输入

结束发声后把琴放在稳定干净表面，先确认琴身、弦和布都干燥。查本琴材质与涂层说明；磨砂和亮面适用清洁剂可能不同，本篇只用干净柔软布。琴盒/琴架需适合尺寸且不会挤压琴颈。家中湿度管理因琴材和地区不同，以本琴资料为准。

### 执行

1. **查看是否只需轻拭。** 观察弦上手汗、普通指印和可见损伤。若有水汽、霉味、开裂、松件或明显污染，不用布强擦或封入湿琴盒，记录异常并查制造商/维修支持。
2. **轻擦触摸处。** 用干净软棉布轻轻带过四根弦可及的外表面和手指常碰区域；布有砂粒或不确定是否干净就换布。避免勾住弦、用溶剂或把液体渗进琴身。
3. **选择安全位置。** 将琴放进适配琴盒并合好，或置于稳固琴架；不让门、重物、热源或日晒碰压琴。干燥室内长期存放的湿度要求按型号/材质判断。
4. **做一次交接检查。** 确认琴不会滑倒、盒盖不会夹弦、附件不会压面板；记下任何异常供下次使用前检查。无需为了收纳把调音旋钮随意松一圈。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

轻拭过干燥弦/外表面，琴在稳固适配的盒或架上且无挤压风险；异常则留有停用和求助记录。

- **布带砂或脏：** 不擦，换干净合适布。
- **琴或盒有潮气：** 不密闭收放，遵照本琴受潮建议。
- **琴架不稳：** 改用适配琴盒或安全平放位置。
- **表面涂层不明：** 不上抛光剂，只做允许的干布轻拭。

### 假设与边界

只适用于普通干燥练习后的日常轻护理。你检查材质和位置；湿度数值、长期存放、受潮恢复和结构问题需本琴制造商指导。

## English

Use this Skill after ordinary **dry indoor** practice on an existing ukulele for a light end-of-session pass without removing strings. Finish with touched strings and safe exterior spots gently wiped, then the instrument in a suitable case or stable stand away from temperature swings, damp and pressure. This does not oil, polish, grind frets, replace strings or repair. For rain exposure, condensation, cracks or loose parts, stop ordinary storage and follow maker advice.

### Preparation and inputs

After sound has stopped, set the instrument on a stable clean surface and confirm instrument, strings and cloth are dry. Check its material and finish guidance; satin and gloss may permit different products, so this Skill uses only a clean soft cloth. The case or stand should fit without pressing the neck. Home humidity control varies by material and region; follow instrument-specific guidance.

### Execution

1. **Check if a light wipe fits.** Look for handling residue, ordinary fingerprints and damage. For water, mould smell, cracks, loose parts or heavy contamination, do not rub hard or seal a wet case; record the issue and consult maker/service guidance.
2. **Gently wipe touched spots.** Pass a clean soft cotton cloth lightly along reachable string exteriors and commonly touched areas. Replace a gritty or uncertain cloth. Do not hook a string, use solvents or let liquid seep into the body.
3. **Choose a safe position.** Fit the instrument in its case or stable stand, away from doors, heavy objects, heat and direct sun. Judge long-storage humidity from this model and material's guidance.
4. **Make a final check.** Confirm it cannot fall, the case lid does not pinch strings, and accessories do not press its top. Note anomalies before next use. Do not randomly slacken tuning pegs merely for ordinary storage.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

Dry strings/exterior are lightly wiped and the ukulele rests in a fitting stable case or stand free from pressure; otherwise an anomaly and stop/help decision are recorded.

- **Cloth is gritty:** Do not wipe until a clean suitable cloth is available.
- **Instrument or case damp:** Do not seal it; follow maker moisture advice.
- **Stand unstable:** Use a fitting case or safe flat location.
- **Finish unknown:** Avoid polish; use only an allowed dry gentle wipe.

### Assumptions and limits

This is daily light care after ordinary dry practice. You check material and location. Humidity values, long storage, wet recovery and structural faults require this instrument's maker guidance.
