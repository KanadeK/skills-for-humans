## 中文

当你发现鞋底、门垫或门口一小段地面带进**确知是普通、已干的沙土与细石**时，用这份 Skill 先截断继续踩入的路径，再按地材说明收集颗粒，避免把硬颗粒拖成划痕。这里处理的是“室外物正被带入”的事件；整屋地面定期清洁仍归普通地面流程。湿泥、污水、动物排泄物、危险粉尘、尖锐玻璃或不明污染退出本篇。

### 准备与输入

从干净一侧看沙粒到哪里、鞋底/门垫是否仍在持续掉砂、地面是木、乙烯基、瓷砖还是地毯，确认本材质和吸尘器是否允许相应工具/刷头。让人暂绕开这条小通道；不要踩着砂去拿工具。准备软扫具/适用吸尘器、簸箕或收集容器，能安全放鞋与门垫的区域。地板起翘、泥水渗入缝隙或面积超出小范围时暂停并查地材/物业指导。

### 执行

1. **先阻断继续带入。** 从干净位置停下，提示同住者绕行或换路；鞋底仍有砂则在安全可及处脱鞋/清理鞋底，不把带砂鞋继续穿过室内。门垫本身装满砂时先留在原位或按材质安全移开，不用力抖向其他房间。
2. **先处理松散颗粒，不先加水。** 按地材和工具说明把可见砂、碎石轻扫入簸箕或用适用吸尘附件收集；移动方向尽量不把砂推到更长的干净路线。硬颗粒夹在抹布下再湿擦可能磨伤表面，先清颗粒后才决定地面是否需另外清洁。
3. **门垫与边缘另查。** 按门垫标签/制造商许可吸尘或清理其积砂，检查门缝和可及踢脚边是否还有颗粒；不为了“彻底”拆地板或门槛。吸尘器刷头/集尘盒按本机说明处理，装得太满或卡住时停用检查，不强行继续。
4. **确认通道可走。** 地面和鞋底不再有明显松散颗粒，门口可安全通过且没有收集工具挡路；若留下潮印、附着污痕或地材损伤，转地面/材料专属流程，不以干砂收集宣称完成所有清洁。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

已知小量干沙被截在入口附近并以地材许可方式收起，不再由鞋/门垫往室内传播，通道恢复；残留污痕有专门地面去向。无法确认粉尘来源或地板表面明显受损时停下求助也是完整结果。

- **越扫沙越远：** 暂停，缩小清扫方向/换合适吸尘工具，不来回用力磨地。
- **一块湿布刚擦就发出沙沙声：** 停止湿擦，先移走颗粒，再按该地材说明处理。
- **门垫一动又掉一地：** 把它当第二个含砂物处理，按标签清理而非在屋内大力抖。
- **吸尘器卡住或刷头划地：** 停机按本机说明清理/换附件，不拆电气部件。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

仅限门口小范围、来源已知的普通干土砂，不处理疑似建筑粉尘、化学品、霉害、玻璃或动物污染。硬木、乙烯基地板与地毯的允许工具不同，地材/机器说明优先。行动不便时让同住者暂停经过，使用轻型可及工具或可信协助；不用跪到不稳位置抢砂。清洁后门垫、簸箕和吸尘附件可能还需清理。

## English

Use this Skill when shoes, a doormat, or a short entry path holds a small amount of **known ordinary dry sand and grit** tracked indoors. Stop further footsteps carrying it in, then collect particles under the floor care so they do not become scratches. This handles an active tracked-in event, not a whole-home floor-cleaning schedule. Wet mud, sewage, animal waste, hazardous dust, sharp glass, or unknown contamination leave this route.

### Preparation and inputs

From the clean side, see how far grit reached and whether shoes or a mat still shed it. Identify wood, vinyl, tile, or carpet and whether its care and your vacuum permit the proposed head or tool. Route people around the short path rather than walking through it for supplies. Have a soft broom or compatible vacuum and a pan or collection bin, plus a place for shoes and mat. Pause for lifting flooring, mudwater inside joints, or more than a small accessible area and seek floor or property advice.

### Execution

1. **Stop more grit entering.** Pause from the clean side and tell others to go around. Remove or clear sandy shoes at a safely reachable point rather than walk them through the home. If the doormat is loaded with grit, leave it until it can be handled under its label; do not shake it into another room.
2. **Collect loose particles before adding water.** Sweep gently into a pan or vacuum with a permitted attachment for this floor and machine. Work without pushing grit farther onto clean flooring. Hard grains beneath a wet cloth can abrade a finish; remove them before deciding on any later wet care.
3. **Check mat and edge separately.** Vacuum or otherwise handle the mat under its label. Check accessible threshold and skirting for remaining grains, without dismantling floor or door. Empty or service the vacuum's user-accessible bin and head under its manual if overloaded or jammed; stop rather than force it.
4. **Release the path.** No obvious loose grit remains on floor or shoe, tools are out of the walkway, and entry is safe to use. A remaining damp mark, adhered soil, or finish damage belongs to the floor and material route; dry grit collection did not complete every cleaning job.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

Known small dry grit was stopped near the entry and collected by a permitted method. Shoes and mat no longer carry it farther and the route is usable; any remaining mark has a floor-specific next step. An unknown dust source or visibly damaged surface produces a clear stop for advice.

- **Sweeping spreads it farther:** Pause, narrow the direction, or use a suitable vacuum rather than grind repeatedly.
- **A damp cloth makes a scraping sound:** Stop wiping, lift grains first, then use material care.
- **Moving the mat drops another pile:** Treat it as a second grit source by its label rather than shaking indoors.
- **A vacuum jams or its head scuffs:** Stop and use its own manual for a user-accessible fix or another tool; do not open electrical parts.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers a small entry trail of known ordinary dry outdoor soil, not building dust, chemicals, mold, glass, or animal contamination. Hardwood, vinyl, and carpet accept different tools; follow surface and machine directions. If movement is limited, ask others to pause, use a light reachable tool or trusted help, and do not kneel in an unstable spot. Mat, dustpan, and vacuum attachment may need their own cleaning afterward.
