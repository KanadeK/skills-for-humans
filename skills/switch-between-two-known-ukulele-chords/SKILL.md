## 中文

你已能分别按出两个清楚和弦，想在**慢速、固定拍点**从 A 换到 B 再回来时使用本篇。完成物是至少两轮可辨认的 A→B→A 往返；若其中一处总跟不上，则标记具体换位动作。这里不教授单个和弦图，也不要求完整伴奏或表演速度。

### 准备与输入

选两张与你的琴调弦一致的已学和弦图，先各扫一次确认仍清楚。说出相同位置可保留的手指、要抬起的手指和新落点。用口数“一、二、三、四”或舒适的节拍器慢速；如果两张图有疼痛或按不出的跨度，换更适合的已学组合。

### 执行

1. **空手预演路线。** 在不扫弦时从 A 慢移到 B，先放能保留或先落的指尖；手不必离琴太远。再回 A 一次，确认你知道去向。
2. **给换位留时间。** 每四拍只在第一拍轻扫，余下拍点用于松开、移动和落指；缓慢执行 A、B、A。不要为了赶拍捏紧手腕。
3. **逐弦听新和弦。** 若到 B 后某弦闷，停拍检查该弦的指尖位置，然后把速度降到能完成的程度；只改一个手指，不重复一长串错误。
4. **重复完整往返。** 再做一轮 A→B→A 并数清拍；两轮都能听出两种和弦才收口。若仍断在同一落点，记下那一指和方向供下次练习。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

至少两轮缓慢清晰的 A→B→A，或一条具体“从哪一指到哪一弦失败”的练习记录。提速不是本篇验收条件。

- **第二个和弦本身还不清：** 回到单和弦检查，再做换位。
- **总是晚半拍：** 保持慢拍并把非发声拍用于移动。
- **某弦换后被闷：** 单独拨它，微调落点再试。
- **手痛：** 停止并休息，不把疼痛当作熟练度训练。

### 假设与边界

两个和弦的指法已单独确认且来源许可。你选慢速与舒适路线；本篇不承诺适用每种和弦，亦不代替 H275 的练习安排。

## English

Use this Skill when you can already sound two separate chords and want to move **slowly on a fixed count** from A to B and back. Finish with at least two audible A→B→A round trips, or name the exact finger move that still fails. It does not teach the first chord chart or demand a full accompaniment or performance tempo.

### Preparation and inputs

Choose two permitted charts matching your tuning and sound each chord alone first. Notice fingers that can stay, fingers to lift, and new landing points. Count one to four aloud or use a comfortable slow metronome. Replace an uncomfortable span with a simpler known pair rather than forcing a painful grip.

### Execution

1. **Rehearse without strumming.** Move slowly from A to B, keeping any useful common contact and placing a leading fingertip first. Return to A and confirm the path.
2. **Leave time for the move.** In each four-count group, strum only count one; use other counts to release, move and land. Play A, B, A slowly without gripping the wrist tight.
3. **Hear the new chord.** If one B string is muted, pause and check that fingertip, then reduce speed. Change one contact detail rather than repeating a long broken run.
4. **Repeat the complete trip.** Make a second A→B→A with a clear count. Stop when both sound distinguishable twice, or record the finger and direction where the move still breaks.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

At least two slow clear A→B→A cycles, or a precise failed landing for the next practice. Speed is not the passing condition.

- **Second chord is unclear alone:** Return to one-chord string checks first.
- **Always late:** Keep a slow count and move on non-sounding counts.
- **One string mutes after moving:** Pluck it alone and correct the landing.
- **Hand hurts:** Stop and rest; pain is not proficiency training.

### Assumptions and limits

Both chord shapes have already been checked independently and are permitted to use. You choose the slow count and comfortable route. This does not promise every chord pair or replace H275's overall practice organization.
