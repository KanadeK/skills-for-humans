## 中文

当别人邀你参加普通饭局、散步、参观或通话，你想接受，并且活动已具体到**足以让你决定是否愿意参与**时加载。目标是把口头/文字“好啊”变成双方理解一致的时间、地点/方式和必要条件。若邀请尚不够明确到能决定，先走含糊计划澄清；若你不想参加，可以拒绝，这份 Skill 不把答应当礼貌义务。

### 准备与输入

看对方实际提出的活动、日期或时间范围、地点/线上方式、预计持续多久和参与者。问自己本次确实能否去、可待多久、需要哪些通行/饮食/费用或其他现实条件；只提出会改变你是否答应的关键项，不要求对方交一份项目计划书。若涉及场馆订位或收费，查本次正式活动信息，别假设“普通见面”一定免费、可随意取消。

### 执行

1. **先把自己的答案定准。** 是愿意参加、需要某项条件才愿意、只能先暂定，还是其实不想去？把“愿意但周六晚上不行”与“已经确认周六晚上”分开。不要为了暂时结束聊天而给出假的确定答复。
2. **只补齐影响履行的空白。** 确认具体日期与当地时间、共同能找到的地点/线上入口、活动大致内容与结束范围；有费用、预约、交通或可及需求会改变选择时，问清谁负责订位、是否需要你先确认支付或是否有可用替代。无关的小细节可以之后再定。
3. **发一个对应实际状态的回复。** 能接受时说“好，我可以__日__时在__参加__，我大约能待到__。__由谁确认？” 条件未满足时说“我愿意，但先确认__；确认前我先不算定下。” 不需要编理由，也不代表其他同行者作承诺。
4. **让对方回到同一个版本。** 对方回复后核对日期、时间、地点/入口或平台、参加者和必要条件是否一致；若对方改了一个会影响你选择的条件，重新决定，而不是把自己的早先“好”自动延伸过去。未回复或只发一个表情时，按实际内容标“待确认”，不强求对方即时回应。
5. **记录与下一动作。** 双方确认后记下当前安排，真正日历邀请另用相应流程；需订位/买票时先确认负责人、官方规则与实际结果。临近前若情况改变，主动改期/取消各有流程，不靠最后一分钟失联让旧计划自己消失。

### 完成与停止

你和邀请者都确认**同一活动、具体日期时间、地点/参与方式、必要条件与下一负责人**，或者清楚知道还有哪一项未确认、当前尚未承诺，本轮完成。**约定成立不等于活动已经发生。** 新条款、费用或安排让你不再愿意时可明确说不；对方未同意条件时也不能把他们写成已承诺。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“下周见”双方想的是不同日子：** 在答应定案前写出星期、日期和当地时间；时区不同再按专门流程核对。
- **说“我应该可以”，对方听成已确定：** 补一句“目前暂定/尚待__，我在__前回复确认”，别让语气代替状态。
- **地点写“那家店”或“门口”：** 确认场所全称与会合入口/地址；现场会合点可以另立明确约定。
- **费用或预订要求后来才出现：** 重新核对你是否仍接受，未同意前不让别人以你的名义付款/占位。
- **对方没有回确认：** 保留你发出的版本，适当时间再问一次；不把送达回执当双方同意。

### 假设、替代与副作用

适用于普通自愿邀约，不处理强迫、骚扰、危机、金融合同或大型集体活动。礼貌习惯和回复速度因关系与文化而异，确认计划只需要清楚、尊重、不替别人答应。听觉、视觉、语言或日历设备不便时可用双方可读的简短文字、复述或纸面记录，不要求同一 App。副作用是聊天多一条确认消息，但周六和周日不再都叫“那个周末”。

## English

Load this Skill when someone invites you to an ordinary meal, walk, visit or call, you want to accept, and the activity is **specific enough for you to decide whether you are willing**. Turn a spoken or typed “sure” into a time, place or mode and necessary terms both people understand. If the invitation is too vague to decide, clarify it first. If you do not want to go, you can decline; agreeing is not a politeness duty.

### Preparation and inputs

Read what was actually proposed: activity, date or time range, place or online mode, likely duration and attendees. Ask whether you can attend, how long you can stay and what access, food, cost or other practical conditions matter to your choice. Ask only for details that change your answer, not a project plan. For a booked or paid venue, check this event's official information; an ordinary meeting is not automatically free or freely cancellable.

### Execution

1. **Choose an honest answer first.** Are you willing, willing only if one condition works, tentatively available, or not interested? “I can do it but not Saturday evening” is different from confirming Saturday evening. Do not give a false yes just to close a chat.
2. **Fill only commitment-changing gaps.** Confirm specific date and local time, a place both can find or the online entry, the activity and approximate end. For cost, booking, travel or access conditions that affect your choice, ask who books, whether you must approve a payment and what alternative exists. Small nonessential details can wait.
3. **Reply with the actual status.** For a real yes: “Yes, I can join __ on __ at __; I can stay until about __. Who will confirm __?” For a conditional yes: “I would like to, but first we need to check __; it is not settled for me until then.” You owe no invented excuse and cannot commit another attendee for them.
4. **Get onto the same version.** After their reply, compare date, time, place or entrance or platform, attendees and essential terms. If they change something that would affect your decision, decide again; your earlier yes does not automatically extend. For no reply or only an emoji, mark pending according to what was actually said instead of demanding an instant answer.
5. **Record the next action.** Once both sides confirm, note the current plan; creating a calendar invitation is another task. For booking or tickets, confirm who is responsible, the official rule and the actual result. If conditions later change, use the change or cancel flow rather than letting the old plan fade through silence.

### Success and stop

You and the inviter have confirmed the **same activity, specific date and time, place or participation mode, essential terms and next owner**, or you know what is still unknown and that you have not yet committed. **A plan is not the event itself.** You may decline a new cost or condition. The other person is not committed to a condition they did not accept.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Next week” means different days:** State weekday, calendar date and local time before calling it settled; use a timezone check for different regions.
- **“I should be able to” was heard as confirmed:** Add “tentative until __; I will confirm by __” so tone does not act as status.
- **“That café” or “the entrance” is unclear:** Confirm full venue and entrance or address; agree a precise on-site meeting point separately.
- **A fee or booking appears later:** Decide whether you still accept. Do not let someone pay or reserve in your name before consent.
- **No acknowledgment arrives:** Keep the version you sent and ask once at a reasonable time. Delivery is not mutual agreement.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

This covers voluntary ordinary invitations, not coercion, harassment, crisis contact, financial contracts or large-group facilitation. Politeness and reply pace vary by relationship and culture; a clear respectful plan does not require agreeing for others. For hearing, sight, language or calendar-device needs, use a brief readable text, spoken readback or paper note without requiring the same app. One extra confirmation message prevents Saturday and Sunday both becoming “that weekend.”

### Sources

- Original synthesis. There is no cross-cultural rule that you must accept an ordinary invitation or reply by one universal deadline. For a paid or booked venue, check this event's own official time, charge and cancellation terms.
