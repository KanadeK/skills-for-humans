## 中文

当你已经[确定模式与层位](../match-oven-mode-and-rack-to-a-recipe/SKILL.md)，却要把一只较大的烤盘或几只烤盘放入烤箱时，加载这份 Skill。目标是**符合本机型和配方的空气流通、门/元件间距与稳定承载**，不是让烤箱容量看起来得到充分利用。烘烤成品是否完成，还需食物特定检查。

### 准备与输入

确认每只烤盘的真实长宽高、食物装载后高度、架位和烤箱说明允许的盘间/盘壁距离；不同机型的具体厘米数不能互抄。查门、风口、加热元件及架子的禁放区域，配方是否允许同时烤多盘、是否需途中调换位置。把烤盘在**冷态**试放，保证能安全推进和拉出，不要求你把装满的盘悬在热箱前寻找空隙。

### 执行步骤

1. **先照配方选数量。** 配方要求单盘或留空时，不因能塞下第二盘就一起烤。若允许多盘，按机型说明确认实际层位、相互间距和烤盘承载；盘与盘不直接重叠，也不堵住热风或排气路径。
2. **冷态试开关门。** 食物加上高度后，盘不能碰门、炉壁、上方架子或加热元件；门能自然关闭。锅盖、铝箔、烘焙纸只按其与本机型说明使用，不把整层架子或炉底包住来省清洁。
3. **检验空气路径和稳定性。** 盘四周需要本机型规定的空间，架子水平、烤盘不摇晃。Whirlpool 某型号要求烤具与壁留出具体距离，但那不是所有烤箱统一值；这里用你自己的手册核对。缺手册或尺寸太紧时，先单盘试做或分批。
4. **给多盘安排检查方法。** 配方和机型若建议途中前后、上下换位，开烤前先写清何时、怎样用隔热工具操作，以及某些脆弱食物是否不宜过早移动。没有可靠指示就不为了“均匀”反复开门、端烫盘。
5. **入炉前作出布置结论。** 记录每盘属于哪层、相互是否干扰、门能否关闭、完成时如何取出；不成立就减盘、换适用容器或分批，不用延长烤时补空气不通。

### 完成与停止

每只盘按配方/机型稳放、热风通路未被堵、门和元件有安全间距、你能用可执行动作取出，就完成摆放。盘被门挤压、烤纸靠近禁区、架子倾斜或你无法安全提盘时先不加热；烟、火、燃气异味和设备故障退出本流程。

### 常见报错与补救

- **两盘并排几乎贴住：** 按手册改层或分批；表面烤得慢不证明该用更大火。
- **为了防滴油把炉底铺满箔纸：** 检查本机型明确允许的接滴方案；禁止遮挡风口或在不允许的位置铺设。
- **食物膨起会碰上层：** 留出配方预期高度，改层或少做一盘，不等膨起才开门救。
- **多盘上色不一：** 先核对摆放与本机型热区；若配方允许，按规定检查点调换，不给脆弱糕体随意提前旋转。
- **盘能进去却拉不出：** 冷态重试取出路径，选轻而适用的盘或现场协助，别等热箱完成尺寸测试。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通家用烤箱中已由配方确认的烤盘安排，不进行设备校准或维修。对流、常规、烤架数量和容器材料会改变热风路径；确切机型手册优先。视力、握力或站立受限时先在冷态用标记和轻盘排练，使用合适隔热工具或现场可信协助；没有安全取出路径就改批次。副作用可能是多烤一轮，但每盘得到真实的热风空间。

## English

Load this Skill after [matching oven mode and rack](../match-oven-mode-and-rack-to-a-recipe/SKILL.md) when a large pan or several pans must enter the oven. Arrange **airflow, door and element clearance, and stable support under this model and recipe**, not maximum apparent capacity. Food completion still needs its own check.

### Preparation and inputs

Know each pan's real dimensions, loaded food height, rack, and the exact oven manual's pan-to-wall or pan-to-pan clearance. Do not copy centimetres from another model. Find forbidden areas around door, vents, elements, and racks. Check whether the recipe permits multiple pans or mid-bake repositioning. Fit everything **while cold** so you do not hold a loaded pan in front of a hot cavity while searching for space.

### Execution steps

1. **Use only the number of pans the recipe supports.** A second pan fitting in the cavity does not override a single-pan instruction. For multiple pans, confirm permitted racks, spacing, and load under your oven manual. Do not stack pans directly or block hot-air or exhaust paths.
2. **Test the door and height cold.** Account for food rising: pans must not touch the door, wall, upper rack, or heating element, and the door should close naturally. Use lids, foil, and parchment only under their and the oven's directions, not as an improvised cover over a whole rack or oven bottom.
3. **Check airflow and support.** Leave the clearances this model requires around bakeware, with level racks and steady pans. A Whirlpool model gives a particular pan-wall distance, but it is not a universal number. Use your own manual; choose one pan or separate batches if instructions or room are inadequate.
4. **Plan a multi-pan check.** If the recipe and model call for front/back or upper/lower changes, decide when and how you can do this with heat protection. Some fragile baked foods should not move too early. Without reliable direction, do not keep opening the door and handling hot pans merely to chase “evenness.”
5. **Record the loading arrangement.** Note each pan's rack, interference check, door closure, and removal route. If the arrangement fails, reduce pans, choose approved bakeware, or divide the bake. More time cannot repair blocked air.

### Success and stop

Every pan sits stably under recipe and model rules, air paths remain open, door and elements have the required clearance, and you can remove the pans safely. Do not heat with a door-pressed pan, liner near a forbidden area, tilted rack, or no safe lift path. Smoke, fire, gas odour, and equipment faults exit this everyday procedure.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Two pans nearly touch:** Move racks under the manual or bake in batches. Slow browning does not prove maximum heat is needed.
- **Foil covers the oven bottom to catch drips:** Check this model's approved drip-control method; do not block vents or cover forbidden surfaces.
- **Food may rise into the rack above:** Leave the recipe's expected height or use another rack or batch before baking.
- **Pans brown differently:** Check layout and this oven's hot spots. Reposition at a supported checkpoint only when the recipe permits; do not rotate a fragile cake early by guesswork.
- **The pan fits but cannot be removed:** Rehearse the removal path while cold, use lighter approved bakeware, or arrange in-person help rather than testing dimensions after baking.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This arranges recipe-approved bakeware in an ordinary domestic oven. It does not calibrate or repair equipment. Convection, conventional modes, rack counts, and pan materials change airflow; the exact manual takes priority. For sight, grip, or standing limits, rehearse with cold pans, marks, appropriate heat protection, or trusted in-person help. Divide the bake when no safe removal route exists. An extra round may give every pan real air space.
