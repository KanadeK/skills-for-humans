## 中文

当你有一套**手动滴滤/手冲**设备和咖啡粉，想让水经过粉层、再把咖啡液与粉渣分开时，用这份 Skill。它处理滤杯、滤纸、注水与结束过滤；不把咖啡机、电动研磨器或咖啡因健康效果写成通用说明。浸泡后再压滤的器具应按自己型号的说明走，不硬套本流程。

### 准备与输入

读滤杯和滤纸的尺寸/材质要求，以及所用咖啡粉或可信冲泡指南给出的粉量、水量、水状态、研磨适配和注水顺序。确认滤杯能稳稳架在足够容量、适合热液体的杯或壶上，水可饮用，接杯不是已经装满的容器。可用秤、量杯或该器具的刻度量取；若来源要求精确测量而你做不到，先选有可执行刻度的做法。倒热水或握壶困难时可用稳定台面、较小安全水壶或可信协助，不在悬空滤杯上练手腕。

### 执行

1. **先装对滤具。** 将与此滤杯匹配的滤纸或指定滤网放好，按制造商说明折边、润湿或预热。若要预冲滤纸，确认下方有接水容器并倒掉这部分水；它不是咖啡成品。滤纸没有卡稳、接杯倾斜或器具说明不明，就先停。
2. **按这套方法量取。** 放入适合该滤具的咖啡粉，用器具/配方给出的粉量与总水量作本次基线。水量不超过滤杯、接杯或安全握持能力的上限；不拿别人设备的一组温度和比例冒充你的默认设置。
3. **让粉层均匀接触水。** 按此方法说明开始少量注水，使粉层都能被润湿；再按说明分次缓慢注入余水，让水透过滤层进入下方容器。手、袖子和电线远离热水与蒸汽，滤杯不稳时暂停倒水。
4. **观察过滤，而非追着流速乱加水。** 水应流入接杯，粉渣留在滤具中。液面接近滤杯或接杯的安全容量时先停注；滴流异常慢时按制造商对粉粗细、滤纸或堵塞的说明排查，别用猛倒加压。
5. **结束萃取。** 按所用方法的停止点等最后一段可用咖啡液流下，随后将滤杯和热滤纸移到安全位置。不要为了多几滴而挤压烫湿滤纸或让粉渣落入杯。确认杯内是咖啡液、滤纸和渣已分开，再等到可安全饮用的温度。
6. **只改一个下次变量。** 若这杯与偏好不同，记下粉量、水量、研磨状态、注水或时间中的一项，再按器具指南调整下一杯；当前杯没必要通过一连串猜测操作“修复”成另一种饮品。

### 成功条件

你按**这套滤具**适用的用量与步骤完成注水和滴滤，成品装在可握持的杯壶，粉渣留在滤具中，没有溢出或烫伤风险未处理；也能说出下次只想调整哪一项。器具不兼容或热水无法安全操作时停止，是完整判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **滤纸尺寸不对或会塌落：** 先换符合滤杯说明的滤纸或滤网，不用手扶热纸继续冲。
- **滴得很慢就继续猛倒：** 停止注水并留出排流时间；冷却后再按可信来源核对研磨与滤具，不让热咖啡漫过杯沿。
- **杯子容量不够：** 开始前换足够容量的耐热接杯或减少整杯配方量；别在半杯热液上临时换杯。
- **没有器具说明、只看到网上别人的精确参数：** 找该型号制造商说明或可信对应做法；无法确认就先不拿热水实验。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 用于普通食品饮品，不作提神、治疗或咖啡因安全量的健康建议。滤杯形状、滤纸、研磨、粉量与水流会改变结果，品牌示例不是统一配方。视力或握力受限时可用明显刻度、稳定支架、小容量壶和可信协助；不要为了所谓手冲仪式举起无法稳控的热水。用过的滤纸和渣需要按当地废物规则处理，热器具需安全放置。

## English

Use this Skill with a **manual pour-over dripper** and coffee grounds when you want water to pass through the bed and the finished drink to separate from the grounds. It covers dripper, filter, pouring, and finishing. It does not provide universal controls for electric machines or grinders or make health claims about caffeine. Use a press-style immersion brewer's own directions instead of forcing it into this flow.

### Preparation and inputs

Read the dripper and filter size and material requirements, plus the grounds or trusted method's dose, total water, water condition, grind suitability, and pouring sequence. Confirm the dripper sits stably on a heat-suitable cup or carafe with enough capacity. Water must be drinkable, and the receiving vessel should not already be full. Measure with a scale, marked cup, or the brewer's own markings. If a method needs precision you cannot achieve, choose one whose marks you can use. If gripping or pouring hot water is difficult, use a stable surface, a smaller controllable kettle, or trusted help rather than practising over a suspended dripper.

### Execution

1. **Fit the filter first.** Put the correct paper or specified filter in the dripper, folding, rinsing, or preheating only as its maker directs. If you rinse paper, catch and discard that water; it is not finished coffee. Stop if paper will not sit properly, the receiving cup tilts, or essential equipment directions are unclear.
2. **Measure for this method.** Add grounds suited to this dripper and use the stated coffee and total-water amounts as your baseline. Water must fit the dripper, vessel, and your safe pouring capacity. Another brewer's precise temperature and ratio are not defaults for yours.
3. **Wet the bed evenly.** Begin with the water amount or pattern in your method so all grounds become wet. Continue its staged gentle pours and let water pass through the filter into the vessel. Keep hands, sleeves, and cords clear of hot water and steam; pause if the dripper becomes unstable.
4. **Watch the filtration, not just the clock.** Liquid should enter the cup while grounds remain in the filter. Stop pouring before the dripper or cup reaches an unsafe level. If flow is unusually slow, follow the maker's grind, paper, or blockage advice after it cools; forceful extra water does not repair a clog.
5. **End the extraction.** At your method's finish point, let the useful final drip fall, then move the dripper and hot filter to a safe place. Do not squeeze wet hot paper for a few more drops or send the grounds into the cup. Confirm drink and grounds are separate, then let the cup cool to a safe sipping temperature.
6. **Change one variable next time.** If the taste differs from your preference, note one of dose, water, grind, pouring, or time and adjust the next cup under suitable guidance. This cup does not need a chain of guessed interventions to become another drink.

### Success

You have followed amounts and steps suitable for **this dripper**, finished dripping into a stable cup or carafe, and kept grounds in the filter without an unresolved overflow or burn risk. You can name one variable to revisit next time. An incompatible setup or uncontrollable hot pour calls for a stop instead.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Paper size is wrong or collapses:** Use paper or a filter matching the maker's instructions. Do not hold hot paper in place with your hand.
- **Flow slows so you pour hard and fast:** Stop adding water and allow drainage. After cooling, check grind and filter guidance rather than flooding the cup.
- **The receiving cup is too small:** Use a heat-suitable vessel with enough capacity before starting, or reduce the planned drink. Do not transfer a half-full hot pour on the fly.
- **There are no device directions, only another person's exact numbers online:** Find the maker's manual or a trusted guide for this model. Do not experiment with hot water while essential setup is unknown.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary food drink, not health advice about alertness, treatment, or safe caffeine amounts. Dripper shape, filter, grind, dose, and flow change the result; a brand example is not a universal recipe. If seeing marks or controlling a kettle is difficult, use large clear markings, a stable support, a smaller vessel, or trusted help. A ceremonial-looking pour is not worth lifting water you cannot control. Dispose of used filters and grounds under local rules and park hot equipment safely.
