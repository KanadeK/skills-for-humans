## 中文

当你准备把现成食品加入一份**不用加热就要吃的普通餐食**，但不确定某项是否能直接食用时，加载这份 Skill。结果是每项分别被标为**可在当前条件下直接用 / 需要完成包装要求的处理 / 关键信息不明而不用**。它不是给生肉、禽、海鲜、婴幼儿食品或严重过敏设计免加热方案，也不靠颜色和气味补写储存史。

### 准备与输入

对每项找原包装或可信的制造商说明，读清“即食/已清洗/需烹煮/需加热”“开封后”“保持冷藏”等具体文字、日期和适用份量；核对它从购买到现在的保存条件是否可靠。已煮熟的剩菜想冷食时，先按既有[熟度与保存流程](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md)确认先前确实煮熟、及时冷却并妥善冷藏；本 Skill 不重建失踪的时间温度记录。

### 执行步骤

1. **先判断是否真的无需进一步烹调。** 包装明确写可直接食用、可冷食或已按其说明完成必要处理的，继续核对。若写“食用前充分加热/煮熟”“生制”或只提供烹调步骤，这件不能直接进入无热餐；“预制”“方便”不是即食同义词。
2. **把储存与日期当作条件。** 需冷藏的东西必须有可信的冷藏史和适用日期；开封后的说明也要满足。包装胀起、漏液、严重破损或储存条件无法确认时停止用它，不让味道或外观投票替代记录。
3. **处理果蔬的实际状态。** 包装明确写“预洗/即食”的蔬菜可按其要求使用；普通未经清洗的新鲜果蔬则按当地可靠建议用可用的安全流动水清洗，保持与生动物性食物和受污染工具分开。没有安全清洗条件时，换确实可直接食用的食品，不用肥皂、清洁剂或“擦一下”冒充洗净。
4. **确认不会在组合时重新污染。** 用干净的手、盘、刀具或餐具；若它们曾碰生肉/禽/海鲜或脏表面，先按食品安全要求清洗/更换。某种食品的食用限制不明时先隔离，不为了拼成一餐猜配料。
5. **逐项给一个可复述结果。** 记录“哪项、凭哪句标签/保存证据、直接用还是先处理或不使用”。能处理但本次没有炉具/清洗/冷藏条件的，实际上就是本餐不使用。

### 完成与停止

每个候选都有明确的当前可直接食用结论和依据，才能交给无热成餐。关键标签、日期、保存或清洁条件缺失时输出“不使用这项”；这可以让餐食少一项，不要求用未知食物填空。食用后不适或过敏症状转适当医疗/紧急渠道，本 Skill 不诊断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“熟食外观”被当作即食标示：** 读完整包装与储存要求；需要再加热的就退出无热候选。
- **预洗沙拉又在脏水里重洗：** 按包装与当地官方说明使用，避免让已处理食品接触不洁工具。
- **冷藏食品在桌上放了多久不清楚：** 不用气味推断安全，按保守的当地食品安全指导处理或不用。
- **罐头是常温货架买的，便以为开封后也能常温留着：** 重新读“开封后”要求；剩余食物进入独立保存流程。
- **只为凑一餐把陌生限制食品加入：** 停止该项，先核对实际标签/需求，不给过敏或医疗限制猜答案。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这是普通人的即时无热成餐入口，不给全球统一日期和室温时限，也不对医疗饮食或高风险人群另订规则。地区标签文字、食品风险和家中冷藏条件不同；用当地权威与产品说明。读不清小字可用放大、可访问商品页或可信的人读回；没有干净餐具/洗手条件时先取得它们或选密封即开即食且可安全使用的选项。副作用是你可能少用一件“差不多”的食物，厨房不用替猜测背书。

## English

Load this Skill before adding a prepared item to an **ordinary meal you will eat without heating** when its ready-to-eat status is uncertain. Give each item one result: **usable as-is under current conditions, needs its stated preparation, or lacks a decisive fact and stays out**. This does not design a no-heat path for raw meat, poultry, seafood, infant food, or severe allergies. Colour and smell cannot reconstruct missing storage history.

### Preparation and inputs

For each item, find its original pack or reliable maker directions. Read “ready to eat,” “pre-washed,” “cook thoroughly,” “heat before eating,” “after opening,” and “keep refrigerated” in context, plus date and portion. Check whether storage from purchase to now can be explained. If previously cooked leftovers will be eaten cold, first use the existing [doneness and storage process](../check-doneness-and-store-leftovers/SKILL.md) to establish proper original cooking, timely cooling, and refrigeration. This Skill cannot recreate missing time and temperature records.

### Execution steps

1. **Decide whether further cooking is required.** Continue for food explicitly ready to eat, permitted cold, or already processed as its directions require. Anything marked cook or heat thoroughly before eating, raw, or only supplied with cooking steps does not enter this no-heat meal as-is. “Convenient” and “pre-prepared” are not the same as ready to eat.
2. **Treat storage and date as conditions.** Chilled food needs credible refrigeration and an applicable date and after-opening instruction. Stop using a swollen, leaking, badly damaged pack or one with unknown storage. Appearance and odour do not vote in place of records.
3. **Handle produce's actual state.** Produce labelled pre-washed or ready to eat may be used under its package. Ordinary unwashed fresh produce needs safe running water under applicable official guidance and separation from raw animal food and contaminated tools. When safe washing is unavailable, choose genuinely ready-to-eat food rather than soap, cleaning chemicals, or a symbolic wipe.
4. **Avoid recontamination during combination.** Use clean hands, plates, knives, and utensils. Clean or replace anything that touched raw meat, poultry, seafood, or a dirty surface under food-safety guidance. Hold an item with unclear restrictions aside rather than guessing ingredients to complete a plate.
5. **Record a repeatable decision.** State the item, the label or storage evidence, and whether to use it now, prepare it first, or exclude it. A food that can be prepared only with heat, washing, or refrigeration you do not have is excluded from this meal.

### Success and stop

Every candidate has a supported current as-is decision before assembly. Missing decisive label, date, storage, or cleaning information means leaving that item out. A smaller meal is a valid result; unknown food need not fill the plate. Illness or allergic symptoms after eating belong to appropriate medical or emergency help, not diagnosis here.

### Common errors and recovery

- **“Looks cooked” substitutes for a ready-to-eat label:** Read full package and storage directions. Food requiring heat leaves the no-heat list.
- **Pre-washed salad is washed again in dirty water:** Follow its pack and local official guidance; keep processed food off dirty tools.
- **Nobody knows how long chilled food sat out:** Do not infer safety from smell. Use conservative local food-safety guidance or exclude it.
- **A pantry can is assumed pantry-safe after opening:** Read the after-opening instruction; remaining food follows a separate storage flow.
- **An unfamiliar restricted food is added to make a meal:** Hold it and confirm the actual label and need. Do not guess an allergy or medical restriction.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an entry check for an immediate ordinary no-heat meal, not one global date or room-temperature limit or a medical-diet rule. Regional labels, food risk, and available refrigeration vary. Follow local authority and product directions. Magnify print, use an accessible listing, or ask a trusted person to read it. Get clean tools and handwashing first, or use an approved sealed ready-to-eat option you can handle safely. One “probably fine” item may leave the meal rather than asking the kitchen to underwrite a guess.
