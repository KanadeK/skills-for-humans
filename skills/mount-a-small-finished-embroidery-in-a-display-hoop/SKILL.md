## 中文

当你有一片**已收线、全干、准备展示的自有小绣样**，想在适用的展示绷里居中摆在稳定室内位置时，加载这份 Skill。结果是图案不被圈边压住、布面平整、背面线尾与针具不外露、展示位不易跌落。它与工作绷上缝制或平放收存不同；不钻孔、不把作品悬在热源或潮湿处，也不声称永久防褪色。

### 准备与输入

确认布与线全干、导线按材料处理、背面尾线牢靠，所有针具已收盒。选尺寸合适、边缘光滑、厂商允许作展示的绣绷或框；面板有足够留边可夹且不会切到针脚。找自有或获准的桌面/支架，避直晒、热源、厨房水汽及儿童/宠物可碰倒的位置。没有展示合用绷可保留平放，不强买。

### 执行

1. **空摆决定视角。** 先把作品在展示绷内圈上轻放，找图案中心与上方方向，查边缘是否留有可固定余布。图案过大、圈压到线迹时换更合适绷，不缩小绣样硬塞。
2. **按展示绷说明缓慢夹布。** 将外圈从一边逐步套上，分四周轻拉至平整，不拽刺绣中心，不把背面尾线挤到正面。确认螺丝/夹边锁稳，但不压坏纤维；不同弹性绷照其产品方法。
3. **整理背面和位置。** 将余布按展示绷允许方式平顺收在背后，不未经计划剪掉将来可能需要的边；查看背面无针头、长松线或凸出硬件。将绷放到稳定支架/桌面，轻触看是否会滚落。
4. **留下照看入口。** 记录放置位置，之后查灰尘、潮气与圈边压痕；若长时间展示造成褪色或布松，先移走、按材质重新决定，不把展示当存放保护箱。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

图案居中可见、布面平整、绷稳、背面无危险针具，并置于获准安全位置；条件不合时平放保留也是正确结果。把湿作品夹进过小绷、靠在易倒边缘，不算完成。

- **绷圈遮住线迹：** 换大一号或其他合用展示法，不硬拉图案。
- **夹布时布起皱/针脚被拉：** 松开重摆，分边缓慢调张力。
- **支架一碰就晃：** 换稳固低位，不把作品放通道边。
- **背面发现针或松尾：** 停止陈列，先清点针具并做背面收尾。
- **视力/握持不便：** 大图、低台、可信协助持绷，由你核对居中、稳定和安全位置。

### 假设与边界

展示绷可长期陈列是具体产品的用途；DMC 对工作绷的“避免长期夹出折痕”仍提醒要区别工作工具与展示器材。普通室内陈列不构成光照、潮湿或文物级保护保证。

## English

Load this Skill when an **owned small embroidery is finished, its threads secured, and its fabric fully dry** and you want it centred in a suitable display hoop on a stable indoor surface. Finish with the motif uncrushed by the ring, cloth flat, no reverse needles or loose tails, and a place unlikely to topple. This differs from stitching in a working hoop or flat storage. Do not drill, hang over heat or dampness, or promise permanent fade resistance.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm cloth and floss are dry, guide marks handled by material directions, reverse tails hold, and needles are cased. Choose a sound smooth hoop or frame whose maker permits display and whose opening leaves enough fabric margin without covering stitches. Find an owned or permitted tabletop or stand away from strong sun, heat, kitchen steam, and child or pet knock-over paths. Keeping art flat is valid if no display hoop fits; no purchase is required.

### Execution

1. **Trial-place for view.** Lay the work lightly over the inner ring and choose centre and top orientation, checking spare margin for a secure hold. If the art is too large and a ring covers stitching, use another size or method rather than pulling the motif smaller.
2. **Clamp gradually by product directions.** Work the outer ring on from one side, easing fabric around all edges until flat. Do not tug the stitched centre or push reverse tails to the front. Lock screw or rim without crushing fibres; a flexible display ring follows its own method.
3. **Arrange reverse and place.** Tuck spare fabric behind under hoop directions without cutting away future display margin by impulse. Check reverse for no needle, long loop, or protruding hardware. Put the hoop on a stable indoor stand or tabletop and touch lightly for rolling or falling.
4. **Set a care check.** Note the site and later inspect dust, humidity, and rim marks. Move or reassess under material care if display begins fading or loosening. A display is not a protective archive box.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

The motif is visible and centred, fabric flat, hoop stable, reverse free of sharp needles, and position permitted and safe. If conditions fail, flat storage is also correct. Clamping damp art in a too-small ring at a fall-prone edge is unfinished.

- **Ring hides stitches:** Use a bigger hoop or another display route, not force-stretch the motif.
- **Fabric puckers or stitches strain while clamping:** Release and reseat, easing tension around all sides.
- **Stand rocks on touch:** Choose a lower stable site away from paths.
- **Needle or loose tail appears behind:** Pause display and account for tools and reverse finishing first.
- **Sight or grip is limited:** Larger art, low table, and trusted hoop-holding help can support your centre, stability, and safe-site check.

### Assumptions and limits

Long-term display is an intended use of a specific display hoop, while DMC warns that a working hoop can leave creases if clamped for too long. Ordinary indoor display gives no guarantee against light, humidity, or heritage-conservation risk.
