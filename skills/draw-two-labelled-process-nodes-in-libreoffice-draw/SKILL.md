## 中文

你已列出一个普通流程里相邻的两个不同动作，想在 Draw 图中先把它们做成可编辑节点时使用本篇。成果是两个形状各写一条短的动作标签、顺序和层级可辨；暂不声称它们已经被连起来。不要把同一句话换颜色复制成两个能力节点。

### 准备与输入

保存 ODG 副本，先用动词写出动作 A、B 各自的输入与结果，确认它们不会合成一件事更清楚。选一套适合普通步骤的简单形状与足够大的文字区域。留出以后连接线的空间，不把节点挤在页边。

### 执行

1. 在页内画第一个普通流程形状，并把动作 A 的短标签放进形状内部。
2. 在阅读方向的后方画第二个同级形状，写动作 B，不复制 A 的句子。
3. 在实际查看尺寸核两条标签完整、字体可读、两个节点没有重叠。
4. 保存重开，逐一选中两个节点确认可独立编辑且位置留得下连接线。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

A 与 B 两节点含义不同、可独立编辑、标签完整，顺序明确。

- **文字被截：** 缩短标签或增大形状，不盲缩微字。
- **两步同义：** 合并为一节点，另找真实下步。
- **形状压边：** 移回页内留连接空间。

### 假设与边界

只画普通流程的两个节点，不证明流程正确或建立连接。你负责动作语义，禁止用作安全关键程序。

## English

Use this Skill when two genuinely different adjacent actions in an ordinary process need editable Draw nodes. Finish with two shapes, each with one short action label and clear order/hierarchy. Do not yet claim they are connected. Recoloring the same sentence twice does not create two meaningful steps.

### Preparation and inputs

Save an ODG copy. Phrase A and B as verbs with different input/result, checking they are not one step artificially split. Choose simple shapes with room for readable labels. Leave space for a later connector and avoid page edges.

### Execution

1. Draw first ordinary process shape and place concise action A label inside it.
2. Draw second peer shape later in reading direction with distinct action B wording.
3. At normal viewing size check both labels complete, readable and not overlapping.
4. Save/reopen and select each node separately, confirming editable text and connector space.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A and B nodes differ in meaning, are separately editable, have complete labels and clear order.

- **Text clipped:** Shorten label or enlarge shape.
- **Steps synonymous:** Combine them and find a real next action.
- **Shape at edge:** Move inward with connector room.

### Assumptions and limits

This draws two ordinary nodes, not proof of process correctness or a link. You own step meaning; exclude safety-critical procedures.
