## 中文

当你准备买有日期标记的普通包装食品，并要判断它能否撑到计划使用日时加载。这里做购买前的日期解释，不处理买回家后的保存、婴幼儿配方食品、医疗饮食或食品安全事故。外观和气味不能替不明日期作证明。

### 准备与输入

找到包装上的**日期数字连同旁边的字**、适用地区、未开封储存要求和你打算使用的日期。还要知道从付款到回家能否满足标示的温度条件。日期模糊时放大照片、请店员展示同批清晰包装，或选另一件；如果屏幕朗读/OCR 识别出数字，仍要核对前后的“截至”“最佳”“销售”等字样。

### 执行

1. **先读词，再读数字。** 把完整日期词组和年月日顺序一起抄下；生产日期、销售日期、最佳品质日期与使用期限不能只因都长得像日历就互换。代码批号也不自动是保质期。
2. **按销售地区找解释。** 用当地官方食品标签说明判断这类日期的含义。英国 FSA 将 use-by 与安全、best-before 与品质区分；美国 USDA FSIS 说明许多美国食品日期主要表达品质，且类似词组未全国统一。不要把任何一地的解释直接翻译成全球规则。
3. **核对储存前提。** 读“冷藏”“冷冻”“避光”等包装条件，并看现场陈列是否相容；日期只有在相应储存条件得到遵守时才有意义。你无法确认必要冷藏或回程条件时，不因日期还远就放行。
4. **对照真实使用日。** 若当地的安全性截止日早于你的计划使用日，或今天已过该期限，换一件或改计划。若标记只谈最佳品质，就按你能接受的品质和当地指导决定；它不是无需检查储存与包装的通行证。
5. **输出三态。** 记为“时间条件符合 / 不符合 / 无法确认”，并留下所用日期词组、计划使用日和关键储存条件。无法确认时查当地官方说明或询问准确批次信息；仍不清楚就不买。

### 成功与停止

你能解释这件商品的日期**在当地究竟指什么**、怎样保存才适用，以及它是否覆盖计划使用日。你不能仅从一个数字宣布食品安全。包装漏损、胀包、变质迹象或储存历史可疑时停止这项日期判定，按当地食品安全建议处理，不尝一口做测试。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看到数字：** 找相邻字样或官方说明；找不到就保持“无法确认”。
- **把 best-before 当成所有地区的强制丢弃日：** 回到该地区官方定义，另外检查品质、包装和储存。
- **把美国 use-by 直接套英国含义，或反过来：** 先确认销售地区和适用官方资料，再重算结论。
- **计划使用日后移：** 重新比较，不让购物时的旧计划继续签发通过票。

### 假设、替代与副作用

本 Skill 假设你能取得商品实际标签与当地官方解释。散装食品或跨境包装可能没有足够日期信息；问清楚或另选，不用猜语言和数字顺序。视觉、语言或现场时间受限时可把照片留待核对后再买。可能因此错过一件便宜商品；促销价不延长日期。

## English

Load this Skill before buying ordinary packaged food with a date mark when you need to know whether it fits your planned use date. It interprets dates before purchase, not storage after arriving home, infant formula, medical diets, or a food-safety incident. Appearance and smell cannot resolve an unreadable date.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the **date together with its adjacent words**, the market where the item is sold, unopened storage directions, and your planned use date. Confirm that the route home can meet any temperature requirement. Enlarge a photo, request a readable pack from the same batch, or choose another item if the mark is unclear. If OCR reads the numbers aloud, also verify the words around them.

### Execution

1. **Read the phrase before the numbers.** Record the complete wording and day/month/year order. Production, sell-by, best-quality, and use dates are not interchangeable just because each resembles a calendar. A lot code is not automatically a consumer expiry date.
2. **Use the selling region's explanation.** Check local official food-label guidance. The UK FSA distinguishes use-by as safety-related and best-before as quality-related; the US USDA FSIS explains that many US date phrases concern quality and are not nationally uniform. Do not translate either jurisdiction's rule into a worldwide rule.
3. **Check the storage premise.** Read refrigeration, freezing, and other directions and compare them with the display. A date depends on the stated storage conditions. If required chilling or your trip home is uncertain, a distant date alone is insufficient.
4. **Compare your actual use date.** If the local safety-related deadline has passed or precedes your planned use, choose another item or change the plan. If the mark is about best quality, decide whether its quality window meets your needs under local guidance; it does not waive checks of packaging and storage.
5. **Record one of three outcomes.** Mark “timing fits / does not fit / unconfirmed,” with the date phrase, planned use, and storage condition. For an unconfirmed mark, consult local official guidance or obtain exact-batch information. If it stays unclear, do not buy it.

### Success and stop

You can explain what this item's date means **where it is sold**, which storage conditions make it relevant, and whether it covers your planned use. One number alone cannot certify food safety. Stop this date decision if the package leaks, bulges, shows spoilage, or has doubtful storage history; use local food-safety advice and do not taste it as a test.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only numbers are visible:** Find the neighbouring phrase or official explanation; otherwise keep it unconfirmed.
- **Treating every best-before as a mandatory discard date:** Return to the local definition, then consider quality, packaging, and storage.
- **Copying US use-by meaning to the UK, or the reverse:** Confirm the selling region and its official guidance, then redo the decision.
- **Your use date moved:** Compare again. The old shopping plan cannot approve a new schedule.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes access to the actual package and local official explanations. Loose food and cross-border packs may not provide enough information; ask or choose another item instead of guessing the language or digit order. If print, language, or time at the shelf is a barrier, photograph the mark and return after checking. You may miss a bargain. A discount does not extend a date.
