## 中文

当你想在**自有普通布**上做一小幅原创、不用于衣物修补的手绣练习，先要决定图形并把布稳定绷起时，加载这份 Skill。结果是居中、留有边距、标记可按材料说明去除、张力均匀的待绣布；还没有缝针。H272 是纸面构图，本篇处理布的缩、标记与绣绷张力，不借别人的付费图案。

### 准备与输入

读布的洗护标签和可去标记笔说明；选不易拉伸、纱线清楚的自有平纹布，留足超出绷圈的边。想预洗时只按布标签做并等全干；不明纤维或贵重/借用布不要用此练习。用废角试标记能否安全去除或可藏于针脚下；水溶笔未必适合禁水布。备完整无毛刺的绣绷、稳定桌面和可读光线。

### 执行

1. **在纸上定一个足够小的原创图。** 可以是一条简单封闭线加一个小填色区，不复制他人的角色、文字标志或文化专用纹样。确认图案能放在绷圈中间，周围留出拿布和未来展示所需边距。
2. **只把可移除导线落到布上。** 按布与标记工具的说明，轻描自己的图形或用获准转印纸；先做一处隐藏测试，发现渗色/不可去就换方法。线条只需指路，不反复压到布纤维损伤。
3. **分开绷圈并均匀绷布。** 松开外圈，把内圈平放，布正面向上居中盖住，外圈压下；沿四周轻轻、分次拉紧并逐步拧紧螺丝，让布平整可缝，但不把图案拉长或把布边扯散。
4. **检查可下针状态。** 图案清楚、没有明显皱折或扭斜，绷圈不滑、布没有被夹破；调整到稳即可。暂不用针时把绷好的布放安全位置，避免长期夹绷造成难去折痕。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

自有布上的原创导线可辨、位置居中且留边，绷圈均匀支撑而不伤布；若材料/标记不兼容，明确暂停改布。只有纸上图，没有布面引导和绷紧，不算本任务完成。

- **标记测试渗色或不可去：** 停止在成品布上画，换布/获准标记方法。
- **绷圈边布滑出：** 松开重放、逐边轻拉再紧固，不一侧猛扯。
- **图案被拉长或布起波纹：** 降一点张力，重新分配四周余布。
- **绷圈有毛刺或夹伤布：** 换完好工具，别靠更紧的螺丝压平伤口。
- **视力/抓握不便：** 大号简单图、清楚对比线、桌面绷圈支架或可信协助持圈，由你核对位置和张力。

### 假设与边界

普通平布通常较易练习，但不同纤维的预洗、导线去除和绷圈压力不通用。绷起的工作布不是可直接展示的完成绣品；尖针仍须收在针盒而不留在无人照看的布上。

## English

Load this Skill when you want a **small original hand-embroidery practice design on owned ordinary fabric**, not clothing repair, and must place and tension it first. Finish with a centred visible guide, enough margin, a mark removable under material directions, and evenly taut cloth; no stitching yet. H272 plans on paper, while this manages cloth shrinkage, marking, and hoop tension. Do not borrow a paid design.

### Preparation and inputs

Read fabric care and removable-pen directions. Choose owned fairly flat non-stretch fabric with clear weave and margin outside the hoop. Prewash only if its label permits, then dry fully. Unknown, valuable, or borrowed fabric is not this trial. Test a mark on scrap for removal or whether it will be covered; a water-soluble pen may not suit no-water fabric. Have a sound burr-free hoop, steady table, and readable light.

### Execution

1. **Choose a small original paper idea.** A simple closed outline and one little fill region are enough. Do not copy another's character, mark, or culture-specific motif. Leave room around the image for holding the cloth and later display.
2. **Transfer only a suitable guide.** Under fabric and tool directions, mark your own lines lightly or use permitted transfer paper, after a hidden test. Stop at bleeding or a mark that will not release. The line guides stitching; repeated hard marking must not damage fibres.
3. **Separate rings and tension evenly.** Loosen the outer ring, put the inner on a flat surface, centre fabric face up over it, and press on the outer ring. Pull edges gently in turns and tighten the screw gradually until flat and stitchable without stretching the motif or fraying the margin.
4. **Check readiness.** See clear motif, no major wrinkles or skew, a nonslipping hoop, and undamaged fabric. Adjust only as much as needed. Keep the prepared cloth safely when pausing; prolonged clamping can create hard-to-remove creases.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

An original guide is traceable on owned cloth with centre and margin, held evenly by a sound hoop, or an incompatible material causes an honest hold. A paper sketch without fabric guide and hoop is not prepared embroidery.

- **Test mark bleeds or will not release:** Stop marking the project and change fabric or approved marking method.
- **Fabric slips at the ring:** Loosen, reseat, and tension around all sides instead of yanking one.
- **Motif stretches or cloth ripples:** Ease tension and redistribute margin.
- **Hoop burrs or pinches fabric:** Change tool rather than crushing damage under a tighter screw.
- **Vision or grip is limited:** Use large simple art, contrast, a table hoop support, or trusted holding help while you verify position and tension.

### Assumptions and limits

Flat ordinary cloth is easier to practise but fibre-specific washing, mark removal, and hoop pressure differ. A hooped guide is not finished display art. Store sharp needles in a case, never unattended in the cloth.
