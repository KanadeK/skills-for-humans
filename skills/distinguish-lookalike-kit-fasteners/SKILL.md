## 中文

当普通用品包装里有两种以上看起来相似的螺丝、销钉、垫片或连接件，而准确说明在不同步骤指定不同编号时加载。你要在装入前**让每种实物与说明的零件编号可靠对应，或保留无法确认的一组**。这里不选螺丝刀头、不实际组装、不给缺件找代用品，也不处理承重或防倾倒安装。

### 准备与输入

拿到准确型号的零件图和原袋标签；先确认所有交付包装已按清单检查。保留袋子或可区分的小区域，防止本来分开的两种件在桌上混合。说明图不一定按实际比例印刷，除非厂家明确标示比例或尺寸，不能用纸上长短判定实物。

### 执行

1. **建立候选对照。** 对每种相似件写说明中的零件号、应有数量、标明的长度/形状、槽型或其他可辨特征，以及它所在的原袋标记。袋号和说明不同或没有袋号就标待核，不先替厂家发明同义词。
2. **比较不会损坏的实物特征。** 在好光线下并排看长度、直径、头部、螺纹、凹槽或销钉端形，使用说明给出的真实尺寸/比例信息。数量只能辅助校验，不能单独判定身份；两种件都各有四颗也可能混错。
3. **把确认的件分开并标编号。** 只有特征与说明一致时才把实物归入该编号的原袋或分格。对仍看不出的件保留“候选 A/B 未确认”，不能通过塞进孔里试哪颗更用力来决定。
4. **对照实际步骤再复核。** 看到某一步要求特定编号时，从对应组取一件，与说明的形状和数量再次核对；孔位、螺丝刀头和整体组装另按其流程处理。
5. **处理冲突或未知。** 实物尺寸/特征与编号不一致、混袋或印刷不清时，停止依赖该件的步骤，保留实物和包装记录，并向准确说明或供应方确认。安全关键固定要求交相应专业渠道，不用相似紧固件代替。

### 完成与停止

每种拟使用的相似件都有可追溯的实物特征、说明编号和单独存放位置，或明确“尚不能辨认”的停止状态；零件总数相等不算身份验证。无法可靠区分、出现损坏或说明指向承重/防倾倒操作时不试装、不强拧。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只比说明图片的打印长度：** 查厂家给出的真实尺寸或袋号；图未声明比例就不能当尺。
- **两种螺丝已混成一堆：** 按可辨特征和说明重新分组；无法恢复编号就保留待核。
- **袋子写 A、说明写六位数字：** 先查厂家有没有明确映射，没有就问，不自行猜 A 对应哪个编号。
- **数量刚好相等却头部不同：** 以确切特征和编号为准，不拿总数抵消类型差异。
- **看不清小件刻印：** 放大照片、补光或请可信者读回；仍看不清就不把它用于关键步骤。

### 假设、替代与副作用

仅限厂家允许的普通非结构性用品准备。地区、品牌、说明版本和零件包装不同；以准确产品文件为准。行动或视力不便时可由可信者摆放并读回，辨认过程不要求你实际拧入。防倾倒、承重、防护和设备内部零件不在本 Skill 可替代判断范围。副作用是“差不多长”的两颗紧固件先各自拿回身份证明。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary kit contains two or more screws, pins, washers or connectors that look alike while its exact guide calls for different IDs at different steps. Before insertion, **map each real type to a reliable guide part ID, or keep the uncertain group apart**. This does not select a driver tip, assemble the item, invent a replacement for a missing part or fit load-bearing or anti-tip hardware.

### Preparation and inputs

Use the exact-model parts diagram and original bag labels, after checking all delivered cartons against the packing list. Keep bags or separate compartments so distinct types do not become one pile. A printed diagram may not be full scale. Do not infer a physical length from paper unless the maker explicitly states the scale or dimensions.

### Execution

1. **Build a candidate map.** For each similar type, write the guide ID, required count, stated length or shape, recess and other distinguishing features, plus the original bag mark. If bag and guide use different codes or a bag is unmarked, leave the mapping pending rather than making up synonyms for the maker.
2. **Compare harmless physical features.** Under good light, compare length, diameter, head, thread, slot or pin end against any real dimensions or scale the guide states. Count only supports a match; it cannot establish identity alone. Two kinds with four each can still be mixed.
3. **Separate and label confirmed types.** Place a real item into its ID group in the original bag or divided tray only when its features agree with the guide. Keep “candidate A/B, unconfirmed” for indistinguishable items. Forcing them into holes is not an identification method.
4. **Recheck at the actual step.** When a later step calls for one ID, take from that group and compare the form and count with the guide once more. Hole choice, driver fit and whole assembly follow their own processes.
5. **Hold conflicts and unknowns.** If dimensions or features disagree with the ID, bags were mixed or printing is unclear, stop the step that depends on that part. Keep the piece and package record and ask the accurate guide or supplier. Specialist channels handle safety-critical fixing; a similar fastener is not a substitute.

### Success and stop

Each similar item to be used has traceable real features, guide ID and separate location, or an explicit “cannot identify yet” stop. An equal total count does not verify identities. Do not trial-fit or force a damaged or unidentifiable part, or one specified for load-bearing or anti-tip work.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only the printed drawing's length was compared:** Check maker-stated real dimensions or bag code. An unscaled picture is not a ruler.
- **Two screw types were poured into one pile:** Regroup by real features and guide details. Hold the set if IDs cannot be recovered.
- **A bag says A but the guide gives six digits:** Find an explicit maker mapping or ask; do not guess which number A means.
- **Totals match but heads differ:** Use the exact feature and ID, not a count that masks mixed types.
- **Tiny marking is unreadable:** Enlarge a photo, improve light or ask a trusted readback. Leave it out of a dependent step if still unclear.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This prepares an ordinary manufacturer-permitted non-structural kit. Region, brand, guide revision and packaging vary; the exact product file governs. A trusted person can lay out and read small pieces when sight or movement is limited; you need not turn a screw to identify it. Anti-tip, load-bearing, protective and internal-equipment parts are outside this substitute judgment. “About the same length” no longer counts as a part ID.
