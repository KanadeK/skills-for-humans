## 中文

当普通用品的全部已交付包装和准确说明已核对，却仍有零件缺少、型号不符、损坏，或说明步骤与实物对不上时加载。你要**停止依赖该件的强行组装，并向实际供应方送出能复查的事实差异与具体问题**。这里不自制/代用紧固件、不修理或强装，也不解释退换、保修、赔偿法律权利。

### 准备与输入

确认手中准确商品型号/货号及说明版本；检查是否还有未到或未开的另一箱。零件号可能有、也可能没有，依实际说明。把已拆的普通半成品放在安全稳定、不会被当成可用成品的位置，保留原袋、外箱标签和相关纸本。安全关键、承重或防倾倒件有疑问时更不能先使用成品。

### 执行

1. **把差异写成一行事实。** 记“商品完整型号／说明版本／步骤 → 应有零件号、特征与数量 → 实际看见的编号、特征与数量 → 已检查哪些包装”。若说明与实物图示冲突，记录孔位或形状的可见差别；未知处写未知，不发明一个件号。
2. **保留适量可核对资料。** 按实际渠道要求准备包装、零件和说明页的照片或文字；只拍必要范围，别在公开帖子贴序列号、订单、地址或他人资料。原包装和不符件先分开放，不让后续复查失去依据。
3. **停住受影响动作。** 不靠相似螺丝、胶、额外力气或电钻让不匹配的部位“先接上”。能否做完全独立的其他步骤只按准确说明决定；部分成品显著标“未完成／勿使用”，不让收工具等于完成组装。
4. **找真实负责的渠道。** 从订单或厂商官方支持确认由零售商、制造商还是组装服务接收这个问题。说明上述事实，明确问：“这个件/说明是否对应我的准确型号？应使用哪份正确资料或件？接下来由谁处理、何时再核对？”不同地区和商家补件规则不同，不预先声称免费或必然有货。
5. **记录回答而不提前关单。** 写联系时间、渠道、对方给的编号/答复、仍未解决的项和下次联系点。若对方只说“看看别的箱”而你已查完，就提供已查记录；若建议代用或继续强装，先索取适用于准确型号的官方确认，未确认就继续暂停。

### 完成与停止

你已把准确差异交到实际负责渠道，留下答复、案件编号或明确下一次查问点；未获确认的零件和半成品仍标未解决。对方没回复或只给模糊答复时，这个问题不算修好。补件到手或新说明出现后，再核对型号、件号和步骤才可考虑恢复普通操作；危险或专业要求仍转专业。

### 常见报错与补救

- **后来发现还有一箱没查：** 先查实际到货与安全可见的包装，更新差异，不把未检查说成厂家漏装。
- **说明没有零件号：** 用准确步骤、形状、尺寸（若厂家给出）和照片描述，诚实写“无件号”。
- **供应方说“任意同尺寸螺丝都行”：** 请求针对准确型号和功能的书面/官方说明；安全关键部位不试装。
- **客服问起型号，你只有系列名：** 回到实物标签/订单取得完整货号，保留变体后缀。
- **多次联系无人答：** 保存已发送内容和时间，按官方渠道复查或换其另一个正式入口，不把未答标成已解决。

### 假设、替代与副作用

只处理普通用品缺件或图示不符的事实沟通，不保证替换件、时间或费用，也不解释权利。IKEA 的件号长度和补件方式只适用于它的实际市场与商品；其他厂家按自己的渠道。视力、阅读或沟通不便时可请可信者读回差异或协助联络，确保回复能由你保存并理解。副作用是“少个东西”终于变成可核对的资料，而不是靠强拧把它隐藏。

## English

Load this Skill when all delivered packages and the exact guide for an ordinary item have been checked, but a part remains missing, wrong, damaged or inconsistent with the step drawing. You will **stop forcing the affected assembly and send a checkable factual difference to the actual supplier**. Do not fabricate or substitute hardware, repair or force a joint, or interpret return, warranty or compensation rights.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the full item model or article ID and guide edition, and check whether another package is still unopened or undelivered. A part may or may not have its own number; follow the real guide. Put any partial ordinary assembly in a stable place where nobody will treat it as a ready item. Keep original bags, carton labels and relevant paper. Do not use an item with an unresolved safety-critical, load-bearing or anti-tip part.

### Execution

1. **Write the discrepancy as one fact row.** Record “complete item ID / guide version / step → expected part ID, feature and count → actually seen ID, feature and count → packages checked.” For a drawing/item conflict, describe visible hole or shape differences. Mark unknown fields unknown rather than inventing a part number.
2. **Preserve only useful evidence.** Prepare the necessary carton, part and guide-page photograph or text under the real channel. Keep serials, orders, addresses and others' information out of public posts. Hold original packaging and mismatched parts separately so the supplier can still inspect the facts.
3. **Pause the affected work.** Do not make a lookalike screw, glue, extra force or power drill “make it fit.” The exact guide decides whether a fully independent step can continue. Mark a partial item “unfinished / do not use”; putting tools away does not finish it.
4. **Use the responsible channel.** From the order or official maker support, confirm whether retailer, manufacturer or assembly service receives the problem. Give the fact row and ask: “Does this part or guide match my exact model? Which correct part or document applies? Who follows up, and when?” Replacement rules vary by provider and region; do not promise free stock or a specific date.
5. **Record the answer without closing early.** Note contact time, channel, reference or response, still-open items and next check. If told only to inspect another box when all have been checked, share that record. If someone suggests a substitute or forced work, request model-specific official confirmation first and stay paused without it.

### Success and stop

The actual responsible channel has a precise discrepancy and you have a reply, case reference or specific next check. Unconfirmed parts and the partial item remain unresolved. Silence or a vague response is not a repair. When a replacement or revised guide arrives, verify model, part ID and step again before considering ordinary continuation; specialist or hazardous work still exits to qualified help.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Another package turns out unsearched:** Check its arrival and safely visible contents, then update the difference. An unchecked carton is not proof of a packing error.
- **The guide has no part ID:** Use exact step, form, maker-stated dimensions if any and a photo, explicitly saying “no part ID shown.”
- **The supplier says any same-size screw will do:** Ask for official model- and function-specific confirmation. Do not trial-fit a safety-critical part.
- **Support asks for a model, but you only have a family name:** Read the full item label or order ID, including variant suffix.
- **Several contacts get no answer:** Keep sent text and times, revisit or use another official channel. No answer stays unresolved.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is factual contact about ordinary item parts or diagrams, not a guarantee of a replacement, timing, price or legal right. IKEA part-number lengths and replacement routes apply only to its real products and markets; other makers use their own process. A trusted person can read the discrepancy back or help contact support when reading, sight or communication is difficult. Keep a reply you can understand. “Something's missing” becomes a checkable record instead of a hidden forced joint.
