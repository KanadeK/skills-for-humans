## 中文

当一顿普通家庭餐已经计划好、**尚未开火**，用餐人数却临时增加或减少时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一个按实际食材、设备、时间和保存条件能完成的新版餐食安排，或诚实告知原计划做不到。它不替你推导每道菜的配方比例，也不处理已经在锅里发生的失败。

### 准备与输入

确认原定人数、目前**确实确认**的人数、开饭时间、每道菜原计划做几份，以及哪些是整顿饭的必要部分。核对现有食材和安全保存史、锅/烤盘/灶眼容量、你能同时照看的任务数、普通饮食偏好和已明确的限制。若人数仍未确定，分别记录已确认与可能增加的人；不要因为群里一条“也许来”就承诺一锅实际不存在的食物。高处拿取、长时间站立或阅读小字困难时，用可达食材、放大说明或可信协助，不把身体负担当成免费的厨房设备。

### 执行

1. **先写变化，不马上把所有数字乘一遍。** 从原计划到现在，多了或少了几位？变动发生在购物之后、备料之前，还是食品已经拆包但还没加热？已加热或安全史不明的食品不在本 Skill 里靠“重算”补救。
2. **找出一顿饭必须保留什么。** 写下主线食物和不可缺的配搭，另列可以省略或不必每人都做一整份的配菜。增加人数时先判断现有可安全使用的食材是否足以完成更多份；不足就更换为能完成的简单组合、减去非必要菜，或调整开饭承诺，不把每道菜都强行放大。人数减少时选择缩小未开工的量，或明确把多做部分当作有真实用途且能安全保存的下一餐。
3. **仅对确定要改变的配方重算。** 用该配方原始产量和实际目标份数逐项核对食材；敏感配方、调味和膨发比例需按可靠配方说明处理，不把所有数值盲乘。具体比例调整可使用专门的份量/配方任务；这里要得到整顿饭的可执行决定。
4. **让器具、时间和安全检查也跟上。** 核对锅、烤盘、灶眼、操作空间及需专注看守的步骤能否容纳新版份量。较大或更密的批量不能只沿用原计时宣布熟了；对需要熟度证据的食品，用当地适用的要求和可靠温度计检查。若器具或时间不够，分批会不会拖过开饭时间？不成立就缩小菜单、改成可完成方案或明确延后，不在热锅上临时解决。
5. **处理减少人数后的剩余计划。** 尚未烹调的安全食材按各自标签继续保存；若决定多做一餐，先确认干净容器、可靠冷藏空间及适用的冷却安排。没有去向就少做，而不是把食品在台面上等下一位食客。
6. **写下并传达新版安排。** 最多用一张短表写“确认人数—每道菜实际做几份—缺什么/删什么—所用器具与顺序—预计开饭时间—剩余食品去向”。把会改变供餐时间或内容的部分告诉相关人；完成标准是计划清楚并可执行，不要求别人对变动表示满意。

### 完成条件

你有一份能用当前食材、器具、注意力与时间完成的修订版餐食安排；份量和真实人数对应，需彻底烹调的食品仍有适用的熟度核对，计划留下的食物有可靠去向。若这些条件不能同时成立，你已缩小/更换菜单或明确延后，而不是只在纸上把份数改大。

### 常见报错与补救

- **多一人就把盐、膨发料和烹调时间一律倍增：** 回到具体配方及设备说明，重新核对敏感步骤；时间由实际厚度、数量和熟度证据决定，不按人数直接乘。
- **锅能装下食材，却无法均匀处理或安全检查：** 在开工前分批、改用合适设备或缩小餐食；不要用更大火力假装锅变宽了。
- **少了人但仍计划全量做完：** 先问多出食物是否有明确下一餐与冷却/储存条件；没有就减少未做的量。
- **新来的人数或普通饮食要求还没确认：** 明确需要核实的事实，先不承诺不受约束的额外份量；严重过敏或医疗饮食不在此临时设计。
- **变动在开火后才出现：** 停止使用这份开工前计划；按实际烹调状态和食品安全条件处理，不把已发生问题写回成“尚未开始”。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这适用于普通家庭餐，份量没有全球统一的“每人几克”。家庭食量、文化、可用器具和用餐时间由你实际确认。阅读、站立、握持或多人协调不便时，可选择少道菜、可及器具和明确协助；不要求一个人同时看守更多热锅。冷却和剩菜具体安全要求以食品及当地权威为准。副作用可能是删掉一道菜或改变开饭时间；那是更新过的现实，不是计划失败的伪装。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary home meal has been planned, **cooking has not begun**, and the diner count suddenly rises or falls. Produce a revised meal that works with real food, equipment, time, and storage—or say clearly that the original promise cannot be met. This does not calculate every recipe ratio or undo a failure already in the pan.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm the original number, the people **actually confirmed** now, serving time, planned yield of each dish, and the parts essential to the meal. Check usable food with a known safe history, pan or tray size, burners, how many tasks you can safely watch, ordinary preferences, and confirmed restrictions. If the count is still changing, separate confirmed diners from possible additions. A chat message saying “maybe” does not create another pan of food. If reaching, standing, or reading small text is difficult, use accessible ingredients, enlarged directions, or trusted help instead of treating your body as free kitchen equipment.

### Execution

1. **Write the change before multiplying numbers.** How many more or fewer diners than planned? Did this happen after shopping, before preparation, or after a package was opened but before heat? Already heated food or food with uncertain safety history cannot be fixed by recalculating this plan.
2. **Keep the meal's essential job.** Name the main food and any indispensable parts; separate optional sides or dishes that do not need a full serving per person. For more diners, check whether food known to be usable can really support more portions. If not, choose a simpler complete combination, remove optional dishes, or revise the serving commitment rather than enlarging everything. For fewer diners, reduce what has not been cooked, or deliberately plan an extra meal with a real future use and safe storage.
3. **Recalculate only recipes you will actually change.** Use each recipe's original yield and the target portions to check ingredients. Sensitive formulas, seasoning, and leavening need the recipe's reliable directions; do not blindly multiply every value. Detailed recipe scaling belongs to the dedicated portion and recipe task. Here you decide the whole meal.
4. **Check equipment, time, and doneness evidence too.** Will pans, trays, burners, workspace, and attention handle the changed batch? A larger or denser batch cannot be declared done by reusing the old clock alone. Where doneness needs proof, use applicable local rules and a reliable food thermometer. If cooking in batches would exceed the serving time, reduce the menu, choose a workable version, or tell people it will be later. Do not attempt to solve this on a hot pan.
5. **Plan what happens when fewer diners leave extra food.** Keep uncooked safe ingredients under their own labels. If you choose to cook for another meal, confirm clean containers, reliable cold space, and an applicable cooling route first. Without a destination, make less instead of leaving food on a counter for an absent diner.
6. **Write and communicate the revised version.** In one small table note “confirmed people—portions per dish—what is missing or removed—equipment and order—serving time—destination for extra food.” Tell relevant people about a material change in timing or food. Success means the plan is clear and executable; it does not require everyone to be pleased with the change.

### Success

You have a revised meal that the current food, equipment, attention, and time can support. Portions match the real diner count, food that must be thoroughly cooked still has an appropriate doneness check, and planned extras have reliable storage. If these conditions do not fit, you have simplified or changed the meal or stated a later serving time. A larger number written on paper is not more dinner.

### Common errors and recovery

- **One more person makes salt, leavening, and cooking time double automatically:** Recheck the particular recipe and equipment directions. Time follows actual size, quantity, and doneness evidence, not a diner multiplier.
- **The pan contains the food but cannot process or check it evenly:** Batch it before starting, choose suitable equipment, or reduce the meal. More heat does not make the pan wider.
- **Fewer diners but you still plan the full recipe:** Check whether the extra has a real next meal and safe cooling and storage. Otherwise reduce the uncooked amount.
- **The new count or ordinary restrictions are unconfirmed:** Mark what needs confirmation rather than promising unconstrained extra portions. Do not improvise severe-allergy or medical-diet plans here.
- **The change arrives after heat has started:** Stop using this pre-cooking plan. Respond to the real cooking state and food-safety conditions instead of rewriting history as “not started.”

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This is an ordinary home meal; there is no global grams-per-person rule. Use the household's actual portions, culture, equipment, and time. If reading, standing, gripping, or coordinating people is difficult, use fewer dishes, accessible tools, and confirmed help. One person need not supervise more hot pans at once. Follow the food and local authority for specific cooling and leftover limits. You may remove a dish or change serving time; that is an honest new plan.
