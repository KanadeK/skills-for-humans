## 中文

当你在沙发、布椅或其他普通纺织家具上发现**小面积、来源已知的日常污点**，想局部处理却不知道能否用水时，用这份 Skill 找准确护理标签和制造商说明，决定“可试水基点清 / 只能按另一路线 / 暂停求助”。它是处理资格判断，不靠面料看起来像棉布就直接浇水。体液、霉斑、危险化学品、虫害、大面积浸透或带电家具进水退出本篇。

### 准备与输入

找家具底部、坐垫附近或产品资料里的完整型号与清洁代码；可拆布套还要单独读**这块布套**的标签。看代码 W、W-S/WS、S、X 或厂家自定说明，查该厂对它的解释，别把别家网页的字母定义直接套过来。记录污点来源、面积、是否已经渗进填充、有没有变色/破损。若不确定旧清洁剂是否残留，先暂停加新产品。

### 执行

1. **先排除危险与不可达状态。** 污液碰到电动机构/插头、需要移开沉重坐垫才可清洁、布料裂开或垫芯湿透时停，按家具厂家/专业指导；不要在通电或带电可能进水的家具上做湿清洁。
2. **按本件真实代码选路径。** 说明明确允许水基局部处理（例如某些 W、W-S/WS）且污点仍局限表面时，可以准备相容产品的小处试验；S 等要求无水方法的家具不拿肥皂水试，X 或明确“仅吸尘”者不试液体，咨询厂家/专业护理。无标签或代码与布套材料不一致时，先停。
3. **核对“可拆洗”的许可。** 只有布套制造商明确准许拆卸、机洗/手洗和干燥方式时，才另按该布套标签处理。拉链看得见不等于整套允许拆洗；固定罩强拆会让复装与保修变复杂。
4. **为水基路线做隐藏小处测试。** 若本件明确许可、拟用产品同时适用，按产品说明在不显眼处轻试，不把液体浸进填充；等到能判断颜色、纹理与原触感变化后再决定是否点清显眼处。测试出掉色、起毛、边缘水圈或变硬立即停。
5. **给家具当前去向。** 记录可家庭水基点清、仅按厂家明确的非水/吸尘方法、或等待厂家/专业处理；避免有人在仍湿、尚未确认的区域坐下或把污点压深。

### 成功条件、常见问题与补救

你已依据这件家具/布套的真实标签和一次适用的隐藏测试作出处理路线或停止决定；没有把 S/X 面料当 W 处理，也没有把填充层“看不见”当成干透证明。点清本身尚未发生。

- **标签只见一个字母，品牌未知：** 查对应厂家说明再选方法，不从搜索结果中随机借一个解释。
- **坐垫有拉链但说明不允许拆洗：** 不强拆，按固定罩护理或请专业处理。
- **隐藏测试出现水圈：** 停止显眼处湿清洁，记录产品和变化咨询厂家。
- **污点已经渗进内垫：** 暂停普通表面点清，不能保证内部干燥或深层除害。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

只判断普通小面积、已知日常污点的护理资格，不教溶剂清洁、深层蒸汽、杀虫或真菌处理。家具涂层、纤维、靠垫与电动结构不同；标签和型号比“看起来一样”可靠。标签在家具下方看不到时可安全拍照放大或请可信的人帮助，不为找标签硬翻重家具。暂时停用座位可能影响安排，但保留材料状态比盲清更可回滚。

## English

Use this Skill for a **small known everyday spot** on a fabric sofa, chair, or similar furniture when you want to treat it but do not know if water is allowed. Find the item's real care tag and maker directions and choose “eligible for a water-based test,” “another documented route,” or “hold for advice.” This decides treatment eligibility; cotton-like appearance is not permission to pour water. Bodily fluids, mold, hazardous chemicals, pests, widespread saturation, and water reaching powered furniture leave this Skill.

### Preparation and inputs

Find the full model and cleaning code under furniture, near cushions, or in product information. A removable cover has **its own** care label too. Note W, W-S/WS, S, X, or maker-specific words and verify that maker's definition rather than borrowing another company's code table. Record source, area, likely penetration into padding, and any dye or fabric damage. Hold new product if old cleaner residue is unknown.

### Execution

1. **Exclude hazards and inaccessible conditions.** Stop for liquid near a powered mechanism or plug, a heavy cushion that must be lifted unsafely, torn fabric, or soaked padding. Use maker or professional guidance, not wet cleaning on an energised or potentially wet-powered chair.
2. **Route by this item's actual code.** Where this tag explicitly allows water-based spot care, as with some W or W-S/WS upholstery, and the mark is still superficial, consider one compatible small test. Do not test soap water on S material requiring a water-free route. Do not apply liquid to X or vacuum-only covers; ask the maker or a professional. Stop for a missing tag or code that conflicts with the cover material.
3. **Verify any removable-cover permission.** Remove, machine wash, hand wash, and dry a cover only if its maker explicitly allows each step. A visible zip does not mean a whole cover may be laundered; forcing a fixed cover can create reassembly problems.
4. **Hidden-test a water-based route.** Only if both item and proposed product permit it, trial as directed in an inconspicuous patch without wetting the filling. Wait until colour, texture, and original feel can be judged before treating the visible mark. Stop for dye loss, fuzz, a water ring, or stiffening.
5. **Give the furniture a current destination.** Record home water-based spot care, maker-supported nonwater or vacuum route, or a hold for maker or professional care. Keep people from sitting on a still-wet or uncertain spot and pressing the mark deeper.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have a route or a stop based on the actual furniture or cover tag and, where suitable, one hidden test. S or X fabric did not receive W treatment, and unseen padding was not pronounced dry. The visible spot treatment still lies ahead.

- **One code letter is visible but maker unknown:** Find that maker's directions before selecting a product; do not borrow a random web definition.
- **The cushion has a zip but washing is not permitted:** Do not force it apart; use fixed-cover care or professional help.
- **A water ring forms in the hidden test:** Stop visible wet cleaning and keep product and change details for the maker.
- **The spot reached the inner cushion:** Pause ordinary surface care; it cannot guarantee inner dryness or deep cleaning.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This checks ordinary small known spots, not solvent cleaning, steam extraction, pests, or fungal treatment. Finish, fibres, cushions, and powered components differ; label and model beat visual similarity. Photograph a hidden tag or ask trusted help if it is hard to see. Do not flip heavy furniture to locate it. Temporarily holding a seat may inconvenience you, but keeps a blind treatment from worsening it.
