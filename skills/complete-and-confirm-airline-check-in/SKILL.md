## 中文

当你已订好普通航班、进入该航司允许的值机时段，需要**选一个这次真实可用的渠道并确认值机到底处于什么状态**时加载。网上/app、机场自助机和柜台只是完成同一件事的不同入口。值机完成不等于行李已托运、安检已通过或人已到登机口；证件、签证和入境资格也不在这里判断。

### 准备与输入

核对出发机场当地日期、航班号、实际承运航司、预订编号或航司要求的检索信息，以及本次适用的值机截止。准备自己合法持有并由航司实际要求查看的旅行材料；不要把密码、一次性验证码或完整支付信息交给非官方人员、链接或截图。若与他人同行，先确认每名旅客/每段航班是否都需要单独完成状态确认。

### 执行

1. **选本航班接受的入口。** 从实际承运航司官网、App 或机场正式标识查看能否网上/手机值机、机场自助值机或使用柜台，以及开放时段。需现场核验、线上入口明确拒绝、没有可用设备或需要人工协助时，选择航司认可的柜台/协助路径，不反复换假冒网站试运气。
2. **在自己控制的官方界面核对行程。** 用航司要求的预订信息打开这次航班，逐项看旅客姓名、出发机场和日期、航班号与目的地是否对应。发现不一致时不要勾选“正确”或擅改证件事实；到官方柜台/客服确认。可选座位或其他附加服务若收费，先看清是否真的需要再决定，不把购买当作值机前置条件。
3. **按该渠道完成必要步骤。** 按航司当前提示确认旅客、联系信息与所需材料；需要现场查验就按指示到柜台。自助机、App 的按钮和可办理项目因航司/机场而异，不绕过拒绝提示，也不代替工作人员判断旅行资格。提交后等待最终状态，不把“页面加载完成”当值机完成。
4. **验证可观察的结果。** 在官方行程中确认值机成功状态，并找到这名旅客、这段航班对应的登机牌或航司明确的领取方式；能打印或保存的按航司许可处理，确保到机场仍可读取。若系统写“已值机，但需柜台领取登机牌/核对文件”，把它记成**待柜台处理**，不是可直接过安检的完整状态。同行人和后续航段逐一核对。
5. **把未完成的后续节点单列。** 如需托运行李，找本次航司正式托运柜台并按其截止办理；没有托运行李且持有效登机牌，也仍须遵守机场安检与登机口流程。记下本次值机结果、来源/时间、下一步与截止点。登机口和航班状态临近起飞还要看官方更新。

### 完成与停止

你已通过航司认可渠道取得**可核对的值机确认及登机牌/明确领取方式**，或拿到准确的柜台处理指令并知道需在何时前办理，本轮完成。后者是“待处理”，不能在记录里写成“已可登机”。若身份、预订或材料被系统拒绝，停止猜填，向实际承运航司求证；本 Skill 不裁决签证、证件或法律资格，也不保证航司一定办理成功。

### 常见报错与补救

- **“找不到预订”：** 检查实际承运航司、出发日期、预订编号来源及姓名输入，仍不符就问航司，不向陌生代办上传证件。
- **App 写自动值机，页面没登机牌：** 在官方航班详情核实每人/每段状态；未给出可用登机牌时按航司提示到柜台/自助机，不只凭通知推断完成。
- **系统提示必须现场核验：** 保留提示，按本航司柜台开放和截止信息到正式地点；不要反复提交猜测材料。
- **自助机失灵或手机没电：** 查看本机场本航司允许的其他正式入口或纸质登机牌方式，考虑实际排队时间。
- **线上值机成功但要托运行李：** 保留行李交接与凭证作为独立未完成事项，不把电子登机牌当行李收据。

### 假设、替代与副作用

值机窗口、可用入口、材料核验、登机牌格式和柜台位置随航司、机场、航线与日期改变。这里不处理证件/入境资格，也不要求把本人账号交给协助者。视觉、听觉、手机或阅读不便时可使用航司实际提供的可及界面、自助设备辅助功能或柜台人员；需要更多时间就按真实截止安排。副作用是你可能获得一张登机牌，也可能诚实得到“此行需找柜台”的待办。

## English

Load this Skill after booking an ordinary flight and entering the operating airline's check-in window, when you need to **choose a route actually available for this flight and verify what check-in has really achieved**. Web or app, airport kiosk and staffed desk are possible routes to the same result. Check-in is not checked baggage, cleared security or arrival at the gate. This Skill does not decide document, visa or entry eligibility.

### Preparation and inputs

Check the departure airport's local date, flight number, operating airline, booking reference or other lookup information it requires, and this flight's actual check-in cutoff. Have the travel materials you lawfully hold and the airline actually asks to see. Do not give passwords, one-time codes or full payment details to unofficial people, links or screenshots. For a party or multi-leg trip, check whether each traveller and leg needs its own confirmed state.

### Execution

1. **Choose an accepted channel for this flight.** On the operating airline's official site or app or the airport's official signs, see whether web/mobile check-in, a kiosk or a staffed desk is offered and when. If document review is required, online check-in explicitly refuses, your device is unavailable or you need human help, use the airline's accepted desk or assistance route instead of cycling through unfamiliar websites.
2. **Check the itinerary in a channel you control.** Open this flight with the details the airline requests. Compare passenger name, departure airport and date, flight number and destination. If anything differs, do not tick “correct” or invent a document fact; clarify with the official desk or support. An optional seat or add-on may cost money; check whether it is needed before accepting a charge. Buying one is not assumed to be part of check-in.
3. **Complete the required steps for that channel.** Follow current prompts for traveller, contact and materials. If it asks for an in-person check, go to the stated desk. Kiosk and app controls differ by airline and airport. Do not bypass a refusal or decide travel eligibility for staff. Wait for a final state after submission; a page loading is not confirmation.
4. **Verify an observable result.** In the official itinerary, confirm the checked-in status and find a boarding pass or a clear collection method for this passenger and flight. Print or save it as allowed and make sure you can read it at the airport. If the system says “checked in, but collect the pass or verify documents at a desk,” record **desk action pending**, not “ready for security.” Check each traveller and later leg separately.
5. **List unfinished later stages.** For a checked bag, use the airline's actual bag-drop place and deadline; that remains separate. Even with no checked bag and a valid pass, follow airport security and gate procedures. Note check-in result, source and time, next action and cutoff. Recheck flight and gate status nearer departure through official information.

### Success and stop

This round is done when an airline-approved channel shows a **verifiable check-in state and boarding pass or explicit collection method**, or gives you a precise staffed-desk action with its deadline. The latter is still pending and cannot be recorded as “ready to board.” If the system rejects identity, booking or materials, stop guessing and ask the operating airline. This Skill does not rule on document or entry rights or guarantee check-in approval.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Booking not found:** Recheck operating carrier, departure date, booking reference source and name entry. Ask the airline if it still fails; do not upload documents to a stranger offering to fix it.
- **App says automatic check-in but shows no pass:** Verify each traveller and leg in official flight details. Follow the airline's kiosk or desk instruction if no usable pass exists.
- **System requires an in-person check:** Keep the notice and go to the real airline desk before its applicable close time; do not resubmit guessed details.
- **Kiosk fails or phone is flat:** Use another airline-approved entrance or paper-pass method available at that airport, allowing for its actual queue.
- **Online check-in succeeded but you have a checked bag:** Keep bag handoff and receipt as a separate unfinished task. A digital boarding pass is not a bag receipt.

### Assumptions, alternatives and side effects

Windows, channels, document checks, pass formats and desk locations vary by airline, airport, route and date. This does not judge document or entry eligibility or require you to hand account control to a helper. If sight, hearing, phone access or reading differs, use the airline's actual accessible site, kiosk aids or staffed desk, and plan against its real cutoff. You may get a boarding pass or an honest “see an agent” task.
