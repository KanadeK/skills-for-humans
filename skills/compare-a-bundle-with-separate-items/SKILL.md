## 中文

当一个普通商品套装把**不同物品**合在一个价格里，而你想知道它是否比按清单逐件买便宜时，加载这份 Skill。目标是为**完全相同的一份清单**比较总应付额；不能把整套价格除以物品件数，假装每件都有相同单价。你是否需要这些物品、它们是否满足用途，应在开始前决定。

### 准备与输入

写出你本来就要买的每件商品及数量，例如一本笔记本和两支指定规格的笔。取得套装的实际内容、每项规格与数量、套装现价，以及同一时点可买到的对应单件现价。确认优惠资格、有效时间、是否能单买，和有没有必须支付但尚未计入的费用。不同品牌或款式只有在你的实际用途允许时才视为对应物；别用商品照片猜套装内容。

### 执行步骤

1. **把清单当成锁定的输入。** 先列“物品—规格—数量”，不要因为套装附送一件陌生物品就把它补进原需求。若清单由别人决定，你无权替换其必需规格时先确认。
2. **逐项核对套装。** 对照实物或明确的商家明细，确认每件是什么、多少、是否与清单要求相同。缺一件、数量不对、期限或尺寸不适用，就不能直接把两组价格排名；需要另买补足时，把补买项及其价格列入套装方案。
3. **算单买整份清单。** 把每件对应商品的**当前适用总价 × 所需件数**相加。若单件也有条件优惠，只用这次符合条件的价格。整份清单需要多少，就算多少；不要只比较套装中最显眼的一个商品。
4. **算套装完成同一清单的总价。** 套装价加上缺项的可确认补买价与不可避免的费用。例：清单是一本笔记本、两支笔；同款单买分别为 8 和每支 2，共 12。套装确实包含这三件、现价 10，则在相同取得条件下少付 2。套装若只有一支笔，须再加一支的现价，不能仍报“省 2”。
5. **只比较相同结果。** 记录两方案的物品与数量、实际应付总额、差额。套装多出未需要的东西时，记录额外物品并交由购买量判断；不把它凭空定价为节省。跨店或配送费用不同且影响结果时，再核对整篮最终应付额。

### 完成与停止

你能用一张清单证明“套装方案”和“单买方案”取得了同样的所需物品与数量，并说出各自当前总应付额，就完成了。套装内容、关键规格、单买价或补买费用无法确认时，输出“暂不可比”，不要用广告写的“省 X”代替计算。

### 常见报错与补救

- **把套装价格平均到每件：** 不同物品没有共同的“每件价值”；回到整份清单的总额。
- **套装缺一件仍说便宜：** 加上补齐这件的可确认费用；补不到则这套方案不满足清单。
- **赠品并非原需求：** 记作额外物品，不从应付价格里扣掉假想价值。你若确实想改清单，先完成需求与数量判断。
- **套装细节藏在小字或线上图片里：** 放大可读明细、查看实物或请店员说明；确认不了内容就停止该套方案。
- **优惠只对部分款式生效：** 核对确切型号和时间，按这次真正可用的价格重算。

### 假设、替代与副作用

这里假设清单与商品适用性已确定，且能取得套装和单件的当前信息。地区、商家、货币、费用和组合方式没有统一规则；只在同一货币和可比取得条件下排名。阅读、听力、站立或拿取不方便时，使用可读的商品清单、照片放大、纸笔、计算器或店员协助；关键明细缺失就不将宣传语当作价格证据。副作用是你可能要把赠品留在广告里，而不是带进家门。

## English

Load this Skill when a bundle puts **different ordinary items** under one price and you want to know whether it costs less than buying your list item by item. Compare the total amount payable for **the exact same list**. Dividing a mixed bundle price by its item count does not give each item a meaningful unit price. Decide what you need and whether each item suits its purpose before starting.

### Preparation and inputs

Write down every item and quantity you already need, such as one notebook and two pens of a required specification. Get the bundle's actual contents, each item's specification and count, the current bundle price, and current prices for matching individual items. Check offer eligibility, dates, whether the items can be bought separately, and any unavoidable charges not yet included. Treat a different brand or variant as equivalent only if your use allows it. A product photo is not a complete contents list.

### Execution steps

1. **Lock the list.** Record item, specification, and quantity before reading the bundle claim. An unfamiliar bonus item does not become a pre-existing need. If someone else set a non-negotiable specification, confirm before replacing it.
2. **Check every bundle component.** Use the actual pack or a clear seller list to confirm what and how many you get. If something is missing, the quantity differs, or a size or date does not work, the prices are not directly comparable. If you can buy the missing item, list its confirmed price in the bundle route.
3. **Price the separate list.** Add each matching item's **current applicable total price × the count needed**. Use a conditional sale price only if this purchase qualifies. Price the whole list, rather than the one item most prominent in the advertisement.
4. **Price the bundle route for that same list.** Add the bundle price, any missing-item purchase, and unavoidable charges. For example, the list is one notebook and two pens. Separately, the notebook costs 8 and each pen 2, so the total is 12. If a bundle contains those exact three items for 10, it costs 2 less under the same purchase conditions. If it contains only one pen, add the current price of another; do not keep reporting a saving of 2.
5. **Compare only equal results.** Note the contents, counts, total payable, and difference for both routes. If the bundle adds something you did not need, record it as an extra and decide on quantity separately; do not assign it an imaginary saving. If stores or delivery charges differ enough to matter, check the final basket totals.

### Success and stop

You can show from one list that the bundle and separate routes deliver the same needed items and quantities, then state each current total payable amount. If bundle contents, an essential specification, separate prices, or a top-up cost cannot be confirmed, output “not comparable yet.” An advertised “save X” is not a substitute for the matching calculation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Averaging the bundle price across items:** Different goods have no shared per-item value. Return to the total for the fixed list.
- **Calling an incomplete bundle cheaper:** Add the confirmed cost of the missing item. If it cannot be obtained, the bundle route does not satisfy the list.
- **Treating a gift as a need:** Mark it as extra rather than subtracting a guessed value from the price. Revisit the needs and quantity decision if you truly want to change the list.
- **Details hide in small print or photos:** Enlarge a readable contents list, inspect the pack, or ask staff. Stop that route if its contents remain uncertain.
- **Only some variants qualify:** Check the exact model and offer time, then recalculate using the price available to you now.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This assumes the list and item suitability are settled, and current bundle and individual prices can be obtained. Sellers, currencies, fees, and bundle rules vary by region. Rank routes only in the same currency and under comparable acquisition conditions. If reading, hearing, standing, or reaching is difficult, use an accessible item list, enlarged photo, paper, calculator, or staff assistance. Missing contents are not evidence that the advertised saving is real. A side effect is leaving the bonus item in the advertisement instead of bringing it home.
