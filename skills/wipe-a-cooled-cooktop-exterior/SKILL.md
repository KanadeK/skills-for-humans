## 中文

当你用完一台**运行正常的普通灶台**，其可接近的外表面有少量溢出物、油点或食物屑，想在下一次使用前恢复日常整洁时，用这份 Skill。目标是按准确机型与表面材质把可轻擦污物去掉、擦干，不触碰设备内部。玻璃、搪瓷、不锈钢和特殊涂层不共享一套刮刀或清洁剂许可；外观擦亮也不证明燃气/电气系统已被检查。

### 准备与输入

确认所有烹饪控件处于关闭位置，并按**准确机型说明**等待表面完全冷却；只看控件关闭不等于表面不烫。查型号、外表面材质、制造商允许的清洁品和软布、控制面板与缝隙避水要求。准备小量厂家/产品标签允许的清洁用品、干净柔软的湿布和干布、可接住松散碎屑的容器。移开松散锅具，保持手边与电线区域清楚；不拆旋钮、炉架、燃烧器、点火器或面板。

型号/材质不明、说明不清、表面裂开、控制异常或闻到不寻常燃气味时，停止普通擦拭，按制造商与当地专业/应急说明处理。本文不试点火、不拆设备、不调燃气阀、不维修电路。

### 执行

1. **把“关”和“冷”都确认。** 按机型把控件关闭，使用设备提供的冷却指示或制造商规定的等待/检查方式确认可清洁；不靠手掌、手背试温。有人仍在烹调或灶台仍热时先等，不把湿布放上去制造蒸汽。
2. **划定本次可擦范围。** 只处理说明书允许接触的外表面，识别玻璃区、金属/搪瓷区、控制标识、缝隙和排气/点火开口。食物屑可先用柔软工具移走，避免推入孔隙。零件下面、缝里或电气/燃气开口的污物若需拆开才可到达，就停在这条边界。
3. **先试一小处再擦。** 按准确机型和清洁品标签选择相容方法，用小量产品在允许处的软布或表面试一处；观察有没有划痕、褪色或标识被擦掉。制造商未确认前不用钢丝球、尖锐刮刀、研磨粉或化学品混合。不同玻璃涂层对刮刀的规则可能正相反，不凭别家教程下刀。
4. **小范围轻擦并去残留。** 在可接近区域用软湿布轻擦普通油点与溢出物，按材质允许的方向与力度处理，不让液体流入控制面板、缝隙或开口。按清洁品说明用洁净湿布去掉残留，再用干布擦干；控制标识保持可读。需要用力刮、需要浸泡设备或越擦越黏时停止，查该机型的顽固污物做法。
5. **收尾再决定能否正常使用。** 看可擦处没有普通污物、清洁品残留或积水，软布和附近区域已收好。只在设备本来运行正常、控制与开口未进水、表面按说明干燥时回到正常使用；若有裂纹、异常气味、控件失灵或液体进入内部的疑虑，维持停用并联系准确设备的服务渠道，不用一次擦拭通过“自检”。

### 成功条件

灶台已停止使用并按机型确认冷却，可接近外表面的普通污物已按材质许可轻擦、去残留并干燥，没有拆件、刮坏涂层或让液体进入开口。若污物超出轻擦范围、设备异常或说明不明，明确停下并查机型/专业处理也完成判断。

### 常见报错与补救

- **控件关了就立刻用手摸灶面：** 别用皮肤试温；等设备的冷却条件满足再开始。
- **照别家视频用刮刀处理玻璃：** 停下查自己的型号；某些涂层可能被刮刀损伤。
- **清洁剂直接喷进控制区或燃烧器缝：** 立即停止继续喷洒，按机型说明处理进液；不拆开设备“擦干里面”。
- **表面有焦硬块，软布不起作用：** 停止加力，查原厂顽固污物说明；这不是把设备当锅底磨的任务。
- **洗完仍有清洁剂膜或水迹：** 在允许范围内用洁净湿布按产品标签复擦、干布收尾；仍不明就不要恢复使用。
- **出现异味、裂纹或控件异常：** 停用并按当地及制造商的专业/应急指引处理，外表面的洁净不能覆盖设备故障。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只处理正常设备的**可接近外表面**及普通日常污物，准确型号与清洁用品标签是唯一可依赖的操作边界。不同品牌玻璃涂层、钢面和燃气/电磁结构不共享拆件、刮刀或液体许可。站立或手部操作不便时等真正冷却后用可及的稳定姿势、小范围动作或可信协助；不能安全接近就停。轻擦后留下的焦结可以另查机型专法，不要求在这次清洁中冒损坏风险。

## English

Use this Skill after a **normally working ordinary cooktop** has cooled and accessible outside surfaces have light spills, grease spots, or food bits. Restore everyday cleanliness before the next use under the exact model and surface rules. The result is ordinary soil gently removed and the surface dried, with no entry into the machine. Glass, enamel, steel, and special coatings do not share one scraper or cleaner permission. A shiny outside is not an inspection of gas or electrical systems.

### Preparation and inputs

Confirm every cooking control is off and wait for the surface to cool fully under the **exact model instructions**. Off does not mean touch-safe. Find the model, exterior material, approved product and soft-cloth route, and any keep-dry limits around controls and seams. Have a small amount of a maker- and product-labelled compatible cleaner, clean soft damp and dry cloths, and a place for loose crumbs. Move loose cookware away and clear your hand and cord path. Do not remove knobs, grates, burners, igniters, or panels.

Stop ordinary wiping when the model or material is unknown, instructions are missing, glass is cracked, controls behave abnormally, or you smell unusual gas. Follow the maker and local professional or emergency direction. This Skill does not test ignition, dismantle the device, adjust a gas valve, or repair wiring.

### Execution

1. **Establish both off and cool.** Set controls off as the model directs. Use its cooling indicator or maker-prescribed wait/check to know cleaning may start; do not test with palm or skin. If someone is cooking or the surface is still hot, wait rather than making steam with a wet cloth.
2. **Mark the accessible area.** Work only on exterior parts the manual permits you to touch. Identify glass, metal or enamel, printed controls, seams, and vent or ignition openings. Lift loose food with a soft tool without pushing it into a gap. Stop if the soil is only reachable by removing parts or entering electrical or gas spaces.
3. **Trial a small compatible area.** Under the model manual and product label, test a little suitable cleaner with a soft cloth or where application is permitted. Check for scratches, fading, or lost control markings. Without maker permission, do not use steel wool, a sharp scraper, abrasive powder, or mixed chemicals. Glass coatings can have opposite scraper rules; a tutorial for another model is not permission for yours.
4. **Wipe small areas and remove residue.** Gently wipe ordinary grease and spills on accessible areas with a soft damp cloth and the material-approved direction and pressure. Keep liquid out of controls, seams, and openings. Use clean damp cloth as the product directs to remove cleaner, then a dry cloth to finish. Keep markings legible. Stop for scraping force, equipment soaking, or soil that becomes stickier, then check the model's stubborn-residue method.
5. **Close and decide whether normal use remains possible.** Check that ordinary soil, cleaner film, and pooled water are gone from the area you handled, and clear cloths and nearby space. Return to normal use only when the device was already working, nothing entered controls or openings, and surfaces have dried under its instructions. Hold it out of use for cracks, unusual smell, failed controls, or concern about liquid inside, and contact the correct service channel; wiping is not a functional self-test.

### Success

The cooktop is off and confirmed cool. Ordinary soil on accessible exterior material is gently removed, cleaner residue wiped away, and the area dried, without removing parts, scraping a coating, or sending liquid into openings. Stopping for tougher soil, abnormal equipment, or missing guidance is also a complete decision.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Controls are off, so you touch the surface immediately:** Do not test temperature with skin. Wait for the model's cooling condition.
- **A glass scraper from another brand's video is used:** Stop and check the exact model. Some coatings can be damaged by scrapers.
- **Cleaner sprays into controls or a burner gap:** Stop adding liquid and follow model guidance for ingress. Do not open the equipment to “dry inside.”
- **A hard burnt patch resists a soft cloth:** Stop adding force and find the maker's specific method. The cooktop is not a pan bottom to scour.
- **Cleaner film or water remains:** Rewipe within approved limits with clean damp and dry cloths under the product label; do not resume use while uncertain.
- **Smell, cracks, or controls become abnormal:** Stop use and follow local and maker professional or emergency instructions. A clean exterior does not erase an equipment fault.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This covers **accessible exterior surfaces** of a normally working device and ordinary soil. The exact model and cleaning-product label define the permitted actions. Glass coatings, steel, gas, and induction designs do not share disassembly, scraper, or liquid rules. For limited standing or hand use, wait for full cooling and work in a stable accessible posture, in small areas, or with trusted help. Stop if access cannot be safe. Stubborn burnt residue may need a later model-specific process; it is not a reason to damage equipment in this round.
