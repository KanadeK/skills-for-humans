## 中文

当你已有一桶**正在分解的普通庭院堆肥**，厨房或院子又积了一小批可能可投的植物边角时，加载这份 Skill。结果是每样材料经许可筛选、少量加入且被干料覆盖，桶能重新合上；与从空桶建第一堆不同。当地收集规则和现有堆的状态优先；发臭、积水或动物已经闯入时先走故障处理，不继续喂料。

### 准备与输入

在封闭暂存容器中拿来一小批来源清楚的果蔬边角、咖啡渣或获准庭院植物碎料，以及足够的干落叶、普通未上蜡纸板等干料。按本地规则和 EPA 的家庭排除表复核，剥掉果蔬贴纸、塑胶袋、胶带；肉奶油脂、宠物粪、病株、喷过不明药的草不进桶。戴合用手套，备可洗的钝铲；洗手设施可用。

### 执行

1. **先看现有堆能否接收。** 盖和屏障完好，没有大量裸露旧食物、异常恶臭或积水。若有这些问题，暂停本批，先做异味/动物或水分检查，不能靠再盖一层新料解决。
2. **逐样筛本批。** 在可见处挑掉包装、贴纸和不合规材料；身份不明或当地服务不接受的东西留在堆外。大块合格植物料可安全手掰小些，不使用危险刀具或机器追求碎度。
3. **小量埋入并盖干料。** 在活跃堆的一处拨开表层干料，放入合格小批，再用足够干料完全盖住可见食物，保持一定空气隙。若干料不够，暂停投这一批并走当地收集；不让果皮裸露招动物。
4. **关盖并收尾。** 确认桶能关闭、外边没有碎屑，记录本次投料类别与日期；暂存桶洗净或按实际情况清理，脱手套洗手。下一次按原维护节奏查看，不把续投当成成熟度检查。

### 完成、常见问题与恢复

一小批合格材料进入原堆、已被干料盖住、桶和周边整洁，或因不合格/干料不足而明确停止。把一整袋未筛厨余直接倾倒，不算本流程。

- **发现贴纸、塑胶或不明袋：** 拣出并按当地规则处理，不赌它会自行分解。
- **干料不够：** 停止本批，先备合格干料或改投当地收集。
- **桶已满或盖不上：** 不在顶上堆料；转到合用新桶/收集服务或等待成熟批次。
- **虫/动物围着裸食物：** 暂停续投，按故障流程改盖和屏障；不要徒手驱赶动物。
- **手眼/行动限制：** 小容器、短距离、可信协助筛料与关盖，但由你核对投料资格。

### 假设与边界

“可堆肥”标识不意味着普通家庭桶能分解该包装；商业设施的接受范围另查。家用桶处理普通合格植物料也不保证杀灭病原体；本篇不指导用于食用作物。

## English

Load this Skill when an **ordinary backyard compost bin is already active** and a small new batch of possible plant scraps has accumulated. Finish with each item screened, a modest accepted amount added and covered with browns, and the bin closed. This differs from starting an empty bin. Local rules and the current pile state come first. If it already smells foul, pools water, or animals have entered, route to troubleshooting before feeding it again.

### Preparation and inputs

Bring a small known batch of fruit/vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, or permitted yard trimmings in a closed holding container, plus enough dry leaves or plain unwaxed cardboard. Recheck local rules and EPA household exclusions. Remove produce stickers, plastic bags, and tape. Keep meat, dairy, grease, pet waste, diseased plants, and uncertain treated grass out. Use suitable gloves and a washable blunt scoop with handwashing available.

### Execution

1. **Check whether the existing pile can accept feed.** Lid and barrier are sound with no large exposed old food, abnormal foul odor, or standing water. If any appears, hold the batch and check odor/animals or moisture first; fresh feed under another thin cover is not the fix.
2. **Screen this batch item by item.** Remove packaging, stickers, and ineligible material where visible. Leave uncertain or locally unaccepted things out. Break large eligible plant pieces by hand if safe, without dangerous blades or machines for a finer size.
3. **Bury a modest portion and cover with browns.** Open one area of the active pile's brown top, put in eligible scraps, and fully cover visible food with enough dry brown material while leaving air space. With too few browns, stop this batch and use local collection instead of leaving peels exposed.
4. **Close and clean up.** Confirm the lid closes and no scraps remain around the bin. Record date and material category; clean the holding container as appropriate, remove gloves, and wash hands. Return to the normal later check; adding feed is not a maturity test.

### Success, common problems, and recovery

A small eligible batch enters the existing pile, is covered with browns, and the bin and surroundings are tidy, or a missing requirement stops the batch. Dumping an unscreened kitchen bag is not this process.

- **Stickers, plastic, or an unknown bag appear:** Remove and follow local disposal rules; do not assume home breakdown.
- **Not enough browns:** Hold the batch until suitable dry cover exists or use local collection.
- **Bin is full or lid will not close:** Do not heap more on top; use a suitable new bin or service, or wait for an older batch.
- **Insects or animals crowd exposed food:** Pause feeding and correct covering and barriers under troubleshooting; do not confront animals bare-handed.
- **Grip, sight, or walking is limited:** Use small containers, a short route, and trusted screening or lid help while you confirm eligibility.

### Assumptions and limits

A “compostable” package need not break down in a home bin; check the industrial facility's accepted list separately. An ordinary home bin is not a pathogen-kill guarantee or a food-crop use instruction.
