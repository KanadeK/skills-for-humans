## 中文

当你想买一台**便携式、用于普通颗粒物过滤**的房间空气净化器，却只看到“适用 X 平方米”宣传时，加载这份 Skill。目标是核对准确型号的颗粒物洁净空气量（CADR）及其面积假设是否覆盖你计划使用的空间。它不诊断空气问题、不承诺治疗过敏或阻止感染，也不处理整屋暖通系统。

### 准备与输入

确定实际要放机器的一间房或连续开放区域，量长、宽和层高；若门通常敞开，记下连通范围。准备候选准确型号的产品说明，找 CADR 数值及单位、对应颗粒物类别、标称适用面积的计算条件和额定风速。想在安静档长期使用时，还要找该档的性能资料；最高档 CADR 不能自动算作低档 CADR。

### 执行

1. **画出要服务的空间。** 用长×宽求地面面积；不规则区域分块相加。开放式空间按实际连通范围算，不把敞开的客餐厅当一个关门小卧室。
2. **读同一种能力。** 核对候选的颗粒物 CADR 与单位，以及它针对烟、灰尘或花粉等哪类测试颗粒。CADR 是颗粒物的性能指标，不是气体或异味的通用清除保证。只写“HEPA”或“适用面积”而无可追溯测试条件时，保留未知。
3. **核对面积假设。** 对照制造商或可信测试机构给出的该型号适用面积及层高、运行档位条件。EPA 的面积/CADR 示例以约 8 英尺层高为前提；层高更高或连续开放区域更大时，不照搬该示例结论。
4. **检查能否按条件使用。** 看预定位置是否允许产品说明要求的进出风空间；如果噪声使你不会用标示 CADR 所在的高档，寻找实际愿意运行档位的性能依据，缺失时标“该用法未确认”。
5. **输出一条有限结论。** 记录“房间面积/层高与连通状态 → 型号与颗粒物 CADR → 测试档位/适用面积条件 → 满足、偏小或未确认”。只让它支持购前规格选择，不把性能数值变成个人健康承诺。

### 成功与停止

完成时，你能说出准确型号在明确的颗粒物类别、风速和空间假设下是否覆盖目标房间。若目标是处理燃气、危险化学暴露、霉菌根源或具体疾病，停止这份普通选购流程并使用相关专业指导；单台净化器不能替代去除污染源和适当通风。

### 常见报错与补救

- **只看“最大适用面积”：** 找对应 CADR、层高、颗粒物类别和档位；条件缺失就问制造商或换资料完整的型号。
- **把关门卧室面积用于开放客厅：** 重新量连通范围；不能确认边界时不要按最小房间下单。
- **安静档当最高档用：** 找该档性能，或承认只能确认最高档条件。较安静不等于仍有同样过滤速度。
- **无法测量或看清小字：** 请有权限的人代量并读回数值，使用可放大的说明书或可读文本；不可靠的读数保留为未确认。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

本 Skill 只比较普通家用便携机器的**颗粒物**额定覆盖，地区单位、测试标准和产品配置可能不同，先统一单位再比。若没有完整面积数据，可先画带尺寸的平面草图再补量；无需上传家庭平面图到陌生服务。你可能发现自己需要改选型号或放置方案，而不需要相信包装上的最大数字。

## English

Load this Skill before buying a **portable room air cleaner for ordinary particle filtration** when the main claim is “covers X square metres.” Your task is to compare the exact model's particle clean air delivery rate (CADR) and room-size conditions with the space you will actually serve. It does not diagnose an air problem, promise allergy treatment or infection prevention, or select a whole-home HVAC system.

### Preparation and inputs

Identify the room or continuously open area where the unit will operate. Measure length, width, and ceiling height; note connected spaces when doors normally stay open. Obtain the exact model's CADR value and unit, particle category, stated room-size assumptions, and rating fan speed. If you expect to use a quiet setting, look for performance information at that setting; maximum-speed CADR does not automatically apply at a lower speed.

### Execution

1. **Map the served space.** Multiply length by width for a rectangular floor; add simple sections for an irregular area. Include connected open space rather than treating an open living/dining area as a closed bedroom.
2. **Compare the same capability.** Check the particle CADR, its unit, and whether it represents smoke, dust, pollen, or another stated test. CADR rates particle removal; it is not a general guarantee about gases or odours. “HEPA” or a room-size slogan without traceable conditions leaves a question open.
3. **Check the area assumptions.** Compare your area and ceiling with the maker's or credible test body's stated room size and operating speed for this model. EPA's example room-size table assumes an approximately eight-foot ceiling. A taller ceiling or larger open area cannot inherit its result unchanged.
4. **Check the intended operating condition.** Make sure the planned position can meet the product's inlet and outlet clearance instructions. If noise means you will not use the high speed behind the advertised CADR, seek data for the speed you will use; otherwise mark that use unconfirmed.
5. **Write a limited result.** Record “room area/height and open connections → exact model and particle CADR → test speed and room assumptions → suitable, undersized, or unconfirmed.” This supports a pre-purchase specification choice, not a personal health outcome.

### Success and stop conditions

You finish when you can state whether the exact model covers the target room for a specified particle category, speed, and space assumption. Stop this everyday shopping route for gas or hazardous-chemical exposure, a mould source, or a specific illness and use appropriate specialist guidance. One air cleaner does not replace pollution-source control and suitable ventilation.

### Common errors and recovery

- **Only “maximum room size” was read:** Find the matching CADR, ceiling, particle category, and speed. Ask the maker or choose a documented model if those are missing.
- **A closed-bedroom area was used for an open living room:** Measure the connected area again. Do not order against the smallest room when its boundary is unknown.
- **Quiet mode was treated as maximum speed:** Find rating data for that mode, or limit your conclusion to the documented high-speed condition. Quieter operation does not preserve the same filtration rate by assumption.
- **Measuring or small print is inaccessible:** Ask someone with access to measure and read the values back, or use an enlarged or readable manual. Keep unreliable measurements unconfirmed.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

This Skill checks rated **particle** coverage for an ordinary portable home unit. Units, test standards, and configurations vary by market; convert units before comparing. If a full room measurement is unavailable, draw a dimensioned sketch and fill gaps later. You need not upload a floor plan to an unfamiliar service. The result may be another model or placement, rather than belief in the largest number on a box.

### Sources

- [US EPA: Guide to Air Cleaners in the Home](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/guide-air-cleaners-home) — CADR applies to particles, its example area has a ceiling-height assumption, and high versus low speeds differ; filtration cannot remove all pollutants or replace source control.
- [ENERGY STAR: Room air cleaner buying guidance](https://www.energystar.gov/products/air_cleaners) — compare room area with CADR and account for higher ceilings; a US-market reference, not a model-specific health guarantee.
