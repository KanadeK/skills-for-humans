## 中文

当你确实读过一段，却只能说“这一段我不懂”，用这份 Skill 找出**哪一句、哪个关系或哪一步**卡住，把它变成能带回原文的问题。结果不是强行回答，也不是向老师发消息；找人请教属于 H258。你可以让问题最终标“原文已解答”或“仍待核”，但不能只留下一个没有出处的大问号。

### 准备与输入

拿着你有权使用的书、长文、网页或音频，以及可定位的版次、章节、小标题、页码/网页段首/时间码。只选已经实际读过的一小段，别从搜索摘要或别人转述制造“自己的阅读困惑”。借阅本不能画就用私人纸/便签，不抄长段受保护文字；低视力、读屏或听书可用标题/时间码替代动态页码。涉及专业医疗、法律、财务内容时只澄清讲法，不把自己的读后推断当决策建议。

### 执行

1. **指出卡住的准确位置。** 写“在____章/小节，作者从____转到____时我跟丢了”，或“____术语在这里是什么意思？”把文句位置用本版本的可回查线索记下。整章都不懂时，先挑第一处断点，不一次问十个问题。
2. **写出你已懂的一半。** 用自己的话写“我认为前一句说____；后一句似乎说____”，把矛盾或缺口露出来。原文确实没交代的就标“这里未见解释”，不想象作者本意。
3. **在有限邻近范围试找答案。** 看本段前后、定义、标题、图注或作者给的例子是否已解释它；若找到，标解答位置并用一句话核是否真的消除困惑。不要无限跳网页或用不可信摘要覆盖原文。
4. **写一个能回答的问题。** 例如“这里的‘它’指上一段哪个对象？”、“作者用图 2 支持哪一步？”、“在____条件下这个说法还成立吗？”保留具体主语、关系和原文位置；不把“请解释全部”当单个问题。
5. **标实际状态与下一步。** 写“已在____位置解答”或“仍未解；下次看____/向____请教”，并留你已经查过哪里，避免重复搜索。如果文本根本不提供答案，问题仍可完成，不编造确定解答。

### 成功、常见问题与补救

你有**一个具体问题、能返回的文本位置、已懂部分、尝试过的相邻检查和已解/未解状态**。只有一个荧光问号，却说不清问什么和在哪里，不满足本篇。

- **问题太大：** 从“为什么整章这样写”缩到第一个无法连上的概念/句子，后续问题另记。
- **问题其实已在下一句回答：** 写解答与位置，仍是一次有效定位；不用为了“保留问题”假装未解。
- **你只记页码，换设备找不到：** 补章节/标题和段首短语或音频时间码。
- **找不到可信答案：** 留未解与已查范围，带到老师/同伴或可靠来源，不用 AI/论坛一句话充当原文证明。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

读者对词汇、背景和注意条件不同，问题大小也不同；不以“读得慢”诊断能力。纸笔、语音便签、读屏或可信协助都可记录位置和疑问；若别人帮你解释，标明它是他人解释，尚可回原文核对。此 Skill结束于形成或澄清一个具体问题，不负责把许多笔记整理成学习资料或保证未来记住答案。

## English

Use this Skill when you have actually read a passage yet can only say “I do not understand this part.” Locate **the sentence, relation, or step** where you got stuck and turn it into a question tied to the text. The outcome is not a forced answer or a message to a teacher; asking another person belongs to H258. Your question can end as “answered in text” or “still open,” but not as a contextless question mark.

### Preparation and inputs

Have your authorised book, long article, page, or audio and a retrievable edition, chapter, heading, page, web opening phrase, or timestamp. Select one small part you really read, not a search snippet or someone else's account. Use private paper or notes for a borrowed copy that must not be marked, and paraphrase rather than copy a long protected passage. With low vision, a screen reader, or audio, heading or timestamp can replace dynamic page numbering. For medical, legal, or financial material this only clarifies wording; your reading inference is no professional decision advice.

### Execution

1. **Point to the exact break.** Write “in chapter or section ____, I lose the connection when the author moves from ____ to ____,” or “what does this term mean here?” Add a locator for this edition. If the whole chapter feels unclear, start at the first break rather than ask ten questions at once.
2. **State the part you did grasp.** In your words, “I think the earlier sentence says ____; the next seems to say ____.” This exposes the missing relation or tension. If the author has not explained it here, mark “no explanation found here,” not an imagined intention.
3. **Try a bounded nearby check.** Look at neighbouring sentences, a definition, heading, figure caption, or example. If an answer appears, note where and test in one sentence whether it resolves the issue. Do not open endless unrelated pages or replace the source with an untrusted summary.
4. **Write one answerable question.** “What does ‘it’ refer to in the previous paragraph?” “Which step does figure 2 support?” or “Does this still apply when ____?” Keep subject, relation, and text location. “Explain everything” is not one question.
5. **Mark status and next path.** Write “answered at ____” or “still open; next check ____ / ask ____,” including what you already tried so you do not repeat the search. A text with no answer can still yield a completed question; do not invent certainty.

### Success, common errors, and recovery

You have **one specific question, a retrievable source location, what you already understand, a nearby check attempted, and an answered or open state**. A coloured question mark without what or where does not complete the task.

- **The question is too broad:** Shrink “why is the whole chapter written this way” to the first unconnected term or sentence. Record later questions separately.
- **The next sentence already answers it:** Record the answer and location. A resolved question is still a useful finding; do not keep it falsely open.
- **Only a page number remains and your device changed:** Add chapter or heading and opening phrase or audio timestamp.
- **No trustworthy answer appears:** Keep the open state and checked range for a teacher, peer, or reliable source. Do not treat one AI or forum sentence as proof of what the text meant.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and side effects

Readers differ in vocabulary, background, and attention. A slow reading pace is no diagnosis. Paper, voice note, screen reader, or trusted help can record location and question. If another person explains it, label that as their interpretation to check against the source. This Skill ends with one formed or clarified question, not a reusable collection of notes or a guarantee of future recall.
