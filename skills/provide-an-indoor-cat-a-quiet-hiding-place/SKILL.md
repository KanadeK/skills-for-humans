## 中文

当你照料的普通室内猫缺少一处能够自己进入、休息并离开的藏身处时，加载这份 Skill。结果是一个**安静、稳定、有退出路、不会被人强行打扰**的真实位置。E005 第一批的玩耍邀请猫参与；这里要给它拒绝参与的空间。

### 准备与输入

看这只猫平时喜欢低处纸盒、半遮挡窝还是安全可达的高处；不强制买专用产品。准备稳固、无尖边/松线、能通风且可清理的空间，避开会突然关上的柜门、危险电线、食水和砂盆旁边。多猫家庭要避免另一只猫堵出口。猫突然比平时躲得多、伴随不适或不吃不喝时先联系兽医，不把加盒子当诊断。

### 执行

1. **选真实可达的位置。** 安静、少有人路过，猫不需跨越危险高度；你也能在必要时看见或安全接近，但不能把门一关困住它。
2. **搭一处稳固遮蔽。** 用完整纸盒、适合的猫窝或现有家具空位，检查承重、通风、没有能缠/吞的小件，并留猫能独立进出的开口。高处只在高度与路径对这只猫安全时使用。
3. **保留原有选择。** 将新处放在猫熟悉的区域，可加它原有且干净的垫物；不要一夜撤掉所有旧藏点或为了“训练”把它抱进去。
4. **向家人说明边界。** 约定猫进去后不拉出来、不用手伸进角落抓、不允许儿童/访客堵口或逗弄；它自己出来时才选择是否互动。
5. **观察并调整。** 记它是否自愿使用，位置是否太吵、太热或被别的宠物占住。持续异常藏匿或身体状态变化应求兽医/合格行为帮助，不能只把盒子搬来搬去。

### 成功条件

有一处稳定、可自主进出的隐蔽位置，家中其他人知道“不打扰”的规则，猫真实使用与否有复查点。猫暂不进去也不是失败；危险位置或健康异常则先停止并解决实际问题。

### 常见报错与补救

- **猫窝放在洗衣机旁：** 改到更安静、猫能到达的地方。
- **纸盒出口被家具挡住：** 重新摆放，保留清楚的进出路径。
- **孩子把猫从窝里拉出：** 立刻停止打扰，明确区域规则并提供监督。
- **猫突然整天躲着且不进食：** 及时联系兽医，而不是新增第二个纸盒作为治疗。

### 假设、替代与现实副作用

这里只安排普通已照料室内猫的一处日常安全退避点，不处理疾病、攻击行为或多猫冲突治疗。你的行动能力有限时可用低位稳固盒子，视力受限时请人确认开口与松线。现实副作用是一块需保留给猫的空间，以及别人要遵守的边界。

## English

Load this Skill when an ordinary indoor cat in your care lacks a place it can enter, rest in, and leave by choice. Finish with a **quiet, stable, escapable refuge people will not force it out of**. E005 part 001's play invites interaction; this provides a way to decline it.

### Preparation and inputs

Notice whether this cat prefers a low box, partly covered bed, or safely reachable higher spot. No branded product is required. Choose a stable, ventilated, cleanable space without sharp edges, loose string, closing cabinet doors, dangerous wiring, or immediate food/water/litter proximity. Another cat must not block the exit. For a sudden increase in hiding, discomfort, or not eating/drinking, contact a veterinarian rather than treating another box as a diagnosis.

### Execution

1. **Choose a truly reachable place.** It should be quiet, off busy traffic, and need no dangerous jump. You should be able to check it safely if needed, but it must not trap the cat behind a shut door.
2. **Make one stable cover.** Use a sound cardboard box, appropriate cat bed, or furniture space. Check support, airflow, swallowable/tangling parts, and an independent exit. Use a higher position only if the route and height suit this cat.
3. **Keep familiar options.** Put the new spot in a known area and add existing clean bedding if useful. Do not remove every old hide overnight or carry the cat into the new one as “training.”
4. **Tell the household the boundary.** Agree not to pull a hiding cat out, reach in and grab it, or let children/guests block or tease it. Interaction is offered when the cat comes out.
5. **Observe and adjust.** Note actual voluntary use, noise, heat, and competition from other animals. Persistent abnormal hiding or bodily changes need veterinary or qualified behavior help, not endless box relocation.

### Success

There is one stable refuge with a cat-controlled exit; other people know the no-disturbance rule, and use has a review point. It is not failure if the cat declines the new place. Stop for an unsafe site or health concern and address the real issue.

### Common errors and recovery

- **The bed is beside a washer:** Move it to a quieter accessible place.
- **Furniture blocks the box opening:** Reposition it to keep a clear route.
- **A child pulls the cat out:** Stop the disturbance, explain the boundary, and supervise access.
- **The cat suddenly hides all day and stops eating:** Contact a veterinarian promptly instead of treating a second box as care.

### Assumptions, alternatives, and known side effects

This is one ordinary retreat for a cat already in your care, not illness care, aggression treatment, or multi-cat conflict therapy. Use a stable low box if mobility limits placement; ask someone to check the opening and loose string if vision is limited. One piece of space now belongs to the cat and its household boundary.
