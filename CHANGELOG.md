### Safety

- Objective food and laundry facts retain short official/primary source links while all prose is original.
- Medical, legal, financial, crisis-intervention, gas/electrical repair, credential-heavy, and real-world-control Skills are excluded.
- No Skill claims Tested on human runtime without an actual recorded human walkthrough.
